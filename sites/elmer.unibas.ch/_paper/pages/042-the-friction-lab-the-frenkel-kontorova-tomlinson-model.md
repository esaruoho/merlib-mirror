Frenkel-Kontorova-Tomlinson Model

The Frenkel-Kontorova model is a

harmonic chain
 (mimic a soft layer of atoms)
contected to a rigid upper plate via leaf springs.
The chain feels a 
spatially periodic potential
 (mimic the substrate).
The upper plate is driven by a constant

velocity
. The chain is damped
by a 
velocity-proportional damping forces

(emitting waves into the substrate and into the upper plate).
