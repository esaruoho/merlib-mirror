The Riess Institute - Mission Statement 

 Mission 
Statement 

 The Riess Institute exists to provide deep-seated potable water for both
people and agriculture. 

Crystalline rock and its intersecting fracture zones have been found to contain large volumes of deep-seated water which, more often than not, is of potable quality. Using knowledge of mineralogy, petrology and structural geology, the Riess Institute has developed an approach for identifying and exploring ancient hydrothermal systems as a potable water resource. Found wherever the geologic terrain and site conditions favor earth-generated water, these natural conduits have replenished the hydrosphere when other sources have failed.
