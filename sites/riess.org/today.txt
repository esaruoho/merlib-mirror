The Riess Institute established in 1983 as the Riess Foundation, is a 
non-profit, public Institute with a 501(c)(3) classification as granted 
by the United States Internal Revenue Service. 

The Riess Concept believes that water is continually produced and 
expelled upward from deep within the earth and is available as a potable 
and abundant resource by standard drilling practices.
