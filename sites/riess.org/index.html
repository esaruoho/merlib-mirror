Welcome to the Riess Institute

The Riess Institute has found new sources of water where others have not. The Institute exists to provide deep-seated potable water to both people and agriculture. The Institute is structured around the concept of earth-generated water and the technology of Stephan Riess and Morad Eghbal in the area of locating and drilling for new water resources in order to replenish and feed a thirsty world.
