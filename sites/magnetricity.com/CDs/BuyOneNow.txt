 Frequently Asked Questions 

 What should I know about EMAIL DELIVERY? 
 Please be sure that your Email Account Mailbox can accept Binary Attachments that are 2MB or larger in size.
 If you're unsure of your Email Account limitations, check with your Service Provider before ordering.
 Mailboxes often have an overall size limitation so please be sure your mailbox has room for an Email of this size. 
 We will make (3) three attempts to deliver your schematic by Email. In the event that your address returns to us as
 undeliverable, we will try to contact you to make other arrangements or you can contact us again after 24 hours.

 What about the schematics themselves?: 
 All schematics are in Web standard JPEG format and are viewable in any Web Browser.
 Averaging 2000 x 1400 pixels at 300 dpi in 256 level gray scale and ranging from 250K to 1.3MB in size.
 Most Email accounts can handle attachments of this size, but check with your ISP to be sure.

 Other General Concerns: 
 All of incoming and outgoing Email traffic is thoroughly Virus Scanned for our mutual protection.
 If you have not received any Email from us within 24 hours of placing your order,
 please use our Contact Form to notify us directly. Or Email us from another account. 
 We will make every effort to deliver your schematic. 
 Your satisfaction is important to us. 
 Refunds arranged at our discretion only.

 Thanks for your Order! and the time you've spent with us Good Luck with your new Project!
