Jeannette Kavanagh





Ph.D, M.Ed, B.Ed, Dip. Learning Solutions in Counselling, Dip Fine Art, Grad Dip Curriculum Administration, Dip Teaching. 



Jeannette established her consultancy Jeannette Kavanagh and Daughter
in 1996, after many years in education.  Since 1986, she’s
provided group and individual coaching in public speaking skills
including how to manage performance anxiety/terror.  



In 2005 Jeannette wanted a career re-alignment.  She was delighted
to find the Diploma of learning solutions in counselling with its focus
on solutions rather than problems, and on client strength rather than
counsellor-as-guru. Dr Kavanagh helps clients to articulate their
concerns and to re-connect with their strengths so that they can create
their unique solutions.  



As well as successful careers in education, writing, consultancy and
coaching, Jeannette has run a wholesaling business.  So
she’s always thrilled to discuss that question confronting any
age group: ‘what will I be when I grow up?’.  



Jeannette’s practice is in Armadale.  

She can be contacted on 0417 379 684 or 
jkavanagh@ains.net.au
