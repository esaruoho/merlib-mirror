Emma Warnecke  
Dr  Emma Warnecke  
MBBS(Hons), FRACGP, Dip.LSH
Emma is an experienced General Practitioner who has been practicing wholistically since 1998.  Her focus has always been on treating the whole person, mind body and spirit, with a strong emphasis on preventive medicine, health enhancement and counselling.  The solution oriented approach works in delightfully with this focus.   She completed a Diploma of Learning Solutions in Hypnotherapy with Dr Rob McNeilly in 2004.   She is also recognised by the Royal Australian College of General practitioners as a doctor with a special interest and post graduate qualifications in focussed psychological strategies.
Originally from Melbourne, she now has a vibrant counselling and hypnotherapy practice in Hobart full of possibilities for clients to explore solutions in an enjoyable, supportive and relaxing environment.  Emma’s areas of interest include stress and anxiety management, sleep enhancement, mood disorders and improving relationships with self and others.  The focus is on wellbeing and quality of life.
