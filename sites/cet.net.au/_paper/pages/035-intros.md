Solution Oriented Family Therapy


A one day introduction


In this 1 day workshop we will explore simple elegant methods to create
a solution mood, and generate new ways for families to be together. The
focus will be on those elements beyond technique that are common to all
therapies that account for effectiveness, and we will explore how we as
therapists can actively generate these essential ingredients of therapy.





This interactive workshop emphasises demonstration and practice over
lecture presentation so that participants can expect to be able to use
the learning from this learning experience in their practice
immediately, and confidently.  








Presenter:   Rob McNeilly and Gabrielle Peacock 


dates - TBA

An Introduction to the Ericksonian Approach

    •  
 the language of hypnosis

Learning Solutions in Counselling


An Introduction to the Solution Orientation





Participants will be offered:-


    •  
 explanations of theoretical
and practical issues related to the latest developments in the field;


    •  
 maps to more effectively
negotiate the process of engaging the client in their therapeutic
process;


    •  
 demonstrations and
supervised practice to gain confidence generating effective solutions
with clients, whether face to face or in telephone conversations.





Presenter:   Rob McNeilly
