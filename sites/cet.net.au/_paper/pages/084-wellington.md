Rob McNeilly MBBS 
was in a suburban Melbourne general medical practice for 10 years, had the privilege of learning directly with 
Milton Erickson

He founded the CET in l988 to introduce Ericksonian Hypnosis and the Solution Oriented Approach to hypnosis, counselling and coaching in Australia. For more than 30 years, the contribution he brings i his teaching style, writing and unique approaches to learning and therapy allow for a ready application into everyday living and are recognised locally, nationally and internationally, including repeated invited presentations at the Milton H Erickson Foundation’s International Congresses in USA since 1980 and workshops in Singapore and Denmark since 1999.

Solution oriented hypnosis and mindfulness skills
a 2 day intensive building on an Ericksonian approach
Wellington
March 22nd & 23rd, 2014
with Dr Rob McNeilly
Mindfulness has been around for centuries and is experiencing a wave of new interest. Originally a spiritual practice, some components are becoming a popular adjunct to psychotherapy with a growing accumulation of evidence for its efficacy in helping a wide range of clinical problems from anxiety and depression to more extreme problems.
When we explore the benefits that the Ericksonian solution approach to hypnosis offers - creating a direct experience of a preferred outcome [a solution] - and integrate this with the healing experiences of mindfulness, each extends the depth and breadth of the other, taking our effectiveness and personal satisfaction to the next level.
This 2 day intensive offers a unique opportunity to be immersed in the experience, principles and applications of two of the most rapidly growing treatment models in contemporary psychotherapy.
The scope of the program is comprehensive, and presents the essential elements in an elegantly accessible way. This workshop is open to practitioners of all levels of experience who are passionate about their work.
The workshop includes lectures, demonstrations and recurrent practice in a safe and supportive environment, and includes:
an introduction to the principles and practice of the solution orientation
experiences of mindfulness skills
exploration of ways of tailoring our approach to each unique individual
playing with invitational language forms
a session outline to start from and vary from
Participants will learn to:
learn and use simple mindfulness skills in daily life
engage the client in their therapeutic process
generate an experience of the client’s preferred outcome to create a lasting resolution
improve their therapeutic efficacy and personal satisfaction
immediately and confidently apply the principles and practices of this approach to specific clinical situations
Registrants will have free access to a 4 hour recorded conference on mindfulness with contributors including Michael Yapko, Buddhist monk Thupten Lekshe, Ronald Segal and Douglas Flemons.
