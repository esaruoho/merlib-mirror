a week of hypnosis
Have you had an interest in hypnosis and want to add it to your practice skills? 
Are you already using hypnosis and interested to learn about Milton Erickson’s approach? 
Would you like to simplify the Ericksonian approach if you are already familiar with it?
Is reconnecting with the basics, not only of Ericksonian hypnosis, interesting to you?
If you could add to your professional effectiveness and personal satisfaction, would that be helpful?
In this 5 day workshop, you will learn:
2 additional fail-safe approaches to invite anyone into hypnosis
6 questions which will refine and clarify therapeutic goals
5 methods to create lasting solutions with any client
There will be 
a minimum of theory
a safe and supportive environment
recurrent opportunities to learn, observe demonstrations, and practise
Milton Erickson held a series of Teaching Seminars in Phoenix in the 1970s, and many people include myself learnt from their experiences and I have decided to follow that tradition. Where Erickson was a consummate teacher, I am better able to create an opportunity for learning to happen.
In this week of hypnosis, there will be time to immerse ourselves in the experience, with no fixed agenda, and allow the opportunity to explore any aspect of hypnosis as it relates to our work or our person. It will be a delightful addition to anyone already familiar with this approach and also provide a delightful opportunity for anyone wanting to learn hypnosis or the Ericksonian approach for the first time. Because the days will be experiential, learning by direct experience can happen without the need for any formal structure. This was how I learnt with Erickson himself.
I continue to look forward to the experience of sitting for five consecutive days with a group of interested people – interested to explore their own learning, their own skills, to extend the experience of the solution oriented approach, and immerse themselves in the mood of this work, interested to consolidate what they have already learnt, interested to extend this.
The seminar will be limited to 12 participants, so there will be a lot of flexibility to design personal work, to meet particular individual’s needs, to practice further in a safe environment, to bring a client, to meet and learn with and from others doing similar yet different work, and to reflect … such a rare and delightful opportunity.
I promise that you will benefit substantially from this experience, and offer a no-questions-asked money-back guarantee
