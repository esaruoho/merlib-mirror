On-line Learning Group
with Rob McNeilly 
Principles
Supervision has been regarded an an opportunity to review cases, get advice, expand our possibilities of perceptions and interventions. In this online learning group, we will explore personal issues which limit our effectiveness in working with clients. Rather than focusing on cases, we will learn together how to expand our personal options as human beings in a safe and respectful environment.
Structure for 2015
The format will be a number of online workshops using zoom [see below for details] and each participant will have up to an opportunity for a personal hypnosis session and there will be time for the others to reflect, comment and learn. I will record each sessions for the individual’s personal use.
This will happen on a Tuesday from 9am - 10 am Hobart Time - May 12 & 26, June 9 & 23, July 7 &21,  August 4 & 18, September 1, 15 & 29, October 6 & 20, November 3 & 17, December 1, 2015.
