monthly hypnosis video club











Serious students of hypnosis have been requesting opportunities to
observe hypnosis sessions with clients as well as participants in our
training programmes.











Since 2007, and with the creation of The Milton H Erickson Institute of
Tasmania, a number of clinicians have asked me to work with their
client to expand options, increase effectiveness, and
contribute
to our shared opportunities to learn together.











Each session is between 30 and 60 minutes duration, and are offered,
with sincere gratitude for the generosity
of the clients and their therapists, and confidentiality is required
and assumed.











Topics include self esteem, confidence, sexual abuse, weight issues,
marijuana use and pain, among a
varied and growing collection.











Members will be emailed a link, and notified as a new session is posted
each month. The previous month's session will be removed, although
these can also be purchased to have permanently for $A35.
