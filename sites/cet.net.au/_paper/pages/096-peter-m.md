Solution Oriented Coaching and Counselling



Peter has worked as a teacher through primary and secondary schools, as
school principal and counselor, education consultant and managing
director of his own company.  He has found the work of Humberto
Maturana, Lao Tzu, Milton H. Erickson, Madeleine Maloney and Rob
McNeilly inspirational. Rather than follow a particular technique, his
approach is to bring to the counseling relationship all that he has
learned and to be fully available for the client’s concerns,
remembering always that we are unique individuals rather than a
condition or label.
