Liz McArthur 

B.A. (Psychology), Post. Grad. Dip (Psychology), Advanced Dip. Learning
Solutions in Hypnotherapy, Cert. IV Health (Clinical Hypnosis) Cert. IV
(Telephone Counselling) 

I have a background in psychology and counselling. After training
in and practicing more conventional hypnotherapy I decided to explore
solution focused hypnotherapy. I love the way this approach
rapidly builds clients&rsquo; connection with and confidence in their
own strengths and their ability to resolve concerns. 

I believe in clients' inner healing capacity and see my role as
respectfully facilitating that process, efficiently assisting them to
discover their own unique path to the future they desire. 

I&rsquo;m interested in working with all kinds of concerns, from
becoming more relaxed and comfortable in certain situations, creating
healthy habits, enhancing relationships and unlocking inner creativity
to improving personal performance for example, sporting performance.
