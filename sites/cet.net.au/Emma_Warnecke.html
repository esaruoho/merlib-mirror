Emma Warnecke

Dr  Emma Warnecke  

MBBS(Hons), FRACGP, Dip.LSH

Emma is an experienced General Practitioner who has been practicing wholistically since 1998.  Her focus has always been on treating the whole person, mind body and spirit, with a strong emphasis on preventative medicine, health enhancement and counselling.  A natural extension of this has been to develop a solution oriented approach. This has been enhanced by completing a Diploma of Learning Solutions in Hypnotherapy with Dr Rob McNeilly.

She developed a vibrant counselling and hypnotherapy practice in Melbourne’s bayside area full of possibilities for clients to explore solutions in an enjoyable, supportive and relaxing environment.  She has now moved to Hobart to continue this effective approach.  Emma’s areas of interest include stress and anxiety management, sleep enhancement, mood disorders and improving relationships with self and others.  The focus is on wellbeing and quality of life.  

To contact Emma for an appointment or enquiry email – emmawarnecke@cet.net.au or phone 0425 784 127
