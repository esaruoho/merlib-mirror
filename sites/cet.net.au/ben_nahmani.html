Ben Nahmani

Bachelor of Social Work, Diploma in Social and Community Services, Member of the Australian Association of Social Workers

Ben offers a non-judgmental and supportive space for people to share and explore difficulties and dilemmas, hopes and possibilities.  

Ben is both a therapist and social worker with extensive experience in the human services sector.  Ben has a long-standing commitment to working with individuals, couples and families in ways that acknowledge and honour their unique worth, dignity, and potential.

Ben also provides a consultancy and supervision service to agencies and other professionals working within the human services sector.

Ben is an Approved Medicare Provider.
