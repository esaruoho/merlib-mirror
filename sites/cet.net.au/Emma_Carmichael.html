Emma Carmichael - Solution oriented counselling, hypnotherapy including hypnobirthing and weight management [Hobart]

MBBS FRACGP


Emma Carmichael worked in general practice for ten years developing her interests in family medicine, weight management and counselling. In 2005 Emma commenced learning with the Centre for Effective Therapy Hobart, studying solution oriented counselling and hypnosis with Drs Gabrielle Peacock and Rob McNeilly.

Emma now runs her own private counselling practice, and chooses to bulk bill pensioners and health care card holders. Within this practice Emma welcomes anybody wishing to discuss and work on any concerns, in a safe and respectful environment. Emma is happy to see individuals, couples and families.

As a GP, Emma developed an interest in weight management and eating disorders. She uses a non dieting approach to weight management based on the work of Dr Rick Kausman. His methods blend beautifully with solution oriented couselling and hypnosis to offer individuals unique opportunities to reach comfortable and sustainable weight and lifestyle changes. The approach is also suitable for children.

Another area of special interest for Emma is hypnobirthing, (hypnosis for pain relief in labour). With colleague Lisa Birrell, Emma offers an evening course called “Easy Labour”. The course is open to women, and their partners, seeking a more comfortable and empowered birthing experience. The course runs over 6 weeks and is best suited to women in their last trimester of pregnancy. Some private health funds provide a partial rebate for these classes.
