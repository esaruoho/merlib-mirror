Madeleine Maloney   

Solution Oriented Therapy and Hypnosis for individuals, couples and families

Madeleine began learning from Rob in 1995 and has completed CET programs in counseling, coaching and hypnosis. She is particularly interested in experiential learning and the link between counseling and education. She is using a solution oriented approach in her work with children, adults, families and teachers. She is exploring and designing experiential learning experiences for children and their families. Madeleine’s love of living and learning, of art and music, of literature and nature, shines through her teaching and counseling. Madeleine is on the national register of Psychotherapy and Counseling Federation of Australia (PACFA) and is a member of the British Association for Counseling and Psychotherapy (BACP).
