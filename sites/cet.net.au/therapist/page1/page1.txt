 Peter W. Thorneycroft B.Sc., M.Ed., M.Ed.Psych., Adv.SOH., M.A.P.S. A psychologist since 1976, in order to facilitate the move from lecturing into beginning a private psychological practice, Peter began learning from Rob McNeilly in 1986. With a background in Educational Psychology, Peter assisted Rob in developing the initial learning assignments for CET programs, and was the Dean of Students at CET between 1989 and 1994. During this time he was also founding editor of etCETera. In 1996 he was awarded a Distinguished Service Award from CET. Peter & Rob have been recording relaxation CD&rsquo;s utilising hypnotic and ontological coaching principles since 1989, and you can order these CD&rsquo;s or simply check out samples of the recordings at Peter&rsquo;s website: www.peterthorneycroft.com In July 2005, in order to focus on his teaching programs, Rob asked Peter to take over his clinical practice. The trust implicit in Rob&rsquo;s request/offer is something Peter holds with gratitude, humility and joy. Peter&rsquo;s private practice is in Greensborough, in Melbourne&rsquo;s North-East.
