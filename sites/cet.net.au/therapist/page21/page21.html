Mark is a registered psychologist from Canberra, Australia, with over 15 years experience in Solution-Oriented/ Solution-Focused approaches to Brief Therapy. He has worked with a broad range of client populations, and in various settings including university counselling, community counselling centres and private practice. His roles have included direct service delivery, training, supervision and consultancy. He continues to practice Solution-Oriented Brief Therapy with enthusiasm for the approach, especially for the respect that it awards clients and the belief that it holds in their resources and potential for change.
