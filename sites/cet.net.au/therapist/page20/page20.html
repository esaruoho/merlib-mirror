 Friendly, professional and confidential service.

You will benefit from Raywyn’s experience in guidance and counselling both children and adults.
Raywyn has in the past been employed as a guidance counselor in schools both pre school, primary, middle and senior high schools where she worked as case coordinator bringing together many different personnel to create effective outcomes for those involved. She is a parent to four adult children and four grandchildren and understands the challenges faced by parents (including teenagers involved with drugs, cutting, body dysmorphia and how these can cause a parent grief and distress affecting daily worklife).
Raywyn has been zonal training coordinator for Far North Queensland Child Safety Out of Home Care (Foster Care) where she was involved in panel coordination for high support needs young people as well as staff and parent training and counselling.
Raywyn has also worked for Early Intervention Services with young people aged 10-17 and their families.

Her love of life is infectious and she particularly invites those who seek a peaceful mind, a happy heart and a playful spirit to spend time learning how to regain balance in all areas of life
