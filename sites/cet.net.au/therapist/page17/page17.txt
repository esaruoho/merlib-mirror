 Peter Maloney Solution Oriented Coaching and Counselling Peter has worked in NSW, Victoria, Western Australia, Poland, Ireland and Northern Territory as a teacher through primary and secondary schools, as school principal and counselor, education consultant and managing director of his own company. He has found inspiration through many, including: Humberto Maturana, Lao Tzu, Jesus, Milton H. Erickson, Madeleine Maloney and Rob McNeilly. Rather than follow a particular technique, his approach is to bring to the counseling relationship all that he has learned and to be fully available for the client's concerns, remembering always that we are unique individuals rather than a condition or label. email maloney.pb@gmail.com.
