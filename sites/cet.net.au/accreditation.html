Accreditation Details
All of Ampersand Australia’s diploma programmes have been carefully designed to achieve National Accreditation by complying with the minimal standards of ASOCHA [Association of Solution Oriented Counsellors and Hypnotherapists of Australia] which is a member organization of PACFA [Psychotherapy and Counselling Federation of Australia].

After successfully completing a 2 year diploma or two diplomas with Ampersand Australia, Full Membership of ASOCHA becomes available.

Ampersand Australia also provides further training and clinical supervision for anyone wanting to further their learning and achieve national recognition on the PAFCA National Register.
