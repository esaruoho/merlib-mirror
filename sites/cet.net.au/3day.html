Ericksonian hypnosis - a 3 day intensive


in Melbourne


simply learning a natural approach to solutions in hypnosis

with Rob McNeilly


In this 3 day intensive, CET offers a unique opportunity to learn the principles and applications of one of the most rapidly growing treatment models in contemporary psychotherapy. The scope of the program is comprehensive, and presents the essential elements in an elegantly accessible way.
The 3 day intensive is open to practitioners of all levels of experience who are passionate about their work.

simply learning a natural approach to solutions in hypnosis

simple

hypnosis has been understood as complex, special, even dangerous, requiring unique restrictions. In this programme we will explore simple, elegant and respectful ways of connecting clients with their own unique styles of experience.

learning

There will be a minimum of theory and explanations, focusing instead on demonstrations and recurrent practice so that participants will have sufficient skills to begin to use their learning in their clinical practice immediately.

a natural approach

Erickson spoke of the everyday trance as a natural experience that can be enhanced in a clinical situation. By utilising individual clients’ individual skills, a fail-safe method will be made obvious and readily useable.

solutions

Ericksonian hypnosis creates a wonderful context for a trusting therapeutic relationship, helping to restore self-trust in individual clients by reconnecting them with their natural resourcefulness.

hypnosis

When we begin with the principle that hypnosis is an experience that involves focus of attention and absorption in that experience, this natural ability can be readily accessed by clients and incorporated in any clinician’s practice.

3 day intensive

In this 3 day workshop, we will enjoy sharing learning:
creating a mood of connection to enhance rapport,
exploring natural links between everyday happenings
and the process of inviting a client into the experience of hypnosis,
establishing easy and natural relationships between specific client resources and their solutions.
