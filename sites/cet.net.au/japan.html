The Brief Therapy Network Japan

presents

What's still missing in brief Therapy 

the contribution of solution emotions and body


with Dr Robert McNeilly

4th & 5th August, 2007

Japan Women's University

executive chair-person Minori Aoki

further details - please contact 宮田敬一Keiichi Miyata



This 2 day workshop will be a combination of lecture, demonstration, and practice in groups of 2 or 3.

Outcomes of the workshop

participants will
  • gain a deeper understanding of the brief therapy approach
  • learn simple and powerful new skills to add to their work
  • practice in a safe, non-judgemental environment
  • feel increased confidence
  • increase their range of therapeutic options
  • resulting in an easy translation into clinical practice.
