Rob McNeilly MBBS was in a suburban Melbourne general medical practice for 10 years, had the privilege of learning directly with Milton Erickson, was inspired by his human approach to therapy, and created his own interpretation to assist clients in a respectful, dignified way with the human dilemmas that affect individuals, couples and families.

He founded the CET in l988 to introduce Ericksonian Hypnosis and the Solution Oriented Approach to hypnosis, counselling and coaching in Australia. For more than 30 years, the contribution he brings i his teaching style, writing and unique approaches to learning and therapy allow for a ready application into everyday living and are recognised locally, nationally and internationally, including repeated invited presentations at the Milton H Erickson Foundation’s International Congresses in USA since 1980 and workshops in Singapore and Denmark since 1999.

He is co-author, with Jenny Brown, of "Healing With Words", "Healing the Whole Person" was published by Wiley, republished as “Doing Change” by St Luke’s Innovative Resources, and Crown House have published a series of DVD demonstrations of counselling and hypnosis.

Students comment on his easy, respectful manner and his elegant ability to make complex issues approachable while retaining their essence.


Gabrielle Peacock MBBS FRACGP is a general practitioner with 17 years experience, the last 10 spent predominantly working with mental health issues. She has 5 years advanced training in Solution Oriented and Ericksonian psychotherapy with individuals, couples and families. She runs workshops locally and internationally teaching Solution Oriented Family Therapy to health professionals, and has been a presenter at ASOCHA, PACFA and AFTA conferences around Australia. She also teaches counselling skills to medical students at the clinical school in Hobart.

Gabrielle works with all issues in individuals, couples and families. She has a wide experience in working with every day human dilemmas to severe chronic mental illness.

She has developed a special interest in working with children, adolescents and families. She is particularly interested in interventions which assist families and individuals to regain their strength and resilience in problem situations such as child and adolescent behavioural problems, bullying, school issues, illness in a family member, and relationship breakdowns within the family.

Solution Oriented Relationship Counselling
[formerly Solution Oriented family Therapy]
a 3 day intensive


In this 3 day workshop we will explore simple, elegant methods to create a solution mood, and generate new ways for couples and family members to be together. The focus will be on those elements beyond technique that are common to all therapies that account for effectiveness, and we will explore how we as therapists can actively generate these essential ingredients of therapy.

This workshop will be of interest to anyone wanting to work with relationship issues including children and their families, or to relationship counsellors or family therapists looking for a forum to extend their work in a supported way. It provides an opportunity for hands on learning around relationship counselling and family therapy, and even those working with couples or families will benefit from this experiential workshop which provides an opportunity to practice.

This approach creates a context for a couple or a family to emerge instead of a group of individuals, while exploring the moves that make this possible. These moves are foundational to this work. Managing the mood, joining, and creating connections to unite members of a relationship will be recurrently emphasised, and this in turn will create the possibility to practice with the couple or family that emerges.

This interactive workshop emphasises demonstration and practice over lecture presentation so that participants can expect to be able to use the learning from this learning experience in their practice immediately, and confidently.
