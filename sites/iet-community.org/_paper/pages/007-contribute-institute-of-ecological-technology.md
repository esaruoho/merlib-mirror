You can make a difference

                  

If you feel that what we focus on is important, you can make a
difference by supporting us financially. Giving a voluntary donation
will help us take another step with our
research and publication efforts. Your help is gratefully appreciated
and will make a difference.

                  

Institute of Ecological Technology (Stiftelsen Institutet för
Ekologisk Teknik) is a non-profit foundation and
distributed self-organizing research institute, situated in Sweden.

                  

                  
On-line
donations

                  

You can make a donation from your PayPal account. If you do not have a
PayPal account you can make a donation securely via PayPal from your
credit card.
