The
International
Workshop on Natual Energies (IWONE) is a workshop covering
non-traditional ways to affect water flow,
water quality, plants, weather and eco-systems, as well as
non-traditional alternative energy sources. Historically the workshop
has had a focus on ideas related to the Austrian naturalist Viktor
Schauberger. 

                  

The workshop is organized every two years by Institute of Ecological
Technology (IET). Since 2003 the workshop takes place at Höör
(near Malmö) in southern Sweden.

                  

The next IWONE conference will take place on August 11th - 13th, 2023.
