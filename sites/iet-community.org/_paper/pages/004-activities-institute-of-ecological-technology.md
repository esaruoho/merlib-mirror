Archive

                            

                            

                              
 We maintain an archive of Schauberger related material,
and unpublished reports/documents. (Some of this material, e.g. from
the expeditions to Oulanka in the early 1980s, we hope to be able
to publish over the next few years.) 

                              

Are you interested in giving us a hand with organizing or preparing
documents for publication?

Publishing

                            

                            

                              
Our idea is to publish quality research reports in
alternative science, and specifically on subjects relating to
Schauberger or self-organization technology in a broader context. 

                              

As part of the IET Community you are free to submit your material.
