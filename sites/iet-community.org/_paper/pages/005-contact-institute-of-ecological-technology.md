Mailing list 

                

                

                  
 Exchange
ideas over our mailing list on "self-organization technology" (a name
we consider being appropriate for the phenomena we are studying). The
word is mostly free if you use the normal "netiquette". All in all, the
topics for this list are the same as those you can find in the 
About
IET
 and 
Research
 sections. 

                  

We do not wish to emphasize only the work and ideas related to Viktor
Schauberger. When it comes to developing a natural technology, there
exist many other interesting ideas as well. But the idea of studying
nature and attempting to learn from and copy the way it works is quite
essential.

                  

To subscribe to the mailing list, send an email to:

                  

                  
self-org-tech-request@iet-community.org

                  

with the word subscribe in the message body (not as subject - no
subject is needed). Later on when you are sending email to the list,
just use the following email address: 

                  

                  
self-org-tech@iet-community.org
