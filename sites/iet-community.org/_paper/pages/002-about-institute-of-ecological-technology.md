What we are - and our vision 

                            

                            

                              
a

                            

                            

                              
The Institute of Ecological Technology is a distributed
self-organizing research institute, with a focus on non-conventional
ideas and solutions within ecological technology. Our focus is on
practical applications and on becoming aware of our interconnectedness
with nature.

History

                            

                            

                              
a

                            

                            

                              
The Institute of Ecological Technology was formed in the
late 1970s, to be a platform for research and projects in alternative
science, and has undergone a series on metamorphoses since.

It traces its roots to the "study and copy nature" perspective that had
been advanced by the Austrian Viktor 

Schauberger about half a century earlier.
