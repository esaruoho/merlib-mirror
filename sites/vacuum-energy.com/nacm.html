The antisymmetry of E-field gradient is very high, above 90% which gives high unidirectional thrust to the Acamod, which is excelent.(see data on right side of image bellow). In other words, above 90% of energy input to Acamod will create unidirectional thrust, in direction to aluminum plate. For more information, check the patent number US6317310

Direct conversion of electrical energy into gravitational pulse obtained with antisymmetrical shaped electrodes, whose E-field gradient change seems to produce unidirectional gravitational pusle:
The mass, size and the shape of Acamod will have big effect on final E-field gradient. The most important seems to be mass of the cylinder electrodes, the more mass - the more thrustis being developed. Also, it seems the copper is better material then aluminum, for cylinder electrode.

The law can be formulated:
Symmetry of the gravitational pulse is equal to the symmetry of electrical field gradient.

In other words, if E-filed gradient is symmetrical, we will get symmetrical gravitational pulse, which means body will accelerate equaly in all directions, and thus stay static, with zero velocity added by pusle.
If E-field gradient is antisymmetrical, we will get antisymmetrical gravitational pusle, or antistatic(unidirectional) velocity, and body will increase its velocity in that direction.

But, if there is not enough mass in the body to use that gravitational pulse, then most of the gravitational pulse energy will be wasted, and thus, the thrust will be minimal.

For more information about importance of the mass, visit Jean Naudin's pages dedicated to the experiments with Lifter and Acamod, the links are located at the bottom of this page.
