Basics about Gravity.




Gravity plays very big role in our lives, and we want to know exactly what it is, what is the true nature of it. One of the reasons why do we want to know what is gravity, is because we want to conquer it, so our space crafts can be free to travel in any direction without experiencing delay while waiting for next launch "window". We also want to be able to protect occupants of the space craft from big acceleration that sometimes might be even fatal. Or we can just create artificial gravity, so much needed in deep space, far away from Earth.



In order to understand gravity, we need to understand vacuum. Vacuum is ocean of fine particles, which are in different form then the particles and matter then we see around us. We hear about 'virtual photons', 'virtual electron-positron pairs', 'vacuum polarization'. Even if they are called 'virtual' those 'virtual' particles and electromagnetic waves are causing measurable effects on 'real' particles and matter. In some cases, virtual particles can change into real, or opposite process can take place, real particle becomes virtual.

Since 'Vacuum' means empty or nothing, it is better to think about space as a ether. We can think about ether as a sort of gaseous matter, with very unique properties, similar to real gas, but still different.
Nikola Tesla wrote about it:
"I showed that the universal medium is a gaseous body in which only longitudinal pulses can be propagated, involving alternating compressions and expansions similar to those produced by sound waves in the air. Thus, a wireless transmitter does not Hertz waves which are a myth, but sound waves in the ether, behaving in every respect like those in the air, except that, owing to the great elastic force and extremely small density of the medium, their speed is that of light."

As in any gas, ether can flow in variety of forms: parallel, rotational, converging. Or, propagation can be in form of longitudinal pulses. Gravity is converging flow of ether. Non-converging flow is not gravity. Converging means flow from one central point or better to say, towards one central point. If we imagine lines of ether that converge towards central point, we will see then becoming closer to each other, or accelerating as they approach the central point.

This is the case when ether accelerates and its dragging the matter with it, this is gravity. Gravity is dragging, pulling. There is similar but different case when matter accelerates, then ether creates friction and matter slows, we need more energy just to keep it accelerating. That is inertia. Gravity is when ether accelerates, inertia is when matter accelerates. Gravity is dragging, inertia is friction, but in essence they are the same, they are just started under different circumstances.

Converging flow of ether, or gravity, is affecting not only matter, but also other forms of ether flow. For example, gravity will affect photon which is traveling against gravity. The photon will experience red-shift, or will become longer, just as sound wave in air will become longer if it travels against the wind. Basis of this case is that symmetrical photon, while traveling against converging, or non-linear flow of ether, is slowly becoming non-symmetrical, anti-symmetrical, and it loses energy. Opposite is also true, when non-symmetrical, anti-symmetrical photons, or anti-symmetrical electromagnetic oscillations are being created. Those non-symmetrical oscillations will alter linear flow of ether and make it non-linear flow, and such ether flow can alter position of matter in space, because that flow is gravity.

Gravity flow dragging, or inertial friction, can affect both electromagnetic oscillations, and can affect matter, both charged electrically or neutral. And matter, electrically charged or neutral, can affect gravity flow or be the cause of inertia. But neutral matter, or particles, can not affect electrically or magnetically charged particles or matter. The opposite is true, electrically or magnetically charged particles can not affect neutral matter. Thus, gravity is superior to the electromagnetic force.

Ether fluctuations are the cause of inertia, since they resist to acceleration of matter, both charged electromagnetically or neutral, and they are superior to electromagnetic fluctuations of the ether. There are many types of ether fluctuations, not all of them are electromagnetic fluctuations. Zero Point Field (ZPF) is electromagnetic fluctuation, and thus, it can not be cause of inertia nor gravitation.

Medium size crafts can have disc or spherical shape, while small crafts do not have to be strictly etherodynamic in shape. But all them, from small to the biggest craft, will have rounded edges, because sharp edge will make too much friction with ether and will be danger for the craft. Passengers of craft would still need gravity inside the craft, so artificial gravity is created inside of it, and it is constant and independent from outside inertia and gravity forces.
