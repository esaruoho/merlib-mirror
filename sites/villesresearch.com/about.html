My name is Ville Piippola and I live in Finland. I have a B.Sc. degree in electrical engineering, and have been interested in free energy since 1998.

In 2001 I designed and implemented a half year magnet motor project for Welfare Technology Foundation. During the project I constructed many non-electric permanent magnet motors, but I didn't succeed to build a self-rotating permanent magnet motor in six months.

However, the project was a good springboard for independent research. After the project I have developed many magnet motor designs and until recently started to publish some of the designs on this site.

I have a special interest in sustainable energy production and subtle energy technologies. These pages contain my researches into unconventional borderlands of science. Feel free to contact me via email if you have any questions or comments.
