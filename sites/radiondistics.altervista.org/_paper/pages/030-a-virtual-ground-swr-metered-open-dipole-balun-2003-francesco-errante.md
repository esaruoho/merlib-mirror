SWR-metered BALUN


              
by 
Francesco Errante

              


              
This version of the 
virtual ground balun
 is designed
              around our 
suppressor
 circuitry and it has 3 built in
              S.W.R. meters, one for each of its ports. Built into its circuit there are also 2
              coaxial switches and 2 dummy loads for the alternate suppression of one or both of
              the branches of the open dipole antenna to be tested.

              

              The 
SWR-metered balun
 is the ideal choice for conducting experiments with
              the 
secondary ionization fluorescence hertzian radiation
 
detector
.
