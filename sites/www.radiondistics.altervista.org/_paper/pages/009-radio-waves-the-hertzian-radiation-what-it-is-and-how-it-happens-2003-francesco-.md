<td style="text-align: left;"><p><em>Hertzian Radiation</em>, <span class="small">(better known as radio-waves) : what it is and how it happens</span></p>
<p>by <em>Francesco Errante</em></p>
<div class="doublespaced">
<p><strong>This is a brief presentation of <em>Francesco Errante's theorem on the physics of the hertzian radiation</em></strong>.</p>
<p>The <em>hertzian radiation</em> is the oldest, yet the least common way to refer to radio-waves. It takes its name after <a href="hertz/KIT_PI_2013_153_Hertz_125_Years_Propagation_of_Electric_Action.pdf" target="_blank"><em>Heinrich Rudolf Hertz</em></a>, the first physicist and engineer to have demontrated the possibility of radio transmissions.</p>
<p>Ever since Hertz experiment and untill now, it was believed that energy would travel all the way from the transmitter to the receiver while maintaining its original form, hence, erroneously crediting electric signals with the property of being able to travel in the empty space. Hertz, him-self, named his own apparatus misterious emissions as "<em>electric waves</em>". Subsequently, they were renamed as "electromagnetic waves".<br />
<br />
First of all, it is paramount to comprehend that <strong>the <em>hertzian radiation</em> is a natural phenomenon with an artificial origin</strong>. Although, the <em>hertzian radiation</em> mirrors the nuclear reactions photonic emission mechanism, it takes place at frequencies far lower than those present in nature - eg: Sun's nuclear fusion and its radiations. See <a href="frequency_wavelength_energy_radiation_calculator.htm"><em>energy and radiation wavelength</em></a>. In nature, radiations get generated by matter's violent energy excitation which involves very high tempertures and consequentially very high frequencies. Radiations of wavelenght longer than those of the infrared rays spectrum, in nature, have no known practical function, although, they still retain scientific relevance. In the <em>hertzian radiation</em>, instead, the excitation is artificially imposed by means of <em>electric oscillation</em>.<br />
<em>Electric oscillation</em> is a means to obtain the excitation of the electric charges and it must not be confused with the <em>hertzian radiation</em> it-self! Unfortunately, this is what has been happening untill now.<br />
<br />
<strong>The purpose of this study is to demonstrate that:</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>1) the mere oscillation of electric charges is not the phenomenon at the origin of the <em>hertzian radiation</em>;<br />
2) <em>hertzian radiation</em> can only take place following an energy transformation, named <em>radio-electric transduction</em>.</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong>Here is a brief introduction to the Author's logical development of his ideas, concepts, observations, deductions and <a href="scientific_index.htm">patented devices</a> leading to his findings.</strong><br />
<br />
<strong>The method.</strong><br />
<em>Simplification</em> is the main rule in the observation and understanding of physical phenomena. Simplification, though, must not be confused with a simplistic approach and it may require the development of tailor-made tools and technology which can them-selves be far from being simple.<br />
<br />
<strong>The stripping of a radiator to its bare essentials.</strong><br />
The � of a wavelength <em>open dipole antenna</em>, since its birth, has always been considered to be the most simple available antenna and therefore it has always been the specimen on which to conduct studies. Those studies, however, were ill at birth as the <em>open dipole</em> is not an aerial as simple as it looks. In addition, having considered the open dipole an "<em>elementary antenna</em>" has shifted the focus of those studies away from its essence and into its properties and its effects. Consequentially, the theories about its intimate functioning are flawed and are responsible for a number of further well radicated misconceptions. Moreover, this has prevented from establishing how the <em>hertzian radiation</em> takes place.</p>

<p>By means of a particular <a href="suppressor.htm">radio-electric circuitry for the suppression of anyone of the two branches of a � of wavelength open dipole</a>, I have demonstrated, once and for all, that the <em>open dipole</em> is not an <em>elementary antenna</em> (by definition an elementary antenna is an aerial where the condition of resonance and radiation cannot take place without the presence of all its parts) but it is, instead, an "<em>elementary array</em>" of 2 elements, of a physical length equal to � of a wavelength each, which are electrically arranged in a counterphase, while being fed in the middle of them.<br />
<br />
Once it has been established that the focus should be placed on the behavior of a single element of physical length equal to � of wavelength, further research has allowed me to come up with a truly "<em><a href="antenna_errante.htm">elementary radiator</a></em>" to further distinguish between the source of the radio-electric signal and the actual radiator.<br />
<br />
The <em>elementary radiator</em>, infact, comprises a radio-electric circuitry and a � of wavelength radiator which can be easily detached and substituted by a dummy load (anti-inductive load), in order to conduct RF measurement on the circuitry alone, while, radio-scopic observations of the actual radiator emissions can be carried out by means of a particular <a href="detector.htm">detector</a> which exploits and, at the meantime, demonstrates the phenomenon of the <em>radioluminescence</em> from secondary ionization induced by <em>hertzian radiation</em>.<br />
<br />
<strong>The radio-scopic observation by <em>radioluminescence</em></strong>.<br />
By employing my <a href="detector_it.htm">detector</a>, I have carried out several non-intrusive radio-scopic measurements on the radiator over the full spectrum of the shortwaves, anywhere between 1 and 30 MHz with an RF power ranging from 100 mW to several kiloWatt.</p>
<p><strong>The experiments I have deviced ed carried out, clearly, demonstrate that:</strong></p>
<p>1) the energy radiated by the radiator has a transitory ionizing power;</p>
<p>2) by injecting a radio-electric signal into a properly resonant radiator, the latter will always radiate energy as radio waves, starting from the point which is always opposite to the point of feeding;</p>
<p>3) that the bulk of the energy is always radiated by the region towards the end of the radiator..</p>

<p><strong>Author's deductions.</strong><br />
What has just been said, reasonably, leads to affirm that the <strong>hertzian radiation takes place whenever charges belonging to a first wavefront having flowed forth along the radiator all the way up to its end, return backwards and hit a new forthcoming wavefront giving origin to collisions with scattering of particles, most likely to be photons, having the same frequency and wave form of the radio-electric signal that has generated it. As it is impossible for the new particles to travel faster than light, the particles will acquire more mass instead.</strong> (if particles could travel faster then light, their emission would, inevitably, end-up generating shorter wavelength radio signals)</p>
<p><strong>This mechanism is, therefore, a form of <em>controlled or limited standing wave regime</em>, if it happens within the length of the radiator then we have <em>resonance</em>, if it exceeds the length of the radiator we have a <em>random standing wave regime</em> with less or no radiation</strong>.</p>

<td style="text-align: center;" width="100%"><p><strong>Conclusions:<br />
<br />
according to this Author's findings, the physical mechanism responsible for the generation of the <em>hertzian radiation</em> is different from what it was previously theorized and accepted as true.<br />
<br />
The forecast of a concatenation between the electric field and the magnetic one, as offered by Maxwell's equations, describe the electric and the magnetic fields engaged in an endless cycle of mutual creation. This interpretation bears a fundamental error which lies in the fact that the concatenation between fields was thought to be in the fashion of an overlaying and NOT in a relay-race like full-cycle progression as it happens to be, instead.<br />
<br />
In the light of this Author's findings, it can be affirmed that there is no such a thing as an electromagnetic wave, an electromagnetic field or an electromagnetic signal. There are, instead, photonic emissions (radio waves) that follow the same wave form and frequency of the signals that have generated them, capable of inducing electromotive forces onto the matter with the same frequency and wave form.<br />
<br />
As a direct consequence of this findings, when it comes to radio-electric transduction, the concept of <em>electromagnetic induction</em> does no longer hold true, either. Accordingly, the mechanism by which an electrical conductor (ie: radio antenna) gets affected by the <em>hertzian radiation</em> must be different from what it was previously thought too and everything points to nothing else than a simple <em>photovoltaic effect</em>, just as observed by <em>Hertz</em> and <em>Hallwachs</em> in the ultraviolet radiation spectrum.</strong></p></td>

This transformations can be repeated an infinite number of times but with regard to energy loss it is an highly disadvantageous exercice.</strong></p>
