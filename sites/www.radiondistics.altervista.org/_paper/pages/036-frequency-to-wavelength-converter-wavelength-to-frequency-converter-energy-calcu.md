<td style="text-align: left;" width="600" height="200" data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="#FFFFFF"><h3 id="frequency-wavelength-energy-calculator">Frequency / Wavelength / Energy Calculator</h3>
<p><br />
Frequency and wavelength of radiations are related to one another through the speed at which light travels.<br />
<br />
Equation: f * λ = c<br />
Equation: E = <sup>hc</sup>/<sub>λ</sub><br />
<br />
where:<br />
f = frequency in Hertz (Hz = <sup>1 cycle</sup>/<sub>sec</sub>)<br />
λ = wavelength in meters (m)<br />
c = the speed of light (approximately equal to 3*10<sup>8</sup> <sup>m</sup>/<sub>s</sub>)<br />
E = energy in electron Volts (eV)<br />
h = Planck's constant (6.626068 × 10<sup>-34</sup> <sup>m<sup>2</sup>kg</sup>/<sub>s</sub>)<br />
<br />
</p>
<p>To convert wavelength to frequency enter the wavelength in microns (μm) and press "Calculate f and E". The corresponding frequency will be in the "frequency" field in GHz.<br />
<br />
OR enter the frequency in gigahertz (GHz) and press "Calculate λ and E" to convert to wavelength.<br />
Wavelength will be in μm.<br />
<br />
OR enter the energy in eV and click "Calculate λ and F" and the values will appear in the corresponding fields.</p>
