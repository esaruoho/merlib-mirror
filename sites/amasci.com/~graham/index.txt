 Work

 Education 

I attended Carleton College for my
bachelor's degree. My senior thesis in Physics was on the use
of CCD devices for spacecraft navigation. I also majored in History, 
focusing on 20th century history and writing my senior thesis on the
role of the German Communist Party in Nazi Germany. 

I went on to the University of Hawaii 
for a MA in Southeast Asia, focusing on Burma. I also spent some time 
on World History. Unfortunately, advanced degrees in History require
study of a foreign language. I spent two years torturing myself learning
Burmese.
