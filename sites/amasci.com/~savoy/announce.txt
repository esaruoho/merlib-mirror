 An all-member meeting was held Mar. 30, and the new board of directors for
 the club has been chosen! Shai Smith is our new president. 
 Others on the new board include Dan Crawford, Tiffany Bond, Bill Zude, and Steve Crawford. A big thanks in advance to all the members
 (and a few non-members) who have said they'd volunteer to help run the
 club! (4/3/01) 

 Upcoming workshops on Monday nights at Sonny's are
 now posted on a schedule page. (4/3/01) 

 Swing with John Holt and his Radio Rhythm Orchestra every Tuesday night 
 from 9 to Midnight ($5 cover) at the China Harbor Restaurant and lounge. 
 There is a beginner level east coast swing dance lesson at 8 ($2) and the 
 band starts immediately afterwards. China Harbor is located at 2040 Westlake 
 Ave. N., Seattle WA by Lake Union. They serve fine Chinese meals and appetizers 
 until 10 p.m. This is a 21 and over event as they have a full bar. (3/9/2001)

 We are now publishing our newsletter, the Swingline , online!. 
 You can view or print the Swingline by visiting the newsletter 
 page . Note that you will need the Adobe Acrobat Reader to view/print 
 the file -- it's in PDF format so that all the formatting and graphics will 
 appear properly regardless of what web browser you use. If you don't have 
 the Acrobat Reader yet, a link is provided for you. While you're at it, 
 you can also see some of our old newsletter articles from years past -- 
 they're in text form and can be viewed on the web without needing the Acrobat 
 Reader. (10/19/2000)
