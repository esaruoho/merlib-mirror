Announcements!

  • The Savoy Fall Class Schedule is now available online.  Features include a 5-week Choreography Workshop and many 1-day workshops.  Check it out now on our Classes page.  (8/29/01)

 

  • Casey MacGill and his band are back in town at the Mountaineers Club for another show!  Don't miss this awesome band -- they don't come to Seattle very often.  (Especially since Casey's moving to southern California soon!)  They play Friday, Sep. 14  from 9pm - midnight.  $13, or $11 for Savoy members or for advance tickets.  (Includes a dance lesson from 8 - 9pm)  The Mountaineers are located at 300 Third Ave West.  More info at http://home1.gte.net/res02z4t/swing.htm  (part 1 of Jitterbug Weekend)  (8/29/01)

 

  • California band Dean Mora and his band are at the Century Ballroom once again on Saturday, September 15.  $15, 21+.  (part 2 of Jitterbug Weekend) (8/29/01)

 

  • Red and the Red Hots are at the Century Ballroom on Sunday, September 16.  $10, all ages.  (part 3 of Jitterbug Weekend) (8/29/01)


  • An all-member meeting was held Mar. 30, and the new board of directors for the club has been chosen!  Shai Smith is our new president.  Others on the new board include Dan Crawford, Tiffany Bond, Bill Zude, and Steve Crawford.  A big thanks in advance to all the members (and a few non-members) who have said they'd volunteer to help run the club!  (4/3/01)

 

  • Upcoming workshops on Monday nights at Sonny's are now posted on a schedule page. (4/3/01)

 

  • Swing with John Holt and his Radio Rhythm Orchestra every Tuesday night from 9 to Midnight ($5 cover) at the China Harbor Restaurant and lounge. There is a beginner level east coast swing dance lesson at 8 ($2) and the band starts immediately afterwards. China Harbor is located at 2040 Westlake Ave. N., Seattle WA by Lake Union. They serve fine Chinese meals and appetizers until 10 p.m. This is a 21 and over event as they have a full bar. (3/9/2001)
