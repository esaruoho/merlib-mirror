Savoy Swing Club Membership

Membership Benefits

Love to Lindy? Want to learn how? Need a place to practice? Then join us!! Club benefits include:

  • Free newsletter (Savoy Swingline) available online or mailed to your home !
  • Free admission to Friday night practices !
  • Reduced-price classes: $30 for all classes instead of the regular $45 price! (save $$$ each class session) !
  • Discounts on admission to co-sponsored dances and workshops (normally a savings of 10-20%) !
  • Lots of opportunities to make your voice heard... the Savoy Swing Club relies on its membership for volunteers to make everything happen !
  • Discounts at participating businesses !

Limited Membership

Each person who enrolls in a Savoy Swing Club class is enrolled as a Limited Club Member during that five- or six-week class series. The fee you pay when you register for a class series entitles you to attend the classes you sign up for as well as attend Friday Night Practice for free. Note that only Annual Members receive all the other club benefits listed above.
