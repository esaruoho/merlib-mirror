The Savoy Swing Club features two performing groups:

  • The Performance Troupe tours the Pacific Northwest performing and teaching Lindy Hop, 1930's jazz, and other Swing Era dances popular in the 1920s, 1930s and 1940s.

  • Emerald City Swing offers participative demonstrations and performances incorporating various Swing forms into their routines, including Lindy Hop, Shag, Balboa, LA Style, and West Coast Swing.

Contact us for information on both of our performing groups, or to request a booking.
