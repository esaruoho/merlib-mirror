Vol 1, Issue 2: Spring 1996

A Heartwarming Event: The Benefit Dance For M. J. Zimmerman

An opportunity to dance to four top Northwest bands, a selection of tasty foods to munch on, a silent auction featuring a variety of items all of this combined to make for a great fund raiser for M. J. Zimmerman, one of the founders of the Savoy Swing Club. It truly was a wonderful event that was well received by the dance community. Over 350 people from all aspects of the dance community attended the event and over $7000 was netted which went directly to M. J. to aid her financially while she is receiving treatment for cancer. The positive energy that abounded at the dance was indicative of the positive feelings shared by all as they contributed to assisting a fellow dancer. The seriousness of the event struck me though when M. J. made a small speech expressing her gratitude. You could tell that her fight with cancer had been an extremely painful experience, but she did say that the results of her treatment were positive.

Needless to say, an event of this magnitude did not occur without a lot of hard work from many people from the club as well as other members of the dance community. A round of applause to all of those that helped out, but in particular a big thanks to Christine Nelson for organizing the refreshments, to Chris Balka and Steve Crawford for handling the silent auction and entrance desk, to Hallie Kupperman for MCing the event, and the biggest thanks to Winfield Hobbs for instigating and organizing the event. Thank you for providing an opportunity to help a fellow dancer while doing one of our favorite activities.

-- Dave Gloger
