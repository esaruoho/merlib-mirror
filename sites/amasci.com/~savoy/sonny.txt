 Swingin' Mondays 

 Come dance Lindy Hop with us every Monday night (check schedule 
in case of holidays or special events) at Sonny Newman's Dance Hall at 201 N. 85th St. in Seattle.
(Dance hall is in the building across from the Taproot Theater on the 2nd floor,
enter in back.) We usually have a drop-in dance workshop from 7 or 7:30
till 8:30
p.m. (Check the schedule for exceptions.) 
 Open dancing to DJ'd music continues until at least 11:30 p.m. All ages
welcome! 

 We also have a map to Sonny's to help you find your way.... 

 The dance costs $3 for members 

 of the Savoy Swing Club, and $5 for the general public. (A bit more when
we occasionally have a live band -- see schedule .) Workshops are
$8
for members/ $12 for others for drop-ins, and multi-session discounts are
available. Plus workshop attendees get in the dance for free!
