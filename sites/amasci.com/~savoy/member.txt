 Membership Benefits

 join us !! Club benefits include:

 online or mailed to your home !

 of the regular $45 price! (save $$$ each class session) ! 
 Discounts on admission to co-sponsored dances 
 and workshops (normally a savings of 10-20%) ! 
 Lots of opportunities to make your voice heard... the Savoy Swing Club relies on its membership for volunteers to make everything happen !
 Discounts at participating businesses !

 Limited Membership 

 Each person who enrolls in a Savoy Swing Club class is enrolled as a Limited Club Member during that five- or six-week class series. 
The fee you pay when you register for a class series entitles you to attend the classes you sign up for as well as attend 
 Friday Night Practice for free. Note that only Annual Members receive all the other club benefits listed above. 

 How to Join 

 Fill out our membership form and mail it with your check to the address shown, or contact the Savoy Swing Club at P.O. Box 75535, Seattle, WA 98125 , (206) 547-7676.
