So, What's Gonna Happen?

Along with the usual assortment of panels, workshops, video rooms, and autograph sessions, Neko-Con Flash  will be trying out some new and innovative programming. Along with Steve Bennetts now famous classes on traditional Cel painting,  there will be one on the basic techniques of DIGITAL Cel painting hosted by Scott Frazier.

We encourage anyone who has an idea they would like to see to contact us.  Join in the fun, be part of the excitement. Have a panel or discussion group you want to hold?  Maybe you wat to tell the world about the joy of Pop Idols, or Mahou Shojou  A panel to tell people what Mahou Shojou is? A workshop perhaps?  Or maybe youre starting your company and want to let the world in on it. Or your current compnay or organization has an announcement that will strike the masses with awe. 
Just contact our head of programming, Matt Beschta.
