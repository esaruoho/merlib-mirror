Hotel Information

Animecon going is once again returning to the wonderful hospitality of the Holiday Executive Center in lovely Virginia Beach, Virginia. (Birthplace of Katsucon and NekoCon) The HIEC has held more animecons than any other hotel east of the Mississippi River. During the year we were away from the hotel, it has gone under a slight transformation. A new look for a new Millennium, while still retaining the laid back and casual atmosphere that our attendees and guests have come to enjoy year after year.

Nightly Rates $79.00 per room per night for Single to Quad Occupany.
This rate will available starting the Thursday night before the convention through to the Monday morning after.


Making Resevations
It is best to place your resevations Mon-Fri between 8AM and 5PM EST directly with the hotel. If you call Holiday Inn's 1-800 national number there is a chance they will either tell you that the hotel is booked, or that they don't know about the convention. (It has happened) When you do make your reservations, You must mention NekoCon in order to get the grouip rate. Deadline to reserve a room under the convention rate is September 29, 2001
You can place your reservations for Neko-Con by either contacting the hotel directlty at the address and phone number below.

The nearest Amtrack or Bus Terminal is located in neighboring Norfolk.  No shuttle service is available. Calling a cab, or arranging for a friend to pick you or drop you off is your best way to  reach the hotel.
