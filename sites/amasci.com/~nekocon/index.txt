Nekocon FLASH! Main Page 

 You have arrived at the website for NekoCon FLASH, a Fourth Year Japanese Animation Convention.
After yet another memorable year NekoCon is back, with a new look, and back in familiar surroundings.
This year will be some what of a homecoming for many people and a new experience for others. 

NekoCon 3 was dubbed by the staff as "The Great Experiment".
With our traditional hotel undergoing a major renovation during the fall of 2000 we had to find an alternative location for a year.
With more space than we knew what to do with, Nekocon 3 was an astounding success.

But, the vast majority of the attendees, our guests, and the staff
included missed the more intimate surroundings of the Holiday Inn Executive Center in Virginia Beach, Virginia.. 

So join us for Nekocon Flash, returning to our loving homested of
the Holiday Inn Executive Center in Virginia Beach which will be hosting a record 6th anime convention,
more than any hotel in the USA, come and see why.
