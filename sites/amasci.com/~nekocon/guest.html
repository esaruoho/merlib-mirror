What's a convention without guests? Each year we here at NekoCon want to bring you guests and panelist that will make everyones trip to the convetion an enjoyable and informative experience. They range from seasoned veterans of the industry, to individuals who are on track with the newest and latest happenings in video, Jpop, costume and other fandom related topics.
