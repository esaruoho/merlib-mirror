Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is a package trip?
    A package trip is a hotel stay and an airfare sold together at one price. A package trip can also include round trip transportation from the airport to your hotel, a car rental, or even meals. When we sell you the airfare with a hotel stay, or some other land portion, we are able to discount everything in the package.

  2. How can Seven Seas Travel get such a good price for a package trip to Hawaii or Mexico?
    We buy all of our package trips through wholesalers and tour companies. These companies are able to provide much lower rates than the published rates because of the special contractual arrangements they have with the airlines, hotels, and car rental companies. The savings are incredible when you compare published prices with package prices.

  3. Can I buy a discounted airplane ticket to Mexico or Hawaii with Seven Seas Travel?
    Yes, we do sell discounted airfares to most of the major destinations to Mexico and Hawaii. Due to the special contractual agreements wholesalers have with the airlines, our discounted tickets to Mexico and Hawaii must be sold with a minimum hotel stay or a minimum car rental. The reason for this minimum land portion is so that these discounted tickets are not in direct competition with the airline tickets that are sold at the published rate. Even with this minimum land requirement, the price of our discounted tickets to Mexico and Hawaii always beats the rate that the airlines advertises. If you are interested in an air only reservation to Mexico or Hawaii, please let us know in the message box of our travel questionnaire.

  4. What are the pros and cons of traveling with a package trip?
    The obvious advantage to package trips is the savings. If you are planning to travel to Mexico or Hawaii, a package trip is the cheapest way to go. The disadvantage to a package trip is the penalties for making changes or cancellations, especially after payment has been made on a reservation. The best way to avoid these penalties is to buy the travelers insurance which can be purchased at a minimal cost.

  5. How can I pay for my package trip?
    In most cases, we will first need to have a deposit a few days after your reservation is made in order to hold the space for you, and then it is usually forty-five days prior to your departure we will need to have payment in full. We accept all major credit cards, but we do request that our Internet clients fax us or call us with their credit card information rather than sending it to us by e-mail. It is not advisable to send credit card numbers by e-mail because of the insecurity of the Internet. We also accept bank checks in payment for our package trips.

  6. How long will it take to receive my travel documents?
    Generally speaking, your travel documents will arrive in our office two to three weeks prior to your departure. As soon as we receive your travel documents, we will send them to you. For clients paying by credit card, we can only send the travel documents to the billing address for your credit card.
