Sunset in Mazatlan* 

 Mexico is a great place for fun and relaxation.
 Some of the best beaches in the world can be found in Mexico, and the history of
the country is fascinating. 

 Olmec Colossal Head 

 The relics of Mexico's ancient past can be found
all over the country. These ancient indian ruins are evidence of the indian
civilizations that once dominated this land long ago. There are several archeological
sites in Mexico open to the public, and some have guided tours. 

 Our Lady of Guadalupe Church, Puerto Vallarta* 

 Mexico's more recent past can be discovered in the
quiet colonial towns that are located throughout the country. The kind hospitality of the
locals combined with an old world atmosphere found in these towns makes for a wonderful
vacation getaway. 

 Sportsfishing in Cabo San Lucas* 

 People come from all over the world to enjoy
sportsfishing in Cabo San Lucas. The water just off the coast of Cabo San Lucas is loaded
with marlin and sailfish. The deep and warm waters off of the coast are also great for
scuba diving and snorkeling. The crystal clear waters of the Caribbean Sea in the south of
Mexico are especially good for scuba diving and snorkeling. In most coastal tourist
centers in Mexico, charter boats are available to take a day trip fishing, scuba diving or
snorkeling. 

 Playa del Sol, Puerto Vallarta 

 Mexico is a beach lover's paradise. The water is
warm, and the sun is never too hot. There are water sports available at many of the
beaches found in the major coastal cities in Mexico. The sugar white sand and the
Caribbean blue waters make the beaches in south of Mexico particularly unforgettable.
