Waikiki Beach, Honolulu 

 Waikiki is just one of the beautiful beaches you
will find on the island of Oahu, home of the capital city of the State of Hawaii,
Honolulu. There are many world class restaurants in Honolulu, and plenty of nightlife.
There are also several tourist attractions on the island of Oahu, such as the Polynesian
Cultural Center, Waimea Falls and the International Market Place to name a few. Oahu is
definitely the busiest of all of the islands. 

 Poipu Beach, Kauai* 

 The seclusion of the island of Kauai provides for a
quieter and more relaxing vacation for those vacationers looking for rest and relaxation.
Kauai is known as the Garden Island for its lush tropical flora. There are nature walks
through large canyons, and plenty of secluded beaches to enjoy under the warm Hawaiian
sun. 

 A Volcanic Eruption on the Island of Hawaii* 
 photographer: Ron Dahlquist 

 The Island of Hawaii, also known as The Big Island,
is the home of two active volcanoes, Kilauea and Mauna Loa, which can be viewed from a
national park on the island. Because of the volcanic activity on The Big Island, some of
the beaches on the island have black sand. The island is also famous for its snorkeling,
sportsfishing, and golfing.
