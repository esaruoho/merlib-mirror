    Help Stop the Genocide! -- Ndihmoni pr t Ndalur Gjenocidin!

    The Kosova conflict has turned into a total humanitarian disaster. With the Yugoslav army trying to destroy the majority Albanian community in Kosovo, NATO commenced air strikes against Yugoslavia. In retaliation, Yugoslav President Slobodan Miloshevich has ordered a total genocide of the Albanians in Kosova. Those who have been able to escape are straining the humanitarian resources of neighboring countries. These hundreds of thousands of refugees have had all their vital records destroyed by the Yugoslav/Serbian government as if they never existed, and are streaming into Albania, F.Y.R.O. Macedonia and other countries, penniless and countryless. In the meantime the air strikes continue, but they do not appear very effective, and have hit civilian as well as military targets, and have caused the former Serbian opposition to Milosevich to dry up.
