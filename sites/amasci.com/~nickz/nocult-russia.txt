I just received this from Prof. Alexander Dvorkin:

========

On February 24 the appeal of Mr. Yakunin's Committee and the cults members was
heard by the Moscow City Court. The lower Court on May 21 1997 has dismissed
their law suit against me and the Russian Orthodox Church in connection to the
book I had written, and the Church has published. The plaintiffs (including
hare Krishna, Scientology, UC, JW and other cult members) have appealed the 
decision. In fact, the verdict of the Khoroshovsky District Court was 
devastating for them. 

Yesterday over two hundred Orthodox Church members have gathered in the room of
the Moscow City Court to support their Church. About 15 cults members were
present as well. The defendants were represented by me, rev. Alexey Uminsky of
the Dept. of Religious Education of the Moscow Patriarchate, Rt. Rev. Bishop
Tichon the vicar bishop of the Patriarch Alexey II, and Dr. Mikhail Gerasimov
of Moscow Parents' Committee.

The hearing lasted about three hours after which the Court has pronounced the
decision: the decision of the lower court was fully supported and upheld. The
plaintiffs who filed the frivolous lawsuit have to pay us back our expences
-- nearly $20000.

Just to add: the plaintiffs were represented by the lawyer Ms Galina Krylova -
a member of the governing board of the scientological Citizen's Commission
of the Human Rights. We were represented by Ms. Geralina Lyubarskaya - on of
the best civil lawyers of Moscow.

I thank everybody for the support and prayers. The mass campaign of support in
Russia was beyond my imagination. It was really our common victory!

God bless you all!

Please, spread the word.
Alexander L. Dvorkin, PhD
