 An interesting corner in upper Manhattan, New York City. 
Really. Want to see some Seaman and Cumming? Just read on! 

 To get here, you go west on Dyckman St. (itself somewhat oxymoronic), and turn
right on Seaman. If you go left, you hit the park and can go up the path
to the Cloisters. If you're coming from the riverside path or the West
Side Highway, e.g. on Fast & Fabulous' Ossining route , then you'll turn left
on Seaman.
