Prince William Sound
Gary was on assignment aboard Faithfully, a Nordhaven 40, in Prince William Sound, AK from 5/6-13, 2004. Learn more about the boat at www.overtheseas.com. See Gary's story of the trip In The Field.

Kayak by Mothership on the Home Shore
Gary will be leading two trips in 2004 out of Sitka, Southeast Alaska with the kayak mothership Home Shore. Dates are 5/24-31 and 6/2-9. Cost is $2,700 per person. Limit 6. The first trip is for kayakers, and has space available. The second trip is also for kayakers, and is full. Check the Home Shore website for details. Gary will also be aboard the Home Shore the week of June 11-18 with Canoe&Kayak editor Ross Prather for a trip around Baranof. If your interested in joining that trip, contact the Home Shore.

Read about Gary's 2003 Home Shore trip In The Field: Highlights included Humpback Whale watching, Brown Bear viewing, a visit to the Tracy Arm tidewater glacier and Lituya Bay.

Port Angeles Sea Kayak Symposium
Gary will be at the 4th Annual Port Angeles Sea Kayak Symposium, April 16-18, 2004, presenting "Photography from a Kayak" on Saturday, 4/17, Noon-1:00 and "Birding by Kayak" Sunday, 4/18, 9:00-10:00am. Cost for each is $3. Symposium admission is free, but attendee's will be asked to donate either $5 or non-perishable food items for the Port Angeles Food Bank. A fee may be charged for individual lectures and clinics. Plenty of kayaks will be available of testing.

The symposium is loaded with knowledgeable, talented BCU and ACA kayak instructors: Les Uhrich, Chris Duff, Andree Hurley, Leon Somme', Shawna Franklin, Bill McKenzie, Debrah Volturno and others.

2004 Santa Cruz Surf Kayak Festival
March 19-21, 2004, Gary will once again be in Santa Cruz for the annual Kayak Surf Festival. See the ad in the March, 2004 Outside Magazine for details, or check on-line at the Adventure Sports website.
