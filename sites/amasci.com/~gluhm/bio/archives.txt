 WILD SIDE EASY 5/13-6/6, 2002. 
 We paddled daily, but pitched no tents, cooked no meals, planned next-to-nothing. On assignment in Southeast Alaska 
 aboard the charter

 Wild Side Easy . . . 

 SANTA CRUZ KAYAK SURF FESTIVAL The March, 2002, Santa Cruz Kayak Surf Festival 
 started out a photo bust with Friday's pelting rain, but two hours of glistening sun on
 Sunday morning saved the shoot. 

 INDIAN HENRY'S, MT. RAINIER On my fourth visit in five years, I finally scored in 
 August, 2001, at Indian Henry's - images from a huge bench of mirror ponds and wildflower meadows, just a six mile backpack in. 

 FLORIDA The February, 2000 trip to Florida was, literally, for the birds. I
 did the major, well-known, bird photography hot-spots, but kayaked to places that were arguably
 even better. 
 South Florida stops included Ding-Darling NWR, the Venice Rookery, Myakka State Park, 
 Corkscrew Swamp Sanctuary . . . . . 

 PARADISE WILDFLOWERS, MT. RAINIER Of the many sub-alpine flower gardens at Mt. Rainier, Paradise Park stands out.
