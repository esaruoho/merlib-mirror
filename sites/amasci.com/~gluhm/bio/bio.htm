Contact Gary
Gary Luhm left his engineering job in 1998 to pursue outdoor and nature photography full-time. His unique imagery stems from a relentless exploration of the water planet by sea kayak, which is his taxi, his toolbox, sometimes even his tripod. He creates solutions to business concepts by drawing from the natural world.

Some recent work has been with small charter boat operators Ursa Major, Home Shore, and Over-the-Seas. Each offers an intimate way to see Alaska - and an alternative to the cruise ship.

Gary is in demand as a speaker on kayak destinations, photography and birding. He has presented at the Seattle and Tacoma Mountaineers, the Washington Kayak Club, the Seattle Sea Kayak Club, the West Coast Sea Kayak Symposium, and a number of Audubon chapters. His photographs and articles have appeared in Photomedia, Sea Kayaker, Canoe and Kayak, Wetdawg, Mariner Kayaks, Outside, National Wildlife, and various calendars, books, and other publications.

Read Gary's destination article on the Slot canyons of the Colorado Plateau (Winter 2000), and Olympic Coast's photogenic hot spots (Winter, 2001) in Photomedia. Follow his July, 2001, Cape Scott, BC kayak trip from journal entries published daily via Globalstar satellite phone at Wetdawg. Look there for Reports From the Water: Cape Scott.

Paddling destinations for 2004 include a return to Monterey Bay, CA, in March; Prince William Sound, AK in May; SE Alaska in June; Sun River, OR in August; Bowron lakes, BC in September.
