SCWS Membership 

 JOIN US! 

 If you have 
found that being owned by a Samoyed is more than just an adventure, then the 
Samoyed Club of Washington is for you! 

 Membership in 
SCWS offers a variety of activities with your dogs, fellowship with other humans 
owned by these wonderful creatures, mentorship, and information on various breed 
issues. 

 Membership 
requires two club members as sponsors and completion of the club application. 
We invite you to join us a club meeting or event and get to know us and our 
dogs! 

 Download the SCWS Application 
 After you fill out the 
application, please mail it to our of your sponsors. 
Thank you!
