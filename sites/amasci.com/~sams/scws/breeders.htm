Breeder Referral

SCWS makes available a list of members in good standing of the Samoyed Club of Washington State, and who have signed its Code of Ethics for responsible care and breeding of Samoyeds. These members may or may not have planned breedings in the future.

This information is provided by the SCWS as a service, but does not endorse one breeder over another, nor guarantee the quality of the animals. It is the responsibility of the buyer to research the breed and negotiate health guarantees with the breeder in writing.

To request a copy of the Samoyed Club of Washington's Breeder Referral list, please send an email to: Starstrucksams@aol.com
