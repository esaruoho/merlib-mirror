RESCUE FUNDRAISER

 

 

Text on Sweatshirt:

On the twelfth day of Christmas,
my Sammy gave to me
Twelve muddy pawprints,
Eleven angry squirrels,
Ten romped-in snowbanks,
Nine soggy chew toys,
Eight sirens howled at,
Seven dug-up gardens,
Six bossy nose-pokes,
Five tons of fur!
Four windows smudges,
Three miffed cats
Two shredded shoes...
And one slightly dampened Pear Tree.
