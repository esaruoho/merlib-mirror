To join the Samoyed Ring, please read the rules below.
Your site must meet these criteria in order to be included in the ring.

The Basic Rules are as follows:

1: You must have at least one page about your Samoyed(s).
2: The page must have at least one picture of your Dog(s).
3: This ring is intended for owners of Samoyeds, not for commercial sites.
4. Litter pictures are permitted, but please don't announce puppies for sale.
5: Your site may not contain material that a reasonable person would find offensive.

Please note: The navigation bar should appear on the first page of your site. If it is not on the first page, there must be an obvious link to it that can be seen within a few seconds.  On sites in multiple languages, the navigation bar must be on the first page of each language.

The purpose of the Samoyed Ring is to share our dogs with other people. If you make the navigation bar hard to find, we can't move on and enjoy the other sites.  If that happens, I reserve the right to suspend your site until the navigation bar is moved.
