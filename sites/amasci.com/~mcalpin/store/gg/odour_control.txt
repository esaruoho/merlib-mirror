 Gamma Force 

 Gamma Force attacks and neutralizes odors at the source eliminating odors caused by tobacco smoke, molds and mildew. It even tackles the unpleasant odors from pets, cooking and damp basements, by generating Ozone - one of the most powerful oxidizers available. Ozone doesn't just mask odors, it neutralizes odors at the source. Gamma Force works silently with the same ozone generating technology available in units costing hundreds of dollars more. Ideal for homes, offices, workplaces... anywhere that can benefit from purer, cleaner air. All Gamma Force products are maintenance free and offer a one-year warranty.

 Heavy-duty, ultra violet ozone generators feature a powdercoated sheetmetal housing. They offer integrated fans and backlit ON/OFF switches. These units use less power than a 100Watt light bulb!

 CO 2 Generator s / CO 2 Test Kits 

 CO 2 is one of the easiest ways to accelerate plant growth. Plants grown with supplemental CO 2 can produce up to 40% more flowers or fruit. A propane or natural gas CO 2 generator is the most cost effective way to add CO 2 to your environment. Many greenhouses use CO 2 generators to boost CO 2 levels safely and economically. 

 GEN-1 

 The GEN-1 is capable of producing between 3 and 13 cubic feet of CO 2 per hour. It comes standard with one burner, producing approximately 3 cubic feet of CO 2 per hour. You can add up to 3 additional burners, increasing the unit is capacity to up approximately 13 cubic feet of CO 2 per hour. 

 GEN-2 

 The GEN-2 is capable of producing between 16 and 26 cubic feet of CO 2 per hour. It comes standard with 5 burners, producing approximately 16 cubic feet of CO 2 per hour. You can add up to 3 additional burners, increasing the unit's capacity to up to aproximately 26 cubic feet of CO 2 per hour. 

 LPC-1 Liquid Propane Conversion Kit 

 This kit will allow the storeowner or retail customer to change their natural gas burner to a liquid propane burner. 

 NGC-1 Natural Gas Conversion Kit 

 This kit will allow the storeowner or retail customer to change their liquid propane burner to a natural gas burner. 

 LXO-1 Propane Tank Crossover 

 This device allows you to connect your propane CO 2 generator to two tanks so when one runs low, it can be changed out without the pilot flame going out. 

 Expansion Burner BNR-1 LP 

 These are our expansion liquid propane burners. Each burner will be individually packaged with an expansion burner, pillow pack of Anti-Seize, Hex wrench or removin cap on manifold and complete installation instructions. 

 Expansion Burner BNR-1 NG 

 These are our expansion natural gas burners. Each burner will be individually packaged with an expansion burner, pillow pack of Anti-Seize, Hex wrench or removin cap on manifold and complete installation instructions.
