Gamma Force

Gamma Force attacks and neutralizes odors at the source eliminating odors caused by tobacco smoke, molds and mildew. It even tackles the unpleasant odors from pets, cooking and damp basements, by generating Ozone - one of the most powerful oxidizers available. Ozone doesn't just mask odors, it neutralizes odors at the source. Gamma Force works silently with the same ozone generating technology available in units costing hundreds of dollars more. Ideal for homes, offices, workplaces... anywhere that can benefit from purer, cleaner air. All Gamma Force products are maintenance free and offer a one-year warranty.
Gamma Force UV1000, UV3000, UV5000

Heavy-duty, ultra violet ozone generators feature a powdercoated sheetmetal housing. They offer integrated fans and backlit ON/OFF switches. These units use less power than a 100Watt light bulb!
Gamma Force UV80HO

An economical sysem that produces ozone using special ultra violet light bulbs. Ideal for rooms uo to 5000 cu.ft. (150m3). fits 8" (20cm) ducts but can be installed into other size ducts using adapters.
Gamma Force CD8

Treats all odors being exhausted by an 8" (20cm) entilation system under most circumstances. Intended for rooms up to 20,000 cu.ft.(600m3). Installs into other size ducts with adapters.

Gamma Force CD12

Adjustable to match the ozone demand load for odor control as well as provide energy conservation in low demand periods. The CD generator does not require servicing. The CD12 ozone generator will treat all odors being exhausted by a 12" (30cm) ventilation system under most circumstances. Intended for rooms up to 100,000 cu.ft.(3000m3). CO2 Generators / CO2 Test Kits

CO2 is one of the easiest ways to accelerate plant growth. Plants grown with supplemental CO2 can produce up to 40% more flowers or fruit. A propane or natural gas CO2 generator is the most cost effective way to add CO2 to your environment. Many greenhouses use CO2 generators to boost CO2 levels safely and economically.
GEN-1
The GEN-1 is capable of producing between 3 and 13 cubic feet of CO2 per hour. It comes standard with one burner, producing approximately 3 cubic feet of CO2 per hour. You can add up to 3 additional burners, increasing the unit is capacity to up approximately 13 cubic feet of CO2 per hour.

GEN-2
The GEN-2 is capable of producing between 16 and 26 cubic feet of CO2 per hour. It comes standard with 5 burners, producing approximately 16 cubic feet of CO2 per hour. You can add up to 3 additional burners, increasing the unit's capacity to up to aproximately 26 cubic feet of CO2 per hour.

 
LPC-1 Liquid Propane Conversion Kit

NGC-1 Natural Gas Conversion Kit

LXO-1 Propane Tank Crossover
This device allows you to connect your propane CO2 generator to two tanks so when one runs low, it can be changed out without the pilot flame going out.
