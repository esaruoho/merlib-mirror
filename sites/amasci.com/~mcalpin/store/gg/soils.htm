CocoTek Organic Growing Medium

CocoTek is inert, so there's no more rockwool itch, no more handling questionable fibres, no funky rockwool taste, no need to change (flush) them, and best of all no more disposal nightmare! CocoTek breaks down on its own in three years. The cubes have been designed with perforated holes to fit rooting plugs. CocoTek does not wick like rockwool - the 6" high cubes is called the "drip cube". It is 100% organic, made from coconuts and natural rubber so it boasts an impressive water to oxygen ratio while being easy and responsible to use.

The coco bricks are ideal for indoor or outdoor gardening. CocoTek is pH ballanced, and contains the lowest known sodium content for coir fibre. It increases water holding capacity and nutrient retention, expanding to 5 times it's condensed brick size (1 brick expands to 3-1/2 gallons). CocoTek can be mixed with any other growing media (Perlite, Grow Rocks, soil, etc) to make the desired final mixture.
