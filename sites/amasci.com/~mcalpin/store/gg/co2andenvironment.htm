Power Handling Controls
UPM-1 Universal Power Module  

Allows the user to run additional HID lights or control multiple fans/air conditioners etc. The UPM-1's "plug-box" was designed to be plugged into a standard household outlet. When the control device turns ON, the relay inside the UPM-1 box applies power to the twin outlets on the front of the UPM-1 turning ON whatever is plugged into it. This allows you to run additional lights etc. from a different circuit with one common control device.
HPR-1 High Power Relay  

Perfect for controlling higher loads such as large air-conditioners or multiple HID ballasts. The user can supply either 120 or 240Volt power to the "input" side of the HPR-1.
MLC-4 Master Lighting Controller  

The best device for controlling up to four HID lights from one time-clock or controller. The MLC-4 features (4) standard 240Volt outlets, which your ballasts are plugged into. It features a 120Volt trigger cable to plug into a standard 24hour timer or controller.
MLC-4a Master Lighting Controller  

The best device for controlling up to four HID lights from one time-clock or controller. The MLC-4a is designed for 12Volt ballasts. However, it can also be used to run ballasts for 240Volt operation which have 120Volt power cords.
MLC-8 Master Lighting Controller  

Ideal for controlling up to 8 HID lights from one time-clock or controller. Power is brought into the MLC-8 by hard wiring up to a 40amp circuit. The MLC-8 features (8) standard 240Volt outlets, which your ballasts are plugged into.

CO2 Generators / CO2 Test Kits

CO2 is one of the easiest ways to accelerate plant growth. Plants grown with supplemental CO2 can produce up to 40% more flowers or fruit. A propane or natural gas CO2 generator is the most cost effective way to add CO2 to your environment. Many greenhouses use CO2 generators to boost CO2 levels safely and economically.
GEN-1
The GEN-1 is capable of producing between 3 and 13 cubic feet of CO2 per hour. It comes standard with one burner, producing approximately 3 cubic feet of CO2 per hour. You can add up to 3 additional burners, increasing the unit is capacity to up approximately 13 cubic feet of CO2 per hour.

GEN-2
