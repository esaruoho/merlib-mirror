Flow!TM Bio-Maintenance for Drains and Septic Systems
Cleans drains and maintains septic systems without potentially dangerous chemicals

Only from gardens alive! Why use harsh chemical solutions to clear slow-running sink, tub and shower drains? Why put up with nasty odors from your drains and septic systems?     Buy Now ......More...

Flow! is a biodegradable, biologically-active formula of bacteria species specially selected for optimum enzyme production. It provides the most efficient breakdown of proteins, carbohydrates, cellulose and fats found in organic waste. The bacteria rapidly digest drain-clogging and odor-causing matter. Odors are not just masked--they're eliminated at the source!

Extensively tested in laboratories and in homes, Flow! has been found safe and effective for all drains and septic systems. The bacterial components are in spore form for product stability. It has a shelf life of up to one year. And Flow! is easy to use! To keep a drain open and running freely, we recommend treating with 2 ozs. every 2-4 weeks. For slow-running drains, use 2-4 ozs. every day for 3 days. For septic system maintenance, use 10 ozs. per 500 gallons once a month (average tank is 1,500 gallons).

More about Flow!

Flow! is a stabilized bacterial spore liquid concentrate, designed to biologically digest and deodorize organic waste naturally and effectively. Proprietary cultures of non-engineered, naturally-occurring aerobic and anaerobic bacteria are selected for optimum extracellular enzyme production, assuring efficient breakdown of proteins, carbohydrates, cellulose and fats. The unique cultures rapidly digest odor-causing organic wastes, eliminating odors at their source. This product is used extensively by professional plumbers and municipal treatment plant managers. Multi-level quality-assurance programs have been established so that customers can use this product with complete confidence. Purity and microbial concentration are guaranteed.
