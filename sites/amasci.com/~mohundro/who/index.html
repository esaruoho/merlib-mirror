The basics: Im from Seattle, born and raised, leaving only to get my degree in journalism from Western Washington University in Bellingham, WA. Since graduation, Ive worked in and out of the tech industry. I am now a resident of Seattle's Ballard neighborhood, where I frequent coffee shops, music stores, and bowling alleys. In my spare time, I read books and comics, play with my two kittens Lucy and Linus, listen to music, go on walks, go to live music shows, watch TV, go to movies. You know, the usual.

To keep myself pop cultured, I turn to a number of online forums to help me find my way, including Fametracker for general celeb talk and Television Without Pity, which helps me sort out good TV, bad TV, and TV thats so bad its good. I lurk on other online forums as well.

I like the rain, a sure sign of a Seattle native, yet don't own any Gore-Tex™. I am Steve, and am at least a bit more interesting than this text indicates.
