Doyle and the Bitra Guard

by Journeyman Harper Tyroc of Bitra Hold

(Sung somewhat to the tune of "(Theme from) the Brady Bunch," though it changes about halfway through)

Tyroc, his eyes twinkling in the light from the hearth, leaned forward and whispered to the Hold children, "Have any of you heard of Doyle?"

One lone hand raised. It was Dibny. The little scamp had wedged himself into the crook of a chair and was grinning, "Yeah, when he attacked Lord Mal, Clark jumped in front and took his arrow!" His eyes sparkled of hero worship.

Tyroc laughed, his deep baritone ringing in the relative emptiness of the Bitra Great Hall. "You're right, young Dibny. But most people don't know about what happened that day. I wrote a song about it. Would you like to learn it?" He addresses the children.

The children cried, "Yay!" and nodded vigorously. Tyroc laughed again and started playing a few chords on his gitar, "Okay, listen closely:"

His fingers began playing a gay little ditty, simple and just about the right sound to get the children giggling. Finally, he began singing.

"Here's a story, of Holder named Malachite
As a Masterminer he tolerated idleness not at all
(aside: except when he took a nap)
Kept up shop at the Crafthall, Crom then Bitra
Yet he was all alone."

Tyroc grinned at the smiles around the group, and continued:

"Here's a story, about a miner named Doyle
Who was thrown out of the Craft on his bum
(aside: by Malachite)
One day found some Renegades, they joined together
Yet he was all alone."

Tyroc's playing grew to a confrontation of one key and one which he gets closer and closer to modulating to.

"But then one day this Renegade attacked this Holder
And it became a fierce battle, no holds barred
(aside: no pun intended)
That three youths would save Mal from Assasination
And these kids would be from the Bitra Guard."

Tyroc launches into the chorus, winking at the children:
"The Bitra Guard, The Bitra Guard
All three youngsters formed the core of the Bitra Guard!"

"Captain Largand, he must have been born a leader
Calmly giving orders to his Guard, confident and true
(aside: most always, anyway)
That day he spied Doyle drawing out his bow and arrow
Ran toward Doyle, tackled down that awful Rogue!"

"Lieutenant Laurel, well she was quick to cry out,
"I've got you Mal!" and drag him out of the way
(aside: good she was there that day!)
She had hair of wavy gold, just like her mother
And she was tough and strong and tall, and oh so brave!"

Tyroc looked over at Dibny, who seems to be waiting for something.

"To Guardsman Clark, loyalty had to be a virtue
And when Doyle shot he lept into his path
(aside: perhaps he tripped?)
Palm got the arrow Doyle had aimed at the Holder
Stupid, but it sure saved Mal's life!"

Dibny shot Tyroc a look but soon changed this into his normal goofy grin as the Harper runs to the chorus again.

"The Bitra Guard, The Bitra Guard
Clark, Laurel, Largand: the Bitra Guard!"

"And since that day Doyle's been locked up in the Barracks
Mal was safe, Clark recovered
(aside: though he's scarred)
Renegades are careful, and never get to close to Bitra
Because if so they'll face the brave Bitra Guard!"

Tyroc launches into the chorus one more time, the children humming along by now:
"The Bitra Guard, The Bitra Guard
All youngsters, brave and strong, the Bitra Guard!

The harper finishes with a flourish, and the children clap. He said, "You see, the Guard's here to protect you and me - so give them some credit. Who knows, maybe this song will change your life some day." He looked pointedly at Dibny and winked. Dibny just smiled.
