The Farmhand Song

by Harper apprentice Bartal

(Sung to the tune of Monty Python's "Finland")

Bartal and the other apprentice launch into a duet of rich chords and a rising line that eventually steps down to a medium pitch. With a look at the other, Bartal starts to sing,

"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand
On the land where I quite want to be
Plowing, tilling and planting
Or just shooting the breeze..."

"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand
The cooking is the way it should,
Be it breakfast or dinner
Or snack lunch in the Hall..."

Bartal's fingers fumble a moment as he reaches the bridge. As the chording from the second apprentice flows with those of Bartal's, he begins to sing again,

"Always so well protected
By the Weyr during Fall
Manning ground crews with the others
It's no bother at all..."

Returning to the key of the beginning strains of the song, Bartal hums along for one verse and then launches back into song,

"Farmhold, farmhold, farmhold
Grubbed land is the best land to work
With hands calloused and dirty
Around every bend, vistas lurk."

With the other apprentice chiming in, he ends the song with the lines,

"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand
It's the job for me."

"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand
It's the job for me."
