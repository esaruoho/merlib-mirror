Other Compositions

  • Doyle and the Bitra Guard - written by Bitra Hold Harper Tyroc (NPC, Jr. Journeyman) two turns before the Tenth Pass. It is a recounting of the legend of the Bitra Guard, a "legend" brought about somewhat because of the disruption of an assasination attempt by a Renegade and former Minecrafter named Doyle on Lord Holder Malachite's life by the Bitra Guard.
  • What a Wonderful Weyr - written by Small Hold Harper Machs (NPC, Sr. Journeyman), approximately five turns after the beginning of the Tenth Pass. It is song about life in a Weyr, Honshu Weyr in particular, where Machs spent some time when he was younger. Machs is Bartal's mentor.
