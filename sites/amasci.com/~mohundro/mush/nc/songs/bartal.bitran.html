Win Like a Bitran

by Senior Harper apprentice Bartal

(Sung to the tune of the Bangles' "Walk Like an Egyptian")

Bartal wastes no time in starting the piece, his fingers gliding along the gitar's neck, creating a short whining sound before launching into a fast-paced, high-energy song. He sings,

"All the old gamblers in the Hall
Practice dragonpoker, don't you know
If you aren't too good, oh-ay-oh
Supply of marks will fall very low"

"All the bakers near the hearth
They wager who'll lose their savings first
All the old gamblers, oh-ay-oh
They drink the wine quick but never drop"

The chording on the gitar modulates up a bit, and although the rhythm changes slightly, the tempo remains the same. Bartal sings,

"The Hold Harper teaching scales sings
Way-oh way-oh
ooh-way-ay-oh ay-oh ...

Win like a sly Bitran"

Bartal hits the side of the gitar once at the end of the line, as a punctuation.

The tone returns to that of the earlier part of the song, frenetic, yet cheery. Bartal sings,

"The kitchen help they bring you food
They spin around then they cross the floor
They've got the moves, oh-ay-oh
You drop your drink then they bring you more"

"All the Hold kids all done with lessons
They like hide-and-seek and other sport
When they play their games, oh-ay-oh
They like to win like a Bitran"

Bartal's gitarwork moves toward the chorus as he sings,

"The watchweyr on the Hold ramp howls
Way-oh way-oh
ooh-way-ay-oh ay-oh ...

Win like a sly Bitran"

Again the harper slaps the side of his gitar in punctuation, then launches into the bridge. Fingers dancing along the strings yield a line that first climbs, then drops, and finally evens out in a new key.

Bartal whistles the tune of the refrain once, the gitar stilled.

After whistling, Bartal goes back to singing:

"The trader clan display their wares
They get the marks for all their troubles
Renegades beware, oh-ay-oh
They really are as tough as they look"

"If you want to find off-duty Guards
They're in the tower, dealing out a round of cards
Captain's got a five of dragons, oh-ay-oh
That's known as a Clutch, don't you know"

Bartal slips into a sneaky grin as he looks about, strumming the gitar strings with vigor. He sings,

"All the crafters with honed skills
When gather comes, up go their tents
And the gamblers know, oh-ay-oh
Their chance to win like a sly Bitran"

Moving toward the chorus and climax, Bartal's playing continues to be insistent, yet clear. He sings,

"All the novices, when dealt great hands, go
Way-oh way-oh
ooh-way-ay-oh ay-oh ...

Gamblers play like sly Bitrans
Try to win like a Bitran"

The words done, Bartal is left with the gitar to finish out the song. He repeats the refrain a few times, fading out as he goes. When it almost seems silent, he increases the volume and ends in a flourish.
