The Waiting

by Harper apprentice Bartal
Bartal effects an anxious expression, looking up toward the sky. He strums his gitar lightly, wistful chords setting a mood as he begins to sing.

"I wish I could say
That I stood on the sands,
But instead I stood far away.
I hear Ista is next to Search,
With an egg-heavy queen;
Maybe tomorrow will be my lucky day."

He finishes the chorus and then his singing voice turns into a story-telling voice.

"When I was twelve I was close
To riding at Benden.
Lounging on the ramp when a dragon did land.
With a stare and a whuffle
It sniffed me,
And went on. (I just know that was planned)"

Bartal looks among the audience at the youngsters, some nodding in agreement despite themselves.

"Cousin Jenni travels
From Weyr to Weyr.
If she isn't Searched she doesn't stay long.
She has no craft,
Just her wandering;
I still think she's going about it all wrong."

Bartal strums the gitar a little, but not too much. He sings the chorus with appropriate longing.

"I wish I could say
That I stood on the sands,
But instead I stood far away.
I hear the Reaches is next to Search,
With an egg-heavy queen;
Maybe tomorrow will be my lucky day."

He continues, speaking his tale to the listeners.

"My friend Wally,
He "talks" to the dragons;
Calls them "wondrous" and "incredible" and stuff.
And when he spends too many hours
Hugging the dragons,
They usually lift up and fly off in a huff."

Bartal pauses to stick a thumb into his chest, before making a light chord.

"Me, I work in the orchard.
I'm accessible (yet not pushy)
And I keep an eye on the sky,
Keep track of the seasons --
And hatching cycles --
Always hoping someday a dragon will drop by."

His voice chokes a bit, as if he's about to cry. He doesn't, though, but just sings the chorus one more time.

"I wish I could say
That I stood on the sands,
But instead I stood far away.
I hear Telgar is next to Search,
With an egg-heavy queen.
Maybe tomorrow will be my lucky day.

Bartal finishes by strumming the light chording and looking upwards once more before stilling the strings.
