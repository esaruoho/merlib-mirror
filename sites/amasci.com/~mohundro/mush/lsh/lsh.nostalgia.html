Ah, LegionMUSH - We hardly knew ye!

Version 3 of LegionMUSH, which diverged slightly from DC Comics' Thirtieth century Legion universe after Legion of Super-Heroes (fourth series) #39 and the formation of the Legionnaires, began on arms.gps.caltech.edu, with a MUSH date of spring 2995. Later, it moved to its final resting place, muds.okstate.edu.

DC Comics' redefining Zero Hour mini-series effectively "rebooted" the timeline of the Legion universe (hence the term "preboot" given to LegionMUSH). As a result of the reboot, the continuity that was part of the old MUSH is no more - except in the hearts of those who played the game and read the comics.

LegionMUSH is no more, Long Live the Legion Outpost portion of DC Universe MUSH (206.16.238.1 1800)!

-- Steve Mohundro, aka Ol Gardan @ Outpost, and at least the following on LegionMUSH: Kid Kid (Gene Gray), Loomis, Warden Tsaquin, Prince Evillo, Garridan Ranzz, Dr. Mantis Morlo, the former Brin Londo, and formerly a certain fellow who's got a special place in my heart: Kent Shakespeare
