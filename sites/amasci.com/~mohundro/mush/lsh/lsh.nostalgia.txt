LegionMUSH (v3) Nostalgia Page 

 Ah, LegionMUSH - We hardly knew ye! 

Version 3 of LegionMUSH, which diverged slightly from DC Comics' 
Thirtieth century Legion universe after Legion of 
Super-Heroes (fourth series) #39 and the formation of the
Legionnaires, began on arms.gps.caltech.edu, with a MUSH date of spring 
2995. Later, it moved to its final resting place, muds.okstate.edu. 

DC Comics' redefining Zero Hour mini-series effectively 
"rebooted" the timeline of the Legion universe (hence the term "preboot" 
given to LegionMUSH). As a result of the reboot, the continuity that was 
part of the old MUSH is no more - except in the hearts of those who 
played the game and read the comics. 

LegionMUSH is no more, Long Live the Legion 
Outpost portion of DC 
Universe MUSH (206.16.238.1 1800) !
