FRIENDS OF THE LEGIONNAIRES

========
NAME: Kid Kid  (aka Gene Gray)
BIRTHDAY: Mar 23, 2982
POWER: Can change into a goat
RELATIONSHIP TO THE LEGIONNAIRES: Member of the SUBS Auxiliary
HAIR: Curly, wooly brown
EYES: grazing green
HEIGHT: 5'11"
HOME PLANET: Deltwan
HOBBIES: Tin Can collection, grazing, funny-animal comic book collecting.
FAVORITE LEGIONNAIRE: Tenzil Kem
THE LEGIONNAIRE GROUPIE SCOOP ON KID KID: Gene's powers are common
on the Gandian world of Deltwan, where natives have developed the power to 
change between goat and humanoid form.  Not content to graze in herds all his
life, 13 year-old Gene managed to get to New Earth and find a place for himself
with the SUBS Auxiliary.  He hopes someday to join his idols in the ranks of 
the Legionnaires!
