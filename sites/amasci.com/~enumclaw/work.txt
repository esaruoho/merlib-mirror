 Garden Work 

 Jordan developed an early
 fascination for his garden equipment. He walked the paths for
 hours by himself, wheeling his wheelbarrow. 

 John wheels an old
 orbiculare, pruned out of its spherical shape for moving, 
 on an even older plant carrier.

 Bob first transported rhododendrons
 (and sometimes John and
 his sister Nancy) on this carrier in the 1940s at their 
 New Jersey rhododendron park.
 It has been in continuous use ever since. 

 Trevor is one of the fourth generation to 
 work in the garden. Here he is planting 
 in the rich black loam that lured his great- 
 grandfather to move 3000 miles and start 
 all over when he was 51.

 while Jordan keeps wheeling
