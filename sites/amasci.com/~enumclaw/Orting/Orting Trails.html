Orting Trails



   Moss and ferns covered
      one of the forest paths. Many of the native plant species of
      the Puget Sound region were found in this forest. Many nurselogs
      supported younger trees. One supported a rhododendorn seedling.
      I moved it, host and all, to the garden in Enumclaw. 


 

  One
      set of paths followed the creeks. This one crossed a log bridge
      by one of several waterfalls. Rhododendrons were planted right
      to the water's edge. 
      





      
 Another trail through
      the rhododendrons led to Morgan Creek. This hemlock seeded itself
      in a rotted stump that has all but disappeared.
