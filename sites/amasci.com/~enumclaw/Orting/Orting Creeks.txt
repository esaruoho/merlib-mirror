 The property was a triangle,
bounded on two sides by a half mile of lakefed creeks. The springfed
creek crossed through the forestland. Salmon spawned in Kapowsin
and Morgan Creeks. 

 Looking down on Morgan Creek
 from the dining room 

 Trails followed the creeks and pass by
 several waterfalls. This was not a trail.
