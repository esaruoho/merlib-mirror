 The Anderson House
in Orting 

 In the same way the stone
house in New Jersey was built from on-site rocks and grew out
of the mid-Atlantic environment, the Orting house was constructed
primarily of western red cedar, hemlock, and douglas fir. Much
of the cedar used in construction came from trees on the site. 

 Decks and overhangs were oriented
to the views and the sun angles of summer and winter for solar
heat gain or shading. The peak to ground roof in the front provided
privacy, while three floors of windows on the back opened to Mount
Rainier and wilderness.
