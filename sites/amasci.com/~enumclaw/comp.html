Eastern dogwood
      blooms in May

Other flowering trees include eastern pink and Korean dogwood, styrax, franklinia, stewartia, viburnum, stranvesia, oxydendrum, star magnolia, flowering cherry, flowering plum, flowering apricot, and Chinese witch hazel.

 Chinese witch hazel blooms in January

 Firs, pines, and oaks 

Several varieties of oaks and pines were planted because of their root structures 
and the non-matting mulches they produce.  Other trees include dawn redwood, 
Serbian spruce, Port Orford cedar, giant sequoia, Douglas fir, grand fir, noble fir,  
and several kinds of Japanese maples.
