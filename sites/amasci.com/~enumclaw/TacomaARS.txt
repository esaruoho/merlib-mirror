 Tacoma Chapter of
the American Rhododendron
Society 

 If you live within driving
 distance of Tacoma/Puyallup, we invite you to join us for a monthly
 program about rhododendrons. You don't need to be a member to
 come and check us out. All people who are interested in rhododendrons
 are welcome, whether you are just beginning to learn about the
 plants or have a lifetime of experience with them. 

 Our members include casual
 gardeners, avid hobbyists, well-known hybridizers, nursery owners,
 and many others. If you ever wondered, "How do I prune a
 rhododendron?" or "How do you take cuttings?"
 or "What is this flower?", just ask one of us. 

 Meeting Information 

 Besides meetings, the Tacoma
 Chapter has some special projects for those interested. In the
 spring of each year, we sponsor a rhododendron
 truss show at the Puyallup Fairgrounds as part of the Spring
 Fair. This past year, we had over 500 trusses entered. 

 In 19 67 ,
 our Chapter established the Rhododendron
 Garden at Point Defiance Park in Tacoma. We have received
 two substantial grants in recent years from the American Rhododendron
 Society for restoration and new plant acquisition. 

 In 1993, we hosted the Annual
 Convention of the American Rhododendron Society. About 1000 people
 from around the world came together in Tacoma to share their
 knowledge and passion for these beautiful plants.
