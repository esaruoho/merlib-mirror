The First Anderson Rhododendron Park in Springfield, New Jersey  1938-1961
Prehistory (before Springfield)


 


      

During
      the Depression, Bob and Betty bought an eight-acre woodland on
      a lake twenty miles outside of New York City. Over the years,
      they built a unique stone house and rhododendron garden that
      have become landmarks for the area. Then, in their fifties, they
      moved across the country and started all over with a hayfield
      in Enumclaw.
