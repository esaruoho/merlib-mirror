 Blueberries 

 In the summer of
 2000, five blueberry bushes produced twenty-nine gallons of berries,
 but a year round supply of this tasty treat is not the only benefit
 of this beautiful cousin of the rhododendron. 

 The fall color of blueberry
 bushes is quite spectacular, and contrasts here with a honey
 locust tree. 

 Kiwi 

 Kiwi, or kiwifruit, is actually
 a berry. We use it as a transition between the herb garden and
 the rhododendrons. 

 Coming: lingonberries, raspberries, marionberries, grapes, alpine strawberries
