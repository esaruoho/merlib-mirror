 Fall Color in Rhododendrons 

 When we imagine fall we
 usually see large masses of saturated colors, but a closeup look
 can dazzle in a different way. Even large leaf rhododendrons
 change color, often brilliantly. The isolated intensity of a
 few previous year's leaves contrasts vividly with current year
 foliage. 

 Captain Jack, left 

 Naomi Nautilus, right 

 Tiffany and Ostbo's Red,
 below 

 The complementary colors of the
 old and new leaves on Tiffany create a striking effect since
 the pattern is quite uniform over the entire plant. 

 Leaves are not the only rhododendron
 color in the fall. Ostbo's Red Elizabeth usually blooms twice
 a year, as shown in this October 15th picture. Note also the
 blackish purple new growth of summer.
