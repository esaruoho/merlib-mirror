 Tacoma ARS Chapter
Plant Auction 

 back to Meeting
Information 

 Jake Fox is our auctioneer.

 This year he auctioned off
 90 high-quality plants at bargain prices. The opportunity to
 build your stock of rhododendron species, hybrids, and companion
 plants is just one benefit of belonging to the Tacoma Chapter
 of the American Rhododendron Society. 

 Jim Senko organized the auction.
 He catalogued all the plants and managed distribution of plants
 to successful bidders. Myrna Brackman recorded the bidders, plants
 and prices during the auction. Other volunteers included Bill
 Brackman, Billie Staab, and Bob Nelson. Contributors of 19 or
 more plants: Shigeki & Mutsuko Higashi, Don & Jean Clark,
 and Stu Imrie. 

 The proceeds of this year's
 auction totalled $1413, up from $1010 in 2003. Thanks to all
 the volunteers, contributors, and bidders.
