 Family at the Time 

 In 1941,
 Bob built this one bedroom cottage as a place for the family
 to live while he built the big house, and to see if his newly
 acquired masonry technique would work. But then war put a stop
 to everything for five years. Bob is thirty here, Betty twenty-eight,
 and Nancy three. I won't be born for two years. 

 Nancy looks at a
 model of the house she will grow up in. 

 Betty Anderson is shown with
 her mother and father in the summer of 1948. Nancy is nine and
 John (me) is five. 

 Following my father's example,
 twenty-nine years later I also designed and built a house using
 native materials (cedar) from a unique site. Click here 
 to see it.
