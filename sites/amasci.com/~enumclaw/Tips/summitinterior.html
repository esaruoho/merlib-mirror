Interior Sample of the Stone House

 

The fireplace wall was constructed of the same stone as the exterior walls. Most of the floors in the house were brick. Ceilings and walls were both redwood.

The entry at right featured a floor to ceiling window and one of two planter boxes open to the ground underneath.
