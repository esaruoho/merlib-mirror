Fall Color in Azaleas


 


      
   Deciduous azaleas
      lose their leaves each year, so that, unlike other rhododendrons,
      their fall color stands by itself. The intensity varies from
      plant to plant, depending upon moisture content and sun exposure.
      In species, such as the schlippenbachii below, a range
      of differences is also possible because of genetic variations.
