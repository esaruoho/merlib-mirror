 Other Strange Weather--November 22, 2003 and December 1965 

 We have had
 hardly any snow for five years, but had two storms in the past
 two weeks. A wet snow before Thanksgiving was followed by this
 dry, granular eight inches on December 3. The heavy accumulation
 transformed paths into deep channels and tunnels. Because it
 was so cold, the snow adhered to the plants for several days.
 The wet snow slid off by noon. 

 The snow, and
 later the ice, weighed down the bamboo to one-third its height.
 When the ice melted, the bamboo sprung back up to its original
 height.
