 Winter Gardening: What Blooms Here in January? 

 Chinese Witchhazel 

 I like the delicate flowers
 of these trees. They invite you to study the details of their
 unique shape and color. 

 Be sure to choose a witchhazel
 while it is in bloom. Many of them are orangish rather than clear
 yellow. 

 Hellebore 

 Hellebore have an exceptionally
 long blooming season--from January through April.
