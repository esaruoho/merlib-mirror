 Fruit Vegetables as Landscaping 

 We
 created the herb and vegetable gardens in 1998. The curves of
 the herb garden form a transition between the vegetable boxes
 and the rhododendron beds and paths. 

 The herb and vegetable beds were
 raised with loam from digging out the paths. 

 Sand and humus were added to
 loosen the soil, and perforated plastic pipe was placed under
 all paths (as it was in the entire garden) to increase drainage. 

 Posts for the grape and berry
 arbors have just been set.
