December 28-31 -- the Cloverdale Minor Hockey Association's Bantam C Tournament at the Fleetwood Arena, located at the Surrey Sports and Leisure Center in Vancouver, B.C

We'll be staying at the Holiday Inn Express Hotel & Suites in Surrey, B.C., just a couple of miles from the rink. Rooms are $99 per night. You can make your reservation by calling 1-866-930-8510. Ask for Nick and mention the group code, which is either SNO or Bantam Blue (sometimes it's under different things).

Important notes:

Neckguards ARE REQUIRED to play in Canada.

Passports are NOT required for driving over the border, but highly recommended (you can do the whole thing at the Bellevue Post office, including pictures, and it only takes a couple of minutes). If you don't have apossport, you will need a valid birth certificate for each person, including parents and kids. If you have any kind of picture I.D. for the kids, that is a help. If you're giving a ride to one of the kids on the team other than your own, you'll need a permission letter, too. Ask James for help with this.
