Adult Leader Requirements

The requirements come directly from the National Office. As such, we do not have the authority to waive or modify them in any way. The requirements for all adult leaders are:

  • participate in the required prejamboree training outing,
  • file a personal health and medical record prior to the prejamboree training,
  • participate in the Youth Protection Guidelines for Volunteer Leaders and Parents for Basic Training,
  • be approved by the local council,
  • meet the service requirement specific to the position:

    Scoutmaster: Have served as Scoutmaster for at least one full year during the three year period prior to July 1, 2005, and have completed Boy Scout Leader Fast Start and Scoutmastership Fundamentals, No. 34550. (Although not required, Wood Badge and/or Scoutmaster's Key is a desirable qualification.)

    First Assistant Scoutmaster: Same qualifications as Scoutmaster.

    Second Assistant Scoutmaster: At least 21 years of age by July 1, 2005.

    Third Assistant Scoutmaster: At least 18 years of age (but not have reached 21) by July 1, 2005.

    Adult Troop Leaders

    Troop Leaders selection is nearly completed. Most of the positions have now been filled, but there are still two spots open. If you are interested, you need to apply immediately. Once the spots are full, we will accept applications for Alternates to fill in in case someone drops out.

    National Staff

    The National Jamboree always has a lot of openings for youth and adult staff members. Staff members pay their own transportation costs and the full Jamboree fee, but those who have worked on a Jamboree staff invariably find it a fun and rewarding experience. Many adults return to work on staff for Jamboree after Jamboree. Jamboree Staff Applications are available now at the National Jamboree website.
