 These pages are dedicated
to early science and technology. Many advances in science depended on
improvements in accuracy of measuring devices and on the ability
of instruments to extend the range of human observations.
Most scientific instruments are often of vital everyday importance.
Surveyor needs theodolite 
 to build roads and railroads,
seaman needs sextant to navigate, doctor needs a microscope. Scientific knowledge
and progress is not possible without an appreciation of tools
available at any given time.
