III. E-mail Services:

  • A big list of car email lists
  • The BMW mailing list(digest only), to subscribe send mail to: E-MAIL with the subject line blank and "subscribe" in the body of the message. Or, read more information on subscribing.
  • The E36 BMW mailing list(digest only), to subscribe send mail to: E-MAIL with the subject line blank and "subscribe e36 your_email@address.com" in the body of the message. The E36-Digest web site is up and running.
  • The 318ti Compact BMW mailing list Subscription information is on it's homepage. E-MAIL with the subject line blank and "subscribe M3 digest" in the body of the message. E-MAIL Chris Kent, and ask to subscribe to the 2002 email list.
  • Driver's school mailing list, to subscribe send mail to: E-MAIL with the subject line blank and subscribe in the body of the message.
  • SCCA mailing list, to subscribe send mail to: E-MAIL with the subject line blank and subscribe in the body of the message.
  • Order Robert Bentley Publishing's excellent repair manuals via E-MAIL
  • BMW Tech Group E-MAIL list, you must be an experienced BMW Tech to subscribe to this private list. Please include: your BMW experience and qualifications; the name of the shop or dealer that you work for; your daytime (work) phone number; and your e-mail address. BMW Tech Group website.
