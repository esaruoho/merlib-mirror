Scott,

I recently became a convert to Simple Green. This stuff is great, besides doing a great job on grease, it does not irritate your skin( well mine at least) and is biodegradeable. Yeah the grease it entrains is only somewhat degradeable in our lifetime, but it really beats the stink and irritation of Gunk or my old favorite -- Brakleen--. Additionally Simple Green does not seem to cause the aluminum oxide formation that Brakleen does. Simpel Green also has a verry high WAF, Wife Acceptance factor. I can not say enough good about it. To remove the grease/gunk, spray with Simple Green wait 5 minutes and then spray off with clean water. I used a 2 gallon all plastic garden chemical sprayer to supply the clean water in a manageable spray. The sprayer cost about $15.
