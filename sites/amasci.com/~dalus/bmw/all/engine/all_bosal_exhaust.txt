>Bosal's system has been recommended to me. I know you're a "stock" kinda 
guy, but what's wrong with Bosal, or others for that matter? Can't I
improve performance and still get a quiet sound with quality? Your opinion

I tried a Bosal system on one of my personal vehicles and it began to rust
within 2 months. Both parts of the system needed replacing within a year.
Mail order houses push Bosal because there's good profit in it (I
accidently received my distributor's invoice when the muffler was
drop-shipped to me). I personally prefer to sell products that I'd use on
my own cars (ie,the original German exhausts). Besides, on this part for a
325e, the difference is only $30-50, not hundreds as on some other components.

 I received a competitor's mail order catalog last week that has a
picture of a typical Bosal exhaust piece. The next time I saw the picture,
it looked like it had already started to rust. SD
