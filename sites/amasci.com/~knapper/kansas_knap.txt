KANSAS KNAP 

 KANSAS
 KNAP-IN AT MAXWELL WILDLIFE REFUGE 

 OCTOBER 1 st and 2 nd , 2003 

 Come join other knappers at scenic Maxwell Wildlife
 Refuge, located 6 miles north and 1 miles west of Canton, Ks. 
 Primitive camping is available on the grounds, or at adjacent McPherson
 State Lake. This event will
 be held in conjunction with the Friends of Maxwell Fall Rendezvous. 
 Non period tents will have a separate camping area. 
 Information on the Fall Rendezvous is detailed on the opposite side of
 this flyer. 
 Bring your rock, trade items, and points for sale,
 a tarp to catch chips, and chair. Setup
 allowed as early as Thursday evening. No vehicles will be allowed in the
 exhibition area after set-up. The
 public will be invited to attend on Friday and Saturday, participants are
 invited to stay for Sunday. 
 Come break some rock in a unique Kansas setting! 
 Last year 1300 visitors attended, approximately 500 school kids on
 Friday. For information call
 Friends of Maxwell at 620.628.4455 or Randy Clark at 316.683.8069 (w) or
 620.543.6584 (h)
