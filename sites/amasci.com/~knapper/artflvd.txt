 The Art of Flintknapping Video Companion 

 D. C. Waldorf is well known as one of the foremost knappers in the United
States. He works with deer and moose antler billets, the traditional tools
used by the American Indians. In this tape he tries to cover areas where
people have a lot of problems, such as raw material processing, biface
reduction by percussion, holding positions and pressure flaking, ect. While
this tape is a must for beginners, it is also extremely useful and informative
for the more advanced knapper. It's also just plain entertaining. Filmed
in Super VHS for superior film quality. 1 hour 50 minutes. Produced by
Mound Builder Books, VHS format, includes a plastic protective case.
