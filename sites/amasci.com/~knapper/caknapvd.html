CAUGHT KNAPPING WITH CRAIG RATZAT

This tape, produced by Neolithics, is directed at the beginner to intermediate knapper. It deals strictly with knapping obsidian, from quarrying, to production of points and blades. Craig begins at Glass Buttes in Central Oregon and works on a variety of pieces, explaining edge preparation and percussion with various tools.

One of its best sections is the pressure flaking, in particular he spends some time notching a point. In fact, this tape has one of the best sections on notching we have seen in any tape, with lots of close ups.

This tape should NOT be confused with a tape produced by Mound Builder Books in the early 1990's, by the same name.

2 hours VHS
