Here is a memo I received from Johnny Maduzia. It explains about the Maxdale Knap In.

MEMO

Date: November 12, 2002

To: Flintknappers

 From: Annette Kylberg,

David and Royce Ann Crawford

Subject: Rocky Hollow Knap-In / Heart of Texas Knap-In (Maxdale)

 The Rocky Hollow Knap-In was originated by Don and Annette Kylberg at their home in the Spring of 1988. In 1990, Don and Annette, and David and Royce Ann Crawford joined together and brought the Rocky Hollow Knap-In (held the third full weekend of March) to Maxdale, Texas, where it has been held at the Retired Sergeant Majors Chili Park ever since. The Heart of Texas Knap-In, at the same location, began several years ago and has been held the first full weekend of October.

 The group that made these knap-ins possible has been the Retired Sergeant Majors Association (RSMA) of our area. They are the ones who maintain the Chili Park. They have been gracious enough over the years to ready the Chili Park for countless numbers of our flintknapping group and our friends. Over the years, they installed bathrooms and outdoor plumbing to accommodate us, mowed and sprayed to prepare for tents, teepees, campers, etc. They have provided onsite food so we could all eat and visit with each other at the same time and not have to make the 20-mile drive into Killeen for food. They have enjoyed, encouraged, and supported our efforts to have a place to come together and learn from each other the art of flintknapping.

 The knappers who attended the Chili Park knap-ins in the past enjoyed the opportunity to go on rock runs to Fort Hood. This privilege was revoked several years ago due to the new archeologist on base. Then 9-11-02 occurred and security on the post became an even bigger issue. Rock runs into Fort Hood are no longer sanctioned by the military authorities.

As many of you may know, the RSMA has had setbacks over the past several years mostly medical challenges. For this reason, the RSMA has decided to turn their efforts to only RSMA endeavors. Therefore, the Rocky Hollow Knap-In and the Heart of Texas Knap-In will cease to exist as we have known them.

 We are very grateful to the owners of the Chili Park property, the W.C. Walden family, and the RSMA for the years they have allowed us the use of their property.

 Even though we are no longer able to continue the Rocky Hollow and Heart of Texas Knap-Ins at Maxdale, it is our desire that a Central Texas knap-in will be continued by other individuals or groups.  We will endeavor to notify you if the knap-ins are rescheduled.  As Don Kylberg said just prior to his death, Flintknapping is a great way to draw friends together. This has been the best eight years of my life. We sincerely hope that the tradition of these knap-ins will continue. Please feel free to forward this memo to individuals who may have attended these knap-ins in the past.

 Sincerely,

 Annette Kylberg

David and Royce Ann Crawford
