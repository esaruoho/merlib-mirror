 Guide to the Palaeo-Indian Artifacts

of North America 

 Dr. Gramly's primary goal was the compilation of all Palaeo

Indian artifacts. His second goal was to standardize terminology related

to such artifacts. The third goal was to show the variation that exists,

rather than trying to simplify or group together artifacts for the sake

of economy. Dr. Gramly is world famous for his studies and excavations

of Palaeo sites. 

 Artifacts are arranged in alphabetical order into an illustrated

dictionary of Palaeo material. If you don't know what a keeled scraper,

grattoir or limace is, you will appreciate this book! They are Palaeo tools

you may be throwing away or leaving behind on your artifact hunts! 

 Chapters are: 

 Acknowledgements 

 Preface 

 Descriptions of Artifacts, Alphabetically Arranged 

 References Cited 

 Index 

 Locations of Specimens Figured in This Study
