THE VOYAGE OF A LIFETIME


In French, Le Voyage means "the journey." This is a journey of transformation. When you apply Le Voyage, the healing properties of the essential oil will transform its wearer. Le Voyage is a handmade fragrance that is created drop by drop with all pure essential oils and absolutes.

There are no synthetics in this fragrance. Each bottle is lovingly filled the way it was meant to be. The olfactory composition is an extravagant floral bouquet with citrus top notes, a heart filled with Bulgarian rose, jasmine, rare and exotic flowers and unique oils, coming together in a woody base warmed by sensuous sandalwood and other precious resins.

The French bottle has a very feminine feel with soft curves and fits well in the palm of your hand. The upscale, exclusive packaging is reminiscent of old classic French style. The exquisite crest is gold embossed, raised and set off beautifully with gold foil stamped piping on the box edges.

Le Voyage is an unparalleled eau de toilette. Each radiant bottle is lovingly blended from rare and precious essential oils to form a harmonious potion. The superior combination of exotic flowers and sacred oils creates a romantic bouquet. The sensuous fusion has a mystical quality with the potential to transform its wearer as the essence of the eau de toilette is emanated from the body. This haunting fragrance embodies mysteries of the universe that will reveal its secrets and wisdom to you. Opening this luscious French parfum decanter will invoke in you a new sense of the divine.

-- This article is taken from Perfume 2000, January 2002
