Use displays to allow customers to see all the different essential oils available from you, and also to present our easy-to-read User Guide. The Guides includes traditional properties of each of the 30 most popular essential oils, plus suggestions for use. The displays are free when you purchase the inventory to fill them. The 36 Oil Display holds 3 each of either 12 oils, or 6 oils and 6 synergies. The 18 Oil Display holds 3 each of 6 oils or synergies. Each display has a holder for User Guides.
