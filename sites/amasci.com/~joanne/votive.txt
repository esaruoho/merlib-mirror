 VOTIVE CANDLE HOLDERS 

 This genuine Austrian crystal candle holder stands 2 5/8 inches high. Various natural
stones, pearls, 
or shells are hand soldered onto the glass. To remove candle wax, place in freezer
briefly, then 
tap on the bottom and the wax will pop out. 
(Code R502) 

VH1 - Turquoise, emerald, and clear quartz crystal
VH3 - Amethyst, sodalite, lepidolite, and clear quartz crystal 
VH4 - Moonstone, quartz, pearl, and clear quartz crystal
VH5 - Garnet, carnelian, rose quartz, and clear quartz crystal
VH6 - Rose quartz, amethyst, abalone, and clear quartz crystal
