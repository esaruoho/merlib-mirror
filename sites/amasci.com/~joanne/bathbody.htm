BODY AND BATH OILS

Our body and bath oils combine cold pressed oils of almond/pecan, apricot kernel, sesame, wheat germ oil, and essential oils to protect the skin from moisture loss and from environmental pollutants. Blue cobalt bottles protect the essential oils.

ENERGY - A stimulating blend of Bergamot, Rosemary and Peppermint pure essential oils
RELAXING - A relaxing and calming blend of Lavender, Tangerine, and Marjoram pure essential oils
SENSUALITY - A highly erotic blend of Vetiver, Ylang Ylang, and Patchouli pure essential oils
MUSCLE RELIEF - A soothing blend of Rosemary, Juniper Berry, Lavender, and Eucalyptus Radiata pure essential oils draw out tightness

Put 1/4 to 1/2 ounce of body and bath oil into warm bath water just before getting in. Stir to enhance the healing effects. Relax! Or, apply body and bath oil to moist skin after shower or bath. These oils may also be used for massage.
