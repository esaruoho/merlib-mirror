About Bassett Aromatherapy 

 Bassett 
 Aromatherapy was created by JoAnne Bassett in 1993. The 
 business grew out of the need to find high quality essential oils. 
 The health food store variety did not feel right energetically. 
 My mission was to find and import oils that were still alive and 
 where you could "feel" the soul of the plant. My mottos 
 became "Smell the Difference" and "Fresh and Alive." 
 I have travelled to 30 countries and have discovered small family 
 operations where people care about the land, the plants, and the 
 proper distillation method to produce the finest therapeutic essential 
 oil. The good intention starts with the small family grower on 
 through distillation, to Bassett Aromatherapy and to the end user 
 the client. 
 I 
 strive to find the highest quality oils possible and stock an 
 extensive collection of wildcrafted and organic essential oils, 
 absolutes, CO2's, and organic hydrosols, rare hydrodistilled hydrosols, 
 and solid parfums. 
 I 
 manufacture synergies, massage oils, bath and body oils, Zone 
 Therapy oils, and body lotions. My latest venture is creating 
 exquisite natural eau de toilettes and parfums in French perfume 
 bottles with exceptional packaging. I import French handmade wooden 
 diffusers that are a piece of art. I stock bottles, carrier oils, 
 and accessories for you to make your own special mixtures.
