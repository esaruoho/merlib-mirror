Foot Care & Carrier Oils

ZONE THERAPY™

This highly concentrated grapeseed base massage oil may be used as a foot massage oil or “spot massage oil”. Try on the back of the neck. Use as a footbath to ease hot and tired “marathon” feet. Add 15 drops to a basin of warm water. Swirl the water to disperse the blended oils and relax for at least 15 minutes.

RELAXING - Contains pure essential oils of Tangerine, Lavender, Lemon, Marjoram, and Petitgrain.
REJUVENATING - Contains pure essential oils of Eucalyptus, Pine, Peppermint, and Geranium.

CARRIER OILS

In most cases it is not recommended to apply essential oils directly to the skin. We recommend adding your essential oils to the following pure unrefined vegetable base oils:

Sweet Almond Oil - Has excellent penetrating properties, particularly good for dry, itchy, sore skin.
Apricot Kernel Oil - Great for skin that is prematurely aged, dry, sensitive, or inflamed.
Grapeseed Oil - Good for all skin types, contains vitamins, minerals, and proteins. Nonallergenic.
Jojoba Oil - Used to treat skin disorders, eczema, acne, highly penetrable, also used in hair care.
