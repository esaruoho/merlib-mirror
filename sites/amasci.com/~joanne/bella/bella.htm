Q & A With Natural Perfumer JoAnne Bassett:

KLB: When did you first start making natural perfumes?

JB: I first began making natural perfumes in 1994. As an aromatherapist for my company Bassett Aromatherapy I would be asked to do custom blending and private label. This would result in my creating new synergies with essential oils that would be used in a line of products for a company. I would create a "feeling" or "ambiance" for a store or spa with a synergy used in my French professional electric diffuser placed within their space.

Periodically the owner would ask if I could make her a "custom" parfum. This would be a time consuming process often taking 4 hours. We would have a consultation, introduction to the essential oils smelling the various groups of fruits, florals, woods, to see what her preferences are. There would be 3 to 5 initial scents created and then we would meet to see what she liked. I would then "tweak" the base and and let it sit a month to "marry". We would then get together to make any adjustments.

This was a fun and creative thing to do but my business was growing and I wasn't able to do many of these blending sessions.

KLB: Tell me more about what your inspiration was behind your fragrances, and some of the ingredients they contain.

JB: I had a series of dreams over the period of one week. During the night I would wake up and write down a thought or a few words. A few hours later I would wake up and be compelled to write a few more words. The words were in French. The name "Le Voyage parfum naturelles " came first. Le Voyage means "the journey" in French and parfum naturelles means natural perfume.

In subsequent dreams I received the name "Reveiller" TM which means awaken. There are more names to be revealed when I create the others. It was very magical and special. This would be my new line. JoAnne Bassett Inc. was born. All natural parfum made the original way. No synthetic fragrance oils and blended by hand drop by drop. At the same time I had been discovering new essential oils from rare and sacred plants from India and from other exotic lands. So this became "my journey" with blending these precious oils. Many of the plants and flowers are harvested by hand from pristine surroundings and distilled by hand.

Le Voyage was the first parfum I created. Everyone who tried it loved it. The comments were, "it is beautiful, you are really on to something", and this makes me want to go deeper into it; I want to journey with it. The parfum was already named Le Voyage so this comment reaffirmed my belief that I had done the right thing in creating this parfum. It was perfect. I hadn't told anyone the name of the parfum! It is a fruity, floral that contains jasmine, rose, sandalwood, and other exclusive and costly essences.

Reveiller TM is an herbal and woody fragrance with floral and citrus topnotes. It also contains concentrated rare essences that are hydrodistilled in glass to preserve the integrity of the plants and flowers. These superior essential oils give the parfum the special qualities that you cannot find in synthetic fragrances.

KLB: How expensive are some of these natural ingredients?

JB: Some of the natural ingredients cost $1,200.00 an ounce! These concentrated essential oils and absolutes are magnificent and very powerful. The old French perfum houses started using the synthetic fragrance oils to bring down their ingredient costs and raise their profits.

The higher end perfumes such as Joy claim they contain jasmine or rose are using less than 1% of the essential oils and absolutes. The total essential oil content in perfumes is no more than 1% to 2%. This information is taken from articles written regarding the perfume industry.

KLB: How does your perfume alcohol base differ from what one might find in a department store?

JB: Our eau de toilettes contain natural grape alcohol which has a soft and fruity smell. The department store eau de toilettes contain SDA - specially denatured alcohol which contain chemical additives to make them undrinkable. They are very harsh smelling and drying to the skin.

KLB: These scents must be great for those with allergies. What has your experience been?

JB: People have told me they can't normally wear fragrance but they are able to wear Le Voyage and Reveiller TM. They are great for people with allergies. Most allergies are a result of the synthetic chemicals contained in the fragrances. A male friend of mine that has serious allergies wears the Reveiller TM everyday and loves it.
