Application: Fungal contamination of room air

Symptoms: Moldy smell

Aromatherapy approach: for decontamination blend equal parts savory, geranium, palmarosa, and tea tree in a diffuser.

Inhalation:
Diffusers are a staple of the aromatherapy industry and are found in many homes. Diffusing essential oils with an electric diffuser with a nebulizer has far-reaching benefits, such as reducing microorganisms in room air. Difffusing essential oils in rooms and buildings is also an effective method of keeping fungal infestations away and reducing the spore count. The electric nebulizing diffuser uses a jet stream of air to disperse oils and does not subject them to heat. These cold air diffusers disperse the essential oils in their complete form. Aroma lamps use heat to evaporate the essential oil so not all of the components will be evaporated. Some components remain in the dish so therefore these lamp diffusers that use candles or a heat source or not as effective.

Effectiveness:
Effectiveness against room-air microorganisms was one of the first properties of essential oils that was very thoroughly documented by science in the mid-1950s. Essential oils diffused into houses and room air contaminated fungal spores, effectively destroying microorganisms and reducing the resulting allergies.

Supporting measures:
Fresh air and dry conditions if possible.
This information taken from “Medical Aromatherapy” by Kurt Schnaubelt.

Toxic mold is a modern day buzz word. There have been environmental toxicology studies done to determine the effects on the people that have been in contact with toxic mold. There are many detrimental effects on health such as decreased respiratory functions, dry cough, shortness of breath and difficulty breathing, allergy type symptoms with watering eyes, coughing up blood, lack of energy, unable to focus, lack of mental clarity, low blood pressure, etc. In some more serious cases there is long term damage and sometimes death from exposure. Animals have also become ill due to toxic mold in their environment.

Some of the essential oils that have been tested to have strong anti fungal properties are cinnamon bark, ginger, lemon, oregano, sweet orange, tea tree, and thyme.

Essential oils to assist breathing are eucalyptus globulus, Balsam fir, hyssop, inula graveolens, pine, ravensara, and black spruce. These essential oils diffused into your environment with an electric diffuser will help with respiratory functions and assist in killing air borne mold spores. A mold remediation of the house or building will also need to be taken care of professionally to insure the continued safety of the inhabitants.
