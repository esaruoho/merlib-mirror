As scratchy is having an amazing performance in ICS, as a comfort I like
to show how even we non-masters can draw Deep Thought :)

Also I take this opportunity to advertise my Perl script that takes ICS
mail and formats it to readable. The following moves are straight from
it, I just added comments.

Played 13.06. at eve:
Mika - scratchy (aka Deep Thought):

1. c2-c4  Ng8-f6  2. Nb1-c3  e7-e5  3. Ng1-f3  Nb8-c6  4. g2-g3  Bf8-b4
5. Bf1-g2  o-o  6. o-o  Rf8-e8  7. d2-d3  d7-d6  8. e2-e4?

Theory gives 8.Nd5 Nxd5 9.cxd5 Nd4 10.Nxd4 exd4 11.e4! dxe3 12.fxe3 Bc5
13.d4 +=

8. ... Bb4-c5!  9. a2-a3  Nc6-d4!  10. b2-b4  Nd4-f3  11. Bg2-f3  Bc5-d4
12. Bc1-d2  c7-c6  13. Ra1-b1  Bc8-h3  14. Bf3-g2  Bh3-g2  15. Kg1-g2
d6-d5 16. f2-f3  d5-c4?!

There was no hurry with this exchange.

17. d3-c4  Qd8-e7  18. Nc3-e2  Re8-d8  19. Qd1-c2  h7-h6  20. Rf1-d1  Rd8-d7
21. Bd2-c3  Ra8-d8  22. c4-c5!  Bd4-c3  23. Ne2-c3  b7-b6  24. Nc3-a4!  b6-b5
25. Na4-c3  a7-a5  26. Rd1-d7  Rd8-d7  27. Rb1-d1  a5-a4?!

Also this was not yet necessary. But I guess it is annoying for a computer to
count pawn catches every move :)

28. h2-h4  Qe7-d8  29. Kg2-f2  Rd7-d1  30. Qc2-d1  Qd8-d1  31. Nc3-d1  Kg8-f8
32. Nd1-e3  g7-g6

I think this is draw. But now comes a weakening.

33. g3-g4?  Kf8-e7  34. Ne3-c2  Ke7-d7  35. Kf2-e3  Kd7-e7  36. Ke3-f2  Ke7-d7

37. Kf2-e3  Kd7-e6  38. Ke3-f2  h6-h5  39. g4-g5  Nf6-d7  40. Kf2-g3  f7-f6
41. g5-f6  Ke6-f7!?  42. Nc2-e3  Kf7-f6  43. f3-f4!

I think 43.Nc2 Nf8 44.Ne3 Ne6 45.Nc2 g5 loses.

43. ...  e5-f4  44. Kg3-f4  Kf6-e6  45. Ne3-c2  Nd7-e5  46. Nc2-d4  Ke6-f6
47. Nd4-c2  Ne5-d3  48. Kf4-e3  Nd3-b2
49. Ke3-f4  Nb2-c4  50. Nc2-d4!

Otherwise king must move and black plays g5 and wins. Now 50.-Nxa3? 51.e5+!
and to me it looks like white wins. So ...

50. ... Nc4-e5  51. Nd4-c2  Ne5-g4  52. Nc2-d4  Ng4-e5
53. Nd4-c2  Ne5-f7  54. Nc2-d4  Nf7-e5  55. Nd4-c2  (1/2)
