One bad side about not knowing so much about end games is that
generally you play end games pretty badly :-).  And you are very
uncertain when trying to assess things.  Seeing that I have a sure
draw against the big beast, I got a high fever, and the world started
spinning.  Who wouldn't be itching to even draw against scratchy?
Scratching my sweaty forehead, I just couldn't get deep in my thoughts
(should I take the c-pawn and even try for a win, at the same time
risking a blunder so typical of me?), so I was glad to see my fingers
type "draw"!

But hey, isn't white winning here?  It is pretty obvious:  41. Kc2!
Nd3 42. Nc4 Ne1+ 43. Kd2 Nxg2 44. a4 Nf4 45. a5 Ne6 46. a6 Nc7 47. Nb6
+-.  During the game I only looked at 41. Kc3 Nd1 42. Kxc4 Ne3+ etc.,
which unfortunately also wins.  But I was so nervous and excited...
