 An Impossible Assignment (last stanzas) 
 by Joe Jesseph 

 Words only point beyond:
 "There, there!...Wherein you cannot see."
 There lies the Truth,
 Unspoken and unseen,
 Without a touch or scent or taste,
 Magnificently more lovely ever
 Than the best of human artifice
 Can be.

 Oh, failing, insufficiet words,
 Relieve me of this pen!
 An impossible assignment this,
 When even great Beethoven
 Could not sing a soundless song,
 Nor transcribe on his noted paper
 An eternal One
 Not marked by beats or tone.

 No tools of this
 Poor separate son
 Do justice to the One!

 And so I lay my pen aside;
 Take up the heartfelt silent song;
 Retire unto an inner spaceless space
 To leave this sense impaired
 And desperate, shattered land;
 To leave this fractured starry universe;
 Surrendering any thought of lives
 In separate places
 Future, parallel or past;
 Absorbed into the Heart of God,
 These words laid down at last.

 - Joe Jesseph
