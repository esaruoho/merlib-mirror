Chantana's Poetry ! 

 Chantana Murugan's poetry for your enjoyment ! 

 Chantana is a Chemistry major at the University of Malaya in 
Malaysia. He also enjoys writing poetry and sent me the poems below 
that he 
has written under the pen-name of Thulasi Sinakanu. 
(See background note at 
end of page.) Personally, I think he has a lot of talent. You may 
 Chantana if so moved !

Countdown ! 
 A 
Rally Without a roar ! 
 Waking 
 up ! 

 Countdown ! 

Am I dawning a new millenium?
Or is it just a day dream?
maybe I'll meet it with dignity ... maybe not
maybe I'll meet it daringly, delectably ... maybe not

Whatever that maybe may be 
It will certainly be an 
open confession , discussion, ...

Only time will tell
What it may be 

Ooops....
Aren't I reminding myself of the countdown!
Which I pretend very hard not to exist?

Till then ...
 it's a mental revolution.

Thulasi Sinakanu
Taman Subang

 A Rally Without a roar ! 

Walking through the passage of time
Resurrection of joy and pain
encountered with a squirrel in a mist of musk
taint the air with a sense of chagrin

Memories wither and strew 
What was once a picture of clear, prohibited sky
was dramatically crucified by an illusionary dance 
dangling and rejoicing in an uncertain wind
... Dreadfully convinced by some careless whispers

Rays illuminated
Melting the ice
Creating an ultimate glacier of dreams
never ever 

then...

When the sun's brightness became dim
Turning the youthful day into premature night
When the chill earth ruptured
Turning the glacier into sunken daydream
ever after

The sago tree was left to discover 
the mystifying climatic change
to justify the squirrel's emergence
which ...
 never had appeared
as ...
 there was already a far better niche

Leaving the sky naked and wicked
Leaving trails of sorrow and darkness
Leaving an unusual activity in one's domain
Letting one spun around like a toy
Letting one in an enormous amount of discontent
Letting one quaking in a lost world

Leaving one to live up to the rally
 ... without A ROAR

Thulasi Sinakanu
Taman Subang

 Waking up ! 

Days have passed 
Gloomy shadows of yesterday is slowly cast aside
Today I awake with a thought

Shock and unsymphatetic web of events 
was
a periodical assessment of fate 
to 
upgrade my inner capacity

Shackling my emotions
I vow not to look back
I bid farewell and the best to the yesterday
I aspire to live for tomorrow

I am now able to see things closer 
... than what they appear
Ever alert to expect the unexpected

I now realise that 
joy and sorrow
is in a dynamic equilibrium
to be encountered alongside the long winding road of life

I now wake up to face a revitalised tomorrow

Thulasi Sinakanu
Taman Subang

( Thulasi is the name of a plant that plays an 
integral part in the
 Indian culture. It is also commonly used as a female's name, hence a
 combination of purity, simplicity and internal expression gave way to the
 incorporation of Thulasi. Sinakanu refers to my father's name , as a 
 mark of respect to the family that has brought me to what I am today .
 Taman Subang is the district where I stay.)
