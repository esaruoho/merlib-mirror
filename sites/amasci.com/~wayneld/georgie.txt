 Comforter, Companion ! 
by Georgie

Comforter, your gentle peace
Reassures me, once again,
For all my great folly,
Grasping even at your message
To weigh and judge others by,
To meet or discard those I would use otherwise,
For all my great folly,
You are always there, 
Seeking but to hold me in the 
Strength of your plan,
Ever more to remind me
That you have not abandoned us here.

 The Second Place ! 
by Georgie

No where to turn now. Beyond the veil, 
within the world, but not of it,
lies the first place that was always ours.
Yet I fear that if I do not lead
my self to do what it must do,
I will be in pain, within the veil.
Perhaps it is too much to ask...
My weary soul still strays from a
border self cannot approach alone.

I fear to walk with You, for You may 
not lead me down the path I can see,
toward my shifting awareness of 
the boarders of my sure beliefs.
I desired a carriage of 
my own design. Look, you say, this is 
what that toy is for. No, I reply, 
I made it not so. Then let me ride 
with you, You offer. I take the reins.

I thought it was career
that led me to fulfillment.
I came with more than enough
to make the world more secure.
How many different trys,
how many promising stages,
gone now, the last attempt
to keep my self distracted,
and make of the world a better place.

I thought it was a mentor
that would show me newfound life,
seeking help to groom my talent,
to be remembered for having 
plucked a thistle, planted a rose, 
wherever he said it need be.
Gone now, the last attempt
to mold my self to another's script,
and make of the world his better place.

I thought it was soul mate,
that led me to fulfillment.
I came with much love to give,
to make his world a better place.
How many different trys,
how many promising loves, 
gone now, the last attempt
to keep my self distracted,
to get my needs, in order to give.

I thought it was a marriage,
children to share history with,
that led me to fulfillment.
I came with so much patience,
to mold a family into 
salvation, into a future.
Gone now, the last attempt
to keep my self distracted,
to build what was never mine to build.

I thought it was a house,
a warm, inviting place, where 
no one, no thought could intrude,
without my willingness to give.
How many cherished times,
it greeted me, "this is yours."
Gone now, the safety of the keep,
that kept my self distracted,
to protect me from my outside me.

I thought it was politics,
where good could stay in the hands
of those who said that they cared,
to tell us what cracks to avoid,
and spread the butter over all
the ever changing common good.
Gone now, my confidence in others
that kept my self distracted,
to delegate what I could not handle.

I thought it was religion,
Holy Mother Church to offer
peaceful coexistence with the 
world, to find me when career, love, 
children, home, society, went 
their separate ways--flicker of hope.
Gone now, bastion of the here and now,
that kept my self distracted,
a fanciful altar, barricading the grips of hell.

I thought it was a soothsayer,
offering promise of another 
form, a hint of forms to come, 
in this world and the next. An 
oracle--this one yes, that no. 
Salvation requires discrimination.
Gone now, smoke screens for alternate scripts,
that kept my self distracted,
using bits of reality to root the dream.

All paths lead to nowhere, where meaning
defines the form. The meaning of each 
form, each scene, goes from promise to 
disappointment, more quickly now than 
in the past. And still I cling, thinking 
they must be this way or that to clear
the path to joy. Overt covertness, 
the insanity of my world comes,
defining my self, defaming Self.

Uncover my eyes, my pilgrim guest.
The murky barricades of blindness 
keep me defending against the hell
of my time within this world of dreams,
my dreams for safety yet another 
level of fear. I feel the mud, 
and ask that I might know that you have 
whiped it from my eyes so that my
blindness might be gone forever.

How many gods I have believed in,
how many gods have failed to stem the
crying of my soul. Good intentions 
framed those sacred gods. The I, I thought 
I knew, kept trying, to force the right 
to come my way. Gone now, my faith in 
self, that kept my Self distracted. I 
choose the second place, to gain the first.
Could it be, You carried me, and Self is You?

 Can I Play Teacher Now ? 
by Georgie

Jesus, you goofed it up!
Encouraging us to be teachers,
Didn't you know how much
We want to be in charge of our
Part of the curriculum?

Now that I know what you said,
How do I proceed?
Some want it this way,
And some want it that.
Why can't they all just accept?

Certainly, you must have known
That teaching requires a committee,
And certainly, there is a curriculum,
Well established and ready for us
To deliver, to those we would coach.

Compare and contrast!
Attention now, this is what 
Must be done, in order for Jesus'
Course to be spread,
To all parts of our mind, signed up for the ride!

What did I forget now?
Let's see, I have my references,
My copyright permission,
My credentials from several course schools.
I've even applied to be reverend!

What's that, you say?
Did you hear what he said?!
Will we ever learn?
Pour on the curriculum, 
He must have missed something!

Child, what are you doing in here?
This is a school for big boys.
Why does it matter?
We must get it right, of course.
We blew it before, but nevermore!

Peace, where are you now?
Could it be that those who flunk,
Those who don't sign up,
Those who get it perfect,
And even those who 'teach',
Might all graduate together? 

But wait a minute!
Who's in charge here?
Are You doing my job for me?
There must be something in
The contract about THAT!

God, I need a sabbatical! 

 To A Misbegotten Story ! 
By Georgie

You say you do not love me,
That there is something missing.
I cry a little, deep inside
And turn away, in my pain.
How can I fight the river?

...You are safe, my child.
All is in order...

But who will hold me now?
Who will share this experience,
And make it OK to awake
In the morning? 
Who will care?

...You are safe, my child.
All is in order...

Another day. Juice, vitamins,
A quick shower.
Where are those hair combs?
Don't forget to unplug the curling iron.
Traffic, does it ever get better?

...Remember the peace, my child.
All is in order...

Damn! Another computer problem!
Another boring day,
Fighting away the fear of layoff,
Not sure if I want to go or stay...
Where is the reward anymore?

...Don't fight the river, my child.
All is in order...

Where is the adventure in life?
Conflict comes, no matter what.
The remains of the gain, yet to be lost.
Where is the child in the tree,
Looking where I've been, seeing where I am?

...Patience, my child.
Much ado to undo...

The void, it keeps catching
Up to me. When does happiness start?
Will I win the lottery or die a pauper?
Will HE fall into my life?
Will I become what I am to become when I grow up?
If I don't do it, who will?

The voice is still. I knew it!
Does IT know what I need or care?
I shall die in the cold,
A bag lady in the end.
Uncared for, unnoticed.

What is that, you say?
Stop wanting someone? No.
Stop wanting them to play my role.
Yet, it doesn't get better? This is all there is?
This is it? This is life? Now? This?

...I love you, my child.
Your discovery is your adventure.
Be with me now.
Trust. All is in order.
I will never leave you...

No matter what, no matter what?

 Wood ! 
by Georgie

Sweet smell of burning pine,
Joined with the deep golden lamp
Flickering warmth pierces the darkness,
Dryness of air, sucks out the moisture
From wood, dampened by snow.

Headlights reflecting on water,
A world apart joins the
Image of the world within
Penetrating the black glass,
Silent images of passing life.

Quiet, quiet, the passages of time,
Silently slip over the images of
The still and moving forms,
Intertwining with the waft of
Air, lacing all things together.

Continuous into the future,
Messages from the past,
Dance in the present,
Never changing, as one blends
Into the other, felt only at points.

January 20,1996
