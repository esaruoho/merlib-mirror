Puppet Show 

 PUPPET SHOW 
 Players on a stage
 Are we.
 The lines 
 The author wrote
 So long ago
 Now acted out
 With passion,
 As though real.

 Actor,
 Be faithful
 To your part,
 Remembering
 The Self
 Who smiles
 And patiently awaits
 The melodrama's end,
 Already penned.

 Oh, actor
 Play your role!
 The Watcher
 Shall be faithful
 To His keep;
 The shepherd faithful
 To his fold.

 Joe Jesseph 11/07/90
