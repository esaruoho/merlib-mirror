DYING

	We come here all,
	Come here all to die;
	All to die in Jesus' arms;
	To let him take
	A tiny, bitter self
	To be forgiven
	And exchanged
	For yet a truer,
	Lovely Self
	Wherein no death is known.

	We come here all to die.
	"We go together, you and I."  1
	We go together to our own
	Where death is life,
	And life is Home.

	We thank you
	Gentle Priestess   
	For the tender caring
	Of your arms
	And for your teachers,
	Our companions,
	Who lead and guide us
	On the way through
	Seeming terror and pain;
	On to comforted surrendering
	Which is no loss at all,
	But only giving
	Turned to gain.

	We come here all to die.
	"We go together, you and I."  1
	Go together through the chamber,
	Go together through the vault,
	Go together through the clouds of guilt
	To set our essence free.
	"I cannot go without you,
	For you are a part of me."  2



              Dedicated to the
              Faculty and Staff of
              The Foundation for
              A Course In Miracles,
              and to their Priestess.
              
                  -Joe Jesseph
                    6/22/95
