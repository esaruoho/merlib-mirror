 BODY AND SOUL 
 By Joe Jesseph 

 Be your body fat or thin,
 It matters not,
 It matters not, my friend,
 But we must let the spirit in
 To be our body's guide,
 And pull the strings,
 And banish thoughts 
 Of "me" and "mine"
 So that the only songs
 Our puppet sings
 Reflect the love of God
 And all we do 
 But manifests
 Forgiving things.

 What shall I eat?
 Seems weighty and profound.
 Oh, what shall I eat!
 I'm told that I become
 But what I eat,
 And who would want to be
 Regurgitated, 
 Dead,
 Red meat?

 Well, there's another way
 Of being,
 I've been told,
 Wherein this body's dross
 Is woven 
 In a ring 
 Of shining gold;
 Becomes a means 
 Of charity and kindness...
 Guided by right mindedness...
 But, sad to say,
 To find this other way,
 I must let this body go.
 "You are His Son"
 Must mean
 The body and the world
 I took for real
 Were ne'r begun.

 "You are his Son"
 Must mean
 The body that 
 I think I am
 Is nothing
 But a dream.

 If I am God's Own Creation
 Then is my being spirit
 All aglow,
 And I not I
 But vaster, far,
 Than petty brain can know?
 Must I let my precious thinking go
 To listen rather inwardly than out;
 Attend within for whispers
 And ignore the outer shout?

 How strange the invitation seems
 That says "You are God's Son".
 And, yet, how disappointing
 And sad
 The other world I thought I was
 And had.

 -Joe Jesseph
