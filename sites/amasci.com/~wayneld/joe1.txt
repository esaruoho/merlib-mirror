 SONG OF LIFE 
 by Joe Jesseph 
 How thin
 The melody
 The body sings!
 How frail
 The song
 Of sinew, bones and blood;
 Of nerves and gut.

 Empty!
 All this fleshy stuff;
 Only forms of "unfulfilled".
 The body stands for 
 Aching loneliness
 And dreams of insecurity;
 Of fear and death.

 How frail the fleshy song
 The body sings, and
 Yet the world would
 Play its tune
 As if there were 
 No other:
 No alternative to emptiness;
 No substitute for fear
 And death.

 How dumb the world's eyes
 Must be,
 And blinded to the sight of God,
 And lost from knowledge
 Of eternity!

 - Joe Jesseph
