Hiking & Biking

Colorado is just full of great places to bike (sorry, "cycle") and hike. Some of the better places I've been include:

  • Mount Falcon
  • Rocky Mountain National Park (the best!)
  • Red Rocks
  • Horsetooth Mountain
  • St. Mary's Glacier
  • Red Feather Lakes
  • Boreas Pass outside Breckenridge
  • Hewlett Gulch in the Poudre River Canyon
