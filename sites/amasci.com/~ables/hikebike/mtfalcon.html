Mount Falcon

Mount Falcon is south and a bit west of Red Rocks park (you can even see Red Rocks from the trail). A man named Walker owned much of the land and built his home (practically a castle) in the early 1900s and had an idea of building what he hoped would become the summer White House, but nothing more than a foundation ever got built. President Wilson wasn't very interested and WWI and an unlucky lightning strike on his home caused Walker a lot of bad luck.
