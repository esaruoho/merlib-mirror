Professional 

I've been crazy about computing since I played the old STARTREK
game on a CDC 6600 in high school and I've been working 
in the computing industry in one way or another
since my freshman year in college. I've done operations,
development, support, consulting, writing, and
training (everything except sales and marketing!).

I started my career in computing at the
 UT Computation Center 
(where I worked during much of the time I was in school).
I learned more about
Unix, the Internet before it was ``in,'' and computing there than in
most of my classes.

I've worked at Cyb Systems (which used to make 68000-based Unix
machines),
 Microelectronics and Computer Technology Corporation (MCC), 
and
 HaL Computer Systems. 
I've also done consulting for several companies both in
Aus tin
and California.

While doing my consulting work, I also contributed several
 articles 
to technical journals and completed a book entitled
 The Keys To Successful Unix System Management 
which was published by
 Prentice-Hall 
in 1994. A German edition was published 1997.

I have also recently completed a new edition of another UNIX book
by another author, Graham Glass.
 UNIX For Programmers And Users is also available from Prentice-Hall. 

My professional interests include systems programming and administration
as well as development and refinement of networking and security tools and
procedures.
