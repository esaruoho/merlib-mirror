A good friend gave me a certificate for an introductory pilot lesson at The Pilot Center based at the Fort Collins Downtown Airport. My flight was originally scheduled for September 11, 2001 (no joke). Obviously I had to reschedule it and I finally did it on October 8.

We took off and circled Fort Collins a bit and then headed for the foothills. I asked the pilot/instructor, Tom Aex, if it was reasonable to buzz where I live and he said sure so we headed that way. He said "take it" and after a bit of nervousness I was flying towards Palisade Mountain (but this shot of Palisade is as we were returning to town so I could take the photo, that's the Big Thompson Canyon with Palisade in the foreground).

As we flew over the couple of foothill ridges leading to where I live, the whole area came into view (just where I thought it would be, incidentally, which I wasn't sure would be true, the map I had in my head was based on my knowledge of the area from the ground, of course). Seeing the true shape of Palisade Mountain from the air is really different than the idea you get from the ground or even from hiking it! My house is at the end of the clearing on the right side of the frame.

We circled around and headed back towards town in the Big Thompson Canyon and flew right by Palisade on the south side where it's a pretty sheer drop into the canyon. I got a very different view of the summit than I get standing on it!

What a great day! I'm afraid I may have the flying bug now. But it's an expensive hobby and I just spent all my credit on a new deck and some important house maintenance, so if I succumb to this bug, it won't be anytime soon. :-(

But it was still a thrill to get to try it and not something I'll soon forget.

Wednesday, October 22, 2003
