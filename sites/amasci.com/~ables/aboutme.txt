About me 

I'm kind of a homebody. Don't get me wrong, I like to get out.
But I find as I get older, I also like spending time with family
or friends just hanging around, too. There's an old saying, "the
more people I meet, the more I like my dog." I paraphrase that
and believe that the more people I meet, the more I appreciate
the truly good people that I call my friends. Really good friends
aren't very common and I have more than my share.

I'm proud of
 where I come from and how I got where I am. 
I like 
 what I do for a living 
and that it allows me to live
 where I live, 
and to do things that I enjoy with my spare time (like
 skiing, 
 hiking and biking, 
and
 volunteering).
