 January 8 & 10, 2003 - Eldora Mountain 

Went with a visiting friend from Austin to the closest place
to my house (90 minutes via Estes Park). I've been told
 Eldora 
isn't that great, and I had only been once. Sure, it's a
bit small compared to other places in Colorado, but the
snow was fine, it's easier to get to, and it has some really
fun runs. I tried some snow
blades the second day and had a blast. I gotta get me some of those!

 November 28, 2002 - Loveland Basin 

Surprisingly, we've had some snow in the past few weeks, after
a summer of drought conditions, and holiday skiing was actually
an option by Thanksgiving! With early-season discounts, and low
crowds since most people were off eating turkey, it seemed too
good to pass up. And I'm glad we didn't, the weather was beautiful,
with hardly any wind (which is very unusual at Loveland).
