1996/1997 Ski Log 
 1996/1997 Ski Log 

 January 23-24, 1997 - Breckenridge 
 February 9-12, 1997 - Whistler, British Columbia 
 March 1, 1997 - Copper Mountain 
 March 8, 1996 - Winter Park 
 March 21-22, 1997 - Keystone 

It took me a while to get my skiing going this year, buying
 a new house 
kept me a little busy during December and January.

 January 23-24, 1997 - Breckenridge 

Some friends from Austin and England came to see the new place and
we took a couple of days to finally go skiing at one of
my favorite places,
 Breckenridge. 
 -- back to index -- 

 February 9-12, 1997 - Whistler, British Columbia 

Finally made it to
 Whistler 
and
 Blackcomb 
north of Vancouver. I've been wanting to go there for years
and it was everything I had heard. Friendly people, reasonable
prices, and great snow. A few days there made me forget
everything else. Go soon though, it's growing fast and probably
won't be nearly as nice in a few more years.
 -- back to index -- 

 March 1, 1997 - Copper Mountain 

Another lousy day in the paradise that is
 Copper Mountain. 
Probably my best day of
skiing ever (as far as skill, or maybe it was luck).
 -- back to index -- 

 March 8, 1996 - Winter Park 

Return to
 Winter Park. Sometime 
I want to take the train up here, coming down from the mountain
at the end of the day you can ski right past it loading up with
people... sure seems like a nice way to get back to Denver.
 -- back to index -- 

 March 21-22, 1997 - Keystone 

Took a day off to spend more than one day skiing. Night
skiing at Keystone is just the best. We even happened
upon fireworks down in town while coming down the mountain.
Seeing fireworks from above is an interesting experience.
Unfortunately, it was too cloudy to see Hale-Bopp.
