December 21, 1997 - Monarch

    We went and stayed a few days at the Mount Princeton lodge in southwestern Colorado. Monarch Resort is only a short drive away and had gotten 19 inches of snow about two weeks earlier, but it was still in good shape.

December 23, 1997 - Breckenridge

    The last day of our trip was spent swinging by Breckenridge on our way back to Denver. They hadn't had snow in even longer than Monarch but between grooming and snow making it was in quite passable shape (how much can you complain about paradise!). The sky was crystal clear blue all day. They said it was 11 degrees but it felt more like 20!

    -- back to index --

February 14, 1998 - Ski Cooper

    A Valentine's Day trip to a little place called Ski Cooper which is just north of Leadville. This is one of the first places went skiing when I was first moving to Colorado and I hadn't been back and it was high time. It's a great place, it's small but nice wide runs, well-behaved snowboarders, and cheap lift tickes! Plus, with President's Day weekend traditionally being one of the highest traffic weekends, we thought a more out of the way place might fit the bill. It was fantastic.

    -- back to index --

March 14, 1998 - Keystone

    There just isn't much new to say about Keystone except that it's a great place. They have built a bunch of new condos, which isn't great, but the architecture is interesting, lots of "old mining town" style structures.

    -- back to index --

April 4, 1997 - Winter Park

    Another great trip to Winter Park. This time we took the train from Union Station in Denver. What a way to go skiing! If you can get to the train station at 6:30 in the morning, the rest is easy! After a hard day of skiing, kick back and have a beer or two on the trip home. If this turns out to be the end of the season, it's a great way to end it!
