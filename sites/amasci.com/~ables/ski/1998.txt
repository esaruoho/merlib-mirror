 December 19-21, 1998 - Keystone 

We took a couple of "ski virgin" friends from Texas for their
first trip. Unfortunately, the snow had been so bad up to this
point that only 25% of Keystone was very good. Of course, they
didn't care since they stayed on the bunny slope. I wound up
taking a snowboard lesson the last day and kept them company there.
Fortunately a lot more snow has fallen since and the season is in
much better shape now. I still think they had fun.
 -- back to index -- 

 January 12, 1999 - Keystone 

What can I tell you, if I'm in a rut, it's a good one. Dee and
I played hookie and went up without the usual weekend crowds.
The snow was way better than it was right before Christmas.
One of the Keystone employees said they'd had 3 feet since then!
It was still strange seeing the tops of small trees sticking out
of the snow one some of the still-closed runs, but that was a
lot better than dirt and rocks! The runs that were open were
much more skiable.
 -- back to index -- 

 January 23, 1999 - Keystone 

Ok, this really does qualify as a rut. But we were meeting
Dee's sister and brother-in-law and they wanted to go to
Keystone. Good thing it's my favorite place! They got a
lot more snow this past week, too! I had a chance to try out
my powder moves (they aren't very good!). Terrible 5-car
pileup on I-70 this morning, though, it took 3 hours to
get there (more than twice as long as normal!).
 -- back to index -- 

 January 30 - February 4, 1999 - Whistler, British Columbia 

A return trip to Whistler in Canada (I don't know, why do I
live in Colorado and fly somewhere to ski!? Because it's a great
place). The only trouble is it snowed the entire time (well,
except for about 2 hours one afternoon!). It sounds silly to
say there was too much snow, but there was too much snow.
 -- back to index -- 

 February 18-20, 1999 - Keystone 

My sister and her boyfriend came to visit and to ski 
(she never has). Turns out she's pretty good, she picked it
up fast! By the end of the 2nd day, I think she was as good
as I was in my first week! The snow was awesome (we had to
drive to dinner the night we arrived, Wednesday, in white-out
conditions, glad I knew the road the condo was on!). Two nights
of night-skiing after late nights and mornings of heavy snow
was beautiful!
 -- back to index -- 

 March 7, 1999 - Keystone 

I'm just going to start writing DITTO here. ;-)
 -- back to index -- 

 March 20, 1999 - Copper Mountain 
Hey, hey, out of my rut! Copper never has been my favorite place,
but I think it's because I just haven't been there regularly enough.
In a season of not nearly enough snow, the conditions were great
and the crowds were quite reasonable (especially for a Spring Break
weekend). I think next year I'm going to Copper more often.
 -- back to index -- 

 March 21, 1999 - Ski Cooper 
The best-kept secret in skiing is Ski Cooper. They don't make snow
and they' aren't on the way to anywhere, but it's a nice, small,
mountain with cheap lift tickets. People who go skiing to "be seen"
don't go there, so it's really a nice place. The first day of "spring
skiing" was fantastic (yes, Spring started yesterday, but not until
7pm local time and we were already off the mountain then).
 -- back to index --
