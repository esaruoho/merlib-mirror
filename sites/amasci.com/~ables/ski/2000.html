November 23, 2000 - Vail

    All the years I've lived here and I had never actually skied Vail before! Yes, it's a bit further to get there, but it's worth it. And even though it's early in the season, the conditions were actually quite good! The only problem with the day was I broke the binding on one of my skis! It still isn't clear if the binding broke and then I took a huge spill or the spill broke the binding, but in either case, I'm fine but without skis. I've been looking for an excuse to buy a new pair, this may be it, but I guess I'll check into repairs first. But even if it cost me my first pair of skis, it was worth it, what a great way to spend Thanksgivng Day!

    Tried a snowboard again and hurt myself again. Something tells me I'm not cut out for this. It's just that when you fall down you fall down on important stuff (all my wipeouts on skis haven't been as bad as my 2 snowboard wrecks, and they were just falling over!). But I tried some snowblades and I think I really like them!

January 27, 2001 - Snowy Range, WY

    Took a quick trip to Wyoming to see what was going on. They had not had much snow recently but conditions were surprisingly good. Tried out some SnowBlades for the 2nd time and I like them but I'm not ready to give up my skis just yet!

    There's a great billboard in Laramie, it's got a silhouette of two cowboys riding into the sunset, much like the old Marlboro ads, and the caption says "I sure miss my right lung, Bob." It takes a minute to sink in and it goes from funny to scary, but it's very effective.

March 24, 2001 - Copper Mountain

    Met a friend from Austin up at Copper for a day. The weather was fantastic, mid-40s and sunshine with short periods of snow when some clouds moved through. This was very different than in Denver and Fort Collins where a bank of fog had moved in from the north and east and shrouded the area in cold and fog. Denver's high was only 37. The weather said you had to get up above 8500 feet to escape it. What a great escape!
