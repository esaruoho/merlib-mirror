February 11, 2000 - Copper Mountain

    It almost makes no sense to reference 1999 in this log since I actually waited until February to begin skiing this year! Some of that was the weather wasn't favorable until January, plus life has been a little weird this year.

    -- back to index --

February 12, 2000 - Ski Cooper

    I just love this place, it's small, a bit out of the way, and almost nobody goes there, even when the snow is great!

    -- back to index --

February 21-24, 2000 - Breckenridge & Keystone

    Got a chance to take my sister and her beau skiing again this year. We stayed at Breckenridge and skied there during the day and took a couple of nights to go night skiing at Keystone (that is, when we weren't drinking beer and eating fish and chips at the Breckenridge Brewery!).

    -- back to index --

February 26, 2000 - Keystone

    BEAUTIFUL snow, too bad it was a weekend and crowded, but even so, a great day.

    -- back to index --

March 10, 2000 - Winter Park

    The HP Ski Club took two buses to Winter Park, where I hadn't been in a while now. Fantastic snow, must have snowed 7" the night before! Skiing with people you work with is... interesting. Really soft powder, unusually easy to turn in!

    -- back to index --

March 11, 2000 - Loveland

    Always the glutton for punishment, we made another trek the next day but only to Loveland (before the tunnel). Nice day, pretty good snow, LOTS of powder on top of the continental divide! Sore knees, though, turning in more normal powder (than yesterday) is hard!
