My actual home 

Yes, it's a dirty job, but somebody has to live here.
I've finally landed in a place I've always dreamed of:

I live in a log home in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains.
At the top of the hill behind the house, the
Roosevelt National Forest begins and drops about a thousand
feet into the Big Thompson River Canyon. It's very peaceful.
Quite a few people live in the area, but my nearest neighbor
is about a quarter of a mile away.
