 Abstract: An apparatus and method provide privacy for the consumer identity and
protects information concerning advertisements accessed by the consumer
while still providing marketing and demographic statistics to the
merchant regarding those advertisement accesses. A consumer user
interface provides the consumer easy control over what advertisements the
consumer is receiving and allows the consumer to easily subscribe and
unsubscribe from advertisements from either particular merchants or
categories of products and services. A commerce server receives the
request for services from the consumer user interface program and
provides the requested advertisements from specific merchants or from
selected categories.
