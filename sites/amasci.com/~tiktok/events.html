Wizard of Oz Events Calendar

Wizard of Oz events are held all around the world, all throughout the year. There is sure to be one held near you at some time. So go meet your fellow Oz fans, or see an Oz stage performance! Events are listed in chronological order. For more information on these events, please use the contact addresses given. The maintainer of this web site presents these events for informational purposes only, and is not responsible for any problems that may arise.

To publicize your local Oz events, use this e-mail link -- the sooner, the better! Please include as much information as possible, including dates and times, location, and a contact address, phone number, and/or e-mail address.

Now playing: Wicked, Gershwin Theater, Broadway, New York City, New York. The award-winning musical based on Gregory Maguire's novel Wicked: The Life and Times of the Wicked Witch of the West. The book is by Winnie Holzman, the music by Stephen Schwartz, and the show is directed by Joe Mantello. Tickets may be purchased online at http://www.ticketmaster.com/venue/24579. For more details, see the show's web page at http://wickedthemusical.com/ or http://www.musicalschwartz.com/wicked.htm.

Now playing: Wicked, Ford Theatre for the Performing Arts, Chicago, Ilinois. The Award-winning musical about what happened in Oz before Dorothy dropped in comes to the Windy City for an open-ended run. For details and tickets, see the website at http://www.wickedthemusical.com/chicago/ or http://www.broadwayinchicago.com. Background information is available at http://www.musicalschwartz.com/wicked-chicago.htm.

December 21, 2005-February 22, 2006: Yellow Brick Road, national telecast, Cinemax, HBO Family Channel, and Max On Demand. This new documentary, directed by Matthew Makar, looks at a group of developmentally challenged actors as they put on a production of The Wizard of Oz. The complete schedule is available via this link. Click on "HBO Documentaries Clips" at the top for a preview.

February 18-20, 2006: The Wizard of Oz, Hollywood Bar and Filmworks, Indianapolis, Indiana. This movie theater that also serves food and drinks will celebrate the classic 1939 film version of The Wizard of Oz on these days, with a showing of the film. Some of the Munchkin actors are slated to be special guests, and they will be available for photos and autographs through the week. For more details, see the Hollywood Boulevard website at http://www.filmworksonline.com/.

April 13-16, 2006: L. Frank Baum Sesquicentennial Celebration, Inver Hills Community College, Inver Grove Heights, Minnesota. The first of what will probably be many celebrations of L. Frank Baum's 150th birthday this year. More details will be coming soon.

April 15-May 28, 2006: The Wizard of Oz, Bridgeport, Connecticut. This is an all new dramatic presentation of the story, presented by the Downtown Cabaret Children's Company. For more details, see http://www.downtowncabaret.org.

May 19-21, 2006: Oz-Mania, Horticultural Building, New York State Fairgrounds, Syracuse, New York. This event honors L. Frank Baum's 150th birthday, and is being held in the same area as he was born. For more details, see the event flyer online at http://www.landofozpres.com/ozmania.html. (WARNING: You may want to turn the volume down on your computer, as this page has a soundtrack and no means of turning it off.)

June 1-4, 2006: Oz Fest, Chittenango, New York. L. Frank Baum's home town honors their favorite son with this annual festival. More information is coming (the schedule is up at http://www.baumozmuseum.com/2006_Schedule.htm).

June 6-7, 2006: Oz Festival, Aberdeen, South Dakota. L. Frank Baum lived in Aberdeen in the 1890s, and the area may have influenced his writing. So the folks in Aberdeen celebrate their connection to Oz with this annual event. For more details, visit the festival website at http://www.ozfestdakota.org/.

June 16-18, 2006: The International Wizard of Oz Club's Ozmopolitan Convention, Naperville, Illinois. The Oz Club's annual convention in the Midwest. For more information, see the International Wizard of Oz Club's convention web page.

July 4, 2006: Fourth of July Celebration, Burien, Washington. Among the attractions in this city's annual Independence Day parade will be a Wizard of Oz-themed float, featuring life-size characters, children, Toto, and "Over the Rainbow."

July 7-23, 2006: The Wizard of Oz, New Century Opera Company, Tarpon Springs, Florida. The New Century Opera Company puts on the 1903 stage version of The Wizard of Oz. Performances are scheduled for evenings on Fridays and Saturdays, and matinees on Sundays. For more information, contact the company director through this e-mail link.

July 14-16, 2006: The International Wizard of Oz Club's Winkie Convention, Asilomar Conference Center, Pacific Grove, California. Highlights include the auction and sales tables, the costume contest, and the annual Winkie Award. For more information on the convention, see the International Wizard of Oz Club's Convention page.

July 25-October 22, 2006: The Wonderful Art of Oz exhibit, The Eric Carle Museum of Picture Book Art, Amherst, Massechusetts. The museum will be hosting an exhibit of original Oz art at this time, curated by Michael Patrick Hearn. For details, keep an eye on the museum's website at http://www.picturebookart.org/.

August 11-13, 2006: The International Wizard of Oz Club's Munhkin Convention, Hyatt Regency Hotel, Princeton, New Jersey. For more information on the convention, see the International Wizard of Oz Club's Convention page.

Opening September 27, 2006: Wicked, The West End, London, England, United Kingdom. It's coming to Europe at last! More information when it becomes avaiable, or keep a watch on http://www.musicalschwartz.com/wicked-uk.htm.

2007: The Dark Side of Oz, Australia. This new musical play, a combination of Ozma of Oz, Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz, and The Emerald City of Oz, debuts in Australia sometime in 2007. For more details, see the website at http://www.freewebs.com/radicalwizardry/.
