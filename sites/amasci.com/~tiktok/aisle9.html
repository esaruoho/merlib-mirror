The Oz Electronic Paperback CD-ROM Collection. An entire Oz book by L. Frank Baum on one CD-ROM, complete with narration read by professional actors. Each CD-ROM works on the PC platform (Macintosh computers will need a PC emulator program).
  • The Wonderful Wizard of Oz.
  • The Marvelous Land of Oz.
  • Ozma of Oz.
  • Dorothy and the Wizard in Oz.
  • The Road to Oz.
  • The Emerald City of Oz.
  • The Patchwork Girl of Oz.
  • Tik-Tok of Oz.
  • The Scarecrow of Oz.
  • Rinkitink in Oz.
  • The Lost Princess of Oz.
  • The Tin Woodman of Oz.
  • The Magic of Oz.
  • Glinda of Oz.
  • All of the above titles in a collected set.
  • All of the above titles in a collected set, without the read aloud portion.

Oz: The Magical Adventure. Help the Scarecrow, Tin Woodman, and Cowardly Lion find Dorothy and save her from the Wicked Witch of the West in this computer edutainment game.
  • Mac/Windows CD-ROM.
  • Windows CD-ROM

Oz: The Magical Adventure Interactive Storybook. An interactive version of the book The Wizard of Oz to read or play with on your computer.
  • Mac/Windows CD-ROM, in the set "First Friendships Fun Pack".
  • Windows CD-ROM.

Oz: The Magical Adventure Double Pack. Both of the above Oz-themed computer games, together in one set.
  • Windows CD-ROMs.

Rugrats: Munchin Land. Tommy, Chuckie, Angelica, and the whole gang from Rugrats are off to a faraway land in their latest video game.
  • CD-ROM for Windows.

Songs from The Wizard of Oz Play-a-Song Book. All of the lyrics from the famous film version of the story, plus an electronic keypad that plays the music so you can sing along.
  • Hardcover book. Battery included.

The Wizard of Oz for the QuantumPad. A version of the story that helps build vocabulary and reading skills.
  • QuantumPad book.
  • QuantumPad electronic device.

The Wizard of Oz video game. For the Super Nintendo system, based on The Movie.
  • Game cartridge.

The Woggle-Bug Book by L. Frank Baum. Baum's rare book, a spin-off of The Marvelous Land of Oz, is now available from Golden Age Publishing on a CD-ROM.
  • CD-ROM from Golden Age Publishing.
