 #tinnitus chat mailing list 

The #tinnitus chat mailing list is a small, low-activity discussion group for people
who participate in tinnitus chats on IRC. It exists mainly to provide
information about chat schedule changes or other IRC issues, and
to supplement the IRC chats in terms of support, information & conversation.
It's likely to be less T-oriented than alt.support.tinnitus simply because
most general T discussion is probably better directed to the newsgroup.
This list lets us deal with IRC without boring newsgroup readers, and also
enjoy the group that's formed without having to "stay on topic" 100% of the
time.

To 
 subscribe ,
send email to tchat-list-request@eskimo.com 
with the word subscribe in the Subject: of the message.
You will receive confirmation email which also tells you how to
send mail to the list, or how to
 unsubscribe 
if desired.

Note: a "rotary" is a server name which will connect you to one of a group of
servers in a round-robin fashion. They're used to distribute the network load
more evenly and to make it easier for people to find servers.
