 Tinnitus Chats on Yahoo 

Phil came up with the idea of meeting on Yahoo for tinnitus chats, which started in January 1999. Thanks Phil!
Many of the participants came from the Tinnitus WWW Messages Board 

These chats are held Wednesday nights at 10pm Eastern time on the 
 Yahoo chat website ,
as a User Room named either Tinnitus or Tinnitus Sufferers,
under the Health and Wellness category.

See the instructions for joining the Yahoo chat.
You need a web browser which supports Java.

 Instructions for Connecting to Yahoo chats 

 Point your browser to the Yahoo chat web site:
 http://chat.yahoo.com . 

First-time users: create a Yahoo chat "identity" by selecting "create/edit identities" and following the instructions.

Once you have a chat "identity", click "Start Chatting", then "Change Room",
then "Health and Wellness", then "User Rooms". Either "Tinnitus" or
"Tinnitus Sufferers" will be listed; click on the room name to enter the chat.

AOL users, if you're having trouble see Yahoo's AOL help.
