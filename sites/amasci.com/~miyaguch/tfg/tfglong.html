     1    This test comprises three subtests: Association (39 items),
Analogies (39) and Space, Time and Hyperspace (28).
     2    There is no time limit.
     3    The use of reference books is allowed.  Discusions with others
are not allowed, either before, during or after taking the test.
Publication of answers will result in prosecution.
     4    Write, type, print or draw your solutions on paper, with your
name, address, age and sex.  If you have any, mention scores on previously
taken IQ and aptitude tests, including the names of the tests.  Mail to
above address.
     5    Score report in two weeks.
     6    Enclose US $12 cash with your solutions or use an International
Postal Order for 25 Dutch guilders.
     7    A few item numbers are missing as those items have been removed
in the latest revision while the numbering is retained.
     8    The nonverbal items are weighted by the factor 3, so maximum
score is 162.
     9    For US $2 one can order a French, German or Dutch version of
this test at the above address.
