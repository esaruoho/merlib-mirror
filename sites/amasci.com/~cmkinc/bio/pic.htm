The Face Behind The Mask (or is THAT the mask?)

Well, maybe jumped isn't exactly the best way to describe it. I do look a bit different than I did in my old picture, though, and I thought some new material might be nice for a change.

I don't work at Gameworks by the way, I just used to go there alot and liked the shirt.
