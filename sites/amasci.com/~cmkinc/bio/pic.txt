 The Face
Behind The Mask (or is THAT the mask?) 

 It's been 2 years
since I've done anything with this page, so when the opportunity arose to have a new
digital snapshot taken I jumped at the chance. Well, maybe jumped isn't exactly
the best way to describe it. I do look a bit different than I did in my old picture,
though, and I thought some new material might be nice for a change. 

 The camera that
captured this image was really designed for real-time webcam applications, not fine
portrait photography. It came out rather grainy and none of my admittedly limited
graphical skills could make a nice, sharp image from it. I was hoping that the space
background might add an air of mystery or something. 

 I don't work at
Gameworks by the way, I just used to go there alot and liked the shirt. 

 Here I am in younger times (all of 3 years ago!). My smile was broader and hair
 was thicker then. Yeah, those were the days! Click on the image if you'd like to see a
 larger version.
