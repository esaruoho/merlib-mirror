PocketChess v1.0
Released: 3/10/99
Size: 40k

PocketChess is a high quality, well featured Chess playing program for your daVinci.

  • Play against the computer or a friend. 
  • Choose from 8 levels of skill.
  • Easy intuitive user interface, play black or white, hint, undo, and more!

PocketChess v1.0 is for pre-lawsuit daVincis (pre-November 17th 1999 daVincis)!! Given that these older daVincis have been discountinued, PocketChess v1.0 for Royal daVinci is being re-released as freeware.
