 PocketChess v1.1 for PocketPC is a high quality, well featured Chess
 playing program for your PocketPC! Also compatible with Windows CE
 Palm-sized PC's. Also available for Windows CE Handheld
 PC here . 
 Play against the computer or a friend. 
 Choose from 8 levels of skill. 
 Export to PGN 
 Many other features including a simple and intuitive user interface, save &
 load games, opening move libary, board setup, full move list, list of captured pieces,
 copy moves to clipboard, undo, hint, reverse board, switch sides, start as black, sound
 effects, full help, and others!
