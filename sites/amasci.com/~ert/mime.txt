How to write to Ert Dredge so he's happy 

 Ert and Email 

 I encourage people to send me PGP-signed and PGP-encryped mail. I encourage people to send me
 X-Face in their headers.

 I encourage people I've had previous Email contact with to send
all sorts of BinHex'ed or MIME enclosures in mail sent to me,
especially audio letters, and I like to know when I can do the same.
I prefer to keep my typing to a minimum, so being able to send long
voice attachments is a plus (RealAudio is best, but anything
conventional and compact is probably OK.)

 If you've never coresponded with me before, please don't start by
sending large files. Introduce yourself first.

 I am sporadic at best with my personal mailing. If you insist that
your letters be answered promptly and you won't write twice in a row
without getting uppity, you'll quickly find corresponding with me
intolerable. That said, I like people who write me whenever they feel
like it, remind me when I haven't written in a while, and are tolerant
of long delays. I'll extend similar courtesies.

 I welcome genuine enquiries on any topic that anyone wants my
opinion on. I don't mind FYI mail that you honestly believe I 
personally will be interested in after having learned a little about
me. I do not want any unsolicied commercial email.

 I use different E-mail addresses for different purposes, the same
way people have a home phone number and a business phone number. Please
try to send mail to the appropriate address...Thanks! -->
