 Kids in the Hall 
 May 1992 - September 1995 

These pages describe KitH's formative years with Sanjay and Ert. The
show was revived in 1996 with Marc and Sanjay, but then stopped
production again.

Back when the world was young and happy there began a wonderful Canadian
Indie/Pop music show called Kids in the Hall on WMBR. Then the Shadows
came and KitH, both the TV and radio
shows, vanished.

 KitH was originally hosted by Ert , who moved to Houston, and
 Sanjay ,
who was crushed by a graduate programme.
