Radio and other junk

From 1988-1997 I was involved with WMBR-FM, MIT's radio station, as a DJ and in management positions. Most recently I initiated the construction of their website and began preparations for regular Internet distribution of WMBR audio.

My move to Houston in 1995 hampered my ability to contribute quite so actively towards WMBR, but it served as an interesting experiment in telecommuting. For 1996-1997 my main radio project was putting Jerry Brown's "We the People" show on the 'net. Please visit the project page.

Over the years I have hosted three shows at WMBR: RPM, a late-80s Progressive/Alternative/Europop Friday night dance show, The Bernie and Ert show, a Monday afternoon experiment in whatever I wanted at the time, and for the last three years I was in Boston, Kids in the Hall, a weeknight exploration of Canadian Music, co-hosted with Sanjay.

Summer '96 I did a morning-drive "standard college radio" show on Rice University's KTRU.

Nowadays I do various things on KPFT, the local Pacifica station (sometimes music, sometimes traffic reports...), and also I'm pretty interested in the A-Infos Radio Project.
