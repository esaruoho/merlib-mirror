Well, as you might have guessed from my involvement with a
 Canadian music radio show , I l
isten to a lot of
Canadian bands, mainly indie and pop.
 Here is the
 list Sanjay and I
made last year of our favourite albums of the first half of the 90s.

Before that for two years I had a progressive/pop dance show. I played
a lot of stuff like Depeche Mode , Pet Shop Boys , New Order ,
 The Cult , Kon Kan , U2 , Visage,
 Front 242 , Nine Inch Nails , OMD , Erasure , etc.

I was program director at
 WMBR 
for a year, and during that time I got into a lot of different stuff. I
like jazz and classical, although I can't claim to be very well versed
in those styles. I enjoy ballroom dancing, and the music that goes
along with it. I also like a lot of types of techno (very fast
through to ambient). Folk music can be nice, and rap and hip-hop when
they're done well.

 Talking Heads . Dead Can Dance . Sting . Kitaro .

 The Lowest of
the Low . Toto .
Velour. The Mountain Goats. The Orb. Ani Difranco. Soul Coughing.
Ride. Combustible Edison. Ali Akbar Khan. The Disposable Heroes of
Hiphoprisy. And a lot more...

I like 1950s-1970s rock music, but since I wasn't very
aware of it (or not
alive) at the time I fear I'm only familiar with the stuff that is fed
to me by the commercial stations that select songs based on baby-boomer
marketing potential. I don't spend enough time researching the period
to be very aware of the lesser-known artists.

I've been getting increasingly fed up with the North American popular
music industry, although I must confess a fondness for 1980s pop that I
listened to at the time and now forms a scaffolding for my memories of
the period.
