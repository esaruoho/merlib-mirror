Hermaphroditism in Mus poschiavinus

Briefly, here was the project:

Evidence for the existence of multiple autosomal genes affecting gonadal development in the mouse is seen when a Y chromosome from the Mus poschiavinus inbred strain is placed on the autosomal background of the C57Black6J inbred strain, causing hermaphroditism in the XY animals. We were mapping the autosomal genes involved in this developmental phenomenon.
