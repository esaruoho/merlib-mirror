 Our experiences with samoyeds
 began two and a half years ago when our daughter got married and moved to
 England and asked us to take care of Tasha. That's when we fell in
 love with the breed. In September 2000 we added a rescue dog to our
 lives. Charlie was a year old, and he has been a wonderful playmate
 for Tasha. Our daughter returned from England in late April this year,
 bringing Dashik with her (and her husband Tim, of course). Dashik and
 Charlie were instant pals, and the three dogs play constantly
 together. We found Grumpy Alex at the local shelter about six years
 ago. He's loyal, affectionate, and friendly to everything except big
 white dogs. Annie the Cat (not pictured) is 17 years old and in charge of
 teaching dogs not to chase cats.
