Ryan and Breeze
 
Ryan had her hands full this year, with Laura Segers' Breeze. Breeze bounced and hopped around the ring as if she had never been on a leash in her life. Ryan still managed to take Best Jr. Handler at the Samoyed Fanciers of Central Florida Northern Breed Match.  Ryan also handled Diana Steele's Khyber in Open for Best of Breed.
