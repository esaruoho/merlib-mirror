Shadow

When we first heard of Shadow in the shelter, we went to meet him. This was Friday, and he had been there since the previous afternoon.

He was dirty, stinky, and had little mats all over him. They said he was picked up as a stray, wandering around Lakewood. We spent time with him in a small meeting room, and realized he was blind.

 
Since they were waiting for his owners to show up and claim him, we couldn't get him until Monday. In the meantime, a shelter volunteer had combed out the loose coat -- but left the mats and the dirt. As Kathy started grooming, his beauty began to show. Here he is after just a few hours of combing. The only mats that had to be cut were behind his ears; they were huge. He loved the attention, and even fell asleep during brushing.
The longer he was here, the happier he became. Shadow is a brave dog who has been blind since birth.

We took him to a veterinary ophthalmologist who told us both retinas are detached and he can only see a bit of light and some movement. There is nothing that can be done to correct this genetic defect. As you can see, he definitely knows where the horse is.
 
Kathy walks the dogs to get the paper every morning, and with a friend's help all the dogs got to go. Left to right, Shadow, Charlie, Alex, Dashik, and Tasha.

 		 We took Shadow to the annual Sammy Olympics, where he was the hit of everyone there. They took turns holding him while we took our dogs to the different events.
 
The owner of the facility agreed to give Shadow some free time in the herding ring, and he shows here that herding is definitely in his blood.
 		 Using his ears and his limited sense of motion, he was able to keep the sheep where they should be.

 

(above) You could tell the Shadow was having the time of his life.

 

(right) His mellow nature was on display all day. He loved the attention of the people, especially the kids who couldn't quit petting him.
Our son Jeff came to visit, and found out Shadow was happy to run around playing with everyone. Tasha on left, Shadow on right.
 

Always happy. After having Shadow here for a while, we believe he is no more than a year old, and may even be younger.
 
He may be blind, but he always knows where the food can be found. Counterclockwise from top: Tasha, Shadow, Charlie, Dashik and Alex (hiding in a sea of white).
 Shadow loves rawhide chews. Here he is in a bit of sunlight, enjoying life.
