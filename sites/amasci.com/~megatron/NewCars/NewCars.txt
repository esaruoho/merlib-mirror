New Cars 

 Hot on the heels of the upcoming M-edition Z3, we have our first look at
Mercedes' rebuttal. Not to be caught resting on their laurels, Mercedes is again having their buddy
AMG build a monster to effectively compete against BMW. It's certain to have a V6, but the displacement
is still unknown. Rumor has it that it will be a 3.2 liter producing near 300bhp!

the most recent line of Civics. Maybe it is a Civic? Hopefully, rumors from the other magazines of the Accord SI-R being
available in the U.S. for the Accord's 30th anniversary hold true!
