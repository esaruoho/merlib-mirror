JONATHAN CREEK EPISODE GUIDE > Page 1, 2

Jonathan Creek is a comedy/mystery series starring Alan Davies as Jonathan Creek, and Caroline Quentin as Maddy Magellan. The series was created (and all episodes written) by David Renwick (One Foot In The Grave).

First Season

1. "The Wrestler's Tomb" (originally aired on BBC-1 May 10, 1997). Guest stars: Anthony Stewart Head (Buffy, The Vampire Slayer), Colin Baker (Doctor Who). 90 minute pilot episode.

2. "Jack In The Box" (May 17, 1997).

3. "The Reconstituted Corpse" (May 24, 1997). Guest star: Nigel Planer (The Young Ones).

4. "No Trace Of Tracy" (May 31, 1997).

5. "The House of Monkeys" (June 7, 1997).

(Episodes 2-5 were cut for showing on PBS. Read why.)

Second Season

6. "Danse Macabre" (January 24, 1998). Guest stars: Peter Davison (Doctor Who), Pippa Haywood (The Brittas Empire).

7. "Time Waits for Norman" (January 31, 1998).

8. "The Scented Room" (February 7, 1998).

9. "The Problem at Gallows Gate" Part 1 (February 14, 1998).

10. "The Problem at Gallows Gate" Part 2 (February 21, 1998).

11. "Mother Redcap" (February 28, 1998). Guest star: Nicola Walker (Touching Evil).

Christmas Special

12. "Black Canary" (December 24, 1998). Guest star: Rik Mayall (The Young Ones). 90 minutes.

Third Season

13. "The Curious Tale Of Mr Spearfish" (November 27, 1999). Guest star: Griff Rhys-Jones (Smith & Jones).

14. "The Eyes of Tiresias" (December 4, 1999). Guest star: Rebecca Front (The Fast Show).

15. "The Omega Man" (December 11, 1999).

16. "Ghost's Forge" (December 18, 1999).

17. "Miracle in Crooked Lane" (December 28, 1999).

18. "The Three Gamblers" (January 2, 2000). Guest stars: Jonathan Ross, John Bird, Hattie Hayridge (Red Dwarf).
