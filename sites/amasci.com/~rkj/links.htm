British Media

  • The British Comedy Library. Andrew Wong's comedy home page stuffed full of FAQs and interviews.
  • British Comedy on American TV. An extensive site with info on just about every Britcom seen in the US.
  • The BBC.
  • Channel 4. The UK's most cool channel has its own site with program listings and really neat stuff (if you're running Netscape). Check it out.
  • Bigglethwaite's UKTV QuickSheet. A massive resource of UK TV links. If it exists, it's here!
  • Anglicon Homepage. Seattle's premiere British media convention to be held in May 1999.
  • Doctor Who
    • The Doctor Who Homepage.
    • Dr Who News Group
    • Society of the Rusting TARDIS Homepage. Seattle British video club.
    • The Time Meddlers of Los Angeles
  • Father Ted Homepage. The brilliant Channel 4 comedy has its own unofficial homepage. Check it out!
  • The ThinkTed Project. Some bawdy songs based on Father Ted!
  • Lee & Herring Homepage Makers of Fist of Fun, a BBC-2 comedy series that is a favorite among Rusting TARDIS members. Worth checking out if only to get the text of subliminal messages that flash during the show.
  • Game On. A fan site devoted to the Andrew Davies comedy.
  • The Official Wallace and Gromit Site! Nick Parks' Aardman Animation has all the low-down on this popular Oscar winning clay-animated duo and their short film adventures.
  • TV Go Home A parody of Radio Times listings, updated every two weeks. Raunchy but funny!
