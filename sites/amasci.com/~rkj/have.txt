"Have I Got News For You" 

 Have I Got News For You (1999) 

 Based on the long-running BBC series (and an American version that's
coming hosted by Norm MacDonald), for the past two years at Anglicon 
I have presented a live version of the current event news quiz Have
I Got News For You . Four panelist have to guess the week's news
stories from various video clips, headlines, and an odd-man out round.
The idea is more to have fun at the expense of the newsworthy, rather than
always getting the correct answer.
 We've been lucky each year to get one of the Anglicon guests to join
us on the panel (along with regulars Jeff Stout - who runs The
Society Of The Rusting TARDIS , and Allen Adams, a "second-tier" British
media guest). In 1998, John Levine ("Sgt Benton" from Dr Who ) was
our celebrity guest, in 1999 we had Jason
Carter from Babylon 5 on the panel.
 The show was recorded using four digital video mini-DV camcorders, with
switching equipment provided by Alan Halfhill, and everything edited together
using Adobe Premiere on my computer afterwards.
