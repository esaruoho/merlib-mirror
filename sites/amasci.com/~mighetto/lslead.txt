 Leadership Training 

 Leaders are
people who are charged with improving systems , rather than getting
results from others, which is the charge of the manager. 

 Leadership training is of value to anyone who aspires to a leadership position,
whether within the family or group, church or school, business or military, politics
or government, or not-for-profit organization. It will also appeal to board
members responsible for overseeing leaders and to individuals who interact with
decision makers during the sale of goods and services.

 Training is obtained by hiring management consultants or professional
educators 
or by obtaining advanced degrees. Recently, computer based products have allowed the
uninitiated to operate at the level of a seasoned leader. The Wall Street Journal calls this kind of
product MBAware . The computer industry calls them expert systems .

 For a training exercise see: leadership styles . Then use the expedia.msn search function with the
word "leader" to identify a leader. You can also look up a
historic site at The
History Channel to learn about a leader. Classify
the leader's predominant style and review the following links.
