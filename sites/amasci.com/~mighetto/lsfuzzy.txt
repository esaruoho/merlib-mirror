 Fuzzy Logic 

 Fuzzy logic
is an approach for dealing with uncertainty that views mathematical
probability as inappropriate, replacing it with an alternate theory called possibility
theory. Fuzzy Truth concerns the likelihood or certainty that a fact or rule is true
rather than frequency.
