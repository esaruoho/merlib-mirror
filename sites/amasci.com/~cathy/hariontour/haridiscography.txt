George Harrison Solo Discography 

 George 
 Harrison Discography, 1968 - 1997 

 courtesy of Diana Poskrop 

 This discography 
 lists officially-released solo albums and singles by George and 
 by the Traveling Wilburys, along with year(s) of release. Not 
 included are promo releases, bootlegs or albums by other artists 
 which were produced by George and/or on which George performs. 
 The two Traveling Wilburys albums are no longer being issued. 
 All of George's albums are currently available on CD. According 
 to Apple, "Wonderwall" is no longer being issued.
