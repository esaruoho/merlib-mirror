Geoffrey Giuliano:
I am a very misunderstood person

Aya B. Makarova's dialogue with Geoffrey Giuliano
August 27 - 28, 1999

Geoffrey Giuliano, who has now legally changed his name to Jagannatha Dasa and runs a Vaishnava Ashram in Lockport, NewYork is also the author of over twenty books on the Beatles including the controversial DARK HORSE/The Life And Art Of George Harrison. I recently spoke with the author via e-mail about both his writing work and spiritual interests. We invite our readership to judge for themselves this controversial but always interesting man. 

GG: First, you should know that I am a full time devotee of Sri Krsna and a follower of George's guru, A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami.  Although Harrison studied with our master for several years, due to the bad association that is unavoidable in rock music he was unable to truly live the lifestyle so important to our Bhakti faith.  We advocate no meat, fish or eggs.  No cigarettes.  No alcohol or drugs. No sex other than with one's wife.  Sadly, George was unable to consistently follow any of those rules and thus, went through a lot of suffering which could have been avoided by seriously adhering to our guru's instructions. When I knew George in the early 80s he was doing cocaine and other drugs as well as cheating on his wife, Olivia.  In those days too I was no angel, but for the past several years I have been strictly following all the rules and regulations of devotional service." 

AM: "But, to my understanding, it was Swami himself who insisted on George remaining a rock musician rather than a "real" full-time devotee? At least that it what I have read." 

GG: "Although my guru told George to remain a musician he did not give him an exemption from following the rules and regulations of our Vaishnava faith.  Any and all persons thinking of themselves as devotees must abstain from eating meat, fish and eggs; intoxication; illicit sex and gambling. Therefore Mr. Harrison in embracing the tenants of Bhakti Yoga would be aware of these strictures and thus be bound by them.  His inability to strictly follow does not mean he is a bad person or insincere in his belief.  I personally sat and talked with this man at some length and therefore I have certain insights into his character which certainly he would never want to share with the public.  A journalist's job though is to tell the truth, not sugar coat it or try and present things in a kinder light. That's what PR men do.  As a devotee we also share a similar love of truth no matter how apparently brutal.  Fairy tales are for children.  I simply reported what  I knew to be true without speculation or any particular emotion whatsoever.  If by so doing George could use that as a mirror to view his own life then fine. If not, I have still done my job both as a journalist and a devotee. 

AM: "But, from my understanding, the Vaishnava etiquette is that one will not say anything bad about a fellow Vaishnava, only good  points. And, knowing George so well, you did know he's a very  private person - not only because he wants to hide his faults and  foibles... Am I wrong?" 

GG: "I am pleased to answer your most recent questions. Firstly, a loving Vaishnava has a duty to try and correct a fault within one of his God brothers so that he might continue to advance in Krishna Bhakti. Your idea that we can never be critical of our brothers is incorrect. 

"Further, devotees are not hippies but rather, harbingers of truth. We therefore regard truth in any form as the highest love." 

AM: "Another question to you: for how long are you a full-time  devotee and how do you view your Ronald McDonald past now?" 

GG: "I first became a devotee in 1970, though only part time. For the last nine years, however, I have grown increasingly serious about my devotional life. 

"As for being Ronald McDonald it now allows me access to the international media to raise issues regarding vegetarianism and nonviolence to animals.  Therefore it was a blessing." 

AM: "After talking to some disciples I met here - who said they knew you - I did not get an image of you as a strict devotee or even a person who was welcomed by Swami." 

GG: "As for some ISKCON devotees apparently not "liking me" I am not concerned. Many do. Isn't that just life? By the way I am not even a member of ISKCON but rather a Gaudiya Vaishnava Srila Prabhupada organization called SRI / The Spiritual Realization Institute. As for Praphupada and myself I can assure you we were most friendly with each other. So that statement is false. 

"I can assure you Srila Prabhupada "liked" me and all of his devotees. I don't know what else to say." 

AM: "So, what about your books?" 

GG: "In many ways my book, DARK HORSE was meant to be a wake up call for Mr. Harrison, but unfortunately, he missed the point and saw it simply as a personal  attack.  The fact is, I have great affection for George, but feel with the advantages he has had he should have made better use of the power God had given him.  Perhaps if his human frailties were brought out into the open he might begin to think more deeply about his life.  Who knows, that may have happened.  I hope that it did. 

"All of my books are honest portrayals of my subjects. I invent nothing but if it happened I, as a journalist and truth teller will include it. I have no interest in making people look good or bad, rather, I simply investigate all material for accuracy then include it when verified. That's all any reporter/author can and should do. To "clean up" peoples images is what PR people do. 

"My next book is called LENNON IN AMERICA 1971-1980. 

"I am a very misunderstood person, but I no longer care about that.  My passion these days is that people should understand that life is a spiritual journey.  That they are not their body, but rather, a soul within a body.  That we are all a qualitative spark of the Supreme Lord Sri Krsna.  And finally, since God is in everything, we should take no life in vain, especially our friends, the animals. 

"Beyond that, I don't really have much to say except I urge your readers to please chant: 

HARE KRSNA HARE KRSNA
KRSNA KRSNA HARE HARE
HARE RAMA HARE RAMA
RAMA RAMA HARE HARE

and you life will become sublime. 

"We are all potential Messiahs.  It is not an exclusive mantle only to be worn by a select few.  Therefore we are all here in this world to not only wake up ourselves, but our brothers as well.  By reading so many different spiritual books, by so many authors you have perhaps become a little confused.  Why don't you sit down and seriously study the Bhagavad-gita As It Is by Bhaktivedanta Swami?  Remember, this book, above all others, has helped shape the consciousness of your hero, George Harrison.  Even if you have read it before, read it again with a really open mind and heart and I promise you the quality of your life will greatly improve. 

"George Harrison is  big boy and an advanced devotee in many respects.  I think perhaps it might be better for you not to meditate so deeply on the particulars of this man's life, but rather, look at your own.  Life is very short and you have been given a golden opportunity to realize your own innate spirituality.  Take that opportunity and run with it. The Beatles were good, no doubt, but that's all done with now.  I have left it behind and so has George.  What about you? I bow down to you for your open heart and deep love." 

AM: "What about your life on the 'Net? Do you have a web site?" 

GG: "Please bookmark our web sites: 
http://www.neonblue.com/sri 
http://www.neonblue.com/indigo 
Would you also kindly add us to your links?" 

AM: "So, why do you do what you do?"

GG: "All things considered I'm sure it's much more interesting and important to see these artists (like the Beatles) as they really are. Surely, the opportunity to see our heroes as human beings is a valuable tool in understanding their work as great artists. The faults, weaknesses, disappointments, bad experiences as well as the greater good of these media personalities all feed into the work which they produce. In order to really understand and appreciate the art you have to first know the artist! That requires a very close look at their lives. The fact is, books I've authored such as JOHN LENNON MY BROTHER Forward By Paul McCartney; THE LOST BEATLES INTERVIEWS; THE LOST JOHN LENNON INTERVIEWS; THINGS WE'VE SAID TODAY CONVERSATIONS WITH THE BEATLES; THE ILLUSTRATED SERIES; THE BEATLES A CELEBRATION and THE BEATLES ALBUM are all virtually one hundred percent positive! Why people so unfairly judge my eighteen books on the Beatles solely on DARK HORSE and BLACKBIRD seems very strange and unreal to me. It's as if people want to see the bad in others. At any rate, I am always happy to hear from my readership no matter what their opinions and look forward to a free exchange of ideas. I wish you all well both now and in the future."
