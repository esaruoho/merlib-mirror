HOT-L Subscription Information

HariOnTour has a fair list load, currently averaging about ten messages a day - more since Hari's passing.

SUBSCRIBING TO HARIONTOUR

You can join the list via email simply by sending an email to:

majordomo@lists.spunge.org

In the *body* of the message, type:

subscribe hariontour user@domain.com

UNSUBSCRIBING FROM HARIONTOUR

Subscribers can unsubscribe from the list simply by sending an email to:

majordomo@lists.spunge.org

In the *body* of the message, type:

unsubscribe hariontour user@domain.com

or by clicking the link at the bottom of each list message.

POSTING MESSAGES TO HARIONTOUR

To post a message to the list, subscribers can send email to:

    hariontour@lists.spunge.org

DIGEST OPTION

An alternative to the regular list form is a digest, which sends out an average of one, maybe two posts a day, depending on the number of individual messages, combining several messages into one.

TO SUBSCRIBE TO THE DIGEST VERSION

Send an email to:

majordomo@lists.spunge.org

In the *body* of the message, type:

subscribe hariontour-d user@domain.com

The -d tells majordomo that you want the digest version of the list. 

TO UNSUBSCRIBE FROM THE DIGEST

Send an email to:

majordomo@lists.spunge.org

In the *body* of the message, type:

unsubscribe hariontour-d user@domain.com

or click the link at the bottom of each digest message.

If you encounter a problem, e-mail Aya Makarova or the list technical advisor and clearly explain the problem. Please use this option only as a last resort after you have followed the above instructions.

Once you are subscribed, you will receive information about the list. By subscribing to the list, you agree to READ and SAVE the instructions you are given.
