 Would you like to know more about her views on animals rights? PETA, Greenpeace

 Did you know Linda has
 three great cookbooks out called, "Linda McCartney's Home Cooking,"
 "Linda's Kitchen," and her latest, "Linda McCartney On Tour?" 

 Linda was a
 photographer before she met Paul. Learn more about her photography books,
 "Sixties," "Sunprints," "Roadworks," and where to find them.
 Also, A & E sometimes reruns a great show about Linda and her photography, called,
 "Linda McCartney: Behind the Lens." 

 Linda combined two
 passions - stained glass and photography before her death. Find out more about the process
 and her photo exhibits.
