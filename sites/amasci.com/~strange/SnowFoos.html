posted: January 8, 2004


It snowed here quite heavily overnight January 5, leaving a field of adventure for our ferrets on January 6th. While I was at work, my husband took these pics of Taz and Dex in the accumulated snow in the cockpit.




Dexter wanted to keep on playing, but Taz got cold and went inside, putting an end to fun. By 7 p.m. that evening, our typical Seattle rain started up and, by this morning, January 8, the snow was as gone as a smoke dream. Too bad. At least we got these, though....
