 Saily-boat! 

 Whoohooo! 
 OK, I'm going to gloat. Cover your ears if you don't want to hear it. 
 I got a sailboat! 
 After a little over of year of insanity and hard work (on the husband's part: I assure you it wasn't mine, since I am a lazy beast, as well as a crazy one) we tracked down and purchased a really nice used boat and moved aboard over the past week. The whole odyssey is recorded on my husband's site, along with pictures. 
 Well, not all the way aboard, but most of the way. I'm still using the old boat as an office and I seem to have stuff spread between one slip and the other like a trail of high-tech breadcrumbs. I'm scrambling about a bit to complete various and sundry bits of work related to the boats and to do the usual stuff. Which is why this is a really lame Kat Litter and why it is 4 days late. 
 I suspect I'll be back to my usual profane and obnoxious self in a few more weeks, however. 
 Just think: I have to buy a new bathing suit for the first time in 10 years. Hmmm... bikini or tank? Whaddya think?
