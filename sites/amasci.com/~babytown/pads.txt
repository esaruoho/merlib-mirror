 Bed and Change Pads 

 Our change pads come in either brushed flannel prints and cotton covered prints and fit easily into most diaper bags. You will want one as soon as you try those cute plastic pads and your baby urinates while you are changing him. Our absorbent change pads solve that problem.
Just throw them in the washer and dryer if they get wet. These pads fit nicely into a cradle or bassinet for extra padding and protection. If you think that the change pad has many uses, wait 'til you read about our multipurpose pad these kids are playing on! 

 Styles 

 Change Pads (17" x 27") 
 The perfect diapering accessory, this pad will absorb any change table accidents in a jiffy. Wash it, put it in the dryer and it is back in action in no time. Folds up small enough to go in the diaper bag. Prints available in Quilted Polyester - $15, Cotton Covered - $18 and Brushed
 Flannel - $15. 

 Multi-Purpose Pads (29" x 36")
