Washing your IndisposablesTM Cotton Diapers
  1. Prewash diapers twice before using.
  2. After Changing your baby's diaper, flush stool down the toilet and place wet or soiled diaper into a diaper pail.
  3. Soak the diapers in a pail filled with 1/2 water and 15ml of IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener, or 1/4 cup vinegar or washing soda. It is not recommended to soak diapers more than three days.
  4. To transfer diapers in the pail to a laundry machine, either hand wring diapers and use lid of pail to carry OR pour all of the diaper pail contents into the washing machine, turn on rinse cycle and spin solutions out of the diapers.
  5. Use the IndisposablesTM Liquid Laundry Concentrate. Do not us Bleach, Detergents with Bleach, Pure Soap Products (such as Ivory Snow), strong detergent products - liquid or powder (such as Tide).
  6. Care in drying your diapers will add to their life. Dry diapers on medium setting for half of the drying cycle and then hang damp diapers to dry for the balance. Softeners in the washing machine and dryer are not recommended as they reduce the absorbency of the diapers.
  7. To remove stains, rub a bar of "white" soap on the wetted down stain before placing in the diaper pail. Try hanging diapers in the sun after laundering to naturally bleach stain away. Add IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener to washing machine. For extra tough stains, mix 5ml IndisposablesTM Liquid Laundry Concentrate with 5ml IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener and apply past to stain. Launder as usual.
