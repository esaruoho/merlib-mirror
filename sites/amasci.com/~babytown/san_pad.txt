 Indisposables TM Washable Cotton Sanitary Pads 

 Our System consists of three cotton interchangeable pads: Single (light days), Double (heavier) or Night Time (maximum) which fit into an Outer Pocket with wings. (VELCRO or Snaps) Breathable cotton pads used by Indisposables TM are natural, safe and comfortable. 

 Make sure you have snug fitting cotton briefs when you try our sanitary pads. Loose fitting panties let the carrier for the pads slip around. You may not realize how uncomfortable and irritating paper pads are until you try our cotton flannel pads. The best way to start is to try the nighttime pads, because you shouldn't use a tampon at night and you will feel more comfortable with cotton next to your
skin.. Our nighttime pads are extra long in front and back so you are protected no matter which position you sleep in. Just rinse them out after use in cold water and put them in the soak pail until you are ready to wash them. Try one carrier with snaps and one with Velcro to see which type suits you. Put an end to indiscriminate use of paper! Yes you can make a difference!
