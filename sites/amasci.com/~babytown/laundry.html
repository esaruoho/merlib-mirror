Laundry Products

Most commercially available products are too hard on the diapers and your baby's clothes. Some contain fillers such as chalk which do not rinse out properly and can irritate the skin interfere with the absorbency of the diapers and make your family's clothes look dull. They can contain scrubbing agents such as sand grits which wear fabrics prematurely. Most commercially available products have the wrong pH balance and this can also cause skin irritations and problems with the diapers. Many products are not biodegradable. We know that our products are safe and work effectively. Try to find a product with a pH level between 5 and 10 like our skin.
