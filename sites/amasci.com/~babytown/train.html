Training and Swim Pants

Finally! This what a training pant should be. Absorbent enough to contain one accident; made with a cotton layer next to the body so it feels wet right away; and waterproof to protect your child's clothing.

Our Swim Pants are great for swim lessons in the pool because they offer the containment of a waterproof nylon pant and the comfort of cotton. They are more fitted than a diaper cover because they are designed to fit next to your baby. The adjustable wrap style allows the swim pant to grow right along with your baby.
