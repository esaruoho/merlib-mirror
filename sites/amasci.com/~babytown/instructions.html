IndisposablesTM Liners & Inserts Laundry Products


Laundry Instructions

When you purchase products from BabyTown, you will be given a set of laundry instructions. We have reproduced the list here for your convenience.

We recommend that all of our products be prewashed before use.
Washing your IndisposablesTM Cotton Diapers
  1. Prewash diapers twice before using.
  2. After Changing your baby's diaper, flush stool down the toilet and place wet or soiled diaper into a diaper pail.
  3. Soak the diapers in a pail filled with 1/2 water and 15ml of IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener, or 1/4 cup vinegar or washing soda. It is not recommended to soak diapers more than three days.
  4. To transfer diapers in the pail to a laundry machine, either hand wring diapers and use lid of pail to carry OR pour all of the diaper pail contents into the washing machine, turn on rinse cycle and spin solutions out of the diapers.
  5. Use the IndisposablesTM Liquid Laundry Concentrate. Do not use Bleach, Detergents with Bleach, Pure Soap Products (such as Ivory Snow), strong detergent products - liquid or powder (such as Tide).
  6. Care in drying your diapers will add to their life. Dry diapers on medium setting for half of the drying cycle and then hang damp diapers to dry for the balance. Softeners in the washing machine and dryer are not recommended as they reduce the absorbency of the diapers.
  7. To remove stains, rub a bar of "white" soap on the wetted down stain before placing in the diaper pail. Try hanging diapers in the sun after laundering to naturally bleach stain away. Add IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener to washing machine. For extra tough stains, mix 5ml IndisposablesTM Liquid Laundry Concentrate with 5ml IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener and apply past to stain. Launder as usual.
Washing your IndisposablesTM V

The washing instructions for the IndisposablesTM V are the same as for The IndisposablesTM Cotton Diaper with the exception of the recommendation that you utilize a dry diaper pail system (no water) versus the soaking method.

Washing your Nylon Pants
(Breathable or Waterproof Pull-Ups, Breathable or Waterproof Wraps, Cotton Covered Pull-Up and Cotton Covered Wraps)
  1. Caring for your IndisposablesTM Pants properly will add to their life. Breathable Nylon fabrics should always be hand washed and hung to dry. Waterproof nylon fabrics can be machine washed. Never place IndisposablesTM Pants in the dryer. To maintain the vivid colors of Cotton Covered Pull Ups and Wraps, soak in a saltwater solution (5ml salt in a basin of water) prior to washing.
  2. For premature babies or those with very tiny legs, hand stitch the Lycra/spandex on the legs to make the leg openings smaller. Undo the stitching as the baby grows.
Washing your IndisposablesTM Diaper Liners, Inserts and Baby Wipes

All of the above can be soaked, laundered and dried with diapers.

FlushabyeTM Diaper Liners

The FlushabyeTM Diaper Liner's size and content make it safe to flush down toilets. It should be noted, however, that plumbing systems and their effectiveness vary. Too many FlushabyesTM flushed down a toilet at one time or during one day in a less than adequate plumbing system may cause problems. To avoid this difficulty, we recommend that only one FlushabyeTM be flushed at one time. We also recommend that they only be flushed when soiled. Wet liners can be disposed of in the garbage. It is also helpful to let the FlushabyeTM Liner sit a few moments in the toilet before flushing. Homes with septic tanks can use the FlushabyesTM if they follow the above tips.

Washing your IndisposablesTM Training Pants

Do not soak Training Pants in the wet diaper pail system. Wash in warm water and dry on a medium setting. To reduce drying time, turn the pants inside out. Soak Cotton Print Training Pants in a saltwater solution (5ml salt in a basin of water) prior to washing

Washing your Denim Diaper Bag and Receiving Blankets

Wash in warm water and dry on a medium setting

Washing your Bibs and Swim Pants

Wash in warm water and dry on a low setting

Washing your Dirty Diaper Bags

Hand wash and hang to dry.

Washing your IndisposablesTM Pads
Change, Multi Purpose, Crib, Twin Bed, and Double Bed Pads)

Wash in warm water and dry on a medium setting

Washing your IndisposablesTM Mattress Protector

Machine wash in warm water and hang to dry.

Washing your IndisposablesTM Nursing Pads

Wash in warm water. Cotton Nursing Pads may be dried on a medium setting in dryer. Nylon Nursing Pads should be hung to dry.

Washing your Nursing Bras

Wash in cold water and hang to dry.

Washing your Cotton Sanitary Pads

* It is recommended that you wash all pads before using them to bring them to their maximum absorbency and wash away any possible manufacturing residues. When washing Outer Pockets, remember to attach the VelcroTM tabs together to prevent it from sticking to other clothes or from collecting lint.

  1. Remove soiled pads and rinse thoroughly in cold water to remove heavy soiling. If it is not convenient to rinse thoroughly immediately after use, then wet the pad and store it in a waterproof bag until you can rinse it. (if allowed to dry soiled, the pad will stain.)
  2. Soak rinsed pads in soaking container with IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener or fill the container with 2/3 water and 2 tablespoons of white vinegar or washing soda. Change water and cleaning solution every day.
  3. When you are ready to wash the pads, throw the pads and the contents directly in the washing machine along with your ordinary "light" wash, and use warm water - or hand wash in warm water. We recommend the use of the IndisposablesTM Liquid Laundry Concentrate or any other environmentally friendly detergent. DO NOT USE Bleach, detergents with bleach, pure soap products, or strong detergents (liquid or powder) to launder the pads.
  4. Care in drying will also add to the life of the product. For machine drying, use the medium setting and try using it for only half of the drying cycle, hang drying for the balance. Softeners are not recommended as they may coat the material fibers and thus reduce absorbency. Full air drying, especially sun drying, is a natural sanitizer and whitener, so try to use these methods whenever possible.

* If you follow these instructions, the pads should come out clean. If for any reason they do become stained, please DO NOT USE Chlorine Bleach. It is harmful to the environment and with repeated use will eventually break down the material fibers of the product. It will turn the VelcroTM, on the outer pocket wings, brown. We recommend the use, in the washing machine, of the IndisposablesTM Laundry Brightener, or any other environmentally friendly, NON CHLORINE BLEACH substitute. Before washing , soak the stained pads in water for at least 24 hours, then wash in warm water as usual.
