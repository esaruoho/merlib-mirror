 Slings 

 Our slings are great because they are wider than any other slings that we have seen.
You really can carry your toddler in it if he needs a cuddle. There are many different
positions you can try for different carrying and nursing situations. Sometimes it takes a
little experimenting for you and your baby to find a comfortable position. If you are
having trouble, ask us or someone who wears a sling successfully to help you. 

 The sling is a very comfortable way to carry your baby because of the proper weight
distribution it offers. Some carriers place all the weight at your shoulders, which
doesn't allow you to bend over easily or wear the carrier for any length of time. Slings
have been recommended for years by parenting organizations like La Leche League as being
better for you and your children.
