 Kozy Kids 

 These products are like a great big hug for your baby. The Kokoon is a deluxe version of an old fashioned "bunting bag" providing the baby with cozy comfort and the parents with convenience. The Kuddle Ring is not just an accessory for nursing mothers but also serves as a pillow for your baby and as a
support when he is learning to sit up. Remember, as helpful as the cuddle ring is, you should not leave your baby in it unattended. 
Made of top quality fabrics in three prints they will keep your kid kuddly and kozy all year round!
