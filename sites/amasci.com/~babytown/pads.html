Bed and Change Pads 

Our change pads come in either brushed flannel prints and cotton covered prints and fit easily into most diaper bags. You will want one as soon as you try those cute plastic pads and your baby urinates while you are changing him. Our absorbent change pads solve that problem. Just throw them in the washer and dryer if they get wet. These pads fit nicely into a cradle or bassinet for extra padding and protection.
