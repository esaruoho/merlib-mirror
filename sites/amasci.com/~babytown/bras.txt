 Nursing
Mother Bra
and Pads 
 The
most recent
innovation in
nursing bras is
"flexible
fit" bras with
cups made of a
stretchy
cotton/spandex
fabric. This new

changes in breast
size that
breastfeeding
mothers experience;
the cups will fit

engorged or

 While

made the bras
uncomfortably hot to

overcome. Through
the use of cooler
fabrics and improved
styling, the new
Nursing Mother Bra
is cooler and even
more comfortable,
yet still offers
ease of use and
plenty of support.
