THE BEST COTTON DIAPER ACCESSORY

Many parents who use cotton diapers during the day find that they need to use paper diapers at night because the urine soaks the diaper and pant, wetting the baby's clothing and bed. The answer is our wonderful insert, which doubles the absorbency of the diaper and prevents leaking during nap and nighttime. Many parents start to use inserts for nap and nighttime when their babies are about four months old.

Here's an idea from one parent: "I used brushed polyester liners at night and sometimes throughout the day. When I noticed that my son reached the stage where he had fairly consistent bowel movements each morning after breakfast, I would use a Flush-a-bye liner in the first diaper of the day and goodbye mess! Flush-a-byes are great to use when you go out because you don't need to try and rinse a dirty diaper in someone else's bathroom. However you use them, liners make diapering a breeze!"

Inserts
Bigger babies often need even more protection at night. If you have different sizes of inserts you can use two at a time; a size large and a size small, thus affording maximum protection while reducing bulk in the diaper. - $3.50 each

When you use inserts you may find that you need the next size diaper pant or wrap because of the extra bulk in the diaper. If the leg or waist openings of the bigger covers are too loose and the diaper leaks, simply hand stitch a dart in the seam of the leg and/or waist binding and release it when your baby has grown enough to fit the new pant properly.
   


Flush-a-byeTM

 Liners
For parents' convenience, Diaper Liners ease the clean up of soiled diapers and prevent staining. 


The Flush-a-byeTM Diaper Liners are made of 100% wood pulp and are safely flushable, even for septic tanks. Many parents leave Flush-a-byes in the diaper if the diaper is just wet, not stained. Flush-a-byesTM can go through the diaper pail and wash. They can then be separated and hung dry. Out of ten Flush-a-byeTM liners you can expect to reuse six, as some will tear. If the odd liner goes through the dryer don't worry, you won't gum up the works. - $5.25 per roll of 100 sheets.
