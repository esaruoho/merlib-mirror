What do I need to get started?
Most of our customers start with a pregnancy or a baby in their family or group of friends. After that, our consultants recommend:
24 - 36 small diapers; any less is difficult to deal with because you run out often and you encounter premature wear of diapers
1 small cover to every 4 - 6 diapers. The number of covers you will need depends on how you use and launder your covers, discuss this with your consultant. Try some pull-up pants and some wrap pants to find out which works best for you.
3-4 small inserts for extra absorbency during nap and night time; you may not need these until your baby is 3 or 4 months old.
Liners; refer to the section on liners to make a decision about which type you will use
1 2l. bottle of laundry concentrate; the smaller size is not as cost efficient and you will run out of it too soon
1 400g bottle of laundry brightener; the smaller size is fine as you only use one or two capfuls at a time. If you really like the product as much as we do, you can buy the larger size later
1 25ml bottle ONA for diaper pail, washing machine etc.
1 diaper pail
1 small dirty diaper bag for going out
12-24 wipes, because if you are going to go cotton, go all the way!
