 Diaper Covers 
 Training and Swim Pants 

 Pants and Wraps Indisposables TM Wraps and Pants are soft and comfortable for your baby. The waterproof diaper covers come in attractive solid colors and our cotton covered breathable covers are available in four exciting
prints. During the summer the cotton covered pants are more than just a diaper cover, they are a fashion accessory! 

 For your older children we have designed a reliable training pant to help reduce the risks when they are ready to make the transition from diapers to "big kid" pants. 

 We also offer an excellent swim pant that offers the very best in containment and comfort for your water baby!
