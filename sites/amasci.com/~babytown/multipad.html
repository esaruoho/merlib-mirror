30 USES OF THE MULTIPURPOSE PAD

  1. Before the baby is born, in your bed, in case your water breaks.
  2. In the car, under yourself, on the way to the hospital.
  3. Home birth - it holds 1 litre of fluid.
  4. Under yourself while you are nursing in bed.
  5. Folded double in the bassinet, cradle or any size bed.
  6. Folded double on the change table for extra padding and protection.
  7. In the play pen.
  8. In the stroller.
  9. On the sofa, carpet or fancy chair.
  10. While laying the baby on the floor to protect from pet hair or soiled carpet.
  11. In the crib.
  12. If your baby has diaper rash, to air out the rash worry-free.
  13. Under your toddler's car seat.
  14. At the beach to protect baby/toddler from the sand.
  15. On the grass to protect baby/toddler from the dampness or tickling grass.
  16. Under a high chair to catch spills.
  17. As an activity blanket.
  18. Under the potty.
  19. In the bed for bed wetter.
  20. Velcro a second pad to the comforter/blanket to protect covering for a bed wetter.
  21. In the car, while/after toilet training to protect the seat against accidents.
  22. In the car, after swimming, when you can't change your clothing.
  23. On concrete or wood pool decks.
  24. To protect upholstery from wet snowsuit.
  25. Under the "bucket" when your child is ill.
  26. On the pillow, if your child goes to bed with wet hair.
  27. In the bed if your child has a fever. The pad will absorb the perspiration.
  28. Inside or under your sleeping bag while camping.
  29. In your cottage or trailer on the bed.
  30. Protects moisture damage to carpet and furniture when under vaporizers or humidifiers.
