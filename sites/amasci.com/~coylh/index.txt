 This kit is 2.5" x 1.5" x 0.75" and weighs about 1.5 ounces. 

 I was inspired by Doug Ritter's 
Equipped To Survive site and PSK, but wanted something smaller, lighter,
and more suited to my urban life. 

 The kit contains: 

 whistle 
 compass 
 flashlight 
 pencil 
 two needles 
 thread 
 wire tie 
 spark lite and two tinder wads 
 safety pin
