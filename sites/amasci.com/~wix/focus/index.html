Greetings!

This is a free clearing house for positions for FOCUS programmers.

To post your Position Available or Position Wanted notice, e-mail it to wix@eskimo.com and please make FOCUS the first word in the Subject of the email!

If you want to keep your name a secret, (Positions Wanted only!) let me know and I will forward any inquiries.

Be sure to provide full contact info, and I won't do any editing, so what they see is what you send! (If you have email addresses I will make them mailto: links as with mine above.) (Text or html is OK, but please, no java, gif, jpg, Word files, etc. NO RESUMES, NO CVs!! Please observe the KISS principle!)

The advantage of using this web page is that all postings will remain until I receive a request from the poster to remove it, or I start running out of room, whichever comes first. In the latter case I will remove oldest first.
