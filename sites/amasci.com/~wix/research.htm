Piney Woods Research Services

Publishers of The Piney Woods Newsletter.

Piney Woods Research Services is primarily involved in Genealogical research concerning people in East Texas. However, we also engage in projects that are not centered in East Texas. At this time our on-site research activities are concentrated in and around Seattle and King County, Washington, but we maintain considerable resources and contacts for researching East Texas ancestors. We will travel to other places if the size of the project warrants. In the course of our research we visit libraries, courthouses, the Federal Archives, The Family History Center
and even a cemetery once in a while.
We also publish books, pamphlets and forms concerning the study of genealogy and genealogical research.
We perform publishing and bulk mailing services of newsletters and other items
for individuals, associations and societies.
Send us e-mail describing the information you are searching for or what your publishing or mailing problem is and we will develop some suggestions of things we can do to assist you.
We would also be interested in hearing about other genealogical projects or joint ventures that you might have in mind.
