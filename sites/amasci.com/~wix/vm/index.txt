Greetings! 
This is a free clearing house for positions for VM, VSE and Linux/390 
 specialists. If the position does not primarily involve one of these operating systems I will not post it.
 To post your Position Available or Position Wanted notice, e-mail it to 
 wix@eskimo.com and please make VM or VSE or 
 Linux/390 the first word in the Subject of the email!
 If you want to keep your name a secret, (Positions Wanted
only!) let me know and I will forward any inquiries to you in confidence.
 Be sure to provide full contact info as I won't do any
editing, so what they see is what you send! (If you have email addresses I will make them mailto: links as with mine above.)
(Text or html is OK, but please, NO java, gif, jpg, style sheets, WORD, etc. Please observe the KISS principle! No Resumes! No CVs! )

The advantage of using this web page is that
all postings will remain until I receive a request from the
poster to remove it, or I start running out of room, whichever comes
first. In the latter case I will remove oldest first, and if I
have time I will send you a note asking if you wish to post
another notice.

Also, I will be sending a reminder to the VM, VSE and Linux/390 lists once
a week to remind them that this service is here.
