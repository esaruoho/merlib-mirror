John Gurley STEWART

Subject Named Male is my paternal Great Grandfather and for whom an extensive records search is in effect. The only factual information discovered thus far from Grandfather's Spanish-American War Records, to wit; William Allen STEWART born 11 September 1872, Lafayette, Upshur County TX; parents John Gurley "of Indiana" and Julia (nee Teal) "of Georgia". Oral history = John Gurley b. Shelbyville, IN ca.1840; parents William Adam "of Virginia" and Eliza (nee Shelby)"of Kentucky"; no further information on parents. Oral history: John Gurley enlists Union Army, post war resides Texas; serving in East Texas as a U.S. Marshal and Postmaster, alternately until death. Also, it's believed he wrangled and sold horses to Army, Texas Rangers and stage lines throughout that area. Without knowing what regiment John Gurley joined, where or in which state (Indiana,Illinois,Kentucky,Ohio) searches of Military Records are prolonged. Therefore, a search in East Texas based on birth of son, William Allen, may produce some factual data.
