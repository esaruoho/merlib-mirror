Jacob KILLION was born 16 March 1755 in Orange County, North
Carolina, died August 1837, Menard County, Illinois.
According to his Revolutionary War pension papers he also
livid in Greenbriar County, Virginia (West Va.), Fleming
County, Kentucky, and Scott County, Indiana. He had two
sons, Michael KILLION and Jacob KILLION, Jr., who also lived
and died in Menard County, Ill. Jacob Sr. also probably had
a daughter named Sarah who married Lloyd KEITH and lived in
Scott County, Indiana.

Did Jacob KILLION, Sr. have other children? Who were his
parents?
