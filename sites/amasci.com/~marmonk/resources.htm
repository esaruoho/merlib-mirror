Origami Resources for Seattle and other Cities

Here are lists of stores where one can find paper and books. Mainly this is for Seattle. I travel occasionally for my job, and included are stores I've visited in some other cities in the USA.

Seattle

Kinokuniya Books - 519 6th Avenue South, Seattle - 206.587.2477

    This is the best bookstore in the area for origami books, both in English and some Japanese imports. There is a selection of washi paper also. The store is located upstairs in Uwajimaya.

Uwajimaya - 519 6th Avenue South, Seattle - 206.624.6248

    The largest supply of pre-cut and packaged origami papers. Some washi paper also. Upstairs from the grocery store area.

Borders - 1501 4th Avenue, Seattle - 206.622.4599

    Of the chain stores, this one has the best selection of origami books. Located across from Westlake Park in the middle of downtown Seattle.

University of Washington Book Store - 4326 University Way NE, Seattle - 206.634.3400

    Their selection of origami books varies. Sometimes quite extensive and sometimes a bit skimpy.

Seattle Art, Inc. - 1816 8th Avenue, Seattle - 206.626.0711

    The best selection of art papers in town. They carry some pre-cut, packaged origami paper also. Located a few blocks north of the State of Washington Convention Center and on the same block as the Greyhound station.

Daniel Smith - 4150 First Avenue South, Seattle - 206.223.9599

    A good selection of art papers. They carry some pre-cut, packaged origami paper also. Located about 1/4 mile south of the West Seattle Freeway overpass, on the east side of the street. Look for the big sign.

de Medici/Ming Fine Paper - 1222A First Avenue, Seattle - 206.624.1983

    A good selection of papers for a small store. Among their stock they carry machine and hand marbled papers and an assortment of thai papers. The store is located on the block to the south of the Seattle Art Museum, under the red awning.

The Paper Cat - 214 1st Avenue South, Seattle - 206.623.3636

    Located in the heart of Pioneer Square, this is mostly a card shop, but they have a fairly wide selection of single sheet patterned wrapping papers. There is an adjacent store full of rubber stamps if you like decorating your own papers. Across the street is Elliot Bay Books. A poor origami section but an outstanding book store.

Other Cities

Honolulu

Bunbundo Bookstore - 655 Keeaumoku Street, Honolulu - 808.947.2525

    Japanese bookstore with imported books, pre-packaged papers, and washi

San Francisco

Kunikuniya Bookstore - 1581 Webster Street, San Francisco - 415.567.7625

    Japanese bookstore with imported books. The card store across the mall has a good collection of washi.

Berkeley

Elica's Paper - 1801 Fourth Street, Berkeley

    An art paper store with a wide selection of washi and hand-made and imported papers.

Los Angeles

Kunikuniya Bookstore - 123 Onizuka Street, Suite 205, Los Angeles - 213.687.4480

    The store is located on the second level of an outdoor mall that is attached to the New Otani Hotel, near Los Angeles City Hall. Japanese bookstore with imported books, prepackaged paper and washi. There are other stores in the area that carry origami books and supplies.

Chicago

The Paper Source - 232 West Chicago Avenue, Chicago - 312.337.0798

    The best art paper store you will find. Extensive selection of all sorts of art and patterned papers. Upper level has a large collection of rubber stamps and supplies. Located about a mile west of North Michigan Avenue. Just below the Chicago Avenue El stop, on the Ravenswood line.

Aiko's Art Materials Import, Inc. - 3347 N. Clark Street, Chicago - 312.404.5600

    A store of imported Japanese paper. An extensive selection of paper types and patterns. Small supply of origami books and pre-packaged papers.

Cambridge

The Paper Source - 1810 Massachusetts Avenue - Cambridge - 617.497.1077

    I have not been to this store, but if it is anything like the one in Chicago, then it is a must visit.
