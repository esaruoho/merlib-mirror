Original Bill Folds

Here is a small collection of original or deriviative designs I have developed. Click on the links to load a GIF file of the folding diagrams. Some of the diagrams are in PDF format. To get a free copy of Adobe's Acrobat Reader, click here.

Three Christian Symbols: The Cross, The Heart, and The "Ichthus." To view the GIF folding diagrams: for the the Cross click on Page 1 (18K) and Page 2 (18K), click here for the Heart (19K), and click here for the Ichthus (22K).

The Cross requires the bill be folded in thirds the long way. Here is a diagram on how to do that (15K).

The President Ring - This design owes itself to the bow tie and dime-in-ring models. The only ring I know of that puts the President's face as the signet. Click here to view the diagrams in GIF format. (22K)

The Presidents Ball - This is the Sonobe cube adapted for a dollar bill. I know it is a cube, but when assembled it struck me more as a ball, hence the name. Fold this model and then show your friends how you can "throw your money around." Click here to download the diagrams in GIF format. (19K)

The Arched Cat - This is Toshie Takahama's design for the Arched Cat. I adapted the design for a dollar bill. The original was from a square piece of paper. Click here to view Page 1 (25K) and Page 2 (14K) of the diagrams in GIF format.

Presidents on Display - A stand-up display of the President. Click here (101K) for diagrams in PDF format. If you do not have Adobe's Acrobat Reader, then follow the link at the top of the page.
