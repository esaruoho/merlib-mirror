 Failed Origami Titles 

 Have you ever had one of those days when you're
all thumbs and none of the creases want to line up correctly. Then, has
someone come along and asked, "What is THAT supposed to be?!?!"
Well, memorize these clever titles and save face until that day when the
magic returns to your fingers and you are producing masterpieces again.
