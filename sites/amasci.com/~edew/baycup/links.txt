than some national champions: Sean McClain has taken over for Saul 
of the Fencing Post for weapons repair and equipment sales. You may 
also contact Sean at 650.969.2071. 
Another is Dmitri Kirk-Gordon, further down in the San Jose area. Dmitri 
can do all rewiring and body-cord repairs. Contact Dmitri by email or 
by phone: 408.829.9663. 
 In San Francisco, American Fencers' Supply Co. is still the best place for 
equipment purchase and repair work. Contact them at amfence@amfence.com 
or by phone: 415.863.7911.
