    13-Dec-2002
    Start your 2003 new year with some strong epee training. Top Bay Area epee fencer and possible 2004 Olympian Eric Hansen is holding a junior epee training camp on January 1. Yes, hit the strip right after that new year partying! Anyway, if you're interested, here's the enrollment form.

    10-Dec-2002
    Some exciting changes to the Bay Cup are in the works. First, there are three new Yahoo! Groups created to help make communication among fencers, coaches, and others easier to deal with. Second, thanks to Jeff Powell of Salle DeCesare, the Bay Cup website will soon be moving to a new URL, perhaps www.thebaycup.org (it's not finalized yet). Then, it'll be easier to remember the URL instead of this convoluted eskimo thingee. More information is in store in the news section. Click there and read all about it.

    09-Dec-2002
    Updated check-in close times to include the Junior Sabre at CFA. That'll be it for the remainder of the calendar year. Have a wonderful holiday season and see you next year! Also, look for new items here on the Bay Cup website. They will be significant!

    21-Nov-2002
    USFA is running a "best on the web" best fencing website promotion. How often have your accessed this site for your fencing information pleasure (or otherwise?). You think? Hey, maybe this site can be nominated. Go follow the rules in the latest American Fencing magazine and vote for the Bay Cup website!
