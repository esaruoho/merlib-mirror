Information for Tournament Hosting

Host organizers are required to do the following:
  • Ensure all fencers are either:
    • Outside the Bay Cup Region (see below)
    • Are Bay Cup members.  This means it has a Bay Cup receipt for this year or is on the Bay Cup

      • member list (see Senior Bay Cup Members or Junior Bay Cup Members or Veteran Bay Cup Members)
    • Signing up for the Bay Cup the first time.  Make sure you have the Bay Cup receipt forms.
  • Download the entry form (page 1, and page 2 if you expect a larger crowd),

    •             the pool sheets,
                the DE table sheets,
                the DE scoresheets (regular 15-touch bouts or Y10/12 2-of-3 5-touch bouts),
                the Final-8 tableau sheet (if needed), and
                the results form
    prior to the tournament.  See below for recommended number of copies to make.
  • Within one to two weeks prior to the event, send email to edew@eskimo.com with the check-in close times,

    • and a potential list of referees (or state that the event is primarily self-refereed).  Send any electronic copy of the
    flyer you are sending out to clubs as well to the above eddress to be placed in the notices.
  • After the event, send via mail (or FedEx, though that's not recommended) the following:
    • Completed results forms, entry forms, completed pool sheets, and DE tableau sheets.  Please include the

      • names of the referees for each pool sheet and DE quadrants.
    • All money for Bay Cup registration and the YELLOW copy of the receipt.  Registration is $40/fencer.

      • Please do not hold back for referee fees.  The Bay Cup will reimburse the host club for referee fees.  See
      below concerning referee reimbursement rules.
  • All host organizers are encouraged to make the events special by providing a theme for the event (e.g., the Memorial

    • events that Golden Gate Fencing Center are hosting, the Wine Open that Sonoma Fencing Academy runs, and several
    others).  All host organizers are encouraged to offer other prizes in addition to the medals.  None of this bullet item
    is mandatory.


Referee reimbursement rules:

  • All Junior Bay Cup events must have hired referees.  There should be no self-refereeing for junior events.  Please

    • check the Referees page for a list of available referees and contact information.
  • Referees are classed as either type 1 or type 2.  Type 1's are competent, experienced referees, possibly with national

    • referee rating.  Type 2 referees are up-and-coming referees with limited or no experience.
  • Suggested payment to referees:
    1. For highly-attended events (>32 competitors): $80/day for fully utilized Type 1's;

      2. $60 for fully utilized type 2's.
    2. For several small (<32 competitors) events, $25/event for fully utilized Type 1's, $15/event for fully utilized

      3. Type 2's.
    3. Fencer-referees will have the entry fee(s) reimbursed fully, if they are the primary referee in their pools and DEs.
    4. Fencer-referees who also referee fully in a secondary event in which they do not compete in (e.g., a male

      5. fencer refereeing a women's event) will be paid $20/event for those events where he or she fully referees.
  • The Bay Cup will reimburse clubs for the following amount, assuming hired referees are used:
    • Minimum amount of $40.
    • The lesser of the following:
      • $4/fencer (total for all events that day at that location)
      • $40/referee, assuming competent, fully utilized referees, and $25/referee for the up-and-coming

        • referees (again, fully utilized)
      • 50% of the club's compensation to referees
      • $280.00
    • Reimbursements for fencer-referees will be a straight 50% only for those events where the referee is also not

      • competing.  There is no reimbursement for using fencer-referees where the person is also competing (item 3 above).
  • Junior Sabre events will be paid separately from the regular Bay Cup account.


Recommended number of scoresheets and pool sizes and other rules of thumb:
 
Number of competitors Number of pools (and thus scoresheets)
1-9 one pool, double strip if possible
10-14, 16 two pools, double strip for 16
15, 17-19 (maybe 20), 21 three pools
(maybe 20), 22-28 four pools
29-35 five pools
36+ arrange to have pools of 6's and 7's

Each DE scoresheet page has eight scoresheets.  You need one scoresheet for each competitor.

Competition format should be one round of pools, 100% up to DE with no repechage.  There is no fence-off for third
place.  In some situations, and with agreement by the competitors, other formats may be used.

Expect a pool of 6 to be completed in one hour, a pool of 7 in one and one-half hours.  Sabre pools will move quicker
and epee pools will move just a bit slower.  Pools of 5 or less may be completed in thirty to forty-five minutes.

Original seeding into the tournament is based on the following in the given order:

  • National Points
  • Letter Rating
  • Year Rating was earned
  • Bay Cup points
  • Random order
Flip lower-seeded fencers to balance out club representation in the pools.

After the pools are completed, seed fencers in the following order:

  • Victories/Bouts
  • Indicators (touches scored - touches received)
  • Touches scored
  • Random order
You do not have to assign new ratings for fencers after the tournament.  The Bay Cup administrator will verify and
forward all new ratings changes to the USFA.

The Bay Cup Region:

Beginning this season, the Bay Cup Region is defined to be within a seventy-five mile radius of Oakland (City Hall).
Fencers belonging to clubs outside the Region (currently, that would include just Cal-Poly SLO, Le Cote d'Escrime,
Monterey Peninsula Fencers  Sierra Fencing Club, and all clubs outside the tri-division), or unattached fencers who
live outside the Region are allowed a two-event waiver of the $40 registration fee.  Please read the members section
for more information.
