Dishonesty of the Watchtower Society

Does "God's Organization" Always Tell the Truth?

Johannes Greber - Spiritualist

For years the Watchtower organization used Johannes Greber as a scholarly source to support the New World Translation's rendering of John 1:1 calling Jesus "a god". They referenced him as support at least 7 times in various Watchtower publications from 1962 through 1976. On page 31 of the April 1, 1983 Watchtower magazine, the Society said that the 1980 edition of Greber's New Testament indicated a close rapport with spiritism, and the Watchtower deemed it improper to make use of that translation. If a close rapport with spiritism makes Greber improper to use in 1983, why was it proper in 1962? On Page 88 of the 1955 booklet, What Do the Scriptures Say About Survival After Death? the Society said:
"It comes as no surprise that one Johannes Greber, a former Catholic clergyman, has become a spiritualist and has published the book entitled Communication with the Spirit World, Its Laws and Its Purpose. (1932, Macoy Publishing Company, New York) In its Foreword he makes the typical misstatement: "The most significant spiritualistic book is the Bible; for its principal contents hinge upon the messages of the beyond to those existing in the present.""

The identical statement was made on page 603 of the October 1, 1955 isue of The Watchtower. A few months later, in the February 15, 1956 Watchtower magazine, pages 110 and 111 said, in part:
"Under this impression Greber endeavors to make his translation read very spiritualistic. ... Very plainly the spirits in which ex-priest Greber believes helped him in his translation."

From 1962 through 1976 the Watchtower used support from a translation they had already denounced as spiritualistic! This sort of dishonesty is inexcusable. All magazine articles are written by committees with Witness elders and approved for publication by members of the 144,000 class, the Governing Body. (See The Watchtower, March 1, 1987, page 15, paragraph 18.) If the Watchtower's 144,000 have put forth untruths, how are we to apply Revelation 14:5, "No lie was found in their mouths; they are blameless" to them? In The Watchtower magazine, April 1, 1991, page 24, talking about the pure language, it states: "There is no allowance in this language for lying, deceit, or a tricky tongue. Those who speak this language must always speak truth."

The Biography of Pastor Russell

On page 63 of the 1959 book Jehovah's Witnesses In the Divine Purpose the statement is made that The Watchtower has never published a biography of their founder and first president, Charles Taze Russell. The Watch Tower magazine of December 1, 1916 contains a short biography of Russell, and the 1923 through 1927 editions of Studies in the Scriptures Series I, The Divine Plan of the Ages contained a biography of him on pages 1-30. Pages 16 and 17 of the same book that denies the biography, Jehovah's Witnesses In the Divine Purpose, refers to the 1926 edition of the biography and quotes from the opening paragraph of the biography. Is this kind of truth from Jehovah or from man?
