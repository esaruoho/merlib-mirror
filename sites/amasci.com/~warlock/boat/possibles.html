Boats we were considering

Even though we have decided on a boat (a 1974 "Cruising" Cal 35 for those who are interested) I thought that the list of boats we were considering might be interesting to others. So here they are. Feel free to drop me a line to say why you think our choice was ill-chosen, or well chosen. All communication on this subject is welcome at the moment. Anyway, here were some possibles.

  • Valient 32:

    Sturdy, a little slow, many have blisters and can be found for a little less because of it.


  • Cal 40

    Fast, tend to be pricey, can be a little hard in rough weather. Not the most comfortable of libveaboard interiors.


  • Columbia 41

    Roomy, not fast or slow, more suited to the livaboard part than the passagemaking, plenty of room for accessories.

  • C&C 34,36 and larger to the budgetary limits.

    Strong, fast boats, a little tight inside for the length.

  • ChrisCraft 35 (yes, Chris Craft made some sailboats.)

    Unusual, strong, pretty well laid out. Older, but if in good shap, that's ok.


  • Columbia 36

    Good compromise between comfort and speed.


  • Ingrid 38

    A little expensive, but a good sturdy hull, bowsprit is a negative.


  • Hans Cristian 33

    Too expensive by far, but an awesome boat, and the wife drools over it. They also tend toward the slow.


  • Cal 35, 36 and 39

    All are strong boats, another good compromise between liveaboard and cruising, At the moment, this seems the most likely

    there are lots of them around, they are a little long in the tooth, so the prices have come down to an affordable level.


Any decent boat we are likely to be able to afford will need some work, and some upgraded systems. But actually, I am kind of looking forward to that.

This is just a partial list, by no means exhaustive. But some ideas. So, know anyone with a boat in their yard they want to sell :)
