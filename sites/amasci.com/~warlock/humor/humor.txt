 Humor is a funny thing, one man's gutbuster is another man's ho-hum. 

 It's been with us since the first caveman laughed at one of his fellows
who burned his hand on some of that fire stuff. One thing has stayed pretty
constant from then 'till now. Humor, with the sole exceptions of word games,
consists of laughing at someone. No matter the joke, if it's not a word game.
(and sometimes if it is) someone is the butt of the joke. 

 There are those who would disagree, OK, send me a joke that's 

 Not a word game, pun or limerick etc 
 Isn't about laughing at someone.
