 College Hosted Big/Scenario Games 
 Planning to attend a scenario or big game? Drop by the NCPA Scenario Forum and let other NCPA club players know where you're going so they can meet up with you and represent college ball!
 College clubs also host scenario and big games. While these events are hosted by college clubs, these events are designed for all paintball players and everyone is encouraged to attend.
