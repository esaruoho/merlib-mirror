 NCPA Rules 
 (Yes, yes we do.) 

 Eligibility at the NCPA national tournament is limitted to teams of full-time, undergraduate students. Eligibility at conference events may vary. Eligible players from multiple colleges or universities may combine to form a team provided that at least three players on a team attend the same college or university and that no college or university has players on more than one team.
