    How do I get this discouinted stuff?

    Ordering is easy.

    • Go to your club's entry in the NCPA College Clubs List, go to the "Discount ID" field, and after the number and dash, enter six digits.
    • Call the retailer, let them know what college you are from, and give them your discount ID. Otherwise order as normal.
    • Wait for the carrier to bring you your stuff.

    That's it. Ship it to your local field (which we recommend as it's a little cheaper) or to your dorm room or wherever else you want it. Combine your order with other students at your club or other clubs in your area to cut shipping costs. The flexibility is yours. Forget your discount ID? No problem, just go enter a new one and use that.

    The only condition is you may *NOT* resell any product (except KC Gun Oil, which you may resell for profit) you obtained with an NCPA discount outside of college paintball. You're welcome to charge a markup on paint for events you hold, sell it to students at your college for rec play, college intramurals, tournament practice, etc, but please do not become the new paint provider for the public at your local field. Your purchase of the product at these prices constitutes acceptance of these terms. Your shipping information may also be compared to the location of your college as well as your club's contact email adress to verify eligibility. If you have questions on appropriate use or verifying eligibililty, please contact Chris (raehl311@yahoo.com). For those of you with support from your college, we can also do purchase orders.
