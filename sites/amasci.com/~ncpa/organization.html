National Collegiate Paintball Association

"A Club for Every Campus"

The National Collegiate Paintball Association is an incorporated, non-profit association dedicated to the promotion of paintball competition at the college and high school levels. The NCPA consists of numerous college paintball clubs across the country who annually elect a Board of Directors to set NCPA policy and appoint officers to execute that policy. As a non-profit organization dedicated to fostering amatuer level paintball competition under section 501(c)3 of the Internal Revenue Code, the NCPA is a tax-exempt organization, and contributions to the NCPA are tax-deductible.
