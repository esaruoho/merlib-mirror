For the first time ever, various college regionals around the country held events as a nationwide league leading up to a National Championship event, making the National Collegiate Paintball Association the first league in paintball to establish a conference structure. The 2001 season also represented one of tremendous growth for college paintball itself, with the number of conferences expanding from two to four, with nine separate colleges making their first ever tournament appearences. This season marks the transition of college paintball from a sport with an uncertain future to one of certain growth. The NCPA would like to thank supporters who made this possible - KiCK'N Paintballs for making affordable paint available at all of the events, the fields who provided us their time and locations, the various sponsors of each regional event, the media supporters including APG, Paintball2Xtremes, Paintball Magazine, and Paintball Sports International, longtime college paintball supporters like AKA, and of course the participants who make this league possible by organizing clubs at the college level and fighting school administravia to provide a positive paintball experience for other students.
