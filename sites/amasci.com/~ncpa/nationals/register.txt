 Register Online Here Now! 

 Online Registration must be completed April 20, 2002 . You may register as many teams from your school as you like. Teams may have up to 7 players on the roster.

 All teams must check in Friday Night at the official event hotel, the Holiday Inn Express , or Saturday morning at the field between 7 and 8 AM. We strongly encourage you to check in on Friday night. ALL TEAM MEMBERS MUST BE PRESENT WITH PROOF OF STUDENT STATUS for your team to check in. Teams who fail to check in will not compete.
