 Links to various sites where old magazines are for sale 

You will probably be quick to notice this site is directing you to my own 
personal site, along with two of my business sites, The Fleming Company and 
Flem-Data. On my sites I am getting rid of a lot of magazines I no longer

 NOTES REGARDING THE SORTING SEQUENCE: This list includes names from 
several languages. Various languages have different rules for sorting. 
For example, in Swedish the letters &Aring;, &Auml;, and &Ouml; come at the 
end of the alphabet. Unfortunately, it is impossible to accommodate the 
correct sorting sequence for all languages in one list.

Therefore, this list uses the English sorting sequence as used in the index 
line below, and letters with diacritical marks (accents, umlauts, etc.) are 
sorted as if the diacritical mark is not there. So &Aacute;, &Agrave;, 
&Aring;, &Auml;, &Acirc;, and &Atilde; will be intermingled with letter A.

 The English words "A", "An", and "The" (articles) are ignored if they occur at the 
beginning of a title and the title will be sequenced according to the first 
letter of the next word. For example, "The Weekly" would be found under "W". 
We also try to ignore initial articles in other languages, but we don't know 
the grammar for most languages so you may have to search both with and without 
the initial article. Hopefully we get it right for Spanish, Portuguese, 
French, German, and Italian.
