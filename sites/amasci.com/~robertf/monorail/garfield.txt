 The Controversy 

There was originally a station planned at West Galer Street, a block south 
of W. Garfield St. in the Interbay area. Now this station has been dropped 
from the plans and instead there would be a station about a mile south, 
at W. Mercer St. or W. Republican St. There would also be a station about a 
mile north, at W. Dravus St., as originally planned.

 My Opinions 

 I THINK A STATION AT GALER OR GARFIELD STILL MAKES MORE SENSE. 
It is more than two miles from the Key Arena station to the W. Dravus St. 
station. A station at Galer/Garfield is roughly centrally located betwen the 
other two stations. The proposed station on Elliott Ave. W. around 
W. Republican St. or W. Mercer St. would be only a few blocks from the Key 
Arena station and leave a gap of about two miles between there and the 
W. Dravus St. station.

 THERE IS A DEVELOPING INDUSTRIAL AREA SOUTH OF W. GALER ST. 
There are already a number of commercial and industrial enterprises along 
Elliott Ave. W. and 16th Ave. W., and a major biotech firm is building a 
large complex off of 16th Ave. W. Additionally, there is a complex of 
commercial and marine enterprises at Terminals 90 and 91, just west of the 
proposed station at Galer/Garfield.

 GARFIELD IS A TRANFER POINT FOR MAGNOLIA BUSES. 
Currently three Metro bus routes use the Magnolia Bridge, which is W. Garfield 
St. A monorail station could be built adjacent to the Magnolia Bridge at 
W. Garfield St., and the City could widen the Magnolia Bridge at this point 
to provide bus zones, and riders could transfer from buses to the monorail.

 A MAJOR DEVELOPMENT IS PLANNED NORTH OF GARFIELD. 
A developer is planning a major commercial and retail center north of W. 
Garfield St. along the west side of 15th Ave. W. The center is intended to 
be mass transit oriented. The developer was expecting a nearby monorail 
station. I was told by a representative of the developer that they prefer a 
station about two or three blocks north of Garfield, but one at Garfield 
would be acceptable.
