 		This table shows the following:
 
Feet from Southern Terminus: The distance in feet from the southern end of the Green Line at the Morgan Junction station. The distance is measured along the monorail route, and is approximate.
 
Feet Between Stations: Derived by subtracting the figures in the first column
 
Station Name: As assigned by The Seattle Monorail Project
Travel Time: The time it takes for the train to get from one station to the next. Northbound and southbound figures are often different due to a hill or other factors.
 
Dwell Time: The time in seconds that the train is supposed to wait at the station between arrival and departure.
