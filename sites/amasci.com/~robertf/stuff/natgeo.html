Surplus National Geographic Magazines for Sale

The magazines on this web site belong to Bob and Emma Fleming. In order to simplify the selling of the magazines that we no longer want, we are consolidating all of our sales through our business that sells surplus items, Flemco Surplus Sales.

These magazines are for sale for US$3.00 each (plus shipping, taxes, etc., as explained below). Supplemental (loose) maps are NOT included because they were saved in a separate map collection.

The purchaser shall be responsible for costs of shipping and handling, taxes, and customs duties as applicable.

For important information regarding the procedures and rules about how to buy from us, please see our Conditions of Sale.

If you are interesting in buying any of our stuff, or otherwise want to contact us, please send an e-mail to surplus@eskimo.com.

For shipping options, please see Shipping Options.

For payment options, please see Payment Options.

If you want to sell magazines, please be aware that we only buy magazines under very limited cconditions!
