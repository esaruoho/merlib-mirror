Recreational Vehicle Stuff to Sell

Intended for Seattle-area buyers

We were converting an old bus into a motorhome, but this project has now been abandoned and we are selling much of the equipment removed from the bus.

For most items, we recommend that you come to our house in north Seattle and see the items for yourself before deciding whether or not to buy. Also, the size and/or weight of some items make it impractical to ship them.

If interested, please send an e-mail to Bob Fleming, at surplus@eskimo.com in order to make a bid. Include the e-mail address we should reply to, along with your name and the amount of your bid.

This is our personal property. However in order to simplify the process of selling things from our various sites, as of May 1, 2002, all items are now being sold through Flemco Surplus Sales. Washington State sales tax of 8.6% applies to sales to people coming to our house or shipped to Washington State addresses.
