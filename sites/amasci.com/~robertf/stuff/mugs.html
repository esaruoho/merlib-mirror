MUG07: Barrel design mug —

This mug, somewhat resembling a beer stein, has a design apparently imitating a wooden barrel or cask.

This mug is in very good condition, except it has one significant defect — a pit or whole in the rim. This is not a chip, but has glaze in it, indicating that the pit was already there at the time the pottery was fired. The pit is visible at the left edge of the rim in the enlarged image. It is only because of this pit that I am not classifying the condition as excellent.
