Payment Options

The purchaser shall be responsible for costs of mailing, taxes, and customs duties as applicable. Payment shall be in U.S. Dollars (USD) or Canadian dollars (CAD).

We do not have facilities for processing credit cards.

FOR PAYMENT IN US DOLLARS: Payment must be by check drawn on a U.S. bank or by money order.

FOR PAYMENT IN CANADIAN DOLLARS: First convert the U.S. cost to Canadian dollars according to the current exchange rate. Payment must be by cheque drawn on a Canadian bank or by money order.

NEVER SEND CASH!   A check or money order is traceable and can be replaced. Cash can't be traced and it can't be replaced. If you send cash and it is lost in the mail, you can not prove you sent it and we can not prove we did not receive it. The money is gone and we suspect each other. SO PLEASE DO NOT SEND CASH!
