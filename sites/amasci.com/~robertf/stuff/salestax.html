Sales Tax Details

Our sales are being handled by Flemco Surplus Sales, which is a small business located in Seattle, Washington, U.S.A. According to the laws of the State of Washington, we are required to collect a sales tax on items delivered to addresses within the State of Washington. We do NOT collect any taxes on items delivered to addresses outside of the State of Washington.

The law requires us to charge the tax according to the total amount of the sale, including shipping costs. The law also requires us to use the tax rate that is in effect for our physical location, which is Seattle. Currently that rate is 8.8%. This includes the 6.5% state tax, 1.9% local tax (King County), and .4% Regional Transit Authority.

Washington State law provides for certain exemptions from the sales tax. If you are exempt, please let us know and give us the appropriate information we need to exempt your purchase. If you are exempt, you will know what information we need.
