Shipping information for magazines and books

For shipping single or sometimes even two or three copies of magazines, I prefer to use Priority Mail. The strong cardboard Priority Mail envelopes are available free from the Postal Service and provide good protection and quick delivery at a reasonable price. The postage for U.S. destinations is $3.50 for one pound and $3.95 up to the capacity of the envelope. For destinations outside the U.S., Global Priority Mail is US$7.00 to Canada and Mexico, and US$9.00 to most other countries for everything that will fit in the envelope up to a maximum of four pounds (about 1¾ kilograms). Please note that if the shipment will not fit in a standard Postal Service envelope the postage will depend upon the weight.

Express Mail (in the U.S.) and Global Express Mail (outside the U.S.) provides safer and faster service, but at somewhat higher cost.

For weights over four pounds to U.S. destinations, Media Mail is cheapest, least secure, and slowest; Express Mail is fastest, safest, and most expensive; and Priority Mail is a good compromise of speed, safety, and cost.

For weights over four pounds to destinations outside the U.S. postal system, The lowest cost is Surface Letter Mail, the next higher cost is Air Letter Mail, and the most expensive is Global Express Mail. Rates depend on the country of destination, and some services are not available to some countries.

If we are unable to use the free envelopes or boxes provided by the post office for Priority Mail and Express Mail, we will charge an additional amount for our cost of packaging materials we will need to buy.
