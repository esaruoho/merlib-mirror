Used Bolex Reporter Magazines 

 Surplus Bolex Reporter Magazines for Sale 

These magazines were published by Paillard Incorporated, the U.S. branch of 
the Swiss manufacturers of the Bolex line of 
motion picture cameras and equipment. The Bolex products include 
semi-professional 16mm cameras and 8mm cameras, and various accessories. 
The magazines contain advise on 
making movies and, of course, information on Bolex products.
