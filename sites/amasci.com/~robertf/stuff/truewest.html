Surplus True West Magazines for Sale

True West “All true — all fact — stories of the real West”. Historical information about the old West (Western United States around the 19th century and early 20th century). True stories of explorers, cowboys, Indians, settlers, cattlemen, bank robbers, outlaws, etc.

True West was published by Western Publications of Austin, Texas. A similar magazine is Frontier Times. Both of these magazines are sought by some collectors of old West history.
