 English for Filipinos 

 This is not a course for Filinos that do not know any English. It is for 
Filipinos that already know English, but want to improve their English.

 Get Rid of Your Filipino Accent! 

That is part of an ad I saw in a Filipino newspaper. It was advertising a
course to help Filipinos get rid of their accent, but it makes me angry! 

They make you feel like it is wrong to have an accent. But the people who
made that advertising are wrong!

You should not be ashamed if you have an accent. You are a Filipino! Your
accent is part of being a Filipino! Many people from all countries have
accents. Even native Americans from different parts of the U.S. have
different accents. Listen to the people from the South, from Boston, from Texas, etc.

The problem is not if you have an accent, but how much of an accent you have.
If your accent causes misunderstandings and confusion, then you might want to
do a few things to make your accent less strong. The purpose of these lessons is 
to show some of the things that are most likely to cause confusion with English-
speaking people and what to do to help improve those parts of your English.
You will still have an accent but it won't be as much of an accent.

The main ways an accent can be confusing are:

 Pronounciation of certain sounds. 
 Some English words that have different meanings in the Philippines
than they do in the U.S. and Canada.
 Some of the grammar that is different.
