Northgate Branch Library

The Fleming Family home page Northgate development home page Northgate Library Thornton Creek

A branch library of the Seattle Public Library system is currently under construction on Fifth Avenue Northeast, across the street from the Northgate Mall.

The library is part of a complex that also includes a community center, recreational facilities, and a park.

It is expected that the entire complex, including the library, will be completed in mid-2006.
