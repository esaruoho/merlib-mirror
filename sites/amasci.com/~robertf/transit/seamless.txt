 Seamless Mass Transit System 

The region's mass transit system needs to be well integrated so that it gives 
riders a seamless trip from one place to another, using a transfer or pass 
to transfer from one route to another to another, regardless of whether that 
route is a bus, monorail, or any other mode of transportation within the 
system.
