One form of transportation usually overlooked in a transportation system is 
bicycles.

Bicycles can hardly be called mass transit, and they definitely are not 
rapid transit. But a lot of people ride bicycles, and obviously, if enough 
people ride bicycles instead of driving, there would be a significant 
lessening of motor vehicle traffic.

I would like to see more effort and money put into building bikeways, 
dedicated narrow roadways specifically for bicyles, although in many cases 
they could be used also by hikers, joggers, walkers, wheel chairs, roller 
skates, skateboards, and other human-powered means of getting around.
