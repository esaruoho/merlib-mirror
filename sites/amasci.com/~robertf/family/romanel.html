About Romanel
Romanel G. Fleming
(Age 12)

Romanel was born in Seattle, Washington on April 14, 1994 and has lived in Seattle ever since. He is interested in a number of things, especially toy cars and using the computer.

Romanel is in the 6th grade in Seattle.

The name "Romanel" was made up from parts of names of four relatives. We thought it was a really unique name. Imagine our surprise to learn that there are two villages in Switzerland with this name: Romanel-sur-Morges and Romanel-sur-Lausanne. Thanks to Mr. Clerc of Romanel-sur-Lausanne for the e-mail telling us about these places.
