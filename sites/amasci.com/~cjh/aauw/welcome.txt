 W E L C O M E ... 

From recent grads to retirees, from young professionals to business owners, from homemakers to elected officials.... 

 Kirkland-Redmond AAUW is united in its support of equity, education, intellectual growth, individual worth and the development of opportunities for women and girls. 

The American Association of University Women promotes equity for all women and girls, lifelong education, and positive societal change. AAUW values and seeks a diverse membership. There shall be no barriers to full participation in this organization on the basis of gender, race, creed, age, sexual orientation, national origin or disability.
