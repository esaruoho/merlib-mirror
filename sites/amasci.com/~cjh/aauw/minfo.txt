 Branch Membership Information 
 Updated Dec. 19, 2000 
 The Kirkland-Redmond Branch of the American Association of University Women values and seeks a diverse membership. There shall be no barriers to full participation in this organization on the basis of gender, race, creed, age, sexual orientation, national origin, disability, or class. 
 Regular Membership 
 Membership is open to those with a bachelor&#39s or higher degree from a regionally accredited institution. The membership fee for the Kirkland Redmond Branch is $62. This fee includes national, state and branch membership. 
 Student Affiliate 
 Undergraduates enrolled full time in a regionally accredited two-year or four-year institution may become student affiliates. The student affiliate fee for the Kirkland-Redmond Branch is $25. 
 Dual Membership 
 If you are a member of another AAUW branch, and would like to join us too, dual membership is available for an additional $15. 
Further information about membership is available from our national website at: www.aauw.org/4000/reasons.html . 
 The membership application for the Kirkland-Redmond Branch is available on this site.
