Please note: The area code of (206) applies to all these numbers, since the numbers are all part of the King County government phone system, based in Seattle.

Active judges are not allowed to do weekday weddings between the hours of 8:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. They usually prefer to perform weddings at the courthouse rather than at a home or rented hall. Usually, you will need to leave a message for the judge giving the date, time, and location of the planned wedding and then wait for a call back regarding the judge's availability and fees. Each judge sets his or her own fees, so they could vary considerably from judge to judge.
