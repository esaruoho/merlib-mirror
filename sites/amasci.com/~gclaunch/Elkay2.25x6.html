Static shielding bags are the choice to protect expensive electronic components from electrostatic discharge. Latent failures in components result from undetected damage caused by electrostatic discharge during the manufacturing or assembly process. Static shielding bags protect against these very expensive failures.

  • Meets electrostatic requirements of MIL B-81705 Type 3.
  • Tested and approved per Hewlett-Packard "ESD Packaging
  • Material Specification" 5951-1589, APP.P, RevG
  • Meets requirements of EIA standard 541
  • Amine/amide-free interior will not attack polycarbonate parts.
  • Heat sealable.
  • Semi-transparent for easy identification of contents.
