This is an adjustable velcro Anti-Static Wrist Strap with 6 foot coil cord to a banana plug and alligator clip. When you are working inside your computer, protect your components from static by wearing the wrist strap and attaching the alligator clip to your computer while the power cord is plugged into a grounded outlet.

You can plug the banana plug into a grounding system outlet, or plug into the alligator clip which can be attached to your computer. When your computer is plugged into a grounded outlet it provides a ground.

The coil cord is attached to the wrist strap with a 360º swivel snap.
