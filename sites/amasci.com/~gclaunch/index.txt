Technical Data Sheet on the Physical and Electrical Properties of the SCS-1000 Bag 

 Metallized Static Shielding Bags are
used to protect static sensitive devices like circuit boards and
other electronic components, especially with exposed semi-conductors.
Static Shielding Bags offer the greatest
protection with the Faraday Cage effect provided by the aluminum
metal layer. 

 The measurements in the "Products
and Prices" information are the INSIDE sizes and the bags
are FLAT, so you have to make allowances for more thick components
like hard drives. Measure your parts carefully and allow wiggle room. 
Some of the smaller bags include an extra 1/8" of width.
