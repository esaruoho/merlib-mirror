 Question 10.14 
 Can I use an #ifdef in a #define line,
to define something two different
ways?

 No.
You can't
``run the preprocessor on itself,''
so to speak.
What you can do is
use one of two
completely separate
 #define lines,
depending on
the
 #ifdef setting.

ANSI Sec. 3.8.3, Sec. 3.8.3.4
