 Question 4.3 
 Does *p++ increment p ,
or what it points to?

 Unary operators like * , ++ , and -- 
all
associate
(group)
from right to left.

Therefore,
 *p++ 
increments p 
(and returns
the value pointed to by p before the increment).
To increment the value pointed to by p ,
use (*p)++ 
(or perhaps ++*p ,
if the order of the side effect doesn't matter).
