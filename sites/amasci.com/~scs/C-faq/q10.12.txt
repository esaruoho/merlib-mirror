 Question 10.12 
 How can I construct preprocessor #if expressions
which compare strings?

 You can't do it directly;
preprocessor #if arithmetic uses only

integers.
You can #define 
several
manifest constants,
however,
and implement conditionals

on those.
 See also question 20.17 .
