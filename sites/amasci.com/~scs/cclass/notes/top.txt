Steve Summit

 These notes are part of the
UW Experimental College 
course on
Introductory C Programming.
They are based on notes prepared (beginning in Spring, 1995)
to supplement the book
 The C Programming Language ,
by Brian Kernighan and Dennis Ritchie,
or K&R as
the book and its authors are affectionately known.
(The second edition was published in 1988 by Prentice-Hall, 
ISBN 0-13-110362-8.)
These notes are now (as of Winter, 1995-6)
intended to be stand-alone,
although
the
sections are still cross-referenced to those of K&R,
for the reader who wants to pursue a more in-depth exposition.
