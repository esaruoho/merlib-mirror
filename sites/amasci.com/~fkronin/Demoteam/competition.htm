Ronin Dojos Competition / Demo Team is a select group of students who have shown great promise as strong, all around Karate-ka.  Students who are members of the team represent Ronin Dojo at tournaments, (local, state, national and international), schools, parades, and fairs.

The team is open to all ages and ranks, but we do ask for a commitment from each member. The students are required to give maximum effort, take extra classes and have good citizenship both in and outside of the Dojo.  The extra classes are 60 minutes long once each week on Mondays at 6:00 PM.  There are no extra fees for participation. (Heavyweight gi and Ronin Dojo jacket are required.)  

Curriculum for the Team includes kata, synchronized kata, ippon kumite, goshindo, sparring, team sparring and board breaking.  Kata is from outside the normal curriculum for students at Ronin Dojo, as well as special drills, ippon kumite, and weapons.

Parental and family support is essential to the student who participates on the team. Family Karate Ronin Dojo has always placed the well being and personal growth of the individual first, and that always means having the family involved.

Current Schedule: Every Monday of the month at 6:00 PM
