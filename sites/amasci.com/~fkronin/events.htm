Bell Ceremony 2002

Every six months the karate-ka of the Dojo come together to celebrate the outstanding work and efforts of all the karate-ka of the Ronin Dojo.  Certificates of rank promotion are distributed to those who, during the previous six months, have passed to their next level.  

This event takes place immediately after each Major Rank Test, the only tests where the rank of Black Belt can be attained.  Bell Ceremony allows the rest of the Dojo an opportunity to congratulate new "Shodans" on their accomplishment.  It is also designed to encourage those who have achieved rank to "Keep up the good work and don't stop now!"  

Hope to see you at the next 'ringing' in March 2003.
