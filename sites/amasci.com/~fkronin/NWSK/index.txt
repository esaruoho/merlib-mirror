 Instructor Bio 
 Sensei Doris Eastbury began
training in Shito Ryu Karate in 1989. In 1998 she achieved Nidan-Sensei
under Hammond Sensei. Eastbury Sensei also holds a Black Belt in
Tae-Kwon-Do, and is very adept at Escrima.
