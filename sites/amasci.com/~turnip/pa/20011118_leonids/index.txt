 Leonids meteor shower 
 November 
 18 , 2001 - Near North Bend, WA 

 |< 

 >| 

 D espite 
 the near-freezing temperatures & competing car stereos blaring bad rap music 
 along the narrow moutain road we found, we ended up in a good viewing spot with 
 clear skies for the show (lucky, considering how it had rained steadily all 
 week). And although we didn't get the 4,000-an-hour meteor storm we had hoped 
 for, we did spot many hundreds in the 2 hours we were there! 

 Two Leonids through Orion

 Back home at 5:00 a.m., I stepped out onto my balcony for a moment before retiring&#151;and
