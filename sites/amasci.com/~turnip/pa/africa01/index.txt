 Background - 
 In 1999 I decided 
 to travel with my morris team to the Sidmouth Folk Festival in Devon, England, 
 a decision made easy when I learned that there would be a total solar eclipse&#151;something 
 I'd always wanted to see&#151;visible nearby only four days after the festival. 
 But the weather didn't cooperate (you can read that sad story here ). 
 Two years later, bitter & disillusioned, I suddenly remembered that the 
 next eclipse was due in June '01 & signed up for an eclipse-and-safari tour 
 I found on the internet; "Along the Zambezi" organized by Civilized 
 Adventures of Calgary, AB. This Shockwave travelogue is the result.
