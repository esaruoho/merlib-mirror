The "Hartman Fractal Hypotenuse" Hypothesis
 
Hypothesis: 
 
The fractal dimension of an infinitely squiggly and infinitesimal staircase across a square has a fractal dimension of 2 and that 2 is the maximum fractal dimension for any such squiggly staircase hypotenuse for all such rectangles!
