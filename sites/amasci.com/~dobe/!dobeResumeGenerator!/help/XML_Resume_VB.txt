 Introduction 
 RW4XML or Resume Wizard for XML , is used to
create a template XML and a template XSL file which will display a
 finished resume within an MSIE browser. 
RW4XML creates files intended to be edited in NotePad , or with
 any other XML editing tools after your first
template is created and saved. 
This sample project includes a DTD , a Schema , a generated XSL
transform file , as well as the source code for the Visual Basic to enable
you to modify the template in any way you wish! 
This is only the first version of RW4XML, which will undoubtedly
progress and evolve through the coming months and years. 
Please send suggestions and feedback to dobe@eskimo.com and mark the subject
line: Dobe's XML_Resume_VB project 
Thanks for taking a look at my project.

 The Document Type Definition file looks like this: 

 <!ELEMENT resume (header , name , docbody )> 
 <!ELEMENT header (street , city , state , zipCode , phone , email )> 
 <!ELEMENT street (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT city (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT state (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT zipCode (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT phone (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT email (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT name (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT docbody (objective , education , experience )> 
 <!ELEMENT objective (primary , secondary , location , note1 , note2 )> 
 <!ELEMENT primary (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT secondary (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT location (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT note1 (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT note2 (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT education (college )> 
 <!ELEMENT college (collegeName , townState , years , program , note1 , note2 )> 
 <!ELEMENT collegeName (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT townState (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT years (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT program (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ATTLIST program GPA CDATA #REQUIRED > 
 <!ELEMENT experience (company , distinctions )> 
 <!ELEMENT company (companyName , townState , years , position , note1 , note2 )> 
 <!ELEMENT companyName (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT position (#PCDATA )> 
 <!ELEMENT distinctions (note1 , note2 )>
