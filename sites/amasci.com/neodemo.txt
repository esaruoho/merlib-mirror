WARNING: if you mess with TWO neodymium supermagnets,
you are bound to pinch your fingers if you let them come together. This
REALLY HURTS, it's like pliers. Also, you should wear safety glasses,
since when two NIB magnets slam together, they tend to launch tiny chips
at high velocities. I've had 3/4" magnets acidentally fall together and
be completely shattered. The things are brittle! The ones larger than 
1/4" are NOT FOR UNSUPERVISED CHILDREN! 

 NEW DANGER: swallowed magnets can cause lethal stomach 
problems. 

Always keep magnets away from computers, floppy disks, credit cards,
wind-up watches, etc. I've erased several credit cards by accidentally
passing an NIB magnet near my pocket, or letting one fall into my lap.

you are bound to pinch your fingers if you let them come together. This
REALLY HURTS, it's like pliers. Also, you should wear safety glasses,
since when two big NIB magnets slam together, they tend to launch tiny 
chips at high velocities. I've had 3/4" magnets acidentally fall together 
and be completely shattered. The things are brittle! LARGE MAGNETS NOT 
FOR UNSUPERVISED CHILDREN! For kids, use NIB magnets under 1/4" across.
