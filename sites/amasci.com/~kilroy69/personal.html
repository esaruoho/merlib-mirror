So a little about me. . . Hmmm. Well, I guess the main thing I want to tell you is that I like the outdoors. That should be rather obvious to those whose web browsers are set to load the GIFs I put here :-)\. I like to go hiking in the mountains. I try to get to the Adirondack mountains in northern New York State as often as possible. I'm a backpacking leader for the Outing Club. This means I can safely take complete beginners up into the mountains in any weather and still have as many when I come out as when I went in :-)/. It also means I have current first aid and CPR training in case someone in the group gets injured (I'm happy to say I've never had to use either, except on Recussi-Annie, the CPR dummy). If you're in New York state and want to see the mountains, look me up. I'm usually happy to go if I have the time (let me know in advance and I might be able to clear the time.)

I'm working on becoming an Adirondack 46'er. Being a 46'er means one has climbed all 46 Adirondack mountains originally surveyed to be over 4,000 feet. I've done 19 so far, most in the fall or winter.


I've also tried a lot of other outdoor stuff that I never dreamed of until I joined the outing club. Since joining, I've become interested in caving, rock climbing, ice climbing, cross country skiing and snow shoeing. These things are all fun, and none of them are as hard as they sound.

Better give you some demographic information to satisfy your curiosity in that area. I'm 6' 1", 30, 200 lbs, and if your browser can't load the GIFs, brown hair and brown eyes.

I'm interested in getting my Private Pilots license at some point in the future. I have 18-19 hours in a Cessna-150, but couldn't continue on a graduate students budget. I eventually want to get my license and a small plane (probably a 4 place/seat one like a Cessna-172), but that'll have to wait for a real job :-)\. Flying is fun, and I recommend that anyone who's ever thought of trying it call their nearest flying club, locate an instructor and tell the instructor you are interested/curious about flying and would like to take an hour lesson to see if you like it.

I'm obviously into computers. You'll find me surfing around IRC and occasionally reading the newsgroups. I'm also getting into homepage design consultation. If you know anyone who needs a homepage designed, send them my way!
