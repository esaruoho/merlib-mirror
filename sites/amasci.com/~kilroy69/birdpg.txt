A Bird In The Hand 

While on my ways to Phelps Mountain, I stopped to eat lunch at the Marcy Dam 
area, a popular spot in the Adirondacks. This bird decided I might be the 
type to share my lunch and landed on my hand to grab a few bites. I managed to

get my camera out, making lots of noise and moving to do so and snap this 
shot. After I took the picture, I ate the bite to the right of the bird and
it didn't fly away until after I was moving the sandwich away from my mouth.
Trusting little fellow :-)\.
