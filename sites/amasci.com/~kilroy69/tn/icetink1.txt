Out of The Adirondacks, Here I am Ice Climbing with the outing club. 
It's fun and not as hard as it sounds. This odd activity involves
wearing "crampons" (Huge Spikes for your boots) and using an ice axe or two.
You're "top-roped" so you're never in any danger unless the ice is weak and 
collapses (don't climb on warm days :-). This is Tinkers Falls, about half 
an hour south of Syracuse.
