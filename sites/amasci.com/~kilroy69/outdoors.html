It's Great To Be Outside!

Time to get out and enjoy the fresh air. I can't bring you the fresh air, but I can take you to some nice spots. Eventually, I want you to be able to "hike" the Adirondacks here (High Peaks Region), but that's a lot of hiking and a lot of scanning from now.

This is the Marcy Dam area, perhaps the most visited area in the Adirondacks. It's an easy walk, 45 minutes at a leisurely pace. Lots of people come here just for the walk, many others are continuing on. This leads through to Mt. Marcy, the highest point in NY State, Phelps Peak, Avalanche Pass, Mt Colden, and so many others I won't list them here. Mt. Colden is the one in the distance with the "slides" (bare rock patches) showing.

This was a Stop on the way to Wright Peak and Algonquin. Abous 2.3 miles in, it's a good spot to eat lunch. Pretty little waterfall (semi-frozen here) to sit by. Don't forget to wear your Gloves :-)\.

This strange looking tree was up near treeline on Wright Peak. A visiting Scholar with a better camera took this on one of my outing club ventures.

Out of The Adirondacks, Here I am Ice Climbing with the outing club. It's fun and not as hard as it sounds. This odd activity involves wearing "crampons" (Huge Spikes for your boots) and using an ice axe or two. You're "top-roped" so you're never in any danger unless the ice is weak and collapses (don't climb on warm days). This is Tinkers Falls, about half an hour south of Syracuse.
Getting Closer to Home Here. This is Deer River about 15 minutes from where I grew up. Up the road a little ways at a larger campsite is a popular party spot.
