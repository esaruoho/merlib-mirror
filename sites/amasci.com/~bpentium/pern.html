Anne McCaffrey - Author of the highly acclaimed Dragon series (Dragon Riders of Pern), Anne is one of the best known SF writers today. Anne lives at Dragonhold, in the hills of County Wicklow, Ireland, with assorted horses, cats, and a dog. She says, "I have green eyes, silver hair, and freckles - the rest changes without notice". Anne has been a long time supporter of Octocon and Irish SF fandom. She was one of the original sponsors of the Irish Science Fiction Association.
Another photo of Anne!

Dragon Riders of Pern - Fantasy series on an alien world where dragons and humans bond for life, and both are united against the menace of "thread" (little bundles of destruction that fall from space every so often. Dragons have the ability to "blink" in and out of time and space to transport anywhere in time or and place.
