Description:

For Sale: Factory Sealed, monaural (mono), #3 (Scranton, Pennsylvania pressing) original copy of The Beatles "Yesterday"...and Today album. Released in 1966 and quickly withdrawn because of "shock and awe" by the public due to the graphics on the front, makes this album an all-time favorite collectible of Beatle fans and collectors.

This copy is in mint condition other than a slightly "knocked" corner on the back right-bottom next to the RIAA number and also seen slightly on the front side, same corner.

The shrink is tight, with breather holes and is complete. No rips. The spline is in excellent shape.
