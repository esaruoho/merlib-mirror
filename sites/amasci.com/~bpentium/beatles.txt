 Beatlology is an exciting Beatles Fanzine dedicated to serious
 Beatles collectors and fans. Published in Canada six times a year by veteran
 collector Andrew Croft, the colorful Beatlology Magazine is the culmination of
 the efforts of some of the top collector's from around the world. Some of the
 extensive articles published so far have been on autographs, ticket collecting,
 the Butcher cover, promotional LP stickers and collecting on the internet, and
 that's just the beginning. The latest issue ( Vol 7 - #6, July/August 2005 ) I N C L U D E S : Comic

 Celebrating the release of a new DVD that showcases some of the best in rare
 Beatles memorabilia. By Mark Naboshek Also: rec.music.beatles - by its founding
 member Pete Nash's Diary of Recent News and Events... and more! Subscription,

 Beatle Reference
 Books by Bruce Spizer. These are a must for your
 collection. Bruce
 Spizer is a first generation Beatles fan and a life-long native of New Orleans,
 Louisiana. He has an extensive Beatles collection, concentrating primarily on
 United States and Canadian first issue records, record promotional items, press
 kits and concert posters. A taxman by day, Bruce is a board certified tax
 attorney and certified public accountant. A paperback writer by night, he is
 the author of the critically acclaimed books, The Beatles Records on
 Vee-Jay, The Beatles' Story on Capitol Records parts 1 & 2, The Beatles on
 Apple Records, The Beatles Solo on Apple Records, and THE BEATLES ARE
 COMING! The Birth of Beatlemania in America . Bruce's articles on the
 Beatles are featured regularly in Beatlology Magazine and Beatlefan.
 Visit his site at
 beatle.net
