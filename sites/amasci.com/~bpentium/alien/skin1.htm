Eye Candy 4000 Released 10/30/2000
Eye Candy 4000 was released to the world today! This major upgrade contains five new special effects filters -- Marble, Wood, Drip, Melt and Corona, along with dramatic improvements that make Eye Candy more practical and easy-to-use than ever before.
Eye Candy 4000 works great with the new Photoshop 6.0.
To order the upgrade for only $69.00 or buy the full version of Eye Candy 4000 for only $169.00, visit http://www.digitalriver.com/AlienSkin/EyeCandy4000. (both CD's and digital downloads are available)
Users familiar with the previous version of Eye Candy will see that all of the former Eye Candy 3 filters have been strengthened with new features. Many have been completely reworked in dazzling ways. Chrome now uses reflection maps to create incredibly realistic metal effects, Shadowlab and Star have new in-preview controls and Fire and Smoke have been dramatically improved, to name only a few of the changes.

In addition, Eye Candy 4000 includes these powerful features:
  • Simple, intuitive interface with clear, understandable controls
  • Huge previews that include underlying layers
  • New Bevel Profile and Color Gradient Editors
  • Unlimited "undo" capability for all filters
  • Seamless tiling for texture effects
  • Hundreds of presets that create complex effects instantly
  • Settings that are tradable via email and across platforms
  • Real-world parameter units for creating resolution-independent effects
  • Maximum support for RGB, CMYK, and other image modes
