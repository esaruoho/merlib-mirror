 In
the Beginning 
 I was born Robert W. York in
Wyoming, Michigan on the
 26th of May in 1955.
At five we moved to Jenison, Michigan and my parents still currently reside
there. Jenison is about 20 east of Lake Michigan. I
graduated from Grandville Highschool in 1973. 

 Joined the United States Airforce 
 In August 1975 I enlisted in the Airforce to see the world and
ended up in Tacoma, Washington at McChord 
 Airforce Base for 4 years.
While in the Airforce I became a Heating Systems Specialist. I installed
furnaces, boilers, fuel tanks and maintained same. I spent two years doing that
and then moved to the Central Heating Plant for another two years. After my 4
years were up I enlisted in Civil Service and have spent 20 more years in the
Central Heating Plant working with high-pressure steam boilers to supply steam
heating to the base through 24 miles of distribution lines.
 Here I am (1996) at work sitting at the console of our
Bailey Network (90) control console and me showing my family around at work.
