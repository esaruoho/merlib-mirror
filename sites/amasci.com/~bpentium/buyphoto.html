FILM FAVORITES, PO Box 133, MCW, Canton, OK 73724 (405) 886-3358 Founded in 1919, they have photos from the silents through the 1980's: serials, westerns, comedies, short-subjects, cartoons, documentaries, dramas and mysteries. Just write and tell them which movies are your favorites. They'll send a list of the many different scenes they have available for each film on your list. They request you limit your inquiry to no more than 10 titles per letter. Or tell-them your favorite movie star and they'll let you know what they have available on that star from movies, portraits and publicity shots. PLEASE INCLUDE A STAMPED, SELF-ADDRESSED ENVELOPE. 1991 prices were: 5x7 = $1.50; 8x10 = $3.50; 11x14= $6.50.)
