Greetings Beatles fans. With the advent of the Beatles Anthology comes a re-emergence of Beatlemania affectionately entitled Beatlemania II. I, as a long time Beatles fanatic am ecstatic about this new interest in the Beatles. I am meeting both old long time collectors and new, much younger persons that weren't around during the 60's. It's rare that one muscial group can span the generations as the Beatles have.

With all of the interest in the new Beatles wave I've compiled some links to the better Anthology related sites on the Internet. I think Anthology will basically be a 1996 thing so I don't know how long these sites will be available on the Internet. But for the time being, please enjoy the new wave.
