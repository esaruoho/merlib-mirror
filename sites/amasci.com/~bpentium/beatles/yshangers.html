Yellow Submarine Hangers

Rare Beatles Yellow Submarine Hangers MIP from 1968 made by Henderson-Hoggard Inc. King Features-Saba Films. These are the SUPER RARE and hard to find Closet Carnival Yellow Submarine Picture Hangers. Pictured are both sides of the one package. They came 4 hangers to one sealed package so two of the hangers are not seen. The dimensions of the package is 17" by 17" approx.
