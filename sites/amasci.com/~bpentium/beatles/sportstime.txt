 The Sportstime Beatles Redemption Cards 

 These are the rarest four cards out of the whole set of
the 1996 Sportstime Beatles Trading card set. The back of the card states that
you send the card to Sportstime with $2.95 and they'll return to you a 24kt Gold
Signature card. Click on the gold part of Ringo's card to
view the back of the card.
