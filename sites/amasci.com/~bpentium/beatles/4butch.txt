4 Pacific Northwest Butchers 

 Robert York, Steve Leicht,
Mitch McGeary and Roy Morlen "Click on Mitch's
Butcher for a closeup" 

 Four
 Sealed Yesterday and Today 1st State
Mono Butcher Covers 

 Owned by four Western Washington collectors all
living within 20 miles from each other. This photo was taken September 6th 1997.
The Butchers were acquired during a period of about 20 years, Steve's just
acquired a few days ago and we thought it would be fun to take a group photo
while holding these rare beauties. There are two other known copies in the
Pacific Northwest within driving distance. Possibly a future photo of all 6 is
forthcoming. 
 The two butchers on the left were pressed at the Scranton, Pennsylvania
(#3) plant and the two on the right were pressed at the Los Angeles, California
Plant (#6).
