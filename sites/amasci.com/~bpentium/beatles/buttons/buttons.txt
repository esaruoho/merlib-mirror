 1964 Gumball Machine 

 This set of buttons from the Green Duck Company in Chicago, Illinois were
issued in 1964. They were made to be given out in 1&#162; gumball machines. The
center tag was a sticker that was adhered to the outside of the machine. This
is a full set of 9 different buttons. Another interesting item is this
 1964 flyer ad for ordering these buttons.
