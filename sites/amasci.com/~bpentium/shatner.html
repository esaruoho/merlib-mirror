Star Trek Convention: William Shatner

#8 of 300 Limited Editon
Autograph Ticket

On May 4th 1997 I had the priveledge of meeting William Shatner at the Portland, Ore Star Trek Convention. At this convention Mr. Shatner agreed to sign 300 autographs only. William Shatner doesn't sign very much and I wanted to get in on this signing. I also had a front row, middle stage seat where I was able to take a full roll of film. It was a wonderful experience.
It was one of the best presentations I've been to.
