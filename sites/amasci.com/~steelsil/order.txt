At Steel Silhouettes, we do decorative
steel cutting. We then make the cut steel into many different
products. The cutting process is computerized. Each design has a
minimum size. This minimum size is the element that controls
whether a given design can be used in a given product. We refer
to artwork as designs, and the objects into which they are made
as products. Thus, a moose is a design, and a plaque, hook, or
lamp is a product. 

 Each design has a minimum size, below which we cannot cut it.
We can, however, readily scale designs up to a maximum of 4'x10'.
Even larger products can be made by cutting the design in pieces
and welding the pieces together. Minimum size varies with
individual figures. Black bears can be 4" high, but our deer
must be at least 7" high. 

 Thus, 23" plaque designs can be made into 46" diameter
plaques, but not into 15" diameter plaques. 8" diameter plaque
designs, however, can be made into 8" plaques, wall sconces,
can-style lights, 8" scene style hooks, etc. We can offer an
amazing and sometimes baffling diversity of products due to
computerization, but each design has a minimum size which cannot
be reduced. Size and shape are therefore our only limitations in
adapting artwork to different products.
