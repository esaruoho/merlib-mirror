Steel Silhouettes

We make art for walls and fences, lightswitch plates, welcome signs, coat hooks and coat racks, votive candle holders, wall sconces, lamps, and chandeliers.

Our Western, Southwestern and American wildlife products are often placed in log cabins, lodges, restaurants, ranches, vacation homes, and any other place where a theme decor is desired. Popular figures include: Moose, bear, wolf, elk, deer, log cabin, heron, "End of Trail", cowboy, mustang, kokopelli, gecko, etc.
