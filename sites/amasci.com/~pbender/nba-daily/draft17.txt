
2017 Draft 

June 22, 2017

Draft-day trades:
a) TRADE: Chicago traded Jimmy Butler and the draft rights to #16 Justin
Patton to Minnesota for Zach LaVine, Kris Dunn, and the draft rights to #7
Lauri Markkanen.

b) TRADE: Sacramento traded the draft rights to #10 Zach Collins to
Portland for the draft rights to #15 Justin Jackson and #20 Harry Giles.

c) TRADE: Denver traded the draft rights to #13 Donovan Mitchell to Utah
for Trey Lyles and the draft rights to #24 Tyler Lydon.

d) Orlando traded the draft rights to #25 Anzejs Pasecniks to Philadelphia
for a conditional 1st round draft pick and 2020 2nd round draft pick.

e) The LA Lakers traded D'Angelo Russell and Timofey Mozgov to Brooklyn
for Brook Lopez and the draft rights to #27 Kyle Kuzma.

f) The LA Lakers traded the draft rights to #28 Tony Bradley to Utah for
the draft rights to #30 Josh Hart and the draft rights to #42 Thomas
Bryant.

g) Charlotte traded the draft rights to #31 Frank Jackson to New Orleans
for the draft rights to $40 Dwayne Bacon and cash.

h) Orlando traded the draft rights to #35 Ivan Rabb to Memphis for a 2019
2nd round draft pick.

i) Chicago traded the draft rights to #38 Jordan Bell to Golden State for
cash.

j) Houston traded the draft rights to $45 Dillon Brooks to Memphis for a
2018 2nd round draft pick.

k) Milwaukee traded the draft rights to #48 Sindarius Thornwell to the LA
Clippers for cash.

l) New Orleans traded the draft rights to #52 Edmond Sumner to Indiana for
cash.
