
June 30

1999 NBA draft results

[Note: there was at least one trade (Indiana sending Antonio Davis to
Toronto for the rights to #5 Jonathan Bender) that will not formally be
announced until August 1, when the salary cap situation for one or both of
the teams changes (base year compensation goes away for a player, the
salary cap goes up, ...).]

a) TRADE: Seattle traded the rights to #13 Corey Maggette, Dale Ellis,
Billy Owens, and Don MacLean to Orlando for Horace Grant, a 2001 2nd round
draft pick, and a 2002 2nd round draft pick.

b) TRADE: Golden State traded the rights to #21 Jeff Foster to Indiana for
the rights to #26 Vonteego Cummings and a future 1st round draft pick.
The pick is lottery protected.

c) TRADE: Atlanta traded the rights to #27 Jumaine Jones to Philadelphia 
for a future 1st round draft pick.  The pick is lottery protected.

d) TRADE: San Antonio trade the rights to #29 Leon Smith to Dallas for the 
rights to #40 Gordan Giricek and Dallas' 2000 2nd round draft pick.

e) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #42 Louis Bullock to Orlando for
cash considerations.

f) TRADE: Atlanta traded the rights to #52 Roberto Bergersen to Portland 
for cash considerations.
