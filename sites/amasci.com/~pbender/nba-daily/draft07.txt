
2007 NBA Draft

The draft took place June 28.

a) TRADE: Orlando traded the #54 pick to Houston for cash.
[This trade came before the draft-day trade deadline - all other trades
were announced during the draft and are called trading the rights to a
pick instead of trading the pick itself.]

b) TRADE: Seattle traded Ray Allen and the draft rights to #35 Glen Davis
to Boston for Wally Szczerbiak, Delonte West, and the draft rights to #5
Jeff Green.

c) TRADE: Golden State traded Jason Richardson and the draft rights to 
#36 Jermareo Davidson to Charlotte for the draft rights to #8 Brandan
Wright.

d) Miami traded the draft rights to #20 Jason Smith to Philadelphia for
the draft rights to #21 Daequan Cook, a 2009 2nd round draft pick, and
cash.

e) Phoenix traded the rights to #24 Rudy Fernandez to Portland for $3
million.

f) Philadelphia traded the draft rights to #30 Petteri Koponen to Portland
for the draft rights to #42 Derrick Byars and cash.

g) Miami traded the draft rights to #39 Stanko Barac to Indiana for a 2009
2nd round draft pick.

h) TRADE: Orlando traded the draft rights to #44 Reyshawn Terry to Dallas
for the draft rights to #60 Milovan Rakovic and cash.

i) TRADE: Portland traded Zach Randolph, Fred Jones, Dan Dickau, and the
draft rights to #53 Demetris Nichols to New York for Steve Francis,
Channing Frye, and a 2008 2nd round draft pick.

j) San Antonio traded the draft rights to #58 Giorgos Pintezis to Toronto
for a 2008 2nd round draft pick.

k) Seattle traded the rights to #31 Carl Landry to Houston for a future
2nd round draft pick and cash.
