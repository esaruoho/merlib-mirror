
2009 NBA Draft

The draft took place June 25.


a) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #18 Ty Lawson to Denver for a
future 1st round draft pick.

b) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #24 B.J. Mullen to Oklahoma City for
the rights to #25 Rodrigue Beaubois and a future 2nd round draft pick.

c) TRADE: The LA Lakers traded the rights to #29 Toney Douglas to New York
for a future 2nd round draft pick and cash.

d) TRADE: Sacramento traded the rights to #31 Jeff Pendergraph to Portland
for Sergio Rodriguez, the rights to #38 Jon Brockman, and cash.

e) TRADE: Washington traded the rights to #32 Jermaine Taylor to Houston 
for cash.

f) TRADE: Denver traded the rights to #34 Sergio Llull to Houston for
cash.

g) TRADE: The LA Lakers traded the rights to #42 Patrick Beverley to Miami
for a future 2nd round draft pick and cash.

h) TRADE: Miami traded the rights to #43 Marcus Thornton to New Orleans for 
2 future 2nd round draft picks.

i) TRADE: Detroit traded the rights to #44 Chase Budinger to Houston for a
future 2nd round draft pick and cash.

j) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #45 Nick Calathes to Dallas for
the lowest of Portland's 2010 2nd round draft picks.

k) TRADE: Charlotte traded the rights to #54 Robert Vaden to Oklahoma City
for cash.
