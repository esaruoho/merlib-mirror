
2013 NBA Draft

The draft occurred June 27.


a) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #9 Trey Burke to Utah for the
rights to #14 Shabazz Muhammad and the rights to #21 Gorgui Dieng.

b) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #13 Kelly Olynyk to Boston for the
rights to #16 Lucas Nogueira and two future 2nd round draft picks.

c) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #16 Lucas Nogueira, the rights to
#44 Mike Muscala, and Jared Cunningham to Atlanta for the rights to #18
Shane Larkin and cash.

d) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #26 Andre Roberson and Malcolm
Lee to Golden State for a 2014 2nd round draft pick and cash.

e) TRADE: Golden State traded the rights to #26 Andre Roberson to Oklahoma
City for the rights to #29 Archie Goodwin and cash.

f) TRADE: Denver traded the rights to #27 Rudy Gobert to Utah for the
rights to #46 Erick Green and cash.

g) TRADE: Golden State traded the rights to #29 Archie Goodwin and Malcolm
Lee to Phoenix for the rights to #30 Nemanja Nedovic.

h) TRADE: Cleveland traded the rights to #31 Allen Crabbe to Portland for
two future 2nd round draft picks.

i) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #35 Glen Rice to Washington
for the rights to #38 Nate Wolters and the rights to #54 Arsalan Kazemi.

j) TRADE: Portland traded the rights to #40 Grant Jerrett to Oklahoma City
for cash.

k) TRADE: Atlanta traded the rights to #50 James Ennis to Miami for a
future 2nd round draft pick.

l) TRADE: Indiana traded the rights to #53 Colton Iverson to Boston for
cash.

m) TRADE: Memphis traded the rights to #55 Joffrey Lauvergne and Darrell
Arthur to Denver for Kosta Koufos.

n) TRADE: Atlanta traded the rights to #47 Raul Neto to Utah for a 2015
2nd round draft pick (originally from Brooklyn).

o) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #38 Nate Wolters to Milwaukee
for the rights to #43 Ricky Ledo and a future 2nd round draft pick.

p) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #43 Ricky Ledo to Dallas for a
2014 2nd round draft pick (originally from Brooklyn via Boston).

[Note that the last 3 trades did not technically occur until Friday, but
I'm including them here as they were agreed to Thursday night.]
