
2010 NBA Draft

The draft took place June 24.

a) TRADE: Portland traded Martell Webster to Minnesota for Ryan Gomes and
the right to #16 Luke Babbitt.

b) TRADE: Oklahoma City traded the rights to #18 Eric Bledsoe to the LA
Clippers for a future (protected) 1st round draft pick.

c) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #23 Trevor Booker and the rights
to #56 Hamady Ndiaye to Minnesota for the rights to #30 Lazar Hayward and
the rights to #35 Nemanja Bjelica.

d) TRADE: In a 3-team trade, Atlanta traded the rights to #24 Damion James
and received the rights to #27 Jordan Crawford, New Jersey traded the 
rights to #27 Jordan Crawford and the rights to #31 Tibor Pleiss and 
received the rights to ##24 Damion James, and Oklahoma City traded cash 
and received the rights to #31 Tibor Pleiss.

e) TRADE: Memphis traded the rights to #25 Dominique Jones to Dallas for
$3 million.

f) TRADE: Miami traded the rights to #48 Latavious Williams to Oklahoma
City for a future 2nd round draft pick.

g) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #50 Solomon Alabi to Toronto for a
2013 2nd round draft pick and cash.

h) TRADE: Oklahoma City traded the rights to #51 Magnum Rolle to Indiana
for the rights to #57 Ryan Reid and cash.
