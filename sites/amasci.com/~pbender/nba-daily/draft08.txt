
2008 NBA Draft

The draft took place June 26.


a) TRADE: Minnesota traded Marko Jaric, Antoine Walker, Greg Buckner, and
the draft rights to #3 O.J. Mayo to Memphis for Mike Miller, Brian 
Cardinal, Jason Collins, and the draft rights to #5 Kevin Love.

b) TRADE: New Orleans traded the draft rights to #27 Darrell Arthur to 
Portland for cash.

c) TRADE: In a 3-team trade, Portland traded the draft rights to #27
Darrell Arthur and the draft rights to #33 Joey Dorsey for the draft
rights to #25 Nicolas Batum; Houston traded the draft rights to #25
Nicolas Batum for the draft rights to #28 Donte Green, the draft rights to
#33 Joey Dorsey, and a 2009 2nd round draft pick from Memphis; and Memphis
traded a 2009 2nd round draft pick for the draft rights to #27 Darrell 
Arthur.

d) TRADE: Detroit traded the draft rights to #29 D.J. White to Seattle for
the draft rights to #32 Walter Sharpe and the draft rights to #46 Trent
Plaisted.

e) TRADE: Minnesota traded the draft rights to #34 Mario Chalmers to Miami
for 2 future 2nd round draft picks and cash.

f) TRADE: In a 3-team trade, Chicago traded the draft rights to #39 Sonny
Weems and 2 future 2nd round draft picks for the draft rights to #36 Omer
Asik; Portland traded the draft rights to #36 Omer Asik for a 2009 2nd
round draft pick from Denver and 2 future 2nd round draft picks from 
Chicago; and Denver traded a 2009 2nd round draft pick for the rights to
#39 Sonny Weems.

g) TRADE: San Antonio traded the draft rights to #45 Goran Dragic to
Phoenix for the draft rights to #48 Malik Hairston, Golden State's 2009 
2nd round draft pick (which Phoenix owned), and cash.

h) TRADE: Washington traded the draft rights to #47 Bill Walker to Boston 
for cash.

i) TRADE: Miami traded the draft rights to #52 Darnell Jackson to
Cleveland for a 2009 2nd round draft pick.

j) TRADE: Portland traded the draft rights to #55 Mike Taylor to the LA
Clippers for a 2009 2nd round draft pick.

k) TRADE: Seattle traded the draft rights to #56 Sasha Kaun to Cleveland
for cash.

[Note: There is a widely reported rumored trade of Toronto trading T.J. 
Ford, Rasho Nesterovic, the rights to #17 (Roy Hibbert) to Indiana for 
Jermaine O'Neal and someone else.  That reported trade has not 
officially occurred.]
