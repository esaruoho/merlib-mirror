
June 24

1998 NBA draft results

a) TRADE: Toronto traded the rights to #4 pick Antawn Jamison to Golden
State for the rights to #5 pick Vince Carter and cash.

b) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #6 Robert Traylor to Milwaukee for
the rights to #9 pick Dirk Nowitzki and #19 pick Pat Garrity.

c) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #19 pick Pat Garrity, Bubba Wells,
Martin Muursepp, and an unprotected 1st round draft pick in 1999 to
Phoenix for Steve Nash.

d) TRADE: Utah traded the rights to #29 pick Nazr Mohammed to Philadelphia
for a future 1st round draft pick.  The draft pick is top 15 protected for
1999, top 14 protected for 2000, top 13 protected for 2001, and top #1 and
#2 protected for 2002 and beyond.

e) TRADE: San Antonio traded the rights to #24 pick Felipe Lopez and Carl
Herrera to Vancouver for Antonio Daniels.

f) TRADE: Denver traded the draft rights to #23 pick Tyronn Lue and Tony
Battie to the LA Lakers for Nick Van Exel.

g) TRADE: The LA Lakers traded the rights to #45 Toby Bailey to Phoenix
for future considerations.
