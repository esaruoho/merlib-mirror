
2011 NBA Draft

The draft took place June 23.


a) TRADE: In a 3 team trade, Charlotte traded away Stephen Jackson, Shaun
Livingston, the rights to #19 Tobias Harris and received Corey Maggette,
and the rights to #7 Bismack Miyombo; Milwaukee traded away Corey
Maggette, John Salmons, and the rights to #10 Jimmer Fredette and received
Stephen Jackson, Beno Udrih, Shaun Livingston, and the rights to #19
Tobias Harris; and Sacramento traded away Beno Udrih and the rights to #7
Bismack Biyombo and received John Salmons and the rights to #10 Jimmer
Fredette.

b) TRADE: San Antonio traded George Hill to Indiana for the rights to #15
Kawhi Leonard, the rights to #42 Davis Bertans, and the rights to 2005
draft pick #46 Erazem Lorbek

c) TRADE: Houston traded Brad Miller, the rights to #23 Nikola Mirotic,
the rights to #38 Chandler Parsons, and a future 1st round draft pick
(that belongs to Memphis, lottery protected 2013-2016 else 2017 2nd round
pick) to Minnesota for Jonny Flynn and the rights to #20 Donatas
Motiejunas.  Houston later traded with Minnesota to get back the rights to
#38 Chandler Parson by sending #1.5 million and a protected 2012 2nd round
pick to Minnesota.

d) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights #23 Nikola Mirotic to Chicago for the 
rights to #28 Norris Cole, the rights to #43 Malcolm Lee, and cash. 

e) TRADE: Boston traded the rights to #25 MarShon Brooks to New Jersey for
the rights to #27 JaJuan Johnson and a 2014 2nd round draft pick.

f) TRADE: In a 3 team traded, Dallas traded away the rights to #26 Jordan
Hamilton and the rights to #57 Targuy Ngombo and received Rudy Fernandez
and the rights to 2007 #30 draft pick Petteri Koponen; Denver traded away
Raymond Felton and received Andre Miller, the rights to #26 Jordan
Hamilton, and a future Portland 2nd round draft pick; and Portland traded
away Rudy Fernandez, Andre Miller, the rights to 2007 #30 draft pick
Petteri Koponen, and a future 2nd round draft pick and received Raymond
Felton and the rights to #57 Targuy Ngombo.

g) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #28 Norris Cole to Miami for the
rights to #31 Bojan Bogdanovic.

h) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #31 Bojan Boganoivc to New Jersey
for a future 2nd round draft pick and cash.

i) TRADE: Cleveland traded the rights to #32 Justin Harper to Orlando for 
2 future 2nd round draft picks.

j) TRADE: Charlotte traded the rights to #39 Jeremy Tyler to Golden State
for cash.

k) TRADE: New Orleans traded the rights to #45 Josh Harrellson to New York
for cash.

l) TRADE: The LA Lakers traded the rights to #56 Chukwudiebere Maduabum to
Denver for a future 2nd round draft pick.
