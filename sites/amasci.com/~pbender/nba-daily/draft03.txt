
June 26, 2003

2003 NBA draft results

a) TRADE: Memphis traded the rights to #13 Marcus Banks and the rights to
#27 Kendrick Perkins to Boston for the rights to #16 Troy Bell and the
rights to #20 Dahntay Jones.

b) TRADE: Seattle traded the rights to #41 Willie Green to Philadelphia for
the rights to #50 Paccelis Morlende and cash.

c) TRADE: San Antonio traded the rights to #28 Leandrinho Barbosa to Phoenix
for a future 1st round draft pick.  The pick is top 25 protected for 2004,
top 21 protected for 2005 - 2009, and unprotected for 2010.

d) TRADE: Milwaukee traded the rights to #43 Keith Bogans to Orlando for
cash.

e) TRADE: Chicago traded the rights to #45 Matt Bonner to Toronto for a
future 2nd round draft pick.

f) TRADE: New Jersey traded the rights to #51 Kyle Korver to Philadelphia
for cash.

g) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #57 Xue Yuyang to Denver for Houston's
2004 2nd round draft pick.  Denver had received the Houston pick as part of
the James Posey trade.
