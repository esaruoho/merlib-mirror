
2015 NBA Draft.

a) TRADE: In a 3 team trade, Atlanta traded away the rights to #15 Kelly
Oubre and received Tim Hardaway and 2 future 2nd round draft picks; New 
York traded away Tim Hardaway and received the rights to #19 Jerian 
Grant; and Washington traded away the rights to #19 Jerian Grant and 2 
future 2nd round draft picks and received the rights to #15 Kelly Oubre.

b) TRADE: Portland traded Steve Blake and the rights to #23 Rondae
Hollis-Jefferson to Brooklyn for Mason Plumlee and the rights to #41 Pat 
Connaughton.

c) TRADE: Cleveland traded the rights to #24 Tyus Jones to Minnesota for
the rights to #31 Cedi Osman, the rights to #36 Rakeem Christmas, and a 
2019 2nd round draft pick.

d) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #35 Guillermo Hernangomez to
New York for 2 future 2nd round draft picks and cash.

e) TRADE: Charlotte traded the rights to #39 Juan Vaulet to Brooklyn for a
2018 2nd round draft pick, a 2019 2nd round draft pick, and cash.

f) TRADE: Memphis traded Jon Leuer to Phoenix for the rights to #44 Andrew
Harrison.

g) TRADE: Toronto traded Greivis Vasquez to Milwaukee for the rights to
#46 Norman Powell and a protected 2017 1st round draft pick.

h) TRADE: Utah traded the rights to #54 Daniel Diez to Portland for cash.

g) TRADE: New Orleans traded the rights to #56 Branden Dawson to the LA
Clippers for cash.
