
2014 NBA Draft

The draft took place June 26.

a) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #10 Elfrid Payton to Orlando
for the rights to #12 Dario Saric, returning the future 1st round draft
pick that Philadelphia owed Orlando, and a 2015 2nd round draft pick.

b) TRADE: Denver traded Anthony Randolph and the rights to #11 Doug
McDermott to Chicago for the rights to #16 Jusuf Nurkic, the rights to #19
Gary Harris, and a 2015 2nd round draft pick.

c) TRADE: Charlotte traded the rights to #24 Shabazz Napier to Miami for
the rights to #26 P.J. Hairston, the rights to #55 Semaj Christon, a 2019
2nd round draft pick, and cash.

d) TRADE: Utah traded the rights to #35 Jarnell Stokes to Memphis for a
2015 2nd round draft pick.

e) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #44 Markel Brown to Brooklyn for
cash.

f) TRADE: Washington traded the rights to #46 Jordan Clarkson to the LA
Lakers for cash.

g) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #47 Russ Smith to New Orleans
for the rights to Pierre Jackson (2013 draft pick, #42).

h) TRADE: Milwaukee traded the rights to #48 Lamar Patterson to Atlanta
for a 2015 2nd round draft pick.

i) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #54 Nemanja Dangubic to San
Antonio for the rights to #58 Jordan McRae and the rights to #60 Cory
Jefferson.

j) TRADE: Charlotte traded the rights to #55 Semaj Christon to Oklahoma
City for cash.

k) TRADE: Orlando traded Arron Afflalo to Denver for Evan Fournier and the
rights to #56 Roy Marble.

l) TRADE: Indiana traded the rights to #57 Louis Labeyrie to New York for
cash.

m) TRADE: Toronto traded the rights to #59 Xavier Thames to Brooklyn for
cash.

n) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #60 Cory Jefferson to Brooklyn
for cash.
