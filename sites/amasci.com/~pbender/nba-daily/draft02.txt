
June 26, 2002

2002 NBA draft results

a) TRADE: Denver traded Antonio McDyess, the rights to #25 Frank Williams,
and a 2003 2nd round draft pick to New York for Marcus Camby, Mark
Jackson, and the rights to #7 Maybyner Hilario.

b) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #16 Jiri Welsch to Golden
State for a 2005 1st round draft pick and highly likely a 2nd round draft
pick in 2004.  The 1st round pick is the one that Philadelphia traded away
as part of the Derrick Coleman 3-team trade.  Philadelphia will receive a
2004 1st round pick if Golden State has one of the top 3 records in the 
league next season, else they will recieve a 2004 2nd round pick.

c) TRADE: Orlando traded the rights to #18 Curtis Borchardt to Utah for
the rights to #19 Ryan Humphrey and the rights to #47 Jamal Sampson.

d) TRADE: The LA Lakers traded Lindsey Hunter and the rights to #27 Chris
Jefferies to Toronto for Tracy Murray and the rights to #20 Kareem Rush.

e) TRADE: Philadelphia traded Speedy Claxton to San Antonio for Mark
Bryant, the rights to #26 John Salmons, and the rights to #57 Randy
Holcomb.

f) TRADE: Sacramento traded the rights to #28 Dan Dickau to Atlanta for a
future 1st round draft pick.

g) TRADE: The LA Clippers traded the rights to #41 Mario Kasun to Orlando
for future considerations.

h) TRADE: Memphis traded Nick Anderson and the rights to #46 Matt Barnes
to Cleveland for Wesley Person.

i) TRADE: Orlando traded the rights to #47 Jamal Sampson to Milwaukee for
the rights to Rashard Griffith (#38 pick in 1994).

j) TRADE: Milwaukee traded the rights to #48 Chris Owens to Memphis for a
2003 2nd round draft pick.

k) TRADE: Philadelphia traded 2004 and 2006 2nd round picks to Atlanta for
the rights to Efthimios Rentzias (#23 pick in 1996).
