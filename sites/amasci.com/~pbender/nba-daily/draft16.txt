
2016 NBA draft 

Draft-day trades:
a) Sacramento traded the draft rights to #8 Marquese Chriss to Phoenix for
the draft rights to #13 Georgios Papagiannis, the draft rights to #28 Skal
Labissiere, the draft rights to 2014 pick (#27) Bogdan Bogdanovic, and a
2020 2nd round draft pick.

b) Oklahoma City traded Serge Ibaka to Orlando for Victor Oladipo, Ersan
Ilyasova, and the draft rights #11 Domantas Sabonis.

c) Boston traded the draft rights to #31 Deyonta Davis and the draft
rights to #35 Rade Zagorac to Memphis for a protected 2019 1st round draft
pick.

d) The LA Clippers traded the draft rights to #33 Cheick Diallo to New
Orleans for the draft rights to #39 David Michineau and the draft rights
to #40 Diamond Stone.

e) Milwaukee traded the draft rights to Patrick McCaw to Golden State for
cash.

f) Utah traded the draft rights to #42 Isaiah Whitehead to Brooklyn for
the draft rights to #55 Marcus Paige and cash.

g) Orlando traded the draft rights to #47 Jake Layman to Portland for a
2019 2nd round draft pick and cash.

h) Atlanta traded the draft rights to #54 Kay Felder to Cleveland for
cash.

i) Denver traded the draft rights to #56 Daniel Hamilton to Oklahoma Cty
for cash.
