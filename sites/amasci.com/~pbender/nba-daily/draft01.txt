
June 27, 2001

2001 NBA draft results

a) TRADE: The LA Clippers traded the rights to #2 Tyson Chandler and Brian
Skinner to Chicago for Elton Brand.

b) TRADE: New Jersey traded the rights to #7 Eddie Griffin to Houston for
the rights to #13 Richard Jefferson, the rights to #18 Jason Collins, and
the rights to #23 Brandon Armstrong.

c) TRADE: Cleveland traded the rights to #20 Brendan Haywood to Orlando
for Michael Doleac.

d) TRADE: Orlando traded the rights to #32 Omar Cook to Denver as part of
completing the 2000 Ron Mercer trade.

e) TRADE: Atlanta traded the rights to #34 Terence Morris to Houston for a
future 1st round draft pick.  The pick is the pick Detroit owes Houston
and can occur between 2002 to 2004 at Detroit's choice.

f) TRADE: Seattle traded the rights to #42 Bobby Simmons to Washington for
the rights to Predrag Drobnjak (1997 #49 pick).

[Note: There is a trade that will be consummated July 18 believed to be 
Vancouver trading Shareef Abdur-Rahim and the rights to #27 Jamaal Tinsley
to Atlanta for the rights to #3 Pau Gasol, Lorenzen Wright, and Brevin 
Knight.  Atlanta has already (but not officially until July 18) traded the
rights to #27 Jamaal Tinsley to Indiana for a lottery protected future 1st
round draft pick.  Tinsley can not participate in Indiana's summer camp
until after July 18.]
