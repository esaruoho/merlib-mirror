
2012 NBA Draft

The draft took place June 28.


a) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #17 Tyler Zeller and Kelenna
Azubuike to Cleveland for the rights to #24 Jared Cunningham, the rights
to #33 Bernard James, and the rights to #34 Jae Crowder.

b) TRADE: Miami traded the rights to #27 Arnett Moultrie to Philadelphia
for the rights to #45 Justin Hamilton and a future 1st round draft pick.

c) TRADE: Sacramento traded the rights to #36 Orlando Johnson to Indiana
for cash.

d) TRADE: Portland traded the rights to #41 Tyshawn Taylor to New Jersey 
for cash.

e) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #54 Tornike Shengelia to New
Jersey for cash.

f) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #55 Darius Johnson-Odom to the LA
Lakers for cash.
