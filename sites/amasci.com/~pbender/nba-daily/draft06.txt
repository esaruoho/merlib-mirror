
The 2006 NBA draft took place.  There were 15 draft-day trades.
2006 NBA draft results  

a) TRADE: Chicago traded the rights to #2 LaMarcus Aldridge and a 2007
2nd round draft pick to Portland for the rights to #4 Tyrus Thomas and
Viktor Khryapa.
b) TRADE: Portland traded Sebstian Telfair, Theo Ratliff, and a 2008 2nd
round draft pick to Boston for Dan Dickau, Raef LaFrentz, and the rights
to #7 Randy Foye.
c) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #6 Brandon Roy to Portland for
the rights to #7 Randy Foye and cash.
d) TRADE: Philadelphia traded the rights to #13 Thabo Sefolosha to Chicago
for the rights to #16 Rodney Carney, a 2007 2nd round draft pick, and
$1 million.
e) TRADE: Phoenix traded the rights to #21 Rajon Rondo, Brian Grant, and
cash to Boston for Cleveland's 1st round draft pick (10 protected in 
2007).
f) TRADE: Phoenix traded the rights to #27 Sergio Rodriguez to Portland
for $3 million.
g) TRADE: Portland traded the rights to #31 James White to Indiana for the
rights to #45 Alexander Johnson, a 2nd round draft pick in 2007, and a 2nd
round draft pick in 2008.
h) TRADE: Minnesota traded the rights to #37 Bobby Jones to Philadelphia
for a future 2nd round draft pick and cash.
i) TRADE: Orlando traded the rights to #44 Lior Eliyahu to Houston for
cash.
j) TRADE: Portland traded the rights to #45 Alexander Johnson to Memphis
for a future 2nd round draft pick.
k) TRADE: Denver traded the rights to #49 Leon Powe to Boston for a 2007
2nd round draft pick.
l) TRADE: The LA Lakers traded the rights to #51 Check Samb to Detroit for
Maurice Evans.
m) TRADE: Toronto traded the rights to #56 Edin Bavcic to Philadelphia for
cash.
n) TRADE: Dallas traded the rights to #58 J.R. Pinnock to the LA Lakers
for Miami's 2nd round draft pick in 2007.
o) TRADE: San Antonio traded the rights to #59 Damir Markota to Milwaukee
for a 2007 2nd round draft pick (the better of the 2 2007 2nd round picks 
that Milwaukees owns).
