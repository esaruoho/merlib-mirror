 Here are some selected pictures from the hikes described on my hiking
page. There are 132 pictures here, so it will take a little bit of time to
load. You can also go to the index which is an
index listing (doesn't bring up the pictures) and bring up the photos
individually or by state. These photos were taken with a basic 35-mm 
point-and-shoot camera. Anyone can take good pictures - just take your
time, look for the best angle, notice everything that is in the picture
frame, watch that thumb and hold the camera steady, use good film and good 
processing, notice where the sun is at (best when it is behind you), and 
pray for good weather. Enjoy!
