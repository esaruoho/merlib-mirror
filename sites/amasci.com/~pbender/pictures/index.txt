 Here are some selected pictures from the hikes described on my hiking
page. Click on the individual name to see the picture or click on the state
name to see all the pictures for that state or click on the All link to see
all the pictures (it will take some time to load). 
These photos were taken with a basic 35-mm point-and-shoot camera. Anyone 
can take good pictures - just take your time, look for the best angle, 
notice everything that is in the picture frame, watch that thumb and hold 
the camera steady, use good film and good processing, notice where the sun 
is at (best when it is behind you), and pray for good weather. Enjoy!
