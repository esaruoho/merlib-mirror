All-Star Saturday Night results 

In a change, the first round of all events had the 3 players (2 teams for
Shooting Stars) for each conference compete against each other and the top
East player compete against the the top West player in the final round.
They also an East/West competition where they combined the East/West
totals in the first round and the East/West winner would get a certain
amount of points and then in the finals the East/West winner would get a
certain amount of points and the total of the points East/West winner
would have $350,000 donated to a specified charity and the loser's
charity would receive $150,000.
