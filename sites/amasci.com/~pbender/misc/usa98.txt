
USA Basketball invited 29 college, CBA, and Americans playing in European
leagues to compete for the 12 roster spots on the 1998 USA team.  The
tryout camp takes place July 8-14.  The roster was reduced to 16 players
on July 11.  The 12 man roster was selected July 24.  Mateen Cleaves
sprained his left ankle July 26 and Trajan Langdon was named to replace
Cleaves.  The World Championship tournament starts July 29 in Greece.
