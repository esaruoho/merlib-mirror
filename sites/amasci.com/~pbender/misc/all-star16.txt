Slam Dunk Contest
[2 dunks each round, maximum 3 attempts, 5 former players judge dunks]

  Aaron Gordon (Orl)    45  49c = 94
  Andre Drummond (Det)  36  39  = 75
  Will Barton (Den)     44  30  = 74 (did not complete 2nd dunk)

a = lob, wrap behind back, reverse dunk
b = lob pass, free throw line jump
c = jump over mascot, between legs dunk

Finals (was supposed to be just 2 dunks, but kept going until tie broken)
  LaVine    50a  50b  50c  50d
  Gordon    50e  50f  50g  47h

a = lob, one-hand grab and dunk
b = free throw line jump, windmill finish
c = lob behind backboard, between the legs reverse dunk
d = free throw line jump, between the legs dunk
e = spinning mascot holding ball, grab ball and one-hand glamour dunk
f = ball on mascot's head, jump over mascot, ball under both legs,
    one-hand slam [best dunk of the night]
g = ball off side of backboard, windmill dunk
h = double-clutch to ankles and back up over the head dunk
