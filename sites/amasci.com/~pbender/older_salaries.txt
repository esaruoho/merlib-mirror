
"Where can I find older salary information" is the most often question 
asked.  I do not have older salary information and I do not know where 
you can easily find it.  You can try looking in past issues of the Dallas
Morning News - David Moore did the salary listings fairly regularly, but I
do not know how far back he did it.  I also don't have the salaries for
89-90 or 86-87.

Another question is where do I get the salary information from - if there
is a source, it is noted at the top of each file.  I don't have further
information available about the sources (some wish to remain anonymous).

I do not have any other salary information available than what is available
on my home page and the Mavs salary listing on my Mavs page.  I also do not
have the salaries in a different format (ie, spreadsheet).  I do not know 
where you can find salary information for other sports.

If you wish to use the data in research papers, feel free to do so.  My
stuff has been used research papers in the past.  [Don't have any specific
examples, but I get a several notes a year from college people (profs, PHD
students, grad students, and undergrads) regarding my salary stuff and
some research they are doing or paper they are writing.]
