About Eric

Eric T. Jorgensen is a system administrator and customer support technician at Eskimo North, a Seattle-based national ISP and a graduate of the University of Washington School of Communications. He is the author of several newspaper articles about technology and education in the Seattle metropolitan area. He lives in Mountlake Terrace, Washington.
