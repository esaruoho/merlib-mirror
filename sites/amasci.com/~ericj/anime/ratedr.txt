Recommended Anime Rated R/NC-17 

 Eric's Recommended Anime Rated R/NC-17 

 These title images do not portray scenes from R/NC-17 rated shows. 

 A quick word about R/NC-17 rated anime -- I've
included "official" (package-listing) "R" and "NC-17" here as well as a close
approximation of the this-age-and-up ratings, using a cutoff of 16 (listing
ages "15" and below as "PG/PG-13" elsewhere). 

 Akira 
 Movie 

 When motorcycle-gang-member Tetsuo gains uncontrollable psychic powers, the gang's leader, Kaneda, struggles to keep his friends alive before a legendary source of similar power, Akira, is awakened.
 Rated R -- contains graphic violence, language, brief nudity 

 Fushigi Yuugi (Mysterious Play) 
 Multiple TV/OVA(s) 

 Two friends reading a book find themselves linked to the ancient world described inside, and on opposite sides of a continental battle. Strong Neverending Story motif, without the flying muppets.
 Rated 16+ -- contains violence, brief nudity 

 Hakkenden (Legend of the Dog Warriors) 
 OVA (13 eps) 

 In 15th-century Japan, warring families battle for supremacy and survival; one side uses demons, while the other side has legendary half-human/half-dog warriors with the eight virtues of the samurai.
 Rated 16+ -- contains graphic violence 

 Key the Metal Idol 
 OVA (15 eps) 

 A young robotic girl searches for a method of becoming human when her grandfather/creator is killed; a female Pinnochio with anime standards of super powers, robots, and idol singers.
 Rated 16+ -- contains violence, brief nudity 

 Now and Then, Here and There 
 TV (13 eps) 

 A young teenager finds himself in another world trying to rescue . . . nearly everyone. Easily the darkest of the AIC/Pioneer "high-school teen in an alternate world" series, and offers quite a few twists. Originally on satellite/cable TV rather than broadcast TV.
 Rated 16+ -- contains graphic violence 

 Serial Experiments Lain 
 TV (13 eps) 

 A shy girl gets an email from a deceased friend and finds herself drawn into the "wired" online world in a chase for sanity and reality.
 Rated 16+ -- contains violence, suicides in early episodes 

 3 x 3 Eyes 
 OVA (7 eps) 

 A young man swerves his motorcycle to avoid hitting a magical girl, who makes him immortal; now he needs to make her human to become human again himself!
 Rated 16+ -- contains graphic violence
