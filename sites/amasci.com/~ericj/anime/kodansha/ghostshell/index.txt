Recommended Anime: Ghost in the Shell 

 It found a voice . . . Now it needs a body 

 T echnology and metaphysical philosophy merge in
the shadowy world of politics and high-tech crime. Just what sets us
apart from machines when our own bodies can be augmented with mechanical
and electrical devices so readily? That's one of the deeper questions in
 Ghost in the Shell , by Shirow Masamune, who also
created some other favorites of mine in the past, Dominion Tank
Police , Appleseed , and Black Magic
M-66 . 

 Story Intro 

 Major Kusanagi and her partner Batou, top-level agents in the police
force, and their comrades are after the "Puppetmaster", a master hacker
who has broken into the minds of many people and the programming of many
androids to evade capture. When the Puppetmaster is finally brought into
the station for questioning, another government agency goes out of their
way to retrieve it from the police, but Kusanagi has her own goals to
learn the truth behind the masquerade and the Puppetmaster. 

 Other Notes 

 The philosophical discussions can be long in this one (it's directed by
Oshii Mamorou, after all), but this is primarily an action movie; the
manga it was based on goes even deeper into the philosophy and technology
than the film.
