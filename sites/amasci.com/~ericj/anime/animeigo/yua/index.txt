Recommended Anime: You're Under Arrest 

 You're Under Arrest 

 A s a fan of anything by Kosuke Fujishima, I
certainly do recommend his You're Under Arrest series.
Based on a short manga series written before his longer Aa! Megami-sama
(Oh My Goddess!) but animated afterward, it goes deeper than the other
anime series in that it was picked up for a full TV run to continue the
hijinks portrayed in the OVA's (a fate that didn't happen with
 Goddess ).

 Story Intro 

 Katsumi rushes along the streets on her way to work; a passing train made
her late on her first day. Breaking all sorts of traffic laws on
her scooter, she finds herself followed by a police woman who's just as
capable of navigating side alleys and shortcuts. Yet this traffic officer
puts the infractions aside and welcomes her to the department; Miyuki is
assigned as her new partner in the traffic police. 

 You're Under Arrest is a fun romp through various
traffic-law scenarios (reckless driving during a typhoon, clearing a traffic
jam, etc.) mixed with romantic comedy and buddy (Miyuki and Katsumi)
stories. The TV series starts where the videos ended (even starting with
episode "5"), with the arrival of a new female officer, Aoi, who is a bit
more than she -- or rather, he -- appears. 

 (I haven't yet seen the special episode or the movie; I've heard that
they are different enough to warrant another one or two parts to the story
intro.)
