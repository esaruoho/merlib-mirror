 A nime's answer to The Right Stuff ,
a nation struggles to launch the first man into space in the epic
 The Wings of Honneamise (or The Royal Space
Force ). 

 Story Intro 

 As a boy, Shiro dreamed of flying the fast jets he watched take off
from a nearby airfield. Low grades and a slacker attitude kept him out of
the military, and his only chance was a branch everyone looked down on:
the royal space force, a branch that would take almost anyone. 
Politicians and warring neighbors threaten to stop the plans of the space
force after many failed tests and lost pilots. 

 After Shiro meets a freindly religious-pamphlet distributor, he
volunteers as the nation's first true astronaut. Shiro helps raise money
for the mission with promotional appearances and interviews but quicky
tires of fame. But the space force needs to hurry; not only is the rocket
itself not finished yet, the neighbors are using more force to get them to
stop -- their armies. And the local politicians are doing the same --
with assassins. 

 Other Notes 

 The first feature from the GAINAX studio, a group of fans that were set on
showing they could create as well, Honneamise became a classic
and favorite. Shortly afterward, GAINAX themselves became a fan favorite
worldwide.
