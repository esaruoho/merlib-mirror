 C lassic western motifs and slapstick comedies
merge well, with serious overtones later in the show, in
 Trigun . 

 A legendary gunslinger with a 60-billion "double-dollar" price on his head
strikes fear in the hearts of everyone in the various towns he wanders. Many
towns and cities have been destroyed, so the legend goes, by this "Humanoid
Typhoon", earning him that nickname as well as "The Stampede". Most people
come out of encounters with Vash with one of two thoughts: 

 Either he's the best gunslinger this world has ever seen, or... 
 He's the luckiest idiot this world has ever seen. 

 While two insurance agents track him down and find the idiot behind the
legend, all is not as it seems. When people from Vash's past show up to cause
even more wide-spread damage, a new side to Vash appears: a dark,
potentially deadly side that even he doesn't like in himself. 

 Trigun 
Distributed by Pioneer Entertainment.
