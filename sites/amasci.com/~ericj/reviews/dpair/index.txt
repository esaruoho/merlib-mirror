 About Me 

 K ei and Yuri are government agents, codenamed
"Lovely Angels", who nab criminals of all sorts; however, due to the wake of
destruction after they're done (but usually not their fault directly), they've
gained a nickname: Dirty Pair . These are the original
anime "babes with big bazookas" -- and I do mean bazookas.

 Other Notes 

 With the worldwide success of the original character design, a complete
redesign was also done as Dirty Pair Flash , with a
younger version of the Lovely Angels, who may or may not be the same 
Kei and Yuri as the original pair. When that version was released to less
success, the older pair were given a further 10 episodes, which were
released here in the US as Original Dirty Pair (not to
be confused with the original episodes , but closer to the original
 designs ).
