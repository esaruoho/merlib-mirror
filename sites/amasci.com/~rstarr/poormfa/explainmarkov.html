Markov {model, chain}
 Markov Generator

Markov uses the exact same process as Travesty but applies it to words rather than to characters. "Word" here means a sequence of non-blank characters and includes punctuation as well as letters. The name comes from the statistical process used here and in Travesty, a Markov chain.

See the explanation of Travesty here for more details.

Markov can be used to scramble a source text before using it in another process such as diastic reading.
