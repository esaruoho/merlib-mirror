Anthologies and Link Sites

Links to on-line anthologies and major link sites.

Anthologies
  • Academy of American Poets. The Listening Booth lets you listen (via RealAudio) to 50 different poets reading.
  • The Atlantic Monthly's poetry pages. Includes a short but useful list of links, including on-line journals. Also provides a link to Dana Giaoa's very controversial (and snippy) essay in The Atlantic, "Can Poetry Matter?"
  • Buffalo Electronic Poetry Center. Where the L=A=N=G=U=A=G=E folks (and many others you'll recognize) hang out. Extensive collection of contemporary poetry and poetics.
  • Canadian Poetry. An extensive collection, including poems by Roo Borson, a recent subject of Robert Hass's Poet's Choice column.
  • Coach House Books publishes books on-line as well as print. Tends toward the experimental. Plenty to read. Note that they ask you to use their order page to "tip" the poet $3 for reading the work on-line. Seems fair to me....
  • The Contemporary American Poetry Archive organized by poets Wendy Battin and Charles O. Hartman as a repository for out-of-print poetry books. Contains 35 books.
  • English Language and Literature Resources at the U. Toronto library. Includes an on-line anthology, Representative Poetry, a collection that "includes over 2,000 English poems by 310 poets." Also links to Middle English and Renaissance resources.
  • Electronic Text Center at University of Virginia. Go to the English page to get the list of holdings in English. Many items restricted to University or Virginia library patrons.
  • The Poetry Archive. Varied collection of poems, including some modern. Essays on each poet.
  • Poetry Daily. A new poem every day. Has an archive of the last year's poems and daily poetry news.
  • The original Project Bartleby is now an archive. The New Bartleby continues its work.
  • Red Frog - An international on-line anthology. Includes an extensive selection of Zbigniew Herbert's poetry from translations now out of print.
  • Sonnet Central. An anthology spanning the whole history of the sonnet.
  • The Wr-eye-tings Scratchpad. A works-in-progress site for visual and/or concrete poetry. Supplement to the Wr-eye-tings email list.
Link Sites
  • American Authors on the Web. Big (155K) link page, by Mitsuharu Matsuoka at Nagoya University.
  • English resources on the Web page at Capital Community-Technical College. Good selection.
  • Links to Modern and Contemporary Poetry Resources. An extensive, unannotated list by Akitoshi Nagahata at Nagoya University including many on-line magazines.
  • Modern and Contemporary American Poetry page for Al Filreis's English 88 class at U. Penn. A very rich list.
  • Voice of the Shuttle. The humanities research website. Includes extensive links to everything imaginable, including contemporary British and American literature and a creative writing page containing a rich list of resources and on-line magazines.
