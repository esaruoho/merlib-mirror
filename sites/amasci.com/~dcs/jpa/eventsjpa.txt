 2002 Events 

 Dr. Polly Young-Eisendrath Seminar October 19, 2002 

 The JPA presented a seminar with Polly Young-Eisendrath, Ph.D. on October 19, 2002, titled, "What's Love Got to Do With It? Transference and Transformation in Psychotherapy and Life." 
 Her presentation was based on an article from her forthcoming book, Subject to Change: Jung, Gender and Subjectivity in Psychoanalysis . Routledge, 2003. An Adobe Acrobat (.pdf) version of this article is available (1.1 MB). 
 In addition, an Acrobat (.pdf) version of the event brochure is available .
