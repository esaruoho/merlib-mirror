 The following list of members is for information purposes only and is not a recommendation of anyone listed. Full members have a graduate degree in one of the mental health disciplines and have been practicing as a psychotherapist and is either certified or licensed, as their degree requires. In addition, they must have completed at least 100 hours of personal analysis with a Jungian Analyst who is a member of the International Association of Analytical Psychology (IAAP). Associate Memers have a graduate degree in one of the mental health disciplines and have been practicing as a psychotherapist and is either registered, certified or licensed, as their degree requires. In addition, they must have completed at least 25 hours of personal analysis with a Jungian Analyst who is a member of the IAAP. Please note that registration is a minimal requirement with the Department of Licensing and does not include recognition of any practice standards, nor necessarily implies the effectiveness of any treatment.
