C.G. Jung Society, Seattle

Library Hours and Location

The Jung Society Library is located in Room 345 of the Good Shepherd Center. Members may borrow books, cassette tapes, video tapes, and periodicals. The library is closed on holidays. In case of poor weather conditions, please phone ahead to make sure the library is open. In general, to double-check library hours, call 206-547-3956 for a phone message.

Note to members from the Librarian:

Please remember, there is always a tentative aspect to the operation of the library. We will adhere to the following schedule as closely as possible. We are trying to get more volunteers to staff the library on Saturdays. Please check the web page or call for any changes to the schedule. I get these in to David and Bunny as soon as I know and they in turn update the web page and phone message respectively. A call on Mondays will let you know I am there for sure and not stuck in traffic or stopped by snow (we tend to have more ice and snow near the Canadian border where I come from).

Thanks for you patience and understanding.
