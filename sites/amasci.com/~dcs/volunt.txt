 C.G. Jung Society, Seattle 

 Volunteers and Donations Needed

 The dedication of individuals makes Jung Society events possible. The purpose of the Seattle Jung Society is to educate the general public about the psychology of C.G. Jung. In order to continue, the Jung Society needs the support of member volunteers to fulfill the many tasks necessary for it to function. The Society is also in need of a number of items. If you can help, please call the Society office or send e-mail to webmaster@jungseattle.org . 
 The Jung Society needs volunteers to help with the following areas. To apply for these positions, please send your resume or summary of qualifications to Diane Bogue at webmaster@jungseattle.org or call 206-547-3956. 
 MEMBERSHIP DIRECTORY COORDINATOR 
 A volunteer is needed to finish updating the Society's 2005 Membership 
 Directory for a completion date of May 1st in order to distribute the 
 Directory to the membership no later than the Annual Membership Meeting in 
 May, 2005. This volunteer position requires experience using Microsoft Excel software and 5-6 hours to finish the directory. In addition to Society 
 members, students with Excel and word-processing experience who need to 
 fulfill community service hour requirements are also invited to volunteer.
