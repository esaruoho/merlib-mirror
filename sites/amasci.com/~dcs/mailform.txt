 C.G. Jung Society, Seattle 

 Society Membership 
 A major benefit of membership is the use of our recently expanded library, which features a research section holding some rare volumes on Jungian thought. Our annual program includes nine lectures with subsequent workshops. C.G. Jung Society dues and contributions are tax deductible. If you have any questions about membership, please direct them to membership@jungseattle.org . 
 To learn about the Society's reciprocity agreements with other local Jung societies, see the About the Society page. 

 Sustaining Memberships 
 Individual: $250 
 Couple Membership: $400 
 Admission for one year to all lectures, registration priority and discounted admission to workshops, plus use of the library. Receipt of the newsletter and announcements of members-only events. Appreciation will be expressed with a name plaque mounted in the library and a listing in the newsletter.
 Contributing Memberships 
 Individual: $90 
 Couple Membership: $145 
 Admission for one year to all lectures, discounted admission to workshops, plus use of the library. Receipt of the newsletter and announcements of members-only events.
 Standard Memberships 
 Individual: $40 
 Couple Membership: $65 
 Student/Senior: $25 
 Discounted admission to lectures and workshops. Use of the library and receipt of the newsletter and announcements of members-only events.
