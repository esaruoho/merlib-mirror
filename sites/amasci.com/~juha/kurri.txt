 The Jari Kurri Fan Page 

 The Man 
 Was born 18th May 1960, Helsinki,
 Finland 
 He stands 6'1" (185 cm) tall and weighs 195 lbs (89 kg)
 Jari was Edmonton's Third Choice, 69th Overall in 1980 Entry Draft
 He wears jersey #17, plays C/RW and shoots right

News 

Jari Kurri had the rare oportunity to get a ride in McLaren Mercedes' F1 twin-seater. McLaren's test driver 
 Oliver Panis and Jari burned some rubber on the track in Brno, Czech Republic. 
 Jari looked excited before the two 
 headed out on the track . 

Jari was one of the hockey legends taking part in the Toronto 2000 All-Star festivities. Check out a
 picture of Jari in Toronto. Thanks goes to Robin for sharing it with all Kurri fans. 

Jari visited the Kings and Ducks' stadiums in January 2000. This was the first time Jari had been in California since he retired from the NHL. When asked if he misses playing hockey, he said that he doesn't miss being on the ice but he does miss the locker room chats with the players. Jari's plans for the future include being involved with hockey in some form but he is not actively pursuing anything right now. "I have received many kinds of hockey related job offers from around the world, but I'm in no hurry to jump into something. After 20 years of playing in the NHL, it is nice to have some free time and just be an ordinary hockey fan eating a hot dog ." Kurri fans have a chance to see Jari again on TV in February during the All Stars game in Montreal. Stay tuned! 

Kurri was among the 2000 guests invited to celebrate Finland's 81st year of independence on December 6th at the 

President's Palace. Here is a picture of 
 Jari in the Palace. 

Jari Kurri received a golden Koho stick on November 9th in recognition of his 601 NHL goals. The Koho stick was given to him in front of a standing ovation before the Finland vs. Sweden game in the 
 Karjala tournament . Jari said the stick will most likely end up on display in his Highlight Cafe restaurant. Players who have received a golden hockey stick before are Wayne Gretzky, Mike Bossy, Michel Goulet and Joe Mullen, who played with Titan sticks, and Mario Lemieux and Dave Andreychuk, who played with Koho sticks. Here is

Jari with the Golden Koho stick. 

Jari Kurri is one of the men behind a new sports restaurant in downtown Helsinki. Opened on June 15th, the 

Highlight Cafe offers tasty food, sports feed through 50 TV monitors, and sports memorabilia on display by many Finnish superstars like Jari Kurri (NHL),
 Mika Hakkinen (Formula 1), 
 Juha Kankkunen (Rally) and 
 Jari Litmanen (Soccer). Jari and some other Finnish NHL stars celebrated the opening with a party. Here is a picture of 
 Jari with Teemu and his wife Sirpa having fun. 

May 02 1998 marked Jari's last appearance in the NHL. He finished his magnificient career on the very same ice where he started it in 1980, the Edmonton Coliseum. We wish you all the best in the future! Thank you, Jari, for all the great moments! 

Although Jari will leave the NHL after this season, he will keep on playing hockey. A Finnish charity hockey team called the Pietarinkadun Oilers made a 2+2 year deal with Jari. The team collects money for childrens' hospital. Here is a
 picture of Jari wearing his future team jersey.

Finland's Hockey Team wins the Bronze Medal in the Olympic Games in Nagano! Jari played an excellent tournament. He scored one goal and got four assists. Jari said he ranks this Olympic Bronze Medal equal to a Stanley Cup Win.

Hockey News included Jari in the list of the
 Top 50 NHL Players of All-Time 

On January 8th Jari Kurri was added as special selection by
NHL Commissioner Gary Bettman to the
 World Team Roster for the first
 NHL All-Star International Showdown. 
This will be Jari's eighth All-Star game appearance. Jari scored a goal in the game, his second All-Star career goal. Congrats Jari!

 Jari Kurri, 17 published in Finland, that he plans to retire after this season. Read the story in the
 Rocky Mountain News. 

 Regular Season Career Stats 

 1251 Games, 
 601 Goals, 797 Assists, 1398 Points 

 Play off Career Stats 

 200 Games, 
 106 Goals, 127 Assists, 233 Points 

 Star Achievements 

 Five time Stanley Cup Winner (1984, 1985, 1987, 1988, 1990) 

 NHL Lady Byng Memorial Trophy 1985 

 Became the eighth player in NHL's history to score 600 goals (Dec.23.1997) 

 NHL Best Goal Scorer (68) 1986 

 NHL 1st All-Star line (RW) (1985, 1987) 

 NHL 2nd All-Star line (RW) (1984, 1986, 1989, 1990, 1993, 1998) 

 50-goal season Four times (1984-1987) 

 60-goal season Two times (1985, 1986) 

 Became the third player in NHL's history to score 70 goals in one season (1985) 

 100-point season six times (1983, 1984, 1985, 1986, 1987, 1989) 

 NHL playoffs best goal scoring (1984, 1985, 1987, 1988) 

 Scored the Stanley Cup winning goal for Edmonton in 1987 

Jari is the All-time best European scorer in the NHL with 1398 points (Peter Stastny is second with 1239 points) 

 Jari holds or shares the following records 

 Holds NHL most goals (12) in one playoff series (1985) 
 Holds NHL most three-or-more-goal games (3) in one playoff series (1985) 
 Holds NHL single-season playoff record for most three-or-more-goal games (4) (1985) 
 Shares NHL single-season playoff record with Leach for most goals (19) (1985) 
 Shares NHL single-season playoff record for most game-winning goals (5) (1987) 
 Shares most power-play goals (3) in one play-off game (1987) 
 Shares most shorthanded goals (2) in one play-off game (1983) 
 Shares most shorthanded goals (2) in one period (April 24, 1983)
