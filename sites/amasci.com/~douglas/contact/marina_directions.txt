 How to get to my boat 

 address 

 Seattle Marina 
 2401 N. Northlake Way (zipcode 98103) 

 slip F4 

 directions 

 From either I-5 or Aurora Avenue, take the 45th street exit
and drive to Stone Way. Turn south on Stone Way and proceed downhill
all the way to the water. Go past the last stoplight (there is a Subway
shop at the intersection) and Stone Way will turn into North Northlake
Way, which will curve around to your left and follow the Lake Union
shoreline past marinas and drydocks. Go past Gasworks Park and start
looking for the public boat ramp, which is just past Dunato's Boatyard.
Seattle Marina is on the east side of the boat ramp (if you get to
Ivars, you have gone too far). Don't park in the marina parking lot
(you can get towed without the proper parking sticker). Park across the
street. My boat is on the dock nearest to the boat ramp (slip number
F-4). There is a locked gate in front of my dock. My phone number is
206-547-0851.
