    A Key to Descriptive Terminology

  • Most books are 8vo (6 x 9 inches) or 12mo (5 x 7 1/2) in size, bound in original cloth, boards or paper unless otherwise stated.

    • Very minor blemishes, inked names or bookplates may not be noted. Former institutional or library copies are described as ex-libris.
  • Books are described first, then dust jacket (DJ) (if present; lack of reference to a DJ means there is none).

    • 4to (Quarto) = 9x12"; basically oversize book.
    8vo (Octavo) = 6x9; avaerage size hardcover or quality paperback.
    12mo (Duodecimo) = 5x7 1/2; smaller than average, but commonly seen.
    DJ = dustjacket;
    ed. = editor or edition;
    ex-library = former institutional or library copy;
    Some book dealers use the terms `wraps' to refer to all forms of paperbacks (as opposed to hardback editions). However, we put most paperback editions into two categories:
    Trade paperback = paper covers, quality paperbacks, chapbooks, pamphlets.
    Mass Market paperback = Small inexpensive pocket editions.

    Condition

    All books listed should indicate noticeable faults or exceptions to the general classifications which are used below:
    G = Good, a decent reading copy that shows many signs of wear.
    VG = Very Good, that is, your average used book in decent shape but not crisp, with obvious reading or shelf wear.
    NF = Near Fine, shows very minimal reading or shelf wear.
    F = Fine, is crisp, basically a book that looks practically new, unread without wear or faults.
    VF, As New = Very Fine, an unread book, basically as issued. We seldom use this grading, as too often we see new books which show signs of shelf wear (DJs with rubbing or scratches are common) & other faults & feel such grading may be deceptive.

    When you see a book described as F/F, this refers first to the condition of the book & then the condition of the DJ.
    If you see an item simply described as VG, you would understand that the book is in Very Good condition & lacks any dust jacket. If it were described as NF/VG, the book in near fine condition & the DJ is in very good condition.

    References

    Many books we catalog include references to various bibliographies which are useful to collectors of certain kinds of literature, usually providing more in-depth information about book contents, printing history, & `points' that are useful in identifying specific printings.
