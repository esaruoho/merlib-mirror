1887 -- Sweden: Andreas Fritzner, anti-militarist activist, lives.


1894 -- Italy: A military tribunal condemns Luigi Molinari to 23 years imprisonment as the instigator of the insurrection in Lunigiana.

Molinari & other anarchists bands armed themselves in support of Sicilian victims of the "State of Siege" (proclaimed at the beginning of January), which aimed to repress the revolts against increased flour prices. See 13 January. Following a popular protest movement, Molinari is amnestied on September 20, 1895.

http://perso.club-internet.fr/ytak/janvier2.html#13



1897 -- Argentina: "El Perseguido" ceases.

In 1888 & '89 immigration into the Argentine Republic increased rapidly & unemployment & strikes made their appearance.

Malatesta seems to have spent this period at Bueno Aires doing active propaganda. Meetings were held on March 18 (1888), on the occasion of the first local strikes, etc., & it is probably that the movement "El Perseguido" was first issued, continued until Jan. 31, 1897, the first of the rapidly developing active & numerous press, culminating in the "Protesta Humana" (June 13, 1897), followed by the (daily) "Protesta) (April 5, 1904), which for so many years weathers all storms.

Max Nettlau's Errico Malatesta: The Biography of an Anarchist

http://www.pitzer.edu/~dward/Anarchist_Archives/malatesta/nettlau/nettlauonmalatesta.html



1899 -- Aristide Lapeyre (1899-1974) lives. Hairdresser, anarchist, militant pacifist & no-Malthusian.

In 1929, with his brothers, Laurent & Paul, Aristide Lapeyre takes part in the creation of the CGT-SR. A participant in the Spanish Revolution of 1936-39, during WWII he helped many comrades escape the Gestapo, & himself was taken hostage by the Nazis. An advocate for abortion rights, & a provider for abortions, on June 19, 1973 he was sentenced to 5 years in prison following the accidental death of a patient. http://perso.club-internet.fr/ytak/mars4.html#23



1899 -- US: Prevented from delivering her lecture today, "Authority versus Liberty," Emma Goldman's comrades print & distribute 5,000 copies of a manifesto containing the text of the anarchist-feminist's barred speech. In America, we extol the virtues of Free Speech.

1924 -- George Simeonov Popov (1900-1924) dies.

George Popov was a Bulgarian teacher, poet, speaker & anarchist organizer.

Popov initiated an insurrectionary movement against the coup d'etat of June 1923, which was quelled after a week of fighting against the army. Popov took refuge in the mountains, forming guerilla anarchist groups. When his hiding place was discovered, he committed suicide rather than fall into the hands of the army.

1968 -- Tet Offensive catches South Vietnam off guard as war escalates.

70,000 Viet Cong troops attack 100 cities. Puts the lie to numerous US government & military & media fantasies of American victory being "in hand."

On 1 January Beloved & Respected Comrade Leader General Westmoreland said: "Our forces have been able to detect impending major offensives & to mount spoiling attacks." Today, Westmoreland & his troops are caught completely by surprise. The fighting lasts until February 24, & undermines US morale abroad & at home. A photo of a police chief shooting a Viet Cong captive in the head is like a swan song.

We poured gasoline into the sawed off fifty-five gallon drums of shit; it mixed with the piss that was covering the shit. We then lit long sections of toilet paper & dropped them into the barrels, hoping for success.

David Willson, REMF (Rear Echelon Mother Fucker)

The Vietcong strike at Saigon, penetrate the U.S. embassy compound, besiege a US Marine base at Khe Sanh, & capture the provincial capital of Hue, among other initial gains. By the end of February, US & South Vietnamese forces repulse the offensive & inflict heavy losses on the Vietcong, but the episode exposes the reality that, contrary to what U.S. military command espouses, an end to the war is not in sight.

1971 -- US: Cold Day in Hell? The Winter Soldier Hearings begin in a Howard Johnson's motel in Detroit. Sponsored by the group Vietnam Veterans Against the War, the hearings are an attempt by soldiers who have served in Vietnam to publicize U.S. conduct in the war.

The veterans testify that the My Lai massacre was not an isolated incident & that American troops have committed atrocities. More than 100 veterans, in fact, testify to brutal US acts. Oregon Senator Mark Hatfield will enter the Winter Soldier hearings into the Congressional Record but, otherwise, the proceedings capture little attention.

1980 -- France: Siege of Plogoff. During the entire six weeks, from January 31 to March 14, the "city hall annexes" & the police guarding them came under constant attack from local people opposed to government plans to construct a nuclear plant. http://www.antenna.nl/~wise/499-500/4934.html



1980 -- Guatemala: Elias Barahona, official spokesman for the Ministry of the Interior, reveals the President, General Romeo Lucas Garca, ordered police to set fire to the Spanish embassy with its occupants inside.

39 people are roasted alive, including 27 Indians peacefully occupying the embassy to denounce massacres in the Quiche region. Barahona also states that Lucas Garcia commands the paramilitary & parapolice bands known as the Squadrons of Death, & helps draw up lists of opponents to disappear. He claims a "Program of pacification" being carried out is based on a 420 page document drawn up by specialists in the US based on their experience in the Vietnam War. In the first half of this year 27 university professors, 13 journalists, & 70 campesino leaders, mainly Indians, have been murdered. The repression has special intensity for Indian communities in the Quiche region, where large oil deposits have recently been discovered.
