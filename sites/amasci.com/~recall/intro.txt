RECOLLECTION USED BOOKS: An Introduction of Sorts 

 An
introduction to Recollection
Fine Used Books 

 Recollection
Used Books is in its fifth year, a successor to a/k/a Used Books
in Seattle's University District. 
 We maintain
a broad selection of literature in most subject areas, with emphasis on
modern, collectible & signed literature, social & radical studies,
labor history & politics, Afro-American & Black Studies, The Beats,
Music (Jazz, blues, rock & folk), The Sixties, the Viet Nam War, The
Cold War, Baseball, Fishing, Mountaineering, cookbooks, chess, mystery
& detective novels, & various other fiction & non-fiction subjects. 
 One of our purposes
of being involved with used books is educational, both for ourselves &
our customers. We are interested in all kinds of books & authors &
related matters, & no matter what expertise or experience we bring
to this endeavor, we are always still learning -- & we hope to share
& make available some of these things to you. The Internet & the
Web especially resolve numerous limitations of printed catalogs, which
are extremely costly to produce & mail & allow little room for
anything more than the briefest of descriptions. 
 The primary
purpose, of course, is to serve as a provider of books, putting people
& books together. Our Web Pages reflect these concerns as we add &
refine presentation & content. We recommend checking in periodically
for improvements & the many books which we are offering in our catalogs.
We presently have over 3,500 books on line, & add 100+ a month. 
 "Who's
on first?" 
 We have a small
staff of nine . . . all standing on first, trying to figure out how they
got there or where exactly home plate is. 

 David
Brown. An original founding member, about 13 years ago, of the collectively
owned & operated a/k/a Used Books, which went out of business a few
months after he left to start Recollection Books. This was part of the
 Left
Bank Books Collective , where he worked from 1978 to 1995. A founder
of Left Bank Publishing
& Distribution & the Books
to Prisoners Project , both still active & on the Internet these
days. Presently BleedMeister for Recollection Books "Daily
Bleed" , an online calendar sent out daily(!) in both email & web
format, with every day of the year archived. 

 Jim
Koehnline. Resident staff artist/master collage man. A very tiny
sampling of his work is available through our Online texts page & our
pages of bookmarks. Jim has produced over 400 bookmarks for the store.
These are intensive graphics & are slow loading for those of us with
slower modems! His own Jubilee
Garden of graphics (for sale) has is worth your visit. Jim began the
Autonomedia Jubilee Saints Calendar which is the opening theme for each
days Daily Bleed, & thus must be SaintMeister . 

 Mike
Payson. Resident technical consultant, & our in-house TechMeister . 

 Rick
HemoMeister. Veteran web surfer, always on the lookout for the next
big one. The glue dat keeps Recollection Used Books going & heavy contributor
to our calendar, the Daily Bleed. 

 Sue
Letsinger. Cataloguer extraordinaire, meaning we now have over 3,500
books, sorted into 60 catalogs, for sale online. 

 These cataloged
books are also offered for sale online through four used book services
which we belong to: Advanced Book Exchange, Bibliofind (recently bought
by Amazon.com), Bibliocity (recently bought by Alibris) & usedbooks.com
(a new Seattle-based startup). All of these are also quickly found under
the combined service of Bookfinder.com 

 Four
Hungry Beasts: Resident woofers. Pappy (Papillon, female), Jellico
(female), Xena (female) & Bucky (male) (Pics: [ 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 ]).
At least one of these vicious beasts in in the store at any given time,
but often all are on hand to keep the customers at bay. 

 Forthcoming,
as in then now & maybe, more or less.... 
 We intend to
add features to our Web Site. Focus is on book-related materials &
quality content. In 1997 we began issuing the Daily
Bleed , ( Better Than Boiled Coffee! ) our own wierd take
on history. In 1998 we began archiving them, & these are accessible
for everyday of the year. The Daily Bleed is sent out daily(!)to our subscription
list & a number of online discussion groups. In 1999 we began adding
a Gallery of Saints
& Sinners in conjunction with the Bleed. In 2000 we began scanning
& posting online scarce interesting materials from the Stan
Iverson Archives which we are custodians of. Recollection
Books is presently active in soliciting funds & book donations
for the Books-to-Prisoners Project 
(sends books into the prisons for free). We presently have the Seattle
Used Bookstore Guide (70+ stores) online, maintained by David &
printed copies (in conjunction with Louis Collins Rare Books), are distributed
free throughout Seattle area bookstores. David also maintains the Pacific
NW Used Bookstore Guide (online only, but downloadable & printable)
which covers Idaho, Oregon, Washington, British Columbia & Alaska.
We also make available our own Vietnam
War Checklist of 4,900 titles, many annotated. Also, possibly, we may
begin issuing a Newsletter & a small catalog of new books (a small
offering relevant to our interests).
