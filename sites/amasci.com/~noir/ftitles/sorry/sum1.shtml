Mini-summary of Sorry, Wrong Number


Wealthy invalid Leona Stevenson waits home alone for her husband Henry. When she calls his office, the wires get crossed and she overhears two men planning to murder a woman that night. As Leona tries to locate Henry, she learns that he's been up to no good and the killer's target is Leona herself.
