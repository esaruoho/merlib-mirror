 Mini-summary of Dark City (1998) 

 A man wakes up to find a woman's mutilated corpse in his hotel room. 
He can't remember if he did it. He can't even remember his own name.
Mysterious strangers try to kill him. The police close in on him as he
struggles to find out his identity and true love in the city at night.
