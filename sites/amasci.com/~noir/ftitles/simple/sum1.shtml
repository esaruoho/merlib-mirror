Mini-summary of A Simple Plan


In the snowy woods, three men stumble on a crashed airplane with a dead pilot and millions of dollars in cash. Best buds Jacob and Lou want to keep it but Jacob's younger brother Hank wants to turn it over to the authorities. The three agree that Hank will keep the money for a few months until they divide it. But it's not long before greed threatens their simple plan.
