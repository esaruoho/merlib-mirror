 A peasant.

 JOHNNY 
 I cheat with my own money, sure. But with your money I wouldn't have

 You see, this way you'll have two friends. You have no idea how

 BALLIN 
 Gambling and women do not mix.

 Those are the very words I use myself.

 Get this, Mr. Mundson. I was born last night when you met me in that

way.

 You can't talk to men the way you would at home. They don't understand

 Understand what?
 JOHNNY

 Mean what?
 JOHNNY 
 Doesn't it bother you at all that you're married?

 What I wanna know is, does it bother you?
