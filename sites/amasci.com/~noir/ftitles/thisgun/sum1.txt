 Mini-summary of This Gun For Hire (1942) 

 Hitman Raven kills two people in order to steal a chemical formula for
Nitro Chemical Corp. shady manager Willard Gates, who sells him out to the
cops. Raven goes after Gates, but magician-singer Ellen Graham tries to
work her magic on Raven.
