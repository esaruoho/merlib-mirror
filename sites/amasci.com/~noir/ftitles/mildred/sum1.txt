 Mini-summary of Mildred Pierce 

 Mildred Pierce begins with a bang. The gunshot victim is Monte Beragon.
The obvious suspect is his wife Mildred. Monte wrecked her business.
Mildred tries to kill herself and fails. Then she tries to set up her old
friend Wally with the dead body. But the truth's more complicated in this
tale of a mother's dotty love for her daughter.
