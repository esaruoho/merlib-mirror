Mini-summary of Moment In Time


Vincent and Angela are a happy couple until he proposes. She leaves him suddenly, but he tries to start over with a new job and new girlfriend, his psychologist Katherine. The mystery deepens when Angela resurfaces. Is she real or is Vincent losing it and imagining things? Vincent finds he must journey back to the past and uncover the secrets about Angela and himself.
