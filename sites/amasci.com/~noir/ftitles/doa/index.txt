 Trivia 

 Guests on the register at the Alison Hotel where Frank stays in Los Angeles include Marty Moss (the 
assistant director for D.O.A.), Ernest Laszlo (director of photography) and Russell Rouse 
(co-screenwriter).
 Filming took place on-location on the city streets of San Francisco and Los Angeles.
