 All cases are purchased brand-new, prepared for painting and given a
base coat of PPG automotive basecoat urethane. Custom airbrush work is done by
World famous airbrush artist Eric "Wiggy" Petring and then the case is
given a top clear coat of PPG high solids urethane clear for a beautiful, shiny,
tough finish. 

 Each case is an original, autographed work of art. 

 Want a different case? Specify the case you would like and we will purchase
it new, paint it and ship it to you. 

 You existing computer case can be custom painted as well. You ship your
computer to us and we disassemble the case, prep, paint, reassemble and ship
back to you. Contact us for more details. 

 Prices depend on complexity, but are generally around $400
