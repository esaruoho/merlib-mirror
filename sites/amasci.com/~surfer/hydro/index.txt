 Our Mission 
 Is to provide hydroplane fans with the ultimate experience. A ride in an
unlimited hydroplane while at the same time raising awareness for the Paul Gordon
Children's Fund which helps children who need organ transplants in conjunction
with the National Transplant Assistance Fund . 

 Until now, no one has ever had the chance to ride in a 4-seat turbine powered
unlimited hydroplane. Why not be one of the first to experience this unique
opportunity? 

 Company Profile 
 Over the years, unlimited hydroplane pilot Mark Evans has repeatedly tried to
answer the most often asked question in interviews: "What's it like?". 

 The intense experience is so hard to fully explain that he has finally
decided to just show people who really want to know. The U-4 hydroplane has been a
long-term project and dream of Mark for years now. A lot of hard (and mostly volunteer) work has paid off and
 finally made the dream a reality.
