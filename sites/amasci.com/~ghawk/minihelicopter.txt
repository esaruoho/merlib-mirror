 Miniature Helicopter 

 The History of Flight Inches Ahead: 

This inch-long helicopter made its maiden flight yesterday in Mainz,
Germany, hovering at 5.2 inches at the Institute for Microtechnology. 

The two blades rotate at more than 100,000 times a minute. About the size
of a German penny (background), the helicopter weighs one-hundredth of an
ounce, is one-third of an inch tall and costs $68,000. The motor is
connected to a source of electricity by copper threads. 

Researchers hope their micromotor will have applications in medicine,
computers and toys.
