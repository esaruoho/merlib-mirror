 Billy 

 The Kilt-wearing dry-humor-spouting 
Renaissance Man 
 Devilish wit, and devilishly handsome to boot. What more 
could you want? His career thusfar has largely been on stage, with some dabbling 
in music as well (he sings and plays guitar and drums) but as can clearly 
be seen from his delightful Pippin, the screen loves him, too. Some have 
criticized the movie-version character as too fluffy, but they're expecting too 
much, too soon. As can be clearly seen once the Fellowship leaves Moria, Pippin 
grows up in an awful hurry (and he's only supposed to be a lad, anyway. Remember 
the bathtub scene in the books?) The 
hell he goes through in TTT and ROTK I expect to give us, as with Merry, a much 
grander idea of just what this talented actor is capable of, which I sense is 
utter brilliance. His chemistry with Dom is also wonderful, and I'm looking 
forward to seeing more of that as well. 
 And, okay, so I'm a sucker for boyish Scots :)
