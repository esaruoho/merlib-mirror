Sean

The Dedicated Family Man and incurable Cuddle Slut

Everything one could want in a trusty manservant! Sean Astin has a list of credits, including writing and producing (even poetry!), as long as your arm. It's been years since he was just a cute Goonie, and also some time since his touching work in Rudy. He's had a few misses, to be sure, but he's been waiting for a breakout role. Sam is certainly it. Probably more than anyone else in the cast, with the possible exception of Ian McKellen, Sean is the perfect embodiment of his character, and is the most true to people's conception of the character from the books. He lives and breathes Sam every second he's onscreen, including in background acting, and thankfully didn't shy away from some of the scenes which most young actors would consider too mushy and open to bother playing fully. Playing a loyal, loving and committed character certainly isn't a stretch: he's been happily married to the lovely Christine for nearly 10 years and has a young daughter (Ali) who appears in ROTK, and a new child on the way soon. He certainly seems to be one of the most affectionate, as can be seen from the group pictures, and it's sweet to know that he and Lij still spend a lot of time with each other in LA. He seems like the nicest, funniest guy anyone could ever meet. Oh, and he's cute, too (Doesn't he look smashing in a tux? Wow...)
