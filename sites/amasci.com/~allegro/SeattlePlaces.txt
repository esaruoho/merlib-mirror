 SEATTLE'S BEST PLACES* 

 * according to us 

 The last few years of living in Seattle have been quite the
adventure for us. We have a wonderful city to explore on a regular basis, and we're always
finding wonderful new places to go and things to do, so we figured we'd share some of them
with you. 

 Previous Reviews 

 Place: Bamboo Garden 

 Type of Place: Vegetarian Chinese restaurant 

 Location: 364 Roy St (206) 282-6616.
Parking's kind of a pain, especially on busy Friday and Saturday nights, and
when there's a major event going on at Seattle Center. I'd recommend going there
for lunch, or a weekday dinner 

 Details: This is an all-vegetarian place- mostly
vegan- with an amazing assortment of traditional Chinese food as well as some
other Asian foods, like Thai noodles and Curries. They use fake meats and do so
well with them you won't miss the meat at all. The soups are absolutely great,
and the prices are very good too. The staff is friendly, the service is quick
and bright, all around, a great experience.
