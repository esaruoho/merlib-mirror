RECOMMENDATIONS:

THE SEATTLE SCHOOL DISTRICT SHOULD HAVE A POLICY TO COVER COMMERCIAL INFLUENCE IN THE PUBLIC SCHOOLS, WHICH WOULD INCLUDE A POLICY ON ADVERTISING.
THE POLICY SHOULD REFLECT THE CONCERNS OF THE COMMUNITY.
THE POLICY SHOULD INCLUDE OPERATIONAL DEFINITIONS OF TERMS USED IN THE POLICY.
THE POLICY SHOULD INCLUDE SPECIFIC EXAMPLES TO ILLUSTRATE THE INTENT OF THE POLICY.
THERE SHOULD BE ADEQUATE PUBLIC COMMENT PERIOD BEFORE THE POLICY IS IMPLEMENTED.
THERE SHOULD BE A COMMITTEE, COMPOSED OF EDUCATORS, PARENTS, STUDENTS, AND CITIZENS, TO SERVE AS AN ADVISORY BOARD TO HELP ADVISE THE SEATTLE PUBLIC SCHOOLS ON EFFECTS OF COMMERCIALISM ON THE EDUCATIONAL ENVIRONMENT.
