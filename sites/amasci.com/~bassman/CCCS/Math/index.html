1. In the Channel One contract, students must watch twelve minutes a day.

2. Over the next three years, the Seattle School District faces a $35 million budget reduction. The chief financial officer of the Seattle School District estimates that the policy will bring in $1 million a year (we are giving him the benefit of the doubt. Colorado Springs has a similar policy and only brought in $73,150 the first two years and $85,000 their third year.). How much money, in units of millions, would the district still be lacking?

$32,000,000 over the next three years.
