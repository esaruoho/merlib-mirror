Stop Whining and Repent

I love America - I am so glad I was born and raised here.  It's such a great country because God has put His blessing on it.  However, I think we need to repent now, as a nation, or God may turn away from us.

I think we've forgotten that it is God who put us here, and made us the powerful nation that we are.  God can also take that away, if we failt to acknowledge Him.  We feel free to drink the riches God has given us in this country, but we don't thank God who gave them.  We have even forgotten what he has done for us in the past - removing Him from our history books.

God has been calling us to repent.  On September 11, God allowed hijackers to infiltrate and harm us.  He let his hand of protection up to show us where we would be without His protection.  Sadly, instead of turning from our ways, everyone who said that God was sending us a message to repent was silenced and called heartless and callous.  Preachers who tried to show us that Semptember 11 was a warning from God was demonized, demoralized, and slandered.

Richard Weaver has tried to communicate this same message to Bush - giving him a letter warning that the U.S. must repent.  His website calls for the leaders of this nation - mayors, governors, senators, and representatives - to lead us in repentance.  I can imagine the reaction if that happened - many would be up in arms that state leaders were asking people to repent.  Everyone would shout - "separation of Church and State!" in a single chorus, and all those who stood up for repentance would again be silenced and slandered.  America has driven itself away from accountability to God by demonizing anyone who demonstrates or mentions what God is trying to do.  We celebrate religion, but scoff at any attempt to make the cause of the Kingdom of God a reality in our lives, or show where God is working.

Well, it's time to stop whining about stepping on each others feelings, or where the line is between Church and State.  We have let our moral situation decay for far too long, and now we just need to stop and repent, right now.  It shows how great, gracious and loving God is that we've lasted this long.

I will start repenting on behalf of myself and this nation, and I hope others will join the call to repent as well.
