Correctly Handling the Word of Truth

An Introduction to biblical Interpretation

This is a course I'm currently writing on biblical interpretation. The target audience is lay members of the church. It shows the process and problems of interpretation of scripture. It will also include a list of books for further study.

Instructor's Outline

The instructor's outline includes the major points the instructor should teach. The instructor should modify this to suit their own teaching style. The instructor's outline is pretty sparse right now, but it kind of shows you what to expect. Please mail me if you have anything you'd like to add, especially scripture references and textbook discussions on similar issues.
