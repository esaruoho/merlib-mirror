If you've ever dreamed about riding off into a desert sunset, here's your big chance! Our Arabian Horse Safari is a ten-day  package that features three action-packed days of  riding and three unforgettable nights of camping in the Sahara Desert. While on safari, you'll be transported back thousands of years in time. And you'll have the rare opportunity to view the world just as the ancient Egyptians did.  

In addition, you'll see--and explore--a number of ancient and mysterious sights that are absolutely off the beaten path! Accompanied by an experienced guide and crew, you'll tour and camp on the grounds of the pyramids in Dashur (site of the recently-opened Red Pyramid), El Lischt and Meidum. The remainder of your package features five exciting days and nights in Cairo.    

The Highlights? Private entrance and exploration time inside the Great Pyramid. A felucca boat ride on the Lake of Qarun near the Fayuum Oasis. A walking tour of Old Cairo. A guided tour of the Egyptian Museum. Five-star hotel accommodations. Shopping for treasures--and bargains--at the world-famous Khan El Khalili Bazaar. Snake charmers, belly dancers and a whole lot more!
