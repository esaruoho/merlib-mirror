Elara Adventure Travel's Red Sea Camel Dive is one of the most unique diving packages known to woman or man!  This 10-day tour combines the Sahara Desert, the Sphinx and the Giza Pyramids with world-class diving and camel trekking along the breathtaking coast of one of the world's most spectacular diving destinations! The Red Sea contains over 200 coral types and more than a thousand different species of fish. 

When it comes to diving, it just doesn't get any better than this! 

Your world-class diving itinerary will be highlighted by the opportunity to explore the underwater seascapes of the world-famous Canyon and Blue Hole.  In addition, you'll travel to a variety of 'best kept secret' sites which are only accessible by camel!  To top it off, your incredibly fun tour itinerary  includes 3 nights and 2 days in Giza--the gateway to the Sahara Desert and the home of both the pyramids and the Sphinx.

Throughout your tour, you'll stay in first-class hotel accommodations in both Giza and Dahab-- a lively, 'modern' village built on the edge of a pre-existing Bedouin community (which, by contrast, consists almost entirely of  palm trees and grass huts). From Dahab, you'll hike up Mount Sinai to watch an unforgettable sun rise, see the treasures of Saint Catherine's Monastery, tour the Colored Canyon and so much more. 

Please note: Snorkelers and non-divers are welcome and 'learn-to-dive' packages are available.
