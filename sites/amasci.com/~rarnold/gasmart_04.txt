 RON ARNOLD provided a roadmap for 
success to 
the natural gas industry in a presentation titled, "Combating Environmental 
Roadblocks to Energy Development" at the GasMart 04 in Denver, Colorado on May 18 , 2004. 
GasMart is sponsored by Intelligence Press, Inc., a natural gas industry 
publishing firm based near Washington, D.C. in Virginia. 

 Arnold pointed to the public 
benefits of clean-burning natural gas but warned 
that environmentalists do not want cleaner energy, they want no energy 
development at all, believing that any development harms nature. 
They claim they support clean air, but they don't - they blockade every attempt 
to bring clean natural gas to consumers and they fight energy self-sufficiency 
for America by trying to stop all domestic energy 
development. 

 The energy industry needs to mobilize masses 
of supporters in the general public, good people who recognize the benefits of 
abundant clean natural gas and will fight to keep it flowing. Arnold gave a 
point-by-point plan to do just that. 

 Arnold presented a detailed program of how 
to build the energy industry into a mass movement by cultivating the grassroots 
goodwill already present in American society, and giving them the tools to 
mobilize against anti-industry groups that won't stop blocking energy 
development. He gave proven tips on negotiation, partnering, and bringing social 
change to anti-industry groups. 

 Arnold concluded with an invitation to 
attendees of GasMart 04 to provide materials for his next book, being kept under 
close wraps for its strategic value.
