the Opposition to the Petfood Industry" at the Pet 
Food Forum, sponsored by Petfood Industry publications, a conference held in 
Chicago on March 30, 2004. 

 Arnold began with a warning: " Today, 
animal rights activists are hitting pet ownership and petfood companies like 
IAMS and Menu hard."

the Defense of Free Enterprise actively tracks this opposition, Arnold said,

 "We see whos bringing lawsuits against industry, whos lobbying 
Congress, whos organizing protest demonstrations and personal harassment, whos
