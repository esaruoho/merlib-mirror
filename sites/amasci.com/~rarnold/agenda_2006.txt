 2006 is a critical year in public 
policymaking. Many forces are trying to shrink free markets, private rights, and 
firm limits on government intrusion. 
 The Center for the Defense of Free 
Enterprise is dedicated to educating and involving the public in important 
free enterprise issues. As part of our mission, we offer AGENDA 2006 - Making a Difference 
as an invitation to public participation. 
 Links to issue papers will be added as the 
issues develop during 2006. 
 The positions offered here will come from a wide 
range of experts and are intended to be suitable for use in the national debate over sound 
public policy. 
 Safe and Strong
