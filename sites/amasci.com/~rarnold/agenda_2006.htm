2006 is a critical year in public policymaking. Many forces are trying to shrink free markets, private rights, and firm limits on government intrusion.

The Center for the Defense of Free Enterprise is dedicated to educating and involving the public in important free enterprise issues. As part of our mission, we offer AGENDA 2006 - Making a Difference as an invitation to public participation.

Links to issue papers will be added as the issues develop during 2006.

The positions offered here will come from a wide range of experts and are intended to be suitable for use in the national debate over sound public policy.

Safe and Strong
  • A safe America - security without oppression
  • A strong America - free enterprise the organizing principle of society
  • Countering opponents of free enterprise - monitoring, exposing, debunking

Thriving and Thrifty

  • Economic growth and sustained development
  • Tax and spend control - discipline for administrators, scrutiny for lawmakers
  • National Abundant Energy Policy - enough power for everybody and everything
  • Government retirement benefit reform - individual choice in funding retirement
  • Legal reform - outlaw nuisance lawsuits against business and industry
  • Affordable quality health care - help for the patient, respect for the provider
  • Death tax reform - stop confiscating people's life work and dying wishes
  • Rural economic development - growing rural culture, shrinking gentrification
  • Offshoring assessment - subject all federal policy to economic consequence study

Nature and Nurture

  • National Environmental Policy reform - encourage good ideas from private sector
  • Endangered Species Policy reform - from obstruction to incentive
  • Federal lands access for resource development - reform dominant use policy
  • Data Quality for environmental measures - defense against obstruction
  • IRS accountability - failure to police unlawful acts by exempt organizations
