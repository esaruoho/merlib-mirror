 Why we care: 
Like millions of Americans, many of the Center's members and staff are pet 
owners. The 
petfood industry, which they rely on for good products, is under attack from the 
animal rights movement, being hit with claims of animal cruelty in scientific 
testing programs. The petfood industry is a 
prime example of consumer choice at work , with 
vast numbers of animal-loving Americans selecting from a wide range of food 
products for their pets. Consumers trust petfood companies to provide safe, 
healthy and nutritious food products tested according to proper standards of 
animal care. Animal rights groups, such as People for the Ethical 
Treatment of Animals (PETA), o pp ose p et ownershi p 
entirel y , and thus take every opportunity to eliminate it as 
a cultural institution. Animal rights groups actively seek out the slightest

vulnerable to attack because the public knows little or nothing about the 
excellent testing programs petfood companies operate. As a result, good 
companies such as IAMS and Menu Foods get beaten up by animal rights groups for
