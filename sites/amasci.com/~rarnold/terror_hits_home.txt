Ron Arnold gave the Keynote Speech to more than 400 attendees at the August 7 
National Association of Home Builders 2003 Executive Officers Seminar in 
Vancouver, British Columbia. 

The Keynote title was "Terror Hits Home," a 45-minute PowerPoint presentation 
that details the impact of ecoterrorism on the home building industry.

images are self-explanatory.
