Terror Hits Home

Keynote Speech by Center Executive Vice President Ron Arnold to
the National Association of Home Builders 2003 Executive Officers Seminar
August 7, 2003, in Vancouver, B.C.

 

Ron Arnold gave the Keynote Speech to more than 400 attendees at the August 7 National Association of Home Builders 2003 Executive Officers Seminar in Vancouver, British Columbia.

The Keynote title was "Terror Hits Home," a 45-minute PowerPoint presentation that details the impact of ecoterrorism on the home building industry.

It is available on CD with images only and without narration for $10.00. The images are self-explanatory.
