Internet PING and TRACEROUTE Connectivity Tools Via WWW

Can the customer PING and TRACEROUTE from their computer to other places on the net? Can you PING and TRACEROUTE from these places to the customer's computer or the places she is trying to reach? Do they or you reach some sites but not others? Does it depend on where you look from and to? This can help determine if the problem is within the customer's computer, his local service provider, the final service they are connecting along some problem along the route.

These tools can be used to see if other sites are having the same connection problems you are having. For example, if you cannot TRACEROUTE to the Internic (rs.internic.net) or major world sites like Microsoft or regional ISP's like eskimo.com from your site, you can use the links below to see if you can do it from various places in the world.
