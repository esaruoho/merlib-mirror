 Warning: Paragliding and Hanggliding can be a very dangerous sport. If you attempt to fly a Paraglider or 
Hangglider without the proper training and check-offs, or do something improper while flying, it very possible you may hurt, 
permanently damage, or kill your self. However, it can be very rewarding and worth a certain level of risk. If a pilot is trained, 
pays attention to what he or she is doing and follows basic common sense rules, it can be generally safe. But remember when you 
are thousands of feet above the ground you better think at all times about what you are doing and where you are. 
The views and things you can see will never be forgotten. You will have the vantage point of an eagle or hawk, and sometimes 
you will fly with them too.
