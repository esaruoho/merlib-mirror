Today, 11/30/1998 in Small Claims Court, King County District
Court, Bellevue Division, 585 - 112th Ave SE, Bellevue,
Washington 98004, Case Number 9801417 the judge ruled for
Benjamin de Lisle, the plaintiff. The defendant was a company
called Top Secrets, Inc. 839 N. Eisenhower, Junction City, Kansas
66441. 

The court ruled that: 
* The local court was the appropriate venue because the
unsolicited advertisements were sent to a recipient in its
jurisdiction. 
* The unsolicited advertisement e-mails violated the Washington
State Law that regulates unsolicited e-mail advertisements. RCW
19.86. 
* The company, Top Secrets, Inc. is responsible and liable for
the actions of their "Independent Representatives" and
their advertising choices. 
* The total judgement was for $2,000.00 plus costs of $23.77. 

 You are hereby ordered to pay
$2,023.77. 

 The company did not appear today, nor did they
have representation. They did file letters with the court
claiming that they themselves (the company) did not advertise via
E-mail, but used "Independent Representatives" and said
that they were responsible, not the company for their
advertisements. Their mere absence would not have necessarily
meant a judgement for or against Top Secrets in this case. 

The sad thing is that I would have dropped the case (settled) if
the company simply would have officially apologized, there was no
indication of that in their letters.
