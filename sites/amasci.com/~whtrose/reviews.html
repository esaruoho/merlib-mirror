1996


"Every Woman's Dream"

...as Mitchell Parker...1996. ???. Jeff plays a lobbyist who loves money, fame and women and has a problem with telling the truth. He marries one woman (Delane Matthews) and tells her he's a CIA operative, a few years later he marries another woman (Kim Cattrell) and tells her he's rich and later that he works for Disney. Trying to manage two wives starts to drain Mitch of his monies and sanity. When things start unraveling when the wives find out about each other and things get deadly. Jeff is a bad guy. Note - Based on real life and the book "Deadly Pretender" - Every Woman's Dream can Be Someone's Worst Nightmare!

Kim Cattrell, Jeff Fahey and Delane Matthews in "Every Woman's Dream"

"The Sweeper"

...as Dale Goddard...1996. R. C. Thomas Howell gets top billing in this movie, cause it is his movie. The disappointing thing for us Jeff fans is that there is not enough Jeff in this movie. Jeff plays a cop who saves his son from bad men when he, the son, is young boy, then he, the cop, gets killed, and 15 years later it shows how his son does or rather does not deal with what happened. Jeff is a good guy, for the time that he is in the movie. Notes - Second movie with Howell, the other being "Curiosity Kills" (info supplied by Kathy Wosnak).

"Darkman III: Die, Darkman, Die!"

...as Peter Rooker (and in a dual part - Darkman Impersonating Peter)...1996. R. Jeff plays, "Peter Rooker," in a dual role. His evil self is attempting to destroy his marriage, his relationship with his daughter and Darkman. His other side (Darkman as Peter) is attemping to repair the damage and make amends. In a sinister tale of deformity and humanity mixed with a ruthless villian attempting control, Darkman weaves a shadowy tale into the darkside of human viciousness. Jeff - good and bad guy. When he's good - he's very good, but when he's bad - he's down right evil! (Info supplied by Cynthia Paroski, Co-President of the Jeff Fahey Internet Fan Club.)
Jeff Fahey in "Darkman III: Die, Darkman, Die!"

1995



"Eye of the Wolf"

...as Paul Weyman ...1995. PG-13. Very good. He plays a geologist in the Canadian wilds. He's definitely a good guy. I recommend this one to you. (Info supplied by Kathryn Oyler).

"Northern Passage"

...as Paul Weyman...1995. PG-13. Sequel to "Eye of the Wolf". Eye of the Wolf tells the story of "Kazan" a half dog, half wolf and Northern Passage tells the story of "Baree," his pup. In Eye of the Wolf man is portrayed as vicous and heartless to the wolves living in the wild. Kazan is brutalized often and hunted and trapped by an evil villian. In Northern Passage Baree turns the tables and is delightful at helping to stop this awful person. Jeff is not in this one often but it is wonderful to see the character he chose to play. Good Guy. (Info supplied by Cynthia Paroski, Co-President of the Jeff Fahey Internet Fan Club.)

"Serpent's Lair"

...as Tom...1995. R. A young couple buys their first new apartment, and become the owners of a strange cat, once belonging to the previous owner, who died a horrible death. Tom starts having erotic dreams and Alex, his wife, starts getting terriorized by the cat, which lands her in the hosipital. Tom is visited by the dead man's "sister" who turns out to be a succubus (female demon who sleeps with men) intent on having a baby with Tom. Jeff is a good guy.
Jeff Fahey in "Serpent's Lair"

"Sketch Artist II: Hands that See"

...as Jack Whitfield...1995. R.
Jeff is again sketch artist Jack Whitfied. Courteney Cox plays a blind woman, who claims to be able to tell what the rapist who attacked her looks like. Great movie, though you must see Sketch Artist I before this one. Great chemistry between Fahey and Cox. Good guy. Notes - Second movie with Cox, the other being "Curiosity Kills."

Jeff Fahey and Courtenay Cox in "Sketch Artist II"

"Virtual Seduction"

...as Liam...1995. R.
Plays a photographer who's fiance is murdered, and years later he uses VR to see her again. Very interesting, especially the finger, cough, painting scene :D Good guy. Notes - a.k.a Addicted to Love.
Amy Dolenz and Jeff Fahey in "Virtual Seduction"

1994


"Freefall"

...as Dex Dellum ...1994. R.
Plays a magazine editor who uses his fiancee for a shady deal, and turns out to be a rotten Interpol agent (I think). Stupid (well depends on who you ask). I fast forwarded through most of it, to get to Jeff's scenes, who has to play second fiddle to Eric "I think I'm good looking" Roberts :P Bad guy. Notes: Listen for Jeff's attempt at a Texas accent, he doesn't quite make it.

"Temptation"

...as Eddie Lanarsky ...1994. R.
Plays a ex-convict out for revenge against his former partner who set him up, ends up falling for said partner's wife. Takes place mainly on a boat. O.k. Some interesting loves scenes. Would have preferred a better ending. Hazy.
Jeff Fahey in "Temptation"

"Wyatt Earp"

...as Ike Clanton ...1994. PG-13.
O.K. Only saw most of the scenes that Jeff was in, the movie was a bit too drawn out. I hear "Tombstone" is better, but this one has Jeff *shrug*, have to look closely to recognize him under the beard. Bad guy. Notes - Second movie with Kevin Costner, the other being "Silverado."

1993


"Blindsided"

...1993...Frank McKenna. PG-13.
Good movie. Plays a guy who is blinded temporarily during a robbery. Falls for the wrong kind of woman. Would have preferred a better ending. Good guy.

"The Hit List"

...as Charlie Pike ...1993. R.
Plays a hit man, who falls for a client. Good movie. Stupid ending. Good guy (or depends on who you ask).

"In the Company of Darkness"

...as Will McCaid ...1993.
Jeff plays a good cop that is tracking down a serial killer, and a female cop at the same time, talk about talent! :) There are wonderful shots of him in this one, even though most of it is spent in a van listening to the female cop via microphone. Certainly a movie worth watching! Jeff - good guy. Notes - Sitcom stars Helen Hunt ("Jamie" on Mad About You) and Steven Webber ("Joe" on Wings) take a dramtic turn in this movie. (Info provided by Cynthia Paroski, Co-President of the Jeff Fahey Internet Fan Club.)

Jeff Fahey and Helen Hunt in "In The Company of Darkness"

"Quick"

...as Muncie ...1993. R.
Plays a bad cop, who's girlfriend is a hit woman, which he uses to bump off people for money. Stupid (but depends on who you ask.). Bad guy.

"Woman of Desire"

...as Jack Lynch...1993. R.
Plays a yacht captain, pulled into a web of deception and murder. Stupid, but worth it to see Jeff's behind and a interesting love scene on a Harley with Bo Derek :)~~~~~~~~~~ . Notes - Jeff and Robert Mitchum meet on this movie, later Robert played Jeff's dad on Jeff's show "The Marshal". Hazy.

Jeff Fahey in "Woman of Desire"

1992


"Iran: Days of Crisis"

...as Hamilton Jordan...1992.
Long, but gripping account of the 1979 American hostage crisis in Iran, which resulted in Jimmy Carter losing the presidency to Ronald Reagan. Excellent performances all round, including our Jeff, who plays Hamilton Jordan, Carter's White House Chief of Staff (I thought he was a bit young to have such an important position!) who does his utmost to help resolve the crisis.Definitely a good guy. Notes - a.k.a L'Amerique en otage (Info supplied by Janine Erasmus).

"The Lawnmower Man"

...as Jobe Smith...1992. R.
Plays a retarded lawnmower man, who becomes super smart through VR with the help of Pierce Brosnan (Bond, James Bond :D), then goes nuts. One of his best :) Hazy.
Jeff Fahey and Pierce Brosnan in "Lawnmower Man"

"Sketch Artist"

...as Jack Whitfield...1992. R.
Plays a sketch artist, who's picture of a suspect looks suspiciously like his wife (Sean Young). Good movie. See this before the sequel. Another great shot of Jeff's behind :)~~~~~~~ Good guy.
Jeff Fahey in "Sketch Artist 1"

"Wrangler"

...as Ben Creed ...1992. PG.
Plays a Australian ranch owner, who helps save the ranch of the woman he loves. Excellent movie! Great acting. Jeff is to die for in a tux. Good guy. Notes - a.ka Fighting Creed, The (1989) Outback (1989) Minnamurra (1989)

1991


"Body Parts"

...as Bill Chrashank ...1991. R.
He looks great in it, but there are times I felt it went overboard in the gore department. He is the good guy. Saves the day in fact. (Info supplied by Kathryn Oyler)

"Iron Maze"

...as Barry Mikowski ...1991. R.
Plays a bell hop in a once booming town fallen to ruin, who falls for the wife (Bridget Fonda) of a Japanese business man who wants to put up a amusement park, who he later says he killed, but did he? Weird movie, hard to follow at times. Jeff looks kind of wierd with really long hair. Good guy.

Jeff Fahey and Bridget Fonda in "Iron Maze"

1990


"Curiosity Kills"

...as Michael Manus ...1990. R.
C. Thomas Howell and Rae Dawn Chong play neighbours in an apartment building who try to solve the murder of another neighbour. Jeff plays a actor who moves into the dead neighbour's apartment, he seems normal but is he?. Note: though this is the first movie with Jeff and Courteney Cox, they shared only a small scene, the other movie they are in together is "Sketch Artist II". Good movie, suspenseful.

C.Thomas Howell, Rae Dawn Chong, Courteney Cox, and Jeff Fahey in "Curiosity Kills"

"Impulse"

...as Stan ...1990. R.
Plays a lawyer who teams up with a female cop (Theresa Russell) to catch some bad guys, falls for her. One of the best movies in my humble opinion of Jeff's. Good guy.
Teresa Russell and Jeff Fahey from "Impulse"

"The Last of the Finest"...a.k.a "Blue Heat"

...as Ricky Rodriguez ...1990. R.
A police drama that has a lot of action. The language got to me in the end. But he plays a young police officer who again, helps to save the day. Good guy. (info supplied by Kathryn Oyler). Notes - a.k.a Blue Heat.

Jeff Fahey, Bill Paxton, Joe Pantoliano and Brian Dennehy in "Last of the Finest"

"Parker Kane"

...as Parker Kane...1990. Jeff plays Parker Kane, a private investigator (former police detective). His childhood friend (David Caruso) is blackmailing his boss for illegally disposing of toxic waste and he gets murdered in front of Parker. Much to the dismay of the entire police department (!), Parker vows to find the killer himself. His character is a very good guy, charming, quirky! Amanda Pays is the token love interest. Marisa Tomei and Billy Wirth have small parts. Sherry Springfield from NYPD Blue plays his ex-wife. Jeff is incredibly handsome and this is one of my very favorite movies of his! (Info provided by Kim Schultz.) Notes - This is the only movie so far that Jeff has used his dancing talent, but only briefly :(

"White Hunter, Black Heart"

...as Peter Viertel...1990. PG.
Plays a writer who helped with the making of the "African Queen", this movie isn't about the "African Queen", but very similar to it, also stars Clint Eastwood, as a John Huston type director, (Huston directed the "Afican Queen"). One of Jeff's better movies. A bit long, but great acting. Good guy.

Clint Eastwood and Jeff Fahey in "White Hunter, Black Heart"

1989


"The Serpent of Death"

...as Jake Bonner...1989. R.
Jeff plays an archiologist who discoveres an ancient ststue of Alexander The Great, Concealed inside is a clay serpent, Jake is now drawn into mystery and intrigue. The serpent is stolen from the museum and Jake is accused of the theft. He now has to find out who set him up and why. Jake begins an adventure to find the serpent and what it means, he is joined by Rene a beautiful girl he met whilst teaching a class about Alexander the Great. The search takes them from Greece to Egypt, where they are pursued by villans who also want the treasure that may or may not exist. There is some beautiful scenery, some fast action and some great close up's of Jeffs georgeous eyes as well as his bod. It is well worth finding this movie, you will not be disapointed. (Info provided by Tracey Ekdahl).
Jeff Fahey in "The Serpent of Death"

"Trueblood"

...as Donny Trueblood...1989. R.
O.k. Plays an ex-delinquent turned Merchant Marine, who comes back to the town where he is still wanted for a murder he didn't commit, to find his brother in a gang, and tries to save him. Brother Vs. Brother movie, that works out in the end. Good guy.

1988


"Split Decisions"

...as Ray McGuinn ...1988. R.
Finds him as a boxer. He dies fairly early in the movie and his younger brother fights to avenge his death. Has some good moments. He's a good guy in this one, too. (Info supplied by Kathryn Oyler).

1987


"Backfire"

...as Donny McAndrew ...1987. R.
Jeff is a Vietnam vet, VERY rich, very disturbed after his terrible experiences. Karen Allen is his wife who is plotting with Dean Martin Jr, her lover, to get rid of him. Keith Carradine is a mysterious drifter who breezes into town after Jeff has fallen victim to Karen's evil plan, which did not succeed totally as he is still alive, but in a catatonic coma. The ending reveals a most excellent and unexpected twist. Good guy. (Info supplied by Janine Erasmus).

1986


"Psycho III"

...as Duane Duke ...1986. R.
I didn't like this one a lot. He plays a drifter who is hazy at best. One time you think he's okay, the next time you think he's not. He has a scene where he wears very little. That was interesting. (Info supplied by Kathryn Oyler).

1985


"The Execution of Raymond Graham"

This was done for television in 1988 or 1989. He plays a convict on death row, hoping for a reprieve. There are m any famous faces in this one. I've only seen it on video, but it's really a good show. I highly recommend it. (Info supplied by Kathryn Oyler).

Jeff Fahey in "The Execution of Raymond Graham"

"Silverado"

Better then "Wyatt Earp". Not of enough Jeff for my taste. Bad guy.Jeff Fahey in "Silverado"
