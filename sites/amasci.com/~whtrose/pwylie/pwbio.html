Biographical Info

Full Name: Paul Stanton Wylie


Paul doing a a spread eagle in "Apollo 13" (Photo Copyrighted by Joyce Torres).

Place of Birth: Dallas, Texas
Lives: Cambridge (or Somerville), MA
Date of Birth: October 28, 1964
Parents: Robert and B.L Wylie
Age: 35
Hair: Brown
Eyes: Blue (sigh! to quote Elton John "I love blue eyes")
Height: 5'4"

Paul skating to "Mission: Impossible". (Photo Copyrighted by Barry Mittan.)

Marital Status: Single
Education:
College - Harvard University, B.A in political science, graduated cum laude in 1991, plans to go to law school someday.
High School - Colorado Academy
Other Jobs: Hosting (Evening of Championship Skating), commentating (speed skating - 94 Olympics Lillehammer), writing articles for newspapers and magazines (Sports Illustrated, Newsweek, and Dallas Morning News), and speaking for corporations (Nabisco, Maxwell House, IBM).
Coach: Evy Scotvold
Choreographer: Mary Scotvold
Pet: A Dog named Hans



Paul after skating to "Apollo 13" at the Sergei Tribute. (Photo Copyrighted by Caroline Eaton).

Hobbies and Faves

(Note: The addition of the years in front of some of the replies is because Paul changed his answers when asked for the 96 - 97 season of SOI.)
Hobbies: Waterskiing, biking, reading, scuba diving, hiking, camping, skiing and listening to music.
Favorite Music: Hootie and the Blowfish, U2, Michael W. Smith
Favorite Books: 'Franny & Zooey' by J.D. Salinger, 'The Bible,' 'The Firm.'
Favorite Television Shows: 'ER,' 'Friends,' 'Seinfeld,' 'Murder One.'
Favorite Comedian: Robin Williams, Sinbad
Favorite Actress: (95 - 96) Meg Ryan, Grace Kelly. (96 - 97) Emma Thompson
Favorite Actor: (95 - 96) Tom Hanks, Kenneth Branagh. (96 - 97) Mel Gibson
Favorite Movies: (95 - 96) 'Ferris Bueller's Day Off,' 'Apollo 13,' and 'Apocalypse Now.' (96 -97) "Braveheart".
Dream Car: "My own car, driving into my garage. No really!"
Person I Would Most Like to Meet: Billy Graham, Tom Hanks
Favorite Dinner (95 - 96) Escargots Bourgnone, Filet Mignon with garlic potatoes and that 'great French brown sauce,' candlelight, great bottle of Bordeaux, (at fountain in Philadelphia.). (96 - 97) "Sushi at Nobu in Tribeca."

Quotes

What Is Your Idea of Perfect Happiness?: "On earth, seeing your children grow up into loving, intelligent people."

If You Were Not Skating Which Talent Would You Most Like to Have?: "That of a software developer."

How Do You Prepare For A Show: "At hotel I like to relax, listen to music and read a little. Once I get to the building, I stretch and warm up to make myself aggressive."

What Words Best Describe You: "Intense, Sensitive, Busy, always Thinking."

Accomplishment I Am Most Proud Of: "Graduating with honors from Harvard and winning the Silver Medal in Albertville within a year of each other."

Philosophy On Life: (95 - 96) "Each one should use whatever gift he has received to serve others, faithfully administering God's grace in its various forms." (96 - 97) "Love my neighbor as myself."

What Is One Thing Your Friends Don't Know About You?: "I miss them."

Why Would You Pick Discover Card Stars on Ice Over Other Skating Shows?: "The production qualities of first rate lights, sound, choreography and costumes; the cast and the fact that the show develops your skating skills rather than forcing you to sit backstage and wait your turn."



(Taken from the SOI Home Page).

"I'm so lost I'm in Zimbabwe" in reference to rehearsals for 92 - 93 SOI to which Scott Hamiton replied "Do you get frequent filer miles for that? I hope so!"

"Is Christopher Bowman in the building?" after his brilliant performance to "Carmina Bruana" at Skates x 2, during which the lights blinked on and off.

"Chris, you shouldn't do that!" also in reference to the lights blinking on and off during his routine to "Carmina Bruana" at Skates x 2.

"Since I'm performing the same programs over and over again, I feel that I want to skate to something that really compells me, and something that will bring me to a different place every time I take the ice, like an actor would prepare to go. I don't want to just put music on that would be background music, but I want the music to transform both me and the audience and to take us to a place where we're thinking on a different level than just skating." During an interview at the 1995 Pro Legends competition.

A heartflet "Thank God for Albertville!" or "God bless Albertville!" from a Holiday Festival on Ice Reno show when Kristi Yamaguchi was showing him around the area! Its something we can all agree with!

Paul hosts the Jimmy Fund show Evening of Championship Skating almost every year, but this year, 1996, he dispensed with the teleprompter and after he skated his new routine "Adagio by Bach", he had to go back to hosting, but they made him retape it: "They didn't like the huffing and puffing, "said Paul, "I'm getting older...."

About the 92 Olympics and winning the Silver


(Photo Copyrighted by Barry Mittan.)

His mental approach to skating in the Olympics: "It was a matter of reducing my brain".

"I'll just try to perform the thing and not think about the ramifications". - in response to how he'll skate the Olympic long program.

"Hey, just how much of a Cinderella story do you guys want?'' at his press conference after he won the Olympic silver, when someone said he could have won the gold.

"I didn't even look at them, I'm sorry. What were they?" - in reponse to a question about his scores given to him by the judges.

When asked if he ever dreamt of winning an Olympic medal he replied "Yeah....but I always woke up" (or something like that).

About being in the Olympics: "I always wanted to skate well in the Olympics," he said. "I wanted to be able to say, 'Yeah, that was great, that was my moment.' It's the happiest moment of my life."


Paul doing a gourgeous Ina Bauer to "Mission: Impossible" (photo copyrighted by Margaret Burwell.)

Some Highlights

Moved to Denver, Colorado at the age of 11 and trained for ten years with Carlo Fassi
At 15, won National Junior Pairs title with Dana Graham
At 16, won U.S. National Junior Championships and World Junior Championships
Moved to Boston in 1985 to train with Evy and Mary Scotvold
Entered Harvard the next year and graduated cum laude in government in 1991
Won Olympic Silver Medal on February 15, 1992 skating to Henry V
Has been touring with SOI since 1992, and will *hopefully* continue (Paul, you reading this?)
