The Jeff Fahey Internet Fan Club was started by me, Sonja Marie, in Sept . 95 shortly after I started The Jeff Fahey Mailing List. Deciding I needed help, Gina Grone, joined me as Co-President of the Fan Club, and we were later joined by Cynthia Paroski. Much thanks must go to Elspeth Fahey, who put up the first ever Jeff Fahey web site, The Jeff Man Pages.

The Jeff Fahey Internet Fan Club is an unofficial fan club (as of now) for Jeff Fahey.
