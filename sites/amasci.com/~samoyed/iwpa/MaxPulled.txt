Max Pulled 

 I am often asked, "What is the maximum weight pulled and what breeds pull the
most?". Thus, I put together this page to help answer the question. 

 I have divided the dog weights into 10 pound increments. Wt. of 30# includes dogs
from 30# through 39#. 

 The top two weights for each range are listed. This could be the same dog on two
separate days; and there could be more dogs that pulled the same weight. 

 The average is for all dogs in the range.
