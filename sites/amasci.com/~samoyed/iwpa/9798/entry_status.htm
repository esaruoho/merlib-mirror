IWPA Pull Entry & Distribution Status

This is a copy of my worksheets to keep track of pulls, when I receive results, have entered them, posted on the web and mailed to the newsletter and in what newsletter they were published.

The columns have the following meanings:

  • 1st Date -- First date of the pull events
  • Last Date -- Last date of the pull events if multiple days, else blank.
  • Location -- City and state/province in which the pull occurred.
  • Pull #s -- Range of numbers assigned to the pulls for this season.
  • Received -- Date I received the pulls for entry.
  • Entered -- Date I entered the pulls. Standings my not have been calculated.
  • Online -- Date I recorded the pulls and the resulting standings on The Web.
  • Mailed NL -- Date I mailed the pulls and the resulting standings to The Newsletter.
  • In NL -- Newsletter in which the pulls were published.
  • Fut. Cal. -- When the pulls were listed in the Future Pull Calendar on The Web.
  • Org. Inf. -- Date I mailed a copy of The Web Pull Calendar to the organizer.
