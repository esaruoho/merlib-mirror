 Pull Location Migration 

 The following map shows pull locations for the 1994/1995 pull
season (red, pink, white circles), and for the 1999/2000 season (green
circles). [See the legend at right] This map is intended to
illustrate how dispersed the pulls are, and show that regions die and others are
born. 
 A few years prior to 1993, there were no pulls in
California. A few years later, the Southern/Central California pulls died
off, but Northern California started activity for about four years, then
suddenly died. 
 The Pacific Northwest had a large dispersal of pulls, and is now
narrowed to just a few places. And prior to 1993, we had many pulls around
Portland, Northern Oregon, and Southern Washington; mostly due to one organizer. 
 Note how Colorado was more dispersed, and now they are
concentrated into one area. 
 Minnesota had several single events spread across the state, and
now that is gone. And, Missouri no longer has activity. 
 Michigan has continually had pulls, but they have moved around
over time. 
 And; now look at the wide dispersal of pulls on the East coast,
and into Ohio, WV, Ontario. This has been the latest hot spot that has grown
over the past three years. 
 We need pull organizers!! 
 Especially, if there are no pulls in your area, consider taking
the initiative to getting pulls started. Look to your local dog clubs to
get a pool of talent that can assemble equipment and the manpower to conduct a
pull. IWPA will lend any help we can to muster assistance on getting you
started. Even if you have pulls in your area; consider
becoming an organizer. Many areas have only one organizer; and when they get
tired and quit, so do the pulls.
