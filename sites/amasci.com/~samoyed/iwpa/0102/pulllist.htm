Color Coding in Pull Listings

If the handler's name is listed with the background color of the page, then the handler is the owner of the dog.

If the handler's name has a different background color, then he is not the owner.  The two background colors imply the following meaning.
The handler belongs to the same membership as the owner The handler belongs to a different membership than the owner

If the ratio of weight pulled to weight of the dog is high enough to qualify for a leg in a Working Dog degree, then the Percentage column has one of the following background colors to indicate the level of the degree in which the dog qualifies.

IWPA Working Dog

IWPA Working Dog Excellent

IWPA Working Dog Superior
