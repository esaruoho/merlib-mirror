 Australian Weight Pull 

 There is apparently a group in Australia that conducts weight pulls. They are not
affiliated with IWPA, but it sounds like their rules are not unlike ours; and possibly
based upon some of ours. Since they are south of the equator, their seasons are opposite
of ours. They pull from about March through September. It sounds like they may have one or
less pulls per month. 

 One such individual, Brett Jarvis , forwarded to
me the results of one of their pulls. The following table is an html conversion of the
Excel spreadsheet sent by Brett. It is in metric units as used in
Australia. 

 I also converted to English units and it follows this table. Or
download the Excel sheet including both units.
