Points Change

The point for being last no longer applies to those placing 1st, 2nd, or 3rd.  At The Board Meeting, the original intent of this rule was discussed.  The thinking was as follows:

Those placing 1st, 2nd, or 3rd receive points, even if they beat no other dogs.  Those placing 4th or beyond, receive the total of the number of dogs beat.  Thus, the last place dog qualified, but would receive nothing for his effort.  The Board felt the point was originally meant to be a consulation prize, but the wording in the rules made it apply to all positions.   Thus, this was a correction to the wording of the rule to make it mean what was thought to be the original intent of the rule.
