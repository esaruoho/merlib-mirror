Fairhill Weight Pull
Fairhill, Maryland

Pull: 28 Organizer(s): Melissa Kehler-Swoyer Wheels Region: 6
Grass Track: It was a tough surface. It had a slight uphill grade and it developed some "ruts". This event was held in conjunction with a gig race. Saturday we had a seminar that went over IWPA rules and then we had a novice pull. Had a nice turnout in spite of rain. Date: 12/6/98
Weather: Unseasonably warm and sunny
