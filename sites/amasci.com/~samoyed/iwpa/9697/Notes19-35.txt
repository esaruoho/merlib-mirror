 Sorry for not having pulls for the last newsletter. All the usual excuses: busy on other things and procrastinating.

pulls I have received to date. 
 E R R O R S

 Pull 23, Portland 
 Aaron Brown (non-member) handled Chaos (owned by Molly McCarty &#150; a member) and it was listed as 
non-provisional. Since pulls for points require the dog to be owned by a member and the handler to be a 
member, I had to downgrade this pull to provisional.
