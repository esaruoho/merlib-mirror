Statistician’s Notes

(Pulls 19 thru 35)
26 January, 1997

Sorry for not having pulls for the last newsletter. All the usual excuses: busy on other things and procrastinating. Then the storm hit at just the wrong time for me to reach deadline. Well, I am now caught-up. This is all the pulls I have received to date.

E R R O R S

Pull 22, Portland

Cobra was listed with a weight of 2. Used the weight of 135 lb. from the previous day.

Pull 23, Portland

Aaron Brown (non-member) handled Chaos (owned by Molly McCarty – a member) and it was listed as non-provisional. Since pulls for points require the dog to be owned by a member and the handler to be a member, I had to downgrade this pull to provisional.

Pull 33, St. Louis

Snippy’s time was 104, which would be 1 minute and 44 seconds. Assume they meant 1:04 or 64.
