The purpose of this site is to illustrate what you can expect from some of the route planning programs and street atlases; and how they compare.

The question often is posed; which one is best? And, you will usually get a biased answer because the person answering has a single viewpoint. Well, when they offer similar features, it depends on your needs, your likes, what you are used to. The question cannot really be answered. The best approach is to give a good comparison with plusses and minuses of each. That is the goal of these pages.

And, so far, everyone is essentially satisfied with Microsoft's and DeLorme's products, and many use them for planning long trips. Little input yet on other vendors. (I do have some on Rand McNally's and plan to have it up soon)

At this time, this is mostly my viewpoint. I have Microsoft's Automap series; and I have seen, played with and discussed DeLorme's Map-N-Go. In order to make these pages meaningful, I need input from users of all the mapping programs. Please feel free to contact me, Ron Bowser. Illustrations are presently from the Microsoft products. I will point out differences where known and will endeavor to add comparison illustrations where significant; but I do have a disk storage constraint.

Herein, you can find the following:

  • How I plan a vacation - all text & very verbose, detailed tutorial.

I will point out differences where known. The images are GIF's captured from an Automap window running at 75% of my screen with 1024x768 pixels and 256 colors.

  • User reviews (new reviews Nov 96) with the actual words from users who sent me somewhat extensive reviews.
