Athanasian Creed. 

 At Morning Prayer. 
 Upon these Feasts; Christmas Day , the Epiphany , Saint Matthias, Easter Day, Ascension Day, Whitsunday , Saint John Baptist , Saint James , Saint Bartholomew , Saint Matthew , Saint Simon and Saint Jude , Saint Andrew ,
 and upon Trinity Sunday , shall be sung or said at Morning Prayer, instead of the Apostles' Creed, this Confession of our Christian Faith, commonly called the Creed of Athanasius , by the Minister and people standing. 

 Quicunque vult. 
 HOSOEVER will be saved : before all things it is necessary that he hold the Catholick Faith. 
 &#160; &#160;Which Faith except every one do keep whole and undefiled : without doubt he shall perish everlastingly. 
 &#160; &#160;And the Catholick Faith is this: That we worship one God in Trinity, and Trinity in Unity; 
 &#160; &#160;Neither confounding the Persons : nor dividing the Substance. 
 &#160; &#160;For there is one Person of the Father, another of the Son : and another of the Holy Ghost. 
 &#160; &#160;But the Godhead of the Father, of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, is all one : the Glory equal, the Majesty co-eternal. 
 &#160; &#160;Such as the Father is, such is the Son : and such is the Holy Ghost. 
 &#160; &#160;The Father uncreate, the Son uncreate : and the Holy Ghost uncreate. 
 &#160; &#160;The Father incomprehensible, the Son incomprehensible : and the Holy Ghost incomprehensible. 
 &#160; &#160;The Father eternal, the Son eternal : and the Holy Ghost eternal. 
 &#160; &#160;And yet they are not three eternals : but one eternal. 
 &#160; &#160;As also there are not three incomprehensibles, nor three uncreated : but one uncreated, and one incomprehensible. 
 &#160; &#160;So likewise the Father is Almighty, the Son Almighty : and the Holy Ghost Almighty. 
 &#160; &#160;And yet they are not three Almighties : but one Almighty. 
 &#160; &#160;So the Father is God, the Son is God : and the Holy Ghost is God. 
 &#160; &#160;And yet they are not three Gods : but one God. 
 &#160; &#160;So likewise the Father is Lord, the Son Lord : and the Holy Ghost Lord. 
 &#160; &#160;And yet not three Lords : but one Lord. 
 &#160; &#160;For like as we are compelled by the Christian verity to acknowledge every Person by himself to be both God and Lord; 
 &#160; &#160;So are we forbidden by the Catholick Religion : to say, There be three Gods, or three Lords. 
 &#160; &#160;The Father is made of none : neither created, nor begotten. 
 &#160; &#160;The Son is of the Father alone : not made, nor created, but begotten. 
 &#160; &#160;The Holy Ghost is of the Father and of the Son : neither made, nor created, nor begotten, but proceeding. 
 &#160; &#160;So there is one Father, not three Fathers; one Son, not three Sons : one Holy Ghost, not three Holy Ghosts. 
 &#160; &#160;And in this Trinity none is afore, or after other : none is greater, or less than another; 
 &#160; &#160;But the whole three Persons are co-eternal together : and co-equal. 
 &#160; &#160;So that in all things, as is aforesaid : the Unity in Trinity and the Trinity in Unity is to be worshipped. 
 &#160; &#160;He therefore that will be saved : must think thus of the Trinity. 
 &#160; &#160;Furthermore, it is necessary to everlasting salvation : that he also believe rightly the Incarnation of our Lord Jesus Christ. 
 &#160; &#160;For the right Faith is, that we believe and confess : that our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, is God and Man; 
 &#160; &#160;God, of the substance of the Father, begotten before the worlds : and Man of the substance of his Mother, born in the world; 
 &#160; &#160;Perfect God and perfect Man : of a reasonable soul and human flesh subsisting. 
 &#160; &#160;Equal to the Father, as touching his Godhead : and inferior to the Father, as touching his manhood; 
 &#160; &#160;Who, although he be God and Man : yet he is not two, but one Christ; 
 &#160; &#160;One, not by conversion of the Godhead into flesh : but by taking of the Manhood into God; 
 &#160; &#160;One altogether; not by confusion of Substance : but by unity of Person. 
 &#160; &#160;For as the reasonable soul and flesh is one man : so God and Man is one Christ; 
 &#160; &#160;Who suffered for our salvation : descended into hell, rose again the third day from the dead. 
 &#160; &#160;He ascended into heaven, he sitteth at the right hand of the Father, God Almighty : from whence he will come to judge the quick and the dead. 
 &#160; &#160;At whose coming all men will rise again with their bodies : and shall give account for their own works. 
 &#160; &#160;And they that have done good shall go into life everlasting : and they that have done evil into everlasting fire. 
 &#160; &#160;This is the Catholick Faith : which except a man believe faithfully, he cannot be saved. 
 &#160; &#160;Glory be to the Father, &c. 
 &#160; &#160;As it was in the beginning, &c.
