Evening Prayer before 1662. 

 Evening Prayer before 1662. 

 1549 
 Begin with the Lord's Prayer . 
 Continue from "O God, make speed..." ; substituting "me" for "us". 
 Continue from "Glory be to the Father..." to "Praise ye the lord" ; from Easter through Trinity Sunday only, add "Alleluia" . 
 Insert the Venite, Exultemus Domine . 
 Continue from the Psalms of the Day and go through the Magnificat . 
 Continue from the Second Lesson through Nunc Dimittis . 
 Insert "Lord have mercy on us. Christ have mercy on us. Lord have mercy on us." 
 Recite the Apostle's Creed . 
 Continue from the Lord's Prayer through "And take not thy holy Spirit from us." . 
 Insert "The Lord be with you. And with thy spirit. Let us pray." 
 Continue from the Collect of the Day through the Third Collect . 

 1552 
 Open at Evening Prayer and continue to "Praise ye the lord" . 
 Continue with the Psalms of the Day and go through Nunc Dimittis or Deus Misereatur . 
 Insert "Lord have mercy on us. Christ have mercy on us. Lord have mercy on us." 
 Continue from the Apostle's Creed through the Third Collect . 

 1559 and 1604 
 Open at Evening Prayer and continue to "Praise ye the lord" . In 1604, add " The Lord's name be praised. " 
 Continue from the Psalms of the Day through O Lord, shew thy mercy... And take not thy holy Spirit from us. In 1559, substitute "Queen" for "King". 
 Continue from the Collect of the Day through the Third Collect .
