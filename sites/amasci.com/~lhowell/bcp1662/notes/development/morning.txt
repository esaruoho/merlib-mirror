 Morning Prayer before 1662. 

 1549 
 Open at the Lord's Prayer and continue to "O Lord, open thou..." substituting "my" for "our" and "me" for "us". 
 Continue from "Glory be to the Father..." to "Praise ye the lord" ; from Easter through Trinity Sunday only, add "Alleluia" . 
 Continue with Venite, Exultemus Domine and go through Te Deum ; in Lent only, substitute Benedicite omnia Opera . 
 Continue from the Second Lesson through Benedictus . 
 Insert "Lord have mercy on us. Christ have mercy on us. Lord have mercy on us." 
 Recite the Apostle's Creed ; On Christmas-day, the Epiphany, Easter, Ascension, Pentecost, Trinity Sunday only, substitute Quicunque Vult . 
 Continue from the Lord's Prayer through the Collect for Grace . 

 1552 
 Open at Morning Prayer and continue to "Praise ye the lord" . 
 Continue with Venite, Exultemus Domine and go through Benedictus or Jubilate Deo . 
 Insert "Lord have mercy on us. Christ have mercy on us. Lord have mercy on us." 
 Continue from the Apostle's Creed through the Collect for Grace . 

 1559 and 1604 
 Open at Morning Prayer and continue to "Praise ye the lord" . 
 Continue from Venite, Exultemus Domine through O Lord, shew thy mercy... And take not thy holy Spirit from us. In 1559, substitute "Queen" for "King". 
 Continue from the Collect of the Day through the Collect for Grace .
