I am a staunch aficionado of Apple 
Macintosh computers, and have always exclusively owned and used them at
home. I program Wintel, however, to put food on my table. I work at the 
 China Lake Naval Air Weapons
Station for The U.S. Navy (you 
might've heard of them). I develop mission planning software for things 
that go boom. 

My philosophical interests are based on capitalism and libertarianism. I
find Ayn Rand's writing highly inspirational, but I am not an
Objectivist. (If you're interested in discussing this more with me, we
can do it over a cup of joe.) My leisure interests range from playing
piano (I'm the pianist for one service each week at my church ) to building and
shooting spud guns .

fiancee pretty much agrees with the description of me! ;-)
