I am reposting this from a thread that originated on one of the UCD 
newsgroups.  It's a crude attempt, with only ASCII pictures for 
illustration.  Note that I in no way will be held liable for your actions 
resulting from these instructions, including possession or use of a spud 
gun.  They were legal in California at the time I posted this; I make no 
implications as to their legality in other states.

===========================================================================

Go to the hardware store and buy:
The following parts of ABS pipe.  This is the BLACK plumbing pipe.  DO 
NOT USE PVC, or white, plumbing pipe as it can shatter.
* 3 feet length of 1.5-inch diameter pipe
* 1 foot length of 3-inch diameter pipe
* a 3-inch to 1.5-inch coupler
    looks like this:
     _
      \_
       _
     _/      (I know, it's crude, but hopefully you can see)
* I don't know what it's called, but basically, a THREADED 3-inch 
  diameter end piece, and accompanying screw cap.  The screw cap is a flat, 
  3-inch disk with threads along the edge, and a cubical piece sticking out 
  of the outside face, to grab onto in order to screw & unscrew it.  I 
  think the ring and cap together are simply called a screwable end cap.
* 1 bottle Plumbers' cement suitable for ABS.

You can probably buy the following at Walmart cheaper:
* 1 Coleman lantern igniter -- it's a little dial thingie with about a 
  2-3 inch shaft and a piece of flint on the end, such that when you flick 
  it vigorously (in a motion as if you're snapping your fingers), the flint 
  sparks.
* one 3-foot long, 1-inch diameter (actually, as big a diameter as 
  possible up to 1.5 inches) wooden rod.  This will be your ramrod.
* Berryman B-12 Chemtool, which is carburetor/choke cleaner, is the 
  propellant I use.  It seems to work even better than hair spray.


Assembly instructions:

1. Couple the 1.5-inch pipe to the 3-inch pipe using the coupler (as if I 
   need to say it, yes, you should GLUE them very well.  They don't just 
   snap together).
2. Glue the 3-inch endpiece on the end of the 3-inch pipe (which, if you 
   haven't figured it out by now, is going to be your chamber) such that 
   you'll be able to screw and unscrew the cap on the end of the gun.
3. Drill a hole in the middle of the chamber piece (3-inch pipe) small 
   enough that the lantern igniter will fit through snugly.  Note: don't 
   GLUE the lantern igniter in there; you'll have to replace it eventually 
   when it wears out!
4. Put the lantern igniter in that hole.  It screws in; you'll see how if 
   you have the right lantern igniter.
5. File the edge at the end of the barrel, mostly along the outside but a 
   little bit on the inside as well, so that the edge is relatively sharp.  
   You will need it to be rather sharp to cut through the potato.
6. Let the gun dry a couple hours or whatever time the Plumbers' cement 
   says to.

The little square bracket on the end of the chamber represents the handle 
of the screw cap.


Shooting instructions:
1. Unscrew the cap from the chamber.
2. Take a spud.
3. Put it on the (barrel) end of the gun, and push down to cut a 1.5-inch 
   cross section through the potato.
4. If there's enough of the potato left for another round, save it.  
   Otherwise, discard potato scraps.
5. Push the potato down the barrel with your 3-foot ramrod, until it's 
   most of the way down but not all the way.  A good measure of judgement is 
   if you're grabbing the ramrod with your fist on the very end of the rod, 
   push down until the edge of your hand touches the barrel end, and go no 
   further.
6. Make sure that the lantern igniter dial is pulled OUT all the way.  
   Otherwise, you may saturate the flint with igniter fluid and burn your 
   flint up in one shot.
7. Squirt a VERY short squirt of B-12 Chemtool (less than half a second 
   long) into the chamber.  Just aim straight in.  Don't use the stupid 
   little straw that comes with the can of Chemtool.  Screw the cap back on 
   the end of the chamber as quickly as possible.
8. Hold the gun at your SIDE, about waist level.  Point it at about a 
   45-degree angle for optimum distance.  Flick the lantern igniter and 
   watch your spud go flying.


A couple VERY IMPORTANT precautions to anyone building a spud gun per my 
instructions:

1. Never, EVER flick the lantern igniter while you have the cap off the 
end.  There have been people who can't get it to spark, and unscrew the 
cap, look in the chamber, and say "gee, I wonder why it's not firing" as 
they flick the igniter, only to get 3rd-degree burns on their face as a 
huge flame bursts from the end.

2. After firing, while all your friends are oo-ing and ah-ing, unscrew 
the cap and let it air out.  It's best to get the remaining vapors out of 
there before you fire it again.
