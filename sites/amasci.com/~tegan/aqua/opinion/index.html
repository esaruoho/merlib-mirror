Yet Another Opinion Page

Ask any group of five comic book fans what they think about the current hot topic of comics, and you'll get at least six opinions. Such is life. Part of the joy of collecting and reading comics is having strong opinions on them. It's certainly safer than a lot of opinions you can have, and a lot more fun.

Rapid Reviews

This is my own version of a weekly reviews column. I don't promise every comic book I read will be in here, but I'm not limiting myself to just comic books, either. What I'm trying to do here is give a quick impression of each book I've read each week without too many spoilers. No critical analysis from me, that's too much effort (and would take the fun out of the books). These may seem a little shallow, but that's intentional. These are first impressions with the goal of letting you know whether or not I found it worth my money, not springboards for discussion nor in-depth coverage.

Also check out my reviews in the Stupid Little Rants section of this page for more reviews and opinions on the state of the industry.
