Aquaman Sightings

This page is my attempt to list all the appearances of Aquaman, ever.

This is in plain old text, and in the more detailed lists I've included notes that I've made while searching for these books. Some of this information is wrong. I have not located and confirmed all these books, and therefore there are mistakes in this list. Do not buy a book solely on the strength of it being in this list.

I plan on completely revamping this list in the future, and including more information about reprints and other appearances. The current format has the full list on the Main Sightings List, and sublists with more details.

The range of sightings in this books goes from "Aquaman as main character" to "Aquaman mentioned". I will reapply my ratings system if there is any demand for it.
