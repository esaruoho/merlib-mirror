The Aquaman Mailing List Page

This list has been set up for:

  1. Discussion of the DC Comics Character Aquaman, his current and past books, his supporting cast, and any merchandise or other books related to him.
  2. General discussion of the comics industry, preferably when it affects Aquaman or the DC Universe.
  3. Information and niceties between list members, relating to their current projects and goals, especially when related to Aquaman or comic books.

GROUNDRULES for Posting

  • No profanity. If you use profanity, you're off the list.

  • I will allow arguments, since good arguments are often just really good discussion. There is a difference between good arguments and "flames," though. Name-calling, insults, and general nastiness, all of which are "flames" by the 'net definition of the word, will not be tolerated. Such action will result in ONE warning. If the warning does not convince the offending member to control her/his temper, he/she will be removed from the list.

  • Graphics and other binary files are not allowed on the list. Please do not send such beasties along. These overload the digest and cause me many headaches. I will get very annoyed if you torture me this way. There is an FTP site set up for binary files relating to Aquaman, you can send the file to me and ask me to add it to the FTP site and announce it. Or you can announce you have it, and send it to individuals who ask for it. Just don't send it to the list.

  • Along the same lines, please don't send HTMLized documents to the list. If you have a mail program that sends messages as both HTML and plain text, please turn off the HTML side of things. Although these don't crash the digest, they can be a headache to digest readers. They also tend to double the size of your e-mail message.

  • Advertisements from list members promoting their own work will be allowed as long as the ad is not intrusive and is related to Aquaman or comic books. For instance, I have no problem with someone mentioning their web page about Spider-Man 2099, and asking for feedback. If the same message is posted every day for a week, it will be considered instrusive, and the offending member will be asked to stop. I suggest that ads be limited to once a month, and be plain text.

  • Copyrighted material is forbidden, of course. This list follows Usenet standards in regards to copyright and reposted material. Please don't type in Tony Isabella's latest column and send it to the list. It's illegal and immoral. If you repost a note from elsewhere on the net, include attributions, and maybe ask the person if the information can be reposted first. There are several on-line comics information magazines and columns: please include a pointer to them rather than posting them to the list. Small excerpts from such columns are allowed, but include a pointer if possible, and all attributions. By "small" I mean no more than a paragraph or two.

  • Do not send notes from this list to another list. If people want to see what is on this list, I expect them to join it. Sending notes from this list to another list will get you booted if I find out about it.

  • If I learn of off-list harrassment that resulted from a discussion/argument on the list, I will consider taking extreme action. I do not want the kind of people who harrass other people for disagreeing with them on this list.

  • Use common sense and common courtesy, and you won't have any problems with the list.
