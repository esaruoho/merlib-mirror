    Note: This incredible, and incredibly detailed, mini-series give the former Aqualad a whole lot of power, and makes him the last of the Teen Titans to be updated. It should be noted that if you put together those original Titans, though, and set them up against the original Justice League, the battle would be one talked about for many years...

    This series is without a doubt the best retcon of any character ever in the DC universe. The details of Garth's past were carefully fit together into a coherent story, then given a push into the realms of magic that Peter David established in The Atlantis Chronicles. This is one series worth buying if you can find it.
