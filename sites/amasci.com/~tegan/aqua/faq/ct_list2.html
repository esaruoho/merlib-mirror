Editor's Note: I have to agree, the PAD origin seems to respect Legend of Aquaman. Recently, PAD mentioned on his newsgroup alt.fan.peter-david, that because "Legend of Aquaman" was published mere months before Atlantis Chronicles, he felt like he should respect the story in it. -Laura
