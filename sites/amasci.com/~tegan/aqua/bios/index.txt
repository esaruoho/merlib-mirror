 Aquaman Characters Who's Who 
 A less detailed account of as many Aquaman friends and foes as I can name. 

 Supporting Cast 
 There have been many people in Aquaman's life, including the lovely Mera, his wife, and his ever faithful friend Garth, known until recently as Aqualad. This is a list of those characters, and eventually will have overviews and appearances for them too.

 GARTH (Aqualad, Tempest, adopted son)
 MERA (Wife)
 TULA (Aquagirl, deceased)
 Arthur (Tom) Curry (Adoptive Father, deceased)
 Porm (Dolphin who saved him as a baby, deceased)
 Arthur Curry, Jr. (Aquababy, Mera's son, deceased)
 AJ (Mera's son, possibly Aquaman's son, born in another dimension)
 Vulko (Scientist, one-time ruler of Poseidonis)
 Atlan (Legendary Mage, Arthur's real father)
 Atlanna (Queen of Poseidonis, Arthur's mother, deceased)
 DOLPHIN (Mysterious water-breather, Arthur's close friend)
 Koryak (Arthur's son by Kako)
 Kako (Corona, one-time lover of Arthur)
 Tsunami (Japanese hero, ally)
 Deep Blue (daughter of Tsunami)
 Power Girl (descendant of Atlantis, friend of Aquaman)
 Quisp (Magical Sea Imp, out of continuity)

 Kordax (Introduced in The Atlantis Chronicles. Get them, read about him)
 Namor (Sub-Mariner, from another universe, not a villain, but a foe)
 Starro (Fought the entire Justice League many times, but also Aquaman alone)
 Fire-Trolls [Aquaman #1 (1960's)]
 Huntress [Aquaman #26 (1960's)]
 O.G.R.E [Aquaman #26 (1960's)]
 Awesome Threesome [Aquaman #36 (1960's)]
 Liquidator [Aquaman #38 (1960's)]
 Kobra [Aquaman #61 (1970's)]
 Captain Demo [Adventure #441 (1975)]

 Marine Marauder [Adventure #449 (1976)]
 Weather Wizard [Adventure #450 (1976)] (Actually Flash's Foe)
 Poseidon [Aquaman #9 (1960's) and Adventure #476 (1980)]
