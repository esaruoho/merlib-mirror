Elseworlds Checklist

This checklist is designed to be a simple list of titles to collect if you like DC's Elseworlds. This is not an official list. This is an unauthorized fan website. Please visit the official DC Comics Website.

Imaginary Stories

These are stories that fit the Elseworlds style, but either came out before the Elseworlds logo and line were conceived, or weren't considered Elseworlds for whatever reason. This is hardly a partial listing of imaginary stories, just the recent and/or notable.

  • Batman: The Dark Knight Returns 1-4 (also collected) (1986)
  • Batman: Digital Justice (1990)
  • Superman: Whatever Happened to the Man of Tomorrow? (first printed in Superman #423 and Action Comics #583) (1986)
  • Watchmen 1-12 (also collected) (1986-87)
  • Superman: The Animated Series: "Brave New Metropolis" (1997 animated TV episode)
  • Batman Beyond (1999 animated TV series and comic book adaptation)
  • The Batman Chronicles #21 (May 2000) (Solicited as Elseworlds, but has no logo)
  • Green Lantern: Dragon Lord 1-3 (Apr-Jun 2001) (reads like an Elseworld, but isn't)
  • Bizarro Comics HC (Jun 2001, SC Mar 2003) (featured story from Elseworld's 80-Page Giant)
  • Smallville (premiered Oct 2001) (Alternate history of Superman)
  • The Dark Knight Strikes Again 1-3 (Dec 2001 - Feb 2002)
  • Smallville: The Comic (Oct 2002)
  • Smallville Comic (ongoing bimonthly starting Mar 2003)

Tangent

Tangent is a shared universe in which things happened differently than in our own or in the DC Universes. Most stories read ok on their own, but are taken better as a whole. Again, these are not technically Elseworlds, but the effect for fans like me is the same.
