 What ARE These Things? 
 These are animated GIF files that each tell a quick and very silly tale. I got the idea from a similar set of tales based on Doctor Who. I admit, the art is grade-school level and the animation is terribly simple, but these do have the potential to amuse. The credits are ALL jokes. None of this should be taken seriously at all. Feel free to contribute your own works if you feel artistic enough to create some.
