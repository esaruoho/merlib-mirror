    Suspended Due To Ballot Stuffing

    I have had this survey up for a few years now, and this is the first time I've had a serious problem with someone voting repeatedly. You see, the survey program I was using was an extremely simple one, designed to take votes through e-mail on a feedback form. Because it was so simple, it was possible to vote more than once in a day. Indeed, possible to vote more than once in an hour. Luckily, I can catch it if someone does that, because I have an archive of the votes through my e-mail program. Unfortunately, I have to reset the votes manually. And I hate doing it. But it had only happened once or twice in the entire time I had the poll up, so it wasn't that big a deal. Until now. Someone, the same person even, stuffed the ballot THREE times. Even after I reset it, this idiot didn't get a clue and stop stuffing the vote. Thus, until I find a polling program that checks for idiots trying to vote repeatedly and stops them without me having to reset the thing, this poll isn't going to be working. I don't care how much you hate the new costume, vote ONCE A DAY you brainless doofus!!! Ahem. Or maybe I'll just wait until I regain my patience...
