from what is listed below, let me know).

 The Society also published Abandoned Cemeteries of Stanly County, NC but
they no longer have any copies available for purchase at this time. 

 Back issues of the Stanly Co. Genealogy Society Journal are $2 per issue 

 The address is: Stanly Co. Genealogy Society P. O. Box 31 Ablemarle, NC 28002-0031 In addition, there are two volumes of cemetery records (not published by Stanly County Genealogical Society) which can be purchased from: Lina J. Owens Boyd P. O.  There is also a 1850 census book published by SK Publications, which
contains photocopies of the handwritten records on the microfilm and an typed
index in the back of the book. So it is different from the 1850 census
transcription produced by the Stanly Co. Genealogical Society.
