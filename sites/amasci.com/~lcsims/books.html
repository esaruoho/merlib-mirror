BOOKS

There are several books available from the Stanly Co. Genealogy Society. Prices might have increased (if anyone orders a book and the price is different from what is listed below, let me know).

  • 1800-1840 Montgomery Censuses (no longer available--out of print)
  • 1850 Stanly Census ($5 + $2 s & h)
  • 1860 Stanly Census ($5 + $2 s & h)
  • 1870 Stanly Census ($15 + $2 s & h)
  • 1880 Stanly Census ($15 + $2 s & h)
  • Marriage Records 1851-1904 ($15 + $2 s & h)

Records of the Court of Pleas and Quarters, 1841-1850 ($12 + $2 s & h)

The Society also published Abandoned Cemeteries of Stanly County, NC but they no longer have any copies available for purchase at this time.

Back issues of the Stanly Co. Genealogy Society Journal are $2 per issue
