Radio programs

In Seattle, listen to 1 1/2 hours of continuous choir and organ music starting at 9:30pm on Sunday evenings.
  • The Compline service at St. Mark's in Seattle is broadcast live at 9:30pm on Sundays on KING-FM (98.1).
  • The Organ Loft radio network on KING-FM (98.1) on Sundays at 6:00am and 10:00pm. In Eugene OR, KWAX-FM (91.1) on Sundays at 8:00am and 6:00pm.
  • Pipedreams is not available in Seattle, but should be! NPR nationwide broadcast. You can listen to the last four programs on your computer from that website.

New Organ Projects

It seems like there's always an interesting project going on. Here are a few:
  • Calvary Lutheran Church in Bellevue has a new Swanson two manual organ.
  • University Presbyterian Church (UPC) is building a new Reuter organ, following the newly remodeled sanctuary. Cost is $1.4 million and it will be completed in November, 1999.
  • St. James Cathedral recently replaced the 1926 Casavant east apse organ with a Rosales organ, called the Archbishop Thomas J. Murphy Millenium Organ.
  • A new Fritts organ is complete at Pacific Lutheran University..
  • A new Fisk organ was recently installed at the Seattle Symphony's Benaroya Hall in July 2000.
