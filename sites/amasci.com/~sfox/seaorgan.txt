at 9:30pm on Sunday evenings.

The Compline 
service at St. Mark's in Seattle is broadcast live at 
9:30pm on Sundays on KING-FM (98.1).

 The Organ Loft 
radio network on KING-FM (98.1) on

 New Organ Projects 
It seems like there's always an interesting project going on. Here are a
few:

 Calvary Lutheran Church in Bellevue has a new Swanson two manual organ.
 University Presbyterian Church (UPC) is building a new 
 Reuter 
organ, following the newly remodeled sanctuary. Cost is $1.4 million and
it will be completed in November, 1999.
 St. James Cathedral recently replaced the 1926 Casavant east apse
organ
with a Rosales organ , called the
Archbishop Thomas J. Murphy Millenium Organ.

 A new Fritts
organ is complete at Pacific Lutheran University.. 
 A new Fisk organ was recently installed at the Seattle
Symphony's Benaroya Hall in July 2000.
