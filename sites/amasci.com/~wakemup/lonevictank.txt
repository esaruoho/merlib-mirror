UNTITLED XXIV

we look inside ourselves
for that
which is not completely
part of us
but is owned
by something
else

and
we arrive
at the cusp
of a moment
and turn the inward eye
toward the silver'd memory
of magic
of truth
of love
or to ourselves
we who live
who toil onward
in the same neighborhood
where these
beautiful deathless children
used to dwell
used to run
skipping
through the streets
through the fields
innocent of bullies
or the larger world

and
we weep
we cry out
for lost things
we grieve
and having watched
the course of our tears
run channels
through our hearts
grooves worn
to familiar smoothness
we pause to light
single candles
placing them
carefully
on windowsills
and hope against hope
for the return of the prodigal

--Vic Day
