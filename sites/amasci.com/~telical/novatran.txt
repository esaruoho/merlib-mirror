 Novalis English Translation Discussion 

It is one of the great yet rarely understood modern misfortunes
of the English Language that the majority of Novalis's ideas have
not been translated. We native speakers of English, or just non-German
speakers should be able to decide for ourselves what is and is not
interesting in the body of work Novalis left behind. This is one attempt to
do this. 

The following is some computer translation of some sections found on the
web.
Our thanks go to the people who first put this in html format.
