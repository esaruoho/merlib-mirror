Category Printer Feature

To get the best results and creative benefits you may want to have the Word Categories to reference outside the machine on paper. It is important to know what words you'll be merging into your sentences. You may find that the way ParaMind presents your Word Categories is good enough for you.

We thought it will be useful to have a clean way to print out the Word Categories by organizing them in columns with the title of the Word Category above each column. We've done this with our "Dictionary Printer" program.

You first use the "Dictionary Printer" by putting it in the directory that contains the categories you want to print. You then type "dictprn" and it will print out ".prn" files that each have two word categories. You can then print them by typing "Print * .prn."

Sometimes it will only be possible to print a fraction of the ones made at a time because the computer's printer buffer can only store a small amount to print. Therefore, you must delete the ones that have printed, and then enter "Print * .prn" and the next few will print. Do this until you've printed them all and then move to the next directory.
