Novalis, the pen name of Friedrich von Hardenberg, is an 18th Century
German Romanticist, is
a writer whose popularity remains strong in the circles who know of him.
A favorite of many
authors and artists, his work still has only been partially translated
into English. Novalis was a
polymath who was a brilliant philosopher as well as poet and novelist.
This all encompassing
understanding of art and science led the Surrealist painter Max Ernst to
call Novalis one of his
all time favorite writers.
