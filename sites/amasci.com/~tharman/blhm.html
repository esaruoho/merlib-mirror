THE RACE:

In the spirit of the popular Beaver Lake Triathlon the Beaver Lake Protection Association puts forth the challenge to all strong-willed, adventureous runners to conquer the awe-inspiring Issaquah-Fall City Hill in a scenic half marathon.

THE COURSE:

The Half Marathon begins and ends at Beaver Lake Middle School Located at 25025 SE 32nd Street Issaquah, Washington on the Issaquah Plateau.

From the school the course takes you around Beaver Lake through a quiet community, then East down Duthie Hill providing you a 180 degree view of the Cascades, Mt. Si and the Snoqualmie Valley, then back up the plateau via Issaquah Fall City Road, where we encourage you to be well prepared for this 2-mile long cardio-inspiring hill.

THE 8K:

Beaver Lake is the venue for this run. The 5-mile loop follows Beaver Lake Drive around Beaver Lake and back to the school. Although the course is mostly flat there is one short hill at mile 2 - just enough to test your anaerobic threshold as you sprint the hill.
