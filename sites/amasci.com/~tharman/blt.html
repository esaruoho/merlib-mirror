    THE RACE:

    Swim 1/4 Mile
    Bike 13.9 Miles
    Run 4-1/4 Mile

    THE COURSE:

    Each event begins and ends at Beaver Lake Park Located at the corner of SE 24th & West Beaver Lake Drive.Issaquah, Washington.

    The 1/4 mile swim begins and ends at the beach at Beaver Lake Park.

    The 13.9 mile Bike ride takes you east of the park over scenic Duthie Hill (views of the Cascade Mountains) along Redmond Fall City roads. The ride, about mid-way has an awe-inspiring hill.

    The 4 and 1/4 mile run, mostly flat goes around Beaver Lake on East and West Beaver Lake Drives onto the finish line at Beaver Lake Park.

    THE WAVES:

    The waves will be divided into four groups:

    Men's competitive
    Men's recreational
    Women's competitive
    Women's recreational
    Relay teams

    REGISTRATION:

    The race is limited to 400 entries. Registration should be received by August 11th. It is likely that the race will be filled up.
