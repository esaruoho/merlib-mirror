 The Business Policy Game 
 An International Strategy Simulation , 5th Edition 

 Players: Starting with the 5th edition, we have changed 
 the way we distribute The Business Policy Game. The Player's Manual and 
 the Player's program may be downloaded from the Player's Area. Passwords for 
 entry may be obtained from your game administrator or professor. Each team must 
 purchase a license to use the manual and the software. The team license costs 
 $100 and may be obtained using a credit card in the Player's Area. It should 
 be purchased between the time you receive the game history from your administrator 
 and the time you make your decisions for Year 4, Quarter 1. A team must have 
 a license prior to entering decisions for Year 4, Quarter 1. ( NOTE : 
 Licenses are sent within 24 hours of the time they are ordered.). 
 Administrator's: Starting with the 5th edition, we have 
 changed the way we distribute The Business Policy Game. The Instructor's 
 Manual and the Administrator's program may be downloaded from the Administrator's 
 Area. Passwords are available from the author. (In your request, please provide 
 the name of your organization, your position within the organization and the 
 purpose for which you wish to use the simulation.) Administrators do not require 
 a license to use the simulation. However each team playing the simulation must 
 obtain a license prior to entering their decisions for Year 4, Quarter 1 regardless 
 of whether the decisions are entered by team members or by the game administrator. 
 ( NOTE : Licenses are sent within 24 hours of the time they are 
 ordered.).
