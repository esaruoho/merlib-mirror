For Tess:

A Blessing

In the name of Light arising, I call the East Wind to your side.

May your mind glow with the golden light of inspiration.

In the name of Light increasing, I call the Fires of the South
to light your path.

May your destiny burn hot and clear, and warm all who you meet.

In the name of Light decreasing, I call the Waters of the West
to soothe your fears.

May your heart find comfort and respite in the gentle waves.

In the name of Light returning, I call the Northern Mountains
to stand beneath your feet.

May the solid rock of a safe haven always greet you, coming home.
