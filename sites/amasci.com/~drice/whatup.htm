 Anne of Green Gables
Lakewood Players

November 29-December 22, 2002

Yes, dear ones, I'm back to my favourite genre of character - mother of an emotionally volatile teenager! Woo-hoo!! ::chuckle:: I'm playing Marilla Cuthbert in Anne of Green Gables. What fun, eh?

Seriously, this is a great show with a fabulous cast - in the round at the Lakewood theatre just south of Tacoma. The show opens the day after Thanksgiving and runs through December 22, 2002.

For more info on tickets, directions to the venue, etc., check out the Lakewood Players website: http://www.lakewoodplayers.com/

For those that find this kind of role to be your fave me to play, I have to say, this one rivals The Cripple of Inishmaan or Little Women or I Never Saw Another Butterfly. This show is an amazing project, an all-new adaptation by Scott Campbell, and I'm playing in a venue that has seen many of my friends grace its stage.

Yes, it's so totally worth the commute! So, come on down to the show!

And, thanks again to everyone who made it down to the Bagley Wright this summer for Trial by Jury and The Sorcerer. You all so rock! I love you guys! :: big bear hugs::

As always, may you have as much peace, light, health, hugs, music and happiness as you can comfortably tolerate in your lives, dear ones.
