In <3g7g2a$aki@polo.iquest.com> larrfull@iquest.com (Larry Fullerton) writes:
>William Beaty (billb@eskimo.com) wrote:
>: The 'Taos Hum,' that low throbbing noise reported by people across the
>: country, turns out not to be detectable by audio or EM instruments.
>: Therefor it does not exist.
>The experiment you describe would be very compelling if successful, but it
>is difficult to tell a subject which part of the wave interference to use
>as the que to tap, so the coherency would be lost.  A sightly different
>experiment might be as convincing, albeit not a coherency test.  Each
>subject could be asked to turn the knob on the audio oscillator to the
>frequency which gives a zero beat, and measure that frequency very
>accurately (0.1 Hz is probably close enough, since a 10 sec. beat is
>beyond the ability of a typical subject to perceive). The spread of
>measured frequencies could then be analyzed and given a confidence level.
>-- Larry
