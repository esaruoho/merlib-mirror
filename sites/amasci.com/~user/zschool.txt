 Phillips Academy ,
often known as Andover, was founded in 1778 and is a
leader in secondary school education. Andover's 400 million endownment
supports students in grades 9-12 from over 40 states and 30 foreign
countries and provides 6.7 million in financial assistance to 40 percent of the
students. Students enjoy small classes (averaging 14 per class),
unparalleled depth and breadth in curriculum and co-curriculum and
consistent contact with approximately 160 faculty (98 percent live on campus) who
hold among them 164 Ph.D.'s and master's degrees. The school is located
25 miles north of Boston.

 Winterhaven - A new K-8 school starting up
in
Portland with an emphasis on accelerated curriculum and meeting the level
and rate needs of students.
WinterHaven is an alternative school with a science, math, and technology
special focus, within the Portland (Oregon) Public Schools system. When
asked if WinterHaven is a program for TAG-identified students, the
answer is a clear "NO." While it is true that the school has an
unusually high number of TAG identified students, that is not the primary
basis on which admission decisions are made. Perhaps the question is
better framed in terms of who would most benefit from and contribute
positively to the school. It is more important to stress such
characteristics as serious learners, children who take responsibility for
themselves and work well in groups, and students who are focused,
committed, and excited by ideas.

Summit Middle School in Boulder, Colorado. This is a 
public charter school
governed by a parent-elected board, scheduled to open in fall 1996. 
 A self-selecting program for
students who feel they want or need more challenge, Summit hopes
to address the needs of high ability and high performance
learners with a cognitively challenging curriculum and flexible
instructional approaches which allow continuous student progress
(this school will not be bound by the Middle School Education
Essentials). One goal of the program is to prepare students for
the new International Baccalaureate program at Fairview High
School or other high-level, college-preparatory high school
programs.
