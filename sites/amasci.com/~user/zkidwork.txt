 Concord Review 

The Concord Review,
the only journal in the world for the academic work of high school
students. Since 1987, they have published 330 exemplary history essays
(average 5,000 words) by high school students in 22 countries. The
website

(http://www.tcr.org) 
has 24 sample essays, an of the first
29 issues, information on thier $2,000 Emerson Prizes, a form to accompany
essays, and other useful information.
