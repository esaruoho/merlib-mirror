Gifted Resources Curriculum

  • CSMP - Comprehensive School Mathematics Program.
    A program that introduces advanced topics through stories and models. Some of the 5th grade topics covered exponential patterns, geometric translations, probability games, Gaussian counting method, combinations, and algebraic functions. These are introduced in ways that are meaningful for younger students; they don't know they're doing algebra and transformational geometry. To get more information, contact:
    McRel Institute
    2550 S. Parker Rd.
    Suite 500
    Aurora, CO 80014
    email: cheidema@mcrel.org
