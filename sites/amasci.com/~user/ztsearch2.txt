Here are five resources I have found recently which a TIP state award
recipient (who didn't qualify for TIP summer programs) could use.

First, TIP has Scholar weekends and if you're in commuting distance of
Duke, they're a wonderful opportunity. (All Talent Search participants are
eligible).

Second, none of the other Talent Searches (e.g. CTY at Johns Hopkins and
CTD at Northwestern) have such high score requirements for their summer
programs. Instead they have a lower cutoff and then select the best
applicants from among those who apply for a course (TIP can tell you in
days if there was room in a course, these others make you wait till May).
If you select less popular courses you could have a good chance of
acceptance.

Third, all the Talent Searches also admit students to their program on the
basis of SAT scores taken later (the required scores are higher).

Fourth, The Center for Talented Youth at Johns Hopkins offers Expository
Writing Tutorials by mail. It sounds like a wonderful opportunity for
careful critiques and advice about writing (it's a series of courses with
assignments every 2 weeks). The verbal score of the SAT for students
