 Gifted Resources Online Chat 
Every Sunday evening at 8:00 eastern time there is a live online chat in Net
Haven (a WWW site). In order to access the chat rooms in Net Haven you need
a password. To obtain a password send e-mail to macjordomo@colegroup.com.
In the body of your message type INFO HAVENSUB a user ID and password will
be mailed back to you.

To get to Net Haven go to http://www.colegroup.com/nethaven. Scroll down to
chat rooms and select chat room one. When you enter the chat room, type in
your name or screen name and send us a message introducing yourself. If you
do not send a message, we won't know you are in the room. You must click on
update about every 30 seconds or so to see what has been said.

A special topic is generally selected in advance.
For example
Sunday (3/7) the topics is heterogeneous grouping as it relates
to the needs of gifted students.

If you have a special topic you would like
to discuss, please e-mail Cindy Johnson at C516J@AOL.COM
