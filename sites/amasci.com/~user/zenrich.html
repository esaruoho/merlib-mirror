Gifted Resources Enrichment Programs

Here are some possible sources for those interested in pursuing independent enrichment in particular subject areas. There is also enrichment information available though the Education section of this web page.
  • Saturday Academy is a non-profit organization that provides hands-on educational experiences to kids all over the state of Oregon.
  • a Math-Science collection.
  • GNA - Globewide Network Academy.
  • Science Resource Guides from U Mich
  • NSF Pubs
  • Cornell Scientists Workbench
  • Science Olympics
  • MegaMath
  • A large collection of kid's special interest links from Uncle Bob's Kids Page
  • NASA main
  • LOGO Computer Language
  • LOGO Enthusiast
  • Clearinghouse for Subject-Oriented Internet Resource Guides
  • Gifted Enrichment Center, Tallahassee FL
  • Astronomy Classroom Project
  • Map info such as USGS
  • Family Math is a newsletter. To subscribe send email to LISTSERV@uic.edu with "sub fam-math yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the email.
  • Holography everything you wanted to know and holography classes as well.
  • US Coast Guard Basic Instructor School web pages and assorted links.
  • National Women's History Project can be reached by phone at: 707-838-6000, or by postal mail to: 
         National Women's History Project
     7738 Bell Road
     Windsor, CA 95492-8518
  • MathMagic, a math enrichment program, can be reached by email. Send a message with "send mathmagic_info" in the body (not the subject) of the email message to: l-server@forum.swarthmore.edu
  • Discovery Channel collaborative learning tools. Also availble through gopher service.
