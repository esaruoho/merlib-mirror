This page is intended to be a convenient starting point for gifted students, their parents and educators to access Gifted Resources, whether on the internet or otherwise.

ERIC Clearinghouse
Since ERIC is such a comprehensive resource, I have highlighted a few resources of particular interest for gifted education. The ERIC Gifted Highlights list points to ERIC resources which are available from the main services and to selected bibliographies which have been derived from the ERIC sources (with permission).

ERIC is probably the most comprehensive clearinghouse of education related material. It includes a number of public domain digests of information related to gifted and talented children. ERIC also provides a bibliography of relevant publications and other useful information. ERIC is available from the ERIC Web Page, or from an alternate WWW page.

ERIC will also respond to email queries. Send your question to askeric@ericir.syr.edu

Research Program
Dr Peter Street is conducting a research study:

I am a school principal in NSW, Australia, and have recently completed my Ph.D. in gifted education, examining the factors that contribute to academic success of gifted middle school students. This information may be of help to parents of gifted students who feel their children arre not achieving at the anticipated levels, or to scholars undertaking similar qualitative research. I am also seeking students and parents willing to participate in a qualitative survey of academic self-concept of gifted students. Any parents wishing to be involved in this research (leading to a Ed.D. degree) can contact my email and I will return their enquiry within 48 hours. I need about 200 participants from the US to ensure the validity of this study.

For more information, please contact Dr Peter Street streetp.@lisp.com.au

Page Mirrors and Link Problems

If you find an out of date version of this page, or notice that links do not work, please reload from the home URL: http://www.eskimo.com/~user/kids.html

These pages are displayed using a pastel background color. Each time the site is updated, all pages have the same background color. If you see different background colors as you navigate around the site, this is a visual cue that you may be being served an out-of-date page from a cache somewhere. You can bypass the cache and request the current up-to-date page by selecting "Refresh" or "Reload" on your browser.

Also, should you notice any problems or know of any applicable resources which are not included here, please send email. This site continues to improve, thanks to reader feedback.
