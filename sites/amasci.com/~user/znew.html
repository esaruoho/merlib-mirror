  2. Added link to: The New Zealand Association for Gifted Children
    The New Zealand Association for Gifted Children has branches throughout the country, organising regional events and an annual national camp. We also assist with academic research, publish a quarterly magazine (Tall Poppies) and annual refereed journal (APEX). In addition, NZAGC lobbies government and holds conferences to educate and inform gifted students, their parents, teachers and the community as a whole.

    The New Zealand Association for Gifted Children also has a web site.

  3. Added reference to: Utah Talent Search
    The Utah Talent Search is conducted by the Utah State Office of Education. It culminates in a summer program known as the Youth Academy of excellence at the University of Utah. The web site is http://www.usoe.k12.ut.us/curr/g&t/talent/
  4. Added link to: Montgomery County PADI
    Web site: www.mcps.k12.md.us/departments/eii/padi describes the Program of Assessment, Diagnosis and Instruction (PADI). The PADI program finds and fosters potential giftedness among elementary children, particularly Hispanic, African American and low socio economic.
