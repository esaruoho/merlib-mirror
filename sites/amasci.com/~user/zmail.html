Gifted Resources Mailing Lists

Special Resources for Gifted and Talented Education

There are quite a few mailing lists of particular interest:

  • gtot-l, the mailing list for gifted tots (generally under 6 years of age). To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe" in the subject (not the body) of the message to GTOT-L-REQUEST@eskimo.com for a regular subscription, or send it to gtot-digest-request@eskimo.com for a digest subscription.
  • TAG-L, the mailing list for Talented and Gifted education. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe TAG-L firstname lastname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserv@listserv.nodak.edu
    Note: TAG-L also has archives available.
  • giftednet-l, the mailing list for the National (USA) Science and Language Arts Curriculum Projects for High Ability Learners. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe giftednet-l your-first-name your-last-name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserver@listserv.cc.wm.edu Please note that the mailing list name ends in lower-case L, not numeral 1. Please also note that the William and Mary curriculum now has its own limited distribution list (WMCURRIC-L).
  • TAGFAM, the mailing list for families and support for TAG or GT children. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe TAGFAM your-first-name your-last-name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserv@sjuvm.stjohns.edu More information about TAGFAM is available on the TAGFAM web page.
  • gt-special, the mailing list for twice-special children and families. All gifted children are special because of their intellectual abilities. Some, however, have disabilities that make them twice-special. Generally, this list discusses any gifted child who has a learning disability (LD) and/or other neurological problem(s) that interfere with the child's ability to reach the full potential of their giftedness. These other neurological problems may but are not limited to ADD, hyperactivity, autism, Asperger's Syndrome, Tourette's Syndrome, PDD, ODD, OCD, anxiety disorders, sensory integration disorders (SI), and depression. Parents/guardians, educators and mentors, and medical and mental health providers who are facing the joys and challenges of supporting and nurturing these children are most welcome. To subscribe to the list, send e-mail with an empty subject line and the body of the message containing the text "subscribe GT-SPECIAL" to majordomo@gtworld.org

    If you still have questions or need any further assistance regarding GT-SPECIAL, contact owner-gt-special@GTworld.org

  • u-achiev, the underachievement list which addresses issues (family, school, peer, self) facing underachieving students (particularly gifted students). To subscribe, send a mail message with "subscribe u-achiev your-email-address" in the body (not the subject) of the message to majordomo@virginia.edu
  • OZGIFTED, a mailing list for teachers and parents of gifted children, and others interested in gifted education, in Australia. The list's primary focus is to facilitate practical, appropriate environments for gifted children's intellectual, social, and emotional growth, by supporting the teachers and parents involved. To subscribe, send a mail message with "subscribe oz-gifted yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to Majordomo@owl.qut.edu.au
  • GIFTED-CHI-L, a new mailing list dedicated to increasing communication and cooperation between parents and educators interested in realising the full potential of gifted children - particularly children themselves and gifted individuals now in adulthood. If there is enough interest we will start a separate list for children themselves. Although based in the UK, all are welcome to join. To subscribe, send a mail message with "subscribe gifted-chi-l" in the subject and with "subscribe gifted-chi-l YourFullName YourAge " in the body of the message to gifted-chi-l@tutorcom.co.uk
  • WMCURRIC-L, limited to subscribers who have a specific interest in the William and Mary gifted curriculum units. If you would like to subscribe to WMCURRIC-L or if you'd like more information, please send an email message to: LNBOYC@FACSTAFF.WM.EDU
  • e-TAG-USA, a national list to discuss advocacy efforts for TAG students including, but not limited to, discussions about obtaining a national law, tips, suggestions, and ideas for advocacy at the district or state level, getting a bill passed, discussion about what state laws have helped or hurt TAG kids and why, and what laws *looked* good on paper but didn't help and more. For informaton on how to subscribe, please contact Monique Lloyd, at monique@rtinet.com
  • OHIOGIFT, a local mailing list for discussion of issues specific to gifted education in Ohio. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe ohiogift firstname lastname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserv@lists.acs.ohio-state.edu
  • disd-tag, for discussion of tag issues in the Dallas Independent School District. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe disd-tag" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserv@netcom.com
  • ORTAG, a mailing list to discuss Oregon TAG education related issues. Contact Monique Lloyd at monique@rtinet.com for information on how to subscribe.

Other Resources

  • The Liszt list of mailing lists - the biggest of mailing lists. Can be slow at times.
  • Tile Net of mailing lists
  • Distance Education Clearinghouse lists of mailing lists
  • Indiana University of mailing lists (http://scwww.ucs.indiana.edu/mlarchive/) is no longer in operation.
  • home-ed, the mailing list for home education and "unschooling" issues. To subscribe send a mail message with "sub home-ed yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to majordomo@world.std.com
  • unschooling-list, the mailing list for unschooling issues. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe unschooling-list yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to majordomo@nilenet.com
  • ld-list, the mailing list for learning disabilities. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe ld-list" in the body (not the subject) of the message to majordomo@curry.edu
  • home-ed-politics, the mailing list for home education political issues. To subscribe send a mail message with "sub home-ed-politics yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listproc@mainstream.com
  • KIDSPHERE, a high volume education related list. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe KIDSPHERE Your Name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to kidsphere-request@vms.cis.pitt.edu
  • RPTCRD, the Daily Report Card, a summary of news in K-12 education. Very newsy. Recently cut back to thrice-weekly and comes out of the National Education Goals Panel. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe RPTCRD Your Name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserv@gwuvm.gwu.edu
  • ECENET-L, the mailing list for Early Childhood Education. This list is sponsored by the ERIC Clearinghouse on Elementary and Early Childhood Education (ERIC/EECE) at the University of Illinois. To subscribe send a mail message with "SUB ECENET-L yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to LISTSERV@VMD.CSO.UIUC.EDU
  • MIDDLE-L, the mailing list for Middle Level Education. This list is sponsored by the ERIC Clearinghouse on Elementary and Early Childhood Education and the Center for Early Adolescence at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. To subscribe send a mail message with "SUB MIDDLE-L yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to LISTSERV@VMD.CSO.UIUC.EDU
  • REGGIO-L, the mailing list for Reggio Emilia Education. This list is sponsored by the ERIC Clearinghouse on Elementary and Early Childhood Education and the Merrill-Palmer Institute at Wayne State University. To subscribe send a mail message with "SUB REGGIO-L yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to LISTSERV@VMD.CSO.UIUC.EDU
  • EDPOL, the Education Policy Digest, a mailing list by educational publisher Scholastic Inc., addressing education and politics, aimed primarily at teachers. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe EDPOL-D Your Name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listproc@scholastic.com
  • VOCNET, a mailing list for vocational education. To subscribe send a mail message with "SUB VOCNET Your Name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserv@cmsa.berkeley.edu
  • waldorf-l, a mailing list for Waldorf education. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe waldorf-l Your Name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to waldorf-l-request@netcom.com
  • VIRTED, the mailing list for Virtual Reality in Education. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe VIRTED your-first-name your-last-name" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listserv@sjuvm.stjohns.edu
  • BR_Cafe, a mailing list for discussion of K-12 books by K-12 students. To subscribe send a mail message with "SUBSCRIBE BR_Cafe yourfirstname yourlastname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listproc@micronet.wcu.edu
  • BR_Review, a moderated list for K-12 student-written book reviews. To subscribe send a mail message with "SUBSCRIBE BR_Review yourfirstname yourlastname" in the body (not the subject) of the message to listproc@micronet.wcu.edu
