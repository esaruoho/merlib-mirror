Gifted Resources for Education

  • Linda Silverman's Gifted Development Center has online gifted info.
  • GT World is an online support community for parents of Gifted and Talented children. Others interested in supporting and nurturing gifted and talented children are also most welcome. GT World offers electronic facilities comprised of a website, the GT-Families mailing list, and the GTworld MOO.
  • A variety of gifted/talented education resources can be found on Miles Abernathy's home page
  • Assiniboine South School Division in Canada has a Gifted Info service called the RightTrack. This is quite extensive.
  • NY Gifted and Talented

  • Hebrew University in Jerusalem's gifted resources site (in Hebrew).
  • Special Needs Education Network from SchoolNet in Ottawa, Canada. Has some gifted resources and a great deal for other special needs.
  • Florida State University and Florida Dept Education joint project on Varying Exceptionalities in Florida's schools.
  • Kidsource has some resources on gifted and talented children, including:
    • Calendar of activities and information and resources for parents of gifted children
    • 41 Articles on Gifted students, Learning Disabilities with giftedness and more
    • Websites (many with reviews)
    • Reviews of books about and for gifted students
  • Douglas Eby's resource list and articles about giftedness in women.
