DOG TALES

 

Well sir, the bluedog and the whitedog were resting on their quads, kindo of leaning back with their knees on the seat and head in elbows. in the summer heat, It was JULY and blistering hot, about 110 in the sun on the sand. We were on the dog drag desert, This was one of the dog days of summer, where the heat will dry your spit before the sand can absorb it. And we were waiting for daddog to start his z400 so we could head back. I was also waiting, and I WAS daddog.

 

The jetting was not right, and we weren't quite sure wether it was heat, or pilot jet, so we were musing to ourselves about self jetting carburetors and fuel injected ignition systems and what it would be like to turbo these things.

 

"It'll melt down! says a dog to the left."

 

"No it won't you can jet it right.". interjects bluedog.

 

"Well, if it is jetted right for boost, it will not be jetted right for pre-boost." drawls another blue dog dryly.

 

"well, we'd have to make it change jets when the boost hits, offers whitedog politely.

 

Daddog gets an idea. "When the boost comes on, you would have to be injecting more fuel so all you would have to do is pressurize the float bowl at the same time whenever the boost hits."

 

We all thought about these things for a while when we saw a tremendous tornado like cloud coming down the sandy/dirt road to our section of the dunes. The pikcup and small trailer had two YFZ's on it and it was careening back and forth, as if trying to stay on the roads three slight curves.

 

It heded into the hot deep powder sand just off the road, and we were counting the seconds till the rear weels would be buried up to the axle, for on such hot days, even the best pickups get stuck quit easily. On hot days the sand looses all water content and it becomes like powder.

 

So were watching them going to get stuck, when Bluedog says "He's going to get stuck."

 

I nodded, because he was going to get stuck, and nodding seemed like the right thing to do now.So we watched. Watching him go to his axle sand inevitable conclusion.

 

Motor reving and gasping for hot air, the wheels clawing through fine powder, he slows and slows and gets to a crawl when the motor stops roaring and the ignition turns off.Out of the pickup go two young men, moving quick, they unstrap the two quads, roll them off, and start them up.

 

Full throttle they roar out to where we are sitting?

 

"Whats up?" I said.

 

"Hmmm" says whitedog.

 

and they drive up to us. One skinny, highly energetic rider in jeans and white T-shirt runs over to Bluedogs quad. "There it is! These are them aren't they? " And he talks to his friend , having a discussion.

 

These are those fast ones I think, they have something done to them. and he drops down and crawls around looking for the bottle. But there was no bottle to be seen.

 

"Where is it? What did you do?" "We heard about you guys, so we said we were jetting today and we had to go now, we didnt get it quite right yet.

 

And they said ok! See you around!

 

We thought it was funny. But maybe we should have asked them about how to turbo these puppies. They might have had ideas.

 

And off they rode into the dessert, disappearing into the waves of heat.
