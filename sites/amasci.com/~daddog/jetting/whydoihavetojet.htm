OK EVERYTIME YOU UPGRADE YOU SHOULD TRY and REJET. GO UP YOUNG MAN!

When you increase the airflow through the carb, the fuel flows differently and your SIGNAL (correct fuel / air ratio gets messed up) so you have to change the JETS (the three little screws with holes in them) that let just the perfect amount of fuel into the engine.

What if I don't jet and add a air filter?Probably nothing but you will loose potential power, and you WILL run hotter, and you COULD melt your engine piston or rings and need an engine rebuild when it seizes up. So take the opportunity of adding the airfilter to rejet. People think the airfilter adds the power. It does NOT. It adds more AIR. AND the velocity changes so the fuel absorbs differently. Large airfilters make the volume greater but the pressure is less due to less air restriction, I believe so not as much fuel gets injected, so the jet size must go up appropriatley (I think that is right, whatever the reason you have to rejet) YOU have to add more fuel to make up for the extra air with lower pressure. The last thing you want in your engine is OXYGEN that is UNBURNED at 1000 degrees! That is BAD, aluminum burns, you know, and aluminum burns HOT. Get RID of that oxygen by adding enough fuel, and that will also (SURPRISE) add more power! (Isnt that what you really want anyway? Not hot air, but more power!)

What does a pipe do? A pipe relieves the back pressure in a four stroke and lets more air through so now you changed your SIGNAL so now you should rejet.

What if I add a really good pipe and a really good airfilter? You need to change the jets even more for even more power. I would say piot 45 Stock needle on #6 or Gytr needle on #4 and 175 main.

What if I just take the baffle out? You need to rejet higher by 5-10 units on the mainjet 1 clip on the needle and 1 size on the pilot upwards to get more power. That would be a 45 pilot and a 168 or 170 mainjet and gytr #4 airscrew 1.5

What if I take the air filter out? Yon need to rejet to get more power by about 5-10 unites on the mainjet and 1 size on the pilot upwards. Piot 45 or so stock clip #4 or 5 mainjet 168-170

 

Ok what size jets do I need?

For the YFZ450 you need anywhere from 165 Main jets to 215 mainjets depending on your modifications. BUT USUALLY YOU WILL BE AROUND 170 give or take 5!

For the PILOT jet which affects the low RPM range you need to be anywhere from 45 pilot to 50 pilot

And the needles are variable, anywhere from clip #3 to clip #6 stock needle depending on mods.

 

The day you buy the quad, the YFZ-450 quad COMES WITH 42 pilot (too lean!) clip #3 and 158 MAINJET (TOO LEAN!)

Then you drive it and look at the plugs, we will have more on that later, today I am just going to show you the basics of jetting. If the electrodes are black then you re too rich, go down in jet numbers. if the electrodes are WHITE you are too lean, go up in jet numbers.

 

YOU CANT HURT THE MOTOR GOING TOO HIGH, you may foul a plug and smoke like a black tornado if you are way high. Just jet down.,.

 

IF YOU AR TOO LEAN you could ruin your motor and it will loose power and suddenly seize up.

 

When you are too rich the motor stumbles and chokes like if you had the choke on.

When you are too lean it doesnt GO RIGHT AWAY when you give it gas! It HESITATES and BACKFIRES a lot and the pipe exhaust glows red hot.

Then your motor may melt down, it gets too hot when too lean. The 450 glows light orange in the dark. Bright red is too hot.

 

DADDOG TRICK if your bike runs slightly better when you turn the choke on, you are TOO LEAN go up on the jets.

 

 

THE DADOG SECRET TO JETTING IS THIS! CHANGE YOUR JETS ONE AT A TIME UNTIL IT RUNS REALLY REALLY GOOD!

This is not rocket science.

 

With several mods, NEVER GO BELOW 165 on the mainjet. Never go below 45 pilot never go below clip #3 on the stock needle.

 

There can be no better way than this, to jet your quad then try it out. WHEN YOU RUN YOUR BEST YOU ARE JETTED YOUR BEST!

Every quad is different. Every jetting is different, only YOU can figure out your best jetting.

 

If you had your shop mechanic jet PERFECTLY he would charge you 3000 dollars, cause it will take a few days of driving, and at 50 bucks an hour you have to learn this yourself.

Or you can just run it stock and get whooped by every other 450.

 

 

Jetting is the process of increasing the fule main jet or the needle jet or the pilot jet. This sit in the carburetor as the pictures below will show you. Whenever you upgrade the quad , you need more fuel because you have more AIR, there has to be a proper ratio, and when the SPEED of the air or the PRESSURE of the air going through the carb changes, your gas jets need to change.

The gas jets are littl HOLES that squirt gas into the carb. Too much gas and it runs rich and rough and even smokes.

To little and it runs really HOT and can freeze up your quad.

It is safer to go too rich rather than too lean.

The YFZ450 comes very lean from the factory, they even passed the california green sticker test so you KNOW it has to be lean.

 

Do I need to jet? Answer: Yes. If you don't it is bad for the motor and you don't go as fast if you run lean, because you don't have as much gas. Fuel is power.

 

If you jet too high, you will loose power, so the trick is to jet just right..
