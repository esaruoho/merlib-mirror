 Badlands 

 Days of Heaven 

 The Thin Red Line 

 Maverick genius director Terrence Malick has made only three films from
1973 till the present day; but what brilliant films they are! I bow and
scrape in awe to the international man of mystery who's made Badlands , Days of
Heaven and The Thin Red Line . 

 Disclaimer: I have no connection to Malick at all--I don't know who his
agent is, whether he has an email account, nor how to contact him. I just
like his films. And I want to be just like him when I grow up. 

 Well, isn't that special?
