Friday Five

1. What is your favorite restaurant and why? mmm....sushi... There's this really great sushi place near my house called Star Midas, I love it there, it doesn't hurt that they're like 5 minutes away from my house! =)


2. What fast food restaurant are you partial to? McDonald's, but probably only because there's not an In-N-Out Burger within driving distance of work. (lunch break driving that is)


3. What are your standards and rules for tipping? 15-20% of the total, including tax. If it's a sushi place that I'm 'known' at or want to be 'known' at, it'll usually be on the higher side.


4. Do you usually order an appetizer and/or dessert? Appetizers yes, if there's anything that sounds tasty =)


5. What do you usually order to drink at a restaurant? If I'm not DRINKING, then Sprite is my soda of choice, if they don't have Sprite, 7-up is an acceptable substitution. Mountain Dew and Slice are NOT acceptable. Root Beer or orange soda will also do.
