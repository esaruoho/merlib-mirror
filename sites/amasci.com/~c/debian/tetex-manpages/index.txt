 Take a look at the complete changelog . 

 The &#8220;fourth draft&#8221; consists of changes in the author information
for e2pall (1) and fontinst (1). 

 The &#8220;third draft&#8221; was changes to the Makefile, addition of a
changelog, and so forth, and wasn't actually released. 

The &#8220;second draft&#8221; was the first draft that was &#8220;ready
to go&#8221;. The main changes were

 Adding information about authorship/bug-fixing responsibility for
 various programs 
 Replacement of stylistic markup with semantic markup (in the Yodl
 files) 
 New manpages for
 texexec (1),
 texshow (1), and
 texutil (1) (the ConTeXt
 manpages) written by Tobias Burnus based on the PDF
 documentation for ConTeXt and rewritten by me. 
 A new manpage for fmtutil.cnf (5) 

 Feedback! 

 I would greatly appreciate feedback on these manpages, especially
from people who use or the authors of the scripts or programs being
documented. Please send any such feedback to me . Thanks!
