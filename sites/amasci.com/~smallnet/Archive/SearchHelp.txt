Search Help

The search capability for the SMALLnet Archive is pretty simple compared
to the luxuriant capabilities of the big search engines like Google
or AltaVista. But it gets the job done!

If you want to find all occurrences of some word in the Archives,
just enter that word and press the Search button. As an example, you
could enter
 CO2 
and the search would find all of the archive postings where CO2 was
mentioned.

But suppose you wanted to find where the phrase
 "CO2 Engine" 
was located. You would enter the phrase in quotes, and the search would
look for the phrase in the archive.

Perhaps you would like to find all of the places where CO2 and diesel
engines were discussed. You would enter something like
 co2 diesel 
and everywhere the word CO2 or Diesel was found would be returned. Some
postings would have CO2 in them, some would have Diesel in them, some
would have both.

Now let's find those postings that would contain BOTH CO2 and diesel
discussions. You would enter
 CO2+diesel 
and the search would list those postings which contain BOTH of the terms.

As you can see, it's fairly capable. And you can combine the capabilities
to make for complex searches. As an example, perhaps you'd like to see all
discussions containing CO2 and contibutions by Bill Lee. You would enter
 CO2+"Bill Lee" 
and back would come those postings. Not to say that it was Bill Lee
that said anything about CO2, but at least they are in the same posting
somewhere! :-)

The search is case insensitive: CO2 is the same as co2.

The search will find the postings for you, but you will have to use your
browser's "Find" capability within a posting. I'm not sure where it is
on every browser, but NetScape users will find it on their Edit menu.
