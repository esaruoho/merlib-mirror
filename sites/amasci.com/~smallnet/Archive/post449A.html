It is with great sadness that I must report that Randy Randolph has passed away. A note from his club:

-----------------------------------
Dear DRCC members:
It is with great sadness that we learned today that our dear friend "Randy" Randolph died unexpectedly about 12:15 p.m. at Baylor Hospital in Dallas.

We have no further information. When we have details of funeral plans, we will alert you by e-mail.

Duane Schoelerman

I will pass on any information I get via this list.

With a great deal of sadness for the loss of a true friend and a great modeler.

Rest in Peace, Randy.
