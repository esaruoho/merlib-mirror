This is a response to whether KeelyNet is still online as a BBS.
---------------------------------------------------------------------
I am Jerry Decker, originator, owner and sysop of KeelyNet in Dallas,
Texas.  KeelyNet is now in its 7th year as an independent BBS, online
24 hours per day, 7 days a week at 214.324.3501.

We are currently working on establishing a web page on the InterNet for
the posting of the hottest and most relevant material received through
our network.  The approximately 7 mirrors on the InterNet are great as
arhives, but a bit out of date as pointed out in numerous emails to my
keelynet@ix.netcom.com address.

The current plan is to establish a domain name and provide for
approximately 10MB to 20MB of online storage.  This should provide
storage for many new files including .GIFS, speculative and project
material.

I am not a big InterNet user and so don't have all the information
needed to setup the HTML's and all that entails, but there are many
offers for assistance which are being considered.

Thanks to everyone out there for the interest and continual exchanges
of information.  The very positive email and letters from people all
over the world makes it all worth the effort.  Of course, it's not all
perfect or exact but it is better to post information, even if it is
unproven, to let people decide for themselves, than keep it locked into
a smaller network where it is forgotten or not recognized for its
potential.

Keelynet currently has about 2300 users (periodically paired down to
about 1500-1800) but it always builds back up.  Some files are not
posted all that rapidly to the InterNet because of the many InterNet
users who have a tendency to flame any new or alternative concepts.  On
KeelyNet, we try to maintain a positive, upbeat approach using
constructive advice or pointing out errors that are honest mistakes.

Such an attitude fosters a much more open sharing of information, ideas
and concepts, because people know they won't be embarrassed or sneered
at in a public forum on the KeelyNet email or though their posted
files.  It has been my experience that once such a tone is set, there
are very few people who even attempt the flame attitude.  On occasion,
I've had to write a personal note asking someone to refrain from such
behavior or to not call KeelyNet.  Like your mother said, if you can't
say something constructive, keep your mouth shut.

New users on KeelyNet are given 30 minutes, refreshed daily, and can
download up to 1MB of files per day.  Once the user is recognized as
being more than just a curious caller, they are given a higher access
level, which simply means access to other email and more time and files
available per call.  All of our files are freely available to anyone
who wants to download them.  If I could afford it, there would be an
800 number for callers, but InterNet provides this option and with our
own web page, our newest files will be InterNet accessible on as timely
a basis as possible.  We don't plan to establish an FTP or TelNet site
any time in the immediate future.

I really enjoy the suggestions for improvements or different approaches
to a problem or question, based on other people's perspectives and
experiences.  Such a diversity of background in each respondent always
leads to something new and unexpected.

So, to answer the query, is KeelyNet still functioning as a BBS, YES
and you are welcome to call anytime.  Thanks again for your interest
and support.
