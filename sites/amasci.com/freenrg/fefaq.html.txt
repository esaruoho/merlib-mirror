FAQ: Free Energy 

 FREE ENERGY FAQ 
 1997 W. Beaty 

 WHAT IS A "FREE ENERGY DEVICE?" 
The term "Free energy device" has a number of meanings:

 A device which collects energy from a source which is not recognized
by current science.
 A device which collects energy at zero cost.
 Another name for "perpetual motion machine."

Definition number one is the most common. By this definition, a nuclear
reactor would be a free energy device... if this were the year 1890. If
some researcher discovers how to harness the neutrino flux, or how to
convert matter into energy, then that will be a free energy device, but
only until the mainstream of science accepts its existence. 

 AREN'T THEY ALL JUST "PERPETUAL MOTION" MACHINES, AND IMPOSSIBLE 
EVEN IN PRINCIPLE? 
Is a solar cell a perpetual motion machine? Or a nuclear battery? After
all, both of these devices just sit there and produce energy from no
obvious source. But a little investigation of either device reveals a
hidden energy source. 

On the other hand, "perpetual motion crackpots" still exist, and they may
attempt to hide behind the name "free energy."

 ISN'T THERE A CONSPIRACY TO SUPPRESS THESE DEVICES? 
Yes and no. Is there a conspiracy to keep women in low-paying jobs? No,
there is just wide-ranging sexism, and sexist employers need not conspire
together. Regarding Free Energy, "science bigots" constantly attack its
adherents, smear the field with derogatory names, and use fairly
underhanded tactics to suppress research. But bigotry is a result of
belief systems. No conspiracy is needed to explain why groups of people
exhibit identical styles of prejudice and ignorance. 

 AREN'T MOST F/E DEVICES REALLY JUST SCAMS TO RIP OFF GULLIBLE PEOPLE? 
No, but some are. The vast majority of F/E researchers are honestly
pursuing the goal fo "fuelless" portable energy sources. But investors in
F/E companies should be extremely wary, because scams are frequent in the
field. 

 WHERE CAN I BUY ONE TO POWER MY HOME OR CAR? 
No successful F/E machine exists. The situation resembles that with
flying machines in 1900: there are occasional successes, lots of crasy
overblown claims, but the "Wright Borthers" have yeat to appear on the
scene and show us how to REALLY do it right.
