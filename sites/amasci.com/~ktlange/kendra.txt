 What's
 that thing on my lip? Mommy and Daddy told me that an
 angel kissed me there a couple weeks after I was
 born. But secretly I heard them talking about it
 and I found out that it is a hemangioma
 ("he-man-gee-oh-ma"). The short
 definition is that it is a temporary birthmark that will
 go away on its own someday. Here's several links if
 you want to read about hemangiomas:
