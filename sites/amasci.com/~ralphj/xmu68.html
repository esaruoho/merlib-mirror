Very old music box.

Originally this box was purchased from the St. Vincent De Paul thrift store in North City Washington.  The lady there would hold out under the counter desirable items for my wife.  This is one that was old  when she bought it it in about 1965.  It is all wood and hand carved.  I do not know the tune.  I have heard it and it is familiar.  It is  associated with a child's tune. I an almost think of it but not quite.  You know how it goes when you can almost  remember something but not quite.
