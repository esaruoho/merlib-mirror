The Swing Era

Seven albums with 3 lp records of the big band, vocalists  and personalities.  
Each  album come with a 9 by 12 hardcover book dedicated to a subject of the swing years.  Not all are necessarily dedicated only to music.  For instance the album for 1937 38 is titled, Vintage years of humor. Then it goes on to write about the men who made the music. Shaw, Raymond Scott and others. A valuable record of the swing years.  You will not find the sweet bands included.  It is for the swing lovers. The songs are recreated by the best bands of Billie May and Glen Gray.  Stereo so it is good quality. The books are worth at least 15.00 each.
Enjoy!

All these pieces are recreations by top musicians from the original music scores.  They are very good quality not the old 78 rpm records.   Most recreations are by  Billy May and Glen Gray
  1.  The swing era. 1940_41  Glenn Miller, Harry James, Artie Shaw, Les Brown, Tommy Dorsey. Woody Herman, Charlie barnet, Earl Hines, Benny Goodman, Jimmie Lunceford, Jan Savitt, Duke Ellington Side 2 and 5 has been dropped and the first two selections are broken off and not playable.  

  2. The Swing Era. 1938_39. Some of the above plus Count Basie, Larry Clinton, John Kirby, Glen Gray, Bob Crosby, Bobby Hackett, Andy Kirk, Raymond Scott, and Benny Carter. 
  3. The Swing Era. 1939_40.  Some of the above including Ray Noble, Will Bradley, Erskine Hawkins,  Gene Krupa. 
  4. The Swing Era. 1944_45. Some of the above plus Nat King Cole,, Eddie Hayward, Stan Kenton, Lionel Hampton. Number one record missing
  5. The Swing Era. 1941_42. Claude Thornhill, plus some of the above.
  6. The Swing Era. 1937_38. Chick Webb,  plus some of the above.
  7. Swing Era. 1936_37. Red Norvo plus some of the above.
