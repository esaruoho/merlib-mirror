 This carving is about 17 inches
tall. It was carved by a sailor who carved wood from all over the
world. On his voyages he would get a piece of wood and carve it.
He made a point of carving wood from all over the world. This is one of
them but I don't know where the wood came from. It looks like a
Polynesian face so probably came from the South Pacific The artists
name is on the bottom signed Hi Miller. He lived in 
Ballard in Seattle. A favorite place for sailors. 
Near Fisherman's Terminal, Lake Union and the Lake Washington Ship Canal
