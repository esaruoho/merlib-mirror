New Guinea Sculpture 

 New Guinea Female Carving 
21 Inches tall from Iron Wood I think. 

This carving was sent home by a Christian Missionary from New Guinea.
It was not done for tourists but was actually from the mantel of
someone's home. It is covered with smoke and dirt. The hair
is real hair. The decoration around the forehead is real sea shells.
Real fur comes from her nostrils. Rope provides her adornments around
her neck wrists and ankles. This is an extraordinary object no longer
available from this part of the world. 

This is museum quality.
