Baskets 

 The
only collection of Howeattle baskets in private hands. 

 Mattie Howeattle was granddaughter of Chief
Seattle. 
In the 1930's she used to sit on Second Avenue in Seattle and sell her
baskets. Geneva Hemry a young school teacher would buy her baskets. She
willed them to my wife and when she passed on my wife got
them. Any expert on NW Indian baskets can verify the design as a
Mattie Howeattle basket.
