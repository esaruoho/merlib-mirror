Eating Human Being. 
This is a cedar carving by a Native American Carver H C Brown. He was N
W Indian artist who sold his works at the Seattle Indian Art Center. It
was purchased in about 1960. 
The carving is 19 inches long and pictures a bear eating two
people. The name of the piece is Eating Human Being. Carved from
cedar then stained.
