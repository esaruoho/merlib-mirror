German Water Color Pictures
Painted by a German artist who painted pictures of the military in action.  From the invasion of France  to the invasion of Russia.

Order by file number.  $5.00 each in 8x10 photo reproduction or Laser reproduction.  Both on double weight paper. Great for framing.  These came f rom a book published in 1944 in Berlin.  The quality of the binding was inferior but the pictures were high quality and maintained their original color.  The U S government has mountains of German art filed away in the archinves and will not allow anyone to see it. The American military had painters and photographers imbedded with the military in  WW2 just as the German did.  I copied the pictures and made photographic copies of good quality.  I used a Hassleblad 2 1/4 inch negative. then had pictures made from these negatives. This kind of art is very rare but I am  making it available.
