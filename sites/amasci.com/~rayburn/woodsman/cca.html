COLT COLLECTORS ASSOCIATION

If you have an interest in Colt firearms or memorabilia, you may want to join the Colt Collectors Association.

The CCA publishes The Rampant Colt, a high quality quarterly magazine with articles of interest to Colt collectors, and has an annual convention and all Colt gun show somewhere in the United States the first weekend of October. October 6-8, 2000 it will be near Washington DC in Reston, VA. Past locations have been in San Francisco, Denver, Houston, Atlanta, Anaheim, Hartford, Portland, Dallas, Orlando, Kansas City, St. Louis, and other locations.
