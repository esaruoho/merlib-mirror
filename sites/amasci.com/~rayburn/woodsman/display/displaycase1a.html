Case 1, shelf A:
Set of three pre-WWII Woodsmans, engraved by the Colt Custom Shop in 1992 and factory fitted with elephant ivory grips made by John Guest.
  1. Sport Model, serial number 150xxx.
  2. Target Model, serial number 135xxx.
  3. Match Target Model, serial number MT6xxx. The wraparound style Match Target grips are a single piece of ivory.
For privacy purposes, the final three digits of the serial numbers have been replaced with xxx.
