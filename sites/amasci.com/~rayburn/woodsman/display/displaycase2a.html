Case 2, shelf A: Master Engraved

    Left side pair was built and cased by Colt on special order for Major R. Rayburn. Ivory stocks added by owner.
  1. Third series Sport, serial number 043xxxS, factory engraved by Master Engraver George Spring, factory gold plated, special 3-1/2 inch barrel.
  2. Third series Target, serial number 043xxxS, factory engraved by Master Engraver George Spring, factory silver plated, special 5 inch barrel.

    Right side pair was built and cased by Colt for the personal collection of Mr. Charles E. Warner, President of Colt Firearms from August of 1975 through May of 1981. Each bears a special "PRESIDENT'S EDITION" logo inlaid in gold, and the serial number of each consists of P.E. (for President's Edition), C.E.W. (Mr. Warner's initials), and the model number of the pistol: S1141 and S3162 for the Sport and Match Target, respectively. Ivory stocks added by present owner.

  3. Third series Sport, factory D engraved, gold inlaid, and signed by Master Engraver Steve Kamyk.
  4. Third series Match Target, 6 inch barrel, factory D engraved, gold inlaid, and signed by Master Engraver Steve Kamyk.
    For the President of Colt Firearms.
For privacy purposes, the final three digits of the serial numbers have been replaced with xxx.
