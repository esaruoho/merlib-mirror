Case 6, shelf C: Transition Guns

    The first 1001 of the 3rd series Woodsmans were numbered in a continuation of the 2nd series serial numbers. Four of them are shown here.

  1. Target Model, Serial number 146xxx-S
  2. Sport Model, Serial number 146xxx-S
  3. Match Target Model, Serial number 146xxx-S
  4. Match Target Model, Serial number 147xxx-S

    This group of early 3rd series Woodsmans includes various mixes of 2nd and 3rd series features. 2nd series characteristics include roll markings, magazines, magazine safety, and boxes.

  5. Sport Model, Serial number 160xxx-S
  6. Match Target Model, Serial number 161xxx-S
  7. Match Target Model, Serial number 163xxx-S
  8. Match Target Model, Serial number 161xxx-S
For privacy purposes, the final three digits of the serial numbers have been replaced with xxx.
