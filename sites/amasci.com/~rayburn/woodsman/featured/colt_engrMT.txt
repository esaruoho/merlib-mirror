Matched Pair of Factory Engraved Match Target Woodsmans 

Only 11 of the First Series Match Target Woodsmans are known to have been factory engraved, and 2 of those 11 were shipped as a pair to two brothers in January, 1939. Each was inscribed with the name of one of the brothers. After many years of separation the pair is united again in this collection.
