 Third Series Woodsman 

 Match Target Model with 6" barrel illustrated. Also came with 4-1/2" barrel.
All third series models are basically the same internally, but there are significant external differences. The Huntsman and Targetsman, for example, lack the automatic slide stop that is standard on the Sport, Target, and Match Target. They also have a slightly heavier, non-tapered barrel, an integral front sight, and different rear sights than the other models. 

Several changes took place within the third series, and not all parts will interchange with earlier or later 3rd series guns, even those of the same model.
