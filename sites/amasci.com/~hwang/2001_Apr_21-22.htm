        Chief Solano Kennel Club
        Solano County Fairgrounds
        900 Fairgrounds Drive
        Vallejo, CA  94589

       Judging Program and Premium Lists:
        21 April 2001 (Saturday)
        22 April 2001 (Sunday)

       Results:
        21 April 2001 (Saturday)
        22 April 2001 (Sunday)

        Notes:  
        (1) Jesse is now Champion Jesse!   But we are getting ahead of ourselves ...
        (2) Day 1 - SUCKED.  Bad day for the WildWind clan.  
        (3) Day 2 - Jesse is thee DAWG!  Winner Dog and Best of Winners!  His sister Emmy was 
             no slouch either.  She took Best of Opposite Sex.  Nancy did a great job handling. 
        (4) The two kids, Peaches and Kobe, were their usual selves.  Mike Stone handled Kobe 
              both days and commented that Kobe needs a lot of work and his "head ripped off a 
              few times."
        (5) The question is, why did the gang do so well Sunday and blow chunks Saturday?
              Nancy's First Theorem of WildWind:  For every bad Judge, there is an equal or better
              male Judge that comes from the Southeastern United States.
              Nancy's Second Theorem of WildWind:  a2 + b2 = win2.  Especially if the entry number, 
              a or b, contains a "7". 
        (6) As always, the Kobester enjoyed his visit with family, especially with his sister.
