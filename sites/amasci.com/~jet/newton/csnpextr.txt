Here are some extracts from Comp.sys.newton.programmer.
+ It is an eclectic collection, corresponding to some of my interests.
+ It is not a frequently asked question list, but some of these are asked a lot.

Notes:
+ Almost all of this was in Comp.sys.newton.programmer at some point.
+ I have stripped out most article headers and signatures, although many
  have a short name of the answerer.
+ The entries were collected in 1993 and (mostly) 1994.
+ Some of these entries are outdated or obsolete.

+ This is a text-only file which I hope DOS & UNIX people can deal with.
+ I have not checked much of this out.
+ Remember that those who ask questions are important contributers to this.
+ It is the work of many people, especially Apple's Newton group, especially
  DTS, which in my seldom humble opinion has worked really hard to support
  developers.

+ This is provided in the hope that it will be useful.
+ If it is useful to you or someone you love please consider saying thank you.

Thanks to all who contributed.
