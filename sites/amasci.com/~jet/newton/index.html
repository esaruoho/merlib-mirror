James Thiele's Newton Stuff

Games for the Newton that I've released as freeware:
  • Animals shows some pictures and plays sounds.
  • Tic Tac Toe plays a children's game. Newton plays against person or two people can play.
  • States shows a U. S. map and shows state names and capitals when a state is tapped on.

  • Unistrokes implements an alernative stylus based writing system created at XEROX PARC
