 James Thiele's Newton Unistrokes 

Unistrokes is a writing system developed at Xerox PARC specifically for 
stylus based computers. A Postscript version of the paper and lists
of strokes is at
 ftp://ftp.parc.xerox.com/pub/unistrokes/ 

A read me for Unistrokes.

Unistrokes version 1.1 can be accessed in the following compressed formats:

 unistrok.zip (13K)
 Unistrokes11.sit (15K)
