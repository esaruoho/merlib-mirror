James Thiele's Newton States 

 James Thiele's Newton States 

Version 1.0

This app shows a U.S. map (in landscape mode) and lists the name of a state
and it's capital (also in landscape mode) when you tap on a state.

 Screen layout for states.
 States can be accessed in the following compressed formats:

 States10.zip (15K) for PC and UNIX.
 States10.sit (15K) for Mac, or
 states-10.hqx (19K) for Mac
if the above doesn't work for you.

Note: You must possess a utility for decompressing these
files in order to use them.
