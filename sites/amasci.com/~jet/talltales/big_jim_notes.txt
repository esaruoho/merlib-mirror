 Notes on the story "Big Jim"

I won first place at the "Tall Tale Contest" at the 1994 Northwest
 Folklife Festival
 with the story "Big Jim".
 I shamelessly stole it from another storyteller I'd heard tell it some years before.
It was not his original story - it was (or so I've heard) used in a skit by Jackie Gleason on on of his TV shows in the fifties or sixties.

"Big Jim" is one of my most popular stories.
