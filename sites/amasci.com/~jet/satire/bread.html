Bread is Dead

Very few people seem to be aware of the cruelty which the baking industry inflicts on living beings. This industry is every bit as cruel as the beef or poultry industry. In particular, few people seem to care about the inhumane practices involved in making bread.

Wheat plants spend their entire lives packed together as closely as agricultural "science" declares efficient in huge "fields" which may stretch as far as the eye can see. They are sprayed with noxious chemicals to prevent any other plants and most insects from surviving. At the prime of their lives they are cut down just above the roots by machines with huge sharp blades. Most of the plant is simply discarded with only the unborn wheat plants being retained. These unborn wheat plants are unceremoniously dumped into huge bins until they are sent to slaughterhouses to be brutally ground to death. The result is called "flour", a name which obscures the fact that this is all that remains of the unborn wheat plants.

When the flour is shipped to a "bakery", it is combined with living yeast cells which are allowed to live a few more minutes or hours as the "dough" rises. Then the yeast cells are BAKED to death while still alive. The final product is bread.

So when someone tells you that "meat is murder" remind them that "Bread is dead", and challenge them to consider the violence their bread consumption contributes to.
