 Committed Couple 
 John Wilkinson and David Davenport 

John Wilkinson and David Davenport have been together since 
1970. This was shot while on vacation in Palm Springs, Calif. 
At the time, they were saving to fly to Hawaii in hopes of 
being legally married, but the Hawaii Supreme Court ruled 
against equal civil rights. 

John and David in 1970 when they lived in Portland, Oregon. 
They curently live in Seattle, Washington with their two cats.
