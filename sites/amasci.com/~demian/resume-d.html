My involvement in the performing arts spans from the 1960s to the present. Major influences on me have been the magic of Disney animation and the brutal social realism of Brecht. Nice combo. I endeavor to use the mediums of film and theater to entertain as well as educate.

My doctoral thesis was based on leading 130 theater workshops, as well as producing and directing a play. The workshops utilized physical and trust exercises, as well as improvisational skill-building. The workshop-developed play focused on themes of sexism, and toured in Massachusetts.

Besides directing my own original scripts, I have also directed showcases for other writers, as well as directing children in summer camp shows such as Oliver and South Pacific.

Since the 1970s, I have directed primarily for my company, Sweet Corn Productions. Shows have ranged from marionette opera for children, to productions on liberation issues.

Besides directing, I am highly skilled in filmmaking, editing, and script doctoring. In the past I have utilized 8 and 16mm film, as well as Sony Port-a-Pack, VHS, and Hi-8 video formats. Currently, I shoot DVcam digital video, and produce DVDs on my own editing suite.
