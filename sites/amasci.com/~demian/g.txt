 Gilgamesh & Enkidu 
 The Oldest Love Story Ever Written 

 Written and Directed by Demian 

 An Independent Video Feature 

 An arrogant ruler is transformed by love &#151; 
transformed, once again, by its loss &#151; 
and thereby becomes a legend. 

It is a very personal story, showing a physical and mental journey begun in greed and abuse of power, and ending in wisdom and peace. 

 Gilgamesh & Enkidu distinguishes itself as a video retelling of the landmark, historic, poem of the Epic of Gilgamesh , in part, because it crosses a host of genre boundaries:

 Fantasy - mythical beings and dreams are mixed with reality
 Historical Docu-drama - it takes place 4,600 years go in Sumer (now Iraq)
 Biographical - Gilgamesh was a real king
 Gay Content - Gilgamesh and Enkidu were lovers
 Religious - the gods frequently interact with the mortals
 Action-Adventure - earthquake, horrendous battles, and a gruesome beheading
