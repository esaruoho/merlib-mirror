In Brief
Steve Bryant is an Accredited Business Communicator and is Chief Creative Officer for Public Relations with Publicis Dialog in Seattle, one of the nation’s top twenty firms.

Steve also works professionally as a classical singer and pianist. A high countertenor, he has made a specialty of music written for the castrati. The castrati were adult male Italian singers, principally of the 17th and 18th centuries, who retained their soprano and alto voices after being castrated before the onset of puberty.

Steve was music director for Sweet Corn Productions from 1981-2002.

He was co-founder and co-director of Partners Task Force for Gay & Lesbian Couples along with Demian, from 1981-2002.

His other interests include four-hand piano playing, organic gardening, vegetarian cooking and the arts in general.
