 &#151;&#151; Books &#151;&#151; 

 Ceremonies of the Heart: Celebrating lesbian unions edited by Becky Butler (1990), $14.95 +$2.24 p&h, Seal Press, 3131 Western Ave., #410, Seattle, WA 98121-1028; 206-283-7844. Couples describe 27 ceremonies and their relationships. 

 Daring to Speak Love&#146;s Name: A gay & lesbian prayer book by Dr. Elizabeth Stuart (1992), $10, Hamish Hamilton - London; distributed by Penguin Books, 375 Hudson St., New York, NY 10014. Prayers for celebrating relationships, coming out; partings, healing liturgies and deaths. 

 The Essential Guide to Lesbian and Gay Weddings by Tess Ayers and Paul Brown (1994), $17, Harper San Francisco (HarperCollins, 10 East 53 St., New York, NY 10022). From Affidavit of domestic partnership to Vichud (seclusion tradition); the minutia of ceremonial wedding events and how to plan for them. 

 In God&#146;s Image: Christian Witness to the Need for Gay/Lesbian Equality in the Eyes of the Church by Robert Warren Cromey (1991), $9.95, Alamo Square Press, Box 14543, San Francisco, CA 94114. Episcopal priest agues for same-sex marriage. 

 In Praise of Gay Marriage (1986), biblical tracts #4-7, 10 cents ea., $1.50 min. order +$1.50 p&h, Puget Sound Publishing Group, 3401 Rucker, East Suite, Everett, WA 98201, 206-339-8338. Contends that Jesus and John were married. 

 Lesbian & Gay Marriage: Private commitments, public ceremonies edited by Suzanne Sherman (1992), $16.95, Temple University Press, Philadelphia PA 19122. Essays on ceremonial marriage and 24 interviews focused on why the couples married or chose not to. Includes an article from Partners Task Force directors. 

 Living in Sin? by John Shelby Spong (1988), $15.95, Harper & Row, Icehouse One, #401, 151 Union St., San Francisco, CA 94111. Includes arguments for blessing same-sex relationships. 

 Recognizing Ourselves: Ceremonies of Lesbian and Gay Commitment by Ellen Lewin (1998) $17/paper $30/hard, Columbia University Press, New York. Includes analysis of the symbolic strategies same-sex couples use in devising their ceremonies; lengthy excerpts from many different kinds of ceremonies, and extensive narrative material from couples. 

 Report on the Task Force on Changing Patterns of Sexuality and Family Life , Send SASE with $.65 postage to Episcopal Diocese of Newark, 24 Rector St., Newark, NJ 07102. Endorses homosexual marriage. 

 Resource Guide for Same-Sex Couples (1988); In the Presence of God &#133; A Quaker Marriage (1988), $5.50 for both, Family Relations Committee, Friends Center, 1515 Cherry St., Philadelphia, PA 19102. Helps couples and meetings prepare for the vows of marriage. 

 Same-Sex Unions in Premodern Europe by John Boswell (1994), $25, Villard Books (Random House), 201 E. 50th St., New York, NY 10022; 800-733-3000. Newly rediscovered liturgies from early Catholic and Orthodox, used throughout Christendom into modern times; contextualized in a detailed, academic treatment. 

 The Two of Us by Larry J. Uhrig, OUT OF PRINT, Alyson Publications, Boston, MA. MCC pastor&#146;s approach to same-sex marriage.
