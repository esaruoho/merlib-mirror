 Workplace Medical Insurance 

 M edical insurance for domestic partners is usually only offered through a workplace. While the insurance is often discounted, it is taxed as if it is real income. 

Also, domestic partners are usually required to testify that they sharing common residence and intend to do so indefinitely, and are jointly responsible for basic living expenses. These are not required of legally married couples. 

As a rule, no insurance company offers discounted services unless a couple is legally married, which completely excludes same-sex couples. Theoretically, the only exception would be Vermont residents with a Civil Union, whoever, we have not heard if anyone has received such a medical plan discount outside of a job. 

Ameritas, CIGNA, Great West Life, and NY Life/NYL Care have indicated that they are interested in underwriting domestic partner coverage in all states.
