Script Doctor


Clarify and enhance your theater or film script.

This service is for scripts needing a seasoned writer/director/actor to give you feedback.

I read your scripts and tell you things your film and theater pals wouldn’t dare say. Such as, “This needs more thoughtful, engaging dialogue” -or- “This character is far too inconsistent, or are you re-making ‘The Three Faces of Eve?’ ”

Mail scripts on paper so I can mark it up and return it to you. Rate: 75 cents a page. Send check or money order, as well as a self-addressed, stamped envelope.
