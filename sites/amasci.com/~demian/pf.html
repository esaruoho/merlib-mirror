Phil’s Fortune
A Feature-Length Melodrama
 
Written by Demian

The Feature
A young man is denied a birthright, and subsequently faces a devastatingly harsh life. He struggles for success, through hard work, honesty, and moral fiber. Greedy relatives conspire to deprive Phil at every turn.
