 &#151;&#151; Books &#151;&#151; 

 Considering Parenthood: A handbook for lesbians by Cheri Pies (1985), $9.95 +$1.50 p&h, Spinsters Book Co., Box 410687, San Francisco, CA 94141. Deciding whether, how to become a parent; much applies to gay men. 

 Co-parents Rights Packet (1992), $15, Lambda Legal Defense and Education Fund, 120 Wall St., #1500, New York, NY 10005; 212-995-8585. Amicus briefs, bibliographies of law review articles and existing cases. 

 Custody/Visitation Packet (1992), $15, Lambda Legal Defense and Education Fund, 120 Wall St., #1500, New York, NY 10005; 212-995-8585. Amicus briefs, psychological articles, bibliography of law review articles, list of cases and laws on custody and visitation and sexual orientation. 

 Different Daughters: A Book by Mothers of Lesbians edited by Louise Rafkin (1987), $9.95, Cleis Press, PO Box 14684, SF, CA 94114; 800-780-2279; cleis@cleispress.com. Accounts of 25 mothers; text on family, community, religion and neighborhoods. 

 Different Mothers: Sons and daughters of lesbians talk about their lives edited by Louise Rafkin (1990), $9.95, OUT OF PRINT, Cleis Press, PO Box 14684, SF, CA 94114; 800-780-2279; cleis@cleispress.com. Accounts of 38 children; some talk about mother&#146;s partner. 

 The Family Next Door edited by Lisa Orta & Karen Rust (bimonthly newsletter), $50/yr. Next Door Publishing, Box 21580, Oakland, CA 94620; 510-482-5778. Focus on lesbian and gay parents. 

 Family Values: Two moms and their son by Phyllis Burke (1993), $21, Random House, 201 E. 15th St., New York, NY 10022. Warm and political autobiographical novel. 

 For Lesbian Parents: Your guide to helping your family grow up happy, healthy, and proud by Suzanne M. Johnson, PhD & Elizabeth O&#146;Conner, PhD (2001), $16.95, Guilford Press, 72 Spring St., New York, NY 10012; www.guilford.com. Packed with very useful information, suggestions, and resources. Any sensible child would want these authors for parents. 

 Gay & Lesbian Parents edited by Frederick W. Bozett, RN DNS (1987), $16.95/38.95 soft/hard, Praeger Publishers, Box 5007, Westport CT 06881. Includes information on gay and lesbian step-parent families. 

 Gay Parenting: A complete guide for gay men and lesbians with children by Joy Schulenburg (1985), OUT OF PRINT, Anchor Press/Doubleday, Garden City, NY. Includes couples with children. Based on survey, interviews, personal experience. 

 Getting Simon: Two gay doctors&#146; journey to fatherhood by Kenneth B. Morgan (1995), $14.95, Bramble Books, 8 Route 212, Bearsville, NY 12409; 914-679-4158. A journal on the extraordinary efforts a same-sex couple must make, and emotional costs they must pay, to adopt. Contains an application and contract for a surrogate mother and a birth plan. 

 Heather Has Two Mommies by Lesl&eacute;a Newman, illustrations by Diana Souza (1989), $7.95 +$1 p&h, Alyson Wonderland, 40 Plympton St., Boston, MA 02118; 800-825-9766. Explains alternative fertilization and alternative families to young children. 

 Jenny Lives With Eric and Martin by Susanne B&ouml;sche, photos by Andreas Hansen (1983), OUT OF PRINT, Gay Men&#146;s Press, London. For 8-12 year olds; a day in the life of a young girl, her gay father and his lover. 

 Lesbian & Gay Parenting Handbook: Creating and raising our families by April Martin, Ph.D. (1993), $15, Harper Collins 10 East 53rd St., New York, NY 10022. A complete guide; includes many examples of couples. 

 Politics of the Heart: A lesbian parenting anthology ed. by Sandra Pollack & Jeanne Vaughn, (1987), $11.95 + $1.75 p&h, Firebrand, 141 The Commons, Ithaca, NY 14850; 607-272-0000. Covers co-parenting, the &#147;other&#148; mother, legal challenges. 

 Whose Child Cries: Children of gay parents talk about their lives by Joe Gantz (1983), OUT OF PRINT, Jalmar Press, CA. Accounts of children, their gay parents, parent&#146;s lovers. Emphasizes challenges.
