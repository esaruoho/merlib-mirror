    Studio “A”


    Studio “A” is 16.5 feet long, and 16 feet wide. Located in the house, next to a bathroom.
    Three white walls. Two windows. Fourth wall is open to the house. White ceiling, carpeted floor.

    Studio “G” is 27 feet long, and 18 feet wide. Located in a detached garage on a quiet alley.
    Insulated ceiling and walls, concrete floor. Slopping roof. Two walls are concrete.
