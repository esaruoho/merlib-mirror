 Casting & Crew Requirements 

 &#151; Cast &#151; 

Racial types preferred: dark-skinned, dark hair; those who would have lived 4,600 years ago in what is now Iraq &#151; people from around the Near East &#151; Semitic, Egyptian, North African, and Asian descent. 

There are at least 35 speaking roles &#151; and hundreds of extras will be needed.
