Sweet Corn Productions - High Notes 

 High Notes 
 Way up there songs 

 Linda Strandberg , Mary Jo DuGaw , Steve Bryant 
sopranos and countertenor 

 High Notes 
 Presented by ArtsWest 

 With singing by Linda Strandberg, Mary Jo DuGaw, Steve Bryant 
and piano by Margaret Brink and Jennifer Hammill 

 Friday, January 11, 2002, 8pm 
ArtsWest Playhouse 
4711 California Ave., SW. (at Alaska), Seattle 

Mary Jo DuGaw and Linda Strandberg, sopranos, and Steve Bryant, countertenor, hit the heights with scintillating songs in seven languages for high voices and piano. Solos, duets and a trio prove that classical music can be funny as well as gorgeous. The program ranges from high drama to low comedy, with a mix of Broadway showstoppers to opera scenes, and artful duets by Gershwin, Tchaikovsky, Dvorak, Rossini, and more. This program of High Notes promises to blow the roof off the ArtsWest Playhouse. 

This performance is made possible by a grant from Poncho .
