“Director’s Commentary”
 
Written and Directed by Demian

A Video Comedy Short
The story follows a house party host in her attempts to play matchmaker. While mutually attracted, the two she has in mind have very different fantasies about each other. Ultimately, we learn what dark secrets lie behind their social masks.

The entire video is produced to give the impression that we are hearing the director’s commentary track on a DVD. On it, an egotistical narrative is heard, delivered by the video’s director. Only on this “DVD,” you cannot shut the commentary off.

21 min. runtime, VHS color, stereo sound.
