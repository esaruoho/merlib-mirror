Sweet Corn Productions - Orfo ed Euridice 

 Orf&eacute;o ed Euridice 
 Music by C.W.Gl&uuml;ck. Libretto by R. de&#146;Calzabigi 
Directed by Steve Bryant, with Douglas Butler conducting from the piano 

 O rf&eacute;o passionately seeks to bring Euridice, his wife, back from the dead. He regains her life only under excruciating circumstances, which lead to greater sorrow and agony. Amore, the child god of love, intervenes by defying death to dispel gloom and loneliness. 

 Steve Bryant, countertenor 

 Orf&eacute;o was mounted as a staged concert, much as Gl&uuml;ck first presented it in a 1762 preview. Our cast included Steve Bryant, Orf&eacute;o; Ren&eacute;e Fladen-Kamm, Euridice; Edmund Kimbell, Amore; and a chorus of twelve. Produced in September 1984 for Old First Concerts in San Francisco.
