 Production&#146;s Progress 

 Facts about Gilgamesh & Enkidu &#151; as of March, 10, 2002: 

The first several rounds of cast auditions took place on August 5, 2001. A good number of strong actors read and offered their monologues. Further auditions are planned as there are many parts to fill. 

The auditions have been temporarily set aside while Demian works on a marionette opera video short titled Scaredy-Kate and the Monsters &#150; or &#150; How to Pay the Rent . 

Cast and crew are still being sought for a sample scene to be shot in the near future, as well as for the feature itself. Talents of all kinds are still being sought, so please send in those resumes. [See Casting & Crew Requirements ] 

A grant from the Arch and Bruce Brown Foundation was received on November 10, 2001. It will be applied toward producing Gilgamesh & Enkidu . 

 Production data: 

 Independently produced.
 Currently in pre-production.
 Script is undergoing a forth major revision.
 A promotional, sample scene sample will be shot in the near future.
 Shooting for the scene sample and the feature itself will be in the Seattle area.
 The project will be shot on DV.
 Production is aimed at the direct-to-video/DVD market.
