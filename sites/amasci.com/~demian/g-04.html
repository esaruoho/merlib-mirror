Donations & Investments
Even with much of the talent and crew’s time donated, it is expected that this production will cost at least $50,000.

A grant from the Arch and Bruce Brown Foundation was received on November 10, 2001. It will be applied toward producing Gilgamesh & Enkidu. There is still a long way to go to bring this video project to financial life.

There are two main methods by which you can help to see this project come to fruition.

Donation:

All donations of $200 or more receive a listing in the film credits, as well as a choice of a copy of the finished video on VHS or on DVD.

Those in the Seattle area will be able to donate their services in the making of this video. Please contact us and let us know what you can offer.

Investment:

A minimum investment of $10,000 is required. When the project has paid back the cost of production and distribution, investors will earn a percentage of the profit based on their share of investment. Investors will receive a listing in the film credits, as well as a choice of a copy of the finished video on VHS or on DVD. A business plan will be sent to those making serious inquiries.


In all financial and production relationships, Demian and Sweet Corn Productions retain ownership of the intellectual properties and retain creative control. That is to say, we have “final cut.”
