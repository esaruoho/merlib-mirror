Workplace Medical Insurance
Medical insurance for domestic partners is usually only offered through a workplace. While the insurance is often discounted, it is taxed as if it is real income.

Also, domestic partners are usually required to testify that they sharing common residence and intend to do so indefinitely, and are jointly responsible for basic living expenses. These are not required of legally married couples.

As a rule, no insurance company offers discounted services unless a couple is legally married, which completely excludes same-sex couples. Theoretically, the only exception would be Vermont residents with a Civil Union, whoever, we have not heard if anyone has received such a medical plan discount outside of a job.

Ameritas, CIGNA, Great West Life, and NY Life/NYL Care have indicated that they are interested in underwriting domestic partner coverage in all states.

Long-Term Care Insurance

The following insurance company offers discounts to same-sex couples, just as they do to married opposite-sex couples.

General Electric Financial offers a 25 percent discount for same-sex couples. This plan applies only to individuals, not group or corporate policies. Applicants sign a “Requirements to Access Special (Spousal) Benefits” form, which holds that the couple are: “Two people who live together and are in a committed relationship as partners, or family members, who share basic living expenses and who: 1) are not married to each other, or to anyone else, and 2) do not belong to different generations of the same family (e.g. father/daughter, aunt/nephew, grandmother/granddaughter).”
