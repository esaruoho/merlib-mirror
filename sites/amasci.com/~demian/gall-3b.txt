 Committed Couple 
 Axel Axgil and Eigil Axgil 

Axel Axgil and Eigil Axgil, World&#146;s first couple 
to obtain a &#147;Registered Partnership.&#148; 
Just after the ceremony, Copenhagen, Denmark, October 1, 1989. 

Axel Axgil and Eigil Axgil passing by city hall 
after their &#147;Registered Partnership&#148; ceremony. 

Axel founded the Danish gay society F-48 and the Norwegian DNF-48. In 1954, he founded the International Homosexual World Organisation. 

The men combined their last names &#151; Eigil Eskildsen and Axel Lundahl-Madsen &#151; to form Axgil as a form of defiance. The police had threatened that the two men live apart after release from prison in 1956. The charges of &#147;speculation in lust&#148; stemmed, in large part, from distributing non-sexual photographs of male nudes. One of the men had served a year, and the other a year-and-a-half. 

Eigil Axgil died 1995. The couple had been together 46 years.
