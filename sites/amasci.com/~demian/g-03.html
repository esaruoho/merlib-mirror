Casting & Crew Requirements

— Cast —

Racial types preferred: dark-skinned, dark hair; those who would have lived 4,600 years ago in what is now Iraq — people from around the Near East — Semitic, Egyptian, North African, and Asian descent.

There are at least 35 speaking roles — and hundreds of extras will be needed.

Characters in Gilgamesh & Enkidu
(listed roughly in order of number of words spoken):

  • Gilgamesh - M: 25-35, huge, fit, acrobatic, sophisticated - major character [nudity, sex scenes]
  • Enkidu - M: 25-35, huge, fit, acrobatic, feral - 2nd major character [nudity, sex scenes]
  • Ninsun - F: 45-60, chief priestess - Gilgamesh’s mother
  • Utnapishtim - M: 15 - immortal, survived the great flood
  • Shamhat - F: 20-35, sex priestess [nudity, sex scene]
  • Ishtar - F: 35-45, heavy-set, Valkyrie-like (same actor as Siduri) - chief goddess of Uruk
  • Old King - M: 70-80s
  • Old Queen - F: 60-70s
  • Utnapishtim’s woman - F: 15 - immortal, survived the great flood
  • Urshanabi - M: 50-70s - the ferryman who aids Gilgamesh
  • Siduri - F: 35-45 (same actor as Ishtar) - Tavern-Keeper by-the-Sea
  • Scorpion-Being - M: any age - intensive makeup
  • Scorpion-Being’s Woman - F: any age - intensive makeup
  • City Directors - M & F: 35-55s - (five needed)
  • Shepherds - M & F (five needed - two have lines)
  • Servant - F: 30s - servant to Gilgamesh
  • Builders - M & F: 20-35s - muscular
  • Humbaba - M: 30-50s - killer demon - intensive makeup
  • Ea - M: 50-90s - wisest of gods
  • Boys - M: 7-11 - two boys playing in the sand [nudity]
  • Bride - F: 16-20 - lovely, vulnerable [sex scene]
  • Shamash - M: any age - god of justice and patron of travelers
  • Anu - M: 80-70s - father of Ishtar and of all the gods
  • Aruru - F: 40-60s - mother of all humans
  • Groom - M: 17-21 - sweet, vulnerable
  • Soldiers - 17-35s - rough (at least 15 needed - a few have lines)
  • Enlil - M: any age - god of wind, earth, and destiny
  • Sin - M: any age - god of the moon
There are also scores of extras needed for the city, battle, and earthquake scenes.

The script requires sex scenes for Gilgamesh and the bride, as well as for Gilgamesh and Enkidu.

Gilgamesh and Enkidu are naked at various times. There is a brief scene of two children who play at soldiers by themselves; they are also nude. Everyone else is costumed all the time.
