Welcome to the of The Business Policy Game

The Business Policy Game is a total enterprise simulation now in its 5th edition. It provides the competitive environment for the annual International Collegiate Business Strategy Competition, the longest running simulation competition in the world. The game is available to educators and training instructors looking for a dynamic process to integrate the business disciplines and provide experience in strategy development. Game manuals and software may be downloaded directly from the Internet. Please visit the links under BPG Simulation for more information. For information on obtaining the mauals and software, contact David Fritzsche at fritzsch@eskimo.com.

If you are interested in strategy you may also like to visit the the business policy and strategy home page.

For more information on educational gaming as a pedagogy, visit ABSEL (The Association for Business Simulation and Experiential Learning) and the Simulation/Gaming Exchange.
