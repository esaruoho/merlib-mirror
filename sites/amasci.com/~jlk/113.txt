Normally I design quilts based on what I see around me. And several people have commented on there being 
no cardinals in the state of Washington. In all honesty, I hadn't noticed it. I just figured I hadn't seen them. 
Afterall, I grew up in Annapolis, MD where we did have cardinals. I just thought they could be found throughout the U.S.
Wrong! There were no cardinals, at least not until now. Because now my cardinal makes his home in Bellevue, WA.

This elegant red bird will grace your walls with his presence when you make this quilt.
