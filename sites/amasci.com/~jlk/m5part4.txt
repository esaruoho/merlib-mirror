 PART FOUR 

If you have not yet completed Part Three , please finish 
before continuing with Part Four.

 1. Arrange 12 triangles of fabric B and 12 triangles of fabric W 
in a line as shown in Figure 17. 

Figure 17

 2. Sew together to ceate a strip of alternating 
 fabric B and fabric W triangles 
(Figure 18). With the fabric B triangles on top, each strip should begin with 
a fabric B triangle and end with a fabric W triangle.

Figure 18

 3. Repeat steps 1 and 2, making a total of 4(36) strips.
