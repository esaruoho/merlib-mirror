After my son Quinton helped design one of my patterns, younger brother Justin had to design a pattern as well. He drew a picture of a cat under a tree in the sunshine. I, of course, had to change it a little. Justin was thrilled, both with the pattern and with the fact that his name now appeared on one of my patterns. His only question was 
"Mommy, why did you call it Did You PET the Cat? I don't understand that." I then explained that it was PUT THE CAT OUT not PET THE CAT.
And that most people put their cats outside at night, something we don't do since our cats are indoor kitties.

You'll never forget to put the cat out with these four frisky felines on your wall.
