Calla Lily Mosaic

#P114..........$7.95
31" X 45"

One of my parents' neighbors had an over abudance of calla lilies and was giving them away. Since I try to grow all of the flowers that I want to do as quilts, I just couldn't pass up the chance to add calla lilies to my garden. I get and give a lot of flowers in this manner.

Shortly after planting my calla lilies, I started hearing a visitor in my garden each night. Not only was I the happy receipient of calla lilies, I now had a frog! Unfortunately, he got lonesome and left to find a friend.

Maybe you'll hide a frog or two (just to make sure they aren't lonely) in your calla lilies!

The majestic beauty of the calla lily will remain far beyond the warm summer days when you display this quilt.
