SEATTLE POLICE ARREST LOCAL SCIENCE HOBBYIST

Mysterious pepper-spray "event" occurs in squad car

Sept. 25, 1999 William J. Beaty


On September ???, the local police turned out in force to invade a drug lab in the downtown Seattle area. The only trouble was... the "drug lab" was the home and workshop of local artist and amateur scientist Dale Travous.

"They came in with guns drawn and made my friend and I lie on the floor" says Mr. Travous. This would be expected in a raid on a drug lab. But why would the police be so certain that Dale's workshop was some sort of crack factory?

"Me and others have tried to get a straight story out of the police, but it keeps changing. First they said they had a tip from someone. Then they said that they were invesigating a burglary."

Helicopters circled and the local press turned out in force. Dale was hauled off to jail for the night while the police and firemen in environmental suits searched his workshop for toxic chemicals and drug-producing equipment. They found none. The charges were reduced to ??????, and Dale was released the next day. The entire event never made it to the local news.

Unfortunately the story does not end there.

"I was sprayed" says Mr. Travous. "I was sitting handcuffed in the back of the police car talking to this really hostile officer, when suddenly something happened. My eyes started watering and I got really confused. There was a solid barrier between me and the front seat, so I think this cop must have rigged up some sort of remote-controlled pepper spray thing in his car so he can torture people by pushing a button." Travous is concerned that it's just his word against that of the officer. "I don't have any evidence except that tears were pouring down my face, so the officers had to lead me along to the station because I couldn't see. Maybe some of the video crews recorded this."

With the Seattle police already under the spotlight for lack of citizens review and for past complaints of corruption, perhaps these events are not so unexpected. The police in Seattle perhaps are no longer on the side of truth and justice? The police should be aware that the internet gives power to the individual, and that misdeeds can no longer be hidden.

Clearly the "war on drugs" in the United States puts any tesla-coil builder or science hobbyist at risk for identical treatment. When the police find all of the equipment in your workshop and "know" that you are a drug lord, being sprayed with pepper spray is the least of your worries. When sitting in the police car, Mr. Travous said to the arresting officer "So, are you going to take me out into the country and shoot me?" The officer replied "I wish I could."

Because he has aired his story, Mr. Travous expects some sort of police retribution or harassment to now begin. He believes that he needs to move out of the city. For many nights there has been a "paddy wagon" vehicle parked in the parking lot of his building, apparantly watching him. Mr. Travous wants the Seattle police to know he has set up a night-vision security system, and that any attempts to plant drugs in his workshop will be recorded and rapidly distributed to the press. This author is considering doing the same. They know where I live...

Is this paranoid? Travous says "Hey, don't you know the old saying? It's not paranoia if they really ARE out to get you."
