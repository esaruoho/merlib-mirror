 This site is a resource intended to collect and distribute information 
 about mail order companies in the United States only. I do not have the 
 time or resources to verify that all the information here is correct 
 and up to date, so if you know whether any of this information is 
 incorrect or out of date, please let me know . 

 The companies are subdivided by category off the main directory, and 
 under each category, they have an alphabetical quick jump index at the 
 top (this may be unnecessary in most cases, but some of the lists are 
 large and it can really help.
