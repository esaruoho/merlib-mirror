Over the last several years that these pages have been available, I have found that I really don't have the time necessary to update them the way they deserve to be. So, I can not really guarantee when a submission will get added to the lists. I apologize in advance for delays.

With that in mind, I still need help in order to build this service up. If you find any errors, or even better, have new entries to add, please email me at:

[yes, it's an image to lower the amount of spam I get.]

I will need this information if possible:
  • Company name
  • Category(ies) of the catalog
  • Phone numbers for ordering
  • Hours for contact
  • Address
  • E-mail and/or Web address, if known
  • Shipping details [if possible]
  • Prices [if possible]
  • How to get a catalog
  • Etc.(anything else about the company) [optional]
Note: Please try to limit submissions to mail order companies that fit into the existing categories (I know that can be a serious constraint) which do primary business via mail order (no web-only companies at this time, please.) Thanks!
