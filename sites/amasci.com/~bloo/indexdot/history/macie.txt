 Jun. 2003 
 Microsoft announces that development on new 
 versions of Mac IE will be halted except for bug-fixing on 5.x. One of the 
 stated reasons for this move is the claim that Microsoft won't be able to

 version final release also announced in June 2003), citing Safari's potential 
 for having greater integration with the OS than Microsoft could achieve.
