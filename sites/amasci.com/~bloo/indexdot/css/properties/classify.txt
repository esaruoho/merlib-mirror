 What Do They Do? 
 The classification properties serve to classify elements and select which
 layout models will be used in their display. In the visual media formatting
 model, an element's rendering is based heavily upon, among other things,
 the positioning method specified (floating, positioned or normal flow
 positioning) and the classification type of the element's box (the 'display'
 property.)
