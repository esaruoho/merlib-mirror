 Types of forms 

 bloo@blooberry.com 
 Revised: Mar. 18, 2001 

 Commercial breast forms come in all shapes, materials and prices to meet 
 a variety of needs. With all the choices available, it can sometimes be 
 difficult to make sense of all the terminology.

 &#160;&#160;&#160; Symmetrical 
 (A form that can be worn on either side of the body.) 

 Triangle (tri-corner) 
 &#160;&#160; 
 This is the most general type of form, and offers the most
 complete and natural bra fit for most users. 
 Teardrop 
 &#160;&#160; 
 The name implies its shape. The tapered end is used for a better fit
 either under the arm or on the upper part of the chest. 

 Heart&#160;shape 
 &#160;&#160; 
 This type of form has double extensions, both for under the arm and
 the upper chest wall. 

 &#160;&#160;&#160; Asymmetrical 
 (A form designed to be left or right side specific.) 

 Curved teardrop 
 &#160;&#160; 
 This is essentially the regular teardrop type with
 one rounded portion of the teardrop having a slight extension for the upper
 chest wall. 
 Extended Triangle 
 &#160;&#160; 
 This is a modification of the symmetric style. One
 of the lower corners of the triangle will have an extension which goes under the
 arm for better fitting in a bra with some wearers. 

 &#160;&#160;&#160; Other
 Types of Breast Forms and Accessories 

 Thin Shell Breast Forms/ 
 Breast Enhancers 
 &#160;&#160; 
 These usually serve as an augmentation for surgeries
 that remove only a portion of the breast. They can also be worn as breast pads to
 augment the size of smaller busts. 
 Attachable
 Forms 
 &#160;&#160; 
 These are a newer type of form that is becoming quite
 popular. Such forms are usually silicone and more expensive than the non-attachable
 types. They are temporarily attached usually by velcro (hook side on the form, 
 soft pad side attached to the chest by adhesive or surgical cement.) 
 &#160;Attachable
 Nipples 
 &#160;&#160; 
 Most breast forms do not have a pre-formed, colored
 nipple built in to the form (only some more expensive ones do.) Several
 manufacturers carry nipples that can be applied to forms that do not have this
 feature. Unlike the form itself, this accessory is rather cheap. A pair of
 silicone nipples usually costs about 20 - 40$US.
