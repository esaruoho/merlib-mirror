The medical purpose of the breast form industry
bloo@blooberry.com
Revised: Mar. 18, 2001

The commercial breast form industry fills the need of supplying replacement prosthetics for breast cancer survivors. A breast prosthesis can help to restore physical symmetry and peace of mind following the devastating effects that breast cancer brings. The breast form industry produces a wide variety of prosthetics for an unfortunately large market. The range of products available to the post-mastectomy patient in order to restore the visual and physical balance between the affected breast area and the non-affected area is truly amazing.
