 Join, Fulfill, Quit, Repeat - In Brief 
Quitting 

Once the membership purchase requirements have been fulfilled, contact
the club's customer service center (be sure to include your account
number) or just return a monthly selection card with PLEASE
CANCEL MY MEMBERSHIP written on it. Prior to the club canceling your
membership, you might receive another monthly mailing. Ignore the monthly
selection reply card. The club will probably send notification when it
has processed the cancellation.
