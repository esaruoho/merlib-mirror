What to bring 

Choose from among the following articles of clothing. Rowing is an all-weather sport. Dress according to the weather, but be prepared for changes. Dressing in layers is a good idea. All clothes should be close-fitting (no baggy shorts or long, loose tops) yet allow you to move freely (no jeans or belted pants). 

Sweats Shorts (bike shorts) Tights 
T-shirt Sweat shirt Polar fleece 
Thick socks Cotton socks Extra socks if you have really small feet 
Running shoes Hat Rain jacket (close fitting) Sun screen 
Sunglasses for sunny weather 
Always bring: Extra set of clothes Towel Water bottle Medical necessities
