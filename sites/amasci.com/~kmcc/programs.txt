 PROGRAMS 

 This is the spring 2003 schedule and is subject to change. 

 Youth (under 19 and/or still in high school): Rowing for all experience levels will be offered Monday through Thursday afternoons. Please arrive between 2:30 and 3:00 p.m. for 3:15 launch. You will be off the water by 5:30 p.m. (Parents and others who will be picking our young rowers up, thank you for being there by 5:30. Our volunteer coaches have evening classes and other commitments and cannot wait with students whose rides have not arrived.) 
 Adults (19 years and over and/or out of high school): Rowing for all experience levels will be offered on Saturdays and Sundays from 7:30 to 10:00 a.m. As the days get longer, there will be the possibility of weekday rows, depending on demand.
