VOLUNTEERS


A community rowing program, Lake Stevens Rowing Club needs volunteers. At present, we need regatta volunteers (no experience needed!)

Home regatta volunteers can choose to work on regatta preparation or to work at the regatta itself. We will have on-going training so that when the big day comes you will be ready for action.

Away regatta volunteers will support our racers in a variety of ways when they are competing at regattas away from Lake Stevens.

If you want to support Lake Stevens Rowing Club in this way, please call us (425.337.6614) or contact Bob Cummins directly.

See you at the races!
