International differences in gestures are remarkable, and these differences are the focus of A World of Gestures. In this still from the video, a Japanese woman demonstrates the gesture for 'I'm angry.' As the video A World of Gestures immediately demonstrates, there is no 'international language' of gestures. Instead, cultures have developed systems of unique gestures, and it is almost never possible for us to understand intuitively the gestures from another culture.
