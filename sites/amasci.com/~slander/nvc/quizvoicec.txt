From the photograph alone, viewers have great difficulty in
trying to guess where the man is from. When you add the powerful
clue of Vocal Paralanguage , answering the question becomes much
easier. Even blindfolded, a person who hears this man count from
1 to 20 correctly guesses that he grew up in New York City. 

As The Human Voice video makes clear, the extraordinary
thing about this is that there are no clues about grammar,
literacy, or vocabulary--the only thing the speaker does is count from
1 to 20. People are able to answer this question powerful
clues lie in the 'instrument' of the human voice itself.
