 The Lost Children is a true story of being lost in the wilderness of northern Wisconsin in 1865 as told by Mary Mess Roth to her children and grandchildren many times. The story was recorded in print by her daughter Catherine Roth. Note, The Lost Children is a PDF file and requires Adobe Reader to open for viewing.
