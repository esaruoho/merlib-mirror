 We decided trek to Wisconsin for the month of December, 2001. We loaded up our Honda Odyssey with
two 3-month old infants - Brock and Maddy, two dogs - Whitby and Cuzco, and two adults - Lisa and Richard, and
drove to Wisconsin from Seattle. 
 The only small hiccup occurred on a mountain pass just east of Wallace, Idaho. Two semis jack-knifed into the
barriers on the sides of the highway due to icy conditions. We waited on the side of the road for 90 minutes
while waiting for the trucks to be cleared away. The rest of I-94 was clear and dry from Bozeman, Montana, to 
Madison, Wisconsin. While the trip back to Seattle had a bit more wintery conditions, we were able to drive it 
in one less day.
