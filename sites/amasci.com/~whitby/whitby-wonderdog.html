whitby companion family member

Whitby
7 May 1989 - 12 May 2003
Part of the family from the beginning, remembered forever.
We are very sad to announce that Whitby the Wonder Dog has passed on in the early hours of May 12th, 2003. After battling an illness we felt that her suffering should end. Fortunately, she had spent a good day at a friend's farm in Carnation, WA on the 10th. She was among sheep, horses and other Border Collies wandering about taking in the sights and smells with Cuzco. Whitby became part of our family from the beginning. We cherish the 14 years of companionship she gave us and will be fondly remembered forever. Rest in peace with frisbee dreams.
