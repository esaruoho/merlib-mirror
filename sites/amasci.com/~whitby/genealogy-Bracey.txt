 Photos and documents from our family lines of BRACEY, GRANT . The ancestry of the Bracey and Grant families trace back to Isle of Wight County, Virginia, and eventually back to England and Scotland.

 Nettie Frances Pierce Bracey 

 Nettie is my great-great-grandmother. Her ghost or spirit has traveled across the country from Isle of Wight County, Virginia, to Seattle with a number of her journals that I obtained from her son Littleton. She had a knack for writing backwards. You need to hold the first image to a mirror to be able to read the page. No worries, I have included a mirror image copy as the second image.
