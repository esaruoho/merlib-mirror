 The Wino 
 We danced the Wino during the reception. Not an easy thing to do on the side
of a hill after drinking chicha at high elevation. Richard got quite a bit more
experience as one of the Willocan women kept asking him to dance. He was not
quite sure if he was doing it right as she only spoke Quechuan. At least he did
not chase his partner around the field like Lisa.

 On to Richard passing out gifts
