Patrick Melia

Patrick immigrated from County Galway, Ireland in the early 1900's. He became a police officer in Chicago where he was killed, 27 April 1910, while on duty by a band of thugs. There is a memorial to slain officers at the located in the lobby of Chicago Police Headquarters, 3510 South Michigan Avenue, where his police badge is displayed on a wall. It is called the Superintendent's Honored Star Case. You can see the dents from the shotgun pellets.
