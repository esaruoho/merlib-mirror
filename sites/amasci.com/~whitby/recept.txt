 Welcome to July 2000 in... 

 Friends and family began to arrive as early as the weekend prior to the party. Hoser, Marguerite, and baby
 Carolyn flew in from Florida. My newest nephew made it out here, leaving only a couple days after he was born,
 By his first week, Nickolas had camped at Yellowstone and Mt. Rainer National Parks. My dad, Bev, Justin,
 Zack and my sister, Sarah joined him as the R.V. made it's way from Wisconsin. Lisa had her brother Chris and
 family in from Moraga, CA, as well as her brother Mark, wife Sona, and niece Amanda attend from Asheville, NC.
 The Hellmuths, Kris and baby Harry, Jill and baby Maya all came up from the Bay Area. Robbie and Blaise arrived
 from Boise, ID. The rest of the gang were either in from Wisconsin or Seattle.

 The Thursday before, a group of us went out to Buco di Beppo's Restaurant up in Lynnwood for an evening of 
 family-style Italian food. We hosted Burrito Night that Friday at our home. The reception party took place on 
 Saturday, July 15th, 2000, Anthony's Homeport on Shilshole Bay in the Ballard neighborhood of Seattle. On early 
 Sunday morning, after closing down Jillian's Billiard Club, the Huggett Hills Exterior Decorators made a special 
 visit to our home to introduce Saeed and Nayereh to the art of toilet papering. Later that day, we 
 reconvened at a park at Cottage Lake, near Woodinville for a cookout.

 We appreciate all that that came to our reception. Thanks for making it a special event for us!

 ...The Emerald City
