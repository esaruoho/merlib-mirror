CompCrypt : A System to compress and encrypt files for intra and inter bank exchange 

 Banks 
 often need to exchange files with : 

 Their Branches 

 Other Banks 

 With Central Banks 

 It 
 is advisable that the files be exchanged such that they are : 

 Compressed to save transmission time 

 Encrypted to ensure security and integrity 

 CompCrypt 
 is utility package that assists Commercial Banks in Compressing 
 and Encrypting the Data Files. 
 Key 
 features of the system are: 

 Encryption 
 To 
 ensure the Authentication, Integrity, Non-Repudiation and Confidentiality 
 of files, BankBridge uses highly secure encryption algorithms which 
 conform to International standards. 

 Compression 

 Standard ASCII files, when compressed, can result in a huge saving in 
 bandwidth and reliability of transfer. CompCrypt compress files by upto 
 95% using a sophisticated compression technology resulting in transfer 
 times of minutes rather than hours. 

 * 
 CompCrypt was released in Q4 2001 and is running live at Member 
 Banks
