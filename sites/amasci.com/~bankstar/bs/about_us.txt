6 of the Worlds Top 100 Banks use Bankstar's Software 

 Bankstar Technologies Limited began as a startup in Q4 1997 
 with a vision to develop a Financial Object Library ("FOL") 
 which could be used to rapidly generate software applications for the 
 banking industry. Our library was ready by Q4 1999 and we got a beta site 
 in October 2000 for our first application. 

 The 
 sophistication and flexibility of our FOL resulted in us being invited 
 by an international consortium (including a USA Fortune 500 company) to 
 bid for a tender to provide a Clearing and Settlement System (CASS) for 
 the Central Bank of a country. We won the bid and successfully got sign-off 
 for it over one month ahead of schedule, a situation almost unheard of 
 in Central Banking Systems. 

 The CASS is today installed and working at the Central Bank and 47 other 
 commercial banks including 6 of the worlds top 100 banks are using software 
 developed by us. 
 We 
 are headquartered in HongKong with a development office in Mumbai, India. 
 We have marketing offices in Bamako, Mali and Dakar, Senegal. We have 
 representatives in New Zealand, Australia, Egypt, Cyprus, Oman and Jordan. 
 Bankstar 
 has a team of about 30 people worldwide with expertise in Banking Functional 
 Requirements, Software Design, Programming, Testing, Graphics Designing, 
 Technical Documentation and Marketing.
