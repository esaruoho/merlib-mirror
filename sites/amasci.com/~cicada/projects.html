an entity's journey across a space called blank

the entity finds itself in a (blank).  in the blank it
finds 5 vertical lines, 2 circles, 1 arc, and 2 lines
which form a 45 degree angle.  the entity begins its
journey westward.


	the entity consists of 1 circle, 1 vertical line,
	1 horizontal line bisected into 2 horizontal lines
	by the vertical line, and 2 lines which form a 
	30 degree angle.

	three letters can be extracted from the entity's
	physical content.  a lower case 'o' can be discerned
	from the circle, or head.  an uppercase 'A' is 
	formed from merging one of the horizontal lines
	(an arm) with the 30 degree angle (legs).  the
	remaining parts, the torso (vertical line) plus
	the other arm (horizontal line) combine to form 
	either a t, a T, or an L.  this is where the 
	ambiguity of the entity begins.

	as for the blank, its infinite configurations in
	space only allows for an interpretation rather 
	than a conclusion.
