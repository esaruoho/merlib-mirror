the multiplication of objects

O=object
V=viewer
  
if the viewer is an object, this substitution can be made:

V=O

here's an equation that describes the object-viewer relationship:

xO  yV = xO  yO = x  y = C

C equals the number of objects created by this equation.

when the viewer looks or sees, the viewer not only acquires and possesses an
image but also creates an image that is unique.  no two viewers can be at the
exact same location at the same time.  this alone assures that no two views
can be the same even if the two viewers have the exact same colour sensitivity 
and depth of vision.

O=object

O  V  T  F = C

C=conceived object

example:

1. let's say you're inside a gallery looking at a painting.  at that point, 
   O=1, V=1, T=1, and F=1.  its safe to say that the painting's location is 
   fixed.    

   you decide to take a closer look at the painting.  it is another view.  you
   see more details which alter your conception of the painting.  this means an
   additional viewer location.  using the C equation, the result is 2 Cs 
   (1  1  1  2 = 2).

   after 10 minutes, you decide to move away to look at another painting.
   another viewer looks at the painting you just left.  two viewers have now
   seen the painting.  at the point from you looking at the painting, then
   going closer, then leaving which enables another viewer to look at the
   painting, four conceived objects have been created (1  2  1  2 = 4).

on change

is change a constant?  this concept is based on the principle that all 
things are subject to change.  the problem is that concepts are things
themselves, from several different viewpoints.  what are the manifestations
of a concept?  

as experience, a concept unfolds through observation.  a concept seems
to materialize as the senses and the mind interpret phenomenon as seeming 
to fit a particular concept.  however, the fit has a duration.  if concepts 
didn't have duration, then the universe would be predictable.

that change is a constant is a concept.  it seems to be accurate.  in fact,
it is accurate but is subject to change.  it is difficult to refute, but
