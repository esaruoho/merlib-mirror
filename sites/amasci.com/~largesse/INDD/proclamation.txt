 Proclamation to Declare May 6, 2002 
 No Diet Day 

 (This is a format you can use when petitioning city, state, county, province,
 or national officials to declare May 6, 2002 No Diet Day.) 

 Whereas: 
 There is a constant barrage of unrealistic and artificial images, especially in the areas of weight and size, advocated
 in all areas of the media as "ideal," especially for women; and 

 Whereas: 
 Scientific research support the conclusion that one result of these images is a continuing epidemic of anorexia,
 bulimia and other potentially fatal eating disorders among the population, especially among teen and pre-teen girls;
 and 

 Whereas: 
 Scientific research supports the conclusion that these images have generated and sustained a climate of prejudice
 and discrimination against the majority of the population who do not fit artificial and current standards of "ideal";
 and 

 Whereas: 
 Scientific research supports the conclusion that "diets don't work;" and 

 Whereas: 
 Citizens of 

 _____________________________________ (name of city, town, state or province) 

 will, on May 6, 2002, join with others around the world to celebrate the rich diversity of body types that is naturally
 inherent in the human race, and the beauty and strength of bodies of all sizes; 

 Therefore: 
 Let it be known to all that May 6, 2002 is proclaimed International No Diet Day in 

 _______________________________. 
 (name of city, town, state or province)
