 Ideas for INDD 
 Events and 
 Activities 

 Film screenings 
 Poetry and book readings 
 Discussion groups 
 Information displays, bulletin boards, literature tables 
 Educational presentations to schools 
 Classroom projects examining advertising for size-negative messages, false claims
 and stereotyping 
 Public celebrations and media events (dinners, rallies, picnics, etc.) 
 Speakers and programs on the dangers of dieting, diet drugs, and the "rehabilitatation"
 of the diet industry 
 Size-positive movement and dance programs 
 Body image workshops and support groups 
 Seminars for health professionals 
 Theatre and street theatre 
 Scale smashings or recyclings 
 Picketing of diet establishments and weight-loss surgery centers 
 Art shows/exhibitions 
 Size-friendly fashion shows 
 Spring cleanings--donating clothes that no longer fit to battered women's shelters 
 Candlelight vigils honoring the victims of weight-loss surgery and eating disorders 
 Collecting signatures on petitions to add "size and weight" to civil rights
 legislation 
 Support for "Truth in Dieting" legislation 
 Comedy skits and routines 
 Group sing-alongs 
 Diet-free dances
