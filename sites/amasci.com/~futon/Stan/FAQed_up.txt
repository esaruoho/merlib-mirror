 Hello! 
 Welcome to my "FAQed-up" page. I hope you find it useful. If you have
any suggestions for FAQ's to add to it, send me a message at 
sgregory@sprynet.com . (Right now I would really like some more hardware FAQ's :) ) 
 Currently, I list FAQ's (and a few other FAQ-like references) in several,
general categories. I try to keep them in near-alphabetical order, but the
word I am sorting by may not be completely obvious, so I have it emphasized 
in my list. A few FAQ titles don't have any word emphasized, which means no
particular word made sense to me to file the FAQ under, and therefore it isn't
sorted. You'll figure it out. :)
