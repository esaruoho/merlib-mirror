For only $169 plus shipping you can get a full-size, three-position Benedict Lounger and full-size, cream-colored "standard" futon mattress (slipcover is additional).

The Benedict Lounger frame allows you to use your futon in three distinct positions: a couch, a bed, and the lounge position.

The slats are "bull-nosed," meaning that they do not have any sharp edges along their length. Many competing futon frames use slats that have sharp edges, possibly damaging your futon mattress or cover.

The slats are set into 2x6's, making it stronger and higher than many other lounger frames which simply use 2x4's or 1x6's.
