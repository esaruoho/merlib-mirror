We carry this line of futon covers for those looking for a very affordable option in print futon covers. These are not clearance futon covers, but rather are made of a very light material, so are best suited for light or short-term use. These covers are a great way to inexpensively add some color to a dorm-room futon!

In addition to these economy futon covers, we also stock solid-color futon slipcovers and SIS futon slipcovers at a great price.
