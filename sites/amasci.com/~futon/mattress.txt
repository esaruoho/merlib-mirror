 Otis Bed's "Stratosphere" Futon Mattress 

 Image Copyright &copy; Otis Bed 

 Otis Bed's "Stratosphere" futon is a true 6 inch mattress.
10 inches of material is compressed to 6 inches to ensure this. 
 This mattress can be used as either a sofa or bed. 
 It is recommended for use on any type of frame. 
 7.25 ounce twill fabric is used to provide the strength needed for this highly compressed mattress. 
 1.5 inch layer of thermal bonded polyester wrap for long mattress life. 
 Two layers of 1.8 inch block high-density foam for superior support and resiliency. 
 Two layers of high-density convoluted foam for support, breathability, and flexibility.
