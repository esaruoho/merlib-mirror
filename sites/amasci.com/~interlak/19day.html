PATTY'S CHINA & YANGTZE RIVER
CRUISE TOUR 2004

Oct. 4 Depart U.S. from Seattle via Vancouver direct flight to Beijing.

5 Arrive Beijing - Met by National Guide and Local guide and transferred
to First Class Hotel in the Central Area of Beijing. Welcoming Dinner
this evening. D

6 Beijing - Visit Tiananmen Square, The Forbidden City, Children's
Palace and a walking tour of the Hutongs. Special Daijiacun Restaurant
and floor show this evening. BLD

7 Beijing - Go to Great Wall at MuTainYu with cable car in both directions
included. Stop at a local village on the way back to the city. BL Dinner
on your own this evening at any of the fine restaurants near the hotel.

8 Beijing - Visit the beautiful Summer Palace, Great Bell Museum, Lama
Temple and visit the Cloisonne Workshop before going to the Temple
of Heaven. Special Peking Duck Dinner this evening. BLD

9 Beijing/Xian - Transfer to the airport for your morning flight to Xian.
After lunch you will visit the old Muslim Quarters, Jade Carving
Workshop and the Wild Goose Pagoda. Special Tang Dynasty Show
this evening after dinner. BLD

10 Xian - You will visit the famous Terra Cotta Warriors site, HuaQing
Pools once the Summer Palace of the Tang Dynasty and the Cave
People of Xian. Special Traditional Dumpling Dinner tonight. BLD

11 Xian/Kunming/LiJiang by mid morning air. Stop in Kunming for Lunch
before going back to the airport for an early evening flight to LiJiang. Transfer to hotel and special traditional Naxi Nationality dinner. BLD

12 LiJiang - You will visit the Yak Meadows with fantastic view of the
Snow Dragon Mountains. Stop at an old Naxi village and perhaps see
Dr. Ho of international Traditional Medicine Fame. Stop at the Black
Dragon Pool Park and Naxi Museum. After Dinner you will see the
performance of the Dongba and his Traditional Naxi Music and Dance
Show. BLD

13 Lijiang - Free Day to explore this wonderful city with 350 small bridges
over the many streams flowing through the city. Great Shopping and
many outdoor cafes with many choices of traditional food as well as
western food. Perhaps Ma Ma Fu's restaurant for dinner which is a
favorite of many foreign guests. B only.

14 LiJiang/Kunming/Guilin by air. Morning free. Transfer to the airport
for your afternoon flight to Kunming and transfer for your flight to
Kunming with a short layover. Arrive in Guilin and transfer to your
hotel and dinner. BD Lunch on your own today.

15 Guilin/Yangshuo via the Li River Cruise. Transfer through the
countryside to the boat dock for your beautiful cruise down the Li
River to the village of Yangshuo. Transfer to your hotel. BLD

16 Yangshuo/Guilin/Chongqing - by evening air. During the day you
are Free to enjoy this wonderful small village. Take a bike ride or
motorized rick shaw into the countryside. Shop for the many
bargains and enjoy the outdoor cafes. B only today. Lunch and
Dinner on your own. Flight to Chongqing is in the evening. Arrive
in Chongqing and transfer to your hotel.

17 Chonqing/Dazu/Chongqing - During the day you will go to Dazu
to see the Budhha Carvings famous all over the world. Wonderful
drive through the countryside and return in time for dinner. Board
your Victoria Line Yangtze River Cruise boat by 7:00 p.m.
BLD

18 Yangtze River Cruise - Shibaozhai or Wanxian shore excursion.
BLD

19 Yangtze River Cruise - Pass the Xiling Gorge, Wu Gorge &
Qutang Gorge. BLD

20 Yangtze River Cruise - Arrive at YiChang in the morning.
Bus to the new Dam Site for an up close tour of the dam.
Transfer to the airport for your flight to Shanghai.  Met in
Shanghai by guide and transferred to your hotel. Balance of
day is free. B only. Lunch and Dinner on your own at one of
the fine restaurants near by.

21 Shanghai - You will visit the beautiful Yu Garden in the old
district one of the finest in China. Visit the new Pearl of the
Orient T.V. Tower from the top for a fantastic view. Lunch
in Pudong. Afternoon visit to the beautiful new Shanghai
National Museum. Special evening performance of the
famous Shanghai Acrobats. BLD

22. Shanghai/Home for the main tour. You will transfer to the
airport for your flight home in the morning.



EXTENSION TOUR

22 Shanghai - Free Day to do as you like. B only. Lunch and dinner on
your own.

23 Shanghai/Suzhou by morning train. Visit the Humble Administrator's
Garden, Tiger Hill Park, the Silk Factory with Fashion Show and lunch,
the Double Sided Embroidery Institute and a boat ride on the Grand
Canal and small canals winding through Suzhou. Transfer to your
hotel. BLD

24 Suzhou/ZhouZhang/Tongli/Suzhou by bus. Visit these two small cities
that are known for having over 60% of the homes still standing from the
Qing and Ming Dynasty once the favorite homes for High Officials.
Many large Markets to visit and explore in both cities before returning
to Suzhou. BLD

25 Suzhou/Shanghai Airport/Home.


Tour includes: National Guide, Airport taxes, Tips, 4 and 5 star hotels or
best available in the remote areas, meals as indicated, local guides
with transportation, sightseeing as listed and Yangtze River Cruise.
 

International Group Air will be quoted 60 days before departure with
departure information from and back to Seattle.  For those who wish to
use Frequent Flyer Miles and make their own arrangements; it will be your
responsibility to arrive in Beijing on time to meet the tour at the first hotel
as well as your departure from the last hotel in Shanghai.  Transfer
will be given to you.
