SAMPLE CUSTOM TOUR

Aug. 10 Depart U.S.

11 Arrive Hong Kong in the evening. (United #1 at 19:20 ) Met by
Agent and transferred to Mandarin Oriental Hotel. On your own here. Agent will give
you your air ticket for Guilin.

12 Hong Kong - On your own here. B only

13 Hong Kong/Guilin in the evening (only flight) Take taxi to airport on your own.
Met by Guide in Guilin and transferred to the Sheraton Hotel on the Li River. B only

14 Guilin/Foggy Resort - Guide will take you on city tour in the morning. After lunch
you will go to the Foggy Resort in the countryside on the Li River. Balance of the
day enjoy the rice fields, countryside village and nearby Crown Cave. Bring your
swimming suits to enjoy the beautiful pool. BLD

15 Foggy Resort and board the Li River Cruiser nearby for your Li River Cruise down to
Yangshuo. Guide will be with you to walk through this little country village for great
bargains where two U.S. Presidents visited. Guide with car will transfer you to the
airport for your flight to Kunming. Met in Kunming by Guide and transferred to the
Harbor Plaza Hotel. BLD Lunch on the boat during your cruise down the LI River.

16 Kunming/LiJiang by morning air (40 minutes) Met by guide and transferred to the
Guan Fang Hotel. Guide will take you to the Dragon Spruce Meadow in this beautiful
Mountain area of snow capped mountains and cooler weather. Visit Yufeng Temple,
Baisha Murals and Black Dragon Pool Park. Special Evening of Classic Music of
Naxi Old People's Band. BLD (Naxi Minority People here in LiJiang)

17 LiJiang to Dali after lunch by van. Morning visit to village Square Markets before
Lunch. Afternoon by van to Dali. Transfer to Asia Star Hotel. Visit the Three Pagodas
Temple and Old Wall. Evening free. BLD

18 Dali - Boat Excursion on Erhai Lake including Bai People's Tea Art Show. Visit
Butterfly Spring and Mr. Yan's House of Xizhou. Visit home of Bai person who makes
the special tie dye clothing and tablecloths. Visit Farmer's Market and beautiful marble
art pieces made locally from the marble being mined on the nearby mountains. BLD

19 Dali/Kunming/Shanghai by air. Pick up luggage in Kunming. Agent will meet you
outside gate and see that you get transferred to the Shanghai flight and luggage check-in.
Met in Shanghai by Guide and transferred to the Grand Nation Hotel on Nanjing Rd. Mall.
B Lunch and Dinner on your own today. (Grand Nation Hotel is located just three blocks
from the Bund area park on the Huangpu River )

20 Shanghai - Guide will take you to the beautiful Yu Garden in the Old District,
Shanghai National Museum (best in China) Oriental Pearl Tower and Children's
Palace (if in session) otherwise a visit to the French Concession area. Evening
special Acrobats Performance. BLD

21 Shanghai/Suzhou by train (50 minutes) Guide will see that you get on the train
and into the right car. Met on the platform in Suzhou by guide and transferred to
the Sheraton Hotel. You will take a boat ride on the Canal which is part of the
world famous Grand Canal and then after lunch go to the Tiger Hill Park. Suzhou
is known for its classic parks and once numbered over 75 parks within the city area.
Walk through the artists district to view the local art. BLD

22 Suzhou/Shanghai by train and met by Agent who will transfer you to the airport
for your flight to Xian. In A.M. Guide will take you to the Silk Factory to see how silk
is processed and to the showroom with samples from all the local factories. A visit
to the Embroidery work shop to see the two sided embroidery process and
showroom is unbelievable. BL Met by Guide in Xian and Transferred to the Grand
New World Hotel. Dinner on your own.

23 Xian - Guide will take you to the Terra Cotta Warriors site, HuaQing Pools and the
Bampo Museum (ancient village site) Evening performance of the Tang Dynasty Show
one of the most beautiful in China. Special Traditional Dumpling Dinner tonight also.
BLD

24 Xian/Beijing by afternoon air. Guide will take you to the Wild Goose Pagoda, Ancient
Culture Street and old Mosque. After lunch walk a short section of the City Wall before
going to the airport (Xian was the old world capital and this Wall is the best preserved
in China) Met in Beijing by Guide and transferred to the Capital Hotel in the central
District just two blocks from Tiananmen Square. (superior room at the Capital hotel)

25 Beijing - On your own today B only

26 Beijing - On your own today B only

27 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Great Wall at Mutianyu with cable car included in
both directions. Stop at the Lama Temple on the way back to the hotel. BLD Special
dinner tonight at the Daijiacun restaurant with free floor show of authentic Dai minority
people

28 Beijing/U.S. departure. Go to airport on your own. Be at airport 2 hours before flight
time for check in.

Included: Hotels, meals as listed, domestic transportation as listed, English speaking
guide with driver,sightseeing as listed and some evening entertainment as listed.
Hotel and restaurant tips are included except for bell boy luggage handling in hotel.
Not included: Tips to guides and drivers (tour packet has guidelines for you) airport
taxes of $6 domestic and $11 international departures payable at booths within the
airport (guides will help you with this procedure) Visa fee ( will send you the forms
and directions) and any expense of a personal nature or not specifically included
above.
