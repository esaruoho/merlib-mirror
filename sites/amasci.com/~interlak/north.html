NORTH CHINA AND MONGOLIA
INCLUDING BEIJING, XIAN AND SHANGHAI

Day 1 Depart the U.S. or Your Country

2 Arrive Beijing - Met by Mr. Zhang Jing (you may call him "Jing")
Transfer to your central area hotel. Welcoming Dinner if you have
an early arrival.

3 Beijing - Jing will take you to Tiananmen Square, the Forbidden
City, Hutongs and the Summer Palace today. BLD

4 Beijing - Jing will take you to the Great Wall at MuTianYu with
Cable car included in both directions. On the way back you will
visit a local village and family home before returning to the city.
BLD

5 Beijing/Datong by night train. During the day you will visit the
Lama Temple with the 4 story tall Buddha carved from one tree
trunk, the Temple of Heaven and the Silk Market shopping area
before dinner. After dinner you will transfer to the train station
for your first class sleeper to Datong. BLD

6 Datong - Met by the local guide at the train station in the morning.
Guide will take you to the Huayan Monastery and visit the
famous Yun Gang Grottoes. Transfer to your hotel. BLD

7 Datong/Baotou - During the day your guide will take you through
the countryside to the amazing "Hanging Temple" over 700 years
old. Guide will take you back to the city to the train station for
your evening train to Baotou. BLD

8 Baotou - Arrive in the morning and met by your local guide at
the station. Guide will take you through the countryside to
the Wu Dang Zhao Temple often referred to as the "Little Potala
Palace" of Tibet. Return to the city and transfer to your hotel. BLD

9 Baotou - Guide will take you on a day trip to the "Singing Sands"
edge of a great desert with 100 foot wall of sand. Take a camel ride
if you wish to really experience the desert. Return to Baotou in the
evening. BLD

10 Baotou/Grasslands - Guide will take you through the countryside to the
Grasslands and transfer to the Mongolian Camp Yurts. Special evening
party put on by the local people. BLD

11 Grasslands/Hohhot by car. Guide will take you to Hohhot in the
afternoon after a visit with a Mongolian family in their home.
Transfer to your hotel in the evening. BLD

12 Hohhot/Xian by air. During the day depending on the air schedule the
Guide may take you to the Muslim Mosque or Mongolian Temple.
Transfer to the airport. In Xian you will be met by the local guide and
transferred to your hotel. Special Evening dinner of Traditional
Dumplings. BLD

13 Xian - Guide will take you to the famous Terra Cotta Warriors site, the
HuaQing Pools once the Summer Palace of the Tang Dynasty and a visit
to the City Market and Jade Carving Workshop. Special evening
performance of the Tang Dynasty Show. BLD

14 Xian/Shanghai by afternoon air. In the morning your guide will take you
to the Wild Goose Pagoda and the Old Muslim Quarters. After lunch you
will take a short walk on the City Wall (best preserved city wall in China)
before transferring to the airport. In Shanghai you will be met by the
local guide and transferred to your central area hotel. BL Dinner on
your own this evening at any of the great restaurants on Nanjing Road.

15 Shanghai - Guide will take you to the famous Yu Garden in the Old
District, The French Concession area with visit to the Xiang Yang
Market on Weihai Road. After lunch you will go to the Shanghai
National Museum, the best in China with many artifacts from ancient
tombs. Special performance of the famous Shanghai Acrobats. BLD

16 Shanghai/Suzhou/Shanghai - Guide will transfer you to the train station
and see that you get seated in your reserved seats. (One hour train ride)
When you arrive in Suzhou Remain on the Platform and the local guide
will find you. Guide will take you to the Humble Administrator's Garden,
the Silk Factory, and the Tiger Hill Park. You will take a short boat ride
on the Grand Canal before dinner. After dinner the guide will take you
back to the train station for your ride back to Shanghai. (Please take a
card from your Shanghai hotel to give to a taxi driver upon arriving back
in Shanghai) BLD

17 Shanghai/Home Guide will transfer you to the airport for your flight
Home.
