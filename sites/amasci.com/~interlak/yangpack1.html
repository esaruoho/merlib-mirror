11 DAY YANGTZE RIVER
CRUISE & TOUR 

DAY 1 Depart your country

2 Arrive Shanghai – Met by guide and transferred to your hotel. 

3 Shanghai – Guide will take you to the Yu Garden in the old district, the
French Concession Area with beautiful old mansions in the French tradition,
and a walk on the Bund Park on the river front. After dinner you will attend
the World Famous Shanghai Acrobats Show. BLD 

4 Shanghai/Chongqing by afternoon air. In the morning you will go to the new
Shanghai National Museum. After lunch you will transfer to the airport for
your afternoon flight to Chongqing. You will be met by a local guide and
taken on a short tour of the city before dinner. After dinner the guide will
take you to the Victoria Line boat dock for evening early boarding. (no
services available in the evening) BLD 

5 Yangtze River Cruise – Depart 09:00. Shore excursion at Fengdu, the
“Ghost City”. Captains cocktail party this evening. BLD

6 Yangtze River Cruise – Sail through the Qutang Gorge. Dock at Wushan
for an off boat excursion through the Small Three Gorges in Sampans. Pass
through the Wu Gorge. Evening show at Zigui. BLD

7 Yangtze River Cruise – Sail through the Xiling Gorge and then an off boat
excursion to see the new Three Gorges Dam being built. Farewell Banquet
tonight with floor show. BLD 

8 Arrive Wuhan after lunch. Local guide will come on board to meet you and
transfer you to the airport for your flight to Beijing. In Beijing you will be
met by a local guide and transferred to your hotel. BLD 

9 Beijing – Guide will take you to Tiananmen Square, Forbidden City and a
short tour of the Hutongs. BL Dinner on your own tonight. 

10 Beijing – Guide will take you to the Great Wall at Badaling with a stop at
the Ming Tombs and Cloisonn workshop. BLD

11 Beijing – Guide will transfer you to the airport for your international flight home.
