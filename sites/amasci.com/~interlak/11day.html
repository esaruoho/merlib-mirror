PATTY'S PLACES SPECIAL
11 DAY YANGTZE RIVER CRUISE
INCLUDING BEIJING & SHANGHAI

Oct. 29 Los Angeles to Beijing

30 Arrive Beijing - Met by Guide and transferred to your 4 star hotel on the famous
Wangfujing Shopping street. Evening arrival.

31 Beijing - Guide will take you to Tiananmen Square, Forbidden City and the
Summer Palace. BLD

Nov. 1 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Great Wall at MuTianYu with cable car ride
in both directions. Stop at the Lama Temple on your return to the city. BLD

2 Beijing/Chongqing by afternoon air. Guide will take you to the Temple of Heaven
in the morning before lunch. After lunch you will be transferred to the airport for
your flight to Chongqing. Met in Chongqing by an Agent and transferred to your
4 star hotel. BLD

3 Board your Regal Cruise Boat in the morning. First stop is the "ghost city" of
Fengdu. BLD

4 Cruise through the Qutang Gorge with dramatic steep cliffs. Take a most exciting
trip in pea pod boats through the Small Three Gorges pulled by trackers like in the
ancient days. Wonderful evening show by the talented crew. BLD

5 Cruise thorugh the Xiling Gorge and then stop at the new Three Gorges Dam site
to see the progress of one of the biggest undertakings in history. Pass through the
dam locks and continue the cruise down stream. BLD

6 Arrive in Wuhan. Met by the local guide and transferred to the airport for your flight
to Shanghai. Met in Shanghai by guide and transferred to your 4 star hotel. BLD
Dinner in Shanghai.

7 Shanghai - Guide will take you to the famous Yu Gardens in the old district and the
Pearl Tower for a fantastic view of the city. After lunch you will visit the new National
Museum, one of the best in China. Take a nice walk down Nanjing Mall near your
hotel before an early dinner. Evening performance of the famous Shanghai Acrotats.
BLD.

8 Shanghai/Los Angeles. Your guide will transfer you to the new PUDONG airport for
your flight back to the U.S. and your connecting flights home.

Included:
International Air from the West Coast to China and all domestic flights. Meals as indicated,
4 star hotels, guides with local transportation and some evening entertainment as listed.
Airport taxes of $6 per person and $11 international will be included.

Not included: Tips to guides and drivers, visa fee, and any expenses of a personal nature
or not specifically listed as included.
