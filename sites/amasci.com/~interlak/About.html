INTERLAKE CHINA TOURS, INC.

We are a tour operator with an office in Seattle, Washington since 1992. We specialize in China Only to people who are looking to see more than just the sights of China but to experience the culture of China as well. Special interests can always be worked into your tour with the abundance of diversity that China as well as Tibet has to offer to all that come to visit and tour.

We do custom group tours for organizations, universities and clubs with special interests or needs. Our specialty is doing customized tours for two to four people and individuals who have special interests or just want a tour that doesn’t fit a standard mold tour. Limited Group tours are available also including the magnificent Yangtze River Cruises or Li River Cruise that can be added on or included.

Our tour planner and owner, Dave Bruels, has traveled China for the past 11 years and over 35 trips to some of the far corners of the country. Dave has lectured in China under the United Nations "Star Program" and worked with government agencies, companies and travel services during his tenure in China. Working with guides, travel services, inspecting hotels, seeing some of the various entertainment shows and new sights are just part of his job in providing great tours for our customers.

As new parts of China are opening up to tourism, Dave travels to each new area to check out the facilities first hand so that our customers are assured of the accommodations and sightseeing meeting their level of comfort and interest. Whether it is some of the remote spots such as the Three Sister Mountain areas, forest areas of beautiful JiuZhaiGou Valley with its water falls and beautiful natural colored water lakes, Huangshan Mountains accessible only by cable car, or the recently opened up areas of Yunnan province such as LiJiang, Dali, Shangri-La and Xishuangbanna with over 27 minority peoples each with their own colorful clothing and culture, you will have the experience of a lifetime. You might even want to visit the only pure matriarchal society left in the world in the Muso people of Lugo Lake at 6000 feet deep in the mountains of Yunnan province. They never marry, have no jails, no police, no city but live around a fantasy like lake surrounded by mountains and yes there is a guest house available to stay with modern facilities.

Dave writes most of his own materials that are updated each year giving his customers the latest in travel information within China. China is changing so rapidly with many modern conveniences that 10 years ago were not even available. China is still a great contrast of the very old and the very new which can be seen all over China. Houses still built without nails or modern equipment and yet beautiful modern hotels and buildings being built in the larger cities.

Most people only go to China once in their life time or maybe a second trip because they were amazed at what a fantastic experience they had the first time. If you are looking for that kind of trip whether you have one week or four weeks time available, call or email Dave and see what he can plan for you. He can plan a tour to fit most budgets or to your special requirements. Photography and people are his hobbies and you will find him a wealth of information about touring China.

We are looking forward to serving you.
