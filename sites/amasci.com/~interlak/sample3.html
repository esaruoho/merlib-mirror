SAMPLE CUSTOM TOUR

July 3 Arrive Beijing United Flight #853 from Tokyo at 19:40 Met by guide and
transferred to the Capital Hotel in the central district.

4 Beijing - Guide will take you through the Forbidden City, Hutongs Tour and
Madam Soong's last home. (example of pre '49 mansions) BLD

5 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Summer Palace, Great Bell Museum and
Lama Temple. Evening performance of Peking Opera. BLD

6 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Great Wall at MuTianYu including the
Cable car up and down to the Great Wall. Stop at Ming Tombs if time
permits. BLD

7 Beijing/Xian by air. Before departure you will visit the Temple of Heaven Park.
Met in Xian by guide and transferred to the Garden Hotel. BLD

8 Xian - Guide will take you to the Terra Cotta Warriors site and Huaqing Pools.
Special Tang Dynasty Show tonight. BLD

9 Xian/Chengdu by air. Guide will take you to the Wild Goose Pagoda and walk
the Old City Wall, one of the few left in China. Met in Chengdu by guide and
transferred to the JinJiang Hotel. BLD.

10 Chengdu/Leshan by car. See the largest Buddha in the world over 1000 years
old by boat and then visit the Temple for a close up view. Transfer to the
Jiazhou Hotel. BLD (best hotel available in this area)

11 Leshan/Chengdu by car by way of Yellow Dragon Creek Village to visit
Sichuan people and see some of the very beautiful countryside. Check back
into the JinJiang Hotel in Chengdu. BLD

12 Chengdu - Guide will take you to the Panda Preserve with a beautiful drive
to this wilderness area about 1 1/2 hours drive from Chengdu and return in the
evening. BLD

13 Chengdu/Lhasa, Tibet by early morning air. Met at the airport by guide and
transferred to the Lhasa Hotel. Afternoon free to get use to the altitude. BLD

14 Lhasa - Guide will take you on a city tour to include the Grand Potala Palace,
Jokang Temple and Sera Monastery. BLD

15 Lhasa/Xigatse by car. Visit the Tashillumpo Monastery (former seat of the
Panchen Lama) Transfer to the Xigatse Hotel. BLD

16 Xigatse/Gyantse by car. You will pass through the Grassland of Yak, sheep and
horses being raised by herdsmen. Visit the Baiju Temple and Pagoda.
Transfer to the Gyantse Hotel. BLD

17 Gyantse/Lhasa by car. On the way you will visit the Nobutinla which is the
Dalai Lama's summer palace. Also see the Drapung monastery and transfer
back to the Lhasa Hotel. BLD

18 Lhasa/Chengdu/Kunming by air. Met by Guide and transferred to the
Kunming Hotel. Free the balance of the afternoon. BLD (someone will meet
you in Chengdu and see that you get on the Kunming flight)

19 Kunming/Lijiang early flight. Home to many minorities people - Surrounded by many
snow capped mountains. Guide will meet you and take you to the Grand LiJiang Hotel.
Visit Dragon Spruce Meadow and Yufeng Temple. BLD (one of my favorite cities)

20 LiJiang - Visit the old section of the city known as the city of waters as you will see.
Baisha Murals and Black Dragon Pool Park sites. Evening performance of Traditional
Naxi music played by the Elders of the area. (hosted in English and Chinese) BLD

21 LiJiang/Kunming/Chongqing by air. Met by guide and taken to the Yangtze hotel. Go
to the Chongqing Zoo to see the Panda Bears. BLD

22 Chongqing/Yangtze River Cruise (brochure enclosed with details of cruise)

23 Yangtze River Cruise

24 Yangtze River Cruise

25 Yangtze River Cruise and arrive in Wuhan - met by agent and transferred to airport for
your flight to Shanghai. In Shanghai met by guide and transferred to the Peace Hotel
(Art Deco Style of the 1920's) remodeled of course but still kept the style. Located on
the Bund next to the Huangpu River. BLD

26 Shanghai - Guide will take you to the famous Yu Garden in the old district and
afternoon to the new National Museum on People's Square. Evening performance of
the famous Shanghai Acrobats. BLD

27 Shanghai/Hangzhou by first class train. (referred to as "soft seat") Met by guide and
taken on the beautiful West Lake Cruise. Transfer to the Sunny Hotel. BLD

28 Hangzhou/Guilin by evening air. During the day you will visit the Lin Yin Temple,
Gold Fish viewing in Hua Gang, Six Harmonies Pagoda and visit the Tea Factory and
tasting house. Met by guide in Guilin and transferred to the Guishan Hotel. BLD

29 Guilin/Yangshuo via the Li River Cruise with stop at Crown Cave and countryside
village and farms. Transfer to the Paradise Hotel. BLD

30 Yangshuo/Guilin/Hong Kong by car and air. Met by Agent in Hong Kong and
transferred to the BP International Hotel on Kowloon Park. BL only

31 Hong Kong - on your own here - no meals included.

Aug. 1 Hong Kong - Transfer to the airport for your continued trip with departure time
from Hong Kong as 15:30

Your tour includes: All transportation, hotels, all meals except where noted, guide with
car and driver,scheduled sight seeing with evening entertainment as listed above. Hong
Kong will only include your hotel and transfers.

Not included are tips to guides and drivers, airport taxes ($6 pp domestic and $11
international) Chinese visa which I will send the forms and instructions. Additional sights
if time permits not included above may incur an additional cost on your part and can be
negotiated with your guide. See terms and conditions sheet for details.
