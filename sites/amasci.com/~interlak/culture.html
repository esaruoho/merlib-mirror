Jing will be your guide in Beijing taking you to all the great sights including the Forbidden City, Tiananmen Square, The Great Wall, Temple of Heaven and many more. Then on to Datong to see the Yun Gang Grottoes, Huayan Monastery and the 700 year old "Hanging Temple". On to Baotou to see the Wu Dang Zhao Temple known as the "Little Potala Palace" of Tibet. See the edge of the desert and the "Singing Sands". You can even take a camel ride out onto the desert sands. Visit the Grasslands and stay in a modern Mongolian Yurt and visit a Mongolian family in their home. In Xian you will see the famous Terra Cotta Warriors, Summer Palace of the Tang Dynasty and other great sights. In Shanghai you will see the famous Yu Garden and National Museum plus seeing the World famous Shanghai Acrobats. A side trip to Suzhou well known as the Venice of the East and traditional Chinese gardens. For details click on the tour title above.

ANCIENT YELLOW RIVER AREA TOUR
Including Beijing, Xian, Shanghai & Suzhou

Includes: Beijing, Xian, LuoYang, Zhengzhou, Shanghai
Suzhou, ZhouZhang, and Tongli

PRICE: From $1495 pp. dbl. occ. and up.

The Yellow River area is the birth place of Chinese history. Visit Beijing and let "Jing" show you the wonders of Beijing. Then to Xian the Old World Capital seeing the Terra Cotta Warriors and HuaQing Pools of the Tang Dynasty. Take a train to LuoYang the ancient capital of China and see the famous Longmen Grottoes and Urban Cave people. Before leaving the city you will visit the Cave People still living in caves today as well as White Horse Temple the first Buddhist Temple in China. On the way to Zhengzhou you will visit the famous Shaolin Temple and mingle with the Monks of Kungfu fame. See the largest wholesale market of Guan Lin before going on to Shanghai. You will feast your eyes on the #1 garden in China, the Yu Garden, and the National Museum which houses so many articles discovered in ancient tombs. A side trip to the water cities of Suzhou, Tongli and ZhouZhang will give you some insights to the Ming and Qing Dynasty as here is where important officials once lived.
