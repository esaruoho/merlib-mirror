15 DAY YANGTZE RIVER CRUISE & TOUR
We use the……..VICTORIA CRUISE LINE

Day 1 Depart from the West Coast and cross over the international date line to
arrive in China the next day. Depart L.A., San Diego, Las Vegas, S.F., Portland,
Seattle, Phoenix & other west coast cities without additional air charge. Most
other major U.S. Cities please add $250 r/t air. Own international Air you may
deduct $595 from the cost.

Day 2 Arrive in Beijing in the early evening and transfer to the Wangfujing Hotel
near Tiananmen Square and the shopping district of central Beijing.

Day 3 Beijing - Visit the Forbidden City, Tiananmen Square, Hutongs Walking
Tour. Afternoon free to get over your jet lag, just walk the Shopping Mall of
Wangfujing Street or visit Xiushu Market on Changon Avenue for great bargains.
BL Dinner on your own this evening at any of many good restaurants near by.

Day 4 Beijing - You will take a bus or car through the countryside to the GREAT
WALL at MuTianYu to include the cable car both ways onto the Wall and back.
On the way back we will go to the Temple of Heaven in Beijing. BLD

Day 5 Beijing - You will visit the Summer Palace of the Empress Dowager of
the Qing Dynasty, and visit the very beautiful Lama Temple. In the evening you
will go to one of the 1930's Tea Houses for snacks and show including some
Peking Opera. BLD

Day 6 Beijing/Xian by air and transfer to the Jianguo hotel. You will visit some
craft factories and perhaps you would like to take a walk on the Wall
surrounding the main part of the city. Tang Dynasty Show in the evening (one
of the best in China) BLD

Day 7 Xian - You will visit one of the Wonders of the World discovered in the
1970's. The TERRA COTTE WARRIORS of Xian is one of the strangest stories
on earth. On the way back you will visit the Ancient Huaqing Pools where the
Emperor and the Concubines bathed in the mineral waters. A visit to the Jade
carving factory will be included before dinner. BLD

Day 8 Xian/Chongqing by air and transfer to the Wanyou Hotel. Some
sightseeing while youare in Chongqing a very old and ancient city. BLD

Day 9 Board your Victoria Cruise Boat in the morning and start our trip down
stream. BLD (Victoria Cruise brochure will give you details of the cruise)

Day 10 Yangtze River Cruise - 3 Minor Gorge side trip today for a fantastic view
on motorized boats with lunch in the Gorge. BLD

Day 11 Continue your cruise through the Gorges viewing river traffic and
beautiful scenery as you continue on down stream to the new Dam site. Off
boat tour by bus to the dam site for a close up view and inspection. BLD

Day 12 Arrive Wuhan afternoon and transfer to the airport for your flight to
Shanghai. If there is time you will do some sight seeing on the way to the airport.
Fly to Shanghai to be met by the local guide and transfer to the Peace Hotel on
the Bund. BLD

Day 13 Shanghai - Visit the very famous Yu Garden in the old district of Shanghai.
See the Bund on the waterfront, perhaps a hand loom rug factory and the new
National Museum. Evening Show with the famous Shanghai Acrobatics. BLD

Day 14 Shanghai/Suzhou/Shanghai by train for the day. Just 45 minutes by train
is Suzhou most famous for its beautiful Formal Gardens, Silk and known as the
Venice of the East because of its many canals running through the city. Return
to Shanghai after a full day tour in Suzhou. BLD

Day 15 Shanghai/West Coast City of your departure. Time for packing or a last
minute shopping on Nanjing Rd. Arrive back in the U.S. on the same day.
