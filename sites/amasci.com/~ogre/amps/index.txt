 Most of us guitarists suffer from an affliction called GAS 
 - G ear A cquisition S yndrome. That means 
 we are buying gear nearly compulsively - more and more often

 Breaking the Law? 
 By Stefan Leonhardt 
 Ok, you have a gig next weekend. Your band has enough songs, 
 you and your bandmates have your parts down and rehearsals 
 are sounding great. You're confident that you'll pull it off 
 without a sweat, nothing can stop you. Nothing? Nothing except 
 a phenomenon called Murphy's Law. Murphy's Law says that if 
 something can go wrong, it will go wrong. The more electronics 
 and gear are involved, the greater the chance of something 
 not functioning. 
 Loop Garou 
 By Stefan Leonhardt 
 Most amps today have more sockets than just "input". 
 Chances are, your amp also has two labeled "send" 
 and "return" - the Effects Loop. What's it good 
 for other than a feature that might give the salesman more 
 to talk about? Read up on it here.
