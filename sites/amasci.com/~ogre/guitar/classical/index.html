Classical Guitar Lessons

The purpose of my Column "The Art of Finger Dancing," is to give the classical guitarist of any level new techniques and insights to advance their creative thinking. With this column I hope to teach the traditional, because within the traditional lies a foundation. However, I hope to stimulate the guitarist with inspiration to reach for new sounds and concepts above and beyond the traditional. "The Art of Finger Dancing" column is designed to be a technique column because only through technique are all things possible.

Rock Guitarist as Classical Guitarist Part 3 - Finger Placement and Finger Preparation
by Logan L. Gabriel (11 Jul 2000)
I was originaly going to write this article about just finger placement, then it dawned on me that finger placement and finger preparation go hand in hand. Finger placement deals with playing the string from the same point of the finger. Finger preparation is just that, preparing the finger in such a way so that the muscles responsible for finger action are trained to place the finger at the same spot each time.
