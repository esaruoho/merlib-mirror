 Progressive Rock Guitar Study 
 Series 

 These studies are designed for intermediate and 
 advanced players who are hungry for that "extra 
 push" to take their playing to an entirely new 
 level. They introduce you to ideas and approaches 
 that change the way you look at your guitar forever. 

 Once you understand the concepts presented 
 in these studies, you'll begin to "think for yourself" 
 and become self-sufficient in your creativity 
 and technique. The techniques presented in these 
 studies challenge your physical ability. As a 
 result, they mold you into an undeniably exciting 
 guitarist. We guarantee it! 
 Full Screen, Easy To Read Sheet Music 
 The sheet music can be viewed full screen on 
 PCs with 800x600 resolution. You have plenty of 
 room to hold your guitar and not be squinting 
 at your computer. It's also an easy read on PCs 
 with 1024x768 screen resolution! 

 Each lesson's sheet music has standard notation 
 as well as guitar tablature right there together 
 so there's no mistake about what to play. The 
 sheet music also shows you which fingers to use 
 for Michael's unique style of right hand tapping. 

 Also included are notation legends so you're 
 never in the dark with tablature symbols. 
 Stereo MP3 Sound Files 

 Each study has two MP3 files. One of Michael 
 playing the study at normal tempo and the other 
 of Michael performing the piece slower so you 
 can clearly hear what he's playing and maximize 
 your learning.
