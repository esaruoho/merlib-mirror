 Blues Feeling 
 Blues Lesson # 4 
 Published By Christian 
 Cayeux 

 This is another intermediate lesson. The goal of this lesson is to show 
 you how to get a good blues feeling. Don't think about it. Just pretend you 
 are Jimi Hendrix and play. 

 Hi! 
 Ok, here it is this first lesson. It's not too difficult to play but if you're 
 a beginner, don't try to play this lesson till now : you could get bad habits. 

 You'll have to play this little piece the more carefully possible. The goal 
 of this lesson is to help you, and show you the way to get a good blues feeling. 

 First, take a look at the scale used : 

--------------------------------10-13---------------------
-------------------------11--13---------------------------
-------------------10-12----------------------------------
-------------10-12----------------------------------------
-------10-13----------------------------------------------
-10-13----------------------------------------------------

 Play it carefelly. First slowly and then play more and more quickly. But don't 
 forget the goal is not to play like a chainsaw! ;-) 

 You got it? Ok let's play the bluuuees. 

 Very important : read carefully the notation and play effects as I tell you. 
 Play in the good timing. 

 Notation : 

 - The 1 & 2 & .... helps you to play in the time. 
 - pb : pre-bend, first bend the string until you get a note one step higher, 
 then strike the string and release... 
 - b : bend. 
 - p : pulling-off. Make sure to make sound it good. 
 - s : silence. Don't make noise! ;-) 
 - h : hammer. 

 Try to make it sound. Don't really think about the way you're playing but just 
 about what you're playing. Just look at BB King, Buddy Guy for example, you 
 can see they're playing with their heart. They're living lessons. Just think 
 you're Jimi Hendrix and play.
