 The following Licks are very simple and as I think they sound authentic.

 Hello there, this is my first sheet of (Ascii) Music. These are only a few 
 Licks I came across the last years. If you like this page, or you have comments 
 or corrections or you want more Licks , mail me ! 
 Special Note: The following Licks are very simple and as I think they sound 
 authentic. Especially for Beginners, they sound cool and 
 help to "open the Blues ear..." 
 Notation Info.... 
 (BU)=Bend Up / (RE)= Release / (HO)=Hammer On / (PU)=Pull Off / (V)=Vibrate 
 / (SL)=Slide
