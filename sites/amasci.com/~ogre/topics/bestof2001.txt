 Now that this year is almost over we are going to spend this 
 month making lists. No, these lists have nothing to do with 
 Christmas shopping. To finish 2001 off with a bang the members 
 of Guitar Noise are going to share their best of lists. We 
 are calling this month's topic "The Joy of Guitar," 
 something that we are glad to share with you all year round.
