 Guitar Noise International 

 As Guitar Noise grows we become more and more international. 
 The double effect of this is the localization our site 
 for different regions, while at the same time giving 
 those regions greater exposure to the rest of the world. 
 Currently we have specialized content sites for Canada 
 and China. We would like to expand to include special 
 European content. If you would like to partner with 
 Guitar Noise by translating content for your country 
 please contact the webmaster . 

 Guitar Noise Canada 
 Canada is the second most popular home for our visitors. To make 
 Candians feel more welcome we have put together a few pages that 
 will certainly qualify under Canadian content regulations. We are 
 not just making this addition to kiss up to all the visitors from 
 the Great White North. Guitar Noise was actually begun in Canada 
 at Carleton University, and two of our writers are Canadians. So 
 don't tell us to take off, eh?
