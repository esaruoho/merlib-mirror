18 Til I Die
by Bryan Adams
The book contains 13 songs, including the title track and: Black Pearl, Do To You, Have You Ever Really Loved A Woman, Let's Make A Night To Remember, The Only Thing That Looks Good on Me Is You and more. (buy online)

The Best Of
by Sarah McLachlan
This includes 12 of her finest songs: Adia, Building a Mystery, Drawn to the Rhythm, Elsewhere, Good Enough, Hold On, Ice, Into the Fire, Lost, The Path of Thorns (Terms), Shelter, Sweet Surrender. Also includes a special color section. (buy online)

The Collection
by The Tea Party
Contains hits from their first three releases: Splendor Solis, The Edges of Twilight and Transmission. Titles are: Army Ants, The Bazaar, Emerald, Fire in the Head, Inanna, Psychopomp, The River, Save Me, Sister Awake, Temptation, Winter Solstice. (buy online)

The Randy Bachman Collection
by Randy Bachman
21 classics, including songs from Bachman Turner Overdrive and The Guess Who. Songs include: American Woman, No Sugar Tonight, No Time, Takin' Care Of Business, These Eyes, You Ain't Seen Nothin' Yet and more. (buy online)

The Best Of
by The Tragically Hip
This superb import folio offers 15 of the band's best including both guitar and bass parts. Songs include: Courage, Locked In The Trunk Of A Car, Fifty Mission Cap, Little Bones, Cordelia, Three Pistols, New Orleans Is Sinking, 38 Years and more. (buy online)

Anthology
by Rush
Songs include: Between the Wheels, The Big Money, The Body Electric, The Camera Eye, Different Strings, The Enemy Within, Free Will, Limelight New World Man, Presto, Roll the Bones, Something for Nothing, Tom Sawyer, The Trees and more. (buy online)

Unplugged
by Neil Young
Authentic Guitar Tab includes: The Old Laughing Lady, World on a String, Like a Hurricane, The Needle and the Damage Done, Helpless, Harvest Moon, Transformer Man, Unknown Legend, Look Out for My Love, Long May you Run, From Hank to Hendrix. (buy online)
