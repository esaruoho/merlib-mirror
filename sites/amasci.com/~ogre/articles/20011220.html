The Best Album Cover Of 2001


Published December 20, 2001
By Paul Hackett

Email to a Friend
Bookmark this page

Let me share a secret with you. I like to collect fake CDs. I don't mean collections of unauthorized MP3s that your friend downloaded and burned on his computer. And I don't mean bootlegged Phish concerts either. I mean counterfeit CDs that were made to look like as much like the genuine article as possible. I mean those phony albums sold in back rooms from cardboard boxes that don't even come with a CD jewel case.

Recently I read in Time magazine that two thirds of CDs and movies sold in Asia are not genuine articles. Living overseas it is not difficult to add to my collection.

Now, I know you may be wondering why I collect these things. Simply put, counterfeiting is an art form. And the forgers always mark their work with some kind of distinguishing mark. If the fake is too good no one can appreciate the work that went into it. I have come to appreciate this type of work, and have started collecting the best examples of it. The tell tale trademark of any good counterfeit CD is a simple spelling error. Sometimes you may have to read through the booklet to find it in the lyrics. Usually you won't have to look that hard. Have no doubt, every fake you are likely to see has some tell tale sign, like a counterfeiter's signature if you will. It is meant to share the secret with the people in the know.

Anyway, this year I added the following CD to my collection. It is by no means my favorite music, but it certainly is the best album cover I came across this year. Do you think I might be able to get it autographed by the artist(s)?

Paul Hackett 2001-12-20
