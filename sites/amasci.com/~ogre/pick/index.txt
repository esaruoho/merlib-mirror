 GUITAR PICKS 

 Guitar Picks features interviews with guitarists and other 
 musicians about their songwriting techniques and experiences 
 with independant or major record labels. These frank answers 
 by people who have already made it often deal with songwriting, 
 inspiration, working within the limits of record companies' 
 wishes, and guitar techniques.
