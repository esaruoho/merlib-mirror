 alternate tunings to coping with the emtional ups and downs

 Where To Start 
 If you're a beginner who wants to know more about theory, 
 we suggest you start with latest theory series which begins 
 with The Musical 
 Genome Project . If you're an intermediate player who wants 
 to learn about alternate tuning and what you can do with it, 
 check out On The 
 Tuning Awry . And of course there's also the wonderful 
 series which teaches you how you can learn songs right from 
 the recordings instead of relying on TAB. Happy 
 New Ear is the first piece of that set.

 Easy 
 Songs For Beginners 
 If you already have a grasp of guitar basics (holding your 
 guitar, tuning it) then the next step is to start building 
 a repetoire of songs. This series of lessons will teach you 
 a bit of music theory and technique while you learn to play 
 some pretty easy and well known songs. For best results you 
 really should begin with the first lesson and work your way 
 up. 
 Songs 
 For Intermediates 
 What we&#146;re going to do with these lessons is take the 
 next logical step in moving from being a strict beginner to 
 being a well-rounded guitarist. This will cover a lot of different 
 topics. If you&#146;ve read any of the Easy Songs For Beginners 
 pieces, you already know that I prefer to teach lessons by 
 using specific songs as examples.
