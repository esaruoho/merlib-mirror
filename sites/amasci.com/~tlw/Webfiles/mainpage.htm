THE ELECTRONIC CLOSET

The Electronic Closet is a small business dedicated to providing cost-effective solutions for the underwater acoustic testing needs of the maritime community. Our staff has over 30 years experience in hardware and software design related to testing underwater acoustic transducers and performing underwater acoustic measurements.

We also provide operation and maintenance support for Cell Sentry system, a portable, security system in use by the US Government and Washington State Government.
