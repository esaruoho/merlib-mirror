Christine's Writing 

 Curse of the Shadow Beasts -- Christine's debut fantasy novel and start
of the MageLore Series, with foreword by Steve Jackson. 

 Gargoyles Fan Fiction -- the longest continuous one-author fanfic universe
in the fandom, with close to fifty stories. Many are for mature readers
only. 

 Other Fan Fiction -- from Disney to Star Trek and beyond. Mature readers
only. 

 Original Fiction -- game-related stories drawn from two different campaigns,
plus several other projects from holiday poems to works submitted for publication. 

 Non-Fiction -- shared moments from Tim and Christine's wedding.
