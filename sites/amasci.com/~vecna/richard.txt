The Loves of Sir Richard by Christine Morgan 

 A word of explanation by the author: these stories are set in
my game-world, which I have been developing since the early 1980's. The
premier deity of that world is Dorian, Goddess of Life. Much of Dorianism
is drawn from Jean M. Auel's "Earth's Children" series. These stories
are dedicated to my husband, Tim, for whom they were written as birthday
and anniversary presents.

Mature readers only, please. 

 Coming of Age 
Vrushenka would rather be a soldier than a lady, until she and her childhood
friend Richard discover what it means to be adults. 

 The King's Champion 
King David of Orain is dead, and his sworn knight must bear the news to
Queen Rowena. 

 Winter Princess 
King Ludwig of Tarlak has locked his daughter in the decrepit north tower.
Can Richard get to her in time?
