Alchemy Recipies

By Christine Morgan
Alchemy: The Secret Art
        Lead into gold? Maybe not. But alchemy in the form of
various useful potions and elixirs is a steadfast part of fantasy
games.
        For simplicity and space considerations, the GURPS
supplements have limited alchemy to descriptions of effects and
costs of materials. But for the GM who wishes to really make his or
her alchemists work for their ingredients, here are recipies for all the
elixirs found in GURPS Magic and GURPS Magic Items.
        Some of the ingredients in these listings are purely the
product of the author's imagination, and GMs are encouraged to
modify these to fit their own game worlds.
        Resources for this article included Jean Auel's wonderful
Earth's Children novels, a book called Charms, Spells, and Potions
(for the modern-day enchanter), a catalog of mail-order herb and
wellness products, and bits and pieces gleaned from other games.
        Naturally, the actual attempt to mix up any of these elixirs
would be at best nauseating and at worst fatal, so don't even try!
        All costs given are for one dose only. Subtract one from the
final skill roll for each extra dose made in the same batch.
        To make a powder, add three days to creation time for drying
and grinding.
        To make a pastille, double materials and time to create. Add
3 dr. of alum and 2 dr. ground flour per dose. Must be formed into
pellets and dried or baked.
        To make an unguent, add two days to creation time and
either:
                6 oz. gum bezoin mixed with 1 dr. jellied spearmint
                or 6 oz. lard mixed with 1 dr. camphor oil
Minimum Necessary Equipment:
        Mortar and pestle
        Vials (glass, metal, or ceramic)
        Beakers and jars
        Distillation device
        Funnel (glass)
        Tongs (metal)
        Brazier (brass)
        Vat (copper)
        Balance and Weights
        Bellows 
        Crucible (for melting metals)
Elixir base (required for all potions)
        5 oz. distilled water
        1 dram lead shavings
        1 dram queen's moss
        1/2 carat powdered opal
Abbreviations:
        dr. = dram (unit of measurement)
        oz. = ounce (likewise)
        ct. = carat (for gems and precious stones)
Measurements:
        1 dr. = 1/8 fl. oz. = 1/2 tsp + 1/8 tsp.
        2 dr. = 1/4 fl. oz. = 1/2 tbsp.
        4 dr. = 1/2 fl. oz. = 1 tbsp.
        6 dr. = 4/3 fl. oz. = 1 tbsp + 1/2 tsp.
        8 dr. = 1 fl. oz. = 2 tbsp.
