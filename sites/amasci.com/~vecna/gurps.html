GURPS has been a very important part of our lives.  We have been playing Steve Jackson's role playing game since 1987.  Between the two of us, we have playtested five GURPS sourcebooks.  My wife and I met while playing GURPS.  We played GURPS on our honeymoon.  We named our daughter after GURPS characters (and she was probably conceived while we were playing GURPS).  We still play to the day, although we are down to playing only once a week.
