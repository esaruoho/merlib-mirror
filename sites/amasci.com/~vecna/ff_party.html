Becca's 'Fear Factor' Birthday Party

Apparently, we're not the only parents who let our kid stay up late on Monday nights to watch 'Fear Factor' on NBC. We had a houseful for Becca's party. The best stunt was this one, in which the kids drew randomly to see whether they'd get a plateful of cold pasta with oil and plastic bugs, or green Jello with plastic snakes and lizards. Whatever critters they could remove without using their hands, they were allowed to keep. Needless to say, it was a hit!
