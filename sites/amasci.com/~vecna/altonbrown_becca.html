Becca with Alton Brown

We were surprised and delighted to find that one of the guests at this year's Northwest Bookfest was Food Network television personality Alton Brown, star of "Good Eats." He was there to push his cookbook and Becca just had to say hi. And who's that guy peeking through in the background? That'll be Tim, because our booth was just on the other side of the curtains from Signing Row.
