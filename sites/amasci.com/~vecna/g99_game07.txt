Sample Character -- 

Ilona of Clan Vancouver 

 Ilona is a young female of a clan based
in British Columbia, northeast of the 
 Vancouver area. She is 48 years old and attractive, with
yellow-green skin and dark 
 brown hair. 
 Ilona is of average strength, but
is quick and clever and hardy. She has no mate and is 
 somewhat solitary, preferring the company of animals
and her own interests to 
 spending time with others. 
 When she was little more than a hatchling,
Ilona found several animals that had been 
 caught in illegal and inhumane traps. This caused her
to develop a strong dislike for 
 cruel hunting methods, a dislike which has extended to
include all forms of animal 
 cruelty. She gets along very well with animals; they
trust and like her in return. 
 Ilona's other main passion is growing
things. She can often be found in her little 
 garden, and has recently begun experimenting with bonsai
after making the 
 acquaintance of an elderly Japanese man who also taught
her a little of his language. 
 She is a regular visitor to area farms,
secretly helping tend the crops and livestock. 
 Her green thumb and herbal remedies are well known to
her clan. 
 She has a very strong "leap before
you look" attitude, especially when she perceives 
 some injustice to animals. This has given her quite a
few narrow escapes over the 
 year, ranging from nearly being shot by an irate farmer
she caught mistreating his 
 horses to getting caught up in an animal rights demonstration-turned-violent. 
 Because she is so willful and feels
so intensely about these issues, and worries too 
 much about things beyond her control (endangered species,
the state of zoo and lab 
 animals), Ilona suffers from frequent migraine headaches
that not even her medicines 
 can much help. 
 One of her favorite pastimes during
hunting season is to seek out and disable snares 
 and traps, even the legal ones. She also enjoys finding
camps of hunters and leading 
 them astray by mimicking animal sounds. More than once,
these hunters have ended 
 up lost, and on a couple of occasions they've ended up
hurt or dead; Ilona argues 
 often and bitterly with her clan leader that hunters
set themselves apart from those 
 that the clan should protect. 
 She tries not to lecture her meat-eating
clan members, seeking instead to lure them 
 around to vegetarianism by experimenting with cooking
tempting meatless dishes. 
 Ilona wears an eagle's claw on a cord
around her neck as a memory of her father, who 
 got it embedded in his arm when he was a foolish youngster
trying to raid a nest. He 
 mended his ways and gave it to her as a reminder to always
respect nature, a lesson 
 that shaped her life.
