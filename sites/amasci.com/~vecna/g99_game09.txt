Pre-Generated Characters -- 

 The following is a list of the pre-generated
characters that the GM will bring to the 
 game, ready to play. Because having some secrets from
your companions is nice, the 
 complete character sheets won't be shown here; just descriptions. 

 Or, if you don't mind anybody and everybody
being able to gawk at your 
 Disadvantages and Skills, the sample characters Ilona 
and Nix are also available. 

 If you absolutely have your heart set
on, will-fall-down-and-start-frothing-if-you- 
 can't-play one of these characters, e-mail
the GM and that character will be reserved 
 for you (provided, of course, that you're actually planning
to attend the Gathering and 
 play in the game). 

 If you'd prefer to play a character
of your own design, e-mail the GM 
with details. 

 And, needless to say, should any of
our gifted artistic friends be so inclined as to 
 sketch any of these folks, effusive gratitude would be
theirs ; )
