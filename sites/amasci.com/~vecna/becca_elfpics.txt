 Becca's Game Illustrations 
 These were drawn while she sat in on some of our RPG
sessions; the game in question features all elven PCs and is set in the
same world as the MageLore and ElfLore novels. 
 She calls these: 
 "The Bad Things That Happened to Tavelorn, Rae,
and Karandis."
