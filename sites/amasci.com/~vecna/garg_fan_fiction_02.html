Summaries:

Original Fanfic Series
 The Guardians -- 
A follow-up series to the original stories, mostly set in the years 2015 to 2030, to
revolve mainly around Amber Maza, Alexander Xanatos, Patricia and Sebastian
St. John, and Orpheus Bluestone.
Recommended pre-Guardians reading:
Ever After
Lyre, Lyre
Baby Makes Three
The Guardians: Alchemist
The Dissidents -- 
Several members of the Avalon Clan have decided to strike out on their own. Set 
in the early 00's, but not likely to involve the Manhattan Clan.
Recommended pre-Dissidents reading:
Mother's Day
Angela's Awakening
Broadway Goes to Avalon
Three of a Kind
What Might Have Been
Beth of Both Worlds
 66.
 Lylth --
Trapped in Hell in the wake of Devil's Night, Demona encounters an ancient 
power and learns of the origins of the gargoyle race.
67.
 Battle of the Sexpots --
A bit of crossover silliness first posted in the Adult Fanfic Archives; mature eyes only.

The Vignettes of
Kimberly Towle
68.
 Shameless Plug II: Heartaches
Set before the events in the "Damien" stories, young Alexander Xanatos gets carried 
away with a spell and brings unexpected visitors to Castle Wyvern. A crossover with 
Christine Morgan's MageLore books.
69.
 Bad Girls --
An explosive fanfic novella inspired by part of Greg Weisman's "master plan," but with 
a few differences ...
70.
 The Legend of Lizard Valley --
Every hundred years, Tom the Guardian left Avalon in search of Goliath and his 
clan. When his travels take him to the late 19th century, he finds himself caught 
up in a strange case of wagers, magical powers, and uncanny lookalikes.
71.
 Shameless Plug III: Labyrinth
An urchin from a medieval city and a prince from a modern one meet when a 
slumber party story spawns a wager ... a wager which pre-teen Alexander Xanatos
is determined to win. A crossover with Christine Morgan's MageLore books.
72.
 Unmentionables
Birdie takes Aiden shopping at Spank Me Mama in a story originally written for the 
Avalon Mists "Titania's Secret Catalog" writer's challenge. 
73.
 Clan of One
It's gargoyle vs. serial killers as Hippolyta tries to find a new life and purpose for herself 
in a small coastal town. Bit of a crossover with the upcoming Trinity Bay books. Adult 
content including language, violence and sex; reader beware.
74.
 Dens Leoninus
In this Gargoyles / Harry Potter crossover written for the Avalon Mists Challenge, Leo's 
got a toothache and Una takes him to Granger Family Dentistry on the very night the 
Hogwarts students are due home. Not a part of the usual series.
75.
 Coventry
The luscious Godiva is back, opening her own Las Vegas resort and ready to present 
her new clan to the world. Joining her are Coldstone, Coldfire, and two of their sons. 
Mature readers only, please, due to adult language, some sexual content, and violence.
