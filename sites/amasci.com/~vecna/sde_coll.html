Ordering Information

The information available on the above pages is rarely 100% up to date.  While it is updated frequently, there will always be some period of time between when orders are placed, and when the item is removed from the list.  Therefore, to avoid any confusion, please e-mail us at vecna@eskimo.com to confirm availablity.  Please include the item number in your request to be sure there are no misunderstandings.  We will confirm your order and pricing.  Payment must be made by check, money order, or credit cards.  Immediately upon recieving your money order, or upon your check clearing our bank, we will send out your order. 

Orders are normally sent by 1st ClassUS Mail, not insured, and all shipping and handling prices are given assuming this method of shipping within the US.  Other shipping arrangements can be easily made upon request, as well as foreign shipping.  Please allow a day or two for a price quote for any of these alternate modes of shipping.
