Our Honeymoon Photo Gallery


The first three days of our honeymoon were spent at Walt Disney World in Orlando, Florida.  Lotsa fun!  We went to Epcot Center, MGM Studios, and of course the Magic Kingdom.  And yes, we rode all the rides, including the new Buzz Lightyear ride for seven-year-olds!  :)

The remaining 11 days we stayed on the island of Nassau, in the Bahamas, at an all-inclusive resort called Sandals.  Specifically, the Sandals Royal Bahamian Resort and Spa.  It was hard on us, What with the warm, white sand, the crystal blue water, sapphire blue sky, tropical breezes; being waited on hand and foot, being forced to eat gourmet food, drinks at the swim-up pools bars; swimming, snorkeling, kayaking, wind-surfing, scuba diving; dancing, music, casinos, and comedy acts; breakfast in bed, roman bath tubs, four-poster canopy bed, ocean view from our room; facials, mud wrap and mud bath, manicures, pedicures, hair in corn rows (Shaindell, not Kevin); work-out room, beach volleyball; champagne upon our arrival, and a fully stocked wet bar in our room; and almost all of this included...Well, it was just awful.
