Shipping Information

We ship USPS priority mail for single items and small Retail orders, including Drop Ships. On heavier and larger items, and large quantity orders, We use UPS.

Retail Shipping.

 

On line orders shipping is automatically calculated in at 3.95 minimum, with rates figured at 20% of order total. Delivery to one address per order. For multiple drop ship orders, please make individual orders or contact us via email, for specific calculations on shipping. (multi order discounts, don't worry it won't cost more, you'll save!)

Please allow at least 3-4 weeks, for fulfillment and delivery. No exceptions, no rush orders. Every customer is a priority, and every order just as important to us!

Wholesale Shipping.

Please allow at least 4-6 weeks, for fulfillment and delivery.
