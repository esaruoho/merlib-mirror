Company Policy.

Crafters are a form of artists in their own right. Hand crafted pieces are not meant to be exact duplications, such as a factory could produce. Each individual piece holds something original and all it's own. We strive for originality with each piece, taking great pride in our workmanship, carefully crafting to every detail. Nothing leaves our shoppe until it has been inspected and we are satisfied, it is ready to be presented. Many of our products are created here in our shoppe. Whether it is a hand made piece or from one of our vendors. Please note when your piece arrives it may or may not look exactly like the image representation in our brochures and at our on line shoppe. Should you ever receive anything less than expected simply return it for a full refund!

Guarantee.

We stand behind our products 100% Whether they are made here in our shoppe or imported from our vendors. If you are unsatisfied simply return the product for a refund. If for any reason we are unable to meet your specifications or if a product is no longer available you will be contacted and promptly refunded!

Products.

Many of our products are created here in our own workshop. These items are customized to a specific color size or fragrance. We do not wholesale our unique product line. We carry a complete selection of accent gifts, imported from around the world, many of these items are not available with customized selections, however we do offer on a wholesale opportunity.

Order Creation and Processing.

It takes approximately 3-4 weeks to create a product, package and ship an order. Some of our accent products are not carried in available inventory and must be ordered. This may cause a slight delay. Availability is checked at the time of the order. You will be notified if an item is out of stock, and promptly refunded.
