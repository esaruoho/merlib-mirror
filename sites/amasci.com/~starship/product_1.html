About our Dreamcatchers

Authentic Native craftsmanship. (Tex'ta) Tim uses only natural colours to enhance tradition. With carefully selected beads, gem stones and feathers; Each piece is an original work of art. No two are exactly alike   As the web is woven; The weavers positive energy is transferred to the web; Enabling the powers of the dream catcher to work today, just as they did in the Legends passed down.

    To fully understand and appreciate the dreamcatcher a framable copy of the Legend comes with each piece.   **Deluxe include bone or turtle shells.
