 Welcome to the story 
 of how our Dreams turned into a Reality...... 

 Welcome, 

Just for You Hand crafted Gifts, is a family owned and operated business, that 
began in Kent, Washington. Many years ago, we started out crafting with our 5 
children, as a way of Holiday Gift giving, within a budget. These gifts were so 
adored they became requests the following years. We expanded 
 by attending Craft Bazaars, Fairs and other events. 

 Tim & 

 Mindy 

 Tim (Tex'ta) 
is of the Wolf Clan, Squamish Nation, in Vancouver B.C. While other members of 
the family are known for their carvings, Tim is known for his dream catchers. 
Being Irish and Native, heritage and traditions are very important to us. 
Mindys independent nature have provided over 20 
experience in self employment opportunities, Crafting with the children a favorite 
 pass time for all. Many relatives have watched the children 
 grow through the variations of their art work. Continually 
 improving talents. 

 Thus 

 beginning a dream...... 

 Eventually, we acquired our own Business license and began to market a 
product we personally love - Native & Traditional Hand crafted Gifts. With 
wonderful results at Bazaars and local crafters events. We bought a computer and 
started "on line". We signed up with Eskimo North , as our internet host; Then, 
began the designing of www.JustforyouHCgifts.com our on line shoppe. We have 
taken great care in selecting our merchant provider and shopping cart program, 
to offer security and the greatest shopping experience 
possible. Along the way we've received a few awards which 
 are displayed to the left and on various pages. 

 To Grow 

 With so much positive response, we then expanded ourselves again. We were 
introduced to a wonderful importer, where we were given a direct opportunity to 
many more wonderful gifts. Now we offer not only, our own unique selection of 
hand crafted gifts, But a large selection of finely crafted gifts from all 
around the world. Our product line has grown bigger than we ever 
imagined. With so much interest in native arts, we've begun to collect on 
 consignment 
 the works of various natives through out the United 
 States and Canada. 

 2002 

 We've bought a new web program and rebuilt 
 our site. We moved out of the mall and opened our own 
 shoppe. A ll 
 new in 2002 Just for You. 

 We now manage 
our shoppe, both physical and virtual environments. Our products are available 
wholesale and retail. We personally manage our web site, and are there in person 
 at our shoppe - on the weekends. Through the acceptance and review of Family and Friends; we bring these gifts to 
you. Our success is yours with much thanks. 
 To Our customers, the greatest people ever to know.
