 Company Policy . 

 Crafters are a form of artists 
 in their own right. Hand crafted pieces are not 
 meant to be exact duplications, such as a factory 
 could produce. Each individual piece holds something 
 original and all it's own. We strive for originality 
 with each piece, taking great pride in our workmanship, 
 carefully crafting to every detail. Nothing leaves 
 our shoppe until it has been inspected and we are 
 satisfied, it is ready to be presented. Many of 
 our products are created here in our shoppe. Whether 
 it is a hand made piece or from one of our vendors. 
 Please note when your piece arrives it may or may 
 not look exactly like the image representation in 
 our brochures and at our on line shoppe. Should 
 you ever receive anything less than expected simply 
 return it for a full refund! 

 Payments . 

 Payment is due at the time of 
 the order. We accept checks, MO, Visa, Mastercard, 
 Discover. You may order on line, by E mail, Mail, 
 or Telephone. Which ever is more convenient for 
 you. Washington state residents will be charged 
 8.8% sales tax. 

 Delivery and Shipping . 

 We use USPS priority mail, and 
 UPS. Fees of 20% are calculated into your order, on 
 line automatically. Wholesale customers please view 
 our wholesale information. Delivery time is set at approximately 
 3-4 weeks. 

 Guarantee . 

 We stand behind our products 
 100% Whether they are made here in our shoppe or 
 imported from our vendors. If you are unsatisfied 
 simply return the product for a refund. If for any 
 reason we are unable to meet your specifications 
 or if a product is no longer available you will 
 be contacted and promptly refunded!
