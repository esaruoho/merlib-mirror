 Thank you for your interest in our product line

Visit our Catalog request department, and enter your wholesale information and be sure to check the box for wholesale. You will receive a full line catalog of products available plus a special pricing list.  This catalog excludes the unique customizable gift items, that we offer exclusively through our web site and in our shoppe.  International catalog requests require a 10.00 deposit, which will be credited towards your first order.

  We have a minimum amount of $100.00.

Orders.

 Many of our customers prefer to order via Email, simply by listing the quantity - product number - cost. Calculating their own total. Including a shipping address where products are to be sent. We then confirm availability, calculate total including shipping and confirm the order via email with an invoice number. Then simply proceed to our payment center, enter your invoice number and amount to be paid and proceed to process your payment.

Payment.

 When you receive your confirmation and total due. You will be able to proceed to our Payment Center, to make your payment. All payments are due at the time of order. You will only receive two notices for payment, then the order will be cancelled without further notice. It takes approximately 3 days for funds to transfer (depending on weekend and holidays at the time of the order)

Delivery.

Please allow at least 4-6 weeks, for fulfillment and delivery. No exceptions, no rush orders. Every customer is a priority, and every order just as important to us!. Delivery may be delayed if payment is delayed. We have no control over the postal regulations and delivery. Since Sept 11, there have been some sudden unexpected delays. You will be sent a delivery confirmation and tracking number once your order is placed into the mail service. You will be notified immediately of any delays known to us.
