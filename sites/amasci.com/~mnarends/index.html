    Online Pioneers

    A monthly online newsletter tailored to genealogy enthusiasts, and keeps subscribers current on new websites, Internet shareware programs and data files, genealogical newsgroups, mailing lists, and developments in cyber genealogy projects. Online Pioneers was formed from the merge of two newsletters: Online Researchers (1995-1996) and Pioneers (1994-1996).

    Picked # 53 on Best Genealogy Links on the WWW!

    "If you can't keep up with the escalating world of computers and genealogy, Online Pioneers newsletter is for you. A newsletter geared to new and experienced users alike, filled with informative, timely articles, Online Pioneers is a one-of-a-kind publication covering both the online and offline computer genealogy community." - Pam Middleton-Lee

    About Marthe Arends

    Marthe Arends has been involved in genealogy for 18 years and is the editor of the computer genealogy newsletter Online Pioneers. She has lectured on computers and genealogy to many groups, has been the SysOp of a Fidonet genealogy BBS, has written articles for a variety of genealogy publications, and has taught several online genealogy classes, including two lessons for Genealogy.com's Online University.
