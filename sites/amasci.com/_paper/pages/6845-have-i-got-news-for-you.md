# *Have I Got News For You* (1999)

<img src="have4.jpg" data-align="LEFT" width="223" height="200" alt="Irene and Jeff" /><img src="have3.jpg" data-align="RIGHT" width="206" height="200" alt="Allen and Jason" /><img src="have5.jpg" data-border="0" width="183" height="200" alt="Ryan presenting" />

Based on the long-running BBC series (and an American version that's coming hosted by Norm MacDonald), for the past two years at [Anglicon](http://www.webwitch.com/anglicon/) I have presented a live version of the current event news quiz *[Have I Got News For You](http://www.haveigotnewsforyou.com/cgi-bin/home.pl)*. Four panelist have to guess the week's news stories from various video clips, headlines, and an odd-man out round. The idea is more to have fun at the expense of the newsworthy, rather than always getting the correct answer.

We've been lucky each year to get one of the Anglicon guests to join us on the panel (along with regulars Jeff Stout - who runs [The Society Of The Rusting TARDIS](http://www.eskimo.com/~onan/tardis/), and Allen Adams, a "second-tier" British media guest). In 1998, John Levine ("Sgt Benton" from *Dr Who*) was our celebrity guest, in 1999 we had [Jason Carter](http://www.jasoncarterfanclub.com/) from *Babylon 5* on the panel.

The show was recorded using four digital video mini-DV camcorders, with switching equipment provided by Alan Halfhill, and everything edited together using Adobe Premiere on my computer afterwards.
