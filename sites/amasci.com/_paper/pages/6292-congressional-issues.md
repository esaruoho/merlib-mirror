Committee and member staffs spend much of their time obtaining, organizing and distributing information that affects the American public.****

**Informational hearings and reports are vital components of congressional responsibility to the American people.**

**The Center tracks significant congressional information that affects free enterprise and posts it here on our website for public education.**
