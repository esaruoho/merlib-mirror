# My actual home

*Source page: `~ables/home/index.html`*

My actual home

<img src="../img/wtcribbon.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" /> Yes, it's a dirty job, but somebody has to live here. I've finally landed in a place I've always dreamed of:

<img src="img/home2.jpg" data-border="0" />

I live in a log home in the foothills of the Rocky Mountains. At the top of the hill behind the house, the Roosevelt National Forest begins and drops about a thousand feet into the Big Thompson River Canyon. It's very peaceful. Quite a few people live in the area, but my nearest neighbor is about a quarter of a mile away.
