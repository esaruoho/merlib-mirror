Blueberries

 

  In the summer of 2000, five blueberry bushes produced twenty-nine gallons of berries, but a year round supply of this tasty treat is not the only benefit of this beautiful cousin of the rhododendron.

 The fall color of blueberry bushes is quite spectacular, and contrasts here with a honey locust tree.
