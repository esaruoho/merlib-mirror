# The Letter~J

<span id="jacobite_script">Jacobite Script</span>  
Ordinal writing system used by the Syrian Monophysites, a Christian sect.

<span id="jawi">Jawi</span>  
A writing script based on the Arabian alphabet, used for writing Malay. It was developed in the fourteenth century, common era.

<span id="jazm">Jazm</span>  
Script that developed into Classical Arabic.

<span id="jealousy">Jealousy</span>  
1.  This is:
2.  Fear of rivalry.
3.  The feeling of being unloved.

<span id="jodi">JoDi</span>  
Tool used for measuring angles in writing that was distributed between 1975 and 1980 by JoDi Incorporated.

<span id="juncus_maritimus">Juncus Maritimus</span>  
Rush plant whose reed was used for making brush-pens in Ancient Egypt. See also [Phragmites Communis](glo_p.html#phragmites_communis).
