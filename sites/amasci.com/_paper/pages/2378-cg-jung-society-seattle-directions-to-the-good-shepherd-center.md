# C.G. Jung Society, Seattle: Directions to the Good Shepherd Center

*Source page: `~dcs/directns.html`*

### Directions to the Good Shepherd Center

4649 Sunnyside Avenue North, Seattle

Latitude: 47.6638\
Longitude: -122.32961

[Online Map](http://city.net/maps/addrresults/?srcaddress=4649+Sunnyside+Ave.+North&srccity=Seattle&srcstate=WA&srclabel=Good+Shepherd+Center)

**From Interstate 5:** Take the 45th Street exit. Travel west toward Wallingford. Turn right from 45th onto Sunnyside Avenue North and until you reach the Good Shepherd Center on the left, just before Sunnyside intersects with 50th Street. Follow entry drive left to the parking lot south of the building. Enter the building at the main entrance. (You can also reach the Center by taking the 50th Street exit from I-5.)

**From Aurora Avenue (Hwy 99):** Exit to 46th Street and follow it east as it becomes 45th Street. Turn left at Sunnyside Avenue North and go a block and a half, then turn left into the Good Shepherd Center just before Sunnyside intersects with 50th Street. Follow entry drive left to the parking lot south of the building. Enter the building at the main entrance.
