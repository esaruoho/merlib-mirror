# Gifted Resources Enrichment Programs

Here are some possible sources for those interested in pursuing independent enrichment in particular subject areas. There is also enrichment information available though the [Education section](zedu.html) of this web page.

- [Saturday Academy](http://www.ogi.edu/~mfunk/satacad.html) is a non-profit organization that provides hands-on educational experiences to kids all over the state of Oregon.

- [a Math-Science collection.](http://www.eskimo.com/~billb/)

- [GNA - Globewide Network Academy.](http://uu-gna.mit.edu:8001/uu-gna/.html)

- [Science Resource Guides from U Mich](ftp://una.hh.lib.umich.edu)

- [NSF Pubs](gopher://stis.nsf.gov)

- [Cornell Scientists Workbench](ftp://info.tc.cornell.edu/pub/swb/)

- [Science Olympics](http://www.brandonu.ca/Departments/Education/SO/Welcome.html)

- [MegaMath](http://www.c3.lanl.gov/mega-math/welcome.html)

- A large collection of kid's special interest links from [Uncle Bob's Kids Page](http://gagme.wwa.com/~boba/kids.html)

- [NASA main](http://www.jsc.nasa.gov/nasa/NASAInternet.html)

- [LOGO Computer Language](ftp://cher.media.mit.edu/pub/logo/)

- [LOGO Enthusiast](http://www.achilles.net/~dsleeth/mw/mainpb.html)

- [Clearinghouse for Subject-Oriented Internet Resource Guides](http://www.lib.umich.edu/chhome.html)

- [Gifted Enrichment Center, Tallahassee FL](http://buerkle.arc.leon.k12.fl.us)

- [Astronomy Classroom Project](http://chandra.astro.indiana.edu/html_astro_cr_project)

- [Map info such as USGS](http://www.eskimo.com/~alpinist/)

- Family Math is a newsletter. To subscribe send email to LISTSERV@uic.edu with "sub fam-math yourname" in the body (not the subject) of the email.

- [Holography](http://www.enter.net/~holostudio) everything you wanted to know and holography classes as well.

- [US Coast Guard Basic Instructor School](http://wco.com/~tcpettpf/tp.htm) web pages and assorted links.

- National Women's History Project can be reached by phone at: 707-838-6000, or by postal mail to:

           National Women's History Project
           7738 Bell Road
           Windsor, CA 95492-8518

- MathMagic, a math enrichment program, can be reached by email. Send a message with "send mathmagic_info" in the body (not the subject) of the email message to: l-server@forum.swarthmore.edu

- [Discovery Channel](http://ericir.syr.edu/Discovery/) collaborative learning tools. Also availble through [gopher service.](gopher://ericir.syr.edu:70/11/Ed/Discovery)
