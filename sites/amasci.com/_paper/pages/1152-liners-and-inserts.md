<strong>Liners and Inserts<br />
THE BEST COTTON DIAPER ACCESSORY</strong></p>
<p><strong>Many parents who use cotton diapers during the day find that they need to use paper diapers at night because the urine soaks the diaper and pant, wetting the baby's clothing and bed. The answer is our wonderful insert, which doubles the absorbency of the diaper and prevents leaking during nap and nighttime. Many parents start to use inserts for nap and nighttime when their babies are about four months old.</strong></p>
<p>Here's an idea from one parent: <em>"I used brushed polyester liners at night and sometimes throughout the day. When I noticed that my son reached the stage where he had fairly consistent bowel movements each morning after breakfast, I would use a Flush-a-bye liner in the first diaper of the day and goodbye mess! Flush-a-byes are great to use when you go out because you don't need to try and rinse a dirty diaper in someone else's bathroom. However you use them, liners make diapering a breeze!"</em></p>
