# Irene's Secret Birthday Page!

 

OK, here's the deal. Last October marked Irene's 50th birthday. She'd said for a LONG time that she'd like to have a food fight for the big 5-0. People were invited, holes cut in garbage bags to serve as make-shift protective clothing, and when the day arrived it was a rare, perfect sunny day in Bothell. (Which, when you consider the alternative of trying to take a massive food fight indoors, is more than a minor piece of good luck!)
