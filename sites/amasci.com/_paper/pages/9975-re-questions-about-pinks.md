    No, I am a Glaciologist in the Geophysics department.  I'm off to 
    for another Antarctic field season on 1 Nov.  On my return trip I will 
    fish the south island of NZ for the month of Feb (the fourth year in a row)!

    My trout experience is limited to my months in NZ.  I've never fished the
    Yakima.  Around here I stick to the steelhead and salmon.  In the past
    2 years I've spent most of my ffing time on the Skykomish.  Before that,
    most of my efforts were dedicated to the N.Fork Stilly.  The fall 1995 Mud 
    slide above Mermaid (below Hazel) and the 1997 slide up Boulder creek has 
    ruined my favorite spots for the past 2 years.  Fishing on that river has been 
    mostly confined to the miles just below Fortson - water well above where
    I prefer to fish.  

    I have fished on occasion in many other fresh water spots as well.  I've 
    spent a bit of time on the Skagit, Sauk, Snoqualmie, Green, Satsop, 
    Chehalis, Cowlitz, Sol Duc, and Hoh.  I'm sure there are many others
    on the list who can lend better information on any of these than I.

    My limited saltwater experience includes a couple trips to Sekiu and 
    lots of fishing in Sound locations.  I hooked a nice silver on a tube fly
    off the south end of Jefferson Head last Saturday.

    cheers,
