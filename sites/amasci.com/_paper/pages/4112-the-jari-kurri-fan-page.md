The Jari Kurri Fan Page

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Man\

Was born 18th May 1960, Helsinki, [Finland](http://www.vn.fi/vn/um/finfo/findeng.html)

He stands 6'1" (185 cm) tall and weighs 195 lbs (89 kg)

Jari was Edmonton's Third Choice,\
   69th Overall in 1980 Entry Draft

He wears jersey \#17, plays C/RW and shoots right

News\
Jari Kurri had the rare oportunity to get a ride in McLaren Mercedes' F1 twin-seater. McLaren's test driver [Oliver Panis and Jari](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/JariPanis.jpg) burned some rubber on the track in Brno, Czech Republic. [Jari looked excited](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/jariF1-4.jpg) before the two [headed out on the track](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/jariInF1.jpg).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Jari was one of the hockey legends taking part in the Toronto 2000 All-Star festivities. Check out a [picture of Jari in Toronto.](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/JARI2000.jpg) Thanks goes to Robin for sharing it with all Kurri fans.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Jari visited the Kings and Ducks' stadiums in January 2000. This was the first time Jari had been in California since he retired from the NHL. When asked if he misses playing hockey, he said that he doesn't miss being on the ice but he does miss the locker room chats with the players. Jari's plans for the future include being involved with hockey in some form but he is not actively pursuing anything right now. "I have received many kinds of hockey related job offers from around the world, but I'm in no hurry to jump into something. After 20 years of playing in the NHL, it is nice to have some free time and just be an [ordinary hockey fan eating a hot dog](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/HODARI.jpg)." Kurri fans have a chance to see Jari again on TV in February during the All Stars game in Montreal. Stay tuned!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Kurri was among the 2000 guests invited to celebrate Finland's 81st year of independence on December 6th at the [President's Palace.](http://www.tpk.fi/eng/index.html) Here is a picture of [Jari in the Palace.](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/LINNA.jpg)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Jari Kurri received a golden Koho stick on November 9th in recognition of his 601 NHL goals. The Koho stick was given to him in front of a standing ovation before the Finland vs. Sweden game in the [Karjala tournament](http://www.finhockey.fi/karjala98/). Jari said the stick will most likely end up on display in his Highlight Cafe restaurant. Players who have received a golden hockey stick before are Wayne Gretzky, Mike Bossy, Michel Goulet and Joe Mullen, who played with Titan sticks, and Mario Lemieux and Dave Andreychuk, who played with Koho sticks. Here is [Jari with the Golden Koho stick.](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/JKKOHO.jpg)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Jari Kurri is one of the men behind a new sports restaurant in downtown Helsinki. Opened on June 15th, the [Highlight Cafe](http://www.highlightcafe.fi/) offers tasty food, sports feed through 50 TV monitors, and sports memorabilia on display by many Finnish superstars like Jari Kurri (NHL), [Mika Hakkinen](http://www.mclaren.co.uk/mclaren/mclfacts/mikabio.htm) (Formula 1), [Juha Kankkunen](http://www.ford.com/motorsport/3-8jkankkunen.html) (Rally) and [Jari Litmanen](http://www.geocities.com/Colosseum/4867/) (Soccer). Jari and some other Finnish NHL stars celebrated the opening with a party. Here is a picture of [Jari with Teemu and his wife Sirpa](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/TeemuSirpaJari.jpg) having fun.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

May 02 1998 marked Jari's last appearance in the NHL. He finished his magnificient career on the very same ice where he started it in 1980, the Edmonton Coliseum. We wish you all the best in the future! Thank you, Jari, for all the great moments!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Although Jari will leave the NHL after this season, he will keep on playing hockey. A Finnish charity hockey team called the Pietarinkadun Oilers made a 2+2 year deal with Jari. The team collects money for childrens' hospital. Here is a [picture of Jari](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/OilersJari.jpg) wearing his future team jersey.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Finland's Hockey Team wins the Bronze Medal in the Olympic Games in Nagano! Jari played an excellent tournament. He scored one goal and got four assists. Jari said he ranks this Olympic Bronze Medal equal to a Stanley Cup Win.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hockey News included Jari in the list of the [Top 50 NHL Players of All-Time](http://ESPNET.SportsZone.com/nhl/news/980109/00527652.html)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

On January 8th Jari Kurri was added as special selection by NHL Commissioner Gary Bettman to the [World Team Roster](http://ESPNET.SportsZone.com/nhl/features/00521859.html) for the first [NHL All-Star International Showdown.](http://www.nhlpa.com/nhlpa/all_stars/) This will be Jari's eighth All-Star game appearance. Jari scored a goal in the game, his second All-Star career goal. Congrats Jari!

Jari Kurri said in his biography [*Jari Kurri, 17*](http://www.eskimo.com/~juha/KurriBio.html) published in Finland, that he plans to retire after this season. Read the story in the [Rocky Mountain News.](http://InsideDenver.com/avalanche/1210aval2.html)
