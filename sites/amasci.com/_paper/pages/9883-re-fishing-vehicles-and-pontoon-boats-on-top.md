<span class="850563320-04122001">I have hauled my pontoon boat on my roof a number of times. I always take off the oars, gear bags and stripping apron. I face it so that the seat is facing backwards. That way it can't come unsnapped and fly up. I use ratchet straps, usually 3 of  them. I make sure the strap goes around both the frame and the pontoon, and cinch them pretty tight. With this setup I took a trip to Montana this summer averaging 70-80 miles an hour with no problems. I could reach out of the window while driving and feel my pontoon boat, and it wasn't moving at all. One thing I always do on trips over passes is let some air out of my pontoons to compensate for the altitude change. Hope this helps.</span>
