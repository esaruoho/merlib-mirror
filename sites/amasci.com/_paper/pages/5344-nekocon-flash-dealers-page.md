<td><strong><u>NekoCon FLASH! Dealer Information</u></strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>No matter what reason people come to a convention, eventually everyone heads for the Dealers Room.  That's the one place where many people take home more of the convention than any place else.
<p>With our return to the Holiday Inn Executive Center the dealers room will not be the cavernous expanse that it was for NekoCon 3. Sometimes, smaller is better. The tables will be slightly smaller and there will be less tables to offer than last year.<br />
</p>
<hr />
<br />
<strong><u>Dealer Table Rates</u></strong><br />
<strong>Single Tables:</strong> 1~27, 30, 36, 40, 45<br />
$200.00 per single table There is a limit of Five(5) tables which can be purchased by one dealer.  (The first Table includes TWO convention memberships and each table purchased afterwards will include ONE membership for each table.
<p><strong>Corner Tables:</strong> 28/29, 31/32, 33/34, 36/37, 38/39, 41/42, 43/44, 46/47<br />
$400.00 per set  Three memberships accompany each corner set sold.</p>
<p><strong>Space Dimensions:</strong> Each space is 6 feet wide by 8 feet deep, with 6 foot by 30 inch table.  Tables are covered by a clothe and additional tables clothes can be provided to cover merchandise at night.</p>
<p><strong>Power</strong>: The walls of the ballroom are lined with power outlets and there are several floor outlets.  The Holiday Inn Executive Center does not charge a fee for the use of their power outlets, but we heavily encourage people to bring their own power cords and power strips.</p>
<p><strong>Phone:</strong> We can not offer phone service at this time. The room is not set up for multiple phone lines.</p>
<hr />
<p><strong>The NekoCon Dealers Reg Form:<a href="pdf/dealer_form.pdf">Download Now!<br />
</a></strong> <strong>NOTE:</strong>To veiw this file you will need Acrobat 3.0 or higher or a compatible PDF plugin for your web browser.</p>
<hr /></td>
</tr>

<td><strong><u>Current Room Layout</u></strong><br />
As tables are sold we will update this as soon as possible.  But, we do suggest that you do contact us before you send in your dealers registration.  Email us at <a href="info@nekocon.org">info@nekocon.org</a> to make sure the tables you want are still available.<br />

<td><strong><u>Dealer Room Policies</u></strong><br />
Not everyone may agree with our policies. Please remember, NekoCon is a private show and we have every right to enforce the stated policies to what ever extent we may  have too. If you do feel that you can not do business at our con by following<br />
these rules, than you shouldn't do business at NekoCon. Please refer questions<br />
to the dealers room coordinator or the convention chairman.
The regulations we enforce are for the protection of the anime companies, the fans in attendance, and our exhibitors. We have worked long and hard to make an event that<br />
reflects the respect we have for an industry that has brought us decades of enjoyment and we will show our support to the fullest

<td data-valign="TOP">Neko-Con does not condone the sales of any merchandise that violates United States Copyright laws, nor the rights of the Byrne International Copyright Treaty.  Items such as noncommercial video tapes, LD¹s, CD¹s, models, posters, T-shirts, trading cards and toys are protected under both the Byrne International Copyright Treaty, and by paragraph code 17, sections 605 and 606 of U.S International Trade Laws.
<p>Authentic merchandise will have the proper Japanese taxation labels applied in an obvious location on the outer packaging of the item.  Most Japanese products carry these government labels anime or not, regardless of the type of merchandise. A few of the rare exceptions are garage kits which may only have photocopied covers on the boxes but still have the proper  copyright information and more often than not still have the official taxation labels. Books and the vast amounts of Doujinshi that circulate the convention circuit are also another exception.</p>
<p>Copyrights should be easily noticeable.  Any product or piece of merchandise that does not carry the proper copyright information, or not all will be consider illegitimate as well. Any such merchandise that can be considered illegitimate in the best judgment by any of the senior staff of the convention, and/or has been brought to our attention by any industry representatives, it will be asked to be removed from your table(s).  Any dealer that persists in the sales of such merchandise after be told to remove it will be removed from the exhibitors hall, and ejected from the convention with out a refund.  Consider this notice your  only warning.</p>
<p>Due to the controversy over the legitimacy of certain items, the products from the following companies are fully banned from the dealers room. These companies are,  Son May Records, aka SM Records, Ever Anime International CD¹s, Ho San CD's, Hook-Ups T-shirts, and the Korean based Academy Models line of Gundam and other amine related kits.</p>
<p>At anytime you may be asked to produce a Bill of Sale, or Import Papers <br />
showing the items came from japan and are for business and not personal use. <br />
These items must have a copyright notice on it somewhere. This includes but is not limited to: </p>
<p>(a) a Japanese tax sticker.<br />
(b) a date of manufacture.<br />
(c) the place of manufacture (i.e. 1997, Made in Japan, etc.)<br />
(d) information as to who the owning studio is (i.e. Bandai, Sony, Pioneer, etc.) <br />
(e) not be a known bootleg item.</p></td>
<td data-valign="TOP">The quality of the items sold will also be under scrutiny.  It is fairly easy to tell the difference between an item that was made by the original manufacturer, and one that was made courtesy of your local Kinko¹s Copy Centers color copier.   These items are usually  8.5 x 11 or 11 x 17 color copies of original art work  from art books which are made into T-shirts and mousepads.
<p>There will be no blatant displaying of adult material.  Art Books, Manga and Doujinshi in comic bags and/or in boxes is acceptable.  Even having a sign which says you have adult material is fine.  The public showing adult animated or live films in any part of the public convention area other than the designated video rooms is not allowed.  Virginia State Law also does not allow the public posting of said suggestive material such as flyers and posters in public areas such as the hotel hallways and lobby.  Animecons are become more and more a family event. When selling merchandise, please show discretion as to whom you are selling it too. </p>
<p>In any event Neko-Con,  The Holiday Inn Executive or any of its staff will not be held liable for lose of equipment, merchandise, revenues or any other  direct or in direct damages how ever caused.  The convention will try to provide the best security possible, but exhibitors are responsible for their property, personal vehicles and displays.</p>
<p>In the event that you cannot attend, Neko-Con must receive a written notice postmarked 30 days prior to the event.  Any notice before this date will  entitle you to a full refund of your fees.  We cannot guarantee such a request after the specified deadline.  Resale of any tables will be the responsibility of Neko-Con, and not that of the vendor.</p>
<p>Any vendor tables not claimed by  NOON  on the second day of the event will be considered forfeit and will be reclaimed by the event to use as they see fit.  To avoid this from happening, in the event that you will be delayed from your arrival to the convention, but are still planing to attend. Contact us as soon as possible.</p>
<p>NO ONE may sell merchandise from their hotel rooms.  This would be a violation of  the hotels policies.  Anyone found doing so will be referred to the hotel security.  That and any other illegal activities will be referred to the proper authorities.</p>
<p>Anyone removed from the convention for any reason will not be issued a refund.  Neko-Con also does reserve the right to refuse the  sale of vendor tables  to anyone for any reason</p></td>
