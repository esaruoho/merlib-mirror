- [Worldwide Virtual Tourist/City Net](http://www.city.net/): visit the world from your armchair: over 3000 cities and all sorts of links: culture, lodging, history, etc.

I recently spent a month travelling through **EASTERN EUROPE** (or "Central Europe" as it is sometimes called); you can [read about my trip](eurtrip.html) here...(the [Gay and Jewish side of my trip](eurjew.html) is here)... These are some of the places I visited:

- [Central Europe Online](http://www.centraleurope.com/): news and travel information about the Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia and other nations in the area; lots of interesting resources and articles
- [Berlin Gay information](http://www.informatik.hu/berlin.de/~holz/privat/)
- [Schwules Museum](http://www.kultur-netz.de/berlin/SchwulesMuseum), the gay museum in Berlin
- <img src="graphics/hradcany.jpg" data-align="right" />Lots of information about [PRAGUE](http://www.andel3w.dk/prague/english/prag.htm), one of the world's most beautiful cities
- ![NEW\>\>\> ](graphics/new8.gif)[Prague for Visitors](http://goeasteurope.about.com/travel/goeasteurope/msubmenu5.htm)
- ![NEW\>\>\> ](graphics/new8.gif)[Sightseeing in Prague](http://goeasteurope.about.com/msubczpr7.htm)
- [Upcoming Cultural Events in Czech Rep.](http://www.muselik.com/czech/events.html)
- [Prague Local Guides](http://dir.yahoo.com/Regional/Countries/Czech_Republic/Provinces_and_Regions/Praha/Cities/Prague/Travel_and_Transportation/Local_Guides/), including [Joseph's Town or The former Jewish Quarter](http://www.judaica.org/praguetour/praguetour.html), aka Josefov, which is home to the [Jewish Museum](http://www.jewishmuseum.cz/)
- [Cybeteria](http://www.cybeteria.cz): a cool cybercafe located at Stepanska 18, Praha 1
- [Prague Trams](http://www.centraleurope.com/ceo/travel/features/ss962001.html)
- [Terezin](http://www.centraleurope.com/ceo/travel/features/ss971001.html), a town outside Prague used as a Nazi concentration "show-camp" to dupe the Red Cross; a disproportionate number of its prisoners were elderly and children, the latter of which produced some famous artwork. Most perished at Auschwitz.
- A bit to the south lies Cesky Budejovice, home of [Budvar](http://www.budweiser.cz), a.k.a. Budweiser: "Budweiser Budvar, the "Original Budweiser" beer, is known for its unique quality, tradition and widely recognized rich, full-bodied taste. The brewing craftsmanship of Budweiser Budvar N. C., originates from a more than 700-year-old brewing tradition in Ceski Budejovice - or Budweis, as it is known in German. Budweiser Budvar guarantees the production of a top lager known through-out the world as a symbol of exceptional quality."
- [Travel, Tourism in Hungary](http://www.hungary.com/hudir/Travel/)
- [A Little Tour in Budapest](http://www.fsz.bme.hu/hungary/budapest/bptour/bptour.htm): thorough, and good pictures
- [Five walks in Budapest](http://www.fph.hu/bphome/cityhall/general/walks/5walks.html)
- [A huge collection of Budapest related links](http://www.fsz.bme.hu/hungary/budapest/bplinks/bp4.html)
- [Budapest Cafes](http://www.centraleurope.com/ceo/travel/features/ss972501.html)
- [Budapest Underpasses](http://www.centraleurope.com/ceo/travel/features/ss963101.html): these are underground passageways which allow people to cross busy streets and reach [trams](http://www.centraleurope.com/ceo/travel/features/ss961901.html) which run in the middle of the street; they house stores and street people
- [An Egghead's Guide to Budapest](http://www.idg.hu/internetto//stb/bpkalauz/doc2.html)
- The Hungarian gay publication: [Masok](http://www.datanet.hu/masok) <img src="graphics/dohanyin.jpg" data-align="left" data-hspace="20" />
- Europe's largest syngagogue: [The Budapest Main Synagogue](http://www.synagogue.hu/), aka Dohany Synagogue (shown at left), where I spent Rosh Hashana 1997
