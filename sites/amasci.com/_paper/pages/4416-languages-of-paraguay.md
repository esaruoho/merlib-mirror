# Languages of Paraguay

*Source page: `~krautm/language.html`*

### <span id="language">Language</span>

Paraguay has two official languages: Spanish and Guaran. 75% of all Paraguayans speak Spanish, it is estimated. About 90% speak Guaran. The concentration of those who speak Guaran only is in the countryside, while those Paraguayans who speak only Spanish live primarily in the capitol city of Asuncin.

If you would like to translate basic terms from English to Spanish, or vice-versa, [**travlang.com**](http://www.travlang.com/languages/index.html) maintains the following useful site:

Here are some examples of the Guaran language:

*Mba'exip* (mbaa-ay-sha-pa): How are you? (somewhat formal)

*Mba' la porte* (mbaa-ay la por-tay): How's it going? (informal)

*ipor* (e-pour-rah): nice, pretty, good

*kara* (kah-rah-e): Mr., lord

*cuata* (koon-ya-tai-e): girl
