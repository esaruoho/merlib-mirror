Are you a fan of the Samoyed? The breed with "Christmas in its face" is a wonderful dog and a great pet. A true working dog, Samoyeds can pull sleds, herd sheep, carry a load of camping gear and supply you with enough fur to make a complete wardrobe.  [Take a tour of the Samoyed Ring and visit with the Samoyed](http://j.webring.com/hub?ring=samring).

Are you already a member?  These are YOUR pages, where you can keep up with what's going on and get a little technical assistance.

Would you like to join the Samoyed Ring?  Click "Join Ring" just below, and read the info on the next page. If your web site meets the criteria, we would all like to see it on the ring.

If you have any questions, please write to me at [ron@samoyedring.com](mailto:ron@SamoyedRing.com).  

Ron Manor            \
