# "Sacred Seasons" booklet Poetry by Shirley Schieber

![](icons/li-br-b1.gif)

**This collection of poetry is from Shirley Schieber's "Sacred Seasons" booklet published by The Dalton Press (714 673-4947). In Shirley's words, it is "A Generic Calendar of Verse and Versatility." The booklet has a blank page, with numbered lines, opposite the poems for daily notations each month.**

Shirley is a Course student and friend of Jeff Bibel, our ACIM Net Family "Falling Awake" poet ! The Course inspiration shines brightly through ! Thanks Shirley for allowing the poems in this Potpourri Collection and thanks Jeff for the idea and getting permission ! I enjoy them and hope to meet you both some day to thank you in person !

          Introductory Page Poem !     
     
     
             The seasons
             in nature
             reflect the cycles
             of the psyche.
             Spring is the
             bursting forth
             of new growth
             new ideas
             new challenges
             which, if implemented
             and complimented
             become the sweet
             fruit of Summer
             ripening in the sun
             rich in color
             and abundance.
             Harvested in the Fall
             these ideas
             are brought forth
             in form selectively
             and sorted for worth.
             Some are completed
             some are discarded
             and some are buried
             back in the psyche
             for a Winter season
             protected and mulched
             into rich compost
             then seeded for Spring.
             
                     
             This little book is seasoned with the universal experience of love.
             May it remind you to remember who you are.
             
                                  Copyright 1994 Shirley Schieber

            January Poem !
       
       
             It seems
             as if an ancient door
             is shut
             and we can't enter.
             It seems so heavy
             so wedged
             into the opening
             that it will never
             budge from a
             moldy sill and
             rusty hinges.
             Yet something says
             that we must enter now
             or be thrown back
             into the dark, dank
             dungeon of our fear.
             The impasse seems
             to last a long, long time
             then we focus on the keyhole
             and see the light
             shining there.
             Our sureness grows
             our strength increases
             our heart expands
             and trembling
             we hold His hand
             and walk right through.

           February Poem !

             We seek
             to complete ourselves
             with substitutes
             for love
             hollow handouts
             profane promises
             little lives and
             lavish lies
             refusing to acknowledge
             how soon idols die.
             We search in vain
             and empty
             search again.
             One day
             we do not search
             for what we want
             but what we are
             and feathered fingers
             of faith
             touch our lost souls
             as we begin
             to grow
             into God's Grace.

           March Poem !

             Fear
             hurls itself
             against a house
             of strength
             as brothers
             hold hands
             together
             like a chain
             against its fury
             and love extends
             to push it
             quite away.
             As fear
             deflates
             like a giant
             ghost balloon
             I feel
             the rush of joy
             flow
             in the room
             where fear
             has flown.

          April Poem !

             When God called
             you answered
             for you had never
             left His love at all.
             You did not know
             the call was
             in your mind.
             A call for eternity
             not time.
             You were hesitant
             and wary
             since the world
             had been
             a disappointing place
             and tired of the incessant race
             that only brought you pain
             you didn t want to trust again
             but in each choice for love
             a lily grew
             and soon your mind
             was filled with flowers
             of forgiveness
             returned as Love
             to you.

            May Poem !

             When we
             run around the rim
             of the wheel of life
             we deny the self
             in the center
             and feel a deep
             longing for love
             not suspecting
             we're so distanced
             by denial
             we are lost
             in the movement
             of the wheel
             believing we are
             going somewhere.
             Only when we stop
             in silence and serenity
             turn inward
             to the truth
             do we know our
             Holy Self.

           June Poem !

             There is a branch
             on the road of life
             where we can continue
             to repeat old ways
             that never served us well
             and keep us rooted
             in resentment
             retribution
             and rebuke
             or we can decide
             there might be
             a better Way
             a Way of Wonder
             Wisdom, Truth
             and Love.
             With this choice
             we are headed
             for Heaven
             and heralds of eternity
             sing brightly
             as God leans down
             and lifts us up.

           July Poem !

             Accept the gift
             of Grace
             for you
             and for your brother
             for it was given
             you together
             and though
             you cast it aside
             to make up
             a different world
             you could not
             cast it out.
             It remains
             within your Holy Mind
             and you
             but need to cast
             a net of love
             inside
             to catch it up
             again.
             
         

      

            August Poem !

             Now is the nightmare
             of the past undone
             as all our thoughts
             come tumbling Home as One
             and we see
             the errors of our ways
             are not caught in years
             or weeks or days
             but only in that
             moment still
             when we turned
             against God's will
             who created us
             as love and joy and peace
             and we turned against
             His union and increase
             and made a kingdom
             in the dark night
             of the mind
             divided into separate
             parts and time
             and into this caged horror
             we crept
             closed our crazed eyes
             and slept.

            September Poem !

             No more, I call
             as shadows
             rise and fall
             I will not look
             at you at all
             for you the echoes
             of my past
             slip in a crack
             and stay and last
             and voices rise
             in anguish and
             in tears
             smothering my mind
             with fears.
             No more, I call
             I only hear one Voice
             the surest of them all
             and in that moment
             all the shadows fall.

            October Poem !

             That which we hide
             in the dark recesses
             of our mind
             will destroy us
             that which we reveal
             is healed.
             When we raise
             our darkness
             up to the blazing
             light of love
             it disintegrates
             and disappears
             into the hollowness
             from which it came.
             Brother
             your one and only
             journey
             is from the
             head to the heart
             the passage
             into Love
             and radiant Truth.

           November Poem !

             I seem
             a weary way
             from home
             crest fallen
             tired
             and all alone
             I reach out
             and find
             a gentle hand
             and from it
             warmth expands
             and in that moment
             love begins to seep
             into my mind
             quite fast asleep
             and I begin
             to waken
             from a dream
             that carried
             me away
             from all I knew
             and I awake
             to all that's true.

            December Poem !

             When you left
             God kept a candle lit
             of you and for you
             for He knew
             you had not wandered far
             and would answer
             to His Call
             since you hadn't gone
             at all.
             You fell asleep
             and dreamed a dream
             a scream of loneliness
             and lost love
             which you looked for
             everywhere
             but where it was.
             Then in the darkness
             of despair
             the candle flickered
             in your mind
             and you looked in
             and saw Love
             shining there.

            January  New Year Poem !   

             Now
             in this moment
             be you certain
             you do not walk alone.
             Your brothers
             gather round
             as moths to flame
             and give you strength
             when yours feels wane
             till you take up
             your function once again
             undoing all
             the silly things of time
             forgiving all
             the fantasies of mind
             and fix your sight
             and focus on the goal
             the eternal yearning
             of the soul
             to join with Him
             Who is your Source
             to look upon
             the truth as is
             to look upon your Self
             as whole
             Holy
             and wholly His.

            Closing Page Poem !

             There is a Kindness
             that undermines
             and overlays
             the world of hate.
             Rest in it.
             There is a Joy
             that bubbles up
             through the world
             of pain and fear.
             Rest in it.
             There is a Certainty
             that stands firm
             in a world
             of uncertainty.
             Rest in it.
             There is a Love
             that opens the heart
             and spreads
             across the mind.
             Rest in it.
             His assurances are true
             His promises are kept
             His Truth is bold
             His Love is real.
             Nothing in the world
             can keep you
             from the Love of God.
             Rest in it.         
        Make this year
             different by making it all the same . . . Love.
             
            " A Course in Miracles"

             
      Publisher --- THE DALTON PRESS    (714) 673-4947
