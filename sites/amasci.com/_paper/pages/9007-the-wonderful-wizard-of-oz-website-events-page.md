# *Wizard of Oz* Events Calendar

*Wizard of Oz* events are held all around the world, all throughout the year. There is sure to be one held near you at some time. So go meet your fellow Oz fans, or see an Oz stage performance! Events are listed in chronological order. For more information on these events, please use the contact addresses given. The maintainer of this web site presents these events for informational purposes only, and is not responsible for any problems that may arise.

To publicize your local Oz events, [use this e-mail link](mailto:webwizard@thewizardofoz.info?subject=Events) -- the sooner, the better! Please include as much information as possible, including dates and times, location, and a contact address, phone number, and/or e-mail address.

**Now playing: *Wicked*, Gershwin Theater, Broadway, New York City, New York.** The award-winning musical based on Gregory Maguire's novel *Wicked: The Life and Times of the Wicked Witch of the West*. The book is by Winnie Holzman, the music by Stephen Schwartz, and the show is directed by Joe Mantello. Tickets may be purchased online at <a href="http://www.ticketmaster.com/venue/24579" target="_blank">http://www.ticketmaster.com/venue/24579</a>. For more details, see the show's web page at <a href="http://wickedthemusical.com/" target="_blank">http://wickedthemusical.com/</a> or <a href="http://www.musicalschwartz.com/wicked.htm" target="_blank">http://www.musicalschwartz.com/wicked.htm</a>.

**December 21, 2005-February 22, 2006: *Yellow Brick Road*, national telecast, Cinemax, HBO Family Channel, and Max On Demand.** This new documentary, directed by Matthew Makar, looks at a group of developmentally challenged actors as they put on a production of *The Wizard of Oz*. The complete schedule is available <a href="http://www.hbo.com/apps/schedule/ScheduleServlet?ACTION_DETAIL=DETAIL&amp;FOCUS_ID=622597" target="_blank">via this link</a>. Click on "HBO Documentaries Clips" at the top for a preview.

**February 18-20, 2006: *The Wizard of Oz*, Hollywood Bar and Filmworks, Indianapolis, Indiana.** This movie theater that also serves food and drinks will celebrate the classic 1939 film version of *The Wizard of Oz* on these days, with a showing of the film. Some of the Munchkin actors are slated to be special guests, and they will be available for photos and autographs through the week. For more details, see the Hollywood Boulevard website at <a href="http://www.filmworksonline.com/" target="_blank">http://www.filmworksonline.com/</a>.

**June 6-7, 2006: Oz Festival, Aberdeen, South Dakota.** L. Frank Baum lived in Aberdeen in the 1890s, and the area may have influenced his writing. So the folks in Aberdeen celebrate their connection to Oz with this annual event.
