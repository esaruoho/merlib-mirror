# Welcome to Rob's Video Poker!

![](cat-card.gif)

**Sorry - you must be running Netscape Navigator v. 2.03 or higher with Macromedia's Shockwave plug-in installed to play. Visit their web sites for more info.**

**This is your basic Jacks-or-Better video poker. Some notes on play:**

- You don't need the mouse to play; you can use the keyboard instead. Right-handed people can use the letters h, j, k, l, ; to select/deselect cards to discard; lefties use a, s, d, f and g. The space bar is used to deal and draw.
- To raise and lower the bet, use the up and down arrow keys.

That's it. Think of me if you win.
