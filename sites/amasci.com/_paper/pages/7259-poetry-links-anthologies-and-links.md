Anthologies

- [Academy of American Poets](http://www.poets.org/). [The Listening Booth](http://www.poets.org/LIT/Listefst.htm) lets you listen (via RealAudio) to 50 different poets reading.
- [The Atlantic Monthly's poetry pages](http://www.theatlantic.com/atlantic/atlweb/poetry/poetpage.htm). Includes a short but useful list of links, including on-line journals. Also provides a link to Dana Giaoa's very controversial (and snippy) essay in *The Atlantic*, ["Can Poetry Matter?"](http://www.theatlantic.com/atlantic/atlweb/poetry/gioia/gioia.htm)
- [Buffalo Electronic Poetry Center](http://wings.buffalo.edu/epc/). Where the L=A=N=G=U=A=G=E folks (and many others you'll recognize) hang out. Extensive collection of contemporary poetry and poetics.
- [Canadian Poetry](http://utl2.library.utoronto.ca/www/canpoetry/index.html). An extensive collection, including poems by [Roo Borson](http://utl2.library.utoronto.ca/www/canpoetry/borson/index.htm), a recent subject of Robert Hass's *Poet's Choice* column.
- [Coach House Books](http://www.chbooks.com/) publishes books on-line as well as print. Tends toward the experimental. Plenty to read. Note that they ask you to use their order page to "tip" the poet \$3 for reading the work on-line. Seems fair to me....
- [The Contemporary American Poetry Archive](http://capa.conncoll.edu/) organized by poets [Wendy Battin](http://camel2.conncoll.edu/ccother/wjbat/) and [Charles O. Hartman](http://camel2.conncoll.edu/ccother/cohar/) as a repository for out-of-print poetry books. Contains 35 books.
- [English Language and Literature Resources](http://utl2.library.utoronto.ca/www/utel/rp/utel.html) at the U. Toronto library. Includes an on-line anthology, [Representative Poetry](http://www.library.utoronto.ca/www/utel/rp/intro.html), a collection that "includes over 2,000 English poems by 310 poets." Also links to Middle English and Renaissance resources.
- [Electronic Text Center](http://etext.lib.virginia.edu/) at University of Virginia. Go to the [English page](http://etext.lib.virginia.edu/eng-on.html) to get the list of holdings in English. Many items restricted to University or Virginia library patrons.
- [The Poetry Archive](http://tqd.advanced.org/3247/index.html). Varied collection of poems, including some modern. Essays on each poet.
- [Poetry Daily](http://www.poems.com/). A new poem every day. Has an [archive](http://www.poems.com/archive.htm) of the last year's poems and daily poetry [news](http://www.poems.com/news.htm).
- The original [Project Bartleby](http://www.columbia.edu/acis/bartleby/) is now an archive. [The New Bartleby](http://www.bartleby.com/) continues its work.
- [Red Frog](http://redfrog.norconnect.no/~poems/) - An international on-line anthology. Includes an extensive selection of [Zbigniew Herbert's poetry](http://redfrog.norconnect.no/~poems/poets/herbert.html) from translations now out of print.
- [Sonnet Central](http://members.aol.com/ericblomqu/sonnet.htm). An anthology spanning the whole history of the sonnet.
- [The Wr-eye-tings Scratchpad](http://www.burningpress.org/wreyeting/index.html). A works-in-progress site for visual and/or concrete poetry. Supplement to the Wr-eye-tings email list.

Link Sites

- [American Authors on the Web](http://www.lang.nagoya-u.ac.jp/~matsuoka/AmeLit.html). Big (155K) link page, by Mitsuharu Matsuoka at Nagoya University.
- [English resources on the Web](http://webster.commnet.edu/webpicks/weblist/english_list.htm) page at Capital Community-Technical College. Good selection.
- [Links to Modern and Contemporary Poetry Resources](http://lang.nagoya-u.ac.jp/~nagahata/Poetry.html). An extensive, unannotated list by Akitoshi Nagahata at Nagoya University including many on-line magazines.
- [Modern and Contemporary American Poetry](http://dept.english.upenn.edu/~afilreis/88/home.html) page for Al Filreis's English 88 class at U. Penn. A very rich list.
- [Voice of the Shuttle](http://humanitas.ucsb.edu/). *The* humanities research website. Includes extensive links to everything imaginable, including [contemporary British and American literature](http://humanitas.ucsb.edu/shuttle/eng-cont.html) and a [creative writing page](http://humanitas.ucsb.edu/shuttle/creative.html) containing a rich list of resources and on-line magazines.
