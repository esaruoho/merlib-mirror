The Church - So named because it is near the Nunnery. The rain god, Chac, can be seen as a series of masks with a hooked nose protruding.

[<img src="thumb/i22.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza22.jpg)

The Nunnery - very big. Mayans just kept adding on rooms and platforms. There were apparently a lot of priestesses -

hmmmm.

[<img src="thumb/i23.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza23.jpg) [<img src="thumb/i24.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza24.jpg) [<img src="thumb/i25.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza25.jpg)

Annex - Connects with Nunnery. Built over and added to during different time periods, so different architectural styles. The stone lattice was interesting. That's me in the doorway.

[<img src="thumb/i26.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza26.jpg) The Red House of Chichan-Chob. Our guide said it was so named because of something to do with blood, but the guide book says the name was given to indicate the red stripe on the portico (porch) walls.

[<img src="thumb/i27.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza27.jpg) [<img src="thumb/i28.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza28.jpg)

The Ossurary. In process of being restored, we could see the work being done. This is a cool looking pyramid, lots of serpent heads lying around. White stone. This was a burial place for high ranking people, the pyramid has many Toltec features

[<img src="thumb/i29.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza29.jpg)

Finally, the observatory or El Caracol (the snail) - a spiral staircase can be found inside. Maya astronomers used this as a stellar observatory as evidence by strategically placed - loopholes- on the chamber walls.

Chichen Itza is a big place. We didn't have enough time to see everything. The guidebook describes at least 11 temples from the Date Group and the Main Group of the Southwest considered to be -in a ruinous state- which we overlooked.

[<img src="thumb/i30.jpg" data-align="BOTTOM" />](photos/itza30.jpg)This is a small model of what the known areas probably looked like.

There is a lot more archeological work to do - a lot more mounds to uncover.

I highly recommend a visit to Chichen Itza. On a scale of 1-10, its a definite 10!
