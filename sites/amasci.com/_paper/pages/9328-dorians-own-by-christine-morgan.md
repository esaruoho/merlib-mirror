<td><em>A word of explanation by the author:</em> these stories are set in my game-world, which I have been developing since the early 1980's. The premier deity of that world is Dorian, Goddess of Life.  Much of Dorianism is drawn from Jean M. Auel's "Earth's Children" series.  These stories are dedicated to my husband, Tim, for whom they were written as birthday and anniversary presents. 
<p>The character Rick Avery was originally created as a 25-point farmboy. He and his friends (other PCs) Damon and Taren became adventurers in a lengthy (four years real time) campaign that would take them all over the world. </p>
<h4 id="many-of-these-stories-are-for-mature-readers-only-please.">Many of these stories are for mature readers only, please. </h4></td>
