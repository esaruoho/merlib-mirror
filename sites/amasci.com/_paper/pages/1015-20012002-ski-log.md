**March 30, 2002 - Loveland**

- <img src="img/loveland.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Loveland" /> Didn't think I'd get another trip in this year, but managed to go once more with some friends and their foreign exchange student who had never been skiing before (and she was quite good after a 2-hour lesson!). A little slushy at the bottom and icy at the top, but that's to be expected for the end of the season. Probably my least active ski season yet, but the times I went were really fun. I promise to do better next year!

- <img src="img/breck.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Breckenridge" /> My sister and her husband made their annual trip to ski and force me to take some time off work! We only skied for a couple of days because we also went to visit Rick's brother in Colorado Springs and Kat wasn't skiing because she's pregnant. And a cold front came through and it was c-c-c-cold! But fun.

**January 21, 2002 - Eldora**

- Well, it took me 7 years to go to the closest resort. Eldora is a small resort west of Boulder and very popular with "the kids" and such, doesn't get great snow, and usually people I ski with never want to go. Today I decided to just take the holiday and go skiing myself so the closest place seemed reasonable. The morning began very windy, in fact I passed several cars leaving the mountain on my way in because they had not yet opened the main lifts. But the wind died down mid-morning and after a couple of warm-up runs they other lifts were open, so it worked out great for me because the crowd was minimal. The conditions were actually pretty good, although by about 1pm I had skied every run on the mountain. Still fun and not so far from home.

**January 26, 2002 - Loveland**

- <img src="img/loveland.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Loveland" /> Ran up to Loveland and again it was really windy and one of the lifts was closed a good part of the day. At least we didn't go on Sunday, a tanker truck overturned on I70 and the westbound lanes were closed for 10 hours, that messed up a bunch of people's ski plans. Yeow.
