<p>PRICES</p>
<p>Prices shown are subject to change without notice. We do our best to keep the prices as low as possible. Merchandise is billed at prices prevailing at the time of shipment. <strong>If firm prices are required, please request a quotation.</strong> Washington State residents must add sales tax to all orders. <strong>We reserve the right to correct any errors in the catalog.</strong></p>
<strong></strong>
<p>MINIMUM ORDER $25.00 or $3.00 handling charge added.</p>
Because of the cost of processing, packaging and shipping the orders, we can only accept orders of $25.00 or more retail. <strong>Any order accepted for less than $25.00 is subject to a $3.00 handling fee.</strong>
<br />
<br />
SHIPPING
<p>Shipment is made through your choice of carriers usually within 24 hours of receipt of your order.</p>
<p>Report any <strong>Shortages</strong> within 48 hours of receipt of the parcel. Give the invoice number and date. Examine the contents and packing material carefully for small items that may have been missed during the unpacking of the parcel.</p>
If merchandise is received <strong>DAMAGED</strong>, advise the Delivery Agency, notify Alpha Supply and save all of the packing material and shipping carton for inspection by the Delivery Agency. You will receive instructions for handling the damage claim. If merchandise is received <strong>DEFECTIVE,</strong> advise Alpha Supply for disposition.
<br />
<br />
BACKORDERS
<p>In the event we are out of an item that you ordered, we will back order it for you if you wish. You may select to have it <strong>Open Backorder, Cancel 30 Days, or Cancel at your Request. Items ordered, but out of stock, with a value of less than $15.00 will not be backordered. Please reorder those items with your next order.</strong></p>
<strong></strong>
<p>RETURNS</p>
<p>If you are not completely satisfied with any product purchased from ALPHA SUPPLY, you may return it within <strong>10 days of receipt,</strong> in its original display carton, in resaleable condition, insured for full face value and shipped prepaid along with a copy of your invoice. We are not responsible for loss or damage on return shipment to us.</p>
<p>Items returned due to customer error, late returns, merchandise not in its original condition, not in the original container or without a copy of the original invoice <strong>will be subject to a 10% restocking fee.</strong></p>
<strong></strong>
<p>Returns over 90 days old are returnable for exchange only.</p>
<strong></strong>
<p>Please Note :</p>
<p>The description and picture representations in this catalog resemble the actual product as closely as possible. However, because of the various manufacturers continued attempts to improve their merchandise, specifications will sometimes vary.</p></td>
<td width="50%" data-valign="top"><strong></strong>
<p>PAYMENT</p>
<p>Payment must be received with the order payable in U.S. funds or charged to a valid Visa, Mastercard, or Discover Card. In order to use these charge cards we must have the proper card account no. and the expiration date. <strong>COD</strong> shipment will be made payable in cash or Postal Money Order for all first time orders. <strong>On all COD shipments refused or undeliverable, the customer will be liable for all handling charges</strong>. Amount due in excess of prepayment may be collected by COD. Foreign orders can not be sent COD</p>
<strong></strong>
<p>There is a $25.00 SERVICE CHARGE on all checks returned to us by the Bank.</p>
<p>On any <strong>PAST DUE AMOUNTS</strong> there is a service charge of 1 1/2<strong>%</strong> per month, effective rate of 18<strong>%</strong> per annum, or the maximum amount allowed by law.</p>
<strong></strong>
<p>HOW TO ORDER</p>
<blockquote>
<p>1. For prompt and accurate service, write the order plainly indicating part number as well as the description, size, color, etc. of the article.</p>
<p>2. Use our order form when possible.</p>
<p>3. Be certain to give your current address and phone number.</p>
<p>4. In order to expedite shipment always include your <strong>ALPHA IDENTIFICATION No. on all orders and correspondence. Your Identification Number is located in the upper left hand corner of the catalog mailing label with your name and address.</strong></p>
<p>5. <strong>Orders only may be placed 24 hours a day on our Toll Free Order Line [800] 257-4211 or our Toll Free Fax Line [800] 257-4244.</strong> In the Seattle area please call 625-0564. <strong>A note about our Toll Free Lines these are ORDER ONLY lines.</strong> Our operators do not have access to information concerning orders not yet received, stock available, technical information, etc.</p>
<p>6. For information on products, stock available, technical information, etc. please call [360] 373-3302.</p>
