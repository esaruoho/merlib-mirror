# Join The OFDA Technical Dive Card Program

<img src="ofdatechcard.gif" data-align="Top" width="496" height="152" />

#### Be an OFDA TECHNICAL DIVING Card Collector. Any OFDA member is pre-qualified to order any of the cards listed below. Your personal, plastic-sealed OFDA TECHNICAL DIVING card will be sent to you in 3 to 5 working days.

#### THE FOLLOWING OFDA TECHNICAL CARD TITLES ARE AVAILABLE:

- East Coast Wrecked Diver
- Nitrox Aware Diver
- West Coast Wrecked Diver
- Nitrox Wary Diver
- Great Lakes Wrecked Diver
- Non-Penetrating Deep Quarry Drift Diver
- Orkney Isl. Wrecked Diver
- Fast Air Breathing Diver
- Tropical Wrecked Diver
- Long Hosed Tech Diver
- Gassy Mix Master
- Master Margarita Mixer
- Rebreather Aware Diver
- Hi-Tech Pony Mounter  (Illegal in some areas)
- East Coast Deep Air -Tool Using Diver
- Rebreather Wary Diver

### To qualify you must:

- Be an OFDA Member.
- Have the cash and a picture of yourself.

### How to order your card:

- Select the card or cards you need.
- Make note of your original (non-tech) OFDA card number. No cheating! I know the names & numbers of members.
- Find or take a passport sized photo for each OFDA card.
- Choose a first and last name for your instructor. We will select one if you forget.
- Secure the cash - \$20 US, 2 titles for \$35, 3 for \$45, additional at \$10 each.

Once you complete the above, just email me and I'll send you the order form. <dickb@eskimo.com>

You may progress through the OFDA technical card maze in any order. Others would have you get Nitrox Aware and Deep Air-Tool prior to getting the Gassy Mix Master card. We let you choose your own way to do it right.

If you want a title that doesn't exist yet please mention it in the email. You will get a discount and credit on this page if the title is accepted by the OTDTAC, the OFDA Technical Diving Title Approval Committee.

Be advised that your OFDA Technical card is **not** a certification card. This card is designed for the sole purpose of allowing you to surprise, impress and amaze your scuba friends. It also is a good conversation starter (or is that stopper) at any party.
