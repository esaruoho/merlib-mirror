**Why we care: Like millions of Americans, many of the Center's members and staff are pet owners. The petfood industry, which they rely on for good products, is under attack from the animal rights movement, being hit with claims of animal cruelty in scientific testing programs. The petfood industry is a prime example of <u>consumer choice at work</u>, with vast numbers of animal-loving Americans selecting from a wide range of food products for their pets. Consumers trust petfood companies to provide safe, healthy and nutritious food products tested according to proper standards of animal care.  Animal rights groups, such as People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals (PETA), <u>o</u>pp<u>ose</u> p<u>et ownershi</u>p <u>entirel</u>y, and thus take every opportunity to eliminate it as a cultural institution. Animal rights groups actively seek out the slightest excuse to attack pet ownership, and have found that the petfood industry is vulnerable to attack because the public knows little or nothing about the excellent testing programs petfood companies operate.  As a result, good companies such as IAMS and Menu Foods get beaten up by animal rights groups for extremist purposes. <u>CDFE su</u>pp<u>orts this industry</u> in their effort to meet the publics need for high quality petfood tested by humane methods.**
