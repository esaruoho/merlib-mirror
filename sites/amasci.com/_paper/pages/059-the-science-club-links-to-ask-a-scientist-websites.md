<h3 id="try-these-links-get-your-science-questions-answered">Try these links, get Your Science Questions Answered!</h3>
<ul>
<li><h3 id="general-science-questions">General Science Questions</h3></li>
<li><a href="http://www.madsci.org/">MAD SCIENTIST NETWORK</a> Highly recommended! Large scientist group!</li>
<li><a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reference_desk/Science">Wikipedia ref desk</a> (then scroll down!)</li>
<li><a href="http://www.reddit.com/r/askscience">Reddit Ask Science</a></li>
<li><a href="http://boards.straightdope.com/sdmb/">The Straight Dope</a> forum</li>
<li><a href="http://wiki.answers.com/Q/FAQ/455">Answers.com</a> science questions</li>
<li><a href="http://www.answerbag.co.uk/topics/scho-seam_2">Answerbag UK: Science</a></li>
<li><a href="http://ask.metafilter.com/">Metafilter: Ask</a> (non-science)</li>
<li><a href="http://www.allexperts.com/cl1/47/Science/">ALLEXPERTS science section</a></li>
<li><a href="http://amasci.com/amateur/answers1.html">Science Hobbyist Answers</a>, part of Madsci Network</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li><a href="http://www.ask.com">'Ask Jeeves'</a>, it understands english</li>
<li><a href="http://clubs.yahoo.com/clubs/sciencehelp">SCIENCEHELP</a> forum, phys/chem/bio w/NASA consultants, (a Yahoo <a href="http://clubs.hayoo.com">"club"</a>)</li>
<li><a href="http://www.sciencenet.org.uk/">Science Net</a>, science questions answered (UK)</li>
<li><a href="http://emissary.ots.utexas.edu/emissary/index.html">Electronic Emissary</a>, K-12 clasrooms interact w/experts</li>
<li><a href="http://www.newton.dep.anl.gov/archive.htm">NEWTON Ask A Scientist</a></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li><a href="telnet://newton.dep.anl.gov/">NEWTON BBS</a> (telnet, use "bbs" as login name)</li>
</ul>

<a href="http://www.telementor.org/">HP email science mentors</a>
