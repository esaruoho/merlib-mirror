# Aug 2 - Market Days!

### Market Days

I've been growing increasingly more connected to the madison 'bear' group, and about 6 of us decided that going down to chicago for the weekend would be fun. Many thanks go out to Dewey for organizing, getting hotel rooms, and generally making things work. Thanks also to Tony for driving, and to other other guys, Trever, Neil, Selim, John and Matt for making this alot of fun!

We headed down to chicago around 8pm...got in around 10:15 to the wonderful travel lodge hotel :) We made room assignments and headed off to..what else...eat! We then checked out a few of the bars..cellblock and bucks. I now know to NOT complain about prices here in madison -- a bottled water cost me 3.25...ack! We had fun ' posing' by the mirrored wall..and just watching the people. I almost forgot to mention...we rode the train -- which was a bit of an adventure for me :-) Got back to the hotel around 3am or so...and zonked out..or tried..our air cond. wasn't doing so well, and the room was sorta hot.

Got up around 10 ish...and went to Ann Sathers for breakfast...yummmm. We then hiked over to Market Days and walked around for a few hours..WOW. I had never been to boys town, so being in a totally gay section of such a big city was something new...knowing that just about everyone you see is, ermm., family -- just is kinda nice..besides..it was HOT out, and guess what...guys took thier shirts off..imagine \*that\* :)

Trever and I decided to hit the virutal realty disney thing...and we were there for a few hours, flying magic carpets, sword fighting, mech warrior piloting -- it was really cool...but we both had headaches after Perhaps we tighted down the vr helmets a little too tight ? who can say...it was way kewl!

Next, the group went to eat at...Roscoes...yummy..filling..and then off too..TADA! Bear night at touch'e...which will EVER be know by a few of us as touchie. Imagine a place bigger then club 5, full of beary guys..The varitey of people was just so interesting..nothing like anything I'd I've seen before. The back room was , ermm, well, the back room. hahaha! I'll spare details...unless you want 'em...suffice to say that it was...interesting. Not a dull moment the entire night!

We all dragged ourselves back to the hotel around 3:30 am....and again, the room was hot...so I got some ice , wrapped it in a towel and put the towel on my head...it worked..!! I'll remember that next time! Granted, I woke up with a wet pillow, but it was worth it :-)

Sunday, we got up at the early hour of 11am...and went off to eat at Bongos...damn!!! IT was GREATTTTT food ..I had a chocalate french toast thing...really, really good, but too much for one guy! We then hit market days again, got some sun, and took a picture of what had to the THE hariest guy I've ever seen..anywhere! (pix will be forthcoming!) Bought a few clothes items...thats about it. Headed back to madison around 5pm...

The actual event was really a minor part of this trip in my book...getting to spend time with really nice guys, relaxing and having fun, was more important. What a good bunch of guys..hope to do another adventure with this group soon!
