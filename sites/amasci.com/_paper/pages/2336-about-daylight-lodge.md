<td>Daylight Lodge meets the 3rd Saturday of the month at 10:00 except for July, August and December. July meetings are at 12:00 noon on the 4th Saturday and we are dark August and December.<br />
<br />
We meet at Greenlake Masonic Temple, 307 Maple Leaf Pl. N.E., Seattle.<br />
