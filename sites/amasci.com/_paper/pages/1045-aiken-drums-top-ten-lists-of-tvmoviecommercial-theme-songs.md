# Aiken Drum's Top Ten Lists of TV/Movie/Commercial Theme Songs

*Source page: `~aiken/topten.html`*

Last updated June 19th, 2005.

There is a lot of good music used on TV and in movies, but sometimes an ad or show picks a song that stops you, can of soda halfway to your mouth, until 20 seconds after it's over and you quietly say, "oh my god," or, "wow," all in lowercase letters, put the can down, to go search the Internet, because you have to know *what that was*.

That's what marketing people are paid for, after all: to grab you for 30 or 60 seconds and leave you with an indelible memory. Sometimes they get it right. This is my list of the ones that I think got it right. I didn't say "oh my god" after *every* one, mind you; some had to grow on me, but regardless they are all a significant part of my inner music and I now own a CD for each one that I could find.

The links below point almost exclusively to Tower Records' website. This is because Tower's site used to have the most consistently representative samples of songs. However, now they have the same crappy samples that everyone else has, the ones that stop just as you get to the good or recognizable part of a song, which are totally useless. I'm just too lazy to change them now.
