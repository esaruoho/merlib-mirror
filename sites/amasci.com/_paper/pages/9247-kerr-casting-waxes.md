**(3)  MASTER PATTERN WAX** - A specially formulated wax which can be carved, sawed, filed, drilled and even turned on a lathe without chipping or flaking. The wax resists adhering to cutting tool surfaces. Available in Red-Soft (melting temp. 203'F), Blue-Medium-Hard (melting temp. 220'F.) and Green-Hard (melting temp. 229'F). These waxes are toolable and vary in hardness when carving. Supplied in 1 Ib. blocks 2 7/8"x7"x1 1/2" deep or 1 Ib. sliced 2 7/8"x1 1/2" in the following assortment: 8 pcs. 1/4" thick; 4 pcs. 1/2" thick; 1 pc. 2 7/8" thick.

**(4 )  SOL-U-CARV WAX - BOTH A CARVABLE AND SOLUBLE YELLOW WAX.** Designed for making hollow beads, hollow pendants, dome rings and other configurations where a hollowed area in the finished pattern is desired. Using Sol-U-Carv Wax, a core can be carved using conventional wax carving tools. The wax can also be melted, (140'F.) poured, or injected into a prepared mold. Cores immersed in water will dissolve, leaving the desired pattern.
