<img src="images/mudhaven.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **Mud-Haven 2.0**\
\
Mud Haven is a comprehensive Macintosh Telnet client designed specifically for mudding. Some of the more prominent features include aliases, triggers, quick keys, and a variety of other shortcuts and automated taskes that give you a significant advantage while mudding. Mud-Haven also provides a plethera of muds pre-set into its intuitive mud manager, ensuring hours upon hours upon hours of pure mudding satisfaction.\

- **License**: Shareware, \$10
- **Author/Publisher**: Keith Kerlan
- **Modification Date**: November 6, 1999
- **Requirements**: 680x0 or PPC

[Direct Download](http://members.tripod.com/DarksidePrd/MudHaven68k.sea.hqx) File Size: 2111 Kb - 68k version\

<img src="images/savitar.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **Savitar 1.1.4**\
\
Savitar is a software application designed to connect people using Macintosh computers to MUVE (Multi-user Virtual Environment) servers. These MUVE servers are found throughout the Earth's Internet. Savitar works as a "client" application which basically means it hooks your computer to one or more of these servers.\

- **License**: Shareware, \$15
- **Author/Publisher**: [Jay Koutavas](mailto:jay@heynow.com)/Heynow Software
- **Modification Date**: May 21, 2000
- **Requirements**: 680x0 or PPC, System 7.1 or better

<img src="images/slashmud.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **Slash MUD 1.0d44**\
\
SlashMUD is a MacOS-based MUD that is currently in the final stages of development. SlashMUD is fully object-oriented and includes many unique features built-in as standard. Much customisation can be done by editing the SlashMUD Data Files, and what can't be done that way can be done by writing a SlashMUD Plugin (implemented as compiled code snippets written in Pascal or C) for customisations.\

- **License**: Freeware
- **Author/Publisher**: Koryn Grant
- **Modification Date**: October 10, 2000
- **Requirements**: PPC

[Direct Download](http://slashmud.sourceforge.net/slashmud-1.0d44-ppc.bin) File Size: 1991 Kb\
[Home Page](http://slashmud.sourceforge.net/contents.html) <span id="tinymush"></span>
