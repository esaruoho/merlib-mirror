<div align="LEFT">

After dark, while I was going to my first dinner at <a href="ndutu.html" target="_self">Ndutu Lodge</a>, a genet greeted me. It walked across the top of the wall separating the dining room from the bar, just below the rafters. From the end of the wall it peered down at me.\
