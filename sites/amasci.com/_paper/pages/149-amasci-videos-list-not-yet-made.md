<h2 id="the-shows-philosophy">THE SHOW'S PHILOSOPHY</h2>
<dl>
<dt><strong>1. NOT AIMED AT THE TV AUDIENCE, NOR EVEN AT YOUTUBE</strong></dt>
<dd>
These YT videos are meant only for myself, but me ...at age 9yrs! <a href="http://amasci.com/amateur/videos.html">This series</a> is a set of adult secrets that I'd wished I'd known about, back when I was a kid.<br />
<br />
Imagine that you had a machine which lets you pass messages back in time to your early years. Which cool secrets and weird tricks do you wish you'd known about back then? My own childhood isn't accessible, too bad. But wait! The childhoods of millions of future adults are sitting right there online, waiting to be altered! :) Make no mistake, this is not about 'Teaching Important Science Facts.' Instead it's about revealing secret tricks and techniques that only grownups know about.<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>2. FEATURE SOME EMBARRASSINGLY CRUDE, SEMI-INCOMPETENT DEMOS</strong></dt>
<dd>
My goal isn't to impress other teachers. That would just convince kids that science is way complicated &amp; adults-only. Instead my goal is to lead kids into saying something like this: "Hey, I could make one of those... but MINE WOULD BE BETTER." So my demos need to be crafted so that an adult science expert will sneer at their crudeness and simplicity. (But in saying that, I realize that R. Feynman would probably be impressed <em>because</em> they are so crude and simple. New goal: impress Feynman.)<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>3. DO INTERESTING DEMOS AND STUNNING PHENOMENA ONLY</strong></dt>
<dd>
Imagine which kind of odd science secrets would have driven you crazy with joy, back when you were nine.<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>4. AVOID THE "EXPERT LOOK"</strong></dt>
<dd>
No professional w/labcoat and glasses. Keep any advanced degrees hidden; instead do some Redneck Science. No suit and tie. Carefully preserve the look of an out-of-shape loser in a t-shirt. Don't shave. Don't clean the kitchen mess. Maybe even blacken a tooth for that white trash look. <em>[Update: I lost a huge filling between front teeth! Cool! Made many videos, but can't tell if anyone noticed. LATER UPDATE: finally had it repaired. No more gap-toothed weirdo look.]</em><br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>5. USE NO LABORATORY</strong></dt>
<dd>
Perform demonstrations on a grubby kitchen counter. Or even better, on a living room couch ...in my lap. Keep pushing the cat away.<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>6. BREAK VIDEO TRADITIONS</strong></dt>
<dd>
No classroom lecture, no professional studio, not even a lab bench. Use tiny handheld camera in the talent's own viewpoint (often clamped under his chin!)<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>7. ELIMINATE ALL JARGON: REPLACE OBSCURE TERMS WITH THEIR FULL DEFINITIONS</strong></dt>
<dd>
Don't say Alternator, say "AC electric generator." Never say ions, instead say "electrically charged atoms." Say "charge flow" instead of electric current, and "energy flow" instead of Power.<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>8. CAREFULLY AVOID USING EXISTING DEMOS</strong></dt>
<dd>
There's no passion in plagarism. So, try to exclude known demos, instead pull new ideas out of nowhere.<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>9. EASILY-FOUND MATERIALS ONLY</strong></dt>
<dd>
Replace gleaming acrylic and machined aluminum with dirty cardboard. Buy all your science equipment from the grocery store, or if there's no other option, from Home Depot and Radio Shack.<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>10. NO TOOLS NEEDED</strong></dt>
<dd>
When I was a kid, drilling a hole in wood was nearly impossible. Our family was all gradeschool teachers with a kitchen tool drawer, no power tools. I only had pointy scissors. Cardboard, glue, and mayo jars are OK. A razor knife really is just for older students.<br />
<br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>11. USE EXPERIMENTS THAT ALWAYS WORK</strong></dt>
<dd>
If it requires considerable expertise to make the demo succeed, it'll be guaranteed to turn kids off to science. Never forget that if kids fail to get the demo working, they'll feel incompetent and blame themselves. Avoid including any such "psychological toxin" which kills off the scientist in everyone.<br />
&#10;</dd>
</dl>
