I haven't scoured the internet for links. There are a number of good search engines out there. These are people/businesses/organizations that I have dealt with and wouldn't hesitate to recommend.

[Cumberland Tactics](http://www.guntactics.com),\
Randy Cain's outfit. A top notch instructor. I've taken a number of classes from Randy, learned a lot and thoroughly enjoyed them (and will be taking more!).

[Fulton Armory](http://www.fulton-armory.com)\
AR-15, M14, M1 Rifle and M1 Carbine Specialists.

[Glenrock Blue](http://www.gunbluing.com)\
Bluing quick and at a reasonable price. They did the finish on my .308 Obermeyer and I'm very happy with it.

[Hall Manfuacturing](http://www.hallmfg.com/)\
The makers of the Hall Express action that is used on my .338 Millard.

[High Noon Holsters](http://www.highnoonholsters.com/)\
Holsters, belts and mag pouches. Great service quickly with a no questions asked money back guarantee.

[Internal Ballistics Home Page](http://www.mcs.net/~sfaber/)\
Internal ballistics info and software

[International Defensive Pistol Association](http://www.idpa.com)\

[McMillan](http://www.mcmfamily.com)\
Fiberglass Rifle Stocks, Custom Rifles, Day-Night Rifle Scopes

[packing.org](http://www.packing.org)\
Great site for concealed carry information.

[Pac-Nor Barreling](http://www.pac-nor.com)\
Great barrels at a good price and on time. Great people to deal with.

[Premier Reticles](http://www.premierreticles.com)\
Reticle conversions for Leupold scopes.
