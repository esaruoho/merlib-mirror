### Chief Seattle Council 2005 Jamboree - Pre-Jamboree Tour Information

|  |
|----|
| **For many Scouts, this will be their first and maybe only trip to the East Coast, so we are working very hard to make sure that the tour includes the most important East Coast highlights. The exact details of the 2005 tour are being ironed out, and we expect to have the itinerary posted here before the end of the year.** |

------------------------------------------------------------------------

<u>Tour Company</u>

For 2005, the decision on which tour company to use will be reviewed. We used Harmon Tours in 1993, Lakeland in 1997, and Worldstrides in 2001. There were pros and cons for each company. The choice of tour companies will be based on their ability to provide quality, service, and price.

<u>The Pre-Jamboree Tour</u>

Here is what we will do and see on the tour:

- <u>Philadelphia</u> Tour historic Philadelphia, including a visit to the Liberty Bell and Independence Hall.

- <u>Gettysburg</u> See the Electric Map, the Museum, and do an extensive battlefield tour.

- <u>Washington D.C. and area</u> We'll spend several days in Washington D.C. visiting all the major sites, including the Smithsonians, the Capital, the White House, all of the major presidential and war monuments, Arlington Cemetery, the Holocaust Museum, Mount Vernon (Washington's Home), and other sites and activities.

  Also in Washington D.C., we'll participate in a wreath-laying ceremony at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldiers, and we'll attend a special performance of the Marine Corps Barracks Silent Drill Team and Parade. This is an absolutely stunning display that always appears on everyone's "must see" list.

- <u>Fun Day!</u> We'll spend an afternoon and evening burning up energy at King's Dominion amusement park. This was an extremely popular activity in 2001. It has incredible rides and lots of other activities.

- <u>Laundry Service</u> During the tour we'll also arrange at least one laundry service so everyone keeps looking and smelling clean and fresh.
