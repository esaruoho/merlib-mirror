# The Zepfest 98 Photo Collection

You are the![](http://www.zoso.net/cgi-bin/Count.cgi?df=zepfest.dat) person to view these photos.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Zepfest 98 was held in Cleveland, Ohio on July 3-4. A great time was had by all who attended. The events included the Led Zeppelin tribute act "The White" and concluded with a Jimmy Page and Robert Plant performing a concert at Gund Arena on July 3. Plenty of fun and partying were going on throughout the whole weekend, and the photos below are provided to bring back some great memories for those who attended, and to provide those who weren't able to attend an idea of what went on. This photo collection will be growing in the near future, thumbnails will be available soon. Anyone wishing to contribute photos please email Jeremy Mixer at <mixer@zoso.net>. Special thanks go out to Grant Burgess for organizing, hosting, and promoting this event.
