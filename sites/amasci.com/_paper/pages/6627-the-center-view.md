**Commentary from Center for the Defense of Free Enterprise\
leaders and senior advisors on key issues of the day**

<img src="Ron%20new-a.png" data-border="0" data-align="left" width="96" height="120" />Welcome! from RON ARNOLD, the Center's Executive Vice President.

Please come in and make yourself at home!

This is your place to see what we're up to at the Center. It's a lively gathering of people in the know and in the loop people who care and people who work to make a difference.

We strive to offer up a zesty stew of opinions, attitudes and outlooks that cover a spectrum of free enterprise issues to keep you informed. We're concerned about individual liberties, free markets, property rights, and limited government things that affect everyone.

Whether it makes you glad or mad, The Center View will keep you thinking and maybe give your emotions a workout, too! Sometimes it's a discussion, sometimes it's a debate. We defend our beliefs, but respect you too.
