<p><em><strong>Why it works!</strong></em></p>
<p>- In tapwater there are many minerals and impurities so there my already br significant amounts of Zinc, Magnesium, etc. When you add hydro fertilizers they already come assuming your water is pure. Often these imbalances show!</p>
<p>- Aside from the obvious culprits clorine and flouride chemicals, there may be small amounts of lead, arsenic, and other "goodies". All of which poison the plants.</p>
<p>- One of the most beneficial aspects of using pure H<sub>2</sub>O is the pH remains stable! This will not mean much if you are not currently growing hydro. But for those of us who have battled to stabilize pH, <strong>Reverse Osmosis is a dream come true!</strong></p>
