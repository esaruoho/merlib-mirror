<h1 id="i-am-famous">I am Famous!</h1>
<strong>Written by Al Wong</strong>
<p>August 18, 2000</p>
<p>Since I wrote my JLC rant over three years ago, it has caused more controversy than I could imagine. English and writing classes in high schools and colleges <strong>around the world</strong> are using my JLC rant in their teachings! It's strange but wonderful.</p>
<p>At first, I thought this was strange considering the JLC movie is a relatively minor movie and the book is far from fine literature. But I came to realize that because there are <strong>so few</strong> movies with an all Chinese American cast, the ones that do exist exert a much greater influence than they normally would!</p>
<p>Recently, I decided to keep more detailed metrics of the visitors that have viewed my JLC rant. It's interesting to see who is coming to my website, what search engine they used, the number of hits per day, etc. It's surprising the things you can find out.</p>
<p>It turns out my JLC rant is getting an average of almost 60 hits per day! The average was much higher before but I suspect a tapering off because of the summer holidays. Again, I did find this many hits/day amazing considering the JLC is a relatively minor movie. All these schools are pointing their students at my JLC rant.</p>
