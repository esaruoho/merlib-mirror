<p>Optivisor MAGNIFIERS<em><br />
Optivisor is a highly versatile precision made binocular magnifier that is worn on the head ... over your regular glasses if desired. . . leaving both hands free.<br />
Can be instantly tilted downward when needed and upward when not in use.<br />
Frame made of light weight high impact resistant material.<br />
Lenses made of optical glass ground to precision standards.<br />
Genuine leather comfort band makes it more comfortable than any other worn magnifier<br />
A twist of the adjustment dial changes head size. ABS high impact visor.</em></p>
