# Weather

### [National Weather Service Marine Product Dissemination Information](http://www.nws.noaa.gov/om/marine/home.htm)

> The above link is a pretty good overview of text and graphical weather reports for the US coastal areas. The stuff below are some specific links that I tend to use on a regular basis.

## text

- coastal areas (close to shore)
  - [Southeast USA coast](http://www.nws.noaa.gov/om/marine/zone/south/stheastmz.htm)
  - [Northeast USA coast](http://www.nws.noaa.gov/om/marine/zone/east/ermzn.htm)
- offshore areas (farther out from shore)
  - [West of Florida](http://weather.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/fmtbltn.pl?file=forecasts/marine/offshore/am/amz080.txt)
  - [East and South of Florida (includes Bahamas)](http://weather.noaa.gov/cgi-bin/fmtbltn.pl?file=forecasts/marine/offshore/gm/gmz086.txt)
  - [other East coast USA offshore areas](http://www.nws.noaa.gov/om/marine/zone/wrdoffmz.htm)
- high seas areas (wayyyyyy out there)
  - [north of Florida](http://weather.noaa.gov/pub/data/forecasts/marine/high_seas/north_atlantic.txt)
  - [south of Florida](http://iwin.nws.noaa.gov/pub/data/text/FZNT02/KNHC.TXT)
