# Example Audio Adapters

*Source page: `~millerd/nicadapter/examples.html`*

## Description

I had to make several of these, so messing with the tiny pins and wires wasn't an option. I etched some double-sided circuit boards and soldered the connector to those. The housing is a hand cast polyurethane. It was cast in two parts and assembled. This was still a manual and time consuming process, but gave much more consistent results.
