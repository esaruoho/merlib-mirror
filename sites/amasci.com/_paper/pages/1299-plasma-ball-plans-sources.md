## Bill B's short Plasma Sphere instructions for experienced electronics hobbyists:

First built a tiny tesla coil based on a flyback-transformer. Flyback transformers can be had from old TV sets or dead computer monitors. Use one of the following schematics and build a tesla coil:\

- [Don Klipstein's Ignition Coil circuit](http://misty.com/people/don/igcoilhv.html)
- [Sam Goldwasser's flyback circuit](http://misty.com/people/don/hvinvert.htm)
- [Mini coil schematic](/tesla/sstesla.gif) from TESLA GROUP (can be used for Plasma Sphere)
- [Another mini coil schematic](/tesla/sstesla2.gif) from Tesla Group

\
\
Your coil needs to be able to generate a spark of about 1.5cm length. Next, obtain a "decorator" 40 watt 4-inch clear spherical light bulb. Large hardward stores like Ernst or Fred Meyer carry these. Connect the high voltage lead from your mini tesla coil to the base of the light bulb. Turn off the lights and turn on your coil, and you'll see purple "plasma fingers" spewing out of the filament supports in the light bulb.\
\
If you want to get ambitious you can build your own glass globe. Use a glass jar, or better yet a boiling flask from a [mailorder chemistry supplier](http://www.eskimo.com/~billb/amasci.html#supl). Stopper with a 3-hole stopper. Provide two hoses, one to inject gas, the other as an outlet. Insert a wire into one hole as the H.V. terminal and seal with epoxy (don't use silicone caulk, the acetic acid fumes destroy the plasma effect. Turn on the tesla coil, turn out the lights, then slowly flush the nitrogen out of the glass globe with argon (welding argon is pure enough.) The small corona discharge on the wire in the globe will grow larger and larger as the nitrogen gets replaced with argon. When the discharge is large and white, turn off the argon and clamp the hoses. See [Plasma Sphere without vacuum pump](http://www.eskimo.com/~billb/tesla/heli1.txt) for more info.\
