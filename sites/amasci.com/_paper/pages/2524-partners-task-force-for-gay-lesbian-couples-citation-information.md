<td data-bgcolor="#FFF9F4"><strong>W</strong>eb-based articles can be used as sources for quotes or facts. They can be used in a thesis, report, or magazine article the same way that books or magazine articles are cited as sources. Citations allow readers to check an article’s accuracy.
<p>Citations can be placed in parentheses or brackets just after a quote, fact, or opinion. They can also be placed at the end of an article as footnotes or endnotes.</p>
<p>Generally, Web citations would have the same information as a cite from a book or an article, with the addition of the Web address:</p>
<ul>
<li>Name of Article</li>
<li>Author (an individual or an organization)</li>
<li>Date</li>
<li>Web Address</li>
<li>Organization Name</li>
<li>Mailing Address</li>
<li>Phone</li>
<li>E-mail</li>
