<td colspan="2" data-valign="TOP" width="80%" data-bgcolor="#ffffff"><p><strong>Sad.</strong></p>
<p>I just found out that a guy who'd been in the chorus - a pretty, young guy who teased and flirted outrageously and annoyed and made everybody smile and occasionally suckered friends out of large sums of money but everybody usually liked him anyway - died. I don't have many details about what happened, but the email I got made me pretty sad. He was an immature asshole, but a likeable one.</p>
<p>So I called my friend Kirsten to make sure she was okay and to see if she needed anything. She and Chris had been really close - their platonic gayboy/bigirl relationship annoyingly much talked about in the chorus - and even though he'd screwed her over, I knew that hearing about his death would be hard.</p>
<p>I called. And it turned out that nobody had thought to let her know. I was the one who told her Chris had died.</p>
<p>I let her know that she could call on me for whatever she needed, I could come over, whatever...</p>
<p>But being the one to tell her... I'm glad she heard it from a person rather than reading it in email. She said so, too.</p>
<p>I'm feeling a bit lost right now. Sad. Wanting to be able to do something, but there's nothing, really, I can do to help.</p>
<p>Goodbye, Chris.</p>
