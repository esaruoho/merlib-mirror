<td data-bgcolor="#F0FFF0" data-valign="top"><p><strong><br />
Finding a new home for your Samoyed</strong></p>
<p>To those of us who love the breed, our sammies are our families. We understand that events in our lives can bring unexpected results, and that sometimes dogs need new homes.</p>
<p><strong>This is who we are:</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>We are all volunteers. Each of us donates our time because we love the breed. We hope you understand that rescue work is done around the rest of our lives and we may not be able to respond immediately -- but we will try to answer as soon as possible. We love the breed and will do everything we can for the good of your dog.</p>
<p>Many times we don't have homes ready and waiting. We need you to take care of your dog while we search for a new home.  It takes time to match your dog to the right new family, and our foster homes are limited (we have only two). If we fill those with owner surrenders we won't have room for shelter dogs in danger of being euthanized.</p>
<p>If your dog barks too much, digs, or has behavior problems, it's probably bored or unhappy. There are trainers in every area who can help you. For the sake of your family friend, <em>please</em> try that first.  If you want our ideas and suggestions, we're happy to give you our time.</p>
<p>If you have a child in the house who has allergies, please make sure it's the dog. Samoyeds have almost no dander and are <em>rarely</em> the cause of allergies.</p>
<p>If you truly love your dog and you have to move, please look for a place that allows dogs.</p>
<p>Finally, if you have already made the decision to part with your samoyed and you know you won't change your mind, tell us. It saves a lot of time and it's easier on all of us.</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong>What we need from you:</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>Your Samoyed must be neutered or spayed and up to date on shots. We need your veterinary records. If we end up taking your dog before it is altered, we have to ask for you to pay for it at the time of surrender.</p>
<p>You need to sign a surrender form releasing the dog to rescue, and provide proof that the dog is yours.</p>
<p>Until a home is found, we need you to keep your dog with you. Again, it's a matter of foster homes. It's also less stress for your dog.</p>
<p>There is a $25 fee to help cover our expenses. Since we are a non-profit organization, it is in the form of a tax-deductible donation. In case of hardship we can waive the fee.</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong>What we will do:</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p>One of our volunteers will come to visit and assess your dog so we can best match it to a new home.</p>
<p>We get applications from people who love Samoyeds and want one in their home. We promise your dog will go to the most suitable home we can find.</p>
<p>Your dog will be added to our state web site and to a national listing service. We're going to do everything possible to unite your dog and his/her perfect new home.</p>
<p><strong>To begin the process, please contact us:</strong><br />
<a href="mailto://rescue@samoyedclubwa.org">rescue@SamoyedClubWa.org</a></p>
</blockquote>
