> <span class="lead">K</span>ei and Yuri are government agents, codenamed "Lovely Angels", who nab criminals of all sorts; however, due to the wake of destruction after they're done (but usually not their fault directly), they've gained a nickname: **Dirty Pair**. These are the original anime "babes with big bazookas" -- and I do mean bazookas.

> ### Other Notes
>
> With the worldwide success of the original character design, a complete redesign was also done as **Dirty Pair Flash**, with a younger version of the Lovely Angels, who may or may not be the *same* Kei and Yuri as the original pair. When that version was released to less success, the older pair were given a further 10 episodes, which were released here in the US as **Original Dirty Pair** (not to be confused with the original *episodes*, but closer to the original *designs*).
>
> - "Original" Pair
>   - **Dirty Pair** (26-episode TV)
>   - **DP: Affair of Nolandia** (1-episode OVA)
>   - **DP: Project Eden** (Movie)
>   - **DP: Flight 005 Conspiracy** (Movie)
>   - **Original Dirty Pair** (10-episode OVA)
> - "Younger" Pair
>   - **Dirty Pair Flash** (11-episode OVA)
