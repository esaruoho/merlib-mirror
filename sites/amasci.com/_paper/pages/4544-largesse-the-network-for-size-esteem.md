# Largesse, the Network for Size Esteem

*Source page: `~largesse/index.html`*

### <img src="largesse.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" data-border="0" width="500" height="228" alt="Largesse, the Network for Size Esteem" />

### ***size esteem, n.*** ****\[phrase originated by Richard K. Stimson\]*******\
--feeling acceptance of, respect for, and pride in one's body, whatever its size or shape***

### ****

> **Largesse, the Network for Size Esteem is an international clearinghouse for information on size diversity empowerment. Our mission is to create personal awareness and social change which promotes a positive image, health and equal rights for people of size. Since 1986 we have been helping to link individuals, groups, businesses and professionals in the size rights community with information, support and each other.**

<td width="100%"><p><strong>Susan L. Mason<br />
1955 - 2002</strong><br />
<br />
Our beloved friend and Largesse Media Liaison, Susan Mason, died on Monday, December 2, 2002 from long-term complications of weight-loss surgery. From the early 1980's Susan was a passionate and articulate advocate for size diversity empowerment and an equally passionate foe of fat oppression and discrimination. Through her frequent media appearances, her comedy, art, writing, and websites, Susan brought the concept of size acceptance to an audience of millions around the globe. Her passing is a huge loss to our community.<br />
She will be sorely missed.<br />
Our tribute to her may be viewed <a href="http://www.largesse.net/Remembering/index.html" target="_blank">here</a>.</p></td>
