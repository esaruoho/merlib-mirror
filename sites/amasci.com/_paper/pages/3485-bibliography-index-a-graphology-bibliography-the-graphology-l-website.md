This bibliography does not list documents written in languages that use a non-Latin writing system.

There are some citations of material that I doubt exist. One example is Preyer (1895b). Preyer (1895a) does exist --- I have a copy of the third edition. My copy does not mention any other text by him. In the last few pages of it, are notices for books by Klages and Sommer.

There are numerous instances where the citations are incomplete --- for example, Keller (1931). As I locate more information about the citation, I will add it to this list.

A somewhat more disturbing error, is where the incorrect journal name is given. These are corrected, as I become aware of them. Sometimes the error occurs because my source gives an incorrect journal name. Other times it occurs because an abreviation for the journal is provided, and I choose the wrong journal.

Abreviations are the bane of bibliographers. I have seen the journal *Zeitschrift für Angewandte Psychologie* abreviated as ZAP, Zeit Angewandte, Z Ang Psy and Z Ang Psych. The individual who used ZAP, provided a list of abreviations he used for journals.

On occasion, an incorrect book title is given. Preyer (1895b) is an example of this error. This is because I list the citation as it is in my source.

Sources for this bibliography consist of my doing one of three processes.

- Transcribing every listing in the bibliography of a book or journal article.
- Running PsychLit and similar searches.
- Going through invidiual journals.

I have not included citations that are in the mailing list *Graphodigest TOC* for two reasons:

- Until the end of 2001, they did not include page numbers. I have since decided that listing those citations, despite the omissions would be a good thing.
- My ISP blocks Topica.Com because of the amount of spam that Topica sends out.

This bibliography does not contain citations that use a writing system other than the Latin writing system. When this has been converted to a UNICODE-4 compliant database, I will be adding some Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hebrew, Greek, and Arabic citations.

Work on this bibliography was halted, while I decided what database program to convert it to. I have decided what DB Program to use. I will have to write/convert the CGI scripts from PHP to Python. I do not know how soon that will happen. In the meantime, I will be adding new citations, as I receive them.
