# Sadako Statue at Peace Park

Near the University of Washington is a "vest-pocket" park called Peace Park. A bronze statue of Sadako holding a paper crane aloft is the centerpiece of the park. At the base of the statue is a plaque with the following inscription:

#### **"Sadako Sasaki** / Peace Child / She gave us the paper crane / to symbolize our yearning for peace in the world.

#### A gift to the people of Seattle / from Fratelli's Ice Cream.

#### Daryl Smith - Sculptor 1990 "
