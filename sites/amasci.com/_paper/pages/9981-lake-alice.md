    TGFOD!  Thank God for opening day and those scrappy 9" hatchery plants!
    Lake Alice finally produced.  Not what it was like 3 yrs. ago with big
    holdovers in Feb. and Mar.  I kept hopin, but never happened again.
    While we were there on Sun. I was the hot rod with my trusty #14 olive,
    wooly-bugger, bead-headed and grizzly hackled.  I think they take it as
    a midge cluster, griffith's Gnat type.  Slow fished with little action
    this is my lake standby, but I can't get too big headed catching
    recently dumped fish.  I caught about 40!   Jere Crosby
