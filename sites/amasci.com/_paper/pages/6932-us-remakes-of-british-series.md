### **US Remakes Of British Series**

Dateline: October 25, 2000\
Updated: March 4, 2002

For decades, American producers have looked across the pond for inspiration for new shows. Here is an ongoing, comprehensive guide to every American show that was directly based on a British series. Mistakes? Omissions? Post them in our [forum](http://forums.about.com/ab-britishtv/messages?lgnF=y&msg=320.1). For purposes of brevity (not to mention sanity) I'm not including pilots that never became series.
