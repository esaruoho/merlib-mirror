## where we two first parted

------------------------------------------------------------------------

            there is a pool of love
            so vast and deep
            that shimmers beyond
            our pale imaginings
            where you and I cavort and play
            in its warmth and protection
            and swim in a velvet sea
            under its silken moon
            that shines and glimmers
            upon the tip of each dancing ripple
            in a blissful caress
            that reaches past understanding
            and into a long-forgotten place
            of serenity and joyful contentedness
            where words and sounds and thoughts coalesce
            and fade in the unfathomable depths
            of a union so profound and all-encompassing
            that a billion billion kindred souls
            like our own
            release and dissolve
            into the one formless dominion
            from whence they came,
            safe in surrender, and
            rapturous in the truth
            that forever nurtures us
            in its loving embrace.  

                                            James Romkey
