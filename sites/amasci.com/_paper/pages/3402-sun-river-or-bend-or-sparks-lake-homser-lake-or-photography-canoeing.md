<td><img src="sunriver01tn.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="83" height="120" alt="Sparks Lake" />
<p>August, 2002. The volcanic spires near Sun River (just south of Bend), OR provide a magnificent setting for quietwater canoeing and kayaking. We paddled Sparks and Hosmer lakes, Crane Prairie Reservior, plus a class 1 river trip down the Deschutes. We scouted, but didn't paddle, Big Lake, with it's famed Mt. Washington backdrop, and Scott Lake, with a fine view of the Sisters.</p>
<p>We didn't research the trip. Instead, we used a stand-by strategy: stop at the Sun River library, where we thumbed through Ray Atkeson pictorals and work by Steve Terrill. On the water, kayakers and canoeist told us a few of their favorite local paddles.</p>
<p>In a week, we couldn't do them all. We missed the lakes of the Newberry Crater, and Waldo - a lake with water so clear you can drop a Secchi disk a hundred feet and still see its black-and-white pattern.</p>
<p>I'm ready for a return trip.</p>
