<td width="100%"><h1 id="guitar-help">GUITAR HELP</h1>
<p>We don't have a monopoly on guitar knowledge but we have answered a lot of questions in the past and have kept many of the answers here.</p>
<p>Frequently Asked Questions</p>
<p><a href="http://www.musiccareers.net/faqs.php"><strong>Click here to search the FAQ</strong></a><br />
Please realize that this FAQ is continually updated and will probably never be complete. These pages serve as a reference for the many questions we have been asked repeatedly over the years. If you have a suggestion for the FAQ, please <a href="../contact.html">contact us</a> here at Guitar Noise. Thank you and we hope this FAQ helps you!</p>
<p><strong><a href="expert.html">Question by Topic</a></strong><br />
If you've searched high and low and still haven't found the answer to your question, it might be time to seek professional help. The <a href="http://www.guitarnoise.com/forum/index.html">Guitar Noise Forum</a> is a lively community board with vibrant and helpful members. Quite likely your question has been answered here before, but no one will bite your head off if you can't find the answer and ask again.</p>

<strong><a href="faqs/index.html">Using this site</a></strong><br />
This page contains replies to some of the common questions people have asked about our site over the years. If you have a question about the site check here first for the answer.</p></td>
