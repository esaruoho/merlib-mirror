# Evening Prayer before 1662.

*Source page: `~lhowell/bcp1662/notes/development/evening.html`*

## Evening Prayer before 1662.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**1549**

Begin with the [Lord's Prayer](../../daily/evening.html#OurFather).

Continue from ["O God, make speed..."](../../daily/evening.html#OGodMake); substituting "me" for "us".

Continue from ["Glory be to the Father..."](../../daily/evening.html#Gloria) to ["Praise ye the lord"](../../daily/evening.html#PraiseYe); from Easter through Trinity Sunday only, add *"Alleluia"*.

Insert the *[Venite, Exultemus Domine](../../daily/morning.html#Venite)*.

Continue from the [Psalms of the Day](../../daily/evening.html#Psalms) and go through the *[Magnificat](../../daily/evening.html#Magnificat)*.

Continue from the [Second Lesson](../../daily/evening.html#Lesson2) through *[Nunc Dimittis](../../daily/evening.html#NuncDimittis)*.

Insert "Lord have mercy on us. *Christ have mercy on us.* Lord have mercy on us."

Recite the [Apostle's Creed](../../daily/evening.html#ApostlesCreed).

Continue from the [Lord's Prayer](../../daily/evening.html#OurFather2) through *["And take not thy holy Spirit from us."](../../daily/evening.html#AndTake)*.

Insert "The Lord be with you. *And with thy spirit.* Let us pray."

Continue from the [Collect of the Day](../../daily/evening.html#CollectDay) through the [Third Collect](../../daily/evening.html#Collect3).

**1552**

Open at [Evening Prayer](../../daily/evening.html) and continue to ["Praise ye the lord"](../../daily/evening.html#PraiseYe).

Continue with the [Psalms of the Day](../../daily/evening.html#Psalms) and go through *[Nunc Dimittis](../../daily/evening.html#NuncDimittis)* or *[Deus Misereatur](../../daily/evening.html#DeusMisereatur)*.

Insert "Lord have mercy on us. *Christ have mercy on us.* Lord have mercy on us."

Continue from the [Apostle's Creed](../../daily/evening.html#ApostlesCreed) through the [Third Collect](../../daily/evening.html#Collect3).

**1559 and 1604**

Open at [Evening Prayer](../../daily/evening.html) and continue to ["Praise ye the lord"](../../daily/evening.html#PraiseYe). In 1604, add "*The Lord's name be praised.*"

Continue from the [Psalms of the Day](../../daily/evening.html#Psalms) through [O Lord, shew thy mercy...*And take not thy holy Spirit from us.*](../../daily/evening.html#OLordShew) In 1559, substitute "Queen" for "King".

Continue from the [Collect of the Day](../../daily/evening.html#CollectDay) through the [Third Collect](../../daily/evening.html#Collect3).
