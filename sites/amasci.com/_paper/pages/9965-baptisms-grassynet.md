We baptized our children at [Blessed Sacrament Church](http://www.blsacrament.org/) in Madison on 30 DEC 2001. Richard attended the school at Blessed Sacrament from 1st grade through 8th grade. We were able to gather afterwards at the Parish Center for a small luncheon and even watch the Packers beat the Vikings.

Sarah and Justin Vance, Richard's sister and her husband, are the godparents to Maddy. Jan Braun and Denny Merlin, Lisa's siblings, are godparents to Brock.
