# Ert's personal page

This is one of those homepages that looks like a fairly randomly-arranged set of factoids, but is really just an opportunity for me to use links to all the fun little places on the Web I want to visit with some frequency.

I'm a feminist environmentalist Canadian. The former means (among other things) I believe in equal pay for equal work. The middle means (among other things) that I believe we as a species have royally screwed things up, and in particular we as North Americans are greedy beyond compare. The latter means (among other things) that if I must use a car, I drive with my lights on rain or shine. Je parle un peu français.

I've recently completed a couple of decades wandering through what might be called "*schooling*": I finished an M.S. in [Software Engineering](http://rbse.jsc.nasa.gov) at [The University of Houston at Clear Lake](http://www.cl.uh.edu). I completed a bachelor's degree in [Biology](http://web.mit.edu/afs/athena.mit.edu/org/b/biology/www/) with a minor in [Economics](http://web.mit.edu/afs/athena.mit.edu/org/e/economics/www/) at [The Massachusetts Institute of Technology](http://web.mit.edu:8001).

[Zephyr](Z_floor.gif) and I live at [Versailles](http://www.dredge.ca/versailles), a co-op in Arlington, MA, USA.

I like to DJ, and have spent a lot of time involved with such radio stations as [WMBR-FM](http://wmbr.mit.edu) and [KTRU](http://www.rice.edu/projects/ktru/).

I grew up in [Burlington](http://wchat.on.ca/cob/index.htm), [Ontario](http://www.city.net/countries/canada/ontario/), about half an hour west of [Toronto](http://www.city.net/countries/canada/ontario/toronto/). After leaving there, I spent a summer living at [the University of Waterloo](http://www.uwaterloo.ca). Then, when I was an undergraduate at [MIT](http://web.mit.edu) I lived at [pika](http://www.mit.edu:8001/activities/pika/home.html) in [Cambridge](http://www.ci.cambridge.ma.us/), for five years. Then I moved to [Somerville, MA](http://www.yahoo.com/Regional/U_S__States/Massachusetts/Cities/Somerville/), and lived on a street named after my boss for two years. Then I just went wandering.

While on the west coast I lived in the Outer Richmond *district* of San Francisco, surrounded by parks and beaches, much to my delight. This is not to be confused with the *city* of Richmond in the San Francisco Bay Area, yet people confuse them, anyway. I tell you they're not to be confused, but go ahead if you dare. You won't find your life enriched, believe you me.

In Texas I lived in Houston and Seabrook (right next to [NASA](http://www.jsc.nasa.gov)), which is a whole essay onto itself. When I first moved there we averaged around 10 wrong numbers a day. Then it settled down to 8 a week, and that was supposed to make us happy. People down there still mainly use hair length to figure out your sex, which works great for transgendered people who can simply cut or grow their hair to cross the line. The local residents put their recycling in the trash and their trash wherever they please, then pave over it all so they can operate more cars simultaneousy. While I was there, George W Bush as governor demonstrated his strength and prowess as a great leader, a suitable president. In theory.

When I was in high school, I attended a summer program called [Shad Valley](http://www.shads.org), which did a fair amount towards having me end up where I am now. Afterwards I spent three years on the Board of Directors for Shad's organizing company, the Canadian Centre for Creative Technology, now called [Shad International](http://www.naviss.com/shad/) .

I'm an [Atheist](http://www.infidels.org/news/atheism/):

- An atheist doesn't have to be someone who thinks \[they have\] a proof that there can't be a god. \[They only have\] to be someone who believes that the evidence on the God question is at a similar level to the evidence on the werewolf question.

  - \- John McCarthy

This doesn't mean that I don't believe in ritual or spirituality. I happen to believe in Gaia, or my personal vision of it, anyway. I believe that humans grope around trying to deal with the 99%+ of the universe they don't understand and come up with all sorts of things. But I don't advocate the interpretation of scriptures as The Word of Nature. [*Pluralitas non est ponenda sine neccesitate*](http://www.xnet.com/~blatura/skep_1.html#1.6)

Here's a picture of my [teeth](teeth.gif) taken a while ago. Note the poorly-oriented third molar on the lower right. There was an [expedition to rescue it](fear.gif) and its friends in late '94. It was quite the adventure, not completely unlike the neverending story.

I enjoy music a lot, and try to listen to a variety of types. (Unfortunately, I sometimes go for long times listening only to Alternative (*sic*) and Canadian Indie/Pop.) Here's a loosely-constructed page that tries (and kinda fails) to describe my [musical tastes](music.html).

I'm a big fan of [proportional representation](http://www.mtholyoke.edu/acad/polit/damy/prlib.htm). and majority preference voting. Feeling that you only have two parties to realistically choose from when you go to the voting booth is a pretty poor substitute for actual democracy.

I'm a Mac user.

Winter 1995 I managed to get myself hooked on snowboarding. So then I moved to Texas for three years. There ya go. I'm also pretty hyped on [picons](http://www.cs.indiana.edu/picons/ftp/index.html), and tend to make them for anyone I correspond with. ("All the better to see you with, my dear...")

I use bizarre expressions involving my name in my email sometimes. If you can identify the source of [all of them](inert-gases.html) I'll visit and make you masala dosas.
