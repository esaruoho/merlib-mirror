<td><p>Markov uses the exact same process as Travesty but applies it to words rather than to characters. "Word" here means a sequence of non-blank characters and includes punctuation as well as letters. The name comes from the statistical process used here and in Travesty, a Markov chain.</p>
<p>See the explanation of Travesty <a href="explaintravesty.html">here</a> for more details.</p>
<p>Markov can be used to scramble a source text before using it in another process such as diastic reading.</p>
<p>Use the back button or click <a href="markov.html">here</a> to go to the Markov Generator page.</p></td>
