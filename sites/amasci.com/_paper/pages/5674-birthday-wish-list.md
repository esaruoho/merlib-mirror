# Birthday Wish List

*Source page: `~omaha/Birthday_Wish_List.htm`*

Raeney Birthday

Wish List

\
 

- Books: favorites are Clifford the Big Red Dog, Winnie the Pooh, and the Mercer Meyer Critter series. Contact me to make sure you don't duplicate a book we already have.
- Clothes: not for now, but for when she gets bigger. We're looking for 3Ts now.
- Music: Raeney loves music, and we don't have very much. Current soundtracks are the Lion King, My Neighbor Totoro, and Alladin, as well as some fannish works (Heather Alexander, Frank Hayes, Golden Bough, and miscelaneous folk music.)
- Video tapes: All we have right now are both Toy Storys, Alladin, the Little Mermaid 2, My Neighbor Totoro, Kiki's Delivery Service, and Tarzan. She's a big fan of Bambi and the Lion King right now. But other videos, especially non-Disney, are certainly welcome and encouraged.
- Toys: Raeney has a lot of toys, but she's getting so old for many of them, that we want to start adding to her collection and taking away ones she doesn't play with anymore. She's very advanced for her age, and is actually playing with games that are meant for three and four year olds sometimes, so don't be afraid to get something challenging. She loves to draw and she loves to build, so maybe something around those lines too.
- Miscellaneous: We have more than enough stuffed toys right now, but she does need more small dolls and other small playthings (like little animals, for example; things that fit in the hand pretty well.) Although she's only just reaching two, her desire to stick things in her mouth has been gone for months, so I'm not too worried about small things. Be creative! :)
