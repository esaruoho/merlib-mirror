<img src="images/taxdaddy.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **TaxDaddy 0.9.1**\
\
TaxDaddy is intended as an easy to use database for keeping taxonomic informationat your fingertips. The program is configured as a flat-file database with manyuser defined fields for taxonomic names, ranges, distributions, synonymies,descriptions, you name it. If you have enough disk space, you can evenincorporate scanned images or other pictures into your data records. Depending onyour configuration and the data you put into the program, TaxDaddy can do complexsearches, such as answering the question: how many brachiopod genera (and theirsynonyms) belonging to family Choenitidae are known from the Devonian of China? Something difficult for a 3 x 5 card to do.\
Besides keeping track of your taxonomic data, TaxDaddy can also calculate variousdiversity metrics. The program automatically calculates taxonomic diversity,originations, and extinctions as well as their proportions and standard errors.It also calculates and plots survivorship curves.\

- **License**: Freeware
- **Author/Publisher**: Christopher A. McRoberts
- **Modification Date**: November 20, 1995
- **Requirements**: 680x0 or PPC - Hypercard Player
