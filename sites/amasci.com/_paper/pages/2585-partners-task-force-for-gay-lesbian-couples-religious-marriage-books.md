<td data-bgcolor="#FAFAFA"><br />
<strong>Ceremonies of the Heart: Celebrating lesbian unions edited</strong> by Becky Butler (1990), $14.95 +$2.24 p&amp;h, Seal Press, 3131 Western Ave., #410, Seattle, WA 98121-1028; 206-283-7844. Couples describe 27 ceremonies and their relationships.
<p><strong>Daring to Speak Love’s Name: A gay &amp; lesbian prayer book</strong> by Dr. Elizabeth Stuart (1992), $10, Hamish Hamilton - London; distributed by Penguin Books, 375 Hudson St., New York, NY 10014. Prayers for celebrating relationships, coming out; partings, healing liturgies and deaths.</p>
<p><strong>The Essential Guide to Lesbian and Gay Weddings</strong> by Tess Ayers and Paul Brown (1994), $17, Harper San Francisco (HarperCollins, 10 East 53 St., New York, NY 10022). From Affidavit of domestic partnership to Vichud (seclusion tradition); the minutia of ceremonial wedding events and how to plan for them.</p>
<p><strong>In God’s Image: Christian Witness to the Need for Gay/Lesbian Equality in the Eyes of the Church</strong> by Robert Warren Cromey (1991), $9.95, Alamo Square Press, Box 14543, San Francisco, CA 94114. Episcopal priest agues for same-sex marriage.</p>
<p><strong>In Praise of Gay Marriage</strong> (1986), biblical tracts #4-7, 10 cents ea., $1.50 min. order +$1.50 p&amp;h, Puget Sound Publishing Group, 3401 Rucker, East Suite, Everett, WA 98201, 206-339-8338. Contends that Jesus and John were married.</p>
<p><strong>Lesbian &amp; Gay Marriage: Private commitments, public ceremonies</strong> edited by Suzanne Sherman (1992), $16.95, Temple University Press, Philadelphia PA 19122. Essays on ceremonial marriage and 24 interviews focused on why the couples married or chose not to. Includes an article from <strong>Partners Task Force</strong> directors.</p>
<p><strong>Living in Sin?</strong> by John Shelby Spong (1988), $15.95, Harper &amp; Row, Icehouse One, #401, 151 Union St., San Francisco, CA 94111. Includes arguments for blessing same-sex relationships.</p>
<p><strong>Recognizing Ourselves: Ceremonies of Lesbian and Gay Commitment</strong> by Ellen Lewin (1998) $17/paper $30/hard, Columbia University Press, New York. Includes analysis of the symbolic strategies same-sex couples use in devising their ceremonies; lengthy excerpts from many different kinds of ceremonies, and extensive narrative material from couples.</p>
<p><strong>Report on the Task Force on Changing Patterns of Sexuality and Family Life</strong>, Send SASE with $.65 postage to Episcopal Diocese of Newark, 24 Rector St., Newark, NJ 07102. Endorses homosexual marriage.</p>
<p><strong>Resource Guide for Same-Sex Couples</strong> (1988); <strong>In the Presence of God … A Quaker Marriage</strong> (1988), $5.50 for both, Family Relations Committee, Friends Center, 1515 Cherry St., Philadelphia, PA 19102. Helps couples and meetings prepare for the vows of marriage.</p>
<p><strong>Same-Sex Unions in Premodern Europe</strong> by John Boswell (1994), $25, Villard Books (Random House), 201 E. 50th St., New York, NY 10022; 800-733-3000. Newly rediscovered liturgies from early Catholic and Orthodox, used throughout Christendom into modern times; contextualized in a detailed, academic treatment.</p>
<p><strong>The Two of Us</strong> by Larry J. Uhrig, OUT OF PRINT, Alyson Publications, Boston, MA. MCC pastor’s approach to same-sex marriage.</p></td>
