<span class="arialblacktitle">HOT-L Subscription Information</span></p>
<p>HariOnTour has a fair list load, currently averaging about ten messages a day - more since Hari's passing.</p>
<div>
<p>SUBSCRIBING TO HARIONTOUR</p>
</div>
<div>
<p>You can join the list via email simply by sending an email to:</p>
</div>
<blockquote>
<div>
<a href="mailto:majordomo@lists.spunge.org"><span class="orangearialbold">majordomo@lists.spunge.org</span></a>
</div>
</blockquote>
<div>
<p>In the *body* of the message, type:</p>
</div>
<blockquote>
<div class="arialblackboldsmall">
subscribe hariontour user@domain.com
</div>
</blockquote>
<div>
<p>UNSUBSCRIBING FROM HARIONTOUR</p>
</div>
<div>
<p>Subscribers can unsubscribe from the list simply by sending an email to:</p>
</div>
<blockquote>
<div>
<p><span class="orangearialbold"><a href="mailto:majordomo@lists.spunge.org" class="orangearialbold">majordomo@lists.spunge.org</a></span></p>
</div>
</blockquote>
<div>
<p>In the *body* of the message, type:</p>
</div>
<blockquote>
<div class="arialblacknormalsmall">
<span class="arialblackboldsmall">unsubscribe hariontour user@domain.com </span>
</div>
</blockquote>
<div>
<p>or by clicking the link at the bottom of each list message.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p>POSTING MESSAGES TO HARIONTOUR</p>
</div>
<div>
<p>To post a message to the list, subscribers can send email to:</p>
</div>
<div>
<p>    <a href="mailto:majordomo@lists.spunge.org" class="orangearialbold">hariontour@lists.spunge.org</a></p>
</div>
<div>
<p>DIGEST OPTION</p>
</div>
<p>An alternative to the regular list form is a digest, which sends out an average of one, maybe two posts a day, depending on the number of individual messages, combining several messages into one.</p>
<p>TO SUBSCRIBE TO THE DIGEST VERSION</p>
<p>Send an email to:</p>
<blockquote>
<p><a href="mailto:majordomo@lists.spunge.org" class="orangearialbold">majordomo@lists.spunge.org</a></p>
</blockquote>
<p>In the *body* of the message, type:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>subscribe hariontour-d user@domain.com</p>
</blockquote>
<p>The -d tells majordomo that you want the digest version of the list. </p>
<p>TO UNSUBSCRIBE FROM THE DIGEST</p>
<p>Send an email to:</p>
<blockquote>
<p><a href="mailto:majordomo@lists.spunge.org" class="orangearialbold">majordomo@lists.spunge.org</a></p>
</blockquote>
<p>In the *body* of the message, type:</p>
<blockquote>
<p>unsubscribe hariontour-d user@domain.com</p>
</blockquote>
<p>or click the link at the bottom of each digest message.</p>
<p>If you encounter a problem, e-mail <a href="mailto:aya@bmmak.usr.pu.ru%20?subject=Hariontour-L" class="orangearialbold">Aya Makarova</a> or the list <a href="mailto:cathy@eskimo.com" class="orangearialbold">technical advisor</a> and clearly explain the problem. Please use this option only as a last resort after you have followed the above instructions.</p>
<p>Once you are subscribed, you will receive information about the list. By subscribing to the list, you agree to <span class="arialblackboldsmall">READ</span> and <span class="arialblackboldsmall">SAVE</span> the instructions you are given.</p>
