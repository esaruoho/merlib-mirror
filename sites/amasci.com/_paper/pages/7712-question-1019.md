### Question 10.19

How can I list all of the pre`#define`d identifiers?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

There's no standard way, although it is a common need. If the compiler documentation is unhelpful, the most expedient way is probably to extract printable strings from the compiler or preprocessor executable with something like the Unix `strings` utility. Beware that many traditional system-specific pre`#define`d identifiers (e.g. \`\``unix`'') are non-Standard (because they clash with the user's namespace) and are being removed or renamed.
