# Ert Dredge's listening habits

Well, as you might have guessed from my involvement with a [Canadian music radio show](kids/), I l isten to a lot of Canadian bands, mainly indie and pop. [Here](kids/plist.12.26.94.html) is the list Sanjay and I made last year of our favourite albums of the first half of the 90s.

Before that for two years I had a progressive/pop dance show. I played a lot of stuff like [Depeche Mode](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Depeche_Mode/), [Pet Shop Boys](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Pet_Shop_Boys/), [New Order](http://www.niagara.edu/New_Order/discography.html), [The Cult](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Cult__The/), [Kon Kan](http://ux5.cso.uiuc.edu/~librizzi/kon_kan.html), [U2](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/U2/), Visage, [Front 242](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Front_242/), [Nine Inch Nails](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Nine_Inch_Nails/), [OMD](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/OMD/), [Erasure](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Erasure/), etc.

I was program director at [WMBR](http://wmbr.mit.edu) for a year, and during that time I got into a lot of different stuff. I like jazz and classical, although I can't claim to be very well versed in those styles. I enjoy ballroom dancing, and the music that goes along with it. I also like a lot of types of techno (very fast through to ambient). Folk music can be nice, and rap and hip-hop when they're done well.

[Talking Heads](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Talking_Heads/). [Dead Can Dance](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Dead_Can_Dance/). [Sting](http://www.galcit.caltech.edu/~joe/sting/sting.html). [Kitaro](http://colossus.net/comeback/kitaro/index.html). [Tribe](http://www-ns.rutgers.edu/fun/tribe/tour.html). [K.D. Lang](http://www.infohouse.com/obviousgossip/). [Sarah McLachlan](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/McLachlan__Sarah/). [Crash Test Dummies](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Crash_Test_Dummies/). [Stigmata](http://american.recordings.com/WWWoM/cgi-bin/ubl?card+6502). [Yes](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Yes/). [The Lowest of the Low](http://www.oswego.edu/~mwillis/lowestlow.html). [Toto](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/TOTO/). Velour. The Mountain Goats. The Orb. Ani Difranco. Soul Coughing. Ride. Combustible Edison. Ali Akbar Khan. The Disposable Heroes of Hiphoprisy. And a lot more...

I like 1950s-1970s rock music, but since I wasn't very aware of it (or not alive) at the time I fear I'm only familiar with the stuff that is fed to me by the commercial stations that select songs based on baby-boomer marketing potential. I don't spend enough time researching the period to be very aware of the lesser-known artists.

I've been getting increasingly fed up with the North American popular music industry, although I must confess a fondness for 1980s pop that I listened to at the time and now forms a scaffolding for my memories of the period.
