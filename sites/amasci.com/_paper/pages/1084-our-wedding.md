Our wedding was really quite an adventure! We had originally intended to have a fairly large, traditional wedding, but we never could quite get all our friends and family coordinated on a date and some other details, so we decided to get married on our honeymoon instead. We first were just going to go on a cruise, but since we were also getting married, we decided to spend the extra money we had from the wedding budget and spend a few days at Walt Disney World before the cruise started. We also decided to take our two best friends, Chris and Diana, with us, since they probably would have killed us if they hadn't been there to watch us get hitched.
