<td data-valign="top" width="580"><h1 id="the-waiting">The Waiting</h1>
by Harper apprentice <a href="../bartal.html">Bartal</a>
<hr />
Bartal effects an anxious expression, looking up toward the sky. He strums his gitar lightly, wistful chords setting a mood as he begins to sing.
<p><em>"I wish I could say<br />
That I stood on the sands,<br />
But instead I stood far away.<br />
I hear Ista is next to Search,<br />
With an egg-heavy queen;<br />
Maybe tomorrow will be my lucky day."</em></p>
<p>He finishes the chorus and then his singing voice turns into a story-telling voice.</p>
<p><em>"When I was twelve I was close<br />
To riding at Benden.<br />
Lounging on the ramp when a dragon did land.<br />
With a stare and a whuffle<br />
It sniffed me,<br />
And went on. (I just know that was planned)"</em></p>
<p>Bartal looks among the audience at the youngsters, some nodding in agreement despite themselves.</p>
<p><em>"Cousin Jenni travels<br />
From Weyr to Weyr.<br />
If she isn't Searched she doesn't stay long.<br />
She has no craft,<br />
Just her wandering;<br />
I still think she's going about it all wrong."</em></p>
<p>Bartal strums the gitar a little, but not too much. He sings the chorus with appropriate longing.</p>
<p><em>"I wish I could say<br />
That I stood on the sands,<br />
But instead I stood far away.<br />
I hear the Reaches is next to Search,<br />
With an egg-heavy queen;<br />
Maybe tomorrow will be my lucky day."</em></p>
<p>He continues, speaking his tale to the listeners.</p>
<p><em>"My friend Wally,<br />
He "talks" to the dragons;<br />
Calls them "wondrous" and "incredible" and stuff.<br />
And when he spends too many hours<br />
Hugging the dragons,<br />
They usually lift up and fly off in a huff."</em></p>
<p>Bartal pauses to stick a thumb into his chest, before making a light chord.</p>
<p><em>"Me, I work in the orchard.<br />
I'm accessible (yet not pushy)<br />
And I keep an eye on the sky,<br />
Keep track of the seasons --<br />
And hatching cycles --<br />
Always hoping someday a dragon will drop by."</em></p>
<p>His voice chokes a bit, as if he's about to cry. He doesn't, though, but just sings the chorus one more time.</p>
<p><em>"I wish I could say<br />
That I stood on the sands,<br />
But instead I stood far away.<br />
I hear Telgar is next to Search,<br />
With an egg-heavy queen.<br />
Maybe tomorrow will be my lucky day.</em></p>
<p>Bartal finishes by strumming the light chording and looking upwards once more before stilling the strings.</p>
