<p>Maverick genius director Terrence Malick has made only three films from 1973 till the present day; but what brilliant films they are! I bow and scrape in awe to the international man of mystery who's made <a href="badlands/index.html">Badlands</a>, <a href="days/index.html">Days of Heaven</a> and <a href="trl/index.html">The Thin Red Line</a>.</p>
<p>Disclaimer: I have no connection to Malick at all--I don't know who his agent is, whether he has an email account, nor how to contact him. I just like his films. And I want to be just like him when I grow up.</p>
<p>Well, isn't that special?</p>
