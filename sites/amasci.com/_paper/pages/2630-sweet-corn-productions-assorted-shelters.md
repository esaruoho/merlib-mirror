<td colspan="2"><strong>Assorted Shelters</strong> grew out of five months of theater workshops developed by Demian as part of his doctoral thesis. Designed at first to be a theatrical study of violence, the intensity of the improvisations proved to be too unnerving for the performers. After a change of participants, the tone and over-all theme were transformed into a dream-like investigation of sexism. The play then took on a humorous (though still dark) tone, presenting a series of abstract and satirical scenes illuminating the tyranny of sex-role stereotypes. Completing the playful survey of social mores, an ending dance/nightmare incorporated elements from each scene.</td>
