<td data-valign="top"><p><strong>dog haiku</strong></p>
<p><em>so you want to own a dog?  this pretty much depicts life in the hwang house. <br />
[author(s) unknown]</em><br />
<u><strong><br />
</strong></u><span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Today I sniffed<br />
Many dog butts - I celebrate</span></p>
<br />
By kissing your face.
<br />
<br />
My human is home!<br />
I am so ecstatic I have<br />
Made a puddle.<br />
<br />
How do I love thee?<br />
The ways are numberless as<br />
My hairs on the rug.<br />
<br />
How do I love thee?<br />
The ways are numberless as<br />
The fleas in my fur.<br />
<br />
<span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">I have lots of fur.<br />
Twice a year, I shed the stuff<br />
All over your floor. </span>
<br />
<br />
I am your best friend,<br />
Now, always, and especially<br />
When you are eating.<br />
<br />
I lie belly-up<br />
In the sunshine, happier than<br />
You ever will be.<br />
<br />
I sound the alarm!<br />
Paperboy -- come to kill us all --<br />
Look! Look! Look! Look! Look!<br />
<br />
I sound the alarm!<br />
Garbage man -- come to kill us all --<br />
Look! Look! Look! Look! Look!<br />
<br />
I sound the alarm!
<br />
Mailman Fiend -- come to kill us all -
<p>-<br />
Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!</p>
<br />
<br />
I sound the alarm!
<br />
Meter reader -- come to kill us all --
<br />
Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!
<br />
<br />
I sound the alarm!
<br />
Neighbor's cat -- come to kill all --
<br />
Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Look!
<br />
<br />
Warning! Get up, Man!<br />
I'm sure I heard a robber.<br />
Or maybe nothing.
<br />
<br />
I lift my leg and<br />
Whiz on each bush. Hello, Spot --<br />
Sniff this and weep.<br />
<br />
Let's go for a "walk":<br />
You stand there freezing your butt<br />
While I sniff some bush.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span><br />
<br />
I Hate my choke chain --<br />
Look, world, they strangle me!<br />
Ack Ack Ack Ack Ack!<br />
<br />
<span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt; mso-bidi-font-family: Arial">I hate my choke chain -<br />
Look, world, look! They strangle me!<br />
My masters are cruel.</span>
<br />
<br />
Sleeping here, my chin<br />
On your foot -- no greater bliss --<br />
Well, maybe catching cats.<br />
<br />
Look in my eyes and<br />
Deny it. No human could<br />
Love you as much I do.<br />
<br />
The cat is not all<br />
Bad -- she fills the litter box<br />
With Tootsie Rolls.<br />
<br />
Dig under fence -- why?<br />
Because it's there. Because it's<br />
There. Because it's there.<br />
<br />
Dig under fence-why?<br />
I dig because it is there<br />
And I must be free.<br />
<br />
You may call them fleas,<br />
But they are far more -- I call<br />
Them a vocation.<br />
<br />
<span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">My owners' mood is</span>
<br />
Romantic - I lie near their
<br />
Feet.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>I blow a big one.
<br />
<br />
I chase the quick fox.<br />
Come back, you lousy varmint!<br />
Oh, it's just my tail.<br />
<br />
<span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Oh, the nifty smell.<br />
I could chew this all day long.<br />
Huh? What are "good shoes"? </span>
<br />
<br />
Behold! my cold nose.<br />
I think I'll shove it right here.<br />
Gosh, you sure jump high.
<br />
<br />
Why do you shake hands?<br />
If you want to greet someone,<br />
'Gotta smell their butt.
<br />
<br />
Eat, sleep, play, poop, drool.<br />
A dog's work is never done.<br />
Eat, sleep, play, poop, drool.
<br />
<br />
That steak sure smells good.<br />
I only want a little.<br />
Look! I've learned to "Sit!"

That I had to urinate.<br />
Now, I want some more.
