# WMBR's "Kids in the Hall" Top 10 CDs of the first half of the 90s

*Source page: `~ert/kids/plist.12.26.94.html`*

WMBR's "Kids in the Hall" Top 10 CDs of the first half of the 90s

# ![](http://www.mit.edu:8001/activities/wmbr/note.GIF) Top 10 CDs for 1990-1994

------------------------------------------------------------------------

On December 26, 1994, we played selections from our favourite 10 CDs of the 1990s (one of them was only released as a demo tape.) Here are the releases, and the selections we played:

1.  **[Lowest of the Low](http://www.oswego.edu/~mwillis/lowestlow.html)**\
    *Shakespeare My Butt...:*
    - Salesmen, cheats and liars
    - Bleed a little while tonight
    - Subversives
2.  **[Sarah McLachlan](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/McLachlan__Sarah/)**\
    *Fumbling Towards Extasy:*
    - Possession
    - Mary
    - Ice cream
3.  **[Spirit of the West](http://gpu.srv.ualberta.ca/~tcampbel/spirit.html)**\
    *Save This House:*
    - Home for a rest
    - The Joneses
    - The old sod
4.  **[Blue Rodeo](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Blue_Rodeo/)**\
    *Lost Together:*
    - Already gone
    - Fools like you
5.  **[Loreena McKennitt](http://www.yahoo.com/Society_and_Culture/Cultures/Celtic/Music/Artists/McKennitt__Loreena/)**\
    *The Visit:*
    - The lady of Shalott
6.  **[Barenaked Ladies](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Barenaked_Ladies/)**\
    *Gordon:*
    - Enid
    - If I had \$1,000,000
7.  **[Moxy Fruvous](http://wwwcsc.cornell-iowa.edu/~jcragun/fruvous.html)**\
    *Moxy Fruvous:*
    - King of Spain
    - Green eggs and ham
    - My baby loves a bunch of authors
8.  **[The Watchmen](http://www.magic.mb.ca:80/~watchmen/)**\
    *McLarenFurnaceRoom:*
    - Crazy days
    - Soul stealer
9.  **Meryn Cadell**\
    *Angel Food for Thought:*
    - Flight Attendant
    - I been redeemed
    - Spelling bee
    - Job application
    - The sweater
10. **[Crash Test Dummies](http://www.yahoo.com/Entertainment/Music/Artists/Crash_Test_Dummies/)**\
    *The Ghosts That Haunt Me:*
    - Comin' back soon
    - Superman's song
