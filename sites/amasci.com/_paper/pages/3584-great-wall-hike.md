# GREAT WALL HIKE &

*Source page: `~interlak/great_wall_hike.html`*

**GREAT WALL HIKE & TOUR**

Day 1 Depart Your country.

2 Arrive Beijing – Met by Guide and transfer to your central area hotel.

3 Beijing – Guide will take you to Tiananmen Square, Forbidden City\
and the Summer Palace. BLD

4 Beijing/Qin Huangdao by train. Guide will transfer you to your hotel.\
Afternoon visit to the Greast Wall Museum, Old Dragon’s Head and\
Shanhai Guan Pass. BLD.

5 Hike the Great Wall from Shanhai Guan Pass to Jiaoshan. Late afternoon\
drive to Panjiakou Lake. Transfer to a local hotel. BLD

6 Boat Excursion on Panjiakou Lake. Drive to Luewenyu section of the\
Great Wall and hike this section of the Wall. Drive to Zunhua and\
transfer to a local hotel. BLD

7 Visit Qing Eastern Tombs. Drive to Huangya Guan and hike this section\
of the Great Wall. Transfer to local hotel. BLD

8 Drive to Miyun Lake. Hike the Great Wall from Simatai to Jinshanling.\
Drive to Chengde. BLD

9 Chengde – Visit the Imperial Garden and Temples. Hike to Hammer\
Rock. Back to the hotel in the evening. BLD

10 Chengde/Beijing by train. On arrival in Beijing you will be transferred\
to a central area hotel. BLD

11 Beijing/Home – Guide will transfer you to the airport for your flight\
home. B. only.

Included: Transfers, hotels, all meals, English speaking guide, local\
transportation and sightseeing as listed.

Not included: Tips to guide, airport departure tax of \$11., visa (we have\
the forms and instructions to obtain the visa) Hiking equipment (good\
hiking boots recommended and day pack)
