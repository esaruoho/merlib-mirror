WEIRD SENSORS<br />
W. Beaty 2022</h1>
<ol>
<li><a href="http://amasci.com/freenrg/timeflo1.html">100MHz crystal-oscillator pair,</a>, detect the freq-difference "beat frequency." It senses ppb of time-distortion (place them far apart, perhaps use opto fiber.) Even better, try a pair of rubidium oscillators, for 10^6 times better sensitivity than just ppb. See <a href="http://amasci.com/weird/xtal10m.txt">Vectron NMR standard</a> xtal-oven 10MHz.</li>
<li><a href="http://amasci.com/freenrg/mie/h2odet.html">Shortwave laser, sent through "psi-charged" super-Q di water</a> supposedly gives Mie-scattering notches in the angular-spectrum. Water is detectably changed after "hands on" treatment by a Practitioner. But can we see these changes occur in real time? So, build a meter-long array of ?32? water-cells with lasers and photodiodes, sweep it past the subject’s hands during a healing session.</li>
<li><a href="http://amasci.com/freenrg/bindet.html">W. Smith UFO detector,</a> some nylon fiber (2lb fishing line leader) stretched into nonlinear stress/strain region, biased by a steel spring (detect small changes in fiber’s length.) It's a "torsion detector" suggested by ETI.</li>
<li>PK detector based on 20uC of Ra223 between two GM counters for differential readings?</li>
<li>Detect human-discharge of watch/phone batteries: a micro-battery amp-hour measurement, charge/discharge zinc electrolyte to glassy carbon, detects anomalous loss/gain of "battery charge," in realtime.</li>
<li>Automated stick-plate, slow motorized rotating glass cylinder with spring-mounted leather pad. Measure changing displacement of the pad (supposedly very sensitive to e-fields, DC to RF, perhaps also sensitive to mysterious non-EM as well?</li>
<li>Neon plasma voltage-monitor at sensitive edge (10uA?) of glow discharge regime, to detect reported “streetlight damping” PK effect</li>
<li><a href="https://amasci.com/freenrg/gravpool.txt">Gravity-pattern sensor</a>, light source with tray of fluid</li>
<li><a href="https://amasci.com/freenrg/bark.html">Shannon's "scalar" detectors</a> Barkhausen etc.</li>
<li><a href="http://amasci.com/freenrg/ufoscope.html">Binoculars with PMT audio channel</a>, listen to star-twinkle, listen to car headlights, modulations of distant UAP lights, earthquake lights and BL. (If UAP lights give a 400Hz buzz, that’s USAF standard!)</li>
<li>Thermal camera rigged for air-temperature sensing, for realtime imaging of “cold spot” hauntings.</li>
<li>Biosensor to detect PSI and human fields, via a <a href="http://amasci.com/freenrg/potato.html">heavily-shielded potato</a></li>
<li>Scanning drum, detects b-field, e-field, and gas ultrasonic absorption, at scale of 1 square meter.</li>
<li>Plasma PK visualizer-plate. Large glass meter-square flat plasma-cell, full of low-pressure nobel gas mixture, held against a metal plate excited by a Tesla coil. Rather than a uniform glow, instead it produces crawling unstable plasma-fingers, looking like ferrofluid-fingers, or animated tiger-stripes or leopard-spots. It's something like a gigantic "Flicker-flame(tm)" neon bulb. This was an art-installation at the Exploratorium science museum. But can human intention cause changes in shapes or behaviors of these nonlinear plasma forms?</li>
<li>How about a pyramid-power detector: opto beam-dump based on stack of 1950s razor blades w/damaged edges, monitored by photodiode (if the bent edges gradually “strop” themselves spontaneously, obviously we must be inside a Cheops pyramid? Does any material *shield* the pyramid-power? Can we reflect or focus it, or turn the effect on and off? Send morse code, or slow data via collimated beams of pyramid-power? Does our razor-based detector sense any EXISTING beams of modulated pyramid-power? Ha! )</li>
</ol>
