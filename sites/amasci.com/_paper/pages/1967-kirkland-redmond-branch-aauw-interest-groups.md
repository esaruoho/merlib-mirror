### Book Group

The Book Group meets monthly in member homes from September through June. Books are selected over the summer for the following year. Members are welcome to attend the Book Group even if they have not had a chance to finish reading the selected book. For additional reading, a [list of recommended books](http://www.aauw.org/5000/adbks00.html) is available <span id="DinnerGroup"></span> from our national website. A list of [last year's recommended books](http://www.aauw.org/5000/adbks99.html) and discussion questions are also available. If you have questions about the book group, please contact [Kathy Lucia.](mailto:kathilucia@aol.com)

### Dinner Group

A dinner group for couples and singles meets from time to time as volunteers offer their homes for these events. While any member may attend, sign up is usually required as space may be limited. The dinner is customarily potluck, but often with a theme.
