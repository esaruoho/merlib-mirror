Based in [downtown Kirkland](http://kirklanddowntown.org/), Judge Hayek performs weddings on *Saturdays and Sundays only* throughout the Puget Sound area, in homes, halls, hotels and parks. The fee for downtown Kirkland weddings is \$150. Judge Hayek's fee for most other wedding locations in King and Snohomish County is \$200.

<span id="schedule"></span>**To schedule a wedding**\
To schedule a wedding with Judge Hayek from 3 weeks to 6 months before your wedding date, first check to see if your date is available by looking at the [schedule of available dates](dates.html). Then send Judge Hayek an e-mail [(CarolynJH@aol.com)](mailto:CarolynJH@aol.com). E-mail is the preferred means to request information, since the message can be answered late at night. In your message, please include your evening and week-end phone numbers, date, time and location of wedding, and the full names of both bride and groom. In most cases, Judge Hayek will respond to e-mail messages the same day. If you have received no response in 48 hours, please try again. If the date is available and you choose to schedule your wedding with Judge Hayek, a deposit of \$50 will be required to hold your date and time on her calendar.

<span id="background"></span>**Background**\
Judge Hayek is a graduate of [Carleton College](http://www.carleton.edu/index.html), Northfield, Minnesota, and the [University of Chicago Law School](http://www.uchicago.edu/). She practiced law for nine years, half of that time with a large corporate law firm in downtown Seattle and half that time as the proprietor of her own firm in Federal Way, Washington. She served as a full-time judge of the Federal Way District Court for 13 years from 1982 to 1995. Judge Hayek is currently employed part-time as Executive Director of the [People's Memorial Association](http://peoplesmemorial.org). She is also an active member of the [American Association of University Women (AAUW)](http://www.aauw.org/), and the [Unitarian Universalist Church](http://www.pnwd.uua.org/). Judge Hayek spends much of her time performing community service in the local community. She is currently serving a 4-year term on the Kirkland Planning Commission. She has coordinated an [English as a Second Language Project](esl/esl.html) for AAUW and has served as webmaster of [Eliot Institute](http://eliotinstitute.org). A portion of every wedding fee is donated to charity. Judge Hayek is listed in *Who's Who in America*.

<span id="license"></span>**Obtaining a marriage license**\
A [marriage license](http://www.metrokc.gov/lars/marriage/) must be obtained no more than 60 days but at least three days before the date of the wedding. You may apply in person or by mail. In King County, a marriage license costs \$54. Call (206) 296-3933 for more information. In Snohomish County, a marriage license costs \$54. Call (425) 388-3627 for more information. Wedding licenses are valid throughout the State of Washington.  <span id="information"></span>

**Information for the Judge**\
Two to six weeks prior to the wedding, send a package of documents to: \
Judge Carolyn J. Hayek\
102 State St. \#206\
Kirkland, WA 98033-6674 

Include the following:

1.  The original marriage license and accompanying documents. (Please remember to fill in the personal background information and social security number on the Health Dept. form.)
2.  A note confirming the date and time of wedding. (Judge Hayek will plan to be there 15-30 minutes before the stated time.)
3.  Directions and a map to the wedding location.
4.  Names of two witnesses who will sign the marriage paperwork along with you on the day of the ceremony. They should be age 18 or older.
5.  Your phone number and e-mail address (if available).
6.  A copy of the wedding ceremony you wish to use. This can be one of the ceremonies posted to this website (with or without modifications) or another ceremony you have found or created.
7.  The unpaid balance of the wedding fee, payable by check to Carolyn J. Hayek
8.  Emergency phone number for wedding location. While it has never happened in many years of performing weddings, Judge Hayek would like a number to call to reach you in case her car breaks down or there is an accident on the way to the wedding.

Please be sure to use the correct postage. At least two stamps will be required. If you prefer to bring the paperwork by in person, please call first to arrange a time. A [map of downtown Kirkland](plazamap.html) will help you find Judge Hayek's location.

<span id="ceremonies"></span>**Wedding Ceremonies**\
Judge Hayek usually uses either the [simple wedding ceremony](simple.html) or the [special wedding ceremony](special.html), but either ceremony can be modified by the parties in any way they choose. Click on the name of the ceremony to see the complete text. Weddings can be performed in English, Spanish, or German.

You can also find sample wedding ceremonies in the book *Into the Garden: A Wedding Anthology*, edited by Robert Hass & Stephen Mitchell. The book also contains (pages 151-155) some very thoughtful quotations from Rainer Maria Rilke. Another book to look at is Robert Fulghum's *From Beginning to End*. It contains some interesting thoughts about wedding ceremonies. You may also want to look through books in the library or your local bookstore for ideas.

<span id="locations"></span>**Wedding Locations**\
While many weddings are performed in hotels, country clubs, or community centers, many other locations are possible. One of the most popular places to get married is in your own home or the home of a friend or relative. Other inexpensive locations include the clubhouse of an apartment or condominium complex where you or a friend or relative live, a gazebo or shelter in a public park, or a restaurant, either in a private room or in the normal dining area during a time when the restaurant is not crowded. Your local [Chamber of Commerce](http://www.kirklandchamber.org) can also help you find an appropriate facility to rent.

In [Kirkland](http://www.ci.kirkland.wa.us/), the [Woodmark Hotel](http://www.thewoodmark.com/), (425) 827-1986, and the [Shumway Mansion](http://www.shumwaymansion.com/), (425) 823-2303, are popular rental locations. The pavillion in Marina Park provides a scenic outdoor setting. For park information call (425) 803-2845. An unusual site for small weddings (2 to 10 guests) is [The Grape Choice Wine Shop](http://www.thegrapechoice.com/) located in an historic building at 220 Kirkland Avenue. Kirkland Mayor Larry Springer is the friendly proprietor. [Northlake Unitarian Universalist Church](http://www.Northlake.kirkland.uua.org), in [downtown Kirkland](http://kirklanddowntown.org/), is available for rental at a very reasonable cost. The officiant for weddings at the church may be a minister or a judge. For further information, call 425-822-0171.

<span id="officiants"></span>**Other Officiants**\
In the State of Washington most District Court Judges are available to perform weddings. Many are willing to come to your location. You can find their names and numbers in the phone book, listed under "Government-County-District Court." For more information see the [short list of phone numbers](judges.html) for King County judges or the [state court directory](http://www.courts.wa.gov/directory/countylist.cfm). Seattle Municipal Court has a wedding request form on-line. You can find it at: [http://www.pan.ci.seattle.wa.us/courts/judmag/marriage.htm](%20http://www.pan.ci.seattle.wa.us/courts/judmag/marriage.htm).\
\
You may also want to consider a minister of the Unitarian Universalist Church. The [Reverend Amanda Aikman](http://www.revamanda.com/) is available for wedding ceremonies as well as pre-marriage counseling. Her [website](http://www.revamanda.com/) will answer many of your questions. Or, call the [University Unitarian Church](http://www.uuchurch.org) in Seattle at (206) 525-8400 for another referral. Consider also Saltwater Unitarian Universalist Church at (253) 839-5200 for information about wedding officiants.

<span id="after"></span>**After the Ceremony**\
You will keep the ceremonial wedding certificate. Be sure you put it in a safe place because Judge Hayek is not able to replace lost certificates. Judge Hayek will mail the other paperwork to the county for filing. After ten days you can request a certified copy of the Marriage Certificate from the Marriage License Office of the county where the license was obtained. For marriages recorded with the [King County Marriage License Office](http://www.metrokc.gov/lars/marriage/), you can also [view marriage certificate filing information on-line](http://146.129.54.93:8193/mar/marDefault.asp?cur=mar&scur=Default) to verify that the certificate was filed following the ceremony.
