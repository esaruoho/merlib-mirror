<td rowspan="3" width="382" height="338" data-valign="TOP"><p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="pedrolrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;pedrolrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=340,height=298,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/pedrosml.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="85" height="74" alt="Pedro" /></a><br />
Our first two llamas were Paco &amp; Pedro, this Pedro, he is now a 7 year old gelding and that look on his face expresses his ATTITUDE! He is not mean nor threatening to us but he is the only one that I can not halter. He was a year old when we first got him and was desentisized or trained in any way shape or form. He has been sick only once and that was one of the few times he willingly accepted human contact. He was so ill that he layed on the ground with his head in my lap. Once he recovered that was the end of that!!!</p>
<p>Pedro is also a strong communicator letting you know what he wants. When we have treats he will come running up to us like the rest of the boys but he will stop a few feet away and refuse anything you offer out of your hand. Instead he looks at you, looks at the treat in your hand and then points his nose towards the ground. You lay it on the ground and he immediately eats.</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="pacolrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;pacolrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=328,height=213,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/paco2sml.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="66" height="43" alt="Paco" /></a><br />
Paco is a few months younger than Pedro, he is also a gelding approaching 7 years old. This photo was taken when he was a teen. I was lucky with him and was able to teach him to accept a halter and to walk with a lead rope. He is not as friendly as my other 5 but so much easier than Pedro. Also Paco will eat pellets from my hand and Pedro never got that far.</p>

<td><p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="babylrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;babylrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=288,height=433,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/babysml.jpg" data-align="LEFT" data-border="0" width="58" height="87" alt="Baby" /></a><br />
Beau Bridges was my third addition, he was only four months old when he arrived at our farm. Because he was my first cria I always called him Baby...still do:^) Baby is now an intact male 6 years old. He is my wise old owl and nobody pushes him around. He is very calm and seldom fights but he makes his presence and wishes known to the other llamas.</p>
<p>When he first arrived it was Pedro that decided to become his serogate mother. Baby was the only llama that we got as a single llama, out of the other 6 boys all came with a buddy. I think it made their adjustment earsier having another llama that they already know. <strong></strong></p>
<p>Also what was interesting about Pedro is that every llama that came after baby he wanted to beat the crap out of...go figure?</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="charlielrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;charlielrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=343,height=245,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/charliesml.jpg" data-align="LEFT" data-border="0" width="86" height="61" alt="Baby" /></a><a href="drakelrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;drakelrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=325,height=295,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/drakesml.jpg" data-align="RIGHT" data-border="0" width="81" height="74" alt="Drake" /></a><br />
Our 4th and 5th boys were Charlie (left) and Drake (right). They came together with two other little boys, all were about 6 months old. They original came from Aryel Llamas and I was supposed to train them so they could go to new homes. Charlie and Drake never left; they are 6 years old now. It was also great for baby because they were all in the same age group and he had other llamas his size to play with.</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="sparkylrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;sparkylrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=328,height=293,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/sparkysml.jpg" data-align="LEFT" data-border="0" width="82" height="73" alt="Sparky" /></a><a href="andrewlrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;andrewlrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=320,height=288,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/andrewsml.jpg" data-align="RIGHT" data-border="0" width="80" height="72" alt="Andrew" /></a><br />
Our last two are Sparky (left) and Andrew (right). Andrew was a gift from Aryel llamas. Their owner was out of town when Andrew was born, and I was watching the farm.
