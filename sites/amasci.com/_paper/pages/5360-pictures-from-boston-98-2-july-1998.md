# Pictures from Boston '98 #2 (July 1998)

*Source page: `~nickz/bh98/b98.html`*

1.  ### Boston

    This is my 15th Boston ride; the first one was in April 1981.

2.  [The Boston Capitol, across from Boston Common, July 11, 1998, a few hours after arrival](bcapitol.jpg)

    ### The Ride Back - Rhode Island

    For the first time since the late 1980's, I detoured and went through Providence and Cranston on the way back. Providence has a nice business district along Westminster and Weybosset St. and near Brown University on Thayer St.
