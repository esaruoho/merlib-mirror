<td>Manfred Humous takes his work home with him — he is a paleontologist — so the house is full of old bones. His wife, Theda, would rather a more middle-class appearance and so decorates, with no bones about it. <strong>ONDINE! … THEDA!</strong> is an intellectual farce. The one-act, two-scene play spins its tale through surreal telephone conversations and shoe shopping.

<strong>ONDINE! … THEDA!</strong> was written by Californian playwright Yarók (John Greene). The play premiered at the <strong>New City Theater’s Director’s Festival</strong> in Seattle in 1986.</td>
