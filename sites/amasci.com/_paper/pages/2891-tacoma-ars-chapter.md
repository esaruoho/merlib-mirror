<td width="50%"><p>If you live within driving distance of Tacoma/Puyallup, we invite you to join us for a monthly program about rhododendrons. You don't need to be a member to come and check us out. All people who are interested in rhododendrons are welcome, whether you are just beginning to learn about the plants or have a lifetime of experience with them.</p>
<p>Our members include casual gardeners, avid hobbyists, well-known hybridizers, nursery owners, and many others. If you ever wondered, "How do I prune a rhododendron?" or "How do you take cuttings?" or "What is this flower?", just ask one of us.</p>

<td width="50%"><p>Besides meetings, the Tacoma Chapter has some special projects for those interested. In the spring of each year, we sponsor a <a href="Tips/Tacoma%20ARS%20Chapter/Spring%20Show.html">rhododendron truss show</a> at the Puyallup Fairgrounds as part of the Spring Fair. This past year, we had over 500 trusses entered.</p>
<p>In 1967, our Chapter established the <a href="Tips/Tacoma%20ARS%20Chapter/Pt%20Defiance%20Rhododendrons.html">Rhododendron Garden</a> at Point Defiance Park in Tacoma. We have received two substantial grants in recent years from the American Rhododendron Society for restoration and new plant acquisition.</p>
<p>In 1993, we hosted the Annual Convention of the American Rhododendron Society. About 1000 people from around the world came together in Tacoma to share their knowledge and passion for these beautiful plants.</p></td>
