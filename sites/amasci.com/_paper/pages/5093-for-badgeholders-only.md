# For Badgeholders Only

*Source page: `~mirader/zeppelin/fbo.htm`*

<div id="Layer4" style="position:absolute; left:219px; top:246px; width:603px; height:2331px; z-index:4">

> The Led Zeppelin Mailing List is now being run at zoso.net on a server owned by Bob Rood. Bob, along with Jeremy Mixer, and Bruce "the Buckeye" Deerhake are the co-administrators. The list was formerly at Stanford University, but had to be moved due to problems with the server there (particularly a lack of digest capability), and apathy by the owners of the server. It is expected that we will soon grow back in membership back to the original over 1500 fans worldwide, including authors and editors of several Led Zeppelin publications. One never knows who might lurk about the Led Zeppelin List! For Badgeholders Only is the premier grapevine of discussion and news that no Led Zeppelin fan should be without. If you are a big Led Zeppelin fan and have an email account, then you need to get connected with us! This is a free service, all you need is email.
>
> Enjoy the list and if you have any questions or problems, please send an email to the list administrators at\
> ***owner-zeppelin at zoso.net*** or here:
>
> Bob at ***mirader at zoso.net***
>
> Jeremy Mixer at ***mixer at zoso.net***
>
> Bruce the Buckeye at ***fbohelp at buckeye-web.com***
>
> *Note: in the above email addresses, make sure you substitute the **@** symbol for the word "**at**" (this is done on this page to guard against spam)*

### To subscribe to The Led Zeppelin Mailing List:

> Please read this carefully -\
> You can Subscribe by sending an email message to:
>
> **majordomo@zoso.net**
>
> with the text: **SUBSCRIBE ZEPPELIN**\
> For the "Digest Mode", where you receive one larger message at the end of the day, instead of individual messages in near-real time. In the body of the email, use this text:
>
> **SUBSCRIBE ZEPPELIN-DIGEST**\
>
>  
>
> If your browser is configured properly, you can use the link below to subscribe.\
> The only thing you need to do is type SUBSCRIBE ZEPPELIN **OR** SUBSCRIBE ZEPPELIN-DIGEST depending upon your preferred method of delivery. This can be changed at a later time. Please keep the welcome message that you will momentarily receive, as it will contain important information about changing your options.
>
> ### [SUBSCRIBE!](mailto:majordomo@zoso.net)
>
> Please DO NOT include your email address in the body of any subscribing emails to the system. Your address will be obtained automatically from the return address of your email.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### To send a message to The Led Zeppelin Mailing List:

> Simply send your message the following address:
>
> **zeppelin@zoso.net**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### To UNsubscribe from The Led Zeppelin Mailing List:

> If you ever want to remove yourself from this mailing list, you can send mail to ****majordomo@zoso.net**** with the following command in the body of your email message:
>
> **UNSUBSCRIBE ZEPPELIN**
>
> or
>
> **UNSUBSCRIBE ZEPPELIN-DIGEST**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### To Change your Delivery Mode from The Led Zeppelin Mailing List:

> If you ever want to change from Digest to Single Messages (or vice-versa): you first UN-subscribe from whichever mode you are in (see directly above) then you RE-subscribe using the method near the top of this page, to the mode you wish to be in.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### To View the Old Messages of The Led Zeppelin Mailing List:

> [**Go to the "official" FBOArchives!**](ftp://ftp.eskimo.com/u/m/mirader/zeppelin-digests)\
> If you want to sample the List before you subscribe, see what you missed when your Email crashed, or just simply get caught up, this is the place you want to go! You can read each day's posts for the current month on-line. Previous month's digests may be downloaded and unzipped.
>
> [**Go to the Zeppinhood FBO Archives!**](http://216.221.172.165/cgi-bin/channelmaster-v1.cgi?cmd=login&cat=ZeppinHoodNet)\
> Here is another way to sample the FBO archives, via a threaded digest reader. Thanks to Joe Hect and the folks at [Zeppinhood](http://www.zeppinhood.com) for making this available. While a more convenient way to read the archives, this does not contain archives going as far back as the above link, it is more appropriate for reading recent messages posted to FBO. Please note, in order to post, you must subscribe.
