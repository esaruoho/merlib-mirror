DIAMOND INFORMATION\
Diamonds have been prized throughout history for their beauty, rarity, hardness and for their unique ability to reflect light. Most commonly referred to as the 4 C's, we offer the following guidelines in evaluating diamonds:\
**CARAT WEIGHT:**\
Carats are a measurement of weight, based on the weight of carob seeds in ancient times. 100 points equal 1 carat... 50 points mean 1/2 carat. These weights also designate sizes. A 1/2 carat may weigh between .48 to .57 carats.\
**CUT:**\
Prior to the early part of the 20th century, diamond cutting was basically a matter of trial and error. Due to the science of optics, it was determined that a diamond be cut to certain angles and proportions to allow for optimum reflection. Each diamond that is sold through Alpha undergoes an inspection under 10X magnification to ensure that he highest cutting standards are maintained.\
**COLOR:**\
In its purest form, diamond is completely devoid of any color, or colorless. Due to slight traces of other elements, most notable nitrogen, some diamonds will have a distinct body color or hue. Diamonds are graded\
table down, at an angle and compared to master set of graded diamonds to determine their color. At Alpha, we follow the standards established by the Gemological Institute of America (see grading scale) to determine color.

\
**CLARITY:**\
Diamonds are pure carbon. Often this element did not crystallize while its surrounding element did. This leads to black carbon spots in the interior of a stone. As you look at the self explanatory clarity grading chart, you will find that the cleaner a stone, or the fewer internal inclusions that may appear are white or clear in color, some being feathers, included crystals and small clouds.\
**FANCY SHAPES:**\
Diamonds are cut into various shapes to take the maximum advantage of the rough. While round is the best cut for maximum brilliance, we also stock: Marquise, Oval, Pearshape, Emerald Princes, Squares, Baguettes, Heartshape and Radiant.
