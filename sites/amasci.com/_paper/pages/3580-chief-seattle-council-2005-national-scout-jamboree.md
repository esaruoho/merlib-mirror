**<u>Adult Leader Requirements</u>**

**The requirements come directly from the National Office. As such, we do not have the authority to waive or modify them in any way. The requirements for all adult leaders are:**

participate in the required prejamboree training outing,

file a personal health and medical record prior to the prejamboree training,

participate in the Youth Protection Guidelines for Volunteer Leaders and Parents for Basic Training,

be approved by the local council,

meet the service requirement specific to the position:

<u>Scoutmaster:</u> Have served as Scoutmaster for at least one full year during the three year period prior to July 1, 2005, and have completed Boy Scout Leader Fast Start and Scoutmastership Fundamentals, No. 34550. (Although not required, Wood Badge and/or Scoutmaster's Key is a desirable qualification.)

<u>First Assistant Scoutmaster:</u> Same qualifications as Scoutmaster.

<u>Second Assistant Scoutmaster:</u> At least 21 years of age by July 1, 2005.

<u>Third Assistant Scoutmaster:</u> At least 18 years of age (but not have reached 21) by July 1, 2005.

The Adult Leader Application is a Microsoft Word document. To open and print it, [**click here.**](adultapp.doc)

NOTE: if the online application does not download correctly, contact Eleanor Pawley at the Scout Office e (206-725-5200) and ask for an Adult Leader Application Packet.

<span id="staff"></span>

**<u>National Staff</u>**

The National Jamboree always has a lot of openings for youth and adult staff members. Staff members pay their own transportation costs and the full Jamboree fee, but those who have worked on a Jamboree staff invariably find it a fun and rewarding experience. Many adults return to work on staff for Jamboree after Jamboree. Jamboree Staff Applications are available now at the [**National Jamboree website.**](http://www.scouting.org/jamboree/staff.html)
