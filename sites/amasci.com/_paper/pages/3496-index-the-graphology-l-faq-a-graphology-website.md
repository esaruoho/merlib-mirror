## <span id="history">Brief History</span>

The Graphology-L FAQ was created as a text file, in April of 1995. In January of 1998, it was converted to HTML format, and placed on the internet. In September 2001, translation of this document into French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese was started.

The official site for The Graphology-L FAQ is [http://www.eskimo.com/~hwa/faq](http://www.eskimo.com/~hwa/faq/index.html)
