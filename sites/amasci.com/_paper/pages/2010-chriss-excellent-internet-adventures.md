<p>Hello Planet Earth!<br />
Welcome to Chris's Home on the Net!</p>
<p>It's been over a year since I last changed this page, and I still don't know what to put on it! I'm tired of the color and the logo, and I'm tired of most of the other pages too. What this place needs is a total slash-and-burn job!</p>
<p>There are no flags here. I'm tired of seeing flags all over the place after September 11. It was a horrible, evil thing that happened that day. Unfortunately horrible, evil things happen every day someplace on earth and most never get noticed. The sudden rush of "patriotism" that followed brought to mind this lyric from (who else?) Rush ("Territories" from Power Windows, 1986):</p>
<p><em>Better the pride that resides<br />
In a citizen of the world<br />
Than the pride that divides<br />
When a colourful rag is unfurled</em></p>
<p>Feel free to look around. Like I said, this so-called web site has its days numbered in current form. The page I put the most effort into would have to be my <span class="maintextbold"><a href="bio/resume.htm">résumé</a></span>, which I completely re-worked and updated just the other day. It's probably no coincidence that it also gets the most page hits (even in our depressed economy).</p>
<p>I also wrote a little bit about my <a href="linux.html">experiences with Linux</a>, which I intended to develop further but never did (maybe because there were so many other cool things to do!). Since writing that page, the hard disk that held my Windows 98/Linux dual-boot configuration became corrupted and unbootable and my focus has been shifted to Windows 2000. I'm different in that I stradle the fence on the Windows vs. Linux debate. I like Windows 2000 alot, and use it for all my daily work. I got a brand new 40 gigabyte, 7200 rpm hard drive for Christmas. Ten of those megabytes are earmarked for a brand new Debian installation (assuming I can get it to work correctly with my current hardware and partitioning scheme, we'll see...).</p>
