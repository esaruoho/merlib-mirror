# <img src="brown.gif" data-align="middle" /> The Algae Programming Language

Welcome to the home of Algae, a high-level interpreted language for numerical analysis. Algae borrows from languages like [MATLAB](http://www.mathworks.com/) and [APL](http://www.cis.ohio-state.edu/text/faq/usenet/apl-faq/faq.html), but it was developed because we needed a free, efficient, and versatile language capable of handling large problems.

Algae is free software. Please use it with [caution](caution.html). Certain [copyrights and disclaimers](COPYING) apply. You may redistribute it and/or modify it under the terms of the [GNU General Public License](LICENSE).

Algae was known as "Alki" prior to 1997. Unfortunately, there was confusion with an outfit called [Alki Software](http://www.alki.com/).

A mailing list for Algae is now active; to subscribe, send mail to <algae-list-request@eskimo.com> with the word "subscribe" as the subject line.

This site is now mirrored at [http://axams1.bo.infn.it:9999/algae](http://axams1.bo.infn.it:9999/algae/). Many thanks to Giuseppe Levi for providing this mirror site.

[Boeing](http://www.boeing.com/) users: Please go to the [intranet site](http://hulk.ds.boeing.com/~ksh/algae/).
