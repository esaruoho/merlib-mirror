# Megalopolis (Μεγαλόπολη)

Megalopolis (literally, Large City or Great City) was originally founded when a number of small cities joined together as one large one.  It was hoped that the strength in numbers would help them to defend against the aggressive Spartans.  This unfortunately didn't work very well in the long run and the glory days of Megalopolis didn't last long.

We didn't really stop in Megalopolis, but while driving through we did happen to see some of the archeological sites by the side of the road and I stopped long enough to take some pictures.
