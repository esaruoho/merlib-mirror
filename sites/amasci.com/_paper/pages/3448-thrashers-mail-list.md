# Thrashers Mail List

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Thrasher Internet Mail List is a group of people interested in the Thrasher Family. After joining the List, you can send and receive E-Mail messages regarding the Thrasher Family.

To subscribe or unsubscribe to the List, simply send a message to the following address:

In the **the subject Line** type in the word: **subscribe** or **unsubscribe**.

<thrasher-list-request@eskimo.com>

If you do not receive a reply, please [contact me](mailto:greg@eskimo.com). Thanks.

After you have been subscribed, all messages you want to send to the Thrasher List should be sent to: <thrasher-list@eskimo.com>

That's all there is to it.

Please Join, the more people on the List, the more connections that can be made.

Thank You...
