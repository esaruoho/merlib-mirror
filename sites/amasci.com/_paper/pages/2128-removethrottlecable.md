# removethrottlecable

*Source page: `~daddog/jetting/removethrottlecable.htm`*

DADOG's REMOVING THROTTLE CABLE

FROM THE CARBURETOR

for BEGINNERS

for the YFZ-450 Quad

------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Ok yo want to rejet and find the needle!

You will have to remove the carburetor.

This isnt rocket science. All you have to do is TURN OFF YOUR GAS so you don't get gas flooding the garage!

If you get a fire you can't ride your quad!

So put a rag under the carb to soak up gas that WILL SPILL OUT of the carb.

Remove the electrical plugs by depressing the safety clips and romove the hoses, rememberering where they go.

The last thing to remove is the FUEL cable.

Unscrew the cover over the fuel cable and follow these pictures...

and the cable is off...

Now the carb is free to rejet.
