<u>Youth Requirements</u>

Boy Scouts and Varsity Scouts must:

have been born after August 3, 1987 and before July 1, 1993,

be a First Class Scout by July 1, 2005

participate in the required, overnight pre-Jamboree training outing two months before the Jamboree,

file a completed personal health and medical record, No. NSJ-34412, between February 1 and April 1, 2005,

have been active in a troop or team for at least six months prior to July 1, 2005,

agree to follow the Jamboree code of conduct.
