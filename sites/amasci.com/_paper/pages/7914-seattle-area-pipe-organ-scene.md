## <span id="Radio">Radio programs</span>

In Seattle, listen to 1 1/2 hours of continuous choir and organ music starting at 9:30pm on Sunday evenings.

- The [Compline](http://www.scn.org/arts/compline) service at St. Mark's in Seattle is broadcast live at 9:30pm on Sundays on KING-FM (98.1).
- [*The Organ Loft*](http://loft.cc) radio network on [KING-FM](http://www.king.org) (98.1) on Sundays at 6:00am and 10:00pm. In Eugene OR, KWAX-FM (91.1) on Sundays at 8:00am and 6:00pm.
- [Pipedreams](http://pipedreams.mpr.org) is not available in Seattle, but should be! NPR nationwide broadcast. You can listen to the last four programs on your computer from that website.

## <span id="New">New Organ Projects</span>

It seems like there's always an interesting project going on. Here are a few:

- Calvary Lutheran Church in Bellevue has a new [Swanson](ORGANS/calvary.htm) two manual organ.
- University Presbyterian Church (UPC) is building a new [Reuter](http://www.reuterorgan.com) organ, following the newly remodeled sanctuary. Cost is \$1.4 million and it will be completed in November, 1999.
- St. James Cathedral recently replaced the 1926 Casavant east apse organ with a [Rosales organ](ORGANS/sj_apse.htm), called the Archbishop Thomas J. Murphy Millenium Organ.
- A new [Fritts organ](ORGANS/plufritt.htm) is complete at Pacific Lutheran University..\
- A new Fisk organ was recently installed at the [Seattle Symphony's](http://www.seattlesymphony.org) Benaroya Hall in July 2000.
