<td colspan="2" data-bgcolor="#f0ffff">The Society is a nonprofit educational corporation formed to provide a forum for the ideas<img src="testlogo.jpeg" data-align="right" width="108" height="107" /> of C.G. Jung. The Jung Society sponsors lectures, workshops, seminars, and study groups by both locally and nationally known Jungian scholars, and its events are, for the most part, intended for the general public. Most events take place at the Good Shepherd Center. <a href="about.html">More about the Society</a>.
<p><em><strong>Office/Library:</strong> Good Shepherd Center, 4649 Sunnyside Ave. N., Room 222, Seattle, WA 98103</em></p>
<p><em><strong>Phone:</strong> (206) 547-3956    <strong>Fax:</strong> (206) 547-7746    <strong><br />
Office e-mail:</strong> <a href="mailto:office@jungseattle.org">office@jungseattle.org</a> <strong>Webmaster:</strong> <a href="mailto:webmaster@jungseattle.org">webmaster@jungseattle.org</a></em></p>
<div data-align="left">
<p>Carl Gustav Jung, Swiss psychiatrist (1875-1961), was one of the great pioneers in the area of depth psychology and the field now known as adult development theory. His ideas probe beyond the rational description of human problems and behavior to the inner focus and meaning of our lives. Jung's contributions to psychology <img src="jungpipe.jpg" data-align="left" data-vspace="4" data-hspace="6" width="111" height="144" alt="C.G. Jung with pipe" />include the concepts of introversion and extraversion, synchronicity, archetype, and the collective unconscious.</p>
</div>
<p>When so many devoted their genius to creating the means to destroy the world, Jung went inward on a lonely and dangerous journey, discovering those creative synthesizing forces within the human psyche that might enable the world to survive. He learned that by honestly facing personal conflicts on the most everyday level, we each confront the deepest spiritual problems of universal human concern.</p>
<p>Jung's life, his mission, and his voice are a challenge and a source of illuminating hope.</p></td>
