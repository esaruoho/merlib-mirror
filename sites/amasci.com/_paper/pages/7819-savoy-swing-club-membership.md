# Savoy Swing Club Membership

## <img src="images/dog1.gif" data-align="bottom" data-hspace="10" data-border="0" width="32" height="42" alt="Lindy Dog" /> Membership Benefits

Love to Lindy? Want to learn how? Need a place to practice? Then [join us](membform.htm)!! Club benefits include:

- Free newsletter *(Savoy Swingline)* [available online](news.htm) or mailed to your home !
- Free admission to [Friday night practices](practice.htm) !
- Reduced-price classes: \$30 for all [classes](classes.htm) instead of the regular \$45 price! (save \$\$\$ each class session) !
- Discounts on admission to co-sponsored [dances](calendar.htm) and [workshops](events.htm) (normally a savings of 10-20%) !
- Lots of opportunities to make your voice heard... the Savoy Swing Club relies on its membership for volunteers to make everything happen !
- [Discounts](discount.htm) at participating businesses !

## <img src="images/dog1.gif" data-align="bottom" data-hspace="10" data-border="0" width="32" height="42" alt="Lindy Dog" /><span id="dues_table"></span> New 2001 Member Rates!

For 2001, annual dues depend on the type of membership and when you join or renew your membership. Annual membership rates are as follows:

|----------------:|------------------:|-----------------:|-----------------:|
| **Member Type** | **Before Feb 15** | **After Feb 15** | **After June 1** |
|      Individual |              \$50 |             \$60 |             \$30 |
|         Student |              \$30 |             \$40 |             \$20 |
|          Family |             \$100 |            \$120 |             \$60 |

## <img src="images/dog1.gif" data-align="bottom" data-hspace="10" data-border="0" width="32" height="42" alt="Lindy Dog" /> Limited Membership

Each person who enrolls in a Savoy Swing Club class is enrolled as a Limited Club Member during that five- or six-week class series. The fee you pay when you register for a class series entitles you to attend the classes you sign up for as well as attend [Friday Night Practice](practice.htm) for free. Note that only Annual Members receive all the other club benefits listed above.

## <img src="images/dog1.gif" data-align="bottom" data-hspace="10" data-border="0" width="32" height="42" alt="Lindy Dog" /> How to Join

Fill out our [membership form](membform.htm) and mail it with your check to the address shown, or contact the **Savoy Swing Club at P.O. Box 75535, Seattle, WA 98125**, (206) 547-7676.
