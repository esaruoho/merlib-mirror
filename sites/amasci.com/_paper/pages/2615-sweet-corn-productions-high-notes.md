<p><strong>Friday, January 11, 2002, 8pm<br />

4711 California Ave., SW. (at Alaska), Seattle</strong></p>
<p>Mary Jo DuGaw and Linda Strandberg, sopranos, and Steve Bryant, countertenor, hit the heights with scintillating songs in seven languages for high voices and piano. Solos, duets and a trio prove that classical music can be funny as well as gorgeous. The program ranges from high drama to low comedy, with a mix of Broadway showstoppers to opera scenes, and artful duets by Gershwin, Tchaikovsky, Dvorak, Rossini, and more. This program of <strong>High Notes</strong> promises to blow the roof off the ArtsWest Playhouse.</p>
