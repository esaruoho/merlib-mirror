# Seventh Seal

## About the Band

Seventh Seal was a band a friend of mine and I started in high school (around 1994, I think). We were a Christian heavy metal band. The original band was started by me and Mark Eldridge. It also included Ashley Webb and Tres Goodman. Mark played guitar, I played bass, Ashley sang, and Tres did the drums. Mark and I wrote most of the music. However, a little while after our first concert, Mark was killed in a car wreck. I still miss him. Later on, we found another guitarist, Phil Martin, who was inspired to learn to play guitar by Mark. He managed to play with the same love for God and music that Mark did. It was truly amazing. Tres Goodman had left the band, and we got Justin (can't remember his last name) to play drums. He was very excellent. We wrote lots of music, and got some of it recorded by someone from Justin's church for free. Later I had it mixed to CD. We broke up (actually, Justin and Phil may still be playing - Ashley and I left the band, and never really kept in touch with the others). Anyway, we did do a few really cool concerts, with Mortal (now known as Fold Zandura), as well as Living Sacrifice. We also played with a really cool local band called SlamCat. Anyway, these are the songs we recorded. Have fun!

## About the Music

All of this music is made available in OGG format. You probably don't have a player for this. However, you can go to [the Vorbis web site](http://www.vorbis.com/) to get a plugin for your player. Why didn't I MP3 them or something normal? Simple, those formats have restrictive licenses, which are unacceptable for file formats which have wide use. Please note, I actually haven't listened to these files, because I don't have a sound card on my computer, so if the quality really sucks, I'm sorry. Also, Phil's guitar sounds like crap because we were running straight from the pre-amp to the mixer (I know its bad, but we got it done for free). The music is also freely redistributable. Give it to whomever you like. Sell it, use it in commercials, whatever. I would appreciate it if you gave the author credit, but its not mandatory. We wrote the music to glorify God, and the more it is played, the more He is glorified.

## The Music

Okay, here's the songs -

- [Know Fear](knowfear.html) by Jonathan Bartlett. This is my favorite song we ever did. Very fast and hard, with an excellent guitar solo by Phil.
- [FRS](frs.html) by Jonathan Bartlett. A fast song about Jesus' life and resurrection. Oh yeah, you'll never guess what FRS stands for.
- [My Lord, My God](mylordmygod.html) by Jonathan Bartlett. A slow song about the life Jesus led.
- [Now](now.html) by Justin (I forgot his last name). A song emphasizing the necessity of making a decision for Christ.
- [Make it Right](makeitright.html) by Margaret Becker. Yes, I know, its illegal to do this and not give her money. Royalties for nonprofit performances are the stupidest thing I've ever heard of.
- [For You](foryou.html) by Jonathan Bartlett and Mark Eldridge. This was our band's first song. It tells the story of Jesus through the eyes of an onlooker. Very rough sound, because it was originally recorded in a Church with a \$20 tape recorder.

Well, that's it. Do what you want with this stuff, it's here for the taking. If you have any questions, [send an email to johnnyb@eskimo.com](mailto:johnnyb@eskimo.com). Hope you enjoy this.
