<img src="images/ptworkstation.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **Personal Training Workstation 3.0**\
\
PTWorkstation is a software system designed specifically for personal trainers and people who are serious about fitness.\
PTWorkstation can keep track of a personal trainer's clients or of multiple users. Nearly anything fitness-related can be tracked using PTWorkstation. From workouts to diet, supplement usage to body measurements, all of a client's or user's vital stats can be stored quickly and easily using PTWorkstation.\
Personal trainers and users can save their favorite routines. Then, they can print out these routines and take them to the gym as a guide, or personal trainers can give them to clients. The printouts leave places for weight amounts, so you can fill them in as you go, and update PTWorkstation later.\
PTWorkstation can also chart users' progress. Charts that describe weight gain/loss, strength gain/loss, body fat gain/loss, and much more, can be printed to help clients and users stay motivated.\
PTWorkstation can generate detailed reports that show changes in exercise and diet. These reports can be printed or saved in HTML format.\
If you do not have much fitness experience, you can use PTWorkstation's Coach feature to get a routine. PTWorkstation will even show you how to do the specified exercises!\
PTWorkstation takes the guesswork out of results. PTWorkstation has the ability to look over a user's entire history of workouts, diet, supplement usage, and aerobics workouts, and list possible reasons for any changes in strength, body fat percentage, or weight. (Now you can find out if a new supplement really works!)\

<img src="images/filemakerfm.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **Stop Smoking 1.0**\
\
Stop Smoking 1.0 is a FileMaker Pro database to help you quit the evil weed. It's features are based on positive Behavior Analysis principles and can be customized to suit your own situation. Each record represents a day.\
It's layout is pretty self explanatory. The idea is that you are rewarding yourself (reinforcing non-smoking) for each waking hour that you do not smoke. You simply check a box for each hour you are smoke free. In the case of this database, the reinforcer is money. Your reinforcer amount is automatically calculated as you enter data.\
