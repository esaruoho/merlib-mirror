# USELDING Beadle County History

*Source page: `~lisanne/uselding/mcewen.html`*

**Beadle County, South Dakota Pioneer Histories:\
Uselding Extractions**

**Between 1955 and 1970, Mildred McEWEN JONES collected over 9,000 pioneer histories. These histories range from a few lines to several pages sent in by living relatives. Her files were bound into five thick volumes, three on Beadle County townships and two on Huron.**

Historical account of Beadle County, South Dakota, 1879-1900, (A), 3 vols.

Pioneer residents of the City of Huron, 2 vols.

Mildred McEWEN JONES, comp. (For reference only at Huron Public Library.)

Fore more information see: [Beadle County, SD, Bibliography of Sources.](http://www.rootsweb.com/~sdbeadle/biblio.html)

Huron Public Library

521 Dakota Avenue South

Huron, SD, 57350-2797

\(605\) 353-8530

Allied Uselding surnames in Beadle County include: Bales, Bussell, Case, Lockwood, Minium, Voelkel and Weingartner.
