It took me a while to get my skiing going this year, buying [a new house](../../home/index.html) kept me a little busy during December and January.

<span id="1"></span>

**January 23-24, 1997 - Breckenridge**

- <img src="img/breck.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Breck logo" /> Some friends from Austin and England came to see the new place and we took a couple of days to *finally* go skiing at one of my favorite places, [Breckenridge.](http://www.brecknet.com/)

  [-- back to index --](#top)

<span id="2"></span>

**February 9-12, 1997 - Whistler, British Columbia**

- <img src="img/whistler.gif" data-align="left" data-border="0" alt="Whistler logo" /> <img src="img/blackcomb.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Blackcomb logo" /> Finally made it to [Whistler](http://www.whistler.net/) and [Blackcomb](http://www.whistler.net/blackcomb/winter/) north of Vancouver. I've been wanting to go there for years and it was everything I had heard. Friendly people, reasonable prices, and great snow. A few days there made me forget everything else. Go soon though, it's growing fast and probably won't be nearly as nice in a few more years.

  [-- back to index --](#top)

<span id="3"></span>

**March 1, 1997 - Copper Mountain**

- <img src="img/copper.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Copper logo" /> Another lousy day in the paradise that is [Copper Mountain.](http://www.aescon.com/ski/copper/) Probably my best day of skiing ever (as far as skill, or maybe it was luck).

  [-- back to index --](#top)

<span id="4"></span>

**March 8, 1996 - Winter Park**

- <img src="img/winterpark.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Winter Park logo" /> Return to [Winter Park. Sometime](http://www.skiwinterpark.com/wpmj) I want to take the train up here, coming down from the mountain at the end of the day you can ski right past it loading up with people... sure seems like a nice way to get back to Denver.

  [-- back to index --](#top)

<span id="5"></span>

**March 21-22, 1997 - Keystone**

- <img src="img/keystone.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Keystone logo" /> Took a day off to spend more than one day skiing. Night skiing at Keystone is just the best. We even happened upon fireworks down in town while coming down the mountain. Seeing fireworks from above is an interesting experience. Unfortunately, it was too cloudy to see Hale-Bopp.
