<span class="small">**Publications**\*</span>

- *<span class="small">The Complete Schipperke</span>* <span class="small">published by Howell Books, sponsored by The Schipperke Club of America. 207 pages, copyright 1993, ISBN 0-87605-303-7.</span>
- *<span class="small">A Schipperke Anthology</span>* <span class="small">by Vella M. Root, published by The Schipperke Club of America.</span>
- *<span class="small">Schipperkes</span>* <span class="small">by Janice F. Martin, published by TFH Publications. 192 pages, copyright 1991, ISBN 0-86622-161-1.</span>

<span class="small">\* The accuracy of the history of the Schipperke breed in these texts are in question.  Resources in Belgium suggest the origins are different than has been represented in England and the United States.  To read a perhaps-more-accurate accounting of Schip history, go <a href="schiphis.htm" target="main">here</a>.</span>

<span class="small">I don't know anything about the following book, so can't count on its accuracy:  *Het Schipperke, by* Kleine, Zwarte belg,   H. C. Rotgans-Rookmaaker Vrienden van het , Schipperke.</span>

**<span class="small">Videos</span>**

<span class="small">The <a href="http://www.akc.org/" target="_top">AKC</a> produces a Schipperke breed video. If you're interested in showing or breeding this is a good information resource.</span>\

<span class="small">**The Email List!**</span>

<span class="small">You, too, can join the Schipperke email list by sending a message to:\
[LISTSERV@MAIL.EWORLD.COM](mailto:listserv@mail.eworld.com)\
with the line 'SUBSCRIBE SKIPPY-L Firstname Lastname'\
in the message body.\
It's that simple!</span>

I do not maintain the list, so please don't ask me to subscribe you to it or send me message about any problems with the list.  When you subscribe to the list you'll receive a set of instructions on whom to contact regarding questions or concerns.
