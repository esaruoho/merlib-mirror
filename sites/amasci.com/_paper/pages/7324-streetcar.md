> # Streetcar
>
> A streetcar is usually a light rail vehicle running on a track in the street. Streetcars are also known as trams and trolleys.
>
> [The Waterfront Streetcar](streetcar99.html) is a streetcar line running along part of the Seattle waterfront and a few blocks east. It is primarily a tourist attraction and uses restored streetcars (trams) from Melbourne, Victoria, Australia.
