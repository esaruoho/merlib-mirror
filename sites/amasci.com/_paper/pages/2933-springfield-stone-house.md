    The Stone House in the First Anderson Garden

     

          One of Bob's
          first jobs as a landscape architect was to redesign the gardens
          of Mr. Tomlinson, president of Nabisco. Mr. Tomlinson introduced
          Bob to a construction technique using concrete poured into forms
          behind a facing of large stones.

          Before building
          the family dreamhouse, Bob experimented with the technique on
          a one-room cottage.
