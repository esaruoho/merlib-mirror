# 11-DAY CHINA TOUR

*Source page: `~interlak/11daypak.html`*

**11 DAY CHINA TOUR\
INCLUDES BEIJING, XIAN, SHANGHAI & SUZHOU\**
\
\
Day 1 Depart Los Angeles or your home city of departure.

2 Arrive Beijing - Guide will meet you and transfer you to your hotel.

3 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Forbidden City, Tiananmen Square/History\
Museum. Evening Dai Minority Show Dinner BLD

4 Beijing - Tour the Hutongs, Jiazi lunch with a Hutong Family.\
Visit the Lama Temple and Temple of Heaven. Fangshand dinner at Beihai Park\
BLD

5 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Great Wall at Mutianyu, Ming Tombs and\
Summer Palace. Special Duck Dinner tonight and Peking Opera performance. BLD

6 Beijing/Xian by morning air. Met by Guide and taken to visit the Wild Goose\
Pagoda, City Wall, History Museum and Big Bell Tower. Transfer to your hotel.\
BLD.

7 Xian - Guide will take you to the Terra Cotta Warriors site, Banpo Museum,\
Muslim Quarter Mosque. In the evening you will be entertained by the beautiful\
Tang Dynasty Show. BLD

8 Xian/Shanghai by morning air. Guide will meet you taking you on tour of the city\
to visit the Bund, French Concession and Nanjing Road. Evening performance of\
the famous Shanghai Acrobatic Show. Transfer to your hotel . BLD

9 Shanghai - Guide will take you to the Jade Buddha Temple and most beautiful garden\
in China, the Yu Gardens. Afternoon visit to the new National Museum at\
Peoples Square. BLD

10 Shanghai/Suzhou/Shanghai by train for the day. Visit Tiger Park, Silk Factory and\
Showrooms and a special visit to the Embroidery Institute. BLD

11 Shanghai/U.S. arriving back at your west coast departure city same day.

Your tour includes: All transportation, meals as indicated, local guides with driver,\
hotel choice of Standard, First class or Deluxe, and sightseeing and entertainment\
as listed.
