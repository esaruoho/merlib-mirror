<td width="501" data-valign="top" height="450"><p><strong>RTGS</strong> has now become an essential component of a National Payment System and one that not only reduces the potential of fraud in a country, but accelerates economic growth. Many Potential Investors now consider an RTGS being a pre-requisite to investing in Government Securities.</p>
<p><strong>SAPIER provides the following basic functionality required of an RTGS:</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong></strong> Straight Through Payment (STP)<br />
<strong><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong></strong> Payment Vs. Payment (PVP)<br />
<strong><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong></strong> Delivery Vs. Payment (DVP)<br />
<strong><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong></strong> Delivery Free of Payment (DFP)</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong>Features of SAPIER :</strong></p>

<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>SAPIER provides an optional interface to SWIFT.</strong><span id="1"></span><br />
All messages generated by SAPIER follow SWIFT standards and can be sent over a Central Bank's WAN or via the SWIFT Network.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Message Warehousing</strong><span id="2"></span><br />
Warehousing facilities are available with a parameter to limit the number of days that a transaction can be warehoused.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Queuing</strong><span id="3"></span><br />
SAPIER allows queuing with options of LIFO, FIFO or FAFO. Queues of Member Banks can be managed by a Central Bank and can, at it's discretion, also allow it's member banks to manage their outgoing queue. A Central Bank can also temporarily freeze the queues of the member banks when events such as Gridlock Resolution occur.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Gridlock Resolution</strong><span id="4"></span><br />
SAPIER takes care of Gridlock based on a "Gridlock Busting" algorithm.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Repo and Buy-back</strong><span id="5"></span><br />
One of the common ways to increase intra-day liquidity is a Repo. Repos can be effected manually or automatically. Buy-backs of these repo-ed securities can also be effected through SAPIER.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Net Settlement of Positions<span id="6"></span></strong><br />
At the end of a day, positions of each participating banks is calculated and a message is sent asking them to settle their positions.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Real-time Position and Drill-down Query</strong><span id="7"></span><br />
A Central Bank can view the real-time positions of Member Banks at any point of time. Details of the selected bank are displayed including payment details, securities transferred, accounting and repos. Credits, Debits and balance for each of these are displayed and each of these can be queried to get the relevant details, the drill-down continues until relevant details of a selected transaction becomes available.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Customizable Charges, penalties and interest calculation</strong><span id="8"></span><br />
Charges, penalties and interest calculation can be set up by a user to meet a Central Bank's evolving requirements.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Positions and Limits<span id="9"></span></strong><br />
Almost any type of Limit can be set up using the Position and Limits (PAL) module. Some examples are: Credit / Debit balance limit for a particular bank, minimum / maximum amount of Payments to be processed in a particular period, the amount and types of Securities that can be Repo-ed etc.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Accounting</strong><span id="10"></span><br />
SAPIER has an on-line interface to Bankstar Technologies' Accounting Software. In addition, should a Central Bank have an existing baseline accounting system, provision has been made to interface to it.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Disaster Recovery</strong><span id="11"></span><br />
Data backup and restore facilities along with other Disaster Recovery facilities such as Hot Swapping and Mirroring ensure smooth functioning of the system.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>External Participant Services</strong><span id="12"></span><br />
A Central Bank can allow External participants to the RTGS to login to SAPIER and access certain services. A Central Bank can allow / disallow the following :</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Securities/Repo Management<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Outgoing Queue Management<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Viewing of Incoming Queues<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Viewing of Accounts Statement<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Viewing Reports</p>
</blockquote></td>
