**What do I need to get started?**  
Most of our customers start with a pregnancy or a baby in their family or group of friends. After that, our consultants recommend:

|  |  |
|----|----|
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 24 - 36 small **[diapers](indisposables.html)**; any less is difficult to deal with because you run out often and you encounter premature wear of diapers |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 1 small **[cover](covers.html)** to every 4 - 6 diapers. The number of covers you will need depends on how you use and launder your covers, discuss this with your consultant. Try some pull-up pants and some wrap pants to find out which works best for you. |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 3-4 small **[inserts](liners.html)** for extra absorbency during nap and night time; you may not need these until your baby is 3 or 4 months old. |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | **[Liners](liners.html)**; refer to the section on liners to make a decision about which type you will use |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 1 2l. bottle of **[laundry concentrate](laundry.html)**; the smaller size is not as cost efficient and you will run out of it too soon |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 1 400g bottle of **[laundry brightener](laundry.html)**; the smaller size is fine as you only use one or two capfuls at a time. If you really like the product as much as we do, you can buy the larger size later |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 1 25ml bottle **[ONA](laundry.html)** for diaper pail, washing machine etc. |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 1 **[diaper pail](laundry.html)** |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 1 small **[dirty diaper bag](laundry.html)** for going out |
| <img src="../_themes/babytown1/fieabul1.gif" data-hspace="13" width="15" height="15" /> | 12-24 **[wipes](pads.html)**, because if you are going to go cotton, go all the way! |

**Do you have...?**  
Chances are we have what you need if it is related to babies. The quickest way for you to find out if we have the item you are searching for is to proceed to the Search page and follow the instructions that you find there.
