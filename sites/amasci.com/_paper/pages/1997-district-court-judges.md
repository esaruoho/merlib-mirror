# District Court Judges

*Source page: `~cjh/judges.html`*

In addition to retired judges, such as [Carolyn J. Hayek](weddings.html), there are many active judges who perform wedding ceremonies in the Seattle area. Here are phone numbers for active District Court Judges who may be able to help with your wedding plans:

Federal Way  
\(206\) 296-7794

Auburn (Aukeen)  
\(206\) 296-7749

Bellevue  
\(206\) 296-3650

Issaquah  
\(206\) 296-7688

Redmond (Northeast)  
\(206\) 296-3667

Renton  
\(206\) 296-3532

Seattle  
\(206\) 296-3620

Southwest (Burien)  
\(206\) 296-0133

\
*Please note:* The area code of (206) applies to all these numbers, since the numbers are all part of the King County government phone system, based in Seattle.

Active judges are not allowed to do weekday weddings between the hours of 8:30 a.m. and 4:30 p.m. They usually prefer to perform weddings at the courthouse rather than at a home or rented hall. Usually, you will need to leave a message for the judge giving the date, time, and location of the planned wedding and then wait for a call back regarding the judge's availability and fees. Each judge sets his or her own fees, so they could vary considerably from judge to judge.

This list has been provided as a public service by [Retired Judge Carolyn Hayek.](weddings.html) If you find the list helpful or discover errors, please [E-Mail (CarolynJH@aol.com)](mailto:CarolynJH@aol.com) your comments to Judge Hayek, phone (425) 889-8101

Additional [judges and courts](http://www.wa.gov/courts/crtinfo/district/home.htm) throughout the State of Washington are listed in the website maintained by the Office of the Administrator for the Courts.
