**Background -**

In 1999 I decided to travel with my morris team to the Sidmouth Folk Festival in Devon, England, a decision made easy when I learned that there would be a total solar eclipse—something I'd always wanted to see—visible nearby only four days after the festival. But the weather didn't cooperate (you can read that sad story [here](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Eturnip/pa/19990811_eclipse/)). Two years later, bitter & disillusioned, I suddenly remembered that the next eclipse was due in June '01 & signed up for an eclipse-and-safari tour I found on the internet; "Along the Zambezi" organized by [Civilized Adventures](http://www.civilizedadv.com/) of Calgary, AB. This Shockwave travelogue is the result.

**Fixes & additions since version 1.0 -**
