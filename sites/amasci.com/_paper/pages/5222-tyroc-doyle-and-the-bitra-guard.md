<td data-valign="top" width="580"><h1 id="doyle-and-the-bitra-guard">Doyle and the <a href="../guard_guide.html">Bitra Guard</a></h1>
by Journeyman Harper Tyroc of Bitra Hold
<p><em>(Sung somewhat to the tune of "(Theme from) the Brady Bunch," though it changes about halfway through)</em></p>
<hr />
Tyroc, his eyes twinkling in the light from the hearth, leaned forward and whispered to the Hold children, "Have any of you heard of Doyle?"
<p>One lone hand raised. It was Dibny. The little scamp had wedged himself into the crook of a chair and was grinning, "Yeah, when he attacked Lord Mal, Clark jumped in front and took his arrow!" His eyes sparkled of hero worship.</p>
<p>Tyroc laughed, his deep baritone ringing in the relative emptiness of the Bitra Great Hall. "You're right, young Dibny. But most people don't know about what happened that day. I wrote a song about it. Would you like to learn it?" He addresses the children.</p>
<p>The children cried, "Yay!" and nodded vigorously. Tyroc laughed again and started playing a few chords on his gitar, "Okay, listen closely:"</p>
<p>His fingers began playing a gay little ditty, simple and just about the right sound to get the children giggling. Finally, he began singing.</p>
<p><em>"Here's a story, of Holder named Malachite<br />
As a Masterminer he tolerated idleness not at all<br />
(aside: except when he took a nap)<br />
Kept up shop at the Crafthall, Crom then Bitra<br />
Yet he was all alone."</em></p>
<p>Tyroc grinned at the smiles around the group, and continued:</p>
<p><em>"Here's a story, about a miner named Doyle<br />
Who was thrown out of the Craft on his bum<br />
(aside: by Malachite)<br />
One day found some Renegades, they joined together<br />
Yet he was all alone."</em></p>
<p>Tyroc's playing grew to a confrontation of one key and one which he gets closer and closer to modulating to.</p>
<p><em>"But then one day this Renegade attacked this Holder<br />
And it became a fierce battle, no holds barred<br />
(aside: no pun intended)<br />
That three youths would save Mal from Assasination<br />
And these kids would be from the Bitra Guard."</em></p>
<p>Tyroc launches into the chorus, winking at the children:<br />
<em>"The Bitra Guard, The Bitra Guard<br />
All three youngsters formed the core of the Bitra Guard!"</em></p>
<p>"Captain Largand, he must have been born a leader<br />
Calmly giving orders to his Guard, confident and true<br />
(aside: most always, anyway)<br />
That day he spied Doyle drawing out his bow and arrow<br />
Ran toward Doyle, tackled down that awful Rogue!"</p>
<p>"Lieutenant Laurel, well she was quick to cry out,<br />
"I've got you Mal!" and drag him out of the way<br />
(aside: good she was there that day!)<br />
She had hair of wavy gold, just like her mother<br />
And she was tough and strong and tall, and oh so brave!"</p>
<p>Tyroc looked over at Dibny, who seems to be waiting for something.</p>
<p><em>"To Guardsman Clark, loyalty had to be a virtue<br />
And when Doyle shot he lept into his path<br />
(aside: perhaps he tripped?)<br />
Palm got the arrow Doyle had aimed at the Holder<br />
Stupid, but it sure saved Mal's life!"</em></p>
<p>Dibny shot Tyroc a <em>look</em> but soon changed this into his normal goofy grin as the Harper runs to the chorus again.</p>
<p><em>"The Bitra Guard, The Bitra Guard<br />
Clark, Laurel, Largand: the Bitra Guard!"</em></p>
<p>"And since that day Doyle's been locked up in the Barracks<br />
Mal was safe, Clark recovered<br />
(aside: though he's scarred)<br />
Renegades are careful, and never get to close to Bitra<br />
Because if so they'll face the brave Bitra Guard!"</p>
<p>Tyroc launches into the chorus one more time, the children humming along by now:<br />
<em>"The Bitra Guard, The Bitra Guard<br />
All youngsters, brave and strong, the Bitra Guard!</em></p>
<p>The harper finishes with a flourish, and the children clap. He said, "You see, the Guard's here to protect you and me - so give them some credit. Who knows, maybe this song will change your life some day." He looked pointedly at Dibny and winked. Dibny just smiled.</p>
