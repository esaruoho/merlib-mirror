But things don't always happen in average ways. The weather in Colorado is really interesting. Even in town, in my first house in Fort Collins, the snow can catch you off-guard. The first snowfall of the 1995 season came on the next-to-last day of summer! Only 4 days before this 5 inch snowfall it was 90° F.

Here are before and after shots from my back yard. The before is taken just as the snow is starting to fall. The flakes are so big that they look almost like hail stones, but they're really flakes of (very wet) snow. The after picture is the next morning. Note how the trees are hanging lower due to the heavy, wet snow. The small tree in my back yard is actually bent over out of view. I had to knock the snow off of it and it took it most of the week to stand up straight again.

In Denver, 110,000 trees were damaged because their leaves were still there to catch the snow and the it was particularly wet (and heavy). 50,000 homes were without power for as long as 5 days. It was no picnic.
