# Shockwave samples online

*Source page: `~turnip/lwtc/samples/index.htm`*

### Here are some links to cool Shockwave sites

Games sites:

- [Shockwave.com](http://www.shockwave.com) (of course!)
- [Spaced Penguin](http://www.bigideafun.com/penguins/arcade/spaced_penguin/info.htm) - try this game!
- [CleverMedia](http://clevermedia.com/)
- [Robot Duck](http://www.robotduck.com/)

Misc. fun stuff:

- [Yuk-yuk](http://www.yukyuk.com/) - Shockwave doodles. Try Professor Nanweep, Dr. Fingo-ho, Cats, and Dancers - then click on stuff!
- [LeCielEstBleu](http://www.lecielestbleu.com/html/main_zoo.htm) - Trippy eye candy.
- [SetPixel.com](http://www.setpixel.com/) - some incredible visuals using advanced "imaging" lingo. Try "Infinity Bobs" and "Sluglets."
- [Mark Mackenzie](http://markmackenzie.home.mindspring.com/ShockStuff/index.html) - a buddy's Shockwave stuff (he also teaches a Director class); try "Dart 270" (arcade-style game) and "Know Zone" (trivia game).

Some 3-D stuff:

- [Lego Studios](http://www.lego.com/studios/backlot/default.asp?bhref%3Dhttp%3A%2F%2Fwww%2Emach8%2Enl%2Fls%2FLegoStudios%2Ehtm%26)
- [17 Keys](http://insight.director-3d.com/studio/keys/) (someone's game demo; not sure how to play but it's fun to fool around with)
- [Journey](http://www.mano1.com/shockwave3d/journey/) (another game demo; if anyone can figure out how to play - or the point - let me know!)
- [SplineTrack](http://www.theburrow.co.uk/d85/demos/splinetrack03.htm) - way cool racing-game demo!

Some of *my* Shockwave stuff:

- [The No Hole Game](http://www.secondstoryrep.org/sprouts/3just.htm) (written for friends' theater's [website](http://www.secondstoryrep.org/); you'll also find some puzzles)
- [Video poker](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Eturnip/dswmedia/vp/Shock-vp.html) (wrote this in '95 when I was learning lingo!)
- [Orbits](../../samples/MS/Encarta/dswMedia/HTM/IA_Orbits.htm) (interactive piece for Microsoft/Encarta - only works on Windows/Internet Explorer)
- [African photo album](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Eturnip/pa/africa01/) (pix of my trip to Africa in '01 to see the solar eclipse)
- Three games I did for Major League Baseball:
  - [Ballpark Vendor](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Eturnip/samples/ms/imp/mlb/vendor/vendor.htm)
  - [Relief Pitcher](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Eturnip/samples/ms/imp/mlb/relief/relief.htm)
  - [Homerun Derby](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Eturnip/samples/ms/imp/mlb/hrd/homerun.htm)
- [Pokémon](http://www.wizards.com/pokemon/DemoFlash/welcome.asp) lessons!
