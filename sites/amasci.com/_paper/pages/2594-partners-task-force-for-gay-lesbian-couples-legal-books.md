<td data-bgcolor="#FAFAFA"><br />
<strong>4 Steps to Financial Security for Lesbian and Gay Couples</strong> by Harold L. Lustig, (1999), $14, Fawcett Paperback Books (Ballentine/Random House). A financial planner with an MBA gives invaluable, detailed advice to reducing your tax burden, increasing wealth and protecting your family.
<p><strong>A Legal Guide for Lesbian &amp; Gay Couples</strong> by Robin Leonard, Hayden Curry, Denis Clifford and Frederick Hertz (10<sup>th</sup> ed. 1999), $25.95 +$4 p&amp;h, Nolo Press, 950 Parker St., Berkeley, CA 94710; 800-992-6656. Complete, accessible guide with detailed methods and forms.</p>
<p><strong>How to change your Name</strong> by David Ventura Loeb &amp; David W. Brown (4<sup>th</sup> ed. 1999), $14.95 +$4 p&amp;h, Nolo Press, 950 Parker St., Berkeley, CA 94710; 800-992-6656. Methods and California-oriented forms.</p>
<p><strong>Legal Affairs: Essential Advice for Same Sex Couples</strong> by Frederick Hertz, (1998), $17.95, Henry Holt (Owl Books), 115 West 18th St., New York, N.Y 10011. Excellent compendium of advice on a wide range of affairs. Read this before you need it.</p>
<p><strong>Lesbian (Out)law: Survival under the rule of law</strong> by Ruthann Robson (1992), $9.95 +$2 p&amp;h, Firebrand Books, 141 The Commons, Ithaca, NY 14850, 607-272-0000. Legal analysis includes discussion of forced separation from lovers, domestic violence and child custody.</p>
<p><strong>Life Planning: Legal documents and protections for lesbians and gay men</strong> (1992), $7.50, Lambda Legal Defense and Education Fund, 120 Wall St., #1500, New York, NY 10005; 212-995-8585; <a href="mailto:general@lambdalegal.org">general@lambdalegal.org</a>; <a href="http://www.lambdalegal.org" target="http://www.lambdalegal.org">www.lambdalegal.org</a>. Vital information on wills, power of attorneys, cohabitation and parenting agreements, and funeral arrangements.</p>
<p><strong>Living Our Lives: Living together agreements for lesbian, gay and unmarried heterosexual couples in Oregon</strong> by Cynthia Cumfer, J.D. (1990), $9.95 +$1.50 p&amp;h, Cynthia Cumfer, PO Box 42281, Portland, OR 97242. Includes sample agreements.</p>
<p><strong>Partnership Protection Documents</strong> (1992), $5, National Center for Lesbian Rights, 870 Market St., #570, San Francisco, CA 94102-3012; 415-392-6257. Durable powers of attorney, physician’s directive, guardianship of minors, etc.</p>
<p><strong>Preserving and Protecting the Families of Lesbians and Gay Men</strong> (1991), $7.50, National Center for Lesbian Rights, 870 Market St., #570, San Francisco, CA 94102-3012; 415-392-6257. Child custody, visitation rights, partnership benefits, same-sex marriages.</p></td>
