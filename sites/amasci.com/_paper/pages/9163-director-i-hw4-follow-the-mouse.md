# Director I HW#4: Follow the mouse

*Source page: `~turnip/lwtc/assg/Director_HW4.htm`*

**MMDP 125 "Director I"\
Homework 4: "Follow the Mouse"**

**Due: Fri. March 8 (beginning of class)\
Value: 10 points**

Create a simple face & place it in the center of the stage. When the movie is running, the eyes should follow the mouse. When the mouse is over the nose, the eyes should cross. If the mouse is clicked on the nose, the eyes should close & the character should say "Ouch!"

BONUS POINT: Make the cursor a fly that buzzes around the face!
