# Snow Outing - February 18, 2001 - Pics by Darlene Rautio

*Source page: `~sams/scws/2001snow1.htm`*

**2001 Snow Outing - Page 1**     (more pictures on [Page 2](2001snow2.htm) and [Page 3](2001snow3.htm))

Sunday, February 18th a group of Samoyed fanciers gathered at the Lake Easton State Park Sno-Park for a SCWS sponsored "Snow Outing". Thanks to George Johnson and Louise Goodman, the Snow Outing gave the opportunity for a number of people to learn about skijoring. Thanks to Cyndy Knighton and her willingness to harness dogs up to run with her boys, Conor and Pete, on her sled team. I'm not sure Cyndy was planning on that many trips! Everyone had a good time, dogs got to play in the snow, puppies got to be socialized and many other trail users got to admire our wonderful breed! 

Here are some visual vignettes of the day...sorry I couldn't get everyone who attended!!

Darlene
