<p><strong>Paul McCartney &amp; Wings: MEGA RARE UK **ABBEY ROAD STUDIOS** 2 EMI CD-R **ACETATES** FOR **WINGSPAN HITS AND HISTORY**</strong></p>
<p><strong>This is an <u>official Abbey Road Reference</u> TWO CD-R ACETATE set for Paul McCartney's<br />
<em>'Wingspan Hits &amp; History'</em> release. Prepared at the Abbey Road Studios on 28 March 2001, in Room 13.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Reference Disc One features 'Wingspan UK Version' and includes 18 tracks (with a duration time of approximately 1 hour and 15 minutes) including 'Juniors Farm' (DJ Edit), and 'With a Little Luck' DJ Edit.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Reference Disc 2 features Wingspan UK, USA &amp; Japanese Version, including 22 tracks (with a duration time of approximately 1 hour and 16 minutes.</strong></li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Each of the CD-R Acetates have the official Custom 'Abbey Road' White labels , and each has the CDR reference printed in the centre of each reference disc. The CDR Acetates each come in a Jewel case with Printed Insert featuring the Custom Abbey Road logo, track listing for each of the CDRs and Name and Studio Number.</strong></p>
<p><strong>Very few of these Significant Abbey Road CDR Acetates become available<br />
- the Ultimate 'Wingspan' collectable - RARE!</strong></p>
<p><strong>Abbey Road referencing CD-Rs are extremely difficult to obtain as only very few are circulated, with not many getting out of the Studio!</strong></p>
