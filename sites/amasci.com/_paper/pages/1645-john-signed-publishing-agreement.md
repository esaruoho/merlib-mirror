| **All of the Lennon-McCartney 1968 composition contracts, regardless of who actually composed the song, were signed by John Lennon, alone, on behalf of the Beatles. Paul McCartney's presence and signature were not needed. Neil Aspinall, the Beatles' trusted friend and Apple executive, signed on behalf of Paul and Maclen Music.** |
