# Web-a-Toons !

Updated twice a week on Mondays and Thursdays

Your browser MUST support backgrounds, background gifs, transparent gifs, and HTML ~3.0 for any of these web-a-toons to make sense. For best effect, maximize your browser window. You can change the layout of some of these cartoons just by scrolling around or resizing the window.

### [Web-a-Toon Archive](archive/default.htm)

![](sep.gif)

Web-a-toons are a very unique way of creating and presenting cartoons using HTML as a part of the cartoon itself. More specifically, the cartoons are created by using a mixture of transparent gifs, background images, background colors, and page layout tags. If any one of these elements is missing, the whole cartoon falls apart. Hence these are really web-cartoons or web-a-toons. I have never seen anyone use this process or technique on the web, so I can very much claim to have invented it. (Hopefully it won't be my only claim to fame). ![](smile.gif)
