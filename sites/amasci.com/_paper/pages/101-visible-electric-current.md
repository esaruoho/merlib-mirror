<td>See what these devices teach? Here's a list. The concepts are somewhat (ahem) shocking. If electrons are the "carriers" of charges of electricity; if electrons are "particles of electricity," then...
<ul>
<li>In an AC wire, the electricity sits in one place and vibrates back and forth.</li>
<li>All conductors are already full of electricity. Electricity is supplied by the metal.</li>
<li>Wires act like water pipes which are always full of water.</li>
<li>The word "Conductor" means the same as "contains movable electricity."</li>
<li>Electricity is not produced by batteries or generators, it flows THROUGH.</li>
<li>Electricity is not consumed by motors or light bulbs, it all just goes through.</li>
<li>Electricity does not flow at the speed of light. It flows quite slowly.</li>
<li>Capacitors don't store any electricity (they store EM energy.)</li>
<li>Individual electrons don't carry the electrical energy.</li>
<li>Electricity is not a form of energy.</li>
<li>Electric current is not a flow of energy.</li>
<li>Circuits are like drive belts, not like strings of coal-cars.</li>
<li>Electric energy and charges of electricity are two different things.</li>
</ul></td>
