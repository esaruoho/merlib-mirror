<td data-bgcolor="#FAFAFA"><br />
<strong>American Couples</strong> by Philip Blumstein, Ph.D., &amp; Pepper Schwartz, Ph.D. (1983), OUT OF PRINT, William Morrow &amp; Co, New York, NY. Detailed, 10-year study of love, sexual preference, money, power and work among heterosexual and homosexual couples.
<p><strong>Gay Couples</strong> by Joseph Harry (1984), Praeger Publishing, New York, NY. Scientific exploration of male same-sex relationships.</p>
<p><strong>Gay in America Reprint</strong> (1989) $4.50, $3 each for 25+ copies, <a href="http://www.glaad.org" target="http://www.glaad.org">GLAAD,</a> 248 West 35th St., 8th Floor, New York, NY 10001; 212-629-3322. Excellent 64-page report by the San Francisco Examiner. Several articles on couples; national poll results.</p>
<p><strong>Journal of Gay and Lesbian Social Services</strong> Vol. 1, #2, Lawrence Kurdick, ed., (1994), Haworth Press, 10 Alice St., Binghamton, NY 13904-1580; 800-342-9678. Academic presentations. Contains key findings from <strong>Partners</strong>’ 1990 survey.</p>
<p><strong>The Male Couple: How relationships develop</strong> by David McWhirter, MD &amp; Andrew Mattison, M.S.W., Ph.D. (1984), $14.95 +$2.96 p&amp;h, Prentice-Hall, NJ. Orders to: Simon &amp; Schuster, Order Processing Center, Box 11071, Des Moines, IA 50336-1071; 800-947-7700 for individuals; 800-922-0579 for libraries. A male couple studies 156 male couples.</p>
<p><strong>Man to Man: Gay Couples in America</strong> by Dr. Charles Silverstein (1982) ISBN 0688-008038, $9.70 + $1.50 p&amp;h, Morrow &amp; Company, New York, NY; 800-843-9389. Orders Dept., Wilmore Inc., 39 Plymouth St., Fairfield, NJ 07004. Psychologist’s sexually frank report, based on 190 interviews.</p>
<p><strong>The Mendola Report: A new look at gay couples in America</strong> by Mary Mendola (1980), OUT OF PRINT, Crown Publishers, New York, NY. Intimate and detailed descriptions of gay couples, based on national survey and interviews.</p>
<p><strong>A More Perfect Union: Why Straight America Must Stand Up for Gay Rights</strong> by Richard D. Mohr (1994), $15, Beacon Press, Boston, Mass. The moral case for equal citizenship; chapter on “Understanding gay marriage.”</p>
<p><strong><a href="survey.html">Partners National Survey of Lesbian &amp; Gay Couples: Results</a></strong> (1990), $4, Partners Task Force, Box 9685, Seattle, WA 98109-0685; 206-935-1206. Reports in-depth information on 1,266 couples. Click here for <a href="orders.html">Partners’ order form</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Social Services for Gay and Lesbian Couples</strong> Lawrence Kurdick, ed., (1994), Haworth Press, 10 Alice St., Binghamton, NY 13904-1580; 800-342-9678. Academic presentations. Contains key findings from <strong>Partners</strong>’ 1990 survey.</p>
<p><strong>Staying Power: Long term lesbian couples</strong> by Susan E. Johnson (1990), $12.95 + $1.95 p&amp;h, Naiad Press, Box 10543, Tallahassee, FL 32302; 904-539-5965. Research on 108 couples together ten years or more. Seven interviewed in depth.</p>
<p><strong>Unbroken Ties: Lesbian Ex-Lovers</strong> by Carol S. Becker, Ph.D. (1988), $9.95 + $1 p&amp;h, Alyson Publications, 40 Plympton St., Boston, MA 02118; 800-825-9766. Easing the transition from being lovers to ex-lovers. Based on nearly 100 interviews.</p></td>
