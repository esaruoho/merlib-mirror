<td data-valign="MIDDLE">Yesterday And Today -<br />
Capitol T/ST 2553 Released Jun 15th 1966. Black Label with rainbow-color band. Referred to as the "first state" cover. Cover has a controversial photo of the group dressed in white butcher smocks showing cuts of raw meat &amp; parts of toy dolls strewn about their apparel. Very few copies were reportedly sold in stores with this cover in it's first state.</td>
