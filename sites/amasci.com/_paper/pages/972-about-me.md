About me

<img src="img/wtcribbon.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" /> <img src="img/king.jpg" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Mug shot" /> I'm kind of a homebody. Don't get me wrong, I like to get out. But I find as I get older, I also like spending time with family or friends just hanging around, too. There's an old saying, "the more people I meet, the more I like my dog." I paraphrase that and believe that the more people I meet, the more I appreciate the truly good people that I call my friends. Really good friends aren't very common and I have more than my share.

I'm proud of [where I come from and how I got where I am.](past.html) I like [what I do for a living](professional.html) and that it allows me to live [where I live,](home/index.html) and to do things that I enjoy with my spare time (like [skiing,](ski/index.html) [hiking and biking,](hikebike/index.html) and [volunteering).](volunteer.html)
