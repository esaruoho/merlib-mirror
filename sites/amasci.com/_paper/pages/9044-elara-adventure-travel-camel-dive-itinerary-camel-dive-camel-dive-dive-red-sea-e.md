<p><em><strong>Day One:</strong></em><br />
Depart New York for Cairo.</p>
<p><em><strong>Day Two:</strong></em><br />
After arriving at the Cairo International Airport, youll take a scenic, 30-minute drive through the city to your hotel in Giza. Hotel check-in is followed by a special welcome party at the home of one of our local tour guides in Nazlet El Samman, a 7,000-year-old village built at the feet of the Sphinx.</p>
<p><strong><em>Day Three:</em></strong><br />
After breakfast, youll take a guided tour of both the Giza Pyramids and of the Sphinx temple complex. Lunch is followed by an optional excursion or some free time. Dinner on your own. Regroup for New Years Eve festivities. Get ready to 'ring in' the New Year in a most memorable way!</p>
<p><em><strong>Day Four:</strong></em><br />
Early morning departure from Cairo to Sharm el Sheik. Transfer to the dive center for a check dive. Lunch is followed by a second dive and a tour of the Colored Canyon. Transfer to your hotel. Overnight in Sharm el Sheik.</p>
<p><em><strong>Day Five:</strong></em><br />
A good breakfast will prepare you for two excellent dives from the famous point of Ras Mohammed in the afternoon. Lunch break at leisure. After your second dive, youll transfer to Dahab. Hotel check in. Overnight, Dahab.</p>
<p><em><strong>Day Six:</strong></em><br />
Breakfast and a check dive. Depart by camel to Gabr El Bint, site of your day's first dive. Lunch is followed by a transfer to Nuweiba where you will have the opportunity to snorkel with a friendly, wild dolphin. Return to Dahab for dinner and a well-deserved rest.</p>
<p><em><strong>Day Seven:</strong></em><br />
Start your day with an early-morning climb up Mount Sinai for the sunrise. After, youll enjoy breakfast a guided tour of Saint Catherine's Monastery. After a few hours of day rest at a hotel, youll depart by camel to Wadi Sagheer for lunch and a late afternoon/early evening dive. Overnight, Dahab</p>
<p><strong><em>Day Eight:</em></strong><br />
After breakfast, you'll do a morning dive at Abu Galum then trek by camel to Blue Hole where you'll have lunch and take an afternoon dive. Overnight, Dahab.</p>
<p><strong><em>Day Nine:</em></strong><br />
Breakfast is followed by a morning dive in The Caves. You'll have lunch at Doda Valley and then camel trek to Assala Village for an afternoon of exploring. Take a few snake charming lessons if you dare! Transfer back to the hotel in Dahab for an overnight.</p>
<p><em><strong>Day Ten:</strong></em><br />
Pack and meet for breakfast. Transfer from Sharm el Sheik to Cairo where youll take a privately guided tour of the Egyptian Museum (highlighted by the magnificent treasures of King Tut). Then, its off to the Old City for a walking tour that will take you back in time to the days of Moses. Over lunch, youll receive a lesson in bargaining before hitting the colorful and fast-paced Khan el Khalili Bazaar. Tonight, youll be treated to a Nile River dinner cruise before your final departure.<br />
