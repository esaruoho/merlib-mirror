A light dusting of snow filters through the rhododendron leaves to the interior path below. On a sunny day, the dark tunnel under the plants opens up repeatedly to splashes of sun in the clearings.

From the gravel path, snow highlights the structure of dogwoods and Japanese maples. dwarfed by the towering pines while reaching through the rhododendrons.
