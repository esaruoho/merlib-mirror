# My Obituary

------------------------------------------------------------------------

More or less as told at 2001 Seattle Folklife Festival's Tall Tale contest

This story won first prize

------------------------------------------------------------------------

One Sunday morning I was awakened by the sound of my girlfriend's cell phone ringing. I heard her answer the phone, and I was trying to ignore it when I heard her say "You're reading what?" then poked me with the cell phone, and said "It's Dan, you'd better talk to him." Dan's an old friend of mine, so I took the phone and said "Hi Dan, what's up?" He said "James you're alive!" I said, "About as alive as I ever am at 7 on a Sunday morning." He said "I'm reading your obituary in The New York Times." I said it was a bit premature to which he responded "I guess so." Then he asked, "Is it true that you once broke a Bulgarian spy ring for the CIA?" I said "You know that I can neither confirm nor deny that I ever worked for the CIA and in any case it was Romania, not Bulgaria."

We talked a little more and after I hung up my girlfriend looked at me with a smile and said "I've never been in bed with a dead man before." Then she got serious and said "You'd probably better call your family before they hear about this from someone else."

So I called my brother back east and and he said "Wait a second while I bring it up on my computer. Hey this is cool. Did you really make props for the Godzilla movie?" I told him I'd just made a thirty foot long cell phone for Godzilla. I called my mom and said "Mom, I'm not dead yet." She said "I'm always glad to hear that, Darling." When I called my sister she started laughing hysterically when I told her and I finally just hung up.

Next I called the New York Times. I finally got hold somebody who knew something and told her there'd been a mistake, that James Thiele wasn't dead. She said, "We checked it and the hospital says that he died after surgery on Friday." Well I actually had some minor wrist surgery on Friday but I hadn't died. So I paged my doctor He called and said "How is your wrist?" I said my wrist felt fine, but the hospital apparently told a New York Times reporter that I died after surgery. My doctor said "I think we'd both know if that had happened. I'll call them and find out what's going on."

My doctor called back and said he'd found out what the problem was. A James Thiele actually did die at that hospital on Friday but he was from Spokane, and had come to Seattle for heart surgery from a specialist. I decided that I should read this obituary and see what it said. what I saw was amazing - except for a few facts such as where I'd gone to college and where I'd been born everything else had been taken from stories I'd told at the tall tale contest over the years. I realized that the reporter had been getting information from someone who was a regular at the contest. So whoever you are if you're out there today I've got just one thing to tell you - keep coming back, by the time I really die we'll have a great tall tale of an obituary for for him to write.
