<td>An 18th-century castrato looks back upon his successful career as a male soprano. He relives the agony of the operation to retain his high-pitched, prepubescent vocal range. He recalls his personal accomplishments, sexual experiences, a beloved teacher, and eventually faces fears of being alone and uncared for in his old age.
<div data-align="center">
<img src="s-sb-1.jpg" data-border="0" width="353" height="411" alt="Steve Bryant" /><br />
Little One and Steve Bryant, countertenor
</div>
<strong>Voice of an Angel: A Castrato Remembers</strong> utilizes authentic 18th-century costume and the period music of Glück, Haydn and Mozart.
<div data-align="center">
<img src="s-sb-3.jpg" data-border="0" width="590" height="329" alt="Little One &amp; Steve Bryant" /><br />
Little One and Steve Bryant, countertenor
</div>
<strong>Voice of an Angel</strong> was written and directed by Demian. The music direction was by Steve Bryant. Pianist was Howard Hoyt.
<p>Steve Bryant, countertenor, performed a solo tour de force, utilizing acuate 18th-century theater gesture, and a marionette figure — four months in construction — created by Demian.</p>
<p>The <strong>Voice of an Angel</strong> was voted <em><strong>“Best of the Week”</strong></em> at the <strong>New City Theater Director’s Festival</strong> in 1986.</p></td>
