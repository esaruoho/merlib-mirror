> 1828 -- US: Premier issue of the "Cherokee Phoenix" published. First U.S. newspaper in a native language, it uses the Cherokee syllabary, was developed by Sequoyah, who assigned symbols to 86 Cherokee syllables.
>
> > Using his new alphabet, Sequoyah taught his daughter to read in less than a week. In 1821, a group of skeptical tribal chiefs mastered the alphabet in seven days, and gave Sequoyah permission to teach the language to the whole tribe. The Phoenix will appear weekly until May 1834.
