<p>Our Greece-AND-Turkey trips sail to our favourite islands in the Dodecanese; from tiny fishing villages where the traditional Greek spontaneity and lively spirit lives on - to the bright lights and party nights of the more developed islands, where one can eat, drink and be merry in modern-day Greece!</p>
<p>Turkey is a land of even more contrasts! From the busy, cosmopolitan port of Bodrum, it's fascinating and fun bazaars, St John's Castle and hopping night life  - to the total contrast of the ancient port of Knidos, dating back to 500BC.... ruins and ancient amphitheatres scattered all around the bay where we anchor.</p>
<p>Our other favourite spots tend to be the quieter, less "touristy" parts of Turkey, where our local friends give us a real family welcome. We explore more ancient sites, luxuriate in Turkish baths - and even learn to belly-dance!</p>
