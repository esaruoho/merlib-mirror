# SCA'96 Weight Pull Workshop

Up through 270 lbs., dogs were baited and coaxed (unless going for points). Weights above 270 were achieved with no assistance.

Those showing a time and ratio (WeighPulled / DogWeight) were going for working dog points and were following IWPA rules.
