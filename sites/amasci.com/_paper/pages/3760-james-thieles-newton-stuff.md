# ***James Thiele's Newton Stuff***

**Games for the Newton that I've released as freeware:**

- [![](animals/animalicon.gif) Animals](animals/index.html) shows some pictures and plays sounds.
- [![](tictactoe/tictactoeicon.gif) Tic Tac Toe](tictactoe/index.html) plays a children's game. Newton plays against person or two people can play.
- [![](states/statesicon.gif) States](states/index.html) shows a U. S. map and shows state names and capitals when a state is tapped on.
