<td><h1 id="att-paradyne-keepintouch-modems-and-the-fujitsu-poqet-pc-plus-handheld-computer">AT&amp;T Paradyne KeepInTouch Modems<br />
and the<br />
Fujitsu Poqet PC Plus Handheld Computer</h1>
There are at least <strong>three different variations</strong> of the <em>AT&amp;T Paradyne KeepInTouch</em> PCMCIA modem. It seems all of these variations will actually work with the PC Plus <strong>but you must upgrade the firmware on the modem first!</strong>
<p>Many thanks to <strong>Danny Ayers</strong> for this information. Danny generously took the time to test the different variations of the <em>AT&amp;T Paradyne KeepInTouch</em> modem on his PC Plus.</p>
<p>I include images of the front and back sides of the three variations of the modem.</p>
<p>It should be noted here that a recent <a href="poqetfax.html">fax from Fujitsu's Technical Support</a> mentions that the <em>AT&amp;T Paradyne KeepInTouch</em> PCMCIA modem will work with the PC Plus.</p>
<p>Also, <a href="poqetmeg.html">some variations of the Megahertz XJ1144 modem</a> will work with the PC Plus.</p>
