<td width="100%"><h1 id="site-tour">SITE TOUR</h1>
<h2 id="getting-started">Getting Started</h2>
<p>If this is your first visit to Guitar Noise get ready to be overwhelmed with information. We have hundreds of pages with lessons, articles, tips and advice. To help you get the most out of this site we have put together this short tour to help you navigate the different departments of this site wisely, without getting lost or overwhelmed.</p>
<p>Learning guitar on the Internet is not such an outrageous idea. We have been teaching people of all ages online for five years and have received thousands of <a href="../about/letters.html">letters</a> from people who wrote to thank us. You are currently surfing one of the most complete and diverse guitar lesson sites on the web. No wonder we are rated among the most popular sites on <a href="http://search.yahoo.com/bin/search?p=free%2Bguitar%2Blessons">Yahoo!</a> and <a href="http://www.google.com">Google</a>.</p>
<p>We have tried to make navigating this site as simple a possible. At the top of each page there are links to each of our main departments: Guitar, Bass, Amps &amp; EFX, Songwriting, and the Other Side. Clicking on one of those links will take you to that department and give you an expanded list of choices. In addition to the navigation at the top we have placed a menu at the left side of the page with links to the most popular features. If ever you feel lost or have a question click on the help link.</p>
<p>We try to offer something for everyone. There are lessons and articles for everyone from beginner to professional. No matter what level you are at, you will probably get the most progress by developing your own study plan and following it. Everyone's study plan with vary depending on what you want or need to learn.</p>
<p>Everything on this website is free, and you don't have to sign up to access certain features.</p>
<p>The next page will introduce each of our departments and give you an idea what sort of curriculum they teach.</p>
<p><img src="images/tips.gif" width="159" height="18" alt="Tips for this site" /></p>
<p><strong>Recommended Browser:</strong><br />
Seventy percent of visitors to GuitarNoise.com use Internet Explorer 5.0 or higher, so most features are designed with that browser in mind. The most recent versions of<br />
Netscape also work very well with this site.</p>
