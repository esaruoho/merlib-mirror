*Source page: `~two/13.htm`*

MIX & MATCH GEMSTONE DISPLAY TRAYS & PADS\
*MAKE A PERFECT DISPLAY FOR YOUR GEMS*

Pick the type and size of tray to best suit your needs, then pick the type and size of pad to finish the display. Trays and cases are beautifully finished black leatherette over strong frames. There are five types of trays available, open top **1**, covered with snap-lid top **2**, removable magnetic lid **3**, hinged removable top **4** and glass top **5**. They come in three sizes full tray – 8 " x 14 ", 1/2 tray – 8 " x 7 " and 1/3 tray – 8 " x 4 ".
