# Gifted Resources from ERIC

## Special Resources for Gifted and Talented Education

- [CEC - ERIC web site](http://www.cec.sped.org/ericec.htm) -- the most comprehensive online source for special ed and gifted information from ERIC.
- [The ERIC Web Page](http://www.aspensys.com/eric/.html)
- [ERIC Digests](http://www.cec.sped.org/ericec.htm) are public domain digests on topics of special interest to the gifted.
- ERIC will respond to email queries. If your browser supports mailto, you can click here to send a question: [Send mail to askeric@ericir.syr.edu](mailto:askeric@ericir.syr.edu)
- [ERIC InfoGuide on Gifted Resouces](http://ericir.syr.edu/cgi-bin/markup_infoguides/Alphabetical_List_of_InfoGuides/Gifted_Talented_Ed-12.94) (last updated 1994, but still useful)
- [List of all ERIC Infoguides](http://ericir.syr.edu/cgi-bin/get_infoguides)
- [ERIC bibliography for TAG Educators](zbib1.html)
- [ERIC bibliography for TAG Parents](zbib2.html)
- [ERIC bibliography for School Reform](zbib3.html)
- [ERIC bibliography for Education Restructuring](zbib4.html)
- [ERIC bibliography for TAG Middle School](zbib5.html)
- [More bibliographies and the online bookstore](zbooks.html)
- [ERIC FAQ Files.](gopher://ericir.syr.edu/11/FAQ/) These references for "Mainstreaming the Gifted" and "Distance Ed" among many others.
- [ERIC Clearinghouses](http://www.aspensys.com/eric/sites/barak.html) are focused on topics of special interest.
- ERIC can also be accessed through the [US Dept Education WWW](http://www.ed.gov/) which includes search tools and links to other resources.
- [ERIC (CEC) products for sale](zericcec.html)
- [Search the ERIC Database](http://ericir.syr.edu/ERIC/eric.html)
