## The Five Matters

\

The sky is made of fire (2),

Our heaven's filled with light (1).

Smoke(3) and steam cloud my horizon,

And shield true cosmic sight.

Water(4)-borne intellects,

Trod solidly on this earth (5);

Call pillared flames (2) a 'vacuum' --

Infinite timelesss rainbow (1),

the 'night.'
