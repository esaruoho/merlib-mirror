### Recommended Books

If you would like an overview of C.G. Jung and his theories, the Jung Society recommends the following books. All of these books are available at the [Society library](library.html), and they can also be purchased from Amazon.com, using the provided link. Keep an eye on this page for more book lists.

**Introduction to Jung:**

- [*Memories, Dreams, Reflections*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0679723951/cgjungsociseat/), by C.G. Jung. (A [cassette version](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0877735549/cgjungsociseat/) is also available.)
- [*Jung's Map of the Soul: An Introduction*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0812693760/cgjungsociseat/), by Murray Stein.
- [*Man and His Symbols*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0440351839/cgjungsociseat/), by C.G. Jung, et. al. (A [cassette version](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/1879557177/cgjungsociseat/) is also available.)
- [*Essential Jung*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0691029350/cgjungsociseat/), by C.G. Jung, introduction by Anthony Storr.
- [*Jung Lexicon: A Primer of Terms and Concepts*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0919123481/cgjungsociseat/), by Daryl Sharp.
- [*Boundaries of the Soul: The Practice of Jung's Psychology*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0385475292/cgjungsociseat/), by June Singer
- [*The Symbolic Quest: Basic Concepts of Analytical Psychology*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0691024545/cgjungsociseat/), by Edward Whitmont
- [*Dreams: God's Forgotten Language*](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/006067055X/cgjungsociseat/), by John Sanford.
