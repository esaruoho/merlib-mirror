# FREUDIAN, VR world in Adobe Atmosphere

\

FREUDIAN started out as a test of tunnel-making in Builder. If you go down through the big funnel, you can still see the original tunnels. It became a gallery/testbed as I gradually added more experiments in Builder and Javascript. I'll keep adding weird new stuff, so keep checking back.\
