**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:Arial">Center files complaint with Internal Revenue Service against People for the Ethical Treatment of Animals (PETA)\
</span>*<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:Arial;color:#999999">Center asks IRS to revoke PETA's non-profit tax exempt status for connections to unlawful activity</span>***

 

**<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:Arial">For the complete text of the complaint, go to <span style="color:black"> <a href="CDFEPetaComplaint.pdf" style="color: blue; text-underline: single; text-decoration: none">www.cdfe.org/CDFEPetaComplaint.pdf</a></span></span>**

 

**<span style="font-family:Arial">Bellevue, </span> <span style="font-family:
  Arial">Washington</span><span style="font-family:Arial"> </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt">― </span>**<span style="font-family:Arial">The non-partisan Center for the Defense of Free Enterprise has filed a complaint against Norfolk, Virginia-based **<span style="color:black">People</span> for the Ethical Treatment of Animals** (PETA) with IRS Commissioner Charles O. Rossotti, asking that PETA's privileged non-profit status be revoked for violations of tax laws and connections to unlawful activity.\
 </span>

<span style="font-family:Arial">Center Executive Vice President Ron Arnold said that the 12-page complaint sets out details of PETA --</span>

- <span style="font-family:
       Arial">Supporting the Animal Liberation Front (ALF), an FBI-declared domestic terrorist group, by acting as a media conduit and providing legal defense funds for a convicted ALF felon linked to PETA;</span>
- <span style="font-family:
       Arial">Stealing trade secrets;</span>
- <span style="font-family:
       Arial">Advocating arson; and</span>
- <span style="font-family:
       Arial">Assaulting business executives\
   </span>

<span style="font-family:Arial">Arnold said PETA is one of the nations most notorious animal rights groups. PETAs philosophy is extreme: animals are not ours to eat, wear, experiment on, or use for entertainment, according to its Web site. Since its founding in 1980, PETAs name has been linked to numerous unlawful acts, ranging from highly publicized anti-fur protest demonstrations to undercover theft of business documents to secrecy-shrouded break-ins and university laboratory arsons. PETA has received recent media notice for its funding of the North American Earth Liberation Front -- a group classified by the FBI as domestic terrorists -- and its funding in support of Animal Liberation Front (ALF) arsonist Rodney Coronado -- ALF is also listed by the FBI as domestic terrorists.\
 </span>

<span style="font-family:Arial">The 1995 Government Sentencing Memorandum of convicted ALF felon Rodney Coronado can be found at <span style="color:black"> <a href="http://www.cdfe.org/Sentencing%20Memo.htm" style="color: blue; text-underline: single; text-decoration: none">www.cdfe.org/Sentencing Memo.htm</a></span>. </span> <span style="font-family:
  Arial">Coronado</span><span style="font-family:Arial"> was sentenced to 57 months in federal prison for the \$2.5 million torching of a Michigan </span> <span style="font-family:
  Arial">State</span><span style="font-family:Arial"> University animal laboratory and is still on parole. According to the prosecutions memorandum, Coronado sent a package containing documents from the torched MSU laboratory to the home of longtime PETA member Maria Blanton for PETA President Ingrid Newkirk. The prosecutor wrote, Newkirk had arranged to have the package delivered to her days before the MSU arson occurred.\
 </span>

<span style="font-family:Arial">Arnold said, "Why hasnt the IRS looked into this? Or Congress? PETA continually encourages unlawful acts. PETA people have numerous arrests. Tax exempt status is for charitable purposes. Theres nothing charitable about encouraging arson. Enough is enough. PETA should be stripped of its tax exempt status.\
 </span>

<span style="font-family:Arial">Center President Alan Gottlieb said, We have been tracking PETA for more than a decade. Ron Arnold and I co-authored a profile of PETA in the 1993 book, Trashing the Economy. What we found was one criminal act after another, all linked in some way to the name of PETA. The IRS has been slack in its oversight of this dangerous group. So we have had to issue a formal complaint to IRS Commissioner Rossotti himself. We hope the IRS will take PETA seriously now.\
 </span>

<span style="font-family:Arial">A history of PETA during the 1980s and 90s can be found at <u><span style="color:black"> <a href="http://www.undueinfluence.com/peta.htm" style="color: blue; text-underline: single; text-decoration: none">www.undueinfluence.com/peta.htm</a></span></u>.  </span>
