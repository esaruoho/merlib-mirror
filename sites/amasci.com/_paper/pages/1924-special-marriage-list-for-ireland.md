These entries were extracted from the Marriage Bonds Index (1751 - 1845) for Cork and Ross counties. You can order and view these films as your local Family History Center (FHC is associated with LDS Church). Or, you can contact the submitter, Diana Hanson, <kylara@inav.net>.
