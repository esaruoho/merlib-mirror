*Source page: `~samoyed/iwpa/9899/Pull40.html`*

National Western Dog Pull\
Denver, Colorado
==========================

Pull: 40

Organizer(s): Longs Peak Pullers - Phil Goerner

Wheels Region: 9

Packed Dirt

Track: Pulled on 2 tracks simultaneously. Some handlers pulled dogs on both tracks - successfully! Make sure to have plenty of weight and workers. --- Pulled in front of 2500-3000 spectators. Pull was part of a 15 day stock show which drew competitors from 4 different states.

Date: 1/16/99

Weather:

Indoors - Not a cloud in the ceiling!
