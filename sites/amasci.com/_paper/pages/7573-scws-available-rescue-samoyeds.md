<p><strong>The following Samoyeds are available for adoption. If you are interested in a Samoyed on this page, please contact  <a href="mailto:rescue@samoyedclubwa.org?subject=Rescue%20Information">Rescue@SamoyedClubWa.org</a></strong><br />
<br />
<em>SCWS Rescue places dogs in new homes in Washington State.<br />
We also accept applications from Northern Oregon, Northern Idaho, and British Columbia.<br />
 </em><strong><em></em></strong></p>
<p><em><strong></strong></em></p>
<hr />
<p><img src="images/abby.jpg" data-border="0" data-align="left" width="200" height="240" /><strong>Abby</strong></p>
<p>Abby is about eight years old and her life lately has been a little rough. When some people saw a car parked by a neighbor's driveway they went to check, and the people in the car dropped Abby out the window and took off. A kind person cleaned her up, took care of her, and contacted rescue.</p>
<p>She is in good health except for stiffness. We recently had Abby x-rayed to find the source of the problem, and found she is afflicted with spondylosis, a type of osteoarthritis of the spine. She is on a program of glucosamine and vitamins and we expect her to improve and live a good long life. She is spayed and up to date on shots.</p>
<p>Abby is a love sponge who wants only to be your friend. She gets along with other dogs (there are five others in the foster home) but we think she would be happier as an only dog or with only one other dog. She leaves the resident cat alone. Because of her stiffness we would like to place her in a home without very young children who might hurt her accidentally.</p>
<p>If Abby sounds like she would be right for your home, she would love to hear from you. <a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~sams/abby.htm">Click Here</a> to see more pictures of Abby.</p>
<em></em>
<p><u>Update</u>: Abby is playing more with the other dogs in her foster home, and although she is stiff she is unfailingly cheerful and loves attention. We still think part of the stiffness is because she was injured when dropped from the car.</p>

<hr />
<p><img src="images/mandy.jpg" data-border="0" data-align="right" data-vspace="4" width="207" height="232" />Mandy<br />
ADOPTED!</p>
<p>Mandy is a 9 year old female that came to rescue when a family was divorced and no could/would make provisions for her.  Unfortunately for Mandy, her life prior to the divorce was not a very good one either. </p>
<p>Mandy had spent most or all of her life chained up outside. The most important event in her life was dinner, and the result was that Mandy was about 30 pounds overweight when she came into rescue.  She has been on a diet and exercise program and has lost 10 pounds so far.  When Mandy first went to her foster home, she had a great difficulty walking around the yard.  We believe a lot of that was caused by the fact that she was chained up and never allowed to move around much. Since being in foster, she has become much more mobile and lively and can actually run around the yard.  She is a very sweet dog, and gets along well with other dogs and cats.  She is spayed and up to date on all her shots. </p>
<p>Mandy is a special needs dog and is going to need a very special and loving family.  When Mandy came to us, she appeared to be incontinent.  Extensive tests were done and it was determined there was no physical cause for this and we believe that Mandy has obsessive/compulsive behavior that manifests itself in excessive drinking of water.  Because of the excessive drinking, she has excessive urinating, and due to Mandys weight and age and lack of prior house training, she sometimes leaks. Update: Mandy's water intake and incontinence is just about cleared up. Now that she knows she will always have water, she only drinks when thirsty!</p>
<p>Mandy is going to need a family that will be committed to keeping her on her diet and exercise program and to monitoring her water intake and occasionally cleaning up after her.  After 9 years on a chain, we are hoping there is a special family that will want to make Mandys last years ever so much happier than her earlier ones.</p>
<p><em>Update: Mandy went to a wonderful new home on June 29th!</em></p>
<p><a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~sams/mandy.htm">Click Here</a> for more pictures of Mandy.</p>
