Read about the NCPA College All-Stars at the Chicago Open in an upcoming issue of [Action Pursuit Games](www.actionpursuitggames.com)!

### Events:

- October 24-27 - Rookie World Cup 10-man in Orlando, FL
- August 23-25 - Rookie Atlantic City Open 10-man
- August 6-11 - Novice Diablo [International Amateur Open](http://www.teameffortevents.com) 7-Man
- June 21-22, 2002 - Rookie NPPL/PSP Chicago Open 10-man at [the Badlandz](http://www.buyshocktech.com/pages/badlandz/badlandz.html) in Crete, IL. 16th Place out of 22 teams.
- May 24, 2002 - Amateur Challenge Cup 10-man, Amateur at [Challenge Park Xtreme](http://www.challengepark.com) in Joliet, IL. Sportsmanship Award and 10th Place out of 10.
