**US State and Federal Census, 1850-1920:\
Uselding(er) Extractions Arranged by State**

**Census takers often struggled with spelling Uselding and later indexers struggled with interpreting the enumerator's handwriting. If you are not able to located someone it may mean their name was just not recorded or indexed accurately, as you can see from some of the following spellings.**

An asterik (\*) below indicates the name as it appears in the census or index. In the "Page" column entries such as 109-13-91 refer to the enumeration district, sheet and line number the entry appears on.
