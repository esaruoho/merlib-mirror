**A Note on iPhone Software Categories:**\
\
- Real [**"iPhone Apps"**](http://www.pure-mac.com/iphone/apps.html) are programs which you can install and run on your iPhone. Apple's announcement of an SDK for developers coming in February may or may not make the hacks moot.\
\
- The [**"Desktop Programs"**](http://www.pure-mac.com/iphone/programs.html) are applications which run on your Desktop Computer (i.e. Mac or PC). They are standard programs which can be used for iPhone development, converting video to iPhone readable formats, etc. etc.\
\
- The [**"Server Side Apps"**](http://www.pure-mac.com/iphone/serverside.html) are server programs (web and otherwise) which run on your desktop computer. You then log into your "home" server with iPhone's Safari and do your thing.\
\
- The [**"Web Apps"**](http://www.pure-mac.com/iphone/webapps.html) section has links to web sites which are specifically made for the iPhone. They may contain games, chat interfaces, actual programs that do something useful or just a pretty interface to a well established site.
