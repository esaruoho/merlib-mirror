**Neko-Con Advertisng Rates and Information **

Advertising in the Neko-Con Flash Program Book is an easy way for the attendees of Neko-Con to see your company with out actually being in attendance at the convention yourself.  And, for exihibtors who are in attendance, it can even help promote your sale at the convention. You can advertise convention specials or mail order sales you may be currently running, as well offer a way for the attendees to easily find information on your business after the convention is over.

**Interior Ads**

Interior ads for the Neko-Con Program book will be Black and White. Please take careful note of the ad space demisions.

<img src="images/ads.jpg" width="385" height="144" />

**Cover Ads**\
**Inside Covers** (7.5x10) B/W \$200.00\
**Inside Covers** (7.5x10) Color \$250.00\
**Outside Back Cover   \$300**

**Reservation Deadline** - We must have reservations of desired space to advertise in the program book no later than Saturday, Septemeber 15, 2000

**Press Deadline** - All materials MUST be to us no later than Saturday, September 22, 2000 This is an absolute deadline No exceptions wil be made.  If you pre-paid for your ad, and the program book has gone to press. Refunds will be handled with AFTER the Convention

**Material Specifications**

**Interior Printing** - The interior of the program book is being printed with a  All Digital Printing Press System which foregoes the use of PMT or Velox films. They can be sent to us in digital format on floppy, Zip or CD-R as well. We can accept files in Photshop 5.5, Pagemaker 6.5, Freehand 7 and Illustrator 7. If you use any custom, or rare fonts, please include them. Please be sure that your ad fits within the specification listed above. If requested, all materials sent to us can will be returned after the convention.

**Cover Printing**\
**Black and White** - Production materials should PMT or Velox print of all ad art with the half-tones and screens in place using at least a 110-line screen, emulsion side down.

**Four-Color** - We will only accept material that has been color seperated with one CROMALIN or similar-type proof. Films should be negative, right-reading emulsion down, using a 150-line screen. Each film must have four-corner registartion marks outside the image area.

**SPECIAL FULL PAGE INFO**\
**Full Page Bleeds** - Page Size: 8.75x11.25 (Trim Size 8.5x11)  Please keep live material (Such as important text) .375 from the bleed edges to allow for trim

All other ads sizes, up to Full Page Non-Bleed, please use the specifications above.
