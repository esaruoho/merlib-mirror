I strap my waterskeeter rivertamer pontoon on top of my Cherokee with adjustable tie down straps similar to motor cycle tie downs.  I have a Yakima rack on top which is a great base.  I have bested speeds of 70mph, and distances as far as Bend Or. and Northern Idaho with my pontoon on top.  I have never had a problem, and would be surprised if I did.  Btw, my pontoon is 8.5 ft long and the weight is about 75lbs.
