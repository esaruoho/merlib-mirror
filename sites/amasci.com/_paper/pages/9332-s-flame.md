\
         Christine Morgan is a popular writer in the [Gargoyles](http://www.eskimo.com/~vecna/gargoyles.html) fandom, and creator of dozens of other stories both [fanfiction](http://www.eskimo.com/~vecna/other_fan_fiction.html) and [original](http://www.eskimo.com/~vecna/original_fiction.html), known best for her erotica. She is a long-time role-playing gamer whose work has appeared in *Pyramid* magazine, *GURPS Villains*, and on the [Steve Jackson Games](http://www.io.com/sjgames) website. She also is the editor of [Avalon Mists](http://www.gargoyles-fans.org/avmists/), a Gargoyles online 'zine on the [Gargoyles-Fans site](http://www.gargoyles-fans.org/), and co-editor and frequent contributor to [Sabledrake Magazine](http://www.sabledrake.com/magazine.htm), an online 'zine dedicated to roleplaying games and fantasy/sci-fi fiction.\
        Her published work to date consists of the [MageLore trilogy](http://magelore.com/) and the first in the subsequent [ElfLore](http://www.rapbooks.com/morgan.html#ElfLore%20I%20--%20Silversilk) trilogy from [Renaissance Alliance Publishing](http://www.rapbooks.com/). She has also written a series of horror novels coming soon to audio book from [TimeFare Productions](http://www.timefareproductions.com) and has a short story in the upcoming anthology [The Book of All Flesh](http://www.allflesh.com/).

    \*\*\*

         Now Christine's talent is available to you, the Silver Flame customer! Let her write your story!

\*\*\*

        You provide the information: names, descriptions, histories, settings ... as much detail as possible.\
        If your story includes action/combat, nudity/sex, or strong language, please specify how graphic you'd prefer it to be.\
        Be aware that Silver Flame specializes in fantasy, magic, and romance. Sci-fi, high-tech, and modern weapons are not the author's area of expertise, but she's always willing to take on a new challenge.
