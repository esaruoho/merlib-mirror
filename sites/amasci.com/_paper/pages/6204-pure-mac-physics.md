<img src="images/macbrownian.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **Mac Brownian Motion 1.3b1**\
\
Mac Brownian Motion is a program that simulates (tah dah...) Brownian Motion. Brownian Motion is exhibited in a variety of situations, including motion of particles. Much of stochastic analysis (introduced in a graduate statistics program) is based on the properties of Brownian Motion. Physicists, engineers, economists, and statisticians all rely on its nice properties. It is primarily an educational tool. In fact, its purpose is (originally) rather narrow: to visualize Brownian Motion in one dimension over time.\
\
\

- **License**: Freeware
- **Author/Publisher**: John D. Johnson
- **Modification Date**: March 1, 1999
- **Requirements**: PPC

[Direct Download](http://metalab.unc.edu/smiley/download/macbm.hqx) File Size: 432 Kb\

<img src="images/partphys.gif" data-align="Center" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> **OnScreen Particle Physics 1.2**\
\
OnScreen Particle Physics simulates a particle-detection chamber, the device used by researchers into the fundamental properties of matter at high-energy accelerator facilities such as Fermilab. OnScreen Particle Physics is primarily aimed at teachers looking for a way to introduce modern physics into their classrooms from a "how we know" perspective, but it is of interest to anyone that wants to know how particle research is done. By enabling students to carry out simulated experiments, OnScreen Particle Physics presents modern physics as a human activity, not just a set of facts. It is accessible to students at various levels, as no advanced mathematics is required.\

- **License**: Demo, \$84
- **Author/Publisher**: Robert Estes
- **Modification Date**: November 17, 1998
- **Requirements**: 680x0 or PPC

[Direct Download](ftp://www.onscreen-sci.com/Mac_OnScreen_Demo.sit.hqx) File Size: 853 Kb\
[Parent Directory](ftp://www.onscreen-sci.com/)\
[Home Page](http://www.onscreen-sci.com/) <span id="yp"></span>
