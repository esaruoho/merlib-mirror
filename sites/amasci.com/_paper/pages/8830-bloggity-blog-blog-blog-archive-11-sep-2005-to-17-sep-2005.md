<strong>Rapid Reviews - 14 September 2005 - Part I</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
I only got six comic books this week... and I still haven't read them all. Very unlike me.<br />
<br />
<strong>Action Comics #831</strong>: "Black &amp; Blue": I wasn't sure what to make of this one in a lot of ways. The main plot with Black Adam against Superman was sensible enough, but it lacked drive. It just seemed to meander into action. The Bizarro back-up was completely nonsensical, which is to be expected, but just didn't work for me. In short, I didn't really like this one. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Action_Comics">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0020.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Star Wars: Empire #34</strong>: "In The Shadows Of Their Fathers": I picked up this one for my friend, and read it. I was a bit confused at first because Luke was taking about his father as if he didn't know much about him. Well, then I noticed that Luke had both hands and the whole thing made much more sense. I wouldn't mind reading the whole storyline, as this ending seems pretty good. A decent issue. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Star_Wars:_Empire">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Green Arrow #54</strong>: "Heading Into The Light": More tie-ins to the huge crossover event, as Green Arrow hunts down Doctor Light. And we get to see Doctor Light and Doctor Light. This one is not bad at all, as it builds nicely to the cliffhanger. A pretty standard superhero book. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Green_Arrow">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a>

<strong>Review Copy Reviews - AIT/Planet Lar</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Just as I was adding up the comic book order and discovering that we don't have quite enough money to swing getting <a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/5fists.shtml">Five Fists of Science</a> this month (hopefully we can order it later), a package arrives from Larry with two books in it! I was delighted to get Electric Girl, which I knew I would enjoy, but I wasn't as sure about Full Moon Fever... except for the concept, which is just so cool...<br />
<br />
<strong><a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/fmf.shtml">Full Moon Fever</a></strong>: Ok. Werewolves. Cool. Werewolves on the MOON. Excuse me, but can you <em>beat</em> that for a concept? It's better than "<a href="http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0417148/">Snakes on a Plane</a>" by a longshot. I mean, think about it. Werewolves... on the moon! Heh. Werewolves... heh... moon... heh heh... Oh right. I'm reviewing. Sorry.<br />
<br />
There were a few too many characters to keep straight at the beginning of the book, but that problem didn't last too long. The artwork helped a lot in keeping the main characters clear, particularly Zeke, who is a face in the crowd at the start of the book. And the story... it was mostly pow after biff after bang, all fun, fast and gross. Gross? Well, yeah. The main characters are plumbers on the moon. Ok, not as cool as werewolves on the moon, but still very, very cool.<br />
<br />
So, the two concepts colliding make for a nice little story. Plumbers versus werewolves on the moon. You can probably guess which group comes out better. And yet, while you might predict the ending, the ride is definitely worth it. Heh. Werewolves on the moon. Heh. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/electricgirl.shtml"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200506_ElectricGirl.jpg" data-border="0" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" /></a><strong><a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/electricgirl.shtml">Electric Girl Vol 3</a></strong>: Another excellent collection of stories featuring Virginia the Electric Girl and her wonder dog Blammo! Speaking of, Blammo has some <em>really</em> good bits in this one, including Oogleeoog getting at him without ever getting near him. And we also get the introduction of Robo-Blammo and an earlier version of the main story from issue #1 that showcases a slightly different visual style. Oh, and the bad hair day story was great too. If you haven't already. I recommend you guys all get all the volumes of this book. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0040.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="4 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
Both these book should be out in stores right now, so run down and check 'em out. Anyone who wants a fun, slightly horrific adventure, get yourself a copy of Full Moon Fever. And just about anybody would enjoy Electric Girl.

<strong>Rapid Reviews - 8 September 2005</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Yaaa! Reviews done before I see the next week's comic books! Yaa for me!<br />
<br />
<strong>Superman #221</strong>: "Jimmy's Day": Bizarro is update at the way Jimmy treats Clark Kent, and so fun ensues. I was mildly amused by this one, but only mildly. Bizarro done right can be great fun, this didn't hit me as one of those kind of stories, in part because the ending had to tie-in with Villians United. It's just an average superhero story. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Superman">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Superman/Shazam: First Thunder #1</strong>: "A Face In The Crowd!": This is very much an introduction issue, set early in the career of Captain Marvel. Artwork is good, but can't say a lot about the story yet since it's just started. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Superman/Shazam:_First_Thunder">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Detective Comics #811</strong>: "City of Crime Part 9: The Experiment": The story goes back to the storyline that was interrupted by the crossover. This isn't a bad piece of the tale, and it actually manages to mostly stand on it's own, as long as you accept the opening premise that Batman is undercover. Not bad. About average. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Detective_Comics">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Outsiders #28</strong>: "Letting It Go": An issue of reflection and decision. Almost no action, certainly no villains or threats. Just team members engaged in introspection. And while it wasn't super-compelling, it wasn't really bad either. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Outsiders">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Villains United #5</strong>: "Victims of Aggression": One thing I'm really beginning to enjoy about this series is the number of DC villains I'm seeing, some of which I don't really expect to see pop up. There's that great double-page spread of attacking villains, right as one of the characters makes an announcement that I actually found surprising. I didn't come close to being able to name all the villains, but I recognized a great many more than I expected to. One more issue. Let's see how it turns out. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Villains_United">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Seven Soldiers: Guardian #4</strong>: "Sex Secrets of the Newsboy Army!": Huh. This one gives away a lot of what's going on in the Seven Soldiers storyline. Enough, I think, that I'm not too disappointed that I'm not going to get the rest. I think I'll get the final bookend issue and have a decent idea what is happening, even if I don't know all the details. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Seven_Soldiers">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Justice League Unlimited #13</strong>: "Nuts and Bolts": This is a Steel and Red Tornado story, and another one about the nature of heroes, including ones who aren't human. In addition, we get a subplot from the show. As usual, this was a strong story. I liked it. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Justice_League_Unlimited">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Aquaman #34</strong>: "The Quality of Mercy": I'm slowly being won over by Arcudi's attention to the past of Aquaman and his cast. For instance, Arcudi knows Koryak's nature, which I was not expecting. Koryak acted <em>exactly</em> as I would expect him to. I do find the fight between Aquaman and Tempest to be a bit much, but that's almost the only problem I have with the issue. Actually... thinking hard about it... that would be how the fight would turn out. Aquaman is one of the few heroes whose former sidekick is now much more powerful than him. I just doubt that they would fight in the first place, especially over Mera's situation. What has happened to Mera is actually a nice twist, not something I've seen before. All-in-all, surprisingly good. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Aquaman">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a>
<a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/aqua/"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/aqua/pics/cvr2003/cvra434.jpg" data-border="0" width="200" height="300" /></a>
<p><strong>Noble Causes #13</strong>: I was a little confused at some of the characters in this one, but at least the back page has a list of who is who. Lots of set-up in this one, and lots of flashbacks that explain who is who and why there is so much hate involved. This is the best superhero soap opera out there. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Noble_Causes">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0035.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Oz: The Manga #3</strong>: The Kalidahs and the Queen of the Field Mice. I'm enjoying this book. I love the character designs, and the origins of the Tin Man and the Scarecrow. I don't have a lot more to say, really. This was good, and I'm looking forward to the rest of the series. I'm sure hubby-Eric and I will review the series over on the <a href="http://blogofoz.blogspot.com/">Oz Blog</a> soon. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Oz:_The_Manga">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0040.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="4 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Stardust Kid #2</strong>: "Book 2: The Woman": I want to like this book. It's just not drawing me in at all. I am following the story, but so far there is no emotional connection for me. Abadazad pulled me in from the first page, but this one seems to be flowing around in a different direction. We'll certainly keep getting it, but I hope it hooks me soon. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Stardust_Kid">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a></p>

<strong>Review Copy Review - Dorothy Gale: Journey to Oz #2</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
<a href="http://www.freefallcomics.com"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200508_DorothyGale.jpg" data-border="0" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" /></a><strong>Dorothy Gale: Journey to Oz #2</strong>: It had been long enough since I read the first issue that I had to pull it out and reread before I got more than a few pages into this second issue. This is a complicated and interesting story, and if you want to follow it, you need to <a href="http://www.freefallcomics.com/order.htm">order</a> the first issue. If you can't, here's an inadequate summary: Dorothy and Toto fall into a cellar while being chased by an old salesman for stealing his blanket, and end up somewhere else. There's a LOT more in the book, but that's the quick summary to get you up-to-date. Now, on to the second issue.<br />
<br />
This is a very twisted and different version of Oz. We learn in the first issue why it's that way, but in this issue things just keep getting worse as we're introduced to the Scarecrow and Tik-Tok, along with paranoid Munchkins. The story moves along <em>fast</em>, and it's almost difficult to keep up with it while reading. And Toto is a riot. I'm telling you, most original Toto ever.<br />
<br />
I <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/02/rapid-reviews-9-february-2005-part-i.html">wasn't thrilled</a> with the first issue... I gave it an "average" rating. But this one... this is a little bit different. A bit better. I can see why 64 pages were needed to get to another good stopping point. Fans of Oz and fantasy will want to check this out. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />

<strong>Flipping Through Previews...</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/Aaaaagh.jpg" title="AAAAAUUUGGGGGHHHHH!!!!" data-align="right" width="100" height="75" alt="AAAAAUUUGGGGGHHHHH!!!!" />Yes, it's that time again... time for "Things in Previews That Look Interesting But I Can't Afford To Get (with a list of things I am getting just for contrast)". I'm going to start with the bulk of the book, and then come back and cover the "big four" at the end. Please remember, I'm listing what looks interesting. My actual purchase list is going to be much smaller. The numbers after each title are usually the page number in <a href="http://previews.diamondcomics.com/">Previews Magazine</a>, so you can follow along at home.<br />
<br />
Let's start with the covers. I have no problem with the back cover, of Superman sitting on a cloud from All Star Superman. But the front cover showing Witchblade is just embarrassing. I hate having it lying around the house.<br />
<br />
On page 3 there is a full-page ad for <a href="http://mercurycomics.com/">Mercury Comics</a> which is now publishing Atomika. I don't get the book, but the ad... nice logo with date (Dec 2005) against stark black background, stood out enough to notice.<br />
<br />
Moving on, let's hit the Splash Page (8-9). Harlequin Romances are going Manga, says the first article. Now, if they can draw in the Harlequin crowd, that will be impressive. That's a fairly large chunk of the female reading public, sadly enough. Also of interest is Local, of which I've read and <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/09/review-copy-review-local-1.html">reviewed</a> the first issue.<br />
<br />
On page 12 and 13 there are "gift ideas", and on page 13 is a Justice League Aquaman paperweight. Gotta have it.<br />
<br />
Ok, time to skip ahead in the book... past the "top four" and into the <a href="http://www.wizarduniverse.com/">Wizard</a> (190-197) section. Apparently the Twisted Toyfare Theatre collections are doing really really well, as they are now on volume six. Right.<br />
<br />
Ok, into the Featured Items section (198-201). If there is enough money to get it, I'm getting Five Fists of Science from <a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/">AiT/Planet Lar</a>. No need to feature this one... More on that in a bit. The George Perez Art Book looks cool, but I'll have to pass. I originally intended to skip the new mini Angel: Old Friends, but the first mini is really very good, and I like Angel, so I may indulge after all. And, of course, there's no question that Local will make the list.<br />
<br />
Page 202 is an ad for the Titans Companion which I would love to get, but I want the JLA one first.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200509_FiveFists.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" />On page 208 there's another cheer for Five Fists of Science, this time a Staff Pick. I don't think this book is being overpromoted. It looks like fun. I won't know for sure until I hold it in my hands, but it really looks like a LOT of fun. And on page 213 we finally get to the actual solicit for it, from <a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/">AiT/Planet Lar</a>. Mark Twain, Nikola Telsa, J.P. Morgan, and Thomas Edison battling it out in New York? I'm so there. I'm a little jolted by the price ($17.95), but I'm hoping we can swing this one this month. And, in case you weren't convinced by the promotion, there's some art samples on page 214.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.aliasenterprises.com">Alias</a> (214-221) has the usual sampling of books, but Lullaby #3 is the one that will make our list. I'm slightly interested in Ed's Terrestrials and Daniel, but not enough to spring for them.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200509_OzTheMange.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />Next up is <a href="http://www.antarctic-press.com/">Antarctic Press</a> (225-229) and the can't-miss Oz: The Manga #6. An excellent series so far, I'm looking forward to seeing the rest.<br />
<br />
We'll no doubt get Futurama Comics #22 from Bongo (246).<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.checkerbpg.com/">Checker Book Publishing</a> (248-250) has Star Trek: The Key Collection Vol 5. I can't remember if we ordered the last one or not. *sigh* I would get it if the money allowed.<br />
<br />

<strong>Entourage</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
In episode eleven (officially <a href="http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode19.html">Episode 19</a>) Vince and Mandy are outed as an item by <a href="http://www.nypost.com/gossip/pagesix.htm">Page Six</a>, a daily gossip column in the New York Post. This leads to concerns that Aquaman will be known as a home-wrecker, since Mandy was engaged to be married in only three weeks and broke off the engagement. Eric's new job is keeping Mandy and Vince apart when photographers might be around. Except for the worry that rumors will sink the movie, not much Aquaman in this one.
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage12_01.jpg" title="Vince and Harpoon" width="300" height="225" alt="Vince fires" /><br />
<em>That's the seventeenth whale you've killed today!</em>
<p>Lots of fun in episode twelve (<a href="http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode20.html">Episode 20</a>). When Vince is late for his physical, and makes Mandy late as well, the agents have a major meeting to determine what to do. Meanwhile, Turtle "discovers" a new singer and goes to see if he can help the guy sell his song. The fun part of this episode for Aquaman fans is the appearance of a mechanical harpoon which Vince is practicing with in one scene.</p>
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage12_03.jpg" title="Vince and Harpoon" width="300" height="225" alt="Vince" /><br />
<em>This thing is impossible.</em>
<p>Everything falls apart in episode thirteen (<a href="http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode21.html">Episode 21</a>) when Mandy admits she doesn't love Vince and Ari stages a failed takeover of the agency. Not much of anything Aquaman in this one... except for the bit that Vince is quitting the series because he can't bear to work with Mandy when his heart is broken.</p>
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage14_01.jpg" title="James and Vince" width="300" height="225" alt="James and Vince" /><br />
<em>The thing about Aquaman is, there's a vulnerability to him... Aquaman is the voice of the Ocean.<br />
</em>
<p>The finale of the season, episode fourteen (<a href="http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode22.html">Episode 22</a>), shows everyone trying to convince Vince to stay with Aquaman, which nobody can do. But Vince's meeting with James Cameron turns him around. The final shot is of the Aquaman poster, so you know it was a happy moment for me. We also got to see Aquaman's outfit on the Aquaman action figure that Cameron gave to Vince, and we got to hear Cameron explain some of his ideas for Aquaman. Listening to him made me want to see this movie.</p>
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage14_02.jpg" title="Vince and Aquaman" width="150" height="225" alt="Vince and Aquaman" /><br />
<em>He already had these made.</em>
<p>Overall, the treatment of Aquaman in this series was surprisingly respectful. True, Aquaman is a plot point more than anything else, but the way they portray Aquaman is good. There are no "Aquaman is lame" jokes, and very little negative at all. And apparently, looking at the action figure, Aquaman will be blond... What will Vince look like blond?</p>
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage14_03.jpg" title="Close-up Aquaman" width="100" height="100" alt="Close-up Aquaman" />
<p>I'd like to see the Aquaman logo unobstructed. And I hope we get to see <em>something</em> of the film in the third season of the show.</p>
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage14_05.jpg" title="Aquaman Poster" width="200" height="220" alt="Aquaman Poster" />
<p>And lastly, here are some other thoughts about the finale: <a href="http://maringuy.blogspot.com/2005/09/three-observations-after-long-weekend.html">Maringuy</a> predicts that <em>Aquaman</em> will bomb. From a narrative point of view, it makes a lot of sense, and Maringuy has some good points. But we're talking about a movie directed by James Cameron, and written by Andrew Kevin Walker. I have my doubts that it would be set up to fail. And <a href="http://swiftchancellor.blogspot.com/2005/09/off-and-on.html">The Swift Chancellor Report</a> thinks we won't see any shooting of <em>Aquaman</em>. Agreed. I think the next season will pick up after the movie is done. Entourage is about the times between jobs, since actors work HARD during movies.</p>
