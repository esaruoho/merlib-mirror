# Radio and other junk

From 1988-1997 I was involved with [WMBR-FM](http://wmbr.mit.edu), MIT's radio station, as a DJ and in management positions. Most recently I initiated the construction of their website and began preparations for regular Internet distribution of [WMBR](http://wmbr.mit.edu) audio.

My move to Houston in 1995 hampered my ability to contribute quite so actively towards [WMBR](http://wmbr.mit.edu), but it served as an interesting experiment in telecommuting. For 1996-1997 my main radio project was putting Jerry Brown's "[We the People](http://www.wtp.org)" show on the 'net. Please [visit the project page](http://www.wtp.org/radio).

Over the years I have hosted three shows at WMBR: *RPM*, a late-80s Progressive/Alternative/Europop Friday night dance show, *The Bernie and Ert show*, a Monday afternoon experiment in whatever I wanted at the time, and for the last three years I was in Boston, *[Kids in the Hall](kids/)*, a weeknight exploration of [Canadian Music](http://www.wimsey.com/~davidh/cmusic.html), co-hosted with [Sanjay](http://www.mit.edu:8001/people/sanj/home.html).

Summer '96 I did a morning-drive "standard college radio" show on Rice University's [KTRU](http://www.rice.edu/projects/ktru/).

Nowadays I do various things on [KPFT](http://www.kpft.org), the local Pacifica station (sometimes music, sometimes traffic reports...), and also I'm pretty interested in the [A-Infos Radio Project](http://www.radio4all.org/radio).
