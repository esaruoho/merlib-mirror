<td colspan="2"><sub>Pacific Northwest Giant Pumpkin Grower's</sub>
<p>We would like to invite you and your family to become members of the Pacific northwest Giant pumpkin growers (PNWGPG). Our organization is dedicated to creating goodwill and friendship through the fascinating sport/hobby of growing Giant Pumpkins</p>
<p>As a member of the PNWGPG, you will receive at least 3 newsletters each year with helpful and informative articles relative to growing the "big ones". Club activities presently include a late winter/early spring growers meeting complete with seed raffle, and we have two Giant Pumpkin competitions to round out the schedule.</p>
<p>The Northwest site of the Great Pumpkin Commonwealth Giant Pumpkin Weigh-off, which currently has 21 sites in North America, is held on the first Saturday of October.</p>
<p>For visiting this page, we would like to provide some information on growing pumpkins. Come and see what a past world record <a href="joel.html"><strong>champion</strong></a> has to say</p></td>
