<h3 id="exploring-the-power-of-creativity."><em>Exploring the power of creativity.</em></h3>
<strong>These Web pages reflect a wide range of interests, especially writing, film, and literature. I do a considerable amount of writing because I am in an MFA program in creative writing, and some of that work appears here. I hope you will find something that interests you. I am constantly adding new content, so please visit often. I also enjoy <a href="mailto:mason@mason-west.com">e-mail</a>, so please feel free to send along questions, comments and so on. My response time varies with the season, but I try to answer everyone.</strong>
