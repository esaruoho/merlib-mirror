<p> </p></td>
<td width="430" data-bgcolor="#FFFFFF"><strong><em>Anne of Green Gables</em></strong><br />
<strong>Lakewood Players</strong>
<p>November 29-December 22, 2002</p>
<p>Yes, dear ones, I'm back to my favourite genre of character - mother of an emotionally volatile teenager! Woo-hoo!! <em>::chuckle::</em> I'm playing Marilla Cuthbert in <em>Anne of Green Gables</em>. What fun, eh?</p>
<p>Seriously, this is a great show with a fabulous cast - in the round at the Lakewood theatre just south of Tacoma. The show opens the day after Thanksgiving and runs through December 22, 2002.</p>
<p>For more info on tickets, directions to the venue, etc., check out the Lakewood Players website: <a href="http://www.lakewoodplayers.com/">http://www.lakewoodplayers.com/</a></p>
<p>For those that find this kind of role to be your fave me to play, I have to say, this one rivals <em>The Cripple of Inishmaan</em> or <em>Little Women</em> or <em>I Never Saw Another Butterfly</em>. This show is an amazing project, an all-new adaptation by Scott Campbell, and I'm playing in a venue that has seen many of my friends grace its stage.</p>
<p>Yes, it's so totally worth the commute! So, come on down to the show!</p>
<p>And, thanks again to everyone who made it down to the Bagley Wright this summer for <em>Trial by Jury</em> and <em>The Sorcerer</em>. You all so rock! I love you guys! <em>:: big bear hugs::</em></p>
<p>As always, may you have as much peace, light, health, hugs, music and happiness as you can comfortably tolerate in your lives, dear ones.</p></td>
