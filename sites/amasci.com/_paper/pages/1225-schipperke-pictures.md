# Here there be Schip Pics!

------------------------------------------------------------------------

<span class="small">If you have pictures of Schipperkes you'd like to see included here, send them to *baubo@eskimo.com*. I can't include everybody's pic, but I'll put up as many as my server storage will allow.  If you have a pic up on your own page, I'll put a link to it from the "Other Schips on the Internet Page".</span>

<span class="small">Please, if you send me pics via email, only send one - your best one.   I love seeing your pics, but getting them from downloaded email is slow on my connection. And remember, give them an unusual name so I can identify them easily. You wouldn't believe how many schip.gifs I have! :) (You might consider using your last name instead of the dog's name.)  Thanks!</span>
