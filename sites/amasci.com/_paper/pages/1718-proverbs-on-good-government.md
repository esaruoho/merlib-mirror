# Proverbs on good government

*Source page: `~bradkir/bible/home/subjects/govt.html`*

Proverbs...on good government:

    Pr.14:35 

    A king delights in a wise servant, but a shameful servant incurs his wrath.
    (NIV)

    Pr.16:10 

    The lips of a king speak as an oracle, and his mouth should not betray justice.
    (NIV)

    Pr.16:12 

    Kings detest wrongdoing, for a throne is established through righteousness.
    (NIV)

    Pr.16:13 

    Kings take pleasure in honest lips; they value a man who speaks the truth.
    (NIV)

    Pr.20:26 

    A wise king winnows out the wicked; he drives the threshing wheel over them.
    (NIV)

    Pr.22:11 

    He who loves a pure heart and whose speech is gracious will have the king
    for his friend. (NIV)

    Pr.25:4-5:

    4 Remove the dross from the silver, and out comes material for [a] the silversmith;
    5 remove the wicked from the king's presence, and his throne will be established
    through righteousness. (NIV) 

    Pr.29:4 

    By justice a king gives a country stability, but one who is greedy for bribes
    tears it down. (NIV)

    Pr.29:14 

    If a king judges the poor with fairness, his throne will always be secure.
    (NIV)

    Pr.31:3-5:

    3 do not spend your strength on women, your vigor on those who ruin kings.
    4 It is not for kings, O Lemuel--not for kings to drink wine, not for rulers
    to crave beer, 5 lest they drink and forget what the law decrees, and deprive
    all the oppressed of their rights. (NIV)
