<td><dl>
<dt>In the name of Light arising, I call the East Wind to your side.</dt>
<dd>
May your mind glow with the golden light of inspiration.
</dd>
<dt>In the name of Light increasing, I call the Fires of the South<br />
to light your path.</dt>
<dd>
May your destiny burn hot and clear, and warm all who you meet.
</dd>
<dt>In the name of Light decreasing, I call the Waters of the West<br />
to soothe your fears.</dt>
<dd>
May your heart find comfort and respite in the gentle waves.
</dd>
<dt>In the name of Light returning, I call the Northern Mountains<br />
to stand beneath your feet.</dt>
<dd>
May the solid rock of a safe haven always greet you, coming home.
</dd>
</dl></td>
</tr>
