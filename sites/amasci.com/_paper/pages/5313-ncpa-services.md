# NCPA Services

*Source page: `~ncpa/services.html`*

## NCPA (Free) Services

The NCPA offers several free services to college paintball and its clubs, teams and players.

- 
- Paint Sponsorship - All college clubs and teams may purchase NCPA paint at \$32/case Rec Grade and \$38/case Tourny Grade, plus actual shipping charges. You also have exclusive access to low prices on many other brands of paint. [Click Here](discount.html) for more details.
- Gear Sponsorship - The NCPA offers discounts on various products through multiple retailers and distributors \*EXCLUSIVELY\* to college and high school clubs and teams, ranging from 5% off and free shipping to 66% off retail. [Click Here](discount.html) for more details.
- [College Paintball Events Calendar](http://www.eskimo.com/~ncpa/cgi-bin/events.cgi) - The College Paintball Events Calendar is where you should list events by or for college paintball, from your club rec events to club fundraiser tournaments to intercollegiate competitions. This is a great way to get attention for your event from players and industry alike, so if you're looking for a college event in your area to attend or just want to know where you can find college players in your area, this is the place to look!
- Online Registration for your events - Is your college hosting an event? Recreational, scenario, for student organizations at your school or a fundraiser with teams in the area, if your club or team is hosting an event, the NCPA can provide online registration for it. For this season's form, <a href="http://www.register.college-paintball.com" target="_parent">Click Here</a>. E-mail online registration setup requests to [Chris (raehl311@yahoo.com)](mailto:raehl311@yahoo.com).
- Free domain name hosting - Get www.yourclubname.college-paintball.com to point to your website and replace any cryptic web adress you may have now, without having to pay to register the domain name yourself. Free to any college club as long as www.college-paintball.com exists. Just e-mail your request to [Chris (raehl311@yahoo.com)](mailto:raehl311@yahoo.com).
- <a href="http://www.collegelist.college-paintball.com" target="_parent">The Official College Clubs List</a> which lists all of the college clubs with other information. This list helps other students, clubs and sponsors get in touch with you. The list is dynamically updated, so make sure to add your club today!
- <a href="http://www.fieldlist.college-paintball.com" target="_parent">The NCPA Friendly Fields List</a> lists fields that have agreed to give you a discount just because you're a college player. Does your field already give college players a discount? Encourage them to list themselves for free added publicity!
- <a href="http://www.match.college-paintball.com" target="_parent">The NCPA Match List</a> helps you find other students at your college and in your area to coordinate with to form clubs and run events.
- [The College Paintball Webboard](http://pub65.ezboard.com/bnationalcollegiatepaintballassociation) - without advertising! Also get a forum for your own club (also without banner ads) for free! (Just post in the "Create a College Club Board" forum.)
- Conference Website Hosting - Trying to start a new college conference? The NCPA can give you the web space to get your presence (and information) on the web.
- General Event Support - Need help selling your event to your college or fields? Getting in touch with college friendly sponsors? The NCPA is full of people who've probably already done what you're trying to do. Get help on the web board or from one of the people in the [Contacts](contacts.html) page.
