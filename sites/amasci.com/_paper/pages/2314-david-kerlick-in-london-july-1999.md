# David Kerlick in London, July 1999

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 7/14-15 Trip & Arrival

Reading "Sleeping Where I Fall" by Peter Coyote

Meet DJ at the King's Arms. Wagamama Waits, Marble Arch, Hyde Park, Serpentine Lake, Snoodling on the Grass at Home.

### Fri 7/16

British Museum, Warren Cup, Coins, Stolen Goods, the K.A. again, mistaken ID.

### Sat 7/17

Leighton House, Holland Park, Kensington, Frolic with BT.

### Sun 7/18

Columbia St. Flower Mart, Highgate Cemeteries, Hampstead Heath

### Mon 7/19

Tate Gallery: Turner WC's, Abracadabra, Picadilly, Old Compton, 7 Dials, the Mother Church of Cheese. Reprise DJ.

### Tue 7/20

Greenwich Observatory, Gay Pub, Naval College, Thames Cruise. DJ again, and again.

Weds 7/21 5 AM taxi to Heathrow, via Prage and Kosice to Uzhgorod, Ukraine
