<p>Hence the reason we sail south to Turkey for this time of year ! It is quite amazing how much difference there is, given the short geographical distance. The main tourist crowds have gone home, and there is a beautifully relaxed and mellow ambiance. Still great swimming/sunbathing temperatures, but not too hot to go explore the many antiquities around the bays where we anchor.</p>
