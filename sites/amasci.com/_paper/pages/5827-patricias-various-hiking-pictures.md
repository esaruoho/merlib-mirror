# Patricia's Various Hiking Pictures

## To my [hiking](http://www.eskimo.com/~pbender/hikes/hike.html) page

## To my [NBA](http://www.dfw.net/~patricia/index.html) page

Here are some selected pictures from the hikes described on my hiking page. There are 132 pictures here, so it will take a little bit of time to load. You can also go to the [index](index.html) which is an index listing (doesn't bring up the pictures) and bring up the photos individually or by state. These photos were taken with a basic 35-mm point-and-shoot camera. Anyone can take good pictures - just take your time, look for the best angle, notice everything that is in the picture frame, watch that thumb and hold the camera steady, use good film and good processing, notice where the sun is at (best when it is behind you), and pray for good weather. Enjoy!
