<h2 id="ah-legionmush---we-hardly-knew-ye">Ah, LegionMUSH - We hardly knew ye!</h2>
Version 3 of LegionMUSH, which diverged slightly from DC Comics' Thirtieth century Legion universe after Legion of Super-Heroes (fourth series) #39 and the formation of the Legionnaires, began on arms.gps.caltech.edu, with a MUSH date of spring 2995. Later, it moved to its final resting place, muds.okstate.edu.
<p>DC Comics' redefining <em>Zero Hour</em> mini-series effectively "rebooted" the timeline of the Legion universe (hence the term "preboot" given to LegionMUSH). As a result of the reboot, the continuity that was part of the old MUSH is no more - except in the hearts of those who played the game and read the comics.</p>
<p>LegionMUSH is no more, Long Live the Legion Outpost portion of <a href="telnet://206.16.238.1:1800">DC Universe MUSH (206.16.238.1 1800)</a>!</p>
<blockquote>
-- Steve Mohundro, aka <a href="kidkid.html">Ol Gardan</a> @ Outpost, and at least the following on LegionMUSH: Kid Kid (Gene Gray), Loomis, Warden Tsaquin, Prince Evillo, Garridan Ranzz, Dr. Mantis Morlo, the former Brin Londo, and formerly a certain fellow who's got <a href="../why-kent.html">a special place in my heart</a>: <a href="../../lsh/shakespeare.html">Kent Shakespeare</a>
</blockquote>
