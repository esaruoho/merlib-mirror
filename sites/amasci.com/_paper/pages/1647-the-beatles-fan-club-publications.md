<td>Throughout the "1960's" <strong>The Beatles (U.S.A.) LTD.</strong> based out of Radio City Station in New York, N.Y. issued monthly and special issue fan club publications. It is unknown just how many subscribers there were at various times but it must of been many. "Hey, the Beatles were popular right"? Below are some of the issues I have in my collection. These are in VG++ condition. The April 1966 issue of the Beatle Bulletin has a ripped page due to two pages originally stuck together because it wasn't cut properly at the printers. Anyway, I have these up for viewing so people that haven't seen them before can see what they looked like.<br />

The above are four photo albums, one for each member. Each one two pages plus the cover and back with 6 photos.
<em><strong>On the inside page is a statement as follows:</strong></em><br />
<strong>"Hello,<br />
Here is your exclusive Beatle Photo Album - and we're sure that you're going to enjoy it.<br />
We have received these photos through the courtesy of BEAT PUBLICATIONS LTD and Johnny Dean, Editor of the BEATLES MONTHLY BOOK. They are samples of the top quality material which subscribers receive each month.<br />
Best wishes from all of us to all of you. Sincerely, BEATLE'S (U.S.A.) LIMITED"</strong></td>
