**<span id="Cuddles and candids">Cuddles and Candids</span>**

Photos from 2001 and previous are now on a [new page](2001pix.htm)

Ain't friendship sweet? It's great to know that the cast got along so well on-set and off, and have continued to maintain their friendships even long after basic filming was complete. It's really refreshing to see young male stars unabashedly showing their affection for their friends, and I hope they and the movie set an example for other young men in the future: that expressing your feelings and cuddling with your pals is a good and honorable thing. More than mere boyish rambunctiousness, it's clear that this cast has a great deal of love and respect for each other. In a time where we're all tired of jaded Hollywood backstabbing, and when the world certainly needs love,  they're just what the doctor ordered. **Grouped by date/location. Scroll down for older pics**
