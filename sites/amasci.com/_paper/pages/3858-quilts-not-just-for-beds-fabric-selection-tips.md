<td width="750"><h2 id="quilts-not-just-for-beds">Quilts: Not Just For Beds</h2>
<p>P.O.Box 1342 * Bellevue,WA 98009 * <a href="mailto:jlk@eskimo.com">jlk@eskimo.com</a></p>
<p><img src="webline.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" /></p>
<p>The most frequently asked question I get is "How do you choose your fabrics?"</p>
<p>I am very lucky to have at least six quilt shops within a one hour driving distance from my house. I go to all of the fabric shops and buy whatever colors I need. I start by picking a fabric that I really like. Then I look for other fabrics in the same color family, making sure that they are all different values. I buy AT LEAST five of each color family. Then I go home and make sure all of the fabrics work together. I also make sure that there are no two fabrics in the same color family that are the same value. Sometimes I will find I have holes that need to be filled, then I do go back and look for just the right fabric.<span id="DDE_LINK1"></span></p>
<p>Then I go home and make sure all of the fabrics work together. I check again (this is very important) to make sure that there are no two fabrics in the same color family that are the same value. Sometimes I will find I have holes that need to be filled, then I do go back and look for just the right fabric. When you have decided on your five fabrics in a color family, using 1 1/2 inch squares, arrange in the following order to make sure they will work in a pleasing manner. I have used the five blues designated as B1, B2, B3, B4 and B5, with B1 being the lightest and B5 being the darkest, as an example.</p>
<p><img src="fabric.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" /></p>
