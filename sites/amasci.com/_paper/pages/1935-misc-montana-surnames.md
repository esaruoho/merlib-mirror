# Misc. Montana Surnames

*Source page: `~chance/montana/intro.html`*

*There's been a change! All surnames must now be sent to the appropriate MTGenWeb Project* <a href="http://www.imt.net/~corkykn/countytbl.html" target="_blank"><em>County Coordinator</em></a> *for posting on their county surname pages. Current postings for specific counties are in the process of being moved to their appropriate county surname page. Due to an increased number of postings, the MTGenWeb Project state Surname Registry will be only for posting surnames from unknown counties or for counties which do not yet have a surname page. Thanks!*
