> - **Switchboard cords** 3-conductor cords for use with telephone switchboards. Some have a standard 3-contact (tip, ring, sleeve) telephone plug on one or both ends, and others have a double 2-contact plug\*.
>   - XTE100: 8-foot cord with tip-ring-sleeve plug on both ends. Also makes a good audio patch cord. 2 available. **US\$2.00**
>   - XTE101: 8-foot cord with double plug\* (#327A) on both ends. 4 available. **US\$2.00**
>   - XTE105: 4-foot cord with tip-ring-sleeve plug on one end and spade lugs on other end for connection inside of switchboard. 1 available. **US\$1.00**
>   - XTE102: 5½-foot cord with double plug\* on one end and other end has a special plug for test jacks on step-by-step switches. 1 available. **US\$1.00**
>
> \*NOTE: The double plugs have two tip-sleeve (no ring) plugs mounted in one handle, with the plugs spaced the correct distance apart to mate with two adjacent jacks in a jack panel or patch panel. The cord is three-conductor, with a ground conductor common to the sleeves of both jacks and a separate conductor for the tip of each plug.\
