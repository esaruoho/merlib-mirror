# Gifted Resources Curriculum

- CSMP - Comprehensive School Mathematics Program.\
  A program that introduces advanced topics through stories and models. Some of the 5th grade topics covered exponential patterns, geometric translations, probability games, Gaussian counting method, combinations, and algebraic functions. These are introduced in ways that are meaningful for younger students; they don't know they're doing algebra and transformational geometry. To get more information, contact:\
  McRel Institute\
  2550 S. Parker Rd.\
  Suite 500\
  Aurora, CO 80014\
  email: cheidema@mcrel.org\
  \

- [Swarthmore](http://forum.swarthmore.edu/~steve/steve/mathlessons.html) sponsors a large collection of lesson plans.

- [The Invention and Design Project](http://jefferson.village.virginia.edu/~meg3c/id/id_sep.html) of the University of Virginia - funded by National Science Foundation, the Fund for the Improvement of Post-Secondary Education, the National Endowment for the Humanities, and the Geraldine R. Dodge Foundation.

- Mendicino CA has online curriculum served courtesy of [NASA's Quest Gopher server](gopher://quest.arc.nasa.gov/11/resources/mendocino)

- [Science Programs That Work](ftp://bbs.augsburg.edu/) as collected by Project Kaleidoscope (often too busy to accept new connections). Look in /files/pkal/PTW.

- Center for Gifted Education at the College of William and Mary has curriculum information [by gopher.](gopher://gopher.wm.edu) Also, through the [Center for Gifted Education gopher.](gopher://gopher.wm.edu:70/11/SOE/CFGE) Information is available by phone at: 804-221-2588, or by postal mail to:

           Center for Gifted Education
           College of William and Mary
           232 Jamestown Road
           Williamsburg, VA 23185

- California Math Curriculum is available by mail from:

           Mathematics Framework for California Public Schools
           California State Dept. of Education
           PO Box 271, Sacramento, CA 95802-0271

- Calvert School carries curriculum from pre-school through grade 8. They can be reached by phone at 410-243-6030 or by postal mail to:

           The Calvert School
           105 Tuscany Road
           Baltimore, MD 21210-3098

  [The Carter G. Woodson Elementary School](http://access.digex.net/~rspath/woodson.html) in Baltimore has just started using the Calvert Curriculum in a partnership with Calvert.

- [U Kansas Explorer Math/Science Project](http://http://unite2.tisl.ukans.edu/wwwhome.html) A collection of Math and Science curriculum information.

- [ERIC](gopher://ericir.syr.edu/), its AskERIC [Virtual Library](http://eryx.syr.edu/), and database of [lesson plans](gopher://128.230.33.81%0A)

- [New York State Education Department](gopher://unix5.nysed.gov/) (NYSED) gopher server of Internet resources

- [Vose Elementary Scho ol](http://www.teleport.com/~vincer/starter.html) list of Internet resources -- very well organized

- [Kid's Web](http://www.npac.syr.edu/textbook/kidsweb/) -- A WWW digital library for kids, very well presented -- from Syracuse University [Illinois Learning Mosa ic](http://calliope.ncsa.uiuc.edu/ILM/ILMHome.html) by NCSA, especially the [resources page](http://calliope.ncsa.uiuc.edu/ILM/IntResource/EduNet.html)

- [Brentwood Elementary](ftp://io.com/pub/usr/zboray/lessonplans.gibson), Austin, Texas

- [Scholastic Internet Center](http://www.scholastic.com/), with lesson plans and assorted classroom materials

- [Minnesota Educational Media Organization](gopher://gopher.mankato.msus.edu:70) (gopher)

- [U Mass K12](gopher://k12.ucs.umass.edu) gopher by kids and completed multimedia projects by kids.

- [Plugged In](http://netmedia.com/ims/pi/PluggedIn/PluggedIn.html), a non-profit group which brings computer-based educational programs to children from low-income communities -- curriculum materials, online projects by kids and completed multimedia projects by kids.

- [MAYAQUEST](http://InforMNs.k12.mn.us/mayaquest) -- a project to work cooperatively, with archeologist mentors, studying the collapse of the Maya civilization.

- [The Jason Project](http://seawifs.gsfc.nasa.gov/scripts/JASON.html) -- electronic field trips for student participation

- [CoVis](http://www.covis.nwu.edu/) -- Learning Through Collaborative Visualization

- [On-line projects](gopher://inspire.ospi.wednet.edu:70/11/Curr_Projects) from the Office of Superintendent of Public Instruction, State of Washington gopher

- [Pathways to School Improvement](http://www.ncrel.org/ncrel/sdrs/pathways.htm) sponsored by North Central Regional Educational Labratory has some links to useful materials and a great deal of background material about school improvements.

- [Circumpolar Expedition](gopher://InforMNs.k12.mn.us:70/11/best-k12/polarex) gopher from TIES

- The University of Michigan Dewey School's [Journey North](http://ics.soe.umich.edu/ed712/IAPIntro.html): an experiment in building a web environment that students can expand. Based on the World School's Journey North activity, the primary feature of the experiment is a series of maps which can be altered by students, as they enter in observations of wildlife migration.

- [KIDLINK](gopher://kids.ccit.duq.edu/) gopher

- Intercultural E-Mail Classroom Connections, or [IECC](http://www.stolaf.edu/network/iecc.html): home of the email collaboration lists, with archives of projects.

- [Home Schooling Info](http://www.armory.com/~jon/hs/HomeSchool.html)

- [Computer Curriculum Corporation](http://www.cccnet.com) service is currently free, but the curriculum component will eventually be subscription-based.
