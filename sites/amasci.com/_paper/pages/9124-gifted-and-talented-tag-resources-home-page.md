# Welcome to the Gifted Resources Home Page

**( <http://www.eskimo.com/~user/kids.html>)** Version 1.91 -- [What's New 23-May-2000 (build 53)](znew.html)

This Gifted Resources Page contains links to all known online gifted resources, enrichment programs, talent searches, summer programs, gifted mailing lists and early acceptance programs, including CTY, EPGY, CTD, NRC/GT, TIP, RMTS, ERIC, NCSSSMST, Odyssey of the Mind, and many, many others. It contains links to 4+ years of TAG-L mailing list archives (over 10 MB). It also contains contact information for many local gifted associations and government (mostly US state) programs. As more resources become available, they will be added.

This page is intended to be a convenient starting point for gifted students, their parents and educators to access Gifted Resources, whether on the internet or otherwise.

This is a free information resource. You may view our [Privacy Policy](zprivacy.html).

**Where to Start**  
If you are new to the field of gifted education, here is a [short summary of giftedness](zbrief.html) and beginners guide to using this site. If you have visited this site before, you might like to look at the [What's New](znew.html) section to see what has changed since you last looked.

**ERIC Clearinghouse**  
Since ERIC is such a comprehensive resource, I have highlighted a few resources of particular interest for gifted education. The [ERIC Gifted Highlights](zeric.html) list points to ERIC resources which are available from the main services and to selected bibliographies which have been derived from the ERIC sources (with permission).

ERIC is probably the most comprehensive clearinghouse of education related material. It includes a number of public domain digests of information related to gifted and talented children. ERIC also provides a bibliography of relevant publications and other useful information. ERIC is available from [the ERIC Web Page,](http://www.aspensys.com/eric/.html) or from [an alternate WWW page.](http://www.indiana.edu/~eric_rec/.html)

ERIC will also respond to email queries. Send your question to askeric@ericir.syr.edu

**[Talent Searches and Summer Programs](ztsearch.html)**  
There are regional talent searches in the US, conducted by Stanford, Northwestern, Duke and other Universities. Candidates identified by the search are then invited to participate in summer enrichment and other programs. Some additional summer programs which are not part of the regional talent searches are also listed.

**Other Summer Programs**  
CEC no longer offers the diskette database of summer programs.

**Mentoring Program**  
HP operates a mentoring program which usually includes entire classrooms, but has been used for selected gifted children in a classroom or in a homeschool situation. General information about the [HP Telementoring program is available on their web site.](http://www.telementor.org/hp/) Specific information about using the program for gifted children or homeschoolers is available by email from [Ann Foster](mailto:staff@telementor.org) or by fax: 888-453-0635 or by voicemail: 888-453-0635

**Research Program**  
Dr Peter Street is conducting a research study:\
\
I am a school principal in NSW, Australia, and have recently completed my Ph.D. in gifted education, examining the factors that contribute to academic success of gifted middle school students. This information may be of help to parents of gifted students who feel their children arre not achieving at the anticipated levels, or to scholars undertaking similar qualitative research. I am also seeking students and parents willing to participate in a qualitative survey of academic self-concept of gifted students. Any parents wishing to be involved in this research (leading to a Ed.D. degree) can contact my email and I will return their enquiry within 48 hours. I need about 200 participants from the US to ensure the validity of this study.

For more information, please contact [Dr Peter Street streetp.@lisp.com.au](mailto:streetp.@lisp.com.au)
