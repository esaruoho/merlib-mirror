III\. E-mail Services:

- A big list of car email [lists](http://www.geocities.com/MotorCity/Speedway/1104/)
- The BMW mailing list(digest only), to subscribe send mail to: [E-MAIL](mailto:bmw-digest-request@mailgate.wizvax.net) with the subject line blank and "subscribe" in the body of the message. Or, read more information on [subscribing.](http://www.digest.net/bmw/#SUB)
- The E36 BMW mailing list(digest only), to subscribe send mail to: [E-MAIL](mailto:majordomo@mailgw.tum.net) with the subject line blank and "subscribe e36 your_email@address.com" in the body of the message. The [E36-Digest web site](http://www.tum.net/bmw/) is up and running.
- [The 318ti Compact BMW mailing list](http://207.181.127.133/bmwcompact/) Subscription information is on it's homepage. [E-MAIL](mailto:garyh@sco.com) with the subject line blank and "subscribe M3 digest" in the body of the message. [E-MAIL](mailto:ckent@ids.net) Chris Kent, and ask to subscribe to the 2002 email list.
- Driver's school mailing list, to subscribe send mail to: [E-MAIL](mailto:school-request@mailgate.wizvax.net) with the subject line blank and subscribe in the body of the message.
- SCCA mailing list, to subscribe send mail to: [E-MAIL](mailto:wheeltowheel@abingdon.sun.com) with the subject line blank and subscribe in the body of the message.
- Order Robert Bentley Publishing's excellent repair manuals via [E-MAIL](mailto:sales@rb.com)
- BMW Tech Group [E-MAIL](mailto:rggroh@EMsport.com) list, you must be an experienced BMW Tech to subscribe to this private list. Please include: your BMW experience and qualifications; the name of the shop or dealer that you work for; your daytime (work) phone number; and your e-mail address. BMW Tech Group [website](http://www.emsport.com/bmwtg.html).
