<td width="750"><h2 id="quilts-not-just-for-beds">Quilts: Not Just For Beds</h2>
<p>P.O. Box 1342 * Bellevue, WA 98009 * <a href="mailto:jlk@eskimo.com">jlk@eskimo.com</a></p>
<p><span id="DDE_LINK1">All patterns come with a full color cover, complete step-by-step instructions and plenty of illustrations to guide you through the process of making your own beautiful quilt.</span> Mosaic patterns are made by following an easy paint by number method. They are NOT watercolor quilts.</p>
<p>Click on an image to see a larger view of a particular quilt.</p>
<p>I've added some stories and/or tidbits of information that pertain to each quilt for your reading enjoyment while waiting for the pictures to load.</p>
<p><img src="new.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" /> I'm really excited about the <a href="floral.htm">new patterns</a> that I have added this spring!!!! They are applique, so you can do them no matter what your skill level. For a quick easy project that can be completed in an afternoon, use fusible web. Or if you prefer handwork, you can do as I do and use needleturn, or use whatever method you are most comfortable with. There are a total of twelve patterns that can be made individually for wall hangings or pillows, or you can combine any combination of the twelve for a 60" X 80" quilt.</p>
