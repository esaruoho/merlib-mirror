During the summer regular Sunday services are replaced with a weekly lay-led series looking at areas of personal growth and items of current or historical interest. A new topic will be explored each week. The meetings will start at 10 a.m and last for approximately an hour. Informality is the order of the day for these sessions. There will be no Religious Education program for the children but child care will be provided.</h2>
