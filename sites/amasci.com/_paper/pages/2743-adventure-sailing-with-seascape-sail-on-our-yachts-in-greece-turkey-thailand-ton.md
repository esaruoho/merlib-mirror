&#10;<p> </p>
The Med might provide some exciting sailing weather at times - but we're not exactly crossing any oceans!
<p>The "Adventure" tag applies not only to the fun, freedom and excitement of being out on the ocean on your own yacht, but also to the SeaScape <em>get-involved</em> approach, where everyone is part of a real "crew" - not just passengers to be ferried about according to some strict itinerary, or the whim of the captain.</p>
<p>There's no obligation to pull on ropes if you don't want to - but if you'd like to learn more about sailing, we'd love to teach you. For already experienced sailors - hey, great ! - come take the helm and tweak the sails as much as you like ! Though we have provisional itineraries, they are always changeable according to the preferences of the group - and of course, the whims of the wind gods ! Our trips are as flexible and laid-back as possible; as fun and hedonistic as the islands themselves.</p>
<p>"Adventure" also applies to the sort of places we sail. To <em>some</em> of the more well-known spots, sure - but we also venture way beyond the usual tourist tracks; we explore the isolated islands &amp; villages only accessible by sailboat - or maybe bribing a local fisherman ! These are the islands you'll never find in any travel agents' window... some of the few remaining unspoiled pockets where traditional Greek culture and hospititality are still very much alive.</p>
<p>What's more, we've been sailing these waters for 9yrs now - and are pretty much part of the family in these little islands. So our SeaScape sailors always get a <em>real</em> warm welcome whenever our yachts sail into port !</p>
<p><em>So - this is no cruise ship - it's a real sailing experience!</em> We won't make your beds or clean up your junk - although we may well serve up the gin n' tonics as you swing in a hammock! We will however, teach you to sail if you wish; take you Greek dancing where the locals go, introduce you to the delights of Turkish baths - and sail you to some of the most beautiful and unspoiled spots still remaining in these waters....</p>
