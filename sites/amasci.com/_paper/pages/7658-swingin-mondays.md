# Swingin' Mondays

Come dance Lindy Hop with us every Monday night (check [schedule](sonnysked.htm#Dance%20Dates) in case of holidays or special events) at Sonny Newman's Dance Hall at 201 N. 85th St. in Seattle. (Dance hall is in the building across from the Taproot Theater on the 2nd floor, enter in back.)  We usually have a drop-in dance workshop from 7 or 7:30 till 8:30 p.m.  (Check the [schedule](sonnysked.htm#Workshops) for exceptions.) Open dancing to DJ'd music continues until at least 11:30 p.m.  All ages welcome!

We also have a [map](sonnymap.htm) to Sonny's to help you find your way....

The dance costs \$3 for [members](member.htm) of the Savoy Swing Club, and \$5 for the general public. (A bit more when we occasionally have a live band -- see [schedule](sonnysked.htm#Dance%20Dates).) Workshops are \$8 for members/ \$12 for others for drop-ins, and multi-session discounts are available.  Plus workshop attendees get in the dance for free!
