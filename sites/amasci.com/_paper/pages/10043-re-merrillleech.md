    Was at Merrill two wks ago for two nights. First night the wind died around
    8:30pm, hatch was prolific and the fish hit, following the "natural
    trend"...first on emergers, then dries. Emergers worked the whole hatch,
    which lasted 60-90 minutes (a slightly earlier hatch of what looked like
    gray drakes -- pretty good size for normal mays -- came off with a bit of
    surface activity).  The second night the wind slowed down, but kept up some
    gusts and the hatch was substantially weaker...hardly worth the up wind
    pull to get there. I caught three fish the first night, one long skinny
    cutthroat & two 13" rainbows. One of my buddies caught a large brown
    (19-20") on an emerger the first night (all the fish from same area).

    Pat Bolton
