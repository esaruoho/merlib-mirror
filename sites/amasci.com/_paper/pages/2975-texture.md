    Design Element 5--Texture 
     

           The variety, contrast,
          and change in the textures of a garden tempt the viewer to stop
          and absorb the detail.

          The indumentum of bureavii,
          the huge leaves of fictolacteum,
          the miniscule foliage of microlucem,
          all make the visual almost tactile.

          The needles and gravel and leaves
          underfoot alter the step as well as the scene.
