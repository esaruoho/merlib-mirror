<strong>January 29, 2003</strong>
<ol>
<li>Wrote a short article, <a href="../writings/100bill.htm">Your Money is No Good Here.</a></li>
</ol>
<hr />
<strong>September 1, 2002</strong>
<ol>
<li>Changed ISPs from Olgrande.net to Eskimo.com. Olagrande went out of business and was absorbed by Eskimo. Made some minor changes to a few webpages. The biggest change is that Eskimo does not support CGIs written in C. Their web server runs on a different platform than the machine supporting their shell accounts. Hopefully, they will get a cross compiler up soon.</li>
</ol>
<hr />
<strong>April 30, 2001</strong>
<ol>
<li>First upload attempt of my personal web page onto Olagrande. I made some massive changes and not everything may work. Still checking links. I decided to get rid of my <em>Public Service Announcements</em> because they were getting old and it didn't seem people were reading them anymore. I have transferred several of my writings that seem to be more popular (via email feedback) or have information which I deem to be of value. At least almost everything is updated now!</li>
</ol>
<hr />
<strong>April 27, 2001</strong>
<ol>
<li><p>I changed ISPs from Primenet to Olagrande a few days ago and am starting to port over my webpages. This is not a trivial task. For one thing, I have to change all references to Primenet including email addresses, URLs, CGIs, etc. It also gives me a chance to fix broken links and add more material. Because of this, I am going to be selective in what I port over as some webpage information is old and/or obsolete.</p>
<p>I have been with Primenet since February 1995(!) and was considered a charter member. Since that time, Primenet has <strong>merged at least three separate times</strong> with other companies! This latest merge is with Earthlink and now Primenet no longer supports dialup service.<br />
Time to bail.</p></li>
</ol>
