        <a href="http://www.infodog.com/clubs/2001123601.HTM" target="_blank">18 February 2001</a> (Sunday)<br />

<p>       Results:<br />
        <a href="http://www.infodog.com/RESULTS/2001123601/2001123601381.HTM" target="_blank">18 February 2001</a> (Sunday)<br />
        <a href="http://www.infodog.com/RESULTS/2001123602/2001123602381.HTM" target="_blank">19 February 2001</a> (Monday)</p>
<p>        Notes:  <br />
        (1) Handler for Kobe on day 1 was Neil.  Handler on day 2 was Nancy.<br />
        (2) Day 1 the whole gang was entered!  Dogs and bitches everywhere.  Everybody did well.<br />
             Disappointed that Jesse didn't win overall.  He needs to win one more major to finish, <br />
             but he still did very well and was the Reserve Winner.  Vanna finished 1st in Class, Emmy <br />
             finished OS, and Peaches finished 1st in Class.<br />
        (3) Day 1 for Kobe went okay.  He needs to work on his standing (among other things).  The <br />
              Judge was kind and went through the motions to give Kobe some experience. <br />
        (4) Day 2, well to put it nicely, <strong>SUCKED!</strong>  Not that Kobe was helping his case, being a bigger <br />
                 butthead than normal (2nd days just doesn't seem to go over well with him).  Even though <br />
              Kobe was the only dog entered in his class, he was awarded 2nd place just so he couldn't <br />
              reenter ring.  Kobe's first taste of AKC politics,  gotta love it.<br />
<br />
        <u>Day 1</u>  (Judge - Mr. John R. Cole)<br />
        (pictures coming soon!)</p>
