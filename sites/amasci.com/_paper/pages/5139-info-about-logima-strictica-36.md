I will later publish another test, LS24, and connected score report to LS24 are offered for free, to those who have forwarded answers to and payment for LS36. IQ norming will be published as soon as possible.

Being introduced in the year 2000, LS36 is so far accepted as entrance test for membership of the following IQ societies:

**Glia Society**, which has an entrance level at IQ 150 i.e. the percentile "one in a thousand". To qualify through LS36 a score of at least 13 correct answers is required.

**Pi Society**, which has an entrance level at IQ 176 i.e. the percentile "one in a million". Still unspecified LS qualifying scores, an updated norming is under construction and will be published as soon as possible.
