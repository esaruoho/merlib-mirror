# MK CRITERION Lapidary Sawblades

*Source page: `~two/231.htm`*

MK CRITERION Lapidary Sawblades

The **Criterion blade** is a notched, high heat, furnace sintered blade. The blade is mechanically notched to effectively accept a diamond matrix stuffing. These are not crimped or rolled as in the case of many lapidary blades. In this high heat sintering process the bond between the diamond matrix and steel core is done in such a manner that the wear factor will be equal throughout the blade,thus providing maximum long life with a smooth, fast, cutting speed.\
Arbor Sizes: Blades 6" thru 12" -1/2 " and 5/8"Except G0615, G0616 standard size is 5/8" arbor, Blades 14" thru 24" - 3/4" and 1"; other sizes are special order. Except G0619, G0628 and G0630 standard size is 1" .
