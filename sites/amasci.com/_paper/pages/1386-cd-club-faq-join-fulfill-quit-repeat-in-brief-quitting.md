<th style="text-align: left;" data-valign="top"><h2 id="join-fulfill-quit-repeat---in-brief-quitting" data-align="center">Join, Fulfill, Quit, Repeat - In Brief<br />
Quitting</h2>
Once the membership purchase requirements have been fulfilled, contact the club's customer service center (be sure to include your account number) or just return a monthly selection card with PLEASE CANCEL MY MEMBERSHIP written on it. Prior to the club canceling your membership, you might receive another monthly mailing. Ignore the monthly selection reply card. The club will probably send notification when it has processed the cancellation.<br />
