Ignition timing is actually the process of making the spark plug fire at the right time. When the spark plug fires, it takes a finite amount of time for the flame to buren, and the speed of the burning is actually slower than the piston speed. If the spark plug would fire right at the top of the stroke, the piston would outrun the flame and the fuel would not get burned up all the way, or at least at the very end, so you wouldn't get much power out of it.

Pre-ignition, is set by experimentation, and the ignition boxes do a good job of making the plug fire as much as 50 degrees before the piston even reaches the top! The faster your engine turns the sooner the plug must fire, that is why, the YFZ-450 is so awesome! There is no way you would get 50 HP from a 450 cc engine unless you have variable ignition timing!

This is latest technology. the carburetor senses how far open the carb is, and the ignition box KNOWS the RPM (because it is making the sparks!) and so these both combine in a pre determined chart in the computer memory to know how many degrees from TDC to fire the spark.

You can buy programmable ignition controllers like the DYNATEK for the YFZ-450 to further tune the ignition FOR EACH RIDER! For example, Bluedog, always has his throtttle wide open. The ignition box keeps record of what percentage of throttle you run, and blue dog is almost 33 percent WOT at all times on the AVERAGE! Whereas Whitedog is about 3/4 throttle nearly 75 % of the time.

Very different styles of driving, and the ignition controller can adjust the preset from the factory.

ALSO, if you run ALCOHOL you HAVE to advance the spark about 7 degreees, and when you run NOS you have to retard the spark 12 degrees. otherwise you 1. Loose efficincy 2. Stress your motor.

can you imagin what the motor feels when a full load of NITROUS ignites 42 degrees before the piton hits TDC? Nitrous flame time is 10 times faster than air/gas and all it will do is run the piston up against a fully ignited gas filled cylender! enourmous stress, and compression ratios go up to 20 to 1 you coul rebuild your engine each weekend if you dont have a programmable ignition box!

Very essentil if you are using alternate fuels or if HILLCLIMBING. Anyway, well put us some ignition curves soon. HINT! for hill climbing at WOT the degree advance should be only 35 degrees, not 42 from the factory. THE DYNATEK WORKS WELL WITHOUT A BATTERY AND WITH THE KICKSTARTER if you replace the STOCK flywheel with a BETTER AND MORE POWERFUL flywheel from TRAIL TECH!. This new flywheel is well balanced and has a much stronger battery, so your ignition gets a stronger pulse and your battery charges faster.

We chose a -4 ounce flywheel, which means it is lighter and revs faster than stock. (Anything to go lighter!) and it works well.

The dynatek computer comtrolled ignition works great for alcohol or nitrous quads and allows you to increase rev limit also. definitely a worthwhile combo to get.
