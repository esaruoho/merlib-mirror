### Movements

Surrealism

- The [Surrealism Server](http://pharmdec.wustl.edu/juju/surr/). Links, [surrealist games](http://pharmdec.wustl.edu/juju/surr/games/games.html). Good place to start though it tries a little too hard to be surreal.
- Surrealist games sites: [Holly's Surrealist Games](http://www.geocities.com/SoHo/Lofts/2297/games.html) - describes Exquisite Corpse; Definitions, or Questions and Answers; and provides examples. Primarily surrealist drawing games are [described here](http://www.sonic.net/fenwick/chaos/surgam.html). [Cybernetic Serendipity](http://www.hrc.wmin.ac.uk/hrc/games/) includes web versions of several games.

### Critical Articles

- Albert Gelpi, "[The Genealogy of Postmodernism: Contemporary American Poetry](http://www.english.upenn.edu/~afilreis/88/gelpi.html)." Although hostile to most L=A=N=G=U=A=G=E poetry, the article points to valid differences among the poets.
- Edward Hirsch, "[How to Read a Poem](http://www.poets.org/LIT/poet/ehirfst2.htm)."
- Marjorie Perloff
  - "[After Free Verse: The New Non-Linear Poetries](http://wings.buffalo.edu/epc/authors/perloff/free.html)."
  - "[Language Poetry and the Lyric Subject: Ron Silliman's Albany, Susan Howe's Buffalo](http://wings.buffalo.edu/epc/authors/perloff/langpo.html)."
  - [Links](http://wings.buffalo.edu/epc/authors/perloff/) to other articles by Perloff.
