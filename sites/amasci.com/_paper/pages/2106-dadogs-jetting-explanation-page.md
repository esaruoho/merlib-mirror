9/01/2004 DOGRIDER FINAL REPORT ON THE 508 CC motor.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Well, remember back in the Forum days when we were told we were taking credit for inventing a 480 motor when in fact it was bought from will at MAXPOWER? I was forced to spill the beens that we had a motor under development that really was totally invented by the boys in the shop (garage) here in doghouse. And I said we would build it even if it wasn't possible and test it and let you know if it blew up.

Our testing has gone through several oil changes and the Dogrider motor 508 cc is: A very nice motor. Yes really. we estimate it at 54 HP pretty solidly, since we stuck on the 5 hp Lowdose NItrous on bluedogs 480 kit and they are about even with that. We knew bluedog was 49.5 hp tbefore the 480 so we figured with the 480 about 51 hp. so with 5 hp NOS Blue can just keep up with him. Without the NOS he drops back about 4-6 lengths with the 480.

The 508 is potent in the low range, blue cannot touch it until he hits NOS in first and second gear. It really gets the drop on throttle down so the holeshot ALWAYS goes to the 508 every time. Even with the 480 on NOS cause the 508 has torque in the low range and takeoff.

We are really happy. Course you know us, if it blew up or broke down we would have told you. But from day ONE, there was not one speck of problem except for the hard start! And if we would have followed jetting charts we would have given up and put the 480 back on it. It just would not start. The boys saved the day and told me my rule. If it doesn't start go up on the pilot. So we did and we did and we did and when we hit 55 pilot it started up! we ran it like that for a while but it did not run right. so we finally spent a day really jetting it and ended up with pilot pilot 62 and NCVM needle at clip \#2 and mainjet 215

IT WILL START FIRST KICK EVEN SOMETIMES WHEN COLD! sometimes cold it takes two kicks. it idles beutiful and it does not get hot.

I WOULD LIKE TO SHARE SOMETHING VERY IMPORTANT WITH YOU GUYS OUT THERE KEEPING TRACK OF THIS! We had EXPERTS on the bullitin boards tell us YOU WILL OVERHEAT YOU WILL OVERHEAT. well we put on the 480 kits and we GOT HOT! But that was before proper jetting, as soon as we upped our jets into NO MANS LAND (anything of jetting charts) we cooled right down and ran beutiful. so you do not overheat because of the BORE you overheat because you have to really jet up, you are running LEAN!

These motors run just about the same temp as stock I would say about 5-10 percent warmer- just noticeable. We run plain distilled water in the radiator, and it never overheats in the hot soummer here and we have no fan, only a kick starter! I WOULD LIKE TO SAY to those who have ears , if you take your antifreezenout and put in distilled water, you will stop your overheating probably! TRY IT! Water is better for the summer. MAke sure to buy new antifreeze for winter, you must have it in there for winter.

How does it run? It PURS, there is less vibration with this 508 than with the stock piston, FALICON balanced it beautiful. and since we are running 11:1 pistons we could inject more NOS and rev higher than the stock piston (lower compression pistons rev much better and quicker) Low dose NOS would take it to 60 hp quite easily with minimal wear. If we do it, we'll post the picks here.

We have one more project and that is called a SUPERCRANK it should be here next week. FALICON takes the stock crank and perfectly balances it , blueprints it, and polishes it for least friction. It looks like CHROME when it is done. and no vibration, it is supposed to screm. We will try that and see if a 480 supercranker can touch a 508 stock crank.

If I was a racer, I would take my stock 439, and send the crank to gGlen at Falicon and supercrank it and offset the crank by 1 mm to get a true 450 that could not be touched! Blueprinted, polished and balanced horizontally and vertically. That would be a winner I'm sure.

Then we could suprcrank the other 508 crank also, we'll see. Will let you know if we can..

Overall we give the 508 5 stars. it is a pleasure to run, nothing outholeshots it and it pulls about 7% harder everywhere and revs to 11,000 GYTR REV BOX with no problem at all. We took the 14 oz weight of the -4 trail tech flywheel and the thing is way more responsive than stock.

If you have MONEY this is a sure bet for a nice motor.

If yo don't have money just get a 480 on Nitrous. They run pretty close.

It is sweet.
