<td width="30%"><br />
<strong>Uselding is a surname most often found in Luxembourg. I am researching the <a href="http://familytreemaker.genealogy.com/users/o/b/e/Lisa-A-Oberg/GENE4-0001.html">descendants of George Uselding ( -1867) and Eva Has ( -1840)</a> of Erpeldange, Diekirch, Luxembourg. They had thirteen known children; seven sons and one daughter survived infancy. Over the course of a twenty year period (1846-1867), six of their sons immigrated to the United States and settled in the Midwestern states of Illinois and Wisconsin, and later South Dakota. This web site is a collection of resources that include references to Useldings in the United States.</strong></td>
