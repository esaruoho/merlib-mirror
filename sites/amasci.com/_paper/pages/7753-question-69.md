### Question 6.9

Someone explained to me that arrays were really just constant pointers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is a bit of an oversimplification. An array name is \`\`constant'' in that it cannot be assigned to, but an array is *not* a pointer, as the discussion and pictures in question [6.2](q6.2.html) should make clear. See also questions [6.3](q6.3.html) and [6.8](q6.8.html).
