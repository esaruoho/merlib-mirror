<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><div data-align="center">
<h3 id="how-tos-of-internet-searching">How-to's of Internet Searching</h3>
</div>
<dl>
<dt><a href="http://www.bbc.co.uk/webwise/basics/"><strong>Finding Stuff on the Web</strong></a></dt>
<dd>
A really cute beginner's tutorial at the BBC WebWise site.
</dd>
<dt><a href="http://library.albany.edu/internet/choose.html"><strong>Guide to Choosing a Search Engine</strong></a></dt>
<dd>
You know how to search the web, you just need to know which engines to use for which kind of content (by file type, search logic, database integrity, etc.). From the University at Albany Library.
</dd>
<dt><a href="http://library.albany.edu/internet/started.html"><strong>Getting Started: Choosing your Research Tool</strong></a></dt>
<dd>
This helps guide you to helpful search engines based on what kind of question you are researching. From the University at Albany Library.
</dd>
<dt><a href="http://library.albany.edu/internet/search.html"><strong>Tutorial on Searching the Web</strong></a></dt>
<dd>
For the beginner, or the person whose fave search engine has begun failing them lately. From the University at Albany Library.
</dd>
<dt><a href="http://www.cs.cmu.edu/Web/searches.html"><strong>W3 Search Engines</strong></a></dt>
<dd>
Just a big list of hundreds of search engines and generally what you can use them for.
</dd>
<dt><a href="http://www.seattletimes.com/news/technology/"><strong>The Seattle Times' Technology Section</strong></a></dt>
<dd>
Just a side benefit of living in the Land of the Geeks, this section of our local newspaper contains regular reviews of internet sites, including search engine reviews and in-depth articles on a variety of technological topics. I look to it when I'm sick of search engines and want to find things in sort of the opposite way. :-} Yes, its archive is searchable.
</dd>
</dl></td>

<div align="center">

### Dr. Ice's Top 23 Favourite Search Engines:

Listed in alphabetical order. :-}\

</div>

 

<div align="center">

<table width="480" data-border="0" data-cellspacing="0" data-cellpadding="0">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.altavista.net/"><strong>Alta Vista</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Search the web by several methods, or peruse directories of web pages. "My Alta Vista" offers you the option of personalizing what you see when you visit the site.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.askjeeves.com/"><strong>Ask Jeeves</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">A novel concept, freeform questions and then an FAQ-formatted list of questions and answers. Very interesting. And an out-of-the-ordinary way to find web sites. Beware of the "frame" it sends with you wherever you go.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://search.cnet.com/"><strong>c|net's Search.com</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Metasearches more than 800 specialized engines from around the Web. Dr. Ice has pretty good luck finding things here, and the interface is nice. A little too colourful for some days, but the layout is easy to navigate.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.dogpile.com"><strong>Dogpile</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Comprehensive searches of all the major search engines.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.excite.com/"><strong>Excite</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Search engine and "channels"-style directory of the Internet. Comprehensive and relatively easy-to-use. Also offers personalization features so you can configure what you see when you go there.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.google.com/"><strong>Google</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">This is just a search engine that gives you a list of sites. No directories, no other features. The only novelty is the "I'm feeling lucky" button, which takes you directly to a page that matches the search, without showing the results list first. Cute!</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.goto.com/"><strong>GoTo.com</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Claims to be "Search Made Simple". The interface sure is, just a search box and simple directory that all fits on one screenful.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.hotbot.com/"><strong>HotBot</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Offers searches by text, date, medium (like, whether or not it contains video) or language. Also has a web page directory and resource driectory.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://guide.infoseek.com/"><strong>Infoseek</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Search engine for web and other resources on the internet. Customizeable interface. Also offers translation of web pages that you find in other languages, online auctioning and MapQuest maps.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.looksmart.com/"><strong>LookSmart</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Website directory by categories, and search utility of its web page database. On this list for web research purposes. Not the best, not the worst. :-}</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.lycos.com/"><strong>Lycos</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Search engine and "Web Guides" categories. Several search methods available. Plus, the customizable "My Lycos" interface. Dr. Ice _loves_ that part! A very cool concept! Personalized portal to all kinds of info, and links in to the My Calendar and My Palm sites. Mmmm-hmmm!</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://magellan.excite.com/"><strong>Magellan</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Directory of "reviewed" sites with search capabilities. Also links to Excite! Maps.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.metacrawler.com/"><strong>Metacrawler</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Searches all the top search engines at once. Also lists channels, online phone directories and other resources.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://home.microsoft.com/access/allinone.asp"><strong>Microsoft All-In-One Search</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">This is where you go when you click on the "Search" toolbar button in MS Internet Explorer. Can be VERY slow to load.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.miningco.com/"><strong>The Mining Company (aka About.com)</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Another novel concept in internet searching. Most search engines rely on web page makers registering their sites with them. This one has "guides", people who review sites and list them. You can sign up to be a guide and help build the resource.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://search.aol.com/"><strong>NetFind at AOL</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Dr. Ice be not a fan of AOL, but it doesn't hurt to look absolutely everywhere when you're doing research.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://home.netscape.com/escapes/search/"><strong>Netscape NetSearch</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Multiple search engine interface. I kinda like this site for the ability to roll your search phrase from one engine to the next. Currently offers Netscape, Ask Jeeves, Google, Overture, Looksmart, Lycos and NBCi all in one interface. This is the page that you get to when you hit the "Search" button on Netscape's toolbar.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="%20http://directory.netscape.com/"><strong>Netscape Open Directory Project</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">A multiple-engine search utility, and a directory that includes "expert reviews" of 2.5 million websites. You can become an editor/reviewer.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.northernlight.com/"><strong>Northern Light</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">This site offers categories of search (including a government search) of the web and its own info resources. Seems to be designed for doing research for business and educational purposes. Results are sorted in folders. Intriguing.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.ci.seattle.wa.us/html/director/"><strong>Public Access Network (PAN) Search</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Dr. Ice's alter-ego is a government employee with the City of Seattle, so this is the search of materials Dr. Ice works on, to check if those materials are being indexed properly. For you, this may be a handy entry point to the CityofSeattle.net website. "City Hall Without the Traffic Jams", as the saying goes.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.snap.com/"><strong>Snap.Com</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">This one's just on the list for the sake of completeness. Not a favorite, as it often places soundex hits above actual complete matches. How bizarre.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.webcrawler.com/"><strong>WebCrawler</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Search engine and "channels"-style directory with lots of features, including headline news</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td colspan="2" data-valign="bottom"><a href="http://www.yahoo.com/"><strong>Yahoo!</strong></a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="20"> </td>
<td data-valign="top">Directory and search, probably the most used and respected Internet directory. Also offers customizable options, Yahoo! Maps, web page directories, online calendaring, online auctions, and more.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
