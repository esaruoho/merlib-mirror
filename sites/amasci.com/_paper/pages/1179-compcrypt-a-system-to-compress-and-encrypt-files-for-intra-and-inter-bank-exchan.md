<td width="508" data-valign="top" height="292"><p>Banks often need to exchange files with :</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Their Branches<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Other Banks<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> With Central Banks</p>
</blockquote>
<p>It is advisable that the files be exchanged such that they are :</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Compressed to save transmission time<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Encrypted to ensure security and integrity</p>
</blockquote>
<p>CompCrypt is utility package that assists Commercial Banks in Compressing and Encrypting the Data Files.</p>
<p>Key features of the system are:</p>
<p><strong><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Encryption</strong></p>
<p>To ensure the Authentication, Integrity, Non-Repudiation and Confidentiality of files, BankBridge uses highly secure encryption algorithms which conform to International standards.</p></td>
<td width="183" data-valign="top" height="292"><div data-align="right">
<img src="pics/compcrypt-softlook.gif" data-align="top" width="174" height="341" />
</div></td>

<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong></strong> <strong>Compression</strong></p>
<p>Standard ASCII files, when compressed, can result in a huge saving in bandwidth and reliability of transfer. CompCrypt compress files by upto 95% using a sophisticated compression technology resulting in transfer times of minutes rather than hours.</p></td>
