- [Winterhaven - A new K-8 school starting up in Portland](http://www.teleport.com/~cgrether/) with an emphasis on accelerated curriculum and meeting the level and rate needs of students. WinterHaven is an alternative school with a science, math, and technology special focus, within the Portland (Oregon) Public Schools system. When asked if WinterHaven is a program for TAG-identified students, the answer is a clear "NO." While it is true that the school has an unusually high number of TAG identified students, that is not the primary basis on which admission decisions are made. Perhaps the question is better framed in terms of who would most benefit from and contribute positively to the school. It is more important to stress such characteristics as serious learners, children who take responsibility for themselves and work well in groups, and students who are focused, committed, and excited by ideas.
