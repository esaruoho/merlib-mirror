<p>The entire video is produced to give the impression that we are hearing the director’s commentary track on a DVD. On it, an egotistical narrative is heard, delivered by the video’s director. Only on <em>this</em> “DVD,” you cannot shut the commentary off.</p>
<p>21 min. runtime, VHS color, stereo sound.</p>
