&#10;<h1 id="recommendations">RECOMMENDATIONS:</h1>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top">THE SEATTLE SCHOOL DISTRICT SHOULD HAVE A POLICY TO COVER COMMERCIAL INFLUENCE IN THE PUBLIC SCHOOLS, WHICH WOULD INCLUDE A POLICY ON ADVERTISING.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top">THE POLICY SHOULD REFLECT THE CONCERNS OF THE COMMUNITY.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top">THE POLICY SHOULD INCLUDE OPERATIONAL DEFINITIONS OF TERMS USED IN THE POLICY.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top">THE POLICY SHOULD INCLUDE SPECIFIC EXAMPLES TO ILLUSTRATE THE INTENT OF THE POLICY.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top">THERE SHOULD BE ADEQUATE PUBLIC COMMENT PERIOD BEFORE THE POLICY IS IMPLEMENTED.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top">THERE SHOULD BE A COMMITTEE, COMPOSED OF EDUCATORS, PARENTS, STUDENTS, AND CITIZENS, TO SERVE AS AN ADVISORY BOARD TO HELP ADVISE THE SEATTLE PUBLIC SCHOOLS ON EFFECTS OF COMMERCIALISM ON THE EDUCATIONAL ENVIRONMENT.</td>
</tr>
