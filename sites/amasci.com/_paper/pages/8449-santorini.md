# Santorini (Thra, Θήρα)

Santorini is one of the most famous islands in the Cyclades.  It was originally called Thira (and in fact many Greeks still call it that).  The island is [volcanic](http://www.geo.aau.dk/palstrat/tom/santorini_homepage/santorini.htm), and in fact a huge eruption in the 16th century BC caused massive destruction and significantly changed the landscape.

Well known for its natural beauty and many picturesque churches, Santorini is very tourist-oriented.  The sheer cliffs on the inside of the crater are stunning, and the caldera in the middle is still active.
