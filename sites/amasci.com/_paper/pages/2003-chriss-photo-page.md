# Chris's Photo Page

*Source page: `~cmkinc/bio/pic.htm`*

<img src="../graphics/cmk99.jpg" data-align="left" data-hspace="8" width="260" height="264" alt="Chris Pic 1 (Summer 1999)" />**The Face Behind The Mask *(or is THAT the mask?)***

It's been 2 years since I've done anything with this page, so when the opportunity arose to have a new digital snapshot taken I jumped at the chance. Well, maybe *jumped* isn't exactly the best way to describe it. I do look a bit different than I did in my old picture, though, and I thought some new material might be nice for a change.

The camera that captured this image was really designed for real-time webcam applications, not fine portrait photography. It came out rather grainy and none of my admittedly limited graphical skills could make a nice, sharp image from it. I was hoping that the space background might add an air of mystery or something.

I don't work at Gameworks by the way, I just used to go there alot and liked the shirt.
