OUR NEW ADDRESS:\
amasci.com
=================

\

No, the address of SCIENCE HOBBYIST is not changing. I've just added an alternate URL which is a bit easier to remember:

> **AMASCI** ( as in Amateur science, specifically http://amasci.com )

------------------------------------------------------------------------

All the eskimo.com addresses will continue to work normally. If you wish to change your pointers to the new address, simply replace the "eskimo.com /~billb" part of each address with "amasci.com". For example, if you're currently pointing to the bookstore page...\

simply change this:

> [http://www. amasci.com/bookstr.html](http://www.eskimo.c%20om/~billb/bookstr.html)

into this:

> <http://amasci.com/bookstr.html>

...and no more need to explain that "tilde" key to the new internet users! ...and don't forget to help out THE SCIENCE CLUB by doing your Amazon.com shopping through the bookstore page! :)
