# Stinking Forest Campaign

*Source page: `~ouija/forestmain.htm`*

Welcome to the Stinking Forest campaign.

As we progress in this campaign I will be entering the adventures here so you too can join in on the exploration of this area of Glorantha that most people do not adventure into or even know about.

Each adventure is a complete stand alone adventure complete with maps, monsters, villains, and lots a goodies all laid out in a good and easy to read RQ standard.
