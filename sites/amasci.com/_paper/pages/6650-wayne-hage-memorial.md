It triggered the federal attack that launched the landmark property rights case, <em>Hage v. United States</em>.</p>
<p>From 1989, Storm Over Rangelands:</p>
<p>Wayne Hage, a native Nevadan born in 1936, is a successful rancher who has lived close to the land all his life. Mr. Hage earned a master's degree in biological science from the University of Nevada at Reno and has served as chairman of numerous range policy boards and committees. He has served as chairman of the Agricultural Land Use Committee of the California state Chamber of Commerce, is currently a member of the American Society of of Farm Managers and Rural Appraisers, and of the Nevada Agricultural Foundation, a trustee of the University of Nevada Foundation, and acts as consultant to financial institutions. Mr. Hage is listed in Who's Who in the West and Who's Who in the Western Livestock Industry.</p>
<p>A veteran of many formal challenges to the federal bureaucracy, Mr. Hage has become a leading authority on private rights in federal lands. His numerous long-term associations with the livestock industry and the academic and legal worlds have provided Mr. Hage with the breadth of knowledge required to develop the complex theses narrated in this book. He is in demand as a speaker and enjoys writing and illustrating short stories.</p>
<p>DEATH ANNOUNCEMENT<br />
FROM<br />
STEWARDS OF THE RANGE</p></td>

<p>June 5, 2006<br />
<br />
Dear Members,<br />
<br />
I am sorry to deliver this news to you, but early this afternoon, my good friend and the great man we all admired and supported, Wayne Hage, passed away peacefully at his home on Pine Creek Ranch.<br />
<br />
A few weeks ago, Margaret showed him the many letters and notes all of you had sent to him, and he was overwhelmed at the warm thoughts and prayers that were within these. They gave him great comfort in his final days. The family wanted to make sure you knew how much these were appreciated by him.<br />
<br />
Funeral arrangements are being made by the family now and we will let you know when the service will occur.<br />
<br />
God Bless Wayne Hage.<br />
<br />
Sincerely,<br />
<br />
Frank T. Duran<br />
President<br />
