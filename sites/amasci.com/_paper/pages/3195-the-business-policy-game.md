# The Business Policy Game:

# An International Strategy Simulation

> 
>
> ------------------------------------------------------------------------
>
> A [total enterprise simulation](BPGoverview.html) for undergraduate and graduate business policy/strategic management courses and for management development programs.
>
> Player's Manual, Instructor's Manual and simulation software downloadable from the Internet.
>
> User licenses for each team purchased directly on the Internet.
>
> ------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 5th Edition, A Domestic or an International Game

> - Markets in North America and a choice of an Eastern European, a Latin American or a Southeast Asian country.
> - Markets in North America only for a domestic competition.
> - International competition has separate demand and economic structures for each country.
> - Funds transferred between countries via international exchange rates.
> - Production plants may be located in one or both countries.
> - Sales offices may be located in one or both countries.
> - Market research provides consumer preferences for different market segments.
> - Products uniquely designed to match the preferences of a specific market segment in each of the four market areas.
> - Many [changes](BPGchanges.html) made to increase realism in the learning environment and to make the game easier to administer.
