**Have Permission, Will Be Posted Soon:**

I post a sketch every Saturday night on [my blog](http://realtegan.blogspot.com/), then add it to the sketchbook here. These are the artists who have given permission for their sketch to be posted and are simply waiting for their Saturday to come up. *If you are an artist on this list and would like to know what Saturday I plan to post your sketch, please feel free to [e-mail me](mailto:tegan@eskimo.com?subject=Aquaman%20Sketchbook) and ask.*
