<h1 id="celebrate-international-no-diet-day-may-6-2002" data-align="CENTER"><strong>Celebrate<br />
International<br />
No Diet Day<br />
May 6, 2002</strong></h1>
<h3 id="international-no-diet-day-indd-is-an-annual-celebration-of-body-acceptance-and-diversity.-it-is-observed-on-may-6-of-each-year.-this-years-theme-is-love-your-body" data-align="CENTER">International No Diet Day (INDD) is an annual celebration of body acceptance and diversity.<br />
It is observed on May 6 of each year.<br />
<br />
This year's theme is "Love Your Body"!</h3>
<table data-border="0" width="100%">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td width="10%"><img src="ribbon2002small.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" data-border="0" width="45" height="66" alt="INDD ribbon" /></td>
<td width="90%"><strong><em>WHAT IS INDD?</em></strong></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
INDD is a day to:
<ul>
<li>Celebrate the beauty and diversity of ALL our natural sizes &amp; shapes</li>
<li>Affirm everyBODY's right to health, fitness, and emotional well-being</li>
<li>Declare a personal one-day moratorium on diet/weight obsession</li>
<li>Learn the facts about weight-loss dieting, health, and body size</li>
<li>Recognize how dieting perpetuates violence against women</li>
<li>Honor the victims of eating disorders and weight-loss surgery</li>
<li>Help end weight discrimination, sizism and fatphobia</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="section"></h4>
<table data-border="0" width="100%">
<tbody>
<tr>
<td width="10%"><img src="ribbon2002small.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" data-border="0" width="45" height="66" alt="INDD ribbon" /></td>
<td width="90%"><strong><em>WHO CELEBRATES INDD?</em></strong></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
INDD is for everyBODY! Since its <a href="origin.htm">origin</a> a decade ago in 1992, INDD has been celebrated by size acceptance, anti-diet, body image, and eating disorders activists, groups, and individuals around the world who want to empower people of all sizes. <a href="calendar.html">Events and activities</a> will be held in cities and towns spanning the globe.

Yes, the light blue ribbon was designated by INDD's founder, <a href="origins.html">Mary Evans Young</a>, as its international symbol. This year on May 6, we invite you to put on a light blue ribbon and join the celebration!
