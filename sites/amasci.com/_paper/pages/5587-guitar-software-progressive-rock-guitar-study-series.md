<td colspan="2"><p>These studies are designed for intermediate and advanced players who are hungry for that "extra push" to take their playing to an entirely new level. They introduce you to ideas and approaches that change the way you look at your guitar forever.</p>
<p>Once you understand the concepts presented in these studies, you'll begin to "think for yourself" and become self-sufficient in your creativity and technique. The techniques presented in these studies challenge your physical ability. As a result, they mold you into an undeniably exciting guitarist. We guarantee it!</p>
<p><strong>Full Screen, Easy To Read Sheet Music</strong></p>
<p>The sheet music can be viewed full screen on PCs with 800x600 resolution. You have plenty of room to hold your guitar and not be squinting at your computer. It's also an easy read on PCs with 1024x768 screen resolution!</p>

<p>Also included are notation legends so you're never in the dark with tablature symbols.</p>
