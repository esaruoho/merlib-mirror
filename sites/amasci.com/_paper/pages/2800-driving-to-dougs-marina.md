Turn south on Stone Way and proceed downhill all the way to the water. Go past the last stoplight (there is a Subway shop at the intersection) and Stone Way will turn into North Northlake Way, which will curve around to your left and follow the Lake Union shoreline past marinas and drydocks. Go past Gasworks Park and start looking for the public boat ramp, which is just past Dunato's Boatyard. Seattle Marina is on the east side of the boat ramp (if you get to Ivars, you have gone too far). Don't park in the marina parking lot (you can get towed without the proper parking sticker). Park across the street. My boat is on the dock nearest to the boat ramp (slip number F-4). There is a locked gate in front of my dock. My phone number is 206-547-0851.<br />
