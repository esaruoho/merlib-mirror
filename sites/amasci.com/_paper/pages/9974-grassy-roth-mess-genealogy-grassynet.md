**Mary Mess Roth** prayer card <a href="lost-children-1865.html" alt=""><br />
<strong>The Lost Children</strong></a> is a true story of being lost in the wilderness of northern Wisconsin in 1865 as told by Mary Mess Roth to her children and grandchildren many times.
