# Vortex-L Discussion Group

|  |
|----|
| The Vortex-L [list](http://www.eskimo.com/~billb/lists.txt) was originally created for discussions of professional research into fluid vortex/cavitation devices which exhibit anomalous energy effects (ie: the inventions of Schaeffer, Huffman, Griggs, and Potapov among others.) Currently it has evolved into a discussion on "taboo" physics reports and research. SKEPTICS BEWARE, the topics wander from Cold Fusion, to reports of excess energy in Free Energy devices, gravity generation and detection, reports of theoretically impossible phenomena, and all sorts of supposedly crackpot claims. Before you [subscribe](#sub), please see the [rules](#rules) below. **This is a public, lightly- moderated smartlist [list.](http://www.eskimo.com/~billb/lists.txt) There is no charge, but donations towards expenses are recommended.** |
