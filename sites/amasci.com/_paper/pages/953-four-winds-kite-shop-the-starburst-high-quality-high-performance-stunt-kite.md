&#10;The <strong>StarBurst</strong> flies like the thoroughbred it is. The efficient trailing edge makes this kite very responsive to a light touch. Precise and responsive, it's capable of all the advanced maneuvers and stalls on command. Excellent performance at the edge, precise straight line tracking, tight snap turns -- This kite does it all with grace and agility. Molded fittings, and two of the best spars on the market add to the quality construction of this exceptional sport kite!
<p>This kite comes in a very distinctive, 8-panel pattern. We manufacture the <strong>StarBurst</strong> exclusively for our shop in order to give our customers something extra special. Each kite is handcrafted to exacting performance standards by Erv and Joey. At any time, this kite is available in a wide range of colors. Or choose your own custom color scheme from any of the many <strong>Icarex</strong> colors available! One week lead time is usually needed for custom production. Ask us about available "venting" on this kite.</p>
