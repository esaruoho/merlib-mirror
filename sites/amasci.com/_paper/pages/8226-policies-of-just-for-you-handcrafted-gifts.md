Each individual piece holds something original and all it's own. We strive for originality with each piece, taking great pride in our workmanship, carefully crafting to every detail. Nothing leaves our shoppe until it has been inspected and we are satisfied, it is ready to be presented. Many of our products are created here in our shoppe. Whether it is a hand made piece or from one of our vendors. Please note when your piece arrives it may or may not look exactly like the image representation in our brochures and at our on line shoppe. Should you ever receive anything less than expected simply return it for a full refund!</p></td>
