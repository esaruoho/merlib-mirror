<td colspan="2"><h3 id="its-official-world-record-set-at-port-elgin-pumpkinfest-with-1446-lb." data-align="center">It’s Official! World Record Set At Port Elgin Pumpkinfest with 1,446 lb.</h3>
<img src="http://www.pumpkinfest.org/pics/IMG_1042.jpg" data-align="left" width="200" />
<p>Today Oct 2 in PORT ELGIN…Port Elgin Pumpkinfest organizers are jumping for joy as a whopping 1,446 lb. new world record giant pumpkin was weighed in by grower Alan Eaton of Richmond, Ontario. Results have been finalized from the 23 other Great Pumpkin Commonwealth (GPC) sites confirming this new world record. Organizers will begin the process of having this pumpkin listed with Guinness as soon as possible.</p>
<p>This must have been a good year for growing as 9 of the top pumpkins weighed in at Port Elgin were over 1,000 lbs.</p>
<p>The previous World record for the pumpkin category was 1,385 lbs. held by Steve Daletas of Oregon.</p>
