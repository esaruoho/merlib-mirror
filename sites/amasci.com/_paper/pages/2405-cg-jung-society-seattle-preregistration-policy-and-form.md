# C.G. Jung Society, Seattle: Preregistration Policy and Form

*Source page: `~dcs/preform.html`*

### Preregistration Policy and Form

Preregistration for workshops and special events helps the Society anticipate attendance; it also ensures a place for you. Preregistration is due two weeks prior to the workshop date. However, preregistration for workshops is not required, and there is no penalty for registering later.

**Preregistration Form**

To preregister for Saturday workshops, please print this form and mail it, with payment, to the address below. To begin or renew your Society membership or ask for a newsletter subscription, please print and use the [Membership/Mailing List Request Form](mailform.html).

C.G. Jung Society, 4649 Sunnyside Avenue North, Room 222, Seattle, WA 98103
