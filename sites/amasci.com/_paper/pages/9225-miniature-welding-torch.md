**(1)   The LITTLE TORCH** - A precision engineered, highly versatile instrument capable of doing seemingly impossible tasks with ease. It produces a perfectly controlled, completely stable flame with temperatures up to 6300' F. Can be used with oxygen and four different fuels: acetylene, hydrogen, LP or natural gas. NOTE : When used with fuels other than acetylene, the two smallest tips, Nos. 1 & 2 cannot be used. Complete kit includes torch body, 5 tips ... 3 ruby orifice, 6 feet hose and complete instructions.

**(4)   PRECIOUS METAL MELTING TIP** - This uniquely designed tip makes your Little Torch even more versatile. The melting tip uses a quadruple orifice design to create four separate flames that form one flame which converge to a single point and is equivalent to four size tips. This technology gives you a flame suitable for melting in a crucible. When used with propane the temperature reaches 5200' F. - with other fuels - 6000' F.
