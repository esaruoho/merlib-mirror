She enjoys jamming with others as often as schedules allow. In order to support her music habit, she works as a neonatologist. Her husband Dan plays bass and writes for this site as well. <a href="mailto:JavaScript%20Protected%20Email%20-%20PLEASE%20ENABLE%20JAVASCRIPT%20TO%20EMAIL%20THIS%20PERSON!">Stefan Leonhardt</a>
-
Stefan has been playing guitar on and off for about 20 years, with 10 years of teaching privately and one year in the professional program of the "Future Music School" in Aschaffenburg, Germany. When playing guitar Stefan usually plays a lot of blues and blues-influenced (classic) rock, but when writing songs, he ends up writing rock and even pop songs. Stefan considers himself more a musician than a guitar player - "the song is more important than the guitar part (though a cool guitar part or solo of course is nice and sometimes makes the song happen)." He doesn't care for classifications of style - "if I like it, I like no matter if it's blues, country, rock, jazz or hard and heavy." Stefan lives in Bavaria together with his wife Freia and two children Vanessa (2 1/2 years) and Patrick (4 weeks).<br />
