<h3 id="garden-loam-potting-soil" data-align="CENTER"><strong>GARDEN LOAM POTTING SOIL</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Pass the garden loam through a 1/4 in (0.6 cm) sieve</li>
<li>Warm oven to 180 degrees F (82 C) for 10 minutes</li>
<li>Place on center rack</li>
<li>Place foil over tray and make sure the soil reaches the above temperature. Use a meat thermometer</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="potting-soils-using-garden-loam" data-align="center"><strong>Potting soils using garden loam</strong></h3>
<ul>
<li>Potting soils should be placed through a sieve of the defined size:</li>
<li>Loam=3/8 in (1 cm)</li>
<li>Peat=3/8 in (1 cm)</li>
<li>Sharp Sand=1/8 in (3 mm) NOTE: do not use builder sand. Make sure the same is washed thoughly</li>
</ul>
