### Red Feather Lakes

<img src="img/redf.jpg" data-border="0" />

Went camping at Red Feather Lakes, northwest of Fort Collins. (At least) three lakes within short hiking distance of each other in a really nice area. Most people are there fishing, but just hiking around was really fun. *Damn* cold at night, though!

Drove up the Poudre River Canyon to get there, where the water was still raging, although within its banks now. Saw a few rafters in what is usually pretty calm water. Looked like a lot of fun, actually.
