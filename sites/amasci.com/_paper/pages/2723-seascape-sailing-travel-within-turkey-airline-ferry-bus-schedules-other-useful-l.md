<p> <strong>Ferries between Greece/Turkey</strong></p>
<p>There are several ferries that link Greece and Turkey on a regular basis. Around our sailing area, here are the main links:</p>
<ul>
<li>Marmaris - Rhodes</li>
<li>Datca-Simi</li>
<li>Bodrum - Kos</li>
<li>Bodrum - Rhodes</li>
<li>Kusadasi - Samos</li>
</ul>
For a very complete timetable and rates from Turkey-to-Greece, check out <a href="http://www.ankertravel.net/ferries.asp">Ankur Travel</a>.
<p> </p>
Other good links:<br />
<a href="http://www.aboutmarmaris.com/marmaris-transport.htm">Marmaris - Rhodes</a>; ferries &amp; hydrofoils<br />

More about ferries from Greece to Turkey ...

<div class="hintbox" alt=" More about ferries from Greece to Turkey ... " style="DISPLAY: none" title="Read more about ferries from Greece to Turkey">
Whilst the Turkish ferry companies and agencies are very efficient with their online schedules and booking, alas the same cannot be said of the Greek side! The timetables shown on the <a href="http://www.ankertravel.net/ferries.asp">Anker</a> website are complete from the Turkish point of view, but there's still a few Greek ferries which are not shown online anywhere we have seen ! Mostly, these are from Kos-Bodrum; so here is our own little addendum to the excellent schedule above:
<strong>Kos-Bodrum</strong>   dep Kos every day around 8.30-9am; takes about 45mins. Dep Bodrum around 3.30pm, ditto.
