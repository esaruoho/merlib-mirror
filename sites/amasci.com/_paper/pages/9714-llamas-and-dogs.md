Our llamas have experience with 3 dogs, each an important part of our family. Max, our 5 pound poodle, Jake (deceased), our large male Dobe they enjoyed for 4 years and Jenna, our little female Doberman. All of the llamas are always aware when Max is underfoot because of his size, but Max doesn't know there is any size difference between himself and a Doberman. He gives the llamas friendly licks on the nose when they bend down to sniff him. The llamas are not uncormortable with Max walking around or underneath them, there has never been an occasion where they have accidently steped on Max. Because of his size he is no threat at all, but some dogs can be a problem, even your friendly family pet.</p>
