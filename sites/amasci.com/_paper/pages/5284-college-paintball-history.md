<td><h1 id="college-paintball-history-in-brief">College Paintball History in Brief</h1>
<p>The history of college paintball is almost as long as that of paintball itself. Below are a few highlights. Do you have a piece of paintball history that should be added? Please email it to Chris at <a href="mailto:raehl311@yahoo.com">raehl311@yahoo.com</a>.</p>
<hr />
<ul>
<li></li>
<li>1986 - The first college paintball club is formed at the <a href="http://www.geocities.com/Pentagon/4152/index2.html" target="_home">United States Military Academy</a>.</li>
<li>1991 - <a href="http://www.clubs.psu.edu/paintball" target="_home">Penn State Paintball Association</a>, the paintball club for Penn State University, is formed.</li>
<li>1991 - <a href="http://www.sos.mtu.edu/paintball/" target="_home">Michigan Tech Paintball Club</a> is formed.</li>
<li>1992 - <a href="http://www.purduepaintball.com" target="home">Purdue University Paintball Club</a> is formed.</li>
<li>April 5, 1997 - West Virginia University hosts the College Challenge at Mountaineer War Games in Morgantown, West Virginia. First Place: <a href="http://www.clubs.psu.edu/paintball" target="_home">Penn State University</a></li>
<li>September 29, 1997 - First <a href="neic/main.html">Northeast Intercollegiate Tournament</a> organized by Andrew Yeh of Drexel University and held at <a href="http://www.topgunpaintball.com" target="_home">Top Gun Paintball</a> in Monmouth, New Jersey. First Place: University of Rochester</li>
<li>March 27, 1999 - First <a href="mict/main.html">Midwest Intercollegiate Tournament</a> organized by Purdue University and held at Sherwood Forest in LaPorte, Indiana. First Place: <a href="http://www.uiuc.edu/ro/ipw">University of Illinois</a></li>
<li>August 17, 2000 - University of Illinois student and MICT III co-organizer Chris Raehl registers www.college-paintball.com and forms the National Collegiate Paintball Association.</li>
<li>September 30, 2000 - First College National Championships are held at Paintfest in LaPorte, Indiana. First Place: <a href="http://www.uiuc.edu/ro/ipw">University of Illinois Illini Paintball Warriors</a></li>
<li>Spring 2001 - The NCPA runs the first College Paintball Season, with four conference events and a National Championships, including the first Great Plains Intercollegiate Conference and South-East Intercollegiate Conference events. Event pricing is as low as $65/team and $45/case, making the college league the lowest priced national league by a wide margin.</li>
<li>September 16, 2001 - First NCPA Elections for the Board of Directors end. Elected are Matt Spitz from Iowa State University, Paul Searl from Iowa State University, Karen Dunn from Purdue University, Mike Ramsey from Iowa State University, Brent Miller from the University of Maryland-College Park, Brad Reardon from Berry College, and Owen Panzica from the University of Arizona.</li>
<li>September 18, 2001 - The Board of Directors holds its first meeting, electing Brad Reardon as the Chairman and Matt Spitz as the Vice-Chairman. They also appoint NCPA Founder Chris Raehl as President by a unanimous vote.</li>
<li>September 24, 2001 - The National Collegiate Paintball Association is incorporated as "NCPA, Inc.", a non-stock (non-profit), tax-exempt organization capable of accepting tax-deductible contributions.</li>
<li>October 20, 2001 - The Fall 2001 MICT at Challenge Park Xtreme sets a college event attendence record of 22 5-man teams from as far as Iowa, New York, Maryland and Georgia.</li>
<li>December 6, 2001 - The <a href="Iowa%20State%20University%20Paintball%20Club%20receives%20$14,000%20from%20Iowa%20State%20University%20to%20construct%20the%20first%20permanent%20on-campus%20paintball%20field%20ever,%20including%20netting,%20an%20airball%20field,%20and%20rentals.%20%20Over%208,000%20students%20walk%20by%20the%20site%20each%20day.%0A%3CP%3E%3CLI%3E%3CFONT%20COLOR=#FF00FF%3EDecember%209,%202001%3C/FONT%3E%20-%20%3CA%20HREF=" http:="" data-www.diablodirect.com"="">Diablo Direct</a> becomes the first NCPA Gold Contributor by pledging to donate $10 towards the entry fees of each college and high school team at every NCPA event.</li>
</ul></td>
