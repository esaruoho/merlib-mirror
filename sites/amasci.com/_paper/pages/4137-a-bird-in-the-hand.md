# A Bird In The Hand

*Source page: `~kilroy69/birdpg.html`*

While on my ways to Phelps Mountain, I stopped to eat lunch at the Marcy Dam area, a popular spot in the Adirondacks. This bird decided I might be the type to share my lunch and landed on my hand to grab a few bites. I managed to <img src="http://web.syr.edu/~jgpoupor/birdhani.gif" data-align="CENTER" alt="GIF of bird that birdLanded On My Hand" /> get my camera out, making lots of noise and moving to do so and snap this shot. After I took the picture, I ate the bite to the right of the bird and it didn't fly away until after I was moving the sandwich away from my mouth. Trusting little fellow :-)\\\
