We will gladly list NOW chapters in the Pacific NW, especially Web and email addresses. So if you are a chapter contact in Oregon, Idaho, Montana, N.California, Alaska, or Hawaii, please send email to <feminist@eskimo.com> and we'll list you.

Remember, if your mailing label ends with WA 0000, you're an at-large member of Washington State NOW. You can affiliate with a local chapter above to receive its newsletter for free. Phone or write us for the current chapter contact. Addresses are current for 1997.

If no chapter exists in your area, why not start one? You don't need a lot of members to do so, and the Eastern and Western VPs would be glad to help you convene a new chapter from NOW members in your area.

*(Note: state policy is not to list chapter contact numbers or non-PO Boxes on the Net unless chapters or contacts request it - if you wish the contact name and phone number, please email the [Secretary](officers.html) to request it.*

If you're in another state, you can check with [National NOW](http://www.now.org) for their list of [Chapter and State Contacts](http://www.now.org/chapters/states.html)
