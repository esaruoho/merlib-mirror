# Used National Geographic Magazines - 1981

*Source page: `~robertf/stuff/natgeo81.html`*

Surplus 1981 *National Geographic* Magazines for Sale

The magazines on this web site belong to Bob and Emma Fleming. In order to simplify the selling of the magazines that we no longer want, we are consolidating all of our sales through our business that sells surplus items, [Flemco Surplus Sales](http://www.eskimo.com/~surplus/index.html).

These magazines are for sale for US\$3.00 each (plus shipping, taxes, etc., as explained below).

Supplemental (loose) maps are <u>NOT</u> included because most are missing. However we do have a few maps. If a map is available for the issue of magazine that you buy, you can buy the map for an additional US\$0.25. [Click for details](http://www.eskimo.com/~surplus/stuff/natgeomaps.html).

The purchaser shall be responsible for costs of shipping and handling, taxes, and customs duties as applicable.
