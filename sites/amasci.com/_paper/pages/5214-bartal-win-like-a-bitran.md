<td data-valign="top" width="580"><h1 id="win-like-a-bitran">Win Like a Bitran</h1>
by Senior Harper apprentice <a href="../bartal.html">Bartal</a>
<p><em>(Sung to the tune of the Bangles' "Walk Like an Egyptian")</em></p>
<hr />
Bartal wastes no time in starting the piece, his fingers gliding along the gitar's neck, creating a short whining sound before launching into a fast-paced, high-energy song. He sings,
<p><em>"All the old gamblers in the Hall<br />
Practice dragonpoker, don't you know<br />
If you aren't too good, oh-ay-oh<br />
Supply of marks will fall very low"</em></p>
<p>"All the bakers near the hearth<br />
They wager who'll lose their savings first<br />
All the old gamblers, oh-ay-oh<br />
They drink the wine quick but never drop"</p>
<p>The chording on the gitar modulates up a bit, and although the rhythm changes slightly, the tempo remains the same. Bartal sings,</p>
<p><em>"The Hold Harper teaching scales sings<br />
Way-oh way-oh<br />
ooh-way-ay-oh ay-oh ...</em></p>
<p>Win like a sly Bitran"</p>
<p>Bartal hits the side of the gitar once at the end of the line, as a punctuation.</p>
<p>The tone returns to that of the earlier part of the song, frenetic, yet cheery. Bartal sings,</p>
<p><em>"The kitchen help they bring you food<br />
They spin around then they cross the floor<br />
They've got the moves, oh-ay-oh<br />
You drop your drink then they bring you more"</em></p>
<p>"All the Hold kids all done with lessons<br />
They like hide-and-seek and other sport<br />
When they play their games, oh-ay-oh<br />
They like to win like a Bitran"</p>
<p>Bartal's gitarwork moves toward the chorus as he sings,</p>
<p><em>"The watchweyr on the Hold ramp howls<br />
Way-oh way-oh<br />
ooh-way-ay-oh ay-oh ...</em></p>
<p>Win like a sly Bitran"</p>
<p>Again the harper slaps the side of his gitar in punctuation, then launches into the bridge. Fingers dancing along the strings yield a line that first climbs, then drops, and finally evens out in a new key.</p>
<p>Bartal whistles the tune of the refrain once, the gitar stilled.</p>
<p>After whistling, Bartal goes back to singing:</p>
<p><em>"The trader clan display their wares<br />
They get the marks for all their troubles<br />
Renegades beware, oh-ay-oh<br />
They really are as tough as they look"</em></p>
<p>"If you want to find off-duty Guards<br />
They're in the tower, dealing out a round of cards<br />
Captain's got a five of dragons, oh-ay-oh<br />
That's known as a Clutch, don't you know"</p>
<p>Bartal slips into a sneaky grin as he looks about, strumming the gitar strings with vigor. He sings,</p>
<p><em>"All the crafters with honed skills<br />
When gather comes, up go their tents<br />
And the gamblers know, oh-ay-oh<br />
Their chance to win like a sly Bitran"</em></p>
<p>Moving toward the chorus and climax, Bartal's playing continues to be insistent, yet clear. He sings,</p>
<p><em>"All the novices, when dealt great hands, go<br />
Way-oh way-oh<br />
ooh-way-ay-oh ay-oh ...</em></p>
<p>Gamblers play like sly Bitrans<br />
Try to win like a Bitran"</p>
<p>The words done, Bartal is left with the gitar to finish out the song. He repeats the refrain a few times, fading out as he goes. When it almost seems silent, he increases the volume and ends in a flourish.</p>
