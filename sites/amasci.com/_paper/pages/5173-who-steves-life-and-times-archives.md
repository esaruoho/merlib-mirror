<img src="../images/who_orig.gif" data-align="top" data-border="1" width="150" height="50" alt="[Origins]" />\
I didn't always know I was going to be a writer, but I did write some stories as a child. I was greatly influenced both by my mother (with her English degree), my father (with his sense of humor) and my grandfather Joseph Pohl.

Grandpa Joe wrote a long series of stories about, and to his grandchildren. Me, my sister and my cousins, plus Mr. Rabbit, Mr. Mouse, Mr. Friddle and a cast of oddball characters populated these stories.

A combination of just plain children's fantasy (flying beds), laugh out-loud humor (I seem to remember a cornflake gun of sorts), and his wonderful drawings spurred my imagination.

When I decided that I would major in journalism, I dedicated this decision to my grandfather's memory. I just hope I can touch someone and make them laugh as he did for me those many years ago (and still).

[<img src="../images/who_tpg.gif" data-border="0" width="231" height="79" alt="[The Polaris Group]" />](http://www.polarisgroup.com)

I'm working full-time now at **The Polaris Group**, doing production and q/a work on the [Microsoft for Partners](http://www.microsoft.com/partner/) Web site. I began in late October 2001 and became a permanent employee this June.\
\

[<img src="../images/who_twsmsft.gif" data-border="0" width="201" height="85" alt="[Microsoft and The Write Stuff]" />](http://www.microsoft.com/sql/)

After working in technology in the Seattle area for years, I finally worked a contract copy editing job at **Microsoft**, working on the *[SQL Server 2000](http://www.microsoft.com/sql/) Resource Kit*.\
\

[<img src="../images/who_onhealth.gif" data-border="0" data-align="right" width="125" height="46" alt="[OnHealth.com]" />](http://onhealth.webmd.com/)

I took a chance at a startup/public company called **[OnHealth Network Company](http://onhealth.webmd.com)** in the throes of a merger (with WebMD) as an Editorial Production Assistant. Despite a good six weeks on the job, I was deemed "redundant" and let go. I'm still in touch with many of the editorial folks (and the site no longer exists).\
\

[<img src="../images/who_twsamzn.gif" data-border="0" width="201" height="85" alt="[Amazon.com]" />](http://www.amazon.com/)

I spent two months as a Copyeditor at **[Amazon.com](http://www.amazon.com)** through the agency **[The Write Stuff](http://www.writestuff.com)**. It was a contract job and ended up being temporary. It was here I learned that "copyeditor" can be one word.\
\

[<img src="../images/abc_blank.gif" data-border="0" width="130" height="85" alt="[ABCNEWS.com]" />](http://abcnews.go.com/)

Seems like only yesterday that I started on the "News project" at Starwave, working on what eventually became **[ABCNEWS.com](http://abcnews.go.com/)**. That was October 1996, and since then I served as an intern up to morning wire editor, from development and launch to its status as the fastest-growing news site on the Web. When I left, my job included assembling a story budget for, keeping tabs on, and posting stories in the Business, Science, Technology, Travel and Health & Living sections, plus Sports and Entertainment. (This was the Seattle newsroom -- there's a larger newsroom in New York.)\
\

[<img src="../images/ool.gif" data-border="0" width="201" height="88" alt="[Outside Online]" />](http://www.outsidemag.com)

[**Outside Online**](http://www.outsidemag.com), produced at Starwave like ABCNEWS.com, was where I was an Editorial intern from June to mid-October, 1996. I wrote and edited stories, coordinated event coverage of the [Leadville 100](http://www.outsidemag.com/events/leadville96/) and helped fix up older pages to standard HTML, among other things. (Starwave no longer produces the site.)\
\

[<img src="../images/epost.gif" data-border="0" width="205" height="58" alt="[E-Post]" />](http://www.wwu.edu/~journal/epost/)

[**The Western Electronic Post**](http://www.wwu.edu/~journal/epost/) was the unofficial e-mail newsletter for the Department of Journalism at Western. I served as Editor from June 1995 to September 1996, and then helped out in the summer of 1997.\
\

[<img src="../images/wfront.gif" data-border="0" width="190" height="88" alt="[Western Front]" />](http://westernfront.wwu.edu/)

[**The Western Front**](http://westernfront.wwu.edu/) is the school newspaper at Western. From September 1994 to June 1996 I served in many capacities on the Front, including reporter, News editor (twice), Graphics editor, Features editor, Assistant News editor and Managing editor.
