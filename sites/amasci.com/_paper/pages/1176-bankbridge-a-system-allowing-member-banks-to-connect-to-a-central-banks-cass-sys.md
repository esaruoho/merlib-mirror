<td width="329" data-valign="bottom" height="93"><p>When Central Banks introduce Clearing and Settlement Systems (CASS) Commercial Banks need to prepare themselves for this change.<br />
Commercial Banks will need to be in a position to provide and receive clearing files electronically to and from the Central Bank.</p>
<p><strong>Most Central Banks require that the files be :</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> Of a format specified them<br />
<img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> Compressed<br />
<img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> Encrypted<br />
<img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> Transferred over FTP</p>
</blockquote></td>
<td width="251" data-valign="top" height="93"><div data-align="right">
<img src="pics/bankbridge-softlook.gif" data-align="top" width="250" height="164" />
</div></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<br />
BankBridge is a sophisticated software package that can assist Commercial Banks in not just meeting these requirements, but ensuring that files created by their legacy systems are validated to ensure the integrity of file data.
<p><strong>Bankbridge Supports :</strong></p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> Validation</strong><br />
Central Banks will specify file formats for the messages being processed, namely:</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Financial Messages such as :</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Cheques<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Promissory notes (bills)<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Transfer orders<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Settlement orders<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> etc.</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Non Financial Messages such as:</strong></p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Inquiry / Response about an account number<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Inquiry / Response about the state of account<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Information about opening/ closing of a branch<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Change in membership condition<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Change of representative / operator<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Suspension or exclusion of bank<br />
<strong><img src="images/blue-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> etc.</p>
</blockquote>
</blockquote>
<p><br />
Commercial Banks will need to prepare and process files that conform to these formats. BankBridge validates files created by existing systemsto ensure any files being sent to Central Banks conforms to the formats prescribed by them.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong></strong> <strong>Encryption</strong><br />
To ensure the Authentication, Integrity, Non-Repudiation and Confidentiality of files, BankBridge uses a highly secure encryption technique.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Compression<br />
</strong>Standard ASCII files, when compressed, can result in a huge saving in bandwidth and reliability of transfer. BankBridge compress files by upto 95% using a sophisticated compression technology resulting in transfer times of minutes rather than hours.</p>
<p><strong><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> FTP Transfer</strong><br />
BankBridge has built in FTP capabilities which allows a Commercial Bank to transfer files via Dial-Up Lines, Leased Lines, VSAT or the Internet.</p>
<p><strong><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> Interface to C-CATS<br />
</strong>BankBridge has a built in interface to C-CATS (the Cheque Clearing Automation System from Bankstar) which will allow banks to Clear Inter-Bank and Intra-Bank cheques electronically using a user friendly interface. C-CATS automatically creates clearing files which conform to the standards specified by The Central Bank.</p></td>
