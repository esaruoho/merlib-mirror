> > Finland: Kalevalan piv -- KALEVALA DAY, commemorating the first printing of the ancient national epic, in 1835, compiled by Elias Lnnrot.
>
> \
>
> 
>
> > The material, old Finnish ballads & lyrical songs depicting "the sons of Kalevala", were published in two editions, first 35 cantos in 1835 & then with 50 cantos in 1849.
> >
> > The central characters are old & wise Vinminen, skilled smooth Ilmarinen, adventurer-warrior Lemminkinen, Louhi, the female ruler of Pohjola, & tragic hero Kullervo. The epic ends with decline of paganism: maid Marjatta gives birth to a son who is baptized king of Karelia & Vinminen departs the land of heroes.
> >
> > Kalevala, translated into 35 languages, has inspired many Finnish artists, the most prominent among them painter Akseli Galln-Kallela & composer Jean Sibelius.
> >
> > <http://www.helsinki.fi/kasv/nokol/kalevala.html%0B>\

> > 1893 -- Ben Hecht lives. American screenwriter, director, producer, playwright, novelist lives. Authored 35 books & created some of the most entertaining screenplays or plays, among them The Front Page (filmed as His Girl Friday), Twentieth Century, Underworld, The Scoundrel, Some Like It Hot, Viva Villa!, etc.\
> > 
> >
> > > Hecht was for a short time a circus acrobat & at 17 he ran away to Chicago, where he worked as a reporter. After WW I he was sent to Berlin to witness the revolutionary movements, which was the basis of his first novel, Erik Dorn (1921).
> > >
> > > His criticism of British policies in Palestine & support of the Jewish resistance movement caused his credits to be removed from all films shown in England for some years. In his honor an illegal immigrant ship was named "Ben Hecht".
> > >
> > > Hecht died while working on the script of Casino Royale (1967), receiving no screen credits. Also among productions credited to other writers are Queen Christina, Gone With the Wind, Foreign Correspondent, The Outlaw, Lifeboat, Gilda, The Paradine Case, Rope, & Roman Holiday.

1998 -- <span id="Senez"></span>Andre Senez, French shoemaker, anarchist, dies.\


> Militant in the Jeunesse Anarchiste Communiste (Anarchist Communist Youth) then in the Union Anarchiste, its parent organisation, & then in the post-war Federation Anarchiste.
