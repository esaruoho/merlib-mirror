# Issues And Decisions You'll Have to Consider

Do I need my own domain? If you're putting up your personal page you probably don't want or need your own domain. Most personal accounts will have a *URL* (address) that looks something like:\
http://www.somedomain.com/~useraccount/\
If you're doing business, you might want your own domain. This does not mean you have to run your own server and hire a computer staff. I can *Virtually Host* your website at a remote site where computer professionals worry about the technical side of maintaining a world wide web server/daemon. With a virtual domain, your *URL* will look something like:\
http://www.YOURDOMAINNAME.com/\
where you get to pick YOURDOMAINNAME (i.e. your company name.) This makes it easier for netsurfers to remember (all they have to remember is the one part you picked, the rest is pretty standard.) It's surprisingly cheaper than you might think! <span id="price"></span>\
