# The History of Writing on Stamps

Since the first postage stamp[<sup>1</sup>](index.html#1) --- The Penny Black --- was issued in 1846 by Great Britain, stamps have depicted topics ranging from space colonization to Neaderthal Man.

The American Topical Association --- founded in 1949 --- has over 500 topical lists available to members. These list barely scratch the surface, in terms of topics that are collected.

I am a handwriting analyst, thus am interested in handwriting and how it developed over the centuries. From the earliest cave art, to the most recent "space age pens."[<sup>2</sup>](index.html#2)

I am still building my topical collection, and finding stamps that belong in it. Along the way, I also am building lists of stamps on related topics.
