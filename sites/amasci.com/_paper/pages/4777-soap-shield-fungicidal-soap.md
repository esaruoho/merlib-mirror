<td><strong>Soap-Shield Fungicidal Soap<br />
An important breakthrough in natural disease control!</strong><br />
<em></em>
<br />
<img src="http://www.gardenalive.com/images/prodimage_large/8066.jpg" data-align="right" /> <em>With Soap-Shield, you'll pick fresh tomatoes much later in the season than ever before! Use Soap-Shield against septoria leaf spot, blights and other tough fungal diseases.</em><br />
&#10;<p><strong>Signature Product.</strong> We're excited to offer this patented garden fungicide to our customers. Soap-Shield is more effective than its competition and environmentally superior. Here's why:<br />
<br />
<br />
    <strong><strong><a href="http://clickserve.cc-dt.com/link/click?lid=41000000003506965&amp;redirect=http://www.gardensalive.com/item_display.asp?ProductNumber=8066">Buy Now .....More...</a></strong></strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Less copper!</strong> Soap-Shield is more effective against fungal diseases than other copper products, even while using less copper in its formula.</li>
<li><strong>Easier to use!</strong> Other copper fungicides work only on wet leaf surfaces, but Soap-Shield works on both wet or dry surfaces. It won't clog your sprayer, either.</li>
<li><strong>Decomposes!</strong> Soap-Shield eventually decomposes to form soluble copper and fatty acid, both necessary to the growth of plants and soil microorganisms.</li>
<li><strong>Controls powdery mildew!</strong> Soap-Shield does an excellent job of controlling powdery mildew, a widespread problem on vegetables and ornamentals. (Other fungicides can't control it without adding sulfur.)</li>
<li><strong>More rain resistant!</strong> Soap-Shield is a "fixed copper" fungicide, so it resists rain better than standard copper sulfate fungicides.</li>
<li><strong>Leaves no ugly residue!</strong> Unlike other fixed copper fungicides, which leave a heavy blue coating on leaf surfaces, Soap-Shield leaves virtually no residue.</li>
</ul>
<strong>Protects Against Dozens of Destructive Fungal Diseases</strong>
<p>With Soap-Shield, you now can grow vegetables, fruits and flowers that you may have given up on because of their susceptibility to disease! Soap-Shield combines copper with a naturally-occurring fatty acid. Together they form a "true soap" with disease-fighting power never before seen in an all-natural fungicide.</p>
<strong>Less Copper, Less Disease</strong>
<p>In our own field and lab tests, Soap-Shield was effective at much lower concentrations of copper than other copper-based products. As Soap-Shield biodegrades, its components are taken up and used by growing plants and soil microorganisms.</p>
<p>We used to think tomato plants just died naturally after ripening their fruits. But now we know that if septoria leaf spot is controlled, the tomato plants keep producing many more, large, juicy tomatoes, greatly extending the season. We'll use Soap-Shield to stop septoria leaf spot (as well as anthracnose) because it is environmentally superior to the standard copper fungicides on the market. Soap-Shield even controls botrytis neck rot disease on onions, so our onions last longer in storage, too.</p>
<p>Soap-Shield controls so many plant diseases that we think of it as an "all-in-one" fungicide. Even if you're not sure which disease is attacking your plants, Soap-Shield is worth a try.</p>
<strong>Odorless and Easy to Use</strong>
<p>Just mix Soap-Shield with water and spray on foliage. For best results, start applying early in the season on plants that are susceptible to disease in your area. Continue spraying to harvest time!<br />
