This Woodsman is in absolutely new condition, along with the box, papers, test target, and tools. It was shipped to LtCol Julian Hatcher in Aberdeen, MD in June, 1938. LtCol Hatcher (later Major General) was a long time technical editor of <strong><em>The American Rifleman</em></strong> and author of <strong><em>Hatcher's Notebook,</em></strong> among many others.</td>
