### In Florida

[![\[US17 bridge over St. Mary's River\]](bord1_t.jpg)](border1.jpg) [<img src="ocala19_t.jpg" data-"="" alt="[19 miles from Ocala]" />](ocala19.jpg)

<span style="background-color: #ffff00"> </span>

In 1983, this was the 1000-mile point of the ride and it was warm, in the 70's and 80's. In 2002, it was about mile 300 and about 50 degrees, not the weather I'd expect for Florida but warmer than frosty South Carolina.

Pictures from the GA-FL border on US 17: [1 of 3](border1.jpg) (hasn't changed) \| [2 of 3](border2.jpg) (they did plant some more trees here) \| [3 of 3](border3.jpg) (but these three trees were here in 1983)

[Back in Nassau County (Florida didn't realize that NY already used the name), on Route 108](nass108.jpg). ;-)

[Cut back through the southernmost tip of Georgia](crawford.jpg) along the St. Mary's River and finally (in background) back across the river into Florida again.

[On US 301, a quick and scenic route towards the Tampa area -- here 19 miles north of Ocala](ocala19.jpg).

[Citra, FL](citra.jpg). Time to give into the garish billboards advertising freshly picked and squeezed orange juice. The billboard to the right is one of many counting down the miles to Citra, and finally pointing to the citrus stand at the left. It was delicious but unpasteurized so I had to drink it all before getting to Ocala.
