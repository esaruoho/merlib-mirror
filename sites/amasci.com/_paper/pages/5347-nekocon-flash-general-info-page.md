<td><strong>Memberships</strong>
Why would you want to Pre-Reg for the con? A few good reasons really. Shorter lines when you check in, You can save a yourself a few extra bucks. It also helps the con to do more and offer more to everyone at the con to make it a better time for all. The current membership level and rates are as follows.
<p><strong>*Single Memberships</strong><br />
These are good for all three days of the convention, including certain events that may happen on Thursday night. You get access to all public functions and events at the convention.<br />
<br />
$30.00 Pre-Reg until October 6, 2001<br />
$40.00 at the Door<br />
Child Rates: Newborn-6 years old = Free,......7-11 years old = Half Price,......over 12 = Full Price</p>
----------
<p><strong>*Group Memberships</strong><br />
Group Memberships are also good for all three days of the convention</p>
<p>$125 for 5 Memberships until October 6, 2001<br />
(thats $25 a person!) additional memberships are $25 each</p>
<p>Group registration is for pre-reg only - not at the door.<br />
Memberships must all come in the same envelope.<br />
No adding people later, ever, no exceptions!</p>
----------
<p><strong>*Nekocon Flash T-shirts</strong><br />
The current Nekocon Flash T-Shirt design is located <a href="/shirts%22"><strong>HERE</strong></a>.</p>
<p><br />
$43.00 Pre-Reg &amp; T-shirt Special (add $2 for 2XL/3XL)<br />
$17.50 T-shirt Only (add $2 for 2XL/3XL)<br />
(prices include shipping)</p>
----------
<p><strong>*Supporting Memberships</strong><br />
What is a Supporting Membership? Its a membership for those who can't attend the con but would like to "support" the con. Immediatly after the con, we will mail you a Nekocon Registration Packet. You will get a color laminated badge with your name on it, program book and any other goodies that are included. .<br />
<br />
$12.00 Supporting Membership until October 6, 2001<br />
$26.00 Supporting Membership &amp; T-shirt until October 6<br />
(T-shirt price includes shipping, add $2 for 2XL/3XL shirt)</p>
<p>If plans change and you are able to attend Nekocon, you can stop by the At-Door Registration desk and upgrade your Supporting Membership to a Single Membership for an additional $30.00</p>
----------
<p>POSTMARKING: The envelope with your registration information and payment should be postmarked by the dates stated above. This just means you have it to your mail carrier, dropped in your mail box or dropped off at the post office by this date. There's no need to spend $12.00 to overnight a letter just to save $5.00, a 34 cents postage letter will do the job.</p>
<p>Help our pre-reg staff. Please Download and print out the forms below. PLEASE fill out the registration form completely, including everyones postal addresses and email information if possible. This will help keep our Pre-reg staff from pulling their hair out and really speed things up when you and your group arrive at the con.</p>
<p><strong>No Memberships will be accepted that are postmarked after Oct 6. Online pre-reg memberships will be accepted till October 13, 2001. After Oct 6 (Oct 13 for Online), please pay at-door.</strong></p>
<p><a href="pdf/gso-reg-form-flash.pdf"><img src="pdficon.gif" data-border="0" width="32" height="32" /></a> <strong>- <a href="pdf/gso-reg-form-flash.pdf">Single (or Supporting) Membership Form (10K)</a><br />

</strong></p>
<p><a href="pdf/gso-group-form-large.pdf"><img src="pdficon.gif" data-border="0" width="32" height="32" /></a> <strong>- <a href="pdf/gso-group-form-large.pdf">Group Membership Form (11K)</a><br />

Nekocon is currently using the services of PAYPAL to allow attendees to purchase SINGLE Pre-Reg Memberships with their credit card.</p>

Restrictions on one day memberships are that you cannot submit art work or place bids on art work in the Art Show, or submit entries in the Music Video Contest.</p>
