# SSC Main Page

*Source page: `~savoy/mainpage.htm`*

<img src="images/ssclogo.gif" width="401" height="150" />

<div align="left">

<div align="center">

The **Savoy Swing Club** was founded in Seattle in 1993 to preserve and promote Savoy-style Lindy Hop, the original form of Swing dance that developed in Harlem during the late 1920's and 1930's. SSC is an <a href="sscboard.htm" target="Viewframe">incorporated</a>, non-profit, volunteer-based social dance organization with more than 250 annual members. The club presents Lindy Hop to the community through <a href="perform.htm" target="Viewframe">performances and demonstrations</a>, [classes](classes.htm), [dances](sonny.htm), [workshops](events.htm) <a href="events.htm" target="Viewframe">and special dance events</a>, and a newsletter mailed to <a href="member.htm" target="Viewframe">members</a>.

The club features two performing groups; the <a href="performa.htm" target="Viewframe">Performance Troupe</a> tours the Pacific Northwest performing Lindy Hop and 1930's jazz, and <a href="http://www.emeraldcityswing.com/f_home.html" target="Viewframe">Emerald City Swing</a> offers participative demonstrations and performances.
