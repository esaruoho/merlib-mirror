    ### Wrestling Organizations and Events

2.  <a href="http://www.metrowrestling.org" target="_blank">Metro Wrestling (MGWA) website -- New York City</a>. Metro holds wrestling practice twice weekly in New York (Wednesday and Sunday) at the Center, and also hosts beginners’ nights, tournaments and other special events.

3.  [Twin Towers Wrestling Club](http://www.twintowerswrestling.com) meets Tuesday, Thursday and some Sundays at the Hamilton Fish Recreation Center on New York's Lower East Side. Or [e-mail Ed](mailto:ee11111111@aol.com) for more information.

4.  <a href="http://www.bostonunionwrestling.com" target="_blank">The Boston Union Wrestling Club</a> meets in the Roxbury area of Boston; check their website for details. They also have a <a href="http://sports.groups.yahoo.com/group/BostonUnionWrestlingClub/" target="_blank">Yahoo group</a>.

5.  <a href="http://eastcoastwrestlingclub.org/" target="_blank">East Coast Wrestling Club, Rhode Island USA</a> (with links to many other sites, vendors and events).

6.  <a href="http://www.metrowrestling.org/other.html" target="_blank">Other Wrestling Clubs</a> around the world <a href="http://man2manfightclub.com/" target="_blank"></a>

    ### Vendors of wrestling supplies

16. <a href="http://ggwc.org/store/" target="_blank">Golden Gate's store -- calendars, workout gear and the latest regulation short-legged singlets</a>.

17. [Grecco Gear - Vintage Cut Wrestling Singlets, Fight Shorts, Accessories, Gallery](http://www.greccogear.com)

18. <a href="http://www.lockergear.com/wrestle.htm" target="_blank">Rodco Sports</a> supplies many teams and individuals with wrestling gear, uniforms and supplies. Rob from Rodco was at Hillside Wrestling Weekend VI selling a wide selection of equipment. You can also send e-mail to Rodco via their website. Items for sale include singlets (spandex, nylon, low-cut, high-cut, regular and jam), shoes, headgear, tights, knee pads, jockstraps and briefs.

19. <a href="http://www.matmanwrestling.com" target="_blank">Matman Wrestling Company</a> -- “Quality Wrestling Supplier since 1969” and supplier to colleges, high schools, teams and individuals.

20. <a href="http://www.marchants.com/e-wrestling.html" target="_blank">Marchant’s</a> in Toronto, sells mats, gear, etc. including international cut reversible singlets (Brute, stock \#0111).

21. <a href="http://eastcoastwrestlingclub.org/gear.html" target="_blank">ECWC’s comprehensive webpage</a> listing vendors of all wrestling supplies and gear.
