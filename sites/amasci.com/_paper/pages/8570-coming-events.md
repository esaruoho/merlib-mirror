<span id="annual"></span>

**ANNUAL EVENTS**

**THE BIG BASH** - Held every February\
A party just for the grown-ups featuring a different ethnic theme each year. Food and music appropriate to the theme are provided.

**CHOCOLATE SUNDAY** - Third or Fourth Sunday in February\
Remember your sweetheart at the chocolate "brunch". Bring a cake, cookies, candies or other goodies made of chocolate to share after the service.

**AUCTION**- Early in March\
Our version of March Madness! There are sumptious dinners, picnics, boat rides, the use of vacation cabins, and many, many treasures to bid on. Dinner is served and drinks are available.

**SILVERTON RETREAT** - Memorial Day Weekend\
An unstructured family camoing retreat to cabins inl the woods on the Mountain Loop Highway. There is no program, just an opportunity to talk, eat, hike, play games and hang out.

**RUMMAGE SALE** - First Weekend in May\
Tons of good stuff at amszingly low prices! It's realy a huge multi-family garage sale where church members bring the best of their unwanted goodies.\>

**SUMMER PICNIC** - Third or Fourth Sunday in August\
A potluck picnic held on the church grounds with fellowship and games for young and old.

**SEABECK CHURCH RETREAT** - Seabeck Conference Center on Hood Canal, September 21, 22, 23\
This is an annual event which provides an opportunity for church families to learn, reflect, read, and play in a beautifully wooded shoreline setting.. This year the program is built around the theme, "Your Time is Up....What's Next?" Imagine it's your last day on earth, what would you do? Who would you do it with? We'll explore what really matters, the impact of our lives, and our expectations and beliefs about what happens after death.

This year we will be joined by members from the Woodinvillle and South Whidby churchs, giving us a chance to get aquainted with those good folks.

**NORTHWEST ARTISTS HOLIDAY SHOW** - The First Weekend in November\
An invitational show featuring artists and craftsmen from around the Pacific Northwest and specialty food products. The "Upstairs Gallery" includes a free buffet hors d'oeurve table.

**AFFINITY GROUPS**

Most of these groups are formed around common interests. They are different from adult classes and activities in that they are generally small, ongoing, and meet throughout the year. The majority remain small to allow each member to feel comfortable in sharing thoughts and feelings.
