- ***Hi. I'm Kendra DiAnn Lange.***

  Well, I'm more than three years old now!! I'm 39 inches tall and I weigh 36 lbs. I love playing at the park and splashing in the pool. I like getting together with my friends any chance I get. I enjoy coloring and painting and I'm a TV addict! I have too many favorite shows to list here.

  p.s. - for those who don't know, I was born on May 14th, 1998.  I weighed 9 lbs, 15 oz and 21 1/2 inches!\
   \
   \

  ***What's that thing on my lip?*** Mommy and Daddy told me that an angel kissed me there a couple weeks after I was born.  But secretly I heard them talking about it and I found out that it is a hemangioma ("he-man-gee-oh-ma").  The short definition is that it is a temporary birthmark that will go away on its own someday.  Here's several links if you want to read about hemangiomas:

  - [Hemangiomas](http://www.healthanswers.com/database/ami/converted/001459.html)
  - [Hemangiomas and Vascular Malformations](http://mcrcr4.med.nyu.edu/PlasticSurg/Divs/hemang.htm)
  - [Hemangiomas of the Head and Neck](http://www.bcm.tmc.edu/oto/grand/62592.html)
  - [Photoderm Treatment of Hemangiomas](http://www.skinspecialist.com/hemangio.htm)
