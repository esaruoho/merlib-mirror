**December 21, 1997 - Monarch**

- <img src="img/monarch.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Monarch logo" /> We went and stayed a few days at the Mount Princeton lodge in southwestern Colorado. Monarch Resort is only a short drive away and had gotten 19 inches of snow about two weeks earlier, but it was still in good shape.

<span id="2"></span>

**December 23, 1997 - Breckenridge**

- <img src="img/breck.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Breck logo" /> The last day of our trip was spent swinging by [Breckenridge](http://www.brecknet.com/) on our way back to Denver. They hadn't had snow in even longer than Monarch but between grooming and snow making it was in quite passable shape (how much can you complain about paradise!). The sky was crystal clear blue all day. They said it was 11 degrees but it felt more like 20!

  [-- back to index --](#top)

<span id="3"></span>

**February 14, 1998 - Ski Cooper**

- <img src="img/cooper.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Cooper logo" /> A Valentine's Day trip to a little place called [Ski Cooper](http://www.skicooper.com) which is just north of Leadville. This is one of the first places went skiing when I was first moving to Colorado and I hadn't been back and it was high time. It's a great place, it's small but nice wide runs, well-behaved snowboarders, and cheap lift tickes! Plus, with President's Day weekend traditionally being one of the highest traffic weekends, we thought a more out of the way place might fit the bill. It was fantastic.

  [-- back to index --](#top)

<span id="4"></span>

**March 14, 1998 - Keystone**

- <img src="img/keystone.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Keystone logo" /> There just isn't much new to say about Keystone except that it's a great place. They have built a bunch of new condos, which isn't great, but the architecture is interesting, lots of "old mining town" style structures.

  [-- back to index --](#top)

<span id="5"></span>

**April 4, 1997 - Winter Park**

- <img src="img/winterpark.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Winter Park logo" /> Another great trip to [Winter Park.](http://www.skiwinterpark.com/wpmj) This time we took the train from Union Station in Denver. What a way to go skiing! If you can get to the train station at 6:30 in the morning, the rest is easy! After a hard day of skiing, kick back and have a beer or two on the trip home. If this turns out to be the end of the season, it's a great way to end it!
