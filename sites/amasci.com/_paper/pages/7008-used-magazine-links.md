> **NOTES REGARDING THE SORTING SEQUENCE:** This list includes names from several languages. Various languages have different rules for sorting. For example, in Swedish the letters Å, Ä, and Ö come at the end of the alphabet. Unfortunately, it is impossible to accommodate the correct sorting sequence for all languages in one list.
>
> Therefore, this list uses the English sorting sequence as used in the index line below, and letters with diacritical marks (accents, umlauts, etc.) are sorted as if the diacritical mark is not there. So Á, À, Å, Ä, Â, and Ã will be intermingled with letter A.
>
> The English words "A", "An", and "The" (articles) are ignored if they occur at the beginning of a title and the title will be sequenced according to the first letter of the next word. For example, "The Weekly" would be found under "W". We also try to ignore initial articles in other languages, but we don't know the grammar for most languages so you may have to search both with and without the initial article. Hopefully we get it right for Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, and Italian.
