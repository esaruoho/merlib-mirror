<td><h3 id="introduction"><strong><u>Introduction:</u></strong></h3>
<strong>     What is this? Ramblings? What the heck does that mean?</strong> <br />
<strong>     Basically, Constant Reader, it is yet another forum in which I, Christine stroke</strong> <strong>my ego and run my mouth about my favorite toipic -- my writing. Specifically, my</strong> <strong>fanfic.</strong> <br />
<strong>     I'll explain how each title and story came to be, what I hoped to accomplish,</strong> <strong>what I actually did accomplish, how certain characters took occasionally unexpected</strong> <strong>turns. I'll also share some behind-the-scenes snippets of what was going through my</strong> <strong>admittedly warped brain at the time.</strong> <br />
<strong>     So, if you want to learn more of the behind-the-scenes goings-on and put up</strong> <strong>with me bragging and strutting, please read on! If not, hit your Back button to</strong> <strong>escape back to the stories ; )</strong> <br />
 &#10;<h3 id="prologue"><strong><u>Prologue:</u></strong></h3>
<strong>     In early 1996, my husband Tim asked me, out of simple curiosity, why I hadn't</strong> <strong>written any Gargoyles fanfic yet.</strong> <br />
<strong>     At the time, I had made a few forays into that arena, most notably with "A</strong> <strong>Matter of Pride," a story that, I'm told, is practically a must-read for all newcomers to</strong> <strong>The Lion King fandom (and here I thought I was deeply disturbed and nobody in their</strong> <strong>right mind would <em>ever</em> want to read something that combined sex and Disney</strong> <br />
<strong>cartoons -- okay, I was still fairly naive at the time, having yet to fully discover how the</strong> <strong>'Net could bring together those who shared certain points of view -- and that there were</strong> <strong>a heck of a lot more of them than I'd ever imagined. I wasn't alone!)</strong> <br />
<strong>     But ... Gargoyles? Because I knew that I had a knack or a flair for writing the</strong> <strong>naughty stuff, and I wasn't sure how I felt about the prospect. Trek, sure, Disney, fine, I</strong> <strong>could write sexy fanfic about those.</strong> <br />
<strong>     But ... Gargoyles? Gargoyles was <em>different</em>! It got ahold of my soul in a way</strong> <strong>that no other show had ever been able to do. Could I do justice to such characters?</strong> <strong>Would I cheapen them, diminish them in so doing? (some would say "absolutely;" you</strong> <strong>know who you are &lt;g&gt;)</strong> <br />
<strong>     I didn't want to, pardon the pun and the vulgarity, fuck it up. If it was to be</strong> <strong>done, it had to be done carefully, lovingly, right.</strong> <br />
<strong>     Another reason I held off was because the show was still going on. But when I</strong> <strong>learned of the hiatus that would take place that summer, between the stirring finale of</strong> <strong>Hunter's Moon and the debut of The Goliath Chronicles, I had several months to play</strong> <br />
<strong>with. Several months without Gargoyles.</strong> <br />
<strong>    I thought I could do it. I <em>wanted</em> to do it. At the time, I had yet to make the</strong> <strong>acquaintance of my fellow Gargophiles and so I had no idea how such stories would be</strong> <strong>received. I figured that nobody outside the pervs in the alt.sex.stories newsgroup would</strong> <strong>even see it.</strong> <br />
<strong>     Still, I wanted to give it a try. And the rest, as they say, is history ...</strong> 
<p><strong>As of this writing (6/20/99), there are 56 stories in my ongoing fanfic saga plus a few others done for TGS and other projects. </strong></p>
