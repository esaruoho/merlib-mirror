# Holy Instant Experience !

    This happened when I was in the U.S. Army, during Desert Storm.  I was
    fortunately frozen in my post in the Republic of Korea, and well out
    of the conflict happening in the Gulf.   I was a Chaplain Assistant,
    which for information purposes is the enlisted worker assigned to help
    the Chaplain (sort of the Army's version of chapel groundskeeper/church 
    secretary.)  I was stationed at Camp Essayons, and later at Camp Stanley 
    in Uijongbu, Korea as the Aviation Brigade Chapel NCO.  A little way 
    down the road (within bike riding distance)was Camp Red Cloud. The 
    CA at that chapel, Terry, was a good friend of mine.

    I had the afternoon off and decided to visit CRC and my buddy.  Since
    it was during the week the front doors to the chapel were locked, and
    the only way into the chapel was through the CA's office in the back. 
    I went in calling for Terry who was not there, although the computer
    and the lights were on.  I knew he'd be back soon.  I thought that he
    might be in the sanctuary of the chapel itself, so I walked in there
    calling for him.

    I saw Her, kneeling on the front row in prayer.  I apologized for
    disturbing her.  She just smiled at me.  The warm glow of Her presence
    filling my being with a profound sense of peace.  At first, I took Her
    to be a nun, She was dressed in what I took to be a blue and white
    nun's habit, holding a rosary.  I went looking for my friend in the
    narthex or entrance area of the chapel.  He was not there.  I came
    back through the sanctuary, and She smiled at me once again.  I nodded
    in recognition.  It was like no other experience I've had - the
    overwhelming sense of peace in the face of the Lady.

    Anyway, I went back into the CA's office, Terry had not returned.  I
    thought that maybe I could ask the "nun" where he went, or have Her
    let him know that I'd been there and would return shortly.  I
    re-entered the sanctuary and She was gone!  Now I have to let you
    realize that, there was no way out of the chapel, except through the
    CA's office where I was.  If She had left (in the ordinary way) she
    would have come by me, but she did not.  It was at this point that a
    thought entered my mind, like a bolt of lightning "Mary Mother of 
    God" (!!!)

    This is really odd since I'm not Catholic, neither has Catholic
    theology or "Mary-ology" ever interested me in the slightest.  The
    "revelation" which I had from this heavenly person was the experience,
    for the briefest moment of my life, of total peace and love. 

    The memory of this incident tends to fade as time passes, almost 
    forgotten until something brings it back up again. While "Our Lady" 
    spoke to me with her presence, GOD never has spoken to me. It is 
    His voice that I desire to hear.     Peace, Danny Shumaker
