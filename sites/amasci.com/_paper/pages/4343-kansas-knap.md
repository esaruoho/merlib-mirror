Come join other knappers at scenic Maxwell Wildlife Refuge, located 6 miles north and 1 miles west of Canton, Ks.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>Primitive camping is available on the grounds, or at adjacent McPherson State Lake.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">   </span>This event will be held in conjunction with the Friends of Maxwell Fall Rendezvous.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>Non period tents will have a separate camping area.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>Information on the Fall Rendezvous is detailed on the opposite side of this flyer.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>

 Bring your rock, trade items, and points for sale, a tarp to catch chips, and chair.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>Setup allowed as early as Thursday evening. No vehicles will be allowed in the exhibition area after set-up.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>The public will be invited to attend on Friday and Saturday, participants are invited to stay for Sunday.
