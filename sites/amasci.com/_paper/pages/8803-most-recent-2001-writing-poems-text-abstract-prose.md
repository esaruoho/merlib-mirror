Most Recent (2001) Writing -- Poems, Text, Abstract Prose

Again, in the spirit of my introduction to my miscillaneous writing page, some of these fragments are in the form of notes. This writing has had the period marks taken out by a formatting operation.

--

Love is the only uplifting field\
to put on a realm of inner obsession\
when life in you singles out one person\
the trick is to focus more on others\
to generate amorous clouds\
that engulf your personality and presence\
back in the thick of life\
and put back in good existence\
in the colors and patterns of virtuous soul\
to finally admit your work is to make no more negative accounts\
to treat them like a black magic spell\
for who is to know their cause\
when you no longer can understand how these thoughts are generated\
that take you away from where your soul grows lush

The white long glassy lake\
sky high to forehead over you\
trees, mounds and hills\
expansive is the earth\
as the water and sky\
yet clearly this lake is contained\
and finite\
and the sky reaches up from cloud white and grays\
to blue and deep black\
The soft, cool air, filling and warming in my lungs\
and the cheerful feeling of my emotions\
this internal energy that is my soul\
a projection out in time\
not for what is temporal and shallow\
but seeing the inner moment grow\
in this direction of nature\
of the thousand shades\
and green, brown and blues of light\
of like yellows in my mind\
and consciousness like a Christmas tree\
the wind beats colder\
like a holiday dream breeze\
and the pastels of life\
can fill us to overflowing

but the degree that the person is your enemy is under your control\

Does the communication attempts with God do some people harm? When people have had abuse, there isn't always an easy way to get passed any voice of their own inner speech to get to a voice of God This is why the monks talked so much about stillness And there is the need to see how people corrupt their lives by always interpreting negative life events in a religious perspective In these cases, God becomes the culprit instead of the high absolute that can help

--

It is a myth that people attracted to esoteric ideas are more spiritual or have more integration of personality than those who simply follow the religion of their land without what seems like complexity The esotericist often denies the help from God, instead of relying arcane studies to "figure it all out" The successful and sincere follower of the religion of their land works in the realm of experience and may have less of that destructive "pride of life" than the esotericist That is, the esotericist may do his spirituality from an angle of vanity, of intellect and position

--

There are two forces that we come under; the sensual world and the world of self or soul or essence The sensual world does not mean that which only comes from the senses, it means the world of events, relationships and mental associations Why is the truth about life so hard for us to see? The necessity of creating nonattachment because we can't taste that it is the ultimate joy and bliss Like the other valuable things in life, it may seem to be the most expensive The best food, the nicest neighborhoods are all costly, so why is not our own inner state and condition likewise following this rule of the universe We can pay that price We can keep paying that price by the sacrifices we make of the sensual world It's a job, a place of employment, to keep the small self out of our lives, so that the spiritual real can shine through How about a forty-hour work week of that? Can we say, "I sacrifice my life for holy bliss"\
        This also means doing the things we should be doing in a type of soft surrender empowered by bliss in realms of responsibly earning a living Can we say that we can sacrifice any aspect of our lives for the sake of a holy bliss that will not be destructive?

Feldenkrais is more about learning to use our bodies and in fact our lives or brains in new ways and unlearning dysfunctional movement patterns for the sake of optimizing our energy Yoga covers a different and as necessary ground When we slow it done, yoga becomes more simple and more effective What is the great cost of bliss? It is facing oneself while the sensual world seeks to have us face only events, people, entertainment, tasks as more important than the relationship between self and God. By facing, I mean giving attention for what we are aware of\
        In the internal world found by self remembering there is found the greater world. The soul that keeps itself in the sensual world, only sees things of the earth level, which as a planet may be considered often very low Self remembering brings us to our highest state, detached from the sensual world so that we can enter the higher world of spiritual influences This is why self remembering must be done; it is the pulling of ourselves away from the lower world into the higher What holds us back from bliss is what we perceive our needs to be Bliss is found, in one way, by self remembering and the active emotional realm of the good Sewing the seeds of good karma so that good will come about brings bliss

I am a potent man\
in my hurricane breeze\
that is everywhere as the air\
like where I have been\
like where I am now\
that is sour and sacred\
that cannot be abused\
as neither can the invisible\
that radiates between women's eyes and sunshine\
that is analyzed to its core\
until all insight frees it\
this tempest gust\
is my masculine trump\
for both sexes I have seen\
want me as very yang\
the strong verses the shadow king\
the activist verses the victim\
the judgment-less acceptance of humankind\
even when deviance must be hindered\
to further free the land for the near innocent\
I am released and invisible\
transparent but never still or stagnant\
in the passage from where I have been\
to where I am now

stilling the mind\
by staring at a store's open sign

like angels from beyond\
coaching the way clear\

covering the sigil from my view\
sending my sight to other objects\
which bring other associations\
so I divide my attention between arms\
and the other objects I see\
and my mind stays stiller\
and I see inside of me,\
the mind behind the mind
