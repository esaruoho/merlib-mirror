# jessamyn.com : me : faq

*Source page: `~jessamyn/me/faq.html`*

<div class="main">

![\[questions that are oftentimes asked of me\]](mepix/faq.gif)

This website has gone over five years without having a FAQ, you may ask "Why start now?" Well, I get a large amount of email, and I generally think of myself as a good correspondent. However, there are some questions I get over and over from people who don't necessarily want to be my pen pal. They are doing homework, or need a picture, or are peeved at something I wrote someplace and need to vent, this is to help them. Sometimes also, men write wanting to "get to know me better" and sometimes I'd rather reply with a URL than any information at all. Most of these are actual questions I've gotten from folks.

I like email, and postal mail, so don't let the presence of this document dissuade you from writing; think of it as giving you more leeway in what you write about. Thanks for reading this far, I'm sorry it's wordy, I tend to go on. 01sep02

<span id="basic"></span> Basic Stats

</div>

My full name is Jessamyn Charity West. I was born in 1968, my birthday is September 5th. I like birthday cards. I grew up and [went to school](http://www.hampshire.edu/) in Massachusetts, and moved to Seattle in 1990. I lived in Romania for a year 94-95. I have a Masters in Librarianship \[yes librarianship, weird huh?\] from the University of Washington. Now I split my time between my house in VT and Seattle. My [mom](http://www.eduwrite.com) and [dad & stepmom](http://www.tomandcindy.com) have websites and my sister does not. For other biographical information, much of it dated, you can see the [Real Me](http://www.jessamyn.com/me/realme5.html) pages. I maintain two websites regularly: [jessamyn.com](http://jessamyn.com) and [librarian.net](http://www.librarian.net). I also maintain a few more websites irregularly: [bendypig.com](http://www.bendypig.com), [sameroomrecords.com](http://www.sameroomrecords.com), <a href="http://www.alkifoundry.com" target="politics">alkifoundry.com</a> & [takebackvermont.com](http://www.takebackvermont.com). You can get in touch with me through [this mail form](http://www.jessamyn.com/mailme.html), postal mail, or sometimes I'm on AIM with the handle <a href="aim:goim?screenname=iamthebestartist&amp;message=hubba+hubba" target="politics">iamthebestartist</a>

<span id="online"></span> My Online World

</div>

<div class="q">

Q. Did you really meet your boyfriend online?\

</div>

<div class="a">

A. Sort of. I had recently tuned up my truck, in Vermont. Greg read the webpage where I discussed this and linked to it on his own site. I caught that in my referrer logs and checked out his web page. I said to myself "wow, that guy can write" When I was scheduled to go to Dublin Ohio for a wedding, I emailed him asking if he wanted to be my date, since I knew he lived in "the midwest." He replied that he lived in Milwaukee, so couldn't go, but asked if I lived near East Corinth because he and his folks would be vacationing there a few weeks off. I told him to drop by when he was in town. He did and the rest is history. We swapped maybe 3-4 emails before we met, so didn't really meet online. Would I have ever met him without the Internet? While I like to think I might have, the answer is probably no.

<span id="politics"></span>Politics

</div>

<div class="q">

Q. Why do you call yourself an anarchist?\

</div>

<div class="a">

A. I do it less and less lately mainly because I am tired of associations with people who are really violent or really knee-jerk reactionary. I think of myself more as anti-capitalist, though I agree with many of the anarchist tenets. Loosely, I believe people would be better off with less governmental interference, I'm against hierarchies, and I believe that humans have a responsibility to look after each other and take care of one another so that everyone's strengths and creativity are utilized and maximized. I also think everyone should be actively working towards this, not just paying lip service towards the idea of the good life. To this end, I think we should shop less and create more, complain less and do more, and argue less and talk more. I'm not good with slogans, feel free to drop me an email if you'd like a more in-depth explanation of all this.

</div>

<div class="q">

Q. Don't you think that if you don't vote you have no right to complain?\

</div>

<div class="a">

A. No. I don't think civic responsibility begins and ends at the ballot box. And I don't believe that choosing between two big-business candidates will materially change the way the world works. Feeding the hungry will. Teaching people to read will. Agitating for change will. I vote on local issues, I just don't vote for representatives. I am also politically active year round, not just at voting times. I discuss issues with people and encourage them to do the same.

</div>

<div class="q">

Q. So what's the answer to the world's problems?\

</div>

<div class="a">

A. I have no idea. For my personal life, I've started small. I try to tell the truth, to lead an ethical life and live low on the food chain, buying very little and trying to shop responsibly when I have to shop. I try to entertain myself easily, visit as many of my friends as possible, travel frequently and never ever say I'm bored. Talking about politics, particularly interpersonal politics and dynamics is very interesting to me. I think the more we are prepared to back up our beliefs, the more we reflect on them and inspect them for flaws.

</div>
