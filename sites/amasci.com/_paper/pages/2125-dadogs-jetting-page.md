We never entered the open class drag event, but the boys asked if they could practice with them. (It takes a race ticket) .

They wanted to go for the RELAY event which is the closest thing sand drgagging association has to a motocross event, and it tests acceleration, speed and timinng.

 

Each year the big shops build thier quickest baddest quads specially designed to do this race. Several had NOS, Almost everyone had real honest super drag Banshees for the final striaght drag, and all the others were speciallized cornering quads.

 

DTR racing had a speciall frame build for their 125 HP banshee that allowed it to use SUSPENSION and yet handle 125 horsepower, they were almost as fast as us.

 

WE CAMPED RIGHT NEXT TO THEM! and when we cam back from the event they had all their SECOND place trophies on the cabin railing, so we put the 1st place trophies on the hood of our truck!

 

The called us the "fast guys" after that until we had to leave. The boys were pleased as punch and walking on clouds after that all weekend.

 

The win really surprised us. Nothing at dunefast that was capable of turning a corner could outholeshot the 450, What a sweet machine.

 

Yamaha, you are a bunch of secret grumps, but you did make the greatest quad in the history of racing.

 

A little more about the relay, it involves four runs.

 

Lights go on, you drag to a CORNER BURRUM 300 feet away, round two pylons, and DRAG BACK, hand the ring to the next guy and he does the same.

 

Same with the third guy.

 

The last guy ALL OUT drags to the finish.

 

Usually the teams that raced had the BIG BAD BANSHEES for the final striahgt drag. and specially built corniering quads for the corner.

 

We just had TWO STOCK framed 450's with STOCK SWINGARMS and PADDLES, and we beat the other teams..

 

The whole race for the relay you need 7 holeshots and three corners.

 

The YFZ-450 rocks.

 

But the Ref who ran the drags saw it drag and asked us if we were going to race tomoorow also, and also said it was impossible for anyone to beat our time, go get your trophy, he's been doing this for 8 years, and for us to get out of line...so we wnet to pick up the trophies.

 

As we left the Ref asked his friend, "Now WHAT CLASS are THOSE quads in?" Like he thought they were cool.

 

Notice the secret tires we have on it? the main question is CAN THAT thing TURN THOSE TIRES, and the answer is, YES probably better than any other tire we ever ran...

 

will tell you at the end of summer what they are... :hey: Ok, they are buffed haulers, 25x11x9. Very nice for us. And YES there is enough power to turn them, With a 15 tooth of front for flat and 14 on front for hills.

 

But on the relays (combined handling and drags) these hondas and banshees lost. DTR had a 120 hp Banshee and a special built quad just for the relay, and we beat em with the 450's by a bit. the 450 in STOCK FORM is the BEST HANDLING ALL AROUND DUNE QUAD THERE IS.

Replacing your valve retainers (the top ones) with Ron Woods TITANIUM valve spring retainers, is a good way to get more RPM. They are 50% lighter, and seeing as the valves are high acceleration objects, even a few GRAMS significantly affects power output - I wouold get these as an addition to your high reving motor. We like them, They are nice quality.

SUPER DOG TIP! When fooling with your cams, doing the cam mod, checking valve spacers or WHATEVER! JUST before you put the last engine cover back on, ALWAYS ALWAYS check all your valve spacers one last time! You will one day save yourself many hours of aggravations. Those SPACERS EASILY DISLODGE under the buckets, and sit on adge and your quad WILL NOT START TO SAVE YOU! If your spacers are right on the money, then the valve spacers CANNOT BE WRONG or on EDGE and it SHALL START! but if you dont do that final check, one day you will be very sorry. AGAIN: the last thing you do before you put your engine cover on again is check the spacing one last time. ONE LAST TIME, yes I KNOW its late and 1 am in the morning, but that is exactly when you should check them one last time. YOUR WELCOME! We gfound a DOG SNEAKER SPACER up on edge last night and it would have been horrible strying to start it, it would not have started for any money. CHECK THEM!

We ordered more pistons cause our friends have bought ours, it seems the daddog piston made by JD pistons is awesome, and you can run pump gas on the stroker motor. It beats the 13:1 piston we run on race gas which is amazing. the chape of the piston top really makes a difference.

Oh By the way stay tuned, when we get the case alloy tested I'll let you all know what it is, that way when you crack your case you will be able to weld it, by ordering the right rod. We suspect aluminum 4157 is the correct welding material for the case. But when we actually weld with it, we will let you know if it works. The test on the case should confirm it.

WHEN REPLACING CAM JOURNALS, MAKE SURE THEY FIT RIGHT BEFORE THEY TORQUE OTHERWISE THEY WILL BIND UP. YOU HAVE TO MAKE SURE THE CAM ROTATES FREELY BOTH INTAKE AND EXHAUST. IF NOT, THEN YOU MUST LOOSEN THE BOLTS AND RETORQUE THEM A DIFFERENT WAY TILL THEY ARE LOOSE. iF YOU DO THIS THEY WILL NEVER BIND UP AND YOU WILL RUN WITH MORE POWER.

USE MOLY LUBE EVERY SINGLE TIME YOU REMOVE THE JOURNALS, AND PUT IT ON BOTH SIDES OF THE JOURNALS AND CAMS, THIS PREVENTS RUINING THE BABBIT COATING ON THE JOURNALS.
