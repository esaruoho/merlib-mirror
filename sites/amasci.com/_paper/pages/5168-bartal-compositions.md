<h1 id="other-compositions">Other Compositions</h1>
<ul>
<li><a href="tyroc.bitra_guard.html">Doyle and the Bitra Guard</a> - written by Bitra Hold Harper Tyroc (NPC, Jr. Journeyman) two turns before the Tenth Pass. It is a recounting of the legend of the Bitra Guard, a "legend" brought about somewhat because of the disruption of an assasination attempt by a Renegade and former Minecrafter named Doyle on Lord Holder Malachite's life by the <a href="../guard_guide.html">Bitra Guard</a>.</li>
<li><a href="machs.wonderful_weyr.html">What a Wonderful Weyr</a> - written by Small Hold Harper Machs (NPC, Sr. Journeyman), approximately five turns after the beginning of the Tenth Pass. It is song about life in a Weyr, Honshu Weyr in particular, where Machs spent some time when he was younger. Machs is Bartal's mentor.</li>
</ul>
