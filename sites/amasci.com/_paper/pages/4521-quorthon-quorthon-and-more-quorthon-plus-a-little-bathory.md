I have one of bathory's old albums and it's really cool, it makes you feel like you're in old viking norway. I like to listen to it when i'm running up a mountainside to the crisp clear cool fresh air at the top. I also put Quorthon in my NBAlive 97 playing for Milwakee. He makes the team one of the best in the game since he stands 7'7" 303, and has perfect stats, he usually is second in the league to Christian Laettner in rebounding, since I edited Laettner's stats too. I got the promo for the new Bathory album and it's hella cool, I recomend going out and getting it if you like cool non-MTV type music.
