# International Weight Pull Association<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:14.0pt"></span>

## **<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span><span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 8.5pt">                         Rules</span>**

**<span style="font-size: 9.0pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 8.5pt">Revised 5/2001</span>**

 

**<span style="font-size: 9.0pt; mso-bidi-font-size: 8.5pt">To download a Word format version that will print a bi-fold double sided pamphlet on legal size, [click here](Rules%2001.doc)</span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>SECTION 1 - Physical Area</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>A. SNOW</span>**

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>The weight pulling chute shall be in an area not less than ten (10) feet nor<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>more than twenty (20) feet wide, and not less than thirty-five (35) feet long. The chute will open on the end toward which the pull is made. The chute shall be as level as hard packed snow permits. If there is any perceptible slope to the chute, the direction of the pull shall be toward the higher end. There shall be at least one (1) physical barrier separating the crowd from the chute. Dogs shall achieve traction on the same surface as that on which the sled rides.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>B. WHEELED</span>**

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>The dimensions and the description of the weight pull chute shall be the same for wheeled pulls as for snow pulls. The chute shall be as level and firm as conditions permit. Acceptable surfaces shall be grass, hard packed dirt, gravel or carpet. Dogs shall achieve traction on the same surface as that on which the cart rides. </span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>**C. HOLDING AREA**</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>A safe and adequate holding area shall be provided for both snow or wheeled pulls. All competing dogs shall remain in a marked or designated holding area. The holding area shall adequately and safely hold the number of dogs in competition.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>SECTION 2 - Equipment </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>A. SNOW</span>**

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>The sled must be capable of safely carrying a load of **4000** lbs. The two runners must be two (2) to four (4) inches wide and at least seven (7) feet long on the contact surface and curved at the front end in a reasonable manner for a sled. The runners shall consist of a p-tex material or a steel runner. The sled must be a minimum of thirty (30) inches in width and no more than forty-eight (48) inches in width. The sled shall be provided with an adequate bridle and tug line of total length so that the point of attachment to the dog's harness will not exceed six (6) feet from the sled. The bridle shall be made of rope or nylon. Chains or cable will not be allowed. The sled bridle shall be tied together at a point 2' - 3' in front of the sled. The bridle will look like a Y. For the twenty (20) and forty (40) pound class, a smaller and lighter sled may be substituted as approved by the Chief Judge.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>B. WHEELED</span>**<span style="font-size:
9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>If the pull is held on a surface other than snow, a wheeled cart shall be used. It must be capable of safely carrying a load that can exceed **4000** lbs. The cart must have four (4) pneumatic tires which must all have the same air pressure prior to the event. The tires shall not be less than eight (8) inches in height and no more than eighteen (18) inches in height. A spare tire for the cart or adequate repair kit should be available at the pull site. The bridle shall be made of rope or nylon and be a minimum of 5' and maximum of 6' from the start line to point of attachment to the dogs harness. Chains or cable will not be allowed.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>The cart bridle shall not be tied.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>The snap should be able to slide freely from side to side.<span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>This bridle will look like a long V</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> For</span><span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> the twenty (20) and forty (40) pound class, a smaller and lighter sled may be substituted as approved by the Chief Judge provided it has pneumatic tires. If using a lightweight cart/sled, when the weight is equal to that of the larger cart/sled,<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>the competition may continue with the larger cart/sled.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>C. WEIGHT</span>**

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>An adequate supply of pre-weighed, inert material suitable for safe, stable stacking shall be provided. This is applicable for both snow and wheeled pulls.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>D. BARRIERS</span>**

<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>The chute barrier shall be a minimum of three (3) feet high and long enough so that dogs being driven from the rear will complete their pull within the barrier. The chute barrier shall be located at the very edge of the pulling pad, within the chute area. The best possible barriers shall be encouraged, but any one material shall not be mandatory. The following would be considered appropriate chute barriers: plywood, snow fence, wood planks, hog panels and other materials that possess a bouncing-off or glancing ability<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>when struck by the sled. (If the sled or cart becomes stuck or lodged on a barrier, it would then be considered a tangle (see Tangle Rule Sec. 5<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>C13). A crowd barrier is required to separate the spectators a minimum distance of three (3) feet from the pulling chute. A rope barrier is considered adequate for this purpose.

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>SECTION 3 - Officials</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>**A. SNOW & WHEELED**</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>The event will be governed by the Chief Judge. To insure that the *I.W.P.A*. has qualified officials at its events, all Chief Judges shall be selected from a list approved by the *I.W.P.A.* Board of Directors. (A list of approved judges comes in the sanctioning kits, or is also available from the sanctioning director.)</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">1. The Chief Judge shall supervise the overall event.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>He/She shall make all final decisions in any protest or dispute, before, during and after the event.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>The Chief Judge has the right to disqualify any dog or handler for any violation of the rules or unsportsmanlike conduct before, during and after the event.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">2.<span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>The organizer of the event shall be responsible for the completion of all forms, handler reports and information required by the *IWPA* and a statement verifying that all *IWPA* rules were adhered to.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Failure to comply with *IWPA* rules, especially rules that deal with the safety and the welfare of the dog, or put the *IWPA* in a liable situation , shall be grounds for loss of sanctioning, including insurance coverage.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:
8.5pt">3.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>The Sled Course Marshal shall be responsible for the safe and proper stacking of weight on the sled/ cart, proper positioning of the sled/cart, hooking up of the dogs to the sled/cart, breaking the sled when pulling on snow, and maintenance of the pulling course. The sled/cart officials may not block the dog. The track is only to be groomed between classes.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">4. A qualified timer equipped with a stop watch capable of timing to a 100th of a second shall time each pull and report that time directly to the official recorder. The recorder shall post the weight pulled and the time required to the official score sheet. (The timer and recorder shall be supervised by the Chief Judge.)</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span></span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">SECTION 4 - Contestants</span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>A. ELIGIBILITY</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:
8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>1. All contestants must be either a regular member of the *I.W.P.A.* or a provisional member to enter a dog in competition in any *I.W.P.A.* sanctioned weight pull. Dogs pulled by provisional members count for points but receive no points. Dogs participating in *I.W.P.A.* sanctioned events for points need to be owned and pulled by members (not necessarily the same member). The dog assumes the address of it's current owner.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">2. All dogs must be at least one (1) year of age and not more than 12 years of age to compete in any *I.WP.A.* event. The physical condition of the dog should always be taken into consideration and the Chief Judge may disqualify any dog they feel will be in danger of injury.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">3. *For the safety and welfare the animal,* all dogs must be currently immunized against any contagious canine diseases (hepatitis, distemper, leptospirosis, parvovirus, kennel cough, rabies etc.). These vaccinations must be recognized by the state/ province in which the dog is pulling. </span>*<span style="font-size:9.0pt;
mso-bidi-font-size:12.0pt">The use of performance altering drugs including but not limited to controlled substances (i.e. illegal), stimulants, depressants, anabolic steroids or their precursors shall be strictly prohibited.</span>*<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:12.0pt"> </span><span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">If an organization intends to test for illegal drug medications, it shall be declared so on the entry form.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>It is the handlers responsibility to submit documentation to declare use of any medication.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">4. All contestants shall fill out an official *I.W.P.A.* entry form or its approved equivalent and sign it. Approval of a non-official entry form shall be up to the Chief Judge.</span>

5\. No bitch in season shall be allowed to compete or allowed to be on the premises.

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">6. No pregnant or nursing bitch<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>shall<span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>be allowed to compete in an *I.W.P.A*. event.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">7. For the safety and protection of the dog, all dogs shall be fitted properly with a freight or<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>weight pull type harness which extends no more than 2 feet from the base of the dogs tail.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">8. Any person<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>found to be participating in a dog fighting competition shall be suspended to the maximum amount, pending possible expulsion, due to abusive or inhumane treatment of dogs.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">B. HANDLER CONDUCT & CONTROL OF DOGS</span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span></span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>All entrants shall be responsible for the conduct of their dogs,<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>family and handlers before, during and after the event.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Vicious or unmanageable dogs shall be barred from competition.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>Disruptive, rude or unsportsmanlike conduct toward animals , officials, handlers or spectators will be cause for disciplinary action and/or disqualification.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>All dogs shall be under physical control,<span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>while in the pull area, except when hooked to the sled or cart. All handlers shall be responsible for cleaning up after their own dogs. Abusive or inhumane treatment of a dog shall be strictly prohibited and shall be cause for disqualification.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">D. WEIGH-INS</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">All dogs shall be weighed prior to each *I.W.P.A.* sanctioned weight pull (multiple or consecutive day pulls by the same organization require only one weigh-in). The weigh-in shall be no more than 24 hours prior the start of the<span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>competition. All recorded dog weights shall be rounded to the lowest whole pound. Dogs shall wear only a standard collar during weigh in.*<span style="color:black"> </span>*<span style="color:black">All weigh-ins will be overseen by an I.W.P.A. certified judge and all dogs will be weight in on the same scales provided for the pull.</span> In the event that the dogs weight exceeds the scale's capacity, but it is obvious the dog is in the Unlimited Class it will be eligible to compete for points, but not Working Dog Certificates or best percentage.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:

mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

# <span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">A. ORGANIZING CLUB OPTION</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-tab-count:
1"> </span><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>The organizing club has the option to run the weight pull event class by class or in conjunction with another class or to run the event concurrently (all classes together).</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">B. COMPETING IN HEAVIER WEIGHT CLASS</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>The dog that wins its weight class shall not be allowed to compete in the next weight class. However the winning dog shall be allowed to pull as many times as it wants to establish a top percentage pull. These percentage pulls shall be at the established load increment.</span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span></span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">C. PROCEDURE OF THE WEIGHT PULL EVENT</span>**

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>1. Each dog shall be required to pull a loaded sled/ cart a measured sixteen (16) feet within 60 seconds. If the sled/cart is in motion the dog shall be allowed to complete the pull and the actual time required to make the pull shall be recorded.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>2. During the pull the handler may not be in contact with the pulling surface any further<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>forward than the front of the sled/cart when driving the dog, or their feet shall be behind the designated line 16 feet from the starting line when calling the dog. The handler shall not touch the dog, equipment or pulling surface between the start and finish line until the sled/cart crosses the 16 foot line.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">3. Once the dog is in position in front of the sled/ cart<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>no physical handling of the dog will be allowed.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>The judge will direct the handler<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>to physically reposition the dog if there is a tangle, false start or equipment failure.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>The dog is considered to be in position when the handler moves more than arms length away from the dog.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">4. Only one handler<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>is allowed to communicate with the dog, either with hand signals and/or voice commands from the course area.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">5. The handler shall be allowed to position his/her dog</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>anywhere between the wheels/runners in front of the sled/cart at the end of the extended tug line. The dog shall be positioned with enough slack in the tug line to prevent the dog from inadvertently moving the sled or cart. There shall not be so much slack as to allow a sling shot start.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>6. The handler may pass one or two turns but must pull on the third turn. Once the dog is hooked up to the sled/ cart the handler may not pass in that round except as stated in Section 5 \#14. (Dogs are no longer required to pull the starting weight.)</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">7. Starting weights to be pulled shall not exceed:</span>

***<u><span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">Class</span></u>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-tab-count:
1"> </span><u>Max, Wt.</u><span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span><u>Class</u><span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span><u>Max Wt.</u></span>***<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt;color:black"><span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>20 lb.<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>100 lbs<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>100 lb<span style="mso-tab-count:
1"> </span>500 lbs.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt;color:black"><span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>40 lb.<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>200 lbs<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>125 lb.<span style="mso-tab-count:
1"> </span>550 lbs.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt;color:black"><span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>60 lb.<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>400 lbs.<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>150 lb<span style="mso-tab-count:
1"> </span>600 lbs.</span>

<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>80 lb.<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>450 lbs.<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>UNL<span style="mso-tab-count:1"> </span>650 lbs.

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">These weights are maximum starting weights. If poor pulling conditions exist, the Chief Judge may adjust the starting weights lower to match the conditions.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:
8.5pt">8. Load increments shall be determined by the Chief Judge and a simple majority of the handlers and shall be set prior to the beginning of the class. (A load increment usually considered appropriate for the 35 pound class is 40 to 50 lbs. For the larger classes it may be as much as 100 to 200 lbs. or more.) The Chief Judge shall recommend the load increments to the handlers, considering the pulling conditions and the welfare of the dogs. The selected increment for each class shall remain consistent throughout the class.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">9. The handler has the option of having the cart placed anywhere on the starting line as long as the wheels are perpendicular to the start line. The handler has the option to reposition the cart one time. No test rolling of the cart shall be allowed.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">10. The time will start once the handler is in the proper position and instructs the dog to pull<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>or<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>the dog starts to pull.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">11. When on snow the sled shall be broken in the following manner: There shall be a break line 2 feet <u>BEHIND</u> the designated start line. The front of the sled shall be positioned at the break line. The Chief Judge shall then direct the following sequence: (1) The sled to be positioned at the start line with the sled runners perpendicular to the start line. (2) The pulling pad to be cleared of all sled marshals (unless the handler requests assistance in hooking up the dog). (3) The handler to leave the on deck circle, hook up the dog and the pull to commence.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">12. Two false starts on any one round by the dog shall</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>constitute the dog's removal from the competition. A false start is defined as any time the dog makes a forward progress with the sled/cart before the handlers feet are either across the 16 foot finish line if calling or behind the start line if driving the dog. </span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">13.Tangles are called at the Chief Judge's discretion. A tangle shall be called by the judge only if the dog or any of the equipment in use becomes entangled. </span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>A.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>In the event of a tangle, at the judges discretion the timer will stop the clock and the handler must realign the dog and<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>harness before the pull is resumed. Two tangles on the same round will constitute the dogs removal from the competition.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"> </span>B.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>If the sled/cart becomes stuck on a barrier of the pulling pad it shall be considered a tangle. The clock would be stopped and the sled/cart would then be freed of the barrier at the point of which it was stuck. The dog would be allowed to continue the pull with the remaining time left on the clock. Two tangles on the barrier on the same round shall constitute the dog's removal from the competition.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">14. If any part of the harness or other equipment breaks, the handler shall have 60 seconds to repeat the pull after repairs. He/She may choose to accept this interrupted pull as a pass.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">15. Whips, noisemakers, muzzles, pinch collars, or related</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">aids shall not be permitted at any time during an *IWPA* event. No food, treats or bait shall be used from the time the dogs class begins until the dog is eliminated from competition and removed from the pull area. Handlers shall not have food or bait on their person during the competition </span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">16. No dogs, including those not entered in competition shall be allowed in the area ahead of the starting line, except the dog that<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>is pulling .<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">17. There<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>shall be a marked "on deck" area behind, but near the starting end of the pull chute. This marked area shall house only the next dog to pull.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">18. The dog pulling the most weight in its class is declared the winner. Each successive dog pulling a lower weight is placed second, third, etc. If two or more dogs pull the same weight the required distance and can pull no more weight the full distance, the winner is determined by the fastest time in the previous pull.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">19. The maximum number of dogs handled in a given weight class by one handler shall be limited to no more than two (2) dogs per weight class.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:
yes"></span>SECTION 6 - Points </span>**

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">THE BASIS ON WHICH POINTS WILL BE AWARDED</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">In order for a weight pull to be recognized and points recorded, it shall be the responsibility of the weight pull organization to properly report the weight pull results to the *I.W.P.A*. as outlined in the sanctioning package, by the sanctioning director. </span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">In order for the dog to be eligible and counted for points he must be entered and eligible as described in<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>SECTION 4 - Contestants, A. ELIGIBILITY. Each dog will receive one (1) point for each dog defeated in his class. The last place dog in each class will earn 1/2 point, providing they have made at least one successful pull and place 4th or below. Dogs failing to make a successful pull<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>shall be counted for points but will receive no points.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">In addition:</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">\* 5 points will be awarded for<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>first place</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">\* 3 points will be awarded for<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>second place</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">\* 1 point will be awarded for<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>third place</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">The dog's five best pulls will be counted in the final standing. Two pulls from out of region (two for snow and two for wheels) can be counted. Wheeled events will be kept separate from the snow events.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">With an approved *<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"></span>I.W.P.A.* Chief Judge, dogs competing in non-sanctioned events or fun pulls shall be eligible to earn legs toward working dog titles.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">SECTION 7 - Reporting Results</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">All forms must be completed with appropriate signatures and statistics and postmarked no later than 5 days following the event.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">SECTION 8 - Protest</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">Any entrant protesting a violation of the rules shall do so </span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">immediately following the round. The protest may be verbal but shall be followed by a written statement directed to the Chief Judge within one (1) hour after the end of the contest. A hearing shall be scheduled and a decision rendered. The decisions of the Chief Judge are final.</span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">SECTION 9 - Weight Pull Season</span>**<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt"></span>

<span style="font-size:9.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:8.5pt">The weight pull season shall run from September 1 through March 31.</span>
