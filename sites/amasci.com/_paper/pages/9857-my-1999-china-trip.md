<h2 id="pic-some-preparations"><img src="images/pandadot.jpg" data-align="top" data-hspace="5" data-border="0" width="46" height="32" alt="[Pic]" /> Some Preparations</h2>
<blockquote>
<strong>10/20/99 Update</strong> - I finally received the big box I mailed to myself from Hong Kong (HK) which contained most of my pictures of China. The HK Post Office told me it would take <strong>1 month</strong> for the box to arrive from HK to the US via slow boat. They lied. It actually took about <strong>2.5 months</strong> to arrive! The moral of this story is to keep your pictures with you no matter how much they weigh (I took hundreds of pictures). Or mail the pictures back via airmail! At any rate, I will be scanning selected pictures onto this trip report. The daily entries which have pictures and/or graphics will be denoted with a panda bear icon, <img src="images/panda.jpg" data-align="top" width="23" height="16" alt="[Pic]" />
</blockquote>
