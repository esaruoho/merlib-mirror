<tr>
<td data-bgcolor="#FAFAFA"><br />
<strong>Considering Parenthood: A handbook for lesbians</strong> by Cheri Pies (1985), $9.95 +$1.50 p&amp;h, Spinsters Book Co., Box 410687, San Francisco, CA 94141. Deciding whether, how to become a parent; much applies to gay men.
<p><strong>Co-parents Rights Packet</strong> (1992), $15, Lambda Legal Defense and Education Fund, 120 Wall St., #1500, New York, NY 10005; 212-995-8585. Amicus briefs, bibliographies of law review articles and existing cases.</p>
<p><strong>Custody/Visitation Packet</strong> (1992), $15, Lambda Legal Defense and Education Fund, 120 Wall St., #1500, New York, NY 10005; 212-995-8585. Amicus briefs, psychological articles, bibliography of law review articles, list of cases and laws on custody and visitation and sexual orientation.</p>
<p><strong>Different Daughters: A Book by Mothers of Lesbians</strong> edited by Louise Rafkin (1987), $9.95, Cleis Press, PO Box 14684, SF, CA 94114; 800-780-2279; cleis@cleispress.com. Accounts of 25 mothers; text on family, community, religion and neighborhoods.</p>
<p><strong>Different Mothers: Sons and daughters of lesbians talk about their lives</strong> edited by Louise Rafkin (1990), $9.95, OUT OF PRINT, Cleis Press, PO Box 14684, SF, CA 94114; 800-780-2279; cleis@cleispress.com. Accounts of 38 children; some talk about mother’s partner.</p>
<p><strong>The Family Next Door</strong> edited by Lisa Orta &amp; Karen Rust (bimonthly newsletter), $50/yr. Next Door Publishing, Box 21580, Oakland, CA 94620; 510-482-5778. Focus on lesbian and gay parents.</p>
<p><strong>Family Values: Two moms and their son</strong> by Phyllis Burke (1993), $21, Random House, 201 E. 15th St., New York, NY 10022. Warm and political autobiographical novel.</p>
<p><strong>For Lesbian Parents: Your guide to helping your family grow up happy, healthy, and proud</strong> by Suzanne M. Johnson, PhD &amp; Elizabeth O’Conner, PhD (2001), $16.95, Guilford Press, 72 Spring St., New York, NY 10012; www.guilford.com. Packed with very useful information, suggestions, and resources. Any sensible child would want these authors for parents.</p>
<p><strong>Gay &amp; Lesbian Parents</strong> edited by Frederick W. Bozett, RN DNS (1987), $16.95/38.95 soft/hard, Praeger Publishers, Box 5007, Westport CT 06881. Includes information on gay and lesbian step-parent families.</p>
<p><strong>Gay Parenting: A complete guide for gay men and lesbians with children</strong> by Joy Schulenburg (1985), OUT OF PRINT, Anchor Press/Doubleday, Garden City, NY. Includes couples with children. Based on survey, interviews, personal experience.</p>
<p><strong>Getting Simon: Two gay doctors’ journey to fatherhood</strong> by Kenneth B. Morgan (1995), $14.95, Bramble Books, 8 Route 212, Bearsville, NY 12409; 914-679-4158. A journal on the extraordinary efforts a same-sex couple must make, and emotional costs they must pay, to adopt. Contains an application and contract for a surrogate mother and a birth plan.</p>
<p><strong>Heather Has Two Mommies</strong> by Lesléa Newman, illustrations by Diana Souza (1989), $7.95 +$1 p&amp;h, Alyson Wonderland, 40 Plympton St., Boston, MA 02118; 800-825-9766. Explains alternative fertilization and alternative families to young children.</p>
<p><strong>Jenny Lives With Eric and Martin</strong> by Susanne Bösche, photos by Andreas Hansen (1983), OUT OF PRINT, Gay Men’s Press, London. For 8-12 year olds; a day in the life of a young girl, her gay father and his lover.</p>
<p><strong>Lesbian &amp; Gay Parenting Handbook: Creating and raising our families</strong> by April Martin, Ph.D. (1993), $15, Harper Collins 10 East 53rd St., New York, NY 10022. A complete guide; includes many examples of couples.</p>
<p><strong>Politics of the Heart: A lesbian parenting anthology</strong> ed. by Sandra Pollack &amp; Jeanne Vaughn, (1987), $11.95 + $1.75 p&amp;h, Firebrand, 141 The Commons, Ithaca, NY 14850; 607-272-0000. Covers co-parenting, the “other” mother, legal challenges.</p>
<p><strong>Whose Child Cries: Children of gay parents talk about their lives</strong> by Joe Gantz (1983), OUT OF PRINT, Jalmar Press, CA. Accounts of children, their gay parents, parent’s lovers. Emphasizes challenges.</p></td>
