<td colspan="2" data-valign="TOP" width="80%" data-bgcolor="#ffffff"><p><strong>We're not hometown-bound after all.</strong></p>
<p>This afternoon I called Mom and told her that our sleep schedules were all screwed up with the holidays and that I was really tired and that I didn't really want to make the long drive down. I'm going to be down that way for job training early week after next anyhow, so we agreed for me to drive down next weekend instead, where I'll hang out at her place and fix her computer.</p>
<p>The quest this afternoon, then, was for cat food. And Mike tacked on filling the gas tank and going to check the mailbox, so it became a lengnthy excursion.</p>
<p>Little by little as I drove along through heavy traffic I felt myself wilting. Headache. Itchy eyes. Stomach ick. At the grocery store I slogged through as few aisles as I could, got the cat food and a bunch of soup, and came home as quickly as possible.</p>
<p>Whereupon I collapsed into my comfy bed, doggie protectively at my side.</p>
<p>I just woke up and it's about three hours later. None of that choice Wolfgang Puck soup we bought earlier, for me, nosiree. Instead it's the good old standby of cheap Campbells chicken noodle soup, and I'm hoping it'll help do the trick.</p>
<p>It's a good thing we didn't drive down to Mom's.</p>
