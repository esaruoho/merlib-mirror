The story of Oz didn't end with the Famous Forty books by the Royal Historians. Many others have continued, or put their own spin on, the story of Oz. These are the ones that are currently available.

***Abducted to Oz* by Bob Evans and Chris Dulabone**. A young American boy named Graham finds a wicked witch breaking through his mirror -- who then kidnaps him and takes him to Oz! And to make matters worse, Ozma doesn't even know about this witch. So who's going to come to Graham's rescue?

- [<img src="pics/toclaf.gif" data-align="middle" data-border="1" width="23" height="30" />](http://members.aol.com/LionCoward/home.html) A paperback edition, illustrated by Dennis Anfuso, is available from [Tails of the Cowardly Lion and Friends](http://members.aol.com/LionCoward/home.html).

***The Amber Flute of Oz* by Donald Abbott.** A new novel from Books of Wonder. Can the three heroes help Glinda find the legendary amber flute to save Oz?
