## Kirkland-Redmond Branch Meeting Locations

### Inglewood Country Club

6505 Inglewood Rd NE\
Kenmore, Washington\
(425)827-0522\
\
<img src="Inglewoodsign.jpg" data-border="1" data-align="bottom" width="99" height="125" alt="Sign at entrance to Inglewood." /> <img src="Inglewood.jpg" data-border="1" data-align="center" width="300" height="125" alt="Photo of Country Club." />\
\
<a href="http://www.mapquest.com" target="_top"><img src="mapquestlogo.gif" data-align="center" alt="Mapquest logo and link." /></a>

###### A special thanks to mapquest.com for the maps and directions.

\
<img src="Inglewoodmap.gif" data-align="center" alt="Map to Inglewood Country Club." />

     1.  Start out going West on NE 85TH ST/SR-908 towards 120th AVE NE.
     2.  Take the I-405 NORTH ramp.
     3.  Merge onto I-405 N.
     4.  Take the N.E. 124th ST. exit, exit number 20B
     5.  Keep LEFT at the fork in the ramp.
     6.  Turn LEFT onto NE 124TH ST
     7.  Turn RIGHT onto 100TH AVE NE.
     8.  Turn LEFT onto SIMONDS RD NE.
     9.  SIMONDS RD NE becomes NE 170TH ST.
    10.  NE 170TH ST becomes W. INGLEWOOD RD.
         (You will see the sign pictured above on the left.)
    11.  W INGLEWOOD RD becomes INGLEWOOD RD NE.
    12.  Follow the winding road till you see the country club.
