<td><p>As Guitar Noise grows we become more and more international. The double effect of this is the localization our site for different regions, while at the same time giving those regions greater exposure to the rest of the world. Currently we have specialized content sites for Canada and China. We would like to expand to include special European content. If you would like to partner with Guitar Noise by translating content for your country please <a href="contact.html">contact the webmaster</a>.</p>
