<li>Film screenings</li>
<li>Poetry and book readings</li>

<li>Information displays, bulletin boards, literature tables</li>
<li>Educational presentations to schools</li>
<li>Classroom projects examining advertising for size-negative messages, false claims and stereotyping</li>

<li>Art shows/exhibitions</li>

<li>Candlelight vigils honoring the victims of weight-loss surgery and eating disorders</li>
<li>Collecting signatures on petitions to add "size and weight" to civil rights legislation</li>
<li>Support for "Truth in Dieting" legislation</li>
<li>Comedy skits and routines</li>
<li>Group sing-alongs</li>
<li>Diet-free dances</li>
