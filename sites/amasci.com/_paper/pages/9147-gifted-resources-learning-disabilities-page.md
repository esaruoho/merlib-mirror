# Learning Disabilities

## Online Resources

- [Linda Silverman's Gifted Development Center has information about many gifted issues, including Learning Disabilities.](http://www.gifteddevelopment.com)

- [The Council for Exceptional Children](http://www.cec.sped.org)

- [ERIC's gopher service](gopher://ericir.syr.edu)

- [Special Needs Education Network](http://schoolnet2.carleton.ca/~kwellar/snehome.html) from SchoolNet in Ottawa, Canada. Has some specifically gifted resources and a great deal of information for other special needs.

- [Ontario Institute's Learning Disabilities gopher](gopher://gopher.oise.on.ca/11/resources/IRes4Ed/issues/learndis/) is quite extensive and links to many other useful resources.

- [CHADD -](http://www.chadd.org/) Children and Adults with ADD online.

- [British Columbia Teachers Federation sponsors Inclusion Resource Network](http://www.bctf.bc.ca/irn/IRNDatabase.html) an extensive collection of information and resources about inclusion, primarily targeted at teachers.

- [Davis Dyslexia Association](http://www.dyslexia.com/) has a good deal of information for parents and their children, as well as links to many other related sources.

- [IEPs and other ideas for meeting the needs of special education students](http://www.oz.net/~lingreen/)

- [ADD InfoNet](http://services.bunyip.com:2331/) a fairly comprehensive guide to ADD resources online.

- [Sarah's big collection of education resources,](http://www.webcom.com/pleasant/sarah/teach/TEACHING.HTML) with special concentration on special education.

- <a href="http://www.edc.org/FSC/NCIP/%3ENational%20Center%20to%20Improve%20Practice%20(N%0ACIP)%3C/a%3E,%0Afunded%20by%20the%20U.S.%20Department%20of%20Education,%20Office%20of%20Special%20Education%20%0APrograms,%20seeks%20to%20promote%20the%20effective%20use%20of%20technology%20to%20enhance%20%0Aeducational%20outcomes%20for%20students%20with%20sensory,%20cognitive,%20physical,%20and%0Asocial/emotional%20disabilities.%0ACan%20also%20be%20contacted%20at:%20%0A%3Cpre%3E%0A%20%20%20%20NCIP%0A%20%20%20%2055%20Chapel%20Street%0A%20%20%20%20Newton,%20MA%2002158-1060%0A%20%20%20%20Phone:%20617-969-7100%20ext.2387%0A%20%20%20%20TTY:%20617-969-4529%0A%20%20%20%20FAX:%20617-969-3440%0A%20%20%20%20email:%20ncip@edc.org%0A%3C/pre%3E%0A%0A%3Cli%3E%3Ca%20href=" http:="" data-call-centre.cogsci.ed.ac.uk="" data-callhome"="">Communication Aids for Language and Learning</a> from Edinburgh University, Scotland. Primarily physical disabilities.

- [MicroMUSE telnet serivce](telnet://micro.musenet.org) (Login as guest) has been suggested as a resource. I have not yet successfully located relevant info, but I am a beginner. There is some help available from their [WWW pages.](http://web.musenet.org/)

- ld-list, the mailing list for learning disabilities. To subscribe send a mail message with "subscribe ld-list" in the body (not the subject) of the message to majordomo@curry.edu

- gt-special, the mailing list for twice-special children and families. All gifted children are special because of their intellectual abilities. Some, however, have disabilities that make them twice-special. Generally, this list discusses any gifted child who has a learning disability (LD) and/or other neurological problem(s) that interfere with the child's ability to reach the full potential of their giftedness. These other neurological problems may but are not limited to ADD, hyperactivity, autism, Asperger's Syndrome, Tourette's Syndrome, PDD, ODD, OCD, anxiety disorders, sensory integration disorders (SI), and depression. Parents/guardians, educators and mentors, and medical and mental health providers who are facing the joys and challenges of supporting and nurturing these children are most welcome. To subscribe to the list, send e-mail with an empty subject line and the body of the message containing the text "subscribe GT-SPECIAL" to majordomo@gtworld.org

  If you still have questions or need any further assistance regarding GT-SPECIAL, contact [owner-gt-special@GTworld.org\>](mailto:owner-gt-special@GTworld.org)
