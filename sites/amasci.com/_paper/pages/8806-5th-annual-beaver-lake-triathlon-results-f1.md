# 5<sup>th</sup> ANNUAL BEAVER LAKE TRIATHLON

# August 22, 1998

The 5<sup>th</sup> annual Beaver Lake Triathlon was held on August 22<sup>nd</sup> at Beaver Lake Park, Issaquah, Washington. A record 435 triathletes participated in the event which included a quarter mile swim, a 13.9 mile bike race and a 4.3 mile run.

The men's race was won by Andrew Neff of Bellevue, Washington with a time of 1 hour 9:39 minutes, The women'a race was won by Julie Pittsinger of Wenatchee, Washington with a time of 1 hour 15:56 minutes.
