*<img src="bra.jpg" data-align="Right" data-border="0" width="200" height="296" alt="Our New Nursing Mother Nursing Bra" />* ***Nursing Mother *** Bra and Pads

The most recent innovation in nursing bras is "flexible fit" bras with cups made of a stretchy cotton/spandex fabric. This new style allows for the changes in breast size that breastfeeding mothers experience; the cups will fit whether she is engorged or depleted.

While mothers have enjoyed the improved comfort and fit, the heavier fabric and sport-back style made the bras uncomfortably hot to wear. Now that problem has been overcome. Through the use of cooler fabrics and improved styling, the new Nursing Mother Bra is cooler and even more comfortable, yet still offers ease of use and plenty of support.
