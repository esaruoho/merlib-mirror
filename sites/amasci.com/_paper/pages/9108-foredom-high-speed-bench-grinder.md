Abrasive Discs

Abrasive discs D-1 and D-2 are rubber bonded, suitable for glass, high speed steel and other hard materials. Disc D-3 is electrically bonded abrasive on very thin Swedish steel suitable for areas difficult to reach with other types of accessories. Disc D-6 is a fiberglass reinforced cut-off wheel used to cut-off, groove and trim metal, wood and ceramics.
