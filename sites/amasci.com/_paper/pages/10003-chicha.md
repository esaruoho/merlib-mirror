**Chicha**

Chicha is the traditional drink of Peru. It is a fermented corn mixture. This chicha was also made with strawberries. As you travel through the Sacred Valley region, you notice long poles with red bandanas sticking out of doorways. This is the signal that there is chicha for sale.\
