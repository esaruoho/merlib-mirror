# Sailing the Dodecanese Greek islands with SeaScape. Yacht charters for singles, couples or crewed charter for groups.

Sailing the Dodecanese Greek islands with SeaScape. Yacht charters for singles, couples or crewed charter for groups.

***Sept 7-20***

September.... and things start to become a little more mellow in the Greek isles. The main heat of Aug dies away, and there is a kind of fall "glow" to the atmosphere. The meltemi starts to fade a little, but there's still plenty of great wind for sailing.

We'll still be doing our favourite 2wk route, sailing from our home island of Leros, down to the traditional sponge-fishing island of Kalymnos, where another SeaScape tradition awaits us - our adopted "Momma"'s homemade dolmades! (stuffed grapeleaves). The route is a mixture of such little, old-fashioned villages and some of the more famous islands like Patmos and Samos... with some of our especially favourite tiny spots included, of course - like our little paradise island of Marathi, with two families, a lovely sandy beach and some of the best food around!

You can join this trip for one week or two; the midpoint for end of wk1/start of wk2 is Pythagoras's island, Samos.
