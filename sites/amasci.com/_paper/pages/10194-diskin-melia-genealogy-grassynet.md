**Patrick Melia**

Patrick immigrated from County Galway, Ireland in the early 1900's. He became a police officer in Chicago where he was killed, 27 April 1910, while on duty by a band of thugs. There is a memorial to slain officers at the located in the lobby of Chicago Police Headquarters, 3510 South Michigan Avenue, where his police badge is displayed on a wall. It is called the <a href="http://www.ci.chi.il.us/CommunityPolicing/AboutCPD/Starcase/Starcase.html" alt="Officer memorial">Superintendent's Honored Star Case</a>. You can see the dents from the shotgun pellets.

**Mary Elizabeth Diskin Grassy**

My grandmother's card commemorating her 90th birthday - 20 DEC 2001. It is a prom picture with a listing of all her current descendents. Mary Beth remembers taking the lilac dress out of storage to play with it as a kid. Many family members came to Onalaska, Wisconsin, to celebrate her 90th birthday with a party. Landon Dane Merlin Grassy, Child of Richard George Grassy III and Brooke Tempest, child of Theresa Rebecca Tempest are Mary's great-grandchildren not listed on the card as they were born after the party.
