# My life is not an episode of Jerry Springer

*Source page: `~allegro/private/Objects.htm`*

My life is not an episode of Jerry Springer. I dont exist solely to be a sideshow so some Rich White Liberals can congratulate themselves for allowing me my quirks, while secretly, theyre relieved that they arent a weirdo like me. I also dont live my life as one big political statement, and I certainly dont take pride in trying to one-up my fellow young freaks by trying to be freakier than thou. All the same, Im fairly confident that I have a rather unusual life. Im a feminist in a time when some consider the Spice Girls radical. I like sex, in all its weird and wonderful forms, as long as its with someone who cares about me outside of the bedroom, and acknowledges that I have a brain. I believed in respect and rights for fat chicks way before Camryn Mannheim won an Emmy. I was bi before bi was cool. I was polyamorous before there was such a word. I think Dan Savage lacks imagination. But really, thats just who I am- I live it, so I have to be ok with it. Or at least try to be. So, imagine my surprise when I recently had my head seriously fucked with.

Theres actually a few more bi women in the group, although most have female partners, and stay pretty quiet about it. Theres even a straight one too. Its a very cool group full of very cool people doing very cool music. So, I was pretty content with my life there, and quietly sitting back, polishing my oh-so-cool faade when Rafe<span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt"> joined the group last spring. </span>

<span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 12.0pt">To someone who has never heard of the concept, the idea of a marriage or relationship that involves more than two people may seem pretty strange.
