### K. Scott Hunziker, Ph.D.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Born and raised in [Yakima, Washington, USA](http://www.ci.yakima.wa.us/).
- Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering, [University of Washington](http://www.washington.edu/), 1981.
- Analyzed structural dynamics of the [IUS](http://www.boeing.com/defense-space/space/ius/index.html) for [Boeing](http://www.boeing.com/), 1981-83.
- Fellowship (and 5 wonderful years) at [Draper Laboratory](http://www.draper.com/), 1983-1988.
- Ph.D. in structural engineering, [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](http://web.mit.edu/), 1988.
- Back to Boeing, working [MESUR](http://mpfwww.jpl.nasa.gov/), [Heliwing](heliwing2.jpg), and [Darkstar](t3f-3.jpg).
- Flutter, aeroservoelasticity, and flight test on the [X-32](x-32b.jpg).
- Co-author of [Algae](http://www.eskimo.com/~ksh/algae/index.html), a high-level programming language for numerical analysis.
- Barely survived the destruction of our [house](house3e.jpg). (**Farmers Insurance** didn't exactly get us "back where we belong". In fact, we would have been better off if we had been uninsured.)
- Made up a cool [signal flags](flags/index.html) page for my kids.
- Fly out of [Crest Airpark](http://www.crestairpark.com/) and from Renton with the [Boeing Employees Flying Association](http://befa.home.mindspring.com/).
- Member (and web guy) of [King of Kings Lutheran Church](http://king-of-kings.org/).
