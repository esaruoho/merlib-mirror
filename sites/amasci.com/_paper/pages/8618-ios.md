# Ios (Ίος)

Ios is a famous "party island."  We didn't actually stop there but got to see it twice while we were on a ferry from Paros to Santorini.  About 20 minutes after our scheduled stop on Ios, the ship turned around and went back!  Apparently there were some doctors or something who had not gotten off for some reason.  I don't know the whole story.  Anyway, they were important enough people to cause the whole ferry to go back to Ios and drop them off.
