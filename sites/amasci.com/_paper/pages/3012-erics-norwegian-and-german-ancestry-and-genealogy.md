> <span class="lead">W</span>elcome fellow genealogists and possible cousins! Below you'll find the various names I'm researching, and eventually info about some families and individuals with those names. The various branches come from (mostly Norway so far):
>
> - **Norway**: Møre-og-Romsdal, and other *fylker* (1400s-1900s);
> - **Germany**: Bayern (1800s-1900s) and Mecklenburg-Schwerin (1500s); and
> - **The United States**: Washington, California, and other states (1900s).
