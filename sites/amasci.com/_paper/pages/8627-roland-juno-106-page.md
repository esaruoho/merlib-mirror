# Roland Juno-106 Page

*Source page: `~syzygy/juno106.html`*

<img src="JunoLogo.GIF" data-border="0" width="282" height="114" />

In the years since I first put this page online, a number of other sites have sprung up with similar information.  Here is the stuff that is of interest to most people:

[The Juno-106 FAQ](juno106faq.html). (If you have a dead voice read this.)

[MIDI implementation chart](juno106midi.html). (Includes all system exclusive messages.)

Original factory patches.  These are in WAV format.  You will need to connect the output of your sound card to the TAPE LOAD jack on your Juno to load these patches.  If you need more information on how to do this, see the owners manual.  (Link is below.)  I have not tested these; they are hosted on the "[Juno-106 Connection](http://www.hinzen.de/midi/juno-106/)" page, another good resource for Juno-106 information.\
Original factory patches, [Group A](http://www.hinzen.de/midi/juno-106/junot020.wav)\
Original factory patches, [Group B](http://www.hinzen.de/midi/juno-106/junot040.wav)

[Owner's Manual](http://www.hinzen.de/midi/juno-106/manual/juno-man.pdf) in Adobe Acrobat PDF format.  To view or print this file, you need [Acrobat Reader](http://www.adobe.com/products/acrobat/readstep.html).
