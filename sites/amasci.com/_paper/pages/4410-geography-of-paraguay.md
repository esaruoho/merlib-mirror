# Geography of Paraguay

*Source page: `~krautm/geo.html`*

[<img src="py_map.gif" data-border="0" width="320" height="335" />](py_map.gif)

 

Paraguay is bordered to the south by [Argentina](http://www.ar:70/), to the east and north by [Brasil](http://www.cr-sp.rnp.br/), and to the west by [Bolivia](http://larch-www.lcs.mit.edu:8001/~evs/bolivia/bolivia.html). Further to the south and east, on the Atlantic ocean, is [Uruguay](http://www.funcity.com/uruguay/)

 

A number of indigenous peoples populate Paraguay. For more information, check the [Ethnologue database](http://www-ala.doc.ic.ac.uk/~rap/Ethnologue/eth.cgi/Paraguay/).
