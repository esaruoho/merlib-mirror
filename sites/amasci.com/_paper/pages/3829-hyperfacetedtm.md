<h1 id="hyperfaceted">Hyperfaceted™</h1>
</div>
<p>Hyperfaceted is proud to present custom songs and single tracks from artists who have original and unique work.</p>
<p>We actively seek <em>local artists*</em> to record their original songs, concepts, and live productions.</p>
<p>It doesn't matter how big or small though admittedly we have a preference for Rock, Metal, Techno, Folk and especially work that features stringed instruments such as guitars, violins, base, banjos, etc.</p>
<h3 id="contact-jill-england">Contact: <a href="email:jill@hyperfaceted.com">Jill England</a></h3>
<p>* What do I mean local artists? I'm in Seattle, which is on the coast of the Pacific, which is on Earth. If you can read this you are close enough. It's a small universe. ;)</p>

<p>Music is Magic, every note, every nuance, every touch on the strings brings life to the universe. With every song you write and perform you cast a spell, which effects yourself and those who hear it. Can you rip this simple rift from your mind? I can't, I hear it over and over, with different chords, different fills, new bass tracks, differnt lyrics.</p>
<p>Every song is a spell. They are Magic of the oldest kind. The ancient symbol of a pentagram inside a circle is a representaion of basic music theory. Wield songs with care, as with all spells they will cut from both sides.</p>
