Very low cost, yet packed with features!</strong><br />

Built-in lock down bracket retracts when not in use. Auto calibration with optional weight. 9V battery included - AC adapter optional. Two year warranty.</p>

<p><strong>(4)  <em>VA-Series Benchtop Scales</em></strong><br />
High capacity scales for all industries. Great for weighing investment and for parts counting. All feature keypad calibration with optional calibration weight, stainless steel platforms, optional RS-232 interface, oversized backlit LCD display mounted on adjustable angle support arm (removable for remote operation, weighing modes (g, kg, ct, lb, oz ozt, tlh, tis, gr, dwt and parts counting, 2nd tare memory, built in application programs and programmable auto off. Two year warranty.</p>
