# Transit Links

*Source page: `~robertf/monorail/mrlinks.html`*

> Monorail and Other Transit Links.\
>
> <div>
>
> **Seattle Area Monorail**\
> \
> [**The Seattle Center Monorail**](http://www.seattlemonorail.com/) The monorail that has been operating in Seattle since 1962.\
>  \
> [**The Seattle Popular Monorail Authority (SPMA), operating as the Seattle Monorail Project (SMP)**](http://www.elevated.org/) Established by a November 5, 2002 ballot measure to build a monorail system in Seattle.\
>  \
> [**Cascadia Monorail Company, LLC**](http://www.cascadiamonorail.com/) The team of companies that has submitted a proposal to design, build, operate, and maintain the Green Line.\
>  \
> [**Friends of the Monorail**](http://www.monorail.org/) This private non-profit organization promotes an expanded monorail system in Seattle.\
>  \
> [**buildthemonorail.com**](http://www.buildthemonorail.com/), A group that wants to see the monorail built.\
>  \
> [**Citizens for King County Monorail**](http://www.kingcountymonorail.org/) A group promoting three monorail lines to connect suburbs of Seattle.\
>  \
> [**Freeway\|monorail**](http://www.freewaymonorail.org/) Freeway\|monorail is a grassroots organization advocating a common-sense alternative to Sound Transit's light rail plan.\
>  \
> [**Monorail Recall Committee**](http://www.exordia.net/monorailrecall//) A group that has, through petition I-83, approved for the November general election, seeks to stop construction of the monorail by prohibiting construction on city streets.\
>  \
> [**No Recall, Go Monorail**](http://www.norecallgomonorail.com/) A group fighting against the Monorail Recall group, and to win a vote in favor of building the monorail.\
>  \
> [**OnTrack**](http://www.monorailontrack.org/) A group seeking greater accountability for the Seattle Monorail Project and that wants the SMP to proceed as promised initially.\
> \
> [**Monorail Now**](http://www.monorailnow.com/) A group pushing for completion of the monorail on schedule, and is fighting opponents seeking delay or cancellation of the project.\
> \
> [**James Robert Deal**](http://www.jamesrobertdeal.com/comprehensivetransportation/%20%20%20%20%20%20%20%20%20monorail.htm)   A 1999 paper promoting monorail and maglev as superior to light rail.\
>  \
> [**“Robert” in the Slow Lane**](http://www.theslowlane.com/other/sound.html)   An individual’s opinion about Link light rail and monorail.\
>  \
> **Light Rail Opponents**\
> \
> [**CETA (Coalition for Effective Transportation Alternatives)**](http://effectivetransportation.org/) Fighting to stop the light rail project and put the money to better use for alternative forms of transportation.\
>  \
>
> **Other Seattle Area Transit**\
>  \
> [**Community Transit**](http://www.commtrans.org/) Serves Snohomish County, Washington (the next county north of Seattle) with extensive bus service.\
>  \
> [**Everett Transit**](http://www.everettwa.org/transit/) Serves the city of Everett, Washington, about 30 miles (about 50 km.) north of Seattle.\
>  \
> [**Metro Transit**](http://transit.metrokc.gov/) Serves King County, Washington (the county Seattle is in) with extensive bus service and the Waterfront Streetcar.\
>  \
> [**Pierce Transit**](http://www.ptbus.pierce.wa.us/) Serves Pierce County, Washington (the next county south of Seattle), including the City of Tacoma, with extensive bus service.\
>  \
> [**Sound Transit**](http://www.soundtransit.org/) Serves Snohomish, King and Pierce counties with high-speed rapid transit utilizing express busses, commuter trains, and now working on a light rail project called *Link.*\
>  \
>
> **Monorail in Other Areas**\
> \
> [**The Monorail Society**](http://www.monorails.org/) An international society of monorail boosters. This site tells about virtually all the world's monorails with links to most of them.\
>  \
> [**Las Vegas Monorail**](http://www.lvnvmonorail.com/) Las Vegas, Nevada, has a new operating monorail, and is building an extension of the existing line.\
>  \
> [**Monorail Malaysia**](http://www.monorail.com.my/) Currently building a monorail system in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.\
> \
> [**New Moscow, Russia, Monorail**](http://www.monorail.ru/) Note: This site is in Russian.\
