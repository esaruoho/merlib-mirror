This page has the results of a ongoing poll to find out what Paul Wylie fans consider are their favourite routines he has done over the years. The results are taken from the members of the Paul Wylie Mailing List (where I started this) and people who have voted from this site. I update the results often and try to show where the routine placed before I updated them again.\
\
To figure out the correct placing I gave each vote (1 thru 5) a certain amount of points:\
\
1 - 25\
2 - 20\
3 - 15\
4 - 10\
5 - 5\
