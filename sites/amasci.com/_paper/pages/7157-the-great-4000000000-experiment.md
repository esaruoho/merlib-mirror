***The Controversy***\
\

Sound Transit is intent on building a light rail line (Central Link) from Downtown Seattle to SeaTac Airport, passing through Seattle’s Rainier Valley district. I, and many other people, believe that light rail is too expensive and has a number of disadvantages. We want a more effective mode of mass transit. My personal preference is for monorail.

However, in October 2003, the United States Congress approved a \$500 million grant to help build Central Link, and in early November 2003 Sound Transit began construction on the new light rail line.

I am very disappointed that \$2,400,000,000 is now going to be spent on the light rail line, but through the gloom I see a new light — we now are about to begin a great \$4,000,000,000 experiment to see which is better, light rail or monorail. The two projects total more than \$4,000,000,000 dollars, so that is why I am calling it “The Great \$4,000,000,000 Experiment.”

As already mentioned, construction has now begun on the new light rail line, scheduled to be finished in 2009. Meanwhile, the Seattle Monorail Project expects to begin construction on the new Green Line monorail next year, with an initial segment scheduled to open in 2007 and the whole line to open in 2009.

So now we have both a monorail project and a light rail project getting underway, both expected to be finished in 2009, making a great opportunity to compare the two as they progress. I am setting up a table with two columns, one for monorail and one for light rail, and will report significant points as they develop. In a few years we should all know whether I am right or wrong about favoring monorail over light rail.
