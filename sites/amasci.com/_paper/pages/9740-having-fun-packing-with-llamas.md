<td><strong>Not all llamas are cut out for PR work, Charlie has a very calm and curious disposition. He likes new people, new places and new experiences. We have been working with him the last year, and he has only had one real outting as a PR llama. We hope by this summer he will be able to start visiting some Rest Homes and some community events.</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Our approach was having Charlie first become comfortable inside our own home. That meant being able to walk on cement, climb stairs, walk on linoleum, carpet or wooden floors. Next we started walking down narrow halls, around corners, through different doorways. Even visited the bathroom where he had to learn how to back out with out feeling any panic. In addition he has been exposed to many friends inside our house, which has helped him feel comfortable with different situations. Charlie is now so comfortable with being in any part of the house that if he finds a door accidently left open...he just walks right in and snoops around.</strong></td>
