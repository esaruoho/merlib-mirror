# THE AQUAMAN MOVIE!

\

[August 10th](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode18.html): I managed to get this image snuck off the set of the new Aquaman Movie, which is currently in pre-production. It's a little small, but this is apparently Vincent Chase and Mandy Moore doing a blue screen test.

<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage10_01.jpg" width="300" height="225" />

[July 16th](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode17.html): Vincent Chase and Mandy Moore officially announced the Aquaman Movie at the [San Diego Comic-Con](http://www.comic-con.org/index.php).

<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/entourage09_01.jpg" width="300" height="225" />

[July 12th](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode16.html): A tipster reminds me that Vincent Chase had a small role in [A Walk To Remember](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0281358/) with Mandy Moore.

[July 10th](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode16.html): Latest rumor mill has [Mandy Moore](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0601553/) playing Aquagirl in the movie. No, I'm not going to speculate on who Aquagirl really is. I didn't see enough of the script to know. I don't think it's Mera, but I think she could pull it off if it is.

[July 3rd](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode13.html): Variety confirms that Vincent Chase has been picked for the role of Aquaman.

[June 27th](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode12.html): Yes, it has been confirmed that [James Cameron](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000116/) will be directing the as-yet-untitled Aquaman movie. While there is some question how well Cameron can get the concept of Aquaman onto the screen, there's also no doubt that his involvement makes this a high profile movie. There are currently rumors that [Leonardo DiCaprio](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000138/) may want the part of Aquaman. While he physically fits the description of Aquaman better than Chase, we'll have to see which guy is picked.

[June 23rd](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode11.html): I'm told that [Vincent Chase](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/) may have the part of Aquaman. Chase has been in [Head On](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season01/episode01.html) and the upcoming [Queens Boulevard](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season01/episode05.html), as well as a few other parts.

[June 15th](http://www.hbo.com/entourage/episode/season02/episode10.html): I've just confirmed that [Andrew Kevin Walker](http://www.imdb.com/name/nm0001825/) wrote the script, which I've seen *part* of. No, I can't share it with you. I didn't see enough to share anyway. I can tell you that the script uses the Modern Age origin of Aquaman with some Silver Age elements in the story itself. What I saw was traditional with a twist.
