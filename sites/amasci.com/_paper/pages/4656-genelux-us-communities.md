<td width="600" data-valign="TOP"><h1 id="luxembourgers-in-the-united-states">Luxembourgers in the United States</h1>
<img src="images/luxline.gif" />
<span id="top"></span>
<a href="#il">Illinois</a> || <a href="#ia">Iowa</a> || <a href="#ks">Kansas</a> || <a href="#mn">Minnesota</a> || <a href="#wi">Wisconsin</a>
<img src="images/il.gif" /> Illinois
<a href="hist.htm#il">Archives, Historical Societies &amp; Special Collections</a>
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/roots-l/USA/il.html">United States Resources: Illinois</a><br />
RootsWeb state page, includes links for genealogy and historical societies, Family History Center Research Outline, Everton's county map and federal census information.
<a href="http://vitalrec.com/il.html">Illinois Vital Records Information</a><br />
State entry from Elizabeth Orsay's comprehensive vital records web site.
<a href="http://www.sos.state.il.us:80/depts/archives/data_lan.html">Illinois Public Domain Land Tract Sales</a><br />
Land sales from the public domain were recorded by field employees of the U.S. General Land Office, state and local officials, and clerks of the Illinois Central Railroad.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~ilcook/cook.htm">Cook County, Illinois Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> site for Cook County, maintainted by Megan Zurawicz and Sharon Dickson. Many Luxembourgers lived in Chicago, particularly the first and second generation Americans, as they began to move off the farms their parents settled.
<a href="http://home.digitalcity.com/chicago/">Digital City Chicago</a><br />
Chicago is famous for its culturally distinct neighborhoods. Although there are not any uniquely Luxembourg neighboods, many Luxembourgers integrated into Chicago's many German communities. The <a href="http://metromix.com/top/1,1419,M-Metromix-Visiting-Attractions!PlaceDetail-4922,00.html">Rogers Park/West Ridge Historical Society</a> features "doughty" Luxembourgers among its exhibits, many of whom operated successful greenhouses in the area.
<a href="http://listserv.nslsilus.org/skkhome/">SkokieNet</a><br />
Located near Chicago, Skokie (originally called Niles Center) was settled by Luxembourgers. A brief history of Skokie is also available from the <a href="http://nsn.nslsilus.org/skkhome/cats/history/index.html">SkokieNet Local History Center</a>.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~ilkane/">Kane County, Illinois Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> site for Kane County, maintained by Bob and Jane Fey. Aurora, an important ethnic Luxembourger community, is located in Kane County.
<a href="#top"><img src="images/arrow.gif" data-border="0" /></a>
<img src="images/ia.gif" /> Iowa
<a href="hist.htm#ia">Archives, Historical Societies &amp; Special Collections</a>
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/roots-l/USA/ia.html">United States Resources: Iowa</a><br />
RootsWeb state page, includes links for genealogy and historical societies, Family History Center Research Outline, Everton's county map and federal census information.
<a href="http://vitalrec.com/ia.html">Iowa Vital Records Information</a><br />
State entry from Elizabeth Orsay's comprehensive vital records web site.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~iajackso/">Jackson County, Iowa Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> page for Jackson County, maintainted by Charlotte Meyer. St. Donatus is one of the best preserved Luxembourg communities and on the National Historic Register.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~iadubuqu/">Dubuque County, Iowa Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> site for Dubuque County, maintained by Julia Krapfl. The city of Dubuque is an important Luxembourg community and was home to Nicholas Gonner, newspaper publisher and author.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~iashelby/">Shelby County, Iowa Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> site for Shelby County, maintained by Dennis Walsh, with a special page highlighting the <a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~iashelby/lux.htm">Luxembourg heritage</a> of the county.
<a href="#top"><img src="images/arrow.gif" data-border="0" /></a>
<img src="images/ks.gif" /> Kansas
<a href="http://skyways.lib.ks.us/genweb/ellis/">Ellis County, Kansas Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> site for Ellis County, maintained by Mike Ward. Early settlers in <a href="http://skyways.lib.ks.us/genweb/ellis/p_hill.html">Pleasant Hill Township</a> came from Luxembourg.
<a href="#top"><img src="images/arrow.gif" data-border="0" /></a>
<img src="images/mn.gif" /> Minnesota
<a href="hist.htm#mn">Archives, Historical Societies &amp; Special Collections</a>
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/roots-l/USA/mn.html">United States Resources: Minnesota</a><br />
RootsWeb state page, includes links for genealogy and historical societies, Family History Center Research Outline, Everton's county map and federal census information.
<a href="http://vitalrec.com/mn.html">Minnesota Vital Records Information</a><br />
State entry from Elizabeth Orsay's comprehensive vital records web site.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~mnstearn/">Stearns County, Minnesota Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> page for Stearns County, maintained by Darrell Schulte. Luxemburg and St. Cloud are both important Luxembourg communities.
<a href="http://www.stearns-museum.org/">Stearns History Museum</a><br />
The Research Center and Archives contains over 400,000 photographs, maps, newspapers, genealogical files and oral history tapes and specializes in genealogical information for Luxembourg.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~mngenweb/lookups.htm">Minnesota Look-Ups</a><br />
Richard Kyle, a Luxembourg researcher for over fifteen years, will search the materials he has collected for Luxembourg immigrants. He notes that there are many Luxembourg descendents in Stearns, Dakota and Scott counties. Connect to this site to get complete information for how to contact him with your Minnesota-Luxembourg questions.
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/~mnwinona/">Winona County, Minnesota Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> site for Winona County, maintained by Rich Pershell. Rollingstone is one of the best-known Luxembourg communities in the United States.
<a href="http://wms.luminet.net/demographics/luxembourg/">The Luxembourgers of Rollingstone</a><br />
Brief history of the Luxembourg community, as part of an overview to the ethnic demographics of Winona County.

Wisconsin's Sesquicentennial<br />
Celebrating 150 Years of Statehood, 1848-1998
<h3 id="wisconsin"><img src="images/wi.gif" /> Wisconsin</h3>
<a href="hist.htm#wi">Archives, Historical Societies &amp; Special Collections</a>
<a href="http://www.rootsweb.com/roots-l/USA/wi.html">United States Resources: Wisconsin</a><br />
RootsWeb state page, includes links for genealogy and historical societies, Family History Center Research Outline, Everton's county map and federal census information.
<a href="http://vitalrec.com/wi.html">Wisconsin Vital Records</a><br />
State entry from Elizabeth Orsay's comprehensive vital records web site.
<a href="http://searches.rootsweb.com/cgi-bin/wisconsin/wisconsin.pl">Wisconsin Land Records</a><br />
Pre-1908 Homestead and Cash Entry Patents from the Bureau of Land Management's General Land Office (GLO) Automated Records Project.
<a href="http://www.west.org">Wisconsin Ethnic Settlement Trail</a><br />
Wisconsin has developed a series of 19 walking and driving tours in honor of its ethnic settlers, including <a href="http://www.west.org/luxembourg.htm">Luxembourgers</a>. Mark Van Ells' <a href="http://www2.h-net.msu.edu/~ethnic/exhibit/exhib-settle.html">review</a> of the program.
<a href="http://www.execpc.com/~bwiegand/Life.html">Life in Wisconsin</a><br />
From Brian Wiegand's Root Celler, a short overview to the Luxembourg experience in Wisconsin, written by Loretta Spies.
<a href="http://geocities.com/Heartland/Cottage/7077/">Ozaukee County, Wisconsin Genealogy</a><br />
<img src="images/usgenweb.gif" /> site for Ozaukee County, maintained by John Robins-Wells. Ozaukee county has several major Luxembourg communities including Belgium, Dacada, Holy Cross and Lake Church.
<a href="http://www.co.ozaukee.wi.us/history/history.html">Early History of Ozaukee County</a><br />
Herbert Peters' interesting highlights of the early history of Ozaukee County, home to many Luxembourg immigrants.
<a href="http://www.execpc.com/~bwiegand/riot.html">Port Washington Draft Riots</a><br />
In 1862, Luxembourg immigrants staged a riot in Port Washington, the Ozaukee County seat, protesting the Civil War draft. Brian Wiegand's page gives a brief accounting of the event and lists those Luxembourgers arrested for civil disobedience. The <a href="http://www.execpc.com/~kap/riots.html">28th Regiment</a> of the Wisconsin Volunteer Infantry was one unit called to respond to the situation, and further information about the riot is available from Kent Peterson's regimental history page.
<a href="http://my.voyager.net/~belgium/">Village of Belgium</a><br />
One of the largest Luxembourg settlements in the United States, Belgium celebrates its ethnic heritage each August with the annual Luxembourg Fest.
<a href="ftp://ftp.rootsweb.com/pub/usgenweb/wi/ozaukee/biographies/">History of Washington and Ozaukee Counties, Wisconsin</a><br />
Biographical sketches for the towns of Belgium, Cedarburg, Fredonia, Grafton, Mequon and Port Washington from this county history published by the Wisconsin Western Historical Co. in 1881. A list is also available of biographies that specifically identify the subjects as <a href="http://members.aol.com/bqkssi/Ozaukee.html">Luxembourgers</a>.
<a href="ftp://ftp.rootsweb.com/pub/usgenweb/wi/ozaukee/cemetery/strosa.txt%0A">St. Rose of Lima Cemetery, Fredonia</a><br />
Index of headstones in this cemetery.
