<td data-bgcolor="#FFF9F4"><strong>A</strong>lthough the marriage contract is governed by state law, <strong>the federal government uses marital status as the qualification for more than 1,049 federally regulated rights and responsibilities.</strong> The so-called “Defense of Marriage Act” [Please see our article: <a href="doma.html">DoMA</a>] changes the role of the federal government to also define legal marriage, a state right under the U.S. Constitution, and block same-sex couples from full citizenship, should any state decide to offer legal marriage.
