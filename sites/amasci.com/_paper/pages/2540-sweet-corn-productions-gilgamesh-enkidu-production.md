<td><em><strong>Facts about Gilgamesh &amp; Enkidu</strong> — as of March, 10, 2002:</em>
<p>The first several rounds of cast auditions took place on August 5, 2001. A good number of strong actors read and offered their monologues. Further auditions are planned as there are many parts to fill.</p>
<p>The auditions have been temporarily set aside while Demian works on a marionette opera video short titled <a href="kate.html"><strong>Scaredy-Kate and the Monsters – or – How to Pay the Rent</strong></a>.</p>
<p>Cast and crew are still being sought for a sample scene to be shot in the near future, as well as for the feature itself. Talents of all kinds are still being sought, so please send in those resumes. [See <a href="g-03.html"><strong>Casting &amp; Crew Requirements</strong></a>]</p>
<p>A grant from the <strong>Arch and Bruce Brown Foundation</strong> was received on November 10, 2001. It will be applied toward producing <strong>Gilgamesh &amp; Enkidu</strong>.</p>
<p><em>Production data:</em></p>
<ul>
<li>Independently produced.</li>
<li>Currently in pre-production.</li>
<li>Script is undergoing a forth major revision.</li>
<li>A promotional, sample scene sample will be shot in the near future.</li>
<li>Shooting for the scene sample and the feature itself will be in the Seattle area.</li>
<li>The project will be shot on DV.</li>
<li>Production is aimed at the direct-to-video/DVD market.</li>
</ul></td>
