<td data-bgcolor="#F0FFF0" data-valign="top"><p><strong><br />
Samoyed Rescue</strong></p>
<p>Every year hundreds of Samoyeds throughout the country become homeless,<br />
most through no fault of their own. These are rescue dogs. They are not<br />
second class dogs, they are dogs that need a second chance.</p>
<p>How do these dogs become homeless? Sometimes they come from broken<br />
homes. Sometimes their people move away or an owner dies or a new baby<br />
arrives. Occasionally, they have been abused. Usually they are adults,<br />
sometimes they are old, once in a while they are young puppies.</p>
<p>Rescue dogs are often housebroken and usually have some training.<br />
Rescuing a Samoyed gives a young dog a new life. It gives an older dog the<br />
chance to live out his last years in comfort. He will give you<br />
unquestioning devotion and a friend for life.</p>
