<td rowspan="3" data-valign="TOP"><p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="goats2lrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;goats2lrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=395,height=225,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/goatsml2.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="79" height="45" alt="Baby goat" /></a><br />
I had no idea until recently, how well llamas and goats get along. Our neighbor has added a mother and baby goat to the pasture and the little one is really taken with our llamas. The patience of the llamas is always amazing especially when you see them with the young goat on their back. It's so funny to see a little goat climbing all over the llamas when they are kushed. My favorite pose is when the little goat gets on Charlie's back, when Charlie is standing up.</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="llama&amp;goat.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;llama&amp;goat.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=300,height=295,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/llama&amp;goatsml.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="75" height="74" alt="Baby goat" /></a><br />
And as luck would have it, I just happened to get a picture while working on this page. In this photo you will see the little goat has jumped on Drake's back and just standing there like he is king of the mountain.</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="llama&amp;goat2.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;llama&amp;goat2.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=263,height=245,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/llama&amp;goat2sml.jpg" data-align="RIGHT" data-border="0" width="66" height="61" alt="Baby goat" /></a><br />
This is just a playful picture of Charlie and the baby goat. The little goat first learned how to get on Charlie's back and now shares that with Drake. These are the only 2 llamas he plays with, wonder if the similar colors of Charlie &amp; Drake has anything to do with his preference?</p>
