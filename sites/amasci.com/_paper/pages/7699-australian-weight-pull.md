# Australian Weight Pull

There is apparently a group in Australia that conducts weight pulls. They are not affiliated with IWPA, but it sounds like their rules are not unlike ours; and possibly based upon some of ours. Since they are south of the equator, their seasons are opposite of ours. They pull from about March through September. It sounds like they may have one or less pulls per month.

One such individual, [Brett Jarvis](mailto:bj@hotkey.net.au), forwarded to me the results of one of their pulls. The following table is an html conversion of the Excel spreadsheet sent by Brett. It is in <span id="metric">metric</span> units as used in Australia.

I also converted to [English units](#English) and it follows this table. Or download the [Excel sheet](wp270997.xls) including both units.
