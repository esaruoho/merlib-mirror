# Midi-ocre Mania

*Source page: `~vikram/midi/default.htm`*

## The Midi-ocre Mania Page

<img src="../img/sep.gif" data-align="bottom" width="600" height="4" />

Well I ain't no Beethoven ... and pardon me for literally tootin' me own trumpet ... but this page aims to give all those armchair (and bathroom) musicians some hope for fame and glory.

Here are some examples of what you can do with a \$49 midi keyboard (and a very cool program called Melody Maestro) ... with these two accessories anyone can be a musician. Check out these "master-pieces" (emphasis on "pieces") featuring mostly original composition and totally original arrangements by yours truly. .
