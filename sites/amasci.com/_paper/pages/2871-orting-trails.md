Orting Trails

      

Moss and ferns covered one of the forest paths. Many of the native plant species of the Puget Sound region were found in this forest. Many nurselogs supported younger trees. One supported a rhododendorn seedling. I moved it, host and all, to the garden in Enumclaw.
