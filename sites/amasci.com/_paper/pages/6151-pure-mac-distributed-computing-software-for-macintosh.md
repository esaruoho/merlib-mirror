<td style="vertical-align: top"><span id="boinc"></span> <img src="images/boinc.png" data-align="top" width="32" height="32" alt="Icon" /> <strong>BOINC 5.10.32</strong><br />
<br />
BOINC lets you donate computing power to scientific research projects such as:<br />
- Climateprediction.net: study climate change<br />
- Einstein@home: search for gravitational signals emitted by pulsars<br />
- LHC@home: improve the design of the CERN LHC particle accelerator<br />
- Predictor@home: investigate protein-related diseases<br />
- Rosetta@home: help researchers develop cures for human diseases<br />
- SETI@home: Look for radio evidence of extraterrestrial life<br />
- Cell Computing biomedical research (Japanese; requires nonstandard client software)<br />
- World Community Grid: advance our knowledge of human disease.<br />
- To participate in a project, download and run BOINC software, then enter the project's URL.<br />
- You can participate in more than one project, and you control the fraction of your computing power that goes to each project. If you participate in several projects, your computer will be kept busy even when one project has no work.<br />
