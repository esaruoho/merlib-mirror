# Kent Shakespeare Original Art

*Source page: `~mohundro/lsh/thumbs.html`*

**DC’s original Impulse:**\
Legionnaire Kent Shakespeare\
\
*Since I became a fan of [Kent Shakespeare](shakespeare.html), I've always dreamed of getting original comic art (in addition to [sketches](shakespeare.html)), and recently I've been able to score part of my "grail" -- his first appearance. That, and another page, are below. These are scans of the published art, so imagine the art without the shading.\
\*

[<img src="images/v4_12_page20_th.jpg" data-border="0" width="150" height="233" alt="[Legion of Super-Heroes v. 4 #12 page 20]" />](images/v4_12_page20_bw.jpg)

 

[<img src="images/v4_13_page1_th.jpg" data-border="0" width="150" height="233" alt="[Legion of Super-Heroes v. 4 #13 page 1]" />](images/v4_13_page1_bw.jpg)

*Legion of Super-Heroes* volume 4, \#12-13 is where "it all began." Kent first appeared on page 17, in the last panel (his first line? "AHNNN!"). I purchased these three pages in a set, pre-framed. Missing from these pages are 19 (Kent doesn't appear or speak), 21 and 22 from \#12, and the cover of \#13 plus pages 2-5 and 8-12. Pencils by Kieth Giffen, Inks by Al Gordon, Letters by Todd Klein, and Story Giffen/Story Assists Gordon and Tom & Mary Bierbaum.\
\

[<img src="images/v4_39_page1_th.jpg" data-border="0" width="150" height="224" alt="[Legion of Super-Heroes v. 4 #39 page 1]" />](images/v4_39_page1_bw.jpg)

*Legion of Super-Heroes* volume 4, \#39 features Stuart Immonen's first appearance as the ongoing penciller of the LSH title. This is his first page. Immonen's first work on the Legion was actually a tryout story that was published later (#42). That story was laid out by Giffen with the standard 9-panel grid. This issue was one of the first times the series finally broke that design. Tom & Mary Bierbaum (writers), Stuart Immonen (penciller), John Dell III (inker) and John Workman (letterer).\
