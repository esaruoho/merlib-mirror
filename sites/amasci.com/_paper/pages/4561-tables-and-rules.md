## Tables and Rules.

### For the Moveable and immovable Feasts; Together with the\
Days of Fasting and Abstinence, Through the Whole Year.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**RULES to know when the Moveable Feasts and Holy-days begin.**

***<img src="../../images/e_smital.gif" data-align="LEFT" width="32" height="36" alt="E" />ASTER DAY*** (on which the rest depend) is always the First *Sunday* after the Full Moon which happens upon, or next after the Twenty-first Day of *March*; and if the Full Moon happens upon a *Sunday*, *Easter Day* is the *Sunday* after.

*Advent Sunday* is always the nearest *Sunday* to the Feast of St. *Andrew*, whether before or after.
