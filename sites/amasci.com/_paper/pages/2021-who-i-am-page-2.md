<hr />
<br />

<hr />
<br />
<br />
I am a staunch aficionado of <a href="http://www.apple.com">Apple</a> Macintosh computers, and have always exclusively owned and used them at home. I program Wintel, however, to put food on my table. I work at the <a href="http://www.nawcwpns.navy.mil/">China Lake Naval Air Weapons Station</a> for <a href="http://www.navy.mil/">The U.S. Navy</a> (you might've heard of them). I develop mission planning software for things that go boom.<br />
<br />
My philosophical interests are based on capitalism and libertarianism. I find Ayn Rand's writing highly inspirational, but I am <em>not</em> an Objectivist. (If you're interested in discussing this more with me, we can do it over a cup of joe.) My leisure interests range from playing piano (I'm the pianist for one service each week at <a href="http://www.gracelutheran.org">my church</a>) to building and shooting <a href="spudgun.txt">spud guns</a>.<br />
