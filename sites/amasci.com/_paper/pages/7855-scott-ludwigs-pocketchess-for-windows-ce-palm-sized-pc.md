<p>PocketChess v1.1 for PocketPC is a high quality, well featured Chess playing program for your PocketPC! Also compatible with Windows CE Palm-sized PC's. Also available for Windows CE Handheld PC <a href="hpcchess.html">here</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Play against the computer or a friend. </li>
<li>Choose from 8 levels of skill.</li>
<li>Export to PGN</li>
<li>Many other features including a simple and intuitive user interface, save  &amp; load games, opening move libary, board setup, full move list, list of captured pieces, copy moves to clipboard, undo, hint, reverse board, switch sides, start as black, sound effects, full help, and others!</li>
</ul>

<p>Below are screen shots of PocketChess for PocketPC and Palm-sized PC. PocketChess makes full use of the available screen space on a Palm-sized PC, providing a full size board with no compromises while also on the same screen including a toolbar, access to full menus, detailed game status, a full move list, and a list of the pieces captured.</p>
