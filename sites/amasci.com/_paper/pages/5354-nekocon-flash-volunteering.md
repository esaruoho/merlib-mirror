<td><h2 id="volunteering">Volunteering</h2>
Like any major movie, broadway stage production, rock concert. There are always the people who go by unseen, but their roll in the production is no less important. Even anime conventions have these people. They're called the volunteers, gophers, reg takers, door watches, or roving security. What ever name they are given, they are the support column of any convention. And like any convention Neko-Con needs volunteers to help to make this the best con it can be.
<p>Right now, you're probably wondering, "Hey what's in it for me?" Ok, so maybe just the warm fuzzy feeling you get for helping out just isn't quite enough to make you jump on the band wagon. That's why we have other goodies just waiting to offer to give you that encouraging boost. After all, you should get something for all your hard earned work. Being a volunteer at Neko-Con will entitle you to,</p>
<p><strong>Free Food</strong><br />
A separate hospitality suite for volunteers will set up away from the hustle and bustle of the convention for people to recharge and collect themselves. Drinks, and quick and easy foods will be there for your consumption. (We're talking real food here folks, just not chips, dips, and ding-dongs)</p>
<p><strong>Sleeping Space</strong><br />
The gopher crash space will be limited to a first come first serve basis.  We will only have so many rooms and you can only fit so many people in a room. And it will give a place to freshen up, take a shower and change clothes. Stacking people like cord-wood could cause more problems than it's worth. And, hanging hammocks for the walls is a sure fire way to upset the hotel.  Two things to remember if you're going to try for the "gopher holes" as a sleeping spot.  Bring a sleeping bag and pillow, and be sure you have a back up in case the rooms are currently filled to capacity.</p>
<p><strong>Goodies</strong><br />
We ask, but not demand that any one who volunteers for 12 hours during the convention.  Twelve hours may seem like a lot but the time will fly by. After you've done your full 12 hours for the weekend you are entitled to something for your time well spent.  And what you will get is one the rare and collectible, Neko-Con Staff T-shirts to proudly wear and sport around the con showing everyone that you are one of the few, the proud, <strong>"The Neko Pack".</strong></p>
<p><strong>What if I do more than 12 hours?</strong><br />
We're glad you asked.  There are those who put in even more of their time and effort into helping out at the con, and in turn we do want to do something for helping us out.  We are offering FREE memberships for next year to next years Neko-Con for those who do volunteer 20 hours or more. </p>
<p>Ok, if you've read this far, then maybe your still interested in signing up? And there are two ways to sign up.</p>
<p><strong>At the Con</strong><br />
After you've registered, stop by con operations and ask to sign up. The staff there be more that happy to take you in, sign you up, and give you the grand tour.</p>
<p><strong>Before the Con</strong><br />
After you send in your pre-reg, you can EMail the head gopher stangler.. err.. gopher wrangler at, <a href="mailto:macross85@hotmail.com">macross85@hotmail.com</a> and say you want to volunteer, and what part of the con you want to help in. Some areas of the con, like video rooms fill up with volunteers very fast. So you may not get the exact spot and hours you want. If you don't have email, just put a note in the envelope when you mail in your pre- registration. Either by email, or US Mail you will be notified that you are pre- registered for the volunteer squad.</p>
<p><strong>Signing up for hours</strong>.<br />
Your volunteer hours are broken down into 2 hour blocks. And you sign up for each of these blocks in whatever area you volunteer to help in. But we will never ask to you work more than 2 blocks in a row (4 hours) You did come to see the convention, didn't you? There will be sign up sheets in the Operations Room at the conventions. And those who sign up early will get first dibs on the hours that will be available.</p></td>
