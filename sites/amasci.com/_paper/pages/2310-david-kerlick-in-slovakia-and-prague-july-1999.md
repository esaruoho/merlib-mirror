# David Kerlick in Slovakia and Prague, July 1999

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Mon 7/26 Uzhorod to Poprad

Van to Kosice, train to Poprad, Hotel X. TEZ to Tatr. Lomnice, dinner, walk about lake.

### Tue 7/27

TEZ to Starij Smokovec, Funicular, Falls, Magistralia. Rap at a ski hotel. Granite stairs down to Novij Smokovec, TEZ back.

### Wed 7/28

Train to Kosice, Hotel Slovan, Pedestrian Zone. Museum, horde of 17th C. gold coins.

### Thu 7/29 Watch the fountain. Fly to Prague. Watch Black Light theater. Naked night at Bar.

### Fri 7/30

Rudolfeum. Shorp for Amber and Garnet. Westside park. Look for Wooden Church. Evropa Hotel. Mucha Museum. Dvorak 4 hands. KFC. Drakes' Pub.

### Tue 7/31

Fly to Istanbul
