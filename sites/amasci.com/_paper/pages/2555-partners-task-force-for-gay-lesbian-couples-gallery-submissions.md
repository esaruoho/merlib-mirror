<td><strong>Your picture can go in the Partner Task Force Committed Couples Gallery!</strong><br />
Here’s how …
<p>Send a photo, scanned photo (mailed or attached to e-mail) or videotape (VHS, SVHS, 8 or Hi8) along with a <strong>co-signed</strong> statement authorizing its use, using the following:</p>
<p><strong>We give permission for Partners Task Force to<br />
place our photo likeness on the Partners Web site.<br />
We hold Partners and its directors blameless for<br />
any possible repercussions.<br />
 <br />
Print name: ________________________<br />
 <br />
&lt;signed&gt; ________________________ date: _______<br />
 <br />
Print name: ________________________<br />
 <br />
&lt;signed&gt; ________________________ date: _______</strong></p>
<p>If sending this agreement by e-mail (along with an attached image),<br />
type “&lt;signed&gt;” before your names.</p>
<p><em>Also include:</em></p>
<ul>
<li>Caption indicating the event pictured, the date, and place.</li>
<li>Who is on the right, who on the left.</li>
<li>City (or at least the state) you live in.</li>
<li>Date when you first regarded yourselves as a couple, or when you first met.</li>
<li>Personal interests or hobbies.</li>
</ul>
<em>Note:</em> We do not caption the photos with URLs or the names of businesses that couples may be engaged in. It is our policy to not advertise in the gallery. This keeps the motivation for gallery photo placement strictly for honoring couples.</td>
