    In India, RBI chooses two of the four Fridays in a month as reporting Fridays when banks have to maintain prescribed reserve ratios. Banks which do not maintain these ratios have to pay considerable penalties and, in order to avoid such penalties banks tend to maintain excess reserves. This is largely due to banks not being able to obtain timely data from their branches. This results in blocking of resources into non-lucrative cash and government securities rather than being profitably deployed as loans or call money.
<p>HORSY is a unique tool used to manage resources effectively and efficiently using cost effective and simple technology.</p>

<strong>Key Features :</strong>
<hr />
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>India Oriented</strong></p>
<p>HORSY has been specifically designed for Indian conditions.</p>
<p><strong><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /></strong> <strong>Cost Effective</strong></p>
<p>HORSY operates on a low end, stand alone PC and dial-up modems making it a very cost effective system.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Fast</strong></p>
<p>HORSY sends data electronically which reaches the head office in seconds.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Currency independent</strong></p>
<p>HORSY allows multiple currencies enabling effective reporting of FCNR deposits and treatment of NRE / NRO funds as independent currencies.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Branch Templates</strong></p>
<p>Head Office can create multiple reporting templates for different branches.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Immediate Consolidation</strong></p>
<p>HORSY sends reports as structured messages hence data from thousands of branches can be consolidated in minutes.</p>
<hr />
<strong>System Features :</strong>
<hr />
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Structured Report Formats</strong></p>
<p>Reports are presented in a structured format allowing an interface to the Head Office baseline system.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Multiple Report Capabilities</strong></p>
<p>HORSY allows branches to create any number of reports allowing Head Office to make multiple investment decisions. Head office can also use HORSY for weekly DTL reporting.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Microsoft Excel Interface</strong></p>
<p>HORSY interfaces to Microsoft Excel allowing Head Office to conduct ad-hoc data analysis.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Security</strong></p>
<p>HORSY uses Server Passwords, Testkeys, Symmetrical Encryption, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) based Asymmetrical Encryption and Hardware tokens making it a highly secure system.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>User Friendly</strong></p>
<p>HORSY is a Windows based product having a graphical interface allowing officers with little or no computer exposure to easily use the system.</p>
<p><img src="images/red-bullet.gif" width="10" height="10" /> <strong>Profit Center</strong></p>
<p>Unlike most systems which are cost centers,HORSY allows a bank to generate profits by effective money market operations.</p></td>
