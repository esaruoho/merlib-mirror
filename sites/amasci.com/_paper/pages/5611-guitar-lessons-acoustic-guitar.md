<td width="100%"><h1 id="acoustic-guitar">ACOUSTIC GUITAR</h1>
<p>The acoustic guitar is a self-contained instrument. It can play chords, solos, orchestral arrangements - even accompany your own voice. It's almost like a portable band of musicians that will travel along with you wherever you go.</p>

<h2 id="acoustic-guitar-workshop-lessons">Acoustic Guitar Workshop Lessons</h2>
<p><a href="20001203.html"><strong>Acoustic Slide Guitar - General Technique and Tips</strong></a><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Rick Payne</span><br />
There have been many objects used to achieve the slide sound. Knives, bottle necks, tubes of all kinds of metals and glass, spark plug sockets, lighters, stone, marble, plastic... anything! At sometime or other I've used them all but to keep things simple and effective, I use a real bottle neck or metal tube, cut long enough to be slightly longer than the pinkie.</p>
<p><a href="20010115.html"><strong>How To Record An Acoustic Guitar</strong><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Steve Elliot</span><br />
</a> Let's face it, capturing a decent acoustic guitar tone is not an easy task. Here are some tips that will help you cover the basics of this deft art and avoid the pitfalls that will render your efforts a jangly and dolorous cacophony. So if you don't want your Dreadnought to sound dreadful, read on.</p>
<p><strong><a href="20001222.html">Origins of the Pentatonic and Relevance to the Blues</a></strong><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Acoustic Guitar Workshop</span><br />
Like many guitarists, I was always confused as to what Pentatonic this and Pentatonic that meant. Guitar books varied in their interpretation of blues scales and Pentatonic, and still do. Most of us get through in the end by using our ears and not the grey stuff in between. It is, however, worth mentioning a few points in connection with the birth of the blues.</p>
<p><a href="20001202.html"><strong>History &amp; Origins of the Slide Guitar in the Blues</strong></a><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Acoustic Guitar Workshop</span><br />
There have been many claims to the origin of the slide guitar. Its haunting sound can be heard across the whole spectrum of musical styles, through blues, rock, country, Hawaiian and even jazz. A sound so haunting, that as fans of Robert Johnson might believe, was born from the devil himself.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.acousticguitarworkshop.com/slidebot/slide_lesson.htm" target="_blank"><strong>Acoustic Slide : D Tuning Workout</strong></a><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Acoustic Guitar Workshop</span><br />
This is a pretty cool lesson on open D tuning and has 3 great slide instrumentals to try in the style of Blind Willie Johnson and Ry Cooder. All the exercises and tunes have audio support as per usual.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.acousticguitarworkshop.com/gertdemeijerlesson.htm" target="_blank"><strong>The challenge to every guitar player : The 12-string guitar</strong></a><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Acoustic Guitar Workshop</span><br />
The 12-string guitar has a long history and we find some astonishing playing among roots-musicians like Huddy Ledbetter (Leadbelly) , Blind Willy Mc Tell and Big Joe Williams. Here I will give you some basic information about the 12-string guitar, some helpful tips and some examples in tablature.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.acousticguitarworkshop.com/timsparkslesson.htm" target="_blank"><strong>Tim Sparks Guitar Lesson</strong></a><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Acoustic Guitar Workshop</span><br />
Spice up your blues be-bop style with Tim Sparks. If you want to get away from the old pentatonic try this free online lesson for intermediate to advanced players.</p>
<p><strong><a href="http://www.acousticguitarworkshop.com/images/altbass_lesson.pdf">Alternating Bass Lesson</a> (PDF file download)</strong><br />
<span class="smauthorname">By Acoustic Guitar Workshop</span><br />
This technique is an essential for an aspiring fingerstylist. It features exercises and an arrangement of Dylan's "Meet Me In The Morning". It has real audios embedded in it and also links to a CD quality mp3 of the tune. You can download the lesson in pdf format from <a href="http://www.acousticguitarworkshop.com/images/altbass_lesson.pdf">this location</a>.</p>
