    co-founder of the respected metempsychosis.com (my personal page and some
    music is at http://metempsychosis.com/orpheus).

    this morning, as is not unusual, i had a dream with music in it. i
    occasionally have some sort of music in a dream or two and endevour to model
    it into a more physical form, e.g. some of the tunes you can hear on my
    page. but i digress. this morning, there was music in my dream, and it
    featured a lady on a fantastic stage. the stage was amazing, with light
    peach curtains contrasted with gold and red, and a huge marble floor. there
    was a lady singing on the stage, sitting on a deep red velvet sofa, wearing
    what looked like light pink satin pyjamas. imagine the whole thing as an
    elaborate film clip, and you've got what it looked and sounded like.

    only i woke up and realised that i knew the voice as Toni Child's voice. the
    song was still playing in my head this morning and i absolutely love it, i'm
    trying to turn it into a tune right now. so the first thing i thought is:
    i've got to contact Toni Childs. i've never actually seen her sing, but i've
    heard her on the radio many times and i figure she might be interested in
    collaborating. so i think.. right, how am i going to get in contact with
    Toni Childs? so i go to a search engine, and type in "contact toni
    childs".....

    which brings me to your page. now, up until now i was excited about this but
    more or less thinking normal. and throughout the day, i've been thinking
    about the song on and off, and i had dismissed yours and a few other pages
    as not having any contact details for Toni on there. so, at the end of the
    day, i ask a friend if he can give me any leads to contact Toni.. he
    responds by.. sending me another link to your site.

    http://www.eskimo.com/~wayneld/tonichilds.html

    only, this time, i scrolled down, and i saw the black and white picture at
    the bottom of the page. like i say, i've never seen Toni Childs before, i
    don't know what she looks like, but if that picture at the bottom of the
    page is Toni Childs, then this is very very strange indeed. that picture,
    looks exactly like the girl in my dream. needless to say, i'm freaking out a
    bit! and i'm now more determined than ever to see if i can contact Toni.

    so, apologies for the long-whinded email. i thought, since you're interested
    in Toni Childs you might find this mildly amusing, and you might be able to
    help. tracking down a celebrity, as you know, is not an easy task.
    especially not getting through the various filters in place to stop you from
    getting to them (and understandably so). so if you have any thoughts or
    suggestions as to how i might get into contact with Toni i would greatly
    appreciate anything you can give me. i'd like to send her this song, and
    this strange story, as i'm now curious what she makes of it all.. hmm

    all my best, and thanks for listening! :)

    orpheus

    Subject:  Toni Childs Live 10/23/97...New Songs

    Toni Childs just played at the House Of Blues in L.A. It was great, she
    came out and played the new CD as a sort of pre-production gig...She said
    they would be going in very soon to record it and were doing a few gigs to
    get ready....NO old songs tonite. 

    The band has changed....She has a six piece band; guitar, bass, drums,
    percussion, cello keyboards, plus 2 back up singers. Toni played guitar on
    one song called "Revolution." She also announced that she just got
    married... "Heart that Matters" was the song apparently most appreciated
    as her encore consisted of her asking which song the crowd liked best and
    they'd play it again.. She stage dived again on one song, and was reading
    off lyric sheets at times..

    It was very enjoyable and as a very long time fan, this CD is her most
    accessible since Union...It has the potential for multiple singles....
    While I love all of the LP's, Union was the only one that got broad appeal
    and I think this one could surpass whatever Union sold......

    Subject: Toni in SF 10/24

    Only a few things to add from Dino's report. Thanks for the set list
    Dino! 

    I chatted during the warm-up with whom may have been Toni's
    manager....he seemed important. Yes Toni is without a label, but 
    this new album should come out in Feb or Mar 98. It has not been 
    recorded yet. As in L.A. the concert was sort of a rehearsal. The 
    material is white hot and hard. Only 2 ballads, the rest were 
    screeching rockers along the lines of Don't Walk Away. If you love 
    that song, you'll love these. Lots of blues and gospel tones, and 
    some Middle East. 

    I was blown away by Promised Land which she sang twice because I 
    screamed so much for her to do it again! As well as Piece of my 
    Heart. Got a feeling Revolution would be the single, a great tune but 
    the above were better if you're reading this Toni! The ballads were 
    well written, but need production. It was the rockers that shown. 

    She was delightfully accessible with the audience, her marriage was 
    just 9 days ago! First time I didn't cry at one of her shows...having 
    seen her 5 times. Instead of leaving wet-eyed, warmed and worn, I 
    left hoarse and exhilarated, hoarse enough to sing Promised Land all 
    the way home in the car and sound good! I want it now!! We all have 
    something to get excited about for 98!!!
