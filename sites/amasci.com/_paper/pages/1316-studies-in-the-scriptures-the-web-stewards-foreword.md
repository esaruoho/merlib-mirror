# Studies In The Scriptures, The Web Steward's Foreword

*Source page: `~billz/Truth/Volumes/Foreword.html`*

"***Studies In The Scriptures***"

**THE WEB STEWARD'S FOREWORD**

The [Studies In The Scriptures](http://www.eskimo.com/~billz/Truth/Volumes) was originally written over a century ago. To this day it remains unique among Christian literature in its approach to understanding mankind's relationship to his Creator in the following ways:

- It is a factual presentation derived from the Scriptures, rather than philosophical theory.
- It examines all of the Scriptures bearing on a subject and reaches harmonious conclusions.
- It gives Scriptural references and encourages the reader to verify every point presented.
- It reveals God's purposes toward mankind from Creation to the present and into the future.
- It manifests the consistency of God's character attributes: Love, Wisdom, Justice, Power.

If you should desire more information regarding God's divine plan of the ages, please [send me some email](mailto:billz@eskimo.com).

Sincerely,

Bill Zietzke
