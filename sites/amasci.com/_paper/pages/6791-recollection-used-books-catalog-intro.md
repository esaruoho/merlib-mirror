These catalogs represent a small portion of our inventory.<br />
If you prefer other editions or reading copies of books listed in our catalogs,<br />
query us; we often have unlisted alternate editions or can attempt to obtain them for you.</td>
<td data-valign="BOTTOM">We accept <strong>VISA/Mastercard</strong>, by email, phone or mail. Libraries billed. <br />
Add $4.00 flat postage/handling for each order (any quantity, 1 book or 100).<br />
For single volumes or small items, we recommend Priority Mail, $5.<br />
International orders will be charged as close to exact postage as possible. <br />
All books are in Very Good (VG) or better condition, exceptions noted.<br />
Dust Jackets (DJ) are indicated when present.<br />
All books are returnable within ten days if not as described. See our <a href="http://www.recollectionbooks.com/order.html">Order Page</a> for further details.</td>
