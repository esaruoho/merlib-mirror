<td width="800" data-valign="top"><blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.whyville.com/" rel="nofollow">WHYVILLE</a></strong><br />
Many games and activities. Safe chat area with thousands of kids (and design your own online avatar.) Write for the Whyville times. Free (or $5 for high speed pass.)
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.howtoons.org/" rel="nofollow">HOWTOONS</a></strong><br />
Comic pages with some GOOD science projects.
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.sandlotscience.com/" rel="nofollow">SANDLOT SCIENCE</a></strong><br />
All sorts of optical illusions, some animated, some with JAVA applets. Makes your <em>brain</em> hurt, not just your eyes.
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.howstuffworks.com" rel="nofollow">HOW STUFF WORKS</a></strong><br />
Very interesting website. Hundreds of pictures and articles which explain almost anything you can think of!
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.inventivekids.com" rel="nofollow">INVENTIVE KIDS</a></strong><br />
Many activities including several games. Find out about inventions and women innovators.
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.kineticcity.com/" rel="nofollow">KINETIC CITY</a></strong><br />
Somewhat fun website from the AAAS science organization. Play "kid science detective" games. Graphics-heavy.
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.bonus.com" rel="nofollow">BONUS.COM</a> (Java Required!)</strong><br />
Activities, online games, and links to interesting stuff. Check out HOW IT WORKS, SO STRANGE!, WHAT'S INSIDE?, and NATURAL DISASTERS. Parents: this site includes its own kid-safe internet browser.
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://dir.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Internet/Devices_Connected_to_the_Internet/" rel="nofollow">INTERESTING DEVICES</a> on the Internet</strong><br />
Peer through Web cameras on mountaintops and in offices. Generate Fractals. Control real robots. Create "A-life" creatures. Interface to real Coke machines???!
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.kidwizard.com/" rel="nofollow">KID WIZARD</a></strong><br />
A story with games, also Science spells, slimy potions
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.4kids.org/coolspots/brainsweat/page1.shtml" rel="nofollow">BRAINSWEAT</a> is the "Cool Science" links at 4-KIDS.ORG.<br />
</strong> Also check out their <a href="http://www.4kids.org/coolspots/creatures/page1.shtml" rel="nofollow">CREATURES</a> section.
</blockquote>
<blockquote>
<strong><a href="http://www.chem4kids.com" rel="nofollow">Chem-4-Kids</a></strong><br />
Ever wanted to figure out Chemistry? Chem-4-kids is a good introduction. Not all boring. Funny stuff included too. Also a Biology section. Try the <a href="http://www.chem4kids.com/biology4kids/key/tour.html" rel="nofollow">Bio Tour</a> and <a href="http://www.chem4kids.com/chem4kids/key/tour.html" rel="nofollow">Chem Tour</a>.
</blockquote></td>
