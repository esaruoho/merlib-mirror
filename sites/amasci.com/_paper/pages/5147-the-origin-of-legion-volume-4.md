A lone figure wanders the scarred wasteland, arriving at a miserable hovel. He pulls aside the grimy curtain and steps inside.
"Hey, Fleming!" he says, greeting the one-armed hobo within.
"Can't talk now Giff. Gotta go. Seeya later."
Fleming hustled out of the hovel, leaving Giff wide-eyed. "But you called me here..."
"No he didn't," said a voice from the shadows, "I did."
"Wha -- Tom! You old Beerbommian sonovagun! What are you doing here?"
"I want to start it up again Giff. Remember the dream? Remember how it felt...

"What? That chick, Mary, she says someone's going to kill Levitz--"
"Got him! OK, got him!"
"Look, it's VOLUME FORCE!"
"We're really going to make a difference..."

"Why, it never occurred to me."
Giff smiled. "You sonova -- so who else is in? Mary?"
