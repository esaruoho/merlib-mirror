<td data-valign="top" width="73%">One form of transportation usually overlooked in a transportation system is bicycles.<br />
 <br />
Bicycles can hardly be called mass transit, and they definitely are not rapid transit. But a lot of people ride bicycles, and obviously, if enough people ride bicycles instead of driving, there would be a significant lessening of motor vehicle traffic.<br />
 <br />
I would like to see more effort and money put into building bikeways, dedicated narrow roadways specifically for bicyles, although in many cases they could be used also by hikers, joggers, walkers, wheel chairs, roller skates, skateboards, and other human-powered means of getting around.<br />
