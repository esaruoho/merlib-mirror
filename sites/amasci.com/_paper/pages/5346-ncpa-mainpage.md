| The National Collegiate Paintball Association is an all-volunteer, non-profit organization for the promotion of paintball at the college level. The NCPA exists for two reasons. The first is to give college students who otherwise would not normally have the opportunity to do so the chance to play paintball in a helpful, inexpensive environment. The second is to give college students an inexpensive way to gain the experience necessary to play tournament paintball and the tournaments to use that experience in. |
