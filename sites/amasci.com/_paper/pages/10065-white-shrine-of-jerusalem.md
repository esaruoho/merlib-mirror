<p><span style="font-size:13.5pt">         We are happy to give you some of the History of </span><span style="font-size:13.5pt;color:red"><strong>Temple Shrine</strong> </span> <span style="font-size:13.5pt">Seattle</span><span style="font-size:
   13.5pt"> </span><span style="font-size:13.5pt">WA</span><span style="font-size:13.5pt"> #13.</span> <span style="font-size:13.5pt"><span style="mso-spacerun:yes"> </span>We were blessed to have been started by Sojourners of a very strong Shrine, Bethlehem Shrine No.1. The time was December 1947. Fraternal Orders were in their prime. Television was just an infant. The screen was small, only black and white, and was limited to a very few programs. The war was over and we were all so thankful to have our Boys and Girls home again. Family ties were strong.</span> <span style="font-size:
  13.5pt">The </span><span style="font-size:13.5pt;color:red">DeMolay, Job's Daughters, and Rainbow Girls,</span><span style="font-size:13.5pt"> were all being organized and doing great.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:13.5pt">   <span style="mso-spacerun:yes">      </span>Just a few years ago, there were five Shrines in the </span> <span style="font-size:13.5pt">Seattle</span><span style="font-size:13.5pt"> area.  We really enjoyed visiting our co-workers. We had <em>Friends Nights, Fun Nights, and Everyone</em> came to visit.  Today, we have </span><strong><span style="font-size:13.5pt;
  color:red">Temple Shrine</span></strong><span style="font-size:13.5pt">. It is a lesson in the facts of life. It is important to hold on to our principles. When I read the words that Lewis Starke said when he presented the Charter to </span> <strong><span style="font-size:13.5pt;
    color:red">Temple</span><span style="font-size:13.5pt;
   color:red"> </span><span style="font-size:13.5pt;
    color:red">Shrine</span></strong><span style="font-size:13.5pt"> on </span><span style="font-size:13.5pt"> May 17, 1948</span><span style="font-size:13.5pt">, I recognize what a dedicated Sojourner he was. What he has done before us should inspire us to walk in his shoes.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:13.5pt">    I can not come up with any items like watching out for the cougars, which incidentally we do have in Washington, but I was inspired when I read the minutes of the Institution of </span><strong><span style="font-size:13.5pt;color:red">Temple Shrine</span></strong><span style="font-size:13.5pt"> and also when the Charter was granted. We have enjoyed over 54 years.</span></p>

<td style="padding: .75pt .75pt .75pt .75pt">What is:<u>THE ORDER OF THE WHITE SHRINE OF JERUSALEM?<span style="mso-spacerun:yes">      </span></u>
<p><span style="mso-spacerun:yes"> </span>The Order of the White Shrine of Jerusalem is a Masonic Affiliated fraternal organization to which men and women can belong .It is an incorporated non-profit body, organized under a charter of incorporation dated October 23, 1894 and recorded in the office of the Recorder of Cook County, in the State of Illinois, June 18, 1895.</p>
<p>                           <span style="color:red"> PURPOSE                    </span> </p>
<p>The White Shrine of Jerusalem Claims a three-fold purpose charitable, social, and fraternal. It aims to bring together women and men with high moral and social character to share their desire to create enjoyable experiences for themselves and to provide aid to those less fortunate. Through regular meetings, their fellowship is maintained. By visiting others, broad friendships are developed.</p>
<p>             <span style="color:red">FOUNDATION OF OUR ORDER</span></p>
<p>The order is based upon the Christian Religion. Its teachings are scriptural and intended to illustrate and impress on the mind of the initiate the humble but glorious birth and life of the glad tidings of the coming of the King and endeavor to bring to the<br />
world, "Peace on Earth, Good will to men."   To do noble deeds, and by acts of kindness and words of cheer bless all mankind.</p>
<p>             <span style="color:red">OUR BENEVOLENT PROGRAM</span></p>
<p>The Basis of our existence is our Benevolent fund - our "Material Objective". Through our voluntary donations and income<br />
from our endowment, we are able to help those in need of rehabilitation regardless of race, creed, sect, or age. They just must<br />
have a need; i.e. used up insurance, etc .Local Shrines are allowed five cases a year up to $5,000.00 a case. A Supreme Board of Five administers the cases. We give upwards of $375,000.00 a year.</p>
<p>                      <span style="color:red"> WHO MAY JOIN</span></p>
<p>Membership in the Order of the White Shrine of Jerusalem consists of the wives, daughters, stepdaughters, stepmothers, step granddaughters, legally adopted daughters, mothers, grandmothers, widows ,sisters, half-sisters, granddaughters, great<br />
granddaughters, aunts and those nieces and grand nieces of Master Masons who have a blood relationship to him, when said<br />
Master Mason is in good and regular standing ,and those whose husbands, sons, fathers, stepfathers ,stepsons , step grandfathers, grandsons, brothers, grandfathers, nephews, or uncles, if deceased, were in good standing in some regular<br />
Masonic Lodge at their death, or Master Masons in good and regular standing and who have no restraining factors imposed<br />
upon them by their specific Grand Lodge.</p>
<p><span style="color:red">HOW TO JOIN</span></p>
<p>It is easy to join the Order of the White Shrine of Jerusalem: simply obtain a petition from a friend or acquaintance or a Shrine Scribe or E-mail to wsj@eskimo.com. .mailto:wsj@eskimo.com. Fill in the information requested and submit it along with an initiation fee through the local Shrine. The Shrine will have members of an investigating committee visit you with a few questions and to verify your Masonic affiliation.  After the results of their visit are reported at a Shrine meeting and a vote taken, you will be notified when to attend for initiation. </p>
<p><span style="color:red">INITIATION PROCEDURE</span></p>
<p>In the Order of the White Shrine of Jerusalem, there is but one degree.  Candidates for the order must confess a belief in the<br />
Christian Religion.   Its obligation is based upon the honor of those who obtain it, and once voluntarily assumed is perpetual. </p>
<p><span style="color:red">ACTIVITIES</span></p>
<p>Members are encouraged to attend at least a monthly meeting of their Shrine.  Some Shrines have two meetings a month.  Most Shrines have other social activities or projects.  Activities such as dinners, breakfasts, and fashion shows are common.  Other activities include bazaars or staffing information booths at fairs.  Shrines select their own method of fund raising.</p>
<p>              <span style="color:red">   FRATERNAL RELATIONS</span></p>
<p>Through dinners of similar activities, the Subordinate Shrines are encouraged to have functions which include members of<br />
concordant and other Masonic affiliated organizations.  Thereby promoting through exchanges of information, good working<br />
relationships with the other Masonic bodies.</p>
<p>                      <span style="color:red">SUPREME SHRINE</span></p>
<p>While Subordinate Shrines provide activities for the members, there is an international level of the organization under which the Subordinate Shrines are organized.  This is the Supreme Shrine and it provides administration for the Constitution and By Laws under which each individual Shrine operates.  Supreme Shrine meets annually in May for their legislative session. <br />
Approximately 550 Subordinate Shrines with about 75,000 members are found throughout the United States and the Provinces of Canada.</p></td>
