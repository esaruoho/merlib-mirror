1675 -- Tituba lives, exact year unknown, West Indies.

> Enslaved in the home of Reverend Samuel Parris in Salem, Massachusetts, Tituba entertained neighborhood girls with stories from her homeland, including tales of voodoo & spirits. The girls, in turn, accuse a series of helpless people, including Tituba, of witchcraft. In 1692, she was sentenced to death. In 1693, however, a jury failed to agree on her fate. To pay for her prison expenses, Tituba was sold to another slave owner.

1804 -- U.S. Navy Lt. Stephen Decatur, accompanied by 75 sailors, climbs aboard U.S. frigate Philadelphia & sets it afire. Tripoli pirates had seized the Philadelphia intact, & the Navy, unable to recapture the frigate, wanted to minimize the Tripolians' advantage. The successful burning of its own vessel was considered a great victory by the Navy, & Decatur was promoted to Captain. []()\

1844 -- James Guillaume (1844-1916) lives, London. Historian of the First International, an anarchist active in the Swiss [Jura Federation](http://recollectionbooks.com/bleed/gallery/galleryindex.htm#JuraFed). Aligned with [Mikhail Bakunin](http://recollectionbooks.com/bleed/gallery/galleryindex.htm#Bakunin), with whom he was kicked out of the International in a coup by Marx & his followers. \

1848 -- France: [Octave Mirbeau](http://recollectionbooks.com/bleed/gallery/galleryindex.htm#MirbeauOctave) (1848-1917) lives. Prolific short story writer (1,200), novelist, anarchist, antimilitarist, dramatist. A founding member of the Acadmie Goncourt. Died on his birthday, 1917.

> Throughout the 32 years of his anarchist period, intensely antimilitarist & anticlerical. Among his other causes were feminism, the abolition of child labor, the fight against Russian Czarism & the repression of Japanese dissidents, abolition of capital punishment, defending the rights of working people & their unions, & the defense of libertarian education.

<http://burn.ucsd.edu/~mai/mirbeau.html>\

1898 -- US: [Emma Goldman](http://recollectionbooks.com/bleed/Encyclopedia/GoldmanEmma.htm) begins a speaking tour, February 16-20.
