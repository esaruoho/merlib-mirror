Speed Up Your Editing

------------------------------------------------------------------------

One way to quicken the editing is by running a word processor and using the "copy and paste" method to dump good material into a clean new file. Another way is to block whole areas that have no important meaning and delete them, leaving the good sentences behind. In a concentrated effort of a few minutes, one can work on a large Merge.txt file and put the good merges in a clean edited file. ParaMind Version 2.0 has an Append function located in the Edit menu.

Append can be speeded up by using the hot-keys alt-e, and then n. When you are finished making the selections, you then select New and Paste in your edited text onto a blank ParaMind screen or any Windows word processor.
