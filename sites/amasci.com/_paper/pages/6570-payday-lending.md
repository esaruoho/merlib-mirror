**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#CC0000">Why we care: The payday advance industry is under attack from a variety of critics, many of whom are acting to further their own causes. Payday advances are great examples of <u>consumer choice at work</u>, with millions of Americans taking advantage of this service every year. Consumers opt for payday advances because theyre <u>cost effective</u> theyre less expensive than credit-card late fees, for example and banks no longer offer small, signature loans.  They help borrowers in a financial pinch. In fact, a recent survey found 92 percent of borrowers agree that <u>these loans are hel</u>p<u>ful to them</u>. As a result, shuttering payday advance stores would deprive Americans of a useful financial resource and demonstrate that activists can hurt any business that they decide to target, no matter how arbitrary their choice may be. For this reason, <u>CDFE su</u>pp<u>orts this industry</u> in its effort to meet the publics need for small, short-term advances.</span>**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Payday advances provide small cash loans designed for short-term needs. These loans are popular because they dont require collateral or a credit check, making them easier to obtain for people with little credit history.</span>**

**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Yet these loans are controversial. Some activists are seeking legislation to ban these loans, saying they charge high interest rates and prey on unsuspecting customers. </span>**

**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Supporters, however, say these loans help consumers cover temporary expenses, providing them with a helping hand when they need it most. In addition, studies show the cost of these loans is less than the average bounced-check fee or late payment penalty.</span>**<span style="font-size:
12.0pt"> </span>

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#3333CC">What are payday advances?</span>**

**<span style="font-family:Verdana">Payday advances are small, unsecured, short-term amounts of cash generally used to cover unexpected financial situations.  They are available to consumers that have an active checking account and regular income.  </span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;
font-family:Verdana;color:#3333CC">How does it work?</span>**

**<span style="font-family:Verdana">Eligible payday advance consumers:</span>**

- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">Read and sign an agreement that discloses the terms of the payday advance; </span>**
- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">Write a personal check for the advance amount plus a fee, after which he/she </span>**
- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">Immediately receives the requested amount minus the agreed upon fee. </span>**

**<span style="font-family:Verdana;color:black">A consumers check is held for an agreed-upon time period (usually about two weeks).  At the end of that time period, either the check is deposited or the customer returns with cash to reclaim the check.</span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#3333CC">Who uses payday advances?</span>**

***<span style="font-family:Verdana;color:black">According to research from <a href="http://www.cfsa.net/mediares/Reports/GeorgetownStudy.pdf" style="color: blue; text-decoration: underline; text-underline: single"><span style="text-decoration: none">Georgetown Universitys Credit Research Center </span></a>in Washington, DC: </span>***

**<span style="font-family:Verdana">Payday advance customers represent the heart of the working middle class:</span>**

- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">Majority have incomes between \$25,000 and \$50,000 </span>**
- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">68% are under 45 years old; only 4% are over 65, compared to 20% of the population </span>**
- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">94% have a high school diploma or better, with 56% having some college or a degree </span>**
- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">42% own their own homes </span>**
- **<span style="font-family:Verdana">100% have a steady income and an active checking account (required for an advance) </span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#336600">Critics say:</span>**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366"> </span>**<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Payday advances have a high annual percentage rate (APR) and are not as beneficial to consumers as some other financial options might be.</span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#3333CC">Research shows:</span>**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;
font-family:Verdana;color:#3333CC"> </span> **<span style="font-size:10.0pt;
font-family:Verdana;color:#003366">When payday advances are used responsibly, they are a valuable credit tool for consumers.  Banks abandoned short-term, small denomination loans, leaving consumers stuck with limited and unsavory options.  Now, for a set fee (usually 15 percent of the loan amount) customers can borrow the few hundred dollars that is sometimes needed to cover a cash shortfall between paydays.  *<u>APR is a red-herring, since these loans are for a few weeks, not long term use.</u>*</span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#336600">Critics say:</span>**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366"> </span>**<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Payday advances perpetuate a cycle of debt.  Lenders turn customers into repeat borrowers by providing easy credit without full disclosure.</span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#3333CC">Research shows:</span>**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366"> </span> **<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">When used appropriately, payday advances can actually *<u>DECREASE</u>* debt.  For example: bank and merchant fees on a \$100 bounced check average \$49 and the late fee on a \$100 credit card payment is about \$29.  However, the fee for a payday loan of \$100 is only about \$15.  A </span> <span style="font-size:
  10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;color:#003366">Georgetown</span><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;color:#003366"> University study found that a customer who takes out a \$100 payday advance to pay a \$50 utility bill and a \$50 credit card bill on time pays less by avoiding both late fees.</span>**

**<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#003366">Payday advances are not meant as regular budgeting tools and most reputable lenders take steps to encourage responsible use.  Customers are required to read and sign an agreement, detailing the payday advance terms, before it is processed.  The national payday advance trade associations best practices also include informing customers about the intended, short-term use of payday advances.  It has even gone to bat for legislation that limits the number of payday advances or renewal rollovers and warns customers about long term use.  </span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#336600">Critics say:</span>**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#336600"> </span>**<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Payday advance businesses are loan sharks who prey on vulnerable communities including low-income and undereducated people.  </span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#3333CC">Research shows:</span>**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366"> </span> **<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Just because customers need help getting through an unplanned expense doesnt mean that they are poverty stricken or ignorant.  Most customers are middle-class, solid citizens, with jobs and income between \$25,000 and \$50,000 per year.  56 percent are college-educated.  </span>**

**<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#003366">Payday advances are also a valuable service for consumers who are just starting out.  Often, these are the people who need the most help but whose borrowing options are limited because they havent developed a credit history yet.  Payday advances dont require a credit check and help young customers (68 percent are under 45 years old) with young families (64 percent have children in the household) get access to money that, in some cases, will keep the lights on and put food on the table.</span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#336600">Critics say:</span>**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366"> </span>**<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">Payday advances are predatory and providers should be put out of business: </span>**

**<span style="font-size:13.5pt;font-family:
Verdana;color:#3333CC">Research shows:</span>**<span style="font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366"> </span> **<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:Verdana;
color:#003366">The payday advance industry is a great example of free markets and consumer choice at work.  The products popularity increased because banks backed out of the market for short-term, small denomination loans.  In 1999, there was a 42 percent increase in payday advance locations, a 71 percent increase in customers and a 104 percent increase in employee payroll.  92 percent of customers agree that these advances are a useful service.  </span>**
