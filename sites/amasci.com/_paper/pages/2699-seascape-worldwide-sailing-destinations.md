<p><strong>The Mediterranean</strong></p>
<p>Our Greek island cruising grounds are the Dodecanese ("Twelve Islands") in the eastern Aegean. "Home" for us is a small island called Leros, where we and our yachts live for most of the year. We sail in these wonderful, little-known islands from mid April to end of October every summer.</p>
<p>The aptly-named "Turquoise Coast" of Turkey is our other favourite sailing spot. The best times to sail here are early and late in the season; it warms up beautifully as early as April in Turkey, and stays lovely until early November.</p>
<p>Our extremely popular Greece-AND-Turkey "combo" trips are designed to take advantage of the best sailing times for both countries; these run during May, June &amp; early July; then again in Sept/Oct.</p>
<p> </p>
<p> <strong>Other worldwide destinations: </strong></p>
<p>Every winter, we choose a new location to explore: Thailand, Malaysia, Tonga, the Seychelles, Australia (Sydney and the Whitsundays) and New Zealand have seen our intrepid SeaScape Sail groups in recent years!</p>
<p>Thailand has become such a favourite that we do at least one trip here every winter. Then we choose one other new location every year...</p>
