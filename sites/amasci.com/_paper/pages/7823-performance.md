# Performance

*Source page: `~savoy/perform.htm`*

<img src="images/ssclogo.gif" width="401" height="150" alt="Savoy Swing Club Logo" />

The Savoy Swing Club features two performing groups:

- The <a href="performa.htm" target="Viewframe">Performance Troupe</a> tours the Pacific Northwest performing and teaching Lindy Hop, 1930's jazz, and other Swing Era dances popular in the 1920s, 1930s and 1940s.

- <a href="http://www.emeraldcityswing.com/f_home.html" target="Viewframe">Emerald City Swing</a> offers participative demonstrations and performances incorporating various Swing forms into their routines, including Lindy Hop, Shag, Balboa, LA Style, and West Coast Swing.

<a href="mainpage.htm#Contactinfo" target="Viewframe">Contact us</a> for information on both of our performing groups, or to request a booking.
