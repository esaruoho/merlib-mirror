Magna - Graver\
The Magna - Graver Model  is designed for easy one-handed engraving operation with precise control at all times. Engineered for the every day hand engraver or users in mass production of engraved items. The Magna-Graver will enable   anyone to chisel clean, bright, straight and curved lines in steel or other  metals with ease, accuracy and speed.**\**
