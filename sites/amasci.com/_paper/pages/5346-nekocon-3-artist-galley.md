<td data-valign="TOP" width="330"><h2 id="pardon-the-mess-page-is-still-under-development">Pardon the mess, Page is still under development</h2>
When Neko-Con was founded 1997 one of our guest, Scott Frazier, asked if he could design our mascot. Like we were going to say no?
<p>Since then a regular flow of art work and images has poured into our email and post office box. Some of the artwork is from the guests of Neko-Con. But a majority of the original art work that we have received comes from the attendees of Neko-Con, and fans of our mascot Reiko-chan. And so we have dedicated a section of our webite to the art of Reiko-chan.</p>
<p>To keep the loading speed of this page down, we refrain from using thumbnail images. Sections are setup by individual artists  Some have contributed a little, some a lot. We've also listed thier websites so you can see what other work these talentes individuals have created..  If you see your name and art listed, but not your web page. Let us know and we'll fix it ASAP.<br />
</p>

\
If you wish to submit art work here are some basic guidelines you should follow.

Hand Drawn\
1. If you send us original art. Make a copy of it.

2\. Mail your art in large enevlopes. Don't fold it. place it between backing boards in the envelope.  It will deter the evil postal workers from folding it ruining it.

3\. Electronic Images are best sent to us in 72dpi, 75dpi or 150dpi. JPEG should be saved in the best quality.
