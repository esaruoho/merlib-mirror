**The bottom line - what it will cost.**

> Total cost for the 2wks is is \$1950 USD per person

Our philosophy is very simple; our costs are as inclusive as we can sensibly make them. We figure most people would prefer to know ahead of time what the *total* vacation cost is; not have to worry about little additional surprises along the way!

On all our Adventure Sailing trips, once you get on the boat, **everything on board is covered** . We provide breakfasts, lunches, drinks (yes, that includes open bar - local liquors, beer, cocktails, etc). Sometimes, this will include dinner too on board too - or perhaps a "beach barbie" !

In Burma, *all* meals and drinks are included on board - being a National Park, there is no "development" on shore - at all! Hence the wonderful "extra" on our Thailand & Burma trips is **our very own Thai chef(s) !!** Yes, we get that superb, fresh, taste-bud-tingling food on the boat too, not just on shore!

Our yachts for this trip are fully equipped with dive compressor and other dive gear, along with qualified scuba instructors. There are also 2 \* 2-person **sea kayaks** on each boat.

 

**Our prices include:**

</div>

**They do not include:**

*  - whilst on board the boats:*

Breakfasts

Lunches and/or dinners on board (frequently both)

Transport to and from the boats - airfares, transfers.

Snacks, aperitifs, fruit,

Personal & medical insurance

Evening cocktail nibblies, etc

Meals and drinks on land

Open bar (local beers, liquors, etc)

Marine park entrance fees; Burmese visa fees

Skipper

Thai chef

 

Fuel, water, mooring fees - all boat running costs

 

Onboard dive equipment, instruction if required

 

(BSAC scuba instructor)

2 sea kayaks on each boat

 

2 nights in the oceanfront "Kata Delight" villas

 

1 night at the Jansom Thara Spa resort, Ranong

 

<span class="small">Pls note: our "adventure sailing" trips officially cover lunches OR dinners on the boat. However, in Burma, there are many days when we have all three meals on board, when we are sailing in the more remote spots. There is no extra charge for this; but we do ask - in the usual spirit of our Adventure Sailing trips! - that everyone pitches in and help our very hard-working chef out at times!</span>

***In short: If it's on the boat - it's covered.\
If it's on the land - it's up to you !***
