<td data-summary="electrostatic books" width="800" data-valign="top"><h1 id="various-electrostatics-books-w.-beaty-2005">VARIOUS ELECTROSTATICS BOOKS<br />
W. Beaty 2005</h1>
<blockquote>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/Electrostatic-Motors-History-Principles-Operation/dp/1935023470/sciencehobbyist" rel="nofollow">ELECTROSTATIC MOTORS</a>: Their History, Types, and Principles of Operation, by Dr. Oleg Jefimenko, 2011. Incredibly extensive collection of e-motors from modern versions back to the Franklin era. Finally reprinted! Yay!<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ISBN=1885540043/sciencehobbyist/" rel="nofollow">ELECTROSTATICS</a>, by A.D. Moore<br />
By the grand old man of electrostatics. Great book, has the Di-Rod generator construction project and lots of other projects. Unavailable for decades, it was FINALLY reprinted recently. Also see <a href="http://www.electrostatic.com/bookstore/books/admoore.htm" rel="nofollow">AD Moore VHS Video</a><br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.powells.com/biblio?partner_id=29205&amp;cgi=product&amp;isbn=1559180900" rel="nofollow">Building Ligntning-bolt Generators</a> by Walt Noon, <a href="http://www.lindsaybks.com/bks/noon/index.html" rel="nofollow">Lindsay publications</a><br />
Plans for building VandeGraaff and other generators<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ISBN=0070215286/sciencehobbyist/" rel="nofollow">HOMEMADE LIGHTNING</a> by R.A. Ford<br />
Contains plans for a high-power Wimshurst style generator, and lots of things to use it for.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ISBN=0071568255/sciencehobbyist/" rel="nofollow">NATURE'S ELECTRICITY</a>, by Charles K. Adams, 1986, McGraw-Hill<br />
A beginners' guide to static electricity, piezoelectricity, and atmospheric electricity, with over 20 simple learn-by-doing experiments. ppbk, 160 pages<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ISBN=0917406028/sciencehobbyist/" rel="nofollow">ELECTROSTATIC MOTORS</a> Their history, types, and principles of operation,<br />
by Dr. Oleg <a href="http://www.as.wvu.edu/coll03/phys/www/jefimenk.htm" rel="nofollow">Jefimenko</a>, 1973. This is a must-have book for anyone interested in the subject, written by "Mr. Electrostatic Motor" himself. pp120, 10 chapters, 68 photos &amp; illustrations. Impossible to find.<br />
</p>
<p>Article: <a href="http://www.rexresearch.com/jefimenko/jefimenko.htm" rel="nofollow">ELECTROSTATIC MOTORS YOU CAN BUILD</a> (by Dr. <a href="http://www.as.wvu.edu/coll03/phys/www/jefimenk.htm" rel="nofollow">Jefimenko</a>)<br />
Popular Science magazine, April 1971, May 1971<br />
Dr. Jefimenko gives plans for various plexiglas and foil motors. He runs them from the clear-weather sky voltage, using a long wire and a balloon! Order these magazine backissues through the interlibrary loan program at your public library.<br />
</p>
<p>Article: <a href="http://www.rexresearch.com/elstatix/esgenmot.htm#sciam" rel="nofollow">ELECTROSTATIC MOTORS</a><br />
Scientific American Magazine, THE AMATEUR SCIENTIST,<br />
October 1974 (obtain back issues via interlibrary loan)<br />
<a href="http://amasci.com/amateur/sciamdx.html">Sci. Am Index of THE AMATEUR SCIENTIST</a><br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/0672210231/sciencehobbyist/" rel="nofollow">ELECTROSTATICS HANDBOOK</a>, by Charles Green<br />
Howard W. Sams Co., 1973, #21023<br />
ISBN: 0-672-21023-1<br />
Numerous static electric build-it projects, including Kelvin Thunderstorm, VandeGraaff, e-motors.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ISBN=047102984X/sciencehobbyist/" rel="nofollow">THE FLYING CIRCUS OF PHYSICS WITH ANSWERS</a>, by Jearl Walker, 1977,<br />
ISBN:0-471-02984-X<br />
Sections 6.7 through 6.18 give a fairly short Q&amp;A about static electricity, but a long, excellent bibliography of scientific papers is provided for further research.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.bakkenmuseum.org/store.html" rel="nofollow">Sparks and Shocks: Experiments from the Golden Age of Static Electricity</a><br />
The Bakken Library and Museum. Dubuque, IA 100pp 1995<br />
<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ISBN=048625237X/sciencehobbyist/" rel="nofollow">ALL ABOUT LIGHTNING</a>, by Dr. Martin Uman<br />
Excellent Question/Answer book by a professional lightning researcher.<br />
Dispels myths, answers all the common questions.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://www.bakkenmuseum.org/store.html" rel="nofollow">THE RAMSDEN MACHINE: MAKE AN ELECTROSTATIC GENERATOR</a><br />
The Bakken, 1993 pp16 $0.95<br />
</p>
<p>Contact and Frictional Electrification<br />
W. R. Harper, Oxford University press (1967)<br />
</p>
<p>STATIC ELECTRICITY, by J. H. Pepper<br />
1889, J. B. Lippincott<br />
Reprints available from <a href="http://www.lindsaybks.com" rel="nofollow">Lindsay Publications</a><br />
</p>
<p>DESCHANEL'S STATIC ELECTRICTY, by A. Privat Deschanel<br />
1884 D. Appleton Co.<br />
Reprints available from <a href="http://www.lindsaybks.com" rel="nofollow">Lindsay Publications</a><br />
</p>
</blockquote>
