# Morning Prayer before 1662.

*Source page: `~lhowell/bcp1662/notes/development/morning.html`*

## Morning Prayer before 1662.

**1549**

Open at the [Lord's Prayer](../../daily/morning.html#OurFather) and continue to ["O Lord, open thou..."](../../daily/morning.html#OLordOpen) substituting "my" for "our" and "me" for "us".

Continue from ["Glory be to the Father..."](../../daily/morning.html#Gloria1) to ["Praise ye the lord"](../../daily/morning.html#PraiseYe); from Easter through Trinity Sunday only, add *"Alleluia"*.

Continue with *[Venite, Exultemus Domine](../../daily/morning.html#Venite)* and go through *[Te Deum](../../daily/morning.html#TeDeum)*; in Lent only, substitute *[Benedicite omnia Opera](../../daily/morning.html#Benedicite)*.

Continue from the [Second Lesson](../../daily/morning.html#Lesson2) through *[Benedictus](../../daily/morning.html#Benedictus)*.

Insert "Lord have mercy on us. *Christ have mercy on us.* Lord have mercy on us."

Recite the [Apostle's Creed](../../daily/morning.html#ApostlesCreed); On Christmas-day, the Epiphany, Easter, Ascension, Pentecost, Trinity Sunday only, substitute *[Quicunque Vult](../../daily/athanasian.html)*.

Continue from the [Lord's Prayer](../../daily/morning.html#OurFather2) through the [Collect for Grace](../../daily/morning.html#CollectGrace).

**1552**

Open at [Morning Prayer](../../daily/morning.html) and continue to ["Praise ye the lord"](../../daily/morning.html#PraiseYe).

Continue with *[Venite, Exultemus Domine](../../daily/morning.html#Venite)* and go through *[Benedictus](../../daily/morning.html#Benedictus)* or *[Jubilate Deo](../../daily/morning.html#JubilateDeo)*.

Insert "Lord have mercy on us. *Christ have mercy on us.* Lord have mercy on us."

Continue from the [Apostle's Creed](../../daily/morning.html#ApostlesCreed) through the [Collect for Grace](../../daily/morning.html#CollectGrace).

**1559 and 1604**

Open at [Morning Prayer](../../daily/morning.html) and continue to ["Praise ye the lord"](../../daily/morning.html#PraiseYe).

Continue from *[Venite, Exultemus Domine](../../daily/morning.html#Venite)* through [O Lord, shew thy mercy...*And take not thy holy Spirit from us.*](../../daily/morning.html#OLordShew) In 1559, substitute "Queen" for "King".

Continue from the [Collect of the Day](../../daily/morning.html#CollectDay) through the [Collect for Grace](../../daily/morning.html#CollectGrace).
