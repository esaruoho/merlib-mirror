Minnesota
<a href="http://www.mnhs.org">Minnesota Historical Society</a>
<a href="http://www.mnhs.org/library/search/vrdb/bsearch.html">Minnesota Historical Society (MHS) Photograph Collection Database</a><br />
The Minnesota Historical Society's Photograph Collection Database contains records for 44,000 individual photographs, approximately ten percent accompanied by digital images. The photographs document Minnesotans' lives, landscapes, leisure activities, and occupations from 1850 to the present.
<a href="http://www.stearns-museum.org/">Stearns History Museum</a><br />
The Research Center and Archives contains over 400,000 photographs, maps, newspapers, genealogical files and oral history tapes and specializes in genealogical information for Luxembourg.
Wisconsin
<a href="http://www.uwm.edu/Dept/Library/arch/">Milwaukee Urban Archives</a><br />
The Milwaukee Urban Archives collects records from Milwaukee, Ozaukee, Sheboygan, Washington, and Waukesha counties in Wisconsin.
