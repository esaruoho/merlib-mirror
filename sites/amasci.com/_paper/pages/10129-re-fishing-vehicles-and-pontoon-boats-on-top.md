When I had a buck's bronco pontoon boat (which I liked a lot) I used to carry it inflated on top going to & fro to BC.  You do have to adjust the air pressure when you change elevation & one time I got lazy when it was underinflated & flopping which wore a nice hole in the pontoon. a good cheap way to attach a fishing buddy to a pontoon is to use a pvc  "T" with a skinny strap to hold the T to the pontoon- the fishing buddy will just slide down & be in perfert position- if you don't extend the transducer down, it won't tangle your line while playing fish ( although I realise that's not a concern for Mr. Embry  :)  )  bill h----- Original Message -----
