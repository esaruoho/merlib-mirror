# Big Jim

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Once when I was younger I was hitchhicking around the country. I'd travel awhile until my money ran out and then I'd pick up some odd jobs until I had some money to move on. I hadn't really planned to run out of cash in a little town in west Texas, but it happened. I use the word "town" loosely - it was really just a wide place on a country road where there were a few houses and a tavern.

I went into the tavern looking for work and got lucky. It turned out that the owner was looking for someone to cover because his day bartender had quit and his replacement couldn't start for a week. He had me put my stuff in a back room that had a cot I could sleep on and showed me how to to run the bar. It wasn't hard - there was one beer on tap and the only liquor was whiskey. Then he said something that I thought was strange.

"If you ever hear that Big Jim is coming, jist get the hell out of town as fast as you can." Well, I didn't know who or what Big Jim was, so I just nodded and said I would.

A couple of uneventful days later I was tending bar and someone ran into the front door of the tavern, yelled "Big Jim's a-comin'!" and ran right back out. Well, I'd never seen such a scene in my life! Guys were knocking over chairs and each other to get out the door. Others jumped out the windows, and the poker players at the table in the corner were in such a hurry to get out they left their money on the table! In no time flat I was alone in the tavern.

As I was thinking about what to do I saw a man riding up the road. He was huge, about six foot five and 250 pounds of hard muscle. He was riding a buffalo, whipping it along with a rattlesnake, and wearing a live mountain lion as a coat. He stopped the buffalo in front of the tavern and got off. He yelled "Stay!", punched the buffalo in the forehead with his fist, knocking it down unconscous. He wadded up the mountain lion in a ball, yelled "Stay!" and threw it down. Then he tied the rattlesnake in a knot and tossed it on the ground.

I figured it was too late to run so I tried to hide behind the bar. He strode in, reached over the bar and picked me up like a feather. "I want a drink," he said, then put me down. I pulled a bottle of whiskey off the shelf and, hands shaking, poured some into a glass and set the glass in front of him.

"I said I wanted a drink," he said as he reached for the shelf and grabbed a bottle of whiskey. He broke the neck of the bottle over the edge of the bar, raised it to his lips, and chugged the entire bottle of whiskey. He slammed the bottle down on the bar, wiped his lips on the back of his hand and said, "I want another drink."

Well, I wasn't stupid enough to make the same mistake twice, so I took a bottle of whiskey off the shelf and set it on the bar in front of him. Again he broke the neck on the edge of the bar, chugged its contents, slammed it down and wiped his lips.

I figured I could handle this, so I looked up hopefully at him and said, "Would you like another drink?"

He said, "No, I can't stay. Big Jim's comin'."
