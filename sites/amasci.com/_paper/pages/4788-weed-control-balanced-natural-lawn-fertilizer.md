&#10;<p><strong>Signature Product.</strong> WOW! Plus lets you control crabgrass, dandelions and other pesky weeds while feeding your lawn with a complete, balanced, all-natural fertilizer! It's easy to apply and saves you time and effort -- you can weed and feed your lawn naturally with just one application!<br />
<br />
<br />
     <strong><strong><a href="http://clickserve.cc-dt.com/link/click?lid=41000000003506965&amp;redirect=http://www.gardensalive.com/%0A%0Aitem_display.asp?ProductNumber=8873">Buy now....More...</a></strong></strong></p>
<p>A by-product of corn syrup production, our pre-emergence weed control:</p>
<ul>
<li>reduces germination of dandelions and other broadleaf weeds by as much as 90%</li>
<li>allows kids and pets to play on the lawn right after application (no need to post "Keep Off: Herbicide Warning" signs!)</li>
<li>provides nitrogen and other nutrients grass needs for strong root growth, stress resistance and a beautiful green appearance</li>
<li>releases nutrients gradually, as nature intended.</li>
</ul>
<strong>How WOW! Plus Works</strong>
<p>Soon after sprouting, young weeds send out secondary "feeder" roots that draw nutrients from the soil. But WOW! Plus stops these roots from developing, so seedlings quickly die--usually before you see any green growth! Dandelions, crabgrass, creeping bentgrass, foxtail, lamb's quarters, purslane, redroot pigweed and many others can't survive.</p>
<p>Don't worry about overapplying WOW! Plus. You can use eight times the recommended rate without harming your grass, and you'll find that high doses are even more effective in controlling weeds.</p>
<p>And don't worry about lingering synthetic chemicals! There aren't any! Unlike traditional chemical treatments, WOW! Plus lets your family and friends and pets enjoy your lawn right after application.</p>
<strong>Start Using it This Spring!</strong>
<p>Apply 10-20 lbs. per 1,000 sq. ft. when forsythia bloom--a good indiction that weather is warm enough for weed seeds to germinate. Yearly spring and fall applications at 10lbs. per 1,000 sq. ft. provide adequate weed control and fertilization. Applications at 20lbs. per 1,000 sq. ft. provide even better control, because the percentage of weed control increases with higher application rates.</p>
<p>Water deeply after application. (Do not use WOW! Plus on recently sown grass, because it will affect lawn seed germination, too. Wait until after the first mowing to apply.) WOW! Plus is granulated for use in either drop or broadcast spreaders. All-natural WOW! Plus is not toxic to humans and pets.</p>
