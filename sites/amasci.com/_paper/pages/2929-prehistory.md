He liked the concept but not the public speaking, so he started a blueberry farm and rhododendron nursery. While he was a speaker, he met many farmers in Virginia and North Carolina who had native rhododendrons growing in their pastures. Chewed down by cows, they became very dense. John dug up these rhododendrons by the boxcar load to clear the farmers' fields. and shipped the plants north for sale. Bob joined him on several of these trips, and later continued the practice himself. |
