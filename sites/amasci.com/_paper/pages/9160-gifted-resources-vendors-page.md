## Prufrock Press

Prufrock produces videos and printed material targeted at a Gifted audience. The company's line of products focuses on encouraging the development of the diverse talents of children and adolescents. Materials that encourage creativity, leadership, academic skills, and many other talent areas fill the pages of Prufrock's catalog. The company currently publishes and distributes a line of more than one hundred book and software products, two popular magazines, and a research journal. ## Borenson and Associates

Hands-On Equations is a patented teaching methodology for teachers and parents to present basic algebraic linear equations to students in grades three and up. Using game-like pawns and numbered cubes, students learn to make "legal moves" to keep an equation "balanced" on a "scale" and reduce the quantity of playing pieces to discover the value of "x". Sample problems 2(x+4) + x = 2x + 10 and 2x - 3(-x) + 20 + x.

         Borenson and Associates
