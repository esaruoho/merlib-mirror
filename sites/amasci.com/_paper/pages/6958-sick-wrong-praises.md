    TO:THE POWERS THAT BE                 FROM:VAGRANT RECORDS
                                          5512 UNIVERSITY WY. N.E.
                                          SEATTLE WA. 98105
                                          (206)  525-0628

                                          4/17/95

          DEAR FRIENDS,

        Sick & Wrong is what Rock n' Roll has needed for years. They're
    fun, they have energy, and they are extremely professional. As the
    producer of their latest batch of recordings, I feel they have 
    perfected a sound that is original, yet reminiscent of many contemporary
    styles, which range from punk rock to surf music, with tinges of
    blues and country thrown in to round things out. Their material is 
    all-original with the odd variation on some traditional cover tunes
    to make things a little more exciting once in a while.
        Their name not only sums up the band, but their music as
    well. Their songs are rockin', but catchy, dealing with subjects
    that are slightly warped and deviant, but presenting them in a 
    lighthearted and playful way. On a whole, they like to have fun both
    on stage and in the studio, which is way I wanted to work with them.
        I know Sick & Wrong may not be everyone's cup of tea, but
    they are pushing the boundaries of Rock n' Roll, which is what keeps
    the music fresh and show exciting. In fact, if you don't work with
    them soon, someone else just might get all the glory.

                                        Thanks for your time,

                                  
                                            ERIK 4-A
                                            (OWNER VAGRANT RECORDS)
