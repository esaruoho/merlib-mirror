# Big Jim

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Once I was working as a bartender at a biker bar. It was a pretty easy job - there was only one beer on tap and only one kind of liquor which was whiskey. But the owner had told me one thing that seemed kind of strange. He said that if I ever heard that Big Jim was coming I should just get the hell out of there. Well I didn't know who or what Big Jim was so I just told him that I would do that.

Things went quietly for a while and then one day a guy screeched his bike to a stop in front of the bar, ran in and yelled "Big Jim's coming" and ran back out. Well I never saw a scene like that in my life. Guys were trying to squeeze out the door and jumping out the window. They left their drinks on the bar and the guys playing poker at the table in the corner left their money on the table. They all fired up their bikes and rode away squealing their tires. In less than a minute I was alone in the bar, wondering what to do.

Then I heard a single Harley coming up the road. Looking out the window I could see the rider. He was a huge guy, 6 ft. 5, at least 260 lbs. of solid muscle. It was too late to run so I just watched as he pulled his bike up in front of the bar and walked in the door. He came straight up to me at the bar and said "I want a drink". I figured he didn't want a beer, so I pulled a bottle of whiskey off the shelf and poured a glass with a shaking hand. He looked me is if I was stupid and said "I said I wanted a drink." He reached behind the bar, grabbed a bottle of whiskey, smashed the neck of the bottle off against the edge of the bar, put the bottle to his mouth and chugged the contents. He slammed the empty bottle on the bar and said "I want another drink." Well I'm not stupid enough to make the same mistake twice, so I took another bottle of whiskey off the shelf and put it in front of him. Again he smashed the neck of the bottle against the edge of the bar and chugged it. As I watched him put the bottle down I figured that I could handle this so I said to him, "Would you like another drink, sir?"

He said, "No I can't stay, Big Jim's coming."
