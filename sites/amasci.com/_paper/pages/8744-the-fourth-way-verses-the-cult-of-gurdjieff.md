Now, the obvious question has to be, "what in the system gives you all this?" It is many things, here is a list of some of them:

1\) The idea of self-remembering as a way to still the mind and draw attention to the unconscious manifestations of the body.

2\) The understanding and eventual ability to break my manifestations down into several parts, intellectual, emotional, moving, etc. which aren't mystical and unnatural such as in theosophy and many others systems of thought, and to observe them in case they become out of control or distasteful.

3\) The ability to see the world as not a gentle place, but on in which all my desires are met with an equal and contrary force, and the need for a third force to reconcile this and impress what is best for me.

4\) The need to see the possibility of a higher understanding for me, and the limitation of it leading towards a concept even of the materiality of knowledge, and that this knowledge and understanding it is not something that everyone has, nor do I always posses it, therefore I often do not need to make it known to anyone but myself or someone who is sincerely searching for it.

5\) The idea of a higher blood chemistry which is improved by practicing the two conscious shocks.

6\) The observing of body tension and releasing it as a way to increase my energy to achieve my aim.

7\) The idea of will and what is not will, not will only being a manifestation of all my desires not my deeper essence wish, and how the law of 7 and 3 influences this.
