# VandeGraaff Machine: Hardware

Where to find shafts, bearings, etc? Try these mail-order companies:\

- [Small Parts, Inc.](http://www.smallparts.com)

Me, I started building a VDG using wheels from old roller skates from a garage sale, and long "threaded rod" from a big hardware store.

### MOTOR

Electric motors can be had from a variety of sources. Here are a few mail-order surplus catalog companies:

- [Herbach & Rademan http://www.herbach.com](http://www.herbach.com)
- [C & H Sales http://www.aaaim.com/CandH/](http://www.aaaim.com/CandH/)
- [Marlin P. Jones http://www.mpja.com](http://www.mpja.com)
