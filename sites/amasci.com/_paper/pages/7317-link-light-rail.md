<td data-valign="TOP" width="73%">Sound Transit is now starting construction on a 14-mile light rail line, called “Central Link light rail” That would extend from Downtown Seattle through the Rainier Valley neighborhood into Tukwila.<br />
<br />
But in today's environment light rail is just not the best transportation alternative. It is too slow over longer distances, it is too damaging to neighborhoods, it is not safe enough, and it costs too much.<br />
<br />
I strongly believe that monorail is superior to light rail because it is safer, faster, quieter, less disruptive to neighborhoods, avoids surface traffic, will have greater ridership, will have more impact on reducing highway traffic, and costs less than light rail.<br />
<br />
If the funding allocated to build Link light rail from Downtown Seattle to SeaTac Airport is instead applied to monorail construction over a similar route, the route can not only also serve Southcenter Mall, but can most likely extend a considerable distance beyond SeaTac Airport into Federal Way or beyond.<br />
<br />
Additionally, monorail will avoid major problems in converting the Downtown Seattle bus tunnel from the current bus-only operation to a mix of busses and light rail.<br />
<br />
To see my proposal to build monorail instead of Link light rail, <a href="../monorail/route3.html">Click here</a>.<br />
