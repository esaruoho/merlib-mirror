<h2 id="our-mission">Our Mission</h2>
<p>To provide a low-cost solution to Information Systems management using the talents of hand picked industry experts.</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="company-profile">Company Profile</h2>
<p>In over 20 years of working with computers, you meet a lot of people. A rare few of them excel at what they do because they love their work.</p>
<p>Technical Voodoo was founded to take advantage of those with specialized skills a passion for technology to provide low cost Information Systems solutions custom tailored to your needs.</p>
<p>Technical Voodoo does not pay for large office space and expensive furniture because that's not what <u>you</u> want to pay for. Our people are always our greatest asset, that's why most of the money you pay goes straight to them and not to overhead. This helps to keep both our customers and our employees happy, and everyone knows that happy employees do the best work.</p>
