<strong>In Brief</strong>
</div>
<strong>S</strong>teve Bryant is an Accredited Business Communicator and is Chief Creative Officer for Public Relations with <strong>Publicis Dialog</strong> in Seattle, one of the nation’s top twenty firms.
<p>Steve also works professionally as a classical singer and pianist. A high countertenor, he has made a specialty of music written for the castrati. The castrati were adult male Italian singers, principally of the 17<sup>th</sup> and 18<sup>th</sup> centuries, who retained their soprano and alto voices after being castrated before the onset of puberty.</p>
<p>Steve was music director for <a href="sweet.html"><strong>Sweet Corn Productions</strong></a> from 1981-2002.</p>
<p>He was co-founder and co-director of <a href="toc-cont.html"><strong>Partners Task Force for Gay &amp; Lesbian Couples</strong></a> along with <a href="demian.html">Demian</a>, from 1981-2002.</p>
<p>His other interests include four-hand piano playing, organic gardening, vegetarian cooking and the arts in general.</p></td>
