# Light Switch Metaphor

*Source page: `~wayneld/switch.html`*

## Light Switch Metaphor ! <img src="icons/bonzai5.gif" data-align="middle" />

------------------------------------------------------------------------

    A Course In Miracles teaches that God did not create the world
    and that we give everything in the world all the meaning it
    has for us. I like to think of the things in the world that "bring"
    us "pleasure" as switches that can put us in touch with the Love
    in our own mind.

    To visualize this, think of electricity and how we accept that a
    flip of the switch brings us light in a room. We don't attribute
    any special power to the switch, it is just a tool for bringing
    light. If the switch breaks, we simply replace the switch and don't
    mourn over it's passing.

    Now consider a 200 year old tree at the end of a nice mile walk
    through the woods. We "know" that we can always go there and forget
    all those pressing troubles that seem to confront us at times. But
    what happens if the tree gets sick and dies? Do we see it as a
    switch that will be replaced or do we feel a deep sense of loss.

    If we see it as a deep loss, we know we have seen the tree through
    the eyes of the ego. The ego has told us, and we bought into the
    belief (consciously at some point), that trees are beautiful and
    have the power to bring us "peace and joy". 

    When seen through the eyes of the Holy Spirit, the tree has been a 
    wonderful switch that we can flip and put us in touch with the Light 
    in our own minds. The tree as seen through the eyes of the HS, 
    reflects back to us that Love that is always there in our minds, but 
    often clouded over by the ego voice that tells us the "specialness" 
    is the True Source of the "joy and peace" in our experience.

    And, guess what....all that happens by seeing things through the eyes 
    of HS, is that we are happy more of the time and conversely, there is 
    just less pain, anxiety and depression. We still enjoy trees, 
    sunsets, etc, (all forms of "specialness") they just don't have the 
    power we had given them before. 

    Have we been bad and should we feel guilt at having seen the tree
    as bringing us "peace", no....only mistaken!  And mistakes beg
    for correction as being "bad" begs for punishment. The tree dying and
    the sense of loss, then can be a wonderful opportunity to stop, own
    our mistake and ask Holy Spirit to help us correct our perception.

    And bingo.....another block to the awareness of Love's presence is
    gone!!!

    Peace!   wayne near seattle :)
