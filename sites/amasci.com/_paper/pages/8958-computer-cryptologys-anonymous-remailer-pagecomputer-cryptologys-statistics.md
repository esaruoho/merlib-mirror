## Computer Cryptology's Anonymous Remailer Page

The information below provides keys and statistics on the anonymous remailer network. The primary location for discussion of anonymous remailers is the Usenet newsgroup [alt.privacy.anon-server](news:alt.privacy.anon-server) (APAS). For an introduction to this newsgroup, see the weekly [welcome message](http://www.eskimo.com/~turing/remailer/FAQ/ptr.1.html) and the monthly list of frequently asked questions ([FAQ](http://www.eskimo.com/~turing/remailer/FAQ/)).
