<p>This highly concentrated grapeseed base massage oil may be used as a foot massage oil or “<strong>spot massage oil</strong>”. Try on the back of the neck. Use as a footbath to ease hot and tired “marathon” feet. Add 15 drops to a basin of warm water. Swirl the water to disperse the blended oils and relax for at least 15 minutes.</p>
