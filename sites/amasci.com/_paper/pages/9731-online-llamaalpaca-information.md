It is requested that you fill out a questionnaire, after which your subscription application will be processed.</td>

</tr>

<td><strong><em>ALSA Web Page.</em></strong> <a href="http://www.llama.org/alsa">http://www.llama.org/alsa</a> This site includes information about upcoming Alpaca &amp; Llama Show Association sanctioned shows, how to have a show sanctioned, a list of ALSA Regional Championship shows, list of award winning llamas, a history of ALSA, a schedule of judging clinics and an ALSA membership application form.</td>
</tr>

To subscribe to the Listserv, send mail to <a href="mailto:LISTSERV@HOME.EASE.LSOFT.COM">LISTSERV@HOME.EASE.LSOFT.COM</a> (no subject line). In the body of the message, type: <strong>SUB CAMELID your real name (example: SUB CAMELID John Doe) We are running in a basic modE</strong>, with a few minor enhancements. All mail addressed to the LIST should be sent to: <a href="mailto:camelid@home.ease.lsoft.com">CAMELID@HOME.EASE.LSOFT.COM</a> (caps not necessary) Mail messages are limited to 500 lines. There are a couple of generic help files available. Send an email message to <a href="mailto:listserv@home.ease.lsoft.com">listserv@home.ease.lsoft.com</a> with the following commands in the body of the message: <strong>INFO GENINTRO</strong> (general information about the listserv) INFO REFCARD to get these files mailed back to you. The annual subscription price is $3.50. Please mail to: Lledge Rock Llamas, Mail list, 4946 Consaul Road, Amsterdam NY 12010. This list is administered by Jay S. Curtis (<a href="mailto:USCurtis@curtislumber.com">JSCurtis@curtislumber.com</a>).</td>

<tr>
<td><strong><em>LlamaLedge</em></strong> <a href="http://members.aol.com/LamaLedge1/LLF1/llf.999.html">http://members.aol.com/LamaLedge1/LLF1/llf.999.html</a> Sharon DaDalt, owner of Llama L'edge Farm in Auburn, NH has established this Web site that has a unique Medical Index of citations to articles from popular lama publications such as Llamas Magazine, Llama Banner, Llama Life, the GALA Newsletter, etc. Over 100 topics of interest on lama health and medicine, as well as herd management, are included. These journals are not indexed by any other database. This site is updated regularly.</td>
