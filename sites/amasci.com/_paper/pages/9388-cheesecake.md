## Easy Swirl Cheesecake

Oven: 350 degrees

Cook for: 40-45 minutes

Chill overnight

#### Crust:

- 1 and 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs

<!-- -->

- 6 tbs. butter or margarine, melted

<!-- -->

- 1 tbs. sugar

<!-- -->

- 1 tsp. cinnamon (optional)

Combine crumbs, butter or margarine, sugar, and cinnamon in bowl. Pat onto bottom of ungreased 9" springform cake pan. Refrigerate.

#### Basic Cake:

- 4 packages cream cheese (8 oz. each), softened

<!-- -->

- 4 eggs

<!-- -->

- 1 and 1/4 cups sugar

<!-- -->

- 2 tsp. vanilla

<!-- -->

- Swirl ingredient (see below)

Beat cream cheese until smooth. Add sugar, eggs, vanilla. Blend well. Scoop 1 cup of mixture into smaller bowl and set aside. Pour remainder into cake pan.  Add swirl ingredient to remaining 1 cup of cheesecake mixture, blend. Spoon resulting batter in dollops on top of cheesecake in pan. Use wooden spoon handle or spatula to swirl together (marbelizing, not completely mixing).

#### Swirl Suggestions:

- Brownie mix (1 box with oil and egg as per directions)

<!-- -->

- Pie filling (lemon is my personal favorite)

<!-- -->

- Cookie dough (peanut butter or chocolate chip)

<!-- -->

- Caramel topping

<!-- -->

- Whatever else sounds good (let me know what you try!)
