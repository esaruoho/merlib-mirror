## OUT ON THE NET:

- 
- [QRD's list of l/g/b/t/q listservs](http://www.qrd.org/qrd/electronic/email/): subscribe today!
- [QueerNet: Home Page](http://www.queernet.org): provides dozens of mailing lists for the gay/lesbian/bisexual/transgendered communities covering a wide variety of interests (queer Asian-Pacific-Americans \* News and discussion for "bears" and their admirers \* Cigar Studs members & Hot Ash news \* Canadian Gay/Lesbian/Bisexual/etc. news and discussion \* Radical Faeries \* lesbians in academia \* News and discussion for Law Enforcement Gays And Lesbians \* Gay/lesbian/bisexual/transgendered Arabs or Middle-Easterns \* and much more!
- Search the web for [l/g/b/t/q USENET groups](http://www.rainbowquery.com/categories/Usenet_Newsgroups.html): a wide variety of newsgroups of queer interest exist
- [PlanetOut](http://www.planetout.com): "the first global online service geared to the social, educational, professional, political and leisure interests of the g/l/b/t communities"
- DQNW: Digital Queers NorthWest, serving the cyberneeds of the gay community
- [Home pages of People OUT on the Net](http://www.infoqueer.org/queer/qis/homepages.html) all over the world, including Aussie [Aragorn's Home Page](http://avoca.vicnet.net.au/~aragorn/welcom.htm)
- [Hein's Ulitmate Gay Links](http://www.xs4all.nl/~heinv/heindoc/gayhttp1.html)
- [M. J. O'Neill's Web Page](http://QueerLinks.com/links.asp): an amazing compilation of links from A to Z; also a great library of articles and poetry on all sorts of queer topics
- The most amazing non-gay gay-friendly page I've ever seen-- read [Jim Bilbrey](http://www.geocities.com/WestHollywood/4254/care.htm)'s page and let him know how much you appreciate it!
- [Rants by Larry-bob](http://www.io.com/~larrybob/), publisher of Holy Titclamps queerzine (search for "rants")
