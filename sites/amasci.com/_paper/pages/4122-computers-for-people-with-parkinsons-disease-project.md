Some of you may be familiar with Parkinson's Disease, many may not be. In brief, Parkinson's Disease (PD) is a mobility disorder that causes slowness of movement, loss of coordination, tremors, stiffness, and a variety of other symptoms including depression.

For many, the depression associated with PD is one of the most troublesome symptoms. The depression frequently leads to the Person With Parkinson's (PWP) isolating him or her self (depression leads to lack of motivation leads to staying home and hermitizing.) This gave me the idea of getting PWP's online! In addition to the useful information that can be obtained, there is a \#Parkinsons chat channel :-). It's a great place to bounce ideas off of people with the same disorder, and it's generally a fun group (if you're wondering, yes, I have PD, young onset case.)

In order to get a PWP online, they have to have a computer. As you can imagine, most people are not able to lay out anywhere from \$1,000 to \$3,000 just to try some internet thing, especially when their disorder has probably led them to a severely reduced income! This is where my plan comes in.

I would like to collect donated (probably older) computers, get them all set up and ready to be used on the internet, then loan (give) them to PWP's on an indefinite basis. They would not be expected to return the machine until they purchased their own (no pressure to do so.) If they bought a new one, they would be expected to return the "loaner" to me at which point I would wipe it clean, set it up fresh and "loan" it to the next PWP in line to get on line.\

# Please Donate Any Computers Or Parts You Can Spare!

\

I will happily accept whole computers, partial computers, parts, anything that works. Send me anything you can spare :-). If you're in a real generous mood and have the capacity to do so, buy a new system (most of the people who will get these systems won't be able to afford anything better for a long time if ever.) Have a spare SIMM, a small working hard drive, a good motherboard, even a case? Send it along :-). I have not yet obtained charitable status, so this stuff is not tax deductable at this point, but if you include contact information I will, assuming it can be done legally, consider that your donation didn't fully occur until I obtain not-for-profit status or until I fail to do so. Either way, it's a good cause, and what were you going to do with that old dinosaur of a computer anyways?

You probably want to [e-mail me](mailto:parkproj@cooltime.com) before you drop this thing in the mail (you don't have to, the address and contact information here will be kept current.)\
