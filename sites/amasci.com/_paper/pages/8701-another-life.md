# Another Life

- [Seattle Haiku](#poem1)

<span id="poem1"></span>

------------------------------------------------------------------------

       Storm in Seattle
     Whitecaps on the reservoir
       Cars shake off the rain.

                     Goth with umbrella
                   Waiting for her bus to come
                     It is late again.

--- --- --- --- ---

By Eric and Laura Gjovaag
