# Proverbs on money

*Source page: `~bradkir/bible/home/subjects/money.html`*

Proverbs...on money:

    Pr.10:4 

    Lazy hands make a man poor, but diligent hands bring wealth. (NIV)

    Pr.10:15 

    The wealth of the rich is their fortified city, but poverty is the ruin
    of the poor. (NIV)

    Pr.10:22 

    The blessing of the LORD brings wealth, and he adds no trouble to it. (NIV)

    Pr.11:4 

    Wealth is worthless in the day of wrath, but righteousness delivers from
    death. (NIV)

    Pr.11:16 

    A kindhearted woman gains respect, but ruthless men gain only wealth. (NIV)

    Pr.11:28 

    Whoever trusts in his riches will fall, but the righteous will thrive like
    a green leaf. (NIV)

    Pr.13:8 

    A man's riches may ransom his life, but a poor man hears no threat. (NIV)

    Pr.13:18 

    He who ignores discipline comes to poverty and shame, but whoever heeds
    correction is honored. (NIV)

    Pr.13:22 

    A good man leaves an inheritance for his children's children, but a sinner's
    wealth is stored up for the righteous. (NIV)

    Pr.13:23 

    A poor man's field may produce abundant food, but injustice sweeps it away.
    (NIV)

    Pr.13:25 

    The righteous eat to their hearts' content, but the stomach of the wicked
    goes hungry. (NIV)

    Pr.14:20 

    The poor are shunned even by their neighbors, but the rich have many friends.
    (NIV)

    Pr.14:23 

    All hard work brings a profit, but mere talk leads only to poverty. (NIV)

    Pr.14:31 

    He who oppresses the poor shows contempt for their Maker, but whoever is
    kind to the needy honors God. (NIV)

    Pr.15:16 

    Better a little with the fear of the LORD than great wealth with turmoil.
    (NIV)

    Pr.18:23 

    A poor man pleads for mercy, but a rich man answers harshly. (NIV)

    Pr.19:1 

    Better a poor man whose walk is blameless than a fool whose lips are perverse.
    (NIV)

    Pr.19:4 

    Wealth brings many friends, but a poor man's friend deserts him. (NIV)

    Pr.19:17 

    He who is kind to the poor lends to the LORD, and he will reward him for
    what he has done. (NIV)

    Pr.20:13 

    Do not love sleep or you will grow poor; stay awake and you will have food
    to spare. (NIV)

    Pr.21:5 

    The plans of the diligent lead to profit as surely as haste leads to poverty.
    (NIV)

    Pr.21:17 

    He who loves pleasure will become poor; whoever loves wine and oil will
    never be rich. (NIV)

    Pr.22:1 

    A good name is more desirable than great riches; to be esteemed is better
    than silver or gold. (NIV)

    Pr.22:2 

    Rich and poor have this in common: The LORD is the Maker of them all. (NIV)

    Pr.22:4 

    Humility and the fear of the LORD bring wealth and honor and life. (NIV)

    Pr.22:7 

    The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender.
    (NIV) 

    Pr.22:9 

    A generous man will himself be blessed, for he shares his food with the
    poor. (NIV)

    Pr.22:16 

    He who oppresses the poor to increase his wealth and he who gives gifts
    to the rich--both come to poverty. (NIV)

    Pr.22:22-23:

    22 Do not exploit the poor because they are poor and do not crush the needy
    in court, 23 for the LORD will take up their case and will plunder those
    der them. (NIV)
