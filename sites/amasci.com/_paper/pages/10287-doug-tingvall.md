# Doug Tingvall

*Source page: `~wow-ray/bios/dougt.htm`*

Doug Tingvall

\
\
<img src="../images/tingvall.jpg" data-border="1" data-align="left" width="96" /> Bass-Baritone **Doug Tingvall** is familiar to Northwest audiences, having appeared in numerous local musical theatre and Gilbert and Sullivan productions. With Bellevue Opera he has appeared as Pooh-Bah in The Mikado, Don Alhambra in The Gondoliers, Pish-Tush in The Mikado, Sergeant Meryll in The Yeomen of the Guard, Marmaduke Poindexter in The Sorcerer, and the Pirate King in The Pirates of Penzance.\
\
With the Seattle Gilbert & Sullivan Society he has been seen as Pish-Tush in The Mikado, and Sergeant Meryll in Yeomen of the Guard. Other appearances have included Pish-Tush in The Mikado with Bremerton Community Theatre, Emile de Becque in South Pacific with Northwest Savoyards, Bill/Mr. Green in Its a Wonderful Life with 5th Avenue Theatre, and in concert with Burien Little Theatre, and West Sound Lyric Theatre Ensemble.\
