Hey, Citizen<br />
© 2003, Demian
</div>
<em>Emperor:</em><br />
        Hey, citizen, heed the wisdom I dispense.<br />
        You’re so lucky to be in my presence.
<p><em>Citizen:</em><br />
        All the Way!<br />
        Wow, it’s the emperor. O.K.!</p>
<p><em>Emperor:</em><br />
        Listen up, I got a job for you to toil.<br />
        My oil interests need protecting on foreign soil.</p>
<p><em>Citizen:</em><br />
        What say?<br />
        Will not solar and wind power play?</p>
<p><em>Emperor:</em><br />
        Be a good little grunt and pack your duffel.<br />
        It’s your patriotic duty, get goin’, shuffle.</p>
<p><em>Citizen:</em><br />
        To go away?<br />
        Leave home today?</p>
<p><em>Emperor:</em><br />
        It won’t take long, and you get a decoration.<br />
        I can use all the soldiers from ’round the nation.</p>
<p><em>Citizen:</em><br />
        Unless gay.<br />
        Or sick, or old and gray.</p>
<p><em>Emperor:</em><br />
        If you’re not with me, you must be terrorism incarnate.<br />
        Don’t hesitate, I <em>am</em> your dully-elected head of state.</p>
<p><em>Citizen:</em><br />
        No way, it was a high court power play.<br />
        Emperor, you have feet of clay.</p>
<p><em>Emperor:</em><br />
        You’re like all others who sympathize with the foe.<br />
        First in my computers, then to my prisons you will go.</p>
<p><em>Citizen:</em><br />
        Have you tried alternatives, like real diplomacy, say?<br />
        Or would you toss the constitution and bill of rights away?</p>
<p><em>Emperor:</em><br />
        No matter how much peace mongering you contrive.<br />
        Citizen, now <em>you</em> are the wanted, dead or alive.</p></td>
