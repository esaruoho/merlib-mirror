### Synergies <span id="SYN"></span>

Because the individual oils used in a synergistic blend actually enhance qualities in each other, Bassett Synergies are more powerful and potent than a single oil used by itself. We offer 15 original synergies in 1/2 oz. blue glass bottles. All synergies contain therapeutic-grade essential oils and NO carrier or base oils.
