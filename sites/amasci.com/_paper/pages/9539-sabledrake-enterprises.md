<td>Sabledrake Enterprises was originally founded to help advertise, distribute and sell Christine Morgan's first book, <em>Curse of the Shadow Beasts</em>, and it takes its name from one of the characters of that book.  Tim, however, had been considering forming a business for some time, in order to sell older, out of print role playing games.  So Sabledrake took on both of these roles, and has since expanded into other areas, including all kinds of role playing games, board games, and war games, as well as Gargoyles merchandise. 
Below you will find links to pages that list all of the items we have for sale.  Take a look and if you see anything you like, read the ordering information below. <br />

<td><h3 id="ordering-information">Ordering Information</h3>
The information available on the above pages is rarely 100% up to date.  While it is updated frequently, there will always be some period of time between when orders are placed, and when the item is removed from the list.  Therefore, to avoid any confusion, please e-mail us at <a href="mailto:vecna@eskimo.com">vecna@eskimo.com</a> to confirm availablity.  Please include the item number in your request to be sure there are no misunderstandings.  We will confirm your order and pricing.  Payment must be made by check, money order, or credit cards.  Immediately upon recieving your money order, or upon your check clearing our bank, we will send out you order. 
<p>Orders are normally sent by 1st ClassUS Mail, <em>not</em> insured, and all shipping and handling prices are given assuming this method of shipping within the US.  Other shipping arrangements can be easily made upon request, as well as foreign shipping.  Please allow a day or two for a price quote for any of these alternate modes of shipping.</p></td>
