## Links, Links, Links

\

Ok, here is my way too long and utterly boring list of sites which are far too important in my life for one reason or another.

\

This is where I'll put links from the Portal page. I'll incorporate the really good ones into the main body the next month

\

Near and dear:

- [Wildpixels](http://www.wildpixels.com) ('net home of my friend Ken George, designer, artist and wildman)

Projects current and recent:

- [The Glove](http://www.nantofx.com/glove/) (short film project for which I adapted the screenplay)

- [Neocron](http://www.neocron.com/) (MMORPG which would probably suck out my brain if I gave it half a chance)
