**JING’S BEIJING COOKING\
SCHOOL** 

Day 1 Depart U.S. or your country of residence. 

2 Arrive Beijing – Met by Mr. Jing Zhang and transferred to your hotel.  

3 Beijing – Jing will take you on a city tour to include Tiananmen Square largest\
in the world, the unique Forbidden City and the old neighborhoods known as\
the Hutongs. Special welcoming dinner this evening. BLD 

4 Beijing – Morning Cooking School – Details listed below \*. Afternoon you will\
visit the Market to learn about Chinese vegetables, meats and the many spices\
that make Chinese cooking special. BLD 

5 Beijing – Morning Cooking School – This afternoon after lunch you will go for a\
Private visit to a well known Chinese Artist at his personal Studio to learn about\
Chinese Art and painting styles. Another special dinner this evening featuring a\
different Chinese cooking style. BLD 

6 Beijing – Morning Cooking School – Afternoon you will visit the Lama Temple\
with the 4 story tall Buddha and a visit to Silk Alley known for it’s many clothing\
bargains. The Friendship Store is near by with many Chinese crafts, jewelry, rugs\
and other Chinese products. BLD 

7 Beijing – Morning Cooking School – Afternoon visit to the Temple of Heaven\
and perhaps Antique street before dinner. This evening you will experience the\
Peking Opera performance. BLD 

8 Beijing – Jing will take you to the Great Wall at Badaling with a stop at the Ming\
Tombs on the way back. A side trip to visit the wonderful Cloisonn workshop\
where you will see how they are made. The showroom is the best in China. BLD\
Special Farwell to Beijing party this evening featuring Traditional Chinese Music\
by professional musicians arranged by your host Jing.  

9 Beijing/Xian by afternoon air. You are free this morning before Jing comes to\
take you to lunch. Transfer to the airport for your flight to Xian with Jing as your\
escort. Arrive in Xian and met by a local guide who will transfer you to your\
hotel. Special Traditional Dumpling Dinner this evening. BLD 

10 Xian – You will visit the famous Terra Cotta Warriors site, HuaQing Pools\
once the Summer Palace of the Tang Dynasty and a stop at the Jade Carving\
workshop to see the craftsmen and women making wonderful carvings out of\
Jade. Special Tang Dynasty Show this evening that everyone enjoys after\
dinner. BLD 

11 Xian/Shanghai by afternoon air. In the morning you will visit the old Muslim\
Quarters and visit the Wild Goose Pagoda before lunch and then transfer to the\
airport for your flight to Shanghai. Met in Shanghai by your local guide and\
transferred to your hotel. BLD 

12 Shanghai – Guide will take you to the Yu Garden, finest in China, in the old\
section and a visit to the French Concession area with its old stately mansions.\
A walk on the Bund, river side park, and visit to the Pudong new area completes\
the afternoon before dinner. Special Shanghai Acrobats performance this\
evening. BLD 

13 Shanghai/Suzhou/Shanghai by train today. Transfer to the train station for your\
day trip to this unique city known for its formal gardens and canals. The local\
guide will meet you on the train platform and take you to the Humble\
Administrator’s Garden, second best in China, the Tiger Hill Park, the Silk\
factory, and the Double Sided Embroidery Institute before dinner. Return to\
Shanghai on an evening train. BLD 

14 Shanghai – Free Day today to explore on your own. Jing will give you\
several ideas such as the new Shanghai National Museum, shopping on Nanjing\
Road and other activities. Meet back at the hotel at a specified time for a special\
Farewell Dinner. BD Lunch on your own. 

15 Shanghai/Home You will be transferred to the airport for your flight home.\
B only. 

Included: 4/5 star hotels, all transportation, Professional Cooking School tuition, English\
speaking Guides, meals as indicated, sightseeing as listed, airport taxes, evening\
entertainment as listed, trip preparation information booklet and all transfers. 

Not included: Tips to guides and drivers, visa application (we have the forms and\
instructions) and any expenses of a personal nature or not specifically listed as included. 

\* During the Cooking school you will learn 20 different dishes and see how they are\
prepared from beginning to end earning the secrets of excellent Chinese cooking. You\
will prepare 8 different dishes yourself, two each day, with individual help from the experts\
of the school. This is a hands on learning experience. You will be able to make many\
Chinese dishes knowing the secrets of Chinese spices and methods of Chinese culinary\
excellence.
