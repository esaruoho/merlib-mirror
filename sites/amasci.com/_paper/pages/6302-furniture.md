**Furniture**\
Antique Table and 4 Chairs made before 1912

Mahogany top and Solid maple legs dining room table and 4 chairs. This table has 2 extra leafs and extends out to six feet. Styled by Pheonix. Slide was made by Wisconson Table Slide Co. before 1912.   The owner of this table was a teacher for decades and it was her prized possession.  She kept it in excellent condition.   [View table here](http://www.eskimo.com/~ralphj/table-1.jpg)  The base were made by the Wisconsin Table Slide Company. The company went out of business in 1912.

\$899.00

Hand Carved Tahitian Dry Bar\
A work of a master carver.

1 Solid 2 inch cedar carved Tahiti dry bar with 3 stools and 4 hanging sculptured decorative figures. An elegant work of art sold by  the Witco company of Mt. Vernon Washington no longer in business.  Be sure to see the picture.  [Dry Bar](http://www.eskimo.com/~ralphj/drybar.jpg) Stools have carved male and female figures supporting the seat. The side panel is reversible. 6 feet long.  Arch is about 6 and a half feet tall. Lighted. The male female motif is throughout. This also includes 4 carved figures to hang on the wall nearby. This work of art created in about 1960.

\$1495.00
