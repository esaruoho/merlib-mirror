**Neycraft Centrifugal Casting Machine**

<div align="center">

Professional casting machine for the discriminating artist or the small production shop. Engineered with quality and safety in mind.

</div>

**Exclusive Features -**

\*Cylindrical, metal safety shell protects user from any possible projection of metal, investment, or other debris.

\*Centrifugal force locks investment flasks into recessed steps, insuring security of flasks.

\*Unique, spring-loaded combination winding-locking and release mechanism creates hands-clear operation for user.

\*Positioning counter balance weight on casting arm easily balances machine.

\*No interchangeable spacers or cradles needed when changing flask sizes.

\*Heavy-duty drive shaft, bearing and power spring for long, trouble-free life.

\*Two way mounting base for portable use or permanent installation.

Comes fully equipped with 1 silica crucible (capacity 9 oz. gold, 6.5 oz, 14K gold, 5 oz. sterling silver), 1 pair universal flask tongs, 2 stainless steel investment flasks, 1 two-way mounting base, and 2 flexible sprue bases. Dimensions : 20 w x 20d x 12h. Maximum flask size : 4 x 4.
