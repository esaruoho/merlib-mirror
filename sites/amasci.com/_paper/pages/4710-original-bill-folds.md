# Original Bill Folds

#### Here is a small collection of original or deriviative designs I have developed. Click on the links to load a GIF file of the folding diagrams. Some of the diagrams are in PDF format. To get a free copy of Adobe's Acrobat Reader, click [here.](http://www.adobe.com)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

<img src="3bills.jpg" data-hspace="10" data-align="BOTTOM" width="392" height="300" />**Three Christian Symbols: The Cross, The Heart, and The "Ichthus." To view the GIF folding diagrams: for the the Cross click on [Page 1](cross.gif) (18K) and [Page 2](cross2.gif) (18K), click [here](dolrhart.gif) for the Heart (19K), and click [here](ichthus.gif) for the Ichthus (22K).**

**The Cross requires the bill be folded in thirds the long way. [Here](dolthrds.gif) is a diagram on how to do that (15K).**

<img src="presring.jpg" data-hspace="10" data-align="BOTTOM" width="180" height="170" />**The President Ring - This design owes itself to the bow tie and dime-in-ring models. The only ring I know of that puts the President's face as the signet. Click [here](presring.gif) to view the diagrams in GIF format. (22K)**

<img src="presball.jpg" data-hspace="10" data-align="BOTTOM" width="250" height="233" />**The Presidents Ball - This is the Sonobe cube adapted for a dollar bill. I know it is a cube, but when assembled it struck me more as a ball, hence the name. Fold this model and then show your friends how you can "throw your money around." Click [here](presball.gif) to download the diagrams in GIF format. (19K)**

<img src="billcat.jpg" data-hspace="10" data-align="BOTTOM" width="204" height="150" />**The Arched Cat - This is Toshie Takahama's design for the Arched Cat. I adapted the design for a dollar bill. The original was from a square piece of paper. Click here to view [Page 1](billcat1.gif) (25K) and [Page 2](billcat2.gif) (14K) of the diagrams in GIF format.**

<img src="presdisp.jpg" data-hspace="10" width="161" height="182" />**Presidents on Display -** **A stand-up display of the President. Click [here](prezdisp.pdf) (101K) for diagrams in PDF format. If you do not have Adobe's Acrobat Reader, then follow the link at the top of the page.**
