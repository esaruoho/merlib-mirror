<td data-valign="top" width="580"><h1 id="the-traders-search">The Trader's Search</h1>
by Jr. Harper apprentice <a href="../bartal.html">Bartal</a>
<p><em>(Sung to the tune of "Eurotrash Girl" by Cracker)</em></p>
<hr />
Bartal begins the gitar line, a long series of plodding, moody chords that might suggest traveling. Very slowly. After a repeat of a twelve-bar phrase, Bartal starts to sing, very deliberately,
<p><em>"I went up to Benden Winecraft, loaded up Benden White<br />
Went down to visit Nerat,</em> something <em>broke in my cart.<br />
And I'll search the North over for that girl with green eyes<br />
Yeah, I'll search my route over for that sweet Harper girl..."</em></p>
<p>Bartal continues, a twinkle in his eye,</p>
<p><em>"Suddenly Thread fell over Keroon, runner dropped me on my Red Butte<br />
Some gambler up in Bitra ripped me off of my marks..."<br />
"And I'll search the North over for that girl with green eyes<br />
Yeah, I'll search my route over for that sweet Harper girl..."</em></p>
<p>Bartal plods along, grinning before launching into the third verse,</p>
<p><em>"Kitchen drudges at Igen fattened me up to the hilt<br />
In the Stands for a Hatching, I rediscovered the pies...<br />
"And I'll search the North over for that girl with green eyes<br />
Yeah, I'll search my route over for that sweet Harper girl..."</em></p>
<p>Bartal reaches a particular point in repeating the chords beneath, and chants the refrain,</p>
<p><em>"Sweet Harper girl, Sweet Harper girl,<br />
Still in the North? Sweet Harper girl."</em></p>
<p>The strumming gitar underneath doesn't change as Bartal starts another verse. The repetitive motion of the song seems to represent the life of a trader well:</p>
<p><em>"Took a detour to Ista, just in case she'd been Searched<br />
Weyrling on ferry duty landed on my bubbly stash.<br />
Ended up near the Fort mountains, Weyr, Hold and Crafts<br />
Gather at Boll produced music -- Wherry stew produced gas."</em><br />
Bartal smirks.</p>
<p><em>"And I'll search the North over for that girl with green eyes<br />
Yeah, I'll search my route over for that sweet Harper girl..."</em></p>
<p>He smiles wistfully, doing more talking than singing, but it seems to fit the mood. He launches into another verse,</p>
<p><em>"And the Reaches were freezing, blanket trading was fast<br />
But try as I did to find her, not a sight of the lass."</em></p>
<p>"And I'll search the North over for that girl with green eyes<br />
Yeah, I'll search my route over for that sweet Harper girl..."</p>
<p>Bartal grins, and plays the chords louder, more insistent as he chants the refrain one last time:</p>
<p><em>"Sweet Harper girl, Sweet Harper girl,<br />
Still in the North? Sweet Harper girl."</em></p>
<p>He ends the gitar chords with an a quick arpeggio up to an unusual note, and then he falls silent.</p>
