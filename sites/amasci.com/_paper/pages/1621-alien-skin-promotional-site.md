<td width="888"><h3 id="the-reason-for-this-site">The reason for this site:</h3>
<p>This site is for <strong>promoting</strong> "Eye Candy" , "XenoFex" and "Splat!" by Alien Skin Software. All questions concerning upgrades/bugs/fixes/compatibility problems or any other technical questions should be directed to the Alien Skins authors. Contact information is located below.</p>
<p>I've been using Adobe Photoshop since version 1.0 and have been on the Internet since 1995 before many of these companies like Alienskin were on the net. Back then there weren't many plugin filter makers around and I was excited to use their first product, The Black Box, set of filters. I wanted to help promote these new filters in hopes that they would continue to create such wonderful products. They have and I've been a beta tester for their products ever since. Keep up the good work guys!!!</p></td>
