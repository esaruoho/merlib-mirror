**December 6th, Friday 2002:** With the lower block done I planted the large new heads on the enormous engine and buttoned them down...ya know, when I removed the heads I could swear they were on with less than 50ft pounds of torque. Can't go farther until I get the lifters, then I can put the galley in and intake on. I will let technology rule of the distributor and go with a stealth electronic that fits inside the original cap. I have a web resource that has the kit for \$85. I need to make sure I post the links here for those that might be rebuilding an Edsel 410 or Lincoln 430.\
\
**December 7th, Saturday 2002:** Making out a wish list for Santa...Tri-Power off a Lincoln Marauder with cool air cleaner would be great...sigh...guess that might look like too drastic of a change from the original. Maybe I will try fashioning my own air cleaner for a tri-power and give it an Edsel look. I have wanted to try some sculpting with aluminum. My parts never came...I will have to call Kanter on Monday and see what the delay is. Once the engine is done, I will place it off to the side and work on the tranny next. My step dad knows a rebuilder that is reliable and might be willing to work the Tele-touch back into shape. Don't know where I am going to get a new torque converter or weather I need to get one, maybe a good scrubbing will suffice...I feel that things are going smoothly so far. I need to get this diary posted with some pics. I have a couple of pics so maybe I will get this up tonight.\

**December 11th, Wednesday 2002:** More parts came today, I know the UPS guy now by name (Thanks George).  Got to paint and install the water pump after first putting on the intake manifold, makes slipping the odd three inch water hose between the two easier if you put the much heavier and awkward intake on first.  Very odd positioning but those guys back in the 50's wanted to be unique so I installed the fuel pump upside down and at the top of the motor, like a jewel in an auto crown.  Cleaned up the oil filter holder and installed it with a new ***Fram PH8A*** filter...filled the filter with oil ahead of time to make things a little easier for me and the engine.  Got the air cleaner element today too, the 58 Edsel Citation and Corsair take the equivalent of a 1958 T-Bird air cleaner for those wondering, it is a ***Fram CA154***...gotta get some sleep, spent until 2am today on the old gal (or is it guy...still out on this one).  Made sure I installed my cab lifting plate so nothing untoward would fall into the engine and cause me to sew a stream of obscenities from here to Poplar Bluff Missouri.  Also, the intake manifold gaskets do have a front and a back...doh!

**December 13th, Friday 2002:** Tricadecaphobia, the fear of the number 13...Today I fixed a chip in the enamel on one of the valve covers...dropped the fitting off my air compressor (the one that sprays air) on it, not on purpose though, I really wasn't trying to test the scratch resistant surface.  Company was over so I did little else to the car (did put the spark plugs in).

**December 14th, Saturday 2002:** Picked up a thermostat, used a 160 in it, I didn't feel like running hot in the summer so we'll see how it warms in the winter, that is an easy change out.  Installed the various plumbing that goes on the intake manifold but I have little clue as to which way many are to pint so I am guestimating from some pictures I have seen of the engine in books.  Picked out a Christmas Tree and hung lights on the house, what the heck, called it a day and enjoyed the family...I seem to remember them, they spend time "in" the house, I am always in the garage... \*wink\*

Here is a before shot of the oil pan, very dented but still solid and heck, the think is as thick as a sheet of plywood so I will just clean it up and give it a fresh coat of new look.  On the right we see the intake, head, and rod valley pan all looking clean and pretty.\

December 15th, Sunday 2002:** Picked up a load of wood in old Goldie today (my 71 3/4 ton Ford *Camper Special*), someday I need to go through her engine too...maybe stretch her cab so I can take the family out in one truck...maybe make her a 4x4...sigh, that would be another web page altogether.  Since the car came with no carb when I got her (or is it him...) I think I will do some research and just get a great one rather than the stock one...who's really gonna see and who cares, I am doing this car for me and for reliability.  I went down to my local carb and intake store <http://www.carbs.com> is their site, and ordered a Speed Demon 750 with Vacuum secondaries and Ford kick-down, the Edelbrock Ford kick-down kit works the same as the more expensive one if you purchase it with the Demon.  Also picked up an electronic ignition kit that fits under the original distributor cap so I can toss away points and old crap that one would normally have to futz with during install time...odd note, the computer spell checker knew the correct spelling of futz.  Carb should be here next week as will the ignition parts.  I need to update the pictures on the site (add a few more).  Unloaded the wood from the truck and ended up hurting my back so pain pills here I come.

December 18th, Wednesday 2002: ** The carb came today along with a feed pipe for the monster and a short riser plate.  A Demon 750 VFE sits atop the Edsel motor akin to a jewel on a crown.  I need to get new valve cover bolts, the originals are trashed and a few were missing...wonder how to setup the kick down linkage...I will have to look at my trucks engine.

**December 19th, Thursday 2002: ** Installed the fuel line to the carb and the valve covers today with their new shiny bolts.  I will hand paint the red lettering sometime this weekend to make it official.  Also installed the exhaust manifolds but somewhere along the line there was a bag of bolts for the left manifold that is gone...back to Napa.  Installed the new thermostat as well as buffed and cleaned the manifold thermostat tube...stood back from the almost done engine, dang this thing is huge, I hadn't realized the size of this big big block, yikes...drool...the power...ahhh.  I need to order some spark plug wires and a coil, then I will be 100% done with the engine.

**December 21st, Saturday 2002:** Installed a new set of exhaust manifold bolts just to be sure they were all strong, the half set that was on there looked like a mismatched set of bolts.  Yanked the old points and condenser out for a great little electronic unit that runs under the original cap.  Can't complain for about 80 bucks, no more worries.  *Lincoln Parts International* has the kit which took about five minutes to install, very easy, here's there site: <http://www.pe.net/~lincprts/engine.htm>.  Almost forgot...need a coil...have no clue where to mount it either.  Such is the adventure of restoring a car that has a lot of parts in boxes.  I still don't regret the project...I think, hehehehe.  With Christmas coming, it will be hard to find time to go further but really I am done, all I have left are some spark plug wires then it's the "bag" for a couple of months for the engine.  On to the tranny!
