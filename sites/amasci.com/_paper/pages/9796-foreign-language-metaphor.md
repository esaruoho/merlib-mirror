# Foreign Language Metaphor

*Source page: `~wayneld/foreign.html`*

![](icons/li-br-m2.gif)

## Foreign Language Analogy ! <img src="icons/bonzai9.gif" data-align="middle" />

------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The Course seems very difficult at times to grasp, accept and
    incorporate into my life, as a way of thinking. I like the analogy 
    of learning the Course as similar to that of learning a foreign 
    language. 

    In this world, we enter and live to a point, *believing* the ego
    thought system is our native language, and experience the world from
    the ego. We eventually come to the conclusion that we are not really
    happy and fulfilled speaking and experiencing this ego "native" 
    language. And have the thought that there might be a better language 
    to speak and actually think, so that we can have the experience of 
    true Peace and Joy....in other words, we say "There must be a better 
    way." 

    The story goes like this.....Jesus hears the "call" and says there
    is another language called the Love Language that one may want to
    learn (actually the process is unlearning the ego thought system) and 
    once it's "learned", and becomes the language of choice, a lasting 
    experience of true Peace and Joy will be the result. 

    Since the Love Language seems foreign, Jesus has given those of 
    us that choose it, a tool we kind of like called A Course In Miracles. 
    And in a form we are familiar with and used all our school days. It 
    has terminology like curriculum, student, teacher, text, workbook, etc..  

    Learning this Course can also have similar results to my school 
    experience in that I can take short cuts and memorize, just do the   
    workbook to score good on tests, sound good to others, etc., and not 
    really bother with all the theoretical foundation stuff. The only 
    "problem" with this approach is that it may delay the ability to 
    think in the new Love Language, and it is the thinking it, that 
    brings the experience of true Peace and Joy that I *really* want. 

    This course can also mirror the experience of learning a foreign
    language in the sense that it is fun for awhile and then can begin to
    feel like a lot of work. Some parts of the Love Language may be
    particularly attractive and helpful at difficult times in my life,
    just as some knowledge of Spanish can be helpful when traveling
    in Spain. But, there is an unwillingness to give up totally the ego 
    native language I came here believing was my heritage. There is pride 
    of authorship (unconscious of course) and other "good" reasons like
    feeling special and superior, to hang on to the old language.  :)

    So the "moral of the story" is, if I truly feel ACIM is the path for
    me to attain and keep a state of Peace, maybe I should follow the
    instructions and learn that tough and often difficult theoretical
    foundation stuff well enough that I can actually think it. For it
    is only in the thinking the language, that I can experience what it
    is like to live the Love Language. 

    Reminds me of the story people have related, wherein once they
    know a foreign language well enough to think it, they have a whole
    new experience when visiting the country. A much richer one at that !
    I am sure my experience will be much the same when I have unlearned
    the ego thought system....at least periodic glimpses tell me so. :)

    And if ACIM isn't really the path of choice, it can still be fun to 
    dabble in because it has some nice ideas, pretty poetry, numerous 
    puns and a lot for the lovers of logic and the intellectually 
    curious. Plus, there are some really cool ACIM people to hang out 
    with on the Net.  :) 

    Should one feel guilty if one doesn't want ACIM to be the answer to
    the "there must be a better way" call.........Definitely not !!!!!!!!
    Because as the Course says, it is only one among thousands of paths
    that will lead to thinking from that Universal Love Language we were
    all created with, but just have chosen to forget for awhile.

    peace,   wayne near seattle  :)
