Place: Bamboo Garden

Type of Place: Vegetarian Chinese restaurant

Location:  364 Roy St  (206) 282-6616. Parking's kind of a pain, especially on busy Friday and Saturday nights, and when there's a major event going on at Seattle Center. I'd recommend going there for lunch, or a weekday dinner

Details: This is an all-vegetarian place- mostly vegan- with an amazing assortment of traditional Chinese food as well as some other Asian foods, like Thai noodles and Curries. They use fake meats and do so well with them you won't miss the meat at all. The soups are absolutely great, and the prices are very good too. The staff is friendly, the service is quick and bright, all around, a great experience.
