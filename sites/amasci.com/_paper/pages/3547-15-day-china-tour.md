# 15-DAY CHINA TOUR

*Source page: `~interlak/15day.html`*

**15 DAY CHINA TOUR**\
INCLUDES: BEIJING, XIAN, GUILIN, HANGZHOU\
SUZHOU AND SHANGHAI

DAY 1 Depart Vancouver, Seattle, L.A. or S.F. (Your Choice)

2 Arrive Beijing - Met by guide and transferred to your hotel.

3 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Forbidden City, Tiananmen Square/History\
Museum. Evening Dai Minority Show & Dinner. BLD

4 Beijing - Tour the Hutongs, Jiazi lunch with a Hutong Family. Visit the Lama\
Temple and Temple of Heaven. Fangshand dinner at Beihai Park.BLD

5 Beijing - Guide will take you to the Great Wall at Mutianyu or Juyongguan, Ming\
Tombs and Summer Palace. Special Peking Duck Dinner and Peiking Opera. BLD

6 Beijing/Xian by a.m. air. Met by Guide and taken to visit the Wild Goose Pagoda,\
Ancient City Wall, History Museum and Big Bell Tower. Transfer to Hotel. BLD

7 Xian - Full day tour to Terra Cotta Warriors site, Banpo Museum, Dwelling Cave\
of Farmers, Muslim Quarter and Mosque. Special Evening performance of the\
Beautiful Tang Dynasty Show. BLD.

8 Xian./Guilin by morning air. Guide will meet you and take you to the 7 Star Park,\
and Reed Flute Cave. Transfer to your hotel. BLD

9 Fantastic Li River Cruise today down to the village of Yangshuo where you will\
have time to shop for bargains in this quaint village on the river. Back to Guilin\
this evening. BLD

10 Guilin/Hangzhou by morning air. Met by Guide and transferred to your hotel.\
Afternoon visit to Lingying Temple and famous Bonzai Garden. BLD

11 Hangzhou - Guide will take you on the West Lake Cruise and then visit Huagang\
Park, Six Harmonies Pagoda and beautiful Tea Garden. BLD

12 Hangzhou/Shanghai by morning train (approx. 2 hrs.) Guide will meet you at the\
train station and take you on a tour of the city to visit the Bund, French Concession\
and Nanjing Road Shopping Area. Evening performance of the famous Shanghai\
Acrobatic Show. transfer to your hotel. BLD

13 Shanghai - Guide will take you to the Jade Buddha Temple, beautiful Yu Garden,\
and the new National Museum at People's Square. BLD.

14 Shanghai/Suzhou/Shanghai by early morning train and return. Guide will meet you\
on the platform and take you to visit Tiger Park, the Silk Factory and Showrooms,\
and a visit to the special Embroidery Institute. (double sided embroidery) Return\
To Shanghai early evening (train is only 50 minutes) BLD

15 Shanghai/U.S. arriving back at your west coast departure city same day.
