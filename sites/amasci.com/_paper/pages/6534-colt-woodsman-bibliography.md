# Colt Woodsman Bibliography

*Source page: `~rayburn/woodsman/biblio.html`*

<span id="BIBLIO"></span>

### BIBLIOGRAPHY

**Books:**\

- Bady, Donald B., Colt Automatic Pistols, Borden Publ., 1973.
- Brink, Edward A., Colt Rimfire Automatic Pistols, 1982.
- Fjestad, S. P., Blue Book of Gun Values, Investment Rarities, One Appletree Square, Minneapolis, MN 55425.
- Grennell, Dean, Handgun Digest, 2nd Edition, DBI Books, 1991. "Notes on Handgun Collecting," pp. 62-81.
- Grennell, Dean, Gun Digest Book of Autoloading Pistols, 1983, pp. 63-67.
- James, Frank, Guns & Ammo Handguns, "Me and My Woodsman," pp. 86-89.
- Serven, James E., Colt Firearms From 1836, Stackpole Books, 1954, 1979.
- Smith, W. B. H., Books of Pistols and Revolvers, first published by the NRA in 1946, this work has gone through many editions and reprints, the most recent being by Stackpole Books. "Colt Woodsman Series," pp. 74-82.
- Sutherland, R. Q. and Wilson, R. L., The Book of Colt Firearms, R. Q. Sutherland, 1971.
- Wilson, R. L., The Book of Colt Engraving, Beinfeld Publications, 1974.
- Wilson, R. L., The Colt Heritage, Simon and Schuster, 1979.

**Magazines:**

- Anderson, R., "Classic Test Report, Colt Woodsman Series," Guns & Ammo, Apr 1981, pp.64-65, 77-78.
- Bevan, Art, "My 50 Years With the Colt Woodsman," Gun Digest, 1988 Annual, pp. 14-19.
- Crossman, Capt E. C., "Just Offhand," Outdoor Life, Sept 1938, pp. 42-43, 66-67, 77. (A review of the "New" Match Target Woodsman is on pages 66, 67, and 77.)
- Greeley, Horace IV, "Colt and the Old Model S," Man at Arms, Sept/Oct 1979, pp. 10-16.
- Grennell, Dean, "The Wonderful Woodsman," Gun World, June 1976, pp. 22-23.
- Haines, Ashley, "A New Cayuse on the Range - The .22 Colt Auto," Outdoor Life, Mar 1916, pp. 287-290
- Hoffschmidt, E. J., "Colt Match Target Woodsman," The American Rifleman, May 1953, pp. 54-55.
- Lisle, Larry, "Colt's Woodsman: a 60 Year Saga," Guns & Ammo, Feb. 1975, pp. 58-59, 84-85.
- Nesbitt, Mike, "The Wonderful Woodsman," Guns, Feb 1986, pp. 42-45.
- Nesbitt, Mike, "Colt Woodsman Carbine," Rifle, Jan/Feb 1987, pp. 22-23.
- Ness, F. C., "Some New .22 Cal. Pistols," American Rifleman, Aug 1938, pp. 28-30.
- Rayburn, Robert, "The Woodsman Series," The Colt Collector's Association Newsletter, June 1985, pp. 7-8.
- Rayburn, Robert, "The Woodsman - First Series, 1915-1947," The Colt Collector's Association Newsletter, part 1, Sept 1985, pp. 13-14, part 2, Dec 1985 pp. 16-17.
- Rayburn, Robert, "The Woodsman - Post War Models, Second Series," The Colt Collector's Association Newsletter, Mar 1986, pp. 8-9.
- Rayburn, Robert, "The Woodsman - Post War Models, Third Series," The Colt Collector's Association Newsletter, Jun 1986, pp. 8-9.
- Rayburn, Robert, "Colt's Versatile Woodsman," Guns & Ammo, June 1986, pp. 56-59, 90, 92.
- Rayburn, Robert, "Collecting the Colt Woodsman," CADA Gun Journal, Oct 1992, pp. 6-8.
