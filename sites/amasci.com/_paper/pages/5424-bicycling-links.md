### The AIDS Rides are popular but expensive, with over half of raised funds going to administrative expenses, and a minimum \$1400 in donations required from the rider and sponsors combined. They could do a great service to all by revising this policy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Florida AIDS Ride has split from the management of the other AIDS Rides and is now called the Florida Red Ribbon Ride; it still goes from Ft. Lauderdale to Orlando. According to the [April 1998 Log Cabin Newsletter](http://www.seflin.org/lcr/log.0498.html) this was due to disagreements with the way the other AIDS Rides were done, including the fact that most of the funds raised went to administrative expenses and paying high salaries for AIDS Ride officials, instead of going to organizations fighting AIDS.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The others (Boston-NY, Philly-DC, Chicago, Texas, California) are still run by [Tanqueray AIDS Rides Network](http://www.aidsride.org/)
