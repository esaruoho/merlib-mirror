#### WWW Search Engines

- [Metacrawler](http://www.metacrawler.com/): Simply the best! Search all the major search engines at once.

<!-- -->

- [Yahoo](http://www.yahoo.com/): fast, searchable index, arranged in categories for browsing and searching
- [WebCrawler](http://webcrawler.com/): key word searching, extremely fast, returns simple list of hypertext links
- [Lycos](http://lycos.cs.cmu.edu/): comprehensive Web index, key word searching, returnes excerpts from the indexed pages.
