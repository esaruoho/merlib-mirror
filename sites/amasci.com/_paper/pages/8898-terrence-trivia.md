<td><h1 id="terrence-malicks-style">Terrence Malick's Style</h1>
<ul>
<li><img src="tm08t.jpg" /></li>
<li>The burning question: will Malick ever direct a film before twenty years more pass? He has said yes, but for now it seems he's concentrating on producing films.<br />
<br />
</li>
<li>Family ties: Malick's third wife is Alexandra "Ecky" W.B. Malick. They married in 1998. Ecky's sons Will and Todd Wallace acted in <a href="trl/index.html">The Thin Red Line</a>.<br />
<br />
</li>
<li>Current residence: Austin, Texas.<br />
<br />
</li>
<li>Current job: Malick and co-producer Edward R. Pressman <a href="badlands/index.html">Badlands</a> have started their own company, Columbine Productions. They have a deal with Sony Pictures Classics to develop a minimum of three films between 1999 and 2002.<br />
<br />
</li>
<li>On his work style: "It takes a while for Terry to trust you and feel comfortable," says John Powell, who composed the musical score for the Malick-produced documentary <u>Endurance</u>.<br />
<br />
More on his work style: Malick worked closely with musical composer Hans Zimmer, who wrote the score for <a href="trl/index.html">The Thin Red Line</a> by setting up an office in Zimmer's own company, Media Ventures. Zimmer was going to compose the score for <u>Endurance</u> but handed the job over to Powell, who's in the "artist pool" for Zimmer's co.<br />
<br />
</li>
<li>Fun fact 1: <a href="trl/index.html">The Thin Red Line</a> actor <a href="trl/jim/index.html">Jim Caviezel</a> describes Malick's voice as a cross between a Texas drawl and Kermit the frog.<br />
<br />
</li>
<li>Fun fact 2: Malick used to have long hair and a long beard, both of which were trimmed around the time of <a href="trl/index.html">The Thin Red Line</a>'s release. Mr. Malick, you have lost your chance to audition for another spot in ZZ Top!<br />
<br />
</li>
</ul>
