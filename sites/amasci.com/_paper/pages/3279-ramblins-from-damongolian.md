<a href="http://fridayfive.org">Friday Five</a><br />
<br />
<strong>1. What's your favorite TV show and why?</strong> Currently I guess The West Wing, ER's getting a little old. I do like Third Watch a lot though.
<p><br />
<strong>2. Who is your favorite television star?</strong> I guess I really don't have one currently I'd have to say Alyson Hannigan is extremely cute, but I don't watch Buffy so go figure...</p>
<p><br />
<strong>3. What was your favorite TV show as a child?</strong> hmmm Nothing really stands out as a FAVORITE so I'm going to say "Gilligan's Lion" if for no other reason than there's an amusing incident when I was about 3 wandering around upstairs at the grocery store that my Mom's family used to own saying that I wanted to watch Gilligan's Lion when of course I really meant Gilligan's Island lol...don't ask me where I came up with that one, I've know idea =) Oh I did rather like the campy reruns of Batman though.</p>
<p><br />
<strong>4. What show do you think should have been cancelled by now?</strong> Law &amp; Order-Criminal Intent, I like the other two L&amp;O incarnations, but something bout Vincent D'Onofrio pouty performance bugs me.</p>
<p><br />
<strong>5. What new show do you hope escapes the axe this season?</strong> None of em, I'm watching too much TV this year, so if they dump one of the ones that I sort of like, I won't cry too much. I'm thinking Leap of Faith, I do miss Inside Schwartz but not enough to lose any sleep over it. Oooh wait, now that I think about it, if they cancel UC but keep Criminal Intent, I shall be very put out especially with X-Files going away this year.</p>
