This page address equations commonly used in small arms ballistics. I'll add to the list as required/requested.

Where applicable, variables and references have been listed at the end of each page.

[Cant](cant.html)\
Derivations of the equations used in corrections to the elevation and azimuth of a canted firarem.

[CD and KD](cdkd.html)\
Derivations of the equations used in the point mass trajectory calculations. It includes equations for CD, BC and KD.

[Crosswind](crosswind.html)\
Derivations of the classic crosswind deflection formula.

[Elevation](elevation.html)\
A method for calculating trajectories for other than a zeroed range (or for changes in shooting conditions) using the constant elevation angle.

[Energy](energy.html)\
A discussion of potential and kinetic energy with a worked example.

[Integration](integrate.html)\
Changing variables of integration to make the trajectory equation more convenient to integrate.

[Recoil](recoil.html)\
Equation for calculating free recoil

[Sectional Density](secdens.html)\
Equation for calculating sectional density

[Runge Kutta Integration](rk.html)\
Equations for numerical integration using Runge Kutta methods.
