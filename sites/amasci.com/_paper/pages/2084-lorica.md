 All 16 ga. 304 stainless steel with bronze eyelet's and brass hinges.\
 

**Note:**   I've received a number of e-mails asking for the pattern for this.  I don't have a pattern and didn't use one when I made the Lorica.  If you can find a copy of "The Armour of Imperial Rome"  by H. Russell Robinson it will have a number of very good line drawings of Lorica Segmentatas (which I used when making mine).\
