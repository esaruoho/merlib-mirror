# David Kerlick in Istanbul, Turkey, July 31 - Aug 9 1999

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Sat 7/31

rrive Istanbul. Taxi to Empress Zoe. Sound and Light. Crooked Cabbie to Taksim. Gay Ripoff.

### Sun 8/1

Haghia Sofia, Cisterns, Blue Mosque.

### Mon 8/2

Topkapi, Harem. Lost in the Covered Market.

### Tue 8/3

Gulhane Park, Egyptian spice Market. Pigeon Mosque.

### Weds 8/4

Chora (closed), Suleymanie Mosque

### Thurs 8/5

Taksim, Dolmabace (closed), elusive ferries.

### Fri 8/6

Dolmabace, Kempinski, dine at Pandolfi, Chora. Walk back via Valens Aqueduct, Istanbul City Hall, shoppers.

### Sat 8/7 The Bosphorus

"Radical" Ferries Captive restaurants at end of line. Sariyer Museum: classical stuff in modern, Ottoman dioramas in Ottoman House. Dinner w/ Kent & co.

### Sun 8/8 Ferry to Yalova, bus to Bursa

Breakfast with HAl, Lee, Kent.
