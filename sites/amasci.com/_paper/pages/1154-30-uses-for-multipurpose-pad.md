<td data-valign="top"><p><br />
<strong>30 USES OF THE <a href="pads.html">MULTIPURPOSE PAD</a></strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Before the baby is born, in your bed, in case your water breaks.</li>
<li>In the car, under yourself, on the way to the hospital.</li>
<li>Home birth - it holds 1 litre of fluid.</li>
<li>Under yourself while you are nursing in bed.</li>
<li>Folded double in the bassinet, cradle or any size bed.</li>
<li>Folded double on the change table for extra padding and protection.</li>
<li>In the play pen.</li>
<li>In the stroller.</li>
<li>On the sofa, carpet or fancy chair.</li>
<li>While laying the baby on the floor to protect from pet hair or soiled carpet.</li>
<li>In the crib.</li>
<li>If your baby has diaper rash, to air out the rash worry-free.</li>
<li>Under your toddler's car seat.</li>
<li>At the beach to protect baby/toddler from the sand.</li>
<li>On the grass to protect baby/toddler from the dampness or tickling grass.</li>
<li>Under a high chair to catch spills.</li>
<li>As an activity blanket.</li>
<li>Under the potty.</li>
<li>In the bed for bed wetter.</li>
<li>Velcro a second pad to the comforter/blanket to protect covering for a bed wetter.</li>
<li>In the car, while/after toilet training to protect the seat against accidents.</li>
<li>In the car, after swimming, when you can't change your clothing.</li>
<li>On concrete or wood pool decks.</li>
<li>To protect upholstery from wet snowsuit.</li>
<li>Under the "bucket" when your child is ill.</li>
<li>On the pillow, if your child goes to bed with wet hair.</li>
<li>In the bed if your child has a fever. The pad will absorb the perspiration.</li>
<li>Inside or under your sleeping bag while camping.</li>
<li>In your cottage or trailer on the bed.</li>
<li><p>Protects moisture damage to carpet and furniture when under vaporizers or humidifiers.</p>
<p><img src="../_themes/babytown1/fierulea.gif" width="600" height="10" /></p></li>
</ol>
