# NCPA Domain Names

------------------------------------------------------------------------

As yet another free service to college paintball clubs, the NCPA offers free domain names of the format www.yourclubname.college-paintball.com. These adresses require no change in the setup of your web space and can be used with any existing web page to replace more cryptic adresses that are hard to remember. As an added bonus, you also get a listing in the NCPA Hosted Domains list on the NCPA Links Page. Mail your request to [Chris Raehl (raehl311@yahoo.com)](mailto:raehl311@yahoo.com).
