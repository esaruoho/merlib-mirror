<h3 id="votive-candle-holders">VOTIVE CANDLE HOLDERS</h3>
<p>This genuine Austrian crystal candle holder stands 2 5/8 inches high. Various natural stones, pearls,<br />
or shells are hand soldered onto the glass. To remove candle wax, place in freezer briefly, then<br />
tap on the bottom and the wax will pop out.<br />
(Code R502)</p>

VH1 - Turquoise, emerald, and clear quartz crystal
VH3 - Amethyst, sodalite, lepidolite, and clear quartz crystal 
VH4 - Moonstone, quartz, pearl, and clear quartz crystal

VH6 - Rose quartz, amethyst, abalone, and clear quartz crystal</code></pre>
