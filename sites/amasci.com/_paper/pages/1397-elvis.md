US military motive?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

A Washington think tank has announced a breakthrough in the search for a pattern in the seemingly random US military aggression since World War Two. "We think they're spelling out a message", explains an unnamed spokesperson. "If we take the first letters of Korea, Vietnam, Libya, Iraq, Iran, El Salvador, Grenada, Nicaragua and Somalia, it spells **'ELVIS \_S KING'**. We just need to find another **'I'** country -- Indiana might do -- to complete the message."
