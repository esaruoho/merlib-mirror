With all the talk, with all the hype, it's sometimes hard to know exactly what separates one kite design from another. It's true they fly differently but oftentimes it's hard to know why. With Aerie kites you can make a determination sitting in your house.

All Aerie kites have an aerodynamic shape cut and sewn into the sail itself. Nearly every other kite in the world is sewn flat and is dependent upon the wind to "inflate" the sail and create an airfoil. Aerie kites have an optimum airfoil shape even before the wind stars to blow. To test this, simply pull all the spares out of an Aerie kite and try to lay it flat on a table. It can't be done.

Why don't other kite manufacturers make kites in this manner? Two reasons: Hundreds of hours of testing are required to attain the correct profile for each sail shape and the construction of each sail is a painstaking process. Hand-cut panels are individually taped, bonded and sewn. All stress-points are mylar reinforced. These kites are not cheap.

So what's this built-in airfoil mean to you? Simple, tremendous light air performance. Aerie kites generate lift from the moment the wind begins to slide over their surface. So why are you still sitting in your house? Get flying!

**Air FX**\
This is the kite that made the company. Built with the philosophy that form follows function the three-dimensional shape of the wings also creates an elegant graphic pattern. Of all the Aerie kites, the Air FX is the most precise, straigthest-tracking model. It's a great team precision kite. Available in a standard Icarex sail or with reinforced mylar panels, or with ultralight frame sets.
