[THE LAMB SITE, A Pioneering Clovis Encampment ](Lamb.html) by R.M. Gramly 

- The latest info on Clovis!

- This site was noteworthy for the discovery of clustered, pristine flaked stone tools.

- These were fashioned of materials from 500-1800 km west of the site, and brought there by a pioneering group of early hunters.

- This site was excavated in 1986-1990 by Dr. Gramly and his team of volunteers.

- Hundreds of fine illustrations and photos.

  Price \$20.00 [To order](http://www.eskimo.com/~knapper/1.html)

[Story in Stone](stonebk.html) Flint types of the Central and Southern U.S. by D. C. and Valerie Waldorf 

- This is a ready reference to the major chipped stone and tool types of the Central and Southern U.S.
- 727 pen and ink drawings of 208 type groups.
- Also included is a section describing over 50 lithic resource sites from which many of the points were made.

  Price \$25.00 [To order](http://www.eskimo.com/~knapper/1.html)
