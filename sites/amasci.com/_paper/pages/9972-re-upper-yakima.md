    Chris,

    You need to fish a 6,7 or 8 wt. The longer the better. I fish a 10', 8wt.
    Long casts help although they aren't necessary for success. Obviously, the
    longer the cast, the longer the fly fishes. Salmon always let you know when
    they are about. Cast to the fish or around the bait. Try to guess which way
    they are heading and cast ahead of them. Run the beach and chase them if
    you can. Try to cast with a minimum of false casts. Be able to pick up and
    lay down quickly. Be mobile, move around, look for the fish. Don't cast to
    dead water. Short quick strips in to you. Also try downtide mends which
    will pull the fly a bit parallel to the beach. If the current is really
    swift, let a belly form and just twitch the fly along. Hope this begins to
    help.

    Leland.
