In 1986 Russ Freeman (guitarist, founder, composer) brought friends like Kenny G and David Benoit into the studio to record his songs on an album called "Moonlighting." Since then, the band has grown into The Rippingtons, consisting of Jeff Kashiwa (sax), Tony Morales (drums), Kim Stone (bass), and Steve Reid(percussion). The Rippingtons have just released their tenth album entitled "<a href="http://www.rippingtons.com/laa.shtml">Live Across America</a>"
<p>Freeman explains the Rippingtons progress by saying "<em><strong>we started out in the studio and then it became a live entity. The live aspect of it has helped sustain it and helped us grow.</strong></em>"</p>
<p>Since The Rippingtons album ("Live in L.A."), Freeman has reunited with Benoit for "The Benoit/Freeman Project" which involved a tour. Freeman has now also founded a new record label, called Peak Records, which is a GRP subsidiary distributed by UNI. The label will focus on hand-picked vocal pop projects, with Freeman acting as executive producer. Freeman says the label came about because, "I am very interested in pop music and the vocal side of it, even though I haven't explored that in my band. I've produced other artists, and my interest in that area has grown. That's how Peak was born."</p>
