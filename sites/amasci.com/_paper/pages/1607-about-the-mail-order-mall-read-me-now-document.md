<br />
For quite a while I pondered whether to keep this site going or not. I do this in my spare time and <em>NONE</em> of the companies listed give me anything to have them listed here - hence there has never been a great incentive to keep up with things.<br />
<br />
But, your emails over the last couple years have taught me that there <em>IS</em> definite use for this site and that it can be a valuable service. So, it is time to think about expanding it. This new update adds about 40-50 new entries, plus corrected entries for about 20-30 others. I also made some structural changes to improve both readability for you and updateability for me (plus a slight facelift.) In the next few months, I am planning to add a method for readers to submit information to be listed on the site as well.<br />
<br />
This site was created at the end of 1994, way back when e-commerce was only a gleam in the eyes of a few. I started the site to serve as a repository of information for contacting mail order companies, at a time when a company having a web page was the absolute exception to the rule.<br />
<br />
Times certainly have changed. The Internet bubble has grown, and popped, but its effect is here to stay. Now, if a catalog retailer does <em>NOT</em> have an online presence, they are making a big mistake. That is good news for <em>ALL</em> consumers, in my opinion. If you can not find what you need here, you can take a look at some <a href="http://dir.yahoo.com/Business_and_Economy/Shopping_and_Services/Retailers/Directories/Catalogs/">other good sites</a> that list mail order catalogs as well.<br />
<br />
-Brian
