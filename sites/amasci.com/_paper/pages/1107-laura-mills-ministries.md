# Laura Mills' Ministries

*Source page: `~archer/LMM/about.html`*

\
\
**Testimony:**

Laura, at age eight, while playing dolls with a six-year-old friend, was told of Jesus. After going to church with her younger friend a few times Laura asked Jesus into her heart. Laura then shared the truth of Jesus with her own family.

At 16 she began attending an Assembly of God church where she received the gift of speaking in tongues, receiving other ministry gifts later.

\
**Music:**

Both of Laura's parents taught music in the public schools and gave lessons at home. They started teaching Laura violin and piano at the age of five. She later taught herself guitar as well.

She was raised on Bach and Beethoven but she heard other music that needed to be played. This was the music she heard within herself.

Laura was fortunate to have a piano in her room when growing up which sparked an interest to compose. She began writing compositions at age nine.

She was first chair violinist in high school and played quartets with her three siblings as well. Laura later went on to change her violin into an electric and jammed with other musicians.

Laura is an actress in movies, commercials and TV. She has recently begun filming a Christian music television program where she is co-founder, co-producer and co-director.

She hopes to create a Christian coffee house for the purpose of leading the unsaved to Christ, while providing an opportunity for Christian musicians to bless others with their God-given talents.

Enjoy the CD & God bless you!\
