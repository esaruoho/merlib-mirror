# How to Submit Pictures

*Source page: `~manor/pics/howto.htm`*

<img src="images/sampicstop.gif" data-border="0" width="205" height="172" />

\
A site dedicated to pictures of Samoyeds\
and the humans they own

 

> Do you have digital pictures of your samoyed?  If you would like to see them here, send them to <sampictures@yahoo.com>.  I'll put them on this site and send you the link, so you can share them with your friends.  *Please don't send pictures over 200k without checking with me first!*
>
> No digital pictures?  If you send me your pictures by regular mail, I'll scan them and put them on the site. If you want the pictures returned, enclose a return envelope with proper return postage. But first, please send me mail at <sampictures@yahoo.com>, and I'll let you know where to send them.
>
> In addition, here's what I need from you:\
>      Your Name\
>      Your Dog's Name(s)\
>      The dates of the pictures (if you have them)\
>      The wording for the entry on the index page (usually "Your Name - Dog's Name)\
> \
> I understand that some of you don't want your full name displayed on the internet for reasons of privacy.  If that's the case, please let me know and we will find some other way to do it.
>
> *Please note:  Anything sent here will be available to everyone who visits this site.  By sending pictures, you agree that they are not copyrighted by anyone else and that you are giving permission for them to be published here.*\
