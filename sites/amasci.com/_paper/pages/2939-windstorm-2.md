    More Windstorm--December 4, 2003
    Windstorm Introduction         Red Oak, Japanese Red Pine, and Honey Locust
     
      Pines
          usually lose a top or break along the trunk, but three of our
          four destroyed shore pines were uprooted by the wind. This one
          created a twenty-four inch hole with eighteen inches of water
          in it. The roots also lifted several large rhododendrons into
          the air.

<img src="../Resources/image54.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" data-border="0" data-naturalsizeflag="3" width="318" height="238" />

<img src="../Resources/image52.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" data-border="0" data-naturalsizeflag="3" width="238" height="318" />

   Doreen stands in the triple top of a large Shore Pine that tangled with a Beauty of Littleworth and won. Another took down a Sourwood tree with it.

We also lost a Pinion Pine and two Japanese Red Pines. In addition, a Scotch Pine, a Japanese Black Pine, a Lodgepole Pine, and an Austrian Pine all tipped over, but I was able to right them with cables.
