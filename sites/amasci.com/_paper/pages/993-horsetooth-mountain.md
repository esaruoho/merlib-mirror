### Horsetooth Mountain

<img src="img/horsetooth.gif" data-border="0" />

Horsetooth Mountain is just west of Fort Collins and looks down on the city like a guardian. Horsetooth Road goes directly west as if it would lead you straight up to the top (but it won't).

It's about a 2 hour hike up one of several trails to the top, but the view of the rock is awesome:

<img src="img/htclose.jpg" data-border="0" />

and the view of town from the inside is even better:

<img src="img/htinside.jpg" data-border="0" />

(that's Horsetooth Reservoir in the foreground).
