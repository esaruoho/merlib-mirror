### British Media

- [The British Comedy Library](http://www.michael.phatcatz.net/awtv/Comedy/index.html). Andrew Wong's comedy home page stuffed full of FAQs and interviews.
- [British Comedy on American TV](http://www.geocities.com/TelevisionCity/7916). An extensive site with info on just about every Britcom seen in the US.
- [The BBC.](http://www.bbc.co.uk/)
- [Channel 4](http://www.channel4.com). The UK's most cool channel has its own site with program listings and really neat stuff (if you're running Netscape). Check it out.
- [Bigglethwaite's UKTV QuickSheet](http://www.bigglethwaite.com/uktvqs.htm). A massive resource of UK TV links. If it exists, it's here!
- [Anglicon Homepage.](http://www.anglicon.com/) Seattle's *premiere* British media convention to be held in May 1999.
- **Doctor Who**
  - [The Doctor Who Homepage](http://nitro9.earth.uni.edu/doctor/homepage.html).
  - [Dr Who News Group](news:rec.arts.drwho)
  - [Society of the Rusting TARDIS Homepage](http://www.eskimo.com/~onan/tardis/). Seattle British video club.
  - [The Time Meddlers of Los Angeles](http://www.gallifreyone.com/)
- [Father Ted Homepage](http://www.geocities.com/Paris/2694/craggy.html). The brilliant Channel 4 comedy has its own unofficial homepage. Check it out!
- [The ThinkTed Project](http://www.thinktoy.com/tv.htm). Some bawdy songs based on *Father Ted*!
- [Lee & Herring Homepage](http://www.leeandherring.com/) Makers of *Fist of Fun*, a BBC-2 comedy series that is a favorite among Rusting TARDIS members. Worth checking out if only to get the text of subliminal messages that flash during the show.
- [Game On](http://www.phill.co.uk/comedy/gameon/index.html). A fan site devoted to the Andrew Davies comedy.
- [The Official Wallace and Gromit Site!](http://www.aardman.com/) Nick Parks' Aardman Animation has all the low-down on this popular Oscar winning clay-animated duo and their short film adventures.
- [TV Go Home](http://www.tvgohome.com/) A parody of *Radio Times* listings, updated every two weeks. Raunchy but funny!
