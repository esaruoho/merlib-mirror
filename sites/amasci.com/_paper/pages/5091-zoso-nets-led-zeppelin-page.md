\- Devoted to Led Zeppelin, this page contains a gallery of some cool, and sometimes rare, memorabelia. Check it out, contributions are welcome!

### [![\* ](http://www.eskimo.com/~mirader/images/m-zoso.gif)Buckeye's Led Zeppelin page](http://www.buckeye-web.com/lz.html)

is your one-stop site for Zeppelin on the internet. Any Led Zeppelin page that means anything is on this page, including Led Zeppelin licence plates, guitar tab, lyrics, etc. One can literally spend hours on this page that Bruce "the buckeye" Deerhake has put a lot of effort into. Also be sure to check out Hugh Jones WWW version of his fanzine [Promimity](http://www.buckeye-web.com/prox). Proximity is one of the best Led Zeppelin fanzines floating around in North America. If the writing isn't enough, the layout is incredibly professional looking and this quarterly fanzine always is full of interesting reading material. This page is also maintained by Bruce Deerhake.

### [![\* ](http://www.eskimo.com/~mirader/images/m-zoso.gif)Merit Adventures](http://www.merit-ad.com)

is the largest source of Led Zeppelin memorabelia in the world. Owned and operated by Rick Barrett, you can purchase anything from Gold Records, T-Shirts, and posters to legit imported CD's, and other hard to find items related to the world of Led Zeppelin.

that was sent to me by Duncan Watson, it should only be listened to by those who have a sense of humor and listen to things such as Dread Zeppelin. I forget what the source of this file is, but when I find out I will let you know. :)\
