# Real Fur Indian Moccasins

*Source page: `~ralphj/moccasins-1.html`*

\<\>

Large size genuine Indian moccasins the way Indians made their moccasins.

Real fur and made by real Indians

These are old!

Made to wear. These are large and measure about 11 ½ inches inside. Rabbit fur on top and some kind of heavy fur on the sides and bottom. These are lined with flannel inside. Solidly made for wearing. Close inspection will show some imperfections due to over the years something either ate some of the fur but mostly it is on the bottom and does not detract from the appearance. You can see when you look at them close up how the Indians could move silently through the forest without making a sound. .

These were made to wear but they are too nice to wear. Fine for display on how the Indians actually made their moccasins. I got these from an old time collector of Indian memorabilia. He was himself was part Indian
