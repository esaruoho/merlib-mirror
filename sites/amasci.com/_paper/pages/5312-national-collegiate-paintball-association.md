<td><h1 id="college-hosted-bigscenario-games">College Hosted Big/Scenario Games</h1>
<p>Planning to attend a scenario or big game? Drop by the <a href="http://pub65.ezboard.com/fnationalcollegiatepaintballassociationfrm25">NCPA Scenario Forum</a> and let other NCPA club players know where you're going so they can meet up with you and represent college ball!</p>
<p>College clubs also host scenario and big games. While these events are hosted by college clubs, these events are designed for all paintball players and everyone is encouraged to attend.</p>
