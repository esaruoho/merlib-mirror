\
The first three days of our honeymoon were spent at [Walt Disney World](http://www.disney.com/DisneyWorld/index2.html) in Orlando, Florida.  Lotsa fun!  We went to Epcot Center, MGM Studios, and of course the Magic Kingdom.  And yes, we rode all the rides, including the new Buzz Lightyear ride for seven-year-olds!  :)

The remaining 11 days we stayed on the island of Nassau, in the Bahamas, at an all-inclusive resort called [Sandals](http://www.sandals.com).  Specifically, the [Sandals Royal Bahamian Resort and Spa](http://www.sandals.com/main/bahamian/sandals-ba-home.html).  It was hard on us, What with the warm, white sand, the crystal blue water, sapphire blue sky, tropical breezes; being waited on hand and foot, being forced to eat gourmet food, drinks at the swim-up pools bars; swimming, snorkeling, kayaking, wind-surfing, scuba diving; dancing, music, casinos, and comedy acts; breakfast in bed, roman bath tubs, four-poster canopy bed, ocean view from our room; facials, mud wrap and mud bath, manicures, pedicures, hair in corn rows (Shaindell, not Kevin); work-out room, beach volleyball; champagne upon our arrival, and a fully stocked wet bar in our room; and almost all of this <u>included</u>...Well, it was just awful.

To see for yourself what a lousy time we had, click on any of the thumbnail photos below to see the full-size picture.
