Top quality. Die cast belt pulleys. Precision cast, machine grooved, and individually tested. Slotted set screw. 1/2" bores have no keyway; 5/8", 3/4", and 1" bore pulleys have keyway on 2" size and larger. Pulley 5" and smaller have solid hubs : 6" and larger have spoke hubs.

Motor For Variable Speed Use

</div>

**(1)   1/8HP, 1800 RPM, 90 VDC** Motor ideal for adding variable speed to your faceter, trim saw, etc. Sturdy motor with ball bearings, reversible rotation, all-position face and base mounting. Has 1/2" drive shaft, 8 3/4" length overall, weighs 9 lbs. External wiring not included, obtain locally. Use with the Speed Controller described below.

**G1556**

ShpWt 10 lbs.

\$215.00

**Speed Controller For 90 VDC Motor** 2 This solid state speed controller converts 115 VAC or 230 VAC to 90 VDC or 180 VDC. Use in conjunction with the 90 VDC Motor described above to make up a variable speed power source. Has adjustable min/max speed, fused control protection, on/off switch, vary speed from 1/10th to max. External wiring not included, obtain locally. Control box is approx. 5" square x 2" deep.

**G1559**

ShpWt 1 lb.
