# Diziet

</div>

<div id="content">

My old and much loved computer was `diziet`, a [PowerComputing PowerCenter 132](http://www.everymac.com/systems/powercc/powercenter/powercenter132.html). That's a Macintosh clone (from the brief reign of Mac clones) with a 132 MHz PowerPC 604e processor. Diziet has 300 MB of RAM, and a variety of hard drives, one of which holds my primary operating system, [Debian GNU/Linux](http://www.debian.org/). I also have a cool monitor, the [Portrait Display Labs Pivot 1700](http://www.macworld.com/1996/03/reviews/1875.html), whose coolest feature—pivoting between landscape and portrait mode—I can't actually use under Linux.

Diziet has now been replaced by `fal`. Even though fal is a lot more machine, I still miss diziet.
