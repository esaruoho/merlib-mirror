<td data-valign="top" width="580"><h1 id="what-a-wonderful-weyr">What a Wonderful Weyr</h1>
by Senior Journeyman Harper Machs of Small Hold, Southern Continent
<p><em>(sung to the tune of "What a Wonderful World" by Louis Armstrong)</em></p>
<hr />
Machs tuned the gitar, and glanced over at young Bartal. "Wonder what it's like for your Uncle Cl'rek to live up there at Benden Weyr?" he asked.
<p>Bartal's eyes got wide, and he asked, "How could you live in caves like that? I mean, what's wrong with living in big Holds out in the open like this?"</p>
<p>Machs chuckled. "Weyrs are wonderful places, son. You know, I wrote a song long time ago when I spent a few months at Honshu Weyr. Might help you understand better."</p>
<p>Bartal nodded quietly, and leaned back on his chair as Machs finished tuning the gitar. Rich, full tones emitted from the gitar as experienced fingers shaped the melody. He began to sing:</p>
<p><em>I see wings of green ... bronze, brown and blue<br />
I see them flaming thread, for me and you<br />
And I think to myself what a wonderful Weyr.</em></p>
<p>I see skies of blue and clouds of white<br />
And the glow of the bowl in the dead of night<br />
And I think to myself what a wonderful Weyr.</p>
<p>The colors of the rainbow, so pretty in dragons' eyes,<br />
Are also on the faces of people going by<br />
I see residents clasping hands, asking how do you do<br />
They're really saying 'I love you.'</p>
<p>I hear babies crying, I watch them grow<br />
Harpers'll teach them more than I'll never know<br />
And I think to myself what a wonderful Weyr,<br />
Yes I think to myself what a wonderful Weyr.</p>
<p>Bartal smiled as the chords faded from memory. "That was nice. Is it really that beautiful in a Weyr? With all those dragons? I mean, Cl'rek's brown isn't that colorful," he flushes in embarassment, and corrects, "Well, compared to say a green. But together they look..." His eyes sparkle, "I can almost see it! Good song, Machs."</p>
<p>Machs ruffled Bartal's hair and said, "Someday, <em>you</em> might write something like this. Bartal just groaned, and said, "Yeah, right."</p>
