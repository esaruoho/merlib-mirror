<td data-bgcolor="#FFF9F4"><strong>T</strong>he <strong>African National Congress</strong> (ANC), the governing party of South Africa, voted for a “Resolution on Discrimination” fully supporting gay and lesbian equality at its 50<sup>th</sup> National Conference, which took place December 18-20, 1997.
<p>The <em>Resolution</em> is a remarkably inclusive statement, which is hoped to eventually become the law of the land. Part of that inclusiveness is the call for legal marriage for same-sex couples and the “recognition of lesbian and gay families.”</p>
<p>Here is the text of the <strong>ANC</strong> <em>Resolution</em>, plus excerpts of a press release from the <strong>National Coalition for Gay and Lesbian Equality / South Africa</strong> (NCGLE).</p>

<strong>Resolution on Discrimination on Sexual Orientation</strong>
</div>
<em>Noting:</em>
<p>The <strong>ANC</strong>’s support for the inclusion of sexual orientation in the equality clause of the Interim Constitution;</p>
<p>The retention of equality on the basis of sexual orientation in the final Constitution, as motivated and voted for by the <strong>ANC</strong> in the Constitutional Assembly;</p>
<p>The <strong>ANC</strong>’s long standing support of equality for all citizens, including lesbians, gay, bisexual and transgendered South Africans as contained in the policy guide: “Ready to Govern” of March 1993;</p>
<p>Government’s obligation in terms of the final Constitution to secure substantive equality for all citizens;</p>
<p>The ongoing discrimination against lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgendered South Africans.</p>
<p><em>Believing:</em></p>
<p>That the extension of substantial equality to lesbian and gay South Africans is an important sign of the <strong>ANC</strong>’s consistent commitment to the ideals of equality, dignity and freedom;</p>
<p>That all persons should be treated equally under the law; and</p>
<p>That the <strong>ANC</strong> should develop and acknowledge its own lesbian and gay members.</p>
<p><em>Resolves that the <strong>ANC</strong> National Conference:</em></p>
<ul>
<li>expresses its solidarity with lesbian and gay comrades in their work for substantive equality for all;</li>
<li>tasks its representatives in all levels of government to establish equality for lesbian and gay people in the following areas in particular:</li>
</ul>
<strong>Employment</strong>
<p>Equal rights for all workers, including lesbian and gay workers in both the private and public sectors;</p>
<p>The recognition of same-sex relationships for worker benefits (e.g. housing, medical aid and bereavement leave).</p>
<p><strong>Health</strong></p>
<p>The appropriate inclusion of the mental health needs of lesbian and gay people in all mental health programmes;</p>
<p>Addressing issues around lesbian and gay people in relation to HIV and AIDS and breast cancer for lesbian women.</p>
<p><strong>Family Rights</strong></p>
<p>Custody and access, maintenance, immigration and adoption rights for lesbian and gay persons and the recognition of lesbian and gay families;</p>
<p>Establishing the equal right to marry for people of the same sex.</p>
<p><strong>Youth</strong></p>
<p>The appropriate inclusion of lesbian and gay youth in all youth programmes, policy and law;</p>
<p>The protection of lesbian and gay youth from discrimination at home, at school, on the streets and in the media;</p>
<p>Equalizing the age of consent;</p>
<p>Running programmes to counter anti-gay prejudice and inclusive sexuality education programmes for all scholars.</p>
<p>Directs that lesbian and gay comrades should be included in the National Assembly list for the 1999 elections to ensure proper representation in the <strong>ANC</strong>.</p>
<p>Directs that all levels of the <strong>ANC</strong> should discuss with lesbian and gay comrades ways in which lesbian and gay equality can be promoted in the <strong>ANC</strong>.</p></td>

<em>Excerpt from a December 20, 1997, Press Release from the NCGLE</em><br />
<strong>ANC Sets Historic Standard on Gay and<br />
Lesbian Equality for All Political Parties</strong>
</div>
The <strong>National Coalition for Gay and Lesbian Equality</strong> salutes the <strong>ANC</strong> for its continued commitment to equality for all South Africans. In an historic and comprehensive resolution before its 50<sup>th</sup> Conference, the African National Congress has adopted a policy which includes amongst other issues:
<ul>
<li>the recognition of same-sex relationships including maintenance, custody, adoption rights</li>
<li>equal employment opportunities and benefits for lesbian and gay people</li>
<li>the protection of lesbian and gay youth from discrimination at home, at school, on the streets and in the media.</li>
<li>an equal age of consent</li>
<li>the equal right to marry</li>
</ul>
The <strong>ANC</strong>’s position on gay and lesbian relationships sets a new principled stand on equality for all political parties and civil society organizations. We challenge all political parties to adopt unequivocal and detailed policies in support of lesbian and gay equality in the same way that the <strong>ANC</strong> has done. Lesbian and gay voters will watch the policy statements of all the other political parties.
<p>The Coalition will launch its 1999 Election Platform early in the New Year — the <strong>ANC</strong>’s commitment has set the standard. Particularly important for translating the policy into reality is the Conference call that “lesbian and gay comrades should be included in the National Assembly list for the 1999 elections to ensure proper representation of all groups in the <strong>ANC</strong>.”</p>
<p>The <strong>ANC</strong>’s commitment allows all lesbian, gay, bisexual and transgendered people to celebrate the New Year with a sense of pride and dignity.</p>
