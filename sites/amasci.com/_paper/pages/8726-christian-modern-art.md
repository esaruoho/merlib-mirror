## Directories

The directories don't contain exclusively modern Christian art, but each has some.

[Christian Creative Arts](http://www.connect.ab.ca/~kwalden/arts.htm) -- Excellent site.

[RE and Religions Resources -- Religious Art and Photography](http://re-xs.ucsm.ac.uk/vademecm/reres/christ/religart.htm)

[This is a directory](http://www.christiandirectory.com/arts/arts.html) of Christian art put together by The Christian Directory.

[This is a directory](http://www.bc.edu/bc_org/avp/cas/relarts/artlinks.html) of Religious Art that includes Christian.

[The Omni-list of Christian Art](http://members.aol.com/clinksgold/omnart.htm#art) -- another directory of Christian art -- Not very large.

[Excellent -- "Christian Resource Index"](http://www.NLNnet.com/xian_art.html)

[The Christian Link directory](http://www.christianlink.com/art/)

[This is a list](http://www.allinone.org/) of Christian directories, many of which have art subdirectories.

[A Christian art site set up by Mountain View United Methodist Church Member Susan Brumbaugh.](http://www.aphids.com/cgi-bin/relres.pl?act=N4&S0=B6)

## Selected Sites

My goal is to cull some artists from the directories and put them here. If you find anything you think should be here, e-mail me the address and I will take a look at it.

New Sites:

["sacra iam splendent - a thousand lights their glory shed by lisa a. johnston" digitalari - contemporary photography and digital art gallery](http://www.digitalari.com/family/)

[Jeffrey Baker](http://www.jeffreybakerstudios.com.au) -- Interesting painting style making use of different panes and bright colors.

[Matthew Parrott](http://www.atabet.com) has some interesting use of digital technlogy. The G1 section has easily identifiable Christian images.

[Sackcloth Gallery -- Karen Howard](http://www.sackclothgallery.com/) -- Wonderful hand carved figurative compositions that are magical and executed precisely.

[Father Hugh Witzmann -- Bronze Sculptor](http://www.sja.osb.org/witzmann/)See the Sacred Sites section.

[JOHN AUGUST SWANSON](http://www.JohnAugustSwanson.com/) makes his home in Los Angeles, California, where he was born in 1938. He paints in oil, watercolor, acrylic and mixed media, and is an independent printmaker of limited edition serigraphs, lithographs and etchings. [](http://www.monsmart.gen.nz/)

Cornelis Monsma -- very attractive work influenced by Marc Chagall, but many have a freeer, more abstract style.

[Curtis Graphics](http://www.curtisgraphics.com/stgls.html) -- Not really thematic or overtly Christian, they do abstract stain glass and other mediums, aiming to bring one to a feeling of the holy.

[EMILIA CLEOPAS -- Very original and striking work. Stimulating and interesting text on website.](http://www.cleopas.com/)\

[Gothic Pueblo](http://members.tripod.com/~gothicpueblo/) -- Rewarding: finding your way around is sort of like the Myst game.\

[The Freedom Project -- Sixty-six monumental paintings](http://www.lefever.com/freedomV1/index.html) depicting the thematic essence of the 66 books of the Canonized Bible.\

[Yvonne Bell's Website](http://www.yvonnebell.u-net.com/yvonne/) Church vestments, icons and modern Christian art.\

[In His Image](http://www.christianart.net) -- Christian Sculptures -- Excellent online gallery of high detail figurative works.\

[Art for God](http://art4god.com/html/a4g.html%22) Figurative work, some in the classic Christian illustration style.\
