# Directions to Plaza on State

*Source page: `~cjh/plazamap.html`*

**Directions to Plaza on State Condominium - West Building\
102 State St. -- Kirkland, WA 98033**

Take the Kirkland/85th St. Exit from I-405 (Exit 18) and go west to the third traffic light (3rd Street - just after Peter Kirk Park and Wendy's). Turn left onto 3rd at the traffic light. At the next stop sign (Kirkland Ave - library is on your left and Grape Choice Wine Shop in on your right), continue through the intersection. 3rd Street has now become State Street and Plaza on State Condominium is on your left. The building closest to the intersection with Kirkland Ave. is the West Building, 102 State St. Do not be confused by the sign for the Ground Floor financial planning firm (U.S. Capital Strategies). The First Floor entrance to the condominium is off of State Street about 100 feet southeast of the corner, directly across the street from 1st Ave. S., a short street that looks like the entrance to a church parking lot.

The entrance to the building is across a courtyard, which you enter from the sidewalk on State Street. Park on the street or in the small parking lot between the East and West Buildings. If no parking is available, return to Kirkland Ave. and use the Municipal Parking garage under the library. There are two garage entrances: one located on Kirkland Ave. and one on 3rd St. You will need to walk back to State St. to enter the building. (The Kirkland Ave. entrance requires a key.) In the foyer of the building, look up the name in the directory and press the appropriate code on the keypad. If the line is busy, you may hear a message, but the lobby phone does not take messages. Try the code again after waiting a minute or two.\
