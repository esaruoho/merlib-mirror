<strong>— Cast —</strong>

<p>Racial types preferred: dark-skinned, dark hair; those who would have lived 4,600 years ago in what is now Iraq — people from around the Near East — Semitic, Egyptian, North African, and Asian descent.</p>
<p>There are at least 35 speaking roles — and hundreds of extras will be needed.</p>
<p><em><strong>Characters in Gilgamesh &amp; Enkidu</strong></em><br />
(listed roughly in order of number of words spoken):</p>
<ul>
<li>Gilgamesh - M: 25-35, huge, fit, acrobatic, sophisticated - major character [nudity, sex scenes]</li>
<li>Enkidu - M: 25-35, huge, fit, acrobatic, feral - 2<sup>nd</sup> major character [nudity, sex scenes]</li>
<li>Ninsun - F: 45-60, chief priestess - Gilgamesh’s mother</li>
<li>Utnapishtim - M: 15 - immortal, survived the great flood</li>
<li>Shamhat - F: 20-35, sex priestess [nudity, sex scene]</li>
<li>Ishtar - F: 35-45, heavy-set, Valkyrie-like (same actor as Siduri) - chief goddess of Uruk</li>
<li>Old King - M: 70-80s</li>
<li>Old Queen - F: 60-70s</li>
<li>Utnapishtim’s woman - F: 15 - immortal, survived the great flood</li>
<li>Urshanabi - M: 50-70s - the ferryman who aids Gilgamesh</li>
<li>Siduri - F: 35-45 (same actor as Ishtar) - Tavern-Keeper by-the-Sea</li>
<li>Scorpion-Being - M: any age - intensive makeup</li>
<li>Scorpion-Being’s Woman - F: any age - intensive makeup</li>
<li>City Directors - M &amp; F: 35-55s - (five needed)</li>
<li>Shepherds - M &amp; F (five needed - two have lines)</li>
<li>Servant - F: 30s - servant to Gilgamesh</li>
<li>Builders - M &amp; F: 20-35s - muscular</li>
<li>Humbaba - M: 30-50s - killer demon - intensive makeup</li>
<li>Ea - M: 50-90s - wisest of gods</li>
<li>Boys - M: 7-11 - two boys playing in the sand [nudity]</li>
<li>Bride - F: 16-20 - lovely, vulnerable [sex scene]</li>
<li>Shamash - M: any age - god of justice and patron of travelers</li>
<li>Anu - M: 80-70s - father of Ishtar and of all the gods</li>
<li>Aruru - F: 40-60s - mother of all humans</li>
<li>Groom - M: 17-21 - sweet, vulnerable</li>
<li>Soldiers - 17-35s - rough (at least 15 needed - a few have lines)</li>
<li>Enlil - M: any age - god of wind, earth, and destiny</li>
<li>Sin - M: any age - god of the moon</li>
</ul>
There are also scores of extras needed for the city, battle, and earthquake scenes.
<p>The script requires sex scenes for Gilgamesh and the bride, as well as for Gilgamesh and Enkidu.</p>
<p>Gilgamesh and Enkidu are naked at various times. There is a brief scene of two children who play at soldiers by themselves; they are also nude. Everyone else is costumed all the time.</p>
