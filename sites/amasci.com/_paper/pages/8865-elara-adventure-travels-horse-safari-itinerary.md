<em><strong>Day One: </strong></em><br />
Depart New York for Cairo 
<p><em><strong>Day Two: </strong> </em><br />
After arriving at the Cairo International Airport, your stay in Egypt will begin with a scenic 30-minute drive through the city to your hotel in Giza where you will stay for two nights. On your first night in Egypt, you will be introduced to the guides and other members of the support team who will be accompanying you on safari. </p>
<p><em><strong>Day Three: </strong> </em><br />
After breakfast, you will take a guided tour of the Giza plateau--site of the famous Giza Pyramids and of the Sphinx. You will have the opportunity to choose a horse and take a trial ride this afternoon. After dinner on your own, you're invited to join us on a short excursion or enjoy some free time before going to bed. </p>
<p><em><strong>Day Four: </strong></em><br />
After breakfast, you will check any extra luggage at the hotel before leaving on safari at 8:00am  from the Giza Pyramids. You will then travel by horse to Saqqara where you will have lunch en route to Dashur where you will shower, eat and camp for the evening. Total riding distance: 40 Kilometers. </p>
<p><em><strong>Day Five:</strong></em>  <em><strong></strong></em><br />
Your trek will continue with breakfast and a guided tour of both the Bent and recently-opened Red Pyramids in Dashur before leaving for El Lischt, the campsite for the evening.  You will stop for lunch en route.  After an opportunity to rest, you will have the opportunity to watch a desert sunset from atop a nearby hill.  A Bedouin-style feast will be prepared for you in the desert tonight!  Total riding distance:  35 Kilometers.  </p>
<p><em><strong>Day Six: </strong> </em><br />
You will have breakfast and depart for Meidum. This portion of the ride is a favorite among many riders due to the scenic sand dune vistas seen in this section of the Sahara. Lunch en route. Arrive the Meidum Pyramid--an unusual sight with a steeply-inclined tower rising from a low surrounding hill.  You will take a private tour of this pyramid after showering and having dinner. Total riding distance:  35 Kilometers. </p>
<p><em><strong>Day Seven:  </strong></em><br />
After breakfast, group members will depart Meidum for the Fayyum Oasis. In Fayyum, you will have the opportunity to take a <em>felucca</em> (sail) boat ride on the Lake of Quran before having lunch.  After lunch, you will be transported back to your hotel where you can reclaim your luggage and rest or shop during the afternoon.  We recommend resting--perhaps by the pool--we have a few surprises planned for you tonight! </p>
<p><strong><em>Day Eight:</em> <em> </em></strong><br />
Your agenda will include a guided tour of the Egyptian Museum and a walking tour of Old Cairo. We will also visit the Khan El Khalili bazaar. Come prepared to bargain!  You will also have the opportunity to go on an optional excursion, do some shopping or simply have free-time. On this final night of organized group activity in Cairo, you'll take a relaxing dinner cruise on the Nile River.  </p>
<p><em><strong>Day Nine:  </strong></em><br />
Time to explore a little bit more!  You'll visit Saqqara, the world's first "city of stone" and Zoser's Step Pyramid.  Lunch is followed by an excursion to Memphis, site of the world-famous statue of Ramses.  An afternoon tour of an Egyptian rug making school (where all work is still done by hand on looms) is followed by free time and dinner on your own.</p>
<p><em><strong>Day Ten: </strong></em><br />
Breakfast is followed by a farewell horseback ride and hotel check out. An optional tour of the Pharonic village or free time will be the highlight of your afternoon. Dinner is followed by tea time at the top of the Cairo Tower. Final departure.</p>
