# Mickey

*Source page: `~lisa/mickey.html`*

### A special friend, my cat Mickey died on November 20, 1998

<img src="images/mickey.jpg" data-border="0" width="300" height="228" alt="Lisa and Mickey" />

Mickey, my cat had a special personality of his own. He brought so much joy and happiness into my life. I was lonely after I lost both my parents and grandparents at 30 years of age. Mick and Jagger came into my life, and made my life so much richer. They were both purebred Sealpoint Himalayans, and so beautiful. Mickey had a special chin that jutted out, and the longest whiskers I'ver ever seen on a cat.

He had a special type of soul that you seldom find in people. He fought so much to defend his life, and even managed to get the dog collar off, so he could show us who killed him. I will never forget Mickey.

All the neighborhood children knew him by name, and he would eagerly let them pet him, and he loved rolling around in the sunshine. He followed me everywhere, but was very subtle about it. He always was at the corner of the bath to have his tea, or at the edge of the hottub, and I would kiss him and he would purr. Each cat has a special personality of its own. For this reason when a cat dies it can not be replaced in our heart by another one. My heart is grieving for the loss of my best friend.
