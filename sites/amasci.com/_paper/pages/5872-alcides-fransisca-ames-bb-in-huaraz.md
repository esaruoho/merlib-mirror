<td width="63%"><p>Alcides and Francisca Ames operate a really excellent guest house very conveniently located in the center of the Huaraz, in the heart of the Cordillera Blanca. They provide:</p>
<ul>
<li>Comfortable rooms with private showers and views</li>
<li>Hot showers, 24 hours a day</li>
<li>Free luggage storage, while you are climbing</li>
<li>Tourist, trekking and climbing information</li>
<li>Excellent local meals and delicacies</li>
</ul>
<p>Many of their visitors are climbers, and Alcides himself, besides being a professional geologist, is a very experienced climber. He also knows many of the guides, arrieros (burro drivers) and people in town. The B&amp;B is very close to the following:</p>
<ul>
<li>Casa de Guias (guide house)</li>
<li>Market</li>
<li>Taxi stand for transportation to mountain valleys</li>
<li>Hills for local jogging</li>
</ul></td>
