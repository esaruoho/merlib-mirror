# Wholesale Sales

Steel Silhouettes sells wholesale to gift stores, catalogs, lighting showrooms, specialty furniture shops, interior decorators and designers, and lighting contractors. Our products are used in homes, vacation homes, restaurants, and resorts.

We frequently work with designers to produce semi-custom or custom lighting fixtures and decorative accents for resorts and restaurants. Designers often specify our plaques for incorporation into furnishings made to their design, such as bedsteads and fireplace screens, when they are decorating resorts.

For gift shops, lighting fixture showrooms, and specialty furniture shops, we design regional product lines to appeal to customer preferences. Customers generally prefer items which remind them of their area's scenery, culture, and history. To respond to this, we have produced product lines for the Northwest, the Southwest, the Rocky Mountain states, the Midewest, the Great Lakes, the South, the Gulf Coast, the Appalachian Mountains, the Mid-Atlantic states, and the Northeast.

We also do what is called OEM work, that is to say that we cut figures for other manufacturers to include in stock products that they offer to stores for resale.

In some areas, we are represented by gift reps and lighting reps, working as a part of independent sales organizations. Please inform us if you are contacting us as a result of their work, so that we can credit them with the commission they have earned by making us known to you.

If you wish to purchase from us for resale, please contact us by phone, fax, E-mail, or by U.S. mail, for information about pricing, availability, terms, and delivery times.
