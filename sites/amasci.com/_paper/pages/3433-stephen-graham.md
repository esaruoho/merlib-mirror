<h2 id="work">Work</h2>
I currently work for the <a href="http://www.ee.washington.edu">Department of Electrical Engineering</a> at the <a href="http://www.washington.edu">University of Washington</a> as a Advising Program Coordinator. The majority of my duties involve handling the technical side of student advising: maintaining databases, managing admissions, creating and updating publications, and anything involved with creating, modifying and scheduling classes. I also do some direct student advising as circumstances dictate.
<p>Details of the past can be found in my <a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~graham/resume.html">resume</a>.</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="education">Education</h2>
I attended <a href="http://www.carleton.edu">Carleton College</a> for my bachelor's degree. My senior thesis in Physics was on the use of CCD devices for spacecraft navigation. I also majored in History, focusing on 20th century history and writing my senior thesis on the role of the German Communist Party in Nazi Germany.
<p>I went on to the <a href="http://www.hawaii.edu">University of Hawaii</a> for a MA in Southeast Asia, focusing on Burma. I also spent some time on World History. Unfortunately, advanced degrees in History require study of a foreign language. I spent two years torturing myself learning Burmese.</p>
