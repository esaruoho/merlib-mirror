<td><strong>Calamus Lovers: Walt Whitman’s working-class camerados</strong> by Charley Shively (1987), $12, Gay Sunshine Press, Box 410690, San Francisco, CA 94141. Letters, poetry and lively commentary document Whitman’s gay relationships.
<p><strong>Counseling Same-Sex Couples</strong> by Douglas Carl (1990), $19.95 (hard), W.W. Norton &amp; Co., 500 Fifth Ave., New York, NY 10110. Theories, issues and techniques for counselors.</p>
<p><strong>Friends and Lovers</strong> edited by John Preston with Michael Lowenthal (1995), $22.95 (hard), Dutton Press (Penguin USA) 375 Hudson St., New York, NY 10014. Whether forced out of blood families, or deciding to forge on their own, gay men describe how they created their own unique families. An anthology by writers that shows lives enriched by enduring relationships that are filled with compassion and love.</p>
<p><strong>Gay Relationships</strong> edited by John De Cecco (1981), $18.95 + $2.75 p&amp;h, Haworth Press, 10 Alice St., Binghamton, NY 13904-1580. How to find, choose a partner, solve problems, maintain relationship.</p>
<p><strong>Gay Relationships: How to form them, how to improve them, how to make them last</strong> by Tina Tessina, Ph.D. (1989), $12.95, Jeremy P. Tarcher, Inc., 5858 Wilshire Blvd., #200, Los Angeles, CA 90036. Guide for communications.</p>
<p><a href="guide.html"><strong>An Indispensable Guide for Gay &amp; Lesbian Couples</strong></a> by Steve Bryant &amp; Demian (1993), $8, Partners Task Force, Box 9685, Seattle, WA 98109-0685; (206) 935-1206. Facts, tips and insights on sex, job benefits, same-sex marriage, children, finances and legal planning; extensive resource list and sources of support. Click here for <a href="orders.html"><strong>Partners’ Order Page</strong></a>.</p>
<p><strong>Intimacy Between Men: How to find and keep gay love relationships</strong> by John H. Driggs, MSW &amp; Stephen E. Finn, Ph.D. (1990), $18.95, Dutton, 375 Hudson St., New York, NY 10014. Developing skills, working on self-fulfillment.</p>
<p><strong>Lesbian Couples: Creating healthy relationships for the 90s</strong> by D. Merilee Clunis &amp; G. Dorsey Green (revised 1993), $12.95 + $1.94 p&amp;h, Seal Press, 3131 Western Ave., #410, Seattle, WA 98121. Excellent, complete guide to building, sustaining relationships; wise counsel. Also on 60 minute audio cassette; $9.95 + $1.94 p&amp;h.</p>
<p><strong>Lesbian Passion: Loving ourselves and each other</strong> by JoAnn Loulan (1987), $11.95 + $1.50 p&amp;h, Spinsters Book Co., Box 410687, San Francisco, CA 94141. Includes “How couples can keep their sex lives exciting.”</p>
<p><strong>Love Between Men: Enhancing intimacy and keeping your relationship alive</strong> by Rik Isensee (1990), $11 + $1 p&amp;h, Prentice-Hall, NJ. Orders to: Simon &amp; Schuster, Mail Order Dept., Old Tappan, NJ 07675; (800) 223-2348. Guidelines on communications skills.</p>
<p><strong>Love Styles: How to celebrate your differences</strong> by Tina B. Tessina (1987), $9.95, Newcastle Publishing, Box 7589, Van Nuys, CA 91409. Self-help exercises for new and long-term couples; examples include lesbian and gay relationships.</p>
<p><a href="visible.html"><strong>Making Love Visible: in Celebration of Gay and Lesbian Families</strong></a> by Geoff Manasse and Jean Swallow (1995), $18.95, Crossing Press, Box 1048, Freedom, CA 95019. Photo essays by Geoff Manasse with interviews by Jean Swallow featuring same-sex couples and their extended families. A testament to love and commitment breaking all social boundaries. 176 pages. Available from <a href="orders.html"><strong>Partners’ Orders Page</strong></a>.</p>
<p><strong>The Male Couple’s Guide to Living Together: What gay men should know about living together and coping in a straight world</strong> by Eric Marcus (revised 1992), $14, Harper Perennial, 10 E. 53rd St., New York, NY 10022. Practical, clear, complete and supportive.</p>
<p><strong>Men Who Beat the Men Who Love Them: Battered gay men &amp; domestic violence</strong> by David Island, Ph.D. &amp; Patrick Letellier, MA, (1991), $19.95 + $2.75 p&amp;h, Haworth Press, 10 Alice St., Binghamton, NY 13904-1580; (800) 342-9678. A self-help and trainer’s/teacher’s guide.</p>
<p><strong>Naming the Violence: Speaking out about lesbian battering</strong> ed. by Kerry Lobel (1986), $12.95 + $1.94 p&amp;h, The Seal Press, 3131 Western Ave., #410, Seattle, WA 98122. Anthology by and for battered lesbians and those who support their empowerment.</p>
<p><strong>Partner and I: Molly Dewson, feminism and New Deal politics</strong> by Susan Ware (1989), $16.95, Yale University Press, 92A Yale Stn., New Haven, CT 06520. Political and personal biography of a woman and her 52-year lesbian partnership.</p>
<p><strong>Permanent Partners: Building gay &amp; lesbian relationships that last</strong> by Betty Berzon, Ph.D. (1988), $9.95, E.P. Dutton, 375 Hudson St., New York, NY 10014. Excellent comprehensive guide for both gay men and lesbians.</p>
<p><strong>Positively Gay</strong> ed. by Betty Berzon, Ph.D. (1992), $11.95, Celestial Arts, Box 7123, Berkeley CA 94707; (510) 845-8414. Includes: “Building Successful Relationships,” “Lesbian Couples in Particular,” and articles on being a gay or lesbian parent.</p>
<p><strong>Why Can’t Sharon Kowalski Come Home?</strong> by Karen Thompson &amp; Julie Andrzejewski (1988), $10.95 +$1.50 p&amp;h, Spinsters Book Co., Box 410687, San Francisco, CA 94141. Documents Thompson’s struggle to restore the dignity of disabled partner Kowalski, whose guardian parents have kept the couple separated since 1985.</p></td>
