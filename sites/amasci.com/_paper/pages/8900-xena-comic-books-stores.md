# Xena Comic Books - Stores

To find Xena comics, first off, try your local comic store. You may find a good local store in the list below. If you can't find a local store, or don't want to go to a comic shop, several of the stores in this list do mail order. Shop around for the best deals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## LOCAL STORES

These shops have a store you can visit, enter, and shop around in. I recommend trying a local shop before mail-order, you will usually get a better deal by default (no shipping) and you'll be supporting your own local economy as well as the Xena franchise.
