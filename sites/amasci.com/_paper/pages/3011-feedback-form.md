# Feedback to Eric

**Thanks** for taking the time to comment on my site and let me know how I can improve it. If your message concerns the format of the site, please mention your system settings (browser used, screen resolution, etc.) if possible. Those of you without forms-capable browsers can email me directly at <a href="mailto:ericj@eskimo.com" class="Local">ericj@eskimo.com</a>. Readers using the Lynx browser can send feedback using the 'c' (comment) command at any time.

No email addresses will be released to anyone other than myself (for the purpose of replying); I don't accept commercial email, so I won't subject you to it, either. The form below must be completely filled out, including the name and address fields; if you don't require a reply and don't wish to give me your address, use something creative.
