<td colspan="2"><p>The Liverpool school attended by John Lennon and George Harrison during their early years, is making ambitious plans to change the face of is Victorian yard. Dovedale Infants wants to create new resources to take it into the next millennium by developing both outdoor and in-school learning. George and John would have played on a boring stretch of concrete, surrounded by brick<br />
buildings dating from 1915, just round the corner from Penny Lane. The yard still has a couple of old air raid shelters, painted with a mural of the Fab Four !<br />
<br />
Now, a group of parents, teachers and governors at Dovedale have set up a Friends' Group (FODIS), as a registered charity to develop plans and raise funds to achieve them. They are focusing on the playground with plans for new quiet areas, beds for plants and vegetables, space for games and outdoor art and environmental resources to help work in the classroom. The school also wants to build a library and upgrade its computers. A very different prospect from the Fifties school of the Beatles years! Target funds have been set at 30,000 to turn the plans into reality.<br />
<br />
To kick off its plans, Dovedale is appealing to Beatles fans for any help they<br />
can offer. This could involve sponsoring areas of the playground , planted<br />
tubs or funiture, buying library 'bricks' or simply making a donation. FODIS<br />
has also launched a Roll Call of Honour to get in touch with former pupils and<br />
ask for their help. Early local publicity brought contacts from many local<br />
people, as well as BBC broadcaster Peter Sissons, an ex-pupil, and a lady now<br />
living in Cornwall.<br />
<br />
Donations should be sent to the following address and cheques made payable to<br />
Friends of Dovedale Infant School. Overseas donations should be made by<br />
Banker's Draft or International Money order payable in sterling and drawn on a<br />
UK bank.<br />
<br />
If you would like any more information about Dovedale School or its plans to<br />
develop into the 21st century, please write to Wen Williams, Headteacher,<br />
Dovedale Infants School, Herondale Road, Liverpool L18 1JX.<br />
</p>
