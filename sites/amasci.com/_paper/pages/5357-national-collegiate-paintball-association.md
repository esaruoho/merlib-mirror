4th Place: Trophy<br />
3rd Place: Bigger Trophy<br />
2nd Place: Even Bigger Trophy<br />
1st Place: Really Big Trophy, and possession of the College Cup until the next National Championships, along with your team name permanently added to the College Cup. (Photo soon)
<p>Wait a minute - you mean the only prizes are trophies?</p>
<p>For placing, the only prizes are trophies. We took all the support we had and let you play for free instead of getting stuff for the winners. HOWEVER... we are putting together a packet of free stuff for every club that attends, and may also have additional prizes which will be raffled off. (Each player gets a raffle ticket, so each player has an equal chance at winning the prizes regardless of how well you do.)</p>

<ul>
<li>A free admission pass for each player to <a href="http://www.challengepark.com" target="_home">Challenge Park Xtreme</a> on Sunday the 28th. This is good for regular rec play or playing in the 10-man exhibition.</li>
<li>A subscription to <a href="http://www.paintballsportsinc.com" target="_home">Paintball Sports International</a>.</li>
<li>Assuming it fits in my car, a carton of <a href="http://www.eat.com/express/yak.asp">Ragu Express</a> for each player, courtesy of Ragu. Yes, we're attempting to bribe your attendence with free food - good stuff. Hot pasta and sauce out of your microwave in 3.5 minutes flat, perfect for all of us cooking-lazy students.</li>
</ul>
