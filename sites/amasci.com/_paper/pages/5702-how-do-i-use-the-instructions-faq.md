## Top Reasons for Disqualification

So you are wondering what all those millions of people who were disqualified were doing wrong.\
According to an email I got from the operator of a [submission service](http://nh.ultranet.com/~kath1206/index.shtml) the top reason for disqualification before the letter even get's opened are

- The native country is not indicated on the envelope.\
  If your mailing address is in your native country, that country needs to appear **twice** on your envelope, like this:\
  **YourCountry**\
  YourName\
  YourStreet\
  YourCity\
  YourCountry
- The letter arrived outside the mail-in period.

The following is an excerpt from a post by [Matthew Udall](http://members.aol.com/MDUdall/ltryinfo.htm) to the usenet group misc.immigration.usa, giving the top reason for disqualification after already having been selected.

At the annual conference for the American Immigration Lawyers Association in June 1999; Mildred Patterson, the managing director for Visa Services at the Department of State, gave the top 3 reasons persons selected in DV2000 were later disqualified. \#1: The applicant failed to personally sign his or her application; \#2: the applicant failed to list all family members; and \#3: the applicant submitted more than 1 application to the lottery. She reported that someone from Ghana actually submitted 500 applications to DV2000!
