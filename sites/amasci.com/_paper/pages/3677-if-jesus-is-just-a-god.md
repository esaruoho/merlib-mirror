# If Jesus Is Just "a god"

<img src="rainbow.gif" width="576" height="4" alt="[rainbow]" />

\
We have a problem with the *New World Translation*'s rendering of John 1:1 calling Jesus "a god". The Bible tells us there is only one True God:

Deuteronomy 6:4, "Yahweh your God is the one, the only Yahweh"\
Isaiah 45:22, "For I am God and there is no other"\
Jeremiah 10:10, "Yahweh is the true God"\
Galations 3:20, "God is one"\
1 Timothy 1:17, "The only God"\
James 2:19, "God is one"\
1 John 5:20, "He is the true God"\
The Watchtower appears to agree with this:\
1984 brochure, *The Divine Name that Will Endure Forever* p. 20:  
"Of course, there is only one true God..."

1988 book, *Insight On The Scriptures Vol. 1* p. 969:  
"The True God Jehovah. The true God is not a nameless God. His name is Jehovah. (De 6:4; Ps 83:18) He is God by reason of his creatorship. (Ge 1:1; Re 4:11) The true God is real (Joh 7:28), a person (Ac 3:19; Heb 9:24), ..."

1988 book, *Insight On The Scriptures Vol. 2* p. 8:  
"In the Hebrew Scriptures the same word ('Elo him') is applied to Jehovah, the true God... The Hebrew may say the Elohim, the true God, in opposition to all false gods; but he never says the Jehovah, for Jehovah is the name of the true God only."

1989 brochure, *Should You Believe In the Trinity* p. 7:  
'Irenaeus, who died about 200 C.E., said that the prehuman Jesus had a separate existence from God and was inferior to him. He showed that Jesus is not equal to the "One true and only God," who is "supreme over all, and besides whom there is no other." "Clement of Alexandria, who died about 215 C.E., called Jesus in his prehuman existence "a creature" but called God "the uncreated and imperishable and only true God ..."

All other "gods" by definition are false gods:

1 Corinthians 8:5,6: "For even if there are so-called gods, whether in heaven or on earth (as indeed there are many "gods" and many "lords" yet for us there is but one God, the Father, from whom all things came and for whom we live; and there is but one Lord, Jesus Christ."

Galatians 4:8: "Formerly, when you did not know God, you were slaves to those who by nature are not gods."

There is no other true God:

Isaiah 43:10: "No God was formed before me, nor will be after me. I, I am Yahweh, and there is no other Savior but me."

Isaiah 44:6: "I am the first and I am the last; apart from me there is no God."

Isaiah 45:5, 6: "I am Yahweh and there is no other, there is no other God except me. Though you do not know me, I have armed you so that it may be known from east to west that there is no one except me. I am Yahweh and there is no other."

Isaiah 46:9: "for I am God and there is no other."

It follows that any man or spirit being that is called "god" cannot be true Deity. If so, any others called "god" are false deity.

John 1:1 of the *New World Translation* calls Jesus "a god". Reason says that Jesus is either the true God, Yahweh (or, Jehovah) or a false god, not true Deity. The 1982 book, *You Can Live Forever In Paradise On Earth* p. 40 calls Satan "a god". He, too, must be either the true God or false deity. The 1985 book, *Reasoning from the Scriptures* p. 213 calls human judges "gods". They also must be either the true God or false Deity. (Refer back to 1 Corinthians 8:5,6)

The apostle John, who wrote the Gospel of John, was a Jew. The apostle Thomas was also a Jew. Judaism is an extremely monotheistic religion that takes the Shema (Deuteronomy 6:4) seriously, and strongly teaches the belief in only one True God. For John to have called Jesus "a god" in John 1:1 and meant him to be another true God would have been unthinkable. The same goes for Thomas calling Jesus "my God" in John 20:28.

So, if Jesus is a true God, he must be the only true God, any other form of deity would be false. The problem is, if Jesus is a false god, we are following a false god. If Jesus is another true God, we have two true Gods. We believe the Bible emphatically states there is only one true God. How do we resolve this?
