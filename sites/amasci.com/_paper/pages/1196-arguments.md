&#10;<h1 id="the-case-against-advertising">The Case Against Advertising</h1>
What's Wrong with Commercializing the Public Schools?
<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 50%" />
<col style="width: 50%" />
</colgroup>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Harming Students</strong> Effective ads will cause students to desire things that are not necessary for their well-being, or may even be harmful to their well-being. Most products that advertisers pitch directly to students are either marginally harmful (fast food, soda pop) or unnecessary and therefore wasteful of resources (expensive shoes, blemish cream).</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Opposing Parents</strong> Ads create perceived needs in youth for products their parents do not want them to have or cannot afford in the family budget; in this, the district is working in direct opposition to the parents' goals and values.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Disproportionality of Negative Effects</strong> Creating a desire for expensive products that children do not need will harm poor families most.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Distraction</strong> We want you to focus on learning. A commercialized environment distracts their attention.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Loss of Trust</strong> We want youth to trust their school staff. Endorsing products which youth know are not good for their bodies, good for the environment, or good for human rights lowers staff credibility.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Incompatible Missions</strong> The mission of a corporation is to make money. The mission of a school is to educate students. The mission of advertising is to influence how or what people think. The mission of education is to teach people to think for themselves.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Slippery Slope</strong> Schools which rely on sugar daddies instead of a fair and equitable source of funding will become dependent...and unable to 'just say no'.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Corruption</strong> To allow advertisers to enter schools is negligence; to get a kick-back for this is corruption.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Waste of Taxpayer Dollars I</strong> Every time school secretaries and/or teachers spend time sorting through their junk mail from corporations, they are wasting taxpayer dollars.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Waste of Taxpayer Dollars II</strong> Every time students and teachers spend class time watching infotainment and television commercials provided by Channel One, they are wasting taxpayer dollars and opportunities for real education.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Incompatible with District Goals</strong> Time wasted by students processing commercials and ads in schools is time taken away from learning. This is counter-productive to building a world-class learning system.<br />
Advertising fashion and beauty items runs counter to the goals of developing girls' self-esteem, teaching them to value their health and fitness more than conventional beauty, and to have serious work/career aspirations. What does the district want girls to be?</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Public vs. Private</strong> The public has paid for public institutions, to serve the public interest. Elected official have no right to sell off portion of the public institutions to private interests. The state has a mandate to fund public education. Parents deserve to have a choice between public and private schools for their children, rather than a choice between privatized and private schools.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Wrong Signal to Legislature</strong> Getting revenue from ads can only undermine current efforts to convince the legislature that we need full funding of education.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td data-valign="top"><img src="Images/starRed.gif" width="25" height="25" alt="[o]" /></td>
<td data-valign="top"><strong>Ignores Public Opinion</strong> All public polls in recent years have stressed 'back to basics' and providing a safe, orderly learning environment. Trying to generate funds to pay for after-school sports and other activities is not putting first things first. Adding ads to the school environment will distract students' focus from learning.</td>
</tr>
