Yosemite National Park and vicinity. Automap Road Atlas relief map mode.

Forest service campgrounds were not selected, else you would see many more campgrounds. You can click on a campground and see details (price, facilities, etc). The campground stuff is from the Destination Campground add-on.

More cities and roads can be requested, but this area is relatively sparse. You can zoom in closer to any region (down to a few square miles). The relief map requires the CD be mounted. Without, you get a flat map.
