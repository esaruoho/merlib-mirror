**The Ultimate in <u>Precision</u> and <u>Accuracy</u>**

**Precious gemstones, nearly flawless facets, maximum precision.   ** Did you ever think you'd hear those words in one breath?  Imagine gazing into a deep "London Blue" topaz or lively red ruby, your faceted creation and seeing natural light bounce back to you as fiery fingers of radiance.  **GemMaster II can make this a reality. \**

**\***One piece reinforced casting of aluminum strontium alloy for greater rigidity and non-warping base.\
\*Major parts previously fabricated of steel are now made of non-corroding stainless steel.\
\*Improved motor control design gives higher torque at low polishing speeds.\
\*Polyurethane belts for longer life and quieter operation, needs no adjusting.\
\*Powdercoat finish for easy cleaning and longer lasting beauty.\
\*Sealed throughout to keep abrasives from entering and wearing bearing surfaces and bearings.\
\*Water container (with cover) and splash pan made of stainless steel.\
\*A "no backlash" cheater.\
\*Aluminum parts are hard coat anodized to reduce wearing.
