**The Center for the Defense of Free Enterprise has received the following release from the U.S. Justice Department:**

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">U.S. Department of Justice\
United States Attorney </span> <span style="font-size:
12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">\
Western District of </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> Washington\
700 Stewart Street\
Seattle, Washington 98101 </span> <span style="font-size:
12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> </span>

<span style="font-size:
12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;;text-transform:uppercase">FOR Immediate RELEASE: </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">\
December 8, 2005 </span> <span style="font-size:
12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> </span>

<span style="font-size:
12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">SIX ARRESTED IN CONNECTION WITH </span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> NORTHWEST ECO-TERROR ATTACKS  </span><span style="font-size:
12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">\
Defendants allegedly tied to fires in </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> Washington and Oregon dating back to 1998. </span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Six people were arrested yesterday in a coordinated law enforcement effort that spanned five states and is the culmination of nine years of investigation. All six people have been indicted by federal grand juries in </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;
  font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Oregon</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> or Washington. The charges relate to four separate arsons in the Northwest dating back to 1998, and the destruction of a Bonneville Power Administration tower at the Millennium. </span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">STANISLAS GREGORY MEYERHOFF, 28, was arrested in Charlottesville, Virginia, where he was attending </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:
  &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Piedmont</span><span style="font-size:
 12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Community college. DANIEL GRRARD MCGOWAN, 31, was arrested in </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;
  font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">New York</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">, NY. MEYERHOFF and MCGOWAN were indicted in </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;
  font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Oregon</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">, for the January 2, 2001 arson at the Superior Lumber Company, in Glendale, Oregon, and the May 21, 2001, arson at the Jefferson poplar Farm in Clatskanie, Oregon. The damages in each of those fires exceeded \$1 million. MEYERHOFF and MCGOWAN face maximum penalties of life imprisonment if convicted. </span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">KEVIN M. TUBBS, 36, was arrested in </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;
  font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Springfield</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">, Oregon. and WILL1AM C. RODGERS, 40, was arrested in </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;
  font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Prescott</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">, Arizona. Both men are indicted in the western District of Washington for the June 21, 1998 arson at the Animal and Plant and Heath Inspection Services (APHIS) facility in Olympia. WA. Damage from this arson was estimated at \$1.2 million. Both TUBBS and RODGERS face a maximum penalty of 20 years in prison if convicted. </span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">SARAH KENDALL <span style="text-transform:uppercase">HARVey</span>, 28, was arrested in Flagstaff, Arizona, where she was a student at </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:
  &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Northern</span><span style="font-size:
 12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Arizona University. HARVEY is indicted in Oregon for the December 27, 1998 arson at U.S. Forest Industries in Medford. Oregon. That fire caused an estimated \$500,000 in damage. </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;
  font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">HARVEY</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;"> faces a maximum penalty of 20 years in prison. </span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">CHELSEA DAWN GERLACH, 28, of </span> <span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:
  &quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Portland</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;
 font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">, </span> <span style="font-size:
  12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">Oregon</span><span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">, is charged with two counts related to the December 30, 1999 destruction of a Bonneville Power Administration power transmission tower near Bend, Oregon. GERLACH faces up to 25 years in prison if convicted of Destruction of an Energy Facility (Conspiracy) and Destruction of an Energy Facility.</span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">These indictments and arrests were the result of a nine year investigation of numerous arsons in the Northwest and other states. In many of the fires the Animal Liberation Front (ALF) and Earth Liberation Front (ELF) claimed responsibility. Participating in the extensive investigation were the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI}, the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms & Explosives (ATF), the Eugene Police Department, the Portland Police Bureau, the Oregon State Police, the U.S. Forest Service, the U.S. Bureau of Land Management, the U.S. Department of Agriculture, the Oregon Department of Justice and the Lane County Sheriff's Office. The investigation is continuing.</span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">An indictment contains allegations that have not yet been proven at trial beyond a reasonable doubt. Defendants are presumed to be innocent unless and until proven guilty. </span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">The defendants are having their initial appearances in the districts where they were arrested. Government prosecutors will ask that they be brought to the districts where the charges were filed for arraignment. </span>

<span style="font-size:12.0pt;font-family:&quot;Times New Roman&quot;">The Western Washington indictments are being prosecuted by Assistant United States Attorney Andrew Friedman. </span>
