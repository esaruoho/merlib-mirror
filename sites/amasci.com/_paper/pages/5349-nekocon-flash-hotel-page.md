<td data-valign="top" width="367"><h3 id="hotel-information">Hotel Information</h3>
Animecon going is once again returning to the wonderful hospitality of the Holiday Executive Center in lovely Virginia Beach, Virginia. (Birthplace of Katsucon and NekoCon) The HIEC has held more animecons than any other hotel east of the Mississippi River. During the year we were away from the hotel, it has gone under a slight transformation. A new look for a new Millennium, while still retaining the laid back and casual atmosphere that our attendees and guests have come to enjoy year after year.<br />
&#10;<p><u>Nightly Rates</u> <strong>$79.00</strong> per room per night for Single to Quad Occupany.<br />
This rate will available starting the Thursday night before the convention through to the Monday morning after.<br />

## Overflow Hotels

**Hampton Inn - 5793 Greenwich Road (757)490-9800**\
122 units. Kings & doubles avail. - free continental breakfast - exercise and weight room - pool - CC/TV - non-smoking rooms

**Courtyard by Marriott - 5700 Greenwich Road (757) 490-2002**\
146 units. A/C - restaurant - lounge - pool - exercise room - whirlpool - CC/TV

Both hotels are next door to the Holiday Inn Exec Center.\
Note: There is no con rate for the overflow hotels but they are the same price or cheaper.
