<td colspan="2" data-valign="TOP" width="80%" data-bgcolor="#ffffff"><p><strong>I got back late last night, and thought about making a quick entry then...</strong></p>
<p>...but decided to be domestic for a while, instead. Mike and I haven't spent much time away from each other since when we first met, and being away five days was plenty for me.</p>
<p>It was a long week. It's been months since I've worked regularly, and getting up early every morning, gussying m'self, getting breakfast, and walking in the cold to the office is not something I'm used to anymore. Overall, though, it was a good time.</p>
<p>Got there early Monday morning, checked into the <a href="http://portland.citysearch.com/profile/8459987/">Mallory</a> (a wonderful place to stay, I might add), then found my way downtown to the office by cab. We spent the day training, talking, and had a congenial business lunch at the <a href="http://portland.citysearch.com/profile/8459675/">Southpark</a> where I had an excellent chicken tagine. More training, and then I walked back to the hotel... and really, I'm not used to walking much anymore, especially not in girly shoes. I was huffing and puffing by the time I arrived in my room - ugh, I am so dreadfully out of shape!</p>
<p>Earlier in the day I'd made all sorts of plans for where to go for dinner. There are a huge number of great restaurants in downtown Portland, and I'd picked out a few I thought I'd try... but first, I wanted to go to <a href="http://www.powells.com/cgi-bin/partner?partner_id=23956">Powell's</a>. It's been years since I've been there, even though it's really not that far away and a worthwhile day trip from Washington.</p>
<p>(By the way... if you use the Powell's website to order books, you might consider using the <a href="http://www.powells.com/cgi-bin/partner?partner_id=23956">link containing the partner ID</a> of the store worker's union. The workers will get a kickback that way, and they deserve it. In other words, use the above link for your Powell's bookmark, eh?)</p>
<p>I drove over to Powell's and braved their parking lot, not realizing that there wasn't an elevator for the lot. Ouch. Powell's already has a huge number of stairs, and adding those in the lot didn't help any. I didn't keep track of the time I was there, but I know it was at least a couple of hours. I found several out of print and hard to find scifi books, and when I left it wasn't because I didn't have other things to look for, but simply because I was tired. Powell's would be well-advised to add some seating here and there for people to rest - after all, the store is so huge that it's easy to get overwhelmed and over-tired while wandering in there.</p>
<p>Eventually I made it back to the Mallory and discovered their dining room closes early. I looked through my list of prospective dining locations... and realized they were all a $5-$10 cab ride away. I didn't want to drive myself (I don't know Portland all that well in the daylight, and in the dark, well...), and it seemed silly to spend so much money to just get to those places in the first place. The fellow at the front desk suggested that I use room service, so I shombled up to my room and took a look at the menu.</p>
<p>And really, I'm glad I did. I was able to unwind, turn on the television and veg out for a while, kick off my shoes, and order an excellent steak perfectly prepared and delivered to the comfort of my room. Yeah, room service is expensive, but still less so than a cab ride to those other place. I was a happy girl.</p>
<p>(A shomble, by the way, is a shuffling womble. Just so's you know.)</p>
<p>(Oh, and a womble? That's a sort of side-to-side casual amble.)</p>
<p>(Really!)</p>
<p>Anyhow, the rest of the week was full of lots of training and room service. I never did get out to any of those other restaurants. I had room service for breakfast and room service for dinner, and I got to eat while I watched Buffy on Tuesday, and I was comfortable and smiling. It was a tiring week of training and my brain is over-full, the driving down, back, and around was tiring as well... and I'm glad to be home!</p>
