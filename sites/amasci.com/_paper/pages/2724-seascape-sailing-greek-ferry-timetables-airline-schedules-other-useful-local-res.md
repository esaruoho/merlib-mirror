<p><strong>Domestic flights</strong></p>
<p><a href="http://www.olympic-airways.gr">Olympic Airways</a> - the main domestic carrier in Greece, and the only one who flies to "our" islands, in the Dodecanese. Their full <a href="http://www.olympic-airways.gr/flights/oaflttimseauk.htm"></a>schedule and <a href="http://www.olympic-airways.gr/flights/oafltfardomuk.htm">rates.</a></p>
<p><strong>Greek ferries</strong>. The ferry lines which offer services to/from Athens and the Dodecanese are:</p>
<ul>
<li>DANE Sealines</li>
<li>GA Ferries</li>
<li>Blue Star ferries</li>
<li>NEL lines - a fast service to Kos and Samos</li>
</ul>
<div class="expander" alt=" More about ferries to the Dodecanese ... ">
More about ferries to the Dodecanese ...
</div>
<div class="hintbox" alt=" More about ferries to the Dodecanese ... " style="DISPLAY: none" title="Read more about ferries to the Dodecanese">
In the summer months, there is always either a DANE or a GA ferry to Leros (sometime both) which leave Athens between 2pm-4pm daily; it is approx 10 hours to Patmos, 11 hours to Leros, 12-14 hours to Kos. From Leros to Athens, there is a ferry which leaves around 9-11pm every day in the summer.
<p><a href="http://www.greekferries.gr/domestic.htm"><strong>Blue Star</strong></a> is a large ferry company who have just added the Dodecanese to their other extensive services around the Aegean. Their boats are fast, very comfortable, even have a swimming pool on deck! From Athens to/from Leros is only 8 hours. Unfortunately, they only have 2 boats a week Athens-Leros at the moment. However, their ferry on Sat night Athens-Leros coincides very nicely with our rendezvous time on Sunday am.</p>
<p><strong>Useful times:</strong><br />
Athens-Leros:dep Pireus Sat 11.30pm, arrive Leros 8.10am Sun. Blue Star ferries<br />
Leros-Athens:Dep Leros Sun 9.45pm, arrive Pireus 7.10am. Blue Star ferries<br />
Athens-Samos: Every day from Pireus at 5pm, arrive Samos 11.45pm. NEL ferry lines<br />
Samos-Athens: Every day from Samos at 7.30am, arrive Athens 2.45pm. NEL ferry lines<br />
Kos-Athens: There are many ferries between Kos and Athens; check out our timetable links for full details.</p>
<p>Online booking is available for Blue Star and NEL ferries; for GA it is coming "real soon now"; DANE ferries can only be booked at local agent/ticket office. If you are going to book online, allow sufficient time for your ticket to be airmailed to you; you cannot pick it up at the port !</p>
<p>Booking a cabin is not expensive, and highly recommended.</p>

<strong>Ferry Schedules to/from Athens and the islands</strong>
</div>
<p> </p>
<a href="http://www.greekferries.gr/domestic.htm">Greekferries.gr</a> - Blue Star, GA &amp; NEL schedules<br />
<a href="http://www.gtp.gr/RoutesForm.asp">Greek Travel Pages</a> - very complete schedule, be sure to select Pireus (TZE) PORT (Athens) to get all departures!<br />
<a href="http://www.greekislands.gr%22">Paleologos</a> - a full agency booking service based in Crete; they have a good search engine for ferries all over Greece. Also flights to/from Crete.
<p> </p>
<strong>Local ferries and hydrofoils</strong> around the Dodecanese and nearby islands:
<p>There is an extensive network of smaller ferries and hydrofoils which connect the Dodecanese island chain. Not all of these are detailed online, but most are shown on the <a href="http://www.gtp.gr/RoutesForm.asp">GTP</a> site.</p>
<div class="expander" alt=" &lt;i&gt;Note: &lt;/i&gt;Summer schedules 2003 ... ">
<em>Note:</em> Summer schedules 2003 ...
</div>
<div class="hintbox" alt=" Summer schedules 2003 ... " style="DISPLAY: none" title="Read more about summer schedules 2003">
Summer schedules may not be shown for some services until March/April; but as a general guide, there are regular hydrofoils running between Samos-Patmos-Lipsos-Leros-Kalymnos-Kos-Rhodes every day. There is also a fast catamaran which has a daily service between Rhodes-Kos-Kalymnos-Leros-Lipsos-Patmos every day.
