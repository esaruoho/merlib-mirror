     Fished the Naches last week, caught 3 fish, largest was a 13 inch beautifull 
    cutthroat. Fishing seemed slower then usuall for this time of year. River was 
    in excellent shape above the Tieton R. The Tieton was running brown. Fish 
    came to a small grey hackle after i had given up on my caddis imtitations.
       Sunday i fished for pinks on the Snohomish. I stuck right around Langus 
    park for about 2 hours. This was my first time flyfishing for pinks. Didnt 
    see any jump and none caught. It was so good I am going again thursday.
     Two weeks ago I fished Icicle creek and saw lots of flyfisherman. I only 
    caught small fish, was wondering if anyone here fishes it and if there are 
    any larger fish available.   Tight lines!    G
