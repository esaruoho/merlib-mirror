<td colspan="2" data-valign="TOP" width="80%" data-bgcolor="#ffffff"><p><strong>Buffy and writing bits.</strong></p>
<p>Little bits of snow falling are steadily outside my window. There are a couple of remaining protected patches from over the weekend, as well. It doesn't look as if the new snow is sticking... just not cold enough. Last night, though... brrr! I stepped out for a minute to test the hottub water, thinking I'd take a dip, but it was so chilly I decided against it.</p>
<p>It's Tuesday, and that's a good thing all in itself. Tuesday is Buffy Day, and I believe there's a new episode on tonight, too. Gimme Buffy and Julie is a happy girl.</p>
<p>Although not particularly healthy, yet. Ah well.  Headachy, stomack icky, and so on.</p>
<p>Today I've done some actual work, calling around looking for spaces where I can do some testing. Hospitals, schools, etc.</p>
<p>That done, I've brought out the book again. It's sitting there on monitor screen (well, underneath the LiveJournal client window, at least), calling to me. I also dug up Chuck's story <em>Daedalus</em> to do some editing on it. It's on the third draft so I don't expect it to need much fixing at all.</p>
<p>Trim, trim, trim... pare away those unnecessary words. I found my notes from the night I read the first part of <em>Beyond the Call</em> at writer's group, and am going through them and fiddling with the suggested bits. I also tried going from the beginning and replacing as much passive voice as I could with active.</p>
<p>At some point I'm going to have to call it good for a while, though, and start writing new parts. Earlier I took out an unecessary 9,000 words, and now at 41,000 words I still haven't come as far in the story as I like. And, of course, as I admitted at writer's group: I'm far too nice to my characters. So I'm trying to add roadblocks into the plot, here and there.</p>
<p>My mantra: if [insert unnamed author here] can do it, so can I.</p>
<p>I know I can finish this. I've paid good money at bookstores for scifi drek that's not half as good as what I've got down so far. If they can finish their drek, so can I.</p>
<p>So can I.</p>
<p>(And to those of you working on novels: so can you!)</p>
