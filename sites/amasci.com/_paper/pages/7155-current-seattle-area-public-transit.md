<td data-valign="TOP" width="73%">Seattle currently has a good mass transit system.<br />
<br />
Seattle is located in King County. King County Metro is a public transit system consisting almost entirely of buses. The only exception is the Waterfront Streetcar.<br />
<br />
Metro uses a very good mix of various types of buses providing both local and express service throughout King County. A bus tunnel under Downtown Seattle is used by many of the buses, providing faster trips through the downtown core and reducing the number of buses on surface streets.<br />
<br />
The next county south of King County, Pierce County, also has a public bus system called Pierce Transit. It provides service within Pierce County, including its largest city, Tacoma.<br />
<br />
The county immediately north of King County, Snohomish County, has a public bus system called Community Transit.<br />
<br />
The largest city in Snohomish County, Everett, has its own bus system.<br />
<br />
In addition to the county and city systems, there is a regional transit authority known as Sound Transit. Sound Transit operates a number of express bus routes and limited commuter rail service between Seattle, Tacoma, and Everett. They also operate a short light rail line in Tacoma and construction has begun on a longer light rail line from Downtown Seattle to Tukwila, just south of Seattle.<br />
