<td><strong>Whilst driving in Austin, at or about 2:30am (at the time, my schedule was nocturnally oriented, so fatigue was not an issue) I was driving home along a main street in my not-so-small hometown. North-bound on South Lamar, and of course, by myself, I saw something crossing the road ahead of me. Make no mistake, the area is very well lit, and was so at the time. What I saw crossing the road looked like a human figure, crawling. I started to slow down from 45 mph, as I was 200 feet or so away, and I didn't want to hit anyone. As I drew closer, the figure accelerated it's movement, going from a slow crawl to what I can only call a sprinting crawl, and proceeded into a field of waist-high grass. We've all seen horror movies, and there was absolutely NO chance of my beating the grass for some wounded (or not-wounded) critter. Description: Approximately 6 feet in length, four limbs, no clothing, well muscled. Pointed ears which exceeded the top of the head by not much, if at all, but the ears were easily identifiable. Hands had slightly longer fingers than normal humans (Michael Jordan sized) and was moving on them. Skin was visibly sagging on the upper-arms, near the torso. I didn't get a clear look at the feet. Movement: It's progress was the same as a person would climb a ladder, if one could climb so quickly. Arms reaching forward, legs swinging out past the frame of the body, and moving opposing arms and legs in slightly staggered timing. Very fluid, exceptionally quick. As I said, I was 200 feet away, closing at 45 mph, and by the time I reached where it had been, it was gone. Anyone else seen anything like it? Performed an autopsy on one?
