# JBM - PM - Changes

***7 December 2002***\
Initial upload.

***20 December 2002***\
Modified the atmosphere `mode` to including corrected and uncorrected pressure. Fixed a couple of bugs in wind (no wind entries incorrectly caused an error) and BC interpolation routines.

***24 January 2003***\
Added millimeters to allowable units.

***19 March 2003***\
Validated HTML documentation

***13 April 2003***\
Added two routines for sorting ballistic coefficient and wind data.

***7 April 2004***\
Changed calling convention to stdcall.
