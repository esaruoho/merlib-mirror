> <span class="lead">A</span> quick word about non-rated anime -- this can run the gamut between kids-fare shows that just didn't happen to have a rating on the packaging I found, to adult-fare shows with sexual situations or graphic violence. I won't list the X-Rated shows (since that's a rating) or shows that would have had an X rating based on graphic sexual content. Some of the below may contain nudity or off-screen sexual content (sounds); I'll try to give warnings to folks looking for content ratings in those cases.
