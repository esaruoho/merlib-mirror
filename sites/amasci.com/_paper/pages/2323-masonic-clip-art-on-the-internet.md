# Masonic Pictures and Emblems

- From Blue Lodge:
  - [the Entered Apprentice](EA/oMain.html)
  - [the Fellowcraft](FC/oMain.html)
  - [the Master Mason](MM/oMain.html)
  - [Officers' Jewels](Offs-Jewels/oMain.html)
  - [Square and Compasses](Sq_Com/oMain.html)
- Symbols from [Other Bodies](Other/oMain.html), and Miscellaneous.
  - [Other Appendant Bodies](OAB/oMain.html)
  - [Eastern Star](OES/oMain.html)
  - [Youth Groups](Youth/oMain.html)
- Information on the design competition for the [US Capitol](http://lcweb.loc.gov/exhibits/us.capitol/s2.html), including a relief sculpture of the [cornerstone ceremony](http://lcweb.loc.gov/exhibits/us.capitol/seventy.jpg).

Some of these graphic collections may be to large to be viewed well over a slow modem. If you wish to view this archive <u>with</u> preview images, go [here](main.html).

These images are in the .gif format. If you desire these images in any other format, I would recommend using the following graphic converters: [LViewPro](ftp://ftp.cdrom.com/pub/simtelnet/win3/graphics/lviewp1b.zip) for Windows 3.x, [LViewPro](ftp://ftp.download.com/pub/win95/multimedia/lviewp1d.zip) for Windows 95, or [GraphicConverter](ftp://wuarchive.wustl.edu/systems/mac/info-mac/gst/grf/graphic-converter-25.hqx) for Macintsosh.

Adapated from Bro. Gary Dryfoo's Masonic Images and from Bro. Roger Ingersoll's web page (<http://www.chrysalis.org/masonry/>).
