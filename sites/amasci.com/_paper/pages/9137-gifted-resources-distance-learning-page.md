# Gifted Resources Distance Learning

## Special Resources for Gifted and Talented Education

There are distance learning opportunities particularly targeted to Gifted learners:

- EPGY at Stanford offers remote courses for Gifted Students. The Educational Program for Gifted Youth can be reached by phone at 800-372-3749 or 415-723-0512, or by [FTP server,](ftp://epgy.stanford.edu/pub/EPGY) or by [Gopher server,](gopher://kanpai.stanford.edu) or by [WWW Web Page,](http://www-epgy.stanford.edu/epgy/) or by postal mail to:

           Stanford EPGY
           Ventura Hall
           Stanford CA 94305-4115

  Here is an [EPGY Information Brochure](http://kanpai.stanford.edu/epgy/pamph/pamph.html)

- EPGY at Stanford offers Course Sequence in Structured Individualized Learning (CSSIL). CSSIL is designed to meet the needs of students who are not sufficiently challenged by their classroom mathematics courses or who want the opportunity to do additional work in mathematics in an individualized, though structured, way, but who do not meet the stringent admission requirements of EPGY. Information is available by phone at: 415-329-9925, or by email to: ravaglia@csli.stanford.edu, or by [FTP server.](ftp://epgy.stanford.edu)

- Duke's Learn on Your Own offers academically talented students an opportunity to take accelerated courses in their hometowns throughout the year. Info available by phone at 919-684-3847 or by postal mail to:

           Duke TIP
           Learn on Your Own
           PO Box 90747
           Durham, NC  27708-0747

- Johns Hopkins Center for Talented Youth offers Expository Writing Tutorial-by-mail. [The Writing Tutorials](http://jhunix.hcf.jhu.edu/~ewt2) are accessable by WWW. Information is also available by phone at 410-516-0337. They also offer [many other programs](www.jhu.edu/~gifted/acadprog/dlp/dlpmain.html) including talent searches, summer programs and other distance learning programs.

           Distance Learning Project partnership EPGY and CTY
           East Coast USA phone: 410-516-0241
           West Coast USA phone: 818-500-9034

- Northwestern University [Center for Talent Development](http://ctdnet.acns.nwu.edu/mark/ctdinfo.html) offers research info and programs, including Letter Links Learning, correspondence courses for academically talented students grade 6 to 12. [Letter Links Program information](http://ctdnet.acns.nwu.edu/mark/lll.html) is available by WWW, as is their [Centerpoint magazine](http://ctdnet.acns.nwu.edu/mark/summer_progs/centerpoint1_1_home.html). Information is available by phone at: 708-491-3782 or by postal mail to:

           Northwestern University
           Center for Talent Development
           Northwestern University
           2003 Sheridan Road
           Evanston, IL  60208

- [The Home Education Network](http://www.then.com) On-line courses from UCLA Extension for business, writing, test prepartion.\
  The Home Education Network (THEN) offers distance learning on-line education through Internet courses in business management, organizational behavior, risk management, accounting, screenwriting, fiction writing, test preparation and teacher certification. THEN on-line courseware is offered in conjunction with the UCLA Extension University. At-home study courses can be completed on-line or through employers and an affiliated Intranet training and distance education network.

- [Virginia Satellite Education Network](http://www.pen.k12.va.us/go/VDOE/Technology/VSEN) offers language and AP classes. Currently enrolling students from 20 states and expanding their services. Contact Greg Weisiger at 804-692-0335.

- Center for Academic Precocity at Arizona State University offers summer and school year programs and talent identification. [Information is available on WWW.](http://www-cap.ed.asu.edu)

- Center For Independent Study at the University of Missouri-Columbia offers challenging courses for high performing students. Information is available by phone at: 800-877-0536.

- The international science olympiads (initiated by UNESCO) are especially targeted at talented children (senior pupils at secondary schools). There is a permanent secretariat for the [International Olympiad in Informatics(IOI)](http://www.win.tue.nl/win/ioi)

- [International Olympiads](http://www.win.tue.nl/win/ioi/misc/scolymp.html) are available for Biology, Chemistry, Informatics (Computer Science), Mathematics and Physics.

## Other Resources

There are other distance learning opportunities of interest:

- [Class.com](http://www.class.com) is a fully accredited high school offering internet based remote study.

- National Distance Learning Database (formerly hosted by University of Kentucky at telnet://ndlc.occ.uky.edu) is a national listing of distance learning programs with a searchable catalog of over 14,000 programs. Now avaiable by [WWW access](http://www.fwl.org/edtech/dlrn.html)

- Distance Education Clearinghouse from University of Wisconsin. This is available as a [web page](http://www.uwex.edu/disted/home.html) or as a [gopher server.](gopher://uwex.edu)

- [UK Distance Learning Database](telnet://acsvax.open.ac.uk) as hosted by International Centre for Distance Learning, Open University, United Kingdom. Use "ICDL" as username to start a search.

- [SERC](http://www.scsn.net/~serc/) - a provider of high school, middle school, and staff development courses. It is a consortium of 24 state departments of education, that provide the curriculum expertise, and public television stations, that provide the production expertise.

- [The Digital Education Network](http://www.actden.com) is a Canadian-based project offering online courses for students in grades 7 through 12.

- [New Brunswick Canada](http://tenb.mta.ca) tele-education site describes distance education available. Also an interesting web site.

- [Los Angeles County Office of Education TEAMSnet.](http://teams.lacoe.edu) TEAMSnet is an instructional service of Los Angeles County Office of Education's TEAMS Distance Learning Project. This is a federally funded Stars Schools project and offers live, interactive distance learning programs via satellite, cable, ITFS, or PBS television in math, science, history, social sciences, English, and language arts.

- University of Washington distance learning. Contact by phone at 206-543-2350 or 800-543-2320 or email to instudy@u.washington.edu

- [List of Distance Education Links](http://cee.indiana.edu/surf/ed_tech.html) from Indiana University, School of Education.

- [GNA - Globewide Network Academy.](http://uu-gna.mit.edu:8001/uu-gna/documents/catalog/.html)

- CASO Internet University [listing of 700+ courses](http://www.caso.com) available online.

- [California Distance Learning Project (CDLP)](http://www.otan.dni.us/cdlp/cdlp.html) a cooperative effort with the San Francisco Bureau of CNN.

- Mind Extension University - cable and video classes (phone: 1-800-777-6463)

- Grade 12 Marketing, Data Processing and other classes. [Available through Langley school web site in British Columbia, Canada.](http://www.sd35.bc.ca/sd35/welcome.html)

- National Consortium for Secondary Schools Specializing in Mathematics, Science and Technology

           NCSSSMST
           1429 Senate Street
           802 Rutledge Building
           Columbia, SC 29201
           Phone: 803-734-8385

- [City University](http://www.cityu.edu) - (Bellevue, WA) provides on-line courses.

- Nebraska Independent Study High School

      Independent Study High School
      Nebraska Center for Continuing Education
      33rd & Holdrege Streets
      Lincoln, NE  68583-0900
      Phone: 402-472-2175

- [Eugene Public School District](http://www.4j.lane.edu/CyberSchool/CyberSchool.html) in Eugene, Oregon USA is offering high school level credit classes over the Internet for students with email and World Wide Web access.These CyberSchool classes are open to students worldwide with no tuition or fee. However, space is severely limited. Contact Tom Layton at layton@4J.lane.edu for more information.

- [Cyber High School](http://www.webcom.com/~cyberhi) is a private high school providing complete instruction over the Internet.
