# Very old Egg Cup from Grandma Hemry

## She earned  the money for the cup by doing several menial jobs.  Kay can remember I don't. This probably cost her about 10 cents at the time but she worked and bought everyone a little christmas gift of some kind. 

<div align="center">

No matter how poor she was.  And that was poor.  But she managed to send several of her kids to college.\
\

</div>

Grandma was very religious and some of her "refined" kids who were college graduates or successful business people would  cringe when she got a little carried away with her religion in our family gatherings which Kay and I used to host.  Or perhaps Frank and Reta might have us over at Thanksgiving.  I think they were embarrassed on my account as I was new to the family.  But it didn't bother me at all as my mother was just as much wrapped up in her religion as Grandma Hemry and  could have shown Grandma a few things.,\
\

<div align="center">

This cup was given to Kay and me early in our married life.\
