# COLT WOODSMAN PARTS

I usually have some parts for sale on Ebay. <a href="http://cgi6.ebay.com/aw-cgi/eBayISAPI.dll?MfcISAPICommand=ViewListedItems&amp;userid=colt22.com&amp;include=0&amp;since=-1&amp;sort=3&amp;rows=25" target="LINK">Click  <img src="ebay_my_button.gif" data-border="0" width="88" height="31" alt="MyEbaySales" /> here</a>  to see my Ebay parts auctions.

I have a large selection of parts for all models in the Woodsman line, including barrels, grips, springs, slides, sights, triggers, sears, etc., and I will sell items that are excess to my personal needs. If you need replacement parts, please write or email your needs and I'll get back to you with pricing and availability. Telephone calls for parts are generally not a good idea, because I never know exactly which parts I have until I go search through them.

To identify the part you need, you must first determine whether your Colt .22 auto is a first, second, or third series, then follow the corresponding link below to get a part number. I will need to know the series and part number. There are also differences within each series, so an approximate serial number (including any alphabetical prefix or suffix, and leading zeroes, if any) and model name will help me identify the correct part.
