**Junior Polishing Set**

**(1)  JUNIOR POLISHING SET** - This unit was designed to meet the needs of an individual craftsman. Motor speed can be varied for use with various grinding, polishing, and buffing wheels. Hood catches polishing debris, keeping area clean.

**Features:**

**Accessories**

\*

1/15 hp. 5000 rpm motor

\*

2 1/2" muslin buff for tripoli

\*

Electronic variable speed control

\*

2 1/2" cotton flannel buff for rouge

\*

Operates on 120V, AC

\*

Tripoli compound

\*

14" wide x 6" deep x 7" high

\*

Red rouge

\*

Attractive hammertone finish

\*

Two bristle wheel brushes

\*

Mounted on rubber feet\
to prevent creeping.

\*

Two felt cones

\*

Two emery ring shells

\*

One wood mandrel for ring shells

\*

Two tapered spindles for motor
