<td data-valign="top" width="100%">Let's get this over with: This is an ego-driven enterprise. Bad enough this Steve guy puts up his own Web pages, but now hes making everyone read about him. Well, then dont read it. (Or, even better, read it and then criticize it! <a href="mailto:mohundro@eskimo.com">Tell me how self-centered I am</a>.)
<p>The basics: Im from Seattle, born and raised, leaving only to get my degree in journalism from <strong><a href="http://www.wwu.edu/">Western Washington University</a></strong> in Bellingham, WA. Since graduation, Ive worked in and out of the tech industry. I am now a resident of Seattle's Ballard neighborhood, where I frequent coffee shops, music stores, and bowling alleys. In my spare time, I read books and comics, play with my two kittens Lucy and Linus, listen to music, go on walks, go to live music shows, watch TV, go to movies. You know, the usual.</p>
<p>To keep myself <em>pop</em> cultured, I turn to a number of online forums to help me find my way, including <strong><a href="http://www.fametracker.com/">Fametracker</a></strong> for general celeb talk and <strong><a href="http://www.televisionwithoutpity.com/">Television Without Pity</a></strong>, which helps me sort out good TV, bad TV, and TV thats so bad its good. I lurk on other online forums as well.</p>
<p>I like the rain, a sure sign of a Seattle native, yet don't own any Gore-Tex™. I am Steve, and am at least a bit more interesting than this text indicates.<br />
</p></td>

<td data-valign="top" width="100%">The "Friends" list is a relic of the mid-90s personal Web page mania. In those heady days, everyone (and sometimes their pets) had Web pages, so you put up a link to your friends' pages. Unfortunately, while many net-savvy folks have either a blog or online journal, having your own URL is no longer universal.
<p>I also found that I was leaving out people that I do consider friends, from <a href="http://www.livejournal.com/users/kent_s/friends/">people on LiveJournal</a>, to co-workers, to former co-workers, to neighbors. The people I did have on this list only go to my Web pages to see if I've libeled them, yet, and it's a pain to keep updated. So, the list is short because until you folks get vain enough to have Web sites, I won't put you here:<br />
