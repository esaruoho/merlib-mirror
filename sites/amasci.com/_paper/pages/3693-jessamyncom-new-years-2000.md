<td>So, what did you do New Year's Eve?
<p>Well, I had dinner with some friends of mine and we ate baklava and couscous and rotisseried meats until we could no longer move. Then I got on the bus and headed to Capitol Hill. I met up with some friends from work and some friends from the bookstore and some friends from around town. There were people in monkey suits [you know the movie 2001] and spaceman suits and lots of miscellaneous cosumes. I was wearing an Odd Fellows robe and had made some <a href="../../00pix/monolith.jpg">monolith magnets</a> for the occasion. The monolith did not make an appearance, elusive bastard.</p>
<p>Then the <a href="http://infernalnoise.org/">Infernal Noise Brigade</a> arrived on the scene and made some of their oh-so-lovely noise The crowd was reaching several hundred people by this time and starting to get fidgety. Lots of people with megaphones. <a href="http://www.chupacabrapolicia.com/">Chupacabra Police</a> in full effect. Seattle police on the perimeter, and their portable pokey van.</p>
