**Ordering information :**

**All Knives are Guaranteed for standard uses.\
Tantos have stronger points than Fighters.\
Boyer Blades have a dual hardening process\
with a hard cutting edge and a spring temper spine.\
If you break or bend one, I will repair it or send a replacement.\
(Due to the hardening process I can not guarantee my knives if thrown.)\
All black powder coated knives will have a LIFETIME FREE POWDER COATING SERVICE, just send me the blade and a return shipping fee and I will re-powder coat and re-wrap the handle for free!**

**Custom orders require a 25% deposit for parts and labor, if a delay is necessary\
