Crystalite Diamond Discs and Wheels\
Peripheral Metal-Bond Diamond Wheel\

<div align="center">

**(1)  <span style="text-transform: uppercase"> Crystal Rings </span>-** A full diameter wheel, accurately machined with a 1" aluminum bore bushed 3/4", 5/8" and 1/2". The wheel core is a lightweight high strength glass reinforced ribbed nylon. The 6" and 8" are designed to nest any Crystalite 5" or 6" disc as a combination roughing and fine grind unit. Designed to run on conventional cabochon units and arbors.

**(2)   <span style="text-transform: uppercase">Spherical Miracle</span>** - Designed with a cutting face having the same convex radius as the wheel radius creating a compound cutting surface. Unique features are: more cutting bite; will grind a spherical depression; high visibility of contact - good for cutting to an outline. All 6" x 1 1/2" with 1" bore plus 3/4", 5/8" and 1/2" arbor bushings. ShpWt 2 1/2lbs.

**(3)  <span style="text-transform: uppercase"> Diamond Back </span>**- A peripheral metal bonded diamond wheel that features a surface pattern representing a compromise between Crystalites standard wheel and the turbine wheel. Has a very aggressive interrupted cutting surface that will maintain its sharpness. Available in 6 & 8" wheels, 1 1/2" wide. 1" bore bushed for 3/4", 5/8" and 1/2". Contains 1/3 more diamond than Crystal Rings, producing a longer lasting wheel with higher cutting rate.

**(4)   <span style="text-transform: uppercase">Turbine Wheel </span>-** A diamond wheel of unique design with a sawtooth profile. As the crests wear, sharp fresh diamond is exposed. The fastest roughing wheel ever devised for preforming all hard stones on grinding machines. Bore is 1" bushed to 3/4, 5/8, and 1/2". Recommended speed; 1,500 to 2,000 rpm.
