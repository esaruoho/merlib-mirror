# Psyche and the U.S. Election: What Happened?

<span style="font-size:18.0px"> </span>Come join us for the conclusion of our series of community conversations on "Psyche and the Spirit of the Times." After an exhausting election season, the results will (hopefully) be known on November 3rd. Two days later, we will meet with a panel of Jungian-oriented thinkers who will help us frame the results of the election and give their view of its effect on the American and world psyche. Panelists include therapist George Callan, professor Randy Morris, and artist Jerry Wennstrom. Bring your own observations and conclusions about the election. There will be plenty to talk about.
