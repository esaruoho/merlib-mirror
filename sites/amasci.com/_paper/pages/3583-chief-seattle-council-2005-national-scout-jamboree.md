### Chief Seattle Council 2005 Jamboree - Kickoff Meeting Information

**The Jamboree Kickoff Meeting will be held October 23, 2004. The location is not yet determined. This is a required event. Every Scout must attend, along with at least one parent or guardian. Scouts should be in full uniform. This means the official Scout shirt, Scout pants, Scout socks, and Scout hat.**

Check-in is between 9:00 a.m. and 9:20, with the program beginning at 9:30 a.m. sharp! Plan to be there early! There will be a break with light refreshments between the main meeting and the troop break-out sessions. The event will be over by 1:00 p.m. at the latest.

At the Kickoff Event you will be provided with a LOT of information on what to expect leading up and during the tour and the Jamboree. You'll learn about Health and Safety issues, equipment, details about the tour, scheduled training, and council-wide, pre-Jamboree activities.

You will also have your first chance to meet as a troop, conduct some initial troop business, and receive schedules and information about where and when your troop will have pre-Jamboree meetings.

Even though the adult leaders will have been working on the Jamboree for over a year already, this event marks the start of the ramp-up to the Jamboree experience for everyone. It will be an interesting, informative, fun, exciting day!

### Chief Seattle Council 2005 Jamboree - Required Pre-Jamboree Campout Information

**The National Office requires every Scout and Adult attending the National Jamboree to attend a pre-Jamboree training event. Ours will be held Saturday and Sunday, May 21-22, 2005. It is a national requirement that you *<u>MUST</u> attend this campout in order to be eligible to go to the Jamboree, so makr it down now.***

Note that neither weekend creates a conflict with other major holidays. Mother's Day weekend is May 8th, 2005, and Memorial Day weekend falls on May 28-30, 2005. In addition, we have condensed the training in order to finish in two days instead of the recommended three full days.

There are a number of things that need to be done before the Jamboree that are accomplished at the "shakedown" campout:
