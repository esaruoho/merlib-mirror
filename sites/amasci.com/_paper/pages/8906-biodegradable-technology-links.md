This field is currently exploding. Doing a search on the word "biodegradable" will now show several dozen serious companies adding resources.\\

NOTE: I realize many of these links have probably gone down or have been changed. However, if you do a search on the article words you may find its new location, or if you simply take off the far right part of the address down to the first "/" and hit return, and then if that doesn't work try to take off the second, you may find the main site still there and the article somewhere else on it. I've found simply copying the title of the article and pasting it into a search engine to be the best way to find outdated pages. I will leave some bad links up so you can at least know the article exists. Good luck and send us any updates you may have.
