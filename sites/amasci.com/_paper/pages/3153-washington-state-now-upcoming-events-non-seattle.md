## <span id="NOWEVENTS">Upcoming Events</span>

(note: if your chapter events aren't listed here, [send email to Will](mailto:feminist@eskimo.com?subject=WANOW-ADD-state-events) or tell him the URL for your web, gopher, or ftp document.)

**November 3**, Tuesday, **GENERAL ELECTION**, EVERYWHERE IN WASHINGTON. Did you check the NOW/PAC and WA NOW/PAC [Endorsements (click here)](wastnowpac.endorsements.html)? No on I-200, No on I-694, Yes on I-688, VOTE!

**November 12**, Thursday, **Seattle NOW Board Meeting**, 7:00pm, Good Shepherd Center, Conference Room 221, Seattle, WA. Election Day Update - what are our gains and losses? Open to all members and interested individuals.

**December 8**, Tuesday, **Seattle NOW Annual Meeting and Elections**, 7:00pm - 9:00pm, [Seattle Central Community College](http://edison.sccd.ctc.edu/), Room 1110 (south end of campus), Seattle, WA. 6:30pm Meet and Greet (new members). Potluck and Fun! A great opportunity to get involved!

**December 17**, Thursday, **Seattle NOWSLETTER Editorial Meeting**, 7:00pm, Good Shepherd Center, Seattle NOW office (Rm 222), Seattle, WA. Articles and volunteers are welcome.

**January 8, 1999**, Friday, **Seattle NOWSLETTER Mailing Party**, 7:00pm, Good Shepherd Center, Conference Room 221, Seattle, WA. Munchies of all kinds welcome!

**February 7-9, 1999**, Sunday-Tuesday, **WOMEN'S LEGISLATIVE WEEKEND**, Olympia, WA. Coordination by [American Women's Work](http://www.aww.org/), Business and Professional Women, Church Women United & Others.

**April, 1999**, TBA, **Northwest Regional NOW Conference**, Place TBA, Washington. For more information, contact [Linda Tosti-Lane](officers.html), Regional Director. Pencil it on the calendar now and start planning to go and split driving and sleeping costs.

**May, 1999**, TBA, **WA State NOW Conference**, elections, and board meeting, Place TBA, WA. For more information, contact [Linda Tosti-Lane](officers.html), Regional Director. All NOW members or potential NOW members are invited to join us for a really **fun** time. SAVE THE DATE!

**July, 1999**, TBA, **1999 National NOW Conference**, Place TBA. *Can't say you didn't have adequate warning. Start saving now.* Preceded by July National Board Meeting. To vote at this conference, you must be elected at your chapter as a delegate in advance. Contact [Linda Tosti-Lane](officers.html), Regional Director, to ask questions about the conference.

### <span id="OTHEREVENTS">Non-NOW Events:</span>

(note: if related events aren't listed here, [send email to Will](mailto:feminist@eskimo.com?subject=WANOW-ADD-state-events-other). Also, if other organizations have Home Pages, send the URL so it can be added to the text.)

**November 21**, Friday, **WA NARAL's Liberty Ball Auction**, 5:30pm, UW HUB Ballroom, Seattle, Tickets \$48, \$67, or \$100 (volunteer and it's free). You could bid on a Guided trip to Nepal, a night at The Inn at Langley, Two Nighta on the Beach, or eight hours of Electrical Work (hundreds of items). So check the [WA NARAL website](http://home.earthlink.net/~wanaral), [email WA NARAL](mailto:wanaral@earthlink.net) or phone 206-624-1990.

**November 25 - December 10**, **"Eighth Annual Campaign: 16 Days of Activism Against Gender Violence**. Contact Linday Posluszny, 16 Days Coordinator, Center for Women's Global Leadership, 160 Ryders Lane, Rutgers University, New Brunswick, NJ 08901-8555, fax 732-932-1180, or [cwg@igc.apc.org](mailto:cwg@igc.apc.org?subject=16Days(WANOW)) .

**April 23-25, 1999**, **Lesbian Rights Summit:** a feminist strategy session in Washington, D.C., summit sponsored by NOW. For more information call 202-221-0066 extn 760 or check the [www.now.org](http://www.now.org)

**March 31 - April 2, 2000**, **Feminist EXPO 2000 for Women's Empowerment**, at the Baltimore Convention Center in Maryland. You can't miss this historic event! For more info, contact the Feminist Majority Foundation at 703-522-2212 or [femmaj@feminist.org](mailto:femmaj@feminist.org?subject=EXPO2000(WANOW)).
