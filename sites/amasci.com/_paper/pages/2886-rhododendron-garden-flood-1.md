<td width="50%"><p>Record rains flooded the gravel paths overnight and slows Doreen down in our morning walk.</p>
<p>When the paths were created, all the topsoil in them was dug out and added to the beds to raise them. A four-inch drain was laid in the center of the excavation and surrounded by rocks. Coarse gravel and then fine crushed stone topped off the rocks. In normal times, excess water flows out through the drains. When floods like this come, the paths themselves become the drain conduits, keeping standing water away from nearly all the rhododendrons. Within a few hours, the paths will be clear.</p></td>
