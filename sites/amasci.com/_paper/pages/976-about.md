# about

*Source page: `~ables/cabincam/about.html`*

The Cabin Cam is really something I did for myself. My house is at about 7400 feet in the foothills west of Loveland but I work in Fort Collins. I can check to see what the weather is doing at home and make an informed decision about whether or not to try to get home at any particular time!

It also gives my out-of-town friends the opportunity to virtually sit out on my front deck. So pour yourself a cold one (or a hot one if it's winter!) and put your feet up. In the summertime you won't even have to deal with the bugs!

I keep two days worth of pictures, "today" and "yesterday." Much past that I don't really care what happened. I'll put anything interesting in the [Cabin Cam Archive.](archive/)
