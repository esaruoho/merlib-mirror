# Wenatchee Mt. Flying Seminar in June, 2000

*Source page: `~c180tom/wmfc.html`*

Here are some photos from our latest Wenatchee Mt. Flying Seminar.

This is one of the four ground school classes, required before flying, but anybody is welcome to attend. Thanks to Wings of Wenatchee, who kindly provided free facilities and support.\
![](wmfgs.jpg)

Here's Rich Alldredge's beautiful PT-18, on it's way to the Sugarloaf mountain course. (Yes, the instructor did have survival gear, in spite of the casual looking T-shirt. Yes, it was a little cool at 7500' over Mt. Howard, but the Stearman did learn about finding lift.\
