# Kids in the Hall

## May 1992 - September 1995

------------------------------------------------------------------------

These pages describe KitH's formative years with Sanjay and Ert. The show was revived in 1996 with Marc and Sanjay, but then stopped production again.

Back when the world was young and happy there began a wonderful Canadian Indie/Pop music show called Kids in the Hall on WMBR. Then the Shadows came and KitH, both the [TV](http://wwwvms.utexas.edu/~barbcarr/index.html) and radio shows, vanished.

KitH was originally hosted by [Ert](http://www.eskimo.com/~ert), who moved to Houston, and [Sanjay](http://www.mit.edu:8001/people/sanj/home.html), who was crushed by a graduate programme.
