[C-Mites at Carnegie Mellon](http://www.cmu.edu/outreach/cmites.html) offers a regional talent search as well as weekend and summer enrichment programs for students in 3rd through 6th grade in Pennsylvania. Regional talent searches are conducted annually in the US to identify gifted students throughout the nation. Typically, seventh grade students qualify for the Talent Searches through scoring in the upper 3% on a nationally normed test. Some programs also search for younger students. Qualified students may be invited to participate in summer residential programs or commuter programs or take advantage of other academic opportunities. Talent search programs are interrelated: Qualified participants from one region may apply for the programs offered by others.

These programs offer identified gifted students the opportunity to enroll in intensive, fast-paced courses in the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, mathematics, and computer science and to participate in numerous enrichment activities. Some programs offer students fast-paced courses where students complete a year-long course in a few weeks, or college level courses for credit. They also offer college- and career-planning assistance.
