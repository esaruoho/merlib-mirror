# The Gear

------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### ADAM-Two Fender Stratocasters(70's models) One is fire-engine red\
and the other pink with psychedelic paisleys earning Adam the\
nickname Rudy.\
The Strats' feature Seymour Duncan Hot Rails pickups. He runs them\
through an early 70's MXR Compressor, distortion box of the week,\
into a Marshall 50 Watt head-driving an old and heavy Kustom 4-12"\
cabinet. No tuner, various picks and strings.\
Adam is an avid guitar, ukele, record and 8-track collector.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Kustom Challenger (2-10") or a 60's VOX (2-10"). For more\
beef he uses a an old Fender cabinet.\
picks-Herco. Strings Ernie Ball 77mm. Korg tuner.\
Static collects musical instruments including-banjos, recorders,\
saxaphones, drums and Schwin Stingrays.
