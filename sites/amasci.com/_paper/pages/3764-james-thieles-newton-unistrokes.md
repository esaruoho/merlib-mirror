# **James Thiele's Newton Unistrokes**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Unistrokes is a writing system developed at Xerox PARC specifically for stylus based computers. A Postscript version of the paper and lists of strokes is at <ftp://ftp.parc.xerox.com/pub/unistrokes/>\
A [read me](README.Unistrokes) for Unistrokes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Unistrokes version 1.1 can be accessed in the following compressed formats:

- [unistrok.zip](unistrok.zip) (13K)
- [Unistrokes11.sit](Unistrokes11.sit) (15K)
