<td data-valign="top" width="580"><h1 id="the-farmhand-song">The Farmhand Song</h1>
by Harper apprentice <a href="../bartal.html">Bartal</a>
<p><em>(Sung to the tune of Monty Python's "Finland")</em></p>
<hr />
Bartal and the other apprentice launch into a duet of rich chords and a rising line that eventually steps down to a medium pitch. With a look at the other, Bartal starts to sing,
<p><em>"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand<br />
On the land where I quite want to be<br />
Plowing, tilling and planting<br />
Or just shooting the breeze..."</em></p>
<p>"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand<br />
The cooking is the way it should,<br />
Be it breakfast or dinner<br />
Or snack lunch in the Hall..."</p>
<p>Bartal's fingers fumble a moment as he reaches the bridge. As the chording from the second apprentice flows with those of Bartal's, he begins to sing again,</p>
<p><em>"Always so well protected<br />
By the Weyr during Fall<br />
Manning ground crews with the others<br />
It's no bother at all..."</em></p>
<p>Returning to the key of the beginning strains of the song, Bartal hums along for one verse and then launches back into song,</p>
<p><em>"Farmhold, farmhold, farmhold<br />
Grubbed land is the best land to work<br />
With hands calloused and dirty<br />
Around every bend, vistas lurk."</em></p>
<p>With the other apprentice chiming in, he ends the song with the lines,</p>
<p><em>"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand<br />
It's the job for me."<br />
</em></p>
<p>"Farmhand, farmhand, farmhand<br />
It's the job for me."</p>
