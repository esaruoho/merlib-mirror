<p>Welcome to Iloilo on the Web! We are here to help you find new friends on the net.<br />
Browse our collection of links, check out our msg board, or participate in our real-time<br />
chat. </p>
<p>#iloilo is one of the most progressive channels on Undernet and still planning to extend<br />
up to the edges of Undernet.org! #iloilo is the new home of ilonggo's abroad! They don't<br />
have to call back home for news, events, etc. All they have to do is to log on to<br />
Undernet and join #iloilo! Chat is more flexible than talking, you can express yourself<br />
through text w/ colors and sounds, and what the heck! You can even send pictures!!!<br />
Isn't that cool?! </p>
<p>Join us now! You have nothing to lose! By joining us, you will gain more friends and<br />
share the fun! Members? NO we don't need members, we need friends! After all FRIENDS<br />
MAKE THE WORLD GO ROUND! Logon to any Undernet server and /j #iloilo now!</p>
