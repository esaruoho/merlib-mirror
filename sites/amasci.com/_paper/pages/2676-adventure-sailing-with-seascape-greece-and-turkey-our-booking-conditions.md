<strong>Booking procedure:</strong>
<p><span lang="EN-US">We will hold an option for one week whilst you decide if you would like to confirm a booking. After a week, it will cancel automatically unless otherwise agreed.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span></span></p>
<p> </p>
<p><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt; mso-bidi-font-family: Times New Roman">To confirm your place, a 25% deposit is required. This can be paid with visa, mastercard or personal cheque.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span> Cheques should made payable to SeaScape Sailing Pty Ltd.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span> Balance is required 90 days before departure. We reserve the right to cancel bookings not fully paid at this time. </span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US">Please note that on our shareboat program, we cannot always guarantee in advance which of our yachts you will be on. We do our best to accommodate personal preferences, and normally are able to do so; but private group bookings may sometimes affect which yachts are available for our shareboat schedule.</span></p>
<p> </p>
<strong><span id="Cancellation_policy">Cancellation policy:</span></strong>
<p><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt; mso-bidi-font-family: Times New Roman">1)<span style="font-style: normal; font-variant: normal; font-weight: normal">      </span> Cancellations 6 months before departure are refunded in full.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt; mso-bidi-font-family: Times New Roman">2)<span style="font-style: normal; font-variant: normal; font-weight: normal">      </span> Cancellations between 3-6 months before start of trip are refunded in full, less a $100 per person administration fee.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt; mso-bidi-font-family: Times New Roman">3)<span style="font-style: normal; font-variant: normal; font-weight: normal">      </span> 90 – 45 days before departure, 50% of total cost is refunded.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt; mso-bidi-font-family: Times New Roman">4)<span style="font-style: normal; font-variant: normal; font-weight: normal">      </span> 45 days before departure, no refunds.</span></p>
<p><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt; mso-bidi-font-family: Times New Roman">SeaScape will always attempt to rebook any last minute cancellations, and according to our discretion, may offer a future trip credit or refund up to the amount we have recovered.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span> We always do our very best to help out if factors arise out of your control, and given enough notice, frequently are able to do so.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span> However, please understand that if you cancel at short notice <em>for whatever reason</em> and we cannot rebook your space, then we must adhere to our cancellation policy as above.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span> <em>We strongly recommend you take out travel insurance.</em></span></p>
<p> </p>
<strong><span id="Booking_changes">Booking changes</span></strong>
<p>We will always do our best to accommodate requests for date changes, given suitable availability. There is no charge for this.</p>
<p> </p>
<strong><span id="Personal_responsibility">Personal responsibility</span></strong>
<p>A sailboat offers limited personal space; an easy-going, cooperative team spirit and respect for others on board are essential to the enjoyment of all.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes"> </span> We reserve the right to terminate the vacation of any client who refuses to comply with instructions from SeaScape staff; or whose behaviour is deemed to be causing distress, damage, danger or annoyance to other clients. Upon such termination, our responsibility for your vacation ceases and we shall not be liable for any extra costs incurred.</p>
