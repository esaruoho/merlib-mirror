| [*KinFolks Korner Genealogy Services*](http://www.kinfolkskorner.com) is primarily involved in Genealogical research concerning people in Central Illinois. However, we also engage in projects that concern other parts of the country. At present our on-site research activities are concentrated in and around Champaign/Urbana and Champaign County, Illinois. However we still maintain extensive resources and contacts for researching East Texas and Pacific Northwest ancestors, and we will travel to any location in the United States if the size of the project warrants. In the course of our research we visit libraries, courthouses, the Federal Archives, Family History Centers, and even a cemetery once in a while.  |
