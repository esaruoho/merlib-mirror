## BOOKS

There are several books available from the Stanly Co. Genealogy Society. Prices might have increased (if anyone orders a book and the price is different from what is listed below, let me know).

- 1800-1840 Montgomery Censuses (no longer available--out of print)
- 1850 Stanly Census (\$5 + \$2 s & h)
- 1860 Stanly Census (\$5 + \$2 s & h)
- 1870 Stanly Census (\$15 + \$2 s & h)
- 1880 Stanly Census (\$15 + \$2 s & h)
- Marriage Records 1851-1904 (\$15 + \$2 s & h)

Records of the Court of Pleas and Quarters, 1841-1850 (\$12 + \$2 s & h)

The Society also published Abandoned Cemeteries of Stanly County, NC but they no longer have any copies available for purchase at this time.

Back issues of the Stanly Co. Genealogy Society Journal are \$2 per issue

     

    The address is:

     

    Stanly Co. Genealogy Society

    P. O. Box 31

    Ablemarle, NC 28002-0031

    In addition, there are two volumes of cemetery records (not published by Stanly County Genealogical Society) which can be purchased from:

    Lina J. Owens Boyd

    P. O. Box 222

    Dover, Arkansas 72837

    Each volume is $15 + $2 s & h

     

There is also a 1850 census book published by SK Publications, which contains photocopies of the handwritten records on the microfilm and an typed index in the back of the book. So it is different from the 1850 census transcription produced by the Stanly Co. Genealogical Society.\
[SK Publications](http://www.skpub.com/genie/census/nc/stanly.html)

Lea Morris has published two Montgomery Co. census transcriptions. 1860 Montgomery Co. census (\$20 which includes s & h) 1880 Montgomery Co. census (\$20 which includes s & h) Her address is: Lea Morris 139 Dark Springs Mt. Tr. Troy, NC 27371 email: <lea@ac.net>
