<td><h1 id="facts-figures-about-dieting-weight-loss" data-align="CENTER">Facts &amp; Figures<br />
About Dieting &amp;<br />
Weight Loss</h1></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<ul>
<li>The average American woman is 5'4", weighs 140 lbs, and wears a size 14 dress.</li>
<li>The "ideal" woman--portrayed by models, Miss America, Barbie dolls, and screen actresses--is 5'7", weighs 100 lbs, and wears a size 8.</li>
<li>One-third of all American women wear a size 16 or larger.</li>
<li>75% of American women are dissatisfied with their appearance.</li>
<li>50% of American women are on a diet at any one time.</li>
<li>Between 90% and 99% of reducing diets fail to produce permanent weight loss.</li>
<li>Two-thirds of dieters regain the weight within one year. Virtually all regain it within five years.</li>
<li>The diet industry (diet foods, diet programs, diet drugs, etc.) takes in over $40 billion each year, and is still growing.</li>
<li>Quick-weight-loss schemes are among the most common consumer frauds, and diet programs have the highest customer dissatisfaction of any service industry.</li>
<li>A recent survey found only 30 percent of 250 randomly chosen women age 21 to 35 had normal bone mass--the researchers concluded women are so afraid eating dairy products will make them gain weight that they are starving themselves into osteoporosis.</li>
<li>Young girls are more afraid of becoming fat than they are of nuclear war, cancer, or losing their parents.</li>
<li>50% of 9-year-old girls and 80% of 10-year-old girls have dieted.</li>
<li>90% of high school junior and senior women diet regularly, even though only between 10% and 15% are over the weight recommended by the standard height-weight charts.</li>
<li>1% of teenage girls, and 5% of college-age women become anorexic or bulimic.</li>
<li>Anorexia has the highest mortality rate (up to 20%) of any psychiatric diagnosis.</li>
<li>Girls develop eating and self-image problems before drug or alcohol problems; there are drug and alcohol programs in almost every school, but no eating disorder programs.</li>
</ul>
