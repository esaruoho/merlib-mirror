 Diane, Thank you, Thank you, Thank you!
<p><img src="../images/snap_elena_ray.jpg" data-align="left" /> When I signed up for my Seascape vacation I didnt really know what to expect. I had never been abroad or done an organized group vacation, so I didnt want to build it up too much in my mind, fearing that might lead to disappointment. I couldnt have been more wrong! The trip was so much better than anything I could have possibly imagined. The boats, the people, the islands, the entire Greek life your trip allowed me to experience are memories I will forever treasure.</p>
<p>The boat was beautiful and stocked with everything imaginable, even including some unimaginables. But, the people, my fellow crewmates and Ray, really made the trip the unbelievable experience that it was. Everyone on the boat got along so well, we learned from each other, enjoyed each other and just generally had a great time together. Ray made me feel at home from the beginning and with some patient training from the crew :), he became a great leader, teacher and friend.</p>
<p>Now, back in my real life, I look up at the map of the islands Ive had posted in my office for almost six months. The islands are no longer just names. When I look at Lipsi, I see the quiet town square where we sat and had a frappe or the beautiful sandy beach just over the hill from the harbor. <img src="../images/snap_elena_party.jpg" data-align="right" /> Kalimnos is the breathtaking sunrise I awoke to, stripes of indescribable color rising over Turkey, framed by the rock cliffs of the harbor entrance, then the chorus of bells as the goats made their descent from those same cliffs. The view from the small caf balcony up at the Monastery is what I see instead of the word Patmos, the sunset from the abandoned village for Marathy, the small stone house on the side of the harbor with a fishing boat in front replaces the name Arki. You made this possible, and for that I am forever grateful.</p>
<p>The only thing that could have possibly made the trip any better would have been more time...</p>
<p>Thank you again Diane &amp; Ray, for making this such an incredible trip!</p>
<p>Sincerely, Elena Slagle<br />
Vassilis Groupie :)</p>

   Dear Diane &amp; RJ: I am going to try and express to the extent that words will allow how much Dyzie and I enjoyed not only the sailing experience but also your company.
<p>When we were initially exploring various possibilities for my 50th birthday, we looked at locations such as Italy, the south of France, hiking in Switzerland and came up with the idea of spending some time sailing. We have talked about it for quite some time and it has always been very intriguing. By some intervention from the Greek Gods, we found Seascapes' web page and an inquiry was sent. This was probably one of the best e-mails that have ever been typed on our keyboard. You folks helped in almost every aspect of making our arrangements an absolute breeze. What a pleasure it was having everything just fall into place. Dyzie and I debated for weeks if we should charter the boat for one or two weeks. We decided that one week would be plenty, thinking that we would be cooped up with a crazy English chick (captain) and a canuk (RJ)!</p>
<img src="../images/snap_vitty_dyzie.jpg" data-align="left" width="164" height="143" />
<p>You made us feel right at home on board the boat, shared your knowledge of the area and made us feel as if all four of us have known each other and been friends for years. We both truly do consider both of you good friends and refer to you as such in telling our friends about this wonderful trip.</p>
<p>We cannot begin to thank you for the heavenly experience especially the naming of a bay after me "Vitty Bay" where we anchored for the night, swam, relaxed, and least we not forget RJ's famous spinach lasagna made with fresh goat cheese homemade by the Grandmother on the island of Marathi, which was Paradise.</p>
<p>Dyzie and I have discussed this trip and asked ourselves what would we do differently? We both come up with the same answer: WE SHOULD HAVE SAILED FOR TWO WEEKS. Beside that, we would have not changed one moment.</p>
<p>We both would sail in Greece again in a heartbeat and look forward to sailing with you in Tonga, Thailand or anywhere else in the world. Please feel free to use us for a recommendation anytime, my e-mail address is in your reference listings already, I believe?</p>
<p>Your friends and sailing companions,<br />
<strong>Vitty &amp; Dyzie</strong>,Queensbury, NY</p>
