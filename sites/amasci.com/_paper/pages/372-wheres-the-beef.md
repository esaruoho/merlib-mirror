# WHERE'S THE GOOD TC INFO?

    > I would like to deeply understand how TCs work, design techniques, etc.
    > I found enormous data and links on the net but could not found what I am
    > looking for yet.  Any advice?

This is yet another instance where the Internet points to references but does not contain the references themselves.\

I obtained my own TC knowledge from a large variety of books and coil plans. Unfortunately I cannot recommend a single book that covers all the material. Also, I haven't purchased the several tesla coil books which have appeared recently, so I don't know if they are worthwhile.\

On internet, browse the archive of the [TCBA NEWS](http://www.teslauniverse.com/builders/tcba/volume-16/issue-4/0), the online copy of many years of backissues with hundreds of coil plans and builders articles. There's also the mirror/archive site of the old [Hot-streamer](http://www.capturedlightning.org/hot-streamer/index.htm) page with a large number of separate pages by famous coilers. Also I highly recommend anything written by Richard Quick. There are a number of his articles on the [Tesla Group's FTP archive](ftp://nic.funet.fi/pub/sci/electrical/tesla) (in compressed format.) See their [README](ftp://nic.funet.fi/pub/sci/electrical/tesla/00README) for info about the files in the [misc](ftp://nic.funet.fi/pub/sci/electrical/tesla/misc/) directory.\

The best technical description of the tesla coil is the seminal CORUM & CORUM paper. Physics equations, smith charts, design examples, everything. "Technical Analysis of Extra Coil as a Slow Wave Helical Resonator", Dr. J.F. Corum & Kenneth Corum, in PROCEEDINGS OF THE 1986 INTERNATIONAL TESLA SYMPOSIUM, (c)1986 Intl Tesla Society, Colorado Springs. You might be able to obtain this volume via interlibrary loan.\

Another source I can recommend: back issues of the [T.C.B.A.](http://amasci.com/tesla/tcba.html) newsletter, particularly the issues containing the articles on TESLA COIL CONSTRUCTION PRINCIPLES and TESLA COIL THEORY. There are links to the TCBA on my tesla page, and to [Steve Cole's](http://webpages.charter.net/stcole59/hv/hv.htm) zip file of the [TCBA Newsletter Index](http://webpages.charter.net/stcole59/hv/tcba/tcba.htm). Once you find the particular issue, back issues can be ordered from the TCBA.\
