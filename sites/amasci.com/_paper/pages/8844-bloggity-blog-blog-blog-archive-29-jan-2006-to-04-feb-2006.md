Power Outages
</div>
<br />
&#10;<div class="entries">
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Three so far. That I've noticed.<br />
<br />
Two were pretty early, during the first run of the storm. I was awake because the rain was coming down so hard it woke me. The first one I almost didn't notice... but there was a sound like "Wooom!" I noticed my clock blinking a few moments later, which is when I realized what had happened. No sooner had my sleepy brain worked through what had happened then it happened again. "Wooom!" Again, just a few moments later the power was restored. I slowly feel asleep.<br />
<br />
Next time was about 5 am, and I was sleeping lightly. The "Wooom!" this time woke me, and I said aloud, "Another power outage!" and heard my hubby answer, "Another?" Again, it didn't last long.<br />
<br />
I didn't sleep well, but at least we have power now (knock on wood) and no trees have hit the house.<br />
<br />
Update: another short outage at 8am. Storm isn't over yet.<br />
<br />
Update: 8:00pm - Power is finally back on. The power went out just after 8:40 am and stayed out for over 11 hours. The Doctor Who party turned into an amusing bust. We had four people show up to the powerless house. We sampled Italian sodas. We started the party by showing Firefly on one of the computers, then we went out for pizza, then came back and started watching a Doctor Who show on another computer... when the power finally popped back on. Let's hope is <em>stays</em> on for the big game.

<div class="post-title">
Go Seahawks!
</div>
<br />
&#10;<div class="entries">
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Let's make this an entirely <a href="http://www.superbowl.com/">Super Bowl</a> entry.<br />
<br />
First off, Go Seahawks!<br />
<br />
&#10;<div class="center">
<a href="http://seahawks.com/"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/SeahawksLogo.jpg" title="We&#39;re goin&#39; to the SUPER BOWL!!!!" width="300" height="139" alt="Seahawks Logo" /></a>
</div>
<br />
I honestly have no clue which team is better. From the records alone, I would go with the Seahawks, but it's hard to ignore the Steeler's recent run (pre-playoffs included). They seem to be peaking at just the right time. I hope the teams are as evenly matched as they seem to be, because that will mean an exciting game. I'm a little surprised that all <a href="http://www.answers.com/idiots">the experts</a> are calling the game for the Steelers, though.<br />
<br />
Hubby-Eric and I are starting to clean up the house for the big party. As I mentioned, the party actually starts tomorrow with <a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/androgums/">our usual Doctor Who shindig</a> (featuring the 8th Doctor). We will party into the night, everybody will go home for a few hours, then we'll have another party on Sunday for the Super Bowl, starting at about 2 pm and going until everyone goes home.<br />
<br />
Everyone is invited, and if you want details or something, drop me a line to tegan@eskimo.com with a subject of "Seahawks Party" so I don't mistake it for spam. You are welcome to root for <a href="http://nfl.com/">any team</a> <em>except</em> for Pittsburgh. I've had one person ask if they can root for <a href="http://www.neworleanssaints.com/">The Saints</a>... that's fine, they aren't the Steelers. Eric and I don't do the alcohol thing, so BYOB if you need it to enjoy the game.<br />
<br />
Not to disrespect <a href="http://www.pittsburgh.net/">Pittsburgh</a>, of course. I've always kinda liked the <a href="http://www.recycle-steel.org/">Steelers</a>. They certainly aren't a team I hate. Overall, I find trash talk between cities to be silly. Almost every city has something cool about it. And, heck, <a href="http://www.ci.detroit.mi.us/default.htm">Detroit</a> is cool too. Never been there, but I can still enjoy <a href="http://www.motownmuseum.com/">Motown</a>.<br />
<br />
Speaking of trash talk, I'm already sick of hearing about some stupid "feud" between <a href="http://www.answers.com/blowhard">Joey Porter</a> of the Steelers and <a href="http://www.answers.com/loudmouth">Jerramy Stevens</a> of the Seahawks. I heard what Stevens said, and it was what any player going into the game should be saying. Porter responded a bit over-the-top, which just makes me wish Stevens had kept his mouth shut.<br />
<br />
The <a href="http://www.weather.com/">weather</a> is threatening to not cooperate. We've got a major windstorm supposedly headed into the state today and tomorrow. Stores that sell or rent <a href="http://www.hondapowerequipment.com/gen.htm">generators</a> have been gearing up just in case. We don't have a backup plan if the power goes out here, so we're just keeping our fingers crossed that the storm is a fizzle.<br />
<br />
I recently bought a <a href="http://www.cafepress.com/airship.44933479">Girl Genius mini-button</a> from Cafepress to go on my "fanbag". I looked all over for a button of the same size with the Seahawks logo on it, but couldn't find anything. All the pins I'm finding are more expensive enamel pins. I'd like a cheap little metal one. And one for the M's, too. Most of my current ones are DC Comics related.<br />
<br />
Right, enough of that. Go Seahawks!<br />

Rapid Reviews - 25 January 2006
</div>
<br />
&#10;<div class="entries">
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
<strong>Batman #649</strong>: "All They Do Is Watch Us Kill Part 2": I'm not sure what to think. If the latest crisis is supposed to be getting rid of darkness, this issue definitely illustrates some of that darkness. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Wonder Woman #225</strong>: "Nothing Finished, Only Abandoned": This is an excellent wrap-up story. It is a bit abstruse in the captions, but it works. The isolation of Diana is complete. Not bad. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>JLA: Classified #16</strong>: "The Hypothetical Woman Part One: Never Brought To Mind": I love the diagnosis on The Flash. Heh. One of the best lines in comics ever. Not a bad set-up for a story, not a bad start. I'm looking forward to seeing what the general's weapon actually does. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Green Lantern Corps: Recharge #4</strong>: "Hunted": I like Soranik Natu. The rest of the characters bore me. The story bores me. The threat doesn't seem threatening. I'm not enjoying this tale. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0020.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Fallen Angel #2</strong>: This just gets more and more complicated and odd. Wait, is this really only the second issue of the new run??!? My. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Pact #4</strong>: It's really late, and it focuses on my least favorite of the four characters. I think if this had come out a month after the others, it would have fit in and I would have a higher opinion of it. But six months after the last issue... and it didn't have any major appealing stuff in it. Not horrible, but not great, either. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0020.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Local #3</strong>: "Theories and Defenses": My least favorite of the series so far, mostly because I just couldn't get into the stories. This was four different tales in one with an overall theme and narration. The artwork continues to grow on me. And it's still head and shoulders above most other comics. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Usagi Yojimbo #90</strong>: "The Ghost in the Well": Ooooh, I love ghost stories in this book. And a nice murder mystery, too. Looking forward to the next issue. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0040.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="4 starfish" /></a>

</div>
<br />
&#10;<div class="entries">
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Hey, time for a few more reviews, eh? It's been a couple-three weeks or so...<br />
<br />
<strong>Angel: Old Friends #2</strong>: Not bad. Not bad. I'm curious about the cliffhanger, and I'm enjoying the artwork. The humor captures the style of the show. Overall, I'm happy with this mini so far. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>JLA #124</strong>: "Envy Is The Key": I really don't like this version of the Key. Nor am I really sure what happened to Manitou Raven, so that bit is just annoying. Nice enough cliffhanger, but overall the story left me cold. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0020.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>JLA: Classified Cold Steel #2</strong>: "The Sleeping World": I wanted to like this story, but it's so confusing that I couldn't follow it without rereading the first issue, and even then it wasn't easy. I love the art, it's got it's own style, but it didn't make the tale any easier to understand. It's good, but hard to read. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Oz: The Manga #7</strong>: "Discovery of Oz, the Terrible": This continues to be a great adaptation, right down to the final journey of Dorothy and her friends from the original book, a bit that is often ignored. I'm really looking forward to seeing the collection of this, and I hope it sells well. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0040.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="4 starfish" /></a>

Random Bits

&#10;<div class="entries">

<br />
A piece of malware that is shipped and installed with a bunch of games has triggered a boycott, and when <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2006/01/30/anticopying_malware_.html">Boing Boing reported on it</a>, the makers of the malware <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2006/01/31/starforce_threatens_.html">threatened to sue Boing Boing</a>. Go look at the <a href="http://www.glop.org/starforce/#games">games list</a> and make sure you don't buy any of them.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.tomatopatch.com/films/sleepless.htm">Sleepless in Seattle recut as a horror film</a>. It works surprisingly well.<br />
<br />
Do you need some graph paper? <a href="http://www.incompetech.com/beta/plainGraphPaper/">Go here</a> and generate your own graph paper in handy PDF formats.<br />
<br />
And, speaking of PDFs, are you irritated by how slowly Adobe Acrobat Reader loads files? Go get the <a href="http://www.foxitsoftware.com/pdf/rd_intro.php">Free FoxIt PDF Reader</a> and never wait 5 minutes for a PDF file to show up again. I've found that it works particularly well in Firefox when combined with the <a href="http://www.rabotat.org/firefox/">PDF Download Extension</a>. I set the PDF files to go to FoxIt and everything loads super-fast.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://news.yahoo.com/s/ap/20060131/ap_on_en_mo/oscar_nominations">Oscar Nominations</a>. I'm as interested in these as I was in the Razzies.

Random Thoughts

<div style="clear:both;">

Here is a cute <a href="http://mfrost.typepad.com/cute_overload/2006/01/you_are_getting.html">picture of a pug</a> for my in-laws to enjoy. I'll note that the picture came from <a href="http://mypugsblog.blogspot.com/">a pug blog</a>.<br />

Colleen Doran has <a href="http://adistantsoil.com/blog/?p=74">a great story</a> about a convention she attended.<br />

The <a href="http://www.razzies.com/default.asp">Razzie Nominations</a> have been announced. Lucky me, I haven't seen most of the films mentioned in the nominations.<br />
<br />
Last Kiss Comics went <a href="http://www.lastkisscomics.com/weekly/week128.htm">Seahawks crazy</a> this week. heh.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2006/01/28/amsterdam_to_vandals.html">Wreck this subway car!</a> "The council in Amsterdam has issued a call for vandals to attempt to trash a prototype of the city's new subway car, which is intended to be proof against even the most determined wrecker. Amsterdam's public transit vandals are the source of perverse pride among the municipal government there, which regards them as the toughest and most extreme vandals in the world." heh.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://money.cnn.com/magazines/fortune/fortune_archive/2006/02/06/8367959/index.htm?section=cnn_topstories">Alternate to Oil</a>. I just wish there was a massive, countrywide initiative to get refineries up and running and stations delivering the new fuel. Or we could just follow Brazil's example and let the market do the work. Oh, just go read the article. It's all in there.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.cnn.com/2006/WORLD/europe/01/30/britain.museum.ap/index.html?section=cnn_topstories">You Break It, You Buy It?</a> No, they are forgiving the museum-goer who tripped on a shoelace and broke three Qing dynasty Chinese vases. Ouch.<br />
<br />
Could obesity be caused <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2006/01/30/is_obesity_caused_by.html">by a virus</a>? Don't laugh. Oh, all right, laugh away. I sure did, and I'm fat. The next question then becomes, is there a cure? If it is true, it would explain a number of my friends who simply cannot lose weight no matter how much they exercise or diet.
