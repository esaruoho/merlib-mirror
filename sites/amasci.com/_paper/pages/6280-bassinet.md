# Bassinet

<div align="center">

This bassinet was originally owned by an Italian  family in Chicago. Several of their girls used it.  It is not a doll buggy.  This information came from the Manager of the local St. Vincent Depaul who had some of the estate to sell.  It was made in about 1900.  In very good shape.\
