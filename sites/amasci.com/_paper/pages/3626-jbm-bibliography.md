This bibliography is divided into three sections, [interior](interior.html), [exterior](exterior.html) and [miscellaneous](misc.html). It's not meant to be the last word on ballistics. It is a list of the references I and others have used in creating ballistics programs. If you have any references that may be of use to others, please email me.

[Exterior](exterior.html)\
Exterior ballistics references. Ballistics dealing with bullet flight after leaving the effects of the propellant gases.

[Interior](interior.html)\
Interior ballistics references. Ballistics dealing with the propellant effects.

[Miscellaneous](misc.html)\
Other references, I've used that aren't necessarily related to ballistics. Or, they just didn't seem to fit into "just interior" or "just exterior".
