<strong>In Brief</strong>
</div>
<strong>D</strong>emian considers himself a “cultural worker,” engaged in performance and presentational arts. He has produced videos, films, photography, slide/tape presentations, theater, radio, comic strips, animations, marionette productions.
<p>Currently, he writes and doctors scripts; produces, shoots, and edits digital videos; takes digital stills; is an actor; programs Web sites; and also does shiatsu-style massage work.</p>
<p>Major threads in his theater and video works have been justice, equality and gay liberation. He created <a href="sweet.html"><strong>Sweet Corn Productions</strong></a> in 1971 to produce many of these efforts.</p>
<p>Demian conducts the day-to-day business of <a href="toc-cont.html"><strong>Partners Task Force for Gay &amp; Lesbian Couples</strong></a> which he co-created in 1986.</p>
<p>Since 1970, Demian has been vegan (no eggs, dairy, fish, etc.) with a Macrobiotic orientation.</p>
<p>He is awfully fond of caring for rabbits and has periodically had from one-to-13 of them.</p>
<p><a href="7-img-05.html"><strong>Stereoscopy</strong></a> — three-dimensional vision — is Demian’s all-time favorite passion.</p>
<div data-align="center">
<strong>Cultural / Liberation Work</strong>
</div>
<br />
&#10;Producer and Host of “Gaybreak” radio magazine for the gay and lesbian community, Amherst, Massachusetts. Interviewed scores of gay men and lesbians. (1973-77)
Co-founder, Pioneer Valley Gay Union, western Massachusetts. (1975)
Mounted a successful campaign in Amherst, Massachusetts, to amend the town law to cover lesbians and gay men in civil rights investigations. (1976)
President, People’s Gay Alliance (a University Club), Amherst, Massachusetts. (1976)
Creator and Facilitator “Self-Affirmation for Gay Men” workshops. (1977)
Playwright, Director, Producer of liberation-theme plays for <a href="sweet.html"><strong>Sweet Corn Productions</strong></a> staged in Boston, San Francisco, and Seattle. (1971-ongoing)
Producer and Host of “GayWay” radio magazine, Boston. (1977 to 1979)
Authored articles on gay issues in the “Utne Reader,” “New Age Journal,” “Gay Community News,” “Seattle Gay News,” “Fag Rag,” “Gay Comix,” “Gay Post,” “Out/Look,” “RFD,” “Win Magazine,” and a monthly column in the 70s for the “Valley Advocate” (a mainstream Massachusetts newspaper).
Writer, Webmaster, Co-founder for <a href="toc-cont.html"><strong>Partners Task Force for Gay &amp; Lesbian Couples</strong></a>. (1986-ongoing)
Newsletter Editor for the Yellow Sun Food Coop (70s), Pioneer Valley Gay Union (1975), Partners Task Force (1986-1993), Legal Marriage Alliance of Washington. (1996-98), Purple Circuit (2000-ongoing)
Webmaster and Associate Editor for <a href="pc.html"><strong>Purple Circuit</strong></a> - resources and information for GLQBT theatre and performance throughout the world. (2000-ongoing)
Webmaster for <a href="http://www.asifa.net/usa-northwest" target="http://www.asifa.net/usa-northwest"><strong>ASIFA-NW</strong></a> - an animators club (2002-ongoing)
