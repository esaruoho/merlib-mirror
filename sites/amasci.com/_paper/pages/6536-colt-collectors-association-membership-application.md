Applicant's name and city will be published in the Association Newsletter, giving members an opportunity to object to his membership. Should a complaint be registered, the Board of Directors will review the complaint and vote on the applicant's acceptance.

THE COLT COLLECTORS ASSOCIATION, INC, is a non-profit organization incorporated in the State of Texas, and is affiliated with the National Rifle Association, but is not directly affiliated with Colt's Manufacturing Co., Inc. It is an international organization, with members in all 50 states and throughout the world. The following membership benefits are offered by the CCA:

1\.     A quality All Colt Show is sponsored annually where members can buy, sell, trade, get to know fellow collectors, and exchange knowledge.\
2.     The Rampant Colt, a quality quarterly publication in which members, and others, contribute articles of interest to Colt collectors. Members may also advertise at reasonable rates for items to buy or sell.\
3.     Membership in an organization dedicated to the preservation of Colt firearins and paraphernalia, the study of various arms and products produced by Colt, and the history related to their development and usage.\
4.     The opportunity to increase your knowledge by contact with other members, either through the publication or at the annual show.\
5.     The association is staffed by elected officers and directors who receive no salary and no travel expense compensation.
