Welcome and I hope you have as much fun with these toys as I do. I will try to keep this list as up to date as possible but given the speed of change on the net, no list will ever be complete. If you know of any really cool toys or other internet applications that are not here, please [send me an e-mail at kilroy69@eskimo.com](mailto:kilroy69@eskimo.com) and tell me the name of the application and where I can find it if you know it. <img src="tn/worlds.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" alt="Worlds Inc. Logo" /> [Worlds Chat AND Alpha World.](http://www.worlds.net/) Worlds Chat is a new internet chat program that allows you to wander around in a 3-D spaceship. In color, with sound effects. This certainly does not replace IRC for chatting, but provides a novel interface to chat in. My observation so far is that people typically end up chatting with one individual at a time. **Try This!!!** It's neat and definitely worth the time to download it.\
\
Alpha World is like a cross between SimCity and IRC where you're one of the Simms. You get to build houses, trees, lakes, waterfalls, sounds, midi music, some animations. **Better than Worlds Chat,** try it too :-).\
\
\
<img src="tn/tucows.gif" data-align="BOTTOM" alt="Tucows Logo" /> [The Ultimate Collection Of Winsock Software.](http://www.tucows.com/) What more can I say, it has everything and stays up to date. FTP clients, daemons, IRC Clients, WinZip, related, If it works through WinSock, it's gotta be on this site. All kinds of stuff for all windows platforms.\
