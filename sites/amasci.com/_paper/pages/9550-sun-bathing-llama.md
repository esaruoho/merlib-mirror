<td rowspan="3" width="382" height="338" data-valign="TOP"><p>This is Pedro in the process of taking a dust bath. Our boys have cleared off several areas on our property for the express purpose of taking a dust bath. It's fun to watch them rolling around in the dust on the back and going from side to side. This activity opens up their wool and some of the debri is released. It's amazing especially after it been raining and they are such a mess, how quickly they clean up using their process of bathing.</p>
<p>Out of the 7 boys that are here, only 2 lay on their back when it's sunny. Suprisingly enough they are the only two black llamas, and you would think the least likely to be sunbathing. Recently somebody suggested that perhaps those that express this behavior have a need for vitamin D that the sun provides. It's rather funny to see a black llama laying in the sun while the light colored guys are standing in the creek to cool off. As you can see he is laying close to the creek but will not go in the water.</p>
