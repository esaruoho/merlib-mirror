<li><h2 id="contracting-for-the-jet-propulsion-laboratory"><span id="JPL">Contracting for the Jet Propulsion Laboratory</span></h2>
<p>I do contract work for the <a href="http://www.jpl.nasa.gov/">Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)</a> in Pasadena, CA. I am on my <strong>Nth</strong> contract with JPL. Below are some brief descriptions of the highlights of my work with JPL in roughly reverse chronological order.</p>
<p>I mostly design and write websites for JPL. Right now, I am writing a web based cost tracking tool for the Mars Future Studies Program. I have also written a few javascript applications and HTML layers. I also have written some heavy duty CGIs (Common Gateway Interface) for these webpages. In fact, I single handedly caused a massive interest in what CGIs can do for webpages in my section.</p>
<p><img src="thumb/afrthumb.gif" data-align="left" data-hspace="10" width="175" height="175" alt="[Africa CD Cover]" /> I also create CDs. I recently helped create a <em>triple</em> CD-ROM which is part of the <em>Global Rain Forest Mapping Project.</em> This triple CD maps out the rain forest of Central Western Africa. This is a joint project with several agencies including NASDA, Japan's counterpart to NASA. The data was generated from JERS-1, a Japanese satellite orbiting the Earth, and is the first of a series of 20-odd CDs to be produced showing the rain forests. One can actually see the destruction of these forests through controlled burning. This CD is also <em>IS0-9660</em> compatible which means it can be read on multiple platforms including PC, Mac and Unix. These CDs are unique because the data included here are not easily accessible or impossible to get from other sources.</p>
<p><img src="thumb/am2thumb.gif" data-align="left" data-hspace="10" width="177" height="175" alt="[AM-2 CD Cover]" /> Earlier, I also helped create a <em>double</em> CD-ROM which is part of the <em>Global Rain Forest Mapping Project.</em> This double CD maps out the rain forest of South America.</p>
<p>I am also an upgrade webmaster for the <a href="http://www.jpl.nasa.gov/galileo/">Galileo homepage.</a> Galileo was a very successful mission to Jupiter. Among other things, I have reorganized the <a href="http://www.jpl.nasa.gov/galileo/faq_top.html">FAQ</a> and made a <a href="http://www.jpl.nasa.gov/galileo/europa/clickmap/europa.html">global clickable map of Europa,</a> one of Jupiter's moons. It is speculated since Europa is one of the few moons in the solar system that contain substantial ice/water, there may be life on it!</p>
<p>I was one of the redesigners for the entire <a href="http://southport.jpl.nasa.gov/">Imaging Radar homepage.</a> I created an animated GIF below. It's pretty cool. Click the image below to see it.</p>

<p><br />
<a href="http://southport.jpl.nasa.gov/education/"><img src="thumb/cd3thumb.gif" data-align="left" data-hspace="10" width="177" height="175" alt="[SIRCED03 CD Cover]" /></a> I ported an educational CD-ROM from the Macintosh onto the PC platform. This project is getting a life of its own! I have ended up rewriting/reformatting most of the HTML and text files on the CD. The CD is now <em>ISO-9660 compatible</em> which means it can be read from multiple platforms including PC, Mac and Unix! In fact, <a href="http://southport.jpl.nasa.gov/education/cdrom/sirced03/cdrom/ROADMAP/GUIDTOUR.HTM">the new CD is now on-line!</a> It is connected directly to the CD-ROM drive on this machine so it may be a little slow in responding.</p>
<p>My previous tasks were to create webpages for the <em><strong>entire</strong> Radar Science and Engineering Section</em> at <a href="http://www.jpl.nasa.gov/">JPL</a> and a <a href="http://southport.jpl.nasa.gov/redcross/">World Watch homepage for disaster preparedness.</a> The latter webpage is a <a href="http://www.redcross.org/">Red Cross</a>-JPL joint effort.</p>
<p>My <em>second</em> six month contract was working with the Imaging Radar group and the Airborne Imaging Radar Synthetic Aperture Radar (AIRSAR) group. One of my tasks is to bring up a web server on a Power Macintosh (Mac) computer and create the AIRSAR homepage on it. I am using the <a href="http://www.starnine.com/">WebSTAR</a> web server software for the Mac. The homepage will have a feature where users may search for various radar images and the order these images from a <a href="http://www.claris.com/">FileMaker Pro</a> database also located on the Mac. I am writing the interface between <a href="http://www.starnine.com/">WebSTAR</a> and <a href="http://www.claris.com/">FileMaker Pro</a> in <a href="http://www.info.apple.com/pr/press.releases/1995/q4/950821.pr.adv.advantage.html">Applescript!</a><br />
<br />
<a href="http://southport.jpl.nasa.gov/"><img src="gifs/jirlogo.gif" data-align="left" data-hspace="10" width="90" height="90" alt="[Imaging Radar Logo]" /></a> I also help maintain the <a href="http://southport.jpl.nasa.gov/">Imaging Radar homepage</a> which resides on a DEC Alpha. <em>Netscape Communications</em> wanted to link to our homepage and asked for a logo but we didn't have one! So <strong>I designed</strong> the the graphic on the left using PhotoShop. This is <strong>the</strong> official logo for the Imaging Radar homepage. We are now listed in the Netscape galleria! Pretty cool, huh?</p>
<p>My <em>first</em> six month contract at <a href="http://www.jpl.nasa.gov/">JPL</a> was programming in <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Programming_Languages/C_and_C__/">C</a> and <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Programming_Languages/Fortran/">Fortran</a> under the <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Software/Operating_Systems/X_Window_System/">X-Windows</a> and <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Software/Operating_Systems/Unix/">Unix</a> environment on a DEC Alpha and Silicon Graphics machines. I was geocoding radar images for the <a href="http://www.asf.alaska.edu/">Alaskan Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) facility.</a></p>
<p>In case you are curious, this is how <a href="../alspace/commute.html">I commute to JPL (62K GIF).</a> How else am I going to beat the <a href="http://www.smartraveler.com./scripts/lsaspeed.asp?city=lsa&amp;cityname=Los_Angeles&amp;area=max">L. A. freeway traffic?</a></p></li>
<li><h2 id="computer-consulting"><span id="Computer">Computer Consulting</span></h2>
<p>To keep track of my work, I have started to keep <a href="webpages.html">a list of webpages</a> that I have created and/or maintain.</p>
<p>Although I have been doing Internet related work for the past <em>six</em> years, I also write custom software for scientific and business applications. <strong>I'm constantly looking for more work.</strong> I program in <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Programming_Languages/C_and_C__/">C,</a> <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Programming_Languages/Fortran/">Fortran,</a> <a href="http://www.yahoo.com/Computers_and_Internet/Programming_Languages/Pascal/">Pascal</a> and just about every assembly language known. I have experience in the three major platforms, PC, Unix and MacIntosh. I have two degrees in Computer Science from the <a href="http://www.usc.edu/">University of Southern California (USC)</a> in Los Angeles, CA and several years of full-time work experience. I mostly work on PCs now but have oodles of experience working on minis and mainframes. Please see <a href="http://www.hummingbird-software.com/">my company.</a> One of my recent projects is in <a href="http://www.hummingbird-software.com/legalbills/feesaver.htm">legal bill review.</a> Although I have been on the Internet for years, I am hooked on the Web and am learning all I can about it. I think it has tremendous potential as an information distribution tool. The potential for marketing products and services is outstanding! My <strong>resume</strong> is available upon request. <a href="http://www.hummingbird-software.com/contact.htm">Contact me.</a></p></li>
<li><h2 id="teaching-about-the-internet"><span id="Teach">Teaching About the Internet</span></h2>
<p>I recently taught two classes at a local adult school. They are <em>Introduction to the Internet</em> and <em>How to Advertise on the Internet</em>. I kind of fell into this because a friend of mine was teaching these classes. He moved to Palo Alto and asked me to cover for him. This has been a good experience for me overall but the preparation time for these classes is more than I bargained for. I've spent <strong>hours</strong> creating transparencies and making handouts. Anyway, I never thought I would find myself teaching a class (although I have been a college tutor, I turned down teaching a computer-related class at another local college) but the Internet is really down my alley!</p></li>
