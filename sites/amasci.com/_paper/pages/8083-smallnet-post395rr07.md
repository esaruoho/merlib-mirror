Andrew Dobos asks about which prop to use with his Goldfire 05. Depending on your application, Master Airscrew electric for the 05 ranging from 7x3 thru 8x7. If you are determined to use direct drive, try something in the middle, say about 7x6 or 8x4. If you have room in your application, use the MA gearbox 3:1 and a 11x9 or 12x8.\

From Johnney Johnson \< <jjohnson@mitre.org> \>\

Glad to have SMALL Net back, thanks for taking over. I recently built a HOB SPACEWALKER .10, great little flyer on a OS FP .10 and four channel. Does everything and does it well. Has symmetrical airfoil. The landing gear design is a little clunky - doesn't hold up well on rough grass. I redesigned and moved the gear to forward bulkhead using spring wire instead of the aluminum provided in the kit. I plan to get another one and kit bash it to a Travelair Mystery Ship. I would also decrease the dihedral a little, maybe even straight wing for pattern type flying and a .12 or .15.( but not for a mystery ship scale though.) Good kit too, laser cut, etc.!
