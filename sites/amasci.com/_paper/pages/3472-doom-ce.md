> > 1.  **You must have 4 mb of free storage space, 2.5 mb of available program memory and Windows CE version 2.0 or later.**
> > 2.  **Download: [Doom1.zip](http://cemonster.com/files2/doom1.zip) , unzip it, and place the doom1.wad file in the "My Documents" folder on your CE machine (internal or storage card).  You can also download the unzipped file [here](files/doom1.wad) but some browsers corrupt the file.**
> > 3.  **Download the correct program file for your machine.  If you don't know if you have a MIPS or an SH3 machine [click here](which_ce_cpu_do_i_have.html).   This software is provided AS-IS and is not offered as a product or piece of merchandise in anyway.  It has no warranty or guarantee.  Use at your own risk.   Really.  By downloading it you agree to this.\

> > 4.  **If you have a Palm-sized PC, you will need to place the DoomCE.exe file somewhere in the \Windows\Start Menu directory on your CE machine.**
> > 5.  **Run the program on the CE machine.**
> > 6.  **Press "Esc" or the exit button to bring up the menu.  You can only play the 1st Episode 'Knee-Deep in the Dead'.**
> > 7.  **On Palm-size PC's you have to tap (or press) on the screen to fire or open a door.  The buttons on the machine are used to move forwards and backwards, and the rocker switch is used to move left and right.**
> > 8.  **On HPC's you can use the arrow keys to move, control to fire and space bar to open doors.  Most of the normal Doom keys work also.\**
