# The Letter~Q

<span id="quadrangle">Quadrangle</span>  
1.  Four strokes form the structure.
2.  Motivated by need for security - or by need for power

<span id="questioned_document_examiner">Questioned Document Examiner</span>  
Individual who examines documents to determine whether or not they are authentic.

<span id="qde">Questioned Document Examination</span>  
The examination of a document to determine whether it was written when, where and by whom it was purported to be written by.

<span id="quenya">Quenya</span>  
High Elven speech of Middle Earth. The writing system has a number of diphthongs. For more details *The Lord of The Rings* (Tolkein: 1965) Part Three, Appendix E describes this language.

<span id="quipus">Quipus</span>  
Inca system of transmitting information, based on knotted cords of various colors and types. It is not known what, if any relation this system has with that found during the palaeothic and neolithic period in China.

<span id="quoc_ngu">Quôc ngu'</span>  
Vietnamese writing system introduced by French Jesuit, Alexandre de Rhodes in 1848 It is based upon the Latin alphabet, with additional diacritic marks, and one character from the Cyrallic alphabet. It takes between three and six months to learn to read and write Vietnamese using this writing system. \[ After August 1945, a intensive literacy campaign pushed literacy levels to above 90%. \] « The final u of ngu should have a tilde on it. »
