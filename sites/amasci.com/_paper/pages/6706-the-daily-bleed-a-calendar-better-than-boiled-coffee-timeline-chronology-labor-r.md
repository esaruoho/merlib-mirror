German physician, director of the state mental hospital in Frankfurt am Main & writer best known for Slovenly Peter, the story of a boy with bad manners. > > > 1840 -- French writer, activist, experimental novelist Emile Zola lives (1840-1902), Paris, France. 
> > >
> > > > Notable in Zola's career was involvement in the Dreyfus affair with his open letter J'accuse. Alfred Dreyfus was a French Jewish army officer, falsely charged with giving military secrets to the Germans. He was eventually cleared & freed, but during the long acrimonious process Zola was sent to prison in 1898, escaped to England, & returned after Dreyfus was cleared. Zola died in 1902 under mysterious circumstances, overcome by carbon monoxide fumes in his sleep.
> > > >
