## Entertainment Weekly, June 17, 1994

------------------------------------------------------------------------

      Title:  The Woman's Boat._(sound recording reviews)
      Author:  Michele Romero
     
      Full Text COPYRIGHT Entertainment Weekly Inc. 1994
     Set sail, and you're in for a sensual voyage that spans the 
     wodd's musical seas. Partly recorded in India, The Woman's Boat 
     is woven with a World Beat catalog of exotic instruments as 
     well as guest appearances from Peter Gabriel, Robert Fripp, and 
     the hot Euro-African a cappello group, Zap Mama.
     
     Childs's voice delivers the emotion of voltage of wailing 
     African griots, while her lyrics probe the deep psyche of 
     our relationships from womb to tomb. Dedicated to "the female 
     spirit in every living thing," The Woman's Boat is the cruise 
     of lifetime.
