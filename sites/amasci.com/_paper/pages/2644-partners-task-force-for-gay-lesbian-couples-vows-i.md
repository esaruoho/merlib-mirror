<td>As you have made an application to the Copenhagen city hall in order to get your partnership lawfully registered, your wish will now be fulfilled.
<p>With this registration you will — with few exceptions — obtain the same social security as married people in Danish society.</p>
<p>Before performing the registration, the municipality wishes to remind you of the meaning and importance of the promise you are giving each other.</p>
<p>Registered partnership implies in general a pledge to live together in mutual affection, helpfulness and tolerance.</p>
<p>In recognition of this, the municipality expresses the wish that throughout your partnership, with all its changes, you will preserve the good intentions, to live together in a harmonious and meaningful fellowship.</p>
<p>I ask you [Name1], do you take [Name2] to be your lawful partner?</p>
<p>Likewise, I ask you [Name2], do you take [Name1] to be your lawful registered partner?</p>
<p>After you now solemnly declared your desire to enter into registered partnership with each other, I hereby ask you to sign.</p></td>
