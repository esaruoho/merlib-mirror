<strong>Training and Swim Pants</strong></p>
<img src="boyblue.jpg" data-align="Right" data-border="0" width="183" height="290" alt="Don&#39;t these training pants look comfy?" />
Finally! This what a training pant should be. Absorbent enough to contain one accident; made with a cotton layer next to the body so it feels wet right away; and waterproof to protect your child's clothing.
<p>Our Swim Pants are great for swim lessons in the pool because they offer the containment of a waterproof nylon pant and the comfort of cotton. They are more fitted than a diaper cover because they are designed to fit next to your baby. The adjustable wrap style allows the swim pant to grow right along with your baby.<br />
