## The Benedict Lounger

<img src="Images/loungers.jpg" data-align="MIDDLE" />

The Lounger frame allows you to use your futon in three distinct positions: a couch, a bed, and the lounge position.

The slats are "bull-nosed," meaning that they do not have any sharp edges along their length. Many competing futon frames use slats that have sharp edges, possibly damaging your futon mattress or cover.

The slats are set into 2x6's, making it stronger and *higher* than many other lounger frames which simply use 2x4's or 1x6's.

Our **Benedict Lounger** Frame is available in "cot," twin, "3/4," full, and Queen size at the following prices:

- "Cot" size *Benedict Lounger* Frames are \$69.00 each.
- Twin size *Benedict Lounger* Frames are \$74.00 each.
- "3/4" size *Benedict Lounger* Frames are \$80.00 each.
- Full size *Benedict Lounger* Frames are \$80.00 each.
- Queen size *Benedict Lounger* Frames are \$90.00 each.

We also carry a complete line of matching [*Benedict Lounger* Arms](arms.html), futon [mattresses](mattress.html), and futon and frame [combination packages](greatbuy.html).\

Shipping (within the contiguous 48 states) is \$25.00 per lounger frame.
