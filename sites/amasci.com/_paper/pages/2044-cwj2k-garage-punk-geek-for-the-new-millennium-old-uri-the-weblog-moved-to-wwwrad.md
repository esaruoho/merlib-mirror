### <span class="entrytitle">Geekery Today has moved</span> January 23, 2004

<div class="blogbody">

**[Geekery Today has moved.](http://www.radgeek.com/)** As promised, there is a new design and new content for the new year. Moreover, **there is a new title and a new URL:**

<div class="linkroll" style="text-align: center; font-size: 1.25em;">

<http://www.radgeek.com/>

</div>

**N.B.:** in spite of the move, all of your old links to pages on the Eskimo web space should still work ([cool URIs don't change](http://www.w3.org/Provider/Style/URI "Tim Berners Lee: Cool URIs don't change")). But many will be redirected to URIs on [www.radgeek.com](http://www.radgeek.com/), and, if I may be so vain as to pretend that some of you have the site bookmarked, you should update that, too.

Some parts of the new website are still a bit dusty and cluttered, but everything is being made increasingly presentable for guests, and most things should be in order very soon if they are not now. I hope you enjoy the redesign; in any case feel free to [let me know what you think](http://www.radgeek.com/contact). **Enjoy!**

</div>
