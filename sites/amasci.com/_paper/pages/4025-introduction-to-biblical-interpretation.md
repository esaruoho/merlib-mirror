# Correctly Handling the Word of Truth

## An Introduction to biblical Interpretation

This is a course I'm currently writing on biblical interpretation. The target audience is lay members of the church. It shows the process and problems of interpretation of scripture. It will also include a list of books for further study.

### Instructor's Outline

The instructor's outline includes the major points the instructor should teach. The instructor should modify this to suit their own teaching style. The instructor's outline is pretty sparse right now, but it kind of shows you what to expect. Please [mail me](mailto:johnnyb@eskimo.com) if you have anything you'd like to add, especially scripture references and textbook discussions on similar issues.

- [View the instructor notes online](InstructorNotes.xml)
- [Download a PDF of the instructor notes](InstructorNotes.pdf)

### Handouts

The handouts correspond to the main points that the student may want to take home and look at from time to time. These are not all finished. Even the ones posted may have a section missing. The current handouts are:

- Views on Biblical Inerrancy
- History of Interpretation
- Bible Translations
- Handouts of Biblical forms
  - Old Testament Narrative
  - Old Testament Law
  - Prophecy
  - Gospels
  - Letters
- Basic Outline of Interpretation
- Reference Books

You can download the handouts as a [tarball of DocBook files](interpretation-handouts.tar.gz), a [Postscript file](handouts.ps), or a [PDF file](handouts.pdf).
