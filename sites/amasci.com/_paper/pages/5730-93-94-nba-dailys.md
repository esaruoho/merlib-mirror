# 93-94 NBA Dailys

These pre-date the web page and I kept them around for my own use (actually haven't looked at them for years). These look to be some of the "nibbles" that I used to occassionally send out regarding interesting things or quotes regarding the NBA, not the dailys that had the game results (the dailys were mostly from ESPN SportCenter and the nibbles were mostly from the Dallas Morning News \[I was living in Austin at the time and would often go up to Dallas for weekends to go to Mavs game and had my parents save the DMN's sports pages for me\]). 93-94 was the first year I did the nba-dailys; unfortunately I didn't save them.\
Note: Google (aka dejanews) has upped the archive of newsgroups to go back to 1981 (WOW). So if you really want to see the old dailys, do an advanced search on http://www.dejanes.com/ for the newsgroup rec.sport.basketball.pro with pj13488@austin.lockheed.com as the author and setting the time frame for what you are looking for. Looks like I used "News from" and "Results from" as the titles. \[And if you want to see something really amazing, do a search for the pj13488 address with "tab" as the key word.\]
