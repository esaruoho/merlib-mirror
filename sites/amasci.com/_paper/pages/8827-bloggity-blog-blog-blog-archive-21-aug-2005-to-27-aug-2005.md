<strong>Wikimania III: Mutant Squid</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Ok, I think it's safe to say, after all the effort I put into <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Main_Page">this thing</a> in the few days it's been up, that I'm not planning on deleting <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Main_Page">this wiki</a> even if nobody but me plays with it. I'm having a blast with it.<br />
<br />
I really <em>would</em> like some help, though.<br />
<br />
After reading through the heavy-duty work that "Silentnietzsche" put into the <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Usagi_Yojimbo">Usagi Yojimbo</a> entry (thank you, Silentnietzsche), I think the absolute most important thing for each entry is the character information. So, if there's a book you read regularly and think you know enough to fill in some blanks, please jump in and play.<br />
<br />
While I'd love it if everyone followed my basic outline for the wiki (see <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Aquaman">Aquaman</a>, <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Usagi_Yojimbo">Usagi Yojimbo</a>, and <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/True_Story%2C_Swear_To_God">True Story, Swear To God</a> for examples), I'm not going to hold anyone to it. I'm far more interested in getting information into the things than in making them all look uniform and perfect.<br />
<br />
That said, I've set up some really basic skeleton wikis for a number of books. All the books linked <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Main_Page#Alphabetically">from the main page</a> have at least a starter wiki already.<br />
<br />
If you don't see the book you want to edit:
<ul>
<li>Go the <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Main_Page#By_Publisher">the publisher page</a> for that book, and check to see if there's a link from there.
<ul>
<li>If there is, and the link is red, it means the page doesn't exist yet (when you click on the link, you'll just get an edit page).<br />
<em>-or-</em><br />
</li>
<li>If there isn't, you need to <a href="http://www.wikicities.com/wiki/Help:New_page">add the link</a> in the same format as the others, then you can click on the red link to edit the page.</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
There's a great <a href="http://www.wikicities.com/wiki/Tutorial">tutorial on editing wikis</a>, and it tells you everything from adding a new page to putting up images.<br />
<br />
I just want to someday be able to pick up the latest Wonder Woman or Superman and not have to ask "who the heck is <em>that</em>?" and never get an answer. In the meantime, I'll keep adding bits and pieces here and there for all the books I personally get, and the shipping information for books I don't get, and maybe eventually this thing will be useful instead of just fun.<br />
<br />
<em>Update:</em> It's already getting useful, as "Davidscarter" has added wikis for <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Ninja_High_School">Ninja High School</a>, <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Gold_Digger">Gold Digger</a>, and <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Banana_Sunday">Banana Sunday</a>, three series I'd heard of but knew nothing about. David also updated a few other entries and added a ton of starter links for other series.

<strong>Rapid Reviews - 24 August 2005</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
<strong>Hero Squared #2</strong>: Another <a href="http://www.boom-studios.com/">Boom! Studios</a> review copy. I ordered this book, as well, so I guess I'll get two copies <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/08/rapid-reviews-27-july-2005.html">again</a>. Anyway, I'm glad I ordered it, because it's a very funny book. It's got humor, it's got superhero stereotypes, it's got two very different points of view. I really enjoyed it. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Angel: The Curse #3</strong>: Angel fights alongside the Rom, and more set-up takes place. We get some flashbacks to the characters who died in the finale of the show... while other bits are left open to interpretation. Particularly the reference to Spike. This isn't a bad issue, it's just got the middle child syndrome going. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Conan #19</strong>: "The Crown of Tiamat": I really enjoy how this series is pulling together all the aspects of Conan. In this particular issue, Conan is seen to mature a bit. It makes me want to re-read all the original stories. I know that some folks don't like the art, but I love it. It works really well for this character. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Day of Vengeance #5</strong>: "The Particle Theory of Darkness": I kind of wish the monkey would continue narrating. This time it's Nightshade doing the narration, and while she's not bad, the monkey was much more interesting. Anyway, the team comes up with a plan, and it's a good plan. It only has one flaw... and that's the cliffhanger. Decent issue. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>JSA: Classified #2</strong>: "Power Trip Part II of IV": Ok. I'm not sure I buy the explanation for the hole in the front of Power Girl's outfit, but it's cute. I'm much more curious about why the character on the cliffhanger page is out to get her. Say no more... I'll find out soon enough. This one has my interest. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Legion of Super-Heroes #9</strong>: With the Legion's very public implosion, the kids outside who supported the Legion are also breaking up. I like the way the characters are handled in this book, even though I'm still having a miserable time keeping them straight. At least this one has a much better ending than the last issue. And the letter column is the best in the business in many ways. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a>

<strong>Random Thoughts</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
I gotta admit, the <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/08/blogaround-challenge.html">Blogaround</a> is working far better than I expected it to. I keep finding new and interesting blogs through the links other folks are posting. Everyone who accepted the challenge: Thank you! It has given new life to my enjoyment of the comic book blogosphere.<br />
<br />
The <a href="http://comicbookwife.blogspot.com/">Comic Book Wife</a> has a low opinion of the artwork on <a href="http://comicbookwife.blogspot.com/2005/08/where-would-you-like-your-body-aquaman.html">the latest Aquaman</a>.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://sevenhells.blogspot.com/2005/08/can-i-just-say-that-i-love-black.html">Devon</a> on Black Manta.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.whiterose.org/howlingcurmudgeons/archives/008586.html">Matt</a> suggests dumping continuity. Which I've always been in favor of, in a limited way, for a long time. Don't dump the character continuity, but tell the story you want to tell, even if it contradicts the past a little. Anyway, read the comments on the post. Lots of good stuff there.<br />
<br />
Scott at Polite Dissent got <a href="http://politedissent.com/archives/876">a thank you</a> in a comic book. I really wish I could do something impressive enough to merit a thank you credit. Especially in Aquaman. Oh well...<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/22/mystery_scream_in_oh.html">Mystery Scream in Ohio</a>. This one is funny if only because of the really cheesy news segment that covers the story.<br />
<br />
Speaking of screams, Political Wire <a href="http://politicalwire.com/archives/2005/08/22/revisiting_deans_scream.html">links</a> to an interesting <a href="http://www.hs.ttu.edu/hdfs3390/deandemo.htm">research paper</a> on the infamous Howard Dean scream. I was fascinated by the results, because I didn't have any negative reaction at all when I first saw the scream and just wondered what the fuss was about. Maybe I first saw the shot that included the audience reaction?<br />
<br />
The Museum of Hoaxes <a href="http://www.museumofhoaxes.com/hoax/weblog/piano_man_was_a_hoax/">tackles Piano Man</a>. But the real reason to check this one out is to read about <a href="http://www.museumofhoaxes.com/pcaraboo.html">Princess Caraboo</a>.<br />
<br />
Augie has found <a href="http://www.variousandsundry.com/index.php?p=2900">waiting list information</a> for hybrid cars. I would love to get a hybrid.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/16756280@N00/sets/789737/">Isotope Comics Toilet Seat Art</a>. Hey, I've got an Aquaman sketchbook, who am I to judge?<br />
<br />
Boing Boing links: <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/22/unintentional_faces_.html">Faces</a>, <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/22/highspeed_amateur_pi.html">Balloons in mid-Pop</a> (look at the expressions of the people doing the popping), <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/23/armed_swat_team_atta.html">more on the Utah Rave</a>, <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/23/gigantic_klein_bottt.html">Gigantic Klein Bottle</a> (hubby-Eric wants one), <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/23/notional_list_of_kli.html">List of Klingon Fairy Tales</a>, and <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/23/labeling_tape_just_b.html">Really cool Labeling Tape</a>.<br />
<br />
For the rest of today, assuming the anti-nausea medication doesn't knock me out, I think I'll go play around in the <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/">wiki</a> and add some more series that I actually have information on. It's kind of promising, so far, with some good stuff in the <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Detective_Comics">Detective Comics</a> entry and some of the basics in the <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Aquaman">Aquaman</a> entry worked out, too.

<strong>Wikimania II: The Return</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Ok. It's up. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com">Right here</a>. Please play in it, and if you like it, add more and more and more until it actually becomes something halfway useful. The <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Aquaman">Aquaman</a> entry has a little bit in it, and some of the others have skeletons of information just waiting to be filled out.<br />
<br />
If you want to become an admin, make sure you have a user name at <a href="http://www.wikicities.com/">Wikicities</a> then let me know. But to start out, just add information and pages (you can add a page by first adding a link on one of the other pages, then clicking the link and editing). Anyone at all can add (which is the whole point of Wikis) so go give it a shot. Add something you know, even if it's just one little tiny piece of information.<br />
<br />
If there isn't enough interest to keep it going after a few months, or there is too much abuse to deal with, I'll ask the wikicities folks to delete it. Otherwise, it's all for fun and all for everyone. And yes, I know it's still <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Main_Page">really basic</a>, but I want to test this idea out and see if it will fly.
