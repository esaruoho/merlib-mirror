**Dapping Tools and Design Blocks**

These tools are used to shape softer metals and waxes by punching into the depression of the block and dies. The punches and blocks are matched so that perfect hemispheres and domes can be formed in specified sizes.

**(1)  FLAT DAPPING BLOCK** - Has 14 hemispherical depressions from 1/8 to 7/8 by 1/16ths, plus 1. High grade, properly hardened steel is used to make this block. Made in USA.

25.050

ShpWt 3 lb 4 oz.

\$58.50
