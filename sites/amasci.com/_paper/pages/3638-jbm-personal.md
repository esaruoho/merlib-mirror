<a href="../index.html" class="outline_link">JBM</a> - Personal

These pages are for my personal shooting/firearm projects. If you want to talk about any of my projects or have questions, just let [me](mailto:jbm@eskimo.com) know.

**NOTE: Any loads, reloading methods, firing techniques mentioned here were safe for the particular firearm, with the particular components (bullet, primer, brass, powder lot, etc), and conditions at the time of firing. I definitely DO NOT recommend anyone attempt to duplicate anything mentioned here unless you are absolutely sure you know what you are doing and you work up to it slowly and safely.**

[.308 Obermeyer](308obermeyer.html)\
My second try at building and customizing a rifle.

[.338 Millard](338millard.html)\
A long range target/hunting rifle based on the Hall Express action.

[USP Trigger Smoothing](usp_trigger/usp_trigger.html)\
Steps I have taken to smooth the triggers on my HK USP pistols.
