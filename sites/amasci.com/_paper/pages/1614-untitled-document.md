#### *Should you carry a knife?*\

#### There is always a lot of controversy over knives in the discussion of personal security. There are those who argue that a knife is a better weapon than a firearm; there are those who argue the opposite. I am ambivalent about the matter with the exception that I recognize a knife as being one of the most vicious weapons available (or useful tool for an emergency). The world over it is the knife, not the sidearm , that is responsible for a greater number of homicides and assaults.

#### A knife is probably the only weapon that requires absolutely no skill what so ever to use to injure, maim or kill. Need proof? Think about the most untrained, docile member of your family with a nine inch kitchen knife in hand. Imagine that person slashing and thrusting in your direction as fast as they can. Now imagine trying to stop the attack with out getting cut or stabbed. Possible? Yes. Probable No.

#### The most important thing to realize when deciding whether or not to use a knife as a defensive tool is that it is a lethal weapon. Like a handgun, or any other lethal weapon, it should not be drawn unless you are ready to use it as such. So before you make your decision to carry a knife for defensive purposes, make sure you fully understand the legal ramifications.

#### By the way, for most all situations I do not believe in carrying a folder for defense. In my own opinion , once you are attacked it is unlikely you will ever get it out and into action. (I do carry a Swiss army knife and a Leatherman for other applications)
