# Rhododendron Bench

*Source page: `~enumclaw/Tips/furniture/rhodobench1.html`*

Rhododendron Bench #1

  This bench includes small end tables, supported from above and below. The trunks in the backgrond are from a large Naomi. *Yakushimanum* and *orbuculare* grow in the foreground.

          

 At the beginning of May, Naomi and Moonstone are blooming. The large PJM on the left and behind the bench finished flowering. The picture is taken looking over the top of a ten foot Blue Diamond, with *yajushimanum* Ken Janeck just coming out.
