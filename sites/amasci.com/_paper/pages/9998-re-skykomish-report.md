    Got to differ with you on this one.  If jet boats are allowed and they are
    in many rivers then they have to be allowed to travel safely.  The bank
    angler, understanding this, waves and says "hi" and waits for his safe
    passage and resumes fishing.  The fly guy has to realize (and I am one of
    them) that Nov., Dec. and Jan. are put'n take, hatchery return times and
    because a fly guy shows up with his fly rod, the rules of the road don't
    change.  If the fly angler wishes "quality" time he should find such  a
    river or wait for native return, hook'n release March time to fish.  I have
    a real problem with the fact that for years 15 guys could be happily fishing
    at a particular drift  and now, because a guy shows up with a flyrod we are
    supposed to change the rules as to how we fish.  Give much more room, don't
    step in below, mad at jet boats etc. etc. It makes me embarrassed to be a
    flyrodder and I hear about it when I'm on the river.  Common sense, to me,
    says at this time of year, I should find my spot and act accordingly.  There
    are lots of newbies out there trying to kill a hatchery fish and that is the
    nature of that fishery.  Our time comes later.  Jere
