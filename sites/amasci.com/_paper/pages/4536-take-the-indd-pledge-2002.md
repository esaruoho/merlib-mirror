<h3 id="i-pledge">I Pledge:</h3>
<ul>
<li>That I will not diet for one day, on May 6, International No Diet Day.</li>
<li>Instead of trying to change my body to fit someone else's standards, I will accept myself just as I am.</li>
<li>I will feed myself if I'm hungry.</li>
<li>I will feel no shame or guilt about my size or about eating.</li>
<li>I will think about whether dieting has improved my health and well-being or not.</li>
<li>And I will try to do at least one thing I have been putting off "until I lose weight."</li>
</ul>
