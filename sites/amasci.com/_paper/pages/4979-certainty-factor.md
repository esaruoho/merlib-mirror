# Certainty Factor

------------------------------------------------------------------------

In many [situations](lssit.htm) it is not possible to answer a question with a Yes or No. In the Decision Maker expert system the user may specify "UNK" for unknown by using the CF (Certainty Factor) function. The expert system will then use [fuzzy logic](lsfuzzy.htm) in searching for efficient method(s).

[![](lscf.jpg)](lscf.jpg)<u></u>

In the above example. The user has answered two questions with a degree of certainty and is in the process of answering a third. A CF of 100 is the same as answering "YES" to the question.
