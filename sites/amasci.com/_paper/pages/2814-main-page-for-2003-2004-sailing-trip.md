# 2003 2004 sailing trip

It looks like Spirit and I will be heading north from the Chesapeake Bay to St Johns in Newfoundland, then returning to the Chesapeake. Blast off date from Annapolis is May 1st. I hope to be in St Johns around July 1st, and then returning to the Chesapeake by mid September.\
