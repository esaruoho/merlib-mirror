<td style="text-align: right;"><strong>Flushabye<sup>TM</sup> Diaper Liners</strong></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>The Flushabye<sup>TM</sup> Diaper Liner's size and content make it safe to flush down toilets. It should be noted, however, that plumbing systems and their effectiveness vary. Too many Flushabyes<sup>TM</sup> flushed down a toilet at one time or during one day in a less than adequate plumbing system may cause problems. To avoid this difficulty, we recommend that only one Flushabye<sup>TM</sup> be flushed at one time. We also recommend that they only be flushed when soiled. Wet liners can be disposed of in the garbage. It is also helpful to let the Flushabye<sup>TM</sup> Liner sit a few moments in the toilet before flushing. Homes with septic tanks can use the Flushabyes<sup>TM</sup> if they follow the above tips.</p>

<td style="text-align: right;" data-bgcolor="#FFF59C"><sup><strong>Washing your Indisposables</strong><sup><strong>TM</strong></sup> <strong>Cotton Diapers</strong></sup></td>

<ol>
<li><sup><strong>Prewash diapers twice before using.</strong></sup></li>
<li><sup>After Changing your baby's diaper, flush stool down the toilet and place wet or soiled diaper into a diaper pail.</sup></li>
<li><sup>Soak the diapers in a pail filled with 1/2 water and 15ml of Indisposables<sup>TM</sup></sup> <a href="public_html/laundry.html"><sup>Laundry Brightener</sup></a><sup>, or 1/4 cup vinegar or washing soda. It is not recommended to soak diapers more than three days.</sup></li>
<li><sup>To transfer diapers in the pail to a laundry machine, either hand wring diapers and use lid of pail to carry OR pour all of the diaper pail contents into the washing machine, turn on rinse cycle and spin solutions out of the diapers.</sup></li>
<li><sup>Use the Indisposables<sup>TM</sup></sup> <a href="public_html/laundry.html"><sup>Liquid Laundry Concentrate</sup></a><sup>. Do not us Bleach, Detergents with Bleach, Pure Soap Products (such as Ivory Snow), strong detergent products - liquid or powder (such as Tide).</sup></li>
<li><sup><em>Care in drying your diapers will add to their life.</em> Dry diapers on medium setting for half of the drying cycle and then hang damp diapers to dry for the balance. Softeners in the washing machine and dryer are not recommended as they reduce the absorbency of the diapers.</sup></li>
<li><sup><em>To remove stains,</em> rub a bar of "white" soap on the wetted down stain before placing in the diaper pail. Try hanging diapers in the sun after laundering to naturally bleach stain away. Add Indisposables<sup>TM</sup></sup> <a href="public_html/laundry.html"><sup>Laundry Brightener</sup></a> <sup>to washing machine. For extra tough stains, mix 5ml Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Liquid Laundry Concentrate with 5ml Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Laundry Brightener and apply past to stain. Launder as usual.</sup></li>
