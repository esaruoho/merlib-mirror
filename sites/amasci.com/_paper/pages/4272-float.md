<u>Float Test</u>

Float tests can be conducted at any pool or public beach by a qualified lifeguard. **Testing must be done before you are allowed on the water.**

Test Requirements:\
In deep water, while wearing workout clothing, you must float, tread water, or swim in place for ten minutes. In the final minute of the test you must put on a life vest while continuing to tread water.

A ten-minute float test is valid for three years.
