**March 10, 2000 - Winter Park**

- <img src="img/winterpark.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Winter Park" /> The HP Ski Club took two buses to Winter Park, where I hadn't been in a while now. Fantastic snow, must have snowed 7" the night before! Skiing with people you work with is... interesting. Really soft powder, unusually easy to turn in!

<span id="6"></span>

**March 11, 2000 - Loveland**

- <img src="img/loveland.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="Loveland" /> Always the glutton for punishment, we made another trek the next day but only to Loveland (before the tunnel). Nice day, pretty good snow, LOTS of powder on top of the continental divide! Sore knees, though, turning in more normal powder (than yesterday) is hard!
