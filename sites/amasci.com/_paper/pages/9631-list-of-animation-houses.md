## Just what am I looking at here?

Originally compiled by Jon Hooper, this list is an attempt to catalog the various animation houses along with their specialties, contact information, and so on. Many thanks to Jon for the amazing amount of work he put it on the list.

Be sure to also check out the [Freelance Animators](freelance.html) list, and the [Animation Schools](schools.html) list.

The list, like the animation field, is constantly changing. If you know of any mistakes, omissions, or new items for this list, please [send email](mailto:grue@xmission.com,hoops!me@netcom.com) to [Dave DeBry](http://www.xmission.com/~grue) (*grue@xmission.com*) and Jon Hooper (*hoops!me@netcom.com*).
