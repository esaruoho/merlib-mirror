This is me on a beach in [Cebu, The Philippines.](http://www.yahoo.com/Regional_Information/Countries/Philippines/) That country is having a host of economical problems (like the US government as I am typing this on Nov-11-95). Not many pictures of myself exist but I thought this one was amusing. I was born with the sunglasses on. They grow and change in shape and color with me. And yes, the suntan oil bottle is genetically attached. I cannot be separated from it for any length of time. It has to do with being from Southern California and especially from Los Angeles (LA). All of us wear sunglasses and carry suntan oil, just in case there's a beach nearby. I also brought my towel with me. This is essential when you are hitch-hiking across the galaxy.
