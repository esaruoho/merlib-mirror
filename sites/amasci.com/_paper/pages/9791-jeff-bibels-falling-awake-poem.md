## "Falling Awake" by Jeff Bibel <img src="icons/bonzai8.gif" data-align="top" />

##### Jeff Bibel--Mon, 24 Jul 1995 <img src="icons/mountns.gif" data-align="absmiddle" />

    "I just got back from the most incredible camping trip in the 
    mountains.  I'm not much of a poet, but I felt inspired one night, 
    and here it is!"  

    "Falling Awake" 

    I lay down last night 
        just as beat as can be. 
    I needed some time 
        for my Father and me.  
    The dusk turned to darkness, 
        and I gently awoke. 
    My mind joined with His, 
        and for hours we spoke. 
    We talked about fear,  
        forgiveness and love. 
    My questions He answered 
        with the stars up above. 
    I want to get closer 
        I begged of the sky. 
    The stars, they just twinkled 
        like the wink of an eye. 
    For the briefest of moments 
        I knew what He meant: 
    That we're already one 
        in His heavenly tent. 
    I felt so alive 
        with understanding so deep. 
    But too soon the sun rose,  
        and I.... I slipped back to sleep. 
     
    J. Bell  1995
