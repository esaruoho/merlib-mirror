**Breastfeeding Information**

**Baby Town and Ameda/Egnell care about you and your child. To help you successfully breastfeed your child, Ameda/Egnell has created a series of informational pamphlets which we have reproduced here. Nothing can replace the attention and support of a certified lactation consultant, but these will help to answer some of the more common questions.**
