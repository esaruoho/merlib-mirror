<a href="http://fridayfive.org">Friday Five</a><br />
<br />
<strong>1. What are your hobbies?</strong> Hobbies? I'm supposed to have time for hobbies, I guess my hobby would be helping friends with their projects and/or start-up businesses. <a href="http://www.coolneon.com">Cool Neon</a> and <a href="http://www.utilikilts.com">Utilikilts</a> are two that leap to mind....
<p><br />
<strong>2. Do you collect anything? If so, what?</strong> I'm not sure what it is, but my house sure is full of it. I tend to have accumulate various Disney Memorabilia but I since it lacks organization I wouldn't call it a collection =)</p>
<p><br />
<strong>3. Is there a hobby you're interested in, but just don't have the time/money to do?</strong> I've bought some <a href="http://www.fimozone.com">Fimo</a> and I worked with some of it the last time my friend <a href="http://www.3ddoodle.com">Howard</a> had a Clay Day. I've got plans to make some nifty pendants to give out to the walkers on the <a href="http://www.bethepeople.com/avon_3days/landing_3day.htm">Avon Breast Cancer 3-day.</a> Hopefully I'll be able to pull that one off.</p>
<p><br />
<strong>4. Have you ever turned a hobby into a moneymaking opportunity?</strong> Not yet, I suppose if I started working w/ the Fimo some more I'd be able to sell some of those pieces if I got good enough.</p>
<p><br />
<strong>5. Besides web-related stuff (burbs, rings, etc.), what clubs do you belong to?</strong> Clubs? yeah right....see #1 =)</p>
