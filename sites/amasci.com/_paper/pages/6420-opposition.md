Opposition to free enterprise comes from many quarters.

**Primary opponents of free enterprise are:**\
****<img src="Arrow.gif" data-border="0" width="7" height="7" />**** Progressivists, a spectrum of socialists, democratic socialists, eco-socialists, and other anti-capitalists who don't want to say the "S" word.\

****<img src="Arrow.gif" data-border="0" width="7" height="7" />**** Nature advocates, including environmentalists and animal rights activists.\
****<img src="Arrow.gif" data-border="0" width="7" height="7" />**** Socialist and communist states and advocates, for social and political reasons.\

**Anti-corporate** advocates are a varied lot. For a sample of straightforward anti-corporate rhetoric, read **<a href="monitor.htm" style="text-decoration: none">the book review of Nick Nichols' <em>Rules for Corporate Warriors</em></a>** by Multinational Monitor.\
Read **<a href="war.htm" style="text-decoration: none">Select Goal - Target Corporations</a>** by Troy Prouty of Indymedia Seattle to see the war and violence rhetoric growing among radical anti-corporate activists.

**Anti-Globalization** advocates range from intellectuals to murder suspects. Some get hero awards, others get a fair trial. Read **<a href="no_hero.htm" style="text-decoration: none">Vandana Shiva is no hero</a>** **** Time magazine award stirs anger in humanitarian organization.

**Nature advocates** tend to be most intolerant of those who disagree with them. For a sample of nearly incredible intolerance of disagreement, read <a href="scientific.htm" style="text-decoration: none; font-weight: 700">the Scientific American diatribe</a> against Bjorn Lomborg, author of the contrarian book, *The Skeptical Environmentalist*. Then read the <a href="London_telegraph.htm" style="text-decoration: none; font-weight: 700">London Telegraph</a> feature on Lomborg's life as a marked man. Then read <a href="lomborg_defended.htm" style="text-decoration: none; font-weight: 700">The Economist's</a> take on a Danish committee that found Lomborg guilty of scientific dishonesty without any evidence.

For another example of intolerance of disagreement, consider the case of **Patrick Moore**, Ph.D., a co-founder of GreenPeace, now a professional forester based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. He is the author of Pacific Spirit, which supports logging. He maintains that the environmental movement has been hijacked by political extremists. His website is <a href="http://www.greenspirit.com" style="text-decoration: none; font-weight: 700">www.greenspirit.com/</a>. Environmentalists put up an attack website <a href="http://www.fanweb.org/patrick-moore/index.shtml" style="text-decoration: none; font-weight: 700">Patrick Moore is a Big Fat Liar</a>. The content is as childish as the title.
