**Kerr Centrifico Casting Machines**

Kerr Centrifico Casting Machines are made to give long-term, trouble-free performance. The casting arm is an aluminum bronze casting, which is then machined, nickel plated, and polished. The base and cover are made from heavy, precision die castings and then given a baked finish. The main and secondary arm pivot on precision bearings.

**4 CASTING MACHINE**

Casts all flask sizes up to 3 1/2 diameter by 4 high (J2, J3 and J4 flasks). Supplied with 2C and 3C flask cradles, J3 flask, T3 spruce base, 7 oz. clay crucible and universal tongs. Casting well (C0143) is optional - See below.

**6 CASTING MACHINE**

Casts all flask sizes 2l diameter by 2 5/8 high (J3 Flask) up to 4 diameter by 6 high (J5 Flask). Supplied with J3C and J4C flask cradles, super heavy duty spring (not visible), J5 flask, T5 sprue base, 20 oz clay crucible and universal tongs. Casting well (C0143) is optional - See below.
