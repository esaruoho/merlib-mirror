<strong>Book Reviews</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
I've been hitting the library a bit recently. Here's a couple of books I enjoyed.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?path=ASIN/159514031X&amp;tag=theaquamanwebsit">Peeps</a> by Scott Westerfeld: This is a cool take on the mythos of vampires, and it's educational as well. Yes, educational. I was referred to this book by Cory Doctorow of <a href="http://www.boingboing.net/2005/08/14/vampire_novel_as_a_w.html">Boing Boing</a>, and I think it pretty much lived up to his hype. It's a quick read, but dense with information on what the disease is and how it's being fought along with more information that most people ever want to know about parasites. I found it a little frustrating at times because the action was moving nicely, but I didn't feel like I was getting anything new. But the slow moments were few and far in between, and the attitude of the narrator made up for a lot of minor faults in my opinion. All in all, a pretty good book, especially if you like vampire stories. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0035.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/redirect?path=ASIN/1400041570&amp;tag=theaquamanwebsit">Mimi and Toutou's Big Adventure: The Bizarre Battle of Lake Tanganyika</a> by Giles Foden: Geoffrey Spicer-Simson was a career loser whose sometimes spectacular failures led to him being the oldest lieutenant commander in the Royal Navy at the start of WWI, stuck at a desk job in the heart of the Admiralty. But a shortage of officers gave him command of a possibly foolhardy mission into the heart of Africa to one of the largest lakes to sink a German ship with two oversized motorboats which had to be dragged from Cape Town to the lake. While this would make a pretty good tale just starting from those facts, when you add in the fact that Spicer had tons of tattoos and a liking for wearing skirts (not kilts, <em>skirts</em>) it becomes hilarious. This one got an unprecedented 20 out of 10 as a rating in Fortean Times, and while I wouldn't give it <em>quite</em> that much, it is a great book, and made all the moreso because it happens to be completely true. If you want a fun romp into the heart of Africa written in an intelligent and sensitive manner, definitely check this one out. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0040.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="4 starfish" /></a>

<strong>Rapid Reviews - 5 October 2005 - Part I</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Reviews are going to be slow in coming over the next week or so because I'm in a foul mood and have discovered I have nearly no interest in actually writing reviews at all. I'll do what I can, but since I'm not getting paid for this I'm not going to force myself to write. It's a hobby, it's supposed to be fun.<br />
<br />
<strong>JSA #78</strong>: "Lost &amp; Found" Part one of three: There is so much happening in this one that I can barely follow it. I don't think Keith Champagne believes in decompression in storytelling. However, what I <em>can</em> follow is way cool. War in the fifth dimension, crossover battles at the Rock of Eternity, Fate missing in action, and ... well, I gotta leave some surprises. It's confusing, yes. It could use a lot of help from the <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/JSA">Wiki</a> (particularly the whole Nabu thing, which I've never worked out). But it was still awesome. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0030.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="3 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Superman/Shazam: First Thunder #2</strong>: "Odd Couples": Still feels like an intro issue, although in this case we are meeting the villains of the piece. The story seems a little thin, but it isn't too bad. About your superhero average. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Superman/Shazam:_First_Thunder">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0025.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 1/2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Detective Comics #812</strong>: "City of Crime Part 10: Pearls and Fine China": For a minute there it got interesting, but then it turned out to be a dream. Or something like that. I'm not entirely sure. And I still can't figure out what Robin is up to. Very odd issue of a very strange story. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Detective_Comics">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0020.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 starfish" /></a><br />
<br />
<strong>Wonder Woman #221</strong>: "Pride of the Amazons": I'd say this one is a bit crucial to understanding what's happening in the whole Identity Crisis thing. Or at least following Wonder Woman's role in it. Not a bad story if it were simply by itself and contained to this book. But there are references within that need a passing knowledge of the tie-in book to really understand, and that's annoying. <a href="http://comics.wikicities.com/wiki/Wonder_Woman">Wiki</a>. <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2005/03/new-ratings-system.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/0020.jpg" data-border="0" width="75" height="15" alt="2 starfish" /></a>

<p>Monday, October 10, 2005 <span id="112898217102633006"></span></p>
<strong>Flipping Through Previews...</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/Aaaaagh.jpg" title="AAAAAUUUGGGGGHHHHH!!!!" data-align="right" width="100" height="75" alt="AAAAAUUUGGGGGHHHHH!!!!" />Yes, it's that time again... time for "Things in Previews That Look Interesting But I Can't Afford To Get (with a list of things I am getting just for contrast)". I'm going to start with the bulk of the book, and then come back and cover the "big four" at the end. Please remember, I'm listing what looks interesting. My actual purchase list is going to be much smaller. The numbers after each title are usually the page number in <a href="http://previews.diamondcomics.com/">Previews Magazine</a>, so you can follow along at home.<br />
<br />
Let's start with the covers. We've got Mickey skating on the front cover, and a very nice sculpture of Sub-Mariner on the back. Probably the most tame covers I've seen in a long time. Ok, Subby isn't wearing much, but at least he looks classic.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_FallenAngel.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />Moving on, let's hit the Splash Page (8-9). Not much of note here, except maybe the <em>Fallen Angel</em> bit. I'm not sure whether we'll be getting the new book... we'll have to see. This is going to be a bad month for us. Lots of stuff we want will have to be cut.<br />
<br />
Skipping past the "Premier Comics", we'll stop at <a href="http://www.wizarduniverse.com/">Wizard</a> (190-196). Not much of interest there, so on to the "Featured Items" of the Comics &amp; Graphic Novels (198-201). I'd be remiss to not mention the <em>Demo</em> trade, and hey, there's that <em>Fallen Angel</em> popping up again. I've been reading <em>Juror 13</em> over at <a href="http://www.tokyopop.com/takuhaionline/">Takuhai</a>, so I'm mildly interested in it... enough to mention it.<br />
<br />
And into the regular listings we go.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_GirlGenius.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" />The first item of real interest for us is <a href="http://www.airshipbooks.com/">Airship Entertainment</a> (209) and the new <em>Girl Genius</em> collections. Of course we're getting it! I have the first three in hardcovers.<br />
<br />
Airwave Comics (209) is putting out another version of <em>The Wizard of Oz</em> with art and character designs by Richard Maurizio. I haven't been able to find a website for the publisher, or any more detail than is in the solicit.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/">AiT/Planet Lar</a> (209-210) has the new collection of <em>Demo</em>, which I recommend to people who would like to try something different. If you've had enough of superheroes, but still want some superpowers, give it a shot.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_Lullaby.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />An annoying company is <a href="http://www.aliasenterprises.com">Alias</a> (210-214), who apparently are giving us an expensive prequel to <em>Lullaby</em>. At $6 and with no evidence that there is any Oz content, I think we'll pass on this one. Another book I'm interested in from Alias is <em>Robert Silverberg's The Seventh Shrine</em>, but I'm afraid the price is out of my range, even for the softcover, so I'll have to pass on this one too. How annoying.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.ambitionstudios.com/">Ambition Studios</a> (218) has a book called <em>Kid Beowulf</em> that I would certainly give a shot if I had the cash. The Previews on the website look good, if a little silly. But worth a look.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_OzTheManga.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" />Next up is <a href="http://www.antarctic-press.com/">Antarctic Press</a> (221-226) who publishes the delightful <em>Oz: The Manga</em> which only has two more issues to go. Oz fans should be checking this one out.<br />
<br />
Hubby-Eric and I ordered the first issue of <em>Paradox</em> from <a href="http://www.arcanastudio.com/">Arcana Studio</a> (227), so we'll get the second.<br />
<br />
Archaia Studios Press (227) is republishing <em>The Lone And Level Sands</em> in color as a hardcover. I enjoyed the trade, but I can't justify buying this again, despite how much I'd like to see it in color.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.committedcomics.com/">Committed Comics</a> (250) is back! As much as I'd like to support them, I didn't love <em>Neo Dawn</em> so I'm afraid I'll probably pass on this one. But I'll keep an eye out for more books from them.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_DorkTower.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />The cover to <em>Dork Tower #33</em> is nearly enough to get me to take the leap and start buying the book from <a href="http://www.gamespy.com/comics/dorkstorm/index.asp">Dork Storm Press</a> (270). But I'm not inclined to add another book. Even more than the cover to DT, however, is the cover to <em>The Collected Dork Tower Volume 8</em>. I had to show that one to hubby-Eric. What I will get from Dork Storm is <em>Nowick #31</em>.<br />
<br />
I absolutely loved the first three issues of <em>Action Philosophers</em>, so I'll be getting the fourth issue from <a href="http://www.eviltwincomics.com">Evil Twin Comics</a> (274).<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_Flare.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" /><a href="http://www.heroicpub.com/">Heroic Publishing</a> (281) has an item of note in that the cover is yet another comic cover homage to Norman Rockwell's <a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/freefromwant_jsa.jpg">Freedom From Want</a>.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.ibooks.net/">ibooks</a> (284) has the next collection of <em>Amelia Rules</em>, this one collecting the Superheroes story.<br />
<br />
And suddenly we get to <a href="http://www.idwpublishing.com/">IDW Publishing</a> (285-291) and the return of <em>Fallen Angel</em>. I'm still not sure we'll be getting it, but we've got a little time to decide. I will be getting <em>Angel: Old Friends #2</em> because I'm addicted.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_Dorothy.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />There's no question that we'll be getting <em>Dorothy #5</em> from <a href="http://www.dorothyofoz.net/">Illusive Productions</a> (292). After all, we like it.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.ludovicotechnique.com/">Ludovico Technique</a> (295) has a neat twist on the super concept: A Witness Protection Program for SUPER VILLAINS. <em>Living In Infamy #1</em> is definitely something I'd like to check out. We'll have to see if it makes the list.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.moonstonebooks.com/">Moonstone Books</a> (296) is showing off <em>Buckaroo Banzai</em> in a preview book. Tempting indeed.<br />
<br />
Continuing from the first issue, we'll be getting <em>Local #2</em> from <a href="http://www.onipress.com/">Oni Press</a> (310). Nothing else jumps out at me.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_Atlantis.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" />Moving along... <a href="http://www.rosenpublishing.com/">Rosen Publishing Group</a> (314-315) has a new series of books that make me want 'em, featuring "Mysteries" like Atlantis, The Bermuda Triangle, Bigfoot, Ghosts, The Loch Ness Monster, and UFOs. I'd like to get the lot, but I'm afraid I can't afford $60 worth of books, even if they do look like fun.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.speakeasycomics.com/">Speakeasy Comics</a> (317-328) has a lot of books. I think we might drop <em>Beowulf</em>, and we've already dropped <em>The Grimoire</em>, so I think there's nothing in there we're likely to jump in on.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_DorkTowerVol8.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />And that concludes the regular comic book section... whew... and off into the back of the book I go. There's an <em>Usagi Yojimbo</em> sourcebook in the Games section on page 493. Looks intriguing, but I'll pass on it. There's a full-page ad for the <em>Hikaru No Go</em> DVD on page 501. I really want to get this, but it's pretty expensive. I wish I could convince somebody to send me a review copy. In any case, the ad says that the anime series is 75 episodes and the Manga has 23 volumes. It's an excellent Manga series, and I'm glad to know how long it'll go now. The most recent solicit of the Manga was volume 5. Only 18 more to go!<br />
<br />
And that gets us through the comics and extras and sends us back to the front of the book to tackle the big four, and thus the bulk of our comic book order...<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_AgeOfBronze.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" /><a href="http://darkhorse.com/">Dark Horse</a> (17-53) is producing a 25 cent <em>Penny Arcade</em> book. I might have to get it. I will be getting both <em>Conan</em> books. I'm addicted. Curse you Kurt! You've got me into another character!!! Ahem. That appears to be it for our Dark Horse purchases this month, as there is no <em>Usagi Yojimbo</em>.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.imagecomics.com/">Image Comics</a> (131-177) has a look into the world of Wonderland with <em>The Looking Glass Wars: Hatter M</em> that hubby-Eric might be wanting. We'll have to see. <em>Age of Bronze #22</em> is certainly making the list, as is <em>Noble Causes #17</em>.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://marvelcomics.com/">Marvel Comics</a> (180). As usual, I turned down the Marvel Previews because having another book just for Marvel is stupid. As for what we'll get... let's see... we dropped <em>Amazing Spider-Man</em> because of a stupid crossover. Dropped <em>Supreme Power</em> because they split it into mini-series. No <em>Powers</em> this month. All that's left are the <em>What If</em> books, and I'll consider them, but it would be so much easier if Marvel just didn't produce anything I was interested in.<br />
<br />
And now I'm down to just one publisher... <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/">DC Comics</a>.<br />
<br />
Batman (59-64): Nothing I'll be getting for myself.<br />
<br />
Superman (65-70): Nothing I'll be getting for myself, but I'll note <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4568">Superman: True Brit</a> is coming out as a softcover.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4572"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/aqua/pics/cvr2003/cvra437.jpg" data-align="right" data-border="0" width="200" height="300" /></a>DC Universe (71-89): <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4572">Aquaman #37</a> makes the list. Other books that will probably make the list are <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4574">Birds of Prey #89</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4419">Green Lantern #7</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4578">Green Lantern Corps: Recharge #4</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4579">Green Arrow #57</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4599">Justice #3</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4600">JLA #123</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4601">JLA: Classified #15</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4602">JSA: Classified #6</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4605">JSA #80</a>, <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4607">Legion of Super-Heroes #13</a>, and <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4612">Solo #8</a>.<br />
<br />
I guess I'll probably get <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4571">Infinite Crisis #3</a> as well, since we started it. We might get <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4609">Outsiders #31</a>, but we might drop it with this issue. I'll probably get <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4613">Teen Titans #31</a> for the Tula zombie appearance. I swear, Tula has made more appearances after her death than before. I thought the whole point of <em>JLA: Classified</em> was to keep all the extra stories in one title, but apparently DC doesn't think that two JLA titles are enough, as they started the a new mini with <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4604">JLA: Classified: Cold Steel #1</a>. I don't know yet if we'll get it, but probably since Aquaman may be in it. And I'm not sure about <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4597">Showcase Presents: Justice League of America Vol 1</a>, either, although I wouldn't mind getting it. I like having "DC Essentials", and hope they do some Golden Age ones soon.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200510_JSA.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />Johnny DC/DC Library/CMX (90-100): <a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4677">Justice League Unlimited #16</a> is it. Nothing else.<br />
<br />
Wildstorm (101-111): Nothing I'll be getting for myself.<br />
<br />
Vertigo (112-122): Nothing I'll be getting for myself.<br />
<br />
DC Direct (123-130): Nothing I'll be getting for myself.<br />
<br />
And there it is... all of Previews. I'm finally all the way through. Huzzah. So that's it for this month's "Things in Previews That Look Interesting But I Can't Afford To Get (with a list of things I am getting just for contrast)". Comments? Questions? Did I miss any really cool books that you know about and would like to point out? Do you disagree with something I wrote, and want to correct me? There's the comments, right below. Post something. And, yes, I accept review copies. I'm willing to give almost anything a try.<br />
