# Benelux Births Deaths & Marriages Exchange

*Source page: `~chance/misc/benelux/index.html`*

***B**enelux*

***B**irths or baptisms,* ***D**eaths &* ***M**arriages* ***E**xchange*

|  |  |  |
|----|----|----|
| **English Version** | [Dutch Version](http://www.eskimo.com/~chance/misc/benelux/benelux_nl.shtml) | [French Version](http://www.eskimo.com/~chance/misc/benelux/benelux_fr.shtml) |

\
The goal of the Benelux\* Births or baptism, Deaths and Marriages Exchange is to provide genealogists a free resource for sharing information about details contained on birth or baptismal, death and marriage certificates registered in the Benelux region\*.

Please remember that for our purposes the word **certificate** includes, but is not limited to

> certified copies\
> photographic or Xerox copies\
> inspections of birth, death and marriage registrations

Since contributing to this project is voluntary, when you receive a request for information, you can a) mail at your expense; or b) ask for a stamped self-addressed envelope to provide the information. How the information is exchanged between individuals will be agreed upon by the interested genealogists.

The certificates which are unwanted and therefore available will show the surname in **BOLD**. For more details from any certificate shown, please click the Email link to contact the owner directly.

\
\* The Benelux region consists of Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg.

If you have any questions regarding this page, please contact [Vicki Lindsay Thauvin](mailto:chance@eskimo.com) or [Jan Daamen](mailto:j.daamen@wxs.nl).

There are currently 114 records in the Benelux BDM Exchange.
