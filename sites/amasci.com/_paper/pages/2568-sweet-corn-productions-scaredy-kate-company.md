<td><strong>Sweet Corn Productions</strong> was founded by Demian in 1971. Since then, Sweet Corn has developed and produced a broad range of video, film and radio drama, as well as live productions and workshops throughout New England, in San Francisco and Seattle. With the addition of Steve Bryant as music director in 1982, Sweet Corn added music concerts to its repertoire.
