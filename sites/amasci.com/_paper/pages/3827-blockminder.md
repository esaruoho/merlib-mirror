<td width="338">You don't have room for a design wall? You've rolled your design wall up after a class to get home and find you have a big mess? You would like to take your piecing project with you while traveling, but you're tired of not remembering where the pieces belong? Or maybe you're tired of the pieces getting all wrinkled in those plastic bags. Well, here's the answer! BlockMinder is a fabric covered book with flannel pages. It helps keep your projects neat and in order while you're working on them. Separator pages keep the pieces from getting mixed up and allow you to use both sides of each flannel page. The 14" X 14" pages make BlockMinder great for working on traditional quilt blocks, applique as well as for use in making my mosaic quilt patterns. Perfect to use in class, while traveling and at home. BlockMinder also works as an easle at your sewing machine.
