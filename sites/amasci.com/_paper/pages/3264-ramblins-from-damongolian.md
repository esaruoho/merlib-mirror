<a href="http://fridayfive.org">Friday Five</a><br />
<br />
<strong>1. What is your favorite restaurant and why?</strong> mmm....sushi... There's this really great sushi place near my house called <a href="http://www.starmidas.com/%20">Star Midas</a>, I love it there, it doesn't hurt that they're like 5 minutes away from my house! =)
<p><br />
<strong>2. What fast food restaurant are you partial to?</strong> McDonald's, but probably only because there's not an <a href="http://www.in-n-out.com/%20">In-N-Out Burger</a> within driving distance of work. (lunch break driving that is)</p>
<p><br />
<strong>3. What are your standards and rules for tipping?</strong> 15-20% of the total, including tax. If it's a sushi place that I'm 'known' at or want to be 'known' at, it'll usually be on the higher side.</p>
<p><br />
<strong>4. Do you usually order an appetizer and/or dessert?</strong> Appetizers yes, if there's anything that sounds tasty =)</p>
<p><br />
<strong>5. What do you usually order to drink at a restaurant?</strong> If I'm not DRINKING, then Sprite is my soda of choice, if they don't have Sprite, 7-up is an acceptable substitution. Mountain Dew and Slice are NOT acceptable. Root Beer or orange soda will also do.</p>
