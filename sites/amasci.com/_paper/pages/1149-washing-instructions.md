Laundry Instructions
<p>When you purchase products from BabyTown, you will be given a set of laundry instructions. We have reproduced the list here for your convenience.</p>
<p>We recommend that all of our products be prewashed before use.</p>
<table data-cellpadding="5" width="100%">
<tbody>
<tr data-bgcolor="fff59c">
<td style="text-align: right;"><strong>Washing your Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Cotton Diapers</strong></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<ol>
<li><strong>Prewash diapers twice before using.</strong></li>
<li>After Changing your baby's diaper, flush stool down the toilet and place wet or soiled diaper into a diaper pail.</li>
<li>Soak the diapers in a pail filled with 1/2 water and 15ml of Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> <a href="laundry.html">Laundry Brightener</a>, or 1/4 cup vinegar or washing soda. It is not recommended to soak diapers more than three days.</li>
<li>To transfer diapers in the pail to a laundry machine, either hand wring diapers and use lid of pail to carry OR pour all of the diaper pail contents into the washing machine, turn on rinse cycle and spin solutions out of the diapers.</li>
<li>Use the Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> <a href="laundry.html">Liquid Laundry Concentrate</a>. Do not use Bleach, Detergents with Bleach, Pure Soap Products (such as Ivory Snow), strong detergent products - liquid or powder (such as Tide).</li>
<li><em>Care in drying your diapers will add to their life.</em> Dry diapers on medium setting for half of the drying cycle and then hang damp diapers to dry for the balance. Softeners in the washing machine and dryer are not recommended as they reduce the absorbency of the diapers.</li>
<li><em>To remove stains,</em> rub a bar of "white" soap on the wetted down stain before placing in the diaper pail. Try hanging diapers in the sun after laundering to naturally bleach stain away. Add Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> <a href="laundry.html">Laundry Brightener</a> to washing machine. For extra tough stains, mix 5ml Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Liquid Laundry Concentrate with 5ml Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Laundry Brightener and apply past to stain. Launder as usual.</li>
</ol>

(Breathable or Waterproof Pull-Ups, Breathable or Waterproof Wraps, Cotton Covered Pull-Up and Cotton Covered Wraps)</strong></td>

<ol>
<li>Caring for your Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Pants properly will add to their life. Breathable Nylon fabrics should always be hand washed and hung to dry. Waterproof nylon fabrics can be machine washed. Never place Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Pants in the dryer. To maintain the vivid colors of Cotton Covered Pull Ups and Wraps, soak in a saltwater solution (5ml salt in a basin of water) prior to washing.</li>
<li>For premature babies or those with very tiny legs, hand stitch the Lycra/spandex on the legs to make the leg openings smaller. Undo the stitching as the baby grows.</li>
</ol>

<p>The Flushabye<sup>TM</sup> Diaper Liner's size and content make it safe to flush down toilets. It should be noted, however, that plumbing systems and their effectiveness vary. Too many Flushabyes<sup>TM</sup> flushed down a toilet at one time or during one day in a less than adequate plumbing system may cause problems. To avoid this difficulty, we recommend that only one Flushabye<sup>TM</sup> be flushed at one time. We also recommend that they only be flushed when soiled. Wet liners can be disposed of in the garbage. It is also helpful to let the Flushabye<sup>TM</sup> Liner sit a few moments in the toilet before flushing. Homes with septic tanks can use the Flushabyes<sup>TM</sup> if they follow the above tips.</p>

<p><strong>* It is recommended that you wash all pads before using them to bring them to their maximum absorbency and wash away any possible manufacturing residues. When washing Outer Pockets, remember to attach the Velcro<sup>TM</sup> tabs together to prevent it from sticking</strong> <strong>to other clothes or from collecting lint.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Remove soiled pads and rinse thoroughly in cold water to remove heavy soiling. If it is not convenient to rinse thoroughly immediately after use, then wet the pad and store it in a waterproof bag until you can rinse it. (if allowed to dry soiled, the pad will stain.)</li>
<li>Soak rinsed pads in soaking container with Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Laundry Brightener or fill the container with 2/3 water and 2 tablespoons of white vinegar or washing soda. Change water and cleaning solution every day.</li>
<li>When you are ready to wash the pads, throw the pads and the contents directly in the washing machine along with your ordinary "light" wash, and use warm water - or hand wash in warm water. We recommend the use of the Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Liquid Laundry Concentrate or any other environmentally friendly detergent. DO NOT USE Bleach, detergents with bleach, pure soap products, or strong detergents (liquid or powder) to launder the pads.</li>
<li>Care in drying will also add to the life of the product. For machine drying, use the medium setting and try using it for only half of the drying cycle, hang drying for the balance. Softeners are not recommended as they may coat the material fibers and thus reduce absorbency. Full air drying, especially sun drying, is a natural sanitizer and whitener, so try to use these methods whenever possible.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>* If you follow these instructions, the pads should come out clean. If for any reason they do become stained, please DO NOT USE Chlorine Bleach. It is harmful to the environment and with repeated use will eventually break down the material fibers of the product. It will turn the Velcro<sup>TM</sup>, on the outer pocket wings, brown. We recommend the use, in the washing machine, of the Indisposables<sup>TM</sup> Laundry Brightener, or any other environmentally friendly, NON CHLORINE BLEACH substitute. Before washing , soak the stained pads in water for at least 24 hours, then wash in warm water as usual.</strong></p></td>
