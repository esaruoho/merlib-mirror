## Legs

\

Occam's razor must have been the cut-throat type.... Certainly it never shaved a leg. This whole leg-shaving thing really should be simple, you do or you don't, but it's not....

I ran across a woman at a dance class a while back (ok, she was about 20 which, in my opinion makes her still a "girl" and her attitude went along with it) who had the all-fired temerity to get on me about shaving my legs. Not that I ought to, but that I ought to stop.

Bite me, chicky. I have great legs, at least from the knee down --I'm still working on that thigh-thing--and I'll be damned if I'm going to obscure the delicate sculpture of my ankles with fuzz so some people can feel secure in their own bullshit.

It is "politically correct", at the moment, for women to "free themselves from the male-dominated view of feminine beauty" by refusing to shave their legs or underarms, eschewing makeup and wearing clothes that make them look like a sack of rags. I read some stupid magazine article recently in which several silly women were being lauded to the skies for "breaking the mold". One had quit her traditional job to start her own business, another had sold her house and bought a motorcycle (must be one hell of a motorcycle is all I can say), one had "given up shaving" her legs. Huh? Is this on a par with boldly taking on the male-establishment in business or pursuing a dream of speed and independence? Heck, this just sounds like either an excuse for being lazy, or a way of apologizing for not playing the game, to me.

Now, I'm hardly a fashion-victim, about as politically-correct as a white sheet at an ACLU meeting and distinctly not interested in whether or not anything I do or say is socially relevant, but I think that deliberately playing coy about what you are and what you want is kind of twisted and selfish (sick and kinky we can get to later...,) especially when you cover it up with a bullshit motive like being politically correct.

I admit, that I'm as lazy as the next nut and I may decide that I don't want to be bothered with something for a while, but, I think my legs look great fuzz-less and, being the self-aggrandizing, insecure, neurotic bitch that I am, I want people to admire them. So, I shave them and to hell with being politically correct. After all, if you've got something that makes you superior, exploit it, say I (and that's as un-PC as you can get). And I've got some superior legs.

Ahhh... but that is making an asset out of the mere luck of physical appearance, so I was told. It's unfair to your fellow-women.... Yeah, right. (I'm not even going to touch the phrase "fellow-women"....) First off, sweet-face, I work damned hard for these legs, though I did start with good material. Secondly, it's just as unfair to my fellow Homo sapiens sapiens that I happen to be above-average in intelligence, born to a culturally powerful ethnicity, in an affluent country and shockingly erudite. I'm not going to play stupid, I'm not going to apologize for the color of my skin or attempt to change it, nor am I going to move to some disadvantaged state so I can ameliorate the sin of being American or suddenly cease to speak and write articulately.

And I'm not going to stop shaving my legs, wearing clothes that compliment my figure, cut off my long hair or start bowing to someone else's politically-sacred cows.

Hmmm... why do I suddenly have an urge for sacred hamburger...?

this was written before the injury to my knee which put me out of action for 8 months, but they are looking pretty good again after quite a bit of time at the gym.

## Such Silliness....

\

Whoopeee!

Sometimes I just get silly for no reason I can pin down. Music, maybe, the company of good friends in a good mood... whatever it is it's fun and all too rare.

Too often the creatives live in tiny boxes and we get so used to staring at the walls of our own inventions and imposed limits that we forget that this isn't the whole big world. We pull down and bury ourselves in the work.

Jump up! Hey, it's kind of cool out here with the silly people. Not as a steady, diet, maybe, but for a lark, it's... well, a lark. Get me lit enough and I'll stand on the back deck and sing Vivaldi or dance tango steps at bus stops. Heck, I don't even have to be drunk on alcohol to do it. Drunk on giddiness will do.

While laughing my head off with a friend over dinner in a pub, noticed that the single geek at the table next over was trying not to stare at us. Poor thing: crouching over his laptop and annoyed that the girls at the next were laughing and talking about sex, music, art and beer....

Obviously the poor fella needed a few more beers himself....
