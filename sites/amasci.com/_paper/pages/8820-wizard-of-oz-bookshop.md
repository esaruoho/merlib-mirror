# Wizard of Oz Bookshop

*Source page: `~tiktok/bookshop.html`*

***Wizard of Oz*** Bookshop\
Lobby
============================

Welcome! This is an exciting place, because here, you can buy Oz books, books by L. Frank Baum and other Oz authors, Oz activity books, cassettes, CDs, videos, even computer software, all at the click of your mouse button. I've tried to give you a wide selection to choose from, in all price ranges, so there's sure to be something you like. (Oz books also make great gifts for the Oz fan on your shopping list!) And I will try to keep it updated, especially when new books are published. (Please note that this bookshop is still under construction, and not all of the inventory has been placed on the shelves yet. And I might add that the book covers shown in this shop do not necessarily reflect the actual covers of the books offered.)

This service is presented in association with [<img src="pics/logo_micro_bw.gif" data-align="middle" data-border="1" width="25" height="23" /> Amazon.com](amazon.html) in the United States, and its British subsidiary, <img src="pics/uk_b_logo_small.gif" data-align="middle" data-border="1" width="100" height="30" /> Amazon.co.uk. To order or find out more about a particular book, including price, simply click on either company's logo. When books are available from another seller as well, the link will take you to a source of more information. This icon [<img src="pics/small_club.gif" data-align="middle" data-border="1" width="25" height="25" />](http://www.ozclub.org/iwocpubs.asp), for instance, will take you to a source of order forms for products available from the International Wizard of Oz Club.

Please note that many of these books are out-of-print or otherwise unavailable. While Amazon.com does have an out-of-print search service for such books, you may want to try other sources as well. [This link](links.html#bookshops) will take you to some online resources that might be of help.

To comment on or ask questions about this bookshop, [click here](mailto:tiktok@eskimo.com?subject=Oz_Bookshop).

## Aisle 1 - The Oz books of L. Frank Baum

The books that started it all. The fourteen Oz novels by the original Royal historian of Oz (including numerous versions of *The Wonderful Wizard of Oz*), and other books Baum wrote about Oz.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle2.html)

## Aisle 2 - The "Official" Oz books by other authors

The rest of the Oz series, by Royal Historians Ruth Plumly Thompson, John R. Neill, Jack Snow, Rachel Cosgrove Payes, and Eloise Jarvis McGraw and Lauren Lynn McGraw. Includes some of their Oz writings outside of the series.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle3.html)

## Aisle 3 - Other Oz books

Many other writers have tried their hand at writing Oz stories outside of the regular series. Here is a wide selection of those that are currently available.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle4.html)

## Aisle 4 - Non-Oz books by Oz authors and illustrators

The Royal Historians of Oz wrote other works as well. Many of L. Frank Baum's works were later tied into the Oz series. You can purchase some of what they've written here.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle5.html)

## Aisle 5 - Non-fiction, reference, and biography

Books and publications about Oz and its authors. Much of the information on this website came from these books.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle6.html)

## Aisle 6 - The Movie

It's the most watched movie in history, so of course there are many books about it. From the movie on video, scripts, music, and behind-the-scenes books, to postcards and cookbooks, if it's about The Movie, it's here.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle7.html)

## Aisle 7 - Just for kids (of all ages)

These products are specially selected for the kid in us all. Adaptations, pop-up books, coloring and activity books, books you cut up and make things with, and other fun stuff.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle8.html)

## Aisle 8 - Dramatic Presentations

You don't have to just read Oz, you can watch it on your VCR, listen to it on your cassette or CD player, or see it on the stage. Find many of them here, as well as books and other items about them.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle9.html)

## Aisle 9 - Everything else

All of the rest, items that don't quite fit into any of the other aisles. Includes computer software, maps, non-Oz books with major Oz references, toys, games, and lots of other surprises.

<img src="pics/colorbar.gif" data-border="0" width="600" height="1" />

[](aisle10.html)

## Aisle 10 - You might also like...

None of these items have to do with Oz, but you might also find them enjoyable. All are books appropriate for children of all ages.
