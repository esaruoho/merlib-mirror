# MARK IV Variable Speed Faceting Machine

MARK IV Variable Speed

Faceting Machine

**FAMOUS GRAVES**

FACETING HEAD

The Mark IV utilizes the proven Graves faceting head with radial vernier (cheater).

New Dial-A-Speed

Variable Speed

Lap speed from 0 RPM to 600 RPM;

Quality Built

Full use of stainless steel and brass parts. Precision built for a life time. Sealed ball bearings; engineered by skilled instrument designers who are experienced faceters.

<div align="center">

The New MARK IV is | truly a compact unit weighing approximately 27 lbs. and needs a space of only 9 1/2" x 17 1/2". Compact in size, yet uses full 8" laps and all standard machine accessories. Ruggedly built for years of trouble-free service. |
