<h2 id="our-mission">Our Mission</h2>
<p>Is to provide hydroplane fans with the ultimate experience. A ride in an unlimited hydroplane while at the same time raising awareness for the <a href="paulfund.htm">Paul Gordon Children's Fund</a> which helps children who need organ transplants in conjunction with the <a href="http://www.transplantfund.org">National Transplant Assistance Fund</a>.</p>
<p>Until now, no one has ever had the chance to ride in a 4-seat turbine powered unlimited hydroplane. Why not be one of the first to experience this unique opportunity?</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="company-profile">Company Profile</h2>
<p>Over the years, unlimited hydroplane pilot Mark Evans has repeatedly tried to answer the most often asked question in interviews: "What's it like?".</p>
<p>The intense experience is so hard to fully explain that he has finally decided to just show people who really want to know. The U-4 hydroplane has been a long-term project and dream of Mark for years now. A lot of hard (and mostly volunteer) work has paid off and finally made the dream a reality.</p>
