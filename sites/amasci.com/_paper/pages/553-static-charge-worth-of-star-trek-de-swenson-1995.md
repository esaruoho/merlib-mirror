&#10;<h1 id="static-charge-worthy-of-star-trek">Static Charge Worthy of "Star Trek"</h1>
David E. Swenson<br />
3M Electrical Specialties Division<br />
Austin, TX<br />
&#10;<h3 id="overview">OVERVIEW</h3>
<ul>
<li>Polypropylene film tape manufacturing plant</li>
<li>S.E. US</li>
<li>Study in month of August - long, long ago</li>
<li>Reports of "strange things happening" around flim slitting machine</li>
<li>Contamination of film web one of the problems reported</li>
</ul>
<br />
&#10;<h3 id="film-extrusion">FILM EXTRUSION</h3>
<ul>
<li>A polypropylene web produced with a width of 21 feet</li>
<li>Static in this area not reported as a problem - used combination induction and nuclear static elimination devides throughout</li>
<li>Wide web converted (slit) into "film jumbos" with width of 3 feet to 5 feet for coating with adhesive to make tape</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="climate-conditions">CLIMATE CONDITIONS</h3>
<ul>
<li>In the month of August, daytime temperature often over 100°F and R.H. above 95%</li>
<li>At time of first measurement in this study, early in the morning, the temperature was 80°F and about 75-80% R.H.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="field-voltage-measurements">FIELD (VOLTAGE) MEASUREMENTS</h3>
<ul>
<li>Early in the morning, voltage was impossible to measure with a 200KV@12 inches measurement device from 20 feet away</li>
<li>Operators of slitting equipment suggested I not get too close for awhile</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="slitter-description">SLITTER DESCRIPTION</h3>
[Figure 1]<br />
&#10;<h3 id="observations">OBSERVATIONS:</h3>
<ul>
<li>Early in the morning, voltage measurements off scale from 20 feet away</li>
<li>Upon entering "tent" created by web unwind stand, possible to feel static charge.</li>
<li>When exactly half-way into area, static charge created a "wall" and t was impossible to walk through.</li>
<li>Observed a fly get caught in the web</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="observations-and-reporting">OBSERVATIONS AND REPORTING</h3>
<ul>
<li>Meeting attended with plant management</li>
<li>Plant Manager went to area, operators working normally around equipment</li>
<li>Plant Manager walked through "tent" with no problem, and said "I must be crazy"</li>
<li>Asked Plant Manager to join me early next morning</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="observations-continued">OBSERVATIONS CONTINUED</h3>
<ul>
<li>Next morning, met Plant Manager at 6:30 and static measurements made - same as previous morning</li>
<li>Plant Manager entered "tent" area and his curly gray heair straightened.</li>
<li>He made it half-way and could not continue</li>
<li>Could not decide if it "should be fixed or sell tickets"</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="corrective-action">CORRECTIVE ACTION</h3>
<ul>
<li>Installed induction static eliminator across web at unwind</li>
<li>Static immediately reduced throughout process to less than 50KV</li>
<li>Possible to make measurements in rest of area</li>
<li>Additional induction units installed at jumbo wind-up areas - &lt;5KV at conclusion</li>
</ul>
