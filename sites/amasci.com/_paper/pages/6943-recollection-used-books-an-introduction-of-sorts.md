# RECOLLECTION USED BOOKS: An Introduction of Sorts

*Source page: `~recall/intro.html`*

**An introduction to *[Recollection Fine Used Books](http://www.recollectionbooks.com/index.html)***

------------------------------------------------------------------------

> Recollection Used Books is in its fifth year, a successor to a/k/a Used Books in Seattle's University District.
>
> We maintain a broad selection of literature in most subject areas, with emphasis on modern, collectible & signed literature, social & radical studies, labor history & politics, Afro-American & Black Studies, The Beats, Music (Jazz, blues, rock & folk), The Sixties, the Viet Nam War, The Cold War, Baseball, Fishing, Mountaineering, cookbooks, chess, mystery & detective novels, & various other fiction & non-fiction subjects.
>
> One of our purposes of being involved with used books is educational, both for ourselves & our customers. We are interested in all kinds of books & authors & related matters, & no matter what expertise or experience we bring to this endeavor, we are always still learning -- & we hope to share & make available some of these things to you. The Internet & the Web especially resolve numerous limitations of printed catalogs, which are extremely costly to produce & mail & allow little room for anything more than the briefest of descriptions.
>
> The primary purpose, of course, is to serve as a provider of books, putting people & books together. Our Web Pages reflect these concerns as we add & refine presentation & content. We recommend checking in periodically for improvements & the many books which we are offering in our catalogs. We presently have over 3,500 books on line, & add 100+ a month.
>
> "Who's on first?"
>
> We have a small staff of nine . . . all standing on first, trying to figure out how they got there or where exactly home plate is.

David Brown. An original founding member, about 13 years ago, of the collectively owned & operated a/k/a Used Books, which went out of business a few months after he left to start Recollection Books. Presently *BleedMeister* for Recollection Books ["Daily Bleed"](http://www.eskimo.com/~recall/bleed/calmast.htm), an online calendar sent out daily(!) in both email & web format, with every day of the year archived.

Forthcoming, as in then now & maybe, more or less....

We intend to add features to our Web Site. Focus is on book-related materials & quality content. In 1997 we began issuing the [Daily Bleed](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Erecall/bleed/calmast.htm), (*Better Than Boiled Coffee!*) our own wierd take on history. In 1998 we began archiving them, & these are accessible for everyday of the year. The Daily Bleed is sent out daily(!)to our subscription list & a number of online discussion groups. In 1999 we began adding a [Gallery of Saints & Sinners](http://www.eskimo.com/%7Erecall/bleed/gallery/) in conjunction with the Bleed. In 2000 we began scanning & posting online scarce interesting materials from the**[Stan Iverson Archives](http://recollectionbooks.com/siml/)** which we are custodians of. Recollection Books is presently active in soliciting funds & book donations for the **Books-to-Prisoners Project** (sends books into the prisons for free). We presently have the [Seattle Used Bookstore Guide](http://www.recollectionbooks.com/seattle.html) (70+ stores) online, maintained by David & printed copies (in conjunction with Louis Collins Rare Books), are distributed free throughout Seattle area bookstores. David also maintains the [Pacific NW Used Bookstore Guide](http://www.recollectionbooks.com/nwbooks.html) (online only, but downloadable & printable) which covers Idaho, Oregon, Washington, British Columbia & Alaska. We also make available our own [Vietnam War Checklist](http://recollectionbooks.com/vietnamlist.html) of 4,900 titles, many annotated. Also, possibly, we may begin issuing a Newsletter & a small catalog of new books (a small offering relevant to our interests).\
