Usually I put political stuff here, but to heck with that. I don't own firearms in order to debate constitutional law, and founder's intent, with pinheads who've never fired a gun, and can't understand why I would.

So here's a section for firearms related stuff, that is, and will remain apolitcal.

For the record, I own numerous firearms, at current count, between my [wife](http://www.eskimo.com/~strange) and I, we house something on the order of a dozen rifles, handguns, and one shotgun.
