# Chinese Finger Trap Part 5

General Bibliography

Here is a list of books that I think that anyone with an interest in the subject about which the book is written might read with profit. After the Tears: Reclaiming the Personal Losses of Childhood., by Jane Middelton-Moz & Lorie Dwinell. Health Communications, Inc., Enterprise Center, 3201 S.W. 15th. St., Deerfield Beach, Fl., 33442. (A psychoanalytic/family of origin view of ACOA issues.) Becoming Orgasmic: A Sexual and Personal Growth Program for Women., by J.R. Heiman, Ph.d. and J.L. Lopiccolo, Ph.D. Prentice Hall Press, New York. (The title says it all.) Daddy's Girl: A memoir., by C. Allen. Simon & Schuster, New York. (A moving book about incest) The Drama of the Gifted Child., by Alice Miller. Basic Books, Inc./Harper Colophon Books. New York. (A book about how narcissistic parents form and deform the emotional lives of their children.) Families and how to survive them., by Robin Skyner and John Cleese. Oxford University Press, New York. (A book about families, written with a psychoanalytic/family system perspective. The book is amusing as well as informative.) The Family Crucible., by August Napier & Carl Whittaker. Harper and Rowe, New York. (A good book about family systems thinking, the book is entertaining and easy to read.) Family Ties That Bind., by Dr. Ronald W. Richardson. Self- Counsel Press, Inc. 2303 N. Northgate Way, Seattle, WA, 98133. (A book for people from conflict-avoidant families.) For Your Own Good., by Alice Miller. Farar, Straus, Giroux, New York. (About physical punishment of children, and its consequences.) Human Sexual Inadequacy., by W.H. Masters, M.D. and V.E Johnson. A Bantam Book, New York. (Short on warmth and charm, but the best book on overcoming a variety of sexual problems.) I Know Why the Caged Bird Sings., by M. Angelou. Random House, New York. (Another moving book about incest.) Intimate Partners: Patterns in Love and Marriage. by Maggie Scarf. Random House, New York. (A psychoanalytic/systemic book about marriage.) Learning to Live Without Violence: A Handbook for Men. by Daniel Jay Sonkin, Ph.D. and Michael Durphy, M.D. Volcano Press Inc., 330 Ellis St., San Francisco, CA (A workbook about teaching yourself anger management.) Repeat After Me., by Claudia Black, Ph.D. M.A.C. Book Dept., 1850 High St., Denver Colorado, 80218. (An ACOA/Family of Origin workbook, meant to help the reader explore his memories and feelings.) Sanity, Madness and the Family., by R.D. Laing and A. Esterson. A Pelican Book published by Penguin Books Inc., 7110 Ambassador Road, Baltimore, Maryland, 21207. (A book about schizophrenia and its causes.)
