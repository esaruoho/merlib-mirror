<th style="text-align: left;" data-valign="top"><h2 id="reasons-to-contact-a-customer-service-center" data-align="center">Reasons to Contact a Customer Service Center</h2>
<br />
&#10;<ul>
<li>Request an application</li>
<li>Inquire about the availability of a title or an artist</li>
<li>Request a print catalog:<br />
BMG: 'Music Central'<br />
BMG Classical: 'Encore: The Collector's Guide'<br />
CH/PLAY: 'A to Z Music Guide'<br />
</li>
<li>Place an order (This can be faster than sending in the reply card.)</li>
<li>Check on the status of an order</li>
<li><a href="monthsel.htm">Decline/accept the Selection of the Month</a></li>
<li><a href="fulfilling.htm">Received an unordered/unwanted selection</a></li>
<li><a href="fulfilling.htm">Received a flawed selection</a></li>
<li>Received a selection with a broken jewel case - request replacement jewel case</li>
<li>Request change of preferred music category</li>
<li><a href="ethical.htm#mail">Request your address be removed from Mailing Lists going to outside companies</a></li>
<li>Going on vacation - Request that account be suspended until return</li>
<li><a href="monthsel.htm">Request to be placed on the "positive option" plan</a></li>
<li>Other questions or problems</li>
<li><a href="quitting.htm">Cancel membership</a></li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> When writing to a club's customer service center by US Mail or email, include your name, complete US Mail address, and club account number (if already a member).</p></th>
