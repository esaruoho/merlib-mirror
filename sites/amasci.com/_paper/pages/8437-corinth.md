# Corinth (Κόρινθος)

Corinth was a major trade and manufacturing center throughout the classical Greek period.  It benefited from being near a narrow isthmus separating the Corinthian Gulf and the Saronic Gulf.  Ships were taken out of the water and pulled across land to get to the other side.  In modern times there is a [canal](http://www.scripophily.net/corcan18.html) built through this isthmus allowing ships to pass.  In fact, the idea of building a canal there goes back thousands of years.  The Roman Emperor Nero ceremoniously started building a canal around 62 BC, but this was unsuccessful and the canal wasn't actually finished until 1893.

Corinth reached its peak later under Roman rule, when it became famous for extravagance and decadence - sort of the Las Vegas of its day.
