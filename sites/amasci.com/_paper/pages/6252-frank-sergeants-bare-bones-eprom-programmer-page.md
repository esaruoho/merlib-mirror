# Frank Sergeant's Bare Bones EPROM Programmer Page

(edited 28 August 2002)

I am no longer distributing or supporting this. I had hopes that I might redo it some day but that looks less likely year by year. I have used it to program 2716, 2764, 27128, 27256 style EPROMs. It probably works with EEPROMs as well. If you would like to play with it, with no guarantees or support, you are welcome to download the following files:

- [bones.zip (135K)](bones.zip) Plans, software, instructions (for DOS IBM PC)
- [pcb.zip (96K)](pcb.zip) PCB.BMP image for the printed circuit board
- [schem.zip (49K)](schem.zip) SCHEM.BMP image for the schematic
- [bonessrc.zip (90K)](bonessrc.zip) the key source files
