<td rowspan="3" width="382" height="338" data-valign="TOP"><p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="babyboy.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;babyboy.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=251,height=348,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/littleboysml.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="50" height="70" alt="Baby" /></a><br />
Beau was my first Cria, my other two were almost a year old when we got them. I took one look at his face and just knew I couldn't live with out him. This is how the addiction begins. This page is being written for those of you that haven't seen baby cria's and don't know what they look like as they mature.</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="bab1f.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;bab1f.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=202,height=248,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/bab1fsmall.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="71" height="87" alt="Baby" /></a><br />
<strong>Beau at 7 Months, now with ears.</strong>This is Beau with a friends daughter, we tried to expose him to different people and allow them to take him for short walks. He was easy to train and well behaved. Notice that he now has ears, in the first picture he was holding his ears back...but he really was born with them, they don't just suddenly appear.</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="show.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;show.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=213,height=188,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/showsml.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="96" height="85" alt="Baby" /></a><br />
<strong>Beau at 14 months.</strong>I felt like Beau was in jail, this was his first &amp; last adventure of going to a show. I was the one that was tramatized, he seemed to do just fine. I didn't realize when I signed up to show him that he had to SPEND THE NIGHT THERE!!! I was back there at 5:30 AM to rescue him and to my amazement no one else was at the fair grounds except for the security people. All the lights in the llama barn were off, not a sound inside and all the llama's sleeping. I didn't know what to do because I didn't want to wake up all the llamas and I couldn't sneak in to get just mine.</p>

<td colspan="2"><p>I waited until another llama owner showed up around 7:00 and then there were two of us. He went in the barn and I was able to go rescue my boy. Had a nice long talk and I kept apologizing for sticking him in this mess. Many people love doing shows, guess I am not cut out for this.</p>

<strong>Beau at 18 months</strong> - Charlie is the llama with the white chest facing you, Beau has the fanny shot with his head turned around looking back at me. At this point I was starting to see some real change. Today his fanny shot would take up much more of the screen.</p>

<strong>Beau at 2 years</strong> - At two Beau is becoming my young adult male. He has such a gentle nature and always reminds me of a wise old owl. There is still another one to two years of growth for him. This photo was taken just a month short of being two. It's funny though, when I look at him now I still see MY Baby.</p>
<p><em>(Click on Photo to enlarge)</em><a href="adult2lrg.html" onclick="window.open(&#39;adult2lrg.html&#39;,&#39;&#39;,&#39;width=247,height=207,scrollbars=NO,menubar=NO,toolbar=NO,location=NO&#39;);return false"><img src="images/adult2sml.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" width="111" height="93" alt="Baby" /></a><br />

<strong>Beau at 3 1/2 years</strong> - At this point he should be pretty much full grown, but he still could grow a bit over the next few months. He is still an intact male but has never (like all the other boys) been used at stud. I love his disposition and didn't want to mess up his life with women:^)) He does however have a very studly presence.</p>
<p>That little bitty baby sure turned into one big boy. Funny though because even when I look at him now I still see my baby, guess I always will. His wonderful mellow disposition has never changed but he is very dominate in the herd.</p></td>
