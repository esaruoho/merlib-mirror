<h4 id="germans-to-america-series"><img src="images/lux.gif" />  Germans to America Series</h4>
Filby, P. William and Ira A. Glazier, eds. Germans to America : lists of passengers arriving at U.S. ports. Wilmington, Del. : Scholarly Resources, 1988 - .
Currently there are over 60 volumes covering the period of 1850-1891. When complete the series will index through 1893.
Although <em>Germans to America</em> is primarily an index to German immigrants to the United States, many Luxembourgers are also included in this index. It is not uncommon in ship's manifests - or U.S. federal census records for that matter - for Luxembourgers to be listed as German.
<p>The original nine volumes of the series, covering the period of 1850-1855, indexed every passenger on a ship that had passengers from Germany. Later volumes list only those passengers identified as German. This resource should not be overlooked as a possible source of immigrant arrival information.</p>
<p>For example, the Anton Mans family emigrated to Ozaukee County, Wisconsin from Redange-sur-Attert, near Luxembourg's western border with Belgium. They sailed aboard the "Elizabeth Denison" from Antwerp to New York City, arriving 12 June 1854. The family is listed in <em>Germans to America</em> as:</p>
