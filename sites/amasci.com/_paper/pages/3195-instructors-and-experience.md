<td data-valign="top"><h1 id="instructors" data-align="center"><span style="text-transform: uppercase; letter-spacing: 5pt"><u>Instructors</u></span></h1>
<p><strong>We here at Ronin Dojo are very proud of our instructors.  Each of our Sensei, (teacher or teachers), have had many years of experience in the martial arts field, with special training in the area of Karate instruction.  It is with great pride that we introduce you to our exceptional staff of Sensei.  (Sensei is pronounced "sen-say").  Our ranks are filled with many other fine examples of Karate-ka's who assist with the education of our students, however, they are too numerous to mention here.</strong></p>

<td width="63%" data-valign="top"><strong><em>Hammond Sensei, Godan,</em></strong><br />
<strong>Kevin Hammond, 5th degree Black Belt, Head Instructor</strong>
<p><span style="mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"><u><span style="font-style: normal; font-family: Times New Roman; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"><strong><em>1963 - 1983</em></strong></span></u><em><span style="font-style: normal; font-weight: normal; font-family: Times New Roman; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"><br />
</span></em><span style="font-style: normal; font-variant: normal; font-weight: normal; font-family: Times New Roman; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt">Studied various martial arts including; Judo, Tae Kwon Do, Goju Ryu, Hwrangdo, Shorin Ryu, and Kyokushinkai, achieving brown belt. </span></span></p>
<p><u><span style="font-style: normal; font-family: Times New Roman; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"><strong><em>From 1983 to Present</em></strong></span></u><span style="font-style: normal; font-variant: normal; font-weight: normal; font-family: Times New Roman; mso-bidi-font-size: 10.0pt"><br />
Studied Hyashi-ha Shito Ryu, Kotaka-ha Shito Ryu, and Gosoku Ryu, achieving Yondan (4th Degree Black Belt) in Shito Ryu and Gosoku Ryu in 1994, Godan (5th Degree) in Seito Shito-Ryu in 2001. </span></p>
<p>"At the age of 10 I began taking Judo at a local club in Chicago, Illinois.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>As you can see, I have maintained my interest in martial arts, but have traveled from style to style.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>Sometimes this change was due to a lack of instructor (they moved on), my relocation, or an interest in another style.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>My training in Shito Ryu began with the Washington Karate Association (WKA) under Thiry Sensei.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>I trained with many instructors at several locations in the Everett Marysville area.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>One of the Everett locations was under Martin Sensei.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>In 1985 there was a 'split' from WKA, and I followed Martin Sensei to Family Karate Center (FKC).  There, in 1985, I earned my black belt."<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">   </span></p>
<p>"FKC under Martin Sensei was a Kotaka-ha Shito Ryu school with an affiliation to Gosoku Ryu under Kubota Sensei.  I was head of technical curriculum, which included research, and development, and curriculum formatting for all FKC Dojos.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>I soon after opened a satellite school under FKC in Marysville, and have been teaching there consistently since that time.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>In 1997 I purchased the Marysville Dojo from FKC and began my own school, Ronin Dojo."</p>
<p>"I maintain my training through a diligence in attendance of seminars, under many instructors, to enhance my knowledge and technique.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>I have judged and coached state, regional, national and international events.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>I am an AAU referee in both Japanese and Chinese martial arts."</p>
<p>"Thank you for your time and attention.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>If you have further questions, please feel free to contact me."</p>

<td width="63%" data-valign="top"><strong><em>Carter Sensei, Sandan</em></strong><br />
<strong>Wilma Carter, 3rd degree Black Belt,</strong>
<p>"I have been involved with martial arts since 1986, all but two of those years with Hammond Sensei. It was because of my six year old grandson that I started my career in Karate. I originally thought of my participation in Karate as more of an exercise program where I might learn some self-defense. It wasn't long before I realized it was a way of life, a philosophy, and something that I wanted to pursue. I found Karate also satisfied a need for teaching, which is something that I had desired to do all my life. It is so satisfying to watch people grow in their self-confidence as they grow in their martial art. I also take great pleasure in teaching Karate to children in the "Kinderate" and "Junior Karate" programs. Most of my life has been dedicated to working with other people, especially children, (I was a school bus driver for 28 years, and have taught sign language at various institutions). A number of years ago, I developed a program called Kenko-Do, "The Way To Health". This program is based on a martial art that is less ballistic than Karate, and utilizes stretching and circular types of movements. In this class we learn healthy breathing techniques along with our Tai Chi and Chi Kung forms. My biggest pleasure is seeing people who were having problems walking, balancing, and moving in general, progress to the point of really feeling good physically and mentally.  I love teaching... young, old, and in between. It is my life."</p>
