<td>Happy New Year! We're almost a week into it, and I hope everyone's 2006 is off to a great start!
<p>We have lots of projects, cons and events coming up, plus various other news and updates:</p>
<p>     --  I am looking forward to once again being a guest at <a href="http://www.radcon.org/">RadCon</a>, over President's Day weekend (Feb 17-19) in Pasco, WA. </p>
<p>     -- The <a href="http://www.gatheringofthegargoyles.com/g2006/">10th Annual Gathering of Gargoyles</a> will be held in Southern California this summer. We'll be there; will you?</p>
<p>     -- For the Gathering, Tim and I will be editing the 3rd in a series of fanfic anthologies to be sold at the con. The previous ones were about the <em>Phoenix Gate</em> and the <em>Eye of Odin</em>; this time, completing the set, the theme is the <em>Grimorum Arcanorum</em>. Submissions for stories and interior art are now open! <a href="http://www.gatheringofthegargoyles.com/g2006/anthology.html">Find the guidelines here</a>!</p>
<p>     -- I expect to finish and post the second part of <a href="only_good_01.html">Only the Good</a> within a few weeks. </p>
<p>     -- Because the ElfLore books are out of print and the rights have reverted to me, <a href="http://www.sabledrake.com/">Sabledrake Enterprises</a> will be publishing a three-in-one volume of the Complete Elflore Trilogy this year. Stay tuned!</p>
<p>     -- I also hope to have the 6th <a href="http://www.silverdoorway.com/">Silver Doorway</a> book, <em>The Alchemist's Girl</em>, out this year!</p>
<p>     -- <em><a href="http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ASIN/0425205401/customeroticasou/104-4732047-9669544">Leather, Lace and Lust,</a></em> an erotic anthology in which appears my short story "I Am ...", has been re-released and is now available through Amazon.com!</p>
<p>     -- In the <a href="original_fiction.html">Original Fiction</a> section, I've posted a short superhero story, "<a href="last_vigil.html">The Last Vigil</a>." Feedback welcome!</p>
<p>     -- The third Trinity Bay book, <em><a href="http://www.christine-morgan.org/changeling_moon.htm">Changeling Moon</a></em>, is getting some good reviews! </p>
<p>     -- I am currently at work on a new horror novel, <em>Birthright</em>, and have three other books (<em>His Blood, Tell no Tales</em>, and <em>Scoot</em>) out awaiting response from various publishers. <br />
 </p></td>

<td>From Nov. 15th --
<p>Nothing much new to add at the moment. I am participating in <a href="http://www.nanowrimo.org/">National Novel Writing Month</a> for the second year in a row. Goal: to write a complete, at least 50,000 word, manuscript in one month. Last year, I did it with <em>Scoot</em>, though since it's a genre-defying quirkfest, I still haven't decided exactly what to do with the darn thing. This year's project is a horror novel called <em>Birthright</em>, one I'd tried to write before and trashed to start over from scratch. As of today, I'm at just over 35,000 words. Not too shabby.</p>
<p>And when I'm not working on that, I've mostly just been spending my time playing City of Heroes and/or City of Villains. Here's <a href="nov05_screenshots.html">some recent screenshot fun</a>!</p></td>

<td>For thousands of years, they have lived among us. Their abilities have given rise to our oldest legends and our deepest fears. They are changelings. Shape-shifters. Hunters. Creatures of the night. Ruled by the moon, and by their own savage hungers. Some wish only to survive unnoticed in our world, to be left alone and have as little to do with us as possible. They keep to their laws and traditions. They keep to themselves. Their first and only loyalty is to the pack.

<p>And, at long last, what some of you have been waiting for ... a new fanfic! In the <a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~vecna/garg_fanfic_guard.html">Guardians</a> series, here is the explosive "<a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~vecna/only_good_01.html">Only the Good, Part One</a>." Please do be advised that Part Two will likely not be available until after the first of the year, so if you don't want to spend the next couple of months wondering what happens next, you might want to consider waiting until both are posted. For reasons of violence, adult language and sexual content, this is for mature eyes only.</p>
<p>Tim and I are on staff for the <a href="http://www.gatheringofthegargoyles.com/g2006/">Gathering of Gargoyles 2006</a>, to be held next June in sunny Southern California! I'll be editing the third Gathering Anthology, details pending but I'll leak the theme now just in case anybody didn't simply guess the obvious -- the <em>Grimorum Arcanorum</em>. Naturally! So get those ideas brewing, folks ... I will be wanting your stories and illustrations!</p>
And because you just can't keep a bad garg down no matter how you try ...
<a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~vecna/ng_jericho.html">Jericho</a> debuts (minus the wings) on the new CoH expansion, <em><a href="http://www.cityofheroes.com/">City of Villains!</a></em>

Beginning on November 1st, I will be working on a horror novel called <em>Birthright</em>. I've tried this one before, got about halfway through before deciding it wasn't working and junking the whole thing. Now I'm switching POV characters and starting the whole thing over from scratch.
