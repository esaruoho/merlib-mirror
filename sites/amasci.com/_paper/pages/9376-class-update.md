# **Our First Attempt**

## **Peter and Brian's class**

**Paideia School, Atlanta, Ga.**

**Wednesday, 25 Oct. 1996**

We went to the library so everyone could view our homepage! Then back to class where we proceeded to build our first trebuchet. We used information from Verne Dewitt, Ron Toms, and Russell Minors to estimate lengths and weights. We used wooden dowel rod, 1" x 1/2 " wood, paperclips, wire, and a plastic spoon to build a 10" long and 6" high working model. we made the arm three times longer on one end, and attached the counter weight on the other. We then took it outside and fired off some acorns!!

The squirrels watched in envy!

Wednesday June 7th, 1997.

The base is 4' x 8'. The center supports are 5' tall, and the arm is a 12' 2x10. We made a frame to hold the counter wait out of scrap lumber, and used 3 bags of concrete (60lbs each). We only threw smaller objects, and the largest was a basketball which sailed 50' to Brian, one of our teacher's. For safety concerns, we kept the projectiles small.
