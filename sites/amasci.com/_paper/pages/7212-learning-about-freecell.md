# Learning About FreeCell

*Source page: `~robertf/romanel/freecell.html`*

**1. The Section of <u>GAME</u> and <u>HELP</u>:**\
\
**Under Game, they give you a selection of…**\
1. New Game or F2\
2. Select Game or F3\
3. Restart Game\
4. Statistics or F4\
5. Options or F5\
6. Undo or F10\
7. Exit\
because you might want to do or use one of these selections. But there's one thing that I want to tell you that you migth want to know very bad before you install it if you just bought it and you don't have it on your computer yet and if you bought it for you or your family and you also have time at the moment that you want to install it, if you want to do or use something that's in the <u>GAME</u> or <u>HELP</u> sections but is a silver like color or a very light color, then that means that you can't use that certain selection at the moment. And if it's a dark color like black, that means that you can use it at that moment.\
**Under Help, they give you a selection of…**\
1. Help Topics\
2. About FreeCell\
because there is a lot of information in each of the 2 selections.\
\
\
**2. The body of the game, <u>FreeCell</u>:**\
**At the top rows of the body part of the game, FreeCell…**\
There is 1 row with 4 empty card spaces on the <u>***LEFT***</u> side of the top part of the body in the game. These spaces are for the cards that are in your way of the cards you need to move that are behind the card.\
