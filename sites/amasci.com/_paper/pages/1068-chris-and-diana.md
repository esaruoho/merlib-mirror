# Chris and Diana

*Source page: `~allegro/chris_and_diana.htm`*

***Chris and Diana***

Chris and Mike have been friends since high school, and Chris and Di started dating just a few months before we did. Shawna met them both at their wedding in May of 1995, and we've gotten closer and closer ever since. They even went on our [wedding trip](our_wedding.htm) with us! Their daughter Zoe Raelynn is a rambunctious 1 year old that I love babysitting. They recently moved up to Seattle to be with us (well, they said they were doing it to go to school... ;) ) and we're thrilled to the gills to finally have them close by again.
