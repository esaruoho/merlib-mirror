A note on these pages-\
\
I originally did serveral verisons of the pages to accommodate all\
known Web browsers. Now, however I'm only writing for\
Internet Explorer 2.1 or Netscape 2.0 or higher.\
Their features greatly enhance the look and 'feel' of the WWW.\
These pages also include Java script and Microsoft's new ActiveX Controls.\
(you need IE 3.0 to see ActiveX. The 32-bit Netscape to enjoy Java.)\
(IE 2.1 is reported to have a DNS bug-Netscape 3.0xbeta's also have a DNS bug)\
\
Remember it's no mystery why Netscape is far and away the most\
popular browser on earth. It's the best and mostly works all the time\
\
I design and maintain these pages in a high resolution\
Xwindows system on a Unix machine.\
I saw them for the first time on a low resolution Mac\
the other day-it's not a pretty sight.\
My apologies (and sympathy) to the high-resolution deficient\
\
I am resisting adding sound etc simply because of the\
bandwidth involved (I'm already hogging up enough).\
