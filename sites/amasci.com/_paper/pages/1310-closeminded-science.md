|----|
| **Skepticism is a primary tool of science. We'd be hypocrites if we never directed a skeptical eye towards [Scientific Skepticism](/weird/wskept.html) itself. Denied imperfections and errors are free to grow without limit, and Skepticism is not immune to this problem. Unbridled gullibility can destroy science, but unbridled disbelief is no less a threat because it brings both the excessive preservation of the status quo and the supression of unconventional ideas. Better to take a middle road between total closed-mindedness and total gullibility. Practice pragmatism, pursue humility, and maintain a clear, honest, and continuing view of ourselves and the less noble of our ***own*** behaviors.** |
