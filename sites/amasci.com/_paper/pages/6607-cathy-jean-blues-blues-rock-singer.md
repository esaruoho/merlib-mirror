# Cathy Jean®, Blues & Blues Rock Singer

*Source page: `~rcgusa/honors.html`*

### <span id="nomin">GRAMMY BALLOTING of *I Want* (*Thank You!*)</span>

*I Want* was balloted in the 42nd Annual [Grammy®](http://www.grammy.com/index.html) Awards for "Best Contemporary Blues Album." *I Want* also was [Grammy®](http://www.grammy.com/index.html) balloted for "Best Engineered Album" (Bob Dawson's work!). *I Want* wasn't selected for the nominations, but being in the *same list* for a while as the "big names" was definitely exciting!

*I Want* was *final round* nominated for "Best Blues Album" in the 14th annual [Wammies!](http://www.wamadc.com/the_wammies.html) The WAMA (Washington Area Music Association) awards show was held on February 6, 2000, at the Hilton Washington, Washington, D.C. (USA), and Cathy Jean was there! *I Want* didn't win a Wammie but getting into the final round was the next best thing! Maybe another time!
