# Download the Book of Common Prayer.

*Source page: `~lhowell/bcp1662/download/index.html`*

## Download the Book of Common Prayer.

Almost as soon as I publicized this site's existence, people began asking me if it was possible to download the files from this website, either all at once or for particular services. For a long time, it wasn't. I'd thought about creating ASCII files for download, but each time I thought about it I got caught up in the problems of preserving as much of the formatting as is necessary to make the files usable: the online version uses color and typeface to distinguish rubrics and instructions from spoken text. When I tried to translate the files into ASCII, I lost a lot of that information. So every time I tried to put together downloadable files, I threw up my hands and decided to wait until I got a better idea of how to do it.

I finally decided to make several sets of downloadable files available. I have zipped the files together into "collections", which correspond to the organization of the [Site Directory](../directory.html). Not all of the files are available in all of the formats. The list at the bottom of this page provides links to available collections.

- The easiest to prepare, obviously, were the HTML documents themselves. I simply zipped them together in batches and stuck them in a directory. All of the files are available in this format.
- Next, I converted everything to RTF. This format allows me to preserve the color and the different type styles, and can be imported into nearly any word processor. I think all of the files are available in this format. Let me know if you want something that I've missed here.
- Because of problems with formatting, some things (particularly the tables) are not available as ASCII files at this point. I will add additional files as I get them worked out.
