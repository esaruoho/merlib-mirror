# The Runequest Source

*Source page: `~ouija/rqwelcome.htm`*

Hi there! This site was created out of a need that I saw that was not met by anyone in a direct manor. On the web there is very little material that is ***RQ reference material*** such as Cults, spells, creatures, or NPC's, when there is material it is buried so deep and referenced so abstractly, that it is pretty much worthless. This site is dedicated to meat and potatoes RQ, thick and full, not a lot of fluff and useless verbiage.

**RuneQuest** and **Glorantha** are parts of a gaming system that is unique and set apart from the standard systems that you can find at your local hobby shop or book store. On the web pages here you will find a great deal of information and detail relating to RuneQuest and Glorantha and I hope that somewhere in here you might find a certain something you needed.

I hope that amongst my other work and hobbies that I can keep this site up to date and living, and with your comments and contributions, I hope to make this site a source of RuneQuest and Glorantha gaming promotion and a place where gamers on the web can publish ideas and other nuggets of cool stuff for RuneQuest and Glorantha. For further information on **Avalon Hill** and **Chaosium**, see these links.
