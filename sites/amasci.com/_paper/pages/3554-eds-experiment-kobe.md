<td data-valign="top"><p>My puppy, <a href="images/kobe/kobe.jpg" target="_blank">Kobe</a>.  He is a male <a href="documents/Meet%20the%20Samoyed.doc" target="_blank">Samoyed</a> (pronounced sam-a-yed).  His hobbies are sleeping, peeing, pooping, playing, chewing, ripping, stealing, barking, jumping, walking, pulling, digging, etc.  In other words, acting like a Samoyed.  This is my second Samoyed.  One would have thought I would have learned my lesson on being owned by a Samoyed, but noooooo!  Subconsciously, I must be into S&amp;M.  Actually, I wouldn't even think twice about owning another breed.  Samoyeds are the best!  Yes, even better than <a href="images/kobe/max.jpg" target="_blank">Shelties</a>. <img src="images/kobe/awink.gif" data-border="0" width="15" height="15" />   </p>
<p>His current favorite thing to do is chewing up my pillow and comforters.  Count so far is one pillow, three comforters (four if you count the guest bed), and two sofas.  Grrr .... Apparently it is a little known fact that polyester fill is a supplementary diet requirement.   </p>
<p>Name: Kobe (pronounced ko-bay)<br />
Birth Name: Gold Boy/Midas<br />
AKC Name:  WildWind's Starlite Bear<br />
Nicknames: Kobi-wan, Obi Konobi (Star Wars), Kobie-Dobie, Hey you!, Bad Puppy!, and Butthead.<br />
Birth Date: 18 May 2000<br />
Birth Weight: 11 oz<br />
Current Weight: 53.3 pounds as of 11 March 2005.  Graphs:  <a href="images/kobe/kobe_wt.gif" target="_blank">Overall</a>   <a href="images/kobe/kobe_wt_yr01.gif" target="_blank">Year 1</a>   <a href="images/kobe/kobe_wt_yr02.gif" target="_blank">2</a>   <a href="images/kobe/kobe_wt_yr03.gif" target="_blank">3</a>   <a href="images/kobe/kobe_wt_yr04.gif" target="_blank">4</a>   <a href="images/kobe/kobe_wt_yr05.gif" target="_blank">5</a><br />
Papa: <a href="images/kobe/Indy_2001Aug.jpg" target="_blank">Indy</a> (Ch. Starlite's Bear in Mind)<br />
Mama:  <a href="images/shows/010324e1.jpg" target="_blank">Emmy</a> (Ch. WildWind's Lil' Miss ShowOff)<br />
Siblings: Peaches (WildWind's Starlite Belle), Molly (WildWind's Silver Starlite), <a href="http://photomail.photoworks.com/sharing/image.asp?key=1~FQQ4aBru.cIn/7HuNCWW.E.jUkkjez/q&amp;idx=30&amp;1=PEUuSgM5lSWOANqeejIX&amp;2=0" target="_blank">Luna</a> (WildWind's Blue Starlite)<br />
Likes: walks, <a href="documents/pets.pdf" target="_blank">car rides</a>, playing toss with stuff animals, treats (especially turkey franks), chasing tennis balls, picking up every piece of garbage on the ground, playing with the nephews, running through sprinklers and water fountains, chewing up comforters, Cheetos, humping legs, crotch sniffing, jumping fences.<br />
Dislikes: being ignored, baths, grooming, baths, teeth brushed, baths, nails clipped, baths.  Oh, did I mention he really, really hates baths? </p>
<p>Kobe?  Where did that name come from?  He is named after the Italian Iron Chef Kobe Masahiko.  Iron Chef is a popular Japanese television cooking show which has a large following in the United States.  Okay, why the Italian Iron Chef?  Well, you didn't expect me to name him after the Chinese Iron Chef, Chen Kenichi?  Or the French Iron Chef Sakai Hiroyuki?  Or the Japanese Iron Chef Morimoto Masaharu?  I didn't think so.  The <a href="http://www.ironchef.com" target="_blank">Iron Chef Compendium</a> website is a good place to visit to learn more about the show.  </p>
<p>Here are the latest movie (Windows Media format).    <a href="movies/scooby256.wmv" target="_blank">Kobe Sings With Scooby, 17 December 2004, 838 kb</a></p>
<p>Kobe has his own <strong>Dogster</strong> site!  Come visit him and his pals <a href="http://www.dogster.com/dog_page.php?i=14095" target="_blank">here</a>.<br />
<br />
Want to bring your dog to Fort Funston or Crissy Field/Presidio?  Check out this brochure published by the Golden Gate National Park Recreation Area, <a href="http://www.nps.gov/goga/maps/bulletins/sb-k9.pdf" target="_blank">Enjoying the Park with your Dog</a>.  The brochure provides the rules and which parks within the Golden Gate NPRA dogs are allowed.  Santa Clara County Parks also provides a webpage on <a href="http://www.parkhere.org/content/0,4745,chid%253D162737%2526ccid%253D30101,00.html" target="_blank">Places to Take Your Dog</a>.  For San Mateo county parks, no pets are allowed in any county park.</p>
