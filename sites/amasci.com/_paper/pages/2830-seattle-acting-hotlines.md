<dl>
<dt><strong>Seattle P-I Newspaper Callboard</strong></dt>
<dd>
Printed every Friday in the <strong>What's Happening?</strong> section of the newspaper. Includes calls for actors, musicians, singers, dancers, and fine artists (painters, sculptors, photographers, etc.) Also available as a FAX, and on their website. To find the web version, surf their Theatre home page (in the <strong>Art &amp; Life</strong> section of the site) and look for links that say <strong>Upcoming Auditions</strong> .<br />
Seattle P-I Theatre Web Page: <a href="http://seattlepi.nwsource.com/theater/"><strong>http://seattlepi.nwsource.com/theater/</strong></a>
</dd>
<dt><strong>Actor's Equity Hotline</strong></dt>
<dd>
425-637-7332
</dd>
<dt><strong>SeattleStage.com Audition Board</strong></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.seattlestage.com/"><strong>http://www.seattlestage.com/</strong></a>
</dd>
<dt><strong>Theatre Puget Sound (TPS) Online</strong></dt>
<dd>
A listing of this week's auditions on the home page:<br />
<a href="http://www.tpsonline.org/"><strong>http://www.tpsonline.org/</strong></a>
</dd>
<dd>
Discussion Forum topics: <strong>Auditions Announcements</strong><br />
<a href="http://www.tpsonline.org/discus/messages/16/16.html?1013811183">http://www.tpsonline.org/discus/messages/16/16.html?1013811183</a>
</dd>
<dt><strong>Performers Callboard</strong> Email Listserv</dt>
<dd>
Performers Callboard is a Yahoo Group managed by a very nice guy, the parent of an actor in the Federal Way (south of Seattle) area. More info on this list: <a href="http://www.performerscallboard.com"><strong>http://www.performerscallboard.com</strong></a>
</dd>
<dt><strong>Onstage Mail</strong> Email Listserv</dt>
<dd>
Onstage Mail is run by Michael Kelley of the<br />
Edge of the World Theatre in Edmonds (north of Seattle). His email addy to request a subscription is: <a href="mailto:mkelley124@aol.com?subject=Please%20subscribe%20me%20to%20Onstage%20Mail&amp;Body=I%20saw%20a%20note%20about%20your%20audition%20email%20list%20at%20http://www.danarice.net/theatre/hotlines.html%20and%20I%20would%20like%20to%20subscribe%20to%20Onstage%20Mail!%20...">mkelley124@aol.com</a> Onstage Mail's format is _very_ nice. It includes lots of info in a nice style. Where and when auditions are, name of the company, the rehearsal and performance schedule so you can come in knowing your conflicts, detailed description of roles being cast, synopsis of the piece to jog your memory... ::happy sigh:: If only every audition call could be like theirs. :-}
</dd>
<dt><strong>Washington State Film Office Jobline</strong></dt>
<dd>
956-3206<br />
(Oregon 503-373-7187)
</dd>
<dt><strong>NWAAT Auditions Hotline</strong></dt>
<dd>
340-1049, box 3
</dd>
<dt><strong>Audition Calls at RedCard.Com</strong></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://redcard.seattletheater.net/"><strong>http://redcard.seattletheater.net/</strong></a><br />
Follow the Auditions Link from the home page. I'm told this is mainly fringe theatres, although it has category headings for music and film. The audition pages were blank the last time I looked, but there are signs of life on the site, so I'm keeping an eye on them... By the way, if you think the TPS discussion boards are too tame and mainstream for you, definitely check out the Red Card ones out... Plus, the showtimes schedules has fresh data - mostly from Taproot's schedule, it looks like...
</dd>
<dt><strong>Seattleperforms.com</strong> Calendar</dt>
<dd>
A fairly new resource that includes all kinds of calendarized information, including a calendar-driven audition schedule. Not too much info yet, and the website design is very frustrating until you get used to how to use it. There's no way to link directly to the auditions information specifically, so read the instructions when you get there. The site is a nice compliment to other sites, in that it lets you view everything by date. You may want to avoid it altogether if you don't use Internet Explorer. ::sigh:: It GPFs my machine when I hit it with Netscape. I'll update this if that turns out to be a temporary problem.<br />
<a href="http://www.seattleperforms.com/">http://www.seattleperforms.com/</a>
</dd>
<dt><strong>The Stranger</strong> Newspaper</dt>
<dd>
Auditions printed at the end of the performing arts schedule. Usually just a few things for theatre. Extensive section in the classifieds for Music, though - Musicians Wanted, Musicians Available, including lots of calls for singers and instrumentalists in many genres. :-} Audition Calls, Artist Calls, and Music Calls on the web at <a href="http://classifieds.thestranger.com/">http://classifieds.thestranger.com/</a><br />
&#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Wiggly World Studios</strong></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.wigglyworld.org/">http://www.wigglyworld.org/</a> Follow the "The Callboard" Link for upcoming film cast and crew calls.
</dd>
<dt><strong>Audition Calls at Greenstage Theatre Organization</strong></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.greenstage.org/audition.shtml">http://www.greenstage.org/audition.shtml</a><br />
<em>Linkrot alert! This link changed a couple of years ago. Check your bookmarks for old versions.</em>
<hr />
</dd>
<dt><strong>Calls for submissions at 911 Media Arts:</strong></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.911media.org/"><strong>http://www.911media.org/</strong></a><br />
Follow the "Bulletin Board" link after you get to the home page.
<hr />
</dd>
<dt><strong>Seattle Weekly</strong> Newspaper</dt>
<dd>
No theatre calls that I've found, but a respectable section in the classifieds for Music Musicians Wanted, Musicians Available, including lots of calls for singers and instrumentalists in many genres. :-} Plus, a nice guide of local open mikes for singers, poets, etc... including karaoke places. (Hey, wanna go brush up on some show tunes?) On the web at <a href="http://www.seattleweekly.com/"><strong>http://www.seattleweekly.com/</strong></a>
</dd>
<dt><strong>Seattle Times Hotline</strong></dt>
<dd>
464-2000 ext. 2787
