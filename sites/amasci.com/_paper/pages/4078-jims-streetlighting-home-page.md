## *I hope you find yourself right in your element!*

You may be wondering why this page exists. Well, the reason is that I have had a lifelong obsession with street lighting. I don't know how it got started, but when I was five years old, I could tell (during the daylight) which street lights were high-pressure sodium and which were mercury vapor. The topic of street lights has always fascinated me, and I always have an eye to the sky.

<img src="graphics/jimslighton.gif" data-align="right" alt="me and my good ole M250" />

I am a graduate of Washington State University, where I earned a degree in Communications. I am always on the lookout for more material for my page. If you have a link or anything you consider relevant, please send me an email at <jrterry@eskimo.com>

This picture to the right is me, with my first street light. It's a GE M250 which was given to me by Puget Power in Seattle. For those interested, I have stripped out the ballast and put in a 160 watt self-ballasted mercury bulb.

You are welcome to join the streetlighting mailing list. We've recently begun to shift it to Yahoo... To subscribe, please go to:<http://groups.yahoo.com/group/streetlights-l/>

Thanks again for visiting... I hope you enjoy what you see!
