# Internet Games

\

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Up until a year or so ago, internet gaming on the Mac was more of an annoyance than a pleasant diversion. But within the past couple years, some excellent services have been developed for the Mac. [GameRanger](http://www.gameranger.com) leads the way with a whole horde of top class internet games. GameRanger is very easy to use so even novice Mac users can join in on the fun. [Bungie.net](http://www.bungie.net) and [Battle.net](http://www.battle.net) offer their top commercial games with full support for the Mac. And the long heralded Mac shareware company, Freeverse, now offers their own game server, [HMS Freeverse](http://www.freeverse.com/hms/index.html), for a number of excellent card games. Older services such as [Kali](http://www.kali.net/software/mac.html/index.html) and utilities such as [IPRemote](gameutils.html#ipremote) are still available for gaming, but the hassles involved no longer seem worth it with the excellent offerings above now available. To top things off the ancient chess and backgammon servers are currently well supported through the programs [Fixation](http://www3.sk.sympatico.ca/cknelsen/fixation/fixation.html) and [MacFIBS](http://www.fibs.com/macfibs/) and Java based board and card games you play through your browser are available at [Excite](http://www.excite.com/games/online_games/) and [Yahoo](http://play.yahoo.com/games/). Overall, internet gaming on the Mac is looking good.\
\
Make sure to check the [Game Utilities](gameutils.html) page at Pure Mac to insure your gaming experience is all it can be.**
