## The White Rose

\
copyright 1990\
\
Written Age 16\
\
The White Rose\
Sits in the garden watching the morning sunrise.\
With cheeks as pure white as the winter's snow,\
Eyes as green as the summer's meadows,\
Lips as red as blood,\
Hair the colour of the sun itself,\
And a heart of pure gold,\
The White Rose\
Sits in the garden watching a love pass her by.\
The green eyes shed a single tear,\
The red lips tremble in fear,\
As the heart of pure gold breaks in two.\
The White Rose\
No longer sits in the garden,\
For now she lays beneath the winter's snow,\
But when the summer sun shines,\
The White Rose,\
In full glory will rise again.\
\

## A Rose In Winter

\
Copyright 1990\
Written Age 17\
A Rose In Winter\
I am like a rose in winter.\
My heart is frozen,\
My emotions are bitter,\
One touch could make me crumble\
With tears of anger and hurt.\
My life is at a stand still,\
Nothing seems to be moving in this barren land.\
I once loved,\
But now it's turned to hate\
Against the person who put me in this state.\
I once knew happiness,\
Now I only know sadness.\
No more tears can fall,\
They have all turned to ice.\
No dreams can I have,\
For they have all turned to dust.\
And all my wishes have fallen upon deaf ears.\
I am like a rose in winter.\
My heart is frozen,\
My emotions are bitter,\
And I have only one question,\
Can I love again?\
\

## The Rose and the Thorn

\
Copyright 1991\
Written Age 17\
I am the rose,\
And you are my thorn.\
I am the beauty,\
You are the pain.\
I make people sigh,\
You make them cry.\
We are opposites,\
But one cannot exist\
Without the other.\
We are one,\
But always apart.\
\

## The Day Comes and Goes

\
Copyright 1990\
Written Age 16\
The night is young\
And so am I.\
The dawn breaks,\
And so does my heart.\
The day comes and goes\
Like so many loves.\
And I know that like the day,\
Another love will come my way.\
\

## This Moment

\
Copyright 1991\
Written Age 17\
This moment\
Shouldn't be happening,\
We shouldn't be together,\
Not now, not ever.\
Time was my enemy,\
Love was my downfall.\
A moment I dreamt of,\
Prayed for,\
A moment that came to late.\
You never cared,\
I loved too much.\
A moment alone together\
I thought would be the answer,\
A chance just to talk.\
But there you are,\
And here I am\
In steely silence, avoiding one's eyes.\
Not enemies, but never friends,\
Just two strangers\
Who should've never met.\
So I walk out,\
And as I do\
I realize that at\
This moment\
A dream has come to an end.\
\

## Them

\
copyright 1992\
\
Written Age 18\
\
I wake up every morning\
Afraid that the past\
I left far behind\
Will catch up with me.\
I go about\
Looking over my shoulders\
Scared to find\
A thousand eyes looking back at me.\
I run away and hide,\
When I hear a knock on the door,\
Or the phone ringing.\
I know it's Them, they've found me!\
I want to tell Them\
To leave me alone,\
But I don't want\
Them to know I can speak.\
They'll want to take that away, too,\
Like They've taken away my freedom.\
And I'm sure if They knew how I felt,\
They would take away my feelings as well.\
So since They have left me with no\
Peace of mind,\
I'll just have to continue to hide\
From Them.\
\

## The Pain, Hate, and Anger in The World Today

\
Copyright 1992\
Written Age 18\
I stand on the edge of a cliff,\
The full moon above\
Reflects the garish, stark white sand below,\
It hurts my eyes,\
But now as much as what I see going on around me these days.\
Sometimes I wish I was blind,\
So I wouldn't have to see,\
All the pain, hate, and anger in the world today.\
The water licks the beach with its waves,\
Like the pain that licks at my heart,\
Because of a love that never came to pass.\
Sometimes I wish I could tear it out,\
And throw it to the rocks below,\
So I wouldn't have to feel\
All the pain, hate, and anger in the world today.\
The sound of the seagulls screeching fills my ears,\
It reminds me of all the people\
Screaming fr something better.\
Sometimes I wish I could cut them off,\
So I wouldn't have to hear\
All the pain, hate , and anger in the world today.\
Only when they end\
Would I wish to be able\
To see, feel , and hear all the beauty\
That I know can exist in the world today.\
\

## Our Love is Strong

\
Copyright 1988\
Written Age 14\
You're the man who'll fight fro my honour\
You're the hero I've dreamt of\
You gave me hope\
When I thought there was none\
You gave me support and faith in love\
Just when I thought I could never love again\
I found you\
And when I gave you my heart\
You took it so gently\
It never had a scar, not even a bruise\
Just a broken heart put back together by you\
Now that it's healed\
I can go on living happily with you\
And we can survive throught anything\
That comes our way\
When I was lost, you found me\
When I was hurt, you healed me\
And when I hid, you revealed me\
You gave me the key to your heart\
And I gave you mine\
I will love you forever\
Because our love is strong\
And can only grow. <span id="used"></span>

## I Used To

\
Copyright 1995\
Written Age 22\
I used to wish upon the stars\
I used to have dreams\
I used to have hope and believe in true love\
I used to be happy\
I used to feel joy\
I used to have idols and believe in fairy tales\
I used to feel jealously\
I used to have a fear of hell\
I used to have faith in heaven and believe in all the angels\
that lived there\
I used to believe in good\
I used to love you, I don't anymore\
Not since you gave up being used to me.\
\
