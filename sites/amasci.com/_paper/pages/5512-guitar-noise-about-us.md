<td colspan="2"><p><strong>Offering free guitar lessons, exclusive interviews, tablature, advice on careers, songwriting, and much more. Serving beginners and pros since 1995. Get free tips from our weekly newsletters. Everything is free and there is no need to register for anything.</strong></p>
<p>GuitarNoise.com was founded by Paul Hackett as the Online Guitar College in 1995. The original idea was to create an ultimate guitar resource with links to everything. Dissapointed with the quality of other sites we began the difficult task of making our own exclusive content. By 1999 the site began to really take off and new helpers joined the team. These new colleagues brought with them so much enthusiasm and new ideas. The site began to spread out to cover different areas. Today, each of these partners continues to breath new life into this project every month. Not everyone out there knows about us, but almost everyone who does has positive things to say about the work we are doing.</p></td>
