<td><strong>A skittish, fearful youth is required to seek a treasure by undertaking a dangerous journey, otherwise she and her mother will loose their home.</strong>
<p><strong>Scaredy-Kate</strong> combines classical music with string and hand puppets in a fanciful tale about courage.</p>
<p>During her travels to find the treasure, three monsters attempt to enslave or destroy her. Through these encounters, she finds the courage to face her deepest fears.</p>
<p><strong>Scaredy-Kate</strong> distinguishes itself as an engaging tale, told through charming and skilled puppetry. It contains eight songs, sung to the classical music of Joseph Haydn.</p>
