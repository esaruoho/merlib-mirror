GET POSSESSED BY THE EMERALD EYE TODAY
<p>Now available for download: the all-new version 2.0 of the Emerald Eye of Ekron, which includes the Eyecaster and Ekron Emulator software to help you take full advantage of the power of the Eye. The Emerald Eye continues to be the premier all-powerful artifact, boasting many user non-configurable features. These include:</p>
<p><strong>Near-total loss of control:</strong> With the Emerald Eye you will no longer make your own decisions, though you may wield just enough control of yourself to create conflict. Still, you will do evil. In addition, the Eye may quite possibly want to alter your physical appearance in terms of body type, slimming you up or increasing muscles or cup size.</p>
<p><strong>Wish fulfillment:</strong> With the Emerald Eye's help you can grant your friends and co-workers their fondest desires. Version 2.0 now contains an option to have this run in the background while you work.</p>
<p><strong>Loss of morals:</strong> With the help of the Emerald Eye you will become a villainous character with the capacity to kill, maim and other heinous acts.</p>
<p><strong>Modified speech patterns:</strong> Depending on your platform or era--plus your degree of control over your actions--the Emerald Eye will make you interchange the words "Eye," "I" and "Aye." This will not be audible, but will affect the text of your word balloons.</p>
<p><strong>Abysmal fashion sense:</strong> You will, upon being possessed by the Eye, gain a new appearance and wardrobe--highlighted by the color green, of course. For females, this will include a substantial increase in the amount of exposed flesh, though it will remain within Comics Code standards.</p>
<h3 id="specifications">Specifications:</h3>
In order to download the Eye you must first find it, which is extremely frustrating. Often times it will find you.
<p><strong>If you are in the 20th century:</strong><br />
Two Emerald Eyes have been shown to exist, but their whereabouts are as of yet unknown. Inquire first at L.E.G.I.O.N. Headquarters on Planet Cairn. Marij'n Bek may be able to assist you.</p>
<p><strong>If you are in the 30th century:</strong><br />
The Emerald Eye recently possessed Shrinking Violet (Salu Digby) of the Legion of Super-Heroes. After a pitched battle with one of its former owners, Mordru, the Eye fled to parts unknown. It is now on reorder.</p>
For more information, check out one of the many <a href="http://www.idyllmtn.com/rac/dc/lsh/lsh_res.htm">Legion of Super-Heroes Web pages</a> on the Net.<br />
