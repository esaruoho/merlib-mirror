This organization is supported in part by grants from the National Science Foundation, the Rathman foundation, the Office of the Superintendant of Public Instruction of Washington State, and the generous donations of folks like you! The Science Club is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization that uses humor and science to get children and adults to learn and play together. All our activities, which use common household materials, promote curiousity and the excitement of experimentation.
