### The Watchtower and Prophecy

\

Do They Teach God's Truth or Man's?

The Watchtower Bible and Tract Society, better known as Jehovah's Witnesses, has long taught that they have the Truth. The Witnesses speak of their religion as the Truth. The Watchtower claims that it is the only "channel" through which God is speaking to His people today. Through study of various publications of the Watchtower organization, it has become apparent that their Truth changes from time to time. The Bible says that God does not change (Malachi 3:6; Hebrews 1:17, 13:8; James 1:17). With this in mind, we need to examine the teachings of the Watchtower Society. Are their teachings from God or from man? The following quotations from Watchtower publications are food for thought. We will attempt to answer the questions raised by these statements in the following pages. *Italics* in the quoted material appear in the original, **bold face** is added by this author for emphasis. Because many of the Watchtower publications we have quoted from are hard to find, even at a well-stocked Kingdom Hall Library, we have full-page photocopy documentation of the material quoted available on request. You may also write for an appointment to study the original Watchtower publications at our library.

First we will examine claims the Watchtower Bible and Tract Society has made about itself.

The Watchtower claims to be the only way to salvation:

"Is there any cause for us to lose faith in Jehovah's visible organization because of mounting difficulties in this world? Those who believe that Jehovah will never desert his faithful witnesses answer, "Absolutely not!" In demonstration of such faith, we will keep on sticking to it and working with it without slacking the hand. Our unwavering faith will be rewarded with victory and **the crown of life!**" - *The Watchtower* Mar. 1, 1979 p. 18 (NOTE: in Watchtower terminology, "Jehovah's organization" or "Jehovah's visible organization" means the Watchtower Bible and Tract Society)

"Unless we are in touch with this channel of communication that God is using, **we will not progress along the road to life**, no matter how much Bible reading we do." - *The Watchtower* Dec. 1, 1981 p. 27

"... **come to Jehovah's organization for salvation** ..." - *The Watchtower* Nov. 15, 1981 p. 21

"**To receive everlasting life** in the earthly Paradise we must identify that organization and serve God as part of it." - *The Watchtower* Feb. 15, 1983 p. 12

Many groups claim to be the only way to salvation. Is there any proof that the Watchtower organization's claims to being the way to salvation are valid? In the next quotation, we find the Watchtower saying that any organization that assumes the position of being the way to salvation should willingly submit to scrutiny and criticism:

"*The Catholic Church occupies a very significant position in the world and claims to be the way of salvation for hundreds of millions of people. Any organization that assumes that position should be willing to submit to scrutiny and criticism.*" - *Awake!* Aug. 22, 1984 p. 28

With their claim to be the way of salvation, this statement demands that we scrutinize the Watchtower, and they should be willing to submit to our examination and criticism.

Next we find the organization telling us we need to examine any religion we are associated with, and not to fear such a test.

"We need to examine, not only what we personally believe, but also what is taught by **any religious organization** with which we may be associated. Are its teachings in full harmony with God's Word, or are they based on the traditions of men? If we are lovers of the truth, **there is nothing to fear from such an examination**." - 1968 and 1981 revision of the book, *The Truth That Leads To Eternal Life* p. 13; identical wording in *The Watchtower* Mar 15, 1969 p. 166

The Watchtower leaders felt strongly enough about their instruction to examine your religion that they repeated it in *The Watchtower* in 1969, and kept it in the "Truth" book when they revised it in 1981.

This next quote is from an article concerning creation vs. evolution, but the statement is just as applicable in any discussion.

"Truly it is "intellectually dishonest" not to want any opposing views heard on such a disputed matter. It has to make reasonable persons ask, **Why**?" - *Awake!* Oct. 22, 1973 p.7

"the true God, who is himself "the God of truth" and who hates lies, will not look with favor on persons who cling to **organizations that teach falsehood**. And, really, would you want to be even associated with a religion that **had not been honest with you**?" - 1974 book, *Is This Life All There Is?* p. 46

Does the history of the Watchtower organization show that it does not teach falsehood? Has it been honest? These and other questions need to be answered.

The following quotations from Watchtower Society publications effectively claim that the organization's publications are considered to have remained accurate over the years.

"**Since 1879** the *Watch Tower* magazine has been used by this collective group to dispense spiritual food regularly to those of this "little flock" of true Christians." - *The Watchtower* Oct. 1, 1967 p. 590

"we should be deeply appreciative of all the fine spiritual food that "the faithful and discrete slave" class has been providing through *The Watchtower*, **now for ninety-four years**." - *The Watchtower* Aug. 1, 1973 p. 460

"In the **second issue** of the *Watch Tower*, we expressed our confidence that it "has ... Jehovah for its backer."" - *The Watchtower* July 1, 1979 p. 4

NOTE: Use of the words "we" and "our" in this quotation shows that the current leadership of the organization recognizes that the same organization was publishing the *Watch Tower* in 1879 as is publishing it today. The second issue was dated August, 1879.

Charles Taze Russell, the founder of the Society, is quoted often in current Watchtower organization works. This indicates that the present leadership of the organization still accepts Russell's work. (See, for example, *The Watchtower* Dec. 1, 1990 p.19)

"For **over a century**, delightful, **correct** words of truth covering every aspect of life have been presented in the Watch Tower Society's publications ..." - *The Watchtower* Dec. 15, 1990 p.26

"A God-fearing man named Charles Taze Russell separated himself from mainstream theology and began publishing **this very journal** - *The Watch Tower*." - *The Watchtower* Feb. 15, 1991 p.7

These quotations tell us that the Watchtower considers itself to have always been accurate, so no Jehovah's Witness should hesitate to study the older publications of the organization. The current leadership acknowledges the work of the early writers, including C. T. Russell to be of the same organization as is writing today, so we should have no fear of this older material. In the next quotations, we are even told to read the older publications.

"And have you read the **older publications** of the Society in your language? To the extent that you can make time to read the material, **there is a blessing in store for you**." - 1971 and 1992 revision of the book, *Theocratic Ministry School Guidebook* p. 21

"It means reading the footnotes in *Watchtower* articles, some of which refer the reader to an **older publication** that provides a fuller explanation of a certain passage or prophecy. It requires digging deeper, putting forth effort to locate that older publication and then studying the pages referred to." - *The Watchtower* June 15, 1985 p. 12

"You will find valuable information in the **older publications**." - *Our Kingdom Ministry*, January, 1988 p. 7

The above quotations from Watchtower publications suggest that we examine the Watchtower organization to see if they teach God's truth or man's. Is the Watchtower Society the right religion? In the following pages we will examine of some of the Watchtower's teachings.

The Watchtower has defined a true prophet like this:

"The three essentials for establishing the credentials of a true prophet, as given through Moses, were: The true prophet would **speak in Jehovah's name**; the things foretold would **come to pass** (De 18:20-22); and his prophesying must **promote true worship**" - 1988 book, *Insight On The Scriptures Vol. 2* p. 696

The three questions that we need to answer here are:\
**1)** Does the Watchtower organization claim to speak in Jehovah's name?\
**2)** Do they claim to promote true worship?\
**3)** Have the things they foretold come to pass?\
The following quotations from Watchtower publications tell their own story. Please read them carefully.

Question number **1)** Does the Watchtower Society claim to speak in Jehovah's name? The next three quotations show that the organization has claimed to be a prophet of Jehovah God, and that all of their publications come from God. They say that Jehovah's Witnesses are the prophet commissioned to speak in Jehovah's name. Please read Deuteronomy 18: 20-22.

"The resolutions adopted by conventions of God's anointed people, **booklets, magazines, and books published by them**, contain the message of God's truth and **are from the Almighty God, Jehovah**, and provided by him through Christ Jesus and his underofficers ... **It is his truth and not man's** ..." - *The Watchtower* May 1, 1938 p. 143

This is a powerful claim, to say that all publications the organization's publications are from Jehovah, and are Jehovah's truth and not man's! If this claim is true, the Watchtower is the Word of God!

"This is not giving any credit to the magazine's \[*The Watchtower*'s\] publishers, but is **due to the great Author of the Bible** with its truths and prophecies, and who now interprets its prophecies. **He it is that makes possible the material that is published in the columns of this magazine** ..." - *The Watchtower* Apr. 15, 1943 p. 127

"Christ Jesus ... **merely uses the "servant" class to publish the interpretation after the Supreme Court by Christ Jesus reveals it**. How does the Lord God make known the interpretation? **By causing the facts to come to pass visibly which are in fulfillment of the prophecy** ..." - *The Watchtower* July 1, 1943 p. 203

The next quotations show that the Watchtower claims to be the modern counterpart of Jeremiah and Ezekiel, and to be Jehovah's commissioned messenger. Jeremiah and Ezekiel were true prophets of God, so the modern counterpart of them would be a true prophet of God. The prophecies of Jeremiah and Ezekiel came to pass as prophesied. Did the Watchtower Society make any prophecies, and if so, did their prophesies come to pass as prophesied? Remember that they claim that all of their publications are from the Almighty God, Jehovah. In these quotations the Watchtower claims to be a "prophet" speaking in Jehovah's name, just like Ezekiel.

"Who will be Jehovah's prophet to the nations, to speak to them everything that He should command? **Who will be the modern Jeremiah**? ... Back there, about forty years ago, that was the question. Today we may ask, How was the question answered? There are facts to show. We should not appeal to religious pride or boasting or self-made claims. **We should appeal to the facts. Let facts speak for themselves**. ... **Whom has God actually used as his prophet**? ... **Jehovah's witnesses** are deeply grateful today that the **plain facts show that God has been pleased to use them**." - *The Watchtower* Jan. 15, 1959 pp. 40, 41

"They have come to know that **there exists a true prophet in spiritual Israel** and that there is no God anywhere in the earth except among the prophet's people, spiritual Israel." - 1961 book, *Let Your Name Be Sanctified* p. 347

"**Who is Ezekiel's present-day counterpart**, whose message and conduct correspond with that of that ancient prophet of Jehovah? Of whom today was he a "sign" or "portent"? Not of some individual man, but of a group of people. ... Who were they? ... they became **Jehovah's witnesses**." - 1971 book, *The Nations Shall Know That I Am Jehovah* pp. 58, 61, 66

"was there any group on whom Jehovah would be willing to bestow the commission to **speak as a "prophet" in His name**, as was done toward Ezekiel back there in 613 B.C.E.? ... forthcoming issues of *The Watchtower* will further discuss the identity and work of Jehovah's commissioned messenger" - *The Watchtower* Mar. 15, 1972 pp. 189, 190

"So, **does Jehovah have a prophet** to help them, to warn them of dangers and to declare things to come? ... He had a "prophet" to warn them. This "prophet" was not one man, but was a body of men and women. It was the small group of footstep followers of Jesus Christ, known at that time as International Bible Students. Today they are known as **Jehovah's Christian witnesses**. They are still proclaiming a warning ..." - *The Watchtower* Apr. 1, 1972 p. 197

"In behalf of such right-hearted individuals **Jehovah has considerately raised up his "prophet to the nations**." ... **the "prophet" whom Jehovah has raised up** has been, not an individual man as in the case of Jeremiah, but a class. ... there is a mere remnant of this "prophet" class yet on earth." - *The Watchtower* Oct. 1, 1982 pp. 26,27

Question number **2)** Does the Watchtower organization claim to promote true worship? There are many quotations that could be used to demonstrate this. The following is a typical one:

"You must search out and find the **true**, unhypocritical **worship** that will bring you God's peace and protection ... Does any such worship exist today? Millions have found it **among Jehovah's Witnesses**." - 1986 book, *True Peace and Security How Can You Find it?*, p.33

The Watchtower tells us to distinguish a false prophet like this:

"The best method of proof is to put a prophecy to the **test of time and circumstances**. The Bible invites such a test." - *The Watchtower* Mar. 1, 1965 p.151

"**Jehovah, the God of the true prophets, will put all false prophets to shame** either by not fulfilling the false prediction of such self-assuming prophets or by having His own prophecies fulfilled in a way opposite to that predicted by the false prophets. **False prophets will try to hide their reason for feeling shame by denying who they really are**." - 1972 book, *Paradise Restored To Mankind By Theocracy*, pp. 353, 354

"Of course, it is easy to say that this group acts as a "prophet" of God. It is another thing to prove it. The only way that this can be done is to **review the record. What does it show**?" - *The Watchtower* Apr. 1, 1972 p. 197

Question number **3)** Do the prophecies of the Watchtower organization pass "the test of time and circumstances", or has Jehovah God "put them to shame"? The Watchtower Society invites us to review the record of their prophecies. In view of the seriousness of the claim to be God's commissioned messenger, we agree that this should be done. A review of the record of the organization is the only way to establish their claim to be the only way to salvation and to be a true prophet of God. In fairness to the Watchtower Society, the Watchtower organization should be allowed to speak for itself. The best way for it to do that is through its publications.

So let's "review the record. What does it show?". Has the Watchtower organization made prophecies, and, if so, are they from God? What are the facts? The Watchtower says it has always been correct, so to be accurate, we must review the record from the beginning. The following is a list of dates and events as set forth in Watchtower publications. Some of these events were to have already happened at the time of the writing, some were direct prophecies of things to come. We will need to answer the question, "Did these events come to pass as written?"

THE BATTLE OF ARMAGEDDON ENDS IN 1915
