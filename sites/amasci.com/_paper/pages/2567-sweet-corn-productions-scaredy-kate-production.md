<td><em><strong>Facts about Scaredy-Kate</strong> — as of March, 10, 2002:</em>
<p>Demian completed the story, lyrics, and puppet construction in 1982. The marionettes and hand figures have been in duffel bags since then, patiently waiting for their big come-back.</p>
<p>Steve Bryant, the music director, rounded up the vocalists and instrumentalists. [For a list of the performers and their biographies, please see the <a href="kate-03.html"><strong>Company</strong></a>.]</p>
<p>Four audio recording sessions took place in November on the 11th, 17th, 18th, and 21st, 2001. These sessions recorded the music, singing and dialogue portions of the audio track.</p>
<p>A studio space has been created out of a large garage. It has been wired to handled studio lights, and is insulated for winter warmth. The set will be assembled there, and the video portion of the shoot will take place there as well.</p>
<p>We are seeking a designer/builder for the miniature set. The figures are scaled to about one-fifth life size.</p>
<em>Production data:</em>
Independently produced.
Currently in production.
The audio portion of the video was recorded on CDs.
The best of the CD tracks were transferred to WAV files.
The WAV files are being edited within a computer video program.
The video portion of the project will be shot on DV.
The best clips will be cut and edited to the already-edited sound track.
Production is aimed at the direct-to-video/DVD market.</td>
