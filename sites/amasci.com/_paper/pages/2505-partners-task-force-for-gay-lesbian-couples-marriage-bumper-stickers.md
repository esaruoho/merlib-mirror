<td data-bgcolor="#EEEEEE"><strong>H</strong>ere are suggestions in support of the freedom to marry for use as bumper stickers, and campaign slogans or ads. These images are copyright and cannot be reproduced without permission. However, we do encourage the use of these phrases and concepts.
<p>One of these has been produced as a bumper sticker by the <a href="http://www.lmaw.org" target="http://www.lmaw.org">Legal Marriage Alliance of Washington</a> and can now be seen all over Western Washington.</p>
<p>For image purposes, we recommend against use a pink triangle. We think that symbol is off target for this affirmative message. The triangle — like the number they tattooed onto those they kidnapped into the death camps — was given us by the Nazis, not one we developed for ourselves. In addition, many gay and non-gay people do not identify with it, or want to be identified by it on their cars. Use of the triangle could significantly reduce the user base, excluding many potential supporters. [Please see <a href="why.html">Why <strong>Partners Task Force</strong> Does Not Use “Queer” to Define Our Community</a>.]</p></td>
