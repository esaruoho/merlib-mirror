**Foredom Turbine Tool**

Ultra High Speed Air Powered Handpiece For: \* Drilling  \*Grinding \* Engraving \* Carving \* Detailing\* Other Precision Applications on Most Metals, Plastic, Stone, Wood and Other Materials.\
The handpiece is powered by user supplied compressed air that spins a turbine surrounding the chuck of the handpiece. The chuck rotates at speed up to 350,00 rpm, 25 times as fast as a flexible shaft machine. Instead of removing relatively large particles at a slow rate of speed, the Turbine Tool grinds away tiny, almost pulverized particles at an extremely rapid rate. The result is greatly increased precision in grinding operations and a much better finish on materials as hard as steel.\
The complete Turbine Tool outfit includes: handpiece, stand with filter regulator, foot pedal, turbine lubricant spray, 12' vinyl tubing and wrench, set of three friction grip (b shank) carbide burs.
