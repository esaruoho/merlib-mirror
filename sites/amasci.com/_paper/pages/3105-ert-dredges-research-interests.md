# Ert's research projects

Research, research, research. If they had told me when I was seven that research meant building cool things out of Java and nucleic acids (mmm...DNA-mocha blend!) I would have liked it then, too.

My second year at UHCL I have been working on a user interface for working with a rule-based system for composite object manufacturing specifications.

My first year at UHCL I worked with the NASA-funded [Research Institute for Computing and Information Systems](http://rbse.jsc.nasa.gov) lab on an agent-based information search engine.

When I was at [Baylor College of Medicine](http://www.bcm.tmc.edu), I did most of my work in two particular labs:

- During my first placement I worked with [Randy Smith](http://ginger.bcm.tmc.edu:8088/department/people/smith/smith.html)'s group on genome informatics. Check out their [BCM Search Launcher](http://kiwi.imgen.bcm.tmc.edu:8088/search-launcher/launcher.html).

  After that, I worked with the [Computational Neurobiology Lab](http://dirac.bcm.tmc.edu/) on their [virtual bee model](http://dirac.bcm.tmc.edu/beefore.html).

I spent two years at The [Whitehead Institute](http://www.wi.mit.edu/) / [MIT](http://web.mit.edu/) [Center for Genome Research](http://www-genome.wi.mit.edu/) doing extensive computational work on the [dense SSLP genetic map of the mouse.](http://www-genome.wi.mit.edu/genome_data/mouse/mouse_index.html)

While at the Whitehead I also worked with [David Page's group](http://web.mit.edu/biology/www/Ar/page.html). I was involved with informatics work on [a mouse hermaphroditism study,](poschiavinus.html), developing software to catalogue the lab's mouse colony, generating [maps of the human Y chromosome](http://www.path.cam.ac.uk/hmgg.html), and analyzing CpG islands.

During 1992 I worked in [Chip Quinn](http://web.mit.edu/afs/athena.mit.edu/org/b/bcs/www/bcshome/quinn.htm)'s lab on a CREB transgene which blocks long-term memory in *Drosophila*

During Summer 1990 I worked with [Colin Lock](http://www.chemistry.mcmaster.ca/faculty/lock.html) in [McMaster University](http://www.mcmaster.ca/)'s [Chemistry Department](http://www.chemistry.mcmaster.ca/index.html), examining toxic properties of gold sodium thiomalate, an arthritis drug. [Dr. Lock](http://www.chemistry.mcmaster.ca/faculty/lock.html) passed away in May 1996.

During Summer 1988 I worked with [Gilles Lajoie](http://www.science.uwaterloo.ca/chemistr/lajoie.html) in the [Chemistry Department](http://www.science.uwaterloo.ca/chemistr/chemistr.html) of the [University of Waterloo](http://www.uwaterloo.ca/) on the design of an elastase inhibitor.
