<td width="50%"><p>Our meetings are a good place to make new plant friends and gather information, but also to acquire new rhododendrons and companion plants. First, each new member receives a free rhododendron. Plants are given away as door prizes. At each meeting we have a raffle with much better odds than the lottery. We have a cutting exchange in the fall and sometimes give out free seeds. Finally, we have a <a href="Plant%20Auction.html">plant auction</a> each year, where you can obtain high quality plants at very reasonable prices.</p>
<p>Come join us at a meeting and sample our friendly rhododendron community. </p></td>
