# David Kerlick in Anatolia, August 8-13,1999

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Sun 8/8 to Bursa

Termal Hotel G. Teleferik, Fanilies picnicking. Dolmus back to Bursa town, Seljuk Mosque. nuts and ice cream, Dolmus. Mineral Bath at Hotel.

### Mon 8/9 Bursa to Ankara

Green Tomb and Mosque. View of Bithynia. Otogar, 6 hour Bus trip starts out like a local. Attendant brings water, towels, &c during the trip. stop in E. (meerschaum, Midas). Hotel Buyuk Surmeli. Kosc restaurant.

### Tue 8/10 Ankara

Arch Museum, Citadel. Augustus Temple, Column of Julian the Apostate, Ruins of Roman Baths, local kids with squirt guns, just the thing for a hot day.

### Weds 8/11 SOLAR ECLIPSE

Rounded up at 6 AM for trip to airport to get Hal and Lee and others. Folks from Soouth Africa, Zimbabwe, Japan. Guide from Ankara. Northeasterly bearing. Stop for snacks in the total zone. Viewing in Osmancik, having the town's 15 minutes of fame. Site is an auto speedway, venue for oil wrestling, alas no performance for us. Good seeing as a wisp of clouds departs. Totality 2 min 16 sec. The dark et bluish light of the sky is unique. Pack at 3:00 and stop at Hattusas - Hittite Capital, then a long ride down 2-lane blacktop to Cappadocia

Club Med hotel, carved in tufa.

### Thur 8/12 Cappadocia

Underground City, Gorême churches and monasteries., Photo Ops., Ceramic studio (buy a 7-forld symmetric plate, their family's 7th generation), carpet studio - quite a showy presentation. No \$75K silk carpets for me, thanks.

### Fri 8/13

Caravanserai, Lunch in the Citadel, Ankara Archeological Museum (2nd visit), Attaturk Mausoleum.

Layover at Ankara Airport, late flight to Antalya.
