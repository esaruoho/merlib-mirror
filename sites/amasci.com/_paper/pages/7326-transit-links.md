# Transit Links

*Source page: `~robertf/transit/tranlinks.html`*

> Transit Links\
>
> <div>
>
> **Seattle Area Transit**\
> \
> [**Community Transit**](http://www.commtrans.org/) Serves Snohomish County, Washington (the next county north of Seattle) with extensive bus service.\
> \
> [**Everett Transit**](http://www.everettwa.org/transit/) Serves the city of Everett, Washington, about 30 miles (about 50 km.) north of Seattle.\
> \
> [**Metro Transit**](http://transit.metrokc.gov/) Serves King County, Washington (the county Seattle is in) with extensive bus service and the Waterfront Streetcar.\
> \
> [**Pierce Transit**](http://www.ptbus.pierce.wa.us/) Serves Pierce County, Washington (the next county south of Seattle), including the City of Tacoma, with extensive bus service.\
> \
> [**Seattle Center Monorail**](http://www.seattlemonorail.com/) The monorail that has been operating in Seattle since 1962.\
> \
> [**Seattle Monorail Project**](http://www.elevated.org/) A project to build a system of monorails in the City of Seattle.\
> \
> [**Sound Transit**](http://www.soundtransit.org/) Serves Snohomish, King and Pierce counties with high-speed rapid transit utilizing express busses, commuter trains, and now working on a light rail project called *Link.*\
> \
>
> **Other Transit Systems**\
> \
> [**Translink:**](http://www.translink.bc.ca/) **Vancouver, B.C.**, regional transit system. Includes buses, elevated light rail, ferries, and bus rapid transit.\
> \
> [**TriMet:**](http://www.trimet.org/) **Portland, Oregon**, regional transit system includes buses and light rail.\
> \
>
> **Transit Organizations**\
> \
> [**CETA (Coalition for Effective Transportation Alternatives)**](http://effectivetransportation.org/) Fighting to stop the Link light rail project and use the money for better forms of mass transit.\
> \
> [**Citizens for King County Monorail**](http://www.kingcountymonorail.org/) A group promoting three monorail lines to connect suburbs of Seattle.\
> \
> [**Light Rail Central**](http://www.lightrail.com/) The North American Light Rail Information Site.\
> \
> [**Monorail links**](../monorail/mrlinks.html) Links to some other monorail-related sites\
> \
> [**Monorail Society**](http://www.monorails.org/) An international society of monorail boosters. This site tells about virtually all the world's monorails with links to most of them.\
