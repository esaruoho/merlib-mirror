&#10;<p>Our SeaScape sailing yachts are all in the 50' range, with matching navy-blue hulls and trendy teal n' pearl striping!</p>
<p>An important point to make here is that <em>"Yes, they are our very OWN SeaScape yachts!"</em>; owned, maintained and skippered by us directly - no absentee owners, and no passing the buck ! Not only do we lavish a great deal of TLC on them, but they have lots more cosy touches and fun toys than the average charter boat!</p>
<p>All the yachts were built in Athens, and designed specifically for Mediterranean conditions—ie, lots of light and air. They're beamy, spacious boats, with plenty of deck &amp; cabin space; built for comfort rather than speed (hey, who really cares about that extra half a knot in the Greek isles??)</p>
<p>They can each sleep up to eight guests in four separate cabins. Some cabins are doubles, some convert to bunkbeds for singles. They have four heads (bathrooms, for the non-nautically inclined!), so with the extra shower on the aft swim platform, that makes five showers in all. . .quite the luxury!! Crew cabins are separate.</p>
<p>They all take part in our <a href="../../sailing_options/the_adventure_sailing_concept.html">Adventure Sailing</a> program, and they are also available for skippered charter, if you have your <a href="../../sailing_options/bring_your_own_group/index.html">own group of friends</a> you'd like to sail with.</p>
