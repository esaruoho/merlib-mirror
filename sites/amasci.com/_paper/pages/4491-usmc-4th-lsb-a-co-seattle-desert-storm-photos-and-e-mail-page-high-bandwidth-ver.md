**Unfortunately, that's all for "During" Desert Storm. I had mailed home 2 full rolls (48 pictures) to be developed, but they never made it home. Kind of still ticks me off to this day!**\
**This next group of 23 pictures takes place After the battles of Desert Storm - as things relaxed a bit ...**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

<img src="MCLangeValler.jpg" data-border="2" width="608" height="402" />\
**3/91 - I ran into Jess Valler, a friend from my small-town high school, (1/2 way around the world) who I hadn't seen for about 5 years!**\
 

<img src="MCcamel.jpg" data-border="2" width="576" height="373" />\
**3/91 - Me, a camel... need I say more? Got the photo op by giving some food and water to a Bedouin passing by.**\
 

<img src="MCaBedouinandhismadCamel3-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="579" height="390" />\
**3/91 - Here's the Bedouin and his mad camel.**\
 

<img src="MCHighwaytoNowhere3-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="579" height="390" />\
**3/91 - Ever feel like your just driving forever and not really getting anywhere?**\
 

<img src="MCTotheMarket3-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="579" height="390" />\
**3/91 - I'm not sure if this one is being bought or sold, but you just don't see this very often anywhere else.**\
 

<img src="MCairjubail.jpg" data-border="2" width="640" height="460" />\
**3/91 - An aerial shot of the Port of Jubail as we flew over during a C-130 resupply mission. Camp Shepard is on the left and the city/town of Al-Jubail is on the right.**\
 

<img src="MC9103McCarthyAbrahmSchulzeKenny.JPG" data-border="2" width="644" height="455" />\
**3/91 - The heat, sunshine, and dry weather made the entire country one huge volleyball court.** **(McCarthy, Abraham, Schulze, Kenny)**\
 

<img src="MC9103CardsThomasFeneisAtShepard.jpg" data-border="2" width="654" height="442" />\
**3/91 - So much time to kill, so few card games to play.** **(Thomas, Feneis)**\
 

<img src="MCHerveyCrosslandLange.jpg" data-border="2" width="608" height="436" />\
**4/91 - King Fahd International Airport. After being "dry" for a while, it didn't take much for us to get pretty tanked up with some "imported" and "disguised" beverages (look closely on the table) that weren't supposed to be in that country.** **(Hervey, Crossland, Me)**\
 

<img src="MCLoadingpalletsofMREsatKingAbdulAzizAirport4-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="466" height="336" />\
**4/91 - \#1 of 3: Loading the Air Force C-130 with supplies at King Abduhl Aziz Airport (AKA "The Scud Bowl" stadium).**\
 

<img src="MCC-130atKingAbduhlAzizAirport4-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="579" height="390" />\
**4/91 - \#2 of 3: Unloading the Air Force C-130 with supplies "somewhere up North" (you kind of got used to having no idea where you were).**\
 

<img src="MCPartyattheScudBowl4-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="579" height="390" />\
**4/91 - \#3 of 3: The Air Force treated us well. They sold me some, ...uh, ...well, "surplus" supplies that they just happened to have in the plane at a very reasonable price and I treated my Squad that day.(Abraham, Boren, Shauger, Heath, McCarthy, Crossland, Hervey, Larson)**\
 

<img src="MCwaterbed.jpg" data-border="2" width="640" height="431" />\
**4/91 - As we did 24 hr shift work at the Jubail Airport, the popular thing to do during your down time was to catch some rays on the world's largest waterbed - which kept surprisingly cool all the time.**\
 

<img src="MCatJubailAirportLatendresseLangeStintonDawsonVilla.jpg" data-border="2" width="681" height="505" />\
**4/91 - Just a group photo at the Jubail Airport.** **(Latendresse, Me, Stinton, Dawson, Villa)**\
 

<img src="MCPlayingCardsDubeLatendresseBettsGould4-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="579" height="390" />\
**4/91 - A lot of down time was spent playing nearly every variation of every card game ever invented...(Dube, Latendresse, Betts, Gould)**\
 

<img src="MCSaudiHighwaySign4-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="579" height="390" />\
**4/91 - An example of a bilingual Saudi freeway sign.**\
 

<img src="MCWheelofMisfortune4-91.jpg" data-border="2" width="390" height="579" />\
**4/91 - The "Wheel of Misfortune" was a classic example of the "OK, we did our job, now get us the hell home" attitude that settled in after a while.** (examples when you spin for a fortune: "Admin Screw-up: You are stuck in-country indefinitely" or "Bad Chow: You end up living through the war, but dying of food poisoning in the chow hall" or even "An overload of too many stupid safety rules pushes you over the edge to suicide")\
 \
 

<img src="MC9104StandingInTheDesert.jpg" data-border="2" width="329" height="488" />\
**4/91 - A shot of me standing at the end of the world.**\
 

<img src="USMC_A_Co_Seattle_Desert_Storm_Gulf_War.jpg" data-border="2" width="824" height="566" />\
**5/91 - A full group photo of Alpha Company 4th L.S.B., Seattle.**\
 

<img src="MCgunnyrambo.jpg" data-border="2" width="640" height="431" />\
**5/91 - As we prepared to head home, "Gunny Rambo" came around collecting all the crap everyone was going to try to sneak home.** **(Latendresse)**\
 

<img src="MC9105CrosslandStintonInCali.jpg" data-border="2" width="653" height="471" />\
**5/91 - One of the first moments back in the good ol' U S of A!!!!** **(Crossland, Stinton)**\
 

<img src="MCCALIFUSA.jpg" data-border="2" width="544" height="432" />\
**5/91 - Back stateside in California on weekend leave(Gunny Dosier from Kentucky, Stinton, Latendresse, Skommesa, Me)**\
 

<img src="MCSSgtJackson.jpg" data-border="2" width="640" height="439" />\
**8/2/91 - Marines always take care of their own - "A" Company and some media welcome S/Sgt Jackson coming home at Sea-Tac Airport after some recovery from his injuries.**
