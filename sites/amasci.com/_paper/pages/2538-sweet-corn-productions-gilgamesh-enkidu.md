<td><strong>An arrogant ruler is transformed by love —<br />
transformed, once again, by its loss —<br />
and thereby becomes a legend.</strong>
<p>It is a very personal story, showing a physical and mental journey begun in greed and abuse of power, and ending in wisdom and peace.</p>
<p><strong>Gilgamesh &amp; Enkidu</strong> distinguishes itself as a video retelling of the landmark, historic, poem of the <strong>Epic of Gilgamesh</strong>, in part, because it crosses a host of genre boundaries:</p>
<ul>
<li>Fantasy - mythical beings and dreams are mixed with reality</li>
<li>Historical Docu-drama - it takes place 4,600 years go in Sumer (now Iraq)</li>
<li>Biographical - Gilgamesh was a real king</li>
<li>Gay Content - Gilgamesh and Enkidu were lovers</li>
<li>Religious - the gods frequently interact with the mortals</li>
<li>Action-Adventure - earthquake, horrendous battles, and a gruesome beheading</li>
</ul>
