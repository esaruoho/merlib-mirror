**`Da Boofers page!`**

Da boofers is a very gentle and delicate creature.  You have to be nice to da boofers.  You can never be mean to da boofers.  The boofers needs lots of nuggles.  You can call the boofers by cute nicknames like boofies, and da boofinator.  Boofers loves ya!  Da Boofers has a friend named Pepe', Pepe' needs love too.  Da Boofers knows who you are.
