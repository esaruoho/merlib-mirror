FLASHBACK TO 2003, winter.DADDOG ANSWERS SOME NOS QUESTIONS AT THE CAMPFIRE\>

------------------------------------------------------------------------

The fire is low and it is about 1 A.M. after the neighbors bottle/gasoline campfire firebomb just sent a flame into the night air folowed by a huge WHUUMPF and a fireball 12 feet across. After the initial startle, the talk shifts to power and how to raise power for the 450.and the talk shifts to power and nitrous oxide:

These are excerpts from fireside chats , and we try to recreated it here as best we could. imagine the dark night sky with millions of sharp speckled stars clear and bright, as it is winter time and a westerly glow in the night sky from the town of El Centro. It is cool enough to be comfortable now, and you dont need a coat. no bugs and sparks coming up. A RINCON is pushing a huge log around the circle of chairs and pinally pushes it into the fire and a shower of sparks goes up, and the rincon jumps up on the log and gets stuck, 5 guys pull the rincoln off and it roars into the nigh... Someone up against the new bigger fire says: What about your NOS, dad? I want to know whats the best way that is the safest? So, Daddog, pulls out the schematic that shows how we independantly developed what we feel is the best and safest way for us to run NITROUS, if we were to run it.

![](daddognos.jpg)

QUESTION1 : Some people say one system is better than another because one depends on pressure and the other doesnt and the pressure drops and nothing works right and youll blow your engine, what about that dad?

.

ANSWER: Not true in our experience.

.

QUESTION 2: Do I need a bottle heater to heat my nos bottle? I want to go with a different system because I dont need a bottle heater.

.

ANSWER: Not true in our esperience.. If you jet the NOS for a bottle heater you will always need a botle heater. if you jet for without a bottle heater you dont need a bottl heater. why jet for a bottle heater and then buy a bottle heater? that is unwisely. Put the bottle on and jet for current conditions and you won't need a bottle heater at all especially if the outside temp is over 50 degrees. If you put the bottle in the wind, it stays perfect and you can run as much as you want, if you hide it, it takes a few minutes to warm up a little. but even then the loss is not that great.

.

QUESTION 3: Is NOS going to hurt my engine?

Answer: Is Gasoline going to hurt your engine? Same question. depends on how you jet. Jetting is everything!

.

QUESTION 4: Ok, then how do I jet? How do you jet nos? I hear they have these little funnels... ANSWER: Yes, the injector has two little funnels, one for NOS and one for GAS. The pressure for the NOS is very high, and the pressure for the gasoline jet is low, like about 4 psi with the fuel pump, so the jet sizes are about the same size but you get about 12 times more NOS going through the jets as you would gasoline through those jets. to jet, you just put one on the NOS side, usually in the range of 13 to 20 and then on the gas side about 15-24 size for gasoline. BUT FIRST, you have to jet your quad right. because most of the race will be without NOS. ok FIRST JET YOUR QUAD RIGHT, if you leave it stock you will have to jet the NOS richer and you wont run as good either way, with or without. Put a 48 pilot in, then jet your needle until it riuns good without NOS and raise your main until it runs perfect. Now you are ready for NOS jetting. We like a 13 size stainless steel funnel jet NOS and a 15 funnel jet gasoline (STAINLESS JETS! they dont vary with use!) you get them from schnitz racing.

.

You want go faster use a 14 NOS jet and 16 gas or even a 15 NOS jet and a 17 gas jet

.

Question: BUT THE KIT COMES WITH A 16 NOS and an 18 GAS?!?

.

ANswer, yes it does, but you dont need theat much. You want to beat them not KILL them. Besides lower jets you can run your nos 4 times as many races. At 13 NOS and 15 Gas jet we can run a bottle for 30 -50 runs or more easiyl and the motor doesnt even breath hard. at 18-21 jets we can run 10 good races and the motor feels the punishment, engine life starts dropping quickly. At 13-15 I daresay your engine will last about as long as stock, like I mean years. at 18-21 you will need new rings in a few months.

.

QUESTION: So why dont you really want to beat them by a long ways?

ANSWER cause if you beat one guy by a hundred feet no one will even race you. and your bored all day.

.

QUESTION: OK OK so I put in the 13-15 jets and the Banshee beats me what then?

ANSWER: It happens. You can get your wrench out and change to 21-24 jets in 2 minutes beat the Banshee, refill your bottle and change the jets back. It gives you something to do.

.

Question oh. ok.

Answer yup.

.

QUESTION: What if when I am running NOS, and the little wire comes off the fuel pump? will the NOS inject?

ANSWER: (playing taps) yes the NOS will still inject. Always carry this number with you 1-877-243-3337 on your cell phone, order a new piston and probably a new crank, and cylinder wall, maybe valves, depending if you heard rattling sounds, and get your NOS tow rope to pull your quad back to camp, load up and go home. We always carry spare cylinder, crank, valves head and chain wherever we go. in case the fuel does not inject. THIS HAS NEVER HAPPENED TO US YET! But if the fuel pump does not inject and you dry inject NOS play taps.

.

Question: Ok, why has it never happened to you?

ANSWER: Cause we are nice guys.

.

QUESTION: Seriously, why has it not happened to you?

ANSWER: Cause we are smart:

.

Question HOW SO?

ANSWER. BECAUSE WE INSTALLED A FUEL PRESSURE SWITCH on the system, so for any reason the fuel pressure drops (wire comes off or whatever) the electrical connection CUTS OFF THE NOS SOLONOID and the NOS WILL NOT INJECT. You can buy one EXTRA from schinitz racing or from holly performance products.

.

ANSWER OK, let me get my black DOG NOTES notebook. AH here it is./ NAPA PART NUMBER 701-1575 about 30 bucks.

QUESTION: Does it really work?

ANSWER: MY WORD, MAN, IT WORKS LIKE A CHARM, it worked three times already. Our video of the z400 going up chokecherry, on that day we would have melted down because the fuel pupm wire was intermittant and corroded and the NOS would not inject. saved the motor.

Twice on the YFZ-450 it refused to inject, one, the wire broke to the fuel pump, and two, we forgot to turn on the fuel pump once by switch, both would have toasted the motor.

.

so three times it saved our motor. We kiss that pressure regulator every day if we run NOS.

.

QUESTION: HOW DO YOU KNOW IF IT WORKS?

ANSWER we set up the NOS system this way: MAIN SWITCH SUPPLIES POWER TO NOS SYSTEM AND FUEL PUMP, WHEN THAT MAIN SWITCH IS ON the FUEL PUMP AUTOMATICALLY RUNS. there is a seecond switch that interupts the NOS solonoid and we turn it off so the NOS solonoid cannot work. Then we go out and ride it. When the throttle switch kicks in and the fuel starts to flow, it bogs down and runs rich (no NOS injecting) WE TEST IT EVERY TIME LIKE THIS! When there is a good BOG, then turn your nos on get ready for some action cause the next shot is gonna full your arms a bit when you take off.

.

ANSWER : IT IS!

ANSWER : YES!.

QUESTION Ok so what do I need to refill my own bottle.?

ANSWER : FOR WHAT? (KIDDING LOL) Ok go to your welding store and WE order a 15 or twenty pound tank of NOS full We order two, so one is always full. WE order na 18 inch high pressure nos hose (WE can get from schnitz) it is the hose that goes from the bottle to the solonoids in the \#3001 kit. It is strong steel braided hose, WE DO NOT USE HOSE UNLESS IT IS LABELED FOR NOS WE COULD DIE. ONE DORKY WELDER WE WENT TOO WAS GOING to sell us a hose that looks the same and we got almost home and it had a rating of 250 PSI, we would have been seriously injured. We dont trust peoploe unless they have done it before.

.

We ake our blue bottle we use 2 pound bottle,

( the single shots dont hold much and the sneaker bottles arent worth more than a few drags. ) WE get the two pound blue bottles. WE throw it in the freezer. If WE are at the dunes WE throw it in the camper freezer for an hour. WE Leave the BIG bottle in the sun or at GLAMIS leave it in the warm trailer. when the blue bottle is COLD WE turn the big NOS tank upside down, put on the 18 inch line and connect to the blue bottle. WE will need an adapter for the big tank. WE bought one. now, WE now open the little bottle and the big bottle, pretend it is water, WE have the little bottle lower than the big bottle, and it will flow into the blue bottle making a weird martian echo sound. it takes US 10 seconds, and it is full. WE let it sit a few more seconds and swirl the blue bottle, all the ice has dessolved off the bottle by now and it is warm, filling is an exothermic reaction. and then WE turn off the bottls. YOU should not try this, only WE can do these things.

.

TIP: We had a leakage problem, we used wall mart washers for WATER HOSES in the adapter part of the big tank and WE will not get a leak again, the pressure is so tremendous the washers last about 5 fillings and they swell up like a sponge, but the work really great. when they swell too much we throw it out and use another. WE use about one each trip. then get another one they are cheap. but no leaks !

.

QUESTION: How do I know when it is empty?

ANSWER: you feel the weight of the bottle and it doesnt fill any more, WE go get it filled then.

.

QUESTION: Sounds easy.

ANSWER: it is. WE NEVER CHILL THE BOTTLE AGAIN to do it twice it will overfill. The bottle is dependant on temperature the fuller it is the higher the pressure. As WE run NOS the fluid drops nad the pressure drops a little, not really that noticeable, soon the races get more close and then you actually go SLOWER when the bottle runs empty cause the GAS makes it rich. THIS IS NOT HARMFUL! you just go OH KAY! WE NEED ANOTHER BOTTLE HERE\> IF it is a serious race all you do is slap on a fresh bottle and turn the jets to 16-18. Throw the other in the freezer and when you come back it is ready to fill.

.

WE have two blue bottles and two big tanks. a lafge tank fill is 50 bucks and the tank is 120 bucks and the blue ;little tanks are 175 bucks! THEY COST MORE! weird. the NOS kit is around 500

that is all you need for nos right around 1000 dollars.

Question so how do I hook up all the wires?

.

Answer: Not too hard. the simple way is to get a smallest microswtich from RADIOSHAK and jbweld it into the thumb throttle. two wires come out the hole on the bottom. when full throttle is hit the switch clicks and the wires get connected. run these wires down the handle bars to a little reed relay, TINY RELAY make the NEGATIVE wirto the relay go up the handlebar into this swithc and back down the handlebar and to the reed relay ground terminal,

.

.

the positive power should remain SHORT not LONG and come from the mainswitch power positive. when the thumb relay is clicked the relay goes on.

.

This is the main part of the system it will take a day of futzing around to get it perfect, we can do ours in 5 minutes now when we want to hook up, we know the perfect place for the microswitch to sit, play with it, youll figure it out.

.

So the MAINSWITCH is any where you turn it ON and POWER goes to the FUEL PUMP whitch starts to run right now. and to this little relay. now this little relay has to poles normally open. run positive to one of them and keep it short, and the negative runstto the NOS solonoid and the gas solonoid and to the daddog delay circuit. the positive from the gas sloloniod just gow to battery, the positive from the nos runs through a little switch then through the FUEL PRESSURE REGULATOR LIFESAVING SWITH and to positive power, and the dad dog circouit goes to positive power also.

.

Now just hook up the gas line (buy new, run it from the tank to the fuel pump downhill. put a t in it for the carb so the quad will run, and then from the fuel pump to the gas solonoid. put a T in the high pressure line and the FUEL PRESSURE LIFESAVER SWITHC T's on to that so it knows the pressure. PULL THE RUBBER BACK off the fuel pressure switch and turn the screw, with the pump on until the switch come on with the fuel pump and off when fuelo pump is off. this is setting the correct pressure on the turnoff safety switch.

.

Now run the solonoids into the injector into the head. You can put it into the rubber carb boot (doesnt work so good, or into the CARB EXIT BAD MOVE YOU CANT REJET! or into the head BEST MOVE! use a brass couble nut drill the head aceptans boot and put injector in there.

when not running NOS just put a bot into it and your set.

.

That is it.

.

We could do it all in one hour the kit comes with a tap screw you can make the tap in 1 minutes.if you mess up you can always weld it or JBweld it, it is low pressure and not critical,

.

The tap goes onto the aluminum head right after the rubber boot from the darb in a nice place on the right side.
