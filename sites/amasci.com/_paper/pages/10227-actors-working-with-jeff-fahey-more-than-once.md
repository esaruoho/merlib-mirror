### Compiled by Stephanie Horan

\
**There was a thread going on this list a while ago about actors who had worked on films/projects with Jeff more than once. I know there were quite a few, but I didn't see a complete list posted here, perhaps no-one kept one. So -- having some time on my hands one morning, I did a bit of browsing through cast lists, and came up with the following, in alphabetical order:**\
\

Michael Beach -\
"The Hit List", "Sketch Artist II"\
\
Kevin Costner -\
"Silverado", "Wyatt Earp"\
\
Courteney Cox -\
"Curiosity Kills", "Sketch Artist II"\
<img src="jfskart.jpg" data-align-middle="" data-border="0" />\
Jeff Fahey and Courteney Cox in "Sketch Artist II"\
\
Brian Dennehy -\
"Last of the Finest", "Silverado"\
\
George Dzundza -\
"Impulse", "Execution of Raymond Graham", "White Hunter, Black Heart"\
\
Darlanne Fluegel-\
"Alfred Hitchcock Presents: Enough Rope For Two", "Darkman III: Die, Darkman, Die"\
\
Pamela Gidley -\
"Last of the Finest", "Freefall"\
\
Gene Hackman -\
"Wyatt Earp", "Split Decisions"\
\
C. Thomas Howell-\
"Curiosity Kills", "The Sweeper"\
\
Brion James -\
"Silverado" (I am \*sure\* he was in this, but is not in the Internet Movie Database cast list), "Sketch Artist II", "The Marshal" episode: "Kissing Cousins"\
\
Jon Kasdan -\
"Wyatt Earp", "Silverado"\
\
Jack Kehler -\
"Wyatt Earp", "Blindsided"\
\
William Lucking -\
"Parker Kane", "The Marshal" episode: "Natural Law"\
\
Michael McGrady -\
"Wyatt Earp", "Quick"\
\
Robert Mitchum -\
"Woman of Desire", "The Marshal" episode: "The New Marshal"\
<img src="jfmarsh.gif" data-align-middle="" data-border="0" />Jeff Fahey in "The Marshal\
\
Zakes Mokae -\
"Body Parts", "Parker Kane".\
\
Joe Pantoliano -\
"Last of the Finest", "The Marshal" episode: "Protection" <img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~whtrose/jfahey/finest2.jpg" data-align-middle="" data-border="0" />\
Jeff Fahey with Brian Dennehy, Bill Paxton and Joe Pantoliano in "The Last of the Finest"\
\
Lisa Jane Persky -\
"Last of the Finest", 2 "The Marshal" episodes: "Twoslip", "Pass the Gemelli" (as Fellton)\
\
Alan Rosenberg -\
"Impulse", "Parker Kane"\
\
Elizabeth Ruscio -\
3 "The Marshal" episodes: "The Great Train Robbery", "Buy Hard", and "These Foolish Things" (she played Sarah Steenbergen)\
\
Miguel Sandoval -\
"Quick", 2 "The Marshal" episodes: "Unprotected Witness", "Gone Fishing" (as U.S. Marshal Lester Villalobos)\
\
James Tolkan -\
"Split Decisions", "Sketch Artist", "Sketch Artist II", "True Blood"\
\
I doubt that this is a definitive list, I'm sure there will be a handful of minor actors that I didn't spot. But if anyone has others to add to the list, please let me know so I can update. By the way, the list above does \*not\* imply that the actor shared a scene with Jeff, only that they did work on the same movie/"The Marshal" episodes\
