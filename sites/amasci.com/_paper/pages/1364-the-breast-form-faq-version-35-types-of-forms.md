<th style="text-align: right;" data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="#ffff99" width="125"><a href="types/enhance.html">Thin Shell Breast Forms/<br />
Breast Enhancers</a></th>
<td data-bgcolor="#ffff99">  </td>
<td data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="#ffff99">These usually serve as an augmentation for surgeries that remove only a portion of the breast. They can also be worn as breast pads to augment the size of smaller busts.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<th style="text-align: right;" data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="#ffffff" width="125">Attachable Forms</th>
<td data-bgcolor="#ffffff">  </td>
<td data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="#ffffff">These are a newer type of form that is becoming quite popular. Such forms are usually silicone and more expensive than the non-attachable types. They are temporarily attached usually by velcro (hook side on the form, soft pad side attached to the chest by adhesive or surgical cement.)</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<th style="text-align: right;" data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="#ffff99" width="125"> Attachable Nipples</th>
<td data-bgcolor="#ffff99">  </td>
<td data-valign="top" data-bgcolor="#ffff99">Most breast forms do not have a pre-formed, colored nipple built in to the form (only some more expensive ones do.) Several manufacturers carry nipples that can be applied to forms that do not have this feature. Unlike the form itself, this accessory is rather cheap. A pair of silicone nipples usually costs about 20 - 40$US.</td>
</tr>
