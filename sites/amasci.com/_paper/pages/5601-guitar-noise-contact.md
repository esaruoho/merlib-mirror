<td width="100%"><h1 id="contact">Contact</h1>
<p>Your feedback is appreciated. In the past many reader comments have led to changes in the site. You can see what others have said already on our <a href="about/letters.html">testimonials</a> page.</p>
<p>If you have a question for us please remember that many questions have been asked in different ways, and the answer may already appear somewhere on Guitar Noise. If you have a question please check the following resources first:</p>
<p><strong>Help Pages</strong><br />
<a href="http://www.guitarnoise.com/help">http://www.guitarnoise.com/help</a></p>
<p><strong>Frequently Asked Questions</strong><br />
<a href="http://www.guitarnoise.com/help/faqs">http://www.guitarnoise.com/help/faqs</a></p>
<p><strong>Guitarnoise Search Engine</strong><br />
<a href="http://www.guitarnoise.com/search.html">http://www.guitarnoise.com/search.html</a></p>
<p><strong>Ask An Expert</strong><br />
<a href="http://www.guitarnoise.com/help/expert.html">http://www.guitarnoise.com/help/expert.html</a></p>
<p> </p>
<p>If you would like to ask a question the best place to do so is in our Discussion Forum. You are not obliged to register to post a question, and by using our active community board you are very likely to get more than one response to your question.</p>
<p><strong>Guitar Noise Online Forums</strong><br />
<a href="http://www.guitarnoise.com/%7Eogre/cgi-bin/yabb/YaBB.cgi">http://www.guitarnoise.com/forum/index.html</a></p>
<p> </p>
<p>If you have a question about a particular lesson on this Website it is best if you Email the author of that lesson. Every lesson includes the author's contact address.</p>
<p> </p>
