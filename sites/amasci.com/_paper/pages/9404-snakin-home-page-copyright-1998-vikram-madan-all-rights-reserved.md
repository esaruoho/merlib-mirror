<td><p>The Snakin' Level Editor, which is part of the Snakin' game package, allows you to create your own Snakin' levels. Registered users of Snakin' can load these new levels into Snakin' and have lots of fun playing them.</p>
<p>If you are a registered user of Snakin', visit this page often to see if there are new downloadable levels available for you.</p>
<p>If you have created Snakin' levels using the Snakin' level editor, and would like to share these levels with other Registered Snakin' users, e-mail your levels to one of the following addresses: <a href="mailto:vikram@eskimo.com">Vikram@Eskimo.com</a> or <a href="mailto:vmadan@bigfoot.com">vmadan@bigfoot.com</a> or <a href="mailto:v_madan@msn.com">v_madan@msn.com</a>. Include your name and the name of your level and attach your Snakin' Level files as attachments to your email message. Once we receive your Snakin' level, we will add it to this page so that other users can then download the level.</p>
