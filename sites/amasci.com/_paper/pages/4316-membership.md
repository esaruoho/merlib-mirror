**MEMBERSHIP**

\

![bar](bar80.gif)\

*Membership is open to all.*

**Youth Annual Membership: \$350.00** This membership is for middle-school and high-school students and others under 19. It includes coaching and (for spring 2003) entry in two local regattas for qualifying boats.

**Adult Annual Membership: \$300.00** This membership is for rowers who are 19 and over and out of high school. It includes coaching.

All checks should be made to **Northwest Legends**.
