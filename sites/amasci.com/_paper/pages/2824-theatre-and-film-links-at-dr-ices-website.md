<h3 id="seattle-area-theatre-entertainment-industry-stuff">Seattle Area<br />
Theatre/ Entertainment Industry Stuff</h3>
<hr />
<dl>
<dt><strong>Seattle Stage</strong> <em>- An incredible resource for Seattle-area theatre folks - Audition and other opportunity callboards, Hints and tips on auditioning, etc... Their audition callboard really rocks. I find that if I check it and print it out first, calling the <a href="hotlines.html">hotlines</a> is almost redundant.</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.seattlestage.com/">http://www.seattlestage.com/</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Theatre Puget Sound</strong> <em>- A Seattle-area " trade and service organization for artists, technicians, administrators, volunteers, audiences, and others involved in the theatre community within our region." Get on these people's email list, it's a great resource for timely info. Post your calls there, too. Just be sure to set up a folder in your inbox to organize the volume of mail you'll get when you join. :-}</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.tpsonline.org/">http://www.tpsonline.org/</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Freehold Theatre Lab Studio</strong> <em>- The web site of the best place in town (Seattle, WA, USA, that is)for actors to hone their craft. Home of the amazing Robin Lynn Smith, whose Meisner-based series of classes will transform your life and art to a higher place. Also home of Jessica Marlowe-Goldstein's auditioning and acting business training and George Lewis' movement training. All of these folks are gurus of their arts. Meet them. Know them. Take their classes, if you dare improve your acting craft. :-}</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.freeholdtheatre.org/">http://www.freeholdtheatre.org/</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Callboard Seattle</strong> <em>- As my friend Ginger says, this site has tons of info for theatre folks, including fresh industry news, audition postings in a calendar format, and a page of links like this one. A nice site.</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.callboardseattle.com/">http://www.callboardseattle.com/</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Liz Fugate's Northwest Theatre Links</strong> <em>- This UW Professor really has it goin' on... Links to Theatres, fresh information, regional leader's comments on arts initiatives. This site rocks the Northwest!</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://faculty.washington.edu/fugate/nwtl.html">http://faculty.washington.edu/fugate/nwtl.html</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>RedCard.Com</strong> <em>- A cool online resource for Seattle Theatre folks, includes an audition/work callboard that's pretty decent, although I think it waits for submissions instead of calling the hotlines and posting everything. One very cool thing on this site is a giant webified bulletin board (The Greenroom) where topics of interest can be discussed by subject. It also has free want ads for theatre folks around here. It also has listings for shows happening tonight, if you're looking to _see_ one. ::chuckle::</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.redcard.com/">http://www.redcard.com/</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Seattle Fringe Festival</strong> <em>- All this site is right now is an online application to be in the festival, but that's a cool thing in itself. This link bypasses their very pretty splash screen. It's not a bad splash screen, I just disdain splash screens as a concept.</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.seattlefringe.org/home.htm">http://www.seattlefringe.org/home.htm</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Media Inc.'s Northwest Production Index</strong> <em>- The actual resource on-line, including crew/talent banks searchable by all sorts of categories. Very nice service being offered, here. The only lameness on this site is how much clicking you have to do because of all the splash screens between real content. Oh, and the fact that you can only view talent by age and gender category. (Ickplthplth! Give me multiple avenues of navigation! ) Otherwise, it's very nice. It even has both the Northwest and the Hawaii Production Indexes.</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.nwfilm.com/">http://www.nwfilm.com/</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
<dt><strong>Ears, Inc.</strong>- <em>Calls itself the "Home of The Actor's Handbook", but it's not the resource online, just a place to buy it. Lameness, unless you want to buy stuff. It also seems to go down a lot, and is often slow to load. If you really need to know the address to that theatre you've got the audition at this evening, (so you can call up a <a href="http://maps.yahoo.com/py/maps.py">Yahoo map</a> to get there) go to the Liz Fugate site above.</em></dt>
<dd>
<a href="http://www.earsinc.com/">http://www.earsinc.com/</a>
</dd>
<dd>
 &#10;</dd>
