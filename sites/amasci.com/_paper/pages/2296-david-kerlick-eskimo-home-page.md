"Christianity is an Eastern religion that has been misunderstood in the West."-- Fr. John Lucas.

Thomas Jefferson's method of reading the New Testament: Cross out everything except that spoken by Jesus! A very big struggle of my life has been to find a compatible life partner. Trying to reconcile the sexual attraction (irrational, self-defeating, hooked on looks) with the emotional qualities (compassion, support) has led to many a blind alley. I'm terrible at the bar scene, being shy and expecting (based on experience) a 97% rejection rate, I have given up trying to make contact in such venues.

### Right (????) <span id="livelihood">Livelihood</span>

I work in the aerospace industry. I am very conflicted about this, because I feel I have been backed into it by economic circumstances. It's not that I haven't looked elsewhere. Reagan zeroed out all work for physicists that wasn't defense related, and Clinton has done zilch to restore it.

My disagreement with the management of the company I work for is virtually complete. They are pro-war anti-labor corporate Republicans. I am anti-war pro-labor social Democrat. They excuse human rights violations in China with "there's plenty of money to be made there." And they're right there with every new weapon of mass destruction that the Dr. Strangeloves in the Pentagon can dream up.

Finding a new job is nontrivial. Both times I've been unemployed before I was on the verge of losing everything. Do I hang in for five more years till I can legally retire, or try to make a big move again, with all the cost that entails? I don't feel like going through moving, selling a house, and all that pain again.

It's a real pinch that I feel. Work for the Evil Empire of Bill Gates, who makes crummy overpriced software, and who treats all workers as "temps?"
