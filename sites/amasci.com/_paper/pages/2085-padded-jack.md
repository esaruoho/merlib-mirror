Padded Jack (15th Century Style)

    Made from Hemp Canvas (Inner and outter shell)  with 8 layers of heavy linen for padding. It's sized for someone who's height is 5'10" (178cm) with a 42" (107cm) chest and a 35" (89cm) waist. 

**Finished Nov. 2000 (Shown)**\
