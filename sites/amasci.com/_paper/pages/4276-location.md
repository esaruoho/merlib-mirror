**LOCATION**

\

![](bar80.gif)

\
\
\

We will be launching from the dock in North Cove. From I-5 north- or southbound, go east on US 2, then north on Hwy 204, then (at Frontier Village Shopping Center) north on SR 9. At the next light, turn east on Lundeen Parkway and follow the lake shore. At the stop sign, keep right onto Vernon Road, which becomes N. Lakeshore Drive. At the next stop sign, turn right onto 124th/Main, then turn right again onto 17th, which will bring you to the dock.
