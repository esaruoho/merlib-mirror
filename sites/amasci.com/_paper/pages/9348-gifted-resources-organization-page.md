## <span id="giftassoc">Some organizations with interests in helping Gifted and Talented learners, their parents and educators:</span>

- DeKalb County Supporters for the Gifted A free computer bulletin board is active in the Atlanta metro area. The BBS is devoted to providing support for gifted students and parents and teachers of the gifted. The BBS has files for educators, such as lists of on line public research databases, quiz makers and teaching tips. For the student there is a mix of fun games, mind challenging puzzles, chess games and various tutor programs such as algebra, physics, and basic arithmetic. For parents there are text files including articles on nurturing the gifted and lists of organizations providing support for the gifted.

  The BBS has over 30,000 files and programs on disk and CDROM, and can be reached in Atlanta Georgia at 404-634-1815.

  The major goal of ECHA is to act as a communications network to promote the exchange of information among people interested in high ability - educators, researchers, psychologists, parents and the highly able themselves. As the ECHA network grows, provision for highly able people improves and these improvements are beneficial to all members of society.

  The basis for this specifically European Council comes from a belief in our common cultural heritage which is distinct from that of other parts of the world. Although Europe is made up of many different countries with many languages, we share the traditions and outlooks of societies in which education has been widely available for centuries. We also share the same problems, and it makes sense to work towards their solution together.

  ECHA intends to reach its goals by:

  - the organisation of biennial European conferences; previous conferences have been held in Zurich (1988), Budapest (1990), Munich (1992) and Nijmegen (1994). The fifth European Conference will take place in Vienna in October 1996;
  - the initiation of regional conferences and workshops;
  - the publication of a newsletter; ECHA NEWS is published three times a year;
  - the publication of the European Journal for High Ability;
  - the publication of books and other informational material;
  - offering, in conjunction with the University of Nijmegen,a two year advanced Diploma in the Education of the Highly Able;
  - the formation of Special Interest Groups;
  - the operation of a European Information Centre in Bonn;
  - the initiation and stimulation of international (applied) research
  - cooperation with all national and international associations concerned with high ability.

  ECHA is supported by Bildung und Begabung e.V. and by the German Federal Ministry of Education and Science. On 20 February 1995 the Secretary General of the Council of Europe has granted official consultative status to ECHA.

- **<span id="worldbel">Belgium</span>**\
  Mentor vzw (non-profit association) has recently been appointed as the official ECHA correspondent for Belgium. Person to contact: Paul van Buitenen, who, as parent of a gifted child, member of Mensa Belgium/Luxemburg and co-founder of the Mentor association, is closely connected with the subject. ECHA is the European Council for High Ability, a recognised NGO with advisory status for the Council of Europe.

  Mentor is a young and fast growing organisation which aims to promote the interests of gifted children in every way possible. Members of Mentor are parents, teachers and relief workers, all dealing with gifted children.

  For parents Mentor performs the function of a self-help group. We organise on a regular basis regional meetings between parents where they can exchange experiences, attend lectures and organise or participate in activities.

  Mentor also organises frequent short and specifically aimed projects for gifted children. We aim to stimulate social contacts among the participants and offer them a stimulating learning environment adapted to their particular needs. Topics addressed so far have been astronomy, pyramids, space travel, bridge and Mesopotamia. During the Easter holidays we are organising a project around multi-media and Internet.

  Furthermore we try to promote suitable schools for the gifted in Belgium. By this we do not mean special schools for gifted children only, but we mean schools which have the necessary flexibility within their curriculum to offer tailor made education if necessary. In this way tag children are not isolated and other (normally gifted) children could benefit as well. For this we operate as a pressure group in the media and in political parties.

  Mentor can be reached on the following e-mail address: vanbuit@mail.interpac.be

  Postal mail address: Mentor vzw, Bergstraat 99, B-3090 Overijse (Belgium)

- **<span id="worldfranc">France</span>**\
  The AFEP (Association Francaise pour les Enfants Precoces), French Association for Gifted Children is a non profit-making organisation whose goals are :
  - To identify gifted children. Thirty psychologists all over France identify them for AFEP.
  - To give them a chance to be recognized and accepted in school as well as in their families.
  - To help them find a proper educational ground so that they can "bloom" instead of losing their talent in a standardized and restrictive educational system.
  - To help teachers to recognize gifted children.
  - To demand the Ministry of Education to supply schools with special classes for gifted children.
  - To support parents when they have difficulties with teachers and officials.
  - To inform them of the rules in favour of their children.
  - To organise special out-of-school activities of interest for their children.
  - To gather information for researchers studying genetic and environmental influences.

  In France, only one state school provides adapted teaching in which the main goals are:
  - To develop the concept of personal effort
