# Astronomy Links

**"How do you tell an astronomer from Seattle?\
By the windshield wipers on his telescope."\
\
-- Ken Applegate, Seattle Astronomical Society,\
who passed on 1/29/2002; he'll be missed.**

> <span class="lead">W</span>elcome fellow amateur astronomers. . . . Below you'll find various astronomy links of interest for many fields of the hobby, with updates being added fairly regularly. These sites are external to my own, and their content is beyond my control; if a link becomes faulty, please let me know. See you under the stars!

Last updated: Feb 04 2002, 22:32:35 (Seattle)
