# Prayer of Serenity

------------------------------------------------------------------------

        
             God grant me the serenity
             to accept the things
             I cannot change.  

             Courage to change
             the things I can
            
             and the wisdom
             to know...
                
             the difference.

                               Reinhold Niebuhr

                (Thanks Linda Balsiger!)
