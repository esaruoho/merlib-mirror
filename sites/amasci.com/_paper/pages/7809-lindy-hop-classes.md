## <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff"><span id="Registration">Registration:</span></span>

> <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">**Class registration forms** are available on-line. You should mail class registrations no later than the Friday before classes start. Because of mail delays, if you miss this deadline, you **should not mail** your form because we probably won't receive it in time to enroll you. Instead, check back for class availability early during the week classes start. Then, show up a half-hour early for class so you can register at the door. If you have questions, please feel free to [ask by emailing us](#Questions).  After printing out the appropriate form, *make sure that you fill it out completely and mail the registration to us.* Your place in class is only reserved when we receive your mailed registration form with your class payment!</span>
>
> <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Not on our mail list? [Email us with your name and address](mailto:savoy@eskimo.com) or call (206) 547-7676 for more information or to have a flier sent to your home.</span>

## <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Class Times:</span>

> <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Unless indicated otherwise, classes last 1-1/4 hour (75 minutes). Students can normally practice to recorded music between classes. Please be courteous (i.e. quiet) while classes are in session.</span>

## <img src="images/dog2.gif" data-border="0" data-hspace="10" width="64" height="83" alt="Lindy Dog" /> <span id="Descriptions" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Class Descriptions:</span>

 

### <span id="Intro1" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Introduction to Lindy Hop - 1</span>

<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Get started with the fundamentals of partnering and 6-count swing. This class is perfect for those new to partner dancing, providing a "survival pack" of fun and easy moves to impress your friends with. *No dance experience required.*</span>

### <span id="Intro2" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Introduction to Lindy Hop - 2</span>

<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Jump into some 8-count with this fun class. Designed to build on the concepts learned in Part 1 (above), this class will teach you the "basics" that form the core of Lindy Hop. *Prerequisites: Intro to Lindy Hop 1 or equivalent.*</span>

### <span id="Essentials" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Lindy Hop Essentials</span>

<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">This level has been divided into four classes designed to develop essential skills and provide a solid foundation for work at the Intermediate level. Classes can be taken in any order, but you must complete all four before advancing. *Prerequisites: Intro to Lindy Hop 1 & 2*</span>

- <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">**Essentials: Charleston**: Get your kicks! Here's where you'll learn those fun Charleston variations, including Kick-Through, Hand-to-Hand, and Tandem. Charleston moves are perfect for fast tempos!</span>
- <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">**Essentials: Spins & Turns**: Is the room spinning faster than you are? Learn how to turn with grace and style, without getting dizzy! This class will teach you a variety of fun turning moves, for follows and leads, and put you in control of your spins.</span>
- <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">**Essentials: Classics**: Get Hep to the Jive. Learn the Mini-Dip, Groucho, Jig Walks, and other characteristic moves that give Lindy Hop its distinctive look.</span>
- <span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">**Essentials: Footwork & Flavor**: T'aint Whatcha Do... This class will improve the way that 'cha do it. Packed full of footwork variations and Jazz movement, this class will help you swing out with style. We recommend taking this one after you've completed the other classes at this level.</span>

### <span id="Intermediate" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Intermediate Lindy Hop</span>

<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">You've learned the basics, now add some shine! Expand your repertoire with fun new moves. Be prepared for a quicker pace, and have a solid foundation in the standard Lindy Hop vocabulary. New moves are taught each session, so you can continue taking this level indefinitely. *Prerequisites: Competence with ALL the material in the Essentials series.*</span>

### <span id="IntAdv" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Intermediate/Advanced Lindy Hop</span>

<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Are you an experienced Lindy Hopper? Ready for some flash and really tricky lead and follow movement? This class features challenging moves and advanced concepts for only the most proficient Lindy Hop dancers. *Prerequisites: Intermediate Lindy Hop level for one year.*</span>

### <span id="Advanced" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Advanced Lindy Hop</span>

<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Flyin' Home... Creativity is where you're at! Explore the ingenious variations and trickerations that make this dance sizzle. If you no longer struggle with complicated moves and routines, this class will help you take your dancing to a higher plane. *Prerequisites: Continuous study at the Intermediate-Advanced level for at least 1 year. Warning: Students slowing the pace of instruction for other dancers will be asked to drop back to a less-advanced class.*</span>

### <span id="Special" style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Special Classes:</span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%"> ***Advanced Styling and Musicality:***  Learn to recognize the structure of swing music, predict that next break or phrase with confidence, and match your movement with the motion of the music. This class will cover basic swing music structure, translating this structure into movement, and explore different types of movement in response to different songs and different sections of songs. </span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%"> </span>*<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff">Prerequisites: </span>*<span style="BACKGROUND: #ffffff"> *be at the Lindy Hop Intermediate level for 6 months.*</span>

**<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%">*<span id="Aerials">Aerials</span>:*</span>**<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:&quot;Courier New&quot;"> </span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">High-flying fun!  We'll work on some exciting air moves that will teach you the basic concepts and partnering techniques necessary to make these impressive moves safe and easy.  Bring a long sleeve shirt and wear clothes with as few buttons, buckles, zippers and things to get caught on as possible.  *All levels. * </span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%">*Partner required!*</span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">***<span id="Choreography Workshop">Choreography Workshop</span>: ***</span>  <span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%">This class will focus on teamwork and creativity as we collectively choreograph a routine.  Learn about musical structure and team formation while sharing moves and techniques with your peers.  *Intermediate to Advanced level.  Partner required!*</span>

***<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%"><span id="Fast Lindy">Fast Lindy</span>: </span>***<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">Not for those with pace-makers!  We'll teach the technique </span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">behind keeping your fast dancing clean, easy and fun, and share some moves that really only work at faster tempos.  Bring an extra T-shirt!  </span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">*Intermediate level and above.*</span><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:&quot;Courier New&quot;"></span>

<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:&quot;Courier New&quot;"></span>

<span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:&quot;Courier New&quot;"></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%">***<span id="Floorcraft">Floorcraft</span>: The Art of Dancing Near Other People***</span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">  Dancing involves more than just two people, and how dancers relate to other dancers on the floor is what we call "floorcraft".  This workshop will cover the various skills required for incident-free and confidence building floorcraft, including: modify various moves for the available space,  awareness exercises for knowing where other couples are and what they are likely to do, and assisting your partner to navigate a crowded dance floor.  *Geared towards the intermediate dancer, although more adventurous essentials-level dancers are welcome. *</span>

**<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%">*How To <span id="Create Your Own Moves">Create Your Own Moves</span>: *</span><span style="font-size: 10.0pt; font-family: Courier New; mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>**<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">Ever wonder where those fun new moves come from?  Todd & Krissy lead you through the process of how to be creative in designing your own signature moves in 8-count lindy.  </span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">*Intermediate level and above.*</span>

**<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%">*Intermediate/ Advanced <span id="Lindy Routines">Lindy Routines</span>: *</span><span style="font-size: 10.0pt; font-family: Courier New; mso-spacerun: yes"> </span>**<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">Lots of moves, lots of fun with energetic instructor Jodi Fleischman.  Plus a warmup with fun jazz steps!   </span><span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">*Intermediate level and above.*</span>

***<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0% 50%"><span id="Jazz Movement">Jazz Movement</span>: </span>***<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%"> Great lindy dancers are great jazz movers!  Well teach you how to develop your general movement with style and add character to all those cool steps!  Whether you have awesome moves already or just generally feel uncoordinated, this class is for you!  *All levels.*</span><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:&quot;Courier New&quot;"></span>

<span style="background-color: #ffffff; background-repeat: repeat; background-attachment: scroll; background-position: 0%">***Playing with <span id="Tandem Charleston">Tandem Charleston</span>:***  We'll show you lots of groovy stuff you can do while in Tandem Charleston, including a Russian kick, switches and ways to move around the floor in tandem.  *Intermediate level and above.*</span><span style="font-size:10.0pt;font-family:&quot;Courier New&quot;"></span>
