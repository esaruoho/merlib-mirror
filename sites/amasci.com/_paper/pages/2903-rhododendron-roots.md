<td width="50%"><p>Rhododendrons have a compact, shallow root mass. The entire system on this 10-foot high plant was less that a foot deep. It is easy to see why rhododendrons benefit so much from mulch to keep the roots moist and cool.</p>
<p>A <a href="storm2003.html">winter windstorm</a> uprooted this rhododendron and it never recovered. I used a pressure washer to remove the dirt while leaving even the hairline roots intact.</p></td>
