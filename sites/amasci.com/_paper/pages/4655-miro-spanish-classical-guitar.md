# MIRO

Though born in London Miro has spent most of his life in Spain where he first began playing guitar.

A late comer in the classical music scenario, it was not until the early 1980's, under the tuition of Manuel Rubio, that he became involved in the study of Spanish classical composers and some of the main transcriptions for guitar.

In spite of not being a full-time musician he has performed in most western European countries and even as far away as New York, Brazil, the UAE, and Japan.

### Passion for Spanish Classical Guitar

<img src="images/miropassion.jpg" data-border="0" width="158" height="158" alt="miropassion" />\

This CD contains 20th century spanish classical guitar performed beautifully by Miro. This CD includes works of Granados, Albeniz, Barrios, as well as Maza, Torroba, Tarrrega and Pipo. It has many spanish dance songs, "danza espanola" and was recorded in Europe.

### Spanish Classical Guitar

<img src="images/miro1.jpg" data-border="0" width="158" height="158" alt="miro1" />\

In this first CD Miro has concentrated on the Spanish classics, the main source of influence in his playing. Includes pieces by Sor, I. Albeniz, Tarrega, Villa-Lobos ...

This CD includes pieces from various times and styles, in an attempt to provide the listener with a collection that would be both unusual and not too difficult to get into.
