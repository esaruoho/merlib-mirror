<strong>I'm <del>Leaving</del> Blogging The Fantastic Four!</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
So thanks to a friend (Thanks Jim!), I got to see a preview of Fantastic Four last night, in the midst of tons of other chaos. The movie was great. I would call it a perfect popcorn movie. If you are expecting it to be high art, go somewhere else. But if you want a decent adaptation of a comic book, this is it.<br />
<br />
I wouldn't say it's as good as Spidey or X-Men, but I enjoyed it quite a bit. The effects were breathtaking, and Michael Chiklis was born to play Ben Grimm. Stan Lee's cameo was unexpectedly funny. Kerry Washington's part was particularly appreciated by me (as I just say her in <em>Ray</em> recently), and she pulled it off wonderfully.<br />
<br />
All told, a nice solid movie, worth a matinee for sure... and I might even pay full price if I wanted to see it again. I'll probably get the DVD as well.

<p>Sunday, July 03, 2005 <span id="112040138053682754"></span></p>
<strong>Flipping Through Previews...</strong>
<p><span class="rss:item"></span></p>
<div style="clear:both;">
&#10;</div>
Yes, it's that time again... time for "Things in Previews That Look Interesting But I Can't Afford To Get (with a list of things I am getting just for contrast)". I'm going to start with the bulk of the book, and then come back and cover the "big four" at the end. Please remember, I'm listing what looks interesting. My actual purchase list is going to be much smaller. The numbers after each title are usually the page number in <a href="http://previews.diamondcomics.com/">Previews Magazine</a>, so you can follow along at home.<br />
<br />
Let's start with the covers. Or maybe not. They are both really boring this month. Nothing of interest here.<br />
<br />
Moving on, let's hit the Splash Page (8-9). I like the idea of T-shirts being sold in clamshell packaging. Good for the customers, good for the store... I wish I could get shirts at the shop I work at in clamshells... as long as the packaging shows <em>clearly</em> what is on the shirt, front and back. The only problem with that is that a few customers always insist on looking at the sizing of the shirts, since sometimes a large is a medium and sometimes it's an extra large.<br />
<br />
Also of interest is the Halloween Ashcans. There's no way Eric and I can compete with <a href="http://realtegan.blogspot.com/2004/10/happy-halloween.html">last year's loot bag</a>, but we certainly intend to get these ashcans as we do every year. Kids who come to our house will leave with a candy bar and a comic book at the very least (as long as they dress up and say the magic Hallowe'en threat).<br />
<br />
Another item of interest to me is Heroclix DC Icons. There will be an Aquaamn figure in this set, and I <em>will</em> get it. Absolutely. The rest I don't care about, but I'll get Aquaman for sure.<br />
<br />
And now let's go into the regular Previews... as usual, let's skip over the Big Four (at least all the pages are here this month) and start with <a href="http://www.wizarduniverse.com/">Wizard</a> (190-196). Not much to look forward to in that section.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_Treehouse.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" />So into the Featured Items Section (198-201) we go, tra-la-la. First item of note is <em>Smoke &amp; Guns</em> from <a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/">AiT/Planet Lar</a>, which as you may recall from my preview was not to my liking, but oh you gotta check out the artwork! Next item of note for me is <em>Bart Simpson's Treehouse of Horror #11</em>. I don't love The Simpsons, but hubby-Eric enjoys the show a lot. The only exception to my lack of enthusiasm of The Simpsons is the annual Treehouse of Horror show, and the companion comic book. So, yes, we'll be getting it. The last item of note is <em>Polly &amp; The Pirates</em>, which looks like it could be a fun all-ages book, only I'm getting a little tired of pirates. Arrr.<br />
<br />
And now I'm into the bulk of the comic's listing. This is going to take awhile.<br />
<br />
First up is <em>Sherman's Room</em> from After Hours Press (210). A quick search didn't find a website for the publisher, only this <a href="http://www.shermansroom.com/">minimal website</a> for the comic. I'd prefer a little more information before jumping into a series, even if it's by Chris Eliopoulos (whose work I'm slightly familiar with already).<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_Colonia.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" /><a href="http://www.ait-planetlar.com/">AiT/Planet Lar</a> (210) is up next with several offerings. First is <em>Ursula</em>, which you ought to get if you haven't already. The same could be said for <em>Colonia Volume 1: Islands &amp; Anomalies</em>. Speaking of Colonia, the second trade is being solicited - <em>Colonia Volume 2: On Into The Great Lands</em>. I wouldn't mind getting it, but I suspect our money situation is going to make us delay this one for awhile. I will own it eventually, just maybe not right away. Lastly is the already-mentioned <em>Smoke &amp; Guns</em>, with art by Fabio Moon, who also worked on <em>Ursula</em>.<br />
<br />
Ok... into the confusing listings for <a href="http://www.aliasenterprises.com">Alias</a> (210-219). First up would be <em>Lullaby #1</em>, which apparently takes up from where <em>Lullaby: Wisdom Seeker</em> leaves off. We'll have to see about that one. Another item of interest that I won't be buying is <em>Armorquest</em>, which was mentioned in <a href="http://seattletimes.nwsource.com/html/faithvalues/2002355240_christiancomics02m.html">today's Seattle Times</a>. We'll likely get <em>Opposite Forces #3</em>.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_OzTheManga.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" />Next up is <a href="http://www.antarctic-press.com/">Antarctic Press</a> (224-228). I'm afraid the only thing we'll be buying from them is <em>Oz: The Manga #4</em>, but I can say already that we'll both enjoy it, as the first two issues were REALLY good. I expect them to collect this series into Manga-Digests, and we'll have to get the collection too. But I'm glad to get the series, as the covers so far have been nicely iconic. This issue has Dorothy's face.<br />
<br />
How's this for a lineup of horror comics creators: John Severin, Angelo Torres, Al Willamson, Mark Schultz, Len Wein, Berni Wrightson, Marv Wolfman and Gene Colan? Guess what book they're doing this month? Yup... <em>Bart Simpson's Treehouse of Horror #11</em> from Bongo Comics (247). Yeah, that's a keeper. Hubby-Eric will also want <em>Futurama #21</em>.<br />
<br />
Now, just in case you haven't had enough of Oz and Oz-related books, <a href="http://www.buymetoys.com">BuyMeToys.com</a> (252) has the zero issue of <em>The Oz/Wonderland Chronicles #0</em>. If you want to see a little more of this, the first few pages are available at <a href="http://www.buymetoys.com/BB/viewforum.php?f=26">The Oz/Wonderland Chronicles Message Board</a>.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_TrueStory.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" /><a href="http://www.tombeland.com">Clib's Boy Comics</a> (253) is next with the unmissable <em>True Story, Swear To God #15</em>, in which Tom goes into the comic book business at Lily's urging. Seriously, buy this comic. If you haven't bought it, you'll never know what you are missing. So get it. Now. Seriously.<br />
<br />
I really would like to get the <em>SPX 2005 Anthology</em> from <a href="http://www.cbldf.org/">CBLDF</a> (253), but I'm not sure I can swing it, and I'm never sure what will be in it. Maybe if we win the lotto by the time I turn the order in (which would be a neat trick, since I'm not buying lotto tickets).<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.eviltwincomics.com">Evil Twin Comics</a> (281) has me hooked, and I think I'll have to get both <em>Action Philsopher's Self Help For Stupid Ugly Losers</em> and <em>Action Philosophers #1 (2nd Printing)</em>. Looks like fun.<br />
<br />
The next volume of the <em>Complete Peanuts</em> is solicited through <a href="http://www.fantagraphics.com/">Fantagraphics</a> (284). Again, it's a pity the cover designs are so ugly, at least the comics inside are reprinted ok. There is also a slipcase box set of volumes 3 &amp; 4.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.fc9.net/">FC9</a> (287) has <em>Genie #4</em>, which could be something truly interesting if the first issue is any indication. I think we'll be getting it.<br />
<br />
Moving into another publisher, <a href="http://www.idwpublishing.com/">IDW Publishing</a> (297-303) has <em>The Keep</em>, adapted by the original author F Paul Wilson into a comic book to make up for the crappy movie. We'll also be getting <em>Angel: The Curse #4</em>, since the first issue was fun.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_Dorothy.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" /><a href="http://www.dorothyofoz.net/">Illusive Productions</a> (304) is collecting the first four chapters of their Oz book, the somewhat fumetti-style <em>Dorothy</em>. I recommend this to anyone who wants to see lush illustrations along with a solid, and original, story. Look for a joint review from hubby-Eric and I of the first three chapters of this book on <a href="http://blogofoz.blogspot.com/">The Wonderful Blog Of Oz</a> soon.<br />
<br />
Flipping further, <a href="http://www.moronik.com/Comiks.htm">Moronik Comics</a> (310) has a couple of slightly promising sounding books. Ok, so the company name doesn't inspire confidence, and the titles themselves, <em>Bug Girl</em> and <em>Dirtboy</em> don't sound overwhelmingly amazing, the descriptions still intrigue. And there are sample pages on the website, so check 'em out.<br />
<br />
I somehow think that hubby-Eric will want <em>The Tick: Days of Drama #2</em> from New England Press (314), since we ordered issue one.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_Polly.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" /><a href="http://www.onipress.com/">Oni Press</a> (314-315) has <em>Polly &amp; The Pirates</em> from the creator of Courtney Crumrin. It looks like a fun adventure.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.randomhouse.com/pantheon/">Pantheon Books</a> (315-316) has a boxed set of <em>Persepolis</em> that would make a nice gift for just about anyone.<br />
<br />
<em>Amelia Rules #15</em> is resolicited from Renaissance Press (316). This was previously solicited as <em>Amelia Rules Superheroes #5</em>.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.gomanga.com/">Seven Seas Entertainment</a> (320) is a publisher of original Manga... including <em>Captain Nemo</em> and <em>Unearthly</em>. Both look very interesting, and I'd love to check them out.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.speakeasycomics.com/">Speakeasy Comics</a> (322-332) has been a good little effort, and we'll get <em>Beowulf #6</em>. I'm not sure about any of the other books, but we'll keep getting that.<br />
<br />
There's a collection of <em>Star Trek: The Next Generation Comics</em> coming out from <a href="http://www.titanbooks.com/">Titan Publishing</a> (333). I don't know that we'll get it, but it's nice to see it coming out.<br />
<br />
From <a href="http://tokyopop.com/">Tokyopop</a> (337-358), I see some of the books from the <a href="http://www.tokyopop.com/takuhaionline/index.php">free on-line preview</a>. The first up is <em>I Luv Halloween</em>, and I also see <em>Dramacon</em>. Apparently you can also get their free print mag through Previews, but it's not free to retailers. So far, Viz still has Tokyopop beat, but once I see Takuhai in my mailbox, if it ever actually comes out, they might change my opinion.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_Owly.jpg" data-align="right" width="100" height="150" /><a href="http://www.topshelfcomix.com/">Top Shelf</a> (360) has the third volume of <em>Owly</em>. I've recently become a big fan of Owly, and I wouldn't mind getting all of them. I'm afraid it won't be soon, but I'll get them sometime.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.viz.com/">Viz Comics</a> (364-374) has a lot of neat stuff that I'm now a little better versed in. <em>Shojo Beat</em> and <em>Shonen Jump</em> are both good investments if you want serious Manga samplers... though I recommend subscribing instead of buying at a shop, because it's a lot cheaper that way... half the cost. I would like to get <em>Hikaru No Go</em>, but I really want to get all of the earlier ones first.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.wizkidsgames.com/">WizKids Games</a> (536) has the DC Icons set, which will have an Aquaman figure. I plan on getting the Aquaman figure, rookie-experienced-veteran for sure. Somehow.<br />
<br />
There's one DVD I really want... page 544. <em>Hikaru No Go Volume 1 DVD</em>. I've seen some of the fansubs of the Anime, and it's excellent. I really want to see them in English.<br />
<br />
That's the end of the book, time to turn around and head back to the big four at the beginning. I think I'll start with Dark Horse today.<br />
<br />
<img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/blog/blogpics/p200507_Usagi.jpg" data-align="left" width="100" height="150" />I'll be getting a couple of books from <a href="http://darkhorse.com/">Dark Horse Comics</a> (17-52), including <em>Conan #20</em>. I'd like to get the trade <em>Conan Volume 2: The God In The Bowl and Other Stories</em>. I'll also get <em>Usagi Yojimbo #87</em> and <em>Samurai Executioner Volume 8</em>.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.imagecomics.com/">Image Comics</a> (129-175) has a few. I would like to get <em>Ronin Hood of the 47 Samurai</em>, but I'm not sure we'll be able to swing it. A couple of other trades I wouldn't mind include <em>Invincible</em> and <em>Shadowhawk</em>. I will be getting <em>Noble Causes #14</em>, but that might actually be the only one.<br />
<br />
Almost done... <a href="http://marvelcomics.com/">Marvel Comics</a> (178)... I haven't yet decided if I'll get either of the <em>Supreme Power</em> minis. If I do, I'll get the one written by JMS. I'll get <em>Amazing Spider-Man</em>, but that looks like it's about it.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.dccomics.com/">DC Comics</a> is the last one on the list. I just get through these, and I can finish for the month.<br />
<br />
Batman (57-63): Nothing of interest at all.<br />
<br />
Superman (64-68): I'm ever so slightly interested in <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4221">Shazam/Superman: First Thunder #1</a></em>, but I don't think I'm going to start another mini-series at the moment. Nothing else is interesting to me.<br />
<br />
<a href="http://www.dccomics.com/features/aquaman/index.html"><img src="http://www.eskimo.com/~tegan/aqua/pics/cvr2003/cvra434.jpg" data-align="right" data-border="0" width="200" height="300" /></a>DC Universe (69-89): <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4203">Crisis on Infinite Earths: The Absolute Edition</a></em> looks good, but I don't think I'd want to spend $100 on yet another reprint of that same old story. I know I'll be getting <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4143">Aquaman #34</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4146">Birds of Prey #86</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4147">Day of Vengeance #6</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4151">Green Lantern #5</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4153">Green Lantern Corps: Recharge #1</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4197">JLA #118</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4198">JLA #119</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4159">JLA Classified #12</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4160">JSA #77</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4161">JSA Classified #3</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4163">Legion of Super-Heroes #10</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4167">Outsiders #28</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4168">Plastic Man #18</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4174">Rann/Thanagar War #5</a></em>, <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4175">Seven Soldiers: Guardian #4</a></em>, and <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4182">Villains United #5</a></em>. Other books I might get... <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4149">The Flash #226</a></em>. I haven't decided about <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4155">Green Arrow #54</a></em>, but there's a decent chance we won't get it. I'd like to get <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4171">Sgt Rock's Combat Tales</a></em> and <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4172">The Secret of the Swamp Thing</a></em> in digest-size, but we may have to wait on that. I also would like to get <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4176">Showcase Presents Superman</a></em> and <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4177">Showcase Presents: Green Lantern</a></em>, as I'd like to support any reprints of older stuff, but those aren't likely to get on our list. I'm also not sure about <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4179">Seven Soldiers: Mister Miracle #1</a></em>.<br />
<br />
Johnny DC (90-92): We stopped picking up <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4187">The Batman Strikes!</a></em>, but we'll get <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4189">Justice League Unlimited #13</a></em>.<br />
<br />
CMX (93-97): Nothing of interest here.<br />
<br />
Wildstorm (98-109): <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4113">Astro City: The Dark Age #4</a></em>. Naturally.<br />
<br />
Vertigo (110-122): <em><a href="http://www.dccomics.com/comics/?cm=4130">Neil Gaiman's Neverwhere #4</a></em>.<br />
<br />
DC Direct (123-128): Nothing of interest here.<br />
<br />
And there it is... all of Previews. I'm finally all the way through. Huzzah. So that's it for this month's "Things in Previews That Look Interesting But I Can't Afford To Get (with a list of things I am getting just for contrast)". Comments? Questions? Did I miss any really cool books that you know about and would like to point out? Do you disagree with something I wrote, and want to correct me? There's the comments, right below. Post something. And, yes, I accept review copies. I'm willing to give almost anything a try.<br />
