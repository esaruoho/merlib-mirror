| **Welcome** to our **1999** web site! This is our third year online and we'd like to thank everyone for visiting us here on the Internet, and at our store. We are devoted to providing the latest and greatest new products from around the world along with the quality product lines that have made kiting so popular and enjoyable over the years. If you're ever in Everett, WA, please stop by our shop. We'll show you around and answer all of your questions. We offer only the highest quality products from the leading manufacturers around the world. We also offer services and answers to your kiting questions. That's our business. **Your Satisfaction is our Top Priority!** |
| ***Erv & Gail Crosby*** |
