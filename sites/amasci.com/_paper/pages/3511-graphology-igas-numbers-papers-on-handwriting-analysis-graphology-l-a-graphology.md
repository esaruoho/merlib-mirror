# IGAS Membership Numbers

On 16 January 2003, IGAS permanently closed its doors. A few people wanted to know how many people that action left stranded. A few others wanted to know how much its growth had dropped over the last few years.

By getting the date that the person signed up the general course in Graphoanalysis, and their membership number, one can make estimates of both of those figures. The document below has the results.

Whilst the data reflects information I was given, I would like to have more datapoints. If you were a member of IGAS, send [Jonathon Blake](mailto:jblake@eskimo.com) an email stating your membership number, and the date that you signed up for the course. I am **not** interested in the date \[ Month and Year \] you graduated from the General Course. That date doesn't help things at all. \[ If given that date, I deduct eighteen months from it, and state that is the date of joining IGAS. There are several instances where I did that in the document below. \]

As I get more numbers and dates, I will update this document.

<a href="number.pdf" class="chapterlinks">The numbers from the spreadsheet. PDF Format</a>.

To read this document software that reads PDF files is needed.\
The following programs allegedly work:\

- <a href="http://www.mbrainsoftware.com/Software.htm" class="externallinks">PDF+ : Symbian OS</a>
- <a href="http://www.pdf.iconbar.com/" class="externallinks">!PDF : RISC OS</a>
- <a href="http://www.foolabs.com/xpdf/download.html" class="externallinks">Xpdf: Solaris / Linux / SPARC / DOS 6.0 / Win32/ Source Code</a>
- <a href="http://www.adobe.com/products/acrobat/readstep2.html" class="externallinks">Adobe Acrobat : They invented the format, and have it available for 24 platforms.</a>
