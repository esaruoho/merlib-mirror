# **Tinnitus Online Chat Sounds**

\

For those times we're distracting from T instead of discussing it...\
These are oriented to pc-clone Windows users, because they are zip archives of wav files. Some other formats can be arranged.

Note: all sounds were collected from public pages and are free for personal use only. None of the clips are particularly offensive as far as I know, but please use your own judgement and delete key as appropriate. Credit for most of these sounds belongs to various pages on the <a href="http://www.webring.org/cgi-bin/webring?ring=wavring;list" target="_top">Wav Ring</a>.

Halloween sounds are collected on a separate page which is available during October.
