Leavenworth Ice Fest\
Leavenworth, Washington
=======================

Pull: 83

Organizer(s):Jeff Parsons

Snow on Grainy Snow Region: 2

Track:

Very grainy snow, but it held up well.

Date: Sat 19 Jan 2002

Weather:

Overcast and 35 degrees at start; 32 degrees at finish.

Day 1 of 2
