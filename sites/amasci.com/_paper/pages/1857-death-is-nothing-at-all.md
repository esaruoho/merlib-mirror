Death Is Nothing At All<br />
<em>by Henry Scott Holland (1847-1918),<br />
Canon of St.Pauls Cathedral</em>
</div>
<p>Death is nothing at all.<br />
I have only slipped away into the next room,<br />
I am I and you are you,<br />
Whatever we were to each other, that we still are.<br />
Call me by my old familiar name,<br />
Speak to me in the easy way which you always used,<br />
Put no difference in your tone,<br />
Wear no forced air of solemnity or sorrow,<br />
Laugh as we always laughed,<br />
At the little jokes we enjoyed together.<br />
Let my name be ever the household word that it always was.<br />
Let it be spoken without effect,<br />
Without the trace of a shadow on it.<br />
Life means all that it ever meant,<br />
It is the same that it ever was.<br />
There is unbroken continuity.<br />
Why should I be out of mind,<br />
Because I am out of sight?<br />
I am waiting for you, for an interval,<br />
Somewhere very near,<br />
Just around the corner, all is well.</p></td>
