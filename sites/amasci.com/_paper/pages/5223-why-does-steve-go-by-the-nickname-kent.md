<td data-valign="top" width="580"><h1 id="why-the-heck-do-i-go-by-the-nickname-of-kent-on-the-net">Why the heck do I go by the nickname of 'Kent' on the net?</h1>
An explanation by Steven Lincoln "Kent" O. Mohundro
<hr />
<img src="../images/resmug.gif" alt="[Mug shot of Steve]" /><img src="images/careful.gif" alt="[Mug shot of Kent Shakespeare]" /><br />
<em>(See, no resemblance)</em>
<ul>
<li><p>For much of my life, I've been a fan of Superman. You know - The Last Son of Krypton, The Man of Steel, The Metropolis Marvel, The Man of Tomorrow, Kal-El, Clark Kent. In recent years, I've felt an affinity for the Clark Kent aspect of Superman's character.</p>
<p>Clark Kent is a crusading journalist, wanting to right wrongs and help the average person. Same goes for me, though the career in journalism has barely begun. Even without his powers, Clark Kent is a writer that can move people, cause people to change their ways.</p>
<p>Clark Kent is also concerned with winning the woman of his dreams - in this case Lois Lane - as himself. The human, Clark Kent. Complete with his failings and successes. Someone mortal and realistic. This is how I try to make friends. If they are unable to accept me the way I am, with my minuses (talking too much) and plusses (puns) then I'm not going to put up a front as someone I'm not.</p></li>
<li><p>In March of 1993, I began playing on a MUSH (Multi-User Shared Hallucination - an online roleplaying environment) that had the long-running DC Comics series Legion of Super-Heroes as a theme. This place was called <a href="lsh/lsh.nostalgia.html">LegionMUSH</a>. (It is now no longer in existance)</p>
<p>On the game, I decided to play the character <a href="../lsh/shakespeare.html">Kent Shakespeare</a>, a member of the older Legion team. Playing the same character through December of the same year, I became known by many of the friends I made on LegionMUSH as Kent. Of course now I've switched to Brin Londo and eventually Loomis, but some old nicknames stick.</p>
<p>On TooMUSH ][, TooMUSH ]I[, Werewolves of London MUSH, TooMUSH IV, Too V ("Just TooMUSH"), Too VI and Too VII, I picked 'Kent' (or more recently 'Richard Kent') as my @name, as most people I know on the net continue to know me as the character. And it'll probably stick for some time in the future.</p></li>
<li>And of course there's the DC Comics character Kent Nelson, aka Doctor Fate, but he has little influence over my life. I try not to trust in fate of any kind too much, though I am a closet romantic. Too many romantic comedies or something...</li>
</ul>
