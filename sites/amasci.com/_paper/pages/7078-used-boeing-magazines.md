> # Surplus *Boeing* Magazines for Sale
>
> ***Boeing Magazine* is a public relations publication of the Boeing Airplane Company.**

> *Boeing Magazine* is a monthly publication, usually containing 16 pages, describing new Boeing developments and products. These magazines could be a good match for collectors of aircraft memorabilia, especially Boeing.
