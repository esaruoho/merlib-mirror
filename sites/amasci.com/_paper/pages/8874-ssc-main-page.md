**<u><span style="font-size:16.0pt;mso-bidi-font-size:12.0pt">Cell Sentry System</span></u>**

**<span style="font-weight:normal">The Cell Sentry is a portable, un-manned, surveillance system that can be used</span>**

**<span style="font-weight:normal">to monitor remote sites.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>A variety of sensors are available for sensing activity</span>**

**<span style="font-weight:normal">including motion, metal, and seismic detectors.<span style="mso-spacerun: yes">  </span>It uses the public telephone system</span>**

**<span style="font-weight:normal">(either cellular or POTS) to relay sensor activity to a central computer which</span>**

**<span style="font-weight:normal">in-turn sends messages to the end user(s) via emails or pages.</span>**
