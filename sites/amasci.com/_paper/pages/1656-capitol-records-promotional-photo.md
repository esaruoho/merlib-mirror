<td data-valign="TOP"><p><strong>This is a 1966 Capitol Records promotional photo for the album "Yesterday and Today" taken after the "Butcher" cover recall. Previously, the only known promotional items from this album had been the two different Capitol promotional posters.</strong></p>
<p><strong>It was recently obtained from a person that worked in radio in 1965 in England. This person worked for several stations in England, and went on to work for many stations in the United States and Australia, and this item among many others were received as a result of her work as a rock journalist (columns and feature articles for newspapers). This person also worked for a major London newspaper group for years, covering concerts and interviewing bands.</strong></p>
