<td><strong>E</strong>ven with much of the talent and crew’s time donated, it is expected that this production will cost at least $50,000.
<p>A grant from the <strong>Arch and Bruce Brown Foundation</strong> was received on November 10, 2001. It will be applied toward producing <strong>Gilgamesh &amp; Enkidu</strong>. There is still a long way to go to bring this video project to financial life.</p>
<p>There are two main methods by which you can help to see this project come to fruition.</p>
<p><strong>Donation:</strong></p>
<p>All donations of $200 or more receive a listing in the film credits, as well as a choice of a copy of the finished video on VHS or on DVD.</p>
<p>Those in the Seattle area will be able to donate their services in the making of this video. Please contact us and let us know what you can offer.</p>
<p><strong>Investment:</strong></p>
<p>A minimum investment of $10,000 is required. When the project has paid back the cost of production and distribution, investors will earn a percentage of the profit based on their share of investment. Investors will receive a listing in the film credits, as well as a choice of a copy of the finished video on VHS or on DVD. A business plan will be sent to those making serious inquiries.</p>
<p><br />
In all financial and production relationships, Demian and Sweet Corn Productions retain ownership of the intellectual properties and retain creative control. That is to say, we have “final cut.”</p></td>
