<h5 id="section"> </h5>
<div class="articletitle">
"Le Voyage" and "Reveiller": Two Natural Fragrances From JoAnne Bassett That You Can't Miss!
</div>
<div class="articletext">
<p>I recently had the chance to sample two exquisite 100% natural eau de toilettes from <a href="http://www.aromaworld.com/">Bassett Aromatherapy</a>, blended drop-by-drop by aromatherapist JoAnne Bassett.<br />
These fragrances, Le Voyage and Reveiller TM are some of the most exciting and truly original all-natural fragrances I have used in my ten years of experience as an aromatherapist and natural products formulator. I reach for these scents several times a day, and look forward to wearing them, layering them and dousing myself with their fresh, totally original, healthily natural and pure plant essences.<br />
Most of all, they do not aggravate my allergies, and I get constant compliments. You know, "What is that you are wearing? I must get some for myself!". <img src="images/voyagespray.jpg" data-align="right" width="126" height="279" /><img src="images/voyagestopper.jpg" data-align="right" width="144" height="189" /><br />
Now you can. I guarantee, that you won't be disappointed.</p>
<p> </p>
<p>Here is an overview of the two fragrances, available in a jojoba oil based parfum, or a grape alcohol based eau de toilette. Both can be purchased <a href="http://www.aromaworld.com/parfum.htm">online</a> from Bassett Aromatherapy: The eau de toilette is available in a 1 oz. glass bottle with mister for $60, and the concentrated eau de parfum in jojoba oil base comes in an 8 ml French glass decanter for $120. Sample sizes are available of each fragrance, so you can try them out with a small investment.</p>
<p><strong>Reveiller TM:</strong></p>
<p>Reveiller is French "to awaken". A poetic medley of intoxicating wood notes with herbal nuances and citrus and floral topnotes, it is grounding, balancing, and pleasant yet sensuous. Subtle yet playful. The complex essential oils, some chosen from rare and sacred plants of India, are married for a lengthy period prior to bottling in the exquisite French parfum bottles. Created drop by drop with intention and lovingly blended by hand to ensure the highest quality natural eau de toilette rich in both character and sophistication. Reveiller eau de toilette is created to be enjoyed by women and men alike and may even be worn in layered combination with Le Voyage. It may lend the power to awaken. Open your heart and awaken to the potential.</p>
<p><strong>Le Voyage :</strong></p>
<p>Le Voyage is an unparalleled eau de toilette. Each radiant bottle is lovingly blended from rare and precious essential oils to form a harmonious potion. The superior combination of exotic flowers and sacred oils creates a romantic bouquet comparable in content only to the classic Chanel No 5. Jasmine, rose, sandalwood and other exotic and unique oils are commingled to compose a symphony of melodious notes, a concerto never before heard. The sensuous fusion has a mystical quality with the potential to transform its wearer as the essence of the eau de toilette is emanated from the body. This haunting fragrance embodies mysteries of the universe that will reveal its secrets and wisdom to you. Opening this luscious French parfum decanter will invoke in you a new sense of the divine. We invite you to begin the journey.</p>
<hr />
<p><strong>Q &amp; A With Natural Perfumer JoAnne Bassett:</strong></p>
<p><strong>KLB: When did you first start making natural perfumes?</strong></p>
<p><strong>JB:</strong> I first began making natural perfumes in 1994. As an aromatherapist for my company Bassett Aromatherapy I would be asked to do custom blending and private label.<br />
This would result in my creating new synergies with essential oils that would be used in a line of products for a company. I would create a "feeling" or "ambiance" for a store or spa with a synergy used in my French professional electric diffuser placed within their space.</p>
<p>Periodically the owner would ask if I could make her a "custom" parfum. This would be a time consuming process often taking 4 hours. We would have a consultation, introduction to the essential oils smelling the various groups of fruits, florals, woods, to see what her preferences are. There would be 3 to 5 initial scents created and then we would meet to see what she liked. I would then "tweak" the base and and let it sit a month to "marry". We would then get together to make any adjustments.</p>
<p>This was a fun and creative thing to do but my business was growing and I wasn't able to do many of these blending sessions.</p>
<p><strong>KLB: Tell me more about what your inspiration was behind your fragrances, and some of the ingredients they contain.</strong></p>
<p><strong>JB:</strong> I had a series of dreams over the period of one week. During the night I would wake up and write down a thought or a few words. A few hours later I would wake up and be compelled to write a few more words. The words were in French. The name "Le Voyage parfum naturelles " came first. Le Voyage means "the journey" in French and parfum naturelles means natural perfume.</p>
<p>In subsequent dreams I received the name "Reveiller" TM which means awaken. There are more names to be revealed when I create the others. It was very magical and special. This would be my new line. JoAnne Bassett Inc. was born. All natural parfum made the original way. No synthetic fragrance oils and blended by hand drop by drop. At the same time I had been discovering new essential oils from rare and sacred plants from India and from other exotic lands. So this became "my journey" with blending these precious oils. Many of the plants and flowers are harvested by hand from pristine surroundings and distilled by hand.</p>
<p>Le Voyage was the first parfum I created. Everyone who tried it loved it. The comments were, "it is beautiful, you are really on to something", and this makes me want to go deeper into it; I want to journey with it. The parfum was already named Le Voyage so this comment reaffirmed my belief that I had done the right thing in creating this parfum. It was perfect. I hadn't told anyone the name of the parfum! It is a fruity, floral that contains jasmine, rose, sandalwood, and other exclusive and costly essences.</p>
<p>Reveiller TM is an herbal and woody fragrance with floral and citrus topnotes. It also contains concentrated rare essences that are hydrodistilled in glass to preserve the integrity of the plants and flowers. These superior essential oils give the parfum the special qualities that you cannot find in synthetic fragrances.</p>
<p><strong>KLB: How expensive are some of these natural ingredients?</strong></p>
<p><strong>JB:</strong> Some of the natural ingredients cost $1,200.00 an ounce! These concentrated essential oils and absolutes are magnificent and very powerful. The old French perfum houses started using the synthetic fragrance oils to bring down their ingredient costs and raise their profits.</p>
<p>The higher end perfumes such as Joy claim they contain jasmine or rose are using less than 1% of the essential oils and absolutes. The total essential oil content in perfumes is no more than 1% to 2%. This information is taken from articles written regarding the perfume industry.</p>
<p><strong>KLB: How does your perfume alcohol base differ from what one might find in a department store?</strong></p>
<p><strong>JB:</strong> Our eau de toilettes contain natural grape alcohol which has a soft and fruity smell. The department store eau de toilettes contain SDA - specially denatured alcohol which contain chemical additives to make them undrinkable. They are very harsh smelling and drying to the skin.</p>
<p><strong>KLB:</strong> These scents must be great for those with allergies. What has your experience been?</p>
<p><strong>JB:</strong> People have told me they can't normally wear fragrance but they are able to wear Le Voyage and Reveiller TM. They are great for people with allergies. Most allergies are a result of the synthetic chemicals contained in the fragrances. A male friend of mine that has serious allergies wears the Reveiller TM everyday and loves it.</p>
