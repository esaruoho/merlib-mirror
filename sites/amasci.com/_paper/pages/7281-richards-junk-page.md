#### TSAN

The Sysop Association Network, or TSAN, was the pirate equivalent of FidoNet. They lasted from 1984 to 1992 and beyond. Here's an [ANSI ad for them.](tsan.ans) There are brief mentions in [Phrack](http://phrack.com/) issues 33 and 37, and in [this paper.](http://www-swiss.ai.mit.edu/6805/student-papers/fall95-papers/lin-pirate.html) Otherwise you will just find it in acronym lists, and in the occasional ansi on [defacto2.net.](http://defacto2.net)\

#### The International Information Academy

Vladimir Sachkov's book \`\`Probabilistic Methods in Combinatorial Analysis'' mentions on the title page that he is an academician in the International Information Academy. It is a theoretical computer science group for information security. There is no home page for this organisation yet they have [about 5000 people!](http://carlisle-www.army.mil/usawc/Parameters/96winter/thomas.htm) [Here is the Moldovan branch](http://www.iia.md) but their link to RIIA - the Russian branch - is a 404! (September 23, 2000) If you're looking for them you're better off searching for "International Informatization Academy". Link added - [(November 14 2001)](http://www.iiaun.ru/) thanks to Margo Cheney. [Canada IIA](http://www.iia-canada.org) is here.\

#### Simon Barere

A really good pianist. Unfortunately, according to the back of the Pearl CD, \`\`his shy and retiring nature rather obscured, and continues to obscure, a reputation which should be much higher than it is''. Check out [Appian Recordings](http://www.aprrecordings.co.uk), [Dejanews](http://www.deja.com/usenet), and [Everything2](http://everything2.com) for more information.\

#### Tetris

Tetris existed in Russia long before it was being sold in the West - in 1985 in fact. I only have two copies of this - one from 1986 from [the Bosnian abandonware site](http://www.bhlegend.com) and one from 1988 from co-author [Vadim Gerasimov's site](http://vadim.www.media.mit.edu/). They are obviously both written in Turbo Pascal, yet the source has not been made available. If anyone has a 1985 version please contact me.\

#### Sargon III

One of the last brute-force chess programs for the PC, probably. Written by Dan and Kathe Spracklen, IBM adaptation by Kevin E. Leavelle in 1983. Read some old unprotection [advice](sargon3.unp) for hard disk owners, which is completely irrelevant unless you own the original, or [download the game.](sargon3.zip) Estimated rating [1859](http://groups.google.com/groups?selm=2k253t%24non%40ratatosk.uninett.no) on a 386-25.\

#### Centaur

Centaur is a chess program from Russia which has a knowledge-based search and competed in several World Microcomputer Chess Championships. Apparently it WON a Soviet Microcomputer Chess Championship around the early '90s, which shows you how bad the competition was! Eugene Nalimov, a computer chess guru who now works at Microsoft, had a comment to make on it on the [ICDchess](http://www.icdchess.com/) discussion board [here](55859.TXT). There is also a demo version which you can probably find by searching [Channel 1 BBS](http://www.filelibrary.com/) for either Centaur or Russoft. And this demo version does not work.\
