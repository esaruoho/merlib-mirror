To test your glaucoma.  \

<div align="left">

You can tell where your blind spots are and how they progress. Turn on a TV to a blank screen the bigger the screen the better.  Pick a channel with no program.  There MUST be snow in the picture.  Get about 3 or  4 feet from the screen and close one eye.  Look straight at the middle of the screen. Your peripheral vision will show the blind  spots caused by your glaucoma.  The further away you are the light is weaker and the blind area will be larger than up close to the screen.  Up close, to the screen, the light is brighter.  This may show you where you will be developing blindness that is not noticeable yet under normal light.\
\
