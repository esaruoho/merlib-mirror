<span id="Preamble"></span>**Preamble**

Theft is a bad thing, the Spammers Ways Must Stop.  !  Know a spammer? Halt them with legal warnings.  Cause them economic pain and grief, burn them with the law, ruin them, they must suffer. Close their accounts, ruin their business. Sue them. Take them to court.  Never ever purchase any product or service from them. Call the Attorney General's Office on them. Their ways and business must stop. They are simply thieves! They have been stealing from us long enough.

**Premise**

It is about time that legal action is being taken against these people who are continuing to send out spam (unsolicited advertisement e-mail.) It is plain and simple a form of theft when they spam people. Reasons are as follows. I/We pay any where from \$10.00 to \$30.00 a month for access to the net, then another ~\$20.00+ a month for phone service (plus long distance for some, or gods forbid cellular,) and the money invested in our equipment. Not to forget the time it takes to download and sort the spam from the real mail.

This is not like postal junk mail where the mailer pays a fee for each message for delivery. This is not like banner ads on web pages that help pay for content. This not like commercials on TV, radio, or magazines and newspapers that help pay for content. Furthermore the increase of junk e-mail is driving up the costs of using ISPs as they have to upgrade some services to handle the load in stead of upgrading other services that may have been wanted by the customers. I have no problem with advertisements on web pages and other media when they pay for the content I am viewing, much of that content may never be there without it. But when then invade my personal communication methods I get a little angry. I also do not tolerate telephone telemarketers, they are just as bad but at least they will honor do not contact requests.

Again. it is a form of theft. It is theft when the relay via third party systems without their permission or knowledge (trespass and use of another's equipment.) It is theft then the spam in violation of the terms of service of their provider, (violation of a contract.) Spamming is theft of time when we have to download their message and separate it from other real e-mail especially then they use deceptive subjects lines (My time has value to me.) It is theft when they cause services to overload and crash, (denial of services.) It is theft when the network is bogging down and we are trying to get data we paid for (again denial of service.) This causes them to raise their prices, (to recover lost costs.) In effect all Unsolicited Advertisement (Junk) E-Mail is sent POSTAGE DUE.

Internet commerce is NOT Bad; it is in fact generally good. I/we do not oppose people trying to promote ideas, services and products via the Internet, while trying to do something about mass unsolicited e-mail advertisements. I/we are NOT opposed to people trying to make money via the Internet nor it's commercialization in the appropriate contexts. Private e-mail boxes are not those places. Spamming is intolerable because unethical business practices and exploitation of other peoples' private systems and of the Internet it is costing all of us time and money. Spammers give legitimate businesses trying to use the Internet for business a bad name, crashing computer systems everywhere, ruining cyber experiences to new and old user alike while filling out private e-mail boxes with tripe at a cost to us.

<span id="explain"></span>\
The below was a note I wrote to someone who asked me to explain my position and how I could apply the current law and the fact that it is not explicit. They were unhappy as I was for having to deal with all the crap the people are sending them.

Dear Mr. XXXXXXX

In reply to your question, I will explain to my best understanding of the problems at hand. Be sure that the current Federal rules dose not explicitly cover E-mail like it dose Fax. However; and it is a very big "however," is that the spirit and intent of the law is based on much of what is outlined below. The law is flexible and is often creatively applied where the technology, meaning or use of something has changed or evolved as is the case here.

The law does not explicitly cover E-mail under the Fax law. Again, it is a construe ration of the existing federal laws covering Fax machines. The law can apply if the intent of the original of a closely related technology can be shown that the costs are being shifted from the sender of a message to an unwilling recipient such was the case with the Fax and is now the case with E-mail. E-mail probably should be explicitly stated or defined in the present code.

The important thing to show is if ANY Costs Or Burdens Are Shifted To The Recipient. This is the key and I will repeat it.

Still, be sure to check with the State or Locality of your area as they may also have something that covers this as well.

There are those people that have taken this to small claim court have gotten different decisions in different jurisdictions. Some winning and some losing. I do not have the court cases or their name/id numbers but I've seen a few. I will be filing one my self when I get the free time.

The primary question to be answered is based on the fairness of shifting the burden of costs to the person that will receive an Unsolicited Advertisement from those who will spew their wares to others.

Some of he basis that I think applies are as follows:

- 1\. Related technology(s) and definitions.
  - 1.1 A Fax machine is a printer/scanner with a modem and a CPU connected to a phone line.
  - 1.2 A computer may have a printer and a modem and is may be connected to a phone line and can receive E-mail. Or is connected to Other network
  - 1.3 Both Fax and E-mail data are often carried on the same phone lines in many cases.
  - 1.4 Some Fax machines are not connected to the phone network but are instead connected to special dedicated Fax networks.
  - 1.5 The Internet is also a special network and much of it is carried over phone lines.
  - 1.6 There specialized Fax-mail services just like E-mail.
- 2\. Burden of costs are shifted to the recipient of the Unsolicited Advertisement E-mail from the sender.
  - 2.1 In many cases people have to pay a service provider a monthly fee to maintain an account for getting E-mail on the Internet.
  - 2.2 Many of those services charge connect rates by the hour or minute.
  - 2.3 Other providers charge the users by the message or byte for E-mail.
  - 2.4 The unwanted E-mail may add delays to the user in downloading E-mail.
  - 2.5 The unwanted E-mail may take up needed disk space that may be low.
  - 2.6 Some providers may have limits of how many messages or much may be stored at a time on the service.
  - 2.7 Often the Spams may clog or bog the mail down so that real messages may be missed or delayed. 2.8 The extra time and work taken to see if the Fax was real or junk.
- 3\. As in Sec. 2 the costs for Junk Fax was shifted to the recipient.
  - 3.1 The junk Fax can tie up the telephone line so that real Fax's could not be received or are delayed.
  - 3.2 The paper and toner used in the fax machine is wasted. Supplies that need to be replaced at the receiver's cost.
  - 3.3 The extra time to see if the Fax was real or Junk.
- 4\. The legality of the advertisements.
  - 4.1 Are the products or services being offered legitimate?
  - 4.2 Dose the person offering have a right to do business?
  - 4.3 Dose he have a business license?
  - 4.4 Is he or she a sleaze?
  - 4.5 Is the advertisement a scam or of a substandard product or service?
  - 4.6 Is what is offered ethical, safe, tested, legal or  illegal?
- 5\. Message tracking information.
  - 5.1 The fax law makes it illegal to send a fax with false information or without sender information in the header line of the fax or on the fax.
  - 5.2 The removal or spoofing of header information in E-mail makes the ad difficult to track it to the sender and is in conflict of the the above rule.
  - 5.3 When a advertiser has to fake or spoof headers to get their message sent there is something dishonest about them to begin with.

There is a lot more but I am too tired to think of anything else.

I am not a lawyer but I am following this very carefully. I am preparing to file my own case against a local (to me) E-mail spammer. The big problem is all of this is that many of the E-mail spammers are all far away and it hard to deal with legal issues over long distances. So as it is I just have to wait and deal with those within an accessible are to my self.
