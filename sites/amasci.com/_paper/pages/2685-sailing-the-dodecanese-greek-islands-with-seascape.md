# Sailing the Dodecanese Greek islands with SeaScape.

*Source page: `~dianee/destinations/greece/_notes/jul27_gr.html`*

Sailing the Dodecanese Greek islands with SeaScape.

*Jul 13-26*

This is well into "meltemi time" now - the summer winds which give us such great sailing here in the islands. They are reliably from the NW, and blow a lovely 20-25knots on average; sometimes we'll get a few days of 30+knots; sometimes a few days of calm - but good sailing is definitely guaranteed!

This is our favourite 2wk route, sailing from our home island of Leros, down to the traditional sponge-fishing island of Kalymnos, where another SeaScape tradition awaits us - our adopted "Momma"'s homemade dolmades! (stuffed grapeleaves). The route is a mixture of such little, old-fashioned villages and some of the more famous islands like Patmos and Samos... with some of our especially favourite tiny spots included, of course - like our little paradise island of Marathi, with two families, a lovely sandy beach and some of the best food around!

You can join this trip for one week or two; the midpoint for end of wk1/start of wk2 is Pythagoras's island, Samos.
