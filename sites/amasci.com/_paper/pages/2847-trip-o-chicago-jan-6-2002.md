# Trip 'o' chicago - Jan 6, 2002

> ### Trip 'o' Chicago - Jan 6, 2002
>
> First of all, \*Happy New Year\* :-) This adventure stars a multitude of people! The one thing I can be really thankful for, is having a group of guys around to hang out with! I think almost 1/2 the fun of going somewhere, doing something, is the company...
>
> Anyways, a group of us guys decided to head down to chicago for 'bear bar night'. We attemtped to leave @ 9, but being gay, doesn't usually mean on-time. So around 10, we rolled out of Madison, all packed into Tony's new minivan. We stopped at some place for a really good breakfast. Yeah, someplace, cause the name escapes me at the moment. All together, there were 7 of us : Tony, Neal, Selim, Walter, Kent, John and myself -- and Casey would be meeting us there. We had a pretty uneventful trip, got to the 'travel lodge' and got checked in. The rooms where, well, cheap. haha..the beds , obviously had seen some action...in some cases, the boxspring was reduced to about 3 inches high....hehehehe.
>
> Anyways, we napped a bit , and then went to ...\*drumroll\*...Malehide -- its a leather store..but that doesn't even begin to describe what the sold there. Leather was foremost...outfront, and in your face. They custom fit everything and I mean \*everything\*..which explains the rather steep prices. They had a plethora of other ummmmm....things for sale some of which I had NO idea what they did..to the great delight of the attendant. We spent almost an hour looking at stuff..and some made a few purchases.
>
> Next on our stop, was , food. We decided on Turkish food, since we had Selim and Walter with us to help pick out the good things. My food was good, not exaclty what I was expecting, but good. And, I had turkish coffee to finish it up...I asked for it 'sweet'...it was served in a small,expresso like cup. It was strong and it was sweet, AND it was as thick as motoroil...wow....it was good tho...just a little gritty at the end :-) I'd drink it again. :-)
>
> We then went back to the hotel to nap, and primp...haha. We arrived at the bar around 10:30 or so...and it was already packed! Wow! I saw a bunch of guys from bearcamp that I knew, some guys from MN I knew, and a bunch 'o guys from chicago I had met at our Madison area 'Octobear fest'. Had a great time people watching, talkin with friends, and just hanging out with 200+ other 'beary' guys. We left around 2:30 am, which was enough smoke and the like for me :) Back to the hotel rooms and to bed with us!
>
> A lazy sunday am, getting up around 11am in our room...hehehe. On the way back , we stopped at the huge furniture store, Ikea...cool! 3 stories of neat, trendy, and sometimes expensive stuff...www.ikea.com. So brings to the end another adventure. It was alot of sex, fun, food and drugs. wait....no it wasn't! It was alot of fun tho -- thanks guys for making it fun! Lookin forward to next time!
