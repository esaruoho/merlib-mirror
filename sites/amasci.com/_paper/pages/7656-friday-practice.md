# <img src="images/wingtips.gif" width="96" height="64" alt="Wingtips" />Lindy Hop Practice

"...Come join us practicing your Lindy Hop dance skills every Friday evening at the Fusion Dance Company (formerly known as "Dance On Capitol Hill") studio at 340 15th Ave. East in Seattle. We start around 8:00 p.m., usually with a short 20-30 minute lesson occurring sometime during the evening.  Check the [upcoming schedule](pracschd.htm) for details and FNP cancellations. Open dancing to taped music continues until 10:00 p.m.

We also have a [map](prac_map.htm) to help you find your way....

This event is specifically intended for [members](member.htm) of the Savoy Swing Club and their guests, but you are welcome to try it out once before joining. "Members" include both Limited Members (those who are currently taking classes) as well as Annual Members who have paid their yearly dues...."
