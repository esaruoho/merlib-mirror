## Micro Kit

![](kit/thumb.jpg)\

This kit is 2.5" x 1.5" x 0.75" and weighs about 1.5 ounces.

I was inspired by [Doug Ritter's Equipped To Survive](http://www.equipped.org) site and PSK, but wanted something smaller, lighter, and more suited to my urban life.

The kit contains:

1.  whistle
2.  compass
3.  flashlight
4.  pencil
5.  two needles
6.  thread
7.  wire tie
8.  spark lite and two tinder wads
9.  safety pin
