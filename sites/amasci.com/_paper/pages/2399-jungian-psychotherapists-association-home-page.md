<blockquote>
<p>The Jungian Psychotherapists Association is a mutually supportive organization of psychotherapists actively engaged in the study and application of Analytical Psychology. The central purpose of the JPA is to further the professional growth, development, and education of its members. To support this aim, the JPA provides professional educational activities with a clinical focus, including: seminars, courses, consultations and study groups. It also provides a forum for informal communication and support among its members.</p>
