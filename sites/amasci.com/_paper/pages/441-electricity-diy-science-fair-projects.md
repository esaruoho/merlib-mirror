<td data-summary="links to all articles" width="800"><h1 id="electricity-electronic-science-fair-projects">ELECTRICITY/ ELECTRONIC SCIENCE FAIR PROJECTS</h1>
<h3 id="william-j.-beaty-electrical-engineer-u.-of-washington"><a href="http://amasci.com/me.html">William J. Beaty</a><br />
Electrical Engineer, U. of Washington</h3>
<br />
&#10;<blockquote>
<hr />
<p>Jump up to <a href="http://amasci.com/ele-edu.html">All Electricity Articles</a></p>
<hr />
<p><br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/amateur/coilgen.html">Ultra-simple Electric Generator</a><br />
Spin the magnet, light a bulb. The complement to the "Beakman Motor". Challenge: make the one run the other.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/emotor.html">ELECTROSTATIC MOTOR made from plastic pop bottles.</a><br />
Who says "static electricity" must remain static, or that it's useless and feeble?<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/chargdet.html">Ridiculously sensitive charge detector</a><br />
Build this simple circuit and sense "static" on your body. Detect miniscule amounts of electric charge at vast distances. Befuddle onlookers with the the "mysterious voice commands" trick<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/tesla/microtc.html">Five Dollar Tesla Coil</a>, makes fluorescent tubes glow, creates hot sparks. An advanced project for older kids.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/neodemo.html">Projects with Supermagnets</a><br />
Lots of magnetic and electromagnetism demonstrations with Neodymium magnets<br />
</p>
<p>Make some "static electricity" using electronics: <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/negion.html">Negative ionizers</a> make great electrostatic power supplies. Power the <a href="%0A/emotor/emotor.html">bottle motor</a>.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/static/electrostatic2.html">Static Electricity Projects</a>, all the High Voltage, low-current stuff in one place. Also try <a href="http://amasci.com/static/electrostatic1.html">Links to other electrostatics projects</a><br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/tesla/heli1.html">Plasma Globe</a> without a vacuum chamber. Create crawling plasma streamers like 'Eye of the Storm͐,' in a plastic bag of Argon gas. You'll first need to build a <a href="http://amasci.com/tesla/plasplan.html">Solid State Tesla Coil</a><br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/vdg.html">VandeGraaff Generators</a><br />
Instructions for having fun with 500,000 volts, including <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/statgen.html">finding a cheap one</a>, <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/vdgplan.html">plans sources</a>, <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/vdgdesc.html">FAQ</a>, <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/vdgbug.html">debug</a>...<br />
</p>
<p>For the really big sparks, you're going to need a <a href="http://amasci.com/tesla/tesla.html">Tesla Coil</a><br />
</p>
<p>High voltage plasmas: <a href="http://amasci.com/weird/microwave/voltage2.html#candl">Ball Lightning</a> in your microwave oven<br />
Demonstrate the 'Maser Theory' of Ball Lightning formation<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/electoph.html">ELECTROSTATIC GENERATOR: The Electrophorus</a><br />
A simple device which can be used to power the pop-bottle motor.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/maglev/maglev.html">MAGLEV CRADLE</a><br />
Lifts a bar magnet from below. Use this to perpetrate a Room Temperature Superconductor hoax?<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/kelvin.html">ELECTROSTATIC GENERATOR</a>, Kelvin's Thunderstorm"<br />
An electrostatic generator based on dribbling water. Also see the <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/ikelv.html">INLINE VERSION</a><br />
</p>
<p>Your own <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/nanoamp.html">picoammeter</a> . If you already have a digital voltmeter, use it in heretical fashion: as a nano-amp meter, even measure picoamps.<br />
</p>
<p>Add an <a href="http://amasci.com/maglev/linmot.html">electronic linear motor</a> and a magnet track to your simple <a href="http://amasci.com/maglev/train.html">maglev train</a>. Or even use it to drive a wheeled cart along the track.<br />
</p>
<p>You can wimp out entirely, and buy a commercial <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/statgen.html">Van de Graaff Electrostatic Generator Kit</a>. But why not build your own <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/vdg.html#plns">from scratch?</a><br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/stathint.html">Hints for electrostatic device construction</a><br />
Building 500,000 volt devices in your basement? Avoid these pitfalls.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/esloud/eslhwto.html">Electrostatic Loudspeakers</a><br />
by Mark Rehorst. No magnets, no magnetism, they use voltage-force. Almost like wiggling the air itself.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/amateur/gold.html">Acid/Base Goldenrod secret</a><br />
Use a battery to draw red lines on wet yellow paper<br />
</p>
<p>"Static electricity" experiments with a roll of tape: <a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/sticky.html">Sticky Electrostatics</a><br />
</p>
<p>Levitate a magnet dangerously, with <a href="http://amasci.com/maglev/magroll.html">High speed rotating copper cylinders</a>.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/duluc.txt">The Duluc Drypile</a><br />
A "battery" with so many cells that Electrostatic effects appear.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/electrom/statbotl.html">3D E-field</a> viewing bottle<br />
Actually SEE invisible electrostatic fields and magnetic fields by harnessing the amazing powers of Baby Oil.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/amateur/capexpt.html">Dangerous experiments with a big capacitor bank</a><br />
Nobody died from simply reading this. But stay away from actually performing any of these demos.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/amateur/clouds.html">High Powered Ultrasound</a> Shatters water into a fine mist. Collect some in a bowl. Probe it with high voltage, see what happens.<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/emotor/humid.html">Solving Humidity Problems</a><br />
Egor! Activate the Dehumidifier Array!<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/electrom/electrom.html">ARRAY ELECTROMETER</a><br />
Build this and SEE electrostatic fields!<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/viselect/viselect.html">VISIBLE CURRENT</a><br />
Build this moving-LEDs device which makes the flowing charges visible in wires. Wouldn't "electricity" be easier to understand if you could SEE it moving?<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/exhibits/hiamp.txt">Incredibly easy way to make a 100-amp cable</a>. Around the world, the physics teachers look at this and say "Doh!"<br />
</p>
<p><a href="http://amasci.com/exhibits/statdrum.html">Rotating disk device makes e-fields visible</a><br />
Idea for dangerous whirling mechanisms which reveal invisible field patterns.</p>
