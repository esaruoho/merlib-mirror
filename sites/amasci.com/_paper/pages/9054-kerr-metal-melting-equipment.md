<p>Electro-Melt Furnaces</p>
<p>The Kerr Electro-Melts are a line of compact electric furnaces for melting metals. They are designed to be hand held for easy pouring of the metal directly from the furnace. All the Electro-Melt furnaces are supplied with a graphite crucible which provides a reducing atmosphere during melting, making them ideal for use in casting, reclaiming, alloying and refining metals. They have a built-in pyrometer and are equipped with a safety switch which deactivates power to the furnace when the lid is opened. 115 volt. 220 volt models available on special order.</p>
<strong></strong>
<p>Manual Electro-Melt</p>
<p>The Manual Electro-Melt has stepless input control that regulates the furnace temperature by using a percentage of time on/time off principle. The dial plate is calibrated in numbers representing the percentage of time power is directed to the heating element. This method gives excellent temperature control. Maximum operable temperature is 2000'F.</p></td>
