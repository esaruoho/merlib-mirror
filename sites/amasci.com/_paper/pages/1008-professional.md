# Professional

*Source page: `~ables/professional.html`*

Professional

<img src="img/wtcribbon.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" /> I've been crazy about computing since I played the old STARTREK game on a CDC 6600 in high school and I've been working in the computing industry in one way or another since my freshman year in college. I've done operations, development, support, consulting, writing, and training (everything except sales and marketing!).

I started my career in computing at the [UT Computation Center](http://wwwhost.cc.utexas.edu/cc/index.html) (where I worked during much of the time I was in school). I learned more about Unix, the Internet before it was \`\`in,'' and computing there than in most of my classes.\

[<img src="img/mcclogo.gif" data-align="left" data-border="0" alt="MCC" />](http://www.mcc.com/) [<img src="img/hallogo.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="HaL" />](http://www.halsoft.com/) I've worked at Cyb Systems (which used to make 68000-based Unix machines), [Microelectronics and Computer Technology Corporation (MCC),](http://www.mcc.com/) and [HaL Computer Systems.](http://www.halsoft.com/) I've also done consulting for several companies both in Austin and California.\

[<img src="img/book1_sm.jpg" data-align="left" data-border="0" alt="book cover" />](img/book1_lg.jpg) [<img src="img/book2_sm.gif" data-align="right" data-border="0" alt="book cover" />](img/book2_lg.gif) While doing my consulting work, I also contributed several [articles](articles.html) to technical journals and completed a book entitled [***The Keys To Successful Unix System Management***](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/ISBN%3D0139483810/gatewaytoinformaA/8120-5413120-289983) which was published by [Prentice-Hall](http://www.prenhall.com) in 1994. A German edition was published 1997.

I have also recently completed a new edition of another UNIX book by another author, Graham Glass. [***UNIX For Programmers And Users*** is also available from Prentice-Hall.](http://www.amazon.com/exec/obidos/tg/detail/-/0136816851/qid=1043353440/sr=1-1/ref=sr_1_1/104-1704849-8630318?v=glance&s=books)\

My professional interests include systems programming and administration as well as development and refinement of networking and security tools and procedures.
