**PART FOUR**

If you have not yet completed [Part Three](m5part3.htm), please finish before continuing with Part Four.

**1.** Arrange 12 triangles of **fabric B** and 12 triangles of **fabric W** in a line as shown in Figure 17.

![](m5fig17.jpg)

Figure 17

**2.** Sew together to ceate a strip of alternating **fabric B** and **fabric W** triangles (Figure 18). With the **fabric B** triangles on top, each strip should begin with a **fabric B** triangle and end with a **fabric W** triangle.

![](m5fig18.jpg)

Figure 18

**3.** Repeat steps 1 and 2, making a total of 4(36) strips.
