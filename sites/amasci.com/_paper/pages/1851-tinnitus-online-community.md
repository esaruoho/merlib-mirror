# Tinnitus Online Support

\

Some of the best help comes from other people who've been there.\
These pages are an attempt to aid online communication between people with tinnitus. They're oriented to English-speakers, though I have listed some [German-](moretinn.html#German) and [French-](moretinn.html#French) language tinnitus resources.

These pages are dedicated to the memory of Susan Moreland.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Chats

Talk (type) to each other in real-time. Most chats require a java-enabled web browser, and in the past that has prevented WebTV and AOL users from participating. However both these groups can join IRC chats. If you don't know what IRC is don't worry, help is available.\
\
Chat times are given in USA Eastern time. USA time zone offsets from Eastern:\
Pacific is 3 hours behind, Mountain is 2 hours behind, Central is 1 hour behind.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

\#tinnitus chat [(IRC NewNet) and (Web NewNet)](tchat.html)

These web pages were created in response to the help given me by this group, which meets on the [NewNet](tchat.html#NewNet) IRC network channel \#tinnitus. The scheduled English chats are on **Fridays** starting about 9:30 pm Eastern time. German chats at times convenient for Europeans were added in January 1999. Other pages on this site give details such as the [chat schedule](tchat.html#chatsched), [help with IRC](irchelp.html), and other information useful to the chat group.

[Yahoo Tinnitus Support Chat (Web)](tyahoochat.html)

Requires java-enabled web browser. Started January 1999 by participants in one of the Tinnitus Message boards. Chats **Wednesdays** at 10 pm Eastern time.

<a href="http://www.representatives.com/tinnitus.html" target="_top">The Tinnitus Support Chat Room (Web)</a>\

Requires java-enabled web browser. **Mondays**, 9-9:30 pm Eastern time.

[America Online Tinnitus Chat](aol://2719:67-2-Tinnitus%20Help) (AOL only)

**Mondays** from 8:30-9:00 pm, Eastern time. You cannot reach this chat unless you have an AOL account and are running AOL software.

[America Online Chat, Meniere's](aol://2719:2-2-menieres) (AOL only)

For people with or interested in Meniere's disease. Chats start **Sundays** at 9 pm Eastern time. You cannot reach this chat unless you have an AOL account and are running AOL software.\
\

Email Lists

To use one of these lists, you must subscribe by sending email in a special format to the list's request address. Subscription is free. After subscribing, you'll get email from other list subscribers and any email you send to the mailing list will be seen and can be responded to by anyone who subscribes to the list.\
You'll usually want to save the subscription acknowledgement email you receive, as it will probably contain:

1.  the email address you'll use to send mail to list subscribers. **This is almost always NOT the address you used to subscribe.**
2.  instructions for unsubscribing, if you decide you no longer want to receive email from the list.

Tinnitus Mailing List

To [subscribe](mailto:requests@smtp.cochlea.com?subject=subscribe%20tinnitus), mail *requests@smtp.cochlea.com* and put **subscribe tinnitus** as the ***subject*** of the message. No message body is needed. You will receive a reply to which you must respond in order to confirm your subscription.

DizziNews Mailing List

To [subscribe](mailto:majordomo@samurai.com?subject=subscribe%20dizzinews&body=SUBSCRIBE%20DIZZINEWS), mail *majordomo@samurai.com* and put SUBSCRIBE DIZZINEWS in the ***body*** of the message.

Hyperacusis Mailing List

To [subscribe](mailto:majordomo@utdallas.edu?subject=subscribe&body=subscribe%20hyperacusis), mail *majordomo@utdallas.edu* and put **subscribe hyperacusis** in the ***body*** of the message.\
\

Message Boards

Read and write messages on an Internet "bulletin board". Most of these are used with a web browser. Usenet Newsgroups can be used with a newsreader program or a web browser.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

<a href="http://pub63.ezboard.com/btinnitussupport92262" target="_top">Tinnitus Support Messages Board</a>

<a href="http://venus.beseen.com/boardroom/c/33308/" target="_top">Tinnitus Message Board</a>

<a href="http://www.cochlea.com/webx" target="_top">Meniere's Message Board</a>

for people with Meniere's disease, which includes tinnitus in its symptoms.

[Usenet newsgroup: alt.support.tinnitus](news:alt.support.tinnitus)

Usenet newsgroups are the original/largest message boards. You use them with a newsreader program, activated by the link above -- or you can read them with your web browser (see the "archives" entries below.)   There is also a [German newsgroup.](moretinn.html)\
\
**WARNING:** *alt.support.tinnitus is an uncensored, unmoderated newsgroup. There is help there, and by far the largest audience, but you have to wade through some appalling nastiness to find it, at a level unusual even for newgroups and worse than other message boards. If some saint would like to start a moderated tinnitus newsgroup and serve as impartial moderator, new tinnitus sufferers will be in their debt.*

<a href="http://groups.google.com/googlegroups/deja_announcement.html" target="_top">Newsgroup archives, Web</a>

Google web archive of Usenet newsgroups, which they call "discussion forums". Search on "tinnitus".

<a href="http://groups.google.com/advanced_group_search" target="_top">Advanced Newsgroup search, Web</a>

Google power search: can search the "forum" of alt.support.tinnitus only, by dates or other criteria
