**Links to defenders of free enterprise**

**<a href="http://www.activistcash.com" style="text-decoration: none">ActivistCash.com</a> Project of the Center for Consumer Freedom, tracks money of anti-free enterprisers. Contact: David Martosko.**

**Contact the <a href="http://www.allianceforamerica.org" style="text-decoration: none">Alliance for America</a>, the largest umbrella group in the wise use movement. President: Rose Comstock-Correia**

## <a href="http://www.amfor.org" style="text-decoration: none">American Forests</a> (formerly the American Forestry Association) American Forests is the oldest conservation organization in the United States today. For more than 120 years--since 1875--American Forests has worked to ensure a sustained future for our nation's forests.

**<a href="http://www.afandpa.org" style="text-decoration: none">American Forest &amp; Paper Association</a> National trade association of the forest, paper, and wood products industry.**

**<a href="http://www.landrights.org" style="text-decoration: none">The American Land Rights Association</a> Nonprofit organization defending property rights across America. Executive Director: Charles S. Cushman.**

**<a href="http://www.sharetrails.org" style="text-decoration: none">The Blue Ribbon Coalition</a> Nonprofit organization defending motorized recreation and public access to government lands across America. Executive Director: Clark Collins.**

**<a href="http://www.foresthealth.org" style="text-decoration: none">California Forestry Association</a> Statewide forest industry trade association. President: David A. Bischel.**

**<a href="http://www.cato.org" style="text-decoration: none">Cato Institute</a> Perhaps the nation's premier libertarian organization. Edward H. Crane, President.**

**<a href="http://www.cei.org" style="text-decoration: none">Competitive Enterprise Institute</a>** **Washington, D.C.-based think tank devoted to free markets and limited government. Founder and President: Fred Smith.**

## <a href="http://www.cfact.org" style="text-decoration: none">Committee for a Constructive Tomorrow</a> (CFACT) Nonprofit organization that deals with many issues, including individual liberty, property rights and sound natural resource policy. President: David Rothbard. Executive Director: Craig Rucker.

## <a href="http://eco.freedom.org" style="text-decoration: none">Environmental Conservation Organization</a> Nonprofit organization emphasizing the international aspects of the wise use movement. Executive Director: Henry Lamb.

**<a href="http://www.ff.org" style="text-decoration: none">Frontiers of Freedom Institute</a> Non-profit, non-partisan research and educational foundation dedicated to defending constitutional rights and promoting a limited government agenda. Founder: Senator Malcolm Wallop, retired. Executive Director: George Landrith.**

**<a href="http://www.dlc.fi/~haf" style="text-decoration: none">Finnish Heidelberg Association</a>  Believes in nature conservation, abilities of man, blessings of scientific research, economic growth and increasing standard of living. Mostly in Finnish, some English.**

**<a href="http://www.heartland.org" style="text-decoration: none">The Heartland Institute</a> Chicago-based non-profit, nonpartisan center for public policy research. Executive Director: Joe Bast.**

**<a href="http://www.heritage.org" style="text-decoration: none">Heritage Foundation</a> Think tank on conservative issues. President: Dr. Edwin J. Feulner.**

**<a href="http://www.highnorth.no" style="text-decoration: none">High North Alliance</a> Norway  --  Working for the future of coastal cultures and the sustained use of marine resources.**

**<a href="http://www.ideachannel.com" style="text-decoration: none">The Idea Channel</a> by the producers of Milton Friedman's "Free To Choose" series.**

**<a href="http://www.mises.org/" style="text-decoration: none">Ludwig von Mises Institute</a>, devoted to the Austrian School of economics and concerned with private property rights, free markets, hard money and less government. Founder and President: Llewellyn H. Rockwell, Jr.**

**<a href="http://www.mecri.org" style="text-decoration: none">Maine Conservation Rights Institute</a>, statewide non-profit organization protecting private property. Founded by the late Bob Voight.**

**<a href="http://www.mountainstateslegal.com" style="text-decoration: none">Mountain States Legal Foundation</a> Public interest law firm dedicated to individual liberty, the right to own and use property, limited government and the free enterprise system. President and Chief Legal Officer: William Perry Pendley.**

## <a href="http://www.nationalcenter.org" style="text-decoration: none">The National Center for Public Policy Research</a> Conservative/free market foundation established in 1982 and located on Capitol Hill. Executive Director: David Almasi.

**<a href="http://www.nesarc.org/" style="text-decoration: none">National Endangered Species Act Reform Coalition</a>  Information about the Endangered Species Act and why this well-intentioned law, which everyone agrees is one of the most important environmental laws ever adopted, needs to be reformed. Chairman: Senator James A. McClure, retired.**

**<a href="http://www.nwi.org" style="text-decoration: none">National Wilderness Institute</a> Nonprofit organization defending rational wilderness use. Executive Director: Rob Gordon.**

**<a href="http://www.pacificlegal.org" style="text-decoration: none">Pacific Legal Foundation</a> Public interest law firm for less government and the preservation of free enterprise, private property rights and individual liberties. President and Chief Executive Officer: Robert K. Best**

**<a href="http://www.perc.org" style="text-decoration: none">Political Economy Research Center</a> Market-oriented think tank focusing on environmental and natural resource issues. Executive Director: Terry L. Anderson.**

**<a href="http://www.PropertyRightsResearch.org" style="text-decoration: none">PropertyRightsResearch.org</a>. Encyclopedic information website covering many vital issues. Operated by Julie Smithson.**
