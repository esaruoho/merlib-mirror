<p>        Notes:  <br />
        (1) Due to Neil's injury, Kobe was handled by Meg on the first day.  Emmy also had a new<br />
             handler, Alisa.  They both did a wonderful job.<br />
        (2) Day 2, Nancy pulled quadruple duty, handling all four furry creatures.<br />
        (3) Heavy rains Saturday night made the campground very muddy.  Ferried the dogs across<br />
              the mud in the truck.<br />
        (4) Kobe had a blast visiting with his Uncle Jesse, Sis' Peaches, Granny Vanna, and Mama<br />
              Emmy.  Its great fun watching them play.<br />
        (5) Saturday evening, Kobe and I spent the night with my nephews Henry and Patrick.<br />
              Patrick is still intimidated by Kobe, Kobe is still bigger than him.  But Henry and Kobe<br />
              played all night!<br />
