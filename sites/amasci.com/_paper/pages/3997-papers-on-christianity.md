## Confessions of Faith

Confessions of faith are just that - confessions. They don't really require proof because the person is simply saying what they believe. Here you will find my confessions. Feel free to discuss them with me if you are interested.

- [A Pragmatic Confession of the Christian Faith](pragmatic-confession.html) - also available in [LaTeX](pragmatic-confession.tex), [PostScript](pragmatic-confession.ps), and [PDF](pragmatic-confession.pdf)

  This is a paper I wrote for class at [OBU](http://www.okbu.edu/), so it contains some parts that were more to fulfill the requirements of the class than something that I thought fit. I believe everything I wrote, but some parts are kind of disjointed because they were required for class.
