</p>
<p>While a lot of time is spent arguing about better highways and better mass transit, one mode of transportation usually overlooked is pedestrian traffic.</p>
<p>In some cases people travelling a short distance walk rather than ride or bike. If more money is spent on making it more convenient and safer for people to walk, more people will walk.</p>
<p>Safe and convenient pedestrian facilities are far cheaper than providing for other modes of transportation, and if more pedestrians mean fewer vehicles on the road.</p>
<p>In many cases <a href="../transportation/bikeways.html">bikeways</a> can be used also for walking, jogging, and running.</p>
<p>In areas of high pedestrian traffic, such as downtown areas and neighborhood business districts, covered walkways would encourage more people to walk in unpleasant weather.</p></td>
