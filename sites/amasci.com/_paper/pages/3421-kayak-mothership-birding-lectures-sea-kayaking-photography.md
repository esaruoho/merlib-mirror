Kayak by Mothership on the Home Shore</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><img src="hshore09a.jpg" title="Lituya Bay, Alaska" data-align="RIGHT" width="150" height="140" alt="Lituya Bay, Alaska" /> Gary will be leading two trips in 2004 out of Sitka, Southeast Alaska with the kayak mothership Home Shore. Dates are 5/24-31 and 6/2-9. Cost is $2,700 per person. Limit 6. The first trip is for kayakers, and has space available. The second trip is also for kayakers, and is full. Check the <a href="http://www.homeshore.com">Home Shore</a> website for details. Gary will also be aboard the Home Shore the week of June 11-18 with Canoe&amp;Kayak editor Ross Prather for a trip around Baranof. If your interested in joining that trip, contact the Home Shore.
<p>Read about Gary's 2003 Home Shore trip <a href="../bio/trips.htm">In The Field</a>: Highlights included Humpback Whale watching, Brown Bear viewing, a visit to the Tracy Arm tidewater glacier and Lituya Bay.<br />

<td class="TEXT2">Port Angeles Sea Kayak Symposium</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><img src="portangeles02a.jpg" title="2003 Port Angeles Sea Kayak Symposium" data-align="RIGHT" width="148" height="100" alt="2003 Port Angeles Sea Kayak Symposium" /> Gary will be at the 4th Annual <a href="http://www.raftandkayak.com/specialevents.html">Port Angeles Sea Kayak Symposium</a>, April 16-18, 2004, presenting "Photography from a Kayak" on Saturday, 4/17, Noon-1:00 and "Birding by Kayak" Sunday, 4/18, 9:00-10:00am. Cost for each is $3. Symposium admission is free, but attendee's will be asked to donate either $5 or non-perishable food items for the Port Angeles Food Bank. A fee may be charged for individual lectures and clinics. Plenty of kayaks will be available of testing.<br />
<br />
The symposium is loaded with knowledgeable, talented BCU and ACA kayak instructors: Les Uhrich, Chris Duff, Andree Hurley, Leon Somme', Shawna Franklin, Bill McKenzie, Debrah Volturno and others.<br />
