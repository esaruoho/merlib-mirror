<h3 id="todd-krissy-biernacki"><span id="TKB"></span>Todd &amp; Krissy Biernacki</h3>
Todd &amp; Krissy Biernacki currently run Blue Skies Dance in addition to teaching for Lykes to Dance &amp; the Savoy Swing Club.  They are back from a 3-year stint in Singapore where they taught and performed extensively, including a show in the Singapore Arts Festival.  Todd &amp; Krissy competed twice at the US Open Swing Dance Championships, taking 3rd in 1997 and 2nd in 1998 (Lindy Hop division).  They taught Lindy Hop workshops on 2 separate occasions in Japan, including one sponsored by Watari-San, the Dance Director of the movie <u>Shall We Dance</u>.   Recently they worked as Dance Choreographers and Coaches on the Steven King miniseries, "Rose Red". <br />

<h3 id="loviça-callisti">Loviça Callisti</h3>
Loviça discovered the joy of Lindy Hop in 1994, and has been consumed by it ever since. Her passion for the dance has taken her to camps around the world, and she has studied under international masters. She currently teaches group classes and private lessons in the Seattle area, and co-directs the premier dance ensemble, Swing This! In addition to teaching for several years, Loviça has contributed to the local dance community by serving on the board of directors for the Savoy Swing Club, helping to organize two major dance camps in the Northwest, judging competitions, and working with performers from several ensembles as they prepared for auditions, exhibitions and competitions.<br />

<h3 id="guy-caridi-nancy-fry">Guy Caridi &amp; Nancy Fry</h3>
Guy &amp; Nancy have over 30 years combined dance experience and have been dance partners for almost nine years. They are the first couple from the Pacific Northwest to teach Savoy-style Lindy Hop and are the original instructors for the Savoy Swing Club. Well-known for their smooth dance style and knowledge of a variety of dances, they continue to teach and perform throughout the region and internationally. At the 1999 U.S. Open Swing Dance Championships in southern California, they brought home trophies in the Lindy Hop and Team divisions, and three of Guy's choreographies were place-winners.<br />

<h3 id="chris-chapman">Chris Chapman</h3>
Hooked on Lindy Hop from the first time he saw it, Chris has been a key figure in the burgeoning Seattle Swing scene for many years: serving as a board member of the Savoy Swing club, starting the UW Swing Kids, long time member of "Swing This!" and runs HepCat Productions where he offers classes and hosts a weekly dance and other events. His love for this exciting dance has taken him all over the world, teaching, performing, and studying its history and contemporary developments amongst the most skilled dancers and knowledgeable people in the world. His passion, expertise, and strong ability to teach ensure fun and exciting classes for all levels of dancers in group classes or in private lessons.<br />

<h3 id="jonathan-garrigues">Jonathan Garrigues</h3>
Jonathan started dancing Lindy Hop in early 1996, and has been teaching for the Savoy Swing Club for two years. He also dances Argentine Tango, and his obsession with good leading and following spills out of this dance into his Lindy Hop teaching. He enjoys the social aspect of the dance, and tries to pass along the value of good floorcraft, friendly dance etiquette, and subtle musicality. He has a degree in music and enjoys sharing his knowledge of swing music structure. He is also a member of the Century Ballroom's dance troupe, Swing This!<br />

<td><span id="Maren"></span>
<h3 id="maren-perry">Maren Perry</h3>
An instructor with Savoy Swing Club since 1999, Maren has been Lindy Hoppin' for the last six years. Her own joy in dancing comes from reveling in the constant discovery and creativity that Lindy Hop allows, and she works to inspire this sense of fun in the students of her classes. Maren believes that this dance is best when leads and follows both add to the dance, and feed off each other to create a unique experience for every dance. In addition to her Savoy schedule, Maren teaches elsewhere in Seattle, all around the Northwest and Canada, many private lessons, and performs with the Century Ballroom's renowned swing dance troupe, "Swing This!"<br />
