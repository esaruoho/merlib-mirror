# Gary on Red Mountain

Red Mountain is 5890 ft tall and located 3 mi NE of Snoqualmie Pass. It is a class 3 climb. Gary and Dad climbed to the top on 6/22/98. Gary was 14 at the time. Behind Gary is Kendall Peak. The rocks on top of Red Mountain were slippery and crumbly . ![](../SJF/HIKE/JPG/98garred.jpg)
