### **Women's Science Fiction Sites:**

#### 

- [A Guide to Feminist Science Fiction Resources in Print and on the Net](http://home.earthlink.net/%7Ekebbo/rg/) - This resource guide covers mostly non-fiction material about or related to feminist science fiction. It includes references to other sources that focus on fiction, but you won't find sample chapters from novels or fiction bibliographies here. *On our own Heather Whipple's site!*
- [Feminist Science Fiction, Fantasy, & Utopia](http://www.feministsf.org/femsf/) - Wealth of worlds that women (and woman-friendly men) have created in science fiction.
- [Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers of America, Inc.](http://www.sfwa.org/)
- [Wiscon](http://www.sf3.org/wiscon/) - The gathering of the feminist SF community
