<td><h1 id="blues-websites">Blues Websites</h1>
<p><a href="http://blueslyrics.tripod.com/">Harry's Blues Lyrics Online</a> <img src="../images/5star.gif" width="55" height="12" alt="5 stars" /><br />
Lyrics and soundclips of blues songs, blues terms and language defined and explained, blues history, blues books selection and more</p>
<p><a href="http://frettedinstrumentsnyc.com">Fretted Instruments School of Guitar and Banjo</a> <img src="../images/4star.gif" width="55" height="12" alt="4 stars" /><br />
A site devoted to quality transcriptions and midi files of Fingerstyle blues and folk guitar, bluegrass banjo and Chet Atkins (note for note) arrangements. All files created in the TablEdit format</p>
<p><a href="http://www.geocities.com/Athens/Acropolis/1567">Jim's Blues School</a><br />
A few informative blues lessons for guitarists who are just starting out.</p>
<p><a href="http://members.aol.com/MBMusic98/">Mel Booker Music!</a><br />
Blues, Jazz/Fusion, and Funk lessons.</p>
<p><a href="http://www.thebluesbarn.com/">The Blues Barn</a><br />
Free backing tracks that have to be heard to be believed!</p>
<p><a href="http://www.openhere.com/music/styles/blues/education/">OpenHere</a><br />
Music, Styles, Blues, Education</p>
