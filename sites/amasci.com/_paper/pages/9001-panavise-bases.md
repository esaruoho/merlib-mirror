<td width="50%" data-valign="top"><strong>TURNS AND TILTS TO ANY POSITION<em><br />
Four functional design features make</em> PANAVISE <em>work holder tool systems the most versatile in the world:</em></strong>
<p>(1) The vise head can turn a full 360' in the "locking unit" in the base.<br />
(2) The vise head can also tilt 90' in any direction (180' tilt action).<br />
(3) The housing containing the "locking unit" also rotates full-circle.<br />
(4) All these movements are controlled by the work at any desired drag-tension, or to lock it firmly, at any position, any angle, facing any direction around the base.</p>
