*Source page: `~dobe/Dobe_ForSale_GO_Worlds01.htm`*

<div id="DobeGoWorldMagazinesForSale_Start">

</div>

<span class="Blue170ic"> Early "GO WORLD" magazines for sale! **Make an offer!** </span>  \
<a href="http://www.eskimo.com/~dobe/index.html#Homonomicon_index_UsingPayPal" class="address">Use Paypal for "john.hartman17@verizon.net"</a>

> Relatively ancient **magazines about an ancient board game called 'GO'**.\
> This will interest the 'GO' player and enthusiast as well as other collectors.\
>
> - [All issues for sale are in **excellent, pristine condition**](#DobeGoWorldMagazinesForSale_IssuesForSale). Details below.
> - *Note: The availability is accurate as of **March 10, 2003**.*
> - *Note: **For Sale** indicates my willingness to listen to your offer. Once terms have been reached, any inspections are complete and money is accepted, **sales are final**.*
> - *Note: Any shipping and insurance incurred, <u>as well as the cost of transferring money through PayPal</u>, will be the responsibility of the buyer.*
> - *Note: Additional charge may be asked for handling any shipping involved.*
> - *Note: The (**.jpg**) digital pictures below represent the actual covers of the original magazines, and are not intended to reproduce anyone's original artwork nor copyrighted work.*
>
> Other sites for Go-related equipment, publications, rules and playing online:
>
> *<a href="http://kgs.kiseido.com/" target="_blank">http://kgs.kiseido.com/</a>  \
> (Kiseido is a leading publishers of Go books and Go World magazine.)*
>
> *<a href="http://www.yutopian.com" target="_blank">http://www.yutopian.com</a>  \
> (Yutopian is a leading retailer of Go-related everything.)*
>
> *To order new Go Worlds or a subscription:   1-800-988-6463 \
> (Some issues are available for \$6.50 each, (#72-#94, plus others) \
> while a new subscription is \$28.00 for 5 issues.)*
>
> *<a href="http://nngs.cosmic.org/" target="_blank">No Name Go Server:   http://nngs.cosmic.org/</a> (NNGS, an on-line Go club, has no charges (for membership or time) for playing web-based GO.)*
>
> *<a href="http://cns.nyu.edu/~mechner/go/improve.html" target="_blank">How to improve at Go</a> by David A. Mechner*
