<td><h3 id="welcome">Welcome!</h3>
<p><strong>Civil Society in Everyday Life breakfast meetings.</strong> The next meeting will be on September 11 (<strong>note</strong> this is the second, not the third, Tuesday of the month).  Our guest will be <strong>Ron Sher</strong>, owner of Third Place Books in Bothell and Elliot Bay Books in Seattle. On October 16, <strong>Jan Levy</strong>, Executive Director of Leadership Tomorrow will join us. See <a href="#csel">below</a> for the usual arrangements.</p>
<hr />
<p>The Evergreen State Society is a Seattle-based nonprofit organization addressing policy issues and management questions in order to strengthen the nonprofit sector in Washington state and beyond.</p>

<p><strong>On a related theme, Washington state government agencies</strong> affect the work of nonprofits in many ways. The Evergreen State Society maintains an online list, with thumbnail sketches of the ways each agency's programs connect to the field. It is available at <a href="http://www.tess.org/misc/gov2.html">http://www.tess.org/misc/gov2.html</a>. <strong></strong> As part of the report on "Building the Entrepreneurial Nonprofit" (see <a href="#000410">next section</a>), Mark Hugh, CPA, of Clark Nuber in Bellevue has posted his <a href="http://www.nwforum.org/nonprofit/sixth_annual/conference_proceedings/bios/mh_handout.htm">outline</a> discussing Washington state taxes that may apply to nonprofit organizations. Dr. Mary Stewart Hall has posted her advice on how to seek a job in the nonprofit sector in the Puget Sound Region at <a href="http://www.mnpl.org/find">http://www.mnpl.org/find</a>. <strong><span id="conf"></span></strong></p>

<p>The handbook includes chapters by Society President Putnam Barber and many other close observers of and experts on the nonprofit sector in Washington state.</p>
<p><strong>"The High Performing Nonprofit" March 26, 2001  </strong> The Seventh Annual Nonprofit Executive Series conference was held in the Northwest Rooms at the Seattle Center.  Speakers' handouts and other notes from the conference are available through the conference website at <a href="http://www.nwforum.org/nonprofit">http://www.nwforum.org/nonprofit</a>; the full conference agenda and some background materials for the sessions are also posted there.</p>
<p>Elizabeth Boris, who is director of the Urban Institute's <a href="http://www.urban.org/centers/cnp.html">Center on Nonprifits and Philanthropy</a> as well as president of the Association for Research on Nonprofit Organizations and Voluntary Action (<a href="http://www.arnova.org/">ARNOVA</a>), and William Ryan of Harvard University's <a href="http://www.ksghauser.harvard.edu/">Hauser Center</a> were among the featured speakers. The plenaries and concurrent sessions outlined the latest research findings and presented  important ideas about what it takes to be a high performing nonprofit.<span id="000410"></span></p>
<p><strong>"Building the Entrepreneurial Nonprofit"</strong>  On April 10, 2000, this conference brought together nationally recognized experts and local leaders to discuss the leadership implications of the pressures on nonprofit organizations to become "more business like" and to earn more of their income from commercial activities.</p>
<p>The reports on many of the presentations in the full agenda and links to useful background  information can be found at <a href="http://www.nwforum.org/nonprofit">http://www.nwforum.org/nonprofit</a>.The conference report also includes summaries of responses to the pre-conference survey and a profile of the more than 400 participants.</p>
<p>"Building the Entrepreneurial Nonprofit" was offered by the <a href="http://www.mnpl.org/">Not-for-Profit Leadership Program</a> of Seattle University, the <a href="http://www.evans.washington.edu/Orgs/NonProfitGateway/index.html">Nonprofit Management Program</a> and the <a href="http://www.nwforum.org/">NW Forum</a> of the Daniel J. Evans School of Public Affairs at the University of Washington, and <a href="http://www.tess.org/">The Evergreen State Society</a>.<span id="comments"></span></p>
<p><strong>Commentaries</strong> by the Society's President appear several times a year in <em>The Chronicle of Philanthropy</em> and other publications.  The Society also responds occasionally to invitations from government agencies for reactions to policy initiatives. Many of these documents are accessible through this <a href="http://www.tess.org/misc/TESSpubs.html">list of published statements.</a></p>
<p><strong>A recent example:</strong> <a href="misc/chron0006.html">"Charities Shouldn't Take Donors for Granted"</a> appeared in the <em>Chronicle of Philanthropy</em> on June 1, 2000..</p>
<p><strong><span id="csel"></span>Civil Society in Everyday Life breakfasts</strong> are held at 8 am on the third Tuesday of most  months in the 1891 Room of Bellarmine Hall on the Seattle University campus (near the intersection of 12th Avenue and  Columbia Street).  At each meeting, a guest with an interest in a subject related to understanding civil society shares experiences and insights.  Recent guests have included Hubert Locke; Norma Straw and LaiLani Ovalles, of the YMCA Youth Engagement Project; Seattle City Council Member Richard Conlin; and Jim Diers, Director of the Seattle Department of Neighborhoods.  An email distribution list is used to announce upcoming guests and send reminders.  To be added to this list, send your request to <a href="mailto:CSEL@TESS.org">CSEL@TESS.org</a>.  There is no charge for these programs; drop-in participation is invited.  The cafeteria of Bellarmine Hall is nearby and offers bagels, coffee and other breakfast items for sale.<strong><span id="inc"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong>The Internet Nonprofit Center and the NONPROFIT FAQ</strong> offer an extensive library and archive of information for leaders and observers of the nonprofit sector in the United States.  To access this resource, visit <a href="http://www.nonprofits.org/">http://www.nonprofits.org/</a>. The Evergreen State Society maintains the <a href="http://www.nonprofits.org/npofaq/">NONPROFIT FAQ</a> and provides webservices through the Internet Nonprofit Center for related activities such as the <a href="http://www.nonprofit-info.org/library/gov/urs/">Multi-State Filer project</a> of the National Association of State Charities Officials.<strong><span id="awards"></span></strong></p>
<p><strong>Evergreen Awards</strong> for Contributions to Community Life in Washington state.  On February 22, 1999, The Evergreen State Society honored the 1999 Evergreen Award recipients in a ceremony at the Old Capitol Building in Olympia. The citations for these ten outstanding contributors to Washington's communities can be read at <a href="./misc/99awards.html">99 Program</a>. The 1998 Evergreen Awards are described online at <a href="./misc/98ev.htm">98 Program</a>. <h3 id="about-the-evergreen-state-society">About The Evergreen State Society</h3>
<p>The Evergreen State Society strengthens healthy communities by fostering nonprofits and more informal organizations like blockwatches and book clubs. It is a civic organization incorporated in Washington state and recognized by the Internal Revenue Service as a public charity under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.</p>
<p>The <a href="http://www.tess.org/misc/TESSbd.html">Board of Directors</a> has five volunteer members from around the state.</p>
<p>Read about some <a href="http://www.tess.org/misc/TESSpast.html">past activities</a> of  The Evergreen State Society. </p>
<p><strong>The work of The Evergreen State Society is made possible by grants and gifts from supporters.</strong> The society is a member of The Giving Network.  You can make an online gift that will support this work on behalf of nonprofits by clicking <a href="http://www.thegivingnetwork.com/cgi-bin/texis/vortex/partnerDetail?ID=37e3e4c874">here</a> and choosing the <strong>"Make a donation now"</strong> button. Both The Giving Network and the Society are registered as required by RCW 19.09 with the Charities Division of the Office of the Secretary of State in Washington.  For more information, contact <a href="http://www.secstate.wa.gov/charities/default.htm">http://www.secstate.wa.gov/charities/</a> or call 1-800-332-GIVE (Washington state callers) or 360/753-0863 (from out of state).</p>
