<p>        Notes:  <br />
        (1) Jesse is now Champion Jesse!   But we are getting ahead of ourselves ...<br />
        (2) Day 1 - SUCKED.  Bad day for the WildWind clan.  <br />
        (3) Day 2 - Jesse is thee DAWG!  Winner Dog and Best of Winners!  His sister Emmy was <br />
             no slouch either.  She took Best of Opposite Sex.  Nancy did a great job handling. <br />
        (4) The two kids, Peaches and Kobe, were their usual selves.  Mike Stone handled Kobe <br />
              both days and commented that Kobe needs a lot of work and his "head ripped off a <br />
              few times."<br />
        (5) The question is, why did the gang do so well Sunday and blow chunks Saturday?<br />
              Nancy's First Theorem of WildWind:  For every bad Judge, there is an equal or better<br />
              male Judge that comes from the Southeastern United States.<br />
              Nancy's Second Theorem of WildWind:  a<strong><sup>2</sup></strong> + b<strong><sup>2</sup></strong> = win<sup><strong>2</strong></sup>.  Especially if the entry number, <br />
              a or b, contains a "7". <br />
        (6) As always, the Kobester enjoyed his visit with family, especially with his sister.<br />
</p>
