**Early Virginia Immigrants, 1623-1666**

The first-mentioned name in every instance is the immigrant and the name following the word "by" is the patentee or party bringing him over. Sometime the patentee becomes a marvelous lead as the patentee would be a land holder and may be a relative. Typically you can find quite a bit of data on patentees.
