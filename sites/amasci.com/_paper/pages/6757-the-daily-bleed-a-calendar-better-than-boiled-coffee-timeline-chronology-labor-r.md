> > 1916 -- Founding of National Women's Party.
> >
> > > In 1923 the National Women's Party pushes an Equal Rights Amendment in Congress. But many women's groups, especially those tied to the labor movement, oppose the effort. The amendment sparks a dispute that rages among feminists even in the 1990s. Some say the sexes are very similar &, therefore, women deserve equal treatment. Others oppose it because, they say, women are superior in many ways. By the 1960s, the party's membership shrinks to just a few hundred -- mostly veterans of the suffrage movement who have endured jail terms & force-feedings during hunger strikes. A new generation of feminists label them "little old ladies in tennis shoes."
