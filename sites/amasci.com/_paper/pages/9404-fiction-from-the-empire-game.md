<td><em>A word of explanation by the author:</em> these stories are set in my game-world, which I have been developing since the early 1980's. The premier deity of that world is Dorian, Goddess of Life.  Much of Dorianism is drawn from Jean M. Auel's "Earth's Children" series.  These stories are dedicated to Tim, Kim, and Dave. 
<p>Many place names andother parts of this game, and consequently these stories, were taken from other sources. Pandathaway was the name of a city in "Guardians of the Flame," Delain was the kingdom in Stephen King's "Eyes of the Dragon," the lion-god Aslan is named after the one in Narnia, etc. I borrowed a lot in those days ; ) </p>
