# Some Dreams of a Better Mouse Trap Eventually Do Get Done

**BACKGROUND:** Biorhythm theory has interested Uncle Chuck for many years, probably because most people he has encountered were quite eager to have their monthly biorhythm charts. Being a computer programmer, Uncle Chuck once tried to come up with a better way of providing monthly biorhythm charts to those individuals who want them. He did! He did! In 1977, Uncle Chuck submitted his manuscript INSTANT BIORHYTHMS for 1978 to a publisher (Crown Publishers, Inc.). It was to be the first of an annual series of books. Surprise! Surprise! It was rejected! Rejected! So, after muttering the D-word a time or two, Uncle Chuck turned his attention from his dream of that better mouse trap to other important matters, which had nothing to do with promoting biorhythm theory. Since that awful rejection letter, twenty years have come and gone. During that period of time, computers evolved (faster, better, cheaper) and personal computers are now in millions of homes. What changes there have been in computing technology, computer applications, and computer ownership! Today's personal computers can inter-communicate with a variety of other computers via the Internet and the World Wide Web. Using their personal computers, people send e-mail to one another, chit-chat on-line, and access all sorts of information from various sources, including the results of complex mathematical computations based on data entered by the personal computer users --- plus other uses too numerous to mention. In 1977, Uncle Chuck's concept of "instantly" accessing one's 1978 biorhythm chart for a given month was the following six-step, non-computer process: (1) Open up a copy of INSTANT BIORHYTHMS for 1978. (2) For a month and year of one's birth and a selected month in 1978, look up the appropriate base numbers of pre-printed P-charts, E-charts, and I-charts. (3) Determine the appropriate pre-printed P-chart, E-chart, and I-chart by adding to each of these base numbers the day of the month of one's birthday. (4) Flip to the appropriate P-chart (top 1/3 page) within the set of 23 pre-printed physical biorhythm charts. (5) Flip to the appropriate E-chart (middle 1/3 page) within the set of 28 pre-printed emotional biorhythm charts. (6) Flip to the appropriate I-chart (bottom 1/3 page) within the set of 33 pre-printed intellectual biorhythm charts. What a concept! INSTANT BIORHYTHMS in book form was what Uncle Chuck considered to be an "instant" process. Compared to other non-computer processes for accessing one's biorhythm chart, it was fairly instant. But the INSTANT BIORHYTHMS book series concept failed to satisfy the publisher, Crown Publishers, Inc. Hmmm... Did that other Charles Dickens ever get a rejection letter? Who knows? Today, it's 1998. Do people, for whatever their reasons, still have interests in obtaining their biorhythm charts? Being curious, Uncle Chuck decided, in April, to search the Web for "biorhythm charting". Yes, there are on the Web various sites specializing in this topic. One such site (provided by Dr. Thomas E. Ferrin of UCSF) caught Uncle Chuck's attention; it allowed Uncle Chuck to specify a multi-month biorhythm chart and immediately generated the chart. Uncle Chuck simply entered a date of birth and a chart-month(s) specification. Bingo! The requested biorhythm chart appeared "instantly" in color on the monitor of Uncle Chuck's personal computer. The site featured no waiting, no fees, no books to buy, no obligations, no surveys, no shipping & handling charges, no sales taxes, and no limits on the number of charts generated. Not bad, huh? Dr. Ferrin's site is [Biorhythm Charting Service.](http://www.cgl.ucsf.edu/home/tef/biorhythm.html) To complete his dream, Uncle Chuck decided to have a site, too. His site, [Uncle Chuck's INSTANT BIORHYTHMS](http:InstantBiorhythm.html) interfaces with Dr. Ferrin's biorhythm chart generator. Yes, some dreams of a better mouse trap eventually do get done --- one way or another. **CREDITS:** Dr. Ferrin gets full credit for the generation of the biorhythm charts that you request via Uncle Chuck's INSTANT BIORHYTHMS site. **POST SCRIPT:** If a reputable book publisher is interested in Uncle Chuck's annual book series INSTANT BIORHYTHMS, [contact Uncle Chuck at Camp Durenne.](mailto:bi186@scn.org%20) No, thank you, Uncle Chuck is not interested in the vanity press.
