<strong>4. What styling products do you use?</strong> What makes you think I have style? =)</p>
<p><br />
<strong>5. What's your worst hair-related experience?</strong> I haven't had any really BAD experiences. I do however have a chronic fear of getting tape stuck in my hair. This stems from my days working at a local retail store preparing their daily deposits for pickup by the armored carriers. I was always afraid I'd get my hair caught in the tamper evident seal on the bags and that we'd have to cut it off so we'd be able to make the deposit on time (the contract w/ the armored company specifies that they have X minutes from the time they enter to the time they leave otherwise there's a pretty steep extra charge.</p>
