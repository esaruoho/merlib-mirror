# MAD Science Mailing List (unused, was shut down years ago)

    The Vortcor-list was created for discussions among the MAD Science
    Network's volunteer science experts.  If you have questions or comments
    about the project, or wish to discuss the Answers Archive material, or
    just want to get to know your fellow Network members, this is the forum
    for you. 

    If you are new to this discussion group, please feel free to jump right in

    Vorcor-list subscription instructions:

      To subscribe, send an email message to vortcor-list-request@eskimo.com
      with the follwing in the SUBJECT: line:

                 subscribe

      Insert your name of course, with no brackets.  You will get an 
      automatic greeting message in response.  Once subscribed, send your 
      email to vortcor-list@eskimo.com.  To unsubscribe, send a empty message
      to vortcor-list-request@eskimo.com with this in the subject line:

                 unsubscribe

    **************************************************************************
    Vortcor-list Rules:

    1. Small email files please.  The limit is set to 40K right now, those
       exceeding the limit will be forwarded to Bill Beaty.  Some
       members are on limited service, or have to pay for received email.
       Diagrams and graphics can be mailed to me  and posted on the webpage
       for viewing.

    2. PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE: when you reply to a message DON'T include the
       ENTIRE message in your reply.  Always edit it a bit and delete
       something.  The entire message should only be included if: (A) you 
       are replying to a message that is many days old, or (B) you are doing 
       a point-by-point reply to many parts of a message.  Many vortex users 
       must pay by the kilobyte for receiving message traffic, and large 
       amounts of redundant messages are irritating and expensive.  So,
       when including a quoted message in your reply, ALWAYS DELETE SOMETHING,
       the more the better.

    3. Flamewars are forbidden.  This list is civilized, it is not a Usenet
       Newsgroup.  If you need to express some negative emotions to another
       subscriber, please do it via private email, don't chance triggering
       a flamewar here by sending it to Vortcor-list.

    4. "Junkmail" email advertizing will not be tolerated.  While not illegal
       yet, widecasting of junk-email ads to listservers is against the
       Unwritten Rules of the Internet.

       - Bill B.
