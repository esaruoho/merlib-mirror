<strong>          Who<br />

<div data-align="center">
<strong>Common Writing Errors</strong>
</div>
<ul>
<li>Self-inflicted praises about the author/director/actor/producer is easily seen as amateurish and, hence, to be ignored. Especially if the author/director/actor/producer is one-and-the-same.</li>
<li>Keep it Very Brief — One printed page is enough.<br />
Most news desks get 200+ releases <em>a day</em>. They usually do not have time to read more than the first two paragraphs.</li>
<li>Run your spell checker <em>and</em> your grammar checker in your word program, before copying the text to e-mail.</li>
<li>Era dates should be “90s” <em>not</em> “90’s” They are not possessive.</li>
<li>Use lower case for position titles. Should be “André d’Artiste, artistic director” <em>not</em> “André d’Artiste, Artistic Director”</li>
<li>Theater locations should have full addresses, including the city and state. Many releases are sent with only a street address. As releases are sent all over the world — especially by e-mail — it is rude to expect every to know where your favorite cozy theater is located.</li>
</ul>

<tr>
<td>HTML formatting</td>
<td>Many e-mail readers cannot translate HTML.<br />
Viruses get passed through HTML.</td>
<td>Turn off the HTML.<br />
Send message as Plain Text.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>ALL CAPS</td>
<td>Rude. Irritating. Visually like shouting.<br />
Cannot tell difference between a title and a descriptive.</td>
<td>Use capitol letters - on first letter only - for play and book titles, proper names.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Large image file attachments</td>
<td>Takes huge time downloading.<br />
95 percent of Internet users are on 56k.</td>
<td>Send small files and ask if larger files are desired.<br />
Send links to a Web page with large images.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Web address unlinked in e-mail</td>
<td>Requires copying or retyping</td>
