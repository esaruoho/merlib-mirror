<div align="LEFT">

I think the description of the Marabou Stork in Collins Guide to African Wildlife is appropriate: "An enormous, ugly bird." The one I photographed was four or five feet tall. It doesn't help its image to know that they are carrion feeders--I didn't know there were any storks that were.\
