<p><strong>Sail for One Week or Two</strong><br />
Our itineraries are mostly based on two week routes however, they are also arranged such that it's easy to join us for one week only -- just inquire about either the first of second half of a two week trip.</p>
<p><strong>Private Parties</strong><br />
As ever, with private group charters - if you fill the boat, we're more than happy to arrange dates/itinerary to suit. And of course, by <a href="bring_your_own_group/index.html">bringing your own group</a> of family/ friends, you get a great deal! Check out the <a href="../our_yachts_and_crew/our_yachts/index.html">our yachts</a> section for pics, design layouts and prices.<br />
</p>
<p><strong>What's Included In the Cost of the Sail?</strong><br />
Our basic philosophy is to make our prices as inclusive as possible (unlike most yacht charters in the Med, where just about everything is "extra" !) Our formula is simple: <em>If it's on the boat, it's included. If it's on land, it's your responsibility.</em> So, all food n' drink on the boat is covered (incl. open bar), as are all boat running expenses, skipper, sailing instruction, etc. The only one exception is dinners - we usually eat ashore in little local tavernas. Read on for <a href="rates/whats_included.html" target="text">more details</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Our Itinerary</strong><br />
Our itineraries have been developed over several years to take best advantage of prevailing wind conditions, and alternating tranqil anchorages with lively ports, etc. However, one of the greatest advantages of a sailboat is the ability to be flexible - and if the whims of the wind gods or those on board dictate that we change our route - so we do ! Each trip does have a <em>provisional</em> itinerary - if you click on any of the trip dates in the <a href="rates/index.html">dates, rates and itineries</a> section, you'll see an example.</p>
