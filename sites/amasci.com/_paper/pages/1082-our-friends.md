# OUR FRIENDS

We've lived a lot of places over the years, and we're fortunate enough to have made a lot of friends along the way- some of whom we're close enough to to consider family. We try to visit as much as we can despite distance and time constraints, because we really miss everyone, even as much as we love Seattle. There's a lot more people there that we love than is pictured here, and maybe someday we'll get more pictures up.
