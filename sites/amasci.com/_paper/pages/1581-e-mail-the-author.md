<hr />
...that is the message you will most often see leading off my response to e-mails that people send me about the site.<br />
<br />
I create and maintain this site alone in my <em>SPARE TIME</em>. It sometimes requires a <em>LOT</em> of effort to do the research and editing needed with only one person, so my e-mail often suffers and the backlog just grows and grows.<br />
<br />
I have finally decided that I can not take the time to respond to <em>EVERY</em> e-mail that I receive <em>AND</em> make headway on maintaining the site. So, something has to suffer - I just can't respond to mails requesting help or easy solutions to problems all the time like I used to try to do. The main source of knowledge that I have to give anyway <em>IS</em> the information on the site, so it can't hurt to look for the answer there. I <em>AM</em> certainly open to requests for addressing new things on the site that aren't there now or are currently unclear.<br />
<br />
So... don't let the previous bit daunt you too much - if you want to send me e-mail, I can be reached at <a href="mailto:indexdot@blooberry.com">indexdot@blooberry.com</a>. It just may take me a <em>LONG</em> time to respond. =)</td>
