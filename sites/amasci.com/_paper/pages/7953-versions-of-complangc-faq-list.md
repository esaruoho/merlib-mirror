# comp.lang.c FAQ list(s)

You probably just came from there, but there is a browsable, web-based [HTML version](http://www.eskimo.com/~scs/C-faq/top.html). (Beware: as of 1999, the web-based version is somewhat out-of-date with respect to the plain-text versions below.) (Please don't ask me for a downloadable archive of the HTML version, as I'm currently unable to provide one. Just browse it [here](http://www.eskimo.com/~scs/C-faq/top.html), or download one of the versions below.)

An expanded, [book-length version](http://www.awl.com/cseng/titles/0-201-84519-9), with even longer answers to even more questions, has been published by [Addison-Wesley](http://www.aw.com/cseng/) (ISBN 0-201-84519-9). Printed books, alas, tend to have a few errors; I've prepared an [errata list](http://www.eskimo.com/~scs/C-faq/book/Errata.html) for this one.

Here is a recent, compressed copy of the [ASCII FAQ list](ftp://ftp.eskimo.com/u/s/scs/C-faq/faq.Z), as posted to Usenet (~100k compressed, ~260k when uncompressed). This is currently the most up-to-date version. \[This and the other compressed files ending in .Z referenced from this page are compressed with the Unix "compress" utility and can be uncompressed with "uncompress" or "gunzip", versions of which are, I believe, available for all popular operating systems.\]

Here is the [abridged version](ftp://ftp.eskimo.com/u/s/scs/C-faq/abridged.Z) (~26k compressed, ~55k when uncompressed).

Here are the [differences from the previous version](ftp://ftp.eskimo.com/u/s/scs/C-faq/diff.Z) (compressed, sometimes quite large; or maybe [uncompressed](ftp://ftp.eskimo.com/u/s/scs/C-faq/diff), if they were minimal). Here is a [collection of incremental differences](ftp://ftp.eskimo.com/u/s/scs/C-faq/diffs/) with respect to even older versions. NOTE: All of these diff lists pertain to the versions posted to Usenet, which are not always synchronized with the web/html version.

Here is a (considerably older) compressed, [PostScript rendition](ftp://ftp.eskimo.com/u/s/scs/C-faq/faq.PS.Z) (152k compressed). BEWARE: the question numbers don't match current versions. (Rather than printing it out, you could -- hint, hint -- get the book.)

There are several translations into other languages:

- to [German](http://home.pages.de/~c-faq/), by [Jochen Schoof](http://www-info2.informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de/staff/joscho) et al. (If that link doesn't work, try [this one](http://www-info2.informatik.uni-wuerzburg.de/dclc-faq/).)
- to Japanese, by Kinichi Kitano. (I don't know of a URL, but it is or was posted regularly to fj.comp.lang.c, and has been published by Toppan, ISBN 4-8101-8097-2.)
- [Seong-Kook Cin](http://pcrc.hongik.ac.kr/~cinsk/) has completed a Korean translation, which is at <http://pcrc.hongik.ac.kr/~cinsk/cfaqs/>.
- A French C FAQ list (not a direct translation of this one) is at <http://www.isty-info.uvsq.fr/~rumeau/fclc/>.

Here is an, um, er, \`\`[alternate version](http://www.plethora.net/~seebs/faqs/c-iaq.html)'' by [Peter Seebach](http://www.plethora.net/~seebs/).

If you're interested in C++, Marshall Cline maintains a [C++ FAQ list](http://www.cerfnet.com/~mpcline/c++-faq-lite/).

For web access to other Usenet FAQ lists, visit [faqs.org](http://www.faqs.org/faqs/).
