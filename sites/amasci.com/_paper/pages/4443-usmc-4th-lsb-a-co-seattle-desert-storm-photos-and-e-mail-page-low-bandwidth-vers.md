[<img src="thnMCday1.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - First day" />](MCday1.jpg)**- 1/91 - At Camp Shepard in the Port of Al-Jubail, in temporary housing waiting to find out where we belong.** **(Dawson, Heath)**

[<img src="thnMCtentcity.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Jubail tent city" />](MCtentcity.jpg)**- 1/91 - Our home base at Camp Shepard in the Port of Al-Jubail, for when we weren't out on the road.**

[<img src="thnMC9101CampShepardFrontGate.JPG" width="83" height="48" />](MC9101CampShepardFrontGate.JPG)**- 1/91 - The entrance gate to Camp Shepard at the Port of Al-Jubail.**

[<img src="thnMCtrio.jpg" data-border="1" width="74" height="50" alt="thumbnail - 3 guys w/ guns" />](MCtrio.jpg)**- 2/91 - At the Port of Al-Jubail, waiting to be assigned the next mission.** **(Dawson, Me, Stinton)**

[<img src="thnMC9102aTrucksAtSaudiMotors.JPG" width="84" height="52" />](MC9102aTrucksAtSaudiMotors.JPG)**- 2/91 - "Saudi Motors" was where we checked out our FINE motor vehicles to cruise around in the desert.**

[<img src="thnMCscudbunker.jpg" data-border="1" width="79" height="50" alt="thumbnail - scud alert" />](MCscudbunker.jpg)**- 2/91 - Port of Al-Jubail. Many a night we were awaken by the Scud alarm to go jump into the bunkers for a while.**

[<img src="thnMCconvoy.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - armored convoy" />](MCconvoy.jpg)**- 2/91 - Moving the 2nd Mar Div stuff where it needed to be along the Baghdad Express.**

[<img src="thnMC9102HeathDawsonAPCsKabritRoad.jpg" width="42" height="58" />](MC9102HeathDawsonAPCsKabritRoad.jpg)**- 2/91 - A couple of heatstroke jarheads enjoying their job too much!** **(Heath, Dawson)**

[<img src="thnMCTypicalDustStorm.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="52" alt="thumbnail - Typical Dust Storm stirred up by truck convoys" />](MCTypicalDustStorm.jpg)**- 2/91 - A Typical Dust Storm stirred up by truck convoys on the Kabrit Road.**

[<img src="thnMC9102CamelStandingInTheDesert.jpg" width="83" height="58" />](MC9102CamelStandingInTheDesert.jpg)**- 2/91 - A loner camel by itself in the middle of nowhere.**

[<img src="thnMCbunker.jpg" data-border="1" width="74" height="50" alt="thumbnail - bunker group" />](MCbunker.jpg)**- 2/91 -A group of us taking a pit stop at a bunker observation post during a supply run.** **(Stinton, Olsen, Schulze, Staley, Dawson, Me)**

[<img src="thnMCcamo.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - US camouflage" />](MCcamo.jpg)**- 2/91 - An example of just how good we (the U.S.) were. If I remember right, this was a full armored company or something like that.**

[<img src="thnMCsupply.jpg" data-border="1" width="74" height="50" alt="thumbnail - convoy" />](MCsupply.jpg)**- 2/91 - A quick break during a food supply run Northward towards the Kuwait border.**

[<img src="thnMCLangetnk.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - tiger brigade track convoy" />](MCLangetnk.jpg)**- 2/91 - On the Kabrit Road moving the Tiger Brigade so they could flank to the East. I'd like to say I drove the track, but I drove the truck.**

[<img src="thnMC9102BlowingSandInTheDesert.jpg" width="78" height="56" />](MC9102BlowingSandInTheDesert.jpg)**- 2/91 - A slight sandstorm breeze made kind of a cool looking scene.**

[<img src="thnMCOilFields.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - scenic shot" />](MCOilFields.jpg)**- 2/91 - Typical scenery there.**

[<img src="thnMCmines.jpg" data-border="1" width="36" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Mines next to roadway" />](MCmines.jpg)**- 2/91 - Watch your step! - Entering Kuwait.**

[<img src="thnMCsunsettrucks.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - trucks w/ sunset" />](MCsunsettrucks.jpg)**- 2/91 - A Peaceful looking sunset as we settled into our "mobile homes" at the end of a day. (before Saddam screwed up the air)**

[<img src="thnMCoilsky.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Oil fires on horizon" />](MCoilsky.jpg)**- 2/91 - The oil fires that SoDamn Insane is responsible for.**

[<img src="thnMCpowcamp.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - POW camp" />](MCpowcamp.jpg)**- 2/91 - We handled and transported POW's by the hundreds and thousands as it began to wind down.**

[<img src="thnMCdeserthwy.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Aerial photo- hwy system" />](MCdeserthwy.jpg)**- 3/91 - The highly evolved Persian Gulf highway system.**

[<img src="thnMCIraqiTankinprotectivebunker3-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Iraqi Tank in protective bunker" />](MCIraqiTankinprotectivebunker3-91.jpg)**- 3/91 - Oops. I guess the bunker wasn't quite enough for this Iraqi tank.**

[<img src="thnMCEnteringKuwait-GouldDube-3-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Entering Kuwait" />](MCEnteringKuwait-GouldDube-3-91.jpg)**- 3/91 - Entering Kuwait.** **(Gould, Dube)**

[<img src="thnMCCapturedIraqiEPWs3-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Captured Iraqi EPWs" />](MCCapturedIraqiEPWs3-91.jpg)**- 3/91 - Just a few of the Iraqi EPW's that were recovered.**

**Unfortunately, that's all for "During" Desert Storm. I had mailed 2 full rolls (48 pictures) home to be developed, but they never made it home. Kinda still ticks me off to this day!**\
**This next group of 23 pictures takes place After the battles of Desert Storm - as things relaxed a bit ...**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

[<img src="thnMCLangeValler.jpg" data-border="1" width="79" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Jess and I" />](MCLangeValler.jpg)**- 3/91 - I ran into Jess Valler, a friend from my small-town high school, (1/2 way around the world) who I hadn't seen for about 5 years!**

[<img src="thnMCcamel.jpg" data-border="1" width="80" height="50" alt="thumbnail - camel" />](MCcamel.jpg)**- 3/91 - Me, a camel... need I say more? Got the photo op by giving some food and water to a Bedouin passing by.**

[<img src="thnMCaBedouinandhismadCamel3-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - A Bedouin and his mad Camel" />](MCaBedouinandhismadCamel3-91.jpg)**- 3/91 - Here's the Bedouin and his mad camel.**

[<img src="thnMCHighwaytoNowhere3-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Highway to Nowhere" />](MCHighwaytoNowhere3-91.jpg)**- 3/91 - Ever feel like your just driving forever and not really getting anywhere?**

[<img src="thnMCTotheMarket3-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Going To the Market?" />](MCTotheMarket3-91.jpg)**- 3/91 - I'm not sure if this one is being bought or sold, but you just don't see this very often anywhere else.**

[<img src="thnMCairjubail.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Jubail from the air" />](MCairjubail.jpg)**- 3/91 - An aerial shot of the Port of Jubail as we flew over during a C-130 resupply mission. Camp Shepard is on the left and the city/town of Al-Jubail is on the right.**

[<img src="thnMC9103McCarthyAbrahmSchulzeKenny.JPG" width="83" height="59" />](MC9103McCarthyAbrahmSchulzeKenny.JPG)**- 3/91 - The heat, sunshine, and dry weather made the entire country one huge volleyball court.** **(McCarthy, Abraham, Schulze, Kenny)**

[<img src="thnMC9103CardsThomasFeneisAtShepard.jpg" width="85" height="57" />](MC9103CardsThomasFeneisAtShepard.jpg)**- 3/91 - So much time to kill, so few card games to play.** **(Thomas, Feneis)**

[<img src="thnMCHerveyCrosslandLange.jpg" data-border="1" width="72" height="50" alt="thumbnail - partytime" />](MCHerveyCrosslandLange.jpg)**- 4/91 - King Fahd International Airport. After being "dry" for a while, it didn't take much for us to get pretty tanked up with some "imported" and "disguised" beverages (look closely on the table) that weren't supposed to be in that country.** **(Hervey, Crossland, Me)**

[<img src="thnMCLoadingpalletsofMREsatKingAbdulAzizAirport4-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="52" alt="thumbnail - Loading pallets of MREs at King Abdul Aziz Airport" />](MCLoadingpalletsofMREsatKingAbdulAzizAirport4-91.jpg)**- 4/91 - \#1 of 3: Loading the Air Force C-130 with supplies at King Abduhl Aziz Airport (AKA "The Scud Bowl" stadium).**

[<img src="thnMCC-130atKingAbduhlAzizAirport4-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - C-130 at King Abduhl Aziz Airport" />](MCC-130atKingAbduhlAzizAirport4-91.jpg)**- 4/91 - \#2 of 3: Unloading the Air Force C-130 with supplies "somewhere up North" (you kind of got used to having no idea where you were).**

[<img src="thnMCPartyattheScudBowl4-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Party at the Scud Bowl" />](MCPartyattheScudBowl4-91.jpg)**- 4/91 - \#3 of 3: The Air Force treated us well. They sold me some, ...uh, ...well, "surplus" supplies that they just happened to have in the plane at a very reasonable price and I treated my Squad that day.(Abraham, Boren, Shauger, Heath, McCarthy, Crossland, Hervey, Larson)**

[<img src="thnMCwaterbed.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Water Buffalo" />](MCwaterbed.jpg)**- 4/91 - As we did 24 hr shift work at the Jubail Airport, the popular thing to do during your down time was to catch some rays on the world's largest waterbed - which kept surprisingly cool all the time.**

[<img src="thnMCatJubailAirportLatendresseLangeStintonDawsonVilla.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="52" alt="thumbnail - at Jubail Airport" />](MCatJubailAirportLatendresseLangeStintonDawsonVilla.jpg)**- 4/91 - Just a group photo at the Jubail Airport.** **(Latendresse, Me, Stinton, Dawson, Villa)**

[<img src="thnMCPlayingCardsDubeLatendresseBettsGould4-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Playing Cards" />](MCPlayingCardsDubeLatendresseBettsGould4-91.jpg)**- 4/91 - A lot of down time was spent playing nearly every variation of every card game ever invented...** **(Dube, Latendresse, Betts, Gould)**

[<img src="thnMCSaudiHighwaySign4-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="70" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Saudi Highway Sign" />](MCSaudiHighwaySign4-91.jpg)**- 4/91 - An example of a bilingual Saudi freeway sign.**

[<img src="thnMCWheelofMisfortune4-91.jpg" data-border="1" width="35" height="52" alt="thumbnail - Wheel of Misfortune" />](MCWheelofMisfortune4-91.jpg)**- 4/91 - The "Wheel of Misfortune" was a classic example of the "OK, we did our job, now get us the hell home" attitude that settled in after a while.** (examples when you spin for a fortune: "Admin Screw-up: You are stuck in-country indefinitely" or "Bad Chow: You end up living through the war, but dying of food poisoning in the chow hall" or even "An overload of too many stupid safety rules pushes you over the edge to suicide")

[<img src="thnMC9104StandingInTheDesert.jpg" width="39" height="58" />](MC9104StandingInTheDesert.jpg)**- 4/91 - A shot of me standing at the end of the world.**

[<img src="thnMCACoSeatl.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - the gangs all here" />](MCACoSeatl.jpg)**- 5/91 - A full group photo of Alpha Company 4th L.S.B., Seattle.**

[<img src="thnMCgunnyrambo.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - Gunny Lat w/ ordinance" />](MCgunnyrambo.jpg)**- 5/91 - As we prepared to head home, "Gunny Rambo" came around collecting all the crap everyone was going to try to sneak home.** **(Latendresse)**

[<img src="thnMC9105CrosslandStintonInCali.jpg" width="78" height="56" />](MC9105CrosslandStintonInCali.jpg)**- 5/91 - One of the first moments back in the good ol' U S of A!!!!** **(Crossland, Stinton)**

[<img src="thnMCCALIFUSA.jpg" data-border="1" width="65" height="50" alt="thumbnail - in CA, USA" />](MCCALIFUSA.jpg)**- 5/91 - Back stateside in California on weekend leave(Gunny Dosier from Kentucky, Stinton, Latendresse, Skommesa, Me)**

[<img src="thnMCSSgtJackson.jpg" data-border="1" width="76" height="50" alt="thumbnail - homecoming" />](MCSSgtJackson.jpg)**- 8/2/91 - Marines always take care of their own - "A" Company and some media welcome S/Sgt Jackson coming home at Sea-Tac Airport after some recovery from his injuries.**
