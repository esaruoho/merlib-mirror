Other Projects:

- New Aquaman writer Rick Veitch has his own website. Check out [his booth](http://www.comicon.com/veitch/) at Comicon.com.
- If you feel like keeping up with former Aquaman writer Peter David, visit his website at <http://www.peterdavid.net>.
- Take a visit to Sunny Lee's [main page](http://www.geocities.com/sunnylee753/) and his [comicbook art page](http://homepage.mac.com/sunny753/album.html). Sunny did the Aquaboy/Lagoon Man one-shot.
- Writer [Ben Raab](http://www.benraab.com/) now has a website. Raab did the Aquaboy/Lagoon Man one shot.
- Former Aquaman inker Howard Shum is selling Aquaman pages from issues \#3-30 of the Peter David run. Check out his page at <http://www.howardshum.com/original.html> or e-mail him at [howard_shum@yahoo](mailto:howard_shum@yahoo.com) for prices and availablity.
- Former Aquaman inker and Eisner winner [Eric Shanower](http://ericshanower.com/) has a book coming out from Image Comics. *Age of Bronze* is a fantastic retelling of the story of Troy. Ask your local shops to order it, or check out [the Age of Bronze website](http://age-of-bronze.com). Also check out the *Oz Story* anthologies for more of [Shanower's work](http://www.hungrytigerpress.com/).
- [Nick Cardy Artwork](http://www.theartistschoice.com/cardy.htm) is available at the Artist's Choice.
- [Joe Rubinstein Artwork](http://www.theartistschoice.com/rubinstein.htm) is available at the Artist's Choice. Rubinstein inked Aquaman in quite a few JLA issues.

<span id="survey"><img src="pics/ac_survey.gif" width="250" height="60" alt="Reader Survey" /></span>

Suspended Due To Ballot Stuffing

I have had this survey up for a few years now, and this is the first time I've had a serious problem with someone voting repeatedly. You see, the survey program I was using was an extremely simple one, designed to take votes through e-mail on a feedback form. Because it was so simple, it was possible to vote more than once in a day. Indeed, possible to vote more than once in an hour. Luckily, I can catch it if someone does that, because I have an archive of the votes through my e-mail program. Unfortunately, I have to reset the votes manually. And I hate doing it. But it had only happened once or twice in the entire time I had the poll up, so it wasn't *that* big a deal. Until now. Someone, the same person even, stuffed the ballot ***THREE*** times. Even after I reset it, this idiot didn't get a clue and stop stuffing the vote. Thus, until I find a polling program that checks for idiots trying to vote repeatedly and stops them without me having to reset the thing, this poll isn't going to be working. I don't care how much you hate the new costume, vote ONCE A DAY you brainless doofus!!! Ahem. Or maybe I'll just wait until I regain my patience... <span id="feature"></span>
