YI JING OR ZICHOU\
ONE INDIVIDUAL'S \[ MIS \] \[ - \] \[ ? \] UNDERSTANDING\
\
Publication History
=========================================================

This document was originally a term paper for James Klee. \[ The author of *Points of Departure, Aspects of the Tao* \] I received an "A" for it, along with comments from my classmates that they didn't understand anything in the paper except the bibliography.

[Appendix L: The Yi Jing as a Model for Growth: A Personal Tale](yi_l.html) was a different paper for James Klee. Copies of it were distributed to classmates, who understood most of it.

A little expansion, and some textual revision, and it was distributed to a few more of my friends. Who still didn't understand most of what I was saying.

In 1991, I found PODS, and promptly put the paper in it. A few revisions, and some discussion with people who both read, and understood what I was saying, resulted in some changes to the script.

In 1995 I signed up with a few Internet mailing lists on Daoism and the *Yi Jing*. Then my paper blossomed in size. For various reasons, I was not on those mailing lists, from 1998 to 2001. I was studying material on the *Yi Jing*, amongst my other projects.

During that time I studied that material, and I changed my outlook about things I had written. Sometimes an expansion, other times a contraction. In still other instances, revising the text to be twice as contradictory as it was, and thrice as obscure. All in effort to increase the clarity of what I meant. Obfuscation implies clarity? Only in a Daoist work, could that begin to make sense.

Note: I didn't number the versions, until I after I distributed them on PODS.

1982: Version 0.0:\
The Term Paper.\
Photocopies were given to everybody in the class.

1984: Some revisions, and photocopies given to a few more people.

1990: Version 1.0:\
First public release of this paper. Distributed on PODS as 1PER_1_0.arj.\
I don't know how many people read it, as a result of it being placed on PODS. I placed it on one BBS on PODS, but after three or so months, found it on about a dozen PODS Boards.

1991: Sometime in 1991, I wrote on PODS Tao Study, that I didn't think anybody other than James Klee --- the psychology professor for whose class I wrote this paper --- had completely read this paper. I promptly recieved several requests for the paper from individuals who claimed that they would read it.

1992: Version 1.5\
Distributed on PODS, NovaNet and BeastNet as 1PER_1_5.arj.\
I have been told that this found its way onto at least one CD-ROM of share-ware/bucket-ware.

December 1993: Version 2.0:\
Distributed on PODS and NovaNet as 1PER_2_0.arj.\
Biggest change was the addition of the Appendices, and an expansion of the bibliography.

1994: Version 2.1\
Main revision was the addition of a list of books on Daoism and the *Yi Jing* that were then in print. Distribution unknown.

1996: Version 2.2\
Distribution unknown.

1997: Version 2.5\
Distributed to one BBS on NovaNet.\
Added a new appendix, and updated the bibliography.

1998: Version 3.0.\
Distribution unknown

Either Version 2.2, 2.5 or 3.0 was briefly available on the World Wide Web.

2000: Version 3.0.1\
Briefly available on the World Wide Web.

April 2001: Version 3.0.2\
Extensive editing of the text. Added several appendices. Formatted the entire paper for the Web, and placed it at http://yi-jing.faithweb.com.

22 May 2001: Version 3.0.3\
Broke the main paper out into different chapters.\
Placed the paper at http://yijing.faithweb.com.\
To anticipate a question. When I obtained yi-jing.faithweb.com, I did not know that yijing.faithweb.com was available.

June: 2001: Version 3.0.4.0\

Segregated paper into two sections.\
