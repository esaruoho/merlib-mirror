  • For starters, I once came very close to a Pulitzer Prize. 80 pages close to be exact! I was reprinted in Best Editorial Cartoons of the Year, 1994, published by Pelican Publishing, edited by Charles Brooks. There I am on page 86 only 80 pages away from the Pulitzer Click here to see the featured cartoon.

    (For those who don't know about it, Best Editorial Cartoons of the Year is the premier annual collection of editorial cartoons in the country featuring carefully selected work from professional cartoonists. To my knowledge, I was the only college editorial cartoonist to be featured in that volume and the only Indian ever to be featured in this collection).

  • Second Prize, Editorial Cartooning, Washington Press Association Communicator in Excellence Awards, 1994.
