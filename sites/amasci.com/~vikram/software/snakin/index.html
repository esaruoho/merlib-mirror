Description:

How long can your sssSlithering sssSnake sssSurvive between hunger pangs and growing pains ???

Maneuver your snake as it hungrily moves around trying to find something to eat. If your snake doesn't eat, it weakens and self-destructs. If your snake does eat, it gets longer and longer and harder to manage, especially with all these walls, stones, poison-bottles and snake-parts coming in the way. (Hey - it's much more fun than it sounds!!)
