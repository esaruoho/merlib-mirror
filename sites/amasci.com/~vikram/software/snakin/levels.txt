 Snakin'
Level Exchange 

 The Snakin' Level Editor, which is
 part of the Snakin' game package, allows you to create
 your own Snakin' levels. Registered users of Snakin' can
 load these new levels into Snakin' and have lots of fun
 playing them. 
 If you are a registered user of
 Snakin', visit this page often to see if there are new
 downloadable levels available for you. 
 If you have created Snakin' levels
 using the Snakin' level editor, and would like to share
 these levels with other Registered Snakin' users, e-mail
 your levels to one of the following addresses: Vikram@Eskimo.com or vmadan@bigfoot.com or v_madan@msn.com . Include
 your name and the name of your level and attach your
 Snakin' Level files as attachments to your email message.
 Once we receive your Snakin' level, we will add it to
 this page so that other users can then download the
 level. 
 [ We reserve the right to refuse
 adding your levels to this page, for any reason
 whatsoever. ] 

 Downloadable Snakin'
 Levels To download a level, click on the
 level name below. The level files are zipped up. You will
 need an unzip utility like pkunzip or WinZip to open these files
 and get the level files out of them. If you don't already
 have a unzip utility, I highly recommend obtaining one
 before proceeding ( Click here to get
 Winzip ). Enjoy!
