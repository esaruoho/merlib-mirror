 Web-a-Toon Archive 

 Web-a-toons are a very unique way of creating and presenting cartoons using HTML as a part of the cartoon itself. 
 More specifically, the cartoons are created by using a mixture of transparent gifs, background images, background colors, and page layout tags.
 If any one of these elements is missing, the whole cartoon falls apart. Hence these are really web-cartoons or web-a-toons.
 I have never seen anyone use this process or technique on the web, so I can very much claim to have invented it. (Hopefully it won't be my only claim to fame).
