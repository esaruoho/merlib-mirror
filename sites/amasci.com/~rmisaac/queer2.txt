Gays-Lesbians-Bisexuals : "Come out in Romania and you can face up to
five years in jail. Now new laws are planned which will "decriminalise"
homosexuality BUT ban gay bars, clubs and media. There will also be such
heavy restrictions on consenting adult gay relationships that they will be
practically impossible. Lesbians and gay men in Romania now fear that a
new wave of arrests is inevitable. The Romania Action for
Gays-Lesbians-Bisexuals Homepage is an online campaign set up to support
the lesbians and gay men in Romania who are standing up to protect their
rights... Find out how much you know about Romania's anti-gay laws at the
website quiz ... Keep in touch with News from Romania and about the
campaign... Information for queers in Romania is on the Support pages ...
A list of available documentation on the situation can be found in the
Archive files..."

 The new quarterly _Journal
of Gay, Lesbian, and Bisexual Identity_ is out. The Journal is an
international interdisciplinary forum dedicated to the exchange of new
knowledge and ideas about every major aspect of lesbian, gay, bisexual,
and transgender life. It features original peer-reviewed scholarly
articles, clinical studies, research papers, personal essays, interviews,
"roundtable discussions," reviews, and poetry." 

 Gay Theological
Journal , subtitled "Homosexual Perspectives on Religion & The 
Scriptures," it "is a new journal dedicated to re-examining and
interpreting the Bible's teaching on homosexuality and homosexual
practices, as well as current thinking among leaders from a broad
spectrum of religions, backgrounds and theological perspectives committed
to making today's congregations more inclusive and welcoming...[it] will
include brief recaps of news, information and insights concerning
homosexuality and religion - resources for ministry, both print and
electronic - book reviews - contact information for organizations working
for dialogue, communication and acceptance within mainline churches and
synagogues - and listings of "Welcoming Congregations" throughout the USA
and beyond."
