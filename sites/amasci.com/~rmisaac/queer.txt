 Hawaii Gay Marriage
Bureau : "issues certificates of marriage to any couple who joins the
registry. They can request an actual marriage registry certificate with
date of the ceremony, minister's name that officiated, couples names and
location/date of where it was performed. They are also give a registry
number the will tie them to our permanent record... The community service
nature of the Hawaii Gay Marriage Bureau is to keep couples (married or
not) informed of the legal marriage lawsuit, that our parent organization,
the GLEA Foundation, initiated. The informational service will let all
registered couples know as soon as possible after legal marriage licenses
will be issued in Hawaii, so they can come here and get LEGALLY married...
The registry also registers licensed ministers and marriage officiants so
that registering couples can get connected should they want to have an
official marriage service performed now or in the future"

 SCIENCE AND SEXUAL ORIENTATION: 

 Biological research of what
makes
people gay or straight : "both a resource for the non-scientist and a 
discussion forum, an on-line magazine on the scientific, social, political
and religious implications of this research. It contains five sections--
Research and Commentary, Discussion Arena, Ongoing Studies, a Clinical
Profile of Sexual Orientation, and Links"

 SEXUAL
ORIENTATION: SCIENCE, EDUCATION AND POLICY : "features work by Dr.
Gregory Herek, (a noted authority on antigay prejudice (or homophobia),
hate crimes, and AIDS stigma) and his Northern California Community
Research Group at the University of California, Davis. It provides factual
information about sexual orientation and HIV/AIDS to promote the use of
scientific knowledge for education and enlightened public policy. Included
is the APA press release and the text of their recent resolutuon (with
support documention) and so much GLB supportive information and
resources."
