    • ONE Institute JUDAICA COLLECTION: an IGLA (International Gay and Lesbian Archives) Special Collection; it "will reflect the range of influence of Jewish thought, culture and history upon evolving lesbian and gay identity. It will document how Judaism has treated homosexuality over the centuries, from biblical prohibitions to recent support of gay and lesbian Jews as members of the spiritual community. It will document how Jewish principles about social justice, equality, human rights, diversity, liberation, interpersonal relationships and suffering have helped develop lesbian and gay activism. And it will document how individual Jews, gay or straight, have contributed to these efforts... The Judaica Collection will include an ongoing living history project to document life stories of lesbian and gay Jews and those who support them. Using techniques of oral and cultural history, these archives will honor how individuals have reconciled their Jewish roots with the lesbian or gay aspects of their being...The Judaica Collection can be an international safe depository for activist organizations, community institutions, and political and legal activities on behalf of lesbian and gay Jews. The Collection can document the work of lesbian and gay synagogues, Jewish oriented lesbian and gay organizations, and Jewish activists who defend homosexual rights and related issues. And it can document the situations of lesbian and gay Jews in different countries."

  • JUDAISM AND HOMOSEXUALITY:

    • "Check out an article written by Jacob Rothman, a student at Bowdoin College, titled "Judaism, Homosexuality, Political Activism. Rothman argues that "As Jews, we can be little or no assistance in the advancement of gay and lesbian politics unless we first understand who we are as individuals. Moreover, to the extent that we are able to synthesize our gay/lesbian lives with our Jewish lives, we receive the added benefit and responsibility of Tikkun Olam (healing or repairing the world).""
    • Judaism and Homosexuality: * What the Bible says about homosexuality * Marriage and the basic family unit * Personal stories of gay and lesbian Jews * Acceptance of homosexuality at the local synagogue level
    • Openhalacha: Homosexuality and Halacha: "Open Halacha is ground-breaking experiment in generating and perfecting halachic argument in an open forum, using cutting-edge technology. With international assistance, Open Halacha will develop arguments, and analyse sources online. Halachists and non-halachists alike are invited to join in this process by contributing their observations,and, in particular, suggesting new reference sources. The combined efforts of a large number of experts, working together, will yield results, and open avenues of approach that would otherwise have remained closed."
