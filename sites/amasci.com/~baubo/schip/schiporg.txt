 Publications * 

 The Complete Schipperke published by Howell
 Books, sponsored by The Schipperke Club of America. 207 pages, copyright
 1993, ISBN 0-87605-303-7. 

 A Schipperke Anthology by Vella M. Root, published
 by The Schipperke Club of America. 

 Schipperkes by Janice F. Martin, published by
 TFH Publications. 192 pages, copyright 1991, ISBN 0-86622-161-1. 

 * The accuracy of the history of the Schipperke breed
in these texts are in question. Resources in Belgium suggest the origins
are different than has been represented in England and the United States.
 To read a perhaps-more-accurate accounting of Schip history, go
 here . 

 I don't know anything about the following book, so can't count on
its accuracy: Het Schipperke, by Kleine, Zwarte belg, 
H. C. Rotgans-Rookmaaker Vrienden van het , Schipperke.

 The Email List! 

 You, too, can join the Schipperke email list by sending a message
to: 
 LISTSERV@MAIL.EWORLD.COM 
with the line ' SUBSCRIBE SKIPPY-L Firstname
Lastname ' 
in the message body. 
It's that simple! 

 I do not maintain the list, so please don't ask me to subscribe
you to it or send me message about any problems with the list. When
you subscribe to the list you'll receive a set of instructions on whom to
contact regarding questions or concerns.
