 And a Happy New Year to all of you, of course! 

 We spent our eve over at a friend's house where we mostly watched movies
 (first Dogma , then Galaxy Quest ) and played Balderdash. It
 was a nice, calm night, actually. I had champagne, of course - I love the
 stuff - but alcohol didn't interest me much. I wasn't in the mood for it,
 really. I enjoyed the asti, all the same, but didn't even come close to a
 buzz of any sort.

 About around 11:45pm we turned the television to Dick Clark, then all waited
 for the ball to drop. I've done so for many years and every time it occurs
 to me that it's a bit silly, considering that it's not a live broadcast,
 and that the new year came to New York three hours earlier than our west
 coast celebration. Oh well. Tradition, and all that.

 After midnight I went outside to hang with Odessa while she smoked and we
 chatted, and discovered there was ice on the ground. Considering how late
 it was and how many drunk drivers there would be on the road, Mike and I
 opted to crash on a couch rather than driving home. Gave us more time to
 play Balderdash, anyhow.

 As for resolutions... I didn't make any. I haven't made a New Year resolution
 in a long time. When you're an overweight person, the standard resolution
 is often This year I'll really lose weight... And then the yearly failure
 to do so makes things all that much more difficult. Continual failure isn't
 fun, eh? Well, I'd decided a long time ago, many years past, that it was
 probably healthier to simply enjoy my life as I am, no matter what weight.
 So I stopped dieting. It was quite a booster to my emotional health, I'll
 tell you. Then when I discovered a few years later that I was overweight
 because I'm diabetic, and not the other way around, it just made dealing
 with it all a lot easier.

 So. No resolutions for Julie, except perhaps to Live Life To The Fullest.
 Yeah. That'll do. 

 Last night the question came up, once again... 

 How old is
 Dick
 Clark , really? 

 This afternoon I did a little research, just for the heck of it.

 At the Fifties Web American
 Bandstand site, it says... American Bandstand began as a local program
 in Philadelphia in 1952. Then it was called Bob Horn's Bandstand. In July
 of 1956 the show got a new host, a clean-cut 26 year old named Dick
 Clark..." and that his birthday is November 30, 1929.

 Lessee...

 2001 (in Nov.) - 1956 = 45... 
 26 years old + 45 = 71.

 Dick Clark is currently seventy-one years old. There you go.
