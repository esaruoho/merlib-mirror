 Lessee... 

 Get up late. Shower. Feed cat. Pet dog. Check email.

 Lose track of time checking on friends' journal entries. Blink at clock.
 Oh.

 Put Winamp on repeat play of Buffy Musical. Make bed. Do laundry. Plan dinner
 (korma, vindaloo, eggplant, lentils, naan). Empty dishwasher. Drive to grocery
 store, wander about, have some hard salami cut at the deli for me to pick
 up later, get various and sundry items and more cat food (Whiskas Homestyle
 Favorites, doncha know - has the most gravy of any other cat food I've found,
 which is of utmost importance to Woodrow), go back for the salami, pay and
 exit. Groan while leaving because I forgot to get His Highness more Cat Sip.
 Go home anyway. (Yes, I spoil my 14-year-old cat.)

 Haul in the groceries. Pet dog, meow at cat. Put groceries away. Do dishes.

 Then - ah ha! - a thought. I went into the craft room and dug out my easel
 and paints, then took them into the rec room where I'll set them up. No painting,
 not yet. I need to get a drop cloth, first. But at least I got them out.

 Next week there will be a break in my exciting routine! Over the weekend
 I'm driving down to Mom's, then on Monday through Wednesday I'll be doing
 some more training for my job. Oh yes, I do have a job, believe it or not.
 Very part time these days, as a researcher for an academic testing company.
 But hey, getting an expense-paid little trip will be nice. I'll even leave
 the computer at home, I think, and just take some books. And some books on
 tape for the drive down. In any case, the paid hours for the training will
 be much appreciated.

 Hey, life is good. Not too exciting, but it's not too sucky, either.
