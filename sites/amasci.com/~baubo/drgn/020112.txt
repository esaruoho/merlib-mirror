 Abstract Thoughts 

 I'm writing this entry at Mom's and uploading it later, by the way... 

 Mom woke me from an uneasy sleep at 6:30 this morning to get me up and going
 on a trip to a casino with her and Chuck. Much moaning and groaning ensued...
 I hardly ever get up that early any more. Well, except to feed the cat, and
 I go back to sleep after that.

 So we drove and drove and drove and eventually ended up at a casino on a
 reservation. I think I've been there with them before, Spirit Mountain. We
 trundled in and plopped down in a booth for the breakfast buffet - thankfully,
 I got coffee pretty quickly. I remember stuffing a lot of breakfasty lowcarb
 food (i.e. eggs, bacon, etc) into my stomach, and then we went to park ourselves
 in front of the nickel slot machines. Mom and I did, at least. I had $25
 to play with (artfully slipped to me by Mom), and over the next couple of
 hours I ended up making a profit of another $25. Three times I got 777 on
 the machines - as I've never really had much luck on slot machines, I was
 pretty surprised and pleased. I'm not much for gambling, and I wouldn't need
 to visit slots reguarly by any means... but it was fun to win for once. I
 gave Mom back the $25 she slipped me.

 After we cashed in our electronic chips, we drove and drove and drove some
 more to Lafayette Antique Mall. We browsed there for quite a long time. I
 found things which were neat, but not anything Mike and I had to have (our
 house is already too full of stuff as it is), and so I left with that $25
 profit still in my pocket. Mom spent the $25 I'd given back to her, of course.

 Eventually after another long drive we got back to Mom's place (I fell asleep
 in the back seat). I hauled in my testing materials from the car to look
 over them (it's been a few months since I've done so) and to give mom the
 Woodcock-Johnson III cognitive battery. To do so would give me needed
 practice in administering the test and would show Mom what it is that I do,
 these days.

 It was really interesting to see how similar the test results are, comparing
 hers to mine. She and I are very, very much alike.

 And that brings me to now, as I sit in Mom's den with cold toes once again.
 Tomorrow I drive into the city where I meet up with my boss and my boss's
 boss and a new tester. Lots of test-taking and test-giving is in store during
 the next three days, for certain. And a bit of company-sponsored eating,
 drinking, and relaxing on my part, in the evenings.
