 Random Pictures 2 / This is the Copper River, famous for its salmon. We may have
fished on it, or else maybe that was some other river. All I remember from the day I took this
picture was that I was not allowed to shower (thanks, Dad) since we woke up so early and as a
result suffered the entire day. I would have otherwise had a good time. Yay, family vacations are
fun! Mike Vargas 7/14/2001
