 London 
 On our flight to Greece, we had an 11 hour layover in London. This was
cool in that we got to see a little bit of London (neither of us had ever been
there). The only drawback was that we hadn't checked any luggage (we were
traveling fairly light) so we ended up carrying our luggage around all
day. This got to be a bit tiring. Oops. 
 Anyway we took the high-speed train from Heathrow airport into town and got
pretty confused at Paddington Station about where we should go. Thanks to
a friendly transit employee who took pity on us because we looked lost (probably
because we were), we finally got on the proper Underground line and found our
way to the touristy part of town. Here are a few of the better pictures
from London.
