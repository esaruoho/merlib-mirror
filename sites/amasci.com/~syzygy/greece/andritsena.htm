Andritsena (Ανδριτσαινα)

We had read about how fantastic the Temple of Vassae is, so we decided to visit it.  (See next page for that.)  However, the Temple of Vassae is in the middle of nowhere and the nearest town is the quaint little mountain village of Andritsena.  So we decided to stop there for the night.  This is definitely a place you could go if you wanted to get away from it all and experience the real Greece without all the tourist trappings.  We didn't meet anyone in town who spoke more than a few words of English.
