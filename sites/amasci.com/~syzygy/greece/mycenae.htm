Mycenae (Μυκήνες)

Mycenae was an ancient city which reached its height in the Late Bronze Age - between the 14th and 12th centuries BC - which means the ruins here are much older than most of the other ones we saw.

Lion Gate
The famous Lion Gate which served as the entrance to the walled city of Mycenae.  Carved of limestone, the lions are the oldest example of monumental sculpture in all of Europe.  You can read more about them here.
Lyn in Doorway of Treasury of Atreus
Although it is called a treasury due to the treasures found within, this was actually a tomb.  Like the ancient Egyptians, the ancient Myceneans buried their royalty in elaborate tombs full of treasures.  This type of tomb (there have been 8 found at Mycenae) is called a "tholos tomb" because of its shape.  (Tholos, as I mentioned on the Delphi page, means "beehive.")
Inside Treasury of Atreus
Lyn is shining a flashlight into the small side room which is off the main room of the tholos tomb.  The purpose of the side room has never been determined.

The acoustics while standing in the center of the room are quite extraordinary.  Echoes coming from the walls alternately cancel and reinforce each other, giving a bizarre effect almost like a flanger.
