Megalopolis 

 Megalopolis (&#924;&#949;&#947;&#945;&#955;&#972;&#960;&#959;&#955;&#951;) 

 Megalopolis (literally, Large City or Great City) was originally founded when
a number of small cities joined together as one large one. It was hoped
that the strength in numbers would help them to defend against the aggressive
Spartans. This unfortunately didn't work very well in the long run and the
glory days of Megalopolis didn't last long. 

 We didn't really stop in Megalopolis, but while driving through we did happen
to see some of the archeological sites by the side of the road and I stopped
long enough to take some pictures. 

 Echoes of the Past 
 I saw this out the car window and had to stop. I was struck by
 the juxtaposition of the ruined columns in the foreground and the
 smokestacks of the power plant in the background. Makes a rather
 poignant statement, I think.
