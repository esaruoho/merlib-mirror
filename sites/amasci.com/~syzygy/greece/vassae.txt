Vassae 

 Vassae (&#914;&#945;&#963;&#963;&#941;&#962;) 

 We had read about the spectacular Temple of Apollo Epicurius at Vassae. 
We had read about how it is very well preserved, and was designed by Ictinus,
the same architect who designed the Parthenon. We had read about how it stands
alone in the middle of a fantastic mountain landscape. Imagine our
surprise when we drove out to see it and instead saw an enormous tent. 

 Terraced Hillside 
 All over the mountainsides on the Peloponnese it is very common to
 see these terraces - remnants of ancient cities, presumably. 

 Canopy Over Temple of Vassae 
 We were expecting to see an incredible temple, but we actually saw
 this. There are renovations currently being performed on the
 Temple of Epicurean Apollo, and this huge canopy has been erected over
 it in order to protect it from the elements. This was obviously
 something of a disappointment.
 The canopy is evidently temporary and will be removed once
 restoration work is completed. Maybe we'll get to go back someday
 and see the temple for real. 
 You can see a picture of the temple without its dust cover on this
 page . It does look pretty impressive.
