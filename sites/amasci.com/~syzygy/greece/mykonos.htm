Mykonos (Μύκονος)

Ah... Mykonos.  This was one of our favorite places.  Mykonos is a very tourist-oriented place, but very beautiful and fun.  Lots of restaurants, shopping, and a famous nightlife scene.  We wished we'd spent more time there.

Pelican In Harbor
Mykonos has a few pelicans that wander around the harbor (and through the streets as you will see).  This one was fishing.
Lyn with Foo-Foo Drink at Paradise Beach
Beautiful beach, even though the water was a bit cold in May.  We stayed in a room at Paradise Beach the first night - Lyn got eaten alive by mosquitoes.  (It's only paradise during the day, apparently.)  Strangely, I only got a few bites.  They seemed to prefer her.  We spent most of the afternoon on the beach the next day.
Windmills Of Mykonos
One of the symbols of Mykonos, the windmills overlooking the water.
Lyn and Pelican
I think this was the back door of a restaurant and the pelican was looking for a handout.
Mykonos Street 1
The streets in the heart of Mykonos Town are all extremely narrow, and they never go in a straight line for very far.  It's incredibly easy to get lost, but getting lost tends to be fun.  You never know what is around the corner.
