Argos 

 Argos (&#902;&#961;&#947;&#959;&#962;) 

 Argos was a major city in ancient Greece, on the scale of Athens, Sparta, and
Corinth. We didn't spend too much time there; only a few hours to visit
the museum and archeological site. 

 Overlooking Theatre and Archeological Site 
 Lyn is sitting on a bench of the ancient theatre which is built into
 the side of a hill overlooking the archeological site (as well as the
 modern town of Argos in the background). The theatre had 81 tiers
 and a seating capacity of 20,000.
 The red brick structure to the right, partially obscured by trees, is
 the remains of the aqueduct (dating from Roman times). There are
 also Roman baths in that area. 

 Lyn Looking At Mosaic Floor 
 I believe this enclosed area, next to the aqueduct, actually was a
 pool containing water. Lyn is examining a tile mosaic floor
 partially showing through the dirt.
