ELECTRICITY MISCONCEPTIONS:

  • 1. BATTERIES & GENERATORS CREATE ELECTRICITY?
  • 2. ELECTRICITY IS A KIND OF EVENT?
  • 3. TOO BAD FRANKLIN SAID ELECTRONS ARE NEGATIVE?
  • 4. "ELECTRICITY" IS A PHENOMENON COMPOSED OF ENERGY?
  • 5. ELECTRONS FLOW AT THE SPEED OF LIGHT?
  • 6. ELECTRICITY LEAVES ONE BATTERY PLATE, THEN RETURNS TO THE OTHER?
  • 7. ELECTRICITY IS MADE OF ELECTRONS?
  • 8. STATIC ELECTRICITY IS THE OPPOSITE OF CURRENT ELECTRICITY?
  • 9. EACH INDIVIDUAL ELECTRON CARRIES ENERGY?

  • 11. ELECTRICITY IS WEIGHTLESS?

  • 13. "ELECTRICITY" IS A FORM OF ENERGY?
