A Little Southern Comfort 

 An Introduction to the Charm of 
Animal Crackers Farm 

 I had the pleasure to visit Animal Crackers first in April of
1996. Since then I have visited several times, this is two of the pictures I
took in 1998 that brings back pleasant memories of the time I spent with Myra.
Below is the view Myra and I had, sitting on her veranda just outside the
kitchen. She has these lovely old high back wooden rocking chairs, we would sit
there rocking and talking, and watching the llamas. You can see the little
bridge that crosses over to the pasture and barn area, there is a lovely little
"babbling brook" running under that bridge. Between the gentle wind
chimes, the natural sounds of the brook, it was so easy to just sit back, relax
and enjoy her Southern Hospitality. I think you will find that same charm on
Myra's web site and in her writing. 

 Please enjoy your visit here, I am sure Myra will delight you with
her Southern Charm and her very special talent as a Writer ....Sandy
