Llost Llama Lloves, Shaquille 

 Owned By 
Bob & Bobbi Griesel 

 GLF Shaquille O'Neil 

 April 18, 1993 - January 11, 1995 

 ILR number is 113092 

 Griesel Llama Farm 

 Shaquille was my gentle boy, a gelding because of his blue eye. We
kept him on the farm and didn't try to sell him, he was so gentle and easy that
we planned to keep him for public relations on the farm. 

 He went with me to my mother's retirement home on a hot August
afternoon, stood quietly while strangers petted him and stood around him. He
went to a show class with me, followed me through halter and showmanship class
but balked at some of the obstacles! 

 We decided to give a female away as a pet and sent Shaquille with
her to her new home and excited new llama owners. A big dog frightened
Shaquille, he jumped the fence and ran across the road with the dog behind him,
going into the wide river alone. Every time I think of him alone and
frightened, tears come to my eyes. Time and time again I see him, beautiful,
big, gentle Shaquille and I miss him, up in the boys pasture with his buddies
among the trees. 

 By Bobbi Griesel
