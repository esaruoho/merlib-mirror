Llost Llama Lloves 

 "For without words, in friendship, all thoughts, all desires, all
 expectations are born and shared, with joy that is unacclaimed." By Kahlil
 Gibran, The Prophet These pages are a memorial to some of our very special Llost Llama
 Lloves. Our wonderful Llamas seem to win a special place in our hearts, they
 become part of our lives and for some of us, they become our dear friend. They
 are there to hum away our stressful day, to look at us with those big eyes that
 somehow make us feel like they understand our hearts. "Their parting, whether we believe it is to a better place, or
 whatever our personal beliefs are, leaves a huge void in our lives. If that
 llama was one of a few or one of a hundred, we grieve the same. The spirits of
 our lost llamas live on in our hearts. Sometimes, just before dusk, when that
 special llama ballet begins, we're sure we sense some of those llama spirits
 joining in. " From Kathie Sprout 
 These pages
 are dedicated to some of those very special llamas.
