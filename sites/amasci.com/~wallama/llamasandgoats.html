I had no idea until recently, how well llamas and goats get along. Our neighbor has added a mother and baby goat to the pasture and the little one is really taken with our llamas. The patience of the llamas is always amazing especially when you see them with the young goat on their back. It's so funny to see a little goat climbing all over the llamas when they are kushed. My favorite pose is when the little goat gets on Charlie's back, when Charlie is standing up.
