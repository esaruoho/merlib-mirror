Having fun packing with Llamas 

 Playful Boys 

 This is typical yearling behavior, practicing
 hormone, cave man stage, who's the most macho games. They are not hurting each
 other , just showing some dominance. A lot of the time this is the way a play
 period starts between these two. Then they switch to nibbling on legs, tail,
 neck and chasing each other around the field. Drake, the one whose back is to
 you, is about 4 months younger than Charlie...they have grown up together.

 Drake scared me half to death one day, I was in the house
 and heard this blood curdling Llama Scream. I ran out just in time to see
 Charlie chasing Drake, not touching him anywhere, and Drake was letting out
 these screams. Later I learned that this is just part of what they do to try to
 intimidate the other llama. In this case it wasn't working. 

 Drakes's screaming is becoming so obnixous that I think
 it's time to call the Vet.
