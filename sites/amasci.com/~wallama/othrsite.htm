Web Development Tools and Resources

If you are in the beginning stages of learning how to Design Web Sites, I would like to offer a few suggestions that might help you get started. It is strongly recommended that you get a few beginner books so that you understand how the Internet works and the basics of HTML. It may look easy when you first begin but there is a lot more to becoming a Web Site Developer than just creating a pretty page. For those of you that are experienced you can jump from here to the Links Menu
Finally you finish your first page and it looks great on your computer, you place it on a server and find out that it looks horrible to other people with different browsers, screen resolutions, monitor sizes etc. Back to the drawing board you go, to try and figure out how to fix your pages so they look good on other systems. Next you think you've got it down pat, then somebody writes and tells you that they are viewing your site from Lynx (a text viewer) and can't read you pages at all, doesn't know where your links go, have no idea what the graphics are supposed to represent. Did you remember to use your ALT tag with your images? This is just one of the many problems you will run into, understanding these differences before you get started with save you a lot of time and frustration.
Oh, did I mention uploading your pages to your server? That was my 1st real surprise, UNIX is what the majority of servers use and it has a different language than DOS. Do you know the UNIX commands that you will need to be able to give permission for people to see your documents, to change directories, make new directories? I suggest you talk to your ISP and find out what you will need to do and then learn a little about how UNIX commands work.
Sometimes you start to feel like your Mission is Impossible, that's when I stop and go back to basics. When I first started I wanted to know how to not only use HTML, but I wanted to learn Cgi, Perl, Java, Java Script, Animation, Graphics...I had books all over the floor and desk until I couldn't find anything. Well I still don't know Perl or Java, but I'm not ready yet and couldn't do anything well if I tried to do it all at once. Each day I learn a few new things and I'm happy with my progress. I started writing web pages 6 months ago and already have written pages for other people
If you want to earn extra income from developing web sites do you know how to market or plan a business? Do you understand networking? Do you know how to promote yourself? In my case I do not have a web site the promotes me as a web designer, I do however get a lot of requests from people that visit my site. I designed my site to be educational and fun...won't think that people visiting a Llama web site would end up doing business with me, would you?
Good luck in your adventure with the Internet, I hope you find some links here that can be of use to you. Please let me know if there are other pages you find that you would like to see linked here.
