 (Click on Photo to enlarge) Sparky drinking water out of a
 jug...he learned to do this by accident. One very hot day I saw him laying all
 sprawled out in the pasture, my first thought was something was wrong. I ran
 down with water in a jug having no idea what I was doing. He was perfectly
 fine, just taking a nap and me sneaking up on him just about gave him a heart
 attach. When pouring the water out he became curious and came over to
 investigating what I was doing. I guess he decided to take advantage of the
 room service and started drinking the water.
