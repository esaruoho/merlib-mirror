As I saw him when we were first married
This is how Jerry looked when we got married 4 years ago. He was so charming and good looking he just swept me off my feet. Everyday he got up, put on his suit and tie and was off to the office. Love a man in a suit...

Senator or Maybe Governor??
Can't you just see him running for a political office, or maybe a Senator in a TV series? Maybe even a preacher ?

Jerry the California Party Animal
This was Jerry's retirement lunch/party, and I hired the belly dancer. He was so embarrassed but enjoyed every moment. This was 4 days and 2 more parties before we left CA to start our new life in WA. Never at that time did we think our lives would change so much, we didn't even know what a Llama was.

Honey is Lunch Ready?
Well, so much for the suit and tie. Besides he has out grown his suit and now wears suspenders. This was when he was still shy and wore them under his shirt. Now he wears them over his shirt. No more polished shoes, rubber knee boots replaced them.

Stranger in the Night?
NEWS FLASH!!!! "Earth's gravity has suddenly increased! Hundreds of people have called to report being stuck to the floor! We'll have exclusive film at eleven." Well, it was a long day...Jerry and Jake are just worn out from building fences, planting the garden, raking up the poop pile, chopping wood...oh, and then there was the trip to the feed store to pick up more Hay. I guess we are really married now and have both been making our subtitle changes. Isn't life grand.
