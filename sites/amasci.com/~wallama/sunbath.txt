Sun Bathing Llama 

 Tip: When shearing a llama you can use sissors,
 sheep shears or electric shears. 

 This is Pedro in the process of taking a dust bath. Our boys
 have cleared off several areas on our property for the express purpose of
 taking a dust bath. It's fun to watch them rolling around in the dust on the
 back and going from side to side. This activity opens up their wool and some of
 the debri is released. It's amazing especially after it been raining and they
 are such a mess, how quickly they clean up using their process of bathing. 
 Out of the 7 boys that are here, only 2 lay on their back when
 it's sunny. Suprisingly enough they are the only two black llamas, and you
 would think the least likely to be sunbathing. Recently somebody suggested that
 perhaps those that express this behavior have a need for vitamin D that the sun
 provides. It's rather funny to see a black llama laying in the sun while the
 light colored guys are standing in the creek to cool off. As you can see he is
 laying close to the creek but will not go in the water.

 Pedro is totally zonked out on a hot day and he is laying in the
 llamas dust bath. Some of the llama's actually lay flat on their back with all
 four legs sticking up in the air. Nobody really knows why some llama's lay on
 their back in the Sun? Many a llama rancher has been informed that he had a
 dead llama in his pasture by some well meaning passer by. If you saw a horse or
 cow like this, well, he probably is dead!
