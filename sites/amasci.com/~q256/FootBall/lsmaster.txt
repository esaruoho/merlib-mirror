Semi Live Stats 

 To track your team: 

 Click on the check mark to the upper left to attempt to connect. 
 If you get 'failure to connect', time out errors or just about any 
 other errors - the server to the service is not up at this time or 
 the internet isn't playing very nice. The server should be running 
 the scoring fromthe kick off of the first Sunday game until I get 
 the final scores after Monday night football. If for some odd reason 
 you think it should be working but isn't please drop me a line 
 q256@eskimo.com 
 If that does not work check out the ' Starting 
 Line Up ' link. This is where you see the posted starting line 
 ups of all the teams. For convience - data is mirrored to the 'starting 
 line up' in case the internet isn't playing nice AND outside of the 
 game(s) times (ie Friday/Saturday of week 1). 

 To refresh the game data during the day - hit the check mark again 
 to refresh the page or use the refresh function on your browser.
