This is the second year of the franchise change. In this year the draft options are more complex for holding players.

2003 Schedule

 

Due to the nature of the franchise league - at the end of the season - all rosters have to drop to 2-qb, 4/5 - Rb, 6/4 - Wr, 2 - Te, 1 - Fran. There can be no other players on the roster. The Def is not a position that is retainable to the next year. No players can be on the IR since that is give that team more players carried to next year vice a team without players on IR. Dropped players will be able to be picked up from the pool of players (as if adding a player during the season). All rosters must be trimmed down after the last game of our year with one additional week allowed for player acquisitions. Also - keep in mind - you can only carry up to 4 players into next year. They have to be number of players per position that your would normally start in that position (Qb, 2 Wr, Wr/Rb, 2 Rb, Te, K). So - you would not be able to carry two Qbs since of course you only start one. That is a reminder - the roster requirement prevents teams of putting several players onto their roster or carrying more players than another team to possible declare later as the retained players.
