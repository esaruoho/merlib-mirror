Who am I?

Yes, I know I should upload a picture, but I'm lazy about tech things, like other Aquarian Single Celtic White Male Feminist Ecological Activists listed in Who's Who in the World. If your name is Affleck, you're probably a relative. My ex-wife Carla Holley and I have joint mutual shared custody of Ian Affleck-Asch, who's 6 years old and very cute (I've been told I am too). I do mostly database work, live in Seattle (deepest darkest Ballard), love alternative music and anything blues or blues-influenced. Tori Amos rules! Used to be a SMOF and a SMOG, published in a number of countries (SF articles, SF&F Role-playing games), did radio scripts (SF and Children's stories) and the first alternative energy TV show in British Columbia (where I lived from '71 to '89), and have two major themes in my life: Applied Chaos Theory and Changing the World.

Also started the movement to get most computer magazines and national magazines to use recycled paper for those magazine insert cards that fall out or are bound in. And to change your junk mail to recycled paper. How do you do this? Easy: applied environmental economics! Send all the cards and junk mail back to the sender on the postage-paid cards and envelopes and write (I use a rubber stamp now) Use Recycled Paper for these cards (and other similar messages) on the part which they expect to find your name and address. And send back the non-recyclable plastic windows too.

I'm silly, serious, sensuous and into a lot of things. Like pirate songs...
