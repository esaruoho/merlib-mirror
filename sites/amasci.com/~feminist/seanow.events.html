Most Seattle NOW meetings are held in The Good Shepherd Center, just off I-5 on N 50th St (entrance on Sunnyside Ave N), Seattle NOW phone 206-632-8547.

November 3, Tuesday, GENERAL ELECTION, EVERYWHERE IN WASHINGTON. Did you check the NOW/PAC and WA NOW/PAC Endorsements (click here)? No on I-200, No on I-694, Yes on I-688, VOTE!

November 12, Thursday, Seattle NOW Board Meeting, 7:00pm, Good Shepherd Center, Conference Room 221, Seattle, WA. Election Day Update - what are our gains and losses? Open to all members and interested individuals.

December 8, Tuesday, Seattle NOW Annual Meeting and Elections, 7:00pm - 9:00pm, Seattle Central Community College, Room 1110 (south end of campus), Seattle, WA. 6:30pm Meet and Greet (new members). Potluck and Fun! A great opportunity to get involved!

December 17, Thursday, Seattle NOWSLETTER Editorial Meeting, 7:00pm, Good Shepherd Center, Seattle NOW office (Rm 222), Seattle, WA. Articles and volunteers are welcome.

January 8, 1999, Friday, Seattle NOWSLETTER Mailing Party, 7:00pm, Good Shepherd Center, Conference Room 221, Seattle, WA. Munchies of all kinds welcome!

April, 1999, TBA, Northwest Regional NOW Conference, Place TBA, Washington. For more information, contact Linda Tosti-Lane, Regional Director. Pencil it on the calendar now and start planning to go and split driving and sleeping costs.

May, 1999, TBA, WA State NOW Conference, elections, and board meeting, Place TBA, WA. For more information, contact Linda Tosti-Lane, Regional Director. All NOW members or potential NOW members are invited to join us for a really fun time. SAVE THE DATE!

July, 1999, TBA, 1999 National NOW Conference, Place TBA. Can't say you didn't have adequate warning. Start saving now. Preceded by July National Board Meeting. To vote at this conference, you must be elected at your chapter as a delegate in advance. Contact Linda Tosti-Lane, Regional Director, to ask questions about the conference.
