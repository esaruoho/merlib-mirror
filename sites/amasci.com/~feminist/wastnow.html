Washington State NOW Mailing Lists

We currently sponsor two email mailing lists, both focussed on the Pacific Northwest (WA, OR, ID, MT, BC, N.CA, HI, AK) and especially Washington state. To join, email either pnwfem-gen-request@eskimo.com or pnwfem-leg-request@eskimo.com with the word subscribe in the Subject: field.

PNWFEM-LEG is a mailing list hosted by Washington State NOW for Legislative and Legal issues, open to all NOW members as well as any feminists interested in women's issues and information and ideas about legislation. The list is not moderated, but postings of a harassing or consistently off-topic nature will not be tolerated. Legislative or legal issues of a local, state, regional, national, and international nature are the focus of this list, as pertain to the Pacific Northwest region (WA, OR, ID, MT, BC, and even AK, HI, and CA). Once you subscribe to the list, you can send email to the list at pnwfem-leg@eskimo.com. Only email from list members is posted to the list.
To get the digest version email pnwfem-leg-digest-request@eskimo.com with the same subscribe message; a digest means you get one big email every day or two instead of 4-20 separate emails. To unsubscribe, send the word unsubscribe in the Subject: field to the request address.

PNWFEM-GEN is a mailing list hosted by Washington State NOW for General issues (non-legislative and non-legal issues), open to all NOW members as well as other feminists. The list is not moderated, but postings of a harassing or consistently off-topic nature will not be tolerated. Non-legislative issues of any feminist-related nature are the focus of this list, as pertain to the Pacific Northwest region (WA, OR, ID, MT, BC, and even AK, HI, and CA). Once you subscribe to thelist, you can send email to the list at pnwfem-gen@eskimo.com. Only email from list members is posted to the list.
To get the digest version email pnwfem-gen-digest-request@eskimo.com with the same subscribe message. To unsubscribe, send the word unsubscribe in the Subject: field to the request address.
