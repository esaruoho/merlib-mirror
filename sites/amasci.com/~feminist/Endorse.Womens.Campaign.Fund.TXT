--WOMEN'S CAMPAIGN FUND ENDORSEMENTS. WCF is the oldest PAC supporting
pro-choice women candidates of both parties. WCF tries to give the
maximum $5,000 contribution to the candidates it supports. Members write
checks to WCF, 734 Fifteenth St NW #500, Washington DC 20005.

WCF has endorsed:
-Natalie Davis (D/AL/+) for the Senate seat being vacated by Howell
Heflin (D/-). Davis, a WLO member and political science professor who
also runs a market research business, needs $1.2 million for the June 4
primary.
-Nancy Mayer (R/RI/+) for the Senate seat being vacated by Claiborne Pell
(D/+). Mayer, the State Treasurer, needs $500,000 to beat her anti-choice
opponent in the September primary.
-Ramona Martinez (D/CO/+) for the Senate seat being vacated by Hank Brown
(R/CO/-). Martinez, who served as President of the Denver City Council
and championed the needs of children and families, needs to win the
support of 30% of the delegates to the June 8 convention in order to
appear on the ballot for the August 13 primary.
-Virginia Blankenbaker (R/IN10/+) for the House seat vacated by Andrew
Jacobs (D/+). Blankenbaker, an investment broker who was previously a
teacher and state senator, faces a Christian-Coalition backed candidate
in the May 7 primary.
-Dolores Briones (see above)
-Kathleen Donovan (R/NJ/+) for the House seat vacated by Bob Torricelli
(D/+). Donovan, the Bergen County Clerk who is the first woman chair of
the Port Authority of NY and NJ and the first woman NJ GOP chair, needs
$1.5 million for the June 4 primary.
-Linda Stensland (D/SD/+) for the state-wide House seat vacated by Tim
Johnson (D/SD/-). Stensland, a former League of Women Voters chapter
president and veteran state legislator, needs $200,000 for the June 4
primary.
