Hyperfaceted™

Hyperfaceted is proud to present custom songs and single tracks from artists who have original and unique work.

We actively seek local artists* to record their original songs, concepts, and live productions.

It doesn't matter how big or small though admittedly we have a preference for Rock, Metal, Techno, Folk and especially work that features stringed instruments such as guitars, violins, base, banjos, etc.

Contact: Jill England

* What do I mean local artists? I'm in Seattle, which is on the coast of the Pacific, which is on Earth. If you can read this you are close enough. It's a small universe. ;)

Artist Comments

Music is Magic, every note, every nuance, every touch on the strings brings life to the universe. With every song you write and perform you cast a spell, which effects yourself and those who hear it. Can you rip this simple rift from your mind? I can't, I hear it over and over, with different chords, different fills, new bass tracks, differnt lyrics.

Every song is a spell. They are Magic of the oldest kind. The ancient symbol of a pentagram inside a circle is a representaion of basic music theory. Wield songs with care, as with all spells they will cut from both sides.

I can't help it, it's just the way it is.
