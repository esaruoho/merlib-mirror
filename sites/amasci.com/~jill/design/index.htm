Software Design
A Comentary

Software is an engineering and math problem. In many ways as in building architecture it is also a form of art. While there are infinite ways to view software only some are proveably correct, only a few methods of software analysis convey the scope of a piece of software and limit the design to just the essential system components.

Essential and brief systems design is required, not excessively verbose. In the end the technology of a software system is in the people who build it and use it and not in the code itself. Technology is in people and not in the artifacts of their creations.

With that in mind it is important to view the software design process as one of growth and learning about what is desired, vs. what is possible. It is a never ending cycle of re-thinking and re-writing.

The bottom line is that a design must have limits and boundaries so that the problem with that scope can be fully understood. Use of industry standard methods, such as Object and Modular designs and use diagrams, and object patterns, is essential for building and thinking about systems.
