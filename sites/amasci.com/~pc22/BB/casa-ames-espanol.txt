 Alcides and Francisca Ames operate a really excellent guest house very
conveniently located in the center of the Huaraz, in the center of the Cordillera
Blanca. They provide: 

 Comfortable rooms with private showers and views
 Hot showers, 24 hours a day
 Free luggage storage, while you are climbing
 Tourist, trekking and climbing information
 Excellent local meals and delicacies

 Many of their visitors are climbers, and Alcides himself, besides being
a professional geologist, is a very experienced climber.
