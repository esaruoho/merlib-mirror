Residencia de Ames "My House" en Huaraz

[English]

Alcides Ames & Francisca de Ames,
Av. 27 de Noviembre 773, (Av. Tarapaca)
Huaraz, Perú, +(51-44) 72.33.75

Alcides and Francisca Ames operate a really excellent guest house very conveniently located in the center of the Huaraz, in the center of the Cordillera Blanca. They provide:

  • Comfortable rooms with private showers and views
  • Hot showers, 24 hours a day
  • Free luggage storage, while you are climbing
  • Tourist, trekking and climbing information
  • Excellent local meals and delicacies

Many of their visitors are climbers, and Alcides himself, besides being a professional geologist, is a very experienced climber.
