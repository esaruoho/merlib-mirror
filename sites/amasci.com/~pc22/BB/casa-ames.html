Alcides Ames & Francisca de Ames,
Av. 27 de Noviembre 773, (Av. Tarapaca)
Huaraz, Perú, +(51-44) 72.33.75

Alcides and Francisca Ames operate a really excellent guest house very conveniently located in the center of the Huaraz, in the heart of the Cordillera Blanca. They provide:

  • Comfortable rooms with private showers and views
  • Hot showers, 24 hours a day
  • Free luggage storage, while you are climbing
  • Tourist, trekking and climbing information
  • Excellent local meals and delicacies

Many of their visitors are climbers, and Alcides himself, besides being a professional geologist, is a very experienced climber. He also knows many of the guides, arrieros (burro drivers) and people in town. The B&B is very close to the following:

  • Casa de Guias (guide house)
  • Market
  • Taxi stand for transportation to mountain valleys
  • Hills for local jogging
