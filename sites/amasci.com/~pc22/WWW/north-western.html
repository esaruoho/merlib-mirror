North Western Cascades

Community Transit (800-562-1375)

Bike racks on all buses.

  • Darrington/Oso: Service from Arlington and Everett. The Mt Higgins and Whitehorse mountain trailheads are still 20-30mins walk from the bus stops; bike combinations are quite reasonable though.
  • Granite Falls: Service from Everett. (The Mountain Loop trailheads are still distant from Granite Falls; a bike and time will be needed)
  • Gold Bar, Sultan and Startup: Service from Everett via Monroe; Wallace Falls trailhead is within walking distance of the bus stop in Gold Bar. There is an excellent mountain bike trail to Wallace Lake from Gold Bar. Sultan Basin trailheads are a grunt with a bike from Sultan, but possible.

 

Ross Lake Water Taxi (206-386-4437)

  • Ross Lake Trailheads: from Memorial day through the end of October, the water taxi can be used to access the North Cascades NP and the Pasayten wilderness via Ross Lake. Advanced reservations advised for round trip or one way pick-up or drop-off.

Skagit Transit (360-757-4433)

Bike racks on all buses.

  • Marblemount/Newhalem: Service from Mt Vernon, via Concrete and Rockport. A bike will be useful to reach trailheads, though the Helen Buttes trailhead is only a mile from the highway.

Northwest Excursion Transportation (360) 714-1111
