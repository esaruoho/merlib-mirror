Climbing Outings

Mountaineers Climbing Outings are usually extended trips to seldom visited areas. They differ from regular Basic and Intermediate climbs in not being part of the regular instructional program, though credit for objectives may often be obtained, with the approval of the appropriate basic or intermediate climbs subcommittee. These trips take place over a number of days, and the exact day and climbing objective is not normally decided until the trip is underway, though of course, several possible objectives and routes are normally listed in an announcement.
