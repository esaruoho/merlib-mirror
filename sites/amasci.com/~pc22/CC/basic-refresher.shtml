2001 Basic Climbing Refresher Course (Course #25507)

This course provides a review of basic climbing skills and provides an opport unity to learn the latest methods for skills such as belaying and crevasse rescu e, as taught in the Seattle climbing courses. The course is open only to member s of The Mountaineers who 1) are graduates of any branch Basic Climbing Course, or 2) have been granted Basic Equivalency, or 3) have approved extensions for th e Basic Climbing Course. This course is offered only once a year.

How to Register: Complete the Course Registration Form (in the back of the G o Guide). Include your graduation date from the Basic Climbing Course, or the d ate you were granted Basic Equivalency, or a copy of your approved extension for the Basic Climbing Course.

Registration Deadline: Monday, March 12, 2001

Course Fee: $60. Payable to "The Mountaineers". Refunds will be made only if you withdraw from the course prior to the second session (or if the course is cancelled due to lack of signup). The course fee does not include the cost of the text, Mountaineering: The Freedom of the Hills (6th Edition) published by Mountaineers Books. Students who do not already have a copy may purchase the text at The Mountaineers Bookstore at the student discount (25%) price of $18.36 (softcover) or $24 (hardcover) plus tax available at the Mountaineers Bookstore at a 25% student discount.

Course Structure: The course consists of three lecture/practice sessions and two all-day field trips. Registrants will receive the Course Syllabus by mail before the first session.

Schedule:

March 25 (Sunday): At the clubhouse, 5-9 pm. Introduction. Review and pra ctice of knots, belays, prusiks, and rope handling.

April 8 (Sunday): At Seattle Vertical World, 7-9 pm. Professional instruct ion and practice of rock climbing techniques.

April 14 (Saturday): At Mt. Erie, all day. Top-roped rock climbing.

April 30 (Monday): At Paramount Playfield, Shoreline, 6:30-9 pm. Crevasse R escue practice.

May 5 (Saturday): At Hyak Ski Area, all day. Snow climbing and crevasse res cue field trip.
