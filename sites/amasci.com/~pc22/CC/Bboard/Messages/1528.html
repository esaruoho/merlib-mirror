Went up to the base of the Turtle on Friday 7/20. Rain and sleet from 3pm through 7am. Clear and 35 40 mph winds at midnight--warm. Nobody went up on Saturday. 11-14" new snow reported at Ingraham Flats. Met two rangers on the way down from the Thumb Friday and the Finger looked good. Reports from a party that turned back on the Kautz Fri said the chute was icy and had water running through it. Don't know how things went Sunday.
