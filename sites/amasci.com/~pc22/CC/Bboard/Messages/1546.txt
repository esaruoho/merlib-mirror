Posted by Gordon Schryer on July 31, 2001 at 11:53:59: 
Snoqualmie 5 Summit Weekend Sept 1-3. The thought is to do Kendal, Red, Thompson, Huckleberry and Chikamin between Saturday and Monday. In that order, two the first day, two the second day and the last on Monday followed by a long walk back. All of these are scrambles with the exception of Thompson which requires IR status to be done on the Sunday. I am also looking for a strong assitant leader for this to take the responsibility of walking the party back to the trailhead on Monday, as my personal plans are to extend the trip into the alpine lakes for the rest of the week. If you are interested in this plan, part of it, or have some suggested alterations, let me know. You can contact me via e-mail or at 206-295-2933. GS.
