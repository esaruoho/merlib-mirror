Club Climbs Summit Savvy

You're all familiar with the Summit Savvy in the back of The Mountaineer magazine right? Well, by popular demand, here's our version. Now, the traditional summit savvy we know and love is in b&w, and a little fuzzy, so even well known summits can be tough to guess. Here though, we have the summit in technicolour, so you can expect them to be a little more obscure.

Feel free to submit your own favourite photos! I can scan almost anything, or you can point me to a JPEG to use if you'd like to make it even easier.

Don't feel limited to summit shots - anything worthwhile can be used... Here's an easy one to get us started.

Now, you can send in your guess right here - the winner will be the first one to submit a correct guess, and will be announced each month.
