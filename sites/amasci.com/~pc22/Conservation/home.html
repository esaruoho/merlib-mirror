Conservation Forestry

Conservationforestry.org is a web site for the private forest owner who wishes to conserve and improve the conservation values of the land he or she owns. Private non-industrial forests in the United States are of enormous ecological and economic importance, but they are threatened by development and subdivision. Fragmentation of forests reduces their utility both as ecological habitat and as working economic units. This web site is a tool kit for the private forest owner who wishes to maintain and improve the value &endash; economic, aesthetic, and ecologic &endash; of his or her forest land.

This site is designed to provide information on a spectrum of conservation options, but concentrates specifically on one specific legal instrument: the conservation easement. Conservation easements are unique because they allow enormous flexibility for the landholder, often confer significant financial benefit, and extend beyond the life of the individual making the easement. Establishing a conservation easement is conceptually straightforward, but the various documents that are used to create the easement can be complex, cumbersome, and expensive. This web site is designed to provide model examples that can be modified to suit the needs of any individual forest owner. Perhaps more importantly, this site is designed to help the private forest owner choose among the many options for conservation and continued use of his or her forest land.

Although this site will provide a set of documents that can be used to achieve a specific legal; purpose, the are not a substitute for sound professional advice. We recommend that each landowner use the professional services of a real estate attorney, appraiser, and consulting forester in the process of establishing a conservation easement. This web site can help streamline the process, but should not be used as the sole basis of creating and legally binding document.
