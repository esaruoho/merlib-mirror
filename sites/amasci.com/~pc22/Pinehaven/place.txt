Pinehaven: Geography 

 Geography 

 We've had great fun exploring and naming features. 
 The fried eggs (Roger's name, from the topographic contours) are neat little hills overlooking Homestead Way (the old jeep track that connects the access road to an old homestead site from the early part of this century. Ball Point overlooks Homestead Way from the the other side. 
 The Troika is a great example of glacial erratics in the middle of the Parklands . Closeby is the Lost World Plateau , an area where the bedrock reaches the surface, and nothing much grows. 
 Eeyore's Corner is indeed a dark and gloomy place, at the extreme northwest of Pinehaven, right underneath Antoine Point , which has expansive views out to the Pasayten wilderness and Loomis Forest to the West , Hailey Mountain to the North, Mt Bonaparte, Mt Phoebe and the Okanogan Forest to the East , and Tunk Mountain to the South. 
 The Western Meadow is at the edge, bordering State land, containing the seasonal outflow from the wetland, which descends further to the Springbox , a deliciously cool place in the August heat. 
 The Wetlands contain a veritable cornucopia of frogs and other acquatic life. In the spring, you'd never get any sleep around here. Directly above the wetlands is Lake Arvia - one of our favourite places, refreshing in Summer, not least of which because the hoards of Dragonflies eat all the bugs! 
 Broken Foot Gluch is a small ravine named in honour of our friend Alina, who bravely scrambled down its contours on our first work party, and leads to the Glade might easily have been the place Robin Hood met his merry men to plot the Sheriff's downfall. 
 The Ravine is the south-eastern portion, leading down to Antoine Creek and contains the lowest elevation of Pinehaven.
