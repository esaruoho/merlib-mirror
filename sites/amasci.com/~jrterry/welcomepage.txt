 I hope you find yourself right in your element! 

 You may be wondering why this page exists. Well, the
reason is that I have had a lifelong obsession with street lighting. I
don't know how it got started, but when I was five years old, I could tell
(during the daylight) which street lights were high-pressure sodium and
which were mercury vapor. The topic of street lights has always
fascinated me, and I always have an eye to the sky. 

I am a graduate of Washington State University, where I earned a degree in
Communications. I am always on the lookout for more material for my page. 
If you have a link or anything you consider relevant, please send me an
email at jrterry@eskimo.com 

This picture to the right is me, with my first street light. It's a GE
M250 which was given to me by Puget Power in Seattle. For
those interested, I have stripped out the ballast and put in a 160 watt
self-ballasted mercury bulb.
