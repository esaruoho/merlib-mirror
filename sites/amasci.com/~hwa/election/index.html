Index Page

This section will contain samples of handwriting of candidates running for political office, and a graphological report of that handwriting.

The rules for inclusion are simple:

  • All candidates for the office have an opportunity to put their writing sample, and graphological profile here.
  • The election must be partisan.
  • Seattle WA Municiple Elections
  • Washington State Elections
  • President of the United States of America

If you are a political candidate for office, who would like to see your handwriting here, write out The Consent Form in your own handwriting, and then write two or three pages. The content of those pages is irrelevent to me. Some candidates find it easiest to simply write out their election platform.

In your writing sample, please include the following:

  • Position you are running for
  • Political Party you are with. Do not abreviate. [ is RP the Revolutionary Party or Republican Party? Or another party that I have not yet heard of. ]
  • When the election is being held. Day, month and year.

  • Where the election is being held. Country, State/Province, County/Shire, City/Town/Village. For Example: USA, Georgia, Carroll County, Carrollton.
  • A contact address for you, or your political organization.

Then mail your handwriting samples to:
Jonathon Blake
P O Box 95229
Seattle WA 98145
USA
