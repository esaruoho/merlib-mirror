 IGAS Membership Numbers

 On 16 January 2003, IGAS permanently closed its doors. A few
 people wanted to know how many people that action left stranded. A few
 others wanted to know how much its growth had dropped over the last few
 years. 

 By getting the date that the person signed up the general course
 in Graphoanalysis, and their membership number, one can make estimates
 of both of those figures. The document below has the results. 

 Whilst the data reflects information I was given, I would like to
have more datapoints. If you were a member of IGAS, send 
 Jonathon Blake an email stating
your membership number, and the date that you signed up for the course.
I am not interested in the date [ Month and Year ] you graduated from the General
Course. That date doesn't help things at all. [ If given that date, I 
deduct eighteen months from it, and state that is the date of joining
IGAS. There are several instances where I did that in the document below.
] 

 As I get more numbers and dates, I will update this document. 

 The numbers from the spreadsheet. PDF Format . 

 To read this document software that reads PDF files is needed. 
 The following programs allegedly work: 

 PDF+ : Symbian OS 
 !PDF : RISC OS 
 Xpdf: Solaris / Linux / SPARC / DOS 6.0 / Win32/ Source Code 

 Adobe Acrobat : They invented the format, and have it available for 24 platforms. 
 Between the above, your operating platform should be covered.
 The only known exception is users of Braille Display Screens and/or screen reader programs.
