 Consent Form 

 Terms and Conditions. 

 The Consent Form must be handwritten
and signed by the individual whose writing is to be analyzed. 

 I do not analyze writing without the written consent of the
individual whose writing is to be analyzed. 

 A minimum of five pages of writing should be provided.
Paper size should be A4, or similar [ 8.5 inches by 11 inches ]. 

 I do not analyze writing that is reproduced, either
electronically or mechanically. That includes, but is not limited
to photocopies, scanned images, faxes or Xerox images. 

Writing samples in any language are accepted if
one of the following conditions are met: 

 Writing system uses an alphabet. 
 Writing system uses an abjad. 
 Writing system uses a syllabary.
