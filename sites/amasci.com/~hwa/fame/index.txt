 Index of The Graphology-L Hall of Fame 

 Current requirements for the Graphology-L Hall of Fame are: 
 The individual must be deceased 
 The organization must be defunct 
 The journal must no longer be published 

 Nominations are accepted at any time. 
 Nominations may be made by anybody. 
 Nominations may be made in any language. 

 Nominations for Individuals should have the following: 
 At least one sample of their usual handwriting 
 A graphological profile drawn from that sample 
 A photograph of the individual 
 An audio lecture done by the individual 
 Complete data on where and when they were born 
 Complete data on where, when, and how they died. 

 Nominations for Journals should have the following: 
 Date of first publication 
 Date of final publication 
 Total number of issues published 
 Name of the editor who founded the publication 
 Name of the editor when the publication ceased operations 
 List of contents of a typical issue. 

 Nominations for Organizations should have the following: 
 Date it was founded 
 Date it ceased operations 
 Name of the Founder 
 Name of the President, CEO, and/or Leader of the organization
 with their title when it ceased operations. 
 Aims of the Organization 
 The Code of Ethics of the Organization 
 A History of the Organization 

 I would encourage individuals from countries other than
 the United States to submit nominations. The Hall of Fame
 is far to US-Centric. 

 I would also encourage nominations for people and organizations
 who pioneered graphology, especially in countries such as
 the Commonwealth of Nations, The Commonwealth of Great Britain,
 Brazil, Argentina, and Japan. 
 People are listed by surname. In some instances there is a
 cross-reference from alternative names.
