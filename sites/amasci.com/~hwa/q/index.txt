 This pages in this section contain graphics, about which between
 five and twenty questions are asked, depending upon the writing
 sample(s). Where there are "correct" answers, I have provided them
 on a linked page. Where there are no "correct" answers, I explain
 why there is no correct answer. For a graphologists who knows their
 field, these questions would simply be a ten minute break -- a relaxation.
 For one who does not know their subject, these questions which do have
 correct answers, are extremely difficult to answer.
