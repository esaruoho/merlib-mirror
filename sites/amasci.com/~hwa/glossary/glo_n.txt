 The Letter~N 

 The Letter~N 

 Nabatae 

 Writing system in Northwestern Arabia that is believed to be the source for Modern
 Arabic.

 Nakhi 

 Also know as Moso, this language spoken in the Yunnan Province of China It uses a
 pictographic script.

 Nastaliq 

 Style of Arabic writing.

 Naskhi 

 Style of Arabic writing.

 Ndjuka 

 Writing system created by Afaka in 1916. The Ndjuka language is an English based
 Creole.

 Negative Correlation 

 The degree to which the presence of one item, is correlated with the absence of a different
 item.

 Negligent Capitals 

 Copybook that is marked by angular, square, irregular formed of
 broken strokes with circumflex finals See also
 Rustic. 

 Nestorian Script 

 Writing system used by Nestorianism, a Christian sect. This script is also a Syriac
 script.

 Ngiyambaa 
 First Nation language
 of Australia that uses an evidential grammar.
 In this instance the forms are: Sensory Evidence Reported Evidence .

 Nominal Numbers 

 Numbers used to name something

 Nominal Scale 

 Observations of unordered variables. 
 Scale in which mutually exclusive items are placed. 

 Non Probability Sampling Techniques 

 Techniques which sample p[opulations, without taking into account probability.

 Normal Curve 

 A frequency curve with a characteristic bell-shaped form.

 Nsibidi 

 Ideographic writing system found in Nigeria.

 Null 

 A latter or symbol in a cipher alphabet chosen to mean nothing. 

 Nuskhuri 
 Form of Khutsuri which uses small letters only.
