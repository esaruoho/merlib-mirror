The Letter~F

FA Slant
Upslant of between 58 and 90 degrees in Graphoanalysis.

Face Validity
The degree to which a measure appears to measure the attribute or behaviour it is supposed to measure.

False Negative
A result that identifies X as being absent, when, in fact, it is present.

False Positive
A result that identifies X as being present, when, in fact, it is absent.

Aunt Fanny Effect
My Aunt was a good person. This person is like my aunt. Therefore this person is a good person.

Featural Script
A type of writing system whose characters denote phonetic features.

Fëanorian
Writing system of Middle Earth. The Elves of the West developed a cursive form, of this writing system. For more details, The Lord of The Rings (Tolkein: 1965). Appendix E describes this language.

Fisher Price Copy Book
This is the copybook that is found on the blocks that Fisher Price sells as toys for babies. Blind children are taught it, as the letters can be fairly easilly felt, and thus "translated" into forms that a blind person can write.

F- Slant
Upslant of between 30 and 58 degrees in Graphoanalysis.

F-- Slant
Upslant of between 0 and 30 degrees in Graphoanalysis.

Five Basic Strokes
    The five basic strokes of the cursive latin alphabet are:
  1. The Line
  2. The Oval
  3. The Circle
  4. The Square
  5. The Angle

Flemish
A dialect of Dutch that is spoken in northern Belgium.

Forensic Document Examiner
  1. Forensic document examiners are not handwriting analysts. What they do, is authenticate that a specific document was produced at a specific time, in a specific place, by a specific person. This term is a synonym for Questioned Document Examiner.
  2. An individual who authenticates that a specific document was produced at a specific time, in a specific place, by a specific person.

Fortis
Certain consonants pronounced with more energy ( opposed to lenis ); EG: English voiceless stops.

Foundation Hand
System of penmanship designed by Edward Johnston. Body leters are 4.5 pen widths high. It is a round and formal hand.

Fountain Pen
  1. A pen made with a reservoir in its stem or holder, to supply ink for some time without replenishing it. Penny Cyclopaedia: 1840

Fox Effect
The person must know what theya re talking about, because they have all these impressive sounding crendentials.

Fraktur
Writing system used for writing German poetry etc between the sixteenth and twentieth century.

The use of it as a writing system was banned in Germany on 3 January 1941, as it was, allegedly, a Schwabacher Judenletter. A claim that is patently false. [ The real reason was probably because those educated outside of Germany were unable to read Fraktur as fluently as they could read Antiqua Writing. ]

Fraser Script
Indic style writing system created by J O Fraser in 1915, for Lisu - a Tibeto-Burman language.

Frequency Distribution
A means of summarizing large amounts of data to permit their interpretation in a simple graphic.

Frequency Table
List that shows the frequency of letters, letter pairs, syllables and words in a language.
