The Letter~E

E+ Slant
Upslant of between 146 and 160 degrees in Graphoanalysis.

E++ Slant
Upslant of between 160 and 180 degrees in Graphoanalysis.

Egyptian Ink
An ink made of carbon --- usually soot --- mixed with water and a binding ink, such as gum. Red Ink was prepared the same way, with the additon of red oxide pigment

Eldarin
Language family of Middle Earth. This language was the root for most of the languages and writing systems of the Third Age on Middle Earth. For more details, read The Lord of The Rings (Tolkein: 1965) Part Three, Appendix E describes this language.

Ellipse
  1. One or 2 strokes form the structure of the formation.
  2. Motivated by emotions - possibly only love

Eng
Letter with the sound value of "ng", at the end of words such as "sing".

Epigraphy

Error of Measurement
Measurements obtained which are different, whilst the attribute being measured are not different.

Esperanto
Language created by Ludwig Zamenhof in 1887. The foundation blocks are:
  • The alphabet consists of 28 letters, each of which has one sound.
  • Six standard accented letters exist: c, g, h, j, s, u.
  • Stress falls on the penultimate syllable of each word.
  • Verbs do not decline for number of person.
  • Number or person are indicated by accompanying pronouns.
  • Verb endings indicate function or tense.
  • Nouns end in -o.
  • Adverbs end in -e.
  • Adjectives end in -a.
  • Suffixes are attached to root words only.

Estrangelo Script
A Syriac script that spread to Central Asia

Evidential Grammar
A system of grammer that requires sentences to answer the implicit question: 'What is the evidence on which this statement is based.' [ Would the mandatory use of such languages by politicians make them honest? Or advertizing less misleading? ]

Expansiveness
Need for space, either physical or psychological or both.
