 The Letter~D 

 The Letter~D 

 Dark Brown Ink 

 Made from gallic acid --- a substance obtained from gall nuts --- and mixed with iron salts
 to produce a purple blank ink, that fades to brown.

 DE Slant 

 Upslant of between 126 and 146 degrees in Graphoanalysis.

 Decode 

 To reverse the method by which a message was hidden.

 Demotic Script 

 Ordinary writing of Egyptian Hieroglyphics

 Dependent Variable 

 This is the variable that is related to the phenomenon of interest. In theory, this really
 represents the process of behaviour being measured.

 Descriptive Statistics 

 Procedures employed to organize and present data in a convenient, usable and communicable
 form.

 Deseret Script 

 Writing system created by Bringham Young, for use by adherents of the Church of Jesus
 Christ of Latter Day Saints.

 Despondancy 

 This is the fear of lonliness.

 Devanagari 

 Script used for most languages found on the Southern part of the Indian sub-continent.

 Deviation 

 The difference between the score, and the mean.

 Diacritic Mark 

 A mark added to a character to indicate a modified pronunciation.

 Digraph 

 A pair of letters denoting a single sound, eg ph ,
 sh . 
 A encipherment in which the plaintext is written using
 letter pairs. 

 Digram 

 A pair of letters.

 Dr Fox Effect 

 An impressive presentation, with zero meaningful content. To dazzle with science, eloquance
 and humour. 

 D'Nealian 

 Copybook distributed by Scott, Foreman and Company in the United States of America.

 Ductus 

 An individual style of handwriting, characteric of a small group of scribes or
 sometimes a single person. ( Daniels & Bright 1996 )
