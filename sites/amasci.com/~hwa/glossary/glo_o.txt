 The Letter~O 

 The Letter~O 

 Ogham 

 Script used in Ireland and Northern Britain between 500 and 700 CE. Letters consist of a
 line crossed with short lines.

 Open Form 

 All formations in writing in which the line does not enclose the entire inner part of the
 formation.

 Operational Definition 

 A defintion which explains a concept solely in terms of the operations used to produce and
 measure it.

 Opish 

 English that has been altered by the addition of OP after
 each consonant. For example. Wednesday becomes wopedopnopesopdopay.

 Ordinal Numbers 

 Numbers used to represent position or order, in a series

 Ordinal Scale 

 Measurement scale that requires observations to be ordered or ranked according to the
 degree to which they reflect the attribute being measured.

 Ordinal Scale Correlation 

 The degree to which two the variables correlate with each other, if their scores have been
 assessed using ordinal scale mesurements.

 Ordinate 

 The vertical axis of a graph.

 Orpiment 

 Arsenic trisulphide. A gold like coloring material, which sparkles like mica.
 Commonly used in Illumination of manuscripts. This substance is
 also known as arsenic trisulphide.

 Orthography 

 Conventional spelling of texts and their principles

 Ostraca 

 Unglazed piece of pottery, used for writing on.

 The name derives from the Greek practice of holding secret ballots, to decide who were
 regarded as being too powerful, or too dangerous to the democracy. Those names were
 written on an ostracon, and placed in urn. If anyone received too many votes, s/he was
 condemned to banishment from Athens for ten years.
