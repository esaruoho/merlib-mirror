 The Letter~G 

 The Letter~G 

 G Factor 

 The General Factor. A psychological measurement that
 pscyhologists attempted to locate, and measure, from circa
 1895 to circa 1920. It is the score that says how well the
 person generally does things.

 Geisteswissenschaft 

 Science that is practiced as an art, a Humanity.

 Glagothic Alphabet 

 Alphabet created in the ninth century, for writing Serb, Bulgar and Russian. This script is
 related to Kirillitsa.

 Gothic 

 Script developed circa 1150 CE in France. It remained popular for roughly three hundred
 years.

 Grammatogeny 

 The invention of a writing system

 Grammatology 

 The discipline that studies writing systems.

 Grapheme 

 Term used to designate a unit of a writing system, parallel to phoneme and morpheme, but in
 practice used as a synonym for letter, diacritic, character or sign.

 Graphoanalysis 

 This is a registered trade mark of IGAS Inc. It refers to a system of handwriting analysis
 devloped by M N Bunker and the American Grapho Analysis Society.

 Graphologie 

 Word coined by Abbe Jean Hippolyte Michon to denote the analysis of handwriting.

 Graphology-L 

 Mailing list that used MajorDomo. Housed first at Netcom.com, in 1996 it migrated to
 Bolis.Com. In 1997 it migrated to Graphology.Org. In 1998 it was donated to COGS.

 Graphomancy 

 This is the study of handwriting, to divine the future. This has no relationship to
 analyzing handwriting, to provide a psychological profile of an individual.

 Graphometrics 

 System of measuring writing, especially signatures, developed by Werner Wolff, that is
 mainly used in questioned document examination.

 Graphotherapuetics 

 Termed coined by de St Colombe, to describe his method of handwriting analysis --- which
 include changing handwriting as a mean of changing the psyche.

 Green Sheet 

 This is sheet issued by IGAS, with the suggested format for a a Graphoanalytical report.
 The name derives from the color of the sheet.

 Guanche 

 Extinct Berber language that was spoken in the Canary Island.

 Gurmukhi 
 Script used in writing Punjab. It means proceeding from the mouth of the
Guru.
