 The Letter~J 

 The Letter~J 

 Jacobite Script 

 Ordinal writing system used by the Syrian Monophysites, a Christian sect.

 Jawi 

 A writing script based on the Arabian alphabet, used for writing Malay. It was developed in
 the fourteenth century, common era.

 Jazm 

 Script that developed into Classical Arabic.

 Jealousy 

 This is:
 Fear of rivalry. 
 The feeling of being unloved. 

 JoDi 
 Tool used for measuring angles in writing that was distributed between 1975 and 1980 by
 JoDi Incorporated. 
 Juncus Maritimus 
 Rush plant whose reed was used for making brush-pens in
 Ancient Egypt. See also 
 Phragmites Communis .
