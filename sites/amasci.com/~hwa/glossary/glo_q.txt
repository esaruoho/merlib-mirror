 The Letter~Q 

 The Letter~Q 

 Quadrangle 

 Four strokes form the structure. 
 Motivated by need for security - or by need for power 

 Questioned Document Examiner 

 Individual who examines documents to determine whether or not they are authentic.

 Questioned Document Examination 
 The examination of a document to determine whether it was
 written when, where and by whom it was purported to be written
 by. 

 Quenya 
 High Elven speech of Middle Earth. The writing system has
 a number of diphthongs. For more details The Lord of The
 Rings (Tolkein: 1965) Part Three, Appendix E describes
 this language. 

 Quipus 
 Inca system of transmitting information,
 based on knotted cords of various colors and types.
 It is not known what, if any relation this system has with
 that found during the palaeothic and neolithic period in
 China. 

 Qu&ocirc;c ngu' 
 Vietnamese writing system introduced by French Jesuit, Alexandre de Rhodes in 1848 
 It is based upon the Latin alphabet, with additional diacritic marks, and one character from
 the Cyrallic alphabet. It takes between three and six months to learn to read and write
 Vietnamese using this
 writing system. [ After August 1945, a intensive literacy campaign
 pushed literacy levels to above 90%. ] &laquo;
 The final u of ngu should have a tilde on it. &raquo;
