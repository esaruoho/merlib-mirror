 The Handwriting Analysis Standards Task Force [ HWASTF ] is a group
 of concerned individuals interested in handwriting analysis, and
 setting a minimal set of standards for the field. Current topics
 for which standards are being set, include such things as: 

 A Standard Glossary of Terms 
 A Primary Source for Research in Handwriting Analysis 
 Systems of Handwriting Analysis: What they are, how they differ, etc. 
 Comparison of Texts used to Teach Handwriting Analysis, and rating them for accuracy and Reliability. 
 Current tools used for analyzing handwriting. 
 Defence for Daubert v Merril Dow, Cameron v Knapp, and similar legal attacks on Handwriting Analysis. 
 Research Studies which meet generally accepted standards for Scientific Research, and demonstrate either the validity, or reliability, or both, of handwriting analysis. 

 Formal Standards 
 At this point in time, no Standards have been formally accepted.
 A few are currently in the discussion stage. When they have been
 formally accepted, they will be added to this section.
