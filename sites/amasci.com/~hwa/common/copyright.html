A Graphology Bibliography

A Graphology Bibliography is Copyright © 1980 - 2002 by Jonathon Blake.

This bibliography list was originally constructed, by adding all of the references found in books on handwriting analysis. Then I added the references found in journal citations. Then I started going through volumes of various journals, adding citations that I thought were relevant. I started work on it in 1978, and distributed a few copies of it 1982, as hard copy. Part of this list was on the BBS I ran between 1990 and 1995. This document is in a process of being continually updated.
