Proverbs...on sin:








Pr.15:8 

The LORD detests the sacrifice of the wicked, but the prayer of the upright
pleases him. (NIV)



Pr.16:6 

Through love and faithfulness sin is atoned for; through the fear of the
LORD a man avoids evil. (NIV)



Pr.20:9 

Who can say, "I have kept my heart pure; I am clean and without sin"?
(NIV)



Pr.24:11-12:

11 Rescue those being led away to death; hold back those staggering toward
slaughter. 12 If you say, "But we knew nothing about this," does
not he who weighs the heart perceive it? Does not he who guards your life
know it? Will he not repay each person according to what he has done? (NIV)



Pr.28:9 

If anyone turns a deaf ear to the law, even his prayers are detestable.
(NIV)



Pr.28:13 

He who conceals his sins does not prosper, but whoever confesses and renounces
them finds mercy. (NIV)
