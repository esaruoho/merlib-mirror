Proverbs on parent-child relationships 

Proverbs...on parent-child relationships: 

 Pr.3:11-12 
11 My son, do not despise the LORD's discipline and do not resent his rebuke,
12 because the LORD disciplines those he loves, as a father [b] the son
he delights in. (NIV) 

Pr.10:1 
The proverbs of Solomon: A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish
son grief to his mother. (NIV) 

Pr.10:5 
He who gathers crops in summer is a wise son, but he who sleeps during harvest
is a disgraceful son. (NIV) 

Pr.13:1 
A wise son heeds his father's instruction, but a mocker does not listen
to rebuke. (NIV) 

Pr.13:24 
He who spares the rod hates his son, but he who loves him is careful to
discipline him. (NIV) 

Pr.14:26 
He who fears the LORD has a secure fortress, and for his children it will
be a refuge. (NIV) 

Pr.15:20 
A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish man despises his mother.
(NIV) 

Pr.17:6 
Children's children are a crown to the aged, and parents are the pride of
their children. (NIV) 

Pr.17:21 
To have a fool for a son brings grief; there is no joy for the father of
a fool. (NIV) 

Pr.19:18 
Discipline your son, for in that there is hope; do not be a willing party
to his death. (NIV) 

Pr.19:26-27: 
26 He who robs his father and drives out his mother is a son who brings
shame and disgrace. 27 Stop listening to instruction, my son, and you will
stray from the words of knowledge. (NIV) 

Pr.20:20 
If a man curses his father or mother, his lamp will be snuffed out in pitch
darkness. (NIV) 

Pr.22:6 
Train [a] a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not
turn from it. (NIV) 

Pr.22:15 
Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, but the rod of discipline will
drive it far from him. (NIV) 

Pr.23:13-16: 
13 Do not withhold discipline from a child; if you punish him with the rod,
he will not die. 14 Punish him with the rod and save his soul from death.
[c] 15 My son, if your heart is wise, then my heart will be glad; 16 my
inmost being will rejoice when your lips speak what is right. (NIV) 

Pr.23:22-25: 
22 Listen to your father, who gave you life, and do not despise your mother
when she is old. 23 Buy the truth and do not sell it; get wisdom, discipline
and understanding. 24 The father of a righteous man has great joy; he who
has a wise son delights in him. 25 May your father and mother be glad; may
she who gave you birth rejoice! (NIV) 

Pr.27:11 
Be wise, my son, and bring joy to my heart; then I can answer anyone who
treats me with contempt. (NIV) 

Pr.29:15 
The rod of correction imparts wisdom, but a child left to himself disgraces
his mother. (NIV) 

Pr.29:17 
Discipline your son, and he will give you peace; he will bring delight to
your soul. (NIV) 

Pr.31:28 
Her children arise and call her blessed; her husband also praises her: (NIV)
