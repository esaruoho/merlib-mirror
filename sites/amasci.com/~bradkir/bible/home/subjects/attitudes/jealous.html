Proverbs...on jealousy:








Pr.14:30 

A heart at peace gives life to the body, but envy rots the bones. (NIV)



Pr.23:17 

Do not let your heart envy sinners, but always be zealous for the fear of
the LORD. (NIV)



Pr.24:1 

Do not envy wicked men, do not desire their company; (NIV)



Pr.27:4 

Anger is cruel and fury overwhelming, but who can stand before jealousy?
(NIV)
