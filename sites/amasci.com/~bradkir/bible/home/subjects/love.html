Proverbs...on love:








Pr.3:3-4:

3 Let love and faithfulness never leave you; bind them around your neck,
write them on the tablet of your heart. 4 Then you will win favor and a
good name in the sight of God and man. 



Pr.9:8 

Do not rebuke a mocker or he will hate you; rebuke a wise man and he will
love you. 



Pr.10:12 

Hatred stirs up dissension, but love covers over all wrongs. 



Pr.14:22 

Do not those who plot evil go astray? But those who plan what is good find
[a] love and faithfulness. 



Pr.15:17 

Better a meal of vegetables where there is love than a fattened calf with
hatred. 



Pr.16:6 

Through love and faithfulness sin is atoned for; through the fear of the
LORD a man avoids evil. 



Pr.17:9 

He who covers over an offense promotes love, but whoever repeats the matter
separates close friends. 



Pr.19:22 

What a man desires is unfailing love [b]; better to be poor than a liar.




Pr.20:6 

Many a man claims to have unfailing love, but a faithful man who can find?




Pr.20:28 

Love and faithfulness keep a king safe; through love his throne is made
secure. 



Pr.21:21 

He who pursues righteousness and love finds life, prosperity [c] and honor.




Pr.27:5 

Better is open rebuke than hidden love.
