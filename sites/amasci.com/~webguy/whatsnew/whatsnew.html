January 29, 2003
  1. Wrote a short article, Your Money is No Good Here.

September 1, 2002
  1. Changed ISPs from Olgrande.net to Eskimo.com. Olagrande went out of business and was absorbed by Eskimo. Made some minor changes to a few webpages. The biggest change is that Eskimo does not support CGIs written in C. Their web server runs on a different platform than the machine supporting their shell accounts. Hopefully, they will get a cross compiler up soon.

April 30, 2001
  1. First upload attempt of my personal web page onto Olagrande. I made some massive changes and not everything may work. Still checking links. I decided to get rid of my Public Service Announcements because they were getting old and it didn't seem people were reading them anymore. I have transferred several of my writings that seem to be more popular (via email feedback) or have information which I deem to be of value. At least almost everything is updated now!

April 27, 2001
  1. I changed ISPs from Primenet to Olagrande a few days ago and am starting to port over my webpages. This is not a trivial task. For one thing, I have to change all references to Primenet including email addresses, URLs, CGIs, etc. It also gives me a chance to fix broken links and add more material. Because of this, I am going to be selective in what I port over as some webpage information is old and/or obsolete.

    I have been with Primenet since February 1995(!) and was considered a charter member. Since that time, Primenet has merged at least three separate times with other companies! This latest merge is with Earthlink and now Primenet no longer supports dialup service.
    Time to bail.
