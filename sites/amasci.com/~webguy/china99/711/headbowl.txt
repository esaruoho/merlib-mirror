 7/11/99 Balancing Bowls, Chinese Acrobats, Beijing 
This is truly an amazing demonstration of balance, strength,
flexibility and concentration.

This lady is doing a handstand on top of a guy who is
supporting both their weight on his head and feet(!)
She is also balancing bowls on her head and feet!

What this picture doesn't show you is that she can put
the bowls on top of her head using only her feet!
The guy also goes through some contortions on the ground as well
while still supporting her.

This is a scan of one of the postcards. The stage was too dark
