 7/11/99 Balancing Glasses, Chinese Acrobats, Beijing 
This is also another amazing demonstration of balance, strength,
flexibility and concentration.

This lady is balancing liquor glasses stacked three high on
both her hands and feet as well as her head! She eventually
rolls completely over while still balancing all of it!
