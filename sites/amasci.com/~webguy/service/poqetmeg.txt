Megahertz XJ1144 Modems for the Fujitsu Poqet PC Plus

Megahertz XJ1144 Modems with XJack 
and the 
Fujitsu Poqet PC Plus Handheld Computer

There are at least five different variations of the 
 Megahertz XJ1144 
PCMCIA modem with XJack. It seems only three of these variations
will actually work with the PC Plus. Fortunately, these
variations are plainly visible on the outside packaging
of the modems themselves.
It appears the older variations of the XJ1144 will work
with the PC Plus. It seems the improvements on the newer
XJ1144s has made them incompatible with the PC Plus
for some reason.

Many thanks to Mike Kopplin for this information.
Mike has several XJ1144s and generously took the time to test
the different variations on his PC Plus.

The table below describes the differences between the 
 Megahertz XJ1144 modems that will work and will NOT work
with the PC Plus. 

I also include images of the front and back sides
of the variations of the XJ1144 modem that will work with the PC Plus.
I have personally tested these variations and they all work
on my PC Plus.

Below are front and back images of the variations
of the Megahertz XJ1144 modems that are known to work with
the PC Plus. See the above table for specific descriptions.
I have tested all variations below and they work fine
with my PC Plus!

 Megahertz XJ1144 Variation 1

Variation 1 is the more desirable modem because the XJack
sits flush with the side of the modem when pushed all the way in.
This variation
also includes a tiny internal speaker so you can hear it dial out.

 Megahertz XJ1144 Variation 2

Variation 2 appears to be the older variation. 
Although the images do not show it,
the XJack nub sticks out about 0.25"
from the side of the modem even when pushed all the
way in. Also, this modem has no internal speaker so you cannot
hear it dial out.

 2/17/00 Update - I found a new variation of the Megahertz XJ1144
modem that works with the PC Plus! See below.

 Megahertz XJ1144 Variation 3

Variation 3 appears to be a cross between
variation 1 and variation 2! 
The frontside resembles variation 1's frontside but
the backside resembles variation 2's backside
including the exact FCC ID number!

The XJack sits flush with the side of the modem
when pushed all the way in like variation 1 but
there is no internal speaker like variation 2!
So you cannot hear the modem dial out.

This modem has a 
registered trademark symbol, &#174;, on the frontside 
near the XJack logo and 
there are more patent numbers on the backside
so supposedly it is newer than both variations above!
But it has no internal speaker (Surely having a speaker is an improvement).
Weird.
