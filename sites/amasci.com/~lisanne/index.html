Luxembourg cooking is said to combine German heartiness with French finesse, but in fact it is in a category all its own. Traditional specialties include the Luxembourg national dish of Judd mat Gaardebou'nen (smoked collar of pork with broad beans), Gromperekniddelen (potato dumplings), Eislecker Ham (Ardennes ham), Träipen (black pudding), and Quetschflued (plum tart).

Culinary Luxembourg, published by Editions Guy Binsfeld, 1997.
