Minnesota Historical Society (MHS) Photograph Collection Database
The Minnesota Historical Society's Photograph Collection Database contains records for 44,000 individual photographs, approximately ten percent accompanied by digital images. The photographs document Minnesotans' lives, landscapes, leisure activities, and occupations from 1850 to the present.
