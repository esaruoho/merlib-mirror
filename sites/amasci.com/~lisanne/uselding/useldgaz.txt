USELDING Luxemburger Gazette Entries 

 Luxembourger Gazette Newspaper Index
(1871-1918): Uselding Extractions 

 The Luxemburger Gazette was published in Dubuque, Iowa, from
1871-1918. It featured news of Luxembourgers and Luxembourg communities
throughout the United States. Because of its broad scope in reporting
personal news of Luxembourg-Americans, the Gazette is an excellent
source for determining marriage and death dates. More importantly,
obituaries often include where an ancestor was born or emigrated from. 

 Volume II of Luxembourgers in the New
World contains an index to personal and place names in the Gazette.
The index includes the name exactly as it appeared in the newspaper so
there can be variation. For example, Nicholas Uselding might be listed as
Uselding, N.; Uselding, Nic.; Uselding, Nick; or Uselding, Nicolas. There
are fewer surname spelling variations than in other genealogical records,
presumably because the newspaper's editors and contributers were
Luxembourgers themselves, but they do still occur.

 Sample Entry: USELDING, Maggie 22.02.1910,
5 

 Each entry consists of the date of newspaper and the page the name
appeared on. Note the European dating: Day - Month - Year, so this entry
appeared in the February 22, 1910 issue on page 5.

 Microfilm copies of the Luxemburger
Gazette are available from the Family History Library and from several
other libraries throughout the U.S.
