USELDING Beadle County History 

 Beadle County, South Dakota Pioneer Histories: Uselding
Extractions 

 Between 1955 and 1970, Mildred McEWEN JONES collected over 9,000
pioneer histories. These histories range from a few lines to several
pages sent in by living relatives. Her files were bound into five thick
volumes, three on Beadle County townships and two on Huron.

 Historical account of Beadle County, South Dakota, 1879-1900, (A), 3 vols.
 Pioneer residents of the City of Huron, 2 vols.
 Mildred McEWEN JONES, comp. (For reference only at Huron Public Library.)
 Fore more information see: Beadle County, SD, 
Bibliography of Sources.
