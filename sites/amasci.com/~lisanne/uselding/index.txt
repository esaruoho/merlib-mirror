Uselding Genealogy 

 Uselding is a surname most often found in Luxembourg. I am
researching the descendants
of George Uselding ( -1867) and Eva Has ( -1840) of Erpeldange,
Diekirch, Luxembourg. They had thirteen known children; seven sons and one
daughter survived infancy. Over the course of a twenty year period
(1846-1867), six of their sons immigrated to the United States and settled
in the Midwestern states of Illinois and Wisconsin, and later South
Dakota. This web site is a collection of resources that include references
to Useldings in the United States.
