 Synchronize! 3.9.1 

Synchronize! automatically keeps the documents on two or more Macs up to date, by copying the newest version of those documents to the
other Mac. It makes sure your desktop Mac and your PowerBook, or your Mac at home, have the most recent versions of your documents.
Using Synchronize!, all you do is select two folders on your desktop. Synchronize! identifies documents which correspond between the two
folders and copies the newest document to the other folder.
Use a floppy disk to carry your files from one Mac to another, or use File Sharing and a network connection, or Apple Remote Access.
Synchronize! makes sure that the newest versions of your documents are in both folders, no matter where you changed them last. 
Synchronize! Pro is an industrial-strength file synchronization, backup and file server mirroring. Version 4.0 works with
