Gene Inspector 1.6

The Gene Inspector can perform many sophisticated sequence analyses, but this new software is much more than just another sequence analysis product. Imagine having the ability to define an entire suite of analyses to be performed each time you have a new sequence, and to design custom style sheets which specify exactly how your output will look for each analysis. Because you can predefine and store analysis parameters for any analysis to fit your needs, anyone in the lab will be able to obtain meaningful and consistent results, whether they are a novice just entering the lab, or an experienced researcher familiar with the analysis routines. TaxDaddy 0.9.1

TaxDaddy is intended as an easy to use database for keeping taxonomic information at your fingertips. The program is configured as a flat-file database with many user defined fields for taxonomic names, ranges, distributions, synonymies, descriptions, you name it. If you have enough disk space, you can even incorporate scanned images or other pictures into your data records. Depending on your configuration and the data you put into the program, TaxDaddy can do complex searches, such as answering the question: how many brachiopod genera (and their synonyms) belonging to family Choenitidae are known from the Devonian of China? Something difficult for a 3 x 5 card to do.
Besides keeping track of your taxonomic data, TaxDaddy can also calculate various diversity metrics. The program automatically calculates taxonomic diversity, originations, and extinctions as well as their proportions and standard errors. It also calculates and plots survivorship curves.

  • Author/Publisher: Christopher A. McRoberts
  • Modification Date: November 20, 1995
