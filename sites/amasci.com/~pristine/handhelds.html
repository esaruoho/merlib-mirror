ZMacSync 2.3.2

ZMacSync is a tool to synchronize the Sharp Zaurus Personal Information with the standard Communication suite of the Apple Macintosh (MacOS X). This includes the Address Book, iCal calendars and to-do lists, as well as Stickies (notes) and the Zaurus system clock. Synchronization is optimized so that only records are interchanged that are added, modified or deleted with as low data transformation loss as possible.
