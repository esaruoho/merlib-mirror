BlockForge 1.1

BlockForge is a new version of the classic game "Tetris", where your mission is to move falling pieces, or blocks, in a pit, trying to fill up horizontal lines. Each horizontal line full of blocks will zap and disappear, giving room for more blocks. The game ends when the pit is full of blocks.
Different from most other Tetris-like games, this game has fully customizable playfield graphics and sounds. The player may place additional "Themes" into Themes folder, giving the game varying graphics and sound effects. There is one theme bundled with BlockForge, the Default Theme.

Snood is a fun puzzle game for Macintosh that will test your intellect rather than your reflexes. Your goal is to free all of the trapped Snoods by launching other Snoods at them. If you connect three or more Snoods, they will transport away. If that leaves any Snoods not connected to the top of the playfield, they will fall to safety. Don't take too long, though.
