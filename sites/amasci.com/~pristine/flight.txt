 googol-Choo-Choo 3D 2.06 

googol-Choo-Choo 3D is a 3D model railroad simulation program. It is
easy to make your own layout by dragging and dropping track items, or
other items, on your layout. You can see the layout in 3D from driver's
seat of a running train or from several other locations. If you want to
create your own trains or structures, you can do it with 3D modeling
software and import the file into googol-Choo-Choo 3D.

 SteamTRAIN 3.1 

You represent the engineer and crew of an 1800's era steam train. Your
vintage train consists of a steam locomotive and tender, plus baggage
cars and passenger coaches.

Your train will travel between stops, up and down grades, negotiate
curves, switch tracks, and couple/uncouple cars. Controls include the
engine's throttle, boiler steam pressure, traction sand, three brake
types, manual couplers, and track switches. Share right-of-way with
Phantom Express. The Dispatcher gives train assignments.
