Internet Call Waiting 3.1

Use a phone line to connect to the Internet? You may be missing important calls while you're online. Download Internet Call Waiting (ICW). It tells you who's calling when you're online and lets you decide how to handle the calls.
Internet Call Waiting:
Tells you when someone is trying to phone you while you're online
Lets you to decide how to handle incoming calls - you stay in control of your phone line
Saves you the expense and inconvenience of a second phone line
Ensures that no one in the family ever misses an important call
How does it work?
If someone calls while you're online, a pop-up window with the caller's name and number appears on your computer screen. You can set up Internet Call Waiting to answer the call on the same phone line you are using to access the Internet, or on a another phone line such as your cell phone play an announcement to the caller or have the caller leave a Voice Note. The Voice Note is sent to your email address as a WAV file. You can even screen the Voice Note, by listening as the caller leaves the message.
