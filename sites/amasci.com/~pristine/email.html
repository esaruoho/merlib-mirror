Mail GoGoGo 2.0.2

How does MailGoGoGo find spam ?
Some other anti-spam tools eliminate spam by referring to a data-base of spammer e-mail addresses. But which spammer will use his real name or e-mail address? Most of them are lying about their addresses and we will have to suspect the effect of the data base.
To find spam, MailGoGoGo reads not only e-mail headers but also the body and replaces the meaning with a numerical degree of spam. We call this, context analysis process.
What is the difference between MailGoGoGo and filters for e-mail clients?
E-mail filters work after receiving e-mail. In other words, after you have already downloaded the e-mail. MailGoGoGo eliminates unnecessary e-mail from your mail server before you download it. This is one of the reasons why MailGoGoGo can delete even mail bombs and script mail. MailGoGoGo is an application program, not a plug-in for other e-mail clients. You can use it with any e-mail client.
