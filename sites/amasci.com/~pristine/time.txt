 Minuteur 4.7.2 

You can also enter a specific time for the alarm to sound, as opposed to
a delay time. If, for example you have to leave at 13h45 for an
appointment: type "1345" on the numeric keypad while pressing Option. On
a laptop, hold the keys Fn Opt, and type the desired time on the virtual
numerical keypad (not the keyboard numerals). Minuteur calculates the
remaining time. The hour can be entered as 3 or 4 digits. The time
cannot exceed 12 hours.
 You may enter a time beyond midnight. If the Mac goes to sleep during a
countdown, the countdown will not be stopped, but nor will the Mac be
woken if it is still asleep at alarm time.

 Stopwatch mode
 Minuteur can also be used in stopwatch mode. To start the
count, click the blue button or press Option+Return/Enter. The program
then emits a sound each minute if the option "Sound effects" is checked.
It is also possible to adjust the time in stopwatch mode by dragging the
rule. To stop, click on the red button or press Enter, Return or Esc.
Timing is limited to 12 hours. To change between timer mode and
stopwatch mode you must first stop.

minute to keep up with the real clock. When it gets to 59, the next
score makes it wrap around to 00, and the left side will score shortly
afterwards to bring the hour forward. At midnight, left's score wraps

 Vremya 2.0 

Vremya is an application that sets your Macintosh clock using version 1 of the 
Network Time Protocol as described in RFC 1059 by D. Mills. This is a simple implementation 
of NTP, but the accuracy is sufficient for the Macintosh clock.
