 System 7.5.5 works fine on all Macs, even those with 68000 processors. 7.5.5 may be a little RAM hungry so you may want to try system 7.0.1 which is distributed by Apple at no cost. 
All the applications listed on this page work properly on a 68000 Mac with System 7.0.1.
Later versions may also work, as will much of the current software, but you'll have to check for yourself.

AppleScript support, and supporting almost all known image file formats, including its new self-mounting image
files.
