NetFone 1.010

Haxial NetFone is an encrypted multi-user Internet telephone. ie it lets you talk (literally, without typing), to 1 or more people over the Internet. You can talk to multiple people at the same time, and your security and privacy is protected by encryption. Not surprisingly, if you want to talk, you will need a working microphone for your computer. If you do not have a microphone, you can still listen, but of course you cannot talk.

Cable modem or better is recommended. You can still use NetFone with a regular modem, but of course the quality will not be as good. Also, being an Internet telephone, NetFone's performance is very much subject to the performance of your Internet access combined with the performance of the Internet access of the person you are speaking to. You do not need a heap of bandwidth, mainly you need a connection that is fast with little delay.
  • License: Shareware, $15
  • Author/Publisher: Haxial
  • Modification Date: November 5, 2001
  • Requirements: Mac OS 9 and CarbonLib 1.3.1 or better and Mac OS X
