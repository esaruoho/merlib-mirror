Vamp is a player for MPEG layer 3 (.mp3) sound files. It's based on Tomislav Uzelac's amp player for linux. Most of the data processing part has been rewritten to optimize it for the PPC.
  • License: Freeware
  • Author/Publisher: Michel Pollet
  • Modification Date: August 19, 1997
  • Requirements: PPC, Incompatible with Ram Doubler
