 BlockForge 1.1 

BlockForge is a new version of the classic game "Tetris", where your mission is
to move falling pieces, or blocks, in a pit, trying to fill up horizontal lines.
Each horizontal line full of blocks will zap and disappear, giving room for more
blocks. The game ends when the pit is full of blocks.

Different from most other Tetris-like games, this game has fully customizable
playfield graphics and sounds. The player may place additional "Themes" into
Themes folder, giving the game varying graphics and sound effects. There is one
theme bundled with BlockForge, the Default Theme.

minesweeper game on the market.
You might have played a game called 'MineSweeper' (MicroSoft), 'Landmines' (Atari Corp), or anything else with the word
'sweeper' or 'mine' in it.
Then you probably know how to play, since the rules of those games and Manic Minefields are very similar. If not, don't worry,

 Snood 2.1.1 

Snood is a fun puzzle game for Macintosh that will test your intellect rather than your reflexes.
Your goal is to free all of the trapped Snoods by launching other Snoods at them. If you connect
three or more Snoods, they will transport away. If that leaves any Snoods not connected to the top
of the playfield, they will fall to safety. Don't take too long, though. Every Snood you launch
increases the Danger Level. When the gauge fills up, the Snoods will shift downward. If the
Snoods reach you, the game is over.
