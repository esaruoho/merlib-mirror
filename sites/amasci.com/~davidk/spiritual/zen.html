Irish Haiku

Seems more like a Zen Limerick to me. From Panoptichrist #1 , Copyright 1995 Carboload 3000, 417 E. Union St. #210, Seattle WA 98122.

There was an old lady from nantucket

Who saw the stars in a bucket

She realized with a start

the allness encompassed by her heart

And said, "I'm enlightened now, so fuck it!"
