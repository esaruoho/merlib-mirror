My disagreement with the management of the company I work for is virtually complete. They are pro-war anti-labor corporate Republicans. I am anti-war pro-labor social Democrat. They excuse human rights violations in China with "there's plenty of money to be made there." And they're right there with every new weapon of mass destruction that the Dr. Strangeloves in the Pentagon can dream up.

I don't feel like going through moving, selling a house, and all that pain again.
