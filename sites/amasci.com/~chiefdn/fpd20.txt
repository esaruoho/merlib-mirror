 EXTRA! EXTRA! Fire District 20 Board of Commissioners
signed a consolidation agreement with Fire District 12 and City of
Marysville, a.k.a. Marysville Fire District. Monday October 13,
1997, the Marysville City Council authorized the Mayor to sign
the agreement, and on Tuesday October 14, Fire District 12's Board
signed the same. The consolidation began January
1, 1998. In May of 2002, Fire District 20 
residents were asked to vote on annexing into Fire District 12. The vote 
was positive, and the merge was formalized. Fire District 20 no longer 
exists, legally. We are now united with the Marysville
Fire District. 

And if you see Chief Ron Barton 
of Fire Dist. 14, tell him where you saw his name!
