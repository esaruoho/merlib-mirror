Mailing lists

  • freemasonry-list is a lightly moderated (disruptive posters eventually get booted off) email list. Traffic is about 45-60 messages/day at the moment (October '97).
    To subscribe or unsubscribe send mail to: freemasonry-list-Request@masonic.org in the message body put subscribe freemasonry or unsubscribe freemasonry as appropriate.
    Instructions for the list can be found at http://www.cacr.caltech.edu/~rfire/masonry/mail_list_instr.html
  • FAMWA-LIST is a closed, unmoderated list created for the use of Freemasons who are interested in exchanging information about upcoming Masonic Family activities within the State of Washington or adjacent locations and for the general exchange of information proper to be made public.
    Closed means subscription is limited to regular Masons and members of Concordant Bodies of Freemasonry.
    To subscribe send a message to famwa-l-request@eskimo.com with Subscribe in the subject line.
  • Canadian Masons is a discussion group for items of interest to Masons in Canada. Instructions for the list can be found at http://www.northroute.net/lists/canmas
  • Hiram Worldwide Newsletter Check out http://freemasonry.org/hiram for more information.
  • Australian Masonic List Check out http://www.fl.net.au/~rosco/list/index.html
  • Masonic-youth is a discussion list for the appendant youth orders (Rainbow, DeMolay, Job's, etc.).
    To subscribe, send mail to masonic-youth-request@nyx10.cs.du.edu with the Subject: SUBSCRIBE your name.
    To post to the list, send mail to masonic-youth@nyx10.cs.du.edu
    List manager: Garrett Fitzgerald.
  • Jodera-l is a roundtable discussion forum for adults and senior members interested in encouraging, promoting, and supporting the Masonic Youth Groups known as Job's Daughters, Rainbow, Triangle, and Demolay.
    jodera-l is a private, unmoderated List. Subscriptions are limited to interested Adults (18YO+), Presiding Officers, and Past Presiding Officers of these Orders.
    To subscribe, send mail to listproc@eskimo.com with
    SUBSCRIBE JODERA-L your email address
    in the body.
  • UKMason-list is a closed, unmoderated list for the use of UK Masons. This means that only Masons recognized by UGLE as regular may subscribe.
    To subscribe send mail to ukmason-list@infohighway.co.uk with Subject: SUBSCRIBE your full e-mail address
    Please include at least your Lodge & Lodge Number, Province and Rank in the body of the message. This will be posted to the group by way of an introduction.
  • The Australian Masonic Mailing List is intended for communications primarily between Masons and other interested persons in the Australian region. The list is open to any and all interested persons.
    To subscribe send mail to oz-mason-r@beastmaster.hna.com.au With "subscribe oz-mason" in the body of the message. A message to the above address with "help" in the body will get you further information on the commands available to you.
  • Mormon-Masons
    Thanks to the efforts of Fred Waid, a new mailing list has been established for those interested in discussing topics related to freemasonry and Mormonism. The list is open to all, but requires that 'you leave your flame-thrower at the door'
    To subscribe, send the message:
    SUBSCRIBE mormon-masons first name last name
    to majordomo@sara.zia.com
  • Nebraska Demolay Listserver
    Josh Denny (see above) has posted:
    Anyone wishing to subscribe to the Nebraska DeMolay listserver can send e-mail to listserv@oasysnet.gonix.com
    Type only the following in the body of your message:
    SUBSCRIBE DEMLIST
