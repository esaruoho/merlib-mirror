Daylight Lodge meets the 3rd Saturday of the month at 10:00 except for July, August and December. July meetings are at 12:00 noon on the 4th Saturday and we are dark August and December.

We meet at Greenlake Masonic Temple, 307 Maple Leaf Pl. N.E., Seattle. 

 Click here for a map and Driving Directions to Daylight Lodge #232.

 Meet our representatives to the Most Worshipfull Grand Lodge of Free and Accepted Masons of Washington.
