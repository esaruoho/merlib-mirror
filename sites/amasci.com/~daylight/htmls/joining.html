A few basic Questions and answers

HOW TO JOIN MASONRY: The Masonic fraternity has an ancient tradition of not directly asking or soliciting men to join. You see on this page 2B1 ASK1. That is short hand to tell you that if you are interested in Freemasonry, you usually find a Mason and ask for a Petition for the Degree. Here are some commonly asked questions.

HOW MUCH DOES IT COST TO JOIN?
HOW IS THE PETITION FEE PAID?
HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE?
HOW LONG BEFORE THE DEGREE CEREMONIES ARE COMPLETED?
HOW MUCH ARE YEARLY DUES?

COST: In the Grand Lodge of Washington no lodge can charge less than $100.00. $50.00 of that is for Grand Lodge.  Each lodge sets its own fee. For example Daylight Lodge sets their fee based upon the Minimum Wage. So for Daylight Lodge, the fee is $54.00 + $50.00 for a total of $104.00.

HOW IS THE PETITION FEE PAID: The fees must be paid in full when the petition is given to the lodge. All lodges take cash or checks. Unlike other lodges, Daylight Lodge also offers convenient credit card payment for all lodge fees, etc.

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE: Two or more months. Each petition given a lodge is read or presented to the lodge members at two different meetings, thirty days apart. After the first meeting a committee of members is assigned to meet with the petitioner and his family. A report is filed with the lodge about the visit. The petition is then presented to the members a second time and a ballot taken. To be accepted to membership, a unanimous ballot is necessary.

HOW LONG BEFORE THE DEGREE CEREMONIES ARE COMPLETED: That is up to you and the lodge. The three degrees can be done every other day, every other week, every other month. Usually it will take about three months after the vote by the lodge.

HOW MUCH ARE YEARLY DUES: That entirely depends on the lodge. Some lodges charge as little as $20.00 a year, others much more. Daylight Lodge for example has dues based on the State Minimum wage $6.72 x 16 = $107.52 plus $18.00 in Grand Lodge fees for a total of $126 which can be placed on a VISA of MasterCard.
