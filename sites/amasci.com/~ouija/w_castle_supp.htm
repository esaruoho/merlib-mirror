Plunder Codex


Axe of Gongkin - Attack Base:20 (As Two handed Axe), AP: 200 (Truestone Iron Alloy), Dam: 2d8+4 Slashing Weapon, in the hands of any non-dwarf it does a small 1d6.  The enchantments are deep and many on this axe, it will allow the holder to understand and speak any language in his presence.  Against elves or trolls, the axe will glow bright orange in battle and on any Special or Slash, it will sever the limb struck or break the weapon struck (unless the defending weapon or shield is made of Truestone).  The axe has a POW: 25 and an INT: 16.  The axe can speak through the holder, in their voice, and can make it's wishes known (kill trolls and elves are about all it thinks about).  It was rumored to have been crafted by Mostal himself for one of his favorite students of smithing.  The legendary Axe was said to be able to drive armies into a frenzy and protect the wielder from elvish arrows and Trollish lead.

Cestus of Stealth - These well made black and green leather cestus are both good for hand to hand combat and assisting in any skill or task that is bound to the characters Stealth score.  Any stealth skill attempted by the wearer of these cestus will gain a +25% to their skill.  These gloves of war work perfectly well as 1d3+2 Cestus in battle too.  Cestus of this sort were usually given to the Spirit Hunter upon initiation and were typically made by the instructor of the class or personal teacher.  Each set are engraved with the tanners name and the receivers name therefore there may be more value in who they belong to that what they grant the wearer. Value: 3,000 L

Cestus of Shadows - These well made elbow to fingertip gauntlets are heavy, made of black thick leather, and studded with what might be smoky quartz rather than sharp metal spikes.  These cestus were thought to have been in the possession of the Master Shadow Hunter at each lodge or temple but many sets were seen in the days of the Vampire Shadow wars.  The cestus confer the ability to move among the shadows, even in daylight, while the magic in the cestus keeps the wearers appearance down to nothing more than a wispy shadow or shade.  The cestus give the wearer a skill increase in Move Quietly by +50% and also cover the person in a ten point Detection Blank spell.  The move score of the wearer is also doubled if the cestus are used under the open sky and at night.  At night or in complete darkness, the gauntlets will also grant the wearer the ability to cause a Shimmer (4) at will on himself. Value: 7,000 L

Craft Bricks - During ages past, Dwarven stone masons built fabulous structures out of stone that was seamless and appeared to be quarried from some gargantuan single stone.  Part of their secret were called Bricks of Craft, or more commonly Craft Bricks.  A Craft Brick was quarried like normal stone and through skill the dwarf would make the shape desired, usually a typical stone shaped like a modern day brick.  Typically the bricks were then shipped to the site of the construction and then the foreman was given the word to activate the stones.  When the right word is spoken to a Craft Stone, it will adhere to any stone it is touching, melding to it and forming a perfect connection so as to appear to be naturally fused and blended.  In this manor, any sort of repairs to stone would appear perfect as if no damage was ever done.  Craft Bricks work once then are set and only ancient Dwarven rune spells were capable of returning the brick back to its individual piece.  Craft Bricks are legend but a few have trickled into museums here and there and are therefore very valuable but impractical to build with in small quantities (but great for patching things).  The Craft Bricks will also take on characteristics of the stone around them for example: A statue with a broken hand could have the hand replaced by placing a brick on the stump, and once the word is spoken, the stone would shape itself into the same form as the original hand and fuse completely.  Stones that are different in material or color will still have their original colors and makeup so it would be best to replace a broken statues hand with stone that is the same or at least the same color. Rumor has it that the Dwarven folk of old had other Craft Stones such as those of gemstone type and further Craft Woods as well.  Value: 500 L per brick or more depending on quantity of the cache.

Hammer of Kalaldis:  This powerful hammer once belonged to the main armorer of the first Fjallhallenberg walking castle hundreds of years ago, the hammer became a mantle piece and was forgotten, some say it passed into legend and is a myth.  The hammer has the following attributes:  (2-Handed Hammer, 2d8+4 and on a critical hit, it will destroy the location or item parrying it unless it is made of Truestone as well, then normal parry rules apply).  If thrown, the hammer will magically return to the wielders hand up to a range of two hundred feet in that same round.  If a Lightning spell is cast on the hammer and it is thrown, it will streak to the target trailed by a massive building bolt of lightning delivering a powerful hit (as per normal thrown damage) however, the target will then be hit with a thunderous clap and electric bolt from the spell, resisting is allowed but as if the target had but half their normal POW score.  The hammer also has a Shield(3) matrix inscribed on it as well as a fanatacism(6).  While holding the hammer, a dwarven wielder will never have fear of any critical hit, they will automatically be parried by the hammer (normal hits are subject to standard parry rules as the hammer believes that they are inconsequential).  Value: The hammer is worth over 15,000L.

Hauberk of the Grand Shade - This hauberk is fashioned out of strips of Grey and black leather and highlighted with brass buckles and studs.  The hauberk is deeply magical and was made from the strips of a real dragon hide thusly it protects for 8AP and any damage to the covered locations by spell (such as Disruption) is ignored and dissipates.  The wearer is also immune to any disease including any caused by chaos and can once per day touch a chaos infected person and reverse a feature they may have. Value: 14,000 L

Kilt of Running - The highlands of Prax sees it's share of warrior clans and bloody forest skirmishes.  This dark patterned kilt with red overlaying thin stripes was used by a powerful clan of Spirit Hunters hundreds of years ago.  The kilt enables the wearer to double his running movement while scarcely breaking a sweat.  The kilts were typically available to fit a SIZ from 8 to 18 but there were known to be larger and smaller kilt wearing warriors.  Each kilt has a matching belt with a buckle that is attuned to the temple where the warrior is from, a badge or clan badge if you will and such a buckle will always have some sort of gemstone in it, typically a POW crystal of Spirit Hunter make, and be scrawled with a matrix. Value: 6,000+ L

Mantle of Corumruvar - The ancient Sword Hunter Corumruvar was said to be the most powerful warrior of Henonfel.  This hood and shoulder cape is fashioned out of rather plain looking sheep's skin and tooled with the runes of Henonfel.  The mantle grants the wearer to see any spirit that is bound into any object that is within sixty feet and to be able to judge the relative strength of that spirit (this includes spirits that are naturally within a body).  The mantle also surrounds the wearer with Spirit Screen (4) and once a day it can do a Spirit Block (4) on the wearer or a person of the wearers choosing.  Value: 12,000 L

Rainbow Ink - Nearly 1000 years ago, a distant relative of Lankor Mhy discovered a well of magical knowledge near the roots of the world tree, just outside the great pyramid.  This well sprang forth with healing waters that also granted knowledge.  Special ink was fashioned from this water and the bark of the tree.  This magical ink which is very rare and considered Holy by all Lankor Mhy, has the following magical properties: Any person using the ink on normal parchment or vellum can copy any scroll of normal or magical nature at 100% accuracy including duplicating whatever magics were on that scroll (spells, curses, enchantments, etc,.).  There is enough ink in a typical well to duplicate ten such items.  Drawbacks: The user must fletch their own quill from an eagle feather in order for the ink to work, any other writing instrument will work and copy with 100% accuracy however, magical duplications of enchantments and spells will not.  Value: 10,000 L per vial.

Shadow Hunter Cloak - These cloaks are often found amongst the lay member folk of the Henonfel cult.  They are ground length with ties inside that enable it to hug the body when necessary and give good hide ability.  Anyone so wrapped up can increase their Hide skill by +20% but of course if they fail to hide the effect is lost.  Each cloak will also grant a Spirit Screen (1) to the wearer as long as it is appropriately worn. Value: 2,000 L

Spear of the Hunter- This ornate spear is over twelve foot long and is hafted with black woods inlaid with silver runes, those of the Henonfel cult.  The spear is made of a special mineral/metal that gives it the following stats: Attack: +25%, Parry: +25%, Pips: 50.  The spear also has two matrix spells on it:  Multi-Missile (3) and Return Missile(1). Value: 5,000 L

Staff of Souls - This long war staff is made of the finest white oak and is spiraled with a thin gold vein and wrapped on the ends with dark fine tooled leather.  The staff adds +10% to a persons attack and parry scores with it and if used two handed, it will confer an additional +5% to each attack and parry skill with it in battle.  Along the length of the staff are numerous smoky quarts gemstones imbedded in the wood.  If the staff strikes a target that has a bound spirit in its possession, that spirit will be drawn from the item and stored within the staff; a bright flash of light will show on the staff and the gemstone will glow orange that houses the spirit.  There are 40 gemstones on the staff.  There is no chance to resist the drawing of the spirit forth however, the staff will only draw forth and store spirits that are bound by the person being hit (damage must get through, simply touching a person will not invoke this ability).  Each spirit in the staff will give the holder the ability to heal one pip per day for example: ten spirits would give the holder the ability to heal by touch himself or another for a total of not more than ten pips each day.  At ten gemstones full, the staff will confer total immunity to all diseases and poisons.  At fifteen gemstones, the staff will hold ten power points for the caster just as if it were a POW crystal.  At twenty gemstones, the staff can cast Invisibility, Spirit Screen (7), and Bludgeon (8) each once a day. At twenty-five gemstones, the holder can change himself into a spirit, being effected by all normal rules that govern spirits, and back again at will.  At thirty gemstones the move of the holder will increase by two times, any stead ridden will be effect by the spell effects of Mobility and Coordination.  At thirty-five stones, the staff can Teleport the holder once a day including up to four other beings. At forty gems, the max it will hold, the staff vanish from the holders hands and return to the spirit plane where it will wait for 40 days then teleport back to the normal world but in a random location: Each spirit within released and free (at a rate of one per day).  Value: 25,000 L

Vial of Spirit - The item often appears as a brass vial with a stopper made of black wax.  If the stopper seal is removed, the spirit inside will be free to do one service for the person doing the freeing.  The spirit inside is able to take a physical shape at will in order to complete the task (think Genie).  The spirit will adopt the physical capabilities of the person freeing him, pips will be doubled, but all other stats will remain similar to that of the freeing person. Such a spirit will perform one deed for the freeing person as long as that deed can be completed within a day.  Henonfel followers look down upon using spirits for anything so remember to be very very frugal and subjective about what duty is asked of a spirit, they can be deceitful and very literal. Value: 5,000 L

  Cult of Henonfel

Spirit Hunters

by Angus Henderson

Mythos and History

Since Time Began

The spirit of those that are week are tools, tools to be used to ones advantage and if that tool breaks or is defective, then it deserves to be obliterated.  Henonfel was involved in the building of the great pyramid of stone that is the universe; some brand him a dwarf, others say he has the looks of a giant, while still others speak of him as a dark brooding ogre like being.  In all cases, Henonfel has worked with those that build structures by hand and without spirit intervention.  Things that are formed or created by an enslaved spirit are warped and polluted:  The only pure structure is one that was built by hands and the mind.

Life After Death

Those that serve faithfully in life can expect to receive the benefit of being a true tool to the cosmos.  Those that die while pure of heart and intention can expect their spirit to find it's way into a tool of creation by it's own accord and never by magical binding for that is pollution of the cosmos.  Those that die while polluted (owning spirits that have been bound against their will) will slip completely from the cosmos, their spirit vanishing forever from the great machine that is the cosmos.  Followers of Henonfel see this sort of death as the most tragic of all. 

Runic Association

Henonfel is tied to the runes of Spirit heavily, then comes darkness followed by mastery.

NATURE OF THE CULT

Reason for Continued Existence

Henonfel is the cosmically appointed guardian of spiritual enslavement.  As long as there are spirits that are bound against their will and left unliberated, there will be a reason for Henonfel followers to roam the lands, seeking to free them by force or convincing the holder in the benefits of spiritual liberation.

Social / Political Position

The cult of Henonfel is made of vigilantes and ruthless hunters.  Those in it's ranks have the distinct honor of being known as the Dark Guard or Spirit Hunters and with that title comes the shroud of mystery that these mercenaries continue to encourage and fuel.  Amongst members can be found retired military leaders, lesser nobles and peasants alike but they do have commonality:  They are skilled in deadly sharp swordplay coupled with an unparalleled skill in Whip.  Hunters as they are typically called, are usually quiet and found lurking behind kings and in the mix of political revenge or inter-cult rivalry.  They are swords for hire but with their own agenda that supercedes whatever political agenda they may appear to have.

Particular Likes or Dislikes

Honor to ones companions is everything to a follower of Henonfel.  Loyalty to the Hunter however is always of the highest priority and the underpinnings of the cult, the belief in the faith that all spirits must be left to seek their own destiny is paramount.  Any spirit that is enslaved is taken from the cosmos, the natural order has been disturbed and everything in the cosmos is lessened.  Hatred is openly shown to those that have bound spirits and contempt is usually shown to those with allied spirits however allied spirits are sometimes known to be with a being as a favor and not against their will.

To bind a spirit or turn traitor to the Hunter is the ultimate sacrilege against Henonfel.  To use the tools of the cosmos to your own advantage is seen as perverse and must be destroyed at all costs including the destruction of all spirits involved or tainted by the action.  This sort of punishment would mean that to a Hunter, if you bound a spirit, you have forfeited your own spirit and the spirit you bound will find a new home in your physical shell and be able to make amends for the "tool gone wrong".

ORGANIZATION

Inter-Cult Organization

The various guilds of the Hunter work together and seek harmony in the areas they are living in.  Some see them as mercenaries with no loyalty to those that hire them so at times their guilds will be hidden however they have nothing to hide.

Intra-Cult Organization

Within the Hunter cult walls a hierarchy exists that is strictly followed.  Within the guild, members of different ranks mingle freely but at meetings and political gatherings, they keep to those within their own rank.  Respect is shared amongst all members regardless of rank to all other members; they are all on the same mission and with the same goal.  They are almost militaristic in their structure however outside of the guild, they are identifieable by small crests or periapts that detail through symbols, their guild home and rank.

Centers of Power and Holy Places

Within each guild is a stone altar, an altar of true stone.  Each season there is a great gathering of members and spirits that have been collected that are polluted due to chaos are cleansed and returned to the spirit plane.

Hold Days and High Holy Days

Henonfel demands little time of his worshippers, he does expect results and strict order therefore each Truthweek is known as hold time and many spirits are expected to be freed. Henonfel prefers that spirits be freed, returned to the spirit plane, or sacrificed if necessary.

LAY MEMBERSHIP

Requirements to Join

Membership is given freely to anyone that does not have a bound spirit.

Requirements to Belong

A lay member is expected to bring in at least one tainted spirit a year to be cleansed; free any bound spirit that is discovered, and purify the spirit of those that have enslaved spirits against their will.

Mundane Benefits

Lay members are housed and fed for free during holy week and will always be given shelter when necessary by another member.  Lay members gain the skill of Sense Spirit and are given special access to skill other training as well.

Skills

Lay members are trained free of charge up to 25% in any of the following skills: Any Sword, Spear, Composite Bow, Camouflage, Jump, Knot Tying, Move Silently, Swim, and Track.  Members are also taught the mystical language that spirits speak, this language knows no barrier of race or time and is magical in nature therefore requires years of experience to increase.

The following skill is unique to the cult: Sense Spirit.
Sense Spirit
Cost 400/800/2000/EXP
Knowledge Skill: Base Chance: 05%

This skill enables the Hunter to sense a spirit and what sort of condition it is in within the object touched.  When the hunter touches the item in question, he must hold it as it would be held during normal usage, i.e. a weapon would be swung, a helmet could be thumped by hand while being worn, a scroll would be looked at as if reading, a door would have to be knocked on or walked through.  If the skill succeeds, then the Hunter knows there is a spirit within the object.  One more successful skill roll at half the normal skill chance would be required if the Hunter wanted to know weather or not the spirit was Bound or Allied.  An additional roll at a quarter of the normal skill chance could be rolled and would enable the hunter to determine the POW score of the spirit and possibly the spirits INT score within a few points.  If the skill roll results in a critical during any sensing roll, the Hunter has contacted the spirit (without the need of rolling a Spirit Speech skill roll) and now has the ability to ask it one question for each five points of INT it has, with at least one question being possible.  The Hunter need not know the language of the spirit as he is able to talk to the spirit using Spirit Speech. 

Spirit Speech
Cost: Experience Only Skill
Knowledge Skill: Base Chance: 00%

This skill is magical in nature and enables the Hunter to speak directly with spirits that are bound or allied within a being or item.  The Hunter is able to talk to a spirit that has established communication with him.  In order for this to occur, the Hunter must have attempted a successful Sense Spirit skill check and may then attempt to speak to the spirit by rolling a successful check of this skill however, in order for the skill to work, the Hunter must overcome the POW score of the spirit in question.  Once the Hunter does this, he can speak to the spirit, asking it one one question for each five points of INT it has, with at least one question being possible.  The Hunter need not know the language of the spirit as he is able to talk to the spirit using Spirit Speech.

RUNE LORD (Sword Hunters)

General Statement

This aspect of the Rune Lord or Henonfel corresponds to Henonfel the Bringer of Justice and Swift Death.  Henonfel Sword Hunters are quiet and act swiftly with accuracy and deadly results.  To obtain this level of honor within the Hunters is indeed deserving of praise.  Sword Hunters are the words and deeds of Henonfel in mortal form, the Rune Lord acts as both judge and jury when dealing with those that seek to bind spirits against their will.

Requirements for Acceptance

The prospective Sword Hunter must first have a Spirit Being sponsor. A spirit of powerful means that has either been freed by the character or is allied in some lawful way.  To qualify, the prospect must have a POW, STR, and CON all of 15 or more. The character must have 90% skills in Whip, any one sword, as well as master of two more skill from among the following: Camouflage, Climb, Jump, Knot Tying, Move Quietly, Shelki, or Tracking. In addition, he must know Dark Armor 2 and Whip Accuracy 2. Finally, he must pass a test abstracted as (POW+CON+STR)/3 times 5 or less on a d100, plus 05% to success chances for each spirit the character has freed on the altar that year. 

Restrictions

An allied spirit is gained which may be placed in a weapon or a willing animal.  This allied spirit must be treated with honor for it is free to leave at any time and is not bound to the character in any way other than mutual benefit such as friendship or owing ones life.

Benefits

Those that are Sword Hunters can can increase all weapon attacks beyond 100% and may split such attacks. The ability of Sword Hunters to communicate with spirits in their own special magical tongue is what sets them apart from other beings.  The Sword Hunter also has access to a special skill that sets him apart from other warriors. The skill of Spirit Strike is gained when a Sword Hunter becomes a Lord.  Rune Spells are available to the Lord as per normal rules however, only the Priest of Henonfel has access to Cult Special Rune Spells.

Spirit Strike

Quick Psychic Powers

Creatures with psychic powers have a few skills that are new.  There is a Psychic Attack skill which is (INT x 5) and Psychic Parry which is (Current POW x 5).  When a psychic attacks a person they roll their Psychic Attack, if the person is not Psychic then they can use their current POW score as a Parry.  A failed parry will damage the POW score of the target for 1d6 points (a non-psychic can never attack).  

When a targets POW is at zero they die as their spirit is released from the body.  Alternately, a psychic can attack the body of the target doing the same 1d6 points to a random body location instead by causing the flesh and vessels beneath the skin to erupt and turn inside out.  Every psychic has a pool of power to draw from to perform their abilities aside from attack and parry.  Each ability functions at a very low level unless fortified by more psychic energy behind it, much like adding POW to a spell to punch through a heavy magical barrier.  Every aspect of an ability can be increased by adding one more point of pool energy added to it, to determine the Pool Energy (PE) level of a creature, add the INT and POW scores then add 1d6 to this.  This pool regenerates just like normal POW.  There is not limit to how much PE can be spent on a skill in any one round but a psychic can never reduce himself to zero or he will die.  Psychic powers require that the target be seen or touched in order for them to work.
