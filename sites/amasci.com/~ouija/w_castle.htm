Fjallhallenberg Castle
 "What walks in Glorantha is not always by the will of Orlanth"

By the Ouija, May.20.2002

Overview: Many centuries ago, maybe dozens, a large clan of Mostali in Prax or the nearby vulture country was lead by a particularly brilliant smith and diamond dwarf known as Gilmain. Gilmain spent his youth dreaming of inventions and creating huge stonework buildings and monuments. When old enough, it was said that his skill of weaving magic and stone was so great that he apprenticed under Mostali himself for one hundred years before graduating. During a hiatus while apprenticing, Gilmain came upon a wonder deep within the world stone, at the core of creation, whereas when a gods blood spilled on the plains and power crystals sprang forth, he found a clutch of bloodstones that were as large wyvern eggs, just as tall as he was. He thought to himself that the amount of magical energy within these stones had to be beyond comprehensionhe knew he had to take them and do something with them, he was a dwarf after all and these were raw gems (in a manor of speaking). 

It is said that he lived in Old Pavis for a time and was a recluse until he discovered a way to tap the power of his treasures and release wonders never seen before by mortal or god. He fled his house of inventions and returned to his home near Vulture Country to discover a great war was being waged. Chaos had invaded and had slaughtered and destroyed most of the people and works of Mostali that day. He once again left but this time along with what was left of his friends and formed the Clan Thorath, or the wanderers. They fled to the Iron Mountains for ten years and built and excavated. They found a cache of knowledge from the ruins of an old Cradle the size of a house, similar to those that floated down the river so named years ago.  From this arose Gilmain's greatest creation of all. Fjallhallenberg Castle. He and his masons blended magic, stone, and will into a great square castle with ten meter high walls being two hundred meters in length, surrounding an inner castle that bristled with space for living and of course war. At the heart of the castle were the stones, his treasures. Through techniques lost long ago to the god learners, he brought the castle itself to life. The living pulse of the stonework could be felt in every part of the castle and it was eerily warm to the touch. At each corner of the castle the workers created massive eight-meter tall legs, powerful in look as that of a true dragon, a pair at each corner ending in massive ornamental claws with metal nails that as sharp as any sword. Also at each corner was a large tower standing thirty meters and topped with a solid roof and ballistae, the inner castle stood over fifty meters tall giving an expansive field of vision from which to plan his attacks; Gilmain sought revenge. 

From his lab he plotted, his workers lusting for revenge, planned with him. Amongst the Mostali of that day was one in the favor of Chaos, he was a broo left shape formed by Thed to infiltrate and seek out the treasures rumored to be held within the castle and especially the rumored gems that were prized at its center. The host of chaos that fell upon Gilmain's old village sought out the remains of the Mostali retreat in the Iron Mountains and came upon the castle during a rainy night. At day break Gilmain woke to see his castle surrounded by hundreds of beasts and creatures, a hoard of vile chaos numbering almost one thousand, lead by a Centaur commander whose belt was adorned with seven heads, two of dwarvish descent, mounted as twitching trophies. They came prepared for battle, this host of chaos united; they were about to discover a terrible secret. The centaur shouted his charge and the hillsides exploded with fury and the host of Chaos spread and filled the areas surrounding the castle.  Great siege machines wrought of chaos magic and evil stone sought to pummel the castle sides and towers to rubble and the hoard greedily searched for the entrance waythere was none. The dwarves built no entry other than climbing their own walls? A momentary pause swept over the hoard and at that same time a great shimmer took hold of the castle and a low thrum emanated from thence. Above the castle walls the high inner castle faded from view, magically gone or invisible and boulders launched from the chaos engines began to pass harmlessly through the area where it was. The dwarves within were able to fire their great black powder cannons from within the ethereal center castle and were devastating any climbing the near smooth outside walls. 

Then it came, in the frenzy Gilmain fled his lookout tower and vanished deep into the castle leaving his sergeant in charge of the defense. A small quake rocked the surrounding country side and the hoard was once again hushed and bewildered. One broo shouted and pointed at one of the massive stonework legs built into the castle corners, the carved stone effect had given the castle the look as if it were sitting upon these massive legs. It moved, the stone flexed and appeared alive, earth gave way and the great castle began to shudder to life, the eight massive corner legs were actually moving, they were alive! With a final lurch the castle broke free from its foundation and stood on those eight legs, the hoard could see completely under the castle to their companions on the other side. Underneath in the center of the castle, a set of stairs and ropes fell and a flood of dwarven warriors emerged to battle the still stunned hoard. Few Mostali fell but as the hoard rallied and followed their retreat to the entrance in the underbelly of the castle, and the last Mostali pulled his feet up, the castle sat down and crushed the entire host of chaos!  The few that were spared fled with stories that none would believe

a hundred years later during a massive war far in the southern plains of Prax where Troll, Dwarf, Elf and Chaos met and died, seven of these powerful castles came into view across the horizon, and with the swiftness of the fastest horse, these mammoth stone beasts rode down the vile hoards and were all but victorious. Giants and dragons were the only to survive the reinforcements and with combined efforts they were able to disable or destroy all but two of the walking castles. When the battle was over, the castles were gone, only rubble and clouds of dust remained. Since then the existence of these castles has been by story aloneuntil now

Adventure Premise: A year ago a set of quakes and subsequent flood near the southern edge of the Stinking Forest by Snakepipe Hollow, uncovered two large tilted towers. Since then the area has become the adopted home of a clan of broos lead by both Broo and Ogre that follow Cacodaemon. Their raids have become more frequent as of late and last week two daughters of a local Sartar Baron were kidnapped and are being held to ransom. The raids seemed far to focused and as of late, have been targeting local libraries and temples where ancient knowledge and historical maps are stored. It's as if someone were driving the raiders to find something, some information No book has been taken or scroll stolen. It appears that the hoard is carefully searching for something hidden in a unique book or scroll, a key or paper perhaps. In fact what they are looking for is a combination code, a code that only Gilmain knew, a code that could bring a Fjallhallenberg castle back to life

Setting: The castle sits near a swamp and volcanic area.  The great fortress wandered here after the battle centuries ago, wounded, and sunk into the swamp which dried long ago. The castle suffered a black powder blast from an accident centuries ago by a few chaos explorers and because of that, coupled with the recent unearthing of this gaping fissure, creatures have had access to the inside of the castle and have begun looting. The castle is dormant but can be awaken if the right code is entered by voice (In Mostali of course) at any command console. The castle is a testimony to the blending of magic, iron and stone, it is a marvel of a era gone by, however, if the lunar empire had even just one of these monstrosities in their army, they would be supreme: Barbarian hoards would be as fleas on a rhino.  The forces within have discovered how to turn the Shimmer Field on so the central castle is not visible from outside the castle walls (it is within though) but have not been able to do much more than that. Welcome to the original Fjallhallenberg castle, the blueprint building Gilmain and his clan built, may you survive.
