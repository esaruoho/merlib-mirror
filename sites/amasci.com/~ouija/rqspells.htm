Spirit Spells for use in RuneQuest
and Glorantha

The battle magic spells listed below are in standard RuneQuest format. These extra spells are in the tradition of RuneQuest and I am sure you will find them as fun and enjoyable as we have. These spells found their way to the folks at Chaosium several years ago and I received a hand written letter from their staff; they liked them too.

Spell Descriptions (Presented Alphabetically)

BOLT
Range-80 meters POW Used-2 points
Type-focused, permanent Cult Ties-None

This spell is designed to rapidly speed up the process of the natural random molecular motion n the targets body. If the caster's POW overcomes the target's POW, then the target takes d4 to a random body location. This damage is not absorbed by armor.


BRITTLE
Range-80 meters POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-focused, passive, temporal Cult Ties-None

This spell which is stackable up to four points will reduce any weapons capability of resisting damage when used for parrying purposes. For each point of this spell, the parrying weapon will become less resistant to damage i.e. for each point of the spell, a weapon will block 2 points less damage. This spell may be used on a shield also. In all cases the target must still fail a resistance check for these effects to occur. Normal rules apply for attempting to parry a hit that does more damage than parrying item has hit points.


CALM BEAST
Range-80 meters POW Used-1 point
Type-focused, passive, temporal Cult Ties-Waha

This spell is essentially the same in all respects to the spell Demoralize but with the following differences: This spell works only on animals or unintelligent creatures, but not familiars. This spell will not work on undead or humanoids under the influence of any other mind effecting spell. This spell has been known to be used by Morokanths as well as Waha shepherds.

 
DARK ARMOR
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-Passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal  Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

This spell is used to strengthen and reinforce the armor of the target against weapons that are inhabited by spirits.  If a weapon with a bound or allied spirit within it strikes the Dark Armor upon the target, the spirit will lose 1d4 points of spirit POW per point of the Dark Armor spell.  This spell is stackable to four points.  If a spirit is dropped below their POW score by means of this spell, the spirit is actually freed and not destroyed.  The spell has no effect on a spirit that is actually within a living being such as that of a familiar or a real person themselves.  The spell is compatible with Protection and Shield, but if either of those spells end or are dispelled, the Dark Armor will vanish with it.

 
DARK WEAPON 
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-Passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal  Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

This spell is used to strengthen a weapon by absorbing the POW score of any spirit that it touches.  If a weapon with the spell of Dark Weapon on it strikes a weapon or armor with a spirit in it of either a bound or allied nature, that spirit will lose 1d4 points of spirit POW per point of the Dark Weapon spell.  This spell is stackable to four points.  If a spirit is dropped below their POW score by means of this spell, the spirit is actually freed and not destroyed.  The spell has no effect on a spirit that is actually within a living being such as that of a familiar or a real person themselves.  This spell is compatible with Blade-Sharp, and Bludgeon, but no other weapon reinforcing spell (Such as Fireblade or Iceblade).

 
DETECT CHAOS
Range-40 meters POW Used-2 points
Type-focused, instant Cult Ties-Stormbull

This spell will give direction and distance from the caster of any being who has been tainted by chaos, unless the caster fails to overcome the POW of the target(s) in the area. The game master will roll a resist for each tainted being in the area separately. Like Detect Magic, this spell will outline the target with a warm white glowing light while it is in effect.


DROP
Range-40 meters POW Used-3 points
Type-focused, instant, permanent Cult Ties-BlackFang

If the caster's power overcomes the target's power, then the target must immediately make a dexterity check of DEX x 3 or drop whatever is held in the chosen hand, or make a check at DEX x 5 to drop whatever is in a random hand. This spell will only function if the caster uses power from a bound spirit to cast this spell. The caster's bound spirit actually passes over the hand of the target, causing it to momentarily go limp. This spell will not effect undead or creatures without a spirit.

 

ELDAR SPIRIT ENBINDMENT
Range-Engaged POW Used-8 points + 5 points per point of spell
Type-passive, permanent  Cult Ties-Umath

This spell is very ancient and was really an experiment taken on by a long lost Spirit cult.  The spell enables the binding of spirits that are of tremendous power, extraordinary spirits used in the manufacture of great monuments that require spirit POW to move or motivate, as in the case of the spirits bound into the legs of the castle itself (in upwards of 200 or 300 points).  Such spirits exist but seldom find their way onto the mortal plane in physical form or by summoning, at least since the gods walked more freely upon the planes of Prax.  Each point of this spell can bind a spirit of POW 20 in the ways a standard spirit can be bound.  Since such a spirit would be unreachable in spirit combat or summoning by normal means, specially designed Rune Spells were needed to open a gate between the Spirit Plane and the Material Plane.  Through this gate, the summoner could further use another Rune Spell to call forth a Mythical Spirit, one of tremendous power.  This spell has not known usage since the creation of the last walking castle.  The Rune Spell to open the gate "Chronos Gate", and the spirit calling Rune Spell known as "Umaths Lamentory" are only vaguely hinted at in the script and are lost to mortal man. 

 

FIRE FEET
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporalCult Ties-Firshala

This spell is similar to Ironhand except that it will effect any being using hooves, claws, feet, or similar natural weapon as their combat weapon. For each point of the spell it will add +1 damage and a +5% to hit. This spell will in addition to normal damage, cause 1d3 points of fire damage to the targets main hit-point score unless they succeed in resisting the casters POW score. Resisting does not negate the spell, just the effects for that round of combat. This spell cannot be enchanted beyond +4 damage and +20% to hit unless special Rune Spells are being used.


FIRE SHIELD
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporalCult Ties-Firshala

This spell when cast upon a shield, enables the shield to cause damage to any weapon that it parries, even from a normal hit. For every point of this spell, the target shield will damage any weapon hitting it for an equal number of hit points. This spell causes the weapon hitting it to superheat and crack, thus causing damage. If the parried weapon is wood, then this fire damage is doubled, while a magical weapon such as one with bladesharp, bludgeon, or similar enchantment is unaffected (even if wooden). This spell cannot be enchanted beyond +4 damage. There is an Ice Shield used by a far northern obscure cult but it is extremely rare, performing in a similar fashion save damage to wooden weapons, wooden weapons are unaffected by this icey cold version.

HARDEN METAL
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal Cult Ties-None

This spell extends the natural resiliency of weapons to be used for parrying purposes. Each point of this spell adds to the three point base that weapons have of parrying damage in a fight. Normally in RuneQuest, if a weapon parries a hit that is four or more points in damage, then those four or more points stay on the weapon through the rest of the fight, and when the hit point score of the weapon is passed, it breaks. This spell adds to this base of three points giving the weapon a better chance of surviving a fight, especially if it was not intended to be used for parrying (bows in particular). One point of this spell pushes this base to five or more, two points to six or more, three points extends the weapons resiliency to seven or more sticks with it. This spell is stackable to six points making the maximum boost of resiliency to nine meaning you could parry any hit of eight points or less all day long without breaking or marring the weapon.


ICEBLADE
Range-Touch POW Used-4 points
Type-focused, active, temporal Cult Ties-None

This spell is similar in all respects to the spell Fire Blade except for the following differences: Damage caused is cold not fire, if the weapon under the effects of this spell is parried with a special result or critical, the caster successfully must make a power resist roll or the weapon under the spell effect shatters due to the brittle effects that the spell causes. This brittle effect does not extend to enemies weapons or shields used to parry it.


MAGIC GLOW
Range-40 meters POW Used-3 points
Type-instant, focused Cult Ties-None

This spell when cast on an object or area, will cause any detection spell cast within a three meter radius of the object to register the opposite of what it should. However, if the spell Detect Detection is cast within the area of effect, all effects of the Magic Glow will vanish.

 
MULTI-AXE
Range-80 meters POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-passive, focused, instant   Cult Ties-Mostal

This variable point spell is ancient and lost to time but was known to the Mostali of old.  The spell was cast on the wielders axe just before throwing it at an opponent.  When hurled, another axe is produced of equal size and weight thus a three point spell would yield a total of four axes hurtling towards the opponent.  Like its young brother Multi-Missile, the axes created cannot critical or impale.  The first axe hit rolled for is the original weapon, all subsequent rolls are the additional axes.  This spell does not work with Bladesharp or Speedart but an axe that is enflamed such as a fire blade can be duplicated by means of this spell however the POW cost of casting the spell triples!  This would produce many flaming axes, very impressive but very costly in POW.

QUICKSTRIKE
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal Cult Ties-None

Each point of this spell will allow the target to increase the speed by which it attacks in combat or performs actions, by one Strike-Rank. This spell will reduce the Strike-Rank of the target (his weapon and his own combined) but not below a score of one. This spell effects only one weapon making it near impossible to effect a bow since one would have to cast it on every arrow to be launched from the bow.

RETURN WEAPON
Range-40 meters POW Used-4 point
Type-passive, unfocused on item, instant  Cult Ties-None

This spell will enable the caster to have his weapon return when it comes to rest after being thrown.  The weapon will fly back unerringly to the hand of the thrower with no roll necessary to catch.  It takes one round for the weapon, no matter what type, to fly back to the casters hand.  While it is returning it moves quickly but still could be grabbed out of the air if a successful jump and catch roll is made, similar to catching a fast ball while running.  The spell will effect a weapon of up to twenty pounds, a weapon heavier than this can be returned but the spell must be cast once for every twenty pounds of material.  Yes, it could be used on a siege bolder but who has 60+ spare points of POW to dump on a boulder in the heat of battle.

 
RUBBER HAND
Range-80 meters POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal   Cult Ties-None

This spell which is stackable up to four points will reduce the effectiveness of natural armors and the physical attacks done with hand, claw and bite (kicks too).  In effect it is the reverse of Ironhand but instead of bolstering it reduces.  Each point of the spell will reduce attack chances by 5% and damage by one point.  This spell has the strange effect of aiding the Parry skill of the person by +5% per point of the spell.  This spell will also reduce damage from falling and crushing blows by half.

 

RUIN ARMOR
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-focused, permanent Cult Ties-Thed

This spell is designed to reduce and weaken the bond that holds metal together. If the caster's power overcomes that of the target, then a random location of armor is reduced in blocking capability. If this capability drops to zero or less, then that location falls completely apart and crumbles. This spell can be used to effect weapons as well. Creative casters could use this spell to effect other metal objects as well, a typical listing of metal objects and their Hit Points are presented here:

Object Avg. Hits
Chest Band 8
Door Hinge, Large 25
Iron Door 150
Goblet 5
Metal Coffer 15
Prison Bar 20
Shackles 30
 


SHIELD BLOCK
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal Cult Ties-None

This spell adds an additional point of protection to any shield per point of spell and boosts the parrying skill of the holder by 5% per point of spell. Critical hits will ignore this effect but impales could be turned away if not enough damage is done. This spell is not compatible with Countermagic, Shimmer, or Spirit Shield.


SPIRIT BLADE
Range-Touch POW Used-4 points
Type-focused, active, temporal Cult Ties-None

This spell only effects a bladed weapon that has a bound spirit in it (See the RuneQuest rules for particulars on this sort of weapon and spirit). This spell gives the blade a +2 to damage and a +10% to hit chance due to manifestation of the spirit and the blade as a wispy glowing weapon of frightening power. When the wielder strikes a target with a critical result, the spirit within the weapon can draw fourth one point of POW from the target unless it resists the power of the spirit within the blade. The spirit can use this point of POW to heal one point of damage on the wielder, one point to itself (weapon damage) or it can use this point to replace an expended POW point of its own. A fumbled resistance versus the spirit within the blade will cause the POW loss to be permanent!

 
SPIRIT CHARM (Steal)
Range-Touch POW Used-4 points
Type-focused, permanent  Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

By means of this spell, the caster can sway away one bound spirit of the targets and bind it to himself.  This process requires that the caster also have the Spirit Binding spell and roll a successful chance at binding the spirit being drawn from the target.  If the binding fails, then the caster is attacked by the spirit and the spirit is treated as hostile.  If the binding succeeds then the spirit is bound to the caster as per the standard spirit binding rules. The target of the spell must make a POW resist against the caster of Spirit Charm in order for the target spirit to be wrestled away.  The caster of Spirit Charm has no choice as to which spirit of the targets is charmed away (allied spirits are immune to this spell) and if there are more than one, the GM will have to randomly determine which is lured.


SPIRIT QUIVER
Range-Touch POW Used-4 points + 1 per arrow
Type-focused, active, temporal Cult Ties-Aldrya

This spell requires that the caster have a spirit bound into his quiver. The spell allows the caster to draw, nock, and ready an arrow shot in only two strike ranks, allowing a possible five shots per round! This is accomplished by the spirit itself within the quiver, moving the arrows into place via the spirit world. This spell requires that one point of POW is spent from caster or spirit to make the arrow manifest on the bow and ready, costing a possible five points of POW per round of full usage. This spell will automatically shut down if the POW of the spirit drops to two or less. A spirit cannot move more than five arrows in this manner per round of battle. This spell is not compatible with Speedart, Multi-missile, or the spell Fire Arrow.


SPIRIT RELEASE
Range-Touch POW Used-3 points
Type-focused, permanent  Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

By means of this spell, the caster can cause any one bound spirit of the target to be released if the target that the spirit is bound to fails a POW resist versus the caster.  The weakest bound spirit will be the target of the spell as the caster has no choice as to which spirit is effected.  The spell has no effect on allied spirits or spirits that have a greater Intelligence score than that of the caster.  This spell will not free a spirit within an item that currently has a spell on it (such as Blade-Sharp, Dull-Blade, 
Fireblade, Iceblade, etc,.).


SPIRIT WALL
Range-80 meters POW Used-3 points
Type-focused, passive, temporal   Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

When this spell is cast, it creates a 10 meter wide by three meter high wall of opaque pearlescence.  The caster of this spell can cause the wall to move three meters per round but it cannot move through objects that are solid.  If any spirit passes through the area whether it is bound or not, will have 1d6 points of POW temporarily removed.  If this results in reducing the spirit below its native POW score, then the spirit is released back the spirit plain. This spell has no effect on items that are covered by another spell at the time of passing through the wall (such as Blade-Sharp, Dull-Blade, Fireblade, Iceblade, etc,.).


STRENGTHEN
Range-Touch POW Used-2 points
Type-passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal Cult Ties-None

This spell doubles the hit point score of a weapon. All damage absorbed will be taken off of these extra hit points before the real weapon hit points are touched. If at the end of the spell duration, the weapon is still above it's original score it will remain unaffected, the damage during the fight will vanish. If the weapon damage is greater than the weapons normal total hit points, then these extra points of damage are retained, possible causing the weapon to fall apart suddenly as the spell ceases.


UNDEAD SIGHT
Range-Touch POW Used-2 points
Type-unfocused on self, focused on others, passive, temporal  Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

This spell enables the target to see in a similar fashion to that of most undead creatures.  The POW score of any living object within the range of 40 meters will show as a bright outline around the object.  Spirits that are bound or allied will show as a different color than that of a native spirit or other natural spirit like POW producing object (such as a Power Crystal).  It total blackness, even magical, the target of this spell can still see everything but in a very stark black and white view with POW being the only color intensity visible.  Darker shades indicate a lower POW rating with bright yellows and whites showing a very high level of POW.  This spell is not compatible with Farsee.  Through a strange fluke, if the target of the spell is blind, they can actually "SEE" again by means of this spell but only as if they are standing in a totally magical dark room. 

 

WHIP ACCURACY
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point/point of spell
Type-Passive, unfocused on self, focused on others, temporal   Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

This spell enables the target Whip to strike better and faster in combat.  For each point of the spell, which is stackable to a total of four points, the user may add +5% to strike and -1 to the SR of the Whip.  The Whip cannot be dropped below zero for SR.  The spell also has a second function if it is known at a rating of four points.  The spell can be cast on a whip just before a strike to influence the aim of the Whip, this takes four points of POW and is lost right after the attack but does not effect spells that are already on the Whip.  By means of this version of the spell, the caster can select the location hit if he rolls a special result when attacking.  If the whip is parried during this sort of attack then the whip is fully deflected; in either case the spell points are expended in the attack.

WHIP QUIET
Range-Touch POW Used-1 point
Type-Passive, unfocused on self, un focused on others, temporal  Cult Ties-Spirit Hunter

This spell enables the Hunter to shroud the sounds of a Whip so as to be able to strike with complete silence.  It is possible with a normal silence spell to attack with a whip and the "Crack" of the whip end up on the outside of the spell effect area, this spell solves that problem.  This spell works only on items made from natural leathers.
