Thanatar Temple to Keche

By the Ouija, 03.04.2000

Outline: This adventure finds the characters thrown into an ancient battle of chaos versus chaos…?  The group will be confronted by a wizened old sorcerer that promises a great prize for those with great spear skills and the knack for impaling when pressured or in the heat of battle…from here they are whisked off to a feud between a group of escaping Vivamorts and a recluse variant cult of Than Atar known as the Kehch.  They will find themselves working for this variant group in an attempt to eliminate the recently escaped Vivamorts before they take over the Kehch temple and begin ravaging the countryside.  A fluke landed the Red Moon Goddess Tower, a magical creation resembling a tower that her armies could travel in and surprise enemies, right in the middle of an island along the Redstream River in the western reaches of the Stinking Forest.  From here the Vivamorts, having been isolated on an island in the center of the river for centuries, were able to quickly rid the tower of the unaware Lunars but in their haste killed those that knew the finer points of causing the tower to move about via it’s complex form of astral-teleportation.  Their first attempt at moving the tower landed the tower in the middle of the Kehch temple and during a chaos holy rite.  The ensuing battle has kept both sides at bay and in peril for a week now.  The party with their skills of impaling and spear, have been recruited, and are now working for the Kehch to rid the Vivamorts from the temple…Here is the catch, will the party be willing to work for the greater cause of law by destroying the stronger of the Chaos foes in favor of a lesser and possibly harmless Chaos foe?  Or will they turn on the Kehch once the vampire foes are eliminated…

Setting: In the north of Sartar within the confines of the silent stinking forest, resides the variant Cult of Thanatar, Kehch.  Their temple to their god is near a ruin on the edge of the Timberlands on the western edge of the Stinking Forest.  An abandoned fort and small village that has become the home to these people and the setting of this adventure.  The Sartar fort once known as Fort Endurance fell into disuse several hundred years ago and since has seen many come and go through it's tiny keep and cluster of a dozen ruined farms and pastures.  This has become the home of the Kehch and they have built a grand temple to their god over the remains of the fort itself, using the ruins beneath the fort as storage and escape tunnels in case of need; their variant cult is outcast and secretive due to their less than acceptable worship of Thanatar (could there really be such a thing?).  There is dense forest to the east of town and to the west is lighter forest that gives way to rolling hills and into the Lunar territory known as New Moon Hills.  To the north are rolling wooded hills and the not so far off town of Bloodvale, a semi-Transylvania like town rumored to be plagued by ghosts and powerful harvest spirits of darkness.  To the south lies the expanse of the Timberlands and farther into the Stinking Forest itself.  The party should approach the area after the encounter with the sorcerer from the south.  They will see the illustration that I included with the tower firmly planted in the temple dome.  When the tower arrived it pushed its way into dome from within causing the rippled stone effect shown.  The party will be greeted by Ashrum who will make way for the far side of the temple where the fighting is at a stalemate, the party is to enter the stairwell at location (4) and begin their sneak assault.

Adventure Lead in: One early morning as the party of well rested adventurers makes their way towards some much needed rest, they will notice that just ahead on the side of the road is a traveling sorcerer.  A single sway-back nag stands lazily eating grass next to the covered wagon embossed with the sign, "Altalon, Wizard Extraordinaire".  The wagon is near the road and a break in the canopy of forest that the party is in.  Near the wagon is a large pasture of meadow grass where the wizard appears to have setup six target dummies, each with a clutch of spears a good twenty paces from it:  a target and throwing range.

The Wagon

COMMON: Aside the road here is a large covered wooden wagon.  It looks worn and travel weary but appears sound.  It is covered with a great canvas covering of dark blue and bears the sign painted in red, "Altalon, Wizard Extraordinaire".  There are two large barrels of water attacked to the wagon, a couple of lamps, and apparent movement from inside, someone appears to be muttering to themselves.  The rear flaps are closed.  An old sway-back nag stands close by and ignores you as it chews fresh sweet grass.

CLOSER: There is a bucket of grease under the buckboard of the wagon that must be used for the wheels, there appear to be hundreds of runes etched into the wood of the wagon, few however appear to be recognizable, most are probably the scribbling that sorcerers are known for writing.  There is a whip and small strongbox also under the buckboard.  Inside the wagon is Altalon himself, busy looking for a small sack of blue scarves he bought in Pavis last fire season.  The interior is a mess, a house on wheels, how absurd.  There is a cot, a few boxes of food goods, a few rolls of canvas and twine, enough dungeon delving supplies to outfit a dozen adventurers.  Hanging in the wagon is a fish net that acts as a cloths net and catchall.  It is cluttered and very packed, it is a wonder whether or not the nag outside could even pull such a mass of junk. 

GM: Altalon will not take kindly to anyone rifling through his goods or going for his strongbox under the buckboard but will otherwise be jovial and happy to see the group.  His desperation for getting the group to try to use his spear field will be very evident.  The locked strongbox contains a Spirit of POW: 14, IN: 9 which will attack anyone but Altalon that opens the box.  In the box are 259 Lunars and 22 Wheels.  There is also a potion of Heal 6, another of Mobility, and one of Invisibility.  In the box can also be found a "Feedbag of Stormbull".  These black leather feedbags were used in ancient times by the Stormbull warriors when traveling far distances in strange lands where the food for their mounts might not be plentiful.  The bag once on the mouth of a beast, will fill with the appropriate food to provide a good meal.  It can do this once a day.  It is enough to survive on for the beast but just barely.  Of the junk in the wagon, little is of real value other than a set of four silver goblets worth 85 Lunars each.

The Wizard

CASUAL: The wizard is jovial, friendly and will ask each person to take a throw of a spear or three, those impaling will be given a goblet of the purest silver or two gold wheels.  In either case they are welcome for lunch, Altalon is a good cook and gracious host if not a bit old and scatter brained.

FRIEND: He will treat you as if you are long lost family, he will also implore you to help a group of his friends who have been assaulted over the last few days by a band of savage zombies (He doesn't know that they are really a group of Vampires).  He will give up his potion of Heal 6 and the invisibility if he thinks they need them.  He will teleport them north about twenty miles instantly so that help can be rendered quickly and before darkness comes again.  He fears that his friends will not withstand another assault.  He only knows that his friends are followers of a darkness cult but are really good people, being a sorcerer he is not concerned with chaos or law...much.

FOE: He will not ask to teleport the group, he will just do it as soon as they impale one of the dummies.  He will not fight a brutish party and will always be able to magically teleport himself, horse, and wagon out of harms way at just the last second.

GM: The wizard will act above as described depending on how he is treated; friendly or as a foe.  Altalon knew the party would be coming this way, the people of Kehch foresaw this and knew the group could help, or at least that is what their divination suggested.  Altalon will seek to employ the party as soon as they each have taken a few throws at the targets and at least one of them has impaled the dummy.  Upon impaling the dummy, Altalon will Teleport the entire party in an instant to just outside the Temple of Kehch, where they will begin just outside the first location on the map (1).  If the party fails to impale or refuses to play along with the game of impaling the dummies, then Altalon will assume that this is the wrong group and will vanish altogether, targets, spears, wagon, and horse, poof, gone.  The party will be instructed when they get their on their mission...simple you say?

The Field

COMMON: A large field of grass surrounded by trees breaks the canopy of the forest up.  The area is warm and bright, to one side of the field are sex target dummies of hay with red circles painted on their chests.  At the other side of the field are target spears.  The grass between the dummies and spears has been cut short and would make a great place for a joust or medieval fair.

CLOSER: The dummies resemble humanoids and are seven foot tall and hang from wooden hangman's posts.  The spears are simple practice spears with a weighted end but are crude and wooden.

GM: Altalon will cheer on the party as they each take turns throwing, that is of course if they are up to the game.  If anyone impales he will become very excited and of course whisk the party off to the aid of his friends.

I. Mythos and History

Before Time
    When the god Tien retreated into the darkest of pockets in Hell away from the pursuing son of Storm Bull, Hrothmir, and was severed by the ax of the avenging hero, Tien was left headless and his head burned and seared by the light shed by Yelm.  Hrothmir distracted by the release of a spirit entrapped by Tien during his above ground travels, was surprised by the still animated body of Tien that quickly dismembered him, devoured his spirit and took his head in place of his own severed one.  The spirit of a powerful warrior, honor bound to guard the imprisoned Yelm, left his post to aid Tien, for it appeared to him that Hrothmir and others that pursued him had him woefully outmatched.  The warrior stood by the now severed god and defended the remains against the onslaught of those in Hell who were attempting to take the fallen gods powers for their own and escape Hell.  This warrior spirit took up the remains of Hrothmir and with physical means, it fought off all attackers and escorted the dazed and weakened god out of Hell.  For his aid to Than during this time, the warrior spirit was so honored and released to command the scattered armies of Tien, he was named Kehch, also known as the honored.  He became a fierce warrior and leader of many of those that followed Tien but soon found that the disorder common to these troops left him unsettled, most likely left over feelings from the original owner of the body he possessed.  He left commanding all armies and became a recluse and vigilante and moved out of the memory of most.

Since Time Began
    During the Dawn Age, Kehch rallied troops against those headhunters that followed the primal beginnings of the god Thanatar.  Kehch always found ways to subvert great battles and cause the chaos that was so much a part of his foes to turn in on themselves and consume them.  Yet always Kehch drew strength from chaos but was always able to temper it with a measure of order and took upon himself the runic association of Stasis.  When the reunion of the god Tien failed and the cult of Than and Atyar merged but remained each unique, Kehch felt a pull towards the god.  The pull was insatiable and unstoppable.  During a high holy day on the last day of the Dawn Age, Kehch burst into the ceremony room of the grand temple to Thanatar and was fell upon by members of both cults.  He was drained of strength and tied to the altar before Thanatar to be sacrificed as the one who really failed, failed to prevent Hrothmir from first severing the god for in fact he was Hrothmir in mind and body, only the closeness to the god Tien at his death, had marked Hrothmir with unclear memories and he had lost himself.  Thanatar severed the head of his once ancient attacker, and began to bind the spirit into the head and found that Kehch had become a spirit more terrible in warrior nature than he himself could imagine.  Hrothmir stood, taking his head under arm, and with reflexes faster than any mortal, took Thanatar's own sickle, and once again severed the gods head from his shoulders.  He then fled before the aghast crowd who quickly returned the head to Thanatar.  Angered beyond description, Thanatar pushed his head back in place and glared at the fleeing Hrothmir and swore an oath of blood and vengeance.

Life After Death
    When a cultist dies he can expect to enter the afterlife or hero plane and here enter into the cycle of life and his spirit server others in duty and honor as Spirit Warriors or familiars of warriors.  It is the goal of each cultist to be of such skill and honor that they are preserved and enshrined by their fellow warriors so that they to can stay warring and battling through the ages.  A favorite saying of a dying member, "If not as a head then as a weapon, shield, or stone. I shall battle with my fellow warriors and never alone!"

Runic Associations
    During the many travels that Kehch made across Glorantha he came to the realization that chaos while a powerful force could be even more of a focus and power in his life if tempered and guided in only the slightest way.  The runic association of Stasis was gained on his travels and with this rune, he was able to temper the wild nature of chaos and give it a new and terrible aim.  Kehch has the runic associations of Death, Darkness, Chaos, and Stasis.

II. Nature of the Cult

Reason For Continued Existence
    As long as there are battles and wars to be fought, the followers of Kehch will be there taking the powerful strength of chaos and focusing it with stasis giving it a tempered edge with greater capacity to war.  The cult is tiny and has only recently resurfaced, therefore membership is low and the cult's main goal is to recruit warriors of any race or creed.  All warriors of chaos or law are regarded with honor and treated so.

Social/Political Position and Power
    The cult is believed to be the same as Thanatar to most that view them without close scrutiny.  Bloody heads and screaming victims are not found on the waist belt of a Kehch; merely the Honor Bags that retain the honored dead so that they can continue to fight alongside with their comrades.  Because of this association, most Kehch are mistaken and treated with contempt, ritualistic execution, and purification of their spirits through wild ceremonies and ancient magics.  The true nature of the Kehch is seldom realized.  Most Kehch realize this anathema and will keep their Honor Bags hidden and disguised as no more than belt pouches and since their grasp chaos with less a fervor than their Thanatar brothers, they are often the friend next door or the local militia commander.  Their numbers remain in recluse and worship in secret groves where training in weapon and spirit is encouraged.

Particular Likes and Dislikes
    Kehch followers have a revered and honorful trust of the skills of any warrior more especially those of the cult of Storm Bull, indirectly their father.  Followers of Stormbull are always honored and healed or more appropriately honored for their heads.  This view of the Storm Bull cult does not sit well with followers of Storm Bull, yes Kehch is Hrothmir, son of Storm Bull but that is where the family ends.  Storm Bull followers will respect those of Kehch but slay them as willingly as any other for the embrace of Chaos is seen as an abomination to all that is Law and particularly due to their claim of the runic association of Stasis.  Followers of Kehch dislike all that follow Thanatar but show respect to most other cults and those that are Rune Lords.

 

III. Organization

Inter-Cult Organization
    Temples are training arenas and one brother in weapon is treated as a brother by all others.  Some temples will specialize in a particular weapon or skill of fighting but all share the commonality of honor and the Honor Bag or Kutach.

Intra-Temple Organization
    Rune Lords are seen as the rulers of the cult.  Military in fashion, each temple is run like a military outpost with generals, captains, sergeants and corporals.  Rune Lords are treated as Master Generals and Rune Priests are treated as Master Priests.  Below these the initiate ranks are broken out into the various ranks established in a typical military.  All lay members are corporals and remain so until promoted through skill and deed.

Center of Power and Holy Places
    There is no center of power for Kehch however holy places are the inner training arenas and coliseums that the cult uses to test and promote warriors.  During battle or war, holy places may even be the battle field.

Holy Days and High Holy Days
    Dark Season and Light Season are holy times for the cult.  High Holy days are every day during Stasis week where many make pilgrimages to the outdoor mock wars and festivals held by many members.  Singing, dancing, eating, and fighting are the big lures of these celebrations of honor and war.

 

IV. Lay Membership

Requirements To Join
    Any race may join the ranks of the warriors of Kehch.  In order to join, he must prove that he has performed a deed of honor or fought bravely in battle.  He must also be willing to maintain the secrecy of some parts of the cult.  It is not the most popular cult to belong to because of the reputation that followers of Thanatar have brought to the world.

Requirements To Belong
    During holy week the member must participate in competitions of weapon and skill, he does not need to win but must show that his intent is earnest and that he embodies honor in the eyes of his fellows and peers.

Mundane Benefits
    Members are guaranteed the protection of the cult while on holy ground and should expect to be fed and clothed as long as they are willing to work or do odd jobs for the temple such as repairs or smithing.

Skills
    Members can expect to have access to training in any weapons they wish unless forbidden them by the cult.  Members gain the ability to resist the rune spell Destroy Mind as a gift from the releasing of the spirit of Lankor Mhy lady of Light and Knowledge.

V. Initiate Membership

Requirements For Initiation
    A member must have proven himself in battle at least four times and for at least four members of the officers staff.  He must prove that he embodies the principals of honor and has shown promise in weapon and skill.  After three years of lay membership an initiate can petition for promotion.  The candidate will be taken by the ranking officer to a great field and given the trials of honor and combat.  This event is to relive the past and origins of Kehch.  He will be stripped naked and don the robes of a barbarian to symbolize the entity of Hrothmir.  He will then fight his way blindfolded past officers that swipe at his feet and claw at his body leaving him a torn mess but alive by the time the battle field is found. Then he will be unmasked and left with a dagger to take his own life to symbolize Hrothmir's own life being taken to enter Hell.  The candidate is stopped before he fully does so but must prove he is willing to die for the goal of honor. From here he is dressed in blackened armor and every inch of his body painted gray while songs and deeds of honor are sung.  At night when the stars glow he is guided to the side of Yelm and basks in the light of his glory and is purified when he succumbs to the visions and sees the god Tien struck down by himself!  He must then rush to prevent his own head from being taken and passes out when he finds himself covered in dark cloth and enfeared.  He wakes to find himself amongst brothers that welcome him into the Kehch warrior clan and quickly doused him with spirits and food and a great feast begins.  The feast is interrupted by a vision of Thanatar taking his head and showing him that he is indeed the embodiment of Hrothmir the son of Storm Bull.  The initiate will nearly lose his head but instead will take a sickle thrust into his hands and take the head of his attacker.  Upon this the startled candidate is hailed as initiated and the feast continues uninterrupted.

Requirements To Remain Initiated
    Do well and perform deeds with honor and justice.  If cowardess is ever observed, this will cause the initiate to lose rank and return to the rank of Lay Member.  Redemption can always be sought via trial by fight.

Mundane Benefits
    The warrior and new initiate is always treated to a new weapon or garrote as a prize for putting up with the hazing.  Initiates are allowed to take one chaos feature at this point, if the outcome is foul then the Priest can use Temper Chaos and possibly turn the curse around to a blessing.

VI. Rune Lord Membership

General Statement

VII. Rune Priesthood

General Statement
    Master Priests of the cult are treated as the tacticians of the clan, they are often field marshals during battles and directly field placement of men and resources.  Priests are in charge the temple and have access to special magics and powers that others may fear and respect.  

Requirements For Acceptance
    To become a priest, a candidate must have shown prowess in battle, brilliance of tactics, and justice of weapon and deed.  He must have been an initiate for over two years and have a POW of 18.  A candidate must pass a test of battle where tactics play the ruling hand, this is worked out to the following formula: INT + POW divided by two and multiplied by five.  This will give the chance he has of beating the tactics dream that he succumbs to during the ceremony of battle.  If he fails he may try again in one years time.  If he wins, he is transformed into a Rune Priest of Kehch also known as the Savant Tactician.

Restrictions
    The priest must officiate at all battles and on holy gatherings including initiations.  The priest must travel wherever war or battle resides and Kehch Rune Lords are fighting.  Each day sacrifice a temporary point of POW to Kehch to show devotion.  Weapon skills are not limited to the standard DEX x 5 for the Savant Tactician.

Benefits
    The priest gains all standard benefits of Rune Priests as laid out in the RuneQuest rules.  The priest gains an allied spirit unless he has one already, this is in the form of an Honor Bag on a necklace, a special family member warrior is often used for this.  The priest gains access to all cult special spells as well as the special Spirit of Reprisal 3-Point spell usable only by priests.

 

VIII. Cult Special Rune Spells or Skills

General Statement
    Followers of Kehch have access to the following special Rune Spells and special cult spells.
Create Skeleton 1-point spell, duration permanent, Range touch, reusable, non-stackable

Using the bones of a fallen member of Kehch, this spell can be used to reanimate them into a Skeleton that will server the temple as a guard or other duties as required.  The skeleton will have the same skill levels of weapons as it did in life.

Temper Chaos 2-point spell, duration permanent, Range touch, reusable, non-stackable

This spell is cast just as a Chaos Feature manifests in the target person, the priest is given insight and can see the formation of the feature and if he deems it unworthy of appearing, he can guide its creation and temper the effects of the feature.  By means of the spell, the priest can guide the feature one or two features above of below the rolled feature.  The priest rolls 1d100 and if the result is 01-10 he may then choose the feature altogether, 11-40 he can influence the choice of the feature by one or two shifts on the chart up or down, and rolls above 40 will allow the priest to move the feature by one shift.  On rolls of 96-100 the feature will remain the poor one but become twice as annoying or defective.

Create Minor Honor Bag 2-point spell, duration permanent, Range touch, reusable, non-stackable

By means of this spell the caster is able to, through ritual, preserve and embalm the head of a recently dead warrior.  Through ritual ancient and steeped in honor of the dead and the care of their remains, the head is shrunken and treated with respect through the three day long ritual.  Special ointments and salves are used in the process that purify the head and preserve it in such a fashion as to make a perfect house for the spirit that once was part of the head.  The priest then calls the spirit forth at the end of the ritual and must roll on 1d100 under the POW score of the spirit times four.  If the spell fails, the head is destroyed and the spirit can never be so summoned again.  Failure indicates that the Rune Priest or Lord performing the ritual is not as pure in honor or deed as he professes and may not attempt the spell for another year.  Success indicates that the head was honored to be one with the caster and is placed in an honor bag.  The battle magic that was known to the spirit is now available to the Kehch as well as Rune Magic, however, Rune Magic reverts to one shot.  Only one head for every four points of CHA the character has is allowed.  No further heads can be made until either one is destroyed by accident or is laid to rest thought casting this spell again in a reverse fashion and in an equally long ritual with similar success chances at the end.  The caster must supply all POW to use the spells of a head and rune spells require four points of POW per point of the rune spell.  The skills of the spirit can also be tapped by means of an Honor Bag.  The skills of the spirit can augment the Kehchs own skills as follows, and these are cumulative with the addition of more heads:

Create Honor Necklace 2-point spell, duration permanent, Range touch, reusable, non-stackable

This spell is exactly the same as Create Minor Honor Bag above but the head is fashioned onto a necklace and is held in a smallish sack made of the fined gold.  The spell follows all the same restrictions for that spell as well.  Only the most honored of family members are petitioned to become an Honor Necklace.

Create Major Honor Bag 3-point spell, duration permanent, Range touch, reusable, non-stackable

This spell is exactly the same as Create Minor Honor Bag above.  The spell follows all the same restrictions for that spell as well.  Only the most honored of family members are petitioned to become a Major Honor Bag.  Major Honor Bags may store their own power and act on their own much in the same fashion as allied spirits do.  Honor bags are normally passive until the Kehch puts POW into a spell but the Major Honor Bag spirits can operate on their own and will do so in favor of the holder always seeking honor and justice in doing so.  These allied spirits are however limited and count as two Honor Bags for the determination of limitation of having the bags and these heads have a permanent Mind-Speech with the Kehch and can converse normally whereas the standard head is quite silent unless the spell Mind Speech is cast by the Kehch.  Rune Spells known by a Major head are re-usable but cost five POW per Rune Magic point to cast, the Kehch may augment the POW of the head or use POW from anywhere to manifest Rune Magic or Battle Magic this way.

Call Spirit of Reprisal 3-point spell, duration 15 minutes against non-cult members or until the mission is accomplished against cult-members, Range 160 meters, reusable, non-stackable

This spell allows the caster to call forth the Axe of Hrothmir, this spirit will punish wayward cult members or can be called to assist the Priest or Lord calling it.  The Spirit Warrior is described later under Subservient Cults.
