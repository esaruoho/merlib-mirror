Rare and Ancient Cults

In this section I cover cults that are rare or so old that they have fallen from memory.  Usually this play a role in one of the adventures here but more often they are fanciful ideas with useful new spells, skills, and more.  If you wish to submit a Cult of your own design here, I can post it as long as it does not violate a copyright somewhere.
