The Tree of Runes

By the Ouija, 11.30.1998

Outline: This smallish roughed out adventure has the party seeking out the ruins of a small knowledge temple. Within this temple they will find the clue they need, that the object of their goal resides deep within the Stinking Forest in some ruins near an area known as Aldryas Seat. It is here within the gardens that the party will find the Tree of Runes. The tree holds the key to the completion of the adventure but has been ignored for over a thousand years, or has it....

The tree and surrounding gardens are now a death trap of chaos and filth, a swamp of gorps and wandering broos. At the gardens center stands a mound of debris that is over fifty feet tall, underneath is the tree they seek, lost to light. With the tangle of debris removed and special attention given to the tree it may produce the object desired or perish. The tree is said to heal those placed at it's base but the details end there, there is more to this than a snooze in the grass...

Setting: Deep within the Stinking Forest amidst a lengthy dank swamp full of nasties and chaos uglies. First the party must travel to a small village near the Stinking Forest called Abrams Villa, this village is very small and has one temple to Lankor Mhy, a general store called Aberths General, and a small tavern called Trixies. This village is a stopover near the Stinking Forest for those travelling through to ElfGuard or Dragons pass from the southern Sartar region.

Adventure Premise: The characters are approached by the town council, their priest, or a stranger to take heed and seek out a cure for the poison root called 'Black Dream'. It turns out that one of the characters and one of the personalities in the town ended up being poisoned by this root at a recent light festival. The characters must play detective and discover who did this and how to make an antidote. There is a Lankor Mhy temple just outside of town run by an insane priest. This would be the best place to start searching for answers...

The galleries on its second level have been closed for ten generations after a horrible fire and giant crushing activity. This is where the party must search for the antidote, in the ancient archives. These archives would have long been cleaned out but due to the remote location and obscurity of the priest, it has been ignored by the cult in general.

Galleries of Tan Delin, Temple of Lankor Mhy: Temple Building

Lower Level:

The lower temple of the floor is kept clean and straight while the stairs to the upper floor and doored/barred/and locked. Tan will allow characters up here but only as long as they do not hold him responsible for what may happen. Tan will help find whatever books are necessary as long as they are on the first floor (which no useful books are). You can decide what books are here on the first floor: such as Cooking Troll Style, Great Chefs of Sartar, The Fungi and You, Sawdust and Swords, How to Build a Better Rubblerunner Trap, ...

1. Entrance

COMMON: There is a small table where visitors sign in. There is a counter where books can be checked out and a massive tapestry acting as a wall from the rest of the library. The floors are laid stone and the ceiling is plaster and timbers.

CLOSER: The last signers to visit the galleries were here over ten years ago and never signed out. The tapestry is woven of heavy cotton and is adorned with elder leaders of the cult, mainly noble looking teachers and dignitaries. The tapestry can easily be parted in several places and acts as a noise deadener. The floor is a checkerboard of greens and reds.

GM: This area is where Tan will greet the party, he is usually busy dusting books in the library and leaves a small bell here to ring for his attention. Tan has no memory of the last adventurers that entered the gallery other than one was a Humakt on a Hero Quest (Yikes!) Tan might check out books if the characters have a library card (or are an associated cult).

2. Back Logs

COMMON: There are piles of books here, a cart, rubbing oil and rags, and stacks of papers.

CLOSER: The papers are records of who has used the temple and contributors to the cause of Lankor Mhy. The rags smell of rubbing oil and appear to be used in keeping the leather book covers in good shape. The cart is loaded with half a dozen books that look ready for re-shelving.

GM: This is where Tan sorts through returned books so that he can correctly check them in and move them about. This area also acts as a back entrance to the library reading areas.

3. Library

COMMON: The library is large and old, the ceiling is 5 meters overhead and made of wood and plaster. In the back are rows of books, a few tables with chairs, there are paintings that line the walls and ceiling.

CLOSER: The paintings are of local scenes but of a more pleasant bent that what now lies outside of the village. The aged timbers that make up the ceiling appear to be hand carved from single pieces of wood. Each table has a large bin of candles and a tinderbox for late studies.

GM: There is a short flight of stairs that leads to the main library from where the study tables are, these are dark hand oiled oak and very beautiful. Unfortunately this level of the library is where the less valuable and useful books were kept so little is left of useful studying. There is a 5% chance per hour of research here that the character will discover that any good information will be gleaned from above, in the ruins. Of course anyone would be able to see this in five minutes, but you know how players may want to look at EVERY book no matter how droll. (Sigh heheh)

4. Stairway

COMMON: The stairwell is blocked by a heavy black wooden door with a great lock in it's center.

CLOSER: The door is covered in law runes and is covered in thick pitch effectively sealing it into the wall preventing any air from above to enter. There is a bucket of pitch and a wooden spatula here. The door looks like an addition and not part of the original library construction.

GM: The door holds Tan's allied spirit Grover. INT: 17, POW: 21, Spells: Demoralize, Binding, and Glue 6 (Yes 6!). Grover will try to prevent anyone from passing that is not allowed to by Tan.

Upper Level:

The galleries are dangerous at best. 300 years ago a giant tramped through the area, Jogriss was his name, and in a fit of rage he attacked the temple. In those days, the temple was topped with the rather slovenly gilded statue of the current priest, a fat and lazy man of little true ability other than the keen ear for his own gas passing his lips (or other places). Jogriss toppled the statue and destroyed the temple upper galleries leaving the lower galleries a burning shambles and the first level untouched. Tan never enters the upper galleries, no one has for the last twelve years.

COMMON: The gallery is dark, there is a ceiling about 5 meters above but it is way to dark to see how sturdy it is. The floor is slate and covered with debris. There is no light seeping in from outside. All is dark.

CLOSER: The ceiling is dark timbers and blackened plaster. There are patches of tar that have seeped in from above leaving sticky black pools on the floor. All is a jumble, the nearby book cases are burned and broken but there are some books that look legible. The far end of this gallery looks badly caved in and debris lies everywhere it would be necessary to climb over it to get to books there.

With this news the party will need to set out for the tree. There is a crude map showing the tree location roughly twenty miles north in what is now the heart of the Stinking Forest, chaos and all!

ON TO THE TREE...

Location Description: Deep within the Stinking Forest lie the ruins of the Gardens of Aldrya. It is said that she came to this area to seek solace during the tribulations of the gods, her touch was life here. It is now the haven of chaos and disease, a band of Malia worshipers has taken up residence and guardianship over the mass that was once the tree of runes. A low two-foot tall wall of white marble, long since dirtied and sullied, surrounds the gardens. The map shows the general location of the walls and the two small tree towers and of course the mound that was the tree of runes.

Travel to and from: It is suggested that the party need to travel from a local city and into the Stinking Forest to really appreciate the comforts of city living. The Stinking Forest and its own harsh life will make the travel hideously difficult. It will take three days of travel once the forest is reached if the party make their mapping rolls in order to find the area that the gardens are in (or rumored to be so).

Night One: An ancient and far away from home Agimori enters the clearing and speaks to the ill character, hands him a feathered head band and medicine bag. He offers to sing a song of peace for him and give him a blessing for the travel ahead: for the character is marked with the touch of death.

Day Two: Trollkin Attack! (Gotta luv em). Six Trollkin attack the party, one is a value Trollkin with Protection 4 and Demoralize!

Night Three: Just before the party reaches the low wall area and the towers and mound, the twisted forms of past adventures will stalk them, now converted to Chaos Plants. (4) Stink Cabbages and (1) Spore Man will attack the party. These will appear to be directed by a broo who flees just after the party sees him. One wears a brooch made of tarnished silver.

Tree of Runes Garden

The garden was once plush and fertile but after Aldrya left and never returned, this place of healing was quickly overrun by chaos. The garden is now the home of the Broo Three Horn and his pack of veggie friends that he controls through old magics. While in the garden there is a 10% chance per half-hour of searching to be discovered by someone. 01-10 it is 1d4 Skunk Cabbages, 11-40 it is 1d4 Spore Men, 41-90 it is 1d4 Broos, and 91-100 it is four broos and Twister the Broo.

1. Entrance to the Gardens

COMMON: The swamp area is steamy and rank, pools of stagnant water stand surrounded by tall cat tail fens and nasty barbed ferns. There is a low barely visible wall that roughly outlines the old walkway through the garden area.

CLOSER: The walls are white marble, broken in places but still clean (Rune Magic here). There are a few visible flag stones that mark the walkway into the garden area. There are many weeds and brambles about. Unkept for over 800 years, the area is a disaster. Far ahead looms a twenty-meter tall mound of sticker bushes and thick thorn brambles.

GM: The party should make spot hidden rolls, those that due will see that the remains of three trolls lie amongst the weeds, they are covered with skunk cabbage vines and warty twitching spore boils. There is a 5% chance to encounter a spirit here, POW: 16, it was freed from the nearby troll when his sword broke. The trolls have no treasure.

2. Ruins of Fountain

COMMON: There was once a fountain of white marble here in a two foot deep blue stone basin.

CLOSER: The fountain was a statue of the likeness of Aldrya, some force tore it apart from within. The basin is choked with thick dark black weeds with bright orange berries that smell rotten.

GM: The berries will cause a temporary increase in INT by 1d6 for 1d4 hours if less than a dozen are eaten. If more are eaten, then the INT of the character will rise by 2d6 for one hour but then drop to 1d3+1 for the rest of the day returning to normal in the morning, leaving a bad headache. If the statue is repaired by some means, each member of the party will receive one point of stat to place anywhere they wish, a gift from Aldrya.

3. Tower of Oak

COMMON: This ancient tree has an acorn of gold over the black wooden doorway. There are vines that nearly cover this entire twenty-foot tall structure. At one time it looks to have been a living oak but all the branches have been burned off or hacked off, sap closes most of the wounds, ancient and pealed. The tower looks very old and careworn.

INSIDE: Inside the interior is old and wooden, the floor is wood as are all furnishings. There is a bed covered with a layer of muddy dust, the tree has developed a recent leak. There is a skeleton lying crumpled next to the bed, a tarnished silver dagger in his back, his head is missing. There is a table with rotten food on it, and a dresser with clothing.

GM: The tower has remained untouched inside and was the residence of the caretaker of the gardens (an Aldrya Priest of course). A POW: 15 spirit guards the entrance way, there are warnings as to this beings presence clearly marked on the outside of the door in spirit speech writing.

SECRET: Hidden in a crack near the ceiling is a golden key on a leather necklace, a small box with 124 gilders, a smallish silver wedding ring with Celtic like designs, and a diary that crumbles if touched.

4. Tower of Ash

COMMON: This ancient tree appears strong and alive. The tree stands sixty feet tall and has a crenellated tower like platform at the thirty-foot level. There is a door there and several narrow arched windows. The door sill is clean, it is obvious that vines have attempted to cling to the structure but have withered and died.

CLOSER: The black wooden door is padlocked from the outside and runes of chaos and undead are scratched in the doors face. The entry door is arched with a silvered bow over the mantle.

INSIDE: The interior is dark, clean, and fairly bare. There is a ladder leading to an upper floor and balcony with a doorway leading out to a ledge. The floor is clean and the only furniture aside from the ladder is a single chair in the center of the room, facing the doorway. Next to the chair is a small end table with a small-unlit oil lamp, a large glass of fresh water with clear cold crystals in it (Yap, ice).

GM: There is a Warding Spell on the doorway, just inside, it packs a 4d3-disruption damage unless the passer can make a POW resist versus a 19 POW. The one liter glass is magical and will always keep fresh ice in whatever beverage is held within it. There are two invisible zombie dark trolls in the room, one on either side of the door. These guards attack any non-broo entering the room. They were placed here by the broos in the area whom also set the warding spell. Zombie #1 "Meatloaf": AP:5, Pips: 18 (6 in head), 2-H Mace 65/65 1d10 HP: 25 (Petrified), 1d10 luners if searched and 1d3 wheels. The second zombie has a Chaos Feature, +3d6 CON! His stats are: Zombie #2 "Nails": AP: 5, Pips: 33 (12 in head), 2-H Mace 65/65 1d10 HP: 25 (Petrified), 1d10 luners if searched, 1d3 wheels, and a gem embedded in the back of his head worth 85 luners.

5. Mound of Debris

COMMON: There is a huge pile of debris here, a massive sticker patch with brambles and twisted vegetation.

CLOSER: There is a hunched over tree deep within the twisted mound but the bushes and thorny shrubs would require hours to hack through. There seems to be a pattern to the growth, a pattern that keeps light from reaching the entombed tree.

GM: It will take one hundred man hours to clear this mess enough to reach the tree, the brambles seem to regrow quickly. This can be split amongst many too speed the process. The tree once freed will straighten slowly in the light and begin to bear leaves. Within two days the tree will appear and a giant weeping willow tree over 100 feet tall, it's dark blackish wood a mesh of intertwined runes of silver and gold hues.

6. Tree of Runes

SPECIAL: If any wounded being is laid here and the blood of a healthy person shed willingly for that person, then the wounded person will be fully restored and healed of all ailments. The healthy person whom shed the blood will have to heal by normal or magical means. A few pips are all the blood required and the giver must be willing. The tree can do this only once a month unless Aldrya is present, then it can perform this feat to the Goddesses content.

7. Body of Centaur

COMMON: There is a dead centaur here amongst the weeds.

CLOSER: The centaur appears to have been stabbed to death by spears and a mace of some sort has smashed off his head. The centaur still has a backpack on.

GM: The backpack contains a small local map of the area, half a dozen potatoes, a lump of green cheese, and a dozen luners. The reason the backpack was left is that the spirit of the centaur attacked twister and was defeated and bound. This lead twister to believe that the body was cursed and left it here for the carrion birds.

8. Stature out of Place

COMMON: There is a statue here of a muscled attractive woman.

CLOSER: The statue bore at one time a name plate but has long since been pried off and gone. The statue is two meters tall and appears very lifelike. It stands on a dark marble one-foot tall pedestal. It appears that this statue was dropped here from some other location. A warrior woman of possibly Aldrya was the model for this work but the model was not elfish which is clearly obvious by bodily proportions. She is in a pose as if to launch a bow shot to the moon and down on one leg.

GM: The marble statue was a living woman at one time but was turned to stone ages ago and moved here by who knows. It appears that maybe some one was trying to steal it away and gave up, it is quite heavy, over 150 kilos.
