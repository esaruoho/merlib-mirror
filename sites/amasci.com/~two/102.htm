TURNS AND TILTS TO ANY POSITION
Four functional design features make PANAVISE work holder tool systems the most versatile in the world:

(1) The vise head can turn a full 360' in the "locking unit" in the base.
(2) The vise head can also tilt 90' in any direction (180' tilt action).
(3) The housing containing the "locking unit" also rotates full-circle.
(4) All these movements are controlled by the work at any desired drag-tension, or to lock it firmly, at any position, any angle, facing any direction around the base.
