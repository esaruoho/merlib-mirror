 fuels other than acetylene, the two smallest tips, Nos. 1 & 2

 PRECIOUS METAL
 MELTING TIP - This uniquely designed tip makes your Little
 Torch even more versatile. The melting tip uses a quadruple
 orifice design to create four separate flames that form one flame
 which converge to a single point and is equivalent to four size
 tips. This technology gives you a flame suitable for melting in a
 crucible. When used with propane the temperature reaches 5200' F.
 - with other fuels - 6000' F.
