The future of sanding belts is here today. 3M Trizact material is a new microreplication technology providing consistent cut rates and uniform finishes throughout the life of the belt. Recommendation: If you currently use a 220 mesh belt, try a 400 in Trizact. You will find that your pre-polish finish will polish out quickly without generating as much heat.
