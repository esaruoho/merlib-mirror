 The handpiece is powered by user supplied compressed air that
 spins a turbine surrounding the chuck of the handpiece. The chuck
 rotates at speed up to 350,00 rpm, 25 times as fast as a flexible
 shaft machine. Instead of removing relatively large particles at a
 slow rate of speed, the Turbine Tool grinds away tiny, almost
 pulverized particles at an extremely rapid rate. The result is
 greatly increased precision in grinding operations and a much
 better finish on materials as hard as steel. 
 The complete Turbine Tool outfit includes: handpiece, stand with
