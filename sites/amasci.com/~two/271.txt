 PRICES 

 Prices shown are subject to change
 without notice. We do our best to keep the prices as low as possible.
 Merchandise is billed at prices prevailing at the time of shipment. If
 firm prices are required, please request a quotation. Washington
 State residents must add sales tax to all orders. We reserve the
 right to correct any errors in the catalog. 

 MINIMUM ORDER $25.00 or $3.00 handling
 charge added. 

 Because of the cost of processing,
 packaging and shipping the orders, we can only accept orders of $25.00
 or more retail. Any order accepted for less than $25.00 is subject to
 a $3.00 handling fee. 

 SHIPPING 

 Shipment is made through your choice
 of carriers usually within 24 hours of receipt of your order. 
 Report any Shortages within 48
 hours of receipt of the parcel. Give the invoice number and date.
 Examine the contents and packing material carefully for small items that
 may have been missed during the unpacking of the parcel. 
 If merchandise is received DAMAGED ,
 advise the Delivery Agency, notify Alpha Supply and save all of the
 packing material and shipping carton for inspection by the Delivery
 Agency. You will receive instructions for handling the damage claim. If
 merchandise is received DEFECTIVE, advise Alpha Supply for
 disposition. 

 BACKORDERS 

 In the event we are out of an item
 that you ordered, we will back order it for you if you wish. You may
 select to have it Open Backorder, Cancel 30 Days, or Cancel at your
 Request. Items ordered, but out of stock, with a value of less than
 $15.00 will not be backordered. Please reorder those items with your
 next order. 

 RETURNS 

 If you are not completely satisfied
 with any product purchased from ALPHA SUPPLY, you may return it within 10
 days of receipt, in its original display carton, in resaleable
 condition, insured for full face value and shipped prepaid along with a
 copy of your invoice. We are not responsible for loss or damage on
 return shipment to us. 
 Items returned due to customer error,
 late returns, merchandise not in its original condition, not in the
 original container or without a copy of the original invoice will be
 subject to a 10% restocking fee. 

 Returns over 90 days old are
 returnable for exchange only. 

 Please Note : 

 The description and picture representations in this
 catalog resemble the actual product as closely as possible. However,
 because of the various manufacturers continued attempts to improve their
 merchandise, specifications will sometimes vary. 

 PAYMENT 

 Payment must be received with the
 order payable in U.S. funds or charged to a valid Visa, Mastercard, or
 Discover Card. In order to use these charge cards we must have the
 proper card account no. and the expiration date. COD shipment
 will be made payable in cash or Postal Money Order for all first time
 orders. On all COD shipments refused or undeliverable, the customer
 will be liable for all handling charges . Amount due in excess of
 prepayment may be collected by COD. Foreign orders can not be sent COD 

 There is a $25.00 SERVICE CHARGE on
 all checks returned to us by the Bank. 

 On any PAST DUE AMOUNTS there
 is a service charge of 1 1/2 % per month, effective rate of 18 % 
 per annum, or the maximum amount allowed by law.
