 Optivisor MAGNIFIERS 
 Optivisor is a highly versatile precision made binocular magnifier that
 is worn on the head ... over your regular glasses if desired. . .
 leaving both hands free. 
 Can be instantly tilted downward when needed and upward when not in use. 
 Frame made of light weight high impact resistant material. 
 Lenses made of optical glass ground to precision standards. 
 Genuine leather comfort band makes it more comfortable than any other
 worn magnifier 
 A twist of the adjustment dial changes head size. ABS high impact visor.
