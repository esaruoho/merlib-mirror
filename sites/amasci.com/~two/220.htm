Crystalmet by Crystalite

Sintered Metal Bond Wheels and Discs

*

New and greatly improved sintered metal bond wheels and discs made with Crystalite's proprietary bond formulation.

*

Manufactured for the lapidary industry. Crystalmet wheels and discs will provide long life, aggressive cutting action with reduced chipping at a consistent rate and with an excellent finish.

*

All wheels and discs are solid steel.

*

Crystalmet sintered metal bond wheels and discs are for those who want high volume production and long life from their wheels.

*

Sintered diamond 3/16" thick on the wheels, 1/8" thick on the disc.

Note: Use the entire face of the wheel/ disc to avoid grooving. If slow cutting occurs, dress the face of the wheel/disc with Crystalites dressing stick to restore cutting action. Use with Crystalcut rust inhibitor.
