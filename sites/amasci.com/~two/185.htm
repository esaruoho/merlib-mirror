Castaldo mold rubber - the industry leader.

White Label

Has a high concentration of pure natural gum rubbers which makes it soft and pliable, qualities that the experienced mold- cutter values in his efforts to produce high-quality molds in the shortest reasonable time. CASTALDO molding rubber has been an industry standard for more than 30 years.

Gold Label

Contains more pure natural gum rubber than White label making it even softer and more pliable. Experienced mold-cutters know that this compound's superior qualities can save substantially on labor costs when used for difficult molds containing under-cuts, where a stiffer mold would break delicate wax patterns. CASTALDO molding rubber has been an industry standard for more than 30 years.

No Shrink Pink

CASTALDO No Shrink Pink jewelry molding rubber is a natural rubber product, just like CASTALDO White Label and Gold Label jewelry molding rubber. It can be used in combination with those standard compounds, or by itself. EVERYTHING about No Shrink Pink is designed to be either compatible with or similar to White and Gold Label.
