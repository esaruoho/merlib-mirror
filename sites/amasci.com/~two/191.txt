 The new MARK
 IV faceting unit is a precision built and designed faceting machine that
 features the most modern design, yet retains the features proven by over
 31 years of faceting machine manufacturing. The New MARK IV is 
 truly a
 compact unit weighing approximately 27 lbs. and needs a space of only 9 
 1/2" x 17 1/2". Compact in size, yet uses full 8" laps
 and all standard machine accessories. Ruggedly built for years of
 trouble-free service. 

 Exclusive
 Features - 

 * 

 Infinitely variable
 speed 0 to 600 RPM-selected with the turn of a conveniently
 located control. This variable speed allows the user to select the
 lap speed needed for cutting and polishing a wide range of
 faceting materials. The powerful DC shunt wound motor with a
 special electronic speed control system gives smooth cogless high
 torque low speed operation. 

 * 

 Precision radial
 cheater that rotates the dop arm-repeatable settings. 

 * 

 The indexing
 hand-piece floats freely on dual precision ball bearings giving
 featherweight feel for cutting of the tiniest gems. 

 * 

 The faceting head
 is engineered so when the stone is raised from the lap for
 inspection, the engraved index plate, the angle protractor scale
 and the radial cheater dial all face the gem cutter-no need to
 switch back and forth to index for the next facet or change
 angles. 

 * 

 Precision vertical
 adjustments are made with a built-in click micrometer. Major
 vertical adjustments are instantly made by sliding the head
 assembly up or down the mast and locking in position with the hand
 screw provided. 

 * 

 Angle stop can be
 read to 0.1'.
