SURFACE DREDGES

Ideal for gold recovery from stream gravel. The dredge rests on the surface of the water and dredges gravel from the stream bed. The dredge consists basically of a flotation assembly, a sluice box, motor, water pump and suction hose. The motor and water pump are employed to create a suction in the hose so that the gravel is sucked up and discharged over the sluice box that separates the gold from the gravel. We have many sizes of dredges available manufactured by Keene Engineering Co., ranging from 2 1/2 inch (suction hose) to 6 inch. These can be broken down for transportation to the recovery site.
The Keene Engineering Co. catalog is available at no change for those interested in dredges and prospecting equipment & supplies.
