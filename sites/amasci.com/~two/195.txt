 NEW! ALPHA TARUS FACETING
MACHINE 

 This updated compact
 version of the original Alpha Tarus is one of the best faceting machines
 on the market today. Constructed of anodized aluminum, stainless steel,
 brass and high precision bearings, no cost-cutting compromises are used
 in manufacturing this unit. Made for cutters of all skill levels the
 Alpha Tarus will give years of enjoyment. The many features of the Alpha
 Tarus make it fast and precise, some of these features are: 
 1/2 inch thick anodized aluminum, surface
 ground base plate. Dovetail slide, maintains mast to arbor
 alignment. Mast, 7/8" stainless steel inner mast and 1
 1/8" outer sleve suspended on sleeve and ball bearing for support
 with no backlash or wobble. Mast travel micrometer provides vertical
 travel of 1 inch in .001 inch increnments. Mounting yoke lock knob,
 has a quick release feature for moving the facet head up and down as
 well as a safety auto lock, to prevent the dropping of the head. 
 Depth dial indicator, indicates stone removal in .001" increments.
 Coarse protractor can be latched every 5 degrees. Protractor
 vernier dial, sets the angle to .1 degree. Quill draw collet, one of
 the most accurate ways to hold a dop. Cheater dial provides + 
 6 degree rotation with repeatable home position. Variable speed
 0-1400 R.P.M. with reversing switch.
