First grind and shape your stone on the 80 and 220 mesh galaxy wheels then spend just enough time on the 280 mesh Nova resin bond diamond sanding wheel to completely remove scratches left by the grinding wheels-only a few minutes in most cases. Proceed to the 600, 1,200 and 14,000 mesh Nova polishing wheels. Most stones properly prepared on the grinding wheels and the 280 Nova wheel take only a few minutes more on the three polishing wheels to achieve a beautiful polish. Both the Genie and Pixie are clean to use and portable; work at your kitchen table.

Nova Sanding and Polishing Wheels and Discs
The diamond in Nova Wheels and Discs is embedded within a tough, flexible plastic resin and backed by 1/2" of soft sponge rubber which allows the surface of the wheel to conform to your  cab to eliminate flat spots and scratches. Nine mesh sizes are available so that you can go from sanding to final polish. Nova wheels require a small amount of lubricant, Gem - Lube or water.

(1)   Nova discs - Available in 4" and 5 1/2" diameters. They are slightly domed for faster sanding of flat surfaces such as pen stands. They have a standard 1/4" x 20 bolt molded into their plastic back-up plate and will fit most machines using disc attachments. (2)   The Nova Peripheral wheels - Available in 4", 6" and 7 5/8" diameters with bushings furnished to fit standard arbor sizes.
