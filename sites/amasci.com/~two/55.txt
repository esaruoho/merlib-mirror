 12 Karat Gold Filled 
 Sheet & Wire 

 1/20 12 Karat gold
 filled, or gold overlay, is a process where a thin layer of gold
 is bonded to a base metal with heat and pressure. The 1/20 refers
 to the ratio of the 12 karat gold layer to the base metal layer by
 weight. This means that 5% of the product weight is 12 karat gold.
 The base metal used is brass. Since the layer of gold is thin,
 care must be taken when polishing or soldering gold filled
 material. After all finishing is completed, it is recommended that
 the item be gold plated to assure uniform color and to cover any
 exposed brass.
