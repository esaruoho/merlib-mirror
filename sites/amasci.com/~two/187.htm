The Ultra Tec Faceting Machine

The Ultra Tec Faceting Machine offers the ultimate in faceting technology. Its precision and repeatability provides the user with the personal satisfaction of stones close to perfection .... and maximum yields from the gem material which means maximum financial value. Years of applied research has resulted in this uniquely precise and versatile machine.

Ultra Tec stands behind the claim of accuracy, and a test and calibration report is provided with each machine.

-- Exclusive Features --

Superior no-guess repeatability is assured.

*

Easy to read ANGLE DIAL provides angle spacing equivalent to those of a 12" diameter protractor, the vernier giving the user direct readout and repeatability of 0.1' increments.

*

The Mast is calibrated over its entire length...readable to 0.01mm (0.0004")...with no mechanism needed.

*

The INDEX GEARS are true involute gears, and the INDEX VERNIER is calibrated in 0.1' increments.

*

A KEYED DOP SYSTEM allows dop removal and replacement, holding both radial and depth positions... and an easy override when that is needed.

EASE-OF-USE is assured:

*

The LOW PROFILE of the machine allows comfortable operation on a standard table or desk top, and knee room if the machine is inserted into a workbench.

*

The SPLASH PAN is long-lasting neoprene, and can be removed easily for cleaning, with no need to disconnect from drain tube.

*

The SCALES are photo-offset, white-on-black for clear non-strain reading.

*

The SPEED CONTROL provides speeds ranging from 100 to 1800 rpm, with a feedback loop that maintains excellent torque even at the slowest speeds ... and provides both CW and CCW operation to handle those stubborn-to-polish gems.

*

The motor is a permanent magnet continous duty D.C. motor.
