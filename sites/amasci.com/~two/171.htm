KERR METAL MELTING EQUIPMENT

Electro-Melt Furnaces

The Kerr Electro-Melts are a line of compact electric furnaces for melting metals. They are designed to be hand held for easy pouring of the metal directly from the furnace. All the Electro-Melt furnaces are supplied with a graphite crucible which provides a reducing atmosphere during melting, making them ideal for use in casting, reclaiming, alloying and refining metals. They have a built-in pyrometer and are equipped with a safety switch which deactivates power to the furnace when the lid is opened. 115 volt. 220 volt models available on special order.

Manual Electro-Melt

The Manual Electro-Melt has stepless input control that regulates the furnace temperature by using a percentage of time on/time off principle. The dial plate is calibrated in numbers representing the percentage of time power is directed to the heating element. This method gives excellent temperature control. Maximum operable temperature is 2000'F.

Automatic Electro-Melts

The Automatic Electro-Melt has the same features of the manual Electro-Melt furnace with the addition of an electronic temperature control. The control dial plate is temperature calibrated in Celsius and Fahrenheit. The electronic control regulates the temperature by providing full power input to the furnace until a selected temperature is attained and will hold the temperature to a +3' C/5' F. The electronics are designed to eliminate overshoot and to protect the element from overheating. Maximum operating temperature is 1120'C/2050'F.
