(3)   25 AMP RECTIFIER While this heavy duty unit will run plating baths up to 5 gallons (rhodium at least 1 gallon) the additional amps are most needed for efficient and quick electro-stripping. Commercial concerns strip their gold, platinum and silver jewelry after pickling and before polishing, which leaves the metal bright so that less polishing is required. The time saved in polishing (especially filigree) pays for the rectifier in a short time. This is a precision built, solid state electronic unit which works on 110V AC, and converts it by means of a powerstate control for 0 to 15 volts DC. Has both DC ammeter and voltmeter for measuring current under load.
