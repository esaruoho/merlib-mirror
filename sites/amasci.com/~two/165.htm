Kerr Centrifico Casting Machines

Kerr Centrifico Casting Machines are made to give long-term, trouble-free performance. The casting arm is an aluminum bronze casting, which is then machined, nickel plated, and polished. The base and cover are made from heavy, precision die castings and then given a baked finish. The main and secondary arm pivot on precision bearings.
