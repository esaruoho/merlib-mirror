Foredom Flexade Flexible Shafts

Foredom Flexade Flexible Shafts are designed for use with drill press,
electric drill or similar power source. They can be operated at speeds up to 14,000 rpm, but for best results with most accessories, motor speed of at least 3,500 rpm is recommended. Series B Flexades have a ball bearing coupling at the motor shaft. To attach to a chuck or collet-equipped power tool, order with a 1/4" coupling and use a length of 1/4" drill rod to connect.
