 DRAWPLATES 

 Wires drawn through the holes in these
 drawplates are reduced and shaped. The holes are progressively smaller
 in proper increments so that wire drawn from preceding hole will go into
 the next easily. Occasional annealing will keep wire soft, and bees wax
 can be used as a lubricant. Tubes can also be formed from flat sheet
 metal. 
 These are tool steel plates for gold, silver, platinum, copper, brass
 and soft steel wire. 
 Sizes are in mm.
