 ANNUAL EVENTS 
 THE BIG BASH - Held every February 
 A party just for the grown-ups featuring a different ethnic theme each year. Food and music appropriate to the
 theme are provided. 
 CHOCOLATE SUNDAY - Third or Fourth Sunday in February 
 Remember your sweetheart at the chocolate "brunch". Bring a cake, cookies, candies or other goodies made
 of chocolate to share after the service. 
 AUCTION - Early in March 
 Our version of March Madness! There are sumptious dinners, picnics, boat rides, the use of vacation cabins, and
 many, many treasures to bid on. Dinner is served and drinks are available. 
 SILVERTON RETREAT - Memorial Day Weekend 
 An unstructured family camoing retreat to cabins inl the woods on the Mountain Loop Highway. There is no program,
 just an opportunity to talk, eat, hike, play games and hang out. 
 RUMMAGE SALE - First Weekend in May 
 Tons of good stuff at amszingly low prices! It's realy a huge multi-family garage sale where church members bring
 the best of their unwanted goodies.> 
 SUMMER PICNIC - Third or Fourth Sunday in August 
 A potluck picnic held on the church grounds with fellowship and games for young and old. 
 SEABECK CHURCH RETREAT - Seabeck Conference Center on Hood Canal, September 21, 22, 23 
 This is an annual event which provides an opportunity for church families to learn, reflect, read, and play in
 a beautifully wooded shoreline setting.. This year the program is built around the theme, "Your Time is Up....What's
 Next?" Imagine it's your last day on earth, what would you do? Who would you do it with? We'll explore what
 really matters, the impact of our lives, and our expectations and beliefs about what happens after death. 
 This year we will be joined by members from the Woodinvillle and South Whidby churchs, giving us a chance to
 get aquainted with those good folks. 
 NORTHWEST ARTISTS HOLIDAY SHOW - The First Weekend in November 
 An invitational show featuring artists and craftsmen from around the Pacific Northwest and specialty food products.
 The "Upstairs Gallery" includes a free buffet hors d'oeurve table.

 AFFINITY GROUPS 
 Most of these groups are formed around common interests. They are different from adult classes and activities
 in that they are generally small, ongoing, and meet throughout the year. The majority remain small to allow each
 member to feel comfortable in sharing thoughts and feelings.

 BIKING 
 Bicycling enthusiasts interested in easy, scenic rides, both urban and rural. 

 BRUNCH BUNCH 
 Meets on the2 nd and 4 th Sundays at 11:45 a. m. in the Stevens Hospital Cafeteria. Share
 thoughts on the morning'a service, exchange news of personal events and discuss what you've been reading. Quiet,
 comfortable surroundings facilitate fellowship. Food generally ordinary, varied, inexpensive, and unimportant. 

 DINNER OUT 
 Meets twice a month on the 2 nd and 4 th Thursdays at local restaurants. This is a great
 way to get to know people! Low key, low cost and always lots of good conversation. Singles and couples, old members
 and new friends welcome. 

 DREAM GROUPS 
 For those interested in dream interpretation, there are currently two groups: one on the 2 nd and
 4 th Sunday evening of the month and and another on Friday evening. Using Jeremy Taylor's approach, in
 which dreams further health and wholeness, group members share and discuss their dreams. 

 FAMILY OUTINGS 
 Family activities planned by the group members. 

 FOLK DANCING 
 Greek, Balkan and Israeli line and circle dancing. Meets in member's homes about once a month for a potluck 

 FRIDAY NIGHT AT THE MOVIES 
 Meets at the church the last Friday of each month to view a variety of favorite movies. Bring snacks to share. 

 HIKING 
 We live in one of the most spectacularly beautiful places in the world!! Onec a month a Saturday day hike of
 5-10 miles into the spectacularly scenic Northwest wilderness. A different person coordinates and leads each hike. 

 RECORDER 
 Learn to play the recorder; play with other group members, share music with others. Beginners and players of all
 levels welcome. Meets every other Saturday, 9:30-11 a.m. 

 SACRED CIRCLE 
 The group started as a 12-week class following Julia Cameron's book, The
 Artists Way. Having finished the class, the group now meets the 1st and 3rd Monday
 of each month. Through projects and discussion of related books, participants work to keep creativity alive by
 letting go of judgments and encouraging each other's unique expression. 

 SYMPOSIUM 
 A small group meeting every Thursday 1a.m. - 1 p.m. in the Friarplace.to report on what's new
 in our lives and discuss what seems important or ridiculous in our church, nation or world. Members share information,
 lend books and CD's, and advise each other on problems big and small. Bring your own brown bag lunch or grab a
 bite afterward on your own. Tunctioning since 1988. Meets every Thrusday 1 

 TUESDAY TOASTMASTERS 
 Sharpen your speaking skills and have fun too. Meets Tuesdays at 7 p.m. 

 TRAVEL GROUP 
 Discussions and activities concerning various aspects of travel. Members share include sharing
 ideas, experriences and recourses for travel information. The group may also plan short trips. 

 WILD WOMEN 
 A social gathering for 40+ single women. Meets the second Saturday of the month In member's
 homes for potlucks. Guests are welcome. 

 WOMEN'S SHARING CIRCLE 
 Meets the 2 nd and 4 th Monday at 7:15 p.m.
