AFFINITY GROUPS

Most of these groups are formed around common interests. They are different from adult classes
and activities in that they are generally small, ongoing, and meet throughout the year. The majority remain small to allow each member to feel comfortable in sharing thoughts and feelings.

   BIKING
Bicycling enthusiasts interested in easy, scenic rides, both urban and rural.

  BRUNCH BUNCH
Meets on the2nd and 4th Sundays, 11:45 a. m. in the Stevens Hospital Cafeteria. Share thoughts on the morning'a service, exchange news of personal events and discuss what you've been reading. Quiet, comfortable surroundings facilitate fellowship. Food generally ordinary, varied, inexpensive, and unimportant.

  DINNER OUT
Meets twice a month on the 2nd and 4th Thursday at local restaurants. This is a great way to get to know people! Low key, low cost and always lots of good conversation. Singles and couples, old members and new friends welcome.

  DREAM GROUPS
For those interested in dream interpretation, there are currently two groups: one on the 2nd and 4th Sunday evening of the month and and another on Friday evening. Using Jeremy Taylor's approach, in which dreams further health and wholeness, group members share and discuss their dreams.		  

  FAMILY OUTINGS
Family activities planned by the group members.

  FOLK DANCING
Greek, Balkan and Israeli line and circle dancing. Meets in member's homes about once a month for a potluck and dancing.

  FRIDAY NIGHT AT THE MOVIES
Meets at the church the last Friday of each month to view a variety of favorite movies. Bring snacks to share.

  HIKING
We live in one of the most spectacularly beautiful places in the world!! Onec a month a Saturday day hike of 5-10 miles into the spectacularly scenic Northwest wilderness. A different person coordinates and leads each hike.

  RECORDER
Learn to play the recorder; play with other group members, share music with others. Beginners and players of all levels welcome. Meets every other Saturday, 9:30-11 a.m. 

  SACRED CIRCLE
The group started as a 12-week class following Julia Cameron's book, The Artists Way. Having finished the class, the group now meets the 1st and 3rd Monday of each month. Through projects and discussion of related books, participants work to keep creativity alive by letting go of judgments and encouraging each other's unique expression.

  SYMPOSIUM
A small group meeting every Thursday 1a.m. - 1 p.m. in the Friarplace.to report on what's new in our lives and discuss what seems important or ridiculous in our church, nation or world. Members share information, lend books and CD's, and advise each other on problems big and small. Bring your own brown bag lunch or grab a bite afterward on your own. Tunctioning since 1988. Meets every Thrusday 1

TUESDAY TOASTMASTERS
Sharpen your speaking skills and have fun besides. Meets Tuesdays at 7 p.m.

  TRAVEL GROUP
Discussions and activities concerning various aspects of travel. Members share include sharing ideas, experriences and recourses for travel information. The group may also plan short trips.

  WILD WOMEN
A social gathering for 40+ single women. Meets the second Saturday of the month In member's homes for potlucks. Guests are welcome.

  WOMEN'S SHARING CIRCLE
Meets the 2nd and 4th Monday at 7:15 p.m.
