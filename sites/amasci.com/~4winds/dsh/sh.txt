 We carry Shanti's full line of products. 

Shanti Kite Company was founded in 1974 by Daniel Prentice. He began cutting

 wooden parts in his basement, dyeing them in his bathtub and assembling them

 on his kitchen table. The result was the first "factory" in America to

 produce high quality kite spools and winders.

For 25 years Shanti has led the kiteworld in the development of quality lines,

 spools and winders. Shanti innovations include: Spinner spools, Windbreaker

 winders, Sky Claw, Grip Light, Crazy-8, Skybond, Power, Zip and Speed line.

Shanti was the first company in the world to braid Spectra fibers for kite

 flying. Shanti's Spectra line, trademarked Speed line, as been used by six

 of the last eight World Cup champions and can be found in kite stores

 throughout the world.

These are the products that will make your kite perform at its best, and bring

 it home safe and sound. Once you've decided what type of line you want, then

 pick a spool and we'll put the two together. With Shanti products, you're

 only limited by your own imagination.
