 Polycarbonate Icarex - $9. 75 per yard 
 The next generation of high performance Icarex ripstop polyester. The new
 Icarex is the first kite fabric in the world to have a polycarbonate
 resin finish and it will outperform and outlast anything on the market.

 Polycarbonate Icarex (PC-31) weights 31 grams/square meter. 
 PC-31 has a 2 kg. tear strength (42% higher than standard Icarex) 
 PC-31 has 50% greater bursting strength than standard Icarex. 
 PC-31 has almost no porosity (.02) even after a 54-day flutter test. 
 PC-31 has a unique "double-ripstop" construction so there's no mistaking it for any other cloth. 
 PC-31 is available in 23 colors.
