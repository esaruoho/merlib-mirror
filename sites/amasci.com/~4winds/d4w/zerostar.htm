Fly inside or out! The ZeroStar doesn't need wind, just a loving touch. Tricks or precision are at your command. We make this kite in two, high-performance variants -- The "standard" Ultralight, with its phenomenal wind range of 1 to 18 mph, and the Indoor version which gives you the freedom to fly anytime, regardless of the weather!

This kite comes in a very striking, 13-panel pattern. We manufacture the ZeroStar exclusively for our shop in order to give our customers something extra special. Each kite is handcrafted to exacting performance standards by Erv and Joey. At any time, this kite is available in a wide range of colors. Or choose your own custom color scheme from any of the many Icarex colors available! One week lead time is usually needed for custom production.
