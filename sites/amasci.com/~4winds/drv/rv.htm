Speed Series

Rev 1.5 SLE
The Rev 1.5 continues to be the most dominant quad-line kite on the market today. For 1998 the Rev 1.5 will be released as the Rev 1.5 SLE. The advances in the kite will be a new polyester sail and a Super Leading Edge. The new leading edge is the result of continuous research in the area of graphite technology. A larger diameter tube with stronger overall bending and hoop strength, give a more nimble and precise performance. Traditional quad-line flight characteristics are enhanced with the new technology.

Phase 2
For years fliers have been searching for the ultimate all-purpose sport kite that excels in all aspects of flying including ballet, precision, team, trick and recreational flying. While other kites have attempted this, the usual result has been a kite equally mediocre in those areas. The Phase 2, designed by master kite flier and instructor Dodd Gross gives the kite flier the one dual line kite to have in their bag. The sail is half-ounce polyester and the frame is Revolution's aerospace quality graphite. The frame is stiff enough for the flier to perform the latest tricks and forgiving enough for aggressive ground maneuvers. The Phase 2 features top quality construction throughout the kite.
