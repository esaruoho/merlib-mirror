Icarex 

Polycarbonate Icarex - $9.75 Per Yard 40" Wide

The next generation of high performance Icarex ripstop polyexter. The new Icarex is the first kite fabric in the world too have a polycarbonate resin finish and it will outperform and outlast anything on the market.

  • Polycarbonate Icarex (PC - 31) weights 31 grams/square meter.

  • PC - 31 has a 2 kg. tear strength (42% higher than standard Icarex)

  • PC - 31 has 50% greater bursting strength than standard Icarex.

  • PC - 31 has almost no porosity (.02) even after a 54-day flutter test.

  • PC - 31 has a unique "double-ripstop" constuction so there's no mistaking it for any other cloth.

  • PC - 31 is available in 23 colors (See Below)
