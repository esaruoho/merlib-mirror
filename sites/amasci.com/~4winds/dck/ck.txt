Flexifoil buggies have been designed exclusively for Flexifoil by Peter
 Lynn, the worlds most experienced buggy designer. Both models are built in
 high-grade stainless steel for durability and strength; both have sealed ball
 bearings and nylong wheels, to withstand virtually all conditions. The buggies
 are very easy to dismantle for transportation and storage. A full range of
 options, including a wide selection of wheels, is available.

 The Original Flexifoil Buggy 
This buggy is one of the world's best selling models and has been competition
 tested under the most extreme conditions. Its very strong construction and
 the bucket type seat make this a good all-around buggy for the demanding buggier.
 The seat has now been widened slightly to allow more body movement, giving extra
 control.

 The Flexifoil Sport Buggy 
This brand new design is ideally suited for the recreational buggier. Made
 to the same exacting standards and using the same quality materials as the
 Original model, it features a full ball bearing head set and a fully adjustable
 seat, to accommodate both freestyle and speed buggying in comfort.
