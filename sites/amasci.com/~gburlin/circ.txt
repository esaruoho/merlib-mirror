 Circumcision Issues 

Circumcision is the alteration or mutilation of genitalia, of
females as well as males. It is a worldwide phenomenon and varies
greatly. An excellent reference for female circumcision is Prisoners
of Ritual : An Odyssey into Female Genital Circumcision
in Africa , Hanny Lightfoot-Klein ,
New York, Haworth Press, 1989. And here are more pages on female circumcision .
