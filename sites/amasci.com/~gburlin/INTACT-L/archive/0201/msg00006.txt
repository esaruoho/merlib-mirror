My letter to SF Chronicle about the bris milah article...
-------------------------------------

How contradictory that such a beautiful people with brilliant minds and good
hearts continue to cut on infant genitalia. If the child was female, hopefully,
the audience would defend her from the knife.

I personally know Jewish parents who have not circumcised their boys; some who
did, and bitterly regret it; and adult Jewish men who resent being cut. They
feel, essentially, as if they were raped. They had no vote in an important
decision about their own bodies. Ironically, they consider circumcision to be
anti-semitic.

I wonder what that little baby boy would have said, if only he could speak? I
know that I hold in my heart a very angry, "Leave me alone!" And now I
understand why. Clitoridectomies were covered by Blue Cross Blue Shield until
1959 in the United States and I was one of the little girls who did not get a
vote about her own genital integrity.

Some day, people will look on all circumcision in the same way that they look at
female genital mutilation today. They will be shocked, offended, outraged.
Some already are. Circumcision hurts infants, hurts the man as an adult,
damages his sexual relationships, and makes for angry men such as those who are
at war in the Middle East. All circumcise: the U.S., Israel, Muslim. Makes
you wonder, doesn't it?
