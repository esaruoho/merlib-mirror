                TO CIRCUMCISE OR NOT TO CIRCUMCISE...
                     TELL US ABOUT YOUR DECISION

        Unless culture or religion dictates your decision, you and your
partner will most likely discuss whether or not to have your newborn baby boy
circumcised.
        Circumcision is an elective surgical process in which the foreskin
of the penis is removed.  It is performed shortly after birth, rarely with
anesthesia, and usually takes about 15 minutes.  The pros:  The penis is
easier to keep clean, there is less risk of urinary-tract infections and
penile cancer and the son will "look like Dad" if the father was
circumcised.  The cons:  Parents worry that the procedure is painful, is an
unnecessary medical expense that may not be covered by insurance, and there
is a chance, though slight, of complications.
        If you are expecting a child, or if you have had a son within the
last two years, please tell us what factors relate to your decision.
