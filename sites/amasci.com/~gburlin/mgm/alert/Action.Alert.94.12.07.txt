ACTION ALERT
RUSH LIMBAUGH, THE TELEVISION PROGRAM, RECORDED 2 DECEMBER 1994  

Rush was talking about an organization in Hiroshima which is outraged because
the U.S. Postal Service is or was planning to print a stamp with a picture of
a mushroom cloud on it.  He said [I paraphrase] that it reminded him of a
group out in San Francisco called NOCIRC, which is trying to "stamp out
circumcision."

He joked about how the guy who is president of NOCIRC might present himself
when meeting people at a party, and of course, the fact (an audience member
filled in the punch line) that NOCIRC must be paid in tips.

Along with inaccuracies, he made it like a big joke.  The most ironic thing
is he has been very good about exposing the hypocrisy of radical feminism and
promoting men's rights in general.  But when it comes to circumcision, he
just doesn't get it.

Writing him might not do any good, but then it couldn't hurt.
