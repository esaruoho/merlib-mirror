Larry McMurtry basic bibliography, with images 

Larry McMurtry page 

 Basic bibliography of main publications:
 1961 - Horseman, Pass By. Harper. NY. app. 5000 cc. Filmed as "Hud",
starring Paul Newman.
 1963 - Leaving Cheyenne. Harper & Row. Originally priced at $4.50,
it was changed to $4.95 on the lower front dust flap.
 1966 - The Last Picture Show. Dial Press. The 1971 Peter Bogdanovich
movie starred Cybill Shepherd, Jeff Bridges, Timothy Bottoms and won Academy
Awards for Supporting Actors Cloris Leachman and Ben Johnson.
 1968 - In A Narrow Grave. Encino Press. Austin, Tx. First printing
is very rare with "skycrappers" instead of "skyscrappers" on page 105.
Second printing has a "B" on the copyright page, tan cloth boards, tan
parchment-like end papers, the 3rd printing has a "C" on the cp, tan paper
boards and flat tan end papers. There is a 1968 signed Limited Edition
of 250 copies with suede spine, in a slipcase.
 1970 - Moving On. Simon & Schuster.
 1972 - All My Friends Are Going To be Strangers. Simon & Schuster.
This was the first book of his I read and it hooked me on both McMurtry
and book collecting.
 1974 - It's Always We Rambled, (essays). Hallman Publ. Signed, 300
numbered copies. No dustjacket, tan paper boards, red lettering.
 1975 - Terms Of Endeament. Simon & Schuster. app. 16,500 copies.
 1978 - Somebody's Darling. Simon & Schuster
 1982 - Cadillac Jack. S&S. Also 250 signed copies in slipcase
 1983 - Desert Rose. S&S. Also 250 signed copies in slipcase
 1985 - Lonesome Dove. S&S. 1986 Pulitzer Prize winner
 1987 - Texasville. S&S
 1987 - Film Flam. S&S.
 1988 - Anything For Billy. S&S
 1989 - Some Can Whistle. S&S
 1990 - Buffalo Girls. S&S
 1992 - The Evening Star. S&S
