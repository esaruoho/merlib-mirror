 JBM - Equations

 This page address equations commonly used in small arms
 ballistics. I'll add to the list as required/requested.

 Where applicable, variables and references have been listed 
 at the end of each page.

 CD and KD 

 Derivations of the equations used in the point mass 
 trajectory calculations. It includes equations for
 CD, BC and KD.

 Crosswind 

 Derivations of the classic crosswind deflection formula.

 Energy 

 A discussion of potential and kinetic energy with a
 worked example.

 Integration 

 Changing variables of integration to make the trajectory
 equation more convenient to integrate.

 Sectional Density 

 Equation for calculating sectional density

 Runge Kutta Integration 

 Equations for numerical integration using Runge Kutta methods.
