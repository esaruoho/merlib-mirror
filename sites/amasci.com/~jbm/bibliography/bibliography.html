JBM - Bibliography

This bibliography is divided into three sections, interior, exterior and miscellaneous. It's not meant to be the last word on ballistics. It is a list of the references I and others have used in creating ballistics programs. If you have any references that may be of use to others, please email me.

Exterior
Exterior ballistics references. Ballistics dealing with bullet flight after leaving the effects of the propellant gases.

Interior
Interior ballistics references. Ballistics dealing with the propellant effects.

Miscellaneous
Other references, I've used that aren't necessarily related to ballistics. Or, they just didn't seem to fit into "just interior" or "just exterior".
