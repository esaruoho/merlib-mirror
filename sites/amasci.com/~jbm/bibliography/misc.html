JBM - Bibliography - Miscellaneous

Map Projections : A Reference Manual ,
Lev M. Bugayevskiy and John P. Snyder, Taylor and Francis, 1995. ISBN 0748403035. I found this book in the library and used it for coordinate transformations (topocentric, geocentric and geographic) in MPM.

Rifle Accuracy Facts ,
Harold R. Vaughn, published by Precision Shooting, Inc., 1998. "The definitive study, made over a number of years, by a leading research scientist, on the subject of why some rifles shoot very well... some shoot fairly well... and others shoot poorly." This book has a little bit of everything.

U.S. Standard Atmosphere ,
1962, National Aeronautics and Space Administration. -- Includes derivation of the ICAO standard atmosphere and tables with density values as a function of altitude.
