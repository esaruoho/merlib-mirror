You know I really get tired when folks try to make this sound like some
affirmative action thing or some lib/conservative type thing. The Indians
get to net because the government (us) got them to give up most of the
other resources and land, in return for their right to keep on fishing.
Thats what the treaty did.

That means that the govt could grant clear titles to land it disbursed.
Without the treaty none of us has a clear title to our house or any other
land. If you don't think thats real, check into the Maine situation and some
of whats going on in Canada.

If we want the treaty provisions to be changed, the govt has to renegotiate,
and probably PAY to compensate for what is taken away. But its unlikely that
the pols or the govt will get serious until more fisherman stop blowing
smoke on this issue and face up to the reality that the Indians hold the
upper legal hand, and by oversight we forgot to screw them out of all the
fish.

Of course we could replay all the Bull or the last 20 years in Washington
State with our same "sportsman leaders" most of whom are just second rate
rejects for the plum lobbying accounts, who like the pols, just tell us what
we want to hear.

Dave
