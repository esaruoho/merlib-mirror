Hello,

Re: lurkers.

I've been in Antarctica (Nov-Jan) and didn't fish. Then I was in NZ for
5 weeks and fished every day. I just got back last Wednesday.

Since I've been back I've fished 3 days (last thurs-saturday). 
1 on the Sky (IRS, Island Drift, Buck Island) - no fish or hookups.
1 on the Sky/Sauk - landed 1 nice native hen on a small bunny leech in very 
low water. 1 day on the Skagit/Sauk - no fish but my fishing buddy hooked
a nice steelhead for a minute and got a couple jumps out of it before it 
became unpinned.

I'll try to contribute when I fish this spring for steelhead, and I'll
send a note as soon as I get the pictures developed of that first 
steelhead of the season - as well as when I get some New Zealand stuff
on my web page.

cheers,
