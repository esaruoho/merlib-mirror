I've seen high angler success ratios lately on the Snoqualmie. I say that
because there's not alot of guys out, but Sunday afternoon I tallied 8 fish
for 10 fishermen. I returned this afternoon to my scene of success on
Sunday but no success this time. There were two landed this morning at the
Fall City Bridge drift. The area below town has been best, but fish will
move into the golf course area and above. I had Dan Lamaisch make me up
another line, or modify my l3ft sinking tip. He adds another larger
diameter portion of floating line behind the sink tip and it casts super.
He did that to my Teeny 300 grain tip this week end and I hooked up with it
the first time out. Mike Kinney, who worked at the Swallows Nest, (I heard
it here first that they were closing up) will be working with Dan at
Creekside Anglers in Issaquah. These two are tops in Steelheading. That
shop is putting together a top notch bunch of guys.
I had one of my scouts out lookin for risers on Lake Alice, but nothing this
morning. This nice weather has got to bring them up. Jere Crosby
