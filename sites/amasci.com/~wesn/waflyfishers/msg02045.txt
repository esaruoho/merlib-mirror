>Dave,

>I did about 18 months of research on pontoon boats before buying mine. I
>have a Outcast PAC 9000. I have used it on the Cowlitz, the Green River
>in Utah, the Snake & South Fork of the Snake in Wyoming & Idaho. Also
>many lakes and the "salt". It has performed beyond my expectations in
>all aspects. It is built with a very strong frame and the pontoons take
>every kind of abuse. I have loaded it with tent, sleeping bag, stove,
>clothes, food, and fishing gear to last a week with no problem. This one
>also has 2 pontoons per side. You may want to watch for at least 2 air
>chambers per side as I have read some articles where regulations may be
>put into effect requiring this on some rivers in the future. Don't ask
>for the articles because it was awhile back and I do not recall where I
>saw them (but I do remember reading it and taking heed).
>I am sure you will get a number of responses to your question but before
