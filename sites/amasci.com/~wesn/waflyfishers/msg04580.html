> > A good beach in the PT area is a place called Government Cut. It's the
> > channel between the mainland and Indian Island. Follow the signs to Fort
> > Flagler State Park and you can either park just across the bridge on the
> > island side or continue down a little farther to a day use only park and

> > me off the list if you would like to get together. I would contact the Port
> > Townsend Angler, their phone number is 360-379-3763 and their email is
> > ptangler@olypen.com.
> >
> > If you tie your own flies, a good pattern is called the Jim Dandy, the
> > recipe is:
> > Hook: #8 2-3x long
> > Cone head
> > Tail: cream/peachy/pale yellow marabou with a few strands of pearl
> > flashabou NOTE: Any one of theese colors will work.
> > Body: leaded(optional), flat silver tinsel with orange floss over tinsel
> > Rib: copper wire
> > NOTE: Between body and cone head there is a dubbing ball. The cone head
> > fits over the dubbing ball. The dubbing ball is: Sunset Orange SLF.
> >
> > There is a picture of this fly at PT Angler's web site the address is:
> > http://www.olypen.com/ptangler/shopping/shopcartpage.htm  scroll down to the
> > bottom of the page. This is a local pattern that was developed by one of the
> > owners of the shop.
> >
> > The Angel Hair Clouser is another good pattern
> > Hook: #6
> > Under wing: pearl green Angel Hair
> > Over wing: Peacock Angle Hair
> > Eyes: Chrome Dumbell
> >
> > PT Anlger sells both of these patterns, so if you don't tie you can still
