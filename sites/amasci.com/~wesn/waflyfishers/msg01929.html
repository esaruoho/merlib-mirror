Tim Coleman and I fished Lenice yesterday with mixed results.  Weather highly variable.  Saw warm sun, rain, hail, wind, calm, and snow.  Really.  Great light.  Water temp about 46. 
 
The normally dependable chironomids this time of year yielded only 1 fish to me and 3 takes.  Some fishing (illegally) glo-bugs caught a few.  Tim did much better than I, showing flexibility and shifting to an olive woolly-bugger.  He caught 10. Overall a slower day than hoped but a good day overall turning anticipation into memories.
