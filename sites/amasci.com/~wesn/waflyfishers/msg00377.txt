> I also understand that there are some pinks that come back to spawn on the
> even years. Why can't the biologists propagate the even year pinks so that
> we can have a pink fishery every year instead of only on the odd years?

The Snohomish system has a small even-year humpy run. I don't know that 
any attempts have been made to increase their numbers or to spread them
over to nearby drainages. Setting up a hatchery would invariably be a 
dissaster. Anyway, there isn't enough money to raise silver
salmon or steelhead in the already existing hatcheries. Given that, 
it seems like it would be a poor decision to invest money in the even-year 
humpies. As a flyfisher, I'd rather that they work on silvers or 
steelhead.
