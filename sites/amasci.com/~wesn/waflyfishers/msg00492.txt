Hello All,

I am leaving for a field season in Antarctica on 1 Nov. I will be down there
until the end of January and will then spend all of February and some of March
flyfishing on the South Island of New Zealand (my 4th year in a row!).

If anyone is interested, you can see a few pictures from last year's NZ
flyfishing on one of my web pages:
 http://weber.u.washington.edu/~tgades/Fishing/NZ/NZ_97.html 

A few pictures from past field work in Antarctica and elsewhere are on: 
 http://weber.u.washington.edu/~tgades/Glacier/glacier_page.html 

Needless to say, I won't be submitting any flyfishing reports for those
4 months (Nov-March). 

I'll stay on the list while I'm gone so I can see what happened during my 
absence.

My apologies for a mostly non-fishing related letter.

Cheers and good luck this winter. See you in time for the big 
Natives on the Sauk, Sky, and Skagit!
