The following messages are archived from the Washington Fly Fishing List. This
is an email list that brings together people with a shared interest in fly
fishing in the Washington State area. To subscribe to the list, send an email
message with the word "subscribe" (no quotes) in the Subject line to:

 waflyfishers-request@eskimo.com 

You should receive a confirmation message welcoming you to the list within 
a short time.
