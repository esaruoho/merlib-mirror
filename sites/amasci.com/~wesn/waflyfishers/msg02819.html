Hi All,

I have been getting some Chums on weighted pink flies on the river.
Watch for the river to come up and then drop back down.  
Dropping water that is clearing is the ticket.  Look for current seams
between fast and calm water and don't wade out far. They 
like to be in quite a way in from the seam in the more slack water.
Funny thing is that all but one have been bucks and all but 1 has had
tons of sea lice!  Nice active fish.

If you are interested in checking river levels, I have links on my
home page listed below.

I scanned in one of the flies I tied if you are interested link
directly with;
