What is it?

At the Detroit Auto Show last January, Ford introduced a concept vehicle
called the Sportsman designed to "fulfill fly anglers' dreams of finding
calm and unspoiled waters of the perfect wilderness fishing location"

What's special about it?

- Based on the 2002 Explorer it's powered by a 240-horsepower, 4.6-liter V8.
- Outfitted with Scott fly-fishing rods.
- The seatbacks also have detachable carry packs that deploy out to function
as workbenches for fly tying. These benches are removable to allow the
angler to tie flys on site.
-A 30-gallon live fish tank with a clean-air filter in the back compartment.
It has rollers for easy loading and unloading.
- Finished in a low-gloss, satin metallic green with satin-finished front
and rear chrome skid plates. A custom integrated hood and grille.(looks
somewhat like an old Land Rover. )
 - The dash has a GPS screen controlled by an on-board mouse
 -The running boards, which deploy when the doors are opened, also serve as
additional storage for fishing rods and other gear.
-The detachable roof rack extends forward and folds down for easy loading
and unloading access.

It's a little over the top for me.  I'm happy with my 11 year old Toyota
4Runner!
