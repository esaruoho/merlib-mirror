Very sad. I was at Hazel Hole the Fri after Thanksgiving and watched 4 guys with spinning gear keep several fish. I wasn't absolutely certain of the regs, so I pulled them out and started reading. As I did this, another fellow walked over and let them know that they were screwing up. He talked with me afterward and said that they had no idea what river they where on or what the rules were. While we watched them fish, I called the Poaching Hotline on my cell phone and got an answering machine (3 day weekend). They then left before I could call anyone else. I didn't feel right calling 911 and didn't know who else to call. Fish and Game returned my call on Monday. Coincidentally I ran into the local Enforcement Officer (nice fellow to talk to by the way) while hanging out at the Flysmith in Marysville a few days later. I told him this story and he said that I should have called 911 and said that this is not an emergency. Next time I will. I'm sure that I will see more boneheads out there.
 
Ray
