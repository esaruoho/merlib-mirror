Buckeye's Led Zeppelin page

is your one-stop site for Zeppelin on the internet. Any Led Zeppelin page that means anything is on this page, including Led Zeppelin licence plates, guitar tab, lyrics, etc. One can literally spend hours on this page that Bruce "the buckeye" Deerhake has put a lot of effort into. Also be sure to check out Hugh Jones WWW version of his fanzine Promimity. Proximity is one of the best Led Zeppelin fanzines floating around in North America. If the writing isn't enough, the layout is incredibly professional looking and this quarterly fanzine always is full of interesting reading material. This page is also maintained by Bruce Deerhake.

Merit Adventures

is the largest source of Led Zeppelin memorabelia in the world. Owned and operated by Rick Barrett, you can purchase anything from Gold Records, T-Shirts, and posters to legit imported CD's, and other hard to find items related to the world of Led Zeppelin.
