Here are some various links, not related to any of The Zoso Network's other pages, that we thought you may be interested in checking out

WKIT 100.3 FM- One of the few locally owned commercial radio stations left in the country. Owned by author Stephen King, this classic rocker operates out of his hometown of Bangor, Maine. Incidentally, the webmaster of zoso.net, Jeremy Mixer, also happens to be one of the DJ's. This radio station is also one of the few that continues to stream their broadcast online! This is one of the few radio stations in the country that represents the way commercial radio used to be!

Eskimo North - The Zoso Network would not exist if it weren't for the services of Seattle's finest Internet Service Provider. Eskimo North also hosts the NewNet IRC Network.

Upon us all a little Rainn must fall- Rainn was one of the original regulars of #ledzep, the now defunct Led Zeppelin IRC channel. She designed the original logo for FBO, as well as many of the beautiful graphics on Buckeye's Led Zeppelin page

Jeremy Mixer's Web Presence- This is the personal page of webmaster Jeremy Mixer, and has not been created yet.

Big O Expresso- This is Northern Seattle's Place for the best expresso

Hot Tuna Offical Page- This is the official page to, in my opinion, one of the best and most underated bands to exist today. Their first album was released in 1969, while guitarist Jorma Kaukonen and bassist Jack Casady were still flying high with Jefferson Airplane, however, they eventually split off to make Hot Tuna a full time gig. Although there have been a couple breakups, the band is still going strong today. Samples of several of their albums are available in Real Audio format at Relix Records who have released much of the bands recent music as well as some of their stuff from the 70's. And for all of you msuician types out there, Jorma and his wife Vanessa have put a lot of work into a summer guitar camp, the Fur Peace Ranch, in which anyone wishing to learn guitar, as well as harmonica, vocals, sound engineering, or various other music-related areas of interest may be interested in attending! Also, be sure to check out Jeff Zahnen's unofficial Jefferson Airplane homepage where you can get the latest news on Hot Tuna, Jefferson Starship, and other related musical projects, including info about 2400Fulton, the Jefferson Airplane Discussion List.

Jean Lorrah's Web Page- Jean Lorrah is a regular contributor to FBO, the Led Zeppelin mailing list, as well as a published Science Fiction author. Her page includes her own "Great Book Search" as well as several Star Trek Links....but most importantly to us at zoso.net is the fact that her homepage was the first one to incorperate lemon squeezing into the Zeppelin-related links! It also has the very interesting and motivational "Top 100 Zeppelin Moments" which is definately worth checking out!
