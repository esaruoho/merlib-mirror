Robin Trower Mailing List Info:

  • To subscribe to the Robin-Trower maiing list and to receive each posting individually, send email to:

listserv@zoso.net

  • Leave the subject line of the message blank, and in the message body type:

subscribe Robin-Trower your name

  • To subscribe to the digest,and get a daily compilation of all posts, please send email to:

majordomo@zoso.net

  • Leave the subject line blank, and in the body of the email type:

subscribe trower-digest your name

  • To post to the list: send email to:

Robin-Trower@zoso.net
