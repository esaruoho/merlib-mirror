Led Zeppelin Gallery

This is a gallery of Led Zeppelin related items. These are all contributions from various people around the world. If you would like to make a submission, please email Webmaster Jeremy Mixer at mixer@zoso.net.

Above Right: A ticket stub from the famous Led Zeppelin show known as Earls Court, courtesy of Steve Z

Left: A ticket stub from "Bonzo's Birthday Party" on May 31, 1973. ht is a ticket stub from the famous Earl's Court show on May 17, 1975. This show has been heavily bootlegged and is a favorite among Led Zeppelin collectors. Courtesy of Steve Z.


Right: One cannot visit Manhattan without visiting this Landmark of the world of Zep, located on East 8th Street in Lower Manhattan. If you compare the building to the album cover, you will notice that the only difference is that the third floor was removed from the album cover. Even the fire hydrant faces the same direction! Located in the lower right hand corner of the building is the "Physical Grafitti" used clothing store. As well as being the building used for PG, this building has also been spotted in Rolling Stones videos, as well as a couple of movies. Zoso Net webmaster Jeremy Mixer is standing near the door on the lower left of the building. Apologies for the "odd distortion" of the building, this was scanned from two photos taken at seperate angles. Photographed by Rainn, this photo courtesy of Jeremy Mixer.
