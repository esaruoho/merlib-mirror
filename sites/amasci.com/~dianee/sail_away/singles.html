Singles Sailing

Finding yourself with a group of people who are compatible is important enough if youre travelling with a partner - its even more so if youre travelling by yourself !

One of our biggest advantages in being a small outfit is that were usually able to make enough personal contact with each of our clients to take a good shot at matching them with a crew theyll find compatible. Were not infallible at this, but our success rate IS pretty darn high ! (See reviews-from-past-crews for more comments on this one).

Our "singles" trips tend to attract the active, get-out-there-and-do-it types. The group dynamics are usually very strong - when travelling solo, people tend to make more effort to get to know one another. With the team-spirit that boat life creates, the laid-back, hedonistic atmosphere of the islands, people get to know each other real fast - many folks go home with close friendships that have lasted years since.

If youre travelling with a partner, but dont necessarily want to be with all couples (cos, well, they can be awfully couple-y'!) - one of our "mostly singles" trips might just fit you as well; the emphasis being on the get-to-know-new-people, fun group spirit. (We've even had a honeymoon couple join one of our singles groups - and had a blast !!) Just let us know what your preferences are, and well do our matchmaking best to put you with the "right" group.

The main aim is to have a

great time with a bunch of fun people!
