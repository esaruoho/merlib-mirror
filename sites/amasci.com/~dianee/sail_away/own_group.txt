 Bring Your Own Group 

Have your own group of friends you'd love to sail with?

 Great&#151;bring 'em all along!! 

And because&#151;obviously&#151;that means a lot less admin work for us, we'll give you a 
great discount! Eg, get a group of 8 people together for Angelina (our 49' yacht), 
and it'll cost you just $700 per person; all our usual inclusions too! Or if you choose to
go for the more luxury option of AnnaMaria , it will still come out to just $862 each.

These prices apply if you like our "Adventure Sailing" concept, and you're happy to deal with
putting breakfasts & lunches together yourselves, etc. We'll still provide all the food n'
drink, of course ! However, if you'd prefer not to have to chop a single tomato,
nor wash a dish - there is also our "Don't-Lift-a-Finger option", where we put another
SeaScape crewmember on board to take care of these details. 

Our trips are usually 6 or 13 days, starting on Sunday am and finishing on Saturday am&#151; 
occasionally, for early
bookings (or those at the beginning or end of season), we can rearrange our schedule to do 10 day trips for full groups. This is not 
always available, however, so pls check in with us.

If you have a smaller group of people, and a 50-55' yacht is just too big for you 
(well, the size would be nice, but the dollars aren't!)&#151;we do also have some 
other great yachts * in the 32'-44' range, on which we can still give you the 
"SeaScape concept"&#151;ie, all-included, no-worries-along-the-way package! (And a 
SeaScape skipper too. . .that's important).

So - whatever your group's dreams and schemes are, do drop us a line giving as many details as
 possible - being a small, personal outfit, flexibility is one of our biggest 
advantages!

*These yachts are owned by our Greek bareboat partners in the Dodecanese, and are ones that we know are as immaculately maintained as our own.
