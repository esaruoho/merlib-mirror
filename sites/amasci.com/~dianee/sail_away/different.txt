Adventure Sailing with SeaScape - sail on our yachts in Greece and Turkey.

OK, so we are a bit of an oddity in the traditional yacht chartering world! 
Our SeaScape "formula" is quite different to most other charter companies. So, here's some help for those of you encountering the Mediterranean sailing scene for the first time, so you can compare "apples with apples" as you're shopping and surfin' around...

The usual skippered charter options in the Med are either "bareboat-with-skipper" or "fully crewed charter". Bareboat means just that&#151;you get the boat, pay extra for everything else. Fully crewed is more the traditional upmarket yacht charter, where there is a strict divide between crew and guests, where you expect to be waited on hand and foot (and pay for the privilege).

Of course, with both of the above options, you have to charter the entire yacht. There are very few other opportunities to sail in Greece or Turkey if you're travelling solo / as a couple. 

There are also the Turkish gulets&#151;some of them beautiful boats, and very reasonable prices. Great for motoring around&#151;don't expect any sailing though. The sails are for decoration only! 

So where DO we fit in? Well, none of the usual niches&#151;our Adventure Sailing concept falls somewhere in between bareboat-and-skipper and the traditional fully crewed yachts. 

For instance, we don't go for the notion of charging people "extra" for this and that en route... once you get on our boats, that's it&#151; it's all paid for! On the other hand, the uptight, white-shirted crew scene isn't us. . .our concept of sailing is easy-going adventure and fun!
