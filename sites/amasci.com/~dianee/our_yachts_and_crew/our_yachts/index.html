    • Our SeaScape sailing yachts are all in the 50' range, with matching navy-blue hulls and trendy teal n' pearl striping!

    An important point to make here is that "Yes, they are our very OWN SeaScape yachts!"; owned, maintained and skippered by us directly - no absentee owners, and no passing the buck ! Not only do we lavish a great deal of TLC on them, but they have lots more cosy touches and fun toys than the average charter boat!

    All the yachts were built in Athens, and designed specifically for Mediterranean conditions—ie, lots of light and air. They're beamy, spacious boats, with plenty of deck & cabin space; built for comfort rather than speed (hey, who really cares about that extra half a knot in the Greek isles??)

    They can each sleep up to eight guests in four separate cabins. Some cabins are doubles, some convert to bunkbeds for singles. They have four heads (bathrooms, for the non-nautically inclined!), so with the extra shower on the aft swim platform, that makes five showers in all. . .quite the luxury!! Crew cabins are separate.

    They all take part in our Adventure Sailing program, and they are also available for skippered charter, if you have your own group of friends you'd like to sail with.
