Whitsundays' sailing season

 


 

 

Our catamaran is based at the marina on Hamilton Island - the only island in the region that is "developed". It has regular daily connections with Sydney, Brisbane and Cairns, and the marina is less than 5mins drive from the airport.

Strict itineraries are never part of our SeaScape sailing philosophy! - we go with the flow of group consensus and whatever the wind gods dictate at the time! However, there's several "must-see" spots - for example, the ancient aboriginal rock paintings in Nara Inlet... snorkelling the magnificent "Woodpile" on Hook Island... dinghying among the mangroves in the upper reaches of Gulnara Inlet.

Then one can't miss a stroll along the fantastic wilderness of Whitehaven Beach (one of the most superb beaches on this planet!), swimming with the green turtles off Chalkies Beach, beach coaming the sand spit at Langford Reef, watching the flying Manta Rays off Luncheon Bay, hand feeding the Wrasse at Border Island...and finally, gazing at the red disk of the setting sun as it slowly sinks beyond the distant Conway Ranges from lying at anchor in Cid Harbour!

Enough excitment for one week ?? :)
