 The bottom line&mdash;what it will cost. 

 Our Australian trips are $995 USD for 7 days sailing 

 Our philosophy is very simple; our costs are as inclusive as we can sensibly make them. We figure most people would prefer to know ahead of time what the total vacation cost is; not have to worry about little additional surprises along the way!

On our Adventure Sailing trips, once you get on the boat, everything on board is covered . We provide provisions for breakfasts, lunches, drinks (yes, that includes open bar - local liquors, beer, wine, cocktails, etc). And in Australia, because some of our anchorages are really quite remote and unspoiled - we frequently have our own private dinner party / barbie !

So our usual SeaScape philosophy applies - no nasty $ surprises en route ! Everything on the boat is included - yes, that's your Fosters too, mate !!
