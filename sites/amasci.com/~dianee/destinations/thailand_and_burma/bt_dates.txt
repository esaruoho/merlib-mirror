 Winter 2005 - Thailand & Burma

We will be running two Thailand-and-Burma trips this winter. The first will start in Phuket (Thailand), sailing 
southwards first to explore the smaller islands... then heading north - an overnight sail to the Mergui National
Park archipelego of Burma.

The second trip will explore much the same islands - except in reverse! We will spend the first half of the
trip sailing & diving in the pristine waters of Burma, then an overnight passage will bring us to 
Phuket, and the other lesser-known islands of southern Thailand.

 DATES: 

 Sat Jan 22nd - Fri Feb 4th 

 Sun Feb 13th - Sat Feb 26th 

 COST: $1950 USD per person 

The 2 weeks includes 11 days on board our 48' catamarans, 2 nights accommodation in beachfront villas in Phuket 
and one night in a hot springs spa resort in Ranong. On board, the majority of food and drink is included; 
generally 2 meals per day in Thailand, all food and drink in Burma. Drinks are also included (yes, that
means your pina coladas too!).

Prices are all per person, based on double cabin/room occupancy. No single
 supplements applicable. Flights are not included.
