Sailing Greece, adventures with SeaScape Sailing in Greece and Turkey

 April 27-May 3 GREECE 

If avoiding the tourist crowds is high on your priority list - late April/early May is a great time
of year to be here! It's quiet, peaceful and just lovely; the hills are still green and cloaked
in spring flowers; a wonderful time to go hike around them and explore. The temperatures are
also very condusive to those who like to get out and be active; high 70s/early 80s Fahrenheitduring the
day, perfect for hiking/biking/exploring the hidden coves and tiny villages around our favourite
Dodecanese islands. 
The winds are usually fairly light, with the occasional strong blow at this time of year.
