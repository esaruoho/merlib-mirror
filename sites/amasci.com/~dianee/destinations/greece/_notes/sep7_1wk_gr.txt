Sailing the Dodecanese Greek islands with SeaScape. Yacht charters
for singles, couples or crewed charter for groups. 

 Sept 7-13 

September.... and things start to become a little more mellow in the Greek isles. The main
heat of Aug dies away, and there is a kind of fall "glow" to the atmosphere. The meltemi 
starts to fade a little, but there's still plenty of great wind for sailing.

This will be a specifically one week route, sailing to as many of our favourite little spots in these islands as we can ! The route is a mixture of little, 
old-fashioned villages and some of the more famous islands like Patmos and Kalymnos... with 
some of our especially favourite tiny spots included, of course - like our little paradise 
island of Marathi, with two families, a lovely sandy beach and some of the best food around!
