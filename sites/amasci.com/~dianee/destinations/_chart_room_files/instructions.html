Welcome to the Seascape Chart Room

Here, we've put together some maps to help you get a feel for the islands and portages around the region.

You'll be able to take your turn at the helm when you sail with us, and you're also welcome to get familiar with plotting a course using the charts, books, and (most importantly) Weather Maps available.

Select a map from the list below, then investigate the ports by clicking with your mouse.
Dodecanese | Turkey

You can read up on the islands by clicking 'more..' or add them to your itinerary using the controls that pop up.

When your trip is set, you can sail it step by step, or just turn on the autopilot and enjoy the cruise.

You can find more goodies on the map by displaying the 'Features' using the check-boxes below the map list.

Really sailing is of course much different than this (It takes longer, but you get lots of fresh air) but we hope you enjoy playing with it.
Watch out for dolphins!
