Greetings from back in rainy ol' Seattle...

Nov 11th

Welcome on board our SeaScape website! Hope you have fun cruising around and checking us out... and of course, the fantastic sailing in Greece and Turkey.

Alas, we are no longer out there in sunny Greece... the '99 season has come to an end, and most of us are back under the grey skies of Seattle, planning our next season's sailing program. The summer 2000 schedule will be posted here in the coming week - stay tuned, or email us for next summer's upcoming dates.

However... as we'd all grow mold if we stayed in Seattle all winter - there is also our winter escape to the Land of Smiles - Thailand ! This is our 3rd year sailing there - and it's time to explore new grounds! In early March '00, we'll be sailing a one-way route from Phuket all the way down to Langkawi (Malaysia). So whilst we'll be visiting some of the old favourites, there'll be new, and completely "untouristy" anchorages to explore. We're pretty excited about it - again, details will be ready Real Soon Now ! :)

Our "mostly singles" trips seem to be getting more and more popular! - so, we're planning even MORE of them for next year ! Also by popular request, we'll be scheduling a few "clothing optional" trips. Details will be posted soon.

Please bear with us as we are in the process of updating our site for '00 in the next couple of weeks - if you don't see what you're looking for - do EMAIL us ! :)

Fair winds !

Diane Edwards
