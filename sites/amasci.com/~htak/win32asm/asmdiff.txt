 MASM and TASM differences

 STRUC may need to be changed to STRUCT (add 'T' at end) 

 EXTRN may need to be changed to EXTERN (add second 'E') 

 EQU may need to be changed to TEXTEQU for text macros 

 LARGE is needed by TASM 4, may need to eliminate for
