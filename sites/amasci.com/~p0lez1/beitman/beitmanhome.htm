EUROPEAN RADIO COLLECTORS-
I NEED YOUR HELP!

I am attempting to compile a collection of Schematics for Vintage European Radios for a future project that will be somewhat similar to the Beitman Compilations for U.S.-Made radios that are being made available for download on this site now.

What I am looking for is decent-quality servicing information for any radio manufactured outside of North America from 1920 thru 1975 or so, whether it was intended for the North Ameriacn market or not.

Some of these radios have a small pouch or envelope on the back which contains servicing information, so you may have this information and not even realize it!

In the U.S. and Canada, these schematics can be very difficult to find, and are sometimes absolutely necessary to troubleshoot problems with the complex circuitry these European sets often have. I would like to make a compilation of these schematics available throughout the world via the internet like I am doing right now with the Beitman books.

If you have any servicing information for one or more of these sets and would like to help, please e-mail me at scott@scottharvey.com
