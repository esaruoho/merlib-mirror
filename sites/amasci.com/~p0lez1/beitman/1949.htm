NOTE: Beitman's publisher cut too many corners with the 1949 book, and it shows. The book is only 160 pages (skinniest of the lot), yet has as many schematics in it as the 192 page books! Many of the schematics are incredibly hard to read because of the tiny print that resulted from rendering full-size diagrams as big as a small envelope.

To allieviate this problem, I have taken some of the harder to read prints and blown them up so that a half page of the book is as big as a full page. This greatly improved the readablity of these teeny weenie schematics. That's why some of the pages don't have page numbers and are "skinnier" than others when printed.
