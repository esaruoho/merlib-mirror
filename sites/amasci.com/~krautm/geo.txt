Geography of Paraguay 

 Paraguay is bordered to the south by Argentina , to the east and north by Brasil , and to the west by Bolivia .
Further to the south and east, on the Atlantic ocean, is Uruguay 

 A number of indigenous peoples populate Paraguay. For more
information, check the Ethnologue
database .
