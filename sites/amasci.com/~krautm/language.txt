Languages of Paraguay 

 Language 

 Paraguay has two official languages: Spanish and Guaran. 75%
of all Paraguayans speak Spanish, it is estimated. About 90%
speak Guaran. The concentration of those who speak Guaran
only is in the countryside, while those Paraguayans who speak
only Spanish live primarily in the capitol city of Asuncin.

 Here are some examples of the Guaran language:

 Mba'exip (mbaa-ay-sha-pa): How are you? (somewhat
formal) 

 Mba' la porte (mbaa-ay la por-tay): How's it going?
(informal) 

 ipor (e-pour-rah): nice, pretty, good 

 kara (kah-rah-e): Mr., lord 

 cuata (koon-ya-tai-e): girl
