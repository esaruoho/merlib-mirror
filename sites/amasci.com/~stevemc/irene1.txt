 Irene's Secret Birthday Page! 

 OK, here's the deal. Last October marked Irene's 50th
birthday. She'd said for a LONG time that she'd like to have a
food fight for the big 5-0. People were invited, holes cut in
garbage bags to serve as make-shift protective clothing, and when
the day arrived it was a rare, perfect sunny day in Bothell.
(Which, when you consider the alternative of trying to take a
massive food fight indoors, is more than a minor piece of good
luck!) 

 Preparations included a trip to the local Costco warehouse
store, where the major ingredients for this party cost less than
the wine or beer found at many a party (for those worried about WASTE .) 

 The party preparations included mixing up
 3 gallons of mashed potatoes, #10 cans of corn, and
 massive amounts of such food fight staples as jello and a
 half-dozen food service size aerosol cans of whipped
 cream. Here's a picture of Maria, hard at work preparing
 for her Mom's birthday party. 

 By mid-day the guests had arrived and assembled in the back
yard, to first eat, and then F I G H T !
