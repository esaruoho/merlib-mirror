Doggie Lifestyles 

 Relaxing on the Couch 

 The fastest breed of dog, Greyhounds can reach a top speed of 45 miles per hour, and can average 
 more than 30 miles per hour for distances up to one mile. 
 The Greyhound is a quiet and docile animal when not racing, and loves a cozy couch to curl up on. 
 Which is why Greyhounds are often called "40 mph couch potatoes".
