Coursing               What is Lure Coursing?  It's something we do almost every weekend. We participate in ASFA  (American Sighthound Field Association) trials along with other sighthounds.   The dogs compete around a course, following 3 white plastic bags that similate a hare running in the field. The dogs are judges on Endurance, Speed, Agility, Follow and Enthusiasm.   next page >
