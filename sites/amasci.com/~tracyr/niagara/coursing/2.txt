 How Does it Work? 
 In lure coursing the hounds chase an artificial lure, usually a strip of white plastic garbage
 bag, tied to a line. The line is laid out with a variety of turns and straight a ways to make
 a course of at least 600 yards. The speed of the lure is controlled by a switch run by the
 lure operator who tries to keep the lure an enticing distance ahead of the lead hound.
