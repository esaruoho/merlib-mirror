The Coffee Adiction chronicles

Yep, I'm slightly addicted to coffee. MMMM...mmmmm...a nice mocha...triple expresso! extra chocolate! Gets me buzzin....what a fine feeling. 

 My coffee addicted friend Scott and I, have been trying out the local coffee shops and what have you....and have become pretty damn picky about what we do and don't like! Imagine gay people with fricking opinions....!!

 At the top of our list, is Micheangelos, which is open till 11 pm, ( a good thing!) and has good coffee, and a rather quiet atmosphere. Next, Motherfools, on Williamson , is a fun place, with zany people, (especially on open-mic fridays) and a layed ba
ck feel. Cafe Assisi has earned HUGE points with us, for being the *ONLY* real coffee place open on Thanksgiving -- having very good coffee, and bright, fun layout...

The next one I'll mention is sure to stir the pot! Starbucks...yep...corperate coffee! how evil...but...damn it, its GOOD. it is NOT bitter. Its consistant. I know some of the 'smaller coffee houses' claim that organic is better, but, HELLO! bitter coffee
 doesn't TASTE good!!

Cafe Express Royale -- awesome smooth coffee -- the expresso drinks are sure to please -- very, very strong. I recomend the rassbery mocha's...yum! And they stay open late...till midnight some week nights!

 I will eventually organize this page so , it might actually be useful for a person wanting to try coffee...
