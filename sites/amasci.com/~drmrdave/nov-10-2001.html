Milkbaby and Realbaby!

Wow. Its been a while. I've had all sorts of little adventures that'd bore the hell out of anyone..but here is one that I find pretty damn entertaining to think about.

Some background information is warranted. I met this neat guy, by the name of Selim. He is originally from Turkey. He is always asking questions concerning what kinda guys I liked, so one day I showed him...I just pointed at a guy in a coffee shop and said 'there, that guy, he is my type'. The guy had glasses, and forever forward, was referred to by the nickname 'the glasses' by Selim. What is really strange/funny, is that, I actually did chat with this person later, online...he asked me if I went for coffee , and had recognized my pic from da webpage. So I gathered my courage and actually said hi to him the next time I saw him.. hehehe :-) More on *that* story later!

MilkBaby is a trance/trible band...really cool stuff! I've seen 'em quite a few times when they play locally, and went to a show in Milw once. So, a group of us, including Selim decided to see them. Selim had wanted to go out to Club 5 later, and sent me a halarious note saying that we'd see Milkbaby, and then go and see Realbaby... hehehe. So after Milkbaby (which totally rocked) we were off to Club Dive (hive? ) oh..5..yeah...feh! I caught up with a bunch of guys I know from chat there...some I hadn't seen for months! I generally am not big on bars, but I really enjoyed my night out, seeing old friends. It made me think on how many really cool guys I wouldn't of ever met, if it wasn't for chat. Yeah, some people call this chat thing 'lame' and that people who use it don't have lives...I don't agree with that at ALL. It is a way to meet other guys, in a neutral setting , without the pretense of smoke, drinking and the like. I'd prolly have bombed something by now if I hadn't run across chat. ha!

So, various things happened at the bar...I actually talked to a stranger! he was the geeky/intelligent looking type, and really appealed to me, so I said hi, and a few other things as well. His name was Tom... HI TOM if you are reading this...:-) I was harassed in the bathroom...nice. Some guy came in right behind me, and started asking if I knew a guy he had seen me talking to. I told him yeah, I know him, but can we talk about it elsewhere...geeez! They really need to get a locking door on that bathroom -- oh wait, what *AM* I thinking...hehehee. We stayed until the ugly lights came on...and watched the sidewalk sale commence... then took off. Fun night all in all!
