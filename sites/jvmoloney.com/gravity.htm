Gravity

Since Sir Isaac Newton, placed his theories of gravity into print, no one, including Albert Einstein, has been able to establish what constitutes gravity.  Newton believed that this force was pulling the planets towards the sun, whilst at the same time the rest of the stars in the universe were acting as a counterbalance by pulling in the opposite direction.

The mathematical calculations (inverse square law) for gravity, magnetism and electricity are the same. This fact alone is leading many scientists to believe that the true force in nature is magnetism because the graviton - gravity particle - has never been located despite many 'expensive' years looking for it.  Scientific thinking has also speculated that gravity (if it exists), light, electricity, magnetism and motion are somehow interlaced.  They are.
