Mirrors have been a mystery for 2500 years.  Find out why they work and discover the second link between mirrors, shop windows, water and light reflections and why a mirror rejects your electric light shadow when you stand in front of it.

Thomas Edison invented the light bulb in the 1880s and now 120 years later the mysterious link between electricity and light is finally solved.

The moon and sun are important for all life here on earth and this connection can be seen, even when we move.  Discover how we are all energetically linked to the sun, stars and the galaxies. In addition it was this orbiting trio, coupled to electricity and light, that created the oceans.

Shadows are all around us even on the dull days, during the night and when we are underground. They are constantly lurking under cars and do not disappear when the vehicle passes under a bridge, or parks in the shade.  Even on the dullest of dull days there is always a shadow beneath trees and shrubs. Grey shadows grow darker when the sun comes out despite the extra light bouncing about. Footballers have numerous shadows when in the centre of the pitch despite the floodlights all around the stadium.

All these scientific mysterious are resolved in OUT OF THE SHADOWS.
