The Book 

 Out of the Shadows 
 Synopsis 
 Out of the Shadows brings together Vincent Moloney's
discoveries about light, energy, and his theories on the nature of the universe which, if correct,
turns the whole of modern science on its head. It covers work in the
fields of light , natural electricity (not grid
electricity, although the two are linked), gravity , the
sun , the moon, and our links with the universe . In addition, it
examines how magnets work, the second current in magnets and all of life, and
the link between mirrors , electricity and light. How all life is
linked to our star (the sun) in two distinct ways, even in the middle of the
night. 
 Written in plain English for the lay-reader, Out of the
Shadows illustrates why there are many issues yet to be explained by modern
science, and describes how these revolutionary discoveries and theories can offer explanatory
power for our universe.
