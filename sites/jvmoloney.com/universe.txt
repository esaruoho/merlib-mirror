 The Universe

 One of the problems of the Big Bang Theory is the
unexplained mystery of why space is completely uniform - it is the same in all
directions - no frontiers where there is emptiness yet to be occupied from the
explosion. 
 We live on a small planet in a vast universe and despite
our tiny size we are part of it and part of the universe is within us all. 
 It is impossible to say that our planet is isolated from the whole and we
stand-alone. 
 Looking out at the stars and galaxies it is difficult to
believe that there is a connection between us and that pinpoint of light so far away that our minds cannot comprehend the distances. 
 The fact that we see small beams of star light that do not disperse and
fade with distance has intrigued men of learning for years. There is now a
simply (scientifically) proven explanation.
