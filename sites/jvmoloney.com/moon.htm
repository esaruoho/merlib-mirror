The Moon

The mystery of the moon is as old as life itself, but why is it there?   Strong evidence suggests that the moon came out of the earth billions of years ago.   Material brought back from the various Apollo missions all point to Mother Earth as being the original source.  The earth and moon are locked together in a double planetary system, with the sun at the centre, working together to produce life.  Remove any one of these three and there will be nothing but rock and dust a dead planet like Venus. What is the point of an orbiting mass simply moving the tides - all that energy going to waste, but there is now a scientific connection with life.

Relatively recent surveys of rock testify to the moon having had its own magnetic field (estimated at 3,000 gauss) when it first went into close orbit.  Where did this magnetic field come from?  The moon simply carried out its share of the earths magnetic field and it became an orbiting magnet inducting electricity from space, year zero.  Some time after the orbiting magnet began its journey it started to rain creating the conditions for life.   At certain times of the year a very weak lunar electric wake is still detectable at ground level.

When I found the first trace of Lunar electricity, what astounded me was the moon's position - very low on the eastern horizon.  The currents were here before the moon arrived.  Something totally unexpected was happening.

In the 1960s it was discovered that the moon is slowly drifting away from the earth and in time it will be gone forever.  As it moves away, towards the sun, there is a slow but constant change on the planet below and whether we like it or not there is no way to stop it.
