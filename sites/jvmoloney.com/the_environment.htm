The Environment

(Global Warming)

When I found indisputable evidence that light, a by-product of hydrogen, has to pass through the earth on its way to the sun, I began to perceive a link with global warming and light pollution.

Climate change has been increasingly noticeable over the last half of the 20th century, at a time when use of electrical power has continued to grow, and we know electricity produces heat and light.  Global electrification is now 100 years old and I believe this is no coincidence.

The other problem is the human race itself - at the last count there were around seven billion of us with the numbers growing relentlessly.  Moreover, to feed these billions we need animals in the millions, all taking in electricity and light and giving off heat.

Therefore, the greatest potential threat to life is, strangely enough, the basis of life itself - electricity and light.
