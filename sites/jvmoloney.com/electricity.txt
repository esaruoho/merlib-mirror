 Electricity

 It is now beyond doubt that all life retains low amounts of electricity. You
only have to walk through a hospital ward to realise how dependent doctors and
nurses are on the plethora of electronic equipment wired to their patients. 
 But where does the electricity come from; do we manufacture it inside our
bodies, or as energy from the sun how does it enter? 
 We know electricity and light are closely connected; electric light bulbs
prove that, so sun light the second energy force must also be within us. 

 Friction machine to collect electricity 
Indeed
it is and it can be measured with the correct equipment. 
 Even in the middle of the night, when we are deep underground, beneath
the oceans in submarines or sealed inside an armoured tank sunlight is still
detectable within us all. A fascinating question that is solved in Out of the
Shadows , how does it manage to penetrate?
