 Mirrors

Researchers
have been fascinated by the way light appears to penetrate the mirror giving the
observers not only a view of themselves but also of everything behind them. 
Quantum
Electro-Dynamics (QED) came closest to solving this problem when it was
suggested that light particles entering the mirror somehow activated the
electrons in the metallic coating behind the glass. 
 Brilliant - but QED couldnt get any further for it was not
able to explain why the particles came out at the exact opposite angle to that
at which the light went in. Additionally,
QED did not attempt to address the mysteries of glass and water, both of which
let light pass through at the same time allowing a plethora of reflections to
appear. 

 A prism acts in the same way as a mirror whilst allowing the
light to pass through 

Another ancient mystery is the stability of
reflection. Despite moving back
from the mirror, your head remains the same size and does not become smaller
with distance. Surprisingly, there
is an electrical link between shadows, light, reflections, mirrors, water, and
magnetism. In addition, our eyes perform like mirrors; they are magnetic because
of the electricity in our body.

 With an electric light behind, casting your
shadow, this same shadow will be rejected and cling to your chest when you stand
in front of a mirror, yet moving to one side your shadow will be cast back upon
the wall. There is clearly a link between the persons electricity, the
mirror, electric light and the Earths electro- magnetic field.
