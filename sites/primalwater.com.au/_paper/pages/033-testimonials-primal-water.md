            Thank you so much(!!!) for finding our bore water. I did ring you the day we struck water in June.

It was amazing – the drillers, Daren Britt, started at about 9 in the morning to drill and we had water by lunch time, around 12 noon, at 24 metres – unbelievable!

I was wondering why they didn't turn up for lunch, Trevor and I couldn't believe it. We were dumbfounded and excited.

The water tested 700, which was fresh and had a good flow rate. We got them to keep going, which continually increased the flow rate. They then struck water again at 45 metres, 55 metres, 80 metres, and 98 metres. We ended up with a flow of 3.8 l/s. We stopped at 104 metres. Thanks so much for your amazing skill in finding and divining the water!!            

            

                Maree Miller            

        

            

            

            Thank you for coming a few weeks ago with the GIS Analytics, LLC team to site two water bores for me. 

You explained to me how the team had worked out, with advanced mapping methods and analysis, why selected locations would produce good quality and a good flow of water. I have had six (6) wells dug over twenty years on my property andthey all came up with dust and no water. 

Fortunately, despite it being mid-winter, I was able to engage a drilling company to take this on. We chose one of the two potential sites that the team recommended, which was the one closest to where I wanted the bore. 

All of your predictions proved to be accurate. Water was discovered at all levels you had indicated and by 124 meters we were getting a stable 13,500 litres/hour (about 3,000 gallons/hour). This volume of water is more than sufficient for my stock and domestic needs. We did not drill any deeper, even though you indicated more water lower down. 

Although it is early days, the water appears to be of excellent quality, as predicted.

Again, thank you very much.             

            

                Charles Smith
