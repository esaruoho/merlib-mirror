 The following is a paper by H. Aspden published in Hadronic Journal, v. 9, pp. 137-140 (1986).

 MESON PRODUCTION BASED ON THOMSON ENERGY CORRELATION 

 Abstract : Attention is drawn to a remarkable energy correlation which uniquely determines the rest-mass energies of all the intermediate particles in the electron-proton energy spectrum. The correlation formula uses a classical expression formulated by J. J. Thomson, which represents the charge of a particle as confined within a sphere of radius 2e 2 /3mc 2 .
