ENERGY SCIENCE UPDATE


Until August 29 this web site had not been updated since May 1999. This was because I, the author, had diverted my attentions to an experimental project. That pursuit is still holding my attention and it will be a little while yet before I can develop these pages further on a regular basis. However, owing to one interested reader having expressed a little humour in sending me the odd E-Mail message asking if I had passed away, I did on August 29, 2000 launch four new web page items. Two of these, notably the third and fourth items, are new and of particular importance in the realm of physics, but the third will interest those concerned with new free energy motor technology, whereas the first is essentially a repetition, for the physics community and those interested in radio science, of an earlier message in these ENERGY SCIENCE web pages. Other Essays have now been added, the last on November 10, 2000.
