For those of you who are interested in papers of free energy devices, I must
recommend the "Proceedings of the 2nd International Symposium on New Energy:
2nd ISNE". These proceedings include more than 650 pages of interesting papers, 
e.g. a description on how to 'condition' the magnets used in Floyd Sweet's VTA!
As you can see below it include papers from very well-known names, e.g.
Harold Puthoff, Tom Bearden and many more

I was very skeptic when I ordered this paper and thought it was an attempt to
con some money, but I was pleasently surprised to see that it was for real, 
and for that matter a real goldmine!
So, I can honestly say that IF you are interested in free energy devices,
GET THIS PAPER ($50 and worth every cent)!

Sincerely

Bjvrn Lundberg
