       Abstract: This report results from an  extensive  survey of various
                 Patents Databases, searching for all patents relating to
                 the non-rocket space propulsion concept (i.e. Spacedrives).
     Definition: Spacedrive is the generic term used to denote a mechanism,
                 or principle by which a spaceship can be propelled
                 - a device, not a rocket -  that  would not have to carry
                 reaction mass to throw away.
Compilers Notes: This listing, and other spacedrives info, will not be
                 automatically posted but will be made available via
                 a website in the near future.
