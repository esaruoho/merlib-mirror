                   KeelyNet File Availability as of 10/7/91

      Due to many calls and messages relating the high cost of telephone
               bills as a result of downloading KeelyNet files,

                  WE HAVE DECIDED TO OFFER OUR FILES BY MAIL
                at $30 for the entire set of 6 - 1.2 MB disks.

   Many of our users have said, "I'd rather pay you than the phone company."

       All KeelyNet files are freely available THROUGH THE PHONE with no
        limitations beyond those imposed by time and quantity per day.

                      We practice what we preach in that
          information should be FREELY AVAILABLE to all who want it,
                           EXCEPT when relayed to us
               as proprietary or NOT to be publicly distributed.

      Since we NEVER INTENDED KeelyNet to be a revenue producing effort,
      we have decided to follow most other boards and charge $5 per disk,
                          with a minimum of 3 disks.

              The disk files will ONLY be distributed by AREA and
                           NOT by individual files.
                  This is because of the hassles involved in
                   selecting each file from a request list.

    ----------------------------------------------------------------------

    Ron and I both work full time (with overtime) jobs and have many other
                     interests which WILL NOT BE DIVERTED
               to casual duplication of readily available info.

         The idea is to get a complete set of files, then periodically
            drop in to update the set.  It is totally up to you....

            We do this only to save OUR USERS on their phone bills.
              Both Ron and I have had phone bills upwards of $300
        for a single month and can easily empathize with everyone else.

         KeelyNet ASCII files as of 10/07/91 span 6 - 1.2 MB floppies.
             This would be $30 for all 6 disks including postage.
         As our files continue to expand, so will the disks required.

            We have also been receiving letters and postcards from
               people interested in printouts of various files.
              In an attempt to provide this information to people
            who do not have computers, we will be offering any file
              at 10 cents per page, shipping will be additional.

          We will eventually show the number of pages per file in the
        file listings to assist in calculating the cost of duplication.
