About us

The Swedish Association for New Physics is a non-profit association. Our purpose is to:

  • be a forum for research at the border of or outside established scientific paradigms
  • contribute to the development of alternative energy sources and other technologies that are positive for humanity and nature
  • co-ordinate and promote research, and act as knowledge database

History

In the spring of 1991 a network was formed by independent reseachers in unconventional energy technology, and in flow technology related to ideas of the Austrian Viktor Schauberger. The purpose was to facilitate co-ordination and to get an overview of the research. In 1992 we got in contact with ”the network for boundary transcending science” in Sweden, an umbrella network for new research areas, and joined this network. Then to create a forum for research projects, and a platform organization, ”the Swedish Association for New Physics” was founded in february 1994, by some of the members of the original network.

Some research ideas

One of the prime objectives of the association is to initiate and co-ordinate research projects. It could be experiments and/or literature overviews within areas of research that members are interested in. One area that have been studied is so-called ”free energy technology”. The notion refers to
some fifty inventions during the 20:th century where is has been claimed that excess energy has been observed. The expression ”free energy” is rather misleading - what one has in view is that the energy is extracted more or less for free. Possibly it could be some kind of energy conversion form an unknown (everywhere present) source of energy. Some physicists has tried to relate the phenomena to the so-called zero point radiation in modern physics.

Members have also studied several difficult to explain or disputed experiments that are related to the above area. There phenomena involve anomalies in electromagnetic theory, potential waves, and some biological effects.

Another area studied by members is phenomena related to the Austrian Viktor Schauberger's ideas about water purification and energy extraction.

International networking

We support the idea of international networking, and an open creative atmosphere. As the lack of structure in unconventional research areas is striking, we support initiatives for creating research programs and a forum where inventors and researchers can get their claims objectively and honestly tested.
