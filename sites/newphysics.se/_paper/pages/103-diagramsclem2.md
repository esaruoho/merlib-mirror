 A few months back, we got a call from a friend who had heard of
this incredible motor that was said to run itself and generate excess
useable power. The details were unclear at the time and our friend
gathered more details and we met for lunch to discuss what he had found
out. A scanned image of the machine as it was described to him with
those details will be included in CLEM2.ZIP (when I get it done). It is
NOW done and is CLEM1.GIF, bundled as CLEM2.ZIP. As we understand it,
inventor Richard Clem died of a heart attack soon after the deal was
signed with the coal company. His workshop was raided by law
enforcement officials and all his notes and drawings were removed.
