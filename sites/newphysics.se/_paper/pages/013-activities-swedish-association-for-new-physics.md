Annual meeting in Stockholm: June 16th, 2012

      
On the annual meeting there will be a comparison between Lorentz     contraction and Sagnac effect. This theme is relevant in understanding     speed measurements like neutrino speed and implications are extensive     for fundamental physics which almost entirely rely on Lorentz     transformations.  Other themes and location of the meeting will be   presented later.
