Ove Tedenstig

Ove Tedenstig, the author of the new physical therory "Matter Unified".
Main ideas of this theory have been published since 1981 with name
"Absolute space theory" and "A new way to physics", that together with
separate articles in different scientific papers. 

The theory is based on some very fundamental laws of physics, Newtons
second law of force, hydromechanical laws and some hypothetical assumtions
of properties of vacuum space. 

On this ground most of all fundamental laws of physics are derived. New
insights in electromagnetism, gravitation, atomic quantum theory, the
atomic core, the elementary particles and the basic nature of light are in
this way offered. 

The author is by profession working by advanced industrial research and
development in electronic design.
