Welcome to our virtual journal Fnysik. Fnysik  is a forum for the discussion of phenomena at the border of, or  outside,  established paradigms.

      
Articles should have a reasonably degree of experimental support,  and/or  theoretical clarity. We reserve the right to scrutinize the articles  submitted  for publication. However, we try to be open-minded and publish as much  as possible.      

      
Views expressed in articles are the author's, of which the  association  takes no responsibility.
