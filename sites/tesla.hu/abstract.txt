 About the website: 

 The creation of this site is linked to a jubilee: the first edition of the
book of Gyrgy Egely, "Forbidden inventions" was published
ten years before. I am concerned with the research of machines and equipment
that are supposed to produce excess energy since then. I found some devices
which have a common attribution: their creator composed something using the
try-and-test method, something he did not know much about, but he could see
that something is out of order. There are two devices emerging out of the
pool. Their common attribute is having high power-density and producing the
most easily treatable form of energy source: electricity. Both are the
product of the past century. Although no operable device subsisted, the
written information we have may be enough to research and study them. These
two devices are the energy converters of Nikola Tesla and of Thomas
Henry Moray . This website treats the study on these two men, their
devices, and the documents, tools and theories related to them.
